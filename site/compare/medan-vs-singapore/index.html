How the climate of Medan, Indonesia compares with Singapore, Singapore: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Singapore is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Singapore is 0.8 °C warmer (Medan 26.3 °C, Singapore 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Medan 32.2 °C (April), Singapore 31.3 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Medan 21.1 °C, Singapore 22.3 °C.
  • Medan gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 2,911 mm against 2,424 mm.
  • Medan gets about 87 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,142 h against 2,055 h; about 1961–1990).
