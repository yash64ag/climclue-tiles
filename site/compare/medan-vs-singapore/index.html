Medan vs Singapore climate

How the climate of Medan, Indonesia compares with Singapore, Singapore, 630 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Singapore is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Singapore is 0.9 °C warmer (Medan 26.1 °C, Singapore 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Medan 30.7 °C (June), Singapore 28.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Medan 21.3 °C, Singapore 24.9 °C.
  • Medan gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 2,910 mm against 2,410 mm.
  • Medan gets about 87 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,142 h against 2,055 h; about 1961–1990).
