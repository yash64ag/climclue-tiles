How the climate of Kathmandu, Nepal compares with Xichang, Sichuan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kathmandu is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 18.7 °C against 18.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Kathmandu is 2.0 °C warmer (Kathmandu 23.0 °C, Xichang, Sichuan 21.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kathmandu 28.8 °C (May), Xichang, Sichuan 28.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kathmandu 2.4 °C, Xichang, Sichuan 4.4 °C.
  • Kathmandu gets 50% more rain and snow a year: 1,634 mm against 1,090 mm.
  • Xichang, Sichuan gets about 302 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,292 h against 1,990 h; about 1961–1990).
