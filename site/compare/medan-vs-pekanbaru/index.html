Medan vs Pekanbaru climate

How the climate of Medan, Indonesia compares with Pekanbaru, Indonesia, 460 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Pekanbaru is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.5 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Pekanbaru is 0.3 °C warmer (Medan 26.1 °C, Pekanbaru 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Medan 30.7 °C (May), Pekanbaru 30.6 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Medan 21.4 °C, Pekanbaru 22.3 °C.
  • Medan gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 2,911 mm against 2,786 mm.
  • Pekanbaru gets about 88 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,230 h against 2,142 h; about 1961–1990).
