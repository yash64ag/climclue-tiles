Herāt vs Shiraz climate

How the climate of Herāt, Afghanistan compares with Shiraz, Iran, 1,052 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shiraz is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 17.7 °C against 16.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Shiraz is 2.6 °C warmer (Herāt 22.3 °C, Shiraz 24.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Herāt 37.0 °C (July), Shiraz 38.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Herāt -1.3 °C, Shiraz -1.4 °C.
  • Shiraz gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 423 mm against 336 mm.
  • Shiraz gets about 239 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,282 h against 3,043 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Herāt 18 cm, Shiraz 36 cm (ERA5-Land).
