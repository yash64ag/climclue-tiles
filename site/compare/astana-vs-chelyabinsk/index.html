Astana vs Chelyabinsk climate

How the climate of Astana, Kazakhstan compares with Chelyabinsk, Russia, 800 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Astana is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 3.5 °C against 3.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Astana is 1.8 °C warmer (Astana 18.6 °C, Chelyabinsk 16.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Astana 25.9 °C (July), Chelyabinsk 23.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Astana -17.3 °C, Chelyabinsk -17.1 °C.
  • Chelyabinsk gets 40% more rain and snow a year: 460 mm against 329 mm.
  • Astana gets about 366 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,450 h against 2,084 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Astana 100 cm, Chelyabinsk 123 cm (ERA5-Land).
