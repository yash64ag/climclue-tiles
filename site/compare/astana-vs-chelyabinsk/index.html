How the climate of Astana, Kazakhstan compares with Chelyabinsk, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Astana is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 3.5 °C against 3.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Chelyabinsk is 2.0 °C warmer (Astana -7.5 °C, Chelyabinsk -5.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Astana 26.7 °C (July), Chelyabinsk 25.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Astana -18.6 °C, Chelyabinsk -18.1 °C.
  • Chelyabinsk gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 460 mm against 334 mm.
  • Astana gets about 366 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,450 h against 2,084 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Astana 100 cm, Chelyabinsk 123 cm (ERA5-Land).
