How the climate of Lanzhou, China compares with Pueblo, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Pueblo is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 12.0 °C against 11.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Lanzhou is 2.3 °C warmer (Lanzhou 13.3 °C, Pueblo 11.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lanzhou 29.8 °C (July), Pueblo 34.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lanzhou -8.8 °C, Pueblo -9.3 °C.
  • Pueblo gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 342 mm against 285 mm.
  • Pueblo gets about 895 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,371 h against 2,476 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lanzhou 7.4 cm, Pueblo 34 cm (ERA5-Land).
