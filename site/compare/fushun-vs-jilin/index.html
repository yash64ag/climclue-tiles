Fushun vs Jilin climate

How the climate of Fushun, China compares with Jilin, China, 305 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fushun is 2.6 °C (4.7 °F) warmer over the year: 8.5 °C against 5.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Fushun is 4.3 °C warmer (Fushun -8.2 °C, Jilin -12.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fushun 28.1 °C (July), Jilin 26.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fushun -18.6 °C, Jilin -22.4 °C.
  • Fushun gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 765 mm against 650 mm.
  • Fushun gets about 29 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,534 h against 2,506 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fushun 72 cm, Jilin 101 cm (ERA5-Land).
