Fortaleza vs Recife climate

How the climate of Fortaleza, Brazil compares with Recife, Brazil, 630 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fortaleza is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Fortaleza is 2.1 °C warmer (Fortaleza 26.6 °C, Recife 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fortaleza 30.2 °C (November), Recife 29.6 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Fortaleza 24.1 °C, Recife 22.4 °C.
  • Recife gets 41% more rain and snow a year: 1,853 mm against 1,314 mm.
  • Fortaleza gets about 67 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,643 h against 2,577 h; about 1961–1990).
