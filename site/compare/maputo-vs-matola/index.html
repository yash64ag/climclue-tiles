Maputo vs Matola climate

How the climate of Maputo, Mozambique compares with Matola, Mozambique, 12 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Matola is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 22.9 °C against 22.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Matola is 0.3 °C warmer (Maputo 25.0 °C, Matola 25.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maputo 28.1 °C (January), Matola 29.1 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Maputo 15.9 °C, Matola 15.0 °C.
  • Maputo gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 785 mm against 759 mm.
  • Maputo gets about 19 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,827 h against 2,808 h; about 1961–1990).
