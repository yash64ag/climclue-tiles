How the climate of Kaduna, Nigeria compares with Sokoto, Nigeria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Sokoto is 3.9 °C (7.0 °F) warmer over the year: 28.9 °C against 25.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Sokoto is 6.3 °C warmer (Kaduna 24.9 °C, Sokoto 31.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kaduna 35.5 °C (March), Sokoto 41.0 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Kaduna 15.1 °C, Sokoto 16.1 °C.
  • Kaduna gets 112% more rain and snow a year: 1,204 mm against 567 mm.
  • Sokoto gets about 104 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,789 h against 2,685 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Sokoto in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Sokoto in 11 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Sunnier: Sokoto in 8 of 12 months
