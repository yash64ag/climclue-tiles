Minhang vs Qingpu climate

How the climate of Minhang, China compares with Qingpu, China, 25 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Minhang is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.7 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Minhang is 0.4 °C warmer (Minhang 7.0 °C, Qingpu 6.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Minhang 31.8 °C (July), Qingpu 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Minhang 1.1 °C, Qingpu 0.7 °C.
  • Minhang gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,467 mm against 1,403 mm.
  • Minhang gets about 1 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,959 h against 1,958 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Minhang 8.6 cm, Qingpu 10 cm (ERA5-Land).
