How the climate of Jeju City, South Korea compares with Yokohama, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yokohama is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 16.0 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Yokohama is 1.2 °C warmer (Jeju City 16.8 °C, Yokohama 18.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jeju City 29.4 °C (August), Yokohama 29.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Jeju City 2.9 °C, Yokohama 2.0 °C.
  • Jeju City gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 1,761 mm against 1,530 mm.
  • Jeju City gets about 277 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,105 h against 1,827 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jeju City 12 cm, Yokohama 11 cm (ERA5-Land).
