Jeju City vs Yokohama climate

How the climate of Jeju City, South Korea compares with Yokohama, Japan, 1,221 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yokohama is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 16.0 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Yokohama is 1.2 °C warmer (Jeju City 16.8 °C, Yokohama 18.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jeju City 27.5 °C (August), Yokohama 27.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Jeju City 4.4 °C, Yokohama 3.1 °C.
  • Jeju City gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 1,761 mm against 1,530 mm.
  • Jeju City gets about 277 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,105 h against 1,827 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jeju City 12 cm, Yokohama 10 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Jeju City in 9 of 12 months
