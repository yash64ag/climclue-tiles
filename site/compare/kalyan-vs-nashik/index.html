Kalyān vs Nashik climate

How the climate of Kalyān, India compares with Nashik, India, 108 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kalyān is 2.7 °C (4.8 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 24.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Kalyān is 3.7 °C warmer (Kalyān 23.9 °C, Nashik 20.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kalyān 34.7 °C (April), Nashik 36.1 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Kalyān 15.9 °C, Nashik 11.0 °C.
  • Kalyān gets 89% more rain and snow a year: 2,234 mm against 1,180 mm.
  • Nashik gets about 52 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,758 h against 2,706 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Kalyān in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Nashik in 11 of 12 months
