Bielsko-Biala vs Munich climate

How the climate of Bielsko-Biala, Poland compares with Munich, Germany, 576 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Munich is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 9.0 °C against 8.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Munich is 1.9 °C warmer (Bielsko-Biala -2.1 °C, Munich -0.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bielsko-Biala 24.0 °C (July), Munich 23.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bielsko-Biala -5.4 °C, Munich -3.5 °C.
  • Munich gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,006 mm against 927 mm.
  • Munich gets about 133 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,655 h against 1,522 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bielsko-Biala 123 cm, Munich 97 cm (ERA5-Land).
