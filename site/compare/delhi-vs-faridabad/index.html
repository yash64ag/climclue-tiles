How the climate of Delhi, India compares with Faridabad, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Faridabad is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 24.9 °C against 24.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Faridabad is 0.3 °C warmer (Delhi 22.5 °C, Faridabad 22.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Delhi 39.6 °C (May), Faridabad 39.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Delhi 6.6 °C, Faridabad 6.7 °C.
  • Faridabad gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 708 mm against 646 mm.
  • Faridabad gets about 2 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,681 h against 2,679 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Faridabad in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Delhi in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Faridabad in 7 of 12 months
