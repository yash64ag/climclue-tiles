Delhi vs Faridabad climate

How the climate of Delhi, India compares with Faridabad, India, 28 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Delhi and Faridabad have the same yearly average temperature (24.8 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Faridabad is 0.1 °C warmer (Delhi 16.9 °C, Faridabad 17.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Delhi 39.3 °C (May), Faridabad 39.4 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Delhi 7.7 °C, Faridabad 7.8 °C.
  • Faridabad gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 693 mm against 641 mm.
  • Faridabad gets about 2 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,681 h against 2,679 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Faridabad in 5 of 12 months (7 equal)
  • Drier: Delhi in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Faridabad in 7 of 12 months
