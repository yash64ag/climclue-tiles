Asunción vs Formosa climate

How the climate of Asunción, Paraguay compares with Formosa, Argentina, 113 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Asunción is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 22.8 °C against 22.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Asunción is 0.8 °C warmer (Asunción 17.3 °C, Formosa 16.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Asunción 32.3 °C (January), Formosa 32.4 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Asunción 13.5 °C, Formosa 12.8 °C.
  • Formosa gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,472 mm against 1,426 mm.
  • Asunción gets about 143 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,764 h against 2,621 h; about 1961–1990).
