How the climate of Asunción, Paraguay compares with Formosa, Argentina: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Asunción is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 22.6 °C against 22.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Asunción is 0.6 °C warmer (Asunción 17.1 °C, Formosa 16.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Asunción 32.7 °C (January), Formosa 33.3 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Asunción 12.4 °C, Formosa 11.2 °C.
  • Formosa gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,484 mm against 1,439 mm.
  • Asunción gets about 143 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,764 h against 2,621 h; about 1961–1990).
