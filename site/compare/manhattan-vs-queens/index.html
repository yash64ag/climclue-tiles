How the climate of Manhattan, New York, United States compares with Queens, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Manhattan, New York and Queens have the same yearly average temperature (12.7 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Manhattan, New York is 0.4 °C warmer (Manhattan, New York 16.7 °C, Queens 16.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manhattan, New York 29.4 °C (July), Queens 28.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Manhattan, New York -3.4 °C, Queens -3.0 °C.
  • Manhattan, New York gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,232 mm against 1,199 mm.
  • Manhattan, New York gets about 6 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,529 h against 2,523 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Manhattan, New York 59 cm, Queens 55 cm (ERA5-Land).
