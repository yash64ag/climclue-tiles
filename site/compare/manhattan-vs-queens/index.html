Main differences

  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Queens is 0.5 °C warmer (Manhattan, New York 0.0 °C, Queens 0.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manhattan, New York 29.1 °C (July), Queens 29.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Manhattan, New York -3.4 °C, Queens -2.8 °C.
  • Manhattan, New York gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,232 mm against 1,199 mm.
