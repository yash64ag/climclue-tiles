Mwanza vs Nairobi climate

How the climate of Mwanza, Tanzania compares with Nairobi, Kenya, 456 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mwanza is 4.2 °C (7.6 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 18.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Mwanza is 5.9 °C warmer (Mwanza 22.7 °C, Nairobi 16.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mwanza 28.9 °C (June), Nairobi 27.4 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Mwanza 16.5 °C, Nairobi 11.5 °C.
  • Mwanza gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 988 mm against 850 mm.
  • Mwanza gets about 324 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,789 h against 2,464 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
