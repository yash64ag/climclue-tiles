Mwanza vs Nairobi climate

How the climate of Mwanza, Tanzania compares with Nairobi, Kenya, 456 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mwanza is 3.8 °C (6.9 °F) warmer over the year: 22.7 °C against 18.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Mwanza is 5.7 °C warmer (Mwanza 22.9 °C, Nairobi 17.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mwanza 25.8 °C (September), Nairobi 26.4 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Mwanza 18.8 °C, Nairobi 12.2 °C.
  • Mwanza gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 1,049 mm against 860 mm.
  • Mwanza gets about 324 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,789 h against 2,464 h; about 1961–1990).
