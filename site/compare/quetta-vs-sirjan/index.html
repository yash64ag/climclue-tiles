How the climate of Quetta, Pakistan compares with Sirjan, Iran: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Sirjan is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 17.8 °C against 17.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Sirjan is 1.7 °C warmer (Quetta 27.7 °C, Sirjan 29.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Quetta 36.8 °C (June), Sirjan 37.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Quetta -2.0 °C, Sirjan -2.0 °C.
  • Quetta gets 68% more rain and snow a year: 317 mm against 189 mm.
  • Quetta gets about 218 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,333 h against 3,115 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Quetta 10 cm, Sirjan 5.3 cm (ERA5-Land).
