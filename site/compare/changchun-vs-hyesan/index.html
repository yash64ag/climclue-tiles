How the climate of Changchun, China compares with Hyesan, North Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Changchun is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 6.5 °C against 5.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Changchun is 3.4 °C warmer (Changchun 21.4 °C, Hyesan 18.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changchun 27.8 °C (July), Hyesan 26.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changchun -19.4 °C, Hyesan -19.8 °C.
  • Changchun gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 635 mm against 618 mm.
  • Changchun gets about 241 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,570 h against 2,329 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changchun 33 cm, Hyesan 87 cm (ERA5-Land).
