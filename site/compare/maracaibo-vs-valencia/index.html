Maracaibo vs Valencia climate

How the climate of Maracaibo, Venezuela compares with Valencia, Venezuela, 399 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Maracaibo is 4.1 °C (7.4 °F) warmer over the year: 28.7 °C against 24.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Maracaibo is 5.0 °C warmer (Maracaibo 28.2 °C, Valencia 23.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maracaibo 34.0 °C (March), Valencia 28.7 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Maracaibo 24.0 °C, Valencia 19.8 °C.
  • Valencia gets 108% more rain and snow a year: 1,379 mm against 663 mm.
  • Valencia gets about 126 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,706 h against 2,580 h; about 1961–1990).
