Maracaibo vs Valencia climate

How the climate of Maracaibo, Venezuela compares with Valencia, Venezuela, 399 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Maracaibo is 3.8 °C (6.8 °F) warmer over the year: 28.5 °C against 24.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Maracaibo is 4.5 °C warmer (Maracaibo 27.9 °C, Valencia 23.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maracaibo 33.8 °C (March), Valencia 28.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Maracaibo 23.9 °C, Valencia 20.0 °C.
  • Valencia gets 88% more rain and snow a year: 1,267 mm against 673 mm.
  • Valencia gets about 126 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,706 h against 2,580 h; about 1961–1990).
