How the climate of Almaty, Kazakhstan compares with Madison, Wisconsin, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Almaty is 2.2 °C (4.0 °F) warmer over the year: 10.4 °C against 8.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Almaty is 3.5 °C warmer (Almaty 11.7 °C, Madison, Wisconsin 8.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Almaty 30.4 °C (July), Madison, Wisconsin 28.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Almaty -9.1 °C, Madison, Wisconsin -12.2 °C.
  • Almaty gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 926 mm against 913 mm.
  • Almaty gets about 37 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,460 h against 2,423 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Almaty 145 cm, Madison, Wisconsin 104 cm (ERA5-Land).
