Almaty vs Madison climate

How the climate of Almaty, Kazakhstan compares with Madison, United States, 10,320 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Almaty is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 9.1 °C against 8.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Almaty is 1.7 °C warmer (Almaty 10.4 °C, Madison 8.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Almaty 27.2 °C (July), Madison 27.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Almaty -9.7 °C, Madison -10.6 °C.
  • Almaty gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,006 mm against 965 mm.
  • Almaty gets about 37 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,460 h against 2,423 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Almaty 145 cm, Madison 103 cm (ERA5-Land).
