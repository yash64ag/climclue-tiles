Madinah vs Makkah climate

How the climate of Madinah, Saudi Arabia compares with Makkah, Saudi Arabia, 339 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Makkah is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 29.2 °C against 27.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Makkah is 4.3 °C warmer (Madinah 17.8 °C, Makkah 22.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Madinah 42.2 °C (August), Makkah 41.2 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Madinah 11.7 °C, Makkah 16.6 °C.
  • Makkah gets 105% more rain and snow a year: 129 mm against 63 mm.
  • Madinah gets about 331 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,599 h against 3,268 h; about 1961–1990).
