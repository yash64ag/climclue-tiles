Bayan Nur vs Ordos climate

How the climate of Bayan Nur, China compares with Ordos, China, 239 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bayan Nur is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 8.4 °C against 7.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Bayan Nur is 2.1 °C warmer (Bayan Nur 24.9 °C, Ordos 22.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bayan Nur 31.5 °C (July), Ordos 28.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bayan Nur -16.4 °C, Ordos -15.8 °C.
  • Ordos gets 101% more rain and snow a year: 330 mm against 164 mm.
  • Bayan Nur gets about 98 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,006 h against 2,907 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bayan Nur 6.4 cm, Ordos 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
