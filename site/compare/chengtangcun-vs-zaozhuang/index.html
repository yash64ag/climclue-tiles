How the climate of Chengtangcun, China compares with Zaozhuang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Chengtangcun is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 14.4 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Chengtangcun is 0.4 °C warmer (Chengtangcun 25.1 °C, Zaozhuang 24.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chengtangcun 31.1 °C (July), Zaozhuang 30.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chengtangcun -5.0 °C, Zaozhuang -4.9 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (735 mm and 738 mm).
  • Chengtangcun gets about 29 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,366 h against 2,337 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chengtangcun 11 cm, Zaozhuang 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
