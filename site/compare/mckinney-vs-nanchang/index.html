How the climate of McKinney, United States compares with Nanchang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Nanchang is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 18.4 °C against 18.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when McKinney is 1.5 °C warmer (McKinney 13.3 °C, Nanchang 11.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: McKinney 35.4 °C (August), Nanchang 33.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: McKinney 0.8 °C, Nanchang 2.7 °C.
  • Nanchang gets 68% more rain and snow a year: 1,796 mm against 1,067 mm.
  • McKinney gets about 969 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,808 h against 1,839 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: McKinney 2.3 cm, Nanchang 6.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
