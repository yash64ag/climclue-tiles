McKinney vs Nanchang climate

How the climate of McKinney, United States compares with Nanchang, China, 12,330 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Nanchang is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 18.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Nanchang is 1.4 °C warmer (McKinney 28.6 °C, Nanchang 30.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: McKinney 34.0 °C (August), Nanchang 33.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: McKinney 3.4 °C, Nanchang 2.2 °C.
  • Nanchang gets 63% more rain and snow a year: 1,801 mm against 1,108 mm.
  • McKinney gets about 969 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,808 h against 1,839 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: McKinney 2.3 cm, Nanchang 6.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
