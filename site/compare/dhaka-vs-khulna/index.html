Dhaka vs Khulna climate

How the climate of Dhaka, Bangladesh compares with Khulna, Bangladesh, 132 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Khulna is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 25.9 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Khulna is 0.9 °C warmer (Dhaka 28.1 °C, Khulna 28.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dhaka 32.6 °C (April), Khulna 34.1 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Dhaka 12.8 °C, Khulna 13.2 °C.
  • Dhaka gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 2,161 mm against 1,886 mm.
  • Dhaka gets about 10 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,099 h against 2,090 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
