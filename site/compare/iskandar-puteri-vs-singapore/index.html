Iskandar Puteri vs Singapore climate

How the climate of Iskandar Puteri, Malaysia compares with Singapore, Singapore, 28 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Singapore is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Singapore is 0.1 °C warmer (Iskandar Puteri 27.1 °C, Singapore 27.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iskandar Puteri 29.0 °C (April), Singapore 28.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Iskandar Puteri 24.3 °C, Singapore 24.9 °C.
  • Singapore gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 2,410 mm against 2,368 mm.
  • Iskandar Puteri gets about 13 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,068 h against 2,055 h; about 1961–1990).
