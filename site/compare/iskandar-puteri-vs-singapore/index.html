How the climate of Iskandar Puteri, Malaysia compares with Singapore, Singapore: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Singapore is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Singapore is 0.2 °C warmer (Iskandar Puteri 27.6 °C, Singapore 27.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iskandar Puteri 31.3 °C (May), Singapore 31.3 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Iskandar Puteri 22.4 °C, Singapore 22.3 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (2,426 mm and 2,424 mm).
  • Iskandar Puteri gets about 13 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,068 h against 2,055 h; about 1961–1990).
