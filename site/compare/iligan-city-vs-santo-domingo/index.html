Iligan City vs Santo Domingo climate

How the climate of Iligan City, Philippines compares with Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, 16,655 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Santo Domingo is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 25.5 °C against 25.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Santo Domingo is 1.0 °C warmer (Iligan City 25.7 °C, Santo Domingo 26.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iligan City 28.0 °C (May), Santo Domingo 30.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Iligan City 21.1 °C, Santo Domingo 21.0 °C.
  • Iligan City gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,883 mm against 1,688 mm.
  • Santo Domingo gets about 558 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,775 h against 2,217 h; about 1961–1990).
