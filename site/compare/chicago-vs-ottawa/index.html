Chicago vs Ottawa climate

How the climate of Chicago, United States compares with Ottawa, Canada, 1,039 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Chicago is 3.7 °C (6.7 °F) warmer over the year: 10.3 °C against 6.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Chicago is 6.2 °C warmer (Chicago -4.0 °C, Ottawa -10.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chicago 27.8 °C (July), Ottawa 26.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chicago -6.7 °C, Ottawa -14.6 °C.
  • Chicago gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,064 mm against 974 mm.
  • Chicago gets about 477 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,470 h against 1,993 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chicago 105 cm, Ottawa 211 cm (ERA5-Land).
