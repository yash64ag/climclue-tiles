Chicago vs Ottawa climate

How the climate of Chicago, United States compares with Ottawa, Canada, 1,039 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Chicago is 3.7 °C (6.7 °F) warmer over the year: 10.3 °C against 6.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Chicago is 6.1 °C warmer (Chicago -4.0 °C, Ottawa -10.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chicago 29.8 °C (July), Ottawa 26.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chicago -8.4 °C, Ottawa -14.6 °C.
  • Chicago gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,073 mm against 960 mm.
  • Chicago gets about 477 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,470 h against 1,993 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chicago 90 cm, Ottawa 185 cm (ERA5-Land).
