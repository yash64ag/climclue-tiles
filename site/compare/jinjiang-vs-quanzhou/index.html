Jinjiang vs Quanzhou climate

How the climate of Jinjiang, China compares with Quanzhou, China, 11 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jinjiang and Quanzhou have the same yearly average temperature (21.4 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Jinjiang is 0.3 °C warmer (Jinjiang 15.1 °C, Quanzhou 14.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinjiang 31.0 °C (July), Quanzhou 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinjiang 9.2 °C, Quanzhou 8.3 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,252 mm and 1,251 mm).
  • Jinjiang gets about 9 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,967 h against 1,958 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 5 months each
  • Drier: Quanzhou in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Jinjiang in 12 of 12 months
