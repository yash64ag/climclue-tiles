How the climate of Jinjiang, Fujian, China compares with Quanzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Quanzhou is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 21.5 °C against 21.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Quanzhou is 0.4 °C warmer (Jinjiang, Fujian 28.7 °C, Quanzhou 29.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinjiang, Fujian 32.4 °C (July), Quanzhou 32.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinjiang, Fujian 9.7 °C, Quanzhou 9.5 °C.
  • Jinjiang, Fujian gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,228 mm against 1,162 mm.
  • Jinjiang, Fujian gets about 9 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,967 h against 1,958 h; about 1961–1990).
