Meishan vs Neijiang climate

How the climate of Meishan, China compares with Neijiang, China, 129 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Neijiang is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 17.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Neijiang is 1.2 °C warmer (Meishan 26.1 °C, Neijiang 27.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Meishan 29.8 °C (July), Neijiang 30.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Meishan 3.3 °C, Neijiang 4.3 °C.
  • Meishan gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,147 mm against 1,088 mm.
  • Meishan gets about 105 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,410 h against 1,305 h; about 1961–1990).
