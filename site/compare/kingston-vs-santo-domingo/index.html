How the climate of Kingston, Jamaica compares with Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kingston is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 26.3 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Kingston is 1.0 °C warmer (Kingston 27.8 °C, Santo Domingo 26.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kingston 32.5 °C (August), Santo Domingo 31.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kingston 19.6 °C, Santo Domingo 18.9 °C.
  • Santo Domingo gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 1,688 mm against 1,383 mm.
  • Santo Domingo gets about 235 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,775 h against 2,540 h; about 1961–1990).
