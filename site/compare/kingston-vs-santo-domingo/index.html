Kingston vs Santo Domingo climate

How the climate of Kingston, Jamaica compares with Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, 731 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kingston and Santo Domingo have the same yearly average temperature (25.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Santo Domingo is 0.2 °C warmer (Kingston 24.4 °C, Santo Domingo 24.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kingston 29.4 °C (August), Santo Domingo 29.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kingston 21.9 °C, Santo Domingo 21.3 °C.
  • Santo Domingo gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,711 mm against 1,500 mm.
  • Santo Domingo gets about 235 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,775 h against 2,540 h; about 1961–1990).
