Kalyān vs Pune climate

How the climate of Kalyān, India compares with Pune, India, 110 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kalyān is 2.1 °C (3.9 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 24.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Kalyān is 2.9 °C warmer (Kalyān 26.1 °C, Pune 23.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kalyān 34.7 °C (April), Pune 37.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Kalyān 15.9 °C, Pune 12.0 °C.
  • Kalyān gets 124% more rain and snow a year: 2,234 mm against 999 mm.
  • Pune gets about 87 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,793 h against 2,706 h; about 1961–1990).
