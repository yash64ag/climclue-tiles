How the climate of Mianyang, Sichuan, China compares with San Luis, Argentina: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Mianyang, Sichuan and San Luis have the same yearly average temperature (17.3 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when San Luis is 17.6 °C warmer (Mianyang, Sichuan 6.5 °C, San Luis 24.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mianyang, Sichuan 30.5 °C (July), San Luis 31.2 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Mianyang, Sichuan 2.6 °C, San Luis 2.7 °C.
  • San Luis gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,110 mm against 919 mm.
  • San Luis gets about 1,627 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,021 h against 1,394 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
