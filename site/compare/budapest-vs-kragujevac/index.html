How the climate of Budapest, Hungary compares with Kragujevac, Serbia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Budapest and Kragujevac have the same yearly average temperature (12.0 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Budapest is 0.4 °C warmer (Budapest 0.8 °C, Kragujevac 0.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Budapest 28.0 °C (July), Kragujevac 28.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Budapest -1.7 °C, Kragujevac -3.0 °C.
  • Kragujevac gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 642 mm against 618 mm.
  • Kragujevac gets about 68 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,968 h against 1,899 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Budapest 55 cm, Kragujevac 58 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Drier: Budapest in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Kragujevac in 8 of 12 months
