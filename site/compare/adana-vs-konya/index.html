Adana vs Konya climate

How the climate of Adana, Turkey compares with Konya, Turkey, 269 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Adana is 6.1 °C (10.9 °F) warmer over the year: 18.9 °C against 12.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Adana is 7.7 °C warmer (Adana 9.8 °C, Konya 2.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Adana 33.8 °C (August), Konya 30.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Adana 4.3 °C, Konya -3.5 °C.
  • Adana gets 74% more rain and snow a year: 714 mm against 410 mm.
  • Adana gets about 72 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,992 h against 2,920 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Adana 0.2 cm, Konya 48 cm (ERA5-Land).
