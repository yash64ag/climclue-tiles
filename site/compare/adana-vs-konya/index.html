How the climate of Adana, Turkey compares with Konya, Turkey: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Adana is 5.8 °C (10.4 °F) warmer over the year: 18.9 °C against 13.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Adana is 7.4 °C warmer (Adana 9.7 °C, Konya 2.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Adana 34.2 °C (August), Konya 32.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Adana 3.7 °C, Konya -2.8 °C.
  • Adana gets 76% more rain and snow a year: 687 mm against 389 mm.
  • Adana gets about 72 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,992 h against 2,920 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Adana 0.2 cm, Konya 48 cm (ERA5-Land).
