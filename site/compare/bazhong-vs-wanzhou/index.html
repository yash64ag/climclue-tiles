Bazhong vs Wanzhou climate

How the climate of Bazhong, China compares with Wanzhou, China, 199 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Wanzhou is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 18.2 °C against 17.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Wanzhou is 1.5 °C warmer (Bazhong 7.3 °C, Wanzhou 8.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bazhong 29.8 °C (July), Wanzhou 31.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bazhong 1.1 °C, Wanzhou 3.4 °C.
  • Wanzhou gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,391 mm against 1,308 mm.
  • Bazhong gets about 55 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,456 h against 1,401 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bazhong 1.6 cm, Wanzhou 0.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
