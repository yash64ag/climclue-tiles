How the climate of Bazhong, China compares with Wanzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Wanzhou is 2.1 °C (3.8 °F) warmer over the year: 19.4 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Wanzhou is 2.5 °C warmer (Bazhong 26.9 °C, Wanzhou 29.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bazhong 31.3 °C (July), Wanzhou 34.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bazhong 3.0 °C, Wanzhou 5.9 °C.
  • Bazhong gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,298 mm against 1,225 mm.
  • Bazhong gets about 55 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,456 h against 1,401 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bazhong 1.6 cm, Wanzhou 0.6 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Wanzhou in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Bazhong in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Bazhong in 8 of 12 months
