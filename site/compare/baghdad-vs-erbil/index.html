Baghdad vs Erbil climate

How the climate of Baghdad, Iraq compares with Erbil, Iraq, 319 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Baghdad is 5.0 °C (9.1 °F) warmer over the year: 25.2 °C against 20.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Baghdad is 6.6 °C warmer (Baghdad 31.1 °C, Erbil 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baghdad 47.1 °C (July), Erbil 41.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baghdad 5.3 °C, Erbil 2.2 °C.
  • Erbil gets 291% more rain and snow a year: 625 mm against 160 mm.
  • Baghdad gets about 289 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,480 h against 3,190 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baghdad 0 cm, Erbil 4.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
