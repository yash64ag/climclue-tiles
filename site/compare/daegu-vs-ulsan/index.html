Daegu vs Ulsan climate

How the climate of Daegu, South Korea compares with Ulsan, South Korea, 74 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ulsan is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 14.6 °C against 13.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Ulsan is 2.9 °C warmer (Daegu 2.0 °C, Ulsan 5.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daegu 29.4 °C (August), Ulsan 28.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Daegu -5.0 °C, Ulsan -1.8 °C.
  • Ulsan gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,192 mm against 1,062 mm.
  • Daegu gets about 193 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,483 h against 2,290 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daegu 20 cm, Ulsan 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
