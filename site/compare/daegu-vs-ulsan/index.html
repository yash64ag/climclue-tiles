How the climate of Daegu, South Korea compares with Ulsan, South Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ulsan is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 14.8 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Ulsan is 2.6 °C warmer (Daegu 2.3 °C, Ulsan 4.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daegu 31.1 °C (August), Ulsan 30.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Daegu -5.3 °C, Ulsan -2.2 °C.
  • Ulsan gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,186 mm against 979 mm.
  • Daegu gets about 193 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,483 h against 2,290 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daegu 13 cm, Ulsan 6.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
