Lilongwe vs Lusaka climate

How the climate of Lilongwe, Malawi compares with Lusaka, Zambia, 613 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lilongwe is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 20.9 °C against 20.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Lilongwe is 1.5 °C warmer (Lilongwe 17.4 °C, Lusaka 15.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lilongwe 30.6 °C (October), Lusaka 30.6 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Lilongwe 9.5 °C, Lusaka 8.4 °C.
  • Lusaka gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 885 mm against 799 mm.
  • Lusaka gets about 133 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,887 h against 2,754 h; about 1961–1990).
