How the climate of Chang-hua, Taiwan compares with Shantou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Chang-hua is 0.9 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 22.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Chang-hua is 2.2 °C warmer (Chang-hua 17.3 °C, Shantou 15.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chang-hua 31.6 °C (July), Shantou 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chang-hua 13.0 °C, Shantou 11.2 °C.
  • Chang-hua gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 1,807 mm against 1,567 mm.
  • Shantou gets about 150 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,008 h against 1,857 h; about 1961–1990).
