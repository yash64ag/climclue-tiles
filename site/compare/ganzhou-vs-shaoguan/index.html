How the climate of Ganzhou, China compares with Shaoguan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Shaoguan is 1.1 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 21.1 °C against 20.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Shaoguan is 2.5 °C warmer (Ganzhou 8.2 °C, Shaoguan 10.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ganzhou 35.0 °C (July), Shaoguan 34.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ganzhou 4.4 °C, Shaoguan 6.3 °C.
  • Shaoguan gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,701 mm against 1,635 mm.
  • Ganzhou gets about 54 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,802 h against 1,749 h; about 1961–1990).
