Gainesville, Florida vs Putian climate

Main differences

  • Putian is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 20.5 °C against 20.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Gainesville, Florida is 2.2 °C warmer (Gainesville, Florida 16.8 °C, Putian 14.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gainesville, Florida 32.2 °C (July), Putian 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gainesville, Florida 5.7 °C, Putian 8.3 °C.
  • Putian gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,434 mm against 1,303 mm.
  • Gainesville, Florida gets about 1,024 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,882 h against 1,859 h; about 1961–1990).
