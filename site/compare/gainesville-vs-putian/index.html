Gainesville vs Putian climate

How the climate of Gainesville, United States compares with Putian, China, 13,480 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Putian is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 20.5 °C against 20.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Gainesville is 2.3 °C warmer (Gainesville 16.9 °C, Putian 14.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gainesville 32.0 °C (July), Putian 31.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gainesville 8.4 °C, Putian 7.6 °C.
  • Putian gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,324 mm against 1,294 mm.
  • Gainesville gets about 1,024 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,882 h against 1,859 h; about 1961–1990).
