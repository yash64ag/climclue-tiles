How the climate of Yunfu, China compares with Zhaoqing, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zhaoqing is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 22.7 °C against 22.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Zhaoqing is 0.5 °C warmer (Yunfu 27.2 °C, Zhaoqing 27.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Yunfu 32.8 °C (July), Zhaoqing 33.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Yunfu 9.6 °C, Zhaoqing 10.0 °C.
  • Yunfu gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,900 mm against 1,703 mm.
  • Yunfu gets about 13 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,827 h against 1,814 h; about 1961–1990).
