Budta vs Malingao climate

How the climate of Budta, Philippines compares with Malingao, Philippines, 6 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Malingao is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 28.3 °C against 28.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Malingao is 0.2 °C warmer (Budta 27.6 °C, Malingao 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Budta 33.3 °C (April), Malingao 33.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Budta 23.5 °C, Malingao 23.5 °C.
  • Budta gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,540 mm against 1,486 mm.
  • Malingao gets about 13 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,303 h against 2,291 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Malingao in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Malingao in 11 of 12 months
