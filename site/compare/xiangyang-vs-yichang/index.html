Xiangyang vs Yichang climate

How the climate of Xiangyang, China compares with Yichang, China, 169 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Yichang is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 17.1 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Yichang is 1.6 °C warmer (Xiangyang 5.3 °C, Yichang 7.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Xiangyang 31.2 °C (July), Yichang 30.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Xiangyang -1.6 °C, Yichang 0.0 °C.
  • Yichang gets 41% more rain and snow a year: 1,326 mm against 943 mm.
  • Xiangyang gets about 236 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,864 h against 1,628 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Xiangyang 15 cm, Yichang 8.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
