Curitiba vs Porto Alegre climate

How the climate of Curitiba, Brazil compares with Porto Alegre, Brazil, 547 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Porto Alegre is 2.3 °C (4.1 °F) warmer over the year: 19.6 °C against 17.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Porto Alegre is 3.9 °C warmer (Curitiba 20.9 °C, Porto Alegre 24.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Curitiba 24.9 °C (February), Porto Alegre 29.3 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Curitiba 9.8 °C, Porto Alegre 10.3 °C.
  • Curitiba gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,535 mm against 1,428 mm.
  • Porto Alegre gets about 565 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,217 h against 1,653 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Porto Alegre in 12 of 12 months
