How the climate of Curitiba, Brazil compares with Porto Alegre, Brazil: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Porto Alegre is 2.3 °C (4.2 °F) warmer over the year: 19.7 °C against 17.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Porto Alegre is 4.0 °C warmer (Curitiba 20.9 °C, Porto Alegre 24.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Curitiba 25.8 °C (January), Porto Alegre 29.6 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Curitiba 7.8 °C, Porto Alegre 9.7 °C.
  • Curitiba gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,510 mm against 1,419 mm.
  • Porto Alegre gets about 565 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,217 h against 1,653 h; about 1961–1990).
