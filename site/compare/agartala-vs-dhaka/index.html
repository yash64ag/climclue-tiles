How the climate of Agartala, India compares with Dhaka, Bangladesh: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Dhaka is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 25.5 °C against 25.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Dhaka is 0.7 °C warmer (Agartala 27.4 °C, Dhaka 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Agartala 32.7 °C (April), Dhaka 33.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Agartala 10.8 °C, Dhaka 11.2 °C.
  • Dhaka gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 2,134 mm against 2,062 mm.
  • Agartala gets about 45 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,145 h against 2,099 h; about 1961–1990).
