Chengtangcun vs Jining, Shandong climate

How the climate of Chengtangcun, China compares with Jining, Shandong, China, 66 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Chengtangcun and Jining, Shandong have the same yearly average temperature (14.5 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Jining, Shandong is 0.5 °C warmer (Chengtangcun 25.1 °C, Jining, Shandong 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chengtangcun 30.6 °C (July), Jining, Shandong 31.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chengtangcun -4.4 °C, Jining, Shandong -5.0 °C.
  • Chengtangcun gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 743 mm against 691 mm.
  • Jining, Shandong gets about 37 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,403 h against 2,366 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chengtangcun 18 cm, Jining, Shandong 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
