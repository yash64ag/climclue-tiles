Chengtangcun vs Jining, Shandong climate

How the climate of Chengtangcun, China compares with Jining, Shandong, China, 66 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jining, Shandong is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 14.5 °C against 14.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Jining, Shandong is 0.6 °C warmer (Chengtangcun 25.1 °C, Jining, Shandong 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chengtangcun 31.1 °C (July), Jining, Shandong 31.6 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Chengtangcun -5.0 °C, Jining, Shandong -5.4 °C.
  • Chengtangcun gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 735 mm against 691 mm.
  • Jining, Shandong gets about 37 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,403 h against 2,366 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chengtangcun 11 cm, Jining, Shandong 8.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
