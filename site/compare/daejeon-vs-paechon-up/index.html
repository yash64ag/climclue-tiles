Daejeon vs Paech’ŏn-ŭp climate

How the climate of Daejeon, South Korea compares with Paech’ŏn-ŭp, North Korea, 206 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Daejeon is 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) warmer over the year: 13.2 °C against 11.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Daejeon is 2.3 °C warmer (Daejeon -0.9 °C, Paech’ŏn-ŭp -3.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daejeon 29.3 °C (August), Paech’ŏn-ŭp 27.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Daejeon -5.8 °C, Paech’ŏn-ŭp -8.4 °C.
  • Daejeon gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,332 mm against 1,278 mm.
  • Daejeon gets about 24 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,425 h against 2,401 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daejeon 27 cm, Paech’ŏn-ŭp 24 cm (ERA5-Land).
