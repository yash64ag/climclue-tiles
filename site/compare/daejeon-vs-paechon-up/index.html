How the climate of Daejeon, South Korea compares with Paech’ŏn-ŭp, North Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Daejeon is 2.0 °C (3.5 °F) warmer over the year: 13.3 °C against 11.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Daejeon is 2.6 °C warmer (Daejeon -0.9 °C, Paech’ŏn-ŭp -3.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daejeon 30.6 °C (August), Paech’ŏn-ŭp 28.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Daejeon -6.2 °C, Paech’ŏn-ŭp -8.2 °C.
  • Paech’ŏn-ŭp gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,291 mm against 1,223 mm.
  • Daejeon gets about 24 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,425 h against 2,401 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daejeon 27 cm, Paech’ŏn-ŭp 24 cm (ERA5-Land).
