Gimcheon vs Zhu Cheng City climate

How the climate of Gimcheon, South Korea compares with Zhu Cheng City, China, 783 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gimcheon is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 13.4 °C against 13.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Gimcheon is 0.7 °C warmer (Gimcheon -0.7 °C, Zhu Cheng City -1.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gimcheon 30.5 °C (August), Zhu Cheng City 29.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gimcheon -5.8 °C, Zhu Cheng City -5.6 °C.
  • Gimcheon gets 56% more rain and snow a year: 1,097 mm against 702 mm.
  • Zhu Cheng City gets about 19 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,450 h against 2,431 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gimcheon 21 cm, Zhu Cheng City 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
