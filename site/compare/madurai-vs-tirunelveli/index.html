How the climate of Madurai, India compares with Tirunelveli, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Madurai is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 29.1 °C against 28.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Madurai is 1.2 °C warmer (Madurai 31.1 °C, Tirunelveli 29.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Madurai 36.2 °C (May), Tirunelveli 35.0 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Madurai 21.8 °C, Tirunelveli 22.0 °C.
  • Madurai gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 877 mm against 823 mm.
  • Madurai gets about 38 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,439 h against 2,401 h; about 1961–1990).
