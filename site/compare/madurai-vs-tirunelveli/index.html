Madurai vs Tirunelveli climate

How the climate of Madurai, India compares with Tirunelveli, India, 141 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Madurai is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 29.1 °C against 28.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Madurai is 1.5 °C warmer (Madurai 31.1 °C, Tirunelveli 29.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Madurai 38.1 °C (May), Tirunelveli 36.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Madurai 19.8 °C, Tirunelveli 20.6 °C.
  • Madurai gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 872 mm against 842 mm.
  • Madurai gets about 38 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,439 h against 2,401 h; about 1961–1990).
