How the climate of Tabriz, Iran compares with Tbilisi, Georgia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tbilisi is 1.1 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 13.6 °C against 12.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Tbilisi is 4.0 °C warmer (Tabriz -1.6 °C, Tbilisi 2.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Tabriz 32.4 °C (July), Tbilisi 31.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Tabriz -5.6 °C, Tbilisi -1.3 °C.
  • Tbilisi gets 140% more rain and snow a year: 567 mm against 236 mm.
  • Tabriz gets about 660 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,707 h against 2,047 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Tabriz 50 cm, Tbilisi 41 cm (ERA5-Land).
