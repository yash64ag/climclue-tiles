How the climate of Tai’an, Shandong, China compares with Zoucheng, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zoucheng is 1.0 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 14.2 °C against 13.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Zoucheng is 1.4 °C warmer (Tai’an, Shandong 6.3 °C, Zoucheng 7.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Tai’an, Shandong 30.3 °C (July), Zoucheng 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Tai’an, Shandong -6.2 °C, Zoucheng -5.6 °C.
  • Tai’an, Shandong gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 809 mm against 729 mm.
  • Tai’an, Shandong gets about 90 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,496 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Tai’an, Shandong 15 cm, Zoucheng 9.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
