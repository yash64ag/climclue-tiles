Tai’an vs Zoucheng climate

How the climate of Tai’an, China compares with Zoucheng, China, 88 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zoucheng is 1.0 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 14.3 °C against 13.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Zoucheng is 1.4 °C warmer (Tai’an 0.1 °C, Zoucheng 1.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Tai’an 30.4 °C (July), Zoucheng 30.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Tai’an -6.5 °C, Zoucheng -4.9 °C.
  • Tai’an gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 826 mm against 724 mm.
  • Tai’an gets about 90 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,496 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Tai’an 23 cm, Zoucheng 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
