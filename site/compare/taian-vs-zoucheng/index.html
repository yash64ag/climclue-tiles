Tai’an vs Zoucheng climate

How the climate of Tai’an, China compares with Zoucheng, China, 88 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zoucheng is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 14.2 °C against 13.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Zoucheng is 1.4 °C warmer (Tai’an 6.3 °C, Zoucheng 7.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Tai’an 30.2 °C (July), Zoucheng 30.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Tai’an -6.5 °C, Zoucheng -4.9 °C.
  • Tai’an gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 809 mm against 729 mm.
  • Tai’an gets about 90 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,496 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Tai’an 15 cm, Zoucheng 9.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
