Rasapūdipalem vs Vijayawada climate

How the climate of Rasapūdipalem, India compares with Vijayawada, India, 315 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Vijayawada is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 28.4 °C against 27.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Vijayawada is 2.9 °C warmer (Rasapūdipalem 30.3 °C, Vijayawada 33.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rasapūdipalem 34.6 °C (May), Vijayawada 38.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Rasapūdipalem 18.0 °C, Vijayawada 19.0 °C.
  • Rasapūdipalem gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,139 mm against 1,041 mm.
  • Vijayawada gets about 116 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,669 h against 2,552 h; about 1961–1990).
