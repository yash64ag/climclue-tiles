Main differences

  • Xinyi, Guangdong is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 22.5 °C against 22.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Xinyi, Guangdong is 1.1 °C warmer (Ma On Shan 24.9 °C, Xinyi, Guangdong 26.0 °C).
