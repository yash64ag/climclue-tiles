New York City vs Philadelphia climate

How the climate of New York City, United States compares with Philadelphia, United States, 130 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Philadelphia is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 13.1 °C against 12.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Philadelphia is 1.5 °C warmer (New York City 4.8 °C, Philadelphia 6.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: New York City 29.6 °C (July), Philadelphia 30.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: New York City -4.2 °C, Philadelphia -3.9 °C.
  • Philadelphia gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,220 mm against 1,193 mm.
  • New York City gets about 36 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,499 h against 2,463 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: New York City 80 cm, Philadelphia 66 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Philadelphia in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: New York City in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: New York City in 7 of 12 months
