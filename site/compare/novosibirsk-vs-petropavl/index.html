Novosibirsk vs Petropavl climate

How the climate of Novosibirsk, Russia compares with Petropavl, Kazakhstan, 879 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Petropavl is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 2.5 °C against 1.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Petropavl is 1.9 °C warmer (Novosibirsk 2.0 °C, Petropavl 4.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Novosibirsk 23.4 °C (July), Petropavl 23.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Novosibirsk -19.5 °C, Petropavl -17.8 °C.
  • Novosibirsk gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 492 mm against 391 mm.
  • Petropavl gets about 56 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,162 h against 2,107 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Novosibirsk 163 cm, Petropavl 122 cm (ERA5-Land).
