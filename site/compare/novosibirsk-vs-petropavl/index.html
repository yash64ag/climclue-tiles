How the climate of Novosibirsk, Russia compares with Petropavl, Kazakhstan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Petropavl is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 2.2 °C against 1.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Petropavl is 1.2 °C warmer (Novosibirsk -16.6 °C, Petropavl -15.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Novosibirsk 25.3 °C (July), Petropavl 25.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Novosibirsk -20.9 °C, Petropavl -19.5 °C.
  • Novosibirsk gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 463 mm against 399 mm.
  • Petropavl gets about 56 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,162 h against 2,107 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Novosibirsk 163 cm, Petropavl 122 cm (ERA5-Land).
