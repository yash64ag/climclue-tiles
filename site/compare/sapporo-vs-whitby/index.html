How the climate of Sapporo, Japan compares with Whitby, Canada: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Sapporo is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 8.3 °C against 8.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Sapporo is 2.0 °C warmer (Sapporo 11.6 °C, Whitby 9.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Sapporo 25.4 °C (August), Whitby 26.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Sapporo -8.5 °C, Whitby -9.5 °C.
  • Sapporo gets 67% more rain and snow a year: 1,573 mm against 942 mm.
  • Whitby gets about 293 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,970 h against 1,677 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Sapporo 417 cm, Whitby 109 cm (ERA5-Land).
