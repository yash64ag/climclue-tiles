Datong vs Hohhot climate

How the climate of Datong, China compares with Hohhot, China, 160 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Datong is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 7.9 °C against 7.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Datong is 1.9 °C warmer (Datong -5.3 °C, Hohhot -7.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Datong 28.3 °C (July), Hohhot 28.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Datong -17.0 °C, Hohhot -18.4 °C.
  • Datong gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 380 mm against 374 mm.
  • Hohhot gets about 124 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,933 h against 2,809 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Datong 15 cm, Hohhot 19 cm (ERA5-Land).
