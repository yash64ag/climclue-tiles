Datong vs Hohhot climate

How the climate of Datong, China compares with Hohhot, China, 160 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Datong is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 8.0 °C against 6.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Datong is 2.4 °C warmer (Datong -5.0 °C, Hohhot -7.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Datong 28.4 °C (July), Hohhot 27.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Datong -16.8 °C, Hohhot -18.5 °C.
  • Datong gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 388 mm against 376 mm.
  • Hohhot gets about 124 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,933 h against 2,809 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Datong 15 cm, Hohhot 19 cm (ERA5-Land).
