How the climate of Datong, Shanxi, China compares with Hohhot, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Datong, Shanxi is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 7.8 °C against 7.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Datong, Shanxi is 0.8 °C warmer (Datong, Shanxi -7.7 °C, Hohhot -8.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Datong, Shanxi 28.8 °C (July), Hohhot 29.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Datong, Shanxi -16.4 °C, Hohhot -16.3 °C.
  • Datong, Shanxi gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 380 mm against 374 mm.
  • Hohhot gets about 124 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,933 h against 2,809 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Datong, Shanxi 15 cm, Hohhot 19 cm (ERA5-Land).
