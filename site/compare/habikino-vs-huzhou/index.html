Habikino vs Huzhou climate

How the climate of Habikino, Japan compares with Huzhou, China, 1,505 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Huzhou is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Huzhou is 1.7 °C warmer (Habikino 19.6 °C, Huzhou 21.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Habikino 32.0 °C (August), Huzhou 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Habikino 0.3 °C, Huzhou 0.3 °C.
  • Habikino gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,390 mm against 1,312 mm.
  • Huzhou gets about 70 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,934 h against 1,864 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Habikino 6.6 cm, Huzhou 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
