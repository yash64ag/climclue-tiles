How the climate of Habikino, Japan compares with Huzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Huzhou is 0.6 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.9 °C against 16.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Huzhou is 2.1 °C warmer (Habikino 19.2 °C, Huzhou 21.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Habikino 32.8 °C (August), Huzhou 32.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Habikino 2.0 °C, Huzhou 0.5 °C.
  • Habikino gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,371 mm against 1,292 mm.
  • Huzhou gets about 70 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,934 h against 1,864 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Habikino 6.6 cm, Huzhou 17 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Huzhou in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Huzhou in 7 of 12 months
