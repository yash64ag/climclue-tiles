How the climate of Heze, China compares with Kaifeng, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kaifeng is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 15.1 °C against 14.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Kaifeng is 1.1 °C warmer (Heze 20.6 °C, Kaifeng 21.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Heze 31.5 °C (July), Kaifeng 32.5 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Heze -4.5 °C, Kaifeng -4.4 °C.
  • Kaifeng gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 658 mm against 647 mm.
  • Heze gets about 78 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,358 h against 2,280 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Heze 11 cm, Kaifeng 14 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Heze in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Heze in 11 of 12 months
