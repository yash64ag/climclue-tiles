Heze vs Kaifeng climate

How the climate of Heze, China compares with Kaifeng, China, 117 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kaifeng is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 15.1 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Kaifeng is 0.5 °C warmer (Heze 0.1 °C, Kaifeng 0.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Heze 31.3 °C (June), Kaifeng 31.4 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Heze -4.7 °C, Kaifeng -4.2 °C.
  • Kaifeng gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 657 mm against 642 mm.
  • Heze gets about 78 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,358 h against 2,280 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Heze 11 cm, Kaifeng 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
