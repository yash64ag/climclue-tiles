Tuen Mun vs Yangjiang climate

How the climate of Tuen Mun, Hong Kong compares with Yangjiang, China, 215 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yangjiang is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 23.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Yangjiang is 0.7 °C warmer (Tuen Mun 22.8 °C, Yangjiang 23.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Tuen Mun 31.6 °C (July), Yangjiang 31.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Tuen Mun 12.2 °C, Yangjiang 11.5 °C.
  • Yangjiang gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 2,689 mm against 2,410 mm.
  • Tuen Mun gets about 19 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,947 h against 1,929 h; about 1961–1990).
