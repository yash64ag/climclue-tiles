Kyoto vs Seogwipo climate

How the climate of Kyoto, Japan compares with Seogwipo, South Korea, 868 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Seogwipo is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 16.0 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Seogwipo is 1.5 °C warmer (Kyoto 5.0 °C, Seogwipo 6.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kyoto 31.9 °C (August), Seogwipo 29.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kyoto 1.0 °C, Seogwipo 2.4 °C.
  • Seogwipo gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 1,924 mm against 1,435 mm.
  • Seogwipo gets about 446 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,121 h against 1,674 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kyoto 27 cm, Seogwipo 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
