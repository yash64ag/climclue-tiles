Seogwipo vs Tokyo climate

How the climate of Seogwipo, South Korea compares with Tokyo, Japan, 1,233 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Seogwipo is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.0 °C against 15.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Seogwipo is 1.4 °C warmer (Seogwipo 6.5 °C, Tokyo 5.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Seogwipo 28.4 °C (August), Tokyo 29.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Seogwipo 3.5 °C, Tokyo -0.3 °C.
  • Seogwipo gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 1,924 mm against 1,516 mm.
  • Seogwipo gets about 283 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,121 h against 1,837 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Seogwipo 16 cm, Tokyo 19 cm (ERA5-Land).
