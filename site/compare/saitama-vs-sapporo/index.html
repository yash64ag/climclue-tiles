Saitama vs Sapporo climate

How the climate of Saitama, Japan compares with Sapporo, Japan, 809 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Saitama is 7.1 °C (12.8 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 8.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Saitama is 9.2 °C warmer (Saitama 4.8 °C, Sapporo -4.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Saitama 30.3 °C (August), Sapporo 24.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Saitama -1.8 °C, Sapporo -9.9 °C.
  • Sapporo gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 1,620 mm against 1,327 mm.
  • Saitama gets about 186 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,863 h against 1,677 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Saitama 20 cm, Sapporo 417 cm (ERA5-Land).
