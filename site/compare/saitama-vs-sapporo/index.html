How the climate of Saitama, Japan compares with Sapporo, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Saitama is 6.9 °C (12.5 °F) warmer over the year: 15.3 °C against 8.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Saitama is 8.9 °C warmer (Saitama 4.8 °C, Sapporo -4.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Saitama 31.1 °C (August), Sapporo 25.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Saitama -1.3 °C, Sapporo -8.5 °C.
  • Sapporo gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 1,573 mm against 1,336 mm.
  • Saitama gets about 186 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,863 h against 1,677 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Saitama 20 cm, Sapporo 417 cm (ERA5-Land).
