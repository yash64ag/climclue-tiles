Maiduguri vs N'Djamena climate

How the climate of Maiduguri, Nigeria compares with N'Djamena, Chad, 207 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • N'Djamena is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 28.8 °C against 27.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when N'Djamena is 1.7 °C warmer (Maiduguri 26.1 °C, N'Djamena 27.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maiduguri 39.9 °C (April), N'Djamena 41.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Maiduguri 14.2 °C, N'Djamena 16.3 °C.
  • Maiduguri gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 574 mm against 480 mm.
  • N'Djamena gets about 165 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,166 h against 3,002 h; about 1961–1990).
