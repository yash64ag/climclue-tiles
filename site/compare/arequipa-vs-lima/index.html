Arequipa vs Lima climate

How the climate of Arequipa, Peru compares with Lima, Peru, 765 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lima is 3.8 °C (6.9 °F) warmer over the year: 19.0 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Lima is 7.7 °C warmer (Arequipa 14.9 °C, Lima 22.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Arequipa 24.7 °C (October), Lima 26.5 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Arequipa 5.5 °C, Lima 13.4 °C.
  • Arequipa gets 340% more rain and snow a year: 168 mm against 38 mm.
  • Arequipa gets about 1,673 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,178 h against 1,505 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Lima in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Lima in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Arequipa in 11 of 12 months
