How the climate of Abakan, Russia compares with Harbin, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Harbin is 2.3 °C (4.2 °F) warmer over the year: 4.8 °C against 2.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Harbin is 4.3 °C warmer (Abakan 10.8 °C, Harbin 15.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abakan 27.2 °C (July), Harbin 27.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Abakan -23.0 °C, Harbin -23.3 °C.
  • Harbin gets 58% more rain and snow a year: 551 mm against 348 mm.
  • Harbin gets about 682 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,550 h against 1,868 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Abakan 49 cm, Harbin 33 cm (ERA5-Land).
