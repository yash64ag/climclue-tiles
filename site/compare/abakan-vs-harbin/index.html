Abakan vs Harbin climate

How the climate of Abakan, Russia compares with Harbin, China, 2,650 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Harbin is 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) warmer over the year: 4.6 °C against 3.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Harbin is 3.6 °C warmer (Abakan 11.3 °C, Harbin 15.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abakan 28.1 °C (July), Harbin 27.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Abakan -23.3 °C, Harbin -24.4 °C.
  • Harbin gets 58% more rain and snow a year: 551 mm against 348 mm.
  • Harbin gets about 682 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,550 h against 1,868 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Abakan 49 cm, Harbin 33 cm (ERA5-Land).
