Bengaluru vs Coimbatore climate

How the climate of Bengaluru, India compares with Coimbatore, India, 229 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Coimbatore is 2.4 °C (4.2 °F) warmer over the year: 25.8 °C against 23.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Coimbatore is 3.0 °C warmer (Bengaluru 21.1 °C, Coimbatore 24.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bengaluru 32.8 °C (April), Coimbatore 35.0 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Bengaluru 14.8 °C, Coimbatore 18.3 °C.
  • Bengaluru gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 895 mm against 820 mm.
  • Coimbatore gets about 31 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,406 h against 2,374 h; about 1961–1990).
