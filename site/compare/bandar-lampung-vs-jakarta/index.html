Bandar Lampung vs Jakarta climate

How the climate of Bandar Lampung, Indonesia compares with Jakarta, Indonesia, 196 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jakarta is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Jakarta is 1.3 °C warmer (Bandar Lampung 26.1 °C, Jakarta 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bandar Lampung 31.6 °C (May), Jakarta 32.4 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Bandar Lampung 20.6 °C, Jakarta 21.7 °C.
  • Jakarta gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 2,513 mm against 2,377 mm.
  • Jakarta gets about 180 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,648 h against 2,468 h; about 1961–1990).
