Haikou, Hainan vs Maoming climate

How the climate of Haikou, Hainan, China compares with Maoming, China, 191 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Haikou, Hainan is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 24.3 °C against 23.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Haikou, Hainan is 2.1 °C warmer (Haikou, Hainan 19.8 °C, Maoming 17.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Haikou, Hainan 32.4 °C (July), Maoming 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Haikou, Hainan 15.3 °C, Maoming 12.6 °C.
  • Maoming gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 2,055 mm against 1,800 mm.
  • Haikou, Hainan gets about 176 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,092 h against 1,916 h; about 1961–1990).
