How the climate of Casablanca, Morocco compares with Rabat, Morocco: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Casablanca and Rabat have the same yearly average temperature (18.5 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Casablanca is 0.2 °C warmer (Casablanca 19.0 °C, Rabat 18.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Casablanca 27.5 °C (August), Rabat 28.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Casablanca 8.3 °C, Rabat 8.6 °C.
  • Rabat gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 485 mm against 363 mm.
  • Casablanca gets about 26 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,895 h against 2,869 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
