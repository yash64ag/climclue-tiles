How the climate of Kobe, Japan compares with Kyoto, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kobe is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.9 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Kobe is 1.8 °C warmer (Kobe 8.8 °C, Kyoto 7.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kobe 32.3 °C (August), Kyoto 31.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kobe 2.7 °C, Kyoto 1.0 °C.
  • Kyoto gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,435 mm against 1,368 mm.
  • Kobe gets about 171 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,846 h against 1,674 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kobe 5.6 cm, Kyoto 27 cm (ERA5-Land).
