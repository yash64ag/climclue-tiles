Jingzhou vs Yichang climate

How the climate of Jingzhou, China compares with Yichang, China, 96 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jingzhou is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 17.4 °C against 17.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Jingzhou is 0.8 °C warmer (Jingzhou 28.8 °C, Yichang 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jingzhou 31.5 °C (July), Yichang 30.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jingzhou 0.5 °C, Yichang 0.0 °C.
  • Yichang gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,326 mm against 1,260 mm.
  • Jingzhou gets about 73 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,701 h against 1,628 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jingzhou 11 cm, Yichang 8.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
