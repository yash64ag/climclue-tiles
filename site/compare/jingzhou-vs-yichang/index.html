How the climate of Jingzhou, China compares with Yichang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yichang is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 17.6 °C against 17.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Yichang is 0.6 °C warmer (Jingzhou 7.0 °C, Yichang 7.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jingzhou 32.5 °C (July), Yichang 32.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jingzhou 0.8 °C, Yichang 1.5 °C.
  • Jingzhou gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,262 mm against 1,225 mm.
  • Jingzhou gets about 73 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,701 h against 1,628 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jingzhou 11 cm, Yichang 8.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
