How the climate of Kagoshima, Japan compares with Nanchang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Nanchang is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 18.6 °C against 18.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Nanchang is 2.8 °C warmer (Kagoshima 20.6 °C, Nanchang 23.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kagoshima 32.1 °C (August), Nanchang 34.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kagoshima 4.2 °C, Nanchang 2.9 °C.
  • Kagoshima gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 2,100 mm against 1,796 mm.
  • Kagoshima gets about 20 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,859 h against 1,839 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kagoshima 0.1 cm, Nanchang 6.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
