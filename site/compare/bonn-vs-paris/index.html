Bonn vs Paris climate

How the climate of Bonn, Germany compares with Paris, France, 400 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Paris is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 11.2 °C against 10.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Paris is 1.4 °C warmer (Bonn 2.5 °C, Paris 4.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bonn 23.0 °C (July), Paris 23.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bonn 0.0 °C, Paris 1.2 °C.
  • Bonn gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 758 mm against 662 mm.
  • Paris gets about 272 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,744 h against 1,473 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bonn 48 cm, Paris 18 cm (ERA5-Land).
