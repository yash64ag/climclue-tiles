How the climate of Bonn, Germany compares with Paris, France: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Paris is 1.3 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 12.0 °C against 10.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Paris is 1.9 °C warmer (Bonn 3.2 °C, Paris 5.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bonn 24.4 °C (July), Paris 24.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bonn -0.2 °C, Paris 2.1 °C.
  • Bonn gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 761 mm against 658 mm.
  • Paris gets about 272 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,744 h against 1,473 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bonn 23 cm, Paris 9.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
