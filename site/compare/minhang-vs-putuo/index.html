How the climate of Minhang, China compares with Putuo, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Minhang and Putuo have the same yearly average temperature (17.0 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Putuo is 0.2 °C warmer (Minhang 24.4 °C, Putuo 24.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Minhang 32.3 °C (July), Putuo 32.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Minhang 1.5 °C, Putuo 1.5 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,467 mm and 1,466 mm).
  • Putuo gets about 10 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,969 h against 1,959 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Minhang 8.6 cm, Putuo 8.9 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Putuo in 10 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Putuo in 10 of 12 months
