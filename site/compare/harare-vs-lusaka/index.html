Harare vs Lusaka climate

How the climate of Harare, Zimbabwe compares with Lusaka, Zambia, 399 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lusaka is 1.7 °C (3.1 °F) warmer over the year: 20.2 °C against 18.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Lusaka is 3.0 °C warmer (Harare 21.1 °C, Lusaka 24.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Harare 28.6 °C (October), Lusaka 30.6 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Harare 5.6 °C, Lusaka 8.4 °C.
  • Lusaka gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 885 mm against 800 mm.
  • Harare gets about 57 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,944 h against 2,887 h; about 1961–1990).
