How the climate of Qujing, China compares with San Salvador de Jujuy, Argentina: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • San Salvador de Jujuy is 1.9 °C (3.5 °F) warmer over the year: 17.4 °C against 15.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when San Salvador de Jujuy is 12.9 °C warmer (Qujing 8.5 °C, San Salvador de Jujuy 21.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qujing 24.6 °C (July), San Salvador de Jujuy 27.6 °C (December). Coldest-month night lows: Qujing 2.3 °C, San Salvador de Jujuy 4.0 °C.
  • San Salvador de Jujuy gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 1,226 mm against 1,007 mm.
  • San Salvador de Jujuy gets about 412 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,345 h against 1,933 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
