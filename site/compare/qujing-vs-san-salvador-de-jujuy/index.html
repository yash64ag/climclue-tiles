Qujing vs San Salvador de Jujuy climate

How the climate of Qujing, China compares with San Salvador de Jujuy, Argentina, 18,904 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • San Salvador de Jujuy is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.0 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when San Salvador de Jujuy is 11.9 °C warmer (Qujing 8.3 °C, San Salvador de Jujuy 20.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qujing 23.1 °C (July), San Salvador de Jujuy 23.4 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Qujing 3.0 °C, San Salvador de Jujuy 6.7 °C.
  • San Salvador de Jujuy gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 1,314 mm against 1,039 mm.
  • San Salvador de Jujuy gets about 412 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,345 h against 1,933 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
