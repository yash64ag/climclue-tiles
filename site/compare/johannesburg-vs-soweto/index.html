Johannesburg vs Soweto climate

How the climate of Johannesburg, South Africa compares with Soweto, South Africa, 20 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Soweto is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 15.7 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Soweto is 0.9 °C warmer (Johannesburg 18.8 °C, Soweto 19.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Johannesburg 24.7 °C (January), Soweto 25.8 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Johannesburg 1.1 °C, Soweto 1.1 °C.
  • Johannesburg gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 821 mm against 678 mm.
  • Soweto gets about 37 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,205 h against 3,169 h; about 1961–1990).
