Baoji vs Xi’an climate

How the climate of Baoji, China compares with Xi’an, China, 156 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Xi’an is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 15.3 °C against 14.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Xi’an is 1.7 °C warmer (Baoji 24.3 °C, Xi’an 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoji 30.4 °C (July), Xi’an 31.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baoji -3.9 °C, Xi’an -3.5 °C.
  • Xi’an gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 674 mm against 670 mm.
  • Baoji gets about 30 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,939 h against 1,909 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoji 85 cm, Xi’an 38 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Baoji in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Baoji in 7 of 12 months
