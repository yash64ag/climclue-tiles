How the climate of Baoji, China compares with Xi’an, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Xi’an is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 15.4 °C against 14.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Xi’an is 1.8 °C warmer (Baoji 24.2 °C, Xi’an 26.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoji 31.3 °C (July), Xi’an 32.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baoji -3.0 °C, Xi’an -3.1 °C.
  • Xi’an gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 664 mm against 653 mm.
  • Baoji gets about 30 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,939 h against 1,909 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoji 6.0 cm, Xi’an 10 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Baoji in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Baoji in 7 of 12 months
