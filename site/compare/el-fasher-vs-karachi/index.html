Main differences

  • Karachi is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.1 °C against 26.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Karachi is 1.1 °C warmer (El Fasher 28.3 °C, Karachi 29.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: El Fasher 37.4 °C (May), Karachi 33.4 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: El Fasher 11.3 °C, Karachi 14.9 °C.
  • El Fasher gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 198 mm against 183 mm.
  • El Fasher gets about 298 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,342 h against 3,044 h; about 1961–1990).
