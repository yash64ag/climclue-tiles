El Fasher vs Karachi climate

How the climate of El Fasher, Sudan compares with Karachi, Pakistan, 4,529 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • El Fasher and Karachi have the same yearly average temperature (26.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when El Fasher is 1.0 °C warmer (El Fasher 28.9 °C, Karachi 27.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: El Fasher 39.3 °C (May), Karachi 34.2 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: El Fasher 9.6 °C, Karachi 12.2 °C.
  • El Fasher gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 197 mm against 180 mm.
  • El Fasher gets about 298 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,342 h against 3,044 h; about 1961–1990).
