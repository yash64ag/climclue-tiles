Main differences

  • Jieyang is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 22.3 °C against 22.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Jieyang is 0.2 °C warmer (Jieyang 14.3 °C, Puning 14.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jieyang 31.4 °C (July), Puning 31.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jieyang 9.2 °C, Puning 9.2 °C.
  • Jieyang gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,753 mm against 1,700 mm.
  • Puning gets about 1 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,980 h against 1,979 h; about 1961–1990).
