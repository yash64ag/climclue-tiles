How the climate of Jieyang, China compares with Puning, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jieyang is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 22.4 °C against 22.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Jieyang is 0.2 °C warmer (Jieyang 15.4 °C, Puning 15.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jieyang 32.4 °C (July), Puning 31.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jieyang 10.3 °C, Puning 10.3 °C.
  • Jieyang gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,731 mm against 1,639 mm.
  • Puning gets about 1 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,980 h against 1,979 h; about 1961–1990).
