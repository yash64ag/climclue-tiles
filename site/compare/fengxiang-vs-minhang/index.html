Fengxiang vs Minhang climate

How the climate of Fengxiang, China compares with Minhang, China, 29 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fengxiang and Minhang have the same yearly average temperature (16.7 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Fengxiang is 0.8 °C warmer (Fengxiang 7.8 °C, Minhang 7.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fengxiang 31.4 °C (August), Minhang 31.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fengxiang 1.6 °C, Minhang 1.1 °C.
  • Minhang gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,467 mm against 1,409 mm.
  • Minhang gets about 22 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,959 h against 1,937 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fengxiang 5.5 cm, Minhang 8.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
