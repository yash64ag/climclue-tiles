How the climate of Bratislava, Slovakia compares with Vienna, Austria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Vienna is 0.4 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 10.9 °C against 10.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Vienna is 0.9 °C warmer (Bratislava -0.5 °C, Vienna 0.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bratislava 26.6 °C (July), Vienna 26.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bratislava -3.1 °C, Vienna -2.1 °C.
  • Vienna gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 626 mm against 620 mm.
  • Bratislava gets about 35 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,774 h against 1,739 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bratislava 64 cm, Vienna 63 cm (ERA5-Land).
