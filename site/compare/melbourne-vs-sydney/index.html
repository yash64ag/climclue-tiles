Melbourne vs Sydney climate

How the climate of Melbourne, Australia compares with Sydney, Australia, 713 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Sydney is 3.2 °C (5.8 °F) warmer over the year: 17.8 °C against 14.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Sydney is 3.7 °C warmer (Melbourne 12.8 °C, Sydney 16.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Melbourne 24.3 °C (February), Sydney 23.6 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Melbourne 7.2 °C, Sydney 10.8 °C.
  • Sydney gets 85% more rain and snow a year: 1,155 mm against 625 mm.
  • Sydney gets about 291 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,488 h against 2,197 h; about 1961–1990).
