How the climate of Melbourne, Australia compares with Sydney, Australia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Sydney is 3.0 °C (5.4 °F) warmer over the year: 18.2 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Sydney is 3.7 °C warmer (Melbourne 12.6 °C, Sydney 16.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Melbourne 26.5 °C (February), Sydney 26.3 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Melbourne 6.3 °C, Sydney 8.8 °C.
  • Sydney gets 74% more rain and snow a year: 1,097 mm against 629 mm.
  • Sydney gets about 291 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,488 h against 2,197 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Sydney in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Melbourne in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Sydney in 8 of 12 months
