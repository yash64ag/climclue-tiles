Kyiv vs Pinsk climate

How the climate of Kyiv, Ukraine compares with Pinsk, Belarus, 361 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kyiv is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 8.2 °C against 7.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Kyiv is 1.6 °C warmer (Kyiv 18.4 °C, Pinsk 16.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kyiv 24.5 °C (July), Pinsk 23.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kyiv -6.2 °C, Pinsk -5.9 °C.
  • Pinsk gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 635 mm against 614 mm.
  • Kyiv gets about 63 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,729 h against 1,666 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kyiv 125 cm, Pinsk 115 cm (ERA5-Land).
