How the climate of Kyiv, Ukraine compares with Pinsk, Belarus: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kyiv is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 8.3 °C against 7.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Kyiv is 1.5 °C warmer (Kyiv 19.4 °C, Pinsk 17.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kyiv 25.1 °C (July), Pinsk 24.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kyiv -6.0 °C, Pinsk -6.4 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (632 mm and 633 mm).
  • Kyiv gets about 63 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,729 h against 1,666 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kyiv 103 cm, Pinsk 94 cm (ERA5-Land).
