How the climate of Norman, United States compares with Yangzhou, Jiangsu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Norman is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 16.0 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Norman is 1.9 °C warmer (Norman 10.9 °C, Yangzhou, Jiangsu 9.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Norman 33.8 °C (August), Yangzhou, Jiangsu 31.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Norman -2.1 °C, Yangzhou, Jiangsu -1.6 °C.
  • Yangzhou, Jiangsu gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,098 mm against 979 mm.
  • Norman gets about 929 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,003 h against 2,074 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Norman 13 cm, Yangzhou, Jiangsu 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
