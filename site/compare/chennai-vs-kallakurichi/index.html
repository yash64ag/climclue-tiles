How the climate of Chennai, India compares with Kallakurichi, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kallakurichi is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 28.1 °C against 27.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Kallakurichi is 1.5 °C warmer (Chennai 29.5 °C, Kallakurichi 30.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chennai 36.0 °C (May), Kallakurichi 37.5 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Chennai 19.8 °C, Kallakurichi 19.1 °C.
  • Chennai gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 1,373 mm against 1,028 mm.
  • Chennai gets about 147 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,675 h against 2,528 h; about 1961–1990).
