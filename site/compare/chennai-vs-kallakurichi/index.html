Chennai vs Kallakurichi climate

How the climate of Chennai, India compares with Kallakurichi, India, 208 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kallakurichi is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 28.1 °C against 28.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Kallakurichi is 1.3 °C warmer (Chennai 29.6 °C, Kallakurichi 30.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chennai 34.7 °C (May), Kallakurichi 37.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Chennai 21.7 °C, Kallakurichi 18.9 °C.
  • Chennai gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 1,359 mm against 1,016 mm.
  • Chennai gets about 147 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,675 h against 2,528 h; about 1961–1990).
