Bacău vs Qingyang, Gansu climate

How the climate of Bacău, Romania compares with Qingyang, Gansu, China, 6,570 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bacău is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 10.1 °C against 9.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Bacău is 2.4 °C warmer (Bacău -2.3 °C, Qingyang, Gansu -4.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bacău 27.9 °C (July), Qingyang, Gansu 27.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bacău -6.1 °C, Qingyang, Gansu -9.6 °C.
  • Qingyang, Gansu gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 643 mm against 546 mm.
  • Qingyang, Gansu gets about 394 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,270 h against 1,876 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bacău 60 cm, Qingyang, Gansu 34 cm (ERA5-Land).
