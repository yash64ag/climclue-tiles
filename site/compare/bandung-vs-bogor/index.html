Bandung vs Bogor climate

How the climate of Bandung, Indonesia compares with Bogor, Indonesia, 97 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bogor is 2.1 °C (3.8 °F) warmer over the year: 25.8 °C against 23.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Bogor is 2.5 °C warmer (Bandung 23.4 °C, Bogor 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bandung 28.2 °C (September), Bogor 30.9 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Bandung 18.9 °C, Bogor 20.9 °C.
  • Bogor gets 49% more rain and snow a year: 3,567 mm against 2,391 mm.
  • Bogor gets about 171 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,625 h against 2,454 h; about 1961–1990).
