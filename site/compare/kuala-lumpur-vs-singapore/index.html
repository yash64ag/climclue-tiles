How the climate of Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia compares with Singapore, Singapore: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Singapore is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Singapore is 0.6 °C warmer (Kuala Lumpur 27.1 °C, Singapore 27.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kuala Lumpur 32.0 °C (March), Singapore 31.3 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kuala Lumpur 21.4 °C, Singapore 22.3 °C.
  • Kuala Lumpur gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 2,940 mm against 2,424 mm.
  • Kuala Lumpur gets about 159 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,214 h against 2,055 h; about 1961–1990).
