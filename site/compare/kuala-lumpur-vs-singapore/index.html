Kuala Lumpur vs Singapore climate

How the climate of Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia compares with Singapore, Singapore, 316 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Singapore is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Singapore is 0.5 °C warmer (Kuala Lumpur 27.1 °C, Singapore 27.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kuala Lumpur 30.6 °C (March), Singapore 28.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kuala Lumpur 21.7 °C, Singapore 24.9 °C.
  • Kuala Lumpur gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 2,938 mm against 2,410 mm.
  • Kuala Lumpur gets about 159 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,214 h against 2,055 h; about 1961–1990).
