Ar Rayyān vs Madinah climate

How the climate of Ar Rayyān, Qatar compares with Madinah, Saudi Arabia, 1,194 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Madinah is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 27.8 °C against 27.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Madinah is 1.8 °C warmer (Ar Rayyān 21.4 °C, Madinah 23.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ar Rayyān 41.0 °C (July), Madinah 42.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Ar Rayyān 12.9 °C, Madinah 11.7 °C.
  • Ar Rayyān gets 54% more rain and snow a year: 97 mm against 63 mm.
  • Madinah gets about 202 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,599 h against 3,397 h; about 1961–1990).
