Anshan vs Shenyang climate

How the climate of Anshan, China compares with Shenyang, China, 83 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Anshan is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 9.6 °C against 8.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Anshan is 1.5 °C warmer (Anshan -9.8 °C, Shenyang -11.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Anshan 28.8 °C (July), Shenyang 28.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Anshan -15.7 °C, Shenyang -17.6 °C.
  • Anshan gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 754 mm against 715 mm.
  • Anshan gets about 37 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,577 h against 2,540 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Anshan 39 cm, Shenyang 37 cm (ERA5-Land).
