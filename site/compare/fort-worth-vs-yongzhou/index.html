How the climate of Fort Worth, United States compares with Yongzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Fort Worth and Yongzhou have the same yearly average temperature (18.9 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Fort Worth is 1.3 °C warmer (Fort Worth 13.9 °C, Yongzhou 12.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fort Worth 35.7 °C (August), Yongzhou 34.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fort Worth 1.9 °C, Yongzhou 4.0 °C.
  • Yongzhou gets 63% more rain and snow a year: 1,454 mm against 895 mm.
  • Fort Worth gets about 1,258 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,838 h against 1,580 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fort Worth 0.5 cm, Yongzhou 6.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
