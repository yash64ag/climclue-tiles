Fort Worth vs Yongzhou climate

How the climate of Fort Worth, United States compares with Yongzhou, China, 12,760 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yongzhou is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 19.0 °C against 18.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Fort Worth is 1.5 °C warmer (Fort Worth 14.2 °C, Yongzhou 12.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fort Worth 34.8 °C (August), Yongzhou 33.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fort Worth 3.8 °C, Yongzhou 2.9 °C.
  • Yongzhou gets 65% more rain and snow a year: 1,454 mm against 880 mm.
  • Fort Worth gets about 1,258 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,838 h against 1,580 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fort Worth 0.4 cm, Yongzhou 6.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
