Khān Yūnis vs Tripoli climate

How the climate of Khān Yūnis, Palestinian Territory compares with Tripoli, Libya, 1,993 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Khān Yūnis is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 20.5 °C against 20.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Khān Yūnis is 1.2 °C warmer (Khān Yūnis 19.1 °C, Tripoli 17.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Khān Yūnis 32.9 °C (August), Tripoli 33.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Khān Yūnis 8.0 °C, Tripoli 8.8 °C.
  • Tripoli gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 299 mm against 276 mm.
  • Khān Yūnis gets about 184 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,403 h against 3,219 h; about 1961–1990).
