How the climate of Chongqing, China compares with Round Rock, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Round Rock is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 19.9 °C against 19.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Round Rock is 1.0 °C warmer (Chongqing 19.8 °C, Round Rock 20.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chongqing 33.8 °C (August), Round Rock 35.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Chongqing 6.9 °C, Round Rock 3.4 °C.
  • Chongqing gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 1,104 mm against 920 mm.
  • Round Rock gets about 1,444 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,642 h against 1,198 h; about 1961–1990).
