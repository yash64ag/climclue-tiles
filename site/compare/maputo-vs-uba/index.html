Maputo vs Ubá climate

How the climate of Maputo, Mozambique compares with Ubá, Brazil, 7,611 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Maputo is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 22.8 °C against 22.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Ubá is 0.6 °C warmer (Maputo 22.4 °C, Ubá 23.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maputo 30.0 °C (January), Ubá 31.8 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Maputo 13.4 °C, Ubá 12.3 °C.
  • Ubá gets 72% more rain and snow a year: 1,351 mm against 785 mm.
  • Maputo gets about 762 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,827 h against 2,065 h; about 1961–1990).
