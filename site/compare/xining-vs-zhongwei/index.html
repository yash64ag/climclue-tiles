Xining vs Zhongwei climate

How the climate of Xining, China compares with Zhongwei, China, 320 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhongwei is 4.8 °C (8.7 °F) warmer over the year: 11.6 °C against 6.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Zhongwei is 8.5 °C warmer (Xining 17.1 °C, Zhongwei 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Xining 22.3 °C (July), Zhongwei 31.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Xining -13.7 °C, Zhongwei -11.2 °C.
  • Xining gets 118% more rain and snow a year: 410 mm against 188 mm.
  • Xining gets about 38 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,734 h against 2,697 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Xining 16 cm, Zhongwei 5.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
