Xining vs Zhongwei climate

How the climate of Xining, China compares with Zhongwei, China, 320 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhongwei is 5.2 °C (9.3 °F) warmer over the year: 11.4 °C against 6.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Zhongwei is 8.7 °C warmer (Xining 16.9 °C, Zhongwei 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Xining 22.2 °C (July), Zhongwei 31.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Xining -14.3 °C, Zhongwei -11.4 °C.
  • Xining gets 134% more rain and snow a year: 433 mm against 185 mm.
  • Xining gets about 38 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,734 h against 2,697 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Xining 16 cm, Zhongwei 5.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
