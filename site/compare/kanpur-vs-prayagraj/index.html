Kanpur vs Prayagraj climate

How the climate of Kanpur, India compares with Prayagraj, India, 187 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Prayagraj is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 25.7 °C against 25.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Prayagraj is 0.8 °C warmer (Kanpur 18.3 °C, Prayagraj 19.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kanpur 39.6 °C (May), Prayagraj 40.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kanpur 8.9 °C, Prayagraj 9.8 °C.
  • Prayagraj gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 907 mm against 876 mm.
  • Prayagraj gets about 3 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,666 h against 2,663 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Prayagraj in 8 of 12 months (3 equal)
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Prayagraj in 7 of 12 months
