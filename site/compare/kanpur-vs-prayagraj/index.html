How the climate of Kanpur, India compares with Prayagraj, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Prayagraj is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 25.7 °C against 25.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Prayagraj is 0.8 °C warmer (Kanpur 23.9 °C, Prayagraj 24.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kanpur 40.7 °C (May), Prayagraj 41.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kanpur 7.2 °C, Prayagraj 8.1 °C.
  • Prayagraj gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 916 mm against 882 mm.
  • Prayagraj gets about 3 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,666 h against 2,663 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Prayagraj in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Prayagraj in 7 of 12 months
