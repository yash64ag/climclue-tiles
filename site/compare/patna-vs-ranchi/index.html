Patna vs Ranchi climate

How the climate of Patna, India compares with Ranchi, India, 251 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Patna is 2.6 °C (4.6 °F) warmer over the year: 25.7 °C against 23.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Patna is 4.1 °C warmer (Patna 29.4 °C, Ranchi 25.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Patna 38.8 °C (May), Ranchi 36.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Patna 9.0 °C, Ranchi 9.0 °C.
  • Ranchi gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 1,413 mm against 1,023 mm.
  • Ranchi gets about 10 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,243 h against 2,233 h; about 1961–1990).
