Jiujiang vs Wuhan climate

How the climate of Jiujiang, China compares with Wuhan, China, 193 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jiujiang is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 17.6 °C against 17.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Jiujiang is 0.6 °C warmer (Jiujiang 12.9 °C, Wuhan 12.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiujiang 33.7 °C (July), Wuhan 33.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jiujiang 0.7 °C, Wuhan 0.1 °C.
  • Jiujiang gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,879 mm against 1,550 mm.
  • Wuhan gets about 59 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,964 h against 1,905 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jiujiang 16 cm, Wuhan 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
