Agartala vs Chattogram climate

How the climate of Agartala, India compares with Chattogram, Bangladesh, 176 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Chattogram is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 25.5 °C against 25.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Chattogram is 1.8 °C warmer (Agartala 19.3 °C, Chattogram 21.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Agartala 32.7 °C (April), Chattogram 31.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Agartala 10.8 °C, Chattogram 13.9 °C.
  • Chattogram gets 54% more rain and snow a year: 3,182 mm against 2,062 mm.
  • Chattogram gets about 46 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,191 h against 2,145 h; about 1961–1990).
