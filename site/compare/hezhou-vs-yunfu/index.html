Hezhou vs Yunfu climate

How the climate of Hezhou, China compares with Yunfu, China, 171 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yunfu is 1.7 °C (3.1 °F) warmer over the year: 22.4 °C against 20.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Yunfu is 3.8 °C warmer (Hezhou 10.1 °C, Yunfu 13.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hezhou 33.4 °C (July), Yunfu 32.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hezhou 6.0 °C, Yunfu 9.6 °C.
  • Yunfu gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 1,900 mm against 1,592 mm.
  • Yunfu gets about 124 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,827 h against 1,703 h; about 1961–1990).
