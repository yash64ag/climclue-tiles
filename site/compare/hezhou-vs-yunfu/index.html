Hezhou vs Yunfu climate

How the climate of Hezhou, China compares with Yunfu, China, 171 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yunfu is 1.5 °C (2.8 °F) warmer over the year: 22.0 °C against 20.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Yunfu is 3.8 °C warmer (Hezhou 9.8 °C, Yunfu 13.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hezhou 31.4 °C (August), Yunfu 31.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hezhou 5.6 °C, Yunfu 9.5 °C.
  • Yunfu gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 1,905 mm against 1,620 mm.
  • Yunfu gets about 124 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,827 h against 1,703 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Yunfu in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: Hezhou in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Yunfu in 10 of 12 months
