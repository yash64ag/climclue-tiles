How the climate of Campinas, São Paulo, Brazil compares with Córdoba, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Córdoba is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 21.4 °C against 21.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Córdoba is 6.1 °C warmer (Campinas, São Paulo 17.6 °C, Córdoba 23.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Campinas, São Paulo 28.7 °C (January), Córdoba 30.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Campinas, São Paulo 10.8 °C, Córdoba 12.2 °C.
  • Córdoba gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,936 mm against 1,607 mm.
  • Campinas, São Paulo gets about 811 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,472 h against 1,661 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
