Changzhou vs Taizhou, Jiangsu climate

How the climate of Changzhou, China compares with Taizhou, Jiangsu, China, 80 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Changzhou is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.3 °C against 15.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Changzhou is 0.8 °C warmer (Changzhou 9.6 °C, Taizhou, Jiangsu 8.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changzhou 31.5 °C (July), Taizhou, Jiangsu 31.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changzhou -0.9 °C, Taizhou, Jiangsu -2.1 °C.
  • Changzhou gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,226 mm against 1,116 mm.
  • Taizhou, Jiangsu gets about 61 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,078 h against 2,017 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changzhou 18 cm, Taizhou, Jiangsu 18 cm (ERA5-Land).
