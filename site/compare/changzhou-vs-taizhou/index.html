Changzhou vs Taizhou, Jiangsu climate

How the climate of Changzhou, China compares with Taizhou, Jiangsu, China, 80 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Changzhou is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.3 °C against 15.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Changzhou is 0.9 °C warmer (Changzhou 5.5 °C, Taizhou, Jiangsu 4.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changzhou 32.0 °C (July), Taizhou, Jiangsu 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changzhou -0.6 °C, Taizhou, Jiangsu -1.5 °C.
  • Changzhou gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,236 mm against 1,115 mm.
  • Taizhou, Jiangsu gets about 61 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,078 h against 2,017 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changzhou 17 cm, Taizhou, Jiangsu 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
