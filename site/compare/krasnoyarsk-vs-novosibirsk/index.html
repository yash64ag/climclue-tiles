Krasnoyarsk vs Novosibirsk climate

How the climate of Krasnoyarsk, Russia compares with Novosibirsk, Russia, 639 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Krasnoyarsk is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 1.6 °C against 1.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Krasnoyarsk is 0.9 °C warmer (Krasnoyarsk -6.3 °C, Novosibirsk -7.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Krasnoyarsk 24.0 °C (July), Novosibirsk 23.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Krasnoyarsk -21.8 °C, Novosibirsk -19.5 °C.
  • Novosibirsk gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 492 mm against 461 mm.
  • Novosibirsk gets about 278 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,107 h against 1,829 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Krasnoyarsk 122 cm, Novosibirsk 163 cm (ERA5-Land).
