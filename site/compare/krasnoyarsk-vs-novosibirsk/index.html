Krasnoyarsk vs Novosibirsk climate

How the climate of Krasnoyarsk, Russia compares with Novosibirsk, Russia, 639 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Krasnoyarsk and Novosibirsk have the same yearly average temperature (1.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Krasnoyarsk is 1.0 °C warmer (Krasnoyarsk -6.2 °C, Novosibirsk -7.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Krasnoyarsk 25.4 °C (July), Novosibirsk 25.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Krasnoyarsk -21.7 °C, Novosibirsk -21.1 °C.
  • Novosibirsk gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 463 mm against 451 mm.
  • Novosibirsk gets about 278 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,107 h against 1,829 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Krasnoyarsk 122 cm, Novosibirsk 163 cm (ERA5-Land).
