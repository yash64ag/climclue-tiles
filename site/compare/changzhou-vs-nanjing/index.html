Changzhou vs Nanjing climate

How the climate of Changzhou, China compares with Nanjing, China, 116 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Changzhou is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.3 °C against 16.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Changzhou is 0.6 °C warmer (Changzhou 5.7 °C, Nanjing 5.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changzhou 31.5 °C (July), Nanjing 31.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changzhou -0.9 °C, Nanjing -1.6 °C.
  • Changzhou gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,226 mm against 1,167 mm.
  • Nanjing gets about 38 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,055 h against 2,017 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changzhou 18 cm, Nanjing 24 cm (ERA5-Land).
