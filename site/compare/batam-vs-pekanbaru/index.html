Batam vs Pekanbaru climate

How the climate of Batam, Indonesia compares with Pekanbaru, Indonesia, 296 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Batam is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Batam is 0.7 °C warmer (Batam 27.8 °C, Pekanbaru 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Batam 31.6 °C (May), Pekanbaru 32.4 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Batam 22.1 °C, Pekanbaru 20.8 °C.
  • Pekanbaru gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 2,786 mm against 2,545 mm.
  • Pekanbaru gets about 193 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,230 h against 2,037 h; about 1961–1990).
