Batam vs Pekanbaru climate

How the climate of Batam, Indonesia compares with Pekanbaru, Indonesia, 296 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Batam is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Batam is 0.7 °C warmer (Batam 27.1 °C, Pekanbaru 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Batam 28.6 °C (May), Pekanbaru 30.6 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Batam 25.4 °C, Pekanbaru 22.4 °C.
  • Pekanbaru gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 2,794 mm against 2,597 mm.
  • Pekanbaru gets about 193 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,230 h against 2,037 h; about 1961–1990).
