How the climate of Kayseri, Turkey compares with Marand, Iran: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kayseri is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 12.3 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Kayseri is 2.4 °C warmer (Kayseri -0.1 °C, Marand -2.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kayseri 34.0 °C (August), Marand 31.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kayseri -5.1 °C, Marand -6.7 °C.
  • Marand gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 365 mm against 347 mm.
  • Kayseri gets about 165 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,867 h against 2,701 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kayseri 47 cm, Marand 61 cm (ERA5-Land).
