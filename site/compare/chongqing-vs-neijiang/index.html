How the climate of Chongqing, China compares with Neijiang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Chongqing is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 19.1 °C against 18.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Chongqing is 1.8 °C warmer (Chongqing 29.0 °C, Neijiang 27.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chongqing 33.3 °C (August), Neijiang 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chongqing 6.5 °C, Neijiang 5.6 °C.
  • Chongqing gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,104 mm against 1,088 mm.
  • Neijiang gets about 107 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,305 h against 1,198 h; about 1961–1990).
