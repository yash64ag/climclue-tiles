How the climate of Bokhtar, Tajikistan compares with Quetta, Pakistan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Quetta is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 17.5 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Bokhtar is 1.6 °C warmer (Bokhtar 29.3 °C, Quetta 27.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bokhtar 37.7 °C (July), Quetta 36.8 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Bokhtar 0.4 °C, Quetta -2.0 °C.
  • Quetta gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 317 mm against 315 mm.
  • Quetta gets about 628 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,333 h against 2,704 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bokhtar 17 cm, Quetta 10 cm (ERA5-Land).
