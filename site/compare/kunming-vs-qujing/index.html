Kunming vs Qujing climate

How the climate of Kunming, China compares with Qujing, China, 118 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kunming is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 15.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Kunming is 1.6 °C warmer (Kunming 12.3 °C, Qujing 10.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kunming 25.9 °C (April), Qujing 24.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kunming 3.3 °C, Qujing 2.3 °C.
  • Qujing gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,007 mm against 929 mm.
  • Kunming gets about 269 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,203 h against 1,933 h; about 1961–1990).
