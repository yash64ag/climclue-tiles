Algiers vs Bizerte climate

How the climate of Algiers, Algeria compares with Bizerte, Tunisia, 606 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bizerte is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 18.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Bizerte is 1.3 °C warmer (Algiers 12.9 °C, Bizerte 14.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Algiers 32.4 °C (August), Bizerte 31.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Algiers 6.7 °C, Bizerte 8.1 °C.
  • Bizerte gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 724 mm against 682 mm.
  • Algiers gets about 197 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,886 h against 2,689 h; about 1961–1990).
