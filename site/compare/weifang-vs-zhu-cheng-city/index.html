How the climate of Weifang, China compares with Zhu Cheng City, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Weifang is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 13.4 °C against 13.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Weifang is 1.2 °C warmer (Weifang 24.3 °C, Zhu Cheng City 23.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Weifang 31.2 °C (July), Zhu Cheng City 29.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Weifang -7.1 °C, Zhu Cheng City -5.6 °C.
  • Zhu Cheng City gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 702 mm against 564 mm.
  • Weifang gets about 75 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,525 h against 2,450 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Weifang 12 cm, Zhu Cheng City 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
