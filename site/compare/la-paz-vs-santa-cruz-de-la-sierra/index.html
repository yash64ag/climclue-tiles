How the climate of La Paz, Bolivia compares with Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Santa Cruz de la Sierra is 15.1 °C (27.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.5 °C against 8.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Santa Cruz de la Sierra is 16.1 °C warmer (La Paz 9.4 °C, Santa Cruz de la Sierra 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: La Paz 16.6 °C (November), Santa Cruz de la Sierra 31.1 °C (November). Coldest-month night lows: La Paz -1.6 °C, Santa Cruz de la Sierra 14.4 °C.
  • Santa Cruz de la Sierra gets 39% more rain and snow a year: 1,479 mm against 1,064 mm.
  • Santa Cruz de la Sierra gets about 316 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,665 h against 2,349 h; about 1961–1990).
