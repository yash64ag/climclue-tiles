La Paz vs Santa Cruz de la Sierra climate

How the climate of La Paz, Bolivia compares with Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia, 547 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Santa Cruz de la Sierra is 17.2 °C (31.0 °F) warmer over the year: 23.5 °C against 6.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Santa Cruz de la Sierra is 18.3 °C warmer (La Paz 7.3 °C, Santa Cruz de la Sierra 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: La Paz 12.7 °C (November), Santa Cruz de la Sierra 29.6 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: La Paz -2.9 °C, Santa Cruz de la Sierra 16.3 °C.
  • Santa Cruz de la Sierra gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,501 mm against 1,356 mm.
  • Santa Cruz de la Sierra gets about 316 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,665 h against 2,349 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: La Paz 20 cm, Santa Cruz de la Sierra 0 cm (ERA5-Land).
