How the climate of Karaj, Iran compares with Tabriz, Iran: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Karaj is 2.8 °C (5.1 °F) warmer over the year: 14.4 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Karaj is 4.5 °C warmer (Karaj 25.6 °C, Tabriz 21.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Karaj 35.3 °C (July), Tabriz 31.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Karaj -4.8 °C, Tabriz -5.9 °C.
  • Karaj gets 102% more rain and snow a year: 478 mm against 236 mm.
  • Karaj gets about 67 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,774 h against 2,707 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Karaj 78 cm, Tabriz 50 cm (ERA5-Land).
