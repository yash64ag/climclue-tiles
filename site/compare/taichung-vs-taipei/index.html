Taichung vs Taipei climate

How the climate of Taichung, Taiwan compares with Taipei, Taiwan, 132 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Taichung is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 23.3 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Taichung is 1.9 °C warmer (Taichung 19.8 °C, Taipei 17.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Taichung 29.9 °C (July), Taipei 30.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Taichung 13.5 °C, Taipei 12.6 °C.
  • Taipei gets 62% more rain and snow a year: 3,155 mm against 1,949 mm.
  • Taichung gets about 135 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,837 h against 1,702 h; about 1961–1990).
