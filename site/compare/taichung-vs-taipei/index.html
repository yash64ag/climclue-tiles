How the climate of Taichung, Taiwan compares with Taipei, Taiwan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Taichung is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 23.3 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Taichung is 2.0 °C warmer (Taichung 19.9 °C, Taipei 17.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Taichung 31.7 °C (July), Taipei 33.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Taichung 13.3 °C, Taipei 12.7 °C.
  • Taipei gets 48% more rain and snow a year: 3,040 mm against 2,055 mm.
  • Taichung gets about 135 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,837 h against 1,702 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Taichung in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: Taichung in 8 of 12 months
