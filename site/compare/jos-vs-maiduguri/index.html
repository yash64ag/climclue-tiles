Jos vs Maiduguri climate

How the climate of Jos, Nigeria compares with Maiduguri, Nigeria, 512 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Maiduguri is 6.0 °C (10.9 °F) warmer over the year: 27.6 °C against 21.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Maiduguri is 9.0 °C warmer (Jos 21.5 °C, Maiduguri 30.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jos 31.5 °C (April), Maiduguri 39.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Jos 12.7 °C, Maiduguri 14.2 °C.
  • Jos gets 108% more rain and snow a year: 1,201 mm against 576 mm.
  • Maiduguri gets about 357 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,002 h against 2,645 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
