How the climate of Buenos Aires, Argentina compares with Santiago, Chile: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Buenos Aires is 2.1 °C (3.7 °F) warmer over the year: 17.7 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Buenos Aires is 2.9 °C warmer (Buenos Aires 13.0 °C, Santiago 10.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Buenos Aires 29.4 °C (January), Santiago 28.3 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Buenos Aires 7.1 °C, Santiago 3.7 °C.
  • Buenos Aires gets 92% more rain and snow a year: 1,189 mm against 618 mm.
  • Santiago gets about 105 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,538 h against 2,433 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Buenos Aires in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Santiago in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
