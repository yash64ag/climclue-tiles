Hong Kong vs Zhuhai, Guangdong climate

How the climate of Hong Kong, Hong Kong compares with Zhuhai, Guangdong, China, 62 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhuhai, Guangdong is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 23.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Zhuhai, Guangdong is 0.4 °C warmer (Hong Kong 27.5 °C, Zhuhai, Guangdong 27.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hong Kong 30.7 °C (July), Zhuhai, Guangdong 31.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hong Kong 13.4 °C, Zhuhai, Guangdong 12.5 °C.
  • Hong Kong gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 2,341 mm against 2,075 mm.
  • Hong Kong gets about 49 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,994 h against 1,945 h; about 1961–1990).
