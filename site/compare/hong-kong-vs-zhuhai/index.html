Hong Kong vs Zhuhai climate

How the climate of Hong Kong, Hong Kong compares with Zhuhai, China, 62 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhuhai is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Zhuhai is 1.0 °C warmer (Hong Kong 27.6 °C, Zhuhai 28.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hong Kong 28.9 °C (July), Zhuhai 29.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Hong Kong 13.0 °C, Zhuhai 12.9 °C.
  • Hong Kong gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 2,352 mm against 2,096 mm.
  • Hong Kong gets about 49 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,994 h against 1,945 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Zhuhai in 9 of 12 months (3 equal)
  • Drier: Hong Kong in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Hong Kong in 11 of 12 months
