Putuo vs Shanghai climate

How the climate of Putuo, China compares with Shanghai, China, 7 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Putuo and Shanghai have the same yearly average temperature (16.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Shanghai is 0.2 °C warmer (Putuo 7.0 °C, Shanghai 7.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Putuo 31.7 °C (July), Shanghai 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Putuo 1.0 °C, Shanghai 1.1 °C.
  • Shanghai gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,476 mm against 1,466 mm.
  • Shanghai gets about 1 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,969 h against 1,969 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Putuo 8.9 cm, Shanghai 7.7 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 5 months each
  • Drier: Putuo in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Shanghai in 4 of 12 months (6 equal)
