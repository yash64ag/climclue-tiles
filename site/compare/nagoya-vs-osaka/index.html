Nagoya vs Osaka climate

How the climate of Nagoya, Japan compares with Osaka, Japan, 139 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Osaka is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 16.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Osaka is 1.1 °C warmer (Nagoya 11.9 °C, Osaka 13.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nagoya 31.0 °C (August), Osaka 32.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Nagoya -1.2 °C, Osaka 0.6 °C.
  • Nagoya gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,644 mm against 1,421 mm.
  • Nagoya gets about 116 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,969 h against 1,852 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nagoya 9.2 cm, Osaka 11 cm (ERA5-Land).
