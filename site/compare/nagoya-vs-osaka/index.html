How the climate of Nagoya, Japan compares with Osaka, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Osaka is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 15.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Osaka is 1.3 °C warmer (Nagoya 6.8 °C, Osaka 8.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nagoya 32.0 °C (August), Osaka 33.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Nagoya 0.4 °C, Osaka 2.0 °C.
  • Nagoya gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 1,689 mm against 1,418 mm.
  • Nagoya gets about 116 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,969 h against 1,852 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nagoya 7.4 cm, Osaka 4.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
