Biên Hòa vs Phnom Penh climate

How the climate of Biên Hòa, Vietnam compares with Phnom Penh, Cambodia, 219 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Phnom Penh is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 28.2 °C against 27.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Phnom Penh is 1.2 °C warmer (Biên Hòa 27.1 °C, Phnom Penh 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Biên Hòa 34.6 °C (April), Phnom Penh 35.6 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Biên Hòa 21.5 °C, Phnom Penh 21.6 °C.
  • Biên Hòa gets 40% more rain and snow a year: 1,949 mm against 1,395 mm.
  • Phnom Penh gets about 808 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,549 h against 1,741 h; about 1961–1990).
