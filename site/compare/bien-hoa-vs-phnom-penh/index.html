Biên Hòa vs Phnom Penh climate

How the climate of Biên Hòa, Vietnam compares with Phnom Penh, Cambodia, 219 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Phnom Penh is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 28.2 °C against 27.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Phnom Penh is 1.3 °C warmer (Biên Hòa 27.1 °C, Phnom Penh 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Biên Hòa 34.0 °C (April), Phnom Penh 34.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Biên Hòa 21.7 °C, Phnom Penh 22.1 °C.
  • Biên Hòa gets 42% more rain and snow a year: 1,976 mm against 1,391 mm.
  • Phnom Penh gets about 808 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,549 h against 1,741 h; about 1961–1990).
