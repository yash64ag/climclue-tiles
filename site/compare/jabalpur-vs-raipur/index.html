Jabalpur vs Raipur climate

How the climate of Jabalpur, India compares with Raipur, India, 276 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Raipur is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.7 °C against 25.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Raipur is 2.6 °C warmer (Jabalpur 20.6 °C, Raipur 23.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jabalpur 40.4 °C (May), Raipur 41.1 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Jabalpur 11.3 °C, Raipur 13.3 °C.
  • Jabalpur gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,351 mm against 1,280 mm.
  • Jabalpur gets about 94 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,716 h against 2,623 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Raipur in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Jabalpur in 11 of 12 months
