Jabalpur vs Raipur, Chhattisgarh climate

How the climate of Jabalpur, India compares with Raipur, Chhattisgarh, India, 276 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Raipur, Chhattisgarh is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 25.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Raipur, Chhattisgarh is 2.7 °C warmer (Jabalpur 20.5 °C, Raipur, Chhattisgarh 23.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jabalpur 41.5 °C (May), Raipur, Chhattisgarh 41.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Jabalpur 9.5 °C, Raipur, Chhattisgarh 12.6 °C.
  • Jabalpur gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,370 mm against 1,277 mm.
  • Jabalpur gets about 94 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,716 h against 2,623 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Raipur, Chhattisgarh in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Jabalpur in 11 of 12 months
