Havana vs Miami climate

How the climate of Havana, Cuba compares with Miami, United States, 368 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Havana is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 25.4 °C against 24.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Havana is 1.6 °C warmer (Havana 23.6 °C, Miami 22.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Havana 31.7 °C (August), Miami 29.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Havana 19.8 °C, Miami 18.9 °C.
  • Miami gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,455 mm against 1,383 mm.
  • Miami gets about 153 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,103 h against 2,950 h; about 1961–1990).
