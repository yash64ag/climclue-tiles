Jiangyin vs Norman climate

How the climate of Jiangyin, China compares with Norman, United States, 11,577 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Norman is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 16.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Norman is 1.7 °C warmer (Jiangyin 9.2 °C, Norman 10.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiangyin 31.8 °C (July), Norman 34.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jiangyin -0.7 °C, Norman -2.4 °C.
  • Jiangyin gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 1,166 mm against 979 mm.
  • Norman gets about 972 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,003 h against 2,031 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jiangyin 14 cm, Norman 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
