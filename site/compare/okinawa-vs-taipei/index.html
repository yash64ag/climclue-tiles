Okinawa vs Taipei climate

How the climate of Okinawa, Japan compares with Taipei, Taiwan, 645 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Okinawa is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 22.8 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Okinawa is 1.9 °C warmer (Okinawa 17.4 °C, Taipei 15.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Okinawa 28.4 °C (August), Taipei 30.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Okinawa 16.5 °C, Taipei 12.6 °C.
  • Taipei gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 3,155 mm against 2,484 mm.
  • Okinawa gets about 119 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,821 h against 1,702 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Okinawa in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Okinawa in 11 of 12 months
