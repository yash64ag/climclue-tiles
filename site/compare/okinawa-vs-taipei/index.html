How the climate of Okinawa, Japan compares with Taipei, Taiwan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Okinawa is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 22.7 °C against 22.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Taipei is 0.9 °C warmer (Okinawa 26.5 °C, Taipei 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Okinawa 31.2 °C (July), Taipei 33.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Okinawa 13.5 °C, Taipei 12.6 °C.
  • Taipei gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 3,040 mm against 2,597 mm.
  • Okinawa gets about 119 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,821 h against 1,702 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Okinawa in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Okinawa in 8 of 12 months
