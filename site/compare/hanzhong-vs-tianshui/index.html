Hanzhong vs Tianshui climate

How the climate of Hanzhong, China compares with Tianshui, China, 205 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Hanzhong is 3.1 °C (5.6 °F) warmer over the year: 15.5 °C against 12.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Hanzhong is 4.8 °C warmer (Hanzhong 4.2 °C, Tianshui -0.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hanzhong 30.1 °C (July), Tianshui 29.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hanzhong 0.1 °C, Tianshui -5.6 °C.
  • Hanzhong gets 97% more rain and snow a year: 981 mm against 499 mm.
  • Tianshui gets about 358 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,026 h against 1,669 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hanzhong 4.4 cm, Tianshui 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
