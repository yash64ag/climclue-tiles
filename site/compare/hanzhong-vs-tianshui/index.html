Hanzhong vs Tianshui climate

How the climate of Hanzhong, China compares with Tianshui, China, 205 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Hanzhong is 4.5 °C (8.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.5 °C against 11.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Hanzhong is 6.1 °C warmer (Hanzhong 4.0 °C, Tianshui -2.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hanzhong 28.9 °C (July), Tianshui 26.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hanzhong -0.9 °C, Tianshui -7.4 °C.
  • Hanzhong gets 73% more rain and snow a year: 1,000 mm against 577 mm.
  • Tianshui gets about 358 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,026 h against 1,669 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hanzhong 4.4 cm, Tianshui 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
