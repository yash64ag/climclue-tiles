How the climate of Isiro, Democratic Republic of the Congo compares with Yaoundé, Cameroon: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yaoundé is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 22.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Isiro is 0.5 °C warmer (Isiro 23.9 °C, Yaoundé 23.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Isiro 31.5 °C (February), Yaoundé 29.8 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Isiro 16.5 °C, Yaoundé 18.0 °C.
  • Isiro gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 2,167 mm against 1,794 mm.
  • Isiro gets about 465 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,228 h against 1,763 h; about 1961–1990).
