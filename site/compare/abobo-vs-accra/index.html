Abobo vs Accra climate

How the climate of Abobo, Ivory Coast compares with Accra, Ghana, 423 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Accra is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.2 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Accra is 1.3 °C warmer (Abobo 25.7 °C, Accra 27.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abobo 30.6 °C (March), Accra 31.7 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Abobo 21.2 °C, Accra 21.0 °C.
  • Abobo gets 150% more rain and snow a year: 2,100 mm against 840 mm.
  • Accra gets about 156 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,268 h against 2,113 h; about 1961–1990).
