Nampula vs Nashik climate

How the climate of Nampula, Mozambique compares with Nashik, India, 5,430 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Nashik is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 24.1 °C against 23.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Nashik is 6.4 °C warmer (Nampula 22.0 °C, Nashik 28.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nampula 31.8 °C (November), Nashik 36.1 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Nampula 15.0 °C, Nashik 11.0 °C.
  • Nampula gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,257 mm against 1,180 mm.
  • Nampula gets about 81 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,838 h against 2,758 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
