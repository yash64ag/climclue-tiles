Nampula vs Nashik climate

How the climate of Nampula, Mozambique compares with Nashik, India, 5,430 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Nashik is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.8 °C against 23.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Nampula is 5.9 °C warmer (Nampula 25.9 °C, Nashik 20.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nampula 32.3 °C (November), Nashik 34.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Nampula 15.7 °C, Nashik 13.9 °C.
  • Nashik gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 1,409 mm against 1,221 mm.
  • Nampula gets about 81 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,838 h against 2,758 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Nashik in 7 of 12 months

  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
