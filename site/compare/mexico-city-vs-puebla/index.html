Mexico City vs Puebla climate

How the climate of Mexico City, Mexico compares with Puebla, Mexico, 106 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Puebla is 1.3 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 16.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Puebla is 1.7 °C warmer (Mexico City 14.3 °C, Puebla 16.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mexico City 24.4 °C (April), Puebla 25.5 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Mexico City 5.7 °C, Puebla 7.4 °C.
  • Puebla gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 739 mm against 719 mm.
  • Puebla gets about 410 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,426 h against 2,016 h; about 1961–1990).
