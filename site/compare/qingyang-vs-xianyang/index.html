How the climate of Qingyang, Gansu, China compares with Xianyang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Xianyang is 5.5 °C (9.9 °F) warmer over the year: 15.0 °C against 9.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Xianyang is 5.9 °C warmer (Qingyang, Gansu -3.4 °C, Xianyang 2.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qingyang, Gansu 27.3 °C (July), Xianyang 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Qingyang, Gansu -9.6 °C, Xianyang -3.2 °C.
  • Qingyang, Gansu gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 643 mm against 578 mm.
  • Qingyang, Gansu gets about 340 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,270 h against 1,931 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Qingyang, Gansu 34 cm, Xianyang 8.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
