Qingyang vs Xianyang climate

How the climate of Qingyang, China compares with Xianyang, China, 180 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Xianyang is 5.6 °C (10.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.4 °C against 9.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Xianyang is 6.0 °C warmer (Qingyang -4.5 °C, Xianyang 1.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qingyang 27.2 °C (July), Xianyang 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Qingyang -9.8 °C, Xianyang -3.5 °C.
  • Qingyang gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 618 mm against 582 mm.
  • Qingyang gets about 340 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,270 h against 1,931 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Qingyang 47 cm, Xianyang 27 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Qingyang in 12 of 12 months
