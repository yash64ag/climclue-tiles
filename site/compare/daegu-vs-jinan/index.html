Daegu vs Jinan climate

How the climate of Daegu, South Korea compares with Jinan, China, 1,043 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jinan is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 14.3 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Jinan is 2.7 °C warmer (Daegu 23.1 °C, Jinan 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daegu 31.1 °C (August), Jinan 31.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Daegu -5.3 °C, Jinan -5.7 °C.
  • Daegu gets 49% more rain and snow a year: 979 mm against 657 mm.
  • Jinan gets about 71 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,554 h against 2,483 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daegu 13 cm, Jinan 10 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
