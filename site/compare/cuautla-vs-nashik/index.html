How the climate of Cuautla, Mexico compares with Nashik, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Nashik is 2.1 °C (3.9 °F) warmer over the year: 24.1 °C against 22.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Nashik is 3.6 °C warmer (Cuautla 24.4 °C, Nashik 28.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cuautla 33.4 °C (April), Nashik 36.1 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Cuautla 10.4 °C, Nashik 11.0 °C.
  • Nashik gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,180 mm against 1,128 mm.
  • Nashik gets about 591 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,758 h against 2,167 h; about 1961–1990).
