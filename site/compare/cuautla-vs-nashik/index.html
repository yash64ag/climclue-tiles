Cuautla vs Nashik climate

How the climate of Cuautla, Mexico compares with Nashik, India, 15,628 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Nashik is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 24.1 °C against 22.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Nashik is 2.5 °C warmer (Cuautla 22.2 °C, Nashik 24.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cuautla 35.3 °C (April), Nashik 36.1 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Cuautla 10.8 °C, Nashik 11.0 °C.
  • Nashik gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,180 mm against 1,128 mm.
  • Nashik gets about 591 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,758 h against 2,167 h; about 1961–1990).
