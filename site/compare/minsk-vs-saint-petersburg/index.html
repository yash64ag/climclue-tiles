How the climate of Minsk, Belarus compares with Saint Petersburg, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Minsk is 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) warmer over the year: 6.3 °C against 4.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Minsk is 2.8 °C warmer (Minsk 13.0 °C, Saint Petersburg 10.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Minsk 22.9 °C (July), Saint Petersburg 21.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Minsk -8.2 °C, Saint Petersburg -9.6 °C.
  • Saint Petersburg gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 690 mm against 686 mm.
  • Minsk gets about 120 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,741 h against 1,621 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Minsk 142 cm, Saint Petersburg 179 cm (ERA5-Land).
