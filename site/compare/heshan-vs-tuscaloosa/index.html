How the climate of Heshan, Hunan, China compares with Tuscaloosa, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tuscaloosa is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 18.1 °C against 17.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Tuscaloosa is 2.1 °C warmer (Heshan, Hunan 11.7 °C, Tuscaloosa 13.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Heshan, Hunan 33.2 °C (July), Tuscaloosa 33.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Heshan, Hunan 2.5 °C, Tuscaloosa 1.4 °C.
  • Heshan, Hunan gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 1,590 mm against 1,354 mm.
  • Tuscaloosa gets about 908 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,496 h against 1,588 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Heshan, Hunan 14 cm, Tuscaloosa 2.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
