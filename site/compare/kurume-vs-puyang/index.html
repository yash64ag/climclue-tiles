Kurume vs Puyang, Zhejiang climate

How the climate of Kurume, Japan compares with Puyang, Zhejiang, China, 1,096 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Puyang, Zhejiang is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 17.7 °C against 16.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Puyang, Zhejiang is 2.8 °C warmer (Kurume 26.9 °C, Puyang, Zhejiang 29.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kurume 32.2 °C (August), Puyang, Zhejiang 34.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kurume 1.8 °C, Puyang, Zhejiang 1.2 °C.
  • Kurume gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,854 mm against 1,592 mm.
  • Puyang, Zhejiang gets about 83 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,868 h against 1,785 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kurume 6.0 cm, Puyang, Zhejiang 26 cm (ERA5-Land).
