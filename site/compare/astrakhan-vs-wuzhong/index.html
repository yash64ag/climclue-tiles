Astrakhan vs Wuzhong climate

How the climate of Astrakhan, Russia compares with Wuzhong, China, 4,772 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Wuzhong is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 11.2 °C against 10.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Wuzhong is 3.7 °C warmer (Astrakhan 2.1 °C, Wuzhong 5.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Astrakhan 31.1 °C (July), Wuzhong 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Astrakhan -6.6 °C, Wuzhong -11.6 °C.
  • Astrakhan gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 236 mm against 185 mm.
  • Wuzhong gets about 401 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,752 h against 2,350 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Astrakhan 39 cm, Wuzhong 6.3 cm (ERA5-Land).
