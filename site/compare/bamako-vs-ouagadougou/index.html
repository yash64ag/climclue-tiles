Bamako vs Ouagadougou climate

How the climate of Bamako, Mali compares with Ouagadougou, Burkina Faso, 700 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ouagadougou is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 28.2 °C against 27.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Ouagadougou is 1.1 °C warmer (Bamako 27.4 °C, Ouagadougou 28.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bamako 39.1 °C (April), Ouagadougou 39.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Bamako 17.1 °C, Ouagadougou 17.2 °C.
  • Bamako gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 893 mm against 728 mm.
  • Ouagadougou gets about 15 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,948 h against 2,933 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Ouagadougou in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: Ouagadougou in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
