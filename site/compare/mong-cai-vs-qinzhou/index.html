How the climate of Móng Cái, Vietnam compares with Qinzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Móng Cái is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 22.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Móng Cái is 0.8 °C warmer (Móng Cái 15.3 °C, Qinzhou 14.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Móng Cái 32.1 °C (July), Qinzhou 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Móng Cái 11.7 °C, Qinzhou 10.8 °C.
  • Móng Cái gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 2,993 mm against 2,418 mm.
  • Qinzhou gets about 28 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,777 h against 1,749 h; about 1961–1990).
