Mwanza vs Tegucigalpa climate

How the climate of Mwanza, Tanzania compares with Tegucigalpa, Honduras, 13,320 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mwanza is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 22.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Mwanza is 2.0 °C warmer (Mwanza 22.8 °C, Tegucigalpa 20.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mwanza 28.9 °C (June), Tegucigalpa 31.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Mwanza 16.5 °C, Tegucigalpa 15.2 °C.
  • Tegucigalpa gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,055 mm against 988 mm.
  • Mwanza gets about 457 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,789 h against 2,332 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Mwanza in 10 of 12 months
