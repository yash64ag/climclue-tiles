Jinjiang vs Lạng Sơn climate

How the climate of Jinjiang, China compares with Lạng Sơn, Vietnam, 1,250 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lạng Sơn is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 21.6 °C against 21.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Lạng Sơn is 2.4 °C warmer (Jinjiang 20.1 °C, Lạng Sơn 22.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinjiang 31.0 °C (July), Lạng Sơn 29.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinjiang 9.2 °C, Lạng Sơn 9.7 °C.
  • Lạng Sơn gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 1,508 mm against 1,252 mm.
  • Jinjiang gets about 349 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,967 h against 1,618 h; about 1961–1990).
