Jinjiang vs Lạng Sơn climate

How the climate of Jinjiang, China compares with Lạng Sơn, Vietnam, 1,250 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lạng Sơn is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 21.8 °C against 21.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Lạng Sơn is 2.7 °C warmer (Jinjiang 19.9 °C, Lạng Sơn 22.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinjiang 30.7 °C (July), Lạng Sơn 30.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinjiang 9.5 °C, Lạng Sơn 9.8 °C.
  • Lạng Sơn gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,491 mm against 1,228 mm.
  • Jinjiang gets about 349 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,967 h against 1,618 h; about 1961–1990).
