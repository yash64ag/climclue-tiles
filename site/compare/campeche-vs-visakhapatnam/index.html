Campeche vs Visakhapatnam climate

How the climate of Campeche, Mexico compares with Visakhapatnam, India, 15,787 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Visakhapatnam is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 27.4 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Visakhapatnam is 1.9 °C warmer (Campeche 25.7 °C, Visakhapatnam 27.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Campeche 34.0 °C (May), Visakhapatnam 34.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Campeche 18.6 °C, Visakhapatnam 18.1 °C.
  • Campeche gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 1,362 mm against 1,087 mm.
  • Visakhapatnam gets about 258 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,555 h against 2,297 h; about 1961–1990).
