Pagadian vs Semarang climate

How the climate of Pagadian, Philippines compares with Semarang, Indonesia, 2,190 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Semarang is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 27.2 °C against 26.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Semarang is 1.5 °C warmer (Pagadian 26.6 °C, Semarang 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pagadian 29.8 °C (April), Semarang 32.6 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Pagadian 24.4 °C, Semarang 22.9 °C.
  • Semarang gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 2,475 mm against 2,190 mm.
  • Semarang gets about 651 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,835 h against 2,184 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Semarang in 6 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Semarang in 9 of 12 months
