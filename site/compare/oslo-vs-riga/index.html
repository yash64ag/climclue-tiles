Oslo vs Riga climate

How the climate of Oslo, Norway compares with Riga, Latvia, 843 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Riga is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 7.1 °C against 6.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Riga is 1.5 °C warmer (Oslo -2.9 °C, Riga -1.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Oslo 21.6 °C (July), Riga 21.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Oslo -7.7 °C, Riga -5.6 °C.
  • Oslo gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 922 mm against 727 mm.
  • Riga gets about 143 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,722 h against 1,579 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Oslo 246 cm, Riga 123 cm (ERA5-Land).
