How the climate of Oslo, Norway compares with Riga, Latvia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Oslo is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 7.2 °C against 7.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Oslo is 1.3 °C warmer (Oslo -2.1 °C, Riga -3.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Oslo 22.4 °C (July), Riga 22.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Oslo -5.0 °C, Riga -6.0 °C.
  • Oslo gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 922 mm against 727 mm.
  • Riga gets about 143 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,722 h against 1,579 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Oslo 246 cm, Riga 123 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Sunnier: Riga in 8 of 12 months
