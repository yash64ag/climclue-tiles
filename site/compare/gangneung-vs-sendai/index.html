Gangneung vs Sendai climate

How the climate of Gangneung, South Korea compares with Sendai, Japan, 1,052 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Sendai is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 12.3 °C against 12.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Sendai is 3.1 °C warmer (Gangneung -1.6 °C, Sendai 1.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gangneung 26.5 °C (August), Sendai 26.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Gangneung -6.5 °C, Sendai -3.0 °C.
  • Gangneung gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,635 mm against 1,518 mm.
  • Gangneung gets about 508 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,327 h against 1,819 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gangneung 78 cm, Sendai 75 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Gangneung in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Gangneung in 12 of 12 months
