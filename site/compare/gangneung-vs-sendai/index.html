How the climate of Gangneung, South Korea compares with Sendai, Miyagi, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Gangneung is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 12.6 °C against 12.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Sendai, Miyagi is 2.6 °C warmer (Gangneung -0.8 °C, Sendai, Miyagi 1.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gangneung 28.2 °C (August), Sendai, Miyagi 27.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Gangneung -5.2 °C, Sendai, Miyagi -1.9 °C.
  • Gangneung gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,559 mm against 1,487 mm.
  • Gangneung gets about 508 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,327 h against 1,819 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gangneung 78 cm, Sendai, Miyagi 75 cm (ERA5-Land).
