Kisangani vs Mbuji-Mayi climate

How the climate of Kisangani, Democratic Republic of the Congo compares with Mbuji-Mayi, Democratic Republic of the Congo, 761 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kisangani is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 24.7 °C against 24.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Kisangani is 1.5 °C warmer (Kisangani 25.9 °C, Mbuji-Mayi 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kisangani 30.9 °C (February), Mbuji-Mayi 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kisangani 21.6 °C, Mbuji-Mayi 18.1 °C.
  • Kisangani gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,658 mm against 1,482 mm.
  • Mbuji-Mayi gets about 283 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,255 h against 1,971 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
