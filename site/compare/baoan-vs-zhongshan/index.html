Bao'an vs Zhongshan climate

How the climate of Bao'an, China compares with Zhongshan, China, 52 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhongshan is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 23.3 °C against 22.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Zhongshan is 1.1 °C warmer (Bao'an 28.4 °C, Zhongshan 29.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bao'an 30.2 °C (August), Zhongshan 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bao'an 12.4 °C, Zhongshan 11.1 °C.
  • Bao'an gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 2,163 mm against 2,016 mm.
  • Bao'an gets about 39 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,927 h against 1,887 h; about 1961–1990).
