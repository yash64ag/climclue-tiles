Main differences

  • Zhongshan, Guangdong is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 23.3 °C against 22.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Zhongshan, Guangdong is 1.0 °C warmer (Bao'an 28.4 °C, Zhongshan, Guangdong 29.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bao'an 31.7 °C (July), Zhongshan, Guangdong 32.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bao'an 11.5 °C, Zhongshan, Guangdong 11.5 °C.
  • Bao'an gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 2,140 mm against 2,037 mm.
  • Bao'an gets about 39 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,927 h against 1,887 h; about 1961–1990).
