Hangzhou vs Huzhou climate

How the climate of Hangzhou, China compares with Huzhou, China, 64 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Hangzhou is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 17.2 °C against 16.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Hangzhou is 0.4 °C warmer (Hangzhou 16.4 °C, Huzhou 16.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hangzhou 33.5 °C (July), Huzhou 32.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hangzhou 0.9 °C, Huzhou 0.5 °C.
  • Hangzhou gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,499 mm against 1,292 mm.
  • Huzhou gets about 52 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,934 h against 1,882 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hangzhou 24 cm, Huzhou 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
