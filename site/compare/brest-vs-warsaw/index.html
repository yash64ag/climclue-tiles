Brest vs Warsaw climate

How the climate of Brest, Belarus compares with Warsaw, Poland, 185 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Warsaw is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 8.5 °C against 8.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Warsaw is 1.3 °C warmer (Brest -2.1 °C, Warsaw -0.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brest 24.5 °C (July), Warsaw 24.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Brest -5.6 °C, Warsaw -4.4 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (584 mm and 582 mm).
  • Brest gets about 144 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,731 h against 1,587 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Brest 82 cm, Warsaw 61 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Warsaw in 10 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Brest in 12 of 12 months
