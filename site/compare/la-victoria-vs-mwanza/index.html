La Victoria vs Mwanza climate

How the climate of La Victoria, Venezuela compares with Mwanza, Tanzania, 11,176 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • La Victoria is 1.4 °C (2.6 °F) warmer over the year: 24.4 °C against 23.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when La Victoria is 2.4 °C warmer (La Victoria 25.4 °C, Mwanza 23.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: La Victoria 31.5 °C (March), Mwanza 28.9 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: La Victoria 17.2 °C, Mwanza 16.5 °C.
  • La Victoria gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 1,141 mm against 988 mm.
  • Mwanza gets about 276 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,789 h against 2,513 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: La Victoria in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Mwanza in 8 of 12 months
