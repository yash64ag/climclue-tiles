How the climate of Baku, Azerbaijan compares with Tabriz, Iran: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Baku is 2.5 °C (4.5 °F) warmer over the year: 15.0 °C against 12.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Baku is 7.5 °C warmer (Baku 6.0 °C, Tabriz -1.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baku 30.2 °C (August), Tabriz 32.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baku 2.8 °C, Tabriz -5.6 °C.
  • Tabriz gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 236 mm against 213 mm.
  • Tabriz gets about 599 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,707 h against 2,108 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baku 7.3 cm, Tabriz 50 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Baku in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Tabriz in 12 of 12 months
