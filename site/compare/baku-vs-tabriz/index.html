Baku vs Tabriz climate

How the climate of Baku, Azerbaijan compares with Tabriz, Iran, 402 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Baku is 3.6 °C (6.4 °F) warmer over the year: 14.9 °C against 11.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Baku is 7.8 °C warmer (Baku 5.7 °C, Tabriz -2.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baku 27.6 °C (August), Tabriz 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baku 4.0 °C, Tabriz -6.7 °C.
  • Tabriz gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 269 mm against 236 mm.
  • Tabriz gets about 599 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,707 h against 2,108 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baku 11 cm, Tabriz 136 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Tabriz in 12 of 12 months
