Novosibirsk vs Ürümqi climate

How the climate of Novosibirsk, Russia compares with Ürümqi, China, 1,292 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ürümqi is 6.2 °C (11.2 °F) warmer over the year: 7.8 °C against 1.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Ürümqi is 8.5 °C warmer (Novosibirsk 2.0 °C, Ürümqi 10.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Novosibirsk 23.4 °C (July), Ürümqi 29.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Novosibirsk -19.5 °C, Ürümqi -13.9 °C.
  • Novosibirsk gets 55% more rain and snow a year: 492 mm against 317 mm.
  • Ürümqi gets about 670 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,777 h against 2,107 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Novosibirsk 223 cm, Ürümqi 103 cm (ERA5-Land).
