Abuja vs Kara climate

How the climate of Abuja, Nigeria compares with Kara, Togo, 694 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kara is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 26.7 °C against 26.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Kara is 1.1 °C warmer (Abuja 24.9 °C, Kara 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abuja 35.3 °C (March), Kara 36.5 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Abuja 20.9 °C, Kara 19.3 °C.
  • Abuja gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 1,468 mm against 1,234 mm.
  • Kara gets about 60 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,532 h against 2,472 h; about 1961–1990).
