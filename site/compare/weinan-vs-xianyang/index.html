How the climate of Weinan, China compares with Xianyang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Weinan is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 15.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Weinan is 1.5 °C warmer (Weinan 27.3 °C, Xianyang 25.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Weinan 34.0 °C (July), Xianyang 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Weinan -3.3 °C, Xianyang -3.2 °C.
  • Xianyang gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 578 mm against 495 mm.
  • Weinan gets about 86 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,017 h against 1,931 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Weinan 5.8 cm, Xianyang 8.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
