Jiangyin vs Zhangjiagang climate

How the climate of Jiangyin, China compares with Zhangjiagang, China, 27 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhangjiagang is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 16.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Zhangjiagang is 0.3 °C warmer (Jiangyin 17.8 °C, Zhangjiagang 18.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiangyin 31.4 °C (July), Zhangjiagang 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jiangyin -1.3 °C, Zhangjiagang -0.8 °C.
  • Zhangjiagang gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,183 mm against 1,147 mm.
  • Jiangyin gets about 5 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,031 h against 2,026 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jiangyin 14 cm, Zhangjiagang 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
