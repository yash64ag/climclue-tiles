How the climate of Hengyang, China compares with Zhuzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hengyang is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 19.1 °C against 18.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Hengyang is 0.6 °C warmer (Hengyang 14.5 °C, Zhuzhou 13.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hengyang 35.0 °C (July), Zhuzhou 34.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hengyang 3.4 °C, Zhuzhou 2.9 °C.
  • Zhuzhou gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,637 mm against 1,588 mm.
  • Hengyang gets about 6 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,612 h against 1,605 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hengyang 6.5 cm, Zhuzhou 6.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
