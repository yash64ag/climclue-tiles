Phổ Yên vs Zhanjiang climate

How the climate of Phổ Yên, Vietnam compares with Zhanjiang, China, 466 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Phổ Yên and Zhanjiang have the same yearly average temperature (23.8 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Phổ Yên is 0.5 °C warmer (Phổ Yên 29.1 °C, Zhanjiang 28.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Phổ Yên 31.9 °C (July), Zhanjiang 31.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Phổ Yên 12.8 °C, Zhanjiang 13.3 °C.
  • Zhanjiang gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,988 mm against 1,975 mm.
  • Zhanjiang gets about 290 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,920 h against 1,630 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Phổ Yên in 6 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: Phổ Yên in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Zhanjiang in 12 of 12 months
