How the climate of Phổ Yên, Vietnam compares with Zhanjiang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Phổ Yên is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 23.8 °C against 23.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Phổ Yên is 0.6 °C warmer (Phổ Yên 29.1 °C, Zhanjiang 28.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Phổ Yên 32.8 °C (July), Zhanjiang 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Phổ Yên 13.5 °C, Zhanjiang 13.3 °C.
  • Zhanjiang gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,982 mm against 1,902 mm.
  • Zhanjiang gets about 290 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,920 h against 1,630 h; about 1961–1990).
