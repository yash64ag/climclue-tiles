Jepara vs Semarang climate

How the climate of Jepara, Indonesia compares with Semarang, Indonesia, 52 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Semarang is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 27.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Semarang is 0.9 °C warmer (Jepara 26.9 °C, Semarang 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jepara 31.0 °C (September), Semarang 33.1 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Jepara 24.0 °C, Semarang 22.9 °C.
  • Jepara gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 2,506 mm against 2,415 mm.
  • Semarang gets about 73 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,835 h against 2,762 h; about 1961–1990).
