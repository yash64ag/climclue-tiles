Jepara vs Semarang climate

How the climate of Jepara, Indonesia compares with Semarang, Indonesia, 52 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Semarang is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 27.2 °C against 27.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Semarang is 0.6 °C warmer (Jepara 27.5 °C, Semarang 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jepara 33.5 °C (September), Semarang 34.1 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Jepara 21.3 °C, Semarang 21.6 °C.
  • Jepara gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 2,502 mm against 2,475 mm.
  • Semarang gets about 73 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,835 h against 2,762 h; about 1961–1990).
