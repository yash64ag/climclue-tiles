How the climate of Lạng Sơn, Vietnam compares with Yulin, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yulin is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 22.5 °C against 21.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Yulin is 1.5 °C warmer (Lạng Sơn 23.2 °C, Yulin 24.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lạng Sơn 31.6 °C (July), Yulin 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lạng Sơn 10.1 °C, Yulin 10.2 °C.
  • Yulin gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 1,753 mm against 1,491 mm.
  • Yulin gets about 167 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,785 h against 1,618 h; about 1961–1990).
