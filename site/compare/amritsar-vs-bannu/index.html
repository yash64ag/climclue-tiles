Amritsar vs Bannu climate

How the climate of Amritsar, India compares with Bannu, Pakistan, 429 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bannu is 0.4 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 23.7 °C against 23.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Bannu is 1.7 °C warmer (Amritsar 32.8 °C, Bannu 34.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amritsar 38.6 °C (June), Bannu 39.6 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Amritsar 5.9 °C, Bannu 4.9 °C.
  • Amritsar gets 35% more rain and snow a year: 758 mm against 562 mm.
  • Bannu gets about 157 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,987 h against 2,830 h; about 1961–1990).
