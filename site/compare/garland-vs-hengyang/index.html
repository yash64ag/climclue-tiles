How the climate of Garland, United States compares with Hengyang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hengyang is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 19.1 °C against 18.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Hengyang is 1.7 °C warmer (Garland 29.1 °C, Hengyang 30.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Garland 35.3 °C (August), Hengyang 35.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Garland 1.6 °C, Hengyang 3.4 °C.
  • Hengyang gets 47% more rain and snow a year: 1,588 mm against 1,079 mm.
  • Garland gets about 1,181 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,793 h against 1,612 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Garland 1.7 cm, Hengyang 6.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
