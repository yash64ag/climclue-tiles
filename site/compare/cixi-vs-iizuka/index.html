How the climate of Cixi, China compares with Iizuka, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Cixi is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 17.4 °C against 17.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Cixi is 1.7 °C warmer (Cixi 29.2 °C, Iizuka 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cixi 32.9 °C (July), Iizuka 32.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Cixi 2.0 °C, Iizuka 2.1 °C.
  • Iizuka gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,669 mm against 1,491 mm.
  • Cixi gets about 124 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,883 h against 1,759 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Cixi 8.8 cm, Iizuka 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
