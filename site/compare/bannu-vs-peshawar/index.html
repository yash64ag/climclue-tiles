How the climate of Bannu, Pakistan compares with Peshawar, Pakistan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bannu is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 23.4 °C against 23.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Bannu is 1.3 °C warmer (Bannu 25.4 °C, Peshawar 24.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bannu 41.8 °C (June), Peshawar 41.4 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Bannu 4.1 °C, Peshawar 4.1 °C.
  • Peshawar gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 583 mm against 576 mm.
  • Bannu gets about 138 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,987 h against 2,849 h; about 1961–1990).
