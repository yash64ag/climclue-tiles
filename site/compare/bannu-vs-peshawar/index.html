Bannu vs Peshawar climate

How the climate of Bannu, Pakistan compares with Peshawar, Pakistan, 145 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bannu is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 23.7 °C against 23.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Bannu is 1.4 °C warmer (Bannu 25.6 °C, Peshawar 24.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bannu 39.6 °C (June), Peshawar 39.8 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Bannu 4.9 °C, Peshawar 4.9 °C.
  • Peshawar gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 590 mm against 562 mm.
  • Bannu gets about 138 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,987 h against 2,849 h; about 1961–1990).
