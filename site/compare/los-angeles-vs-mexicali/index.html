How the climate of Los Angeles, United States compares with Mexicali, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Mexicali is 5.3 °C (9.5 °F) warmer over the year: 23.7 °C against 18.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Mexicali is 11.5 °C warmer (Los Angeles 22.9 °C, Mexicali 34.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Los Angeles 28.8 °C (August), Mexicali 42.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Los Angeles 8.2 °C, Mexicali 6.3 °C.
  • Los Angeles gets 450% more rain and snow a year: 431 mm against 78 mm.
  • Mexicali gets about 459 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,652 h against 3,193 h; about 1961–1990).
