Los Angeles vs Mexicali climate

How the climate of Los Angeles, United States compares with Mexicali, Mexico, 304 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mexicali is 6.5 °C (11.8 °F) warmer over the year: 24.4 °C against 17.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Mexicali is 11.6 °C warmer (Los Angeles 23.1 °C, Mexicali 34.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Los Angeles 32.0 °C (August), Mexicali 42.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Los Angeles 6.7 °C, Mexicali 8.3 °C.
  • Los Angeles gets 452% more rain and snow a year: 426 mm against 77 mm.
  • Mexicali gets about 459 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,652 h against 3,193 h; about 1961–1990).
