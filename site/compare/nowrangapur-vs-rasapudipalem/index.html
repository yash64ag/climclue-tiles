Nowrangapur vs Rasapūdipalem climate

How the climate of Nowrangapur, India compares with Rasapūdipalem, India, 185 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Rasapūdipalem is 2.8 °C (5.0 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 24.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Rasapūdipalem is 4.7 °C warmer (Nowrangapur 18.9 °C, Rasapūdipalem 23.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nowrangapur 36.3 °C (May), Rasapūdipalem 34.6 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Nowrangapur 11.5 °C, Rasapūdipalem 18.0 °C.
  • Nowrangapur gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 1,528 mm against 1,139 mm.
  • Rasapūdipalem gets about 19 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,552 h against 2,533 h; about 1961–1990).
