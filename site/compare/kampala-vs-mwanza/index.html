Kampala vs Mwanza climate

How the climate of Kampala, Uganda compares with Mwanza, Tanzania, 317 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mwanza is 1.4 °C (2.6 °F) warmer over the year: 22.7 °C against 21.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Mwanza is 2.2 °C warmer (Kampala 20.6 °C, Mwanza 22.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kampala 25.4 °C (February), Mwanza 25.8 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Kampala 17.2 °C, Mwanza 18.8 °C.
  • Kampala gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 1,395 mm against 1,049 mm.
  • Mwanza gets about 335 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,789 h against 2,454 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Mwanza in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Mwanza in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Mwanza in 11 of 12 months
