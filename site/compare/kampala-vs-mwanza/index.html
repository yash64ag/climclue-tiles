Kampala vs Mwanza climate

How the climate of Kampala, Uganda compares with Mwanza, Tanzania, 317 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mwanza is 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 21.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Mwanza is 2.2 °C warmer (Kampala 21.2 °C, Mwanza 23.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kampala 25.1 °C (February), Mwanza 25.7 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Kampala 17.8 °C, Mwanza 19.3 °C.
  • Kampala gets 41% more rain and snow a year: 1,388 mm against 988 mm.
  • Mwanza gets about 335 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,789 h against 2,454 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Mwanza in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Mwanza in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Mwanza in 11 of 12 months
