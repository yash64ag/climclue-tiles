Bursa vs Chilliwack climate

How the climate of Bursa, Turkey compares with Chilliwack, Canada, 9,680 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bursa is 4.6 °C (8.3 °F) warmer over the year: 15.1 °C against 10.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Bursa is 6.1 °C warmer (Bursa 11.2 °C, Chilliwack 5.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bursa 31.1 °C (August), Chilliwack 24.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bursa 1.8 °C, Chilliwack -0.9 °C.
  • Chilliwack gets 69% more rain and snow a year: 1,813 mm against 1,076 mm.
  • Bursa gets about 1,106 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,772 h against 1,666 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bursa 344 cm, Chilliwack 143 cm (ERA5-Land).
