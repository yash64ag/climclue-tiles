Bursa vs Chilliwack climate

How the climate of Bursa, Turkey compares with Chilliwack, Canada, 9,680 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bursa is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 11.3 °C against 10.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Bursa is 2.5 °C warmer (Bursa 7.5 °C, Chilliwack 5.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bursa 25.2 °C (August), Chilliwack 25.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bursa -2.9 °C, Chilliwack -1.4 °C.
  • Chilliwack gets 32% more rain and snow a year: 1,849 mm against 1,406 mm.
  • Bursa gets about 1,106 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,772 h against 1,666 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bursa 344 cm, Chilliwack 143 cm (ERA5-Land).
