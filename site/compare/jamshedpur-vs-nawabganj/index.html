How the climate of Jamshedpur, India compares with Nawābganj, Bangladesh: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jamshedpur is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 25.9 °C against 25.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Jamshedpur is 1.8 °C warmer (Jamshedpur 31.4 °C, Nawābganj 29.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jamshedpur 38.3 °C (May), Nawābganj 36.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Jamshedpur 10.8 °C, Nawābganj 10.6 °C.
  • Nawābganj gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,569 mm against 1,468 mm.
  • Jamshedpur gets about 100 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,228 h against 2,129 h; about 1961–1990).
