Jamshedpur vs Nawābganj climate

How the climate of Jamshedpur, India compares with Nawābganj, Bangladesh, 291 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jamshedpur is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 25.7 °C against 25.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Jamshedpur is 1.7 °C warmer (Jamshedpur 31.4 °C, Nawābganj 29.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jamshedpur 37.8 °C (May), Nawābganj 35.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Jamshedpur 11.4 °C, Nawābganj 12.0 °C.
  • Nawābganj gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,558 mm against 1,543 mm.
  • Jamshedpur gets about 100 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,228 h against 2,129 h; about 1961–1990).
