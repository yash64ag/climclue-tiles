How the climate of Kota, India compares with Umarkot, Pakistan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Umarkot is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 26.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Umarkot is 2.2 °C warmer (Kota 28.3 °C, Umarkot 30.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kota 42.5 °C (May), Umarkot 42.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kota 9.7 °C, Umarkot 7.4 °C.
  • Kota gets 183% more rain and snow a year: 751 mm against 266 mm.
  • Umarkot gets about 610 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,477 h against 2,867 h; about 1961–1990).
