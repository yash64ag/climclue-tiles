Kota vs Umarkot climate

How the climate of Kota, India compares with Umarkot, Pakistan, 613 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Umarkot is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 26.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Umarkot is 2.4 °C warmer (Kota 28.1 °C, Umarkot 30.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kota 41.8 °C (May), Umarkot 41.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kota 10.4 °C, Umarkot 11.0 °C.
  • Kota gets 184% more rain and snow a year: 760 mm against 267 mm.
  • Umarkot gets about 610 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,477 h against 2,867 h; about 1961–1990).
