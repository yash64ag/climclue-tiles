Amritsar vs Ludhiana climate

How the climate of Amritsar, India compares with Ludhiana, India, 122 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ludhiana is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 23.4 °C against 23.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Ludhiana is 0.8 °C warmer (Amritsar 26.1 °C, Ludhiana 26.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amritsar 38.5 °C (June), Ludhiana 38.3 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Amritsar 5.8 °C, Ludhiana 6.3 °C.
  • Ludhiana gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 821 mm against 749 mm.
  • Amritsar gets about 62 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,830 h against 2,768 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Ludhiana in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Amritsar in 7 of 12 months
