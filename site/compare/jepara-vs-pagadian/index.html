Jepara vs Pagadian climate

How the climate of Jepara, Indonesia compares with Pagadian, Philippines, 2,139 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jepara is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Jepara is 1.0 °C warmer (Jepara 27.7 °C, Pagadian 26.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jepara 31.3 °C (September), Pagadian 29.8 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Jepara 23.8 °C, Pagadian 24.4 °C.
  • Jepara gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 2,502 mm against 2,190 mm.
  • Jepara gets about 578 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,762 h against 2,184 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Jepara in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Jepara in 8 of 12 months
