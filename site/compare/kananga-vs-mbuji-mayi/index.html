How the climate of Kananga, Democratic Republic of the Congo compares with Mbuji-Mayi, Democratic Republic of the Congo: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Mbuji-Mayi is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 24.4 °C against 24.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Mbuji-Mayi is 0.5 °C warmer (Kananga 24.5 °C, Mbuji-Mayi 25.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kananga 33.2 °C (July), Mbuji-Mayi 32.1 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Kananga 15.9 °C, Mbuji-Mayi 17.1 °C.
  • Kananga gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,542 mm against 1,473 mm.
  • Mbuji-Mayi gets about 150 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,255 h against 2,105 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Mbuji-Mayi in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Mbuji-Mayi in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Mbuji-Mayi in 11 of 12 months
