How the climate of Dhaka, Bangladesh compares with Rangpur, Bangladesh: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Dhaka is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 25.5 °C against 24.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Dhaka is 1.2 °C warmer (Dhaka 28.6 °C, Rangpur 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dhaka 33.3 °C (April), Rangpur 33.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Dhaka 11.2 °C, Rangpur 10.2 °C.
  • Rangpur gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 2,429 mm against 2,134 mm.
  • Rangpur gets about 95 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,194 h against 2,099 h; about 1961–1990).
