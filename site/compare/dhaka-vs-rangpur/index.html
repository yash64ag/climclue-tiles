Dhaka vs Rangpur climate

How the climate of Dhaka, Bangladesh compares with Rangpur, Bangladesh, 255 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dhaka is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 25.5 °C against 24.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Dhaka is 1.2 °C warmer (Dhaka 28.6 °C, Rangpur 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dhaka 32.6 °C (April), Rangpur 32.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Dhaka 12.8 °C, Rangpur 11.7 °C.
  • Rangpur gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 2,428 mm against 2,161 mm.
  • Rangpur gets about 95 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,194 h against 2,099 h; about 1961–1990).
