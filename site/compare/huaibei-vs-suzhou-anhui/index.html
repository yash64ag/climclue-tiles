Huaibei vs Suzhou, Anhui climate

How the climate of Huaibei, China compares with Suzhou, Anhui, China, 41 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Suzhou, Anhui is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 15.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Suzhou, Anhui is 0.3 °C warmer (Huaibei 16.1 °C, Suzhou, Anhui 16.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huaibei 31.1 °C (July), Suzhou, Anhui 31.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Huaibei -3.7 °C, Suzhou, Anhui -3.5 °C.
  • Suzhou, Anhui gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 897 mm against 869 mm.
  • Huaibei gets about 28 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,229 h against 2,201 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Huaibei 24 cm, Suzhou, Anhui 25 cm (ERA5-Land).
