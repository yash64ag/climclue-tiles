How the climate of Huaibei, China compares with Suzhou, Anhui, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Suzhou, Anhui is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 14.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Suzhou, Anhui is 0.4 °C warmer (Huaibei 2.9 °C, Suzhou, Anhui 3.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huaibei 31.5 °C (July), Suzhou, Anhui 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Huaibei -3.8 °C, Suzhou, Anhui -3.4 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (907 mm and 904 mm).
  • Huaibei gets about 28 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,229 h against 2,201 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Huaibei 18 cm, Suzhou, Anhui 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
