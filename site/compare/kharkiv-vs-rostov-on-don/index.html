How the climate of Kharkiv, Ukraine compares with Rostov-on-Don, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Rostov-on-Don is 2.0 °C (3.6 °F) warmer over the year: 10.1 °C against 8.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Rostov-on-Don is 2.6 °C warmer (Kharkiv 20.2 °C, Rostov-on-Don 22.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kharkiv 26.5 °C (July), Rostov-on-Don 29.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kharkiv -7.7 °C, Rostov-on-Don -6.0 °C.
  • Rostov-on-Don gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 616 mm against 526 mm.
  • Rostov-on-Don gets about 334 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,086 h against 1,752 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kharkiv 105 cm, Rostov-on-Don 98 cm (ERA5-Land).
