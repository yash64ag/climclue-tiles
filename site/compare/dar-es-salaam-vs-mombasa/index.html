Dar es Salaam vs Mombasa climate

How the climate of Dar es Salaam, Tanzania compares with Mombasa, Kenya, 311 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mombasa is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.2 °C against 26.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Mombasa is 0.8 °C warmer (Dar es Salaam 26.4 °C, Mombasa 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dar es Salaam 31.0 °C (February), Mombasa 31.9 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Dar es Salaam 21.7 °C, Mombasa 21.9 °C.
  • Dar es Salaam gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,142 mm against 1,092 mm.
  • Mombasa gets about 209 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,940 h against 2,731 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Mombasa in 8 of 12 months
