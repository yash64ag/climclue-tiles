Dar es Salaam vs Mombasa climate

How the climate of Dar es Salaam, Tanzania compares with Mombasa, Kenya, 311 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mombasa is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.2 °C against 26.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Mombasa is 0.7 °C warmer (Dar es Salaam 26.4 °C, Mombasa 27.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dar es Salaam 32.5 °C (February), Mombasa 32.5 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Dar es Salaam 18.9 °C, Mombasa 20.3 °C.
  • Dar es Salaam gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,166 mm against 1,110 mm.
  • Mombasa gets about 209 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,940 h against 2,731 h; about 1961–1990).
