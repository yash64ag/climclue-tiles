How the climate of Buenos Aires, Argentina compares with Córdoba, Argentina: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Córdoba is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 18.0 °C against 17.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Córdoba is 1.7 °C warmer (Buenos Aires 17.4 °C, Córdoba 19.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Buenos Aires 29.3 °C (January), Córdoba 30.1 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Buenos Aires 7.0 °C, Córdoba 4.1 °C.
  • Buenos Aires gets 35% more rain and snow a year: 1,189 mm against 878 mm.
  • Córdoba gets about 159 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,592 h against 2,433 h; about 1961–1990).
