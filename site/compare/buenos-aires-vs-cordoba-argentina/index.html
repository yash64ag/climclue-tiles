Buenos Aires vs Córdoba climate

How the climate of Buenos Aires, Argentina compares with Córdoba, Argentina, 649 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Córdoba is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 16.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Córdoba is 2.1 °C warmer (Buenos Aires 16.4 °C, Córdoba 18.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Buenos Aires 26.7 °C (January), Córdoba 28.8 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Buenos Aires 8.0 °C, Córdoba 5.2 °C.
  • Buenos Aires gets 35% more rain and snow a year: 1,189 mm against 878 mm.
  • Córdoba gets about 159 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,592 h against 2,433 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each

  • Sunnier: Córdoba in 10 of 12 months
