Isfahan vs Shiraz climate

How the climate of Isfahan, Iran compares with Shiraz, Iran, 348 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shiraz is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 17.7 °C against 16.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Shiraz is 3.5 °C warmer (Isfahan 4.1 °C, Shiraz 7.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Isfahan 37.7 °C (July), Shiraz 38.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Isfahan -3.8 °C, Shiraz -1.4 °C.
  • Shiraz gets 195% more rain and snow a year: 423 mm against 144 mm.
  • Shiraz gets about 97 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,282 h against 3,185 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Isfahan 19 cm, Shiraz 36 cm (ERA5-Land).
