Isfahan vs Shiraz climate

How the climate of Isfahan, Iran compares with Shiraz, Iran, 348 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shiraz is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.4 °C against 16.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Shiraz is 2.3 °C warmer (Isfahan 4.0 °C, Shiraz 6.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Isfahan 36.6 °C (July), Shiraz 35.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Isfahan -4.4 °C, Shiraz -1.8 °C.
  • Shiraz gets 216% more rain and snow a year: 477 mm against 151 mm.
  • Shiraz gets about 97 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,282 h against 3,185 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Isfahan 18 cm, Shiraz 34 cm (ERA5-Land).
