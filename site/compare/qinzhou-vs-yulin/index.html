How the climate of Qinzhou, China compares with Yulin, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Qinzhou is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 22.9 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Qinzhou is 0.7 °C warmer (Qinzhou 28.6 °C, Yulin 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qinzhou 32.1 °C (July), Yulin 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Qinzhou 10.8 °C, Yulin 10.2 °C.
  • Qinzhou gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 2,418 mm against 1,753 mm.
  • Yulin gets about 8 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,785 h against 1,777 h; about 1961–1990).
