How the climate of Fuyang, Anhui, China compares with Huainan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Huainan is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.8 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Fuyang, Anhui is 0.4 °C warmer (Fuyang, Anhui 25.8 °C, Huainan 25.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuyang, Anhui 31.9 °C (July), Huainan 31.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuyang, Anhui -2.5 °C, Huainan -2.1 °C.
  • Huainan gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,028 mm against 1,013 mm.
  • Fuyang, Anhui gets about 24 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,134 h against 2,111 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fuyang, Anhui 23 cm, Huainan 19 cm (ERA5-Land).
