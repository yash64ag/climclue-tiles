Jilin vs Ussuriysk climate

How the climate of Jilin, China compares with Ussuriysk, Russia, 433 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jilin is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 5.9 °C against 5.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Jilin is 3.9 °C warmer (Jilin 20.8 °C, Ussuriysk 16.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jilin 26.9 °C (July), Ussuriysk 25.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Jilin -22.4 °C, Ussuriysk -22.7 °C.
  • Jilin gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 650 mm against 606 mm.
  • Jilin gets about 248 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,506 h against 2,258 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jilin 101 cm, Ussuriysk 97 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Jilin in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Jilin in 11 of 12 months
