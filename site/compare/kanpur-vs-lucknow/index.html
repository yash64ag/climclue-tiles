Kanpur vs Lucknow climate

How the climate of Kanpur, India compares with Lucknow, India, 71 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kanpur is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 25.3 °C against 25.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Kanpur is 0.5 °C warmer (Kanpur 33.1 °C, Lucknow 32.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kanpur 40.7 °C (May), Lucknow 40.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kanpur 7.2 °C, Lucknow 7.5 °C.
  • Lucknow gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 900 mm against 882 mm.
  • Kanpur gets about 52 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,663 h against 2,611 h; about 1961–1990).
