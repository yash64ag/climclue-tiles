Haiphong vs Hanoi climate

How the climate of Haiphong, Vietnam compares with Hanoi, Vietnam, 89 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Hanoi is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 24.1 °C against 24.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Hanoi is 0.6 °C warmer (Haiphong 24.1 °C, Hanoi 24.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Haiphong 31.5 °C (July), Hanoi 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Haiphong 14.2 °C, Hanoi 13.3 °C.
  • Hanoi gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,874 mm against 1,852 mm.
  • Haiphong gets about 78 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,723 h against 1,645 h; about 1961–1990).
