How the climate of Goyang-si, South Korea compares with Kaesŏng, North Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Goyang-si is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 12.0 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Goyang-si is 0.7 °C warmer (Goyang-si 6.3 °C, Kaesŏng 5.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Goyang-si 29.4 °C (August), Kaesŏng 29.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Goyang-si -7.7 °C, Kaesŏng -8.6 °C.
  • Goyang-si gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,429 mm against 1,376 mm.
  • Kaesŏng gets about 44 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,375 h against 2,331 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Goyang-si 27 cm, Kaesŏng 29 cm (ERA5-Land).
