Accra vs Lagos climate

How the climate of Accra, Ghana compares with Lagos, Nigeria, 410 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lagos is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 26.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Lagos is 1.0 °C warmer (Accra 23.8 °C, Lagos 24.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Accra 31.7 °C (March), Lagos 32.2 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Accra 21.0 °C, Lagos 21.7 °C.
  • Lagos gets 94% more rain and snow a year: 1,627 mm against 840 mm.
  • Accra gets about 390 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,268 h against 1,879 h; about 1961–1990).
