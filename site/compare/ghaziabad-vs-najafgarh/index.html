Main differences

  • Najafgarh is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 24.9 °C against 24.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Najafgarh is 0.5 °C warmer (Ghāziābād 25.1 °C, Najafgarh 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ghāziābād 39.6 °C (May), Najafgarh 39.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Ghāziābād 6.6 °C, Najafgarh 6.4 °C.
  • Ghāziābād gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 692 mm against 604 mm.
  • Najafgarh gets about 29 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,702 h against 2,674 h; about 1961–1990).
