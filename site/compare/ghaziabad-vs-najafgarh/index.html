Ghāziābād vs Najafgarh climate

How the climate of Ghāziābād, India compares with Najafgarh, India, 45 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Najafgarh is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 24.9 °C against 24.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Najafgarh is 0.6 °C warmer (Ghāziābād 25.1 °C, Najafgarh 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ghāziābād 39.2 °C (May), Najafgarh 39.6 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Ghāziābād 7.6 °C, Najafgarh 7.4 °C.
  • Ghāziābād gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 698 mm against 597 mm.
  • Najafgarh gets about 29 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,702 h against 2,674 h; about 1961–1990).
