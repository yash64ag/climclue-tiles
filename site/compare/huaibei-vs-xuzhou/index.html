How the climate of Huaibei, China compares with Xuzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Xuzhou is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.1 °C against 14.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Xuzhou is 0.3 °C warmer (Huaibei 21.9 °C, Xuzhou 22.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huaibei 31.5 °C (July), Xuzhou 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Huaibei -3.8 °C, Xuzhou -3.5 °C.
  • Huaibei gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 907 mm against 886 mm.
  • Xuzhou gets about 33 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,262 h against 2,229 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Huaibei 18 cm, Xuzhou 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
