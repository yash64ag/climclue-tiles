Huaibei vs Xuzhou climate

How the climate of Huaibei, China compares with Xuzhou, China, 52 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Huaibei is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 15.0 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Huaibei is 0.3 °C warmer (Huaibei 25.6 °C, Xuzhou 25.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huaibei 31.1 °C (July), Xuzhou 30.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Huaibei -3.7 °C, Xuzhou -4.0 °C.
  • Xuzhou gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 888 mm against 869 mm.
  • Xuzhou gets about 33 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,262 h against 2,229 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Huaibei 18 cm, Xuzhou 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
