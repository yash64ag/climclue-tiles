Main differences

  • Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.7 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl is 0.5 °C warmer (Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl 19.5 °C, Mexico City 19.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl 27.7 °C (May), Mexico City 27.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl 4.4 °C, Mexico City 4.3 °C.
  • Mexico City gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 728 mm against 710 mm.
  • Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl gets about 1 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,017 h against 2,016 h; about 1961–1990).
