How the climate of Arusha, Tanzania compares with Nairobi, Kenya: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Arusha is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 19.3 °C against 18.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Arusha is 2.0 °C warmer (Arusha 20.6 °C, Nairobi 18.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Arusha 29.0 °C (February), Nairobi 27.4 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Arusha 11.0 °C, Nairobi 11.5 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (854 mm and 850 mm).
  • Arusha gets about 174 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,639 h against 2,464 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Arusha in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: Arusha in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
