How the climate of Atyrau, Kazakhstan compares with Bayan Nur, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Atyrau is 1.8 °C (3.2 °F) warmer over the year: 10.2 °C against 8.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Atyrau is 4.5 °C warmer (Atyrau -5.6 °C, Bayan Nur -10.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Atyrau 33.5 °C (July), Bayan Nur 31.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Atyrau -10.2 °C, Bayan Nur -16.4 °C.
  • Atyrau gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 172 mm against 164 mm.
  • Bayan Nur gets about 461 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,006 h against 2,545 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Atyrau 41 cm, Bayan Nur 6.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
