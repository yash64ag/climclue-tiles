Fuyang vs Lu’an climate

How the climate of Fuyang, China compares with Lu’an, China, 145 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lu’an is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 16.0 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Lu’an is 0.7 °C warmer (Fuyang 4.2 °C, Lu’an 4.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuyang 31.3 °C (July), Lu’an 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuyang -2.7 °C, Lu’an -1.9 °C.
  • Lu’an gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 1,301 mm against 1,013 mm.
  • Fuyang gets about 97 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,134 h against 2,037 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fuyang 23 cm, Lu’an 29 cm (ERA5-Land).
