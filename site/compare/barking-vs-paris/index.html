How the climate of Barking, United Kingdom compares with Paris, France: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Paris is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 11.9 °C against 10.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Paris is 2.3 °C warmer (Barking 17.6 °C, Paris 19.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Barking 22.7 °C (July), Paris 24.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Barking 1.7 °C, Paris 2.0 °C.
  • Paris gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 658 mm against 612 mm.
  • Paris gets about 323 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,744 h against 1,421 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Barking 4.6 cm, Paris 9.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
