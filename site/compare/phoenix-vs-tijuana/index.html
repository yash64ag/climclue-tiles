How the climate of Phoenix, United States compares with Tijuana, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Phoenix is 5.9 °C (10.7 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 17.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Phoenix is 13.0 °C warmer (Phoenix 33.9 °C, Tijuana 20.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Phoenix 40.9 °C (July), Tijuana 26.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Phoenix 5.6 °C, Tijuana 7.4 °C.
  • Tijuana gets 36% more rain and snow a year: 296 mm against 217 mm.
  • Phoenix gets about 749 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,815 h against 3,067 h; about 1961–1990).
