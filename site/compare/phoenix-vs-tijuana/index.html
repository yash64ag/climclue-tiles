Phoenix vs Tijuana climate

How the climate of Phoenix, United States compares with Tijuana, Mexico, 472 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Phoenix is 6.8 °C (12.3 °F) warmer over the year: 23.3 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Phoenix is 15.7 °C warmer (Phoenix 33.0 °C, Tijuana 17.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Phoenix 41.5 °C (July), Tijuana 23.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Phoenix 5.0 °C, Tijuana 11.0 °C.
  • Tijuana gets 31% more rain and snow a year: 285 mm against 217 mm.
  • Phoenix gets about 749 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,815 h against 3,067 h; about 1961–1990).
