Phoenix vs Tijuana climate

How the climate of Phoenix, United States compares with Tijuana, Mexico, 472 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Phoenix is 6.9 °C (12.4 °F) warmer over the year: 23.3 °C against 16.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Phoenix is 15.6 °C warmer (Phoenix 33.0 °C, Tijuana 17.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Phoenix 42.6 °C (July), Tijuana 25.0 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Phoenix 4.2 °C, Tijuana 7.3 °C.
  • Tijuana gets 36% more rain and snow a year: 296 mm against 217 mm.
  • Phoenix gets about 749 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,815 h against 3,067 h; about 1961–1990).
