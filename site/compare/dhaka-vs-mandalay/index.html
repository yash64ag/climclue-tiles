How the climate of Dhaka, Bangladesh compares with Mandalay, Myanmar: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Mandalay is 1.8 °C (3.3 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Mandalay is 3.9 °C warmer (Dhaka 28.1 °C, Mandalay 31.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dhaka 33.3 °C (April), Mandalay 39.1 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Dhaka 11.2 °C, Mandalay 13.5 °C.
  • Dhaka gets 109% more rain and snow a year: 2,134 mm against 1,019 mm.
  • Mandalay gets about 277 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,377 h against 2,099 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Mandalay in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Mandalay in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Mandalay in 8 of 12 months
