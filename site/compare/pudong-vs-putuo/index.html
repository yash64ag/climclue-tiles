Pudong vs Putuo climate

How the climate of Pudong, China compares with Putuo, China, 11 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Pudong and Putuo have the same yearly average temperature (16.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Pudong is 0.5 °C warmer (Pudong 7.5 °C, Putuo 7.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pudong 30.5 °C (July), Putuo 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Pudong 1.5 °C, Putuo 0.4 °C.
  • Putuo gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,454 mm against 1,431 mm.
  • Pudong gets about 4 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,973 h against 1,969 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Pudong 6.4 cm, Putuo 8.9 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Drier: even, 6 months each
