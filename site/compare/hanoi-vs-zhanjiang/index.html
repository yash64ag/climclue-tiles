Hanoi vs Zhanjiang climate

How the climate of Hanoi, Vietnam compares with Zhanjiang, China, 472 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Hanoi is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 24.1 °C against 23.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Hanoi is 1.0 °C warmer (Hanoi 29.5 °C, Zhanjiang 28.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hanoi 33.1 °C (July), Zhanjiang 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hanoi 14.1 °C, Zhanjiang 13.3 °C.
  • Zhanjiang gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,982 mm against 1,891 mm.
  • Zhanjiang gets about 275 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,920 h against 1,645 h; about 1961–1990).
