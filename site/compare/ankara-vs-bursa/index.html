Ankara vs Bursa climate

How the climate of Ankara, Turkey compares with Bursa, Turkey, 324 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ankara is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 12.7 °C against 11.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Ankara is 3.7 °C warmer (Ankara 24.4 °C, Bursa 20.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ankara 30.2 °C (August), Bursa 25.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Ankara -3.6 °C, Bursa -2.9 °C.
  • Bursa gets 220% more rain and snow a year: 1,406 mm against 439 mm.
  • Ankara gets about 39 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,811 h against 2,772 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ankara 37 cm, Bursa 344 cm (ERA5-Land).
