Ankara vs Bursa climate

How the climate of Ankara, Turkey compares with Bursa, Turkey, 324 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bursa is 2.2 °C (3.9 °F) warmer over the year: 15.1 °C against 12.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Bursa is 4.3 °C warmer (Ankara 3.2 °C, Bursa 7.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ankara 32.3 °C (August), Bursa 31.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Ankara -2.4 °C, Bursa 1.8 °C.
  • Bursa gets 156% more rain and snow a year: 1,076 mm against 420 mm.
  • Ankara gets about 39 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,811 h against 2,772 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ankara 37 cm, Bursa 344 cm (ERA5-Land).
