Ecatepec de Morelos vs Mexico City climate

How the climate of Ecatepec de Morelos, Mexico compares with Mexico City, Mexico, 21 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ecatepec de Morelos is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 17.0 °C against 16.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Ecatepec de Morelos is 1.4 °C warmer (Ecatepec de Morelos 19.2 °C, Mexico City 17.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ecatepec de Morelos 25.7 °C (April), Mexico City 24.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Ecatepec de Morelos 6.4 °C, Mexico City 5.8 °C.
  • Mexico City gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 728 mm against 654 mm.
  • Ecatepec de Morelos gets about 132 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,148 h against 2,016 h; about 1961–1990).
