Lima vs Trujillo climate

How the climate of Lima, Peru compares with Trujillo, Peru, 489 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Trujillo is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 19.6 °C against 18.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Trujillo is 1.1 °C warmer (Lima 17.3 °C, Trujillo 18.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lima 24.8 °C (February), Trujillo 25.4 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Lima 14.8 °C, Trujillo 15.0 °C.
  • Trujillo gets 99% more rain and snow a year: 69 mm against 35 mm.
  • Trujillo gets about 427 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,933 h against 1,505 h; about 1961–1990).
