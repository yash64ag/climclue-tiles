Mosul vs Yerevan climate

How the climate of Mosul, Iraq compares with Yerevan, Armenia, 444 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mosul is 9.4 °C (17.0 °F) warmer over the year: 21.0 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Mosul is 10.6 °C warmer (Mosul 31.9 °C, Yerevan 21.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mosul 42.4 °C (July), Yerevan 31.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Mosul 2.6 °C, Yerevan -7.3 °C.
  • Yerevan gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 483 mm against 430 mm.
  • Mosul gets about 699 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,196 h against 2,497 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Mosul 1.0 cm, Yerevan 92 cm (ERA5-Land).
