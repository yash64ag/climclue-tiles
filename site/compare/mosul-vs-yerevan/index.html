How the climate of Mosul, Iraq compares with Yerevan, Armenia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Mosul is 8.5 °C (15.4 °F) warmer over the year: 21.0 °C against 12.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Mosul is 9.6 °C warmer (Mosul 31.9 °C, Yerevan 22.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mosul 44.3 °C (July), Yerevan 33.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Mosul 2.3 °C, Yerevan -6.4 °C.
  • Yerevan gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 446 mm against 435 mm.
  • Mosul gets about 699 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,196 h against 2,497 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Mosul 1.0 cm, Yerevan 92 cm (ERA5-Land).
