Jinjiang vs Longyan climate

How the climate of Jinjiang, China compares with Longyan, China, 159 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jinjiang is 1.7 °C (3.1 °F) warmer over the year: 21.4 °C against 19.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Jinjiang is 2.8 °C warmer (Jinjiang 15.1 °C, Longyan 12.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinjiang 31.0 °C (July), Longyan 30.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinjiang 9.2 °C, Longyan 5.8 °C.
  • Longyan gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 1,682 mm against 1,252 mm.
  • Jinjiang gets about 121 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,967 h against 1,846 h; about 1961–1990).
