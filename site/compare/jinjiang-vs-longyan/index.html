Jinjiang, Fujian vs Longyan climate

How the climate of Jinjiang, Fujian, China compares with Longyan, China, 159 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jinjiang, Fujian is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 21.4 °C against 20.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Jinjiang, Fujian is 2.2 °C warmer (Jinjiang, Fujian 15.2 °C, Longyan 13.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinjiang, Fujian 32.4 °C (July), Longyan 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinjiang, Fujian 9.7 °C, Longyan 7.5 °C.
  • Longyan gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 1,570 mm against 1,228 mm.
  • Jinjiang, Fujian gets about 121 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,967 h against 1,846 h; about 1961–1990).
