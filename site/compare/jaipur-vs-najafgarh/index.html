How the climate of Jaipur, India compares with Najafgarh, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jaipur and Najafgarh have the same yearly average temperature (24.9 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Najafgarh is 1.8 °C warmer (Jaipur 28.0 °C, Najafgarh 29.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jaipur 40.5 °C (May), Najafgarh 39.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Jaipur 6.9 °C, Najafgarh 6.4 °C.
  • Jaipur gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 634 mm against 604 mm.
  • Jaipur gets about 129 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,831 h against 2,702 h; about 1961–1990).
