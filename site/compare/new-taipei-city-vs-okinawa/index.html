How the climate of New Taipei City, Taiwan compares with Okinawa, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Okinawa is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 22.7 °C against 22.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when New Taipei City is 1.0 °C warmer (New Taipei City 24.9 °C, Okinawa 23.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: New Taipei City 33.2 °C (July), Okinawa 31.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: New Taipei City 12.6 °C, Okinawa 13.5 °C.
  • New Taipei City gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 3,215 mm against 2,597 mm.
  • Okinawa gets about 97 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,821 h against 1,724 h; about 1961–1990).
