New Taipei City vs Okinawa climate

How the climate of New Taipei City, Taiwan compares with Okinawa, Japan, 651 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Okinawa is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 22.8 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Okinawa is 2.0 °C warmer (New Taipei City 15.4 °C, Okinawa 17.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: New Taipei City 31.1 °C (July), Okinawa 28.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: New Taipei City 12.3 °C, Okinawa 16.5 °C.
  • New Taipei City gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 3,293 mm against 2,484 mm.
  • Okinawa gets about 97 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,821 h against 1,724 h; about 1961–1990).
