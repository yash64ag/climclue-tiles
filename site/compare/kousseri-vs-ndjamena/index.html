How the climate of Kousséri, Cameroon compares with N'Djamena, Chad: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kousséri and N'Djamena have the same yearly average temperature (28.8 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Kousséri is 0.1 °C warmer (Kousséri 24.6 °C, N'Djamena 24.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kousséri 41.4 °C (April), N'Djamena 41.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Kousséri 15.1 °C, N'Djamena 15.1 °C.
  • Kousséri gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 493 mm against 487 mm.
  • N'Djamena gets about 5 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,166 h against 3,162 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 4 months each
  • Drier: N'Djamena in 7 of 12 months (5 equal)
  • Sunnier: N'Djamena in 10 of 12 months (1 equal)
