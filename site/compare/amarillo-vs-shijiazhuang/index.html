Amarillo vs Shijiazhuang climate

How the climate of Amarillo, United States compares with Shijiazhuang, China, 11,051 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Amarillo is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 14.3 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Amarillo is 4.8 °C warmer (Amarillo 2.9 °C, Shijiazhuang -1.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amarillo 32.5 °C (July), Shijiazhuang 32.1 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Amarillo -1.4 °C, Shijiazhuang -6.9 °C.
  • Amarillo gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 542 mm against 504 mm.
  • Amarillo gets about 677 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 2,566 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amarillo 23 cm, Shijiazhuang 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
