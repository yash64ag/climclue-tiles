Main differences

  • Amarillo is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 14.2 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Amarillo is 4.6 °C warmer (Amarillo 2.7 °C, Shijiazhuang -2.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amarillo 33.0 °C (July), Shijiazhuang 33.0 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Amarillo -5.1 °C, Shijiazhuang -7.7 °C.
  • Amarillo gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 550 mm against 502 mm.
  • Amarillo gets about 677 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 2,566 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amarillo 23 cm, Shijiazhuang 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
