How the climate of Changzhou, China compares with Norman, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Changzhou is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.3 °C against 16.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Changzhou is 1.5 °C warmer (Changzhou 11.7 °C, Norman 10.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changzhou 32.0 °C (July), Norman 34.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changzhou -0.6 °C, Norman -2.4 °C.
  • Changzhou gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 1,236 mm against 979 mm.
  • Norman gets about 986 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,003 h against 2,017 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changzhou 17 cm, Norman 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
