How the climate of Matola, Mozambique compares with Teófilo Otoni, Brazil: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Teófilo Otoni is 0.9 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 23.8 °C against 22.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Teófilo Otoni is 1.9 °C warmer (Matola 19.4 °C, Teófilo Otoni 21.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Matola 30.5 °C (January), Teófilo Otoni 32.5 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Matola 12.7 °C, Teófilo Otoni 14.7 °C.
  • Teófilo Otoni gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 969 mm against 759 mm.
  • Matola gets about 864 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,808 h against 1,944 h; about 1961–1990).
