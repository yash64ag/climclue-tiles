Matola vs Teófilo Otoni climate

How the climate of Matola, Mozambique compares with Teófilo Otoni, Brazil, 7,588 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Teófilo Otoni is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 23.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Teófilo Otoni is 1.1 °C warmer (Matola 19.4 °C, Teófilo Otoni 20.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Matola 29.3 °C (January), Teófilo Otoni 29.8 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Matola 14.9 °C, Teófilo Otoni 15.7 °C.
  • Teófilo Otoni gets 46% more rain and snow a year: 1,115 mm against 764 mm.
  • Matola gets about 864 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,808 h against 1,944 h; about 1961–1990).
