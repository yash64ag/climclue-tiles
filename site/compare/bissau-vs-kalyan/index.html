Bissau vs Kalyān climate

How the climate of Bissau, Guinea-Bissau compares with Kalyān, India, 9,445 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bissau is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 27.1 °C against 26.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Bissau is 2.2 °C warmer (Bissau 26.6 °C, Kalyān 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bissau 34.1 °C (March), Kalyān 35.8 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Bissau 20.4 °C, Kalyān 16.7 °C.
  • Kalyān gets 43% more rain and snow a year: 2,265 mm against 1,583 mm.
  • Kalyān gets about 31 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,706 h against 2,675 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Bissau in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Kalyān in 8 of 12 months
