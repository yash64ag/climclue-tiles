Gujranwala vs Ludhiana climate

How the climate of Gujranwala, Pakistan compares with Ludhiana, India, 210 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gujranwala is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 23.6 °C against 23.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Gujranwala is 1.0 °C warmer (Gujranwala 33.4 °C, Ludhiana 32.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gujranwala 39.2 °C (June), Ludhiana 38.4 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Gujranwala 5.9 °C, Ludhiana 6.4 °C.
  • Gujranwala gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 923 mm against 815 mm.
  • Gujranwala gets about 201 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,969 h against 2,768 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Gujranwala in 6 of 12 months (2 equal)
  • Drier: Ludhiana in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Gujranwala in 12 of 12 months
