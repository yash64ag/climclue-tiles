How the climate of Faisalabad, Pakistan compares with Multan, Pakistan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Multan is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 24.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Multan is 1.7 °C warmer (Faisalabad 32.6 °C, Multan 34.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Faisalabad 40.9 °C (June), Multan 42.4 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Faisalabad 4.8 °C, Multan 5.3 °C.
  • Faisalabad gets 84% more rain and snow a year: 424 mm against 230 mm.
  • Multan gets about 104 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,083 h against 2,978 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Multan in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Multan in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Multan in 10 of 12 months
