Faisalabad vs Multan climate

How the climate of Faisalabad, Pakistan compares with Multan, Pakistan, 205 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Multan is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 25.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Multan is 1.7 °C warmer (Faisalabad 32.8 °C, Multan 34.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Faisalabad 40.0 °C (June), Multan 41.3 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Faisalabad 6.4 °C, Multan 7.4 °C.
  • Faisalabad gets 87% more rain and snow a year: 421 mm against 225 mm.
  • Multan gets about 104 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,083 h against 2,978 h; about 1961–1990).
