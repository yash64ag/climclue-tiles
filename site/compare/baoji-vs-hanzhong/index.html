How the climate of Baoji, China compares with Hanzhong, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hanzhong is 1.1 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 15.5 °C against 14.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Hanzhong is 2.7 °C warmer (Baoji 2.6 °C, Hanzhong 5.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoji 31.3 °C (July), Hanzhong 30.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baoji -3.0 °C, Hanzhong 0.1 °C.
  • Hanzhong gets 50% more rain and snow a year: 981 mm against 653 mm.
  • Baoji gets about 270 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,939 h against 1,669 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoji 6.0 cm, Hanzhong 4.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
