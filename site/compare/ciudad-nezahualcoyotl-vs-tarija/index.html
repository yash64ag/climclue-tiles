Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl vs Tarija climate

How the climate of Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl, Mexico compares with Tarija, Bolivia, 5,884 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.4 °C against 16.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl is 5.4 °C warmer (Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl 18.6 °C, Tarija 13.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl 27.5 °C (April), Tarija 25.7 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl 4.3 °C, Tarija 2.3 °C.
  • Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 710 mm against 529 mm.
  • Tarija gets about 884 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,901 h against 2,017 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
