How the climate of Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl, Mexico compares with Tarija, Bolivia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl and Tarija have the same yearly average temperature (16.3 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Tarija is 5.7 °C warmer (Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl 12.8 °C, Tarija 18.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl 27.7 °C (May), Tarija 25.7 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl 3.5 °C, Tarija 2.3 °C.
  • Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 710 mm against 529 mm.
  • Tarija gets about 884 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,901 h against 2,017 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Tarija in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Tarija in 12 of 12 months
