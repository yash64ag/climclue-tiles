Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl vs Tarija climate

How the climate of Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl, Mexico compares with Tarija, Bolivia, 5,884 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 16.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl is 5.9 °C warmer (Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl 18.9 °C, Tarija 12.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl 24.9 °C (April), Tarija 22.7 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl 6.3 °C, Tarija 5.9 °C.
  • Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 690 mm against 570 mm.
  • Tarija gets about 884 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,901 h against 2,017 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

  • Sunnier: Tarija in 12 of 12 months
