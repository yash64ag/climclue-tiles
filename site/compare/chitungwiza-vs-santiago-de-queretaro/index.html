How the climate of Chitungwiza, Zimbabwe compares with Santiago de Querétaro, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Chitungwiza is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 19.0 °C against 18.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Santiago de Querétaro is 7.0 °C warmer (Chitungwiza 14.3 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 21.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chitungwiza 28.7 °C (October), Santiago de Querétaro 30.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Chitungwiza 6.0 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 6.0 °C.
  • Chitungwiza gets 68% more rain and snow a year: 766 mm against 456 mm.
  • Chitungwiza gets about 428 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,975 h against 2,547 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
