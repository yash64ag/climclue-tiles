Ma On Shan vs Shantou climate

How the climate of Ma On Shan, Hong Kong compares with Shantou, China, 271 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ma On Shan and Shantou have the same yearly average temperature (22.2 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Ma On Shan is 1.0 °C warmer (Ma On Shan 18.6 °C, Shantou 17.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ma On Shan 30.4 °C (July), Shantou 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ma On Shan 11.9 °C, Shantou 11.1 °C.
  • Ma On Shan gets 53% more rain and snow a year: 2,395 mm against 1,567 mm.
  • Shantou gets about 44 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,008 h against 1,964 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Shantou in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Shantou in 7 of 12 months
