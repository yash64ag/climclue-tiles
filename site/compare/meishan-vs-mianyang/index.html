Main differences

  • Meishan is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 17.8 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Meishan is 1.1 °C warmer (Meishan 8.8 °C, Mianyang, Sichuan 7.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Meishan 30.4 °C (July), Mianyang, Sichuan 30.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Meishan 4.5 °C, Mianyang, Sichuan 2.6 °C.
  • Meishan gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 1,147 mm against 919 mm.
  • Meishan gets about 16 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,410 h against 1,394 h; about 1961–1990).
