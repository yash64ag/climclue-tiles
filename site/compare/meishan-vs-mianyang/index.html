Meishan vs Mianyang climate

How the climate of Meishan, China compares with Mianyang, China, 178 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Meishan is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 17.8 °C against 17.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Meishan is 1.0 °C warmer (Meishan 7.5 °C, Mianyang 6.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Meishan 29.9 °C (July), Mianyang 29.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Meishan 3.3 °C, Mianyang 2.0 °C.
  • Meishan gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,156 mm against 956 mm.
  • Meishan gets about 16 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,410 h against 1,394 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Meishan 1.8 cm, Mianyang 2.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
