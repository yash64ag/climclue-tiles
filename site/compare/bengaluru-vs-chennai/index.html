Bengaluru vs Chennai climate

How the climate of Bengaluru, India compares with Chennai, India, 291 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Chennai is 4.5 °C (8.2 °F) warmer over the year: 28.0 °C against 23.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Chennai is 6.8 °C warmer (Bengaluru 23.9 °C, Chennai 30.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bengaluru 32.8 °C (April), Chennai 34.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Bengaluru 14.8 °C, Chennai 21.7 °C.
  • Chennai gets 52% more rain and snow a year: 1,359 mm against 895 mm.
  • Chennai gets about 300 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,675 h against 2,374 h; about 1961–1990).
