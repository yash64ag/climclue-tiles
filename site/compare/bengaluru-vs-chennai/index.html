How the climate of Bengaluru, India compares with Chennai, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Chennai is 4.7 °C (8.5 °F) warmer over the year: 27.9 °C against 23.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Chennai is 6.8 °C warmer (Bengaluru 23.8 °C, Chennai 30.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bengaluru 33.2 °C (April), Chennai 36.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Bengaluru 14.6 °C, Chennai 19.8 °C.
  • Chennai gets 50% more rain and snow a year: 1,373 mm against 914 mm.
  • Chennai gets about 300 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,675 h against 2,374 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Chennai in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Chennai in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Chennai in 10 of 12 months
