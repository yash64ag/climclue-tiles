Iizuka vs Putuo climate

How the climate of Iizuka, Japan compares with Putuo, China, 911 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Iizuka is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 17.1 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Iizuka is 1.7 °C warmer (Iizuka 6.2 °C, Putuo 4.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iizuka 32.6 °C (August), Putuo 31.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Iizuka 2.1 °C, Putuo 1.0 °C.
  • Iizuka gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,669 mm against 1,466 mm.
  • Putuo gets about 210 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,969 h against 1,759 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Iizuka 14 cm, Putuo 8.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
