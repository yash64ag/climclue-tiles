Main differences

  • Chula Vista is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 16.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Chula Vista is 0.6 °C warmer (Chula Vista 14.4 °C, Tijuana 13.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chula Vista 25.3 °C (September), Tijuana 25.0 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Chula Vista 7.6 °C, Tijuana 7.3 °C.
  • Tijuana gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 296 mm against 268 mm.
  • Chula Vista gets about 1 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,068 h against 3,067 h; about 1961–1990).
