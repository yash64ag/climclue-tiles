Chula Vista vs Tijuana climate

How the climate of Chula Vista, United States compares with Tijuana, Mexico, 17 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Chula Vista is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Chula Vista is 0.6 °C warmer (Chula Vista 21.1 °C, Tijuana 20.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chula Vista 25.0 °C (August), Tijuana 23.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Chula Vista 9.9 °C, Tijuana 11.0 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (286 mm and 285 mm).
  • Chula Vista gets about 1 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,068 h against 3,067 h; about 1961–1990).
