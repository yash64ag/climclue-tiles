How the climate of Chula Vista, United States compares with Tijuana, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Chula Vista is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 17.6 °C against 17.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Chula Vista is 0.7 °C warmer (Chula Vista 15.1 °C, Tijuana 14.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chula Vista 26.1 °C (August), Tijuana 26.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Chula Vista 7.8 °C, Tijuana 7.4 °C.
  • Tijuana gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 296 mm against 268 mm.
  • Chula Vista gets about 1 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,068 h against 3,067 h; about 1961–1990).
