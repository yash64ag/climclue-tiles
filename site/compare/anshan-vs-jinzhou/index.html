Main differences

  • Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 10.1 °C against 9.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) is 2.5 °C warmer (Anshan -9.8 °C, Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) -7.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Anshan 28.8 °C (July), Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) 27.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Anshan -15.7 °C, Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) -12.0 °C.
  • Anshan gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 754 mm against 582 mm.
  • Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) gets about 100 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,677 h against 2,577 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Anshan 39 cm, Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
