How the climate of Milan, Italy compares with Munich, Germany: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Milan is 4.6 °C (8.2 °F) warmer over the year: 13.6 °C against 9.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Milan is 6.2 °C warmer (Milan 24.6 °C, Munich 18.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Milan 30.4 °C (July), Munich 23.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Milan -0.6 °C, Munich -3.5 °C.
  • Munich gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,006 mm against 981 mm.
  • Milan gets about 278 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,933 h against 1,655 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Milan 28 cm, Munich 97 cm (ERA5-Land).
