Kakamega vs Kampala climate

How the climate of Kakamega, Kenya compares with Kampala, Uganda, 241 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kampala is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 21.5 °C against 20.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Kampala is 1.8 °C warmer (Kakamega 19.4 °C, Kampala 21.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kakamega 29.6 °C (February), Kampala 28.8 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Kakamega 12.3 °C, Kampala 15.1 °C.
  • Kakamega gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,674 mm against 1,388 mm.
  • Kakamega gets about 302 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,755 h against 2,454 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Kampala in 9 of 12 months
