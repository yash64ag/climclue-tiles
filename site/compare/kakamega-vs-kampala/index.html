Kakamega vs Kampala climate

How the climate of Kakamega, Kenya compares with Kampala, Uganda, 241 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kampala is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 21.3 °C against 20.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Kampala is 1.3 °C warmer (Kakamega 19.6 °C, Kampala 20.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kakamega 27.8 °C (February), Kampala 25.4 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Kakamega 14.7 °C, Kampala 17.2 °C.
  • Kakamega gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 1,729 mm against 1,395 mm.
  • Kakamega gets about 302 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,755 h against 2,454 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Kampala in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Kakamega in 12 of 12 months
