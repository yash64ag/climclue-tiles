Accra vs Lomé climate

How the climate of Accra, Ghana compares with Lomé, Togo, 169 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lomé is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 26.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Lomé is 0.6 °C warmer (Accra 24.5 °C, Lomé 25.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Accra 29.4 °C (March), Lomé 29.9 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Accra 22.9 °C, Lomé 23.2 °C.
  • Lomé gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 853 mm against 840 mm.
  • Lomé gets about 62 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,330 h against 2,268 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Lomé in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Lomé in 9 of 12 months
