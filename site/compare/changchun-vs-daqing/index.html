Changchun vs Daqing climate

How the climate of Changchun, China compares with Daqing, China, 302 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Changchun is 1.8 °C (3.2 °F) warmer over the year: 6.3 °C against 4.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Changchun is 3.8 °C warmer (Changchun -11.8 °C, Daqing -15.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changchun 27.1 °C (July), Daqing 27.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changchun -20.9 °C, Daqing -23.9 °C.
  • Changchun gets 35% more rain and snow a year: 616 mm against 456 mm.
  • Daqing gets about 84 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,653 h against 2,570 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changchun 60 cm, Daqing 43 cm (ERA5-Land).
