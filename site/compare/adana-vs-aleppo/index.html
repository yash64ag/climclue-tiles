Adana vs Aleppo climate

How the climate of Adana, Turkey compares with Aleppo, Syria, 186 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Adana is 1.1 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 18.9 °C against 17.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Adana is 2.4 °C warmer (Adana 10.3 °C, Aleppo 7.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Adana 33.8 °C (August), Aleppo 35.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Adana 4.3 °C, Aleppo 2.1 °C.
  • Adana gets 79% more rain and snow a year: 714 mm against 400 mm.
  • Aleppo gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,999 h against 2,992 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Adana 1.7 cm, Aleppo 5.2 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Adana in 10 of 12 months
  • Drier: Aleppo in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
