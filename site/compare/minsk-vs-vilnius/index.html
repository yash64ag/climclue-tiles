How the climate of Minsk, Belarus compares with Vilnius, Lithuania: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Vilnius is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 6.9 °C against 6.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Vilnius is 1.0 °C warmer (Minsk -3.6 °C, Vilnius -2.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Minsk 23.4 °C (July), Vilnius 23.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Minsk -7.3 °C, Vilnius -6.7 °C.
  • Vilnius gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 691 mm against 686 mm.
  • Minsk gets about 132 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,741 h against 1,608 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Minsk 142 cm, Vilnius 131 cm (ERA5-Land).
