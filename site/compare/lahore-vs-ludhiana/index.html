Lahore vs Ludhiana climate

How the climate of Lahore, Pakistan compares with Ludhiana, India, 160 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lahore is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 23.9 °C against 23.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Lahore is 1.2 °C warmer (Lahore 31.3 °C, Ludhiana 30.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lahore 39.1 °C (June), Ludhiana 38.3 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Lahore 6.0 °C, Ludhiana 6.3 °C.
  • Ludhiana gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 821 mm against 684 mm.
  • Lahore gets about 169 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,937 h against 2,768 h; about 1961–1990).
