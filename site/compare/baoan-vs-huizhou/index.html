Bao'an vs Huizhou climate

How the climate of Bao'an, China compares with Huizhou, China, 83 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bao'an is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 22.9 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Bao'an is 1.4 °C warmer (Bao'an 16.9 °C, Huizhou 15.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bao'an 31.7 °C (July), Huizhou 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bao'an 11.5 °C, Huizhou 9.9 °C.
  • Bao'an gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 2,140 mm against 1,962 mm.
  • Bao'an gets about 29 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,927 h against 1,898 h; about 1961–1990).
