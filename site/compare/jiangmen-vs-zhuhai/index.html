Jiangmen vs Zhuhai, Guangdong climate

How the climate of Jiangmen, China compares with Zhuhai, Guangdong, China, 60 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jiangmen and Zhuhai, Guangdong have the same yearly average temperature (23.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Zhuhai, Guangdong is 0.9 °C warmer (Jiangmen 14.8 °C, Zhuhai, Guangdong 15.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiangmen 32.7 °C (July), Zhuhai, Guangdong 31.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jiangmen 11.2 °C, Zhuhai, Guangdong 12.5 °C.
  • Jiangmen gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 2,139 mm against 2,075 mm.
  • Zhuhai, Guangdong gets about 71 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,945 h against 1,875 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Zhuhai, Guangdong in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Zhuhai, Guangdong in 11 of 12 months
