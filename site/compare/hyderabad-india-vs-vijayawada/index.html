Hyderabad vs Vijayawada climate

How the climate of Hyderabad, India compares with Vijayawada, India, 253 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Vijayawada is 1.9 °C (3.4 °F) warmer over the year: 28.4 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Vijayawada is 3.1 °C warmer (Hyderabad 25.8 °C, Vijayawada 28.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hyderabad 37.7 °C (May), Vijayawada 38.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Hyderabad 15.8 °C, Vijayawada 18.9 °C.
  • Vijayawada gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 1,040 mm against 837 mm.
  • Hyderabad gets about 23 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,691 h against 2,669 h; about 1961–1990).
