Hyderabad vs Vijayawada climate

How the climate of Hyderabad, India compares with Vijayawada, India, 253 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Vijayawada is 1.9 °C (3.4 °F) warmer over the year: 28.4 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Vijayawada is 3.1 °C warmer (Hyderabad 25.8 °C, Vijayawada 28.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hyderabad 38.8 °C (May), Vijayawada 38.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Hyderabad 15.1 °C, Vijayawada 19.0 °C.
  • Vijayawada gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,041 mm against 859 mm.
  • Hyderabad gets about 23 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,691 h against 2,669 h; about 1961–1990).
