Bissau vs Thāne climate

Main differences

  • Bissau is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 27.1 °C against 26.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Bissau is 2.4 °C warmer (Bissau 26.6 °C, Thāne 24.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bissau 33.9 °C (March), Thāne 33.0 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Bissau 20.4 °C, Thāne 18.5 °C.
  • Thāne gets 62% more rain and snow a year: 2,597 mm against 1,606 mm.
  • Thāne gets about 0 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,675 h against 2,675 h; about 1961–1990).
