Bissau vs Thāne climate

How the climate of Bissau, Guinea-Bissau compares with Thāne, India, 9,428 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Bissau is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 27.1 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Bissau is 2.5 °C warmer (Bissau 26.6 °C, Thāne 24.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bissau 34.1 °C (March), Thāne 32.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Bissau 20.4 °C, Thāne 18.8 °C.
  • Thāne gets 66% more rain and snow a year: 2,629 mm against 1,583 mm.
  • Thāne gets about 0 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,675 h against 2,675 h; about 1961–1990).
