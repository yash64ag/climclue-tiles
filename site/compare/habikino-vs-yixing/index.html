How the climate of Habikino, Japan compares with Yixing, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yixing is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.7 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Yixing is 1.9 °C warmer (Habikino 19.5 °C, Yixing 21.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Habikino 33.0 °C (August), Yixing 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Habikino 1.5 °C, Yixing -0.1 °C.
  • Habikino gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,371 mm against 1,213 mm.
  • Yixing gets about 118 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,982 h against 1,864 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Habikino 6.6 cm, Yixing 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
