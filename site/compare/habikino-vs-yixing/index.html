Habikino vs Yixing climate

How the climate of Habikino, Japan compares with Yixing, China, 1,512 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Yixing is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Yixing is 1.9 °C warmer (Habikino 19.6 °C, Yixing 21.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Habikino 32.0 °C (August), Yixing 31.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Habikino 0.3 °C, Yixing 0.7 °C.
  • Habikino gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,390 mm against 1,234 mm.
  • Yixing gets about 118 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,982 h against 1,864 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Habikino 17 cm, Yixing 21 cm (ERA5-Land).
