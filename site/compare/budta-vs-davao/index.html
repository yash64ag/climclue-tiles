Budta vs Davao climate

How the climate of Budta, Philippines compares with Davao, Philippines, 130 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Budta is 0.9 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 28.2 °C against 27.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Budta is 2.0 °C warmer (Budta 29.1 °C, Davao 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Budta 33.3 °C (April), Davao 30.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Budta 23.5 °C, Davao 24.2 °C.
  • Davao gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,741 mm against 1,503 mm.
  • Budta gets about 3 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,291 h against 2,288 h; about 1961–1990).
