How the climate of Havana, Cuba compares with Port-au-Prince, Haiti: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Port-au-Prince is 1.6 °C (2.9 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 25.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Port-au-Prince is 3.3 °C warmer (Havana 22.1 °C, Port-au-Prince 25.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Havana 32.4 °C (July), Port-au-Prince 34.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Havana 17.8 °C, Port-au-Prince 20.0 °C.
  • Havana gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,368 mm against 1,242 mm.
  • Havana gets about 154 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,950 h against 2,795 h; about 1961–1990).
