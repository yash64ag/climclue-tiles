Havana vs Port-au-Prince climate

How the climate of Havana, Cuba compares with Port-au-Prince, Haiti, 1,161 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Port-au-Prince is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 25.9 °C against 25.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Port-au-Prince is 2.2 °C warmer (Havana 22.1 °C, Port-au-Prince 24.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Havana 31.7 °C (August), Port-au-Prince 31.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Havana 19.8 °C, Port-au-Prince 19.9 °C.
  • Port-au-Prince gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,402 mm against 1,383 mm.
  • Havana gets about 154 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,950 h against 2,795 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Port-au-Prince in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Havana in 7 of 12 months
