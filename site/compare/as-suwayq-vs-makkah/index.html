As Suwayq vs Makkah climate

How the climate of As Suwayq, Oman compares with Makkah, Saudi Arabia, 1,826 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Makkah is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 29.2 °C against 28.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Makkah is 1.4 °C warmer (As Suwayq 31.8 °C, Makkah 33.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: As Suwayq 40.2 °C (June), Makkah 41.2 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: As Suwayq 15.7 °C, Makkah 16.6 °C.
  • Makkah gets 71% more rain and snow a year: 129 mm against 75 mm.
  • As Suwayq gets about 168 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,435 h against 3,268 h; about 1961–1990).
