How the climate of Rui’an, China compares with Taizhou, Zhejiang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Rui’an is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 19.3 °C against 18.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Rui’an is 1.9 °C warmer (Rui’an 11.6 °C, Taizhou, Zhejiang 9.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rui’an 32.4 °C (July), Taizhou, Zhejiang 31.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Rui’an 5.8 °C, Taizhou, Zhejiang 4.5 °C.
  • Taizhou, Zhejiang gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,707 mm against 1,640 mm.
  • Taizhou, Zhejiang gets about 16 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,811 h against 1,795 h; about 1961–1990).
