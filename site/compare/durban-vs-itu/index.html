How the climate of Durban, South Africa compares with Itu, Brazil: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Durban is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 21.2 °C against 21.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Itu is 1.2 °C warmer (Durban 20.4 °C, Itu 21.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Durban 28.4 °C (February), Itu 28.9 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Durban 12.0 °C, Itu 11.3 °C.
  • Itu gets 39% more rain and snow a year: 1,268 mm against 914 mm.
  • Durban gets about 261 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,513 h against 2,252 h; about 1961–1990).
