Iizuka vs Qingpu climate

How the climate of Iizuka, Japan compares with Qingpu, China, 940 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Iizuka is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 17.1 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Iizuka is 1.9 °C warmer (Iizuka 6.2 °C, Qingpu 4.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iizuka 32.6 °C (August), Qingpu 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Iizuka 2.1 °C, Qingpu 0.7 °C.
  • Iizuka gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 1,669 mm against 1,403 mm.
  • Qingpu gets about 199 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,958 h against 1,759 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Iizuka 14 cm, Qingpu 10 cm (ERA5-Land).
