Kawasaki vs Sendai climate

How the climate of Kawasaki, Japan compares with Sendai, Japan, 322 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kawasaki is 3.5 °C (6.3 °F) warmer over the year: 15.8 °C against 12.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Kawasaki is 4.3 °C warmer (Kawasaki 6.2 °C, Sendai 1.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kawasaki 28.6 °C (August), Sendai 26.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kawasaki 2.0 °C, Sendai -3.0 °C.
  • Sendai gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,518 mm against 1,508 mm.
  • Kawasaki gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,826 h against 1,819 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kawasaki 12 cm, Sendai 75 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Kawasaki in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Sendai in 7 of 12 months
