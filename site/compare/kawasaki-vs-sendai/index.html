Kawasaki, Kanagawa vs Sendai, Miyagi climate

How the climate of Kawasaki, Kanagawa, Japan compares with Sendai, Miyagi, Japan, 322 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kawasaki, Kanagawa is 3.4 °C (6.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 12.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Kawasaki, Kanagawa is 4.3 °C warmer (Kawasaki, Kanagawa 6.3 °C, Sendai, Miyagi 2.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kawasaki, Kanagawa 29.9 °C (August), Sendai, Miyagi 27.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kawasaki, Kanagawa 1.6 °C, Sendai, Miyagi -1.9 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,489 mm and 1,487 mm).
  • Kawasaki, Kanagawa gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,826 h against 1,819 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kawasaki, Kanagawa 12 cm, Sendai, Miyagi 75 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Sendai, Miyagi in 7 of 12 months
