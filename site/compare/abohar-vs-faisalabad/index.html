Abohar vs Faisalabad climate

How the climate of Abohar, India compares with Faisalabad, Pakistan, 176 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Abohar is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 25.2 °C against 25.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Abohar is 0.7 °C warmer (Abohar 21.9 °C, Faisalabad 21.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abohar 39.7 °C (June), Faisalabad 40.0 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Abohar 7.0 °C, Faisalabad 6.4 °C.
  • Faisalabad gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 421 mm against 386 mm.
  • Faisalabad gets about 71 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,978 h against 2,908 h; about 1961–1990).
