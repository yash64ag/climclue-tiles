Dammam vs Riyadh climate

How the climate of Dammam, Saudi Arabia compares with Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, 391 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Riyadh is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.2 °C against 26.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Dammam is 2.9 °C warmer (Dammam 18.4 °C, Riyadh 15.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dammam 38.4 °C (July), Riyadh 42.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Dammam 12.8 °C, Riyadh 6.6 °C.
  • Riyadh gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 103 mm against 90 mm.
  • Riyadh gets about 62 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,360 h against 3,298 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
