How the climate of Dammam, Saudi Arabia compares with Riyadh, Saudi Arabia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Riyadh is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 26.5 °C against 26.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Riyadh is 2.3 °C warmer (Dammam 30.1 °C, Riyadh 32.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dammam 41.5 °C (July), Riyadh 43.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Dammam 11.7 °C, Riyadh 8.5 °C.
  • Riyadh gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 103 mm against 90 mm.
  • Riyadh gets about 62 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,360 h against 3,298 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Riyadh in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Dammam in 5 of 12 months (3 equal)
  • Sunnier: Riyadh in 9 of 12 months
