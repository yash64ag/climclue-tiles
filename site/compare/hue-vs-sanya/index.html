Huế vs Sanya climate

How the climate of Huế, Vietnam compares with Sanya, China, 285 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Huế is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 25.8 °C against 24.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Huế is 1.6 °C warmer (Huế 29.4 °C, Sanya 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huế 32.5 °C (July), Sanya 29.5 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Huế 18.7 °C, Sanya 18.2 °C.
  • Huế gets 91% more rain and snow a year: 3,151 mm against 1,651 mm.
  • Huế gets about 121 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,260 h against 2,138 h; about 1961–1990).
