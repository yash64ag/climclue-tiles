How the climate of Hanzhong, China compares with Lubbock, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lubbock is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 15.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Lubbock is 1.2 °C warmer (Hanzhong 3.6 °C, Lubbock 4.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hanzhong 30.1 °C (July), Lubbock 33.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hanzhong -0.2 °C, Lubbock -2.8 °C.
  • Hanzhong gets 90% more rain and snow a year: 981 mm against 516 mm.
  • Lubbock gets about 1,485 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,154 h against 1,669 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hanzhong 4.4 cm, Lubbock 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
