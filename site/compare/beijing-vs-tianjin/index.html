Beijing vs Tianjin climate

How the climate of Beijing, China compares with Tianjin, China, 108 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tianjin is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 13.3 °C against 13.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Tianjin is 1.5 °C warmer (Beijing 4.0 °C, Tianjin 5.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Beijing 31.9 °C (June), Tianjin 31.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Beijing -9.3 °C, Tianjin -7.9 °C.
  • Beijing gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 568 mm against 539 mm.
  • Beijing gets about 75 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,704 h against 2,630 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Beijing 22 cm, Tianjin 18 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Tianjin in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: Tianjin in 7 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Sunnier: Beijing in 12 of 12 months
