How the climate of Beijing, China compares with Tianjin, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tianjin is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 13.4 °C against 13.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Tianjin is 0.8 °C warmer (Beijing 4.9 °C, Tianjin 5.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Beijing 31.7 °C (July), Tianjin 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Beijing -8.0 °C, Tianjin -7.6 °C.
  • Beijing gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 563 mm against 543 mm.
  • Beijing gets about 75 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,704 h against 2,630 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Beijing 12 cm, Tianjin 9.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
