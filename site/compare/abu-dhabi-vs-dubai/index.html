Abu Dhabi vs Dubai climate

How the climate of Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates compares with Dubai, United Arab Emirates, 115 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dubai is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 27.7 °C against 27.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Dubai is 1.1 °C warmer (Abu Dhabi 34.1 °C, Dubai 35.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abu Dhabi 40.4 °C (August), Dubai 41.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Abu Dhabi 14.3 °C, Dubai 13.6 °C.
  • Dubai gets 60% more rain and snow a year: 113 mm against 71 mm.
  • Dubai gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,449 h against 3,443 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Abu Dhabi in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Abu Dhabi in 6 of 12 months (1 equal)
