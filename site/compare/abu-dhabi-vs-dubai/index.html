Abu Dhabi vs Dubai climate

How the climate of Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates compares with Dubai, United Arab Emirates, 115 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dubai is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 27.6 °C against 27.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Dubai is 1.4 °C warmer (Abu Dhabi 34.0 °C, Dubai 35.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abu Dhabi 39.0 °C (August), Dubai 43.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Abu Dhabi 16.2 °C, Dubai 13.2 °C.
  • Dubai gets 64% more rain and snow a year: 116 mm against 71 mm.
  • Dubai gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,449 h against 3,443 h; about 1961–1990).
