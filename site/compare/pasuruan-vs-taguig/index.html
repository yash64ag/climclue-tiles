Pasuruan vs Taguig climate

How the climate of Pasuruan, Indonesia compares with Taguig, Philippines, 2,625 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Pasuruan and Taguig have the same yearly average temperature (27.5 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Taguig is 1.6 °C warmer (Pasuruan 27.6 °C, Taguig 29.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pasuruan 32.2 °C (October), Taguig 32.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Pasuruan 23.1 °C, Taguig 23.3 °C.
  • Taguig gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 2,013 mm against 1,803 mm.
  • Pasuruan gets about 978 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,340 h against 2,362 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
