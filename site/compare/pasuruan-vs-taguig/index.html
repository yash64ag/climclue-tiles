How the climate of Pasuruan, Indonesia compares with Taguig, Philippines: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Pasuruan is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 27.5 °C against 27.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Pasuruan is 1.8 °C warmer (Pasuruan 28.5 °C, Taguig 26.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pasuruan 33.4 °C (October), Taguig 34.6 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Pasuruan 22.6 °C, Taguig 20.5 °C.
  • Taguig gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,901 mm against 1,828 mm.
  • Pasuruan gets about 978 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,340 h against 2,362 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each

  • Sunnier: Pasuruan in 11 of 12 months
