Gimcheon vs Weifang climate

How the climate of Gimcheon, South Korea compares with Weifang, China, 809 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Weifang is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 13.5 °C against 13.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Weifang is 1.5 °C warmer (Gimcheon 22.9 °C, Weifang 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gimcheon 29.2 °C (August), Weifang 31.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gimcheon -5.7 °C, Weifang -6.2 °C.
  • Gimcheon gets 87% more rain and snow a year: 1,063 mm against 570 mm.
  • Weifang gets about 94 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,525 h against 2,431 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gimcheon 38 cm, Weifang 24 cm (ERA5-Land).
