Maracaibo vs Port-au-Prince climate

How the climate of Maracaibo, Venezuela compares with Port-au-Prince, Haiti, 882 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Maracaibo is 1.6 °C (3.0 °F) warmer over the year: 28.7 °C against 27.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Maracaibo is 2.7 °C warmer (Maracaibo 28.2 °C, Port-au-Prince 25.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maracaibo 33.9 °C (July), Port-au-Prince 34.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Maracaibo 23.5 °C, Port-au-Prince 20.0 °C.
  • Port-au-Prince gets 87% more rain and snow a year: 1,242 mm against 663 mm.
  • Port-au-Prince gets about 215 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,795 h against 2,580 h; about 1961–1990).
