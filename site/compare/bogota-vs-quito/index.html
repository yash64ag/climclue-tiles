How the climate of Bogotá, Colombia compares with Quito, Ecuador: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bogotá is 0.9 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 13.3 °C against 12.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Bogotá is 1.6 °C warmer (Bogotá 13.6 °C, Quito 12.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bogotá 20.2 °C (January), Quito 22.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bogotá 6.0 °C, Quito 3.0 °C.
  • Quito gets 126% more rain and snow a year: 2,072 mm against 917 mm.
  • Quito gets about 343 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,875 h against 1,532 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
