Bogotá vs Quito climate

How the climate of Bogotá, Colombia compares with Quito, Ecuador, 730 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bogotá is 1.9 °C (3.4 °F) warmer over the year: 13.1 °C against 11.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Bogotá is 2.4 °C warmer (Bogotá 13.4 °C, Quito 11.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bogotá 18.6 °C (September), Quito 16.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bogotá 8.8 °C, Quito 7.8 °C.
  • Quito gets 116% more rain and snow a year: 2,262 mm against 1,046 mm.
  • Quito gets about 343 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,875 h against 1,532 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
