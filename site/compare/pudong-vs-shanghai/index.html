How the climate of Pudong, China compares with Shanghai, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Pudong and Shanghai have the same yearly average temperature (17.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Pudong is 0.2 °C warmer (Pudong 13.8 °C, Shanghai 13.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pudong 32.0 °C (July), Shanghai 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Pudong 1.9 °C, Shanghai 1.6 °C.
  • Shanghai gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,476 mm against 1,413 mm.
  • Pudong gets about 3 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,973 h against 1,969 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Pudong 6.4 cm, Shanghai 7.7 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Pudong in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: Pudong in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Pudong in 8 of 12 months (1 equal)
