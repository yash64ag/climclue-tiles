Daejeon vs Gwangju climate

How the climate of Daejeon, South Korea compares with Gwangju, South Korea, 139 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Daejeon is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 13.2 °C against 12.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Daejeon is 1.3 °C warmer (Daejeon 22.8 °C, Gwangju 21.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daejeon 29.3 °C (August), Gwangju 28.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Daejeon -5.8 °C, Gwangju -4.9 °C.
  • Gwangju gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 1,666 mm against 1,332 mm.
  • Daejeon gets about 146 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,425 h against 2,279 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daejeon 33 cm, Gwangju 31 cm (ERA5-Land).
