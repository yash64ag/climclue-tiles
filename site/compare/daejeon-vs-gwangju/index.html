Main differences

  • Gwangju, Gwangju is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 13.6 °C against 13.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Gwangju, Gwangju is 1.3 °C warmer (Daejeon -0.9 °C, Gwangju, Gwangju 0.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daejeon 30.6 °C (August), Gwangju, Gwangju 30.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Daejeon -6.2 °C, Gwangju, Gwangju -4.1 °C.
  • Gwangju, Gwangju gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 1,550 mm against 1,223 mm.
  • Daejeon gets about 146 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,425 h against 2,279 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daejeon 27 cm, Gwangju, Gwangju 38 cm (ERA5-Land).
