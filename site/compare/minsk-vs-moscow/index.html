How the climate of Minsk, Belarus compares with Moscow, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Minsk is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 6.3 °C against 5.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Minsk is 2.7 °C warmer (Minsk -5.0 °C, Moscow -7.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Minsk 22.9 °C (July), Moscow 23.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Minsk -8.2 °C, Moscow -11.1 °C.
  • Moscow gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 691 mm against 686 mm.
  • Minsk gets about 40 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,741 h against 1,701 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Minsk 142 cm, Moscow 185 cm (ERA5-Land).
