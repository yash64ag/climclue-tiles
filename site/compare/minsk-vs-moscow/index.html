Minsk vs Moscow climate

How the climate of Minsk, Belarus compares with Moscow, Russia, 675 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Minsk is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 6.2 °C against 5.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Minsk is 2.6 °C warmer (Minsk -5.0 °C, Moscow -7.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Minsk 22.0 °C (July), Moscow 23.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Minsk -8.0 °C, Moscow -10.8 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (694 mm and 691 mm).
  • Minsk gets about 40 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,741 h against 1,701 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Minsk 160 cm, Moscow 193 cm (ERA5-Land).
