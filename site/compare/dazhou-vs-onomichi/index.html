How the climate of Dazhou, China compares with Onomichi, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Dazhou is 1.4 °C (2.6 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 16.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Dazhou is 3.6 °C warmer (Dazhou 13.4 °C, Onomichi 9.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dazhou 33.1 °C (August), Onomichi 32.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Dazhou 4.4 °C, Onomichi 1.8 °C.
  • Onomichi gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,305 mm against 1,286 mm.
  • Onomichi gets about 586 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,979 h against 1,394 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Dazhou 1.3 cm, Onomichi 6.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
