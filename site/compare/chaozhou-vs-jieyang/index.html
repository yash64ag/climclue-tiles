How the climate of Chaozhou, China compares with Jieyang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jieyang is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 22.4 °C against 22.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Jieyang is 0.3 °C warmer (Chaozhou 21.8 °C, Jieyang 22.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chaozhou 32.1 °C (July), Jieyang 32.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chaozhou 10.4 °C, Jieyang 10.3 °C.
  • Chaozhou gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,803 mm against 1,731 mm.
  • Chaozhou gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,986 h against 1,979 h; about 1961–1990).
