How the climate of Changchun, China compares with Fushun, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Fushun is 2.1 °C (3.7 °F) warmer over the year: 8.5 °C against 6.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Fushun is 3.1 °C warmer (Changchun -11.3 °C, Fushun -8.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changchun 27.6 °C (July), Fushun 29.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changchun -19.5 °C, Fushun -18.2 °C.
  • Fushun gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 772 mm against 635 mm.
  • Changchun gets about 35 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,570 h against 2,534 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changchun 33 cm, Fushun 42 cm (ERA5-Land).
