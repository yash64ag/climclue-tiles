How the climate of Berlin, Germany compares with Copenhagen, Denmark: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Berlin is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 9.5 °C against 9.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Berlin is 1.9 °C warmer (Berlin 14.1 °C, Copenhagen 12.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Berlin 24.4 °C (July), Copenhagen 21.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Berlin -2.1 °C, Copenhagen -1.0 °C.
  • Copenhagen gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 660 mm against 594 mm.
  • Copenhagen gets about 38 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,652 h against 1,614 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Berlin 51 cm, Copenhagen 41 cm (ERA5-Land).
