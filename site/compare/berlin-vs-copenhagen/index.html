Berlin vs Copenhagen climate

How the climate of Berlin, Germany compares with Copenhagen, Denmark, 355 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Berlin is 0.9 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 9.4 °C against 8.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Berlin is 2.7 °C warmer (Berlin 13.9 °C, Copenhagen 11.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Berlin 23.3 °C (July), Copenhagen 20.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Berlin -2.0 °C, Copenhagen -1.1 °C.
  • Copenhagen gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 652 mm against 603 mm.
  • Copenhagen gets about 38 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,652 h against 1,614 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Berlin 57 cm, Copenhagen 69 cm (ERA5-Land).
