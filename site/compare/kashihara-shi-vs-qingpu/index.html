Kashihara-shi vs Qingpu climate

How the climate of Kashihara-shi, Japan compares with Qingpu, China, 1,420 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Qingpu is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 16.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Qingpu is 1.3 °C warmer (Kashihara-shi 27.1 °C, Qingpu 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kashihara-shi 31.8 °C (August), Qingpu 31.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kashihara-shi -0.2 °C, Qingpu -0.1 °C.
  • Kashihara-shi gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,488 mm against 1,399 mm.
  • Qingpu gets about 123 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,958 h against 1,835 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kashihara-shi 28 cm, Qingpu 10 cm (ERA5-Land).
