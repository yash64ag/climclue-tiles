Saitama vs Yokohama climate

How the climate of Saitama, Japan compares with Yokohama, Japan, 53 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yokohama is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Yokohama is 2.5 °C warmer (Saitama 6.5 °C, Yokohama 8.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Saitama 30.3 °C (August), Yokohama 27.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Saitama -1.8 °C, Yokohama 2.7 °C.
  • Yokohama gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,537 mm against 1,327 mm.
  • Saitama gets about 36 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,863 h against 1,827 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Saitama 20 cm, Yokohama 10 cm (ERA5-Land).
