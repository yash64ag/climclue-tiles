Port Harcourt vs Yaoundé climate

How the climate of Port Harcourt, Nigeria compares with Yaoundé, Cameroon, 510 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Port Harcourt is 3.1 °C (5.5 °F) warmer over the year: 26.1 °C against 23.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Port Harcourt is 3.6 °C warmer (Port Harcourt 26.6 °C, Yaoundé 23.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Port Harcourt 31.7 °C (February), Yaoundé 29.9 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Port Harcourt 22.8 °C, Yaoundé 19.7 °C.
  • Port Harcourt gets 31% more rain and snow a year: 2,261 mm against 1,729 mm.
  • Yaoundé gets about 391 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,763 h against 1,373 h; about 1961–1990).
