Matola vs Soweto climate

How the climate of Matola, Mozambique compares with Soweto, South Africa, 461 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Matola is 7.3 °C (13.1 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Matola is 10.3 °C warmer (Matola 19.4 °C, Soweto 9.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Matola 29.3 °C (January), Soweto 24.9 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Matola 14.9 °C, Soweto 2.8 °C.
  • Matola gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 764 mm against 703 mm.
  • Soweto gets about 397 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,205 h against 2,808 h; about 1961–1990).
