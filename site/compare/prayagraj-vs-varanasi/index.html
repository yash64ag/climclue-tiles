Prayagraj vs Varanasi climate

How the climate of Prayagraj, India compares with Varanasi, India, 118 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Varanasi is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 25.8 °C against 25.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Varanasi is 0.3 °C warmer (Prayagraj 24.7 °C, Varanasi 25.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Prayagraj 41.2 °C (May), Varanasi 41.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Prayagraj 8.1 °C, Varanasi 8.2 °C.
  • Varanasi gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 1,052 mm against 916 mm.
  • Prayagraj gets about 184 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,666 h against 2,482 h; about 1961–1990).
