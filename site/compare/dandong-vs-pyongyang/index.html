Dandong vs Pyongyang climate

How the climate of Dandong, China compares with Pyongyang, North Korea, 168 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Pyongyang is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 11.1 °C against 9.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Pyongyang is 2.0 °C warmer (Dandong -7.4 °C, Pyongyang -5.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dandong 28.3 °C (August), Pyongyang 29.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Dandong -12.6 °C, Pyongyang -10.7 °C.
  • Dandong gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,040 mm against 936 mm.
  • Dandong gets about 81 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,508 h against 2,427 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Dandong 33 cm, Pyongyang 26 cm (ERA5-Land).
