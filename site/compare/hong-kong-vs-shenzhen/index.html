How the climate of Hong Kong, Hong Kong compares with Shenzhen, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hong Kong is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 23.6 °C against 23.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Hong Kong is 0.9 °C warmer (Hong Kong 16.9 °C, Shenzhen 16.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hong Kong 31.0 °C (July), Shenzhen 32.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hong Kong 14.8 °C, Shenzhen 12.0 °C.
  • Hong Kong gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 2,341 mm against 2,176 mm.
  • Hong Kong gets about 54 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,994 h against 1,940 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Shenzhen in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Hong Kong in 9 of 12 months (1 equal)
