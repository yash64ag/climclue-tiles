How the climate of Hong Kong, Hong Kong compares with Shenzhen, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Shenzhen is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 23.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Hong Kong is 0.6 °C warmer (Hong Kong 16.0 °C, Shenzhen 15.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hong Kong 30.7 °C (July), Shenzhen 32.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hong Kong 13.4 °C, Shenzhen 11.2 °C.
  • Hong Kong gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 2,341 mm against 2,176 mm.
  • Hong Kong gets about 54 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,994 h against 1,940 h; about 1961–1990).
