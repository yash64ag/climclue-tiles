Dublin vs London climate

How the climate of Dublin, Ireland compares with London, United Kingdom, 462 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • London is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 10.5 °C against 10.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when London is 1.5 °C warmer (Dublin 15.8 °C, London 17.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dublin 18.5 °C (July), London 21.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Dublin 3.3 °C, London 1.7 °C.
  • Dublin gets 39% more rain and snow a year: 944 mm against 681 mm.
  • London gets about 67 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,459 h against 1,392 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Dublin 18 cm, London 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
