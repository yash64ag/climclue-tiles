Córdoba vs Welkom climate

How the climate of Córdoba, Argentina compares with Welkom, South Africa, 8,514 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Córdoba is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 17.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Welkom is 2.0 °C warmer (Córdoba 14.6 °C, Welkom 16.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Córdoba 30.2 °C (January), Welkom 29.8 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Córdoba 3.1 °C, Welkom 0.2 °C.
  • Córdoba gets 51% more rain and snow a year: 878 mm against 580 mm.
  • Welkom gets about 657 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,249 h against 2,592 h; about 1961–1990).
