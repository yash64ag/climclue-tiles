Ecatepec de Morelos vs Santiago de Querétaro climate

How the climate of Ecatepec de Morelos, Mexico compares with Santiago de Querétaro, Mexico, 178 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Santiago de Querétaro is 0.9 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 17.9 °C against 17.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Santiago de Querétaro is 1.5 °C warmer (Ecatepec de Morelos 19.8 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 21.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ecatepec de Morelos 25.8 °C (April), Santiago de Querétaro 28.1 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Ecatepec de Morelos 6.3 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 7.1 °C.
  • Ecatepec de Morelos gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 576 mm against 549 mm.
  • Santiago de Querétaro gets about 399 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,547 h against 2,148 h; about 1961–1990).
