How the climate of Ecatepec de Morelos, Mexico compares with Santiago de Querétaro, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Santiago de Querétaro is 2.2 °C (3.9 °F) warmer over the year: 19.1 °C against 17.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Santiago de Querétaro is 2.7 °C warmer (Ecatepec de Morelos 19.8 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 22.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ecatepec de Morelos 28.8 °C (April), Santiago de Querétaro 30.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Ecatepec de Morelos 4.1 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 7.1 °C.
  • Ecatepec de Morelos gets 43% more rain and snow a year: 654 mm against 456 mm.
  • Santiago de Querétaro gets about 399 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,547 h against 2,148 h; about 1961–1990).
