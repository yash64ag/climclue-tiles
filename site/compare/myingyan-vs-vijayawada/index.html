Myingyan vs Vijayawada climate

How the climate of Myingyan, Myanmar compares with Vijayawada, India, 1,644 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Vijayawada is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 28.4 °C against 28.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Vijayawada is 2.3 °C warmer (Myingyan 22.1 °C, Vijayawada 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Myingyan 38.8 °C (April), Vijayawada 38.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Myingyan 16.0 °C, Vijayawada 18.9 °C.
  • Vijayawada gets 47% more rain and snow a year: 1,040 mm against 707 mm.
  • Vijayawada gets about 290 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,669 h against 2,379 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Sunnier: Vijayawada in 10 of 12 months
