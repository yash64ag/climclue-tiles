Myingyan vs Vijayawada climate

How the climate of Myingyan, Myanmar compares with Vijayawada, India, 1,644 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Vijayawada is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 28.4 °C against 28.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Vijayawada is 2.3 °C warmer (Myingyan 22.1 °C, Vijayawada 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Myingyan 38.7 °C (April), Vijayawada 38.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Myingyan 15.8 °C, Vijayawada 18.8 °C.
  • Vijayawada gets 43% more rain and snow a year: 1,041 mm against 726 mm.
  • Vijayawada gets about 290 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,669 h against 2,379 h; about 1961–1990).
