Arlington, Texas vs Chongqing climate

Main differences

  • Chongqing is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 19.2 °C against 18.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Chongqing is 1.4 °C warmer (Arlington, Texas 8.5 °C, Chongqing 9.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Arlington, Texas 35.5 °C (August), Chongqing 33.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Arlington, Texas 1.8 °C, Chongqing 6.6 °C.
  • Chongqing gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 1,104 mm against 963 mm.
  • Arlington, Texas gets about 1,617 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,815 h against 1,198 h; about 1961–1990).
