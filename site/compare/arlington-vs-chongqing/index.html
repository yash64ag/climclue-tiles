Main differences

  • Arlington, Texas and Chongqing have the same yearly average temperature (18.9 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Arlington, Texas is 1.4 °C warmer (Arlington, Texas 27.1 °C, Chongqing 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Arlington, Texas 34.8 °C (August), Chongqing 31.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Arlington, Texas 3.9 °C, Chongqing 4.8 °C.
  • Chongqing gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 1,146 mm against 926 mm.
  • Arlington, Texas gets about 1,617 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,815 h against 1,198 h; about 1961–1990).
