Main differences

  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Arlington, Texas is 1.4 °C warmer (Arlington, Texas 27.2 °C, Chongqing 25.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Arlington, Texas 35.5 °C (August), Chongqing 33.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Arlington, Texas 2.2 °C, Chongqing 6.5 °C.
