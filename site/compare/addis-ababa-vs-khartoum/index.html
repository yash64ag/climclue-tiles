Addis Ababa vs Khartoum climate

How the climate of Addis Ababa, Ethiopia compares with Khartoum, Sudan, 991 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Khartoum is 15.2 °C (27.3 °F) warmer over the year: 29.7 °C against 14.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Khartoum is 19.4 °C warmer (Addis Ababa 14.8 °C, Khartoum 34.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Addis Ababa 22.0 °C (March), Khartoum 40.5 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Addis Ababa 6.2 °C, Khartoum 15.2 °C.
  • Addis Ababa gets 1,003% more rain and snow a year: 1,199 mm against 109 mm.
  • Khartoum gets about 684 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,512 h against 2,828 h; about 1961–1990).
