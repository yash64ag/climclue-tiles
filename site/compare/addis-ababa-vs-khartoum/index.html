How the climate of Addis Ababa, Ethiopia compares with Khartoum, Sudan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Khartoum is 14.5 °C (26.0 °F) warmer over the year: 29.6 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Khartoum is 18.7 °C warmer (Addis Ababa 15.5 °C, Khartoum 34.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Addis Ababa 23.9 °C (March), Khartoum 42.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Addis Ababa 5.7 °C, Khartoum 14.6 °C.
  • Addis Ababa gets 967% more rain and snow a year: 1,142 mm against 107 mm.
  • Khartoum gets about 684 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,512 h against 2,828 h; about 1961–1990).
