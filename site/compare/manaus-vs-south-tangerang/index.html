Manaus vs South Tangerang climate

How the climate of Manaus, Brazil compares with South Tangerang, Indonesia, 18,210 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Manaus is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Manaus is 0.6 °C warmer (Manaus 27.4 °C, South Tangerang 26.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manaus 32.3 °C (September), South Tangerang 32.3 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Manaus 22.2 °C, South Tangerang 21.4 °C.
  • South Tangerang gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 2,884 mm against 2,429 mm.
  • South Tangerang gets about 874 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,641 h against 1,767 h; about 1961–1990).
