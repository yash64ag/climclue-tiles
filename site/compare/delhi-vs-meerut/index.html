Delhi vs Meerut climate

How the climate of Delhi, India compares with Meerut, India, 59 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Delhi is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 24.8 °C against 24.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Delhi is 0.9 °C warmer (Delhi 25.4 °C, Meerut 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Delhi 39.3 °C (May), Meerut 38.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Delhi 7.7 °C, Meerut 7.8 °C.
  • Meerut gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 887 mm against 641 mm.
  • Delhi gets about 20 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,679 h against 2,659 h; about 1961–1990).
