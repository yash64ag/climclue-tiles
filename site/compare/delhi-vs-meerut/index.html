How the climate of Delhi, India compares with Meerut, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Delhi is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 24.8 °C against 24.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Delhi is 0.8 °C warmer (Delhi 25.3 °C, Meerut 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Delhi 39.6 °C (May), Meerut 39.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Delhi 6.6 °C, Meerut 6.5 °C.
  • Meerut gets 35% more rain and snow a year: 875 mm against 646 mm.
  • Delhi gets about 20 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,679 h against 2,659 h; about 1961–1990).
