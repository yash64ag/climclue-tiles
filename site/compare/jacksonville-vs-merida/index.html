Main differences

  • Mérida is 6.0 °C (10.9 °F) warmer over the year: 26.3 °C against 20.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Mérida is 10.8 °C warmer (Jacksonville, Florida 12.2 °C, Mérida 22.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jacksonville, Florida 32.6 °C (July), Mérida 35.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Jacksonville, Florida 6.0 °C, Mérida 17.2 °C.
  • Jacksonville, Florida gets 44% more rain and snow a year: 1,459 mm against 1,016 mm.
  • Jacksonville, Florida gets about 210 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,845 h against 2,635 h; about 1961–1990).
