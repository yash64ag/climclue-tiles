Jacksonville vs Mérida climate

How the climate of Jacksonville, United States compares with Mérida, Mexico, 1,311 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mérida is 6.1 °C (11.0 °F) warmer over the year: 26.4 °C against 20.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Mérida is 10.8 °C warmer (Jacksonville 12.2 °C, Mérida 22.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jacksonville 31.8 °C (July), Mérida 35.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Jacksonville 8.6 °C, Mérida 18.9 °C.
  • Jacksonville gets 43% more rain and snow a year: 1,457 mm against 1,021 mm.
  • Jacksonville gets about 210 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,845 h against 2,635 h; about 1961–1990).
