How the climate of Hanoi, Vietnam compares with Nanning, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hanoi is 1.5 °C (2.8 °F) warmer over the year: 24.1 °C against 22.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Hanoi is 3.4 °C warmer (Hanoi 16.9 °C, Nanning 13.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hanoi 33.1 °C (July), Nanning 32.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hanoi 14.1 °C, Nanning 9.8 °C.
  • Hanoi gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 1,891 mm against 1,426 mm.
  • Nanning gets about 40 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,684 h against 1,645 h; about 1961–1990).
