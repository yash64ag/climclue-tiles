Hanoi vs Nanning climate

How the climate of Hanoi, Vietnam compares with Nanning, China, 324 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Hanoi is 1.8 °C (3.2 °F) warmer over the year: 24.1 °C against 22.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Hanoi is 3.5 °C warmer (Hanoi 16.9 °C, Nanning 13.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hanoi 32.5 °C (July), Nanning 31.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Hanoi 13.3 °C, Nanning 9.6 °C.
  • Hanoi gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 1,874 mm against 1,479 mm.
  • Nanning gets about 40 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,684 h against 1,645 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
