How the climate of Abuja, Nigeria compares with Ilorin, Nigeria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ilorin is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 26.7 °C against 26.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Ilorin is 1.1 °C warmer (Abuja 23.6 °C, Ilorin 24.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abuja 35.7 °C (February), Ilorin 35.7 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Abuja 19.3 °C, Ilorin 19.7 °C.
  • Abuja gets 36% more rain and snow a year: 1,467 mm against 1,075 mm.
  • Abuja gets about 253 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,472 h against 2,219 h; about 1961–1990).
