Piteşti vs Sofia climate

How the climate of Piteşti, Romania compares with Sofia, Bulgaria, 269 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Sofia is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 10.5 °C against 10.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Sofia is 0.7 °C warmer (Piteşti 10.6 °C, Sofia 11.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Piteşti 25.7 °C (July), Sofia 25.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Piteşti -4.6 °C, Sofia -5.5 °C.
  • Sofia gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 716 mm against 701 mm.
  • Sofia gets about 84 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,045 h against 1,962 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Piteşti 101 cm, Sofia 160 cm (ERA5-Land).
