Piteşti vs Sofia climate

How the climate of Piteşti, Romania compares with Sofia, Bulgaria, 269 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Sofia is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 11.1 °C against 10.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Sofia is 1.2 °C warmer (Piteşti 10.6 °C, Sofia 11.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Piteşti 25.8 °C (July), Sofia 26.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Piteşti -4.5 °C, Sofia -5.1 °C.
  • Piteşti gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 683 mm against 612 mm.
  • Sofia gets about 84 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,045 h against 1,962 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Piteşti 78 cm, Sofia 95 cm (ERA5-Land).
