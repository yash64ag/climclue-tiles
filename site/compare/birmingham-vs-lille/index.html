How the climate of Birmingham, United Kingdom compares with Lille, France: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lille is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 10.9 °C against 9.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Lille is 2.3 °C warmer (Birmingham 16.1 °C, Lille 18.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Birmingham 21.3 °C (July), Lille 23.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Birmingham 0.9 °C, Lille 1.3 °C.
  • Lille gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 793 mm against 737 mm.
  • Lille gets about 229 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,592 h against 1,363 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Birmingham 6.3 cm, Lille 9.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
