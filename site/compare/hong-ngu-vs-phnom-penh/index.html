How the climate of Hồng Ngự, Vietnam compares with Phnom Penh, Cambodia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Phnom Penh is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 28.2 °C against 27.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Phnom Penh is 0.9 °C warmer (Hồng Ngự 27.4 °C, Phnom Penh 28.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hồng Ngự 35.0 °C (April), Phnom Penh 35.6 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Hồng Ngự 21.5 °C, Phnom Penh 21.6 °C.
  • Hồng Ngự gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,507 mm against 1,395 mm.
  • Phnom Penh gets about 318 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,549 h against 2,231 h; about 1961–1990).
