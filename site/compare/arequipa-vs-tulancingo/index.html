How the climate of Arequipa, Peru compares with Tulancingo, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Arequipa is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 15.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Arequipa is 2.8 °C warmer (Arequipa 15.8 °C, Tulancingo 13.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Arequipa 24.7 °C (October), Tulancingo 25.6 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Arequipa 5.5 °C, Tulancingo 4.1 °C.
  • Tulancingo gets 175% more rain and snow a year: 461 mm against 168 mm.
  • Arequipa gets about 744 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,178 h against 2,434 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
