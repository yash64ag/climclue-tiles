Brisbane vs Melbourne climate

How the climate of Brisbane, Australia compares with Melbourne, Australia, 1,375 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Brisbane is 5.6 °C (10.1 °F) warmer over the year: 20.0 °C against 14.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Brisbane is 6.8 °C warmer (Brisbane 20.5 °C, Melbourne 13.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brisbane 28.3 °C (January), Melbourne 24.6 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Brisbane 10.2 °C, Melbourne 6.8 °C.
  • Brisbane gets 66% more rain and snow a year: 1,042 mm against 629 mm.
  • Brisbane gets about 636 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,833 h against 2,197 h; about 1961–1990).
