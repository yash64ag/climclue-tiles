Biskra vs Phoenix climate

How the climate of Biskra, Algeria compares with Phoenix, United States, 10,036 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Phoenix is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.3 °C against 22.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Phoenix is 1.7 °C warmer (Biskra 26.3 °C, Phoenix 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Biskra 39.3 °C (July), Phoenix 41.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Biskra 5.9 °C, Phoenix 5.0 °C.
  • Phoenix gets 60% more rain and snow a year: 217 mm against 136 mm.
  • Phoenix gets about 723 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,815 h against 3,092 h; about 1961–1990).
