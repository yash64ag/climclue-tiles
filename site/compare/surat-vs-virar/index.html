How the climate of Surat, India compares with Virār, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Surat is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Surat is 1.1 °C warmer (Surat 29.4 °C, Virār 28.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Surat 36.3 °C (April), Virār 33.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Surat 13.8 °C, Virār 16.0 °C.
  • Virār gets 72% more rain and snow a year: 2,434 mm against 1,412 mm.
  • Surat gets about 162 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,865 h against 2,703 h; about 1961–1990).
