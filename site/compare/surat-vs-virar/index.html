Surat vs Virār climate

How the climate of Surat, India compares with Virār, India, 193 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Surat is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Surat is 1.2 °C warmer (Surat 29.6 °C, Virār 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Surat 34.4 °C (April), Virār 33.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Surat 17.2 °C, Virār 18.1 °C.
  • Virār gets 70% more rain and snow a year: 2,454 mm against 1,443 mm.
  • Surat gets about 162 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,865 h against 2,703 h; about 1961–1990).
