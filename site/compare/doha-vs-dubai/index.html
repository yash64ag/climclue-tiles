How the climate of Doha, Qatar compares with Dubai, United Arab Emirates: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Dubai is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 27.7 °C against 27.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Dubai is 1.6 °C warmer (Doha 18.4 °C, Dubai 20.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Doha 40.6 °C (July), Dubai 41.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Doha 13.3 °C, Dubai 13.6 °C.
  • Dubai gets 31% more rain and snow a year: 113 mm against 86 mm.
  • Dubai gets about 45 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,449 h against 3,405 h; about 1961–1990).
