Doha vs Dubai climate

How the climate of Doha, Qatar compares with Dubai, United Arab Emirates, 381 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dubai is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 27.6 °C against 27.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Dubai is 1.5 °C warmer (Doha 18.4 °C, Dubai 19.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Doha 39.5 °C (July), Dubai 43.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Doha 14.2 °C, Dubai 13.2 °C.
  • Dubai gets 35% more rain and snow a year: 116 mm against 86 mm.
  • Dubai gets about 45 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,449 h against 3,405 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Doha in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Dubai in 7 of 12 months
