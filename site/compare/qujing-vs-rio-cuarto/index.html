Qujing vs Río Cuarto climate

How the climate of Qujing, China compares with Río Cuarto, Argentina, 18,586 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Río Cuarto is 1.1 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 15.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Río Cuarto is 14.7 °C warmer (Qujing 8.5 °C, Río Cuarto 23.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qujing 24.6 °C (July), Río Cuarto 29.7 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Qujing 2.3 °C, Río Cuarto 2.7 °C.
  • Qujing gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,007 mm against 887 mm.
  • Río Cuarto gets about 789 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,722 h against 1,933 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
