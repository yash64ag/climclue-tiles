Onomichi vs Yichang climate

How the climate of Onomichi, Japan compares with Yichang, China, 2,090 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yichang is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 17.6 °C against 16.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Yichang is 3.4 °C warmer (Onomichi 19.3 °C, Yichang 22.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Onomichi 32.6 °C (August), Yichang 32.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Onomichi 1.8 °C, Yichang 1.5 °C.
  • Onomichi gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,305 mm against 1,225 mm.
  • Onomichi gets about 352 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,979 h against 1,628 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Onomichi 6.6 cm, Yichang 8.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
