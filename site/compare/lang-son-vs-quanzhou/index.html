How the climate of Lạng Sơn, Vietnam compares with Quanzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lạng Sơn is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 21.8 °C against 21.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Lạng Sơn is 2.4 °C warmer (Lạng Sơn 22.6 °C, Quanzhou 20.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lạng Sơn 31.6 °C (July), Quanzhou 32.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lạng Sơn 10.1 °C, Quanzhou 9.5 °C.
  • Lạng Sơn gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 1,491 mm against 1,162 mm.
  • Quanzhou gets about 340 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,958 h against 1,618 h; about 1961–1990).
