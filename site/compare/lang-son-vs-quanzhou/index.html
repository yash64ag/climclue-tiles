Lạng Sơn vs Quanzhou climate

How the climate of Lạng Sơn, Vietnam compares with Quanzhou, China, 1,253 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lạng Sơn is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 21.6 °C against 21.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Lạng Sơn is 2.2 °C warmer (Lạng Sơn 18.1 °C, Quanzhou 15.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lạng Sơn 29.9 °C (July), Quanzhou 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lạng Sơn 9.7 °C, Quanzhou 8.3 °C.
  • Lạng Sơn gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,508 mm against 1,251 mm.
  • Quanzhou gets about 340 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,958 h against 1,618 h; about 1961–1990).
