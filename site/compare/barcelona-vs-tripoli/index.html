Barcelona vs Tripoli climate

How the climate of Barcelona, Spain compares with Tripoli, Libya, 1,358 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tripoli is 4.2 °C (7.5 °F) warmer over the year: 20.4 °C against 16.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Tripoli is 5.3 °C warmer (Barcelona 18.1 °C, Tripoli 23.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Barcelona 26.1 °C (August), Tripoli 31.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Barcelona 7.0 °C, Tripoli 10.2 °C.
  • Barcelona gets 108% more rain and snow a year: 603 mm against 290 mm.
  • Tripoli gets about 834 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,219 h against 2,385 h; about 1961–1990).
