How the climate of Barcelona, Spain compares with Tripoli, Libya: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tripoli is 4.3 °C (7.7 °F) warmer over the year: 20.3 °C against 16.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Tripoli is 5.9 °C warmer (Barcelona 13.1 °C, Tripoli 19.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Barcelona 28.2 °C (August), Tripoli 33.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Barcelona 5.1 °C, Tripoli 8.8 °C.
  • Barcelona gets 112% more rain and snow a year: 634 mm against 299 mm.
  • Tripoli gets about 834 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,219 h against 2,385 h; about 1961–1990).
