Konya vs Shahrud climate

How the climate of Konya, Turkey compares with Shahrud, Iran, 1,995 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shahrud is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 14.0 °C against 13.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Shahrud is 1.9 °C warmer (Konya 21.5 °C, Shahrud 23.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Konya 32.5 °C (August), Shahrud 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Konya -2.8 °C, Shahrud -3.4 °C.
  • Konya gets 63% more rain and snow a year: 389 mm against 238 mm.
  • Shahrud gets about 40 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,960 h against 2,920 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Konya 48 cm, Shahrud 50 cm (ERA5-Land).
