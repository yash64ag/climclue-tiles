Manila vs Zamboanga climate

How the climate of Manila, Philippines compares with Zamboanga, Philippines, 864 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Manila is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 27.8 °C against 27.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Manila is 2.1 °C warmer (Manila 29.5 °C, Zamboanga 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manila 34.9 °C (April), Zamboanga 32.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Manila 21.6 °C, Zamboanga 22.0 °C.
  • Manila gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 2,057 mm against 1,695 mm.
  • Manila gets about 194 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,404 h against 2,211 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Manila in 10 of 12 months
  • Drier: Manila in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Manila in 9 of 12 months
