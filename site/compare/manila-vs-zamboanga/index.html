How the climate of Manila, Philippines compares with Zamboanga, Philippines: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zamboanga is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 27.9 °C against 27.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Zamboanga is 1.9 °C warmer (Manila 25.8 °C, Zamboanga 27.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manila 35.1 °C (April), Zamboanga 32.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Manila 20.8 °C, Zamboanga 23.1 °C.
  • Manila gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 2,057 mm against 1,695 mm.
  • Manila gets about 194 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,404 h against 2,211 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Zamboanga in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: Manila in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Manila in 9 of 12 months
