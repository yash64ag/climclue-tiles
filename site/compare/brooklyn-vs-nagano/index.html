How the climate of Brooklyn, United States compares with Nagano, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Nagano is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 12.4 °C against 12.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Nagano is 1.7 °C warmer (Brooklyn 23.6 °C, Nagano 25.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brooklyn 28.8 °C (July), Nagano 30.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Brooklyn -3.6 °C, Nagano -4.7 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,240 mm and 1,237 mm).
  • Brooklyn gets about 714 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,518 h against 1,803 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Brooklyn 56 cm, Nagano 194 cm (ERA5-Land).
