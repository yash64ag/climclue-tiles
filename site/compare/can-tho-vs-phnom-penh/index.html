Cần Thơ vs Phnom Penh climate

How the climate of Cần Thơ, Vietnam compares with Phnom Penh, Cambodia, 194 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Phnom Penh is 1.1 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 28.2 °C against 27.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Phnom Penh is 1.8 °C warmer (Cần Thơ 26.4 °C, Phnom Penh 28.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cần Thơ 33.3 °C (April), Phnom Penh 34.8 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Cần Thơ 21.9 °C, Phnom Penh 22.0 °C.
  • Cần Thơ gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 1,717 mm against 1,395 mm.
  • Phnom Penh gets about 532 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,549 h against 2,017 h; about 1961–1990).
