How the climate of La Paz, Bolivia compares with Puno, Peru: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Puno is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 8.6 °C against 8.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Puno is 0.9 °C warmer (La Paz 8.0 °C, Puno 8.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: La Paz 16.6 °C (November), Puno 18.7 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: La Paz -1.6 °C, Puno -4.2 °C.
  • Puno gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 1,338 mm against 1,064 mm.
  • Puno gets about 515 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,864 h against 2,349 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: La Paz 5.6 cm, Puno 0.9 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Puno in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Puno in 12 of 12 months
