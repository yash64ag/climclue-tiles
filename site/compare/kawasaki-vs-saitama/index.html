Kawasaki vs Saitama climate

How the climate of Kawasaki, Japan compares with Saitama, Japan, 43 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kawasaki is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.8 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Kawasaki is 2.1 °C warmer (Kawasaki 8.6 °C, Saitama 6.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kawasaki 28.6 °C (August), Saitama 30.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kawasaki 2.0 °C, Saitama -1.8 °C.
  • Kawasaki gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,508 mm against 1,327 mm.
  • Saitama gets about 37 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,863 h against 1,826 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kawasaki 24 cm, Saitama 34 cm (ERA5-Land).
