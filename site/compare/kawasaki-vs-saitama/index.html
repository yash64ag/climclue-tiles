How the climate of Kawasaki, Kanagawa, Japan compares with Saitama, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kawasaki, Kanagawa is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 15.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Kawasaki, Kanagawa is 2.2 °C warmer (Kawasaki, Kanagawa 8.7 °C, Saitama 6.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kawasaki, Kanagawa 29.9 °C (August), Saitama 31.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kawasaki, Kanagawa 1.6 °C, Saitama -1.3 °C.
  • Kawasaki, Kanagawa gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,489 mm against 1,336 mm.
  • Saitama gets about 37 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,863 h against 1,826 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kawasaki, Kanagawa 12 cm, Saitama 20 cm (ERA5-Land).
