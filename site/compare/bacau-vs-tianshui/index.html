Bacău vs Tianshui climate

How the climate of Bacău, Romania compares with Tianshui, China, 6,510 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tianshui is 0.9 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 11.0 °C against 10.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Tianshui is 2.1 °C warmer (Bacău 4.3 °C, Tianshui 6.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bacău 26.2 °C (July), Tianshui 26.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bacău -5.8 °C, Tianshui -7.4 °C.
  • Tianshui gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 577 mm against 529 mm.
  • Tianshui gets about 150 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,026 h against 1,876 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bacău 117 cm, Tianshui 79 cm (ERA5-Land).
