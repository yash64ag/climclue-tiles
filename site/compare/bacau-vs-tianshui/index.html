How the climate of Bacău, Romania compares with Tianshui, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tianshui is 1.7 °C (3.1 °F) warmer over the year: 11.8 °C against 10.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Tianshui is 2.5 °C warmer (Bacău -0.5 °C, Tianshui 2.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bacău 27.8 °C (July), Tianshui 28.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bacău -5.7 °C, Tianshui -5.7 °C.
  • Bacău gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 546 mm against 499 mm.
  • Tianshui gets about 150 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,026 h against 1,876 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bacău 60 cm, Tianshui 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
