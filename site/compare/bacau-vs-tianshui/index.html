Bacău vs Tianshui climate

How the climate of Bacău, Romania compares with Tianshui, China, 6,510 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tianshui is 2.3 °C (4.2 °F) warmer over the year: 12.4 °C against 10.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Tianshui is 3.6 °C warmer (Bacău 4.2 °C, Tianshui 7.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bacău 27.9 °C (July), Tianshui 29.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bacău -6.1 °C, Tianshui -5.6 °C.
  • Bacău gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 546 mm against 499 mm.
  • Tianshui gets about 150 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,026 h against 1,876 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bacău 60 cm, Tianshui 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
