How the climate of Jiaxing, China compares with Kadoma, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jiaxing is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 16.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Jiaxing is 1.3 °C warmer (Jiaxing 28.8 °C, Kadoma 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiaxing 32.6 °C (July), Kadoma 33.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Jiaxing 0.8 °C, Kadoma 1.9 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,455 mm and 1,461 mm).
  • Jiaxing gets about 94 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,922 h against 1,828 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jiaxing 13 cm, Kadoma 5.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
