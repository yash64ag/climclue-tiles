Huainan vs Suzhou, Anhui climate

How the climate of Huainan, China compares with Suzhou, Anhui, China, 112 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Huainan is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.8 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Huainan is 1.2 °C warmer (Huainan 4.5 °C, Suzhou, Anhui 3.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huainan 31.4 °C (July), Suzhou, Anhui 31.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Huainan -2.0 °C, Suzhou, Anhui -3.5 °C.
  • Huainan gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 976 mm against 897 mm.
  • Suzhou, Anhui gets about 91 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,201 h against 2,111 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Huainan 26 cm, Suzhou, Anhui 25 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Drier: Suzhou, Anhui in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Suzhou, Anhui in 10 of 12 months
