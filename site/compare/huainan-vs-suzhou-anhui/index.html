How the climate of Huainan, China compares with Suzhou, Anhui, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Huainan is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.8 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Huainan is 1.1 °C warmer (Huainan 4.4 °C, Suzhou, Anhui 3.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huainan 31.9 °C (July), Suzhou, Anhui 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Huainan -2.1 °C, Suzhou, Anhui -3.4 °C.
  • Huainan gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,028 mm against 904 mm.
  • Suzhou, Anhui gets about 91 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,201 h against 2,111 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Huainan 19 cm, Suzhou, Anhui 17 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Sunnier: Suzhou, Anhui in 10 of 12 months
