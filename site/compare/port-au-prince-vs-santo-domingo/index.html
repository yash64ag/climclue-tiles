Port-au-Prince vs Santo Domingo climate

How the climate of Port-au-Prince, Haiti compares with Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, 258 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Port-au-Prince is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 25.9 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Port-au-Prince is 0.7 °C warmer (Port-au-Prince 27.4 °C, Santo Domingo 26.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Port-au-Prince 31.5 °C (July), Santo Domingo 29.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Port-au-Prince 19.9 °C, Santo Domingo 21.3 °C.
  • Santo Domingo gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 1,711 mm against 1,402 mm.
  • Port-au-Prince gets about 20 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,795 h against 2,775 h; about 1961–1990).
