Port-au-Prince vs Santo Domingo climate

How the climate of Port-au-Prince, Haiti compares with Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, 258 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Port-au-Prince is 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Port-au-Prince is 1.9 °C warmer (Port-au-Prince 28.6 °C, Santo Domingo 26.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Port-au-Prince 34.2 °C (July), Santo Domingo 31.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Port-au-Prince 20.0 °C, Santo Domingo 18.9 °C.
  • Santo Domingo gets 36% more rain and snow a year: 1,688 mm against 1,242 mm.
  • Port-au-Prince gets about 20 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,795 h against 2,775 h; about 1961–1990).
