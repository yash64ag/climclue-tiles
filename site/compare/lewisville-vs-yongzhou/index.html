How the climate of Lewisville, United States compares with Yongzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yongzhou is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 18.9 °C against 18.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Yongzhou is 1.3 °C warmer (Lewisville 13.2 °C, Yongzhou 14.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lewisville 35.5 °C (August), Yongzhou 34.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lewisville 1.4 °C, Yongzhou 4.0 °C.
  • Yongzhou gets 45% more rain and snow a year: 1,454 mm against 1,001 mm.
  • Lewisville gets about 1,244 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,824 h against 1,580 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lewisville 1.9 cm, Yongzhou 6.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
