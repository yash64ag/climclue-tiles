How the climate of Abengourou, Ivory Coast compares with Kumasi, Ghana: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Abengourou is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.1 °C against 26.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Abengourou is 0.3 °C warmer (Abengourou 25.5 °C, Kumasi 25.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abengourou 34.1 °C (February), Kumasi 33.9 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Abengourou 20.5 °C, Kumasi 20.9 °C.
  • Kumasi gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,364 mm against 1,240 mm.
  • Abengourou gets about 67 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,005 h against 1,937 h; about 1961–1990).
