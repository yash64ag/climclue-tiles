Abengourou vs Kumasi climate

How the climate of Abengourou, Ivory Coast compares with Kumasi, Ghana, 207 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Abengourou and Kumasi have the same yearly average temperature (26.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Kumasi is 0.2 °C warmer (Abengourou 26.8 °C, Kumasi 26.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abengourou 33.7 °C (February), Kumasi 33.5 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Abengourou 21.6 °C, Kumasi 21.7 °C.
  • Kumasi gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,335 mm against 1,266 mm.
  • Abengourou gets about 67 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,005 h against 1,937 h; about 1961–1990).

  • Sunnier: Abengourou in 11 of 12 months
