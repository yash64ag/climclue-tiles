How the climate of Ufa, Russia compares with Ust-Kamenogorsk, Kazakhstan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ust-Kamenogorsk is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 4.2 °C against 3.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Ust-Kamenogorsk is 2.2 °C warmer (Ufa 17.3 °C, Ust-Kamenogorsk 19.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ufa 25.7 °C (July), Ust-Kamenogorsk 28.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ufa -17.1 °C, Ust-Kamenogorsk -19.0 °C.
  • Ufa gets 45% more rain and snow a year: 684 mm against 472 mm.
  • Ust-Kamenogorsk gets about 526 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,469 h against 1,944 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ufa 229 cm, Ust-Kamenogorsk 162 cm (ERA5-Land).
