Makkah vs Riyadh climate

How the climate of Makkah, Saudi Arabia compares with Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, 793 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Makkah is 2.8 °C (5.1 °F) warmer over the year: 29.1 °C against 26.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Makkah is 8.1 °C warmer (Makkah 21.9 °C, Riyadh 13.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Makkah 40.6 °C (June), Riyadh 42.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Makkah 15.4 °C, Riyadh 6.7 °C.
  • Makkah gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 131 mm against 102 mm.
  • Riyadh gets about 92 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,360 h against 3,268 h; about 1961–1990).
