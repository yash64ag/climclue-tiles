Sumy vs Voronezh climate

How the climate of Sumy, Ukraine compares with Voronezh, Russia, 317 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Sumy is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 7.4 °C against 6.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Sumy is 1.4 °C warmer (Sumy -5.0 °C, Voronezh -6.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Sumy 24.6 °C (July), Voronezh 25.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Sumy -8.4 °C, Voronezh -9.6 °C.
  • Sumy gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 621 mm against 616 mm.
  • Voronezh gets about 179 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,891 h against 1,713 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Sumy 147 cm, Voronezh 161 cm (ERA5-Land).
