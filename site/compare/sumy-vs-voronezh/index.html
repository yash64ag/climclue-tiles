How the climate of Sumy, Ukraine compares with Voronezh, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Sumy is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 7.5 °C against 7.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Sumy is 1.3 °C warmer (Sumy -5.0 °C, Voronezh -6.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Sumy 25.7 °C (July), Voronezh 26.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Sumy -8.3 °C, Voronezh -9.7 °C.
  • Voronezh gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 637 mm against 595 mm.
  • Voronezh gets about 179 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,891 h against 1,713 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Sumy 122 cm, Voronezh 145 cm (ERA5-Land).
