How the climate of Huzhou, China compares with Jiaxing, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Huzhou is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.9 °C against 16.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Huzhou is 0.5 °C warmer (Huzhou 16.0 °C, Jiaxing 15.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huzhou 32.8 °C (July), Jiaxing 32.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Huzhou 0.5 °C, Jiaxing 0.8 °C.
  • Jiaxing gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,455 mm against 1,292 mm.
  • Huzhou gets about 12 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,934 h against 1,922 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Huzhou 17 cm, Jiaxing 13 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Huzhou in 6 of 12 months (2 equal)
  • Drier: Huzhou in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Huzhou in 9 of 12 months
