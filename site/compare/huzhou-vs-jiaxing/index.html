Huzhou vs Jiaxing climate

How the climate of Huzhou, China compares with Jiaxing, China, 64 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jiaxing is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 16.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Jiaxing is 0.5 °C warmer (Huzhou 12.4 °C, Jiaxing 12.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huzhou 32.1 °C (July), Jiaxing 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Huzhou 0.3 °C, Jiaxing 0.3 °C.
  • Jiaxing gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,447 mm against 1,312 mm.
  • Huzhou gets about 12 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,934 h against 1,922 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Huzhou 17 cm, Jiaxing 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
