Faisalabad vs Muktsar climate

How the climate of Faisalabad, Pakistan compares with Muktsar, India, 172 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Faisalabad is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 24.9 °C against 24.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Faisalabad is 0.7 °C warmer (Faisalabad 32.0 °C, Muktsar 31.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Faisalabad 39.9 °C (June), Muktsar 39.5 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Faisalabad 6.3 °C, Muktsar 6.6 °C.
  • Muktsar gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 484 mm against 424 mm.
  • Faisalabad gets about 106 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,978 h against 2,872 h; about 1961–1990).
