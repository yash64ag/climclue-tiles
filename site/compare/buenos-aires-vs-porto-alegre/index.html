Buenos Aires vs Porto Alegre climate

How the climate of Buenos Aires, Argentina compares with Porto Alegre, Brazil, 842 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Porto Alegre is 2.9 °C (5.1 °F) warmer over the year: 19.7 °C against 16.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Porto Alegre is 3.8 °C warmer (Buenos Aires 10.3 °C, Porto Alegre 14.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Buenos Aires 28.3 °C (January), Porto Alegre 29.6 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Buenos Aires 6.5 °C, Porto Alegre 9.7 °C.
  • Porto Alegre gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 1,419 mm against 1,189 mm.
  • Buenos Aires gets about 216 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,433 h against 2,217 h; about 1961–1990).
