Buenos Aires vs Porto Alegre climate

How the climate of Buenos Aires, Argentina compares with Porto Alegre, Brazil, 842 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Porto Alegre is 2.8 °C (5.0 °F) warmer over the year: 19.6 °C against 16.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Porto Alegre is 3.9 °C warmer (Buenos Aires 10.2 °C, Porto Alegre 14.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Buenos Aires 27.1 °C (January), Porto Alegre 29.3 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Buenos Aires 7.8 °C, Porto Alegre 10.3 °C.
  • Porto Alegre gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 1,428 mm against 1,142 mm.
  • Buenos Aires gets about 216 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,433 h against 2,217 h; about 1961–1990).

  • Drier: Buenos Aires in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Buenos Aires in 9 of 12 months
