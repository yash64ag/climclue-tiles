Dazhou vs Nanchong climate

How the climate of Dazhou, China compares with Nanchong, China, 139 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dazhou and Nanchong have the same yearly average temperature (18.3 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Dazhou is 0.4 °C warmer (Dazhou 28.4 °C, Nanchong 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dazhou 33.1 °C (August), Nanchong 32.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Dazhou 4.4 °C, Nanchong 5.1 °C.
  • Dazhou gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 1,286 mm against 1,119 mm.
  • Dazhou gets about 102 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,394 h against 1,292 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Dazhou 1.3 cm, Nanchong 0.1 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Nanchong in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Nanchong in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Dazhou in 12 of 12 months
