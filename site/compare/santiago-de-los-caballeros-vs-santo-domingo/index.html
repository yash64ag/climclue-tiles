Santiago de los Caballeros vs Santo Domingo climate

How the climate of Santiago de los Caballeros, Dominican Republic compares with Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, 137 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Santo Domingo is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 25.5 °C against 25.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Santo Domingo is 1.1 °C warmer (Santiago de los Caballeros 22.8 °C, Santo Domingo 24.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Santiago de los Caballeros 32.7 °C (August), Santo Domingo 30.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Santiago de los Caballeros 18.8 °C, Santo Domingo 21.0 °C.
  • Santo Domingo gets 36% more rain and snow a year: 1,688 mm against 1,242 mm.
  • Santo Domingo gets about 286 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,775 h against 2,489 h; about 1961–1990).
