How the climate of Daegu, South Korea compares with Hanzhong, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hanzhong is 1.3 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 15.3 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Hanzhong is 3.4 °C warmer (Daegu 2.8 °C, Hanzhong 6.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daegu 31.1 °C (August), Hanzhong 30.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Daegu -5.3 °C, Hanzhong -0.2 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (979 mm and 981 mm).
  • Daegu gets about 814 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,483 h against 1,669 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daegu 13 cm, Hanzhong 4.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
