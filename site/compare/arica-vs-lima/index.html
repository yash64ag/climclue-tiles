How the climate of Arica, Chile compares with Lima, Peru: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lima is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 19.0 °C against 18.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Lima is 1.8 °C warmer (Arica 14.8 °C, Lima 16.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Arica 28.4 °C (February), Lima 26.5 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Arica 11.2 °C, Lima 13.4 °C.
  • Lima gets 36% more rain and snow a year: 38 mm against 28 mm.
  • Arica gets about 1,208 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,713 h against 1,505 h; about 1961–1990).
