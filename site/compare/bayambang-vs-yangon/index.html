Bayambang vs Yangon climate

How the climate of Bayambang, Philippines compares with Yangon, Myanmar, 2,594 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bayambang is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 27.4 °C against 27.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Yangon is 1.8 °C warmer (Bayambang 29.3 °C, Yangon 31.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bayambang 34.1 °C (April), Yangon 37.0 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Bayambang 20.7 °C, Yangon 17.6 °C.
  • Yangon gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 2,796 mm against 2,424 mm.
  • Bayambang gets about 49 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,623 h against 2,574 h; about 1961–1990).
