How the climate of Bengaluru, India compares with Tegucigalpa, Honduras: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bengaluru is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 23.2 °C against 22.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Bengaluru is 2.4 °C warmer (Bengaluru 25.6 °C, Tegucigalpa 23.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bengaluru 33.2 °C (April), Tegucigalpa 31.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Bengaluru 14.6 °C, Tegucigalpa 15.2 °C.
  • Tegucigalpa gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 1,055 mm against 914 mm.
  • Bengaluru gets about 43 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,374 h against 2,332 h; about 1961–1990).
