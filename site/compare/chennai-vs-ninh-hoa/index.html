Chennai vs Ninh Hòa climate

How the climate of Chennai, India compares with Ninh Hòa, Vietnam, 3,127 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Chennai is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 27.9 °C against 27.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Chennai is 1.7 °C warmer (Chennai 30.7 °C, Ninh Hòa 29.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chennai 36.0 °C (May), Ninh Hòa 33.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Chennai 19.8 °C, Ninh Hòa 20.4 °C.
  • Ninh Hòa gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 1,644 mm against 1,373 mm.
  • Chennai gets about 655 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,675 h against 2,020 h; about 1961–1990).
