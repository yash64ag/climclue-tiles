How the climate of Agra, India compares with Faridabad, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Agra is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 25.2 °C against 24.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Agra is 0.7 °C warmer (Agra 23.4 °C, Faridabad 22.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Agra 40.9 °C (May), Faridabad 39.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Agra 6.7 °C, Faridabad 6.7 °C.
  • Agra gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 719 mm against 708 mm.
  • Agra gets about 25 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,707 h against 2,681 h; about 1961–1990).
