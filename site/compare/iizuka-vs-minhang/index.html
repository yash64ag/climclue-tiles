Iizuka vs Minhang climate

How the climate of Iizuka, Japan compares with Minhang, China, 918 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Iizuka is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 17.1 °C against 16.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Iizuka is 1.6 °C warmer (Iizuka 6.2 °C, Minhang 4.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iizuka 32.6 °C (August), Minhang 31.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Iizuka 2.1 °C, Minhang 1.1 °C.
  • Iizuka gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,669 mm against 1,467 mm.
  • Minhang gets about 200 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,959 h against 1,759 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Iizuka 14 cm, Minhang 8.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
