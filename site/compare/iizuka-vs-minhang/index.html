How the climate of Iizuka, Japan compares with Minhang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Iizuka is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 17.1 °C against 17.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Minhang is 1.4 °C warmer (Iizuka 27.4 °C, Minhang 28.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iizuka 32.6 °C (August), Minhang 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Iizuka 2.1 °C, Minhang 1.5 °C.
  • Iizuka gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,669 mm against 1,467 mm.
  • Minhang gets about 200 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,959 h against 1,759 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Iizuka 14 cm, Minhang 8.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
