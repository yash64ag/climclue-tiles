Manp’o vs Shenyang climate

How the climate of Manp’o, North Korea compares with Shenyang, China, 248 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shenyang is 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) warmer over the year: 8.9 °C against 7.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Shenyang is 2.6 °C warmer (Manp’o -9.9 °C, Shenyang -7.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manp’o 26.8 °C (July), Shenyang 28.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Manp’o -21.9 °C, Shenyang -17.6 °C.
  • Manp’o gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 849 mm against 717 mm.
  • Shenyang gets about 109 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,540 h against 2,430 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Manp’o 135 cm, Shenyang 56 cm (ERA5-Land).
