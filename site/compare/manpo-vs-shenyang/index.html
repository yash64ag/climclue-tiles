Manp’o vs Shenyang climate

How the climate of Manp’o, North Korea compares with Shenyang, China, 248 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shenyang is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 8.9 °C against 8.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Shenyang is 2.1 °C warmer (Manp’o -9.5 °C, Shenyang -7.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manp’o 28.8 °C (July), Shenyang 29.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Manp’o -19.2 °C, Shenyang -17.1 °C.
  • Manp’o gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 808 mm against 715 mm.
  • Shenyang gets about 109 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,540 h against 2,430 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Manp’o 61 cm, Shenyang 37 cm (ERA5-Land).
