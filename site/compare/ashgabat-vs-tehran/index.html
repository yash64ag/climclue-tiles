Ashgabat vs Tehran climate

How the climate of Ashgabat, Turkmenistan compares with Tehran, Iran, 668 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ashgabat is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 17.4 °C against 16.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Ashgabat is 3.0 °C warmer (Ashgabat 5.3 °C, Tehran 2.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ashgabat 38.9 °C (July), Tehran 37.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ashgabat -1.1 °C, Tehran -3.8 °C.
  • Tehran gets 45% more rain and snow a year: 397 mm against 274 mm.
  • Tehran gets about 163 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,870 h against 2,708 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ashgabat 13 cm, Tehran 33 cm (ERA5-Land).
