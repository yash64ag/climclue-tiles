Ashgabat vs Tehran climate

How the climate of Ashgabat, Turkmenistan compares with Tehran, Iran, 668 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ashgabat is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Ashgabat is 2.3 °C warmer (Ashgabat 3.4 °C, Tehran 1.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ashgabat 36.3 °C (July), Tehran 35.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ashgabat -0.9 °C, Tehran -4.5 °C.
  • Tehran gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 384 mm against 278 mm.
  • Tehran gets about 163 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,870 h against 2,708 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ashgabat 28 cm, Tehran 84 cm (ERA5-Land).
