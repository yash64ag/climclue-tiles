Iizuka vs Jiaxing climate

How the climate of Iizuka, Japan compares with Jiaxing, China, 988 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Iizuka is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 17.1 °C against 16.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Iizuka is 1.7 °C warmer (Iizuka 6.2 °C, Jiaxing 4.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iizuka 32.6 °C (August), Jiaxing 32.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Iizuka 2.1 °C, Jiaxing 0.8 °C.
  • Iizuka gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 1,669 mm against 1,455 mm.
  • Jiaxing gets about 163 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,922 h against 1,759 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Iizuka 14 cm, Jiaxing 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
