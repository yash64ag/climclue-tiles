How the climate of Pati, Indonesia compares with Zamboanga, Philippines: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zamboanga is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 27.9 °C against 27.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Zamboanga is 1.3 °C warmer (Pati 26.8 °C, Zamboanga 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pati 33.6 °C (October), Zamboanga 32.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Pati 20.7 °C, Zamboanga 23.1 °C.
  • Pati gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 2,148 mm against 1,695 mm.
  • Pati gets about 611 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,821 h against 2,211 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Zamboanga in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Pati in 8 of 12 months
