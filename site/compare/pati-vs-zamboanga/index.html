Pati vs Zamboanga climate

How the climate of Pati, Indonesia compares with Zamboanga, Philippines, 1,951 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Pati and Zamboanga have the same yearly average temperature (27.0 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Pati is 0.8 °C warmer (Pati 27.6 °C, Zamboanga 26.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pati 31.8 °C (September), Zamboanga 29.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Pati 22.8 °C, Zamboanga 25.5 °C.
  • Pati gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 2,148 mm against 1,695 mm.
  • Pati gets about 611 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,821 h against 2,211 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Zamboanga in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Pati in 8 of 12 months
