Brisbane vs Tubarão climate

How the climate of Brisbane, Australia compares with Tubarão, Brazil, 13,367 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tubarão is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 20.4 °C against 20.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Tubarão is 1.2 °C warmer (Brisbane 15.5 °C, Tubarão 16.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brisbane 28.3 °C (January), Tubarão 28.3 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Brisbane 10.2 °C, Tubarão 12.1 °C.
  • Tubarão gets 35% more rain and snow a year: 1,406 mm against 1,042 mm.
  • Brisbane gets about 727 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,833 h against 2,106 h; about 1961–1990).
