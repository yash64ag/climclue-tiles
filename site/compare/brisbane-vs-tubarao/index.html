Brisbane vs Tubarão climate

How the climate of Brisbane, Australia compares with Tubarão, Brazil, 13,367 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Brisbane and Tubarão have the same yearly average temperature (20.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Brisbane is 1.0 °C warmer (Brisbane 20.6 °C, Tubarão 19.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brisbane 28.1 °C (January), Tubarão 28.3 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Brisbane 10.6 °C, Tubarão 11.4 °C.
  • Tubarão gets 45% more rain and snow a year: 1,548 mm against 1,070 mm.
  • Brisbane gets about 727 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,833 h against 2,106 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Brisbane in 10 of 12 months
