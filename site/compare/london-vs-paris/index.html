London vs Paris climate

How the climate of London, United Kingdom compares with Paris, France, 344 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Paris is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 11.2 °C against 10.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Paris is 2.0 °C warmer (London 17.4 °C, Paris 19.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: London 21.2 °C (July), Paris 23.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: London 1.7 °C, Paris 1.2 °C.
  • London gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 681 mm against 662 mm.
  • Paris gets about 285 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,744 h against 1,459 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: London 5.3 cm, Paris 9.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
