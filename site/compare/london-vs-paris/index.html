How the climate of London, United Kingdom compares with Paris, France: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Paris is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 11.3 °C against 10.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Paris is 1.9 °C warmer (London 17.5 °C, Paris 19.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: London 22.1 °C (July), Paris 24.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: London 2.0 °C, Paris 1.6 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (661 mm and 658 mm).
  • Paris gets about 285 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,744 h against 1,459 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: London 5.3 cm, Paris 9.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
