Jaipur vs Jodhpur climate

How the climate of Jaipur, India compares with Jodhpur, India, 286 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jodhpur is 1.8 °C (3.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 24.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Jodhpur is 2.4 °C warmer (Jaipur 14.3 °C, Jodhpur 16.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jaipur 39.4 °C (May), Jodhpur 40.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Jaipur 8.0 °C, Jodhpur 9.9 °C.
  • Jaipur gets 70% more rain and snow a year: 628 mm against 370 mm.
  • Jodhpur gets about 261 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,092 h against 2,831 h; about 1961–1990).
