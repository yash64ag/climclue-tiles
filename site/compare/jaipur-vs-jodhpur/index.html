Jaipur vs Jodhpur, Rajasthan climate

How the climate of Jaipur, India compares with Jodhpur, Rajasthan, India, 286 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jodhpur, Rajasthan is 1.6 °C (2.9 °F) warmer over the year: 26.5 °C against 24.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Jodhpur, Rajasthan is 2.3 °C warmer (Jaipur 14.4 °C, Jodhpur, Rajasthan 16.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jaipur 40.5 °C (May), Jodhpur, Rajasthan 41.5 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Jaipur 6.9 °C, Jodhpur, Rajasthan 8.5 °C.
  • Jaipur gets 69% more rain and snow a year: 634 mm against 375 mm.
  • Jodhpur, Rajasthan gets about 261 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,092 h against 2,831 h; about 1961–1990).
