Liupanshui vs Tandil climate

How the climate of Liupanshui, China compares with Tandil, Argentina, 18,093 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tandil is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 13.4 °C against 12.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Tandil is 17.0 °C warmer (Liupanshui 3.5 °C, Tandil 20.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Liupanshui 22.7 °C (July), Tandil 26.6 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Liupanshui -0.5 °C, Tandil 3.5 °C.
  • Liupanshui gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 1,327 mm against 960 mm.
  • Tandil gets about 799 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,448 h against 1,649 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Liupanshui 14 cm, Tandil 0.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Tandil in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Tandil in 8 of 12 months
