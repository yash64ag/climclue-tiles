Berlin vs Warsaw climate

How the climate of Berlin, Germany compares with Warsaw, Poland, 517 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Berlin is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 9.4 °C against 8.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Berlin is 1.9 °C warmer (Berlin 0.3 °C, Warsaw -1.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Berlin 23.3 °C (July), Warsaw 23.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Berlin -2.0 °C, Warsaw -4.2 °C.
  • Berlin gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 603 mm against 560 mm.
  • Berlin gets about 26 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,614 h against 1,587 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Berlin 51 cm, Warsaw 61 cm (ERA5-Land).
