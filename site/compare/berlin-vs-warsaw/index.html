Berlin vs Warsaw climate

How the climate of Berlin, Germany compares with Warsaw, Poland, 517 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Berlin is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 9.5 °C against 8.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Berlin is 2.1 °C warmer (Berlin 0.3 °C, Warsaw -1.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Berlin 23.3 °C (July), Warsaw 23.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Berlin -2.0 °C, Warsaw -4.2 °C.
  • Berlin gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 594 mm against 582 mm.
  • Berlin gets about 26 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,614 h against 1,587 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Berlin 51 cm, Warsaw 61 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Berlin in 10 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Berlin in 9 of 12 months
