How the climate of Guadalajara, Mexico compares with Lilongwe, Malawi: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lilongwe is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 20.9 °C against 20.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Guadalajara is 6.5 °C warmer (Guadalajara 23.9 °C, Lilongwe 17.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guadalajara 33.2 °C (May), Lilongwe 30.6 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Guadalajara 8.1 °C, Lilongwe 9.5 °C.
  • Guadalajara gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 864 mm against 799 mm.
  • Lilongwe gets about 116 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,754 h against 2,638 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
