How the climate of Durban, South Africa compares with Gqeberha, South Africa: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Durban is 3.0 °C (5.5 °F) warmer over the year: 21.2 °C against 18.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Durban is 3.5 °C warmer (Durban 23.9 °C, Gqeberha 20.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Durban 28.4 °C (February), Gqeberha 25.8 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Durban 12.0 °C, Gqeberha 9.3 °C.
  • Durban gets 64% more rain and snow a year: 914 mm against 558 mm.
  • Gqeberha gets about 226 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,739 h against 2,513 h; about 1961–1990).
