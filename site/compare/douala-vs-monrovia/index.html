How the climate of Douala, Cameroon compares with Monrovia, Liberia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Douala is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 25.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Douala is 0.6 °C warmer (Douala 27.2 °C, Monrovia 26.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Douala 31.6 °C (February), Monrovia 32.1 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Douala 21.9 °C, Monrovia 21.1 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (3,194 mm and 3,199 mm).
  • Monrovia gets about 63 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,767 h against 1,704 h; about 1961–1990).
