How the climate of Haiphong, Vietnam compares with Zhanjiang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Haiphong is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 24.0 °C against 23.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Haiphong is 0.7 °C warmer (Haiphong 29.3 °C, Zhanjiang 28.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Haiphong 32.9 °C (July), Zhanjiang 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Haiphong 13.8 °C, Zhanjiang 13.3 °C.
  • Zhanjiang gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,982 mm against 1,869 mm.
  • Zhanjiang gets about 197 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,920 h against 1,723 h; about 1961–1990).
