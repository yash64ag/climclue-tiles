How the climate of Lu’an, China compares with Tulsa, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lu’an is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.0 °C against 15.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Lu’an is 1.0 °C warmer (Lu’an 21.5 °C, Tulsa 20.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lu’an 32.2 °C (July), Tulsa 34.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Lu’an -1.5 °C, Tulsa -2.9 °C.
  • Lu’an gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 1,301 mm against 1,057 mm.
  • Tulsa gets about 721 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,758 h against 2,037 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lu’an 29 cm, Tulsa 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
