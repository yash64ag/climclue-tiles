Lu’an vs Tulsa climate

How the climate of Lu’an, China compares with Tulsa, United States, 11,741 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lu’an is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 15.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Lu’an is 0.9 °C warmer (Lu’an 21.6 °C, Tulsa 20.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lu’an 31.7 °C (July), Tulsa 33.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lu’an -1.8 °C, Tulsa -0.6 °C.
  • Lu’an gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 1,288 mm against 1,078 mm.
  • Tulsa gets about 721 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,758 h against 2,037 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lu’an 32 cm, Tulsa 32 cm (ERA5-Land).
