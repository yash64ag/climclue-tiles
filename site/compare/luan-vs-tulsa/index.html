Lu’an vs Tulsa climate

How the climate of Lu’an, China compares with Tulsa, United States, 11,741 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lu’an is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.0 °C against 15.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Tulsa is 0.8 °C warmer (Lu’an 9.8 °C, Tulsa 10.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lu’an 32.2 °C (July), Tulsa 34.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lu’an -1.5 °C, Tulsa -3.0 °C.
  • Lu’an gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 1,301 mm against 1,057 mm.
  • Tulsa gets about 721 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,758 h against 2,037 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lu’an 29 cm, Tulsa 20 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Sunnier: Tulsa in 12 of 12 months
