Bogotá vs Cuenca climate

How the climate of Bogotá, Colombia compares with Cuenca, Ecuador, 997 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bogotá and Cuenca have the same yearly average temperature (13.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Bogotá is 1.0 °C warmer (Bogotá 13.1 °C, Cuenca 12.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bogotá 18.6 °C (September), Cuenca 17.4 °C (November). Coldest-month night lows: Bogotá 8.8 °C, Cuenca 8.3 °C.
  • Bogotá gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 1,046 mm against 875 mm.
  • Cuenca gets about 170 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,702 h against 1,532 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
