How the climate of Bogotá, Colombia compares with Cuenca, Ecuador: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bogotá is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 14.0 °C against 13.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Bogotá is 1.7 °C warmer (Bogotá 14.1 °C, Cuenca 12.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bogotá 20.6 °C (February), Cuenca 21.4 °C (November). Coldest-month night lows: Bogotá 7.4 °C, Cuenca 5.9 °C.
  • Bogotá gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 917 mm against 798 mm.
  • Cuenca gets about 170 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,702 h against 1,532 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
