León de los Aldama vs Zomba climate

How the climate of León de los Aldama, Mexico compares with Zomba, Malawi, 15,443 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zomba is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 20.6 °C against 19.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Zomba is 6.9 °C warmer (León de los Aldama 15.9 °C, Zomba 22.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: León de los Aldama 32.2 °C (May), Zomba 29.0 °C (November). Coldest-month night lows: León de los Aldama 6.8 °C, Zomba 11.1 °C.
  • Zomba gets 77% more rain and snow a year: 1,117 mm against 631 mm.
  • Zomba gets about 360 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,642 h against 2,282 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
