Davao vs Malingao climate

How the climate of Davao, Philippines compares with Malingao, Philippines, 126 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Malingao is 1.0 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 28.3 °C against 27.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Malingao is 2.2 °C warmer (Davao 27.1 °C, Malingao 29.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Davao 32.7 °C (April), Malingao 35.0 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Davao 22.0 °C, Malingao 23.1 °C.
  • Davao gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 1,741 mm against 1,453 mm.
  • Malingao gets about 15 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,303 h against 2,288 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Malingao in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Malingao in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Malingao in 8 of 12 months
