Kawanishi vs Pudong climate

How the climate of Kawanishi, Japan compares with Pudong, China, 1,356 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Pudong is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Pudong is 0.9 °C warmer (Kawanishi 27.1 °C, Pudong 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kawanishi 31.1 °C (August), Pudong 30.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kawanishi 0.8 °C, Pudong 1.1 °C.
  • Kawanishi gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,445 mm against 1,413 mm.
  • Pudong gets about 200 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,973 h against 1,773 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kawanishi 15 cm, Pudong 6.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
