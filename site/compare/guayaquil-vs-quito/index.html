Guayaquil vs Quito climate

How the climate of Guayaquil, Ecuador compares with Quito, Ecuador, 266 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Guayaquil is 13.4 °C (24.0 °F) warmer over the year: 24.6 °C against 11.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Guayaquil is 14.8 °C warmer (Guayaquil 25.9 °C, Quito 11.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guayaquil 28.6 °C (March), Quito 16.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Guayaquil 20.5 °C, Quito 7.8 °C.
  • Quito gets 72% more rain and snow a year: 2,262 mm against 1,319 mm.
  • Quito gets about 791 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,875 h against 1,084 h; about 1961–1990).
