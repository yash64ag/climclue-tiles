Chennai vs Tiruchirappalli climate

How the climate of Chennai, India compares with Tiruchirappalli, India, 306 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tiruchirappalli is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 29.1 °C against 27.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Tiruchirappalli is 2.3 °C warmer (Chennai 27.4 °C, Tiruchirappalli 29.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chennai 34.2 °C (May), Tiruchirappalli 38.6 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Chennai 22.0 °C, Tiruchirappalli 19.9 °C.
  • Chennai gets 68% more rain and snow a year: 1,373 mm against 819 mm.
  • Chennai gets about 182 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,675 h against 2,493 h; about 1961–1990).
