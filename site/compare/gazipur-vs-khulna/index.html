Gazipur vs Khulna climate

How the climate of Gazipur, Bangladesh compares with Khulna, Bangladesh, 158 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Khulna is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 25.9 °C against 25.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Khulna is 1.1 °C warmer (Gazipur 27.9 °C, Khulna 28.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gazipur 32.6 °C (April), Khulna 34.1 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Gazipur 12.3 °C, Khulna 13.2 °C.
  • Gazipur gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 2,184 mm against 1,886 mm.
  • Gazipur gets about 16 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,106 h against 2,090 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Khulna in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Khulna in 7 of 12 months
