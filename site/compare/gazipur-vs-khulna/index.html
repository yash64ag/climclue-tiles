How the climate of Gazipur, Bangladesh compares with Khulna, Bangladesh: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Khulna is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 25.9 °C against 25.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Khulna is 1.2 °C warmer (Gazipur 27.8 °C, Khulna 29.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gazipur 33.1 °C (April), Khulna 34.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Gazipur 11.0 °C, Khulna 11.6 °C.
  • Gazipur gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 2,188 mm against 1,889 mm.
  • Gazipur gets about 16 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,106 h against 2,090 h; about 1961–1990).
