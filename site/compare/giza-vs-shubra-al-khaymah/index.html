How the climate of Giza, Egypt compares with Shubrā al Khaymah, Egypt: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Giza is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 22.1 °C against 21.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Giza is 0.4 °C warmer (Giza 25.2 °C, Shubrā al Khaymah 24.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Giza 35.7 °C (July), Shubrā al Khaymah 35.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Giza 8.3 °C, Shubrā al Khaymah 8.2 °C.
  • Shubrā al Khaymah gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 20 mm against 18 mm.
  • Giza gets about 1 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,330 h against 3,329 h; about 1961–1990).
