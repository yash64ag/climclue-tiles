How the climate of Kawasaki, Kanagawa, Japan compares with Yokohama, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kawasaki, Kanagawa and Yokohama have the same yearly average temperature (15.9 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Yokohama is 0.4 °C warmer (Kawasaki, Kanagawa 6.0 °C, Yokohama 6.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kawasaki, Kanagawa 29.9 °C (August), Yokohama 29.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kawasaki, Kanagawa 1.6 °C, Yokohama 2.0 °C.
  • Yokohama gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,530 mm against 1,489 mm.
  • Yokohama gets about 2 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,827 h against 1,826 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kawasaki, Kanagawa 13 cm, Yokohama 11 cm (ERA5-Land).
