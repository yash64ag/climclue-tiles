How the climate of Benin City, Nigeria compares with Ibadan, Nigeria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ibadan is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 25.8 °C against 25.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Ibadan is 0.6 °C warmer (Benin City 27.0 °C, Ibadan 27.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Benin City 32.7 °C (February), Ibadan 33.6 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Benin City 20.9 °C, Ibadan 20.7 °C.
  • Benin City gets 63% more rain and snow a year: 2,278 mm against 1,397 mm.
  • Ibadan gets about 176 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,931 h against 1,755 h; about 1961–1990).
