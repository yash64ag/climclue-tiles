How the climate of Tuscaloosa, United States compares with Yichun, Jiangxi, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yichun, Jiangxi is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 18.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Tuscaloosa is 1.9 °C warmer (Tuscaloosa 13.8 °C, Yichun, Jiangxi 12.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Tuscaloosa 33.7 °C (July), Yichun, Jiangxi 34.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Tuscaloosa 1.4 °C, Yichun, Jiangxi 2.3 °C.
  • Yichun, Jiangxi gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 1,797 mm against 1,354 mm.
  • Tuscaloosa gets about 776 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,496 h against 1,721 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Tuscaloosa 2.0 cm, Yichun, Jiangxi 7.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
