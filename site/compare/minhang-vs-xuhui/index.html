Minhang vs Xuhui climate

How the climate of Minhang, China compares with Xuhui, China, 12 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Minhang is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.7 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Minhang is 0.2 °C warmer (Minhang 20.4 °C, Xuhui 20.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Minhang 31.8 °C (July), Xuhui 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Minhang 1.1 °C, Xuhui 1.1 °C.
  • Xuhui gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,499 mm against 1,467 mm.
  • Xuhui gets about 1 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,960 h against 1,959 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Minhang 8.6 cm, Xuhui 7.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
