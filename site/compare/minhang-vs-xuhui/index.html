How the climate of Minhang, China compares with Xuhui, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Minhang and Xuhui have the same yearly average temperature (17.0 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Xuhui is 0.2 °C warmer (Minhang 7.3 °C, Xuhui 7.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Minhang 32.3 °C (July), Xuhui 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Minhang 1.5 °C, Xuhui 1.6 °C.
  • Xuhui gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,499 mm against 1,467 mm.
  • Xuhui gets about 1 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,960 h against 1,959 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Minhang 8.6 cm, Xuhui 7.5 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Sunnier: Xuhui in 4 of 12 months (7 equal)
