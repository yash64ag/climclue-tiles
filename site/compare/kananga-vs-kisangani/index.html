Kananga vs Kisangani climate

How the climate of Kananga, Democratic Republic of the Congo compares with Kisangani, Democratic Republic of the Congo, 777 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kisangani is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 24.7 °C against 24.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Kisangani is 1.6 °C warmer (Kananga 24.2 °C, Kisangani 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kananga 33.2 °C (July), Kisangani 31.9 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Kananga 15.9 °C, Kisangani 18.8 °C.
  • Kisangani gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,649 mm against 1,542 mm.
  • Kananga gets about 134 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,105 h against 1,971 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
