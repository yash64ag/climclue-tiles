Kananga vs Kisangani climate

How the climate of Kananga, Democratic Republic of the Congo compares with Kisangani, Democratic Republic of the Congo, 777 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kisangani is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 24.7 °C against 24.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Kisangani is 1.4 °C warmer (Kananga 24.4 °C, Kisangani 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kananga 31.8 °C (July), Kisangani 30.9 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Kananga 19.4 °C, Kisangani 21.6 °C.
  • Kisangani gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,658 mm against 1,484 mm.
  • Kananga gets about 134 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,105 h against 1,971 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
