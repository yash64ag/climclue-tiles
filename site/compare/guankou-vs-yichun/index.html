Guankou vs Yichun, Jiangxi climate

How the climate of Guankou, China compares with Yichun, Jiangxi, China, 84 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Guankou and Yichun, Jiangxi have the same yearly average temperature (18.3 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Guankou is 0.2 °C warmer (Guankou 8.3 °C, Yichun, Jiangxi 8.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guankou 34.2 °C (July), Yichun, Jiangxi 34.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Guankou 2.3 °C, Yichun, Jiangxi 2.3 °C.
  • Yichun, Jiangxi gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,797 mm against 1,774 mm.
  • Yichun, Jiangxi gets about 69 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,721 h against 1,652 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Guankou 6.8 cm, Yichun, Jiangxi 7.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
