Guankou vs Yichun, Jiangxi climate

How the climate of Guankou, China compares with Yichun, Jiangxi, China, 84 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Yichun, Jiangxi is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 18.2 °C against 18.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Yichun, Jiangxi is 0.3 °C warmer (Guankou 19.4 °C, Yichun, Jiangxi 19.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guankou 32.9 °C (July), Yichun, Jiangxi 33.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Guankou 1.8 °C, Yichun, Jiangxi 2.3 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,785 mm and 1,777 mm).
  • Yichun, Jiangxi gets about 69 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,721 h against 1,652 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Guankou 14 cm, Yichun, Jiangxi 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
