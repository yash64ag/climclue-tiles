Chiquimula vs Pune climate

How the climate of Chiquimula, Guatemala compares with Pune, India, 15,888 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Chiquimula and Pune have the same yearly average temperature (24.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Pune is 1.7 °C warmer (Chiquimula 26.6 °C, Pune 28.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chiquimula 32.2 °C (April), Pune 36.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Chiquimula 18.4 °C, Pune 15.2 °C.
  • Pune gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 999 mm against 882 mm.
  • Pune gets about 13 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,793 h against 2,780 h; about 1961–1990).
