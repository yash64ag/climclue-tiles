How the climate of Rio Grande, Brazil compares with Sydney, Australia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Rio Grande is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 18.4 °C against 17.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Rio Grande is 1.7 °C warmer (Rio Grande 20.3 °C, Sydney 18.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rio Grande 27.1 °C (February), Sydney 26.9 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Rio Grande 10.0 °C, Sydney 7.4 °C.
  • Rio Grande gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,247 mm against 1,097 mm.
  • Sydney gets about 86 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,488 h against 2,402 h; about 1961–1990).
