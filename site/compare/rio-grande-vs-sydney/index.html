Rio Grande vs Sydney climate

How the climate of Rio Grande, Brazil compares with Sydney, Australia, 12,291 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rio Grande is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 18.4 °C against 17.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Rio Grande is 1.8 °C warmer (Rio Grande 22.8 °C, Sydney 20.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rio Grande 24.5 °C (February), Sydney 23.9 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Rio Grande 12.1 °C, Sydney 10.4 °C.
  • Rio Grande gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,247 mm against 1,097 mm.
  • Sydney gets about 86 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,488 h against 2,402 h; about 1961–1990).
