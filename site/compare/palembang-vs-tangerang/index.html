Palembang vs Tangerang climate

How the climate of Palembang, Indonesia compares with Tangerang, Indonesia, 418 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tangerang is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Tangerang is 0.5 °C warmer (Palembang 26.6 °C, Tangerang 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Palembang 31.6 °C (September), Tangerang 31.4 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Palembang 22.9 °C, Tangerang 22.7 °C.
  • Palembang gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 3,044 mm against 2,445 mm.
  • Tangerang gets about 201 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,623 h against 2,422 h; about 1961–1990).
