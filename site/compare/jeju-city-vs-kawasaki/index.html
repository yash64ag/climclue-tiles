Jeju City vs Kawasaki, Kanagawa climate

How the climate of Jeju City, South Korea compares with Kawasaki, Kanagawa, Japan, 1,228 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kawasaki, Kanagawa is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Kawasaki, Kanagawa is 1.4 °C warmer (Jeju City 16.8 °C, Kawasaki, Kanagawa 18.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jeju City 29.4 °C (August), Kawasaki, Kanagawa 29.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Jeju City 2.9 °C, Kawasaki, Kanagawa 1.6 °C.
  • Jeju City gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 1,761 mm against 1,489 mm.
  • Jeju City gets about 279 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,105 h against 1,826 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jeju City 12 cm, Kawasaki, Kanagawa 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
