Jeju City vs Kawasaki climate

How the climate of Jeju City, South Korea compares with Kawasaki, Japan, 1,228 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kawasaki is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 15.8 °C against 15.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Kawasaki is 1.5 °C warmer (Jeju City 16.8 °C, Kawasaki 18.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jeju City 27.4 °C (August), Kawasaki 28.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Jeju City 4.3 °C, Kawasaki 2.0 °C.
  • Jeju City gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 1,782 mm against 1,508 mm.
  • Jeju City gets about 279 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,105 h against 1,826 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jeju City 20 cm, Kawasaki 24 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Kawasaki in 6 of 12 months (2 equal)
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Jeju City in 9 of 12 months
