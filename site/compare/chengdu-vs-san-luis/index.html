How the climate of Chengdu, China compares with San Luis, Argentina: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • San Luis is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 17.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when San Luis is 17.8 °C warmer (Chengdu 6.3 °C, San Luis 24.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chengdu 29.8 °C (July), San Luis 31.2 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Chengdu 2.9 °C, San Luis 2.7 °C.
  • San Luis gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,110 mm against 995 mm.
  • San Luis gets about 1,725 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,021 h against 1,296 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
