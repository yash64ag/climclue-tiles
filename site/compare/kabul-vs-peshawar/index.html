Kabul vs Peshawar climate

How the climate of Kabul, Afghanistan compares with Peshawar, Pakistan, 229 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Peshawar is 11.1 °C (20.0 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 11.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Peshawar is 14.4 °C warmer (Kabul -0.8 °C, Peshawar 13.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kabul 31.4 °C (July), Peshawar 41.4 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Kabul -8.4 °C, Peshawar 4.1 °C.
  • Peshawar gets 68% more rain and snow a year: 583 mm against 346 mm.
  • Kabul gets about 183 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,031 h against 2,849 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kabul 128 cm, Peshawar 0 cm (ERA5-Land).
