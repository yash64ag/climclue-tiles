Main differences

  • Heshan, Hunan is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 17.9 °C against 17.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Heshan, Hunan is 1.8 °C warmer (Heshan, Hunan 13.3 °C, Little Rock 11.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Heshan, Hunan 33.2 °C (July), Little Rock 33.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Heshan, Hunan 2.5 °C, Little Rock -0.1 °C.
  • Heshan, Hunan gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 1,590 mm against 1,342 mm.
  • Little Rock gets about 1,439 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,027 h against 1,588 h; about 1961–1990).
