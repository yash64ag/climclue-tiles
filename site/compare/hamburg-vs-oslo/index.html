Hamburg vs Oslo climate

How the climate of Hamburg, Germany compares with Oslo, Norway, 709 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Hamburg is 2.5 °C (4.5 °F) warmer over the year: 9.2 °C against 6.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Hamburg is 5.3 °C warmer (Hamburg 1.3 °C, Oslo -4.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hamburg 22.4 °C (July), Oslo 22.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hamburg -1.1 °C, Oslo -7.1 °C.
  • Oslo gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 922 mm against 813 mm.
  • Oslo gets about 44 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,579 h against 1,535 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hamburg 40 cm, Oslo 246 cm (ERA5-Land).
