How the climate of Hamburg, Germany compares with Oslo, Norway: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hamburg is 2.3 °C (4.2 °F) warmer over the year: 9.5 °C against 7.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Hamburg is 4.1 °C warmer (Hamburg 2.0 °C, Oslo -2.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hamburg 22.9 °C (July), Oslo 22.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hamburg -0.8 °C, Oslo -5.0 °C.
  • Oslo gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 922 mm against 813 mm.
  • Oslo gets about 44 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,579 h against 1,535 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hamburg 40 cm, Oslo 246 cm (ERA5-Land).
