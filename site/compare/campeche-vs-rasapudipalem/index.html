How the climate of Campeche, Mexico compares with Rasapūdipalem, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Rasapūdipalem is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 26.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Rasapūdipalem is 2.3 °C warmer (Campeche 25.2 °C, Rasapūdipalem 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Campeche 35.1 °C (May), Rasapūdipalem 34.6 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Campeche 16.4 °C, Rasapūdipalem 18.0 °C.
  • Campeche gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 1,362 mm against 1,139 mm.
  • Rasapūdipalem gets about 255 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,552 h against 2,297 h; about 1961–1990).
