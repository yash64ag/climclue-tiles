Main differences

  • Changsha, Hunan is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 17.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Changsha, Hunan is 0.6 °C warmer (Changsha, Hunan 24.9 °C, Heshan, Hunan 24.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changsha, Hunan 33.9 °C (July), Heshan, Hunan 33.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changsha, Hunan 2.9 °C, Heshan, Hunan 2.5 °C.
  • Changsha, Hunan gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,689 mm against 1,590 mm.
  • Changsha, Hunan gets about 1 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,589 h against 1,588 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changsha, Hunan 9.1 cm, Heshan, Hunan 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
