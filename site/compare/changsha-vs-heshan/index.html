Main differences

  • Changsha, Hunan is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 17.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Changsha, Hunan is 0.7 °C warmer (Changsha, Hunan 19.4 °C, Heshan, Hunan 18.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changsha, Hunan 33.8 °C (July), Heshan, Hunan 33.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changsha, Hunan 2.8 °C, Heshan, Hunan 2.5 °C.
  • Changsha, Hunan gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,689 mm against 1,590 mm.
  • Changsha, Hunan gets about 1 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,589 h against 1,588 h; about 1961–1990).
