How the climate of Hiroshima, Japan compares with Osaka, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Osaka is 0.4 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 16.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Osaka is 0.9 °C warmer (Hiroshima 27.9 °C, Osaka 28.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hiroshima 31.8 °C (August), Osaka 33.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Hiroshima 2.0 °C, Osaka 2.0 °C.
  • Hiroshima gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,546 mm against 1,418 mm.
  • Hiroshima gets about 43 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,896 h against 1,852 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hiroshima 14 cm, Osaka 4.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
