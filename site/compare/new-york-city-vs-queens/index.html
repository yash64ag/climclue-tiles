How the climate of New York City, United States compares with Queens, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • New York City and Queens have the same yearly average temperature (12.7 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when New York City is 0.3 °C warmer (New York City 16.8 °C, Queens 16.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: New York City 29.8 °C (July), Queens 29.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: New York City -3.6 °C, Queens -2.8 °C.
  • Queens gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,199 mm against 1,193 mm.
  • Queens gets about 24 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,523 h against 2,499 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: New York City 57 cm, Queens 55 cm (ERA5-Land).
