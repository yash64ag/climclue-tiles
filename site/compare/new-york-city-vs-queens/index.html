New York City vs Queens climate

How the climate of New York City, United States compares with Queens, United States, 15 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • New York City and Queens have the same yearly average temperature (12.3 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when New York City is 0.5 °C warmer (New York City 16.4 °C, Queens 15.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: New York City 29.6 °C (July), Queens 28.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: New York City -4.2 °C, Queens -3.3 °C.
  • Queens gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,199 mm against 1,193 mm.
  • Queens gets about 24 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,523 h against 2,499 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: New York City 80 cm, Queens 73 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: New York City in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Queens in 11 of 12 months
