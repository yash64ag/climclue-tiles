How the climate of Lomé, Togo compares with Medina Estates, Ghana: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lomé is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Lomé is 0.3 °C warmer (Lomé 26.1 °C, Medina Estates 25.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lomé 32.0 °C (March), Medina Estates 31.7 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Lomé 21.5 °C, Medina Estates 21.7 °C.
  • Medina Estates gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 887 mm against 853 mm.
  • Lomé gets about 74 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,330 h against 2,256 h; about 1961–1990).
