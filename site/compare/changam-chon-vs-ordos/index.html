Changam-ch’on vs Ordos climate

How the climate of Changam-ch’on, North Korea compares with Ordos, China, 1,617 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Changam-ch’on is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 8.5 °C against 7.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Changam-ch’on is 3.7 °C warmer (Changam-ch’on 2.8 °C, Ordos -1.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changam-ch’on 26.5 °C (August), Ordos 28.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changam-ch’on -12.4 °C, Ordos -15.8 °C.
  • Changam-ch’on gets 68% more rain and snow a year: 553 mm against 330 mm.
  • Ordos gets about 651 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,907 h against 2,256 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changam-ch’on 62 cm, Ordos 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
