Mogadishu vs Nairobi climate

How the climate of Mogadishu, Somalia compares with Nairobi, Kenya, 1,017 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mogadishu is 7.5 °C (13.5 °F) warmer over the year: 26.3 °C against 18.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Mogadishu is 8.4 °C warmer (Mogadishu 27.1 °C, Nairobi 18.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mogadishu 30.1 °C (April), Nairobi 26.3 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Mogadishu 23.3 °C, Nairobi 12.0 °C.
  • Nairobi gets 55% more rain and snow a year: 850 mm against 548 mm.
  • Mogadishu gets about 634 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,098 h against 2,464 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
