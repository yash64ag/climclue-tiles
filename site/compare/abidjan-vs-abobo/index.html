How the climate of Abidjan, Ivory Coast compares with Abobo, Ivory Coast: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Abidjan is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 25.8 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Abidjan is 0.5 °C warmer (Abidjan 26.2 °C, Abobo 25.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abidjan 30.9 °C (March), Abobo 30.6 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Abidjan 21.3 °C, Abobo 21.2 °C.
  • Abobo gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 2,100 mm against 1,884 mm.
  • Abidjan gets about 5 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,118 h against 2,113 h; about 1961–1990).
