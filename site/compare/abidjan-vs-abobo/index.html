Abidjan vs Abobo climate

How the climate of Abidjan, Ivory Coast compares with Abobo, Ivory Coast, 7 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Abidjan is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 25.8 °C against 25.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Abidjan is 0.5 °C warmer (Abidjan 26.1 °C, Abobo 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abidjan 29.2 °C (March), Abobo 30.3 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Abidjan 22.7 °C, Abobo 21.9 °C.
  • Abobo gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 2,046 mm against 1,908 mm.
  • Abidjan gets about 5 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,118 h against 2,113 h; about 1961–1990).
