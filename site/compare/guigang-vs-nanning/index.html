Guigang vs Nanning climate

How the climate of Guigang, China compares with Nanning, China, 135 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Nanning is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 22.3 °C against 21.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Nanning is 0.9 °C warmer (Guigang 22.1 °C, Nanning 23.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guigang 30.8 °C (August), Nanning 31.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Guigang 8.7 °C, Nanning 9.6 °C.
  • Guigang gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 1,960 mm against 1,479 mm.
  • Guigang gets about 24 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,708 h against 1,684 h; about 1961–1990).
