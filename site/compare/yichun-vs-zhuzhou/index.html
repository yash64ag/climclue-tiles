Main differences

  • Zhuzhou is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 18.6 °C against 18.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Zhuzhou is 0.7 °C warmer (Yichun, Jiangxi 28.7 °C, Zhuzhou 29.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Yichun, Jiangxi 34.4 °C (July), Zhuzhou 34.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Yichun, Jiangxi 2.3 °C, Zhuzhou 2.9 °C.
  • Yichun, Jiangxi gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,797 mm against 1,637 mm.
  • Yichun, Jiangxi gets about 115 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,721 h against 1,605 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Yichun, Jiangxi 7.1 cm, Zhuzhou 6.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
