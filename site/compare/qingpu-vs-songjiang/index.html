How the climate of Qingpu, China compares with Songjiang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Songjiang is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 16.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Songjiang is 0.2 °C warmer (Qingpu 12.9 °C, Songjiang 13.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qingpu 32.3 °C (July), Songjiang 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Qingpu 0.8 °C, Songjiang 1.0 °C.
  • Songjiang gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,427 mm against 1,403 mm.
  • Qingpu gets about 6 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,958 h against 1,952 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Qingpu 10 cm, Songjiang 9.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
