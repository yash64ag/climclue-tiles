How the climate of Jaipur, India compares with Kot Radha Kishan, Pakistan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jaipur is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 24.9 °C against 24.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Kot Radha Kishan is 3.1 °C warmer (Jaipur 28.0 °C, Kot Radha Kishan 31.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jaipur 40.5 °C (May), Kot Radha Kishan 40.3 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Jaipur 6.9 °C, Kot Radha Kishan 5.2 °C.
  • Jaipur gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 634 mm against 539 mm.
  • Kot Radha Kishan gets about 102 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,933 h against 2,831 h; about 1961–1990).
