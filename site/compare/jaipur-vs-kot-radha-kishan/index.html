Jaipur vs Kot Radha Kishan climate

How the climate of Jaipur, India compares with Kot Radha Kishan, Pakistan, 500 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jaipur is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 24.8 °C against 24.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Kot Radha Kishan is 3.0 °C warmer (Jaipur 28.1 °C, Kot Radha Kishan 31.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jaipur 39.4 °C (May), Kot Radha Kishan 39.5 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Jaipur 8.0 °C, Kot Radha Kishan 6.4 °C.
  • Jaipur gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 628 mm against 542 mm.
  • Kot Radha Kishan gets about 102 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,933 h against 2,831 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Jaipur in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: Jaipur in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
