Incheon vs Pyongyang climate

How the climate of Incheon, South Korea compares with Pyongyang, North Korea, 194 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Incheon is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 12.0 °C against 11.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Incheon is 3.2 °C warmer (Incheon -2.3 °C, Pyongyang -5.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Incheon 27.3 °C (August), Pyongyang 28.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Incheon -6.8 °C, Pyongyang -11.5 °C.
  • Incheon gets 50% more rain and snow a year: 1,411 mm against 938 mm.
  • Pyongyang gets about 73 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,427 h against 2,354 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Incheon 30 cm, Pyongyang 45 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Drier: Pyongyang in 12 of 12 months
