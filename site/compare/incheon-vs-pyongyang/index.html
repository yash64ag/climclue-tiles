How the climate of Incheon, South Korea compares with Pyongyang, North Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Incheon is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 12.3 °C against 11.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Incheon is 3.1 °C warmer (Incheon -2.4 °C, Pyongyang -5.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Incheon 29.0 °C (August), Pyongyang 29.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Incheon -6.1 °C, Pyongyang -10.7 °C.
  • Incheon gets 51% more rain and snow a year: 1,414 mm against 936 mm.
  • Pyongyang gets about 73 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,427 h against 2,354 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Incheon 24 cm, Pyongyang 26 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Incheon in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: Pyongyang in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Pyongyang in 12 of 12 months
