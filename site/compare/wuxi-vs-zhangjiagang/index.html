Wuxi vs Zhangjiagang climate

How the climate of Wuxi, China compares with Zhangjiagang, China, 41 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Wuxi is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 16.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Wuxi is 0.6 °C warmer (Wuxi 21.1 °C, Zhangjiagang 20.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Wuxi 31.2 °C (July), Zhangjiagang 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Wuxi 0.1 °C, Zhangjiagang -0.8 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,183 mm and 1,183 mm).
  • Zhangjiagang gets about 27 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,026 h against 1,999 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Wuxi 16 cm, Zhangjiagang 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
