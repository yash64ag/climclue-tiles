Abobo vs Kumasi climate

How the climate of Abobo, Ivory Coast compares with Kumasi, Ghana, 300 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kumasi is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Kumasi is 1.5 °C warmer (Abobo 26.6 °C, Kumasi 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abobo 30.6 °C (March), Kumasi 33.9 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Abobo 21.2 °C, Kumasi 20.9 °C.
  • Abobo gets 54% more rain and snow a year: 2,100 mm against 1,364 mm.
  • Abobo gets about 175 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,113 h against 1,937 h; about 1961–1990).
