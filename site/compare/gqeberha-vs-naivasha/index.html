Gqeberha vs Naivasha climate

How the climate of Gqeberha, South Africa compares with Naivasha, Kenya, 3,865 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gqeberha is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 18.2 °C against 17.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Gqeberha is 2.7 °C warmer (Gqeberha 21.1 °C, Naivasha 18.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gqeberha 25.8 °C (February), Naivasha 27.8 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Gqeberha 9.3 °C, Naivasha 9.5 °C.
  • Naivasha gets 67% more rain and snow a year: 930 mm against 558 mm.
  • Gqeberha gets about 764 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,739 h against 1,975 h; about 1961–1990).
