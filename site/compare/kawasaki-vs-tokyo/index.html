How the climate of Kawasaki, Kanagawa, Japan compares with Tokyo, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kawasaki, Kanagawa is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.0 °C against 15.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Kawasaki, Kanagawa is 1.2 °C warmer (Kawasaki, Kanagawa 8.4 °C, Tokyo 7.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kawasaki, Kanagawa 30.5 °C (August), Tokyo 30.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kawasaki, Kanagawa 1.6 °C, Tokyo -0.2 °C.
  • Tokyo gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,516 mm against 1,489 mm.
  • Tokyo gets about 12 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,837 h against 1,826 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kawasaki, Kanagawa 13 cm, Tokyo 19 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Kawasaki, Kanagawa in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Tokyo in 8 of 12 months
