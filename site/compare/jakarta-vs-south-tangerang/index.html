Jakarta vs South Tangerang climate

How the climate of Jakarta, Indonesia compares with South Tangerang, Indonesia, 16 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jakarta and South Tangerang have the same yearly average temperature (26.8 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Jakarta is 0.1 °C warmer (Jakarta 27.1 °C, South Tangerang 26.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jakarta 31.5 °C (September), South Tangerang 31.5 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Jakarta 22.5 °C, South Tangerang 22.4 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (2,701 mm and 2,702 mm).
  • Jakarta gets about 6 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,648 h against 2,641 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Jakarta in 2 of 12 months (9 equal)
  • Drier: South Tangerang in 6 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Sunnier: Jakarta in 9 of 12 months
