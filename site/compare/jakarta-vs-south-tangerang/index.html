Jakarta vs South Tangerang climate

How the climate of Jakarta, Indonesia compares with South Tangerang, Indonesia, 16 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jakarta is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Jakarta is 0.3 °C warmer (Jakarta 27.1 °C, South Tangerang 26.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jakarta 32.4 °C (September), South Tangerang 32.3 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Jakarta 21.7 °C, South Tangerang 21.4 °C.
  • South Tangerang gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 2,884 mm against 2,513 mm.
  • Jakarta gets about 6 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,648 h against 2,641 h; about 1961–1990).
