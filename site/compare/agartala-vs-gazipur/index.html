Agartala vs Gazipur climate

How the climate of Agartala, India compares with Gazipur, Bangladesh, 89 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gazipur is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 25.4 °C against 25.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Gazipur is 0.5 °C warmer (Agartala 27.4 °C, Gazipur 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Agartala 31.9 °C (April), Gazipur 32.6 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Agartala 12.2 °C, Gazipur 12.3 °C.
  • Gazipur gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 2,184 mm against 2,079 mm.
  • Agartala gets about 39 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,145 h against 2,106 h; about 1961–1990).
