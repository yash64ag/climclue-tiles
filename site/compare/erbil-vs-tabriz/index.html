Erbil vs Tabriz climate

How the climate of Erbil, Iraq compares with Tabriz, Iran, 292 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Erbil is 8.6 °C (15.5 °F) warmer over the year: 20.2 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Erbil is 10.2 °C warmer (Erbil 34.5 °C, Tabriz 24.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Erbil 41.4 °C (July), Tabriz 31.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Erbil 1.4 °C, Tabriz -6.5 °C.
  • Erbil gets 164% more rain and snow a year: 625 mm against 236 mm.
  • Erbil gets about 483 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,190 h against 2,707 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Erbil 4.6 cm, Tabriz 50 cm (ERA5-Land).
