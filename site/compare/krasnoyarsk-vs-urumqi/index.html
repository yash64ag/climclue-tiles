Krasnoyarsk vs Ürümqi climate

How the climate of Krasnoyarsk, Russia compares with Ürümqi, China, 1,412 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ürümqi is 5.9 °C (10.5 °F) warmer over the year: 7.5 °C against 1.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Ürümqi is 7.4 °C warmer (Krasnoyarsk 2.8 °C, Ürümqi 10.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Krasnoyarsk 25.4 °C (July), Ürümqi 29.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Krasnoyarsk -21.7 °C, Ürümqi -16.3 °C.
  • Krasnoyarsk gets 41% more rain and snow a year: 451 mm against 319 mm.
  • Ürümqi gets about 948 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,777 h against 1,829 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Krasnoyarsk 122 cm, Ürümqi 72 cm (ERA5-Land).
