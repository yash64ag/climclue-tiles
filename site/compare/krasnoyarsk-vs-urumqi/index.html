How the climate of Krasnoyarsk, Russia compares with Ürümqi, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ürümqi is 5.9 °C (10.6 °F) warmer over the year: 8.2 °C against 2.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Ürümqi is 8.1 °C warmer (Krasnoyarsk 3.1 °C, Ürümqi 11.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Krasnoyarsk 25.4 °C (July), Ürümqi 30.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Krasnoyarsk -19.7 °C, Ürümqi -15.6 °C.
  • Krasnoyarsk gets 41% more rain and snow a year: 451 mm against 319 mm.
  • Ürümqi gets about 948 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,777 h against 1,829 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Krasnoyarsk 122 cm, Ürümqi 72 cm (ERA5-Land).
