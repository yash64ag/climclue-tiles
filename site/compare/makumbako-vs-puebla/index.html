Makumbako vs Puebla climate

How the climate of Makumbako, Tanzania compares with Puebla, Mexico, 14,838 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Makumbako is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 17.7 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Makumbako is 4.7 °C warmer (Makumbako 19.4 °C, Puebla 14.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Makumbako 25.1 °C (November), Puebla 25.5 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Makumbako 10.7 °C, Puebla 7.4 °C.
  • Makumbako gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 818 mm against 739 mm.
  • Makumbako gets about 300 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,726 h against 2,426 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
