Makumbako vs Puebla, Puebla climate

How the climate of Makumbako, Tanzania compares with Puebla, Puebla, Mexico, 14,838 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Makumbako is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 17.6 °C against 17.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Makumbako is 4.6 °C warmer (Makumbako 19.3 °C, Puebla, Puebla 14.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Makumbako 25.7 °C (November), Puebla, Puebla 27.0 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Makumbako 8.2 °C, Puebla, Puebla 6.6 °C.
  • Makumbako gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 808 mm against 724 mm.
  • Makumbako gets about 300 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,726 h against 2,426 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
