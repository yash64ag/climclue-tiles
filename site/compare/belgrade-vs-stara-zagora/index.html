Belgrade vs Stara Zagora climate

How the climate of Belgrade, Serbia compares with Stara Zagora, Bulgaria, 493 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Stara Zagora is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 12.6 °C against 12.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Stara Zagora is 0.9 °C warmer (Belgrade 18.1 °C, Stara Zagora 19.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belgrade 28.7 °C (July), Stara Zagora 30.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Belgrade -2.2 °C, Stara Zagora -2.5 °C.
  • Belgrade gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 702 mm against 601 mm.
  • Stara Zagora gets about 293 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,284 h against 1,991 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Belgrade 45 cm, Stara Zagora 63 cm (ERA5-Land).
