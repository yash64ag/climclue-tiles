Belgrade vs Bucharest climate

How the climate of Belgrade, Serbia compares with Bucharest, Romania, 448 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Belgrade is 0.9 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 12.6 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Belgrade is 1.9 °C warmer (Belgrade 0.8 °C, Bucharest -1.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belgrade 28.2 °C (July), Bucharest 27.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Belgrade -2.4 °C, Bucharest -4.5 °C.
  • Belgrade gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 684 mm against 611 mm.
  • Bucharest gets about 84 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,075 h against 1,991 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Belgrade 45 cm, Bucharest 68 cm (ERA5-Land).
