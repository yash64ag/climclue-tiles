How the climate of Ahmedabad, India compares with Hadejia, Nigeria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hadejia is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 27.5 °C against 27.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Hadejia is 2.4 °C warmer (Ahmedabad 22.1 °C, Hadejia 24.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ahmedabad 41.1 °C (May), Hadejia 39.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Ahmedabad 11.6 °C, Hadejia 13.6 °C.
  • Ahmedabad gets 67% more rain and snow a year: 876 mm against 524 mm.
  • Ahmedabad gets about 4 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,001 h against 2,998 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Hadejia in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Hadejia in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Ahmedabad in 7 of 12 months
