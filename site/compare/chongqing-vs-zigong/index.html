Chongqing vs Zigong climate

How the climate of Chongqing, China compares with Zigong, China, 174 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Chongqing is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 19.0 °C against 18.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Chongqing is 1.7 °C warmer (Chongqing 28.8 °C, Zigong 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chongqing 31.9 °C (August), Zigong 30.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chongqing 4.8 °C, Zigong 4.5 °C.
  • Chongqing gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,146 mm against 1,118 mm.
  • Zigong gets about 170 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,368 h against 1,198 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chongqing 1.2 cm, Zigong 1.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
