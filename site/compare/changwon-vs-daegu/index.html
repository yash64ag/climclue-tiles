How the climate of Changwon, South Korea compares with Daegu, South Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Changwon is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 14.5 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Changwon is 1.7 °C warmer (Changwon 4.0 °C, Daegu 2.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changwon 30.4 °C (August), Daegu 31.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Changwon -3.2 °C, Daegu -5.3 °C.
  • Changwon gets 48% more rain and snow a year: 1,452 mm against 979 mm.
  • Daegu gets about 63 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,483 h against 2,419 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changwon 8.6 cm, Daegu 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
