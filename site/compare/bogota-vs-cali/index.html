Bogotá vs Cali climate

How the climate of Bogotá, Colombia compares with Cali, Colombia, 301 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Cali is 7.8 °C (14.0 °F) warmer over the year: 20.9 °C against 13.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Cali is 8.5 °C warmer (Bogotá 12.6 °C, Cali 21.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bogotá 18.6 °C (September), Cali 24.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bogotá 8.8 °C, Cali 16.6 °C.
  • Cali gets 31% more rain and snow a year: 1,365 mm against 1,046 mm.
  • Cali gets about 178 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,711 h against 1,532 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Cali in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Bogotá in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Cali in 8 of 12 months
