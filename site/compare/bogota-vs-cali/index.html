Bogotá vs Cali climate

How the climate of Bogotá, Colombia compares with Cali, Colombia, 301 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Cali is 8.1 °C (14.6 °F) warmer over the year: 22.2 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Cali is 8.7 °C warmer (Bogotá 13.8 °C, Cali 22.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bogotá 20.6 °C (February), Cali 28.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bogotá 7.4 °C, Cali 16.5 °C.
  • Cali gets 36% more rain and snow a year: 1,251 mm against 917 mm.
  • Cali gets about 178 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,711 h against 1,532 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Bogotá in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Cali in 8 of 12 months
