Goyang-si vs Suwon climate

How the climate of Goyang-si, South Korea compares with Suwon, South Korea, 45 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Suwon is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 12.2 °C against 12.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Suwon is 0.9 °C warmer (Goyang-si -3.3 °C, Suwon -2.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Goyang-si 29.4 °C (August), Suwon 29.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Goyang-si -7.7 °C, Suwon -7.5 °C.
  • Goyang-si gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,429 mm against 1,303 mm.
  • Suwon gets about 63 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,395 h against 2,331 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Goyang-si 27 cm, Suwon 26 cm (ERA5-Land).
