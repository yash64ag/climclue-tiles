Jinzhong vs Shijiazhuang climate

How the climate of Jinzhong, China compares with Shijiazhuang, China, 156 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shijiazhuang is 2.5 °C (4.6 °F) warmer over the year: 14.0 °C against 11.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Shijiazhuang is 3.2 °C warmer (Jinzhong 13.3 °C, Shijiazhuang 16.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinzhong 30.1 °C (July), Shijiazhuang 32.1 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Jinzhong -9.6 °C, Shijiazhuang -6.9 °C.
  • Shijiazhuang gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 504 mm against 467 mm.
  • Shijiazhuang gets about 27 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,566 h against 2,539 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinzhong 16 cm, Shijiazhuang 12 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Shijiazhuang in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Shijiazhuang in 7 of 12 months
