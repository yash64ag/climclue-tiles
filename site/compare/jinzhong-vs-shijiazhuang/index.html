How the climate of Jinzhong, China compares with Shijiazhuang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Shijiazhuang is 2.6 °C (4.7 °F) warmer over the year: 14.0 °C against 11.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Shijiazhuang is 3.3 °C warmer (Jinzhong 23.2 °C, Shijiazhuang 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinzhong 30.4 °C (July), Shijiazhuang 33.0 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Jinzhong -10.6 °C, Shijiazhuang -7.7 °C.
  • Shijiazhuang gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 502 mm against 463 mm.
  • Shijiazhuang gets about 27 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,566 h against 2,539 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinzhong 16 cm, Shijiazhuang 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
