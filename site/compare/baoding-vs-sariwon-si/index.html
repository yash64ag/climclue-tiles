Main differences

  • Baoding, Hebei is 2.3 °C (4.2 °F) warmer over the year: 13.8 °C against 11.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Baoding, Hebei is 4.9 °C warmer (Baoding, Hebei 22.3 °C, Sariwŏn-si 17.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoding, Hebei 33.1 °C (June), Sariwŏn-si 29.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Baoding, Hebei -8.4 °C, Sariwŏn-si -9.4 °C.
  • Sariwŏn-si gets 64% more rain and snow a year: 846 mm against 516 mm.
  • Baoding, Hebei gets about 181 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,609 h against 2,428 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoding, Hebei 10 cm, Sariwŏn-si 26 cm (ERA5-Land).
