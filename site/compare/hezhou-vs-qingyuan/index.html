Hezhou vs Qingyuan climate

How the climate of Hezhou, China compares with Qingyuan, China, 168 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Qingyuan is 1.1 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 21.5 °C against 20.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Qingyuan is 2.8 °C warmer (Hezhou 9.8 °C, Qingyuan 12.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hezhou 31.4 °C (August), Qingyuan 30.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Hezhou 5.6 °C, Qingyuan 8.4 °C.
  • Qingyuan gets 50% more rain and snow a year: 2,431 mm against 1,620 mm.
  • Qingyuan gets about 55 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,758 h against 1,703 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Qingyuan in 8 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: Hezhou in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Qingyuan in 7 of 12 months
