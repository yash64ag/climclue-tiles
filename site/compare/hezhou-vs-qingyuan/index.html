How the climate of Hezhou, China compares with Qingyuan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Qingyuan is 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) warmer over the year: 22.2 °C against 20.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Qingyuan is 3.1 °C warmer (Hezhou 10.1 °C, Qingyuan 13.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hezhou 33.4 °C (July), Qingyuan 33.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hezhou 6.0 °C, Qingyuan 8.7 °C.
  • Qingyuan gets 32% more rain and snow a year: 2,101 mm against 1,592 mm.
  • Qingyuan gets about 55 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,758 h against 1,703 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
