Barquisimeto vs Barranquilla climate

How the climate of Barquisimeto, Venezuela compares with Barranquilla, Colombia, 601 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Barranquilla is 2.3 °C (4.2 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 24.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Barranquilla is 3.4 °C warmer (Barquisimeto 24.1 °C, Barranquilla 27.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Barquisimeto 30.9 °C (March), Barranquilla 31.7 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Barquisimeto 18.5 °C, Barranquilla 22.6 °C.
  • Barranquilla gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 857 mm against 700 mm.
  • Barquisimeto gets about 120 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,600 h against 2,481 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Barranquilla in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Barranquilla in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
