How the climate of Onomichi, Japan compares with Wuxi, Jiangsu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Onomichi is 0.4 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 16.9 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Onomichi is 2.6 °C warmer (Onomichi 8.7 °C, Wuxi, Jiangsu 6.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Onomichi 32.6 °C (August), Wuxi, Jiangsu 32.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Onomichi 1.8 °C, Wuxi, Jiangsu -0.1 °C.
  • Onomichi gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,305 mm against 1,210 mm.
  • Wuxi, Jiangsu gets about 19 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,999 h against 1,979 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Onomichi 6.6 cm, Wuxi, Jiangsu 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
