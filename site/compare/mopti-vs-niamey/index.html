Mopti vs Niamey climate

How the climate of Mopti, Mali compares with Niamey, Niger, 687 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mopti is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 29.7 °C against 29.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Mopti is 0.8 °C warmer (Mopti 28.6 °C, Niamey 27.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mopti 41.6 °C (May), Niamey 40.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Mopti 15.7 °C, Niamey 16.1 °C.
  • Mopti gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 475 mm against 447 mm.
  • Mopti gets about 199 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,103 h against 2,904 h; about 1961–1990).
