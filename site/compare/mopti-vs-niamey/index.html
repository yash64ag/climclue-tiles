Mopti vs Niamey climate

How the climate of Mopti, Mali compares with Niamey, Niger, 687 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mopti is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 29.7 °C against 29.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Mopti is 1.0 °C warmer (Mopti 28.6 °C, Niamey 27.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mopti 40.8 °C (May), Niamey 40.5 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Mopti 16.9 °C, Niamey 15.9 °C.
  • Mopti gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 481 mm against 462 mm.
  • Mopti gets about 199 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,103 h against 2,904 h; about 1961–1990).
