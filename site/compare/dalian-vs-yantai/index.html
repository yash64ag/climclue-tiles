Dalian vs Yantai climate

How the climate of Dalian, China compares with Yantai, China, 160 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yantai is 1.3 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 12.5 °C against 11.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Yantai is 3.3 °C warmer (Dalian 13.5 °C, Yantai 16.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dalian 25.4 °C (August), Yantai 27.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Dalian -4.0 °C, Yantai -3.7 °C.
  • Yantai gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 690 mm against 597 mm.
  • Dalian gets about 102 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,662 h against 2,560 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Dalian 17 cm, Yantai 28 cm (ERA5-Land).
