Dalian vs Yantai, Shandong climate

How the climate of Dalian, China compares with Yantai, Shandong, China, 160 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yantai, Shandong is 1.3 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 12.5 °C against 11.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Yantai, Shandong is 3.3 °C warmer (Dalian 13.5 °C, Yantai, Shandong 16.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dalian 27.3 °C (August), Yantai, Shandong 28.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Dalian -5.5 °C, Yantai, Shandong -4.3 °C.
  • Yantai, Shandong gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 690 mm against 597 mm.
  • Dalian gets about 102 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,662 h against 2,560 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Dalian 17 cm, Yantai, Shandong 28 cm (ERA5-Land).
