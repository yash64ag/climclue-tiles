Xi’an vs Xianyang climate

How the climate of Xi’an, China compares with Xianyang, China, 23 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Xianyang is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.4 °C against 15.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Xianyang is 0.2 °C warmer (Xi’an 16.8 °C, Xianyang 16.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Xi’an 31.8 °C (July), Xianyang 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Xi’an -3.5 °C, Xianyang -3.5 °C.
  • Xi’an gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 674 mm against 582 mm.
  • Xianyang gets about 21 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,931 h against 1,909 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Xi’an 10 cm, Xianyang 8.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
