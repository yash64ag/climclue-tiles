How the climate of Xi’an, China compares with Xianyang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Xianyang is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 15.4 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Xianyang is 0.4 °C warmer (Xi’an 16.5 °C, Xianyang 16.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Xi’an 32.7 °C (July), Xianyang 32.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Xi’an -3.0 °C, Xianyang -3.0 °C.
  • Xi’an gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 664 mm against 578 mm.
  • Xianyang gets about 21 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,931 h against 1,909 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Xi’an 10 cm, Xianyang 8.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
