How the climate of Nanyang, China compares with Xuchang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Nanyang is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 15.7 °C against 15.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Nanyang is 1.1 °C warmer (Nanyang 2.2 °C, Xuchang 1.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nanyang 31.7 °C (July), Xuchang 32.6 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Nanyang -2.8 °C, Xuchang -3.8 °C.
  • Nanyang gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 843 mm against 762 mm.
  • Xuchang gets about 185 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,193 h against 2,008 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nanyang 16 cm, Xuchang 18 cm (ERA5-Land).
