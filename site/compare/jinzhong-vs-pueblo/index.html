How the climate of Jinzhong, China compares with Pueblo, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Pueblo is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 12.0 °C against 11.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Pueblo is 3.5 °C warmer (Jinzhong -3.9 °C, Pueblo -0.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinzhong 30.4 °C (July), Pueblo 34.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinzhong -10.6 °C, Pueblo -9.3 °C.
  • Jinzhong gets 35% more rain and snow a year: 463 mm against 342 mm.
  • Pueblo gets about 832 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,371 h against 2,539 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinzhong 16 cm, Pueblo 53 cm (ERA5-Land).
