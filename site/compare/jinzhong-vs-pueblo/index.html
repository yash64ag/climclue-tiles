Jinzhong vs Pueblo climate

How the climate of Jinzhong, China compares with Pueblo, United States, 10,745 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Pueblo is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 11.8 °C against 11.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Pueblo is 3.2 °C warmer (Jinzhong -3.9 °C, Pueblo -0.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinzhong 30.1 °C (July), Pueblo 32.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinzhong -9.6 °C, Pueblo -6.5 °C.
  • Jinzhong gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 467 mm against 364 mm.
  • Pueblo gets about 832 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,371 h against 2,539 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinzhong 33 cm, Pueblo 109 cm (ERA5-Land).
