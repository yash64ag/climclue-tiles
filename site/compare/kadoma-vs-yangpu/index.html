How the climate of Kadoma, Japan compares with Yangpu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yangpu is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 17.1 °C against 16.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Yangpu is 1.2 °C warmer (Kadoma 27.4 °C, Yangpu 28.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kadoma 33.2 °C (August), Yangpu 31.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kadoma 1.9 °C, Yangpu 1.9 °C.
  • Kadoma gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,461 mm against 1,430 mm.
  • Yangpu gets about 144 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,973 h against 1,828 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kadoma 5.1 cm, Yangpu 6.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
