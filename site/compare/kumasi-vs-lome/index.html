How the climate of Kumasi, Ghana compares with Lomé, Togo: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lomé is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 26.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Lomé is 1.5 °C warmer (Kumasi 25.7 °C, Lomé 27.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kumasi 33.9 °C (February), Lomé 32.0 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Kumasi 20.9 °C, Lomé 21.5 °C.
  • Kumasi gets 60% more rain and snow a year: 1,364 mm against 853 mm.
  • Lomé gets about 393 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,330 h against 1,937 h; about 1961–1990).
