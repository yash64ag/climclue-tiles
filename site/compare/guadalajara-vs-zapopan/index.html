How the climate of Guadalajara, Mexico compares with Zapopan, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Guadalajara is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 20.6 °C against 20.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Guadalajara is 0.2 °C warmer (Guadalajara 22.7 °C, Zapopan 22.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guadalajara 33.2 °C (May), Zapopan 33.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Guadalajara 8.1 °C, Zapopan 8.0 °C.
  • Zapopan gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 901 mm against 864 mm.
  • Zapopan gets about 9 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,647 h against 2,638 h; about 1961–1990).
