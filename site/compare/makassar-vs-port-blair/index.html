Makassar vs Port Blair climate

How the climate of Makassar, Indonesia compares with Port Blair, India, 3,494 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Port Blair is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Port Blair is 1.1 °C warmer (Makassar 27.1 °C, Port Blair 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Makassar 29.7 °C (September), Port Blair 28.8 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Makassar 23.9 °C, Port Blair 25.5 °C.
  • Makassar gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 3,336 mm against 2,986 mm.
  • Makassar gets about 651 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,852 h against 2,201 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Port Blair in 7 of 12 months (2 equal)
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Makassar in 8 of 12 months
