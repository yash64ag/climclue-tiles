How the climate of Makassar, Indonesia compares with Port Blair, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Port Blair is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Port Blair is 1.0 °C warmer (Makassar 27.1 °C, Port Blair 28.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Makassar 32.3 °C (September), Port Blair 32.5 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Makassar 21.7 °C, Port Blair 21.9 °C.
  • Makassar gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 3,326 mm against 2,967 mm.
  • Makassar gets about 651 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,852 h against 2,201 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
