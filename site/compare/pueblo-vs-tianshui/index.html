Pueblo vs Tianshui climate

How the climate of Pueblo, United States compares with Tianshui, China, 11,333 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tianshui is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 12.4 °C against 12.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Tianshui is 2.3 °C warmer (Pueblo 11.1 °C, Tianshui 13.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pueblo 34.2 °C (July), Tianshui 29.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Pueblo -9.3 °C, Tianshui -5.6 °C.
  • Tianshui gets 46% more rain and snow a year: 499 mm against 342 mm.
  • Pueblo gets about 1,345 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,371 h against 2,026 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Pueblo 34 cm, Tianshui 15 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Pueblo in 12 of 12 months
