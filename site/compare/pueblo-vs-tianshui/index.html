How the climate of Pueblo, United States compares with Tianshui, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tianshui is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 11.9 °C against 11.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Tianshui is 2.5 °C warmer (Pueblo 10.5 °C, Tianshui 13.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pueblo 34.0 °C (July), Tianshui 28.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Pueblo -9.8 °C, Tianshui -5.7 °C.
  • Tianshui gets 46% more rain and snow a year: 499 mm against 342 mm.
  • Pueblo gets about 1,345 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,371 h against 2,026 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Pueblo 53 cm, Tianshui 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
