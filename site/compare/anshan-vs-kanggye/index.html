Anshan vs Kanggye climate

How the climate of Anshan, China compares with Kanggye, North Korea, 302 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Anshan is 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) warmer over the year: 9.6 °C against 8.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Anshan is 2.7 °C warmer (Anshan -6.2 °C, Kanggye -8.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Anshan 28.8 °C (July), Kanggye 27.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Anshan -15.7 °C, Kanggye -21.2 °C.
  • Kanggye gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 787 mm against 754 mm.
  • Anshan gets about 171 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,577 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Anshan 39 cm, Kanggye 65 cm (ERA5-Land).
