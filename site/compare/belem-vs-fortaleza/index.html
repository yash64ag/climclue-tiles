Belém vs Fortaleza climate

How the climate of Belém, Brazil compares with Fortaleza, Brazil, 1,135 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Belém is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Fortaleza is 0.9 °C warmer (Belém 26.1 °C, Fortaleza 26.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belém 31.5 °C (October), Fortaleza 30.2 °C (November). Coldest-month night lows: Belém 24.1 °C, Fortaleza 24.1 °C.
  • Belém gets 106% more rain and snow a year: 2,708 mm against 1,314 mm.
  • Fortaleza gets about 433 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,643 h against 2,210 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Fortaleza in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Fortaleza in 11 of 12 months
