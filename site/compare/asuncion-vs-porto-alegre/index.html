How the climate of Asunción, Paraguay compares with Porto Alegre, Brazil: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Asunción is 2.9 °C (5.2 °F) warmer over the year: 22.6 °C against 19.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Asunción is 4.0 °C warmer (Asunción 23.5 °C, Porto Alegre 19.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Asunción 32.7 °C (January), Porto Alegre 29.6 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Asunción 12.4 °C, Porto Alegre 9.7 °C.
  • Asunción gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,439 mm against 1,419 mm.
  • Asunción gets about 547 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,764 h against 2,217 h; about 1961–1990).
