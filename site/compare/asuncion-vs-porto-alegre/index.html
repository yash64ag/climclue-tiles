Asunción vs Porto Alegre climate

How the climate of Asunción, Paraguay compares with Porto Alegre, Brazil, 823 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Asunción is 3.2 °C (5.7 °F) warmer over the year: 22.8 °C against 19.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Asunción is 4.3 °C warmer (Asunción 23.6 °C, Porto Alegre 19.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Asunción 32.3 °C (January), Porto Alegre 29.3 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Asunción 13.5 °C, Porto Alegre 10.3 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,426 mm and 1,428 mm).
  • Asunción gets about 547 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,764 h against 2,217 h; about 1961–1990).
