Andong vs Yishui climate

How the climate of Andong, South Korea compares with Yishui, China, 910 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yishui is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 12.9 °C against 12.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Yishui is 1.1 °C warmer (Andong 22.1 °C, Yishui 23.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Andong 28.5 °C (August), Yishui 29.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Andong -6.6 °C, Yishui -6.2 °C.
  • Andong gets 49% more rain and snow a year: 1,069 mm against 718 mm.
  • Andong gets about 76 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,513 h against 2,437 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Andong 30 cm, Yishui 26 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Yishui in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Andong in 7 of 12 months
