How the climate of León de los Aldama, Mexico compares with Zapopan, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zapopan is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 20.5 °C against 19.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Zapopan is 1.6 °C warmer (León de los Aldama 16.9 °C, Zapopan 18.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: León de los Aldama 32.4 °C (May), Zapopan 33.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: León de los Aldama 5.1 °C, Zapopan 8.0 °C.
  • Zapopan gets 43% more rain and snow a year: 901 mm against 631 mm.
  • Zapopan gets about 366 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,647 h against 2,282 h; about 1961–1990).
