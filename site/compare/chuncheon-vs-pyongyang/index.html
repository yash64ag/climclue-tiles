Chuncheon vs Pyongyang climate

How the climate of Chuncheon, South Korea compares with Pyongyang, North Korea, 214 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Chuncheon is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 11.4 °C against 11.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Chuncheon is 1.2 °C warmer (Chuncheon -4.3 °C, Pyongyang -5.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chuncheon 27.9 °C (August), Pyongyang 28.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Chuncheon -10.9 °C, Pyongyang -11.5 °C.
  • Chuncheon gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 1,292 mm against 938 mm.
  • Pyongyang gets about 94 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,427 h against 2,334 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chuncheon 40 cm, Pyongyang 26 cm (ERA5-Land).
