Qingdao vs Yishui climate

How the climate of Qingdao, China compares with Yishui, China, 161 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Qingdao is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 13.1 °C against 12.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Yishui is 3.8 °C warmer (Qingdao 15.3 °C, Yishui 19.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qingdao 26.7 °C (August), Yishui 29.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Qingdao -1.5 °C, Yishui -6.2 °C.
  • Qingdao gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 728 mm against 688 mm.
  • Qingdao gets about 33 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,470 h against 2,437 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Qingdao 11 cm, Yishui 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
