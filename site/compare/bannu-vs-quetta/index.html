How the climate of Bannu, Pakistan compares with Quetta, Pakistan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bannu is 5.9 °C (10.7 °F) warmer over the year: 23.4 °C against 17.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Bannu is 7.1 °C warmer (Bannu 31.1 °C, Quetta 24.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bannu 41.8 °C (June), Quetta 36.8 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Bannu 4.1 °C, Quetta -2.0 °C.
  • Bannu gets 81% more rain and snow a year: 576 mm against 317 mm.
  • Quetta gets about 346 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,333 h against 2,987 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bannu 0 cm, Quetta 82 cm (ERA5-Land).
