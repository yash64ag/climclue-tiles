Prague vs Vienna climate

How the climate of Prague, Czechia compares with Vienna, Austria, 253 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Vienna is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 10.3 °C against 9.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Vienna is 1.4 °C warmer (Prague 19.4 °C, Vienna 20.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Prague 23.9 °C (July), Vienna 25.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Prague -3.5 °C, Vienna -3.7 °C.
  • Vienna gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 629 mm against 541 mm.
  • Vienna gets about 99 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,739 h against 1,640 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Prague 42 cm, Vienna 63 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Vienna in 11 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: Prague in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Vienna in 12 of 12 months
