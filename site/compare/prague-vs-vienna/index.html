How the climate of Prague, Czechia compares with Vienna, Austria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Vienna is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 10.9 °C against 10.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Vienna is 0.9 °C warmer (Prague 10.3 °C, Vienna 11.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Prague 25.3 °C (July), Vienna 26.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Prague -1.8 °C, Vienna -2.1 °C.
  • Vienna gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 626 mm against 525 mm.
  • Vienna gets about 99 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,739 h against 1,640 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Prague 42 cm, Vienna 63 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Vienna in 10 of 12 months
  • Drier: Prague in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Vienna in 12 of 12 months
