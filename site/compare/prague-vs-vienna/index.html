Prague vs Vienna climate

How the climate of Prague, Czechia compares with Vienna, Austria, 253 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Vienna is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 10.5 °C against 9.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Vienna is 1.4 °C warmer (Prague 19.7 °C, Vienna 21.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Prague 25.4 °C (July), Vienna 26.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Prague -3.2 °C, Vienna -2.7 °C.
  • Vienna gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 626 mm against 525 mm.
  • Vienna gets about 99 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,739 h against 1,640 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Prague 42 cm, Vienna 63 cm (ERA5-Land).
