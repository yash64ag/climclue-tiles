How the climate of Guayaquil, Ecuador compares with Trujillo, Peru: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Guayaquil is 4.8 °C (8.7 °F) warmer over the year: 24.6 °C against 19.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Guayaquil is 6.0 °C warmer (Guayaquil 23.3 °C, Trujillo 17.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guayaquil 31.9 °C (April), Trujillo 27.6 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Guayaquil 16.8 °C, Trujillo 13.9 °C.
  • Guayaquil gets 1,632% more rain and snow a year: 1,314 mm against 76 mm.
  • Trujillo gets about 849 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,933 h against 1,084 h; about 1961–1990).
