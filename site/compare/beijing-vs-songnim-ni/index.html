Beijing vs Songnim-ni climate

How the climate of Beijing, China compares with Songnim-ni, North Korea, 805 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Beijing is 1.8 °C (3.2 °F) warmer over the year: 13.0 °C against 11.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Beijing is 4.7 °C warmer (Beijing 22.1 °C, Songnim-ni 17.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Beijing 31.9 °C (June), Songnim-ni 28.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Beijing -9.1 °C, Songnim-ni -10.5 °C.
  • Songnim-ni gets 55% more rain and snow a year: 874 mm against 563 mm.
  • Beijing gets about 270 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,704 h against 2,434 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Beijing 12 cm, Songnim-ni 24 cm (ERA5-Land).
