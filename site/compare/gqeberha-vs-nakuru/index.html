Gqeberha vs Nakuru climate

How the climate of Gqeberha, South Africa compares with Nakuru, Kenya, 3,898 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Nakuru is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 18.7 °C against 18.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Nakuru is 1.9 °C warmer (Gqeberha 16.1 °C, Nakuru 18.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gqeberha 23.3 °C (February), Nakuru 26.6 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Gqeberha 12.9 °C, Nakuru 12.2 °C.
  • Nakuru gets 58% more rain and snow a year: 882 mm against 558 mm.
  • Gqeberha gets about 315 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,739 h against 2,424 h; about 1961–1990).
