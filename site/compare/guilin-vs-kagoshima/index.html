Guilin vs Kagoshima climate

How the climate of Guilin, China compares with Kagoshima, Japan, 2,096 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Guilin is 1.5 °C (2.6 °F) warmer over the year: 20.0 °C against 18.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Guilin is 3.6 °C warmer (Guilin 24.2 °C, Kagoshima 20.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guilin 31.5 °C (August), Kagoshima 30.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Guilin 5.6 °C, Kagoshima 4.7 °C.
  • Guilin gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 2,144 mm against 2,100 mm.
  • Kagoshima gets about 295 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,859 h against 1,565 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Guilin in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Guilin in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Kagoshima in 8 of 12 months
