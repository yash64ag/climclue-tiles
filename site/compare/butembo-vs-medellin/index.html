Butembo vs Medellín climate

How the climate of Butembo, Democratic Republic of the Congo compares with Medellín, Colombia, 11,648 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Medellín is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 17.7 °C against 17.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Medellín is 1.2 °C warmer (Butembo 16.8 °C, Medellín 17.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Butembo 24.0 °C (February), Medellín 23.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Butembo 12.9 °C, Medellín 12.6 °C.
  • Butembo gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 2,251 mm against 2,015 mm.
  • Butembo gets about 172 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,994 h against 1,823 h; about 1961–1990).
