Butembo vs Medellín climate

How the climate of Butembo, Democratic Republic of the Congo compares with Medellín, Colombia, 11,648 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Medellín is 2.4 °C (4.3 °F) warmer over the year: 20.0 °C against 17.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Medellín is 3.1 °C warmer (Butembo 17.2 °C, Medellín 20.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Butembo 24.5 °C (February), Medellín 25.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Butembo 11.5 °C, Medellín 14.7 °C.
  • Butembo gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 2,173 mm against 1,768 mm.
  • Butembo gets about 172 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,994 h against 1,823 h; about 1961–1990).
