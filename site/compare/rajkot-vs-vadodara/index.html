How the climate of Rājkot, India compares with Vadodara, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Vadodara is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Vadodara is 1.4 °C warmer (Rājkot 26.4 °C, Vadodara 27.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rājkot 39.3 °C (May), Vadodara 39.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Rājkot 11.4 °C, Vadodara 12.7 °C.
  • Vadodara gets 43% more rain and snow a year: 1,053 mm against 738 mm.
  • Rājkot gets about 159 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,075 h against 2,916 h; about 1961–1990).
