Green Bay vs Ottawa climate

How the climate of Green Bay, United States compares with Ottawa, Canada, 974 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Green Bay is 1.3 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 7.9 °C against 6.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Green Bay is 2.6 °C warmer (Green Bay -7.5 °C, Ottawa -10.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Green Bay 28.2 °C (July), Ottawa 26.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Green Bay -12.5 °C, Ottawa -14.6 °C.
  • Ottawa gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 960 mm against 787 mm.
  • Green Bay gets about 439 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,433 h against 1,993 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Green Bay 105 cm, Ottawa 185 cm (ERA5-Land).
