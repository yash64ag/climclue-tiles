Green Bay vs Ottawa climate

How the climate of Green Bay, United States compares with Ottawa, Canada, 974 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Green Bay is 1.3 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 7.9 °C against 6.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Green Bay is 2.7 °C warmer (Green Bay -7.5 °C, Ottawa -10.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Green Bay 27.3 °C (July), Ottawa 26.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Green Bay -11.8 °C, Ottawa -14.6 °C.
  • Ottawa gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 974 mm against 799 mm.
  • Green Bay gets about 439 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,433 h against 1,993 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Green Bay 143 cm, Ottawa 211 cm (ERA5-Land).
