How the climate of Barquisimeto, Venezuela compares with Caracas, Venezuela: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Barquisimeto is 2.2 °C (4.0 °F) warmer over the year: 25.0 °C against 22.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Barquisimeto is 3.4 °C warmer (Barquisimeto 24.6 °C, Caracas 21.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Barquisimeto 32.1 °C (March), Caracas 28.4 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Barquisimeto 19.5 °C, Caracas 16.7 °C.
  • Caracas gets 60% more rain and snow a year: 1,121 mm against 700 mm.
  • Barquisimeto gets about 215 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,600 h against 2,385 h; about 1961–1990).
