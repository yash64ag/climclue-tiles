Barquisimeto vs Caracas climate

How the climate of Barquisimeto, Venezuela compares with Caracas, Venezuela, 275 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Barquisimeto is 2.9 °C (5.3 °F) warmer over the year: 24.5 °C against 21.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Barquisimeto is 3.7 °C warmer (Barquisimeto 24.4 °C, Caracas 20.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Barquisimeto 30.6 °C (March), Caracas 26.8 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Barquisimeto 19.3 °C, Caracas 16.8 °C.
  • Caracas gets 76% more rain and snow a year: 1,347 mm against 765 mm.
  • Barquisimeto gets about 215 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,600 h against 2,385 h; about 1961–1990).
