Barquisimeto vs Caracas climate

How the climate of Barquisimeto, Venezuela compares with Caracas, Venezuela, 275 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Barquisimeto is 2.3 °C (4.2 °F) warmer over the year: 24.7 °C against 22.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Barquisimeto is 3.0 °C warmer (Barquisimeto 24.1 °C, Caracas 21.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Barquisimeto 30.9 °C (March), Caracas 28.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Barquisimeto 18.5 °C, Caracas 15.6 °C.
  • Caracas gets 60% more rain and snow a year: 1,121 mm against 700 mm.
  • Barquisimeto gets about 215 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,600 h against 2,385 h; about 1961–1990).
