Yinchuan vs Zhongwei climate

How the climate of Yinchuan, China compares with Zhongwei, China, 143 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhongwei is 1.5 °C (2.6 °F) warmer over the year: 11.4 °C against 9.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Zhongwei is 2.3 °C warmer (Yinchuan 4.2 °C, Zhongwei 6.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Yinchuan 30.0 °C (July), Zhongwei 31.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Yinchuan -12.1 °C, Zhongwei -11.4 °C.
  • Yinchuan gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 191 mm against 185 mm.
  • Yinchuan gets about 149 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,845 h against 2,697 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Yinchuan 19 cm, Zhongwei 22 cm (ERA5-Land).
