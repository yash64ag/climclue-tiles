Minsk vs Warsaw climate

How the climate of Minsk, Belarus compares with Warsaw, Poland, 476 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Warsaw is 2.2 °C (4.0 °F) warmer over the year: 8.5 °C against 6.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Warsaw is 4.1 °C warmer (Minsk -5.0 °C, Warsaw -0.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Minsk 22.1 °C (July), Warsaw 23.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Minsk -7.9 °C, Warsaw -4.2 °C.
  • Minsk gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 686 mm against 582 mm.
  • Minsk gets about 153 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,741 h against 1,587 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Minsk 142 cm, Warsaw 61 cm (ERA5-Land).
