Fuzhou, Fujian vs New Taipei City climate

How the climate of Fuzhou, Fujian, China compares with New Taipei City, Taiwan, 243 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • New Taipei City is 2.7 °C (4.9 °F) warmer over the year: 22.5 °C against 19.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when New Taipei City is 5.0 °C warmer (Fuzhou, Fujian 10.4 °C, New Taipei City 15.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuzhou, Fujian 31.8 °C (July), New Taipei City 31.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuzhou, Fujian 6.2 °C, New Taipei City 12.3 °C.
  • New Taipei City gets 144% more rain and snow a year: 3,293 mm against 1,350 mm.
  • Fuzhou, Fujian gets about 39 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,763 h against 1,724 h; about 1961–1990).
