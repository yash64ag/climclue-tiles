How the climate of Fuzhou, Fujian, China compares with New Taipei City, Taiwan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • New Taipei City is 2.5 °C (4.5 °F) warmer over the year: 22.6 °C against 20.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when New Taipei City is 5.0 °C warmer (Fuzhou, Fujian 10.6 °C, New Taipei City 15.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuzhou, Fujian 33.9 °C (July), New Taipei City 33.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuzhou, Fujian 6.8 °C, New Taipei City 12.6 °C.
  • New Taipei City gets 149% more rain and snow a year: 3,215 mm against 1,289 mm.
  • Fuzhou, Fujian gets about 39 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,763 h against 1,724 h; about 1961–1990).
