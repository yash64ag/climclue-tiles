Lubumbashi vs Mansa climate

How the climate of Lubumbashi, Democratic Republic of the Congo compares with Mansa, Zambia, 163 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mansa is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 21.3 °C against 21.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Mansa is 0.4 °C warmer (Lubumbashi 18.1 °C, Mansa 18.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lubumbashi 30.9 °C (October), Mansa 30.8 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Lubumbashi 11.8 °C, Mansa 13.1 °C.
  • Mansa gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,162 mm against 1,135 mm.
  • Lubumbashi gets about 113 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,732 h against 2,619 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Mansa in 8 of 12 months (4 equal)
  • Drier: even, 5 months each
  • Sunnier: Lubumbashi in 7 of 12 months
