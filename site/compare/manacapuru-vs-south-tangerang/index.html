Manacapuru vs South Tangerang climate

How the climate of Manacapuru, Brazil compares with South Tangerang, Indonesia, 18,251 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Manacapuru is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when South Tangerang is 0.5 °C warmer (Manacapuru 26.4 °C, South Tangerang 26.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manacapuru 30.2 °C (October), South Tangerang 31.5 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Manacapuru 23.9 °C, South Tangerang 22.4 °C.
  • South Tangerang gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 2,702 mm against 2,612 mm.
  • South Tangerang gets about 863 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,641 h against 1,779 h; about 1961–1990).
