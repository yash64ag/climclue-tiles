Delhi vs Najafgarh climate

How the climate of Delhi, India compares with Najafgarh, India, 25 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Najafgarh is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 24.9 °C against 24.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Najafgarh is 0.3 °C warmer (Delhi 28.7 °C, Najafgarh 29.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Delhi 39.6 °C (May), Najafgarh 39.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Delhi 6.6 °C, Najafgarh 6.4 °C.
  • Delhi gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 646 mm against 604 mm.
  • Najafgarh gets about 23 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,702 h against 2,679 h; about 1961–1990).
