Daegu vs Huaibei climate

How the climate of Daegu, South Korea compares with Huaibei, China, 1,096 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Huaibei is 0.9 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 14.9 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Huaibei is 2.4 °C warmer (Daegu 23.1 °C, Huaibei 25.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daegu 31.1 °C (August), Huaibei 31.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Daegu -5.3 °C, Huaibei -3.8 °C.
  • Daegu gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 979 mm against 907 mm.
  • Daegu gets about 253 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,483 h against 2,229 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daegu 13 cm, Huaibei 18 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Huaibei in 11 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Daegu in 9 of 12 months
