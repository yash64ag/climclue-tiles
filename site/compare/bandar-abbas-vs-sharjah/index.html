Bandar Abbas vs Sharjah climate

How the climate of Bandar Abbas, Iran compares with Sharjah, United Arab Emirates, 223 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bandar Abbas is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 27.6 °C against 27.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Bandar Abbas is 0.9 °C warmer (Bandar Abbas 27.3 °C, Sharjah 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bandar Abbas 37.6 °C (June), Sharjah 41.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bandar Abbas 15.3 °C, Sharjah 15.1 °C.
  • Bandar Abbas gets 51% more rain and snow a year: 183 mm against 121 mm.
  • Sharjah gets about 351 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,420 h against 3,069 h; about 1961–1990).
