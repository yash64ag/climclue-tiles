How the climate of Bandar Abbas, Iran compares with Sharjah, United Arab Emirates: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bandar Abbas and Sharjah have the same yearly average temperature (27.4 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Sharjah is 1.3 °C warmer (Bandar Abbas 33.4 °C, Sharjah 34.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bandar Abbas 38.3 °C (June), Sharjah 40.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bandar Abbas 13.5 °C, Sharjah 14.0 °C.
  • Bandar Abbas gets 54% more rain and snow a year: 182 mm against 118 mm.
  • Sharjah gets about 351 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,420 h against 3,069 h; about 1961–1990).
