Rome vs Tripoli climate

How the climate of Rome, Italy compares with Tripoli, Libya, 1,003 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tripoli is 4.4 °C (8.0 °F) warmer over the year: 20.4 °C against 15.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Tripoli is 6.3 °C warmer (Rome 12.4 °C, Tripoli 18.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rome 29.8 °C (July), Tripoli 31.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Rome 3.7 °C, Tripoli 10.2 °C.
  • Rome gets 188% more rain and snow a year: 836 mm against 290 mm.
  • Tripoli gets about 795 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,219 h against 2,424 h; about 1961–1990).
