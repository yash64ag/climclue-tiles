How the climate of Rome, Italy compares with Tripoli, Libya: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tripoli is 4.5 °C (8.1 °F) warmer over the year: 20.3 °C against 15.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Tripoli is 7.0 °C warmer (Rome 12.0 °C, Tripoli 19.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rome 32.1 °C (July), Tripoli 33.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Rome 2.5 °C, Tripoli 8.8 °C.
  • Rome gets 158% more rain and snow a year: 770 mm against 299 mm.
  • Tripoli gets about 795 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,219 h against 2,424 h; about 1961–1990).
