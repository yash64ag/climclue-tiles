How the climate of Cali, Colombia compares with Kampala, Uganda: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Cali is 2.0 °C (3.7 °F) warmer over the year: 23.5 °C against 21.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Cali is 3.2 °C warmer (Cali 24.0 °C, Kampala 20.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cali 29.9 °C (August), Kampala 28.8 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Cali 17.9 °C, Kampala 15.1 °C.
  • Kampala gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,388 mm against 1,251 mm.
  • Kampala gets about 743 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,454 h against 1,711 h; about 1961–1990).
