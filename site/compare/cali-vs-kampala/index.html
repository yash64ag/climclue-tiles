Cali vs Kampala climate

How the climate of Cali, Colombia compares with Kampala, Uganda, 12,125 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kampala is 0.4 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 21.3 °C against 20.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Kampala is 1.2 °C warmer (Cali 20.9 °C, Kampala 22.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cali 24.9 °C (August), Kampala 25.4 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Cali 16.6 °C, Kampala 17.2 °C.
  • Kampala gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,395 mm against 1,365 mm.
  • Kampala gets about 743 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,454 h against 1,711 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Kampala in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Kampala in 12 of 12 months
