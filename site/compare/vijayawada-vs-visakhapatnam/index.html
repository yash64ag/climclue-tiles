Vijayawada vs Visakhapatnam climate

How the climate of Vijayawada, India compares with Visakhapatnam, India, 301 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Vijayawada is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 28.4 °C against 27.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Vijayawada is 3.0 °C warmer (Vijayawada 33.1 °C, Visakhapatnam 30.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Vijayawada 38.8 °C (May), Visakhapatnam 33.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Vijayawada 18.9 °C, Visakhapatnam 20.5 °C.
  • Visakhapatnam gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,147 mm against 1,040 mm.
  • Vijayawada gets about 114 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,669 h against 2,555 h; about 1961–1990).
