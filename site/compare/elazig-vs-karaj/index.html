Elazığ vs Karaj climate

How the climate of Elazığ, Turkey compares with Karaj, Iran, 1,088 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Karaj is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 14.4 °C against 13.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Karaj is 2.4 °C warmer (Elazığ 23.1 °C, Karaj 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Elazığ 35.0 °C (July), Karaj 35.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Elazığ -2.8 °C, Karaj -4.8 °C.
  • Karaj gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 478 mm against 410 mm.
  • Elazığ gets about 230 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,004 h against 2,774 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Elazığ 63 cm, Karaj 78 cm (ERA5-Land).
