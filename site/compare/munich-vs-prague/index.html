Munich vs Prague climate

How the climate of Munich, Germany compares with Prague, Czechia, 300 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Prague is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 9.6 °C against 9.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Prague is 1.4 °C warmer (Munich 17.9 °C, Prague 19.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Munich 23.7 °C (July), Prague 25.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Munich -3.5 °C, Prague -3.2 °C.
  • Munich gets 92% more rain and snow a year: 1,006 mm against 525 mm.
  • Munich gets about 15 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,655 h against 1,640 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Munich 97 cm, Prague 42 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Sunnier: Munich in 8 of 12 months
