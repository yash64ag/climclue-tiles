Munich vs Prague climate

How the climate of Munich, Germany compares with Prague, Czechia, 300 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Prague is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 9.4 °C against 9.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Prague is 1.1 °C warmer (Munich 17.9 °C, Prague 19.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Munich 22.7 °C (July), Prague 23.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Munich -3.8 °C, Prague -3.5 °C.
  • Munich gets 84% more rain and snow a year: 997 mm against 541 mm.
  • Munich gets about 15 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,655 h against 1,640 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Munich 126 cm, Prague 74 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Prague in 8 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: Prague in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Munich in 8 of 12 months
