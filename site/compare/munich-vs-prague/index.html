How the climate of Munich, Germany compares with Prague, Czechia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Prague is 1.7 °C (3.1 °F) warmer over the year: 10.5 °C against 8.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Prague is 2.5 °C warmer (Munich 13.0 °C, Prague 15.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Munich 23.7 °C (July), Prague 25.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Munich -4.2 °C, Prague -1.8 °C.
  • Munich gets 92% more rain and snow a year: 1,006 mm against 525 mm.
  • Munich gets about 15 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,655 h against 1,640 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Munich 97 cm, Prague 42 cm (ERA5-Land).
