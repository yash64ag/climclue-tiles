How the climate of Rio de Janeiro, Brazil compares with São Paulo, Brazil: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Rio de Janeiro is 4.0 °C (7.2 °F) warmer over the year: 24.0 °C against 20.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Rio de Janeiro is 4.6 °C warmer (Rio de Janeiro 22.8 °C, São Paulo 18.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rio de Janeiro 31.5 °C (February), São Paulo 27.5 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Rio de Janeiro 16.6 °C, São Paulo 11.4 °C.
  • São Paulo gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,470 mm against 1,310 mm.
  • Rio de Janeiro gets about 311 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,091 h against 1,780 h; about 1961–1990).
