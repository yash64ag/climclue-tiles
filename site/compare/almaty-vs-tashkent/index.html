How the climate of Almaty, Kazakhstan compares with Tashkent, Uzbekistan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tashkent is 4.5 °C (8.2 °F) warmer over the year: 14.9 °C against 10.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Tashkent is 6.7 °C warmer (Almaty -2.7 °C, Tashkent 4.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Almaty 30.4 °C (July), Tashkent 35.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Almaty -9.1 °C, Tashkent -2.2 °C.
  • Almaty gets 106% more rain and snow a year: 926 mm against 450 mm.
  • Tashkent gets about 330 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,790 h against 2,460 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Almaty 145 cm, Tashkent 58 cm (ERA5-Land).
