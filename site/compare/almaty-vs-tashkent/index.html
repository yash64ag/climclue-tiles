Almaty vs Tashkent climate

How the climate of Almaty, Kazakhstan compares with Tashkent, Uzbekistan, 670 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tashkent is 4.3 °C (7.7 °F) warmer over the year: 13.7 °C against 9.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Tashkent is 6.0 °C warmer (Almaty -6.3 °C, Tashkent -0.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Almaty 27.9 °C (July), Tashkent 33.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Almaty -9.8 °C, Tashkent -5.6 °C.
  • Almaty gets 106% more rain and snow a year: 926 mm against 450 mm.
  • Tashkent gets about 330 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,790 h against 2,460 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Almaty 145 cm, Tashkent 58 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Tashkent in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Tashkent in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Tashkent in 10 of 12 months
