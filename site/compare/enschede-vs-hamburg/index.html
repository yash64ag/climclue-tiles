Enschede vs Hamburg climate

How the climate of Enschede, The Netherlands compares with Hamburg, Germany, 255 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Enschede is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 9.9 °C against 9.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Enschede is 1.2 °C warmer (Enschede 5.7 °C, Hamburg 4.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Enschede 22.9 °C (July), Hamburg 22.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Enschede -0.2 °C, Hamburg -1.1 °C.
  • Enschede gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 878 mm against 813 mm.
  • Hamburg gets about 174 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,535 h against 1,361 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Enschede 22 cm, Hamburg 40 cm (ERA5-Land).
