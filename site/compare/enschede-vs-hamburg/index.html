Enschede vs Hamburg climate

How the climate of Enschede, The Netherlands compares with Hamburg, Germany, 255 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Enschede is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 9.9 °C against 9.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Enschede is 1.2 °C warmer (Enschede 2.5 °C, Hamburg 1.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Enschede 22.2 °C (July), Hamburg 22.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Enschede 0.0 °C, Hamburg -0.9 °C.
  • Enschede gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 865 mm against 812 mm.
  • Hamburg gets about 174 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,535 h against 1,361 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Enschede 22 cm, Hamburg 40 cm (ERA5-Land).
