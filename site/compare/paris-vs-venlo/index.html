How the climate of Paris, France compares with Venlo, The Netherlands: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Paris is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 11.9 °C against 10.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Paris is 1.7 °C warmer (Paris 5.2 °C, Venlo 3.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Paris 24.9 °C (July), Venlo 24.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Paris 2.0 °C, Venlo 0.4 °C.
  • Venlo gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 802 mm against 658 mm.
  • Paris gets about 365 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,744 h against 1,380 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Paris 9.9 cm, Venlo 19 cm (ERA5-Land).
