How the climate of Kayseri, Turkey compares with Tabriz, Iran: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kayseri is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 12.3 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Kayseri is 2.0 °C warmer (Kayseri 2.1 °C, Tabriz 0.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kayseri 34.0 °C (August), Tabriz 31.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kayseri -5.1 °C, Tabriz -5.9 °C.
  • Kayseri gets 47% more rain and snow a year: 347 mm against 236 mm.
  • Kayseri gets about 159 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,867 h against 2,707 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kayseri 47 cm, Tabriz 50 cm (ERA5-Land).
