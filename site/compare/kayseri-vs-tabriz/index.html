How the climate of Kayseri, Turkey compares with Tabriz, Iran: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tabriz is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 12.5 °C against 12.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Tabriz is 1.7 °C warmer (Kayseri 24.1 °C, Tabriz 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kayseri 34.0 °C (August), Tabriz 32.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kayseri -5.1 °C, Tabriz -5.6 °C.
  • Kayseri gets 47% more rain and snow a year: 347 mm against 236 mm.
  • Kayseri gets about 159 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,867 h against 2,707 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kayseri 47 cm, Tabriz 50 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Tabriz in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Tabriz in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Kayseri in 8 of 12 months
