Plano vs Zhuzhou climate

How the climate of Plano, United States compares with Zhuzhou, China, 12,551 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zhuzhou is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 18.6 °C against 18.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Zhuzhou is 1.5 °C warmer (Plano 28.8 °C, Zhuzhou 30.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Plano 34.0 °C (August), Zhuzhou 33.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Plano 3.6 °C, Zhuzhou 2.3 °C.
  • Zhuzhou gets 49% more rain and snow a year: 1,637 mm against 1,102 mm.
  • Plano gets about 1,199 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,804 h against 1,605 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Plano 2.0 cm, Zhuzhou 6.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
