Anshan vs Fushun climate

How the climate of Anshan, China compares with Fushun, China, 116 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Anshan is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 9.6 °C against 8.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Anshan is 2.1 °C warmer (Anshan -9.8 °C, Fushun -11.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Anshan 29.0 °C (July), Fushun 29.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Anshan -15.2 °C, Fushun -18.2 °C.
  • Fushun gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 772 mm against 754 mm.
  • Anshan gets about 42 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,577 h against 2,534 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Anshan 39 cm, Fushun 42 cm (ERA5-Land).
