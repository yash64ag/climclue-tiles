Anshan vs Fushun climate

How the climate of Anshan, China compares with Fushun, China, 116 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Anshan is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 9.7 °C against 8.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Anshan is 2.2 °C warmer (Anshan -9.7 °C, Fushun -11.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Anshan 28.9 °C (July), Fushun 28.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Anshan -15.5 °C, Fushun -18.6 °C.
  • Fushun gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 765 mm against 745 mm.
  • Anshan gets about 42 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,577 h against 2,534 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Anshan 53 cm, Fushun 72 cm (ERA5-Land).
