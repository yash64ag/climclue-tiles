Qingyang vs Xi’an climate

How the climate of Qingyang, China compares with Xi’an, China, 199 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Xi’an is 5.6 °C (10.0 °F) warmer over the year: 15.3 °C against 9.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Xi’an is 6.0 °C warmer (Qingyang -0.8 °C, Xi’an 5.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qingyang 27.2 °C (July), Xi’an 31.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Qingyang -9.8 °C, Xi’an -3.5 °C.
  • Xi’an gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 674 mm against 618 mm.
  • Qingyang gets about 361 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,270 h against 1,909 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Qingyang 47 cm, Xi’an 38 cm (ERA5-Land).
