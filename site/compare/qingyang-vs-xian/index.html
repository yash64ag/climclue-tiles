How the climate of Qingyang, Gansu, China compares with Xi’an, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Xi’an is 5.7 °C (10.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 9.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Xi’an is 6.0 °C warmer (Qingyang, Gansu 2.4 °C, Xi’an 8.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qingyang, Gansu 27.3 °C (July), Xi’an 32.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Qingyang, Gansu -9.6 °C, Xi’an -3.0 °C.
  • Xi’an gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 664 mm against 643 mm.
  • Qingyang, Gansu gets about 361 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,270 h against 1,909 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Qingyang, Gansu 34 cm, Xi’an 10 cm (ERA5-Land).
