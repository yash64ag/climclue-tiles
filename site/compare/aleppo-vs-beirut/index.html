How the climate of Aleppo, Syria compares with Beirut, Lebanon: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Beirut is 1.9 °C (3.4 °F) warmer over the year: 20.0 °C against 18.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Beirut is 6.3 °C warmer (Aleppo 8.1 °C, Beirut 14.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aleppo 35.9 °C (July), Beirut 31.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Aleppo 2.8 °C, Beirut 8.8 °C.
  • Beirut gets 171% more rain and snow a year: 992 mm against 366 mm.
  • Aleppo gets about 57 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,999 h against 2,942 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Aleppo 2.4 cm, Beirut 0 cm (ERA5-Land).
