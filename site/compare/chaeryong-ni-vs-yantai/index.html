Main differences

  • Yantai, Shandong is 1.1 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 12.5 °C against 11.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Yantai, Shandong is 1.9 °C warmer (Chaeryŏng-ni -3.1 °C, Yantai, Shandong -1.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chaeryŏng-ni 28.5 °C (August), Yantai, Shandong 28.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Chaeryŏng-ni -7.9 °C, Yantai, Shandong -4.3 °C.
  • Chaeryŏng-ni gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 812 mm against 690 mm.
  • Yantai, Shandong gets about 103 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,560 h against 2,457 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chaeryŏng-ni 26 cm, Yantai, Shandong 28 cm (ERA5-Land).
