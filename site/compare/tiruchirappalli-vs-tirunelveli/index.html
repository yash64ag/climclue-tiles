Tiruchirappalli vs Tirunelveli climate

How the climate of Tiruchirappalli, India compares with Tirunelveli, India, 257 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Tiruchirappalli is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 29.1 °C against 28.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Tiruchirappalli is 1.7 °C warmer (Tiruchirappalli 31.4 °C, Tirunelveli 29.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Tiruchirappalli 38.7 °C (May), Tirunelveli 36.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Tiruchirappalli 19.9 °C, Tirunelveli 20.6 °C.
  • Tirunelveli gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 842 mm against 807 mm.
  • Tiruchirappalli gets about 92 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,493 h against 2,401 h; about 1961–1990).
