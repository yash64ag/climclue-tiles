How the climate of Tiruchirappalli, India compares with Tirunelveli, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tiruchirappalli is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 29.1 °C against 28.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Tiruchirappalli is 1.5 °C warmer (Tiruchirappalli 31.4 °C, Tirunelveli 29.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Tiruchirappalli 37.2 °C (May), Tirunelveli 35.0 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Tiruchirappalli 21.3 °C, Tirunelveli 22.0 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (819 mm and 823 mm).
  • Tiruchirappalli gets about 92 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,493 h against 2,401 h; about 1961–1990).
