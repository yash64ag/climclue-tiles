Incheon vs Seoul climate

How the climate of Incheon, South Korea compares with Seoul, South Korea, 27 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Incheon and Seoul have the same yearly average temperature (12.0 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Seoul is 1.3 °C warmer (Incheon 20.6 °C, Seoul 21.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Incheon 27.3 °C (August), Seoul 28.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Incheon -6.8 °C, Seoul -9.0 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,411 mm and 1,405 mm).
  • Incheon gets about 34 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,354 h against 2,320 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Incheon 24 cm, Seoul 27 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Seoul in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Incheon in 12 of 12 months
