Incheon vs Seoul climate

How the climate of Incheon, South Korea compares with Seoul, South Korea, 27 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Incheon is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 12.1 °C against 11.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Incheon is 1.5 °C warmer (Incheon 0.9 °C, Seoul -0.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Incheon 28.9 °C (August), Seoul 29.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Incheon -6.3 °C, Seoul -7.7 °C.
  • Seoul gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,443 mm against 1,414 mm.
  • Incheon gets about 34 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,354 h against 2,320 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Incheon 24 cm, Seoul 27 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Incheon in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Incheon in 12 of 12 months
