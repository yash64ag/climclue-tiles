Luoyang, Henan vs Zhengzhou climate

Main differences

  • Zhengzhou is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Zhengzhou is 0.3 °C warmer (Luoyang, Henan 15.8 °C, Zhengzhou 16.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Luoyang, Henan 32.6 °C (June), Zhengzhou 32.6 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Luoyang, Henan -4.4 °C, Zhengzhou -4.3 °C.
  • Zhengzhou gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 632 mm against 566 mm.
  • Zhengzhou gets about 75 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,257 h against 2,182 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Luoyang, Henan 14 cm, Zhengzhou 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
