Luoyang, Henan vs Zhengzhou climate

How the climate of Luoyang, Henan, China compares with Zhengzhou, China, 111 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Luoyang, Henan and Zhengzhou have the same yearly average temperature (15.2 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Zhengzhou is 0.3 °C warmer (Luoyang, Henan 15.8 °C, Zhengzhou 16.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Luoyang, Henan 32.1 °C (June), Zhengzhou 31.6 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Luoyang, Henan -4.1 °C, Zhengzhou -4.0 °C.
  • Zhengzhou gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 632 mm against 582 mm.
  • Zhengzhou gets about 75 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,257 h against 2,182 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Luoyang, Henan 14 cm, Zhengzhou 15 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Luoyang, Henan in 6 of 12 months (2 equal)
  • Drier: Luoyang, Henan in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Zhengzhou in 10 of 12 months
