Maseru vs Soweto climate

How the climate of Maseru, Lesotho compares with Soweto, South Africa, 341 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Soweto is 1.1 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 15.7 °C against 14.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Soweto is 2.5 °C warmer (Maseru 6.6 °C, Soweto 9.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maseru 26.7 °C (January), Soweto 24.9 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Maseru -0.6 °C, Soweto 2.7 °C.
  • Soweto gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 678 mm against 658 mm.
  • Soweto gets about 61 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,205 h against 3,144 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Soweto in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Soweto in 8 of 12 months
