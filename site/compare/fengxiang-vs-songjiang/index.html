Fengxiang vs Songjiang climate

How the climate of Fengxiang, China compares with Songjiang, China, 30 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fengxiang is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.7 °C against 16.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Fengxiang is 1.0 °C warmer (Fengxiang 7.8 °C, Songjiang 6.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fengxiang 31.4 °C (August), Songjiang 32.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fengxiang 1.6 °C, Songjiang 0.8 °C.
  • Songjiang gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,427 mm against 1,409 mm.
  • Songjiang gets about 16 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,952 h against 1,937 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fengxiang 5.5 cm, Songjiang 9.8 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Songjiang in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Songjiang in 9 of 12 months
