How the climate of Changzhou, China compares with Yixing, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yixing is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 16.7 °C against 16.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Yixing is 0.6 °C warmer (Changzhou 18.0 °C, Yixing 18.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changzhou 32.0 °C (July), Yixing 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changzhou -0.6 °C, Yixing -0.1 °C.
  • Changzhou gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,236 mm against 1,213 mm.
  • Changzhou gets about 35 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,017 h against 1,982 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changzhou 17 cm, Yixing 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
