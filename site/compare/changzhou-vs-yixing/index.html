Changzhou vs Yixing climate

How the climate of Changzhou, China compares with Yixing, China, 48 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yixing is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 16.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Yixing is 0.6 °C warmer (Changzhou 11.8 °C, Yixing 12.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changzhou 31.5 °C (July), Yixing 31.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changzhou -0.9 °C, Yixing 0.7 °C.
  • Yixing gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,234 mm against 1,226 mm.
  • Changzhou gets about 35 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,017 h against 1,982 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changzhou 18 cm, Yixing 21 cm (ERA5-Land).
