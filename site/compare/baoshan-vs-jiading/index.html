Baoshan, Shanghai vs Jiading climate

How the climate of Baoshan, Shanghai, China compares with Jiading, China, 23 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Baoshan, Shanghai and Jiading have the same yearly average temperature (16.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Baoshan, Shanghai is 0.3 °C warmer (Baoshan, Shanghai 13.2 °C, Jiading 12.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoshan, Shanghai 30.9 °C (July), Jiading 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baoshan, Shanghai 1.3 °C, Jiading 0.4 °C.
  • Jiading gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,411 mm against 1,358 mm.
  • Baoshan, Shanghai gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,986 h against 1,979 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoshan, Shanghai 7.6 cm, Jiading 9.2 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 5 months each
  • Drier: Baoshan, Shanghai in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Baoshan, Shanghai in 11 of 12 months
