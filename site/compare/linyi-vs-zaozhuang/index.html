Linyi vs Zaozhuang climate

How the climate of Linyi, China compares with Zaozhuang, China, 75 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zaozhuang is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 14.2 °C against 13.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Zaozhuang is 0.6 °C warmer (Linyi 14.3 °C, Zaozhuang 14.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Linyi 30.3 °C (July), Zaozhuang 30.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Linyi -5.1 °C, Zaozhuang -4.5 °C.
  • Linyi gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 835 mm against 761 mm.
  • Linyi gets about 25 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,362 h against 2,337 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Linyi 13 cm, Zaozhuang 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
