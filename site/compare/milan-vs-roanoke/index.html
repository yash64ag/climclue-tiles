How the climate of Milan, Italy compares with Roanoke, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Milan is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 14.8 °C against 14.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Milan is 2.3 °C warmer (Milan 10.9 °C, Roanoke 8.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Milan 30.8 °C (July), Roanoke 30.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Milan 1.2 °C, Roanoke -2.5 °C.
  • Milan gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 981 mm against 967 mm.
  • Roanoke gets about 533 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,465 h against 1,933 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Milan 28 cm, Roanoke 30 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Milan in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Roanoke in 10 of 12 months
