Milan vs Roanoke climate

How the climate of Milan, Italy compares with Roanoke, United States, 7,104 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Roanoke is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 13.7 °C against 13.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Roanoke is 1.1 °C warmer (Milan 21.8 °C, Roanoke 22.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Milan 30.4 °C (July), Roanoke 30.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Milan -0.6 °C, Roanoke -3.1 °C.
  • Milan gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 981 mm against 967 mm.
  • Roanoke gets about 533 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,465 h against 1,933 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Milan 28 cm, Roanoke 41 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Drier: even, 6 months each
