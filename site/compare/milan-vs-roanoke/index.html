Milan vs Roanoke climate

How the climate of Milan, Italy compares with Roanoke, United States, 7,104 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Milan is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 13.6 °C against 13.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Milan is 1.3 °C warmer (Milan 9.4 °C, Roanoke 8.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Milan 29.3 °C (July), Roanoke 29.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Milan -0.9 °C, Roanoke -2.1 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (985 mm and 990 mm).
  • Roanoke gets about 533 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,465 h against 1,933 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Milan 28 cm, Roanoke 41 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Milan in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Roanoke in 10 of 12 months
