Changzhou vs Yangzhou climate

How the climate of Changzhou, China compares with Yangzhou, China, 85 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Changzhou is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 16.3 °C against 15.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Changzhou is 0.7 °C warmer (Changzhou 3.4 °C, Yangzhou 2.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changzhou 31.5 °C (July), Yangzhou 31.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changzhou -0.9 °C, Yangzhou -1.6 °C.
  • Changzhou gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,226 mm against 1,084 mm.
  • Yangzhou gets about 56 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,074 h against 2,017 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changzhou 18 cm, Yangzhou 20 cm (ERA5-Land).
