Kirkuk vs Mosul climate

How the climate of Kirkuk, Iraq compares with Mosul, Iraq, 150 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kirkuk is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 22.1 °C against 21.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Kirkuk is 1.7 °C warmer (Kirkuk 27.1 °C, Mosul 25.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kirkuk 42.6 °C (July), Mosul 42.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kirkuk 3.6 °C, Mosul 2.6 °C.
  • Kirkuk gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 457 mm against 430 mm.
  • Kirkuk gets about 122 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,318 h against 3,196 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kirkuk 1.7 cm, Mosul 3.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
