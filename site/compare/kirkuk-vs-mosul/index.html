How the climate of Kirkuk, Iraq compares with Mosul, Iraq: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kirkuk is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 21.8 °C against 21.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Kirkuk is 1.2 °C warmer (Kirkuk 26.7 °C, Mosul 25.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kirkuk 43.7 °C (July), Mosul 44.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kirkuk 3.4 °C, Mosul 2.3 °C.
  • Kirkuk gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 498 mm against 435 mm.
  • Kirkuk gets about 122 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,318 h against 3,196 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kirkuk 0.2 cm, Mosul 1.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
