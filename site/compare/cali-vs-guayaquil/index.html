How the climate of Cali, Colombia compares with Guayaquil, Ecuador: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Guayaquil is 2.4 °C (4.4 °F) warmer over the year: 24.6 °C against 22.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Guayaquil is 3.7 °C warmer (Cali 22.3 °C, Guayaquil 26.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cali 28.2 °C (August), Guayaquil 31.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Cali 16.5 °C, Guayaquil 16.8 °C.
  • Guayaquil gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,314 mm against 1,251 mm.
  • Cali gets about 627 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,711 h against 1,084 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Guayaquil in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Guayaquil in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Cali in 11 of 12 months
