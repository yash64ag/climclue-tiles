Harare vs Lichinga climate

How the climate of Harare, Zimbabwe compares with Lichinga, Mozambique, 673 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lichinga is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 18.8 °C against 18.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Lichinga is 1.5 °C warmer (Harare 13.9 °C, Lichinga 15.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Harare 27.4 °C (October), Lichinga 26.9 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Harare 7.6 °C, Lichinga 9.8 °C.
  • Lichinga gets 50% more rain and snow a year: 1,196 mm against 795 mm.
  • Harare gets about 440 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,944 h against 2,504 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Lichinga in 7 of 12 months (2 equal)
  • Drier: Harare in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Harare in 12 of 12 months
