Nagano vs Queens climate

How the climate of Nagano, Japan compares with Queens, United States, 10,817 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Queens is 0.9 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 12.3 °C against 11.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Queens is 1.8 °C warmer (Nagano 19.4 °C, Queens 21.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nagano 28.0 °C (August), Queens 27.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nagano -6.3 °C, Queens -3.2 °C.
  • Nagano gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,391 mm against 1,199 mm.
  • Queens gets about 720 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,523 h against 1,803 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nagano 466 cm, Queens 83 cm (ERA5-Land).
