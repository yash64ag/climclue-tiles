Nagano vs Queens climate

How the climate of Nagano, Japan compares with Queens, United States, 10,817 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Nagano is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 12.4 °C against 12.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Nagano is 1.6 °C warmer (Nagano 25.3 °C, Queens 23.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nagano 30.0 °C (August), Queens 28.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nagano -4.7 °C, Queens -3.3 °C.
  • Nagano gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,237 mm against 1,199 mm.
  • Queens gets about 720 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,523 h against 1,803 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nagano 194 cm, Queens 73 cm (ERA5-Land).
