How the climate of Nagano, Japan compares with Queens, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Queens is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 12.7 °C against 12.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Queens is 1.2 °C warmer (Nagano 4.3 °C, Queens 5.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nagano 30.0 °C (August), Queens 28.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nagano -4.7 °C, Queens -3.0 °C.
  • Nagano gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,237 mm against 1,199 mm.
  • Queens gets about 720 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,523 h against 1,803 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nagano 194 cm, Queens 55 cm (ERA5-Land).
