How the climate of Fukuyama, Japan compares with Yichang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yichang is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 18.0 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Yichang is 3.2 °C warmer (Fukuyama 19.8 °C, Yichang 23.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fukuyama 33.2 °C (August), Yichang 32.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fukuyama 2.3 °C, Yichang 2.4 °C.
  • Fukuyama gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,283 mm against 1,225 mm.
  • Fukuyama gets about 356 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,984 h against 1,628 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fukuyama 6.0 cm, Yichang 8.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
