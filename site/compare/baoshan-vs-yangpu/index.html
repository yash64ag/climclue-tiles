How the climate of Baoshan, Shanghai, China compares with Yangpu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Baoshan, Shanghai is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 17.2 °C against 17.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Baoshan, Shanghai is 0.2 °C warmer (Baoshan, Shanghai 10.1 °C, Yangpu 9.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoshan, Shanghai 32.0 °C (July), Yangpu 31.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baoshan, Shanghai 1.9 °C, Yangpu 1.9 °C.
  • Yangpu gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,430 mm against 1,400 mm.
  • Baoshan, Shanghai gets about 14 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,986 h against 1,973 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoshan, Shanghai 7.1 cm, Yangpu 6.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
