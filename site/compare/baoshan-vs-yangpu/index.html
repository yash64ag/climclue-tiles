Baoshan, Shanghai vs Yangpu climate

How the climate of Baoshan, Shanghai, China compares with Yangpu, China, 16 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yangpu is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Yangpu is 0.4 °C warmer (Baoshan, Shanghai 7.0 °C, Yangpu 7.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoshan, Shanghai 30.9 °C (July), Yangpu 30.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baoshan, Shanghai 1.3 °C, Yangpu 1.5 °C.
  • Yangpu gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,431 mm against 1,358 mm.
  • Baoshan, Shanghai gets about 14 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,986 h against 1,973 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoshan, Shanghai 7.1 cm, Yangpu 6.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
