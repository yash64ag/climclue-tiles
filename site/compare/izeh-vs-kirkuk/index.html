Īẕeh vs Kirkuk climate

Main differences

  • Kirkuk is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 21.8 °C against 20.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Kirkuk is 1.8 °C warmer (Īẕeh 34.0 °C, Kirkuk 35.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Īẕeh 42.4 °C (July), Kirkuk 43.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Īẕeh 0.9 °C, Kirkuk 3.4 °C.
  • Īẕeh gets 63% more rain and snow a year: 812 mm against 498 mm.
  • Kirkuk gets about 369 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,318 h against 2,948 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Īẕeh 11 cm, Kirkuk 0.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
