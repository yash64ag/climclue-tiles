How the climate of Fuzhou, Jiangxi, China compares with Yichun, Jiangxi, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Fuzhou, Jiangxi is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 19.1 °C against 18.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Fuzhou, Jiangxi is 1.1 °C warmer (Fuzhou, Jiangxi 29.8 °C, Yichun, Jiangxi 28.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 34.9 °C (July), Yichun, Jiangxi 34.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 3.1 °C, Yichun, Jiangxi 2.3 °C.
  • Fuzhou, Jiangxi gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,977 mm against 1,797 mm.
  • Fuzhou, Jiangxi gets about 102 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,823 h against 1,721 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 6.6 cm, Yichun, Jiangxi 7.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
