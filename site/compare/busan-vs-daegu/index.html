Busan vs Daegu climate

How the climate of Busan, South Korea compares with Daegu, South Korea, 94 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Busan is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 14.9 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Busan is 4.2 °C warmer (Busan 6.5 °C, Daegu 2.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Busan 29.0 °C (August), Daegu 31.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Busan -0.1 °C, Daegu -5.3 °C.
  • Busan gets 67% more rain and snow a year: 1,638 mm against 979 mm.
  • Daegu gets about 194 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,483 h against 2,289 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Busan 6.1 cm, Daegu 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
