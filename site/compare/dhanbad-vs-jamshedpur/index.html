Dhanbad vs Jamshedpur climate

How the climate of Dhanbad, India compares with Jamshedpur, India, 113 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jamshedpur is 0.9 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 25.9 °C against 24.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Jamshedpur is 1.8 °C warmer (Dhanbad 16.4 °C, Jamshedpur 18.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dhanbad 37.2 °C (May), Jamshedpur 37.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Dhanbad 10.2 °C, Jamshedpur 11.6 °C.
  • Dhanbad gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,564 mm against 1,468 mm.
  • Jamshedpur gets about 42 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,228 h against 2,186 h; about 1961–1990).
