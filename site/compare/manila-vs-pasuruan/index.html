Manila vs Pasuruan climate

How the climate of Manila, Philippines compares with Pasuruan, Indonesia, 2,629 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Manila is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 27.8 °C against 27.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Manila is 1.9 °C warmer (Manila 29.5 °C, Pasuruan 27.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manila 34.9 °C (April), Pasuruan 33.4 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Manila 21.6 °C, Pasuruan 22.6 °C.
  • Manila gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 2,057 mm against 1,828 mm.
  • Pasuruan gets about 936 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,340 h against 2,404 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
