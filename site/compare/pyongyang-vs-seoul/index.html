Pyongyang vs Seoul climate

How the climate of Pyongyang, North Korea compares with Seoul, South Korea, 195 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Seoul is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 12.0 °C against 11.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Seoul is 2.2 °C warmer (Pyongyang -5.5 °C, Seoul -3.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pyongyang 28.2 °C (August), Seoul 28.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Pyongyang -11.5 °C, Seoul -9.0 °C.
  • Seoul gets 50% more rain and snow a year: 1,405 mm against 938 mm.
  • Pyongyang gets about 107 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,427 h against 2,320 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Pyongyang 26 cm, Seoul 27 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Pyongyang in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Pyongyang in 12 of 12 months
