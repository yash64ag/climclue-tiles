Abu Dhabi vs Dammam climate

How the climate of Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates compares with Dammam, Saudi Arabia, 484 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Abu Dhabi is 1.5 °C (2.6 °F) warmer over the year: 27.6 °C against 26.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Abu Dhabi is 3.3 °C warmer (Abu Dhabi 19.4 °C, Dammam 16.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abu Dhabi 40.4 °C (August), Dammam 40.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Abu Dhabi 14.3 °C, Dammam 11.5 °C.
  • Dammam gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 90 mm against 71 mm.
  • Abu Dhabi gets about 145 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,443 h against 3,298 h; about 1961–1990).
