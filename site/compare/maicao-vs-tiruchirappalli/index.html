Maicao vs Tiruchirappalli climate

How the climate of Maicao, Colombia compares with Tiruchirappalli, India, 15,965 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tiruchirappalli is 1.7 °C (3.1 °F) warmer over the year: 29.1 °C against 27.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Tiruchirappalli is 4.5 °C warmer (Maicao 28.1 °C, Tiruchirappalli 32.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maicao 32.5 °C (August), Tiruchirappalli 38.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Maicao 23.1 °C, Tiruchirappalli 19.9 °C.
  • Maicao gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 839 mm against 807 mm.
  • Tiruchirappalli gets about 41 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,493 h against 2,452 h; about 1961–1990).
