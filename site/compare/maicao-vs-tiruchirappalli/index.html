How the climate of Maicao, Colombia compares with Tiruchirappalli, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tiruchirappalli is 1.5 °C (2.8 °F) warmer over the year: 29.1 °C against 27.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Tiruchirappalli is 4.4 °C warmer (Maicao 28.2 °C, Tiruchirappalli 32.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maicao 33.5 °C (August), Tiruchirappalli 37.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Maicao 22.0 °C, Tiruchirappalli 21.3 °C.
  • Tiruchirappalli gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 819 mm against 794 mm.
  • Tiruchirappalli gets about 41 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,493 h against 2,452 h; about 1961–1990).
