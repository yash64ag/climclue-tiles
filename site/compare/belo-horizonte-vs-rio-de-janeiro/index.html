Belo Horizonte vs Rio de Janeiro climate

How the climate of Belo Horizonte, Brazil compares with Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, 341 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rio de Janeiro is 2.9 °C (5.3 °F) warmer over the year: 24.0 °C against 21.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Rio de Janeiro is 3.9 °C warmer (Belo Horizonte 23.3 °C, Rio de Janeiro 27.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belo Horizonte 28.4 °C (February), Rio de Janeiro 31.5 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Belo Horizonte 11.7 °C, Rio de Janeiro 16.6 °C.
  • Belo Horizonte gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 1,615 mm against 1,310 mm.
  • Belo Horizonte gets about 293 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,383 h against 2,091 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Rio de Janeiro in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Belo Horizonte in 9 of 12 months
