Belo Horizonte vs Rio de Janeiro climate

How the climate of Belo Horizonte, Brazil compares with Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, 341 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rio de Janeiro is 3.4 °C (6.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.8 °C against 20.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Rio de Janeiro is 4.4 °C warmer (Belo Horizonte 22.6 °C, Rio de Janeiro 26.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belo Horizonte 26.7 °C (February), Rio de Janeiro 30.1 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Belo Horizonte 11.4 °C, Rio de Janeiro 17.4 °C.
  • Belo Horizonte gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 1,697 mm against 1,368 mm.
  • Belo Horizonte gets about 293 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,383 h against 2,091 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Rio de Janeiro in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Belo Horizonte in 9 of 12 months
