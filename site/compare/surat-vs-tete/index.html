Surat vs Tete climate

How the climate of Surat, India compares with Tete, Mozambique, 5,964 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Surat is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Surat is 6.8 °C warmer (Surat 29.7 °C, Tete 22.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Surat 33.9 °C (April), Tete 36.1 °C (November). Coldest-month night lows: Surat 17.3 °C, Tete 17.1 °C.
  • Surat gets 116% more rain and snow a year: 1,412 mm against 654 mm.
  • Tete gets about 126 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,991 h against 2,865 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
