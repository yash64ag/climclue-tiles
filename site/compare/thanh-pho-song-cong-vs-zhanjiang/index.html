How the climate of Thành phố Sông Công, Vietnam compares with Zhanjiang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Thành phố Sông Công and Zhanjiang have the same yearly average temperature (23.8 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Thành phố Sông Công is 0.4 °C warmer (Thành phố Sông Công 29.0 °C, Zhanjiang 28.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Thành phố Sông Công 32.7 °C (July), Zhanjiang 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Thành phố Sông Công 13.4 °C, Zhanjiang 13.3 °C.
  • Thành phố Sông Công gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 2,013 mm against 1,982 mm.
  • Zhanjiang gets about 290 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,920 h against 1,630 h; about 1961–1990).
