Gustavo Adolfo Madero vs Santiago de Querétaro climate

How the climate of Gustavo Adolfo Madero, Mexico compares with Santiago de Querétaro, Mexico, 181 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Santiago de Querétaro is 1.9 °C (3.4 °F) warmer over the year: 17.9 °C against 16.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Santiago de Querétaro is 2.9 °C warmer (Gustavo Adolfo Madero 18.4 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 21.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gustavo Adolfo Madero 24.4 °C (April), Santiago de Querétaro 28.1 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Gustavo Adolfo Madero 5.7 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 7.1 °C.
  • Gustavo Adolfo Madero gets 31% more rain and snow a year: 719 mm against 549 mm.
  • Santiago de Querétaro gets about 531 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,547 h against 2,016 h; about 1961–1990).
