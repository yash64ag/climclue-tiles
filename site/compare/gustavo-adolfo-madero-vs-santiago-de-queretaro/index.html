Main differences

  • Santiago de Querétaro is 2.4 °C (4.3 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 16.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Santiago de Querétaro is 3.2 °C warmer (Gustavo Adolfo Madero 18.7 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 21.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gustavo Adolfo Madero 27.4 °C (April), Santiago de Querétaro 30.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Gustavo Adolfo Madero 3.4 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 5.8 °C.
  • Gustavo Adolfo Madero gets 47% more rain and snow a year: 670 mm against 456 mm.
  • Santiago de Querétaro gets about 531 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,547 h against 2,016 h; about 1961–1990).
