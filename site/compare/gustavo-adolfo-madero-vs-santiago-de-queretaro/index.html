Gustavo Adolfo Madero vs Santiago de Querétaro climate

How the climate of Gustavo Adolfo Madero, Mexico compares with Santiago de Querétaro, Mexico, 181 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Santiago de Querétaro is 2.8 °C (5.1 °F) warmer over the year: 19.1 °C against 16.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Santiago de Querétaro is 3.5 °C warmer (Gustavo Adolfo Madero 18.9 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 22.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gustavo Adolfo Madero 27.4 °C (April), Santiago de Querétaro 30.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Gustavo Adolfo Madero 4.0 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 7.1 °C.
  • Gustavo Adolfo Madero gets 47% more rain and snow a year: 670 mm against 456 mm.
  • Santiago de Querétaro gets about 531 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,547 h against 2,016 h; about 1961–1990).
