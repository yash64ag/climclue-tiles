Main differences

  • Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 10.3 °C against 10.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Bacău is 2.1 °C warmer (Bacău -2.3 °C, Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) -4.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bacău 26.1 °C (July), Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 27.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bacău -5.9 °C, Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) -9.8 °C.
  • Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 571 mm against 546 mm.
  • Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) gets about 511 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,387 h against 1,876 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bacău 60 cm, Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 35 cm (ERA5-Land).
