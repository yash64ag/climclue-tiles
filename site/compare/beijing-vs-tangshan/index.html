Beijing vs Tangshan climate

How the climate of Beijing, China compares with Tangshan, China, 155 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Beijing is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 13.0 °C against 12.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Beijing is 2.1 °C warmer (Beijing 26.2 °C, Tangshan 24.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Beijing 32.5 °C (June), Tangshan 30.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Beijing -9.5 °C, Tangshan -10.3 °C.
  • Tangshan gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 630 mm against 563 mm.
  • Beijing gets about 33 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,704 h against 2,672 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Beijing 12 cm, Tangshan 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
