How the climate of Fengxiang, China compares with Osaka, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Fengxiang and Osaka have the same yearly average temperature (16.8 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Osaka is 0.9 °C warmer (Fengxiang 28.0 °C, Osaka 28.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fengxiang 31.5 °C (July), Osaka 33.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Fengxiang 1.7 °C, Osaka 2.0 °C.
  • Osaka gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,418 mm against 1,409 mm.
  • Fengxiang gets about 84 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,937 h against 1,852 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fengxiang 5.5 cm, Osaka 4.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
