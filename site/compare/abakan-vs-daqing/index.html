Abakan vs Daqing climate

How the climate of Abakan, Russia compares with Daqing, China, 2,493 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Daqing is 1.4 °C (2.6 °F) warmer over the year: 4.5 °C against 3.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Daqing is 3.8 °C warmer (Abakan 11.3 °C, Daqing 15.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abakan 26.2 °C (July), Daqing 27.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Abakan -21.6 °C, Daqing -23.9 °C.
  • Daqing gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 456 mm against 350 mm.
  • Daqing gets about 785 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,653 h against 1,868 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Abakan 49 cm, Daqing 19 cm (ERA5-Land).
