Abakan vs Daqing climate

How the climate of Abakan, Russia compares with Daqing, China, 2,493 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Daqing is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 4.5 °C against 3.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Daqing is 3.7 °C warmer (Abakan 11.3 °C, Daqing 15.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abakan 28.1 °C (July), Daqing 28.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Abakan -23.3 °C, Daqing -24.6 °C.
  • Daqing gets 32% more rain and snow a year: 460 mm against 348 mm.
  • Daqing gets about 785 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,653 h against 1,868 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Abakan 49 cm, Daqing 19 cm (ERA5-Land).
