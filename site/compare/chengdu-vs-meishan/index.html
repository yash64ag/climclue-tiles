How the climate of Chengdu, China compares with Meishan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Meishan is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 17.8 °C against 17.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Meishan is 1.2 °C warmer (Chengdu 7.7 °C, Meishan 8.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chengdu 29.8 °C (July), Meishan 30.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chengdu 3.1 °C, Meishan 4.5 °C.
  • Meishan gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 1,147 mm against 995 mm.
  • Meishan gets about 114 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,410 h against 1,296 h; about 1961–1990).
