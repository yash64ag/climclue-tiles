Cape Town vs Gqeberha climate

How the climate of Cape Town, South Africa compares with Gqeberha, South Africa, 663 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gqeberha is 2.5 °C (4.5 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 15.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Gqeberha is 3.1 °C warmer (Cape Town 12.7 °C, Gqeberha 15.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cape Town 21.8 °C (February), Gqeberha 23.3 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Cape Town 10.3 °C, Gqeberha 12.9 °C.
  • Cape Town gets 69% more rain and snow a year: 941 mm against 558 mm.
  • Cape Town gets about 219 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,958 h against 2,739 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Gqeberha in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Cape Town in 8 of 12 months
