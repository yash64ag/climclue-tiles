How the climate of Ganzhou, China compares with Yichun, Jiangxi, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ganzhou is 1.8 °C (3.2 °F) warmer over the year: 20.0 °C against 18.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Ganzhou is 2.3 °C warmer (Ganzhou 10.4 °C, Yichun, Jiangxi 8.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ganzhou 35.0 °C (July), Yichun, Jiangxi 34.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ganzhou 4.4 °C, Yichun, Jiangxi 2.3 °C.
  • Yichun, Jiangxi gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,797 mm against 1,635 mm.
  • Ganzhou gets about 82 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,802 h against 1,721 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ganzhou 0.2 cm, Yichun, Jiangxi 7.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
