How the climate of Ch’ŏngdan-ŭp, North Korea compares with Incheon, South Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Incheon is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 12.1 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Incheon is 0.8 °C warmer (Ch’ŏngdan-ŭp -2.9 °C, Incheon -2.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ch’ŏngdan-ŭp 28.9 °C (August), Incheon 28.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Ch’ŏngdan-ŭp -7.4 °C, Incheon -6.3 °C.
  • Incheon gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 1,414 mm against 1,159 mm.
  • Ch’ŏngdan-ŭp gets about 71 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,425 h against 2,354 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ch’ŏngdan-ŭp 21 cm, Incheon 24 cm (ERA5-Land).
