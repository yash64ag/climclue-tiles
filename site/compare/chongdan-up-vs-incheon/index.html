Ch’ŏngdan-ŭp vs Incheon climate

How the climate of Ch’ŏngdan-ŭp, North Korea compares with Incheon, South Korea, 88 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Incheon is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 12.0 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Incheon is 0.6 °C warmer (Ch’ŏngdan-ŭp 4.3 °C, Incheon 5.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ch’ŏngdan-ŭp 27.0 °C (August), Incheon 27.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Ch’ŏngdan-ŭp -6.9 °C, Incheon -6.8 °C.
  • Incheon gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 1,411 mm against 1,173 mm.
  • Ch’ŏngdan-ŭp gets about 71 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,425 h against 2,354 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ch’ŏngdan-ŭp 21 cm, Incheon 24 cm (ERA5-Land).
