How the climate of Almaty, Kazakhstan compares with Shymkent, Kazakhstan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Shymkent is 2.1 °C (3.8 °F) warmer over the year: 12.5 °C against 10.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Shymkent is 3.1 °C warmer (Almaty 3.5 °C, Shymkent 6.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Almaty 30.4 °C (July), Shymkent 34.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Almaty -9.1 °C, Shymkent -5.9 °C.
  • Almaty gets 52% more rain and snow a year: 926 mm against 608 mm.
  • Shymkent gets about 352 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,812 h against 2,460 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Almaty 145 cm, Shymkent 134 cm (ERA5-Land).
