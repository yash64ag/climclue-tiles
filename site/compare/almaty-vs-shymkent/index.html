Almaty vs Shymkent climate

How the climate of Almaty, Kazakhstan compares with Shymkent, Kazakhstan, 606 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shymkent is 3.2 °C (5.8 °F) warmer over the year: 12.3 °C against 9.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Shymkent is 4.4 °C warmer (Almaty -6.3 °C, Shymkent -1.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Almaty 27.2 °C (July), Shymkent 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Almaty -9.7 °C, Shymkent -6.7 °C.
  • Almaty gets 59% more rain and snow a year: 1,006 mm against 631 mm.
  • Shymkent gets about 352 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,812 h against 2,460 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Almaty 282 cm, Shymkent 145 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Shymkent in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Shymkent in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Shymkent in 10 of 12 months
