Ahmedabad vs Rājkot climate

How the climate of Ahmedabad, India compares with Rājkot, India, 201 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ahmedabad is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 27.1 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Ahmedabad is 1.7 °C warmer (Ahmedabad 33.4 °C, Rājkot 31.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ahmedabad 40.0 °C (May), Rājkot 39.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Ahmedabad 13.6 °C, Rājkot 13.4 °C.
  • Ahmedabad gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 874 mm against 741 mm.
  • Rājkot gets about 73 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,075 h against 3,001 h; about 1961–1990).
