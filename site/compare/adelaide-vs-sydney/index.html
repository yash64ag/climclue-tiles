Adelaide vs Sydney climate

How the climate of Adelaide, Australia compares with Sydney, Australia, 1,162 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Sydney is 1.6 °C (2.9 °F) warmer over the year: 17.8 °C against 16.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Sydney is 2.3 °C warmer (Adelaide 13.4 °C, Sydney 15.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Adelaide 27.9 °C (February), Sydney 25.6 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Adelaide 7.1 °C, Sydney 8.4 °C.
  • Sydney gets 55% more rain and snow a year: 1,097 mm against 705 mm.
  • Adelaide gets about 195 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,682 h against 2,488 h; about 1961–1990).
