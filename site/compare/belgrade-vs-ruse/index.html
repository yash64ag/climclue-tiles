Belgrade vs Ruse climate

How the climate of Belgrade, Serbia compares with Ruse, Bulgaria, 449 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ruse is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 12.6 °C against 12.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Ruse is 1.2 °C warmer (Belgrade 23.0 °C, Ruse 24.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belgrade 28.7 °C (July), Ruse 30.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Belgrade -2.2 °C, Ruse -3.7 °C.
  • Belgrade gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 702 mm against 592 mm.
  • Ruse gets about 120 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,112 h against 1,991 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Belgrade 45 cm, Ruse 67 cm (ERA5-Land).
