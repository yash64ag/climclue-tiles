Belgrade vs Ruse climate

How the climate of Belgrade, Serbia compares with Ruse, Bulgaria, 449 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Belgrade and Ruse have the same yearly average temperature (12.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Belgrade is 1.0 °C warmer (Belgrade 0.8 °C, Ruse -0.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belgrade 28.2 °C (July), Ruse 28.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Belgrade -2.4 °C, Ruse -3.5 °C.
  • Belgrade gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 684 mm against 595 mm.
  • Ruse gets about 120 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,112 h against 1,991 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Belgrade 61 cm, Ruse 95 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Drier: Ruse in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Ruse in 11 of 12 months
