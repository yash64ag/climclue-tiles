How the climate of Arica, Chile compares with Callao, Peru: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Arica is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 18.8 °C against 18.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Arica is 1.8 °C warmer (Arica 23.2 °C, Callao 21.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Arica 28.4 °C (February), Callao 26.2 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Arica 11.2 °C, Callao 12.8 °C.
  • Arica gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 28 mm against 21 mm.
  • Arica gets about 1,208 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,713 h against 1,505 h; about 1961–1990).
