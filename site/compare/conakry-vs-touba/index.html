Conakry vs Touba climate

How the climate of Conakry, Guinea compares with Touba, Senegal, 638 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Touba is 1.1 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 26.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Touba is 3.1 °C warmer (Conakry 25.5 °C, Touba 28.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Conakry 32.0 °C (December), Touba 37.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Conakry 20.9 °C, Touba 15.1 °C.
  • Conakry gets 652% more rain and snow a year: 3,517 mm against 468 mm.
  • Touba gets about 847 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,032 h against 2,185 h; about 1961–1990).
