Kharkiv vs Odesa climate

How the climate of Kharkiv, Ukraine compares with Odesa, Ukraine, 563 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Odesa is 2.9 °C (5.2 °F) warmer over the year: 11.0 °C against 8.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Odesa is 5.1 °C warmer (Kharkiv 0.8 °C, Odesa 5.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kharkiv 25.9 °C (July), Odesa 26.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kharkiv -7.8 °C, Odesa -2.8 °C.
  • Kharkiv gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 528 mm against 474 mm.
  • Odesa gets about 372 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,124 h against 1,752 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kharkiv 140 cm, Odesa 59 cm (ERA5-Land).
