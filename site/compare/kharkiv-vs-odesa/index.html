How the climate of Kharkiv, Ukraine compares with Odesa, Ukraine: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Odesa is 3.0 °C (5.3 °F) warmer over the year: 11.0 °C against 8.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Odesa is 5.1 °C warmer (Kharkiv 0.8 °C, Odesa 5.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kharkiv 26.1 °C (July), Odesa 27.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kharkiv -7.4 °C, Odesa -2.7 °C.
  • Kharkiv gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 526 mm against 458 mm.
  • Odesa gets about 372 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,124 h against 1,752 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kharkiv 105 cm, Odesa 62 cm (ERA5-Land).
