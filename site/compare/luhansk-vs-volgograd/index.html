How the climate of Luhansk, Ukraine compares with Volgograd, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Volgograd is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 9.1 °C against 9.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Luhansk is 2.2 °C warmer (Luhansk -3.6 °C, Volgograd -5.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Luhansk 28.7 °C (July), Volgograd 30.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Luhansk -7.0 °C, Volgograd -9.1 °C.
  • Luhansk gets 32% more rain and snow a year: 511 mm against 387 mm.
  • Volgograd gets about 97 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,116 h against 2,020 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Luhansk 102 cm, Volgograd 99 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Volgograd in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Volgograd in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Volgograd in 11 of 12 months
