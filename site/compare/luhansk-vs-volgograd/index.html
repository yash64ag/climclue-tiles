Luhansk vs Volgograd climate

How the climate of Luhansk, Ukraine compares with Volgograd, Russia, 382 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Luhansk is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 9.0 °C against 8.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Luhansk is 2.0 °C warmer (Luhansk -4.2 °C, Volgograd -6.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Luhansk 28.0 °C (July), Volgograd 30.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Luhansk -7.3 °C, Volgograd -9.6 °C.
  • Luhansk gets 32% more rain and snow a year: 511 mm against 387 mm.
  • Volgograd gets about 97 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,116 h against 2,020 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Luhansk 102 cm, Volgograd 99 cm (ERA5-Land).
