Luhansk vs Volgograd climate

How the climate of Luhansk, Ukraine compares with Volgograd, Russia, 382 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Luhansk is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 8.8 °C against 8.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Luhansk is 2.0 °C warmer (Luhansk -4.3 °C, Volgograd -6.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Luhansk 27.2 °C (July), Volgograd 28.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Luhansk -7.4 °C, Volgograd -9.1 °C.
  • Luhansk gets 32% more rain and snow a year: 543 mm against 411 mm.
  • Volgograd gets about 97 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,116 h against 2,020 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Luhansk 102 cm, Volgograd 99 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Sunnier: Volgograd in 11 of 12 months
