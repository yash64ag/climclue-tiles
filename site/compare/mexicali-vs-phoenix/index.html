Mexicali vs Phoenix climate

How the climate of Mexicali, Mexico compares with Phoenix, United States, 328 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mexicali is 1.1 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 24.5 °C against 23.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Mexicali is 3.1 °C warmer (Mexicali 15.2 °C, Phoenix 12.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mexicali 43.7 °C (July), Phoenix 42.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Mexicali 6.2 °C, Phoenix 4.2 °C.
  • Phoenix gets 178% more rain and snow a year: 217 mm against 78 mm.
  • Phoenix gets about 163 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,815 h against 3,652 h; about 1961–1990).
