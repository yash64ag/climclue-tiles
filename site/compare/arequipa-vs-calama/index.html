Arequipa vs Calama climate

How the climate of Arequipa, Peru compares with Calama, Chile, 727 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Arequipa is 0.9 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 14.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Arequipa is 3.5 °C warmer (Arequipa 14.2 °C, Calama 10.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Arequipa 21.9 °C (October), Calama 23.6 °C (December). Coldest-month night lows: Arequipa 7.9 °C, Calama 2.4 °C.
  • Arequipa gets 448% more rain and snow a year: 168 mm against 31 mm.
  • Arequipa gets about 191 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,178 h against 2,987 h; about 1961–1990).
