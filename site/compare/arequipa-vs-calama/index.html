Arequipa vs Calama climate

How the climate of Arequipa, Peru compares with Calama, Chile, 727 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Calama is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 14.4 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Calama is 2.9 °C warmer (Arequipa 13.8 °C, Calama 16.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Arequipa 20.8 °C (October), Calama 23.5 °C (November). Coldest-month night lows: Arequipa 6.6 °C, Calama 2.7 °C.
  • Arequipa gets 541% more rain and snow a year: 208 mm against 33 mm.
  • Arequipa gets about 191 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,178 h against 2,987 h; about 1961–1990).
