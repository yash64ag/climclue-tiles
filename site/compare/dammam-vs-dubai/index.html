Dammam vs Dubai climate

How the climate of Dammam, Saudi Arabia compares with Dubai, United Arab Emirates, 543 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dubai is 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) warmer over the year: 27.6 °C against 26.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Dubai is 2.7 °C warmer (Dammam 17.3 °C, Dubai 19.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dammam 38.4 °C (July), Dubai 43.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Dammam 13.0 °C, Dubai 13.2 °C.
  • Dubai gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 116 mm against 89 mm.
  • Dubai gets about 152 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,449 h against 3,298 h; about 1961–1990).
