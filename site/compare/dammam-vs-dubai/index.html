Dammam vs Dubai climate

How the climate of Dammam, Saudi Arabia compares with Dubai, United Arab Emirates, 543 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dubai is 1.6 °C (2.9 °F) warmer over the year: 27.7 °C against 26.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Dubai is 2.9 °C warmer (Dammam 16.1 °C, Dubai 18.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dammam 40.6 °C (July), Dubai 41.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Dammam 11.5 °C, Dubai 13.6 °C.
  • Dubai gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 113 mm against 90 mm.
  • Dubai gets about 152 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,449 h against 3,298 h; about 1961–1990).
