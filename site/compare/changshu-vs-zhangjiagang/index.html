How the climate of Changshu, China compares with Zhangjiagang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Changshu is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.3 °C against 16.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Changshu is 0.4 °C warmer (Changshu 3.8 °C, Zhangjiagang 3.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changshu 31.8 °C (July), Zhangjiagang 31.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changshu 0.0 °C, Zhangjiagang -0.4 °C.
  • Changshu gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,268 mm against 1,192 mm.
  • Zhangjiagang gets about 22 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,026 h against 2,004 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changshu 12 cm, Zhangjiagang 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
