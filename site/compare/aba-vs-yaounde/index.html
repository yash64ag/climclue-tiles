How the climate of Aba, Nigeria compares with Yaoundé, Cameroon: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Aba is 1.7 °C (3.1 °F) warmer over the year: 25.6 °C against 23.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Aba is 2.4 °C warmer (Aba 26.3 °C, Yaoundé 23.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aba 32.8 °C (February), Yaoundé 31.0 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Aba 20.8 °C, Yaoundé 15.5 °C.
  • Aba gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 2,485 mm against 1,794 mm.
  • Yaoundé gets about 265 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,763 h against 1,498 h; about 1961–1990).
