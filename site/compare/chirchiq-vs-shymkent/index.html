How the climate of Chirchiq, Uzbekistan compares with Shymkent, Kazakhstan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Chirchiq and Shymkent have the same yearly average temperature (12.5 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Shymkent is 0.6 °C warmer (Chirchiq 24.3 °C, Shymkent 24.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chirchiq 33.6 °C (July), Shymkent 34.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chirchiq -5.6 °C, Shymkent -5.9 °C.
  • Chirchiq gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 783 mm against 608 mm.
  • Shymkent gets about 37 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,812 h against 2,775 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chirchiq 117 cm, Shymkent 134 cm (ERA5-Land).
