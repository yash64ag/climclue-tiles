Chirchiq vs Shymkent climate

How the climate of Chirchiq, Uzbekistan compares with Shymkent, Kazakhstan, 94 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Chirchiq is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 12.6 °C against 12.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Chirchiq is 0.8 °C warmer (Chirchiq 0.9 °C, Shymkent 0.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chirchiq 32.1 °C (July), Shymkent 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chirchiq -6.9 °C, Shymkent -6.7 °C.
  • Chirchiq gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 739 mm against 631 mm.
  • Shymkent gets about 37 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,812 h against 2,775 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chirchiq 147 cm, Shymkent 145 cm (ERA5-Land).
