How the climate of Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia compares with Pekanbaru, Indonesia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kuala Lumpur is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.7 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Kuala Lumpur is 0.4 °C warmer (Kuala Lumpur 26.9 °C, Pekanbaru 26.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kuala Lumpur 32.0 °C (March), Pekanbaru 32.4 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kuala Lumpur 21.4 °C, Pekanbaru 20.8 °C.
  • Kuala Lumpur gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 2,940 mm against 2,786 mm.
  • Pekanbaru gets about 16 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,230 h against 2,214 h; about 1961–1990).
