Kuala Lumpur vs Pekanbaru climate

How the climate of Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia compares with Pekanbaru, Indonesia, 293 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kuala Lumpur is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Kuala Lumpur is 0.3 °C warmer (Kuala Lumpur 26.9 °C, Pekanbaru 26.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kuala Lumpur 30.6 °C (March), Pekanbaru 30.6 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kuala Lumpur 21.7 °C, Pekanbaru 22.4 °C.
  • Kuala Lumpur gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 2,938 mm against 2,794 mm.
  • Pekanbaru gets about 16 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,230 h against 2,214 h; about 1961–1990).
