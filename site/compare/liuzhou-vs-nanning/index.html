How the climate of Liuzhou, China compares with Nanning, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Nanning is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 22.6 °C against 21.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Nanning is 2.6 °C warmer (Liuzhou 11.2 °C, Nanning 13.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Liuzhou 33.5 °C (August), Nanning 33.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Liuzhou 7.3 °C, Nanning 10.4 °C.
  • Liuzhou gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,503 mm against 1,426 mm.
  • Nanning gets about 116 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,684 h against 1,568 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Nanning in 10 of 12 months
  • Drier: Nanning in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Nanning in 10 of 12 months
