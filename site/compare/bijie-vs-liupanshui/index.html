How the climate of Bijie, China compares with Liupanshui, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bijie is 1.8 °C (3.3 °F) warmer over the year: 15.0 °C against 13.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Bijie is 3.2 °C warmer (Bijie 23.2 °C, Liupanshui 20.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bijie 28.0 °C (July), Liupanshui 24.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bijie 1.7 °C, Liupanshui -0.4 °C.
  • Liupanshui gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 1,243 mm against 973 mm.
  • Liupanshui gets about 135 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,649 h against 1,514 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bijie 31 cm, Liupanshui 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
