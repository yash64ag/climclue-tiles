Bijie vs Liupanshui climate

How the climate of Bijie, China compares with Liupanshui, China, 91 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bijie is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 13.5 °C against 13.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Bijie is 1.7 °C warmer (Bijie 21.7 °C, Liupanshui 20.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bijie 26.8 °C (July), Liupanshui 24.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bijie 0.1 °C, Liupanshui -0.4 °C.
  • Liupanshui gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 1,243 mm against 973 mm.
  • Liupanshui gets about 135 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,649 h against 1,514 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bijie 31 cm, Liupanshui 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
