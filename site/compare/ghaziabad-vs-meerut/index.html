Main differences

  • Ghāziābād is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 24.7 °C against 24.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Ghāziābād is 0.6 °C warmer (Ghāziābād 25.1 °C, Meerut 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ghāziābād 39.6 °C (May), Meerut 39.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Ghāziābād 6.6 °C, Meerut 6.5 °C.
  • Meerut gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 875 mm against 692 mm.
  • Ghāziābād gets about 14 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,674 h against 2,659 h; about 1961–1990).
