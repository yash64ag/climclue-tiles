How the climate of Belo Horizonte, Brazil compares with Chimoio, Mozambique: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Belo Horizonte is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 21.0 °C against 20.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Belo Horizonte is 1.4 °C warmer (Belo Horizonte 17.9 °C, Chimoio 16.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belo Horizonte 28.4 °C (February), Chimoio 28.4 °C (November). Coldest-month night lows: Belo Horizonte 11.7 °C, Chimoio 10.7 °C.
  • Belo Horizonte gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 1,615 mm against 1,238 mm.
  • Chimoio gets about 422 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,806 h against 2,383 h; about 1961–1990).
