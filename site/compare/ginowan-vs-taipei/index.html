Ginowan vs Taipei climate

How the climate of Ginowan, Japan compares with Taipei, Taiwan, 639 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ginowan and Taipei have the same yearly average temperature (22.5 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Taipei is 1.8 °C warmer (Ginowan 25.7 °C, Taipei 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ginowan 30.2 °C (July), Taipei 33.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ginowan 14.5 °C, Taipei 12.6 °C.
  • Taipei gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 3,040 mm against 2,661 mm.
  • Ginowan gets about 130 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,833 h against 1,702 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Ginowan in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Ginowan in 11 of 12 months
