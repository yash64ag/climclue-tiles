Chelyabinsk vs Yekaterinburg climate

How the climate of Chelyabinsk, Russia compares with Yekaterinburg, Russia, 195 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Chelyabinsk is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 3.1 °C against 2.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Chelyabinsk is 1.7 °C warmer (Chelyabinsk 12.4 °C, Yekaterinburg 10.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chelyabinsk 23.2 °C (July), Yekaterinburg 22.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chelyabinsk -17.1 °C, Yekaterinburg -16.8 °C.
  • Yekaterinburg gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 556 mm against 460 mm.
  • Chelyabinsk gets about 208 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,084 h against 1,876 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chelyabinsk 151 cm, Yekaterinburg 181 cm (ERA5-Land).
