How the climate of Chelyabinsk, Russia compares with Yekaterinburg, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yekaterinburg is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 3.2 °C against 3.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Yekaterinburg is 1.9 °C warmer (Chelyabinsk -5.5 °C, Yekaterinburg -3.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chelyabinsk 25.1 °C (July), Yekaterinburg 24.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chelyabinsk -18.1 °C, Yekaterinburg -16.1 °C.
  • Yekaterinburg gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 546 mm against 460 mm.
  • Chelyabinsk gets about 208 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,084 h against 1,876 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chelyabinsk 123 cm, Yekaterinburg 152 cm (ERA5-Land).
