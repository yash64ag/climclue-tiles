Jepara vs Muricay climate

How the climate of Jepara, Indonesia compares with Muricay, Philippines, 2,142 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jepara is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Jepara is 1.2 °C warmer (Jepara 27.4 °C, Muricay 26.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jepara 31.0 °C (September), Muricay 29.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Jepara 24.0 °C, Muricay 23.9 °C.
  • Jepara gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 2,506 mm against 2,158 mm.
  • Jepara gets about 578 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,762 h against 2,184 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
