How the climate of Jepara, Indonesia compares with Muricay, Philippines: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Muricay is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 27.1 °C against 27.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Muricay is 0.8 °C warmer (Jepara 27.2 °C, Muricay 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jepara 33.5 °C (September), Muricay 33.1 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Jepara 21.3 °C, Muricay 22.0 °C.
  • Jepara gets 40% more rain and snow a year: 2,502 mm against 1,788 mm.
  • Jepara gets about 578 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,762 h against 2,184 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Muricay in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Jepara in 8 of 12 months
