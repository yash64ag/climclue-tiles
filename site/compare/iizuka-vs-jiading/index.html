How the climate of Iizuka, Japan compares with Jiading, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Iizuka is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 17.1 °C against 17.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Iizuka is 1.6 °C warmer (Iizuka 6.2 °C, Jiading 4.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iizuka 32.6 °C (August), Jiading 32.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Iizuka 2.1 °C, Jiading 1.4 °C.
  • Iizuka gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 1,669 mm against 1,422 mm.
  • Jiading gets about 220 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,979 h against 1,759 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Iizuka 14 cm, Jiading 9.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
