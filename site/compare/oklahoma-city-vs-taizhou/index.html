Main differences

  • Oklahoma City is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 15.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Oklahoma City is 1.8 °C warmer (Oklahoma City 10.6 °C, Taizhou, Jiangsu 8.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Oklahoma City 33.6 °C (July), Taizhou, Jiangsu 31.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Oklahoma City -0.4 °C, Taizhou, Jiangsu -2.1 °C.
  • Taizhou, Jiangsu gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 1,116 mm against 905 mm.
  • Oklahoma City gets about 953 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,031 h against 2,078 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Oklahoma City 25 cm, Taizhou, Jiangsu 18 cm (ERA5-Land).
