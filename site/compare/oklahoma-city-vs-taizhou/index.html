Oklahoma City vs Taizhou, Jiangsu climate

How the climate of Oklahoma City, United States compares with Taizhou, Jiangsu, China, 11,511 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Oklahoma City is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 15.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Oklahoma City is 1.8 °C warmer (Oklahoma City 10.6 °C, Taizhou, Jiangsu 8.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Oklahoma City 34.4 °C (July), Taizhou, Jiangsu 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Oklahoma City -2.8 °C, Taizhou, Jiangsu -1.5 °C.
  • Taizhou, Jiangsu gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 1,115 mm against 906 mm.
  • Oklahoma City gets about 953 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,031 h against 2,078 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Oklahoma City 17 cm, Taizhou, Jiangsu 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
