Iizuka vs Ningbo climate

How the climate of Iizuka, Japan compares with Ningbo, China, 959 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ningbo is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 17.5 °C against 17.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Ningbo is 1.4 °C warmer (Iizuka 27.4 °C, Ningbo 28.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iizuka 32.6 °C (August), Ningbo 32.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Iizuka 2.1 °C, Ningbo 2.6 °C.
  • Iizuka gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,669 mm against 1,653 mm.
  • Ningbo gets about 112 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,871 h against 1,759 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Iizuka 14 cm, Ningbo 7.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
