Dhanbad vs Lahān climate

How the climate of Dhanbad, India compares with Lahān, Nepal, 325 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dhanbad is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 25.1 °C against 25.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Dhanbad is 1.0 °C warmer (Dhanbad 31.1 °C, Lahān 30.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dhanbad 37.4 °C (May), Lahān 35.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Dhanbad 10.5 °C, Lahān 10.6 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,500 mm and 1,501 mm).
  • Dhanbad gets about 33 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,186 h against 2,153 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
