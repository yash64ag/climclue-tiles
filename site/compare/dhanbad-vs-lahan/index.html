How the climate of Dhanbad, India compares with Lahān, Nepal: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lahān is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 25.1 °C against 24.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Lahān is 0.8 °C warmer (Dhanbad 27.8 °C, Lahān 28.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dhanbad 37.7 °C (May), Lahān 36.5 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Dhanbad 9.2 °C, Lahān 9.4 °C.
  • Dhanbad gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,564 mm against 1,479 mm.
  • Dhanbad gets about 33 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,186 h against 2,153 h; about 1961–1990).
