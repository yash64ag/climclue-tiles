Anyang vs Xinxiang climate

How the climate of Anyang, China compares with Xinxiang, China, 114 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Xinxiang is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 15.0 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Xinxiang is 1.0 °C warmer (Anyang -0.7 °C, Xinxiang 0.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Anyang 32.9 °C (June), Xinxiang 32.7 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Anyang -5.8 °C, Xinxiang -4.9 °C.
  • Xinxiang gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 606 mm against 552 mm.
  • Anyang gets about 103 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,406 h against 2,303 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Anyang 11 cm, Xinxiang 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
