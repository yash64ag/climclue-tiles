How the climate of Aleppo, Syria compares with Amman, Jordan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Aleppo is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 18.1 °C against 16.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Aleppo is 3.7 °C warmer (Aleppo 28.8 °C, Amman 25.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aleppo 35.9 °C (July), Amman 31.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Aleppo 2.8 °C, Amman 3.1 °C.
  • Aleppo gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 366 mm against 326 mm.
  • Amman gets about 309 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,308 h against 2,999 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Aleppo 2.4 cm, Amman 1.3 cm (ERA5-Land).
