Daejeon vs Suwon climate

How the climate of Daejeon, South Korea compares with Suwon, South Korea, 108 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Daejeon is 0.9 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 13.2 °C against 12.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Daejeon is 1.4 °C warmer (Daejeon -0.9 °C, Suwon -2.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daejeon 29.3 °C (August), Suwon 28.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Daejeon -5.8 °C, Suwon -7.7 °C.
  • Daejeon gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,332 mm against 1,297 mm.
  • Daejeon gets about 30 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,425 h against 2,395 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daejeon 33 cm, Suwon 34 cm (ERA5-Land).
