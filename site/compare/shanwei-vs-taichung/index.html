Shanwei vs Taichung climate

How the climate of Shanwei, China compares with Taichung, Taiwan, 565 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Taichung is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 23.3 °C against 22.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Taichung is 1.6 °C warmer (Shanwei 16.9 °C, Taichung 18.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Shanwei 29.3 °C (August), Taichung 29.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Shanwei 12.2 °C, Taichung 13.3 °C.
  • Shanwei gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 2,387 mm against 2,055 mm.
  • Shanwei gets about 167 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,004 h against 1,837 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Taichung in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: Taichung in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Shanwei in 7 of 12 months
