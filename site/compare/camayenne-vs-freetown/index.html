Camayenne vs Freetown climate

How the climate of Camayenne, Guinea compares with Freetown, Sierra Leone, 127 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Camayenne is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.2 °C against 26.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Camayenne is 0.4 °C warmer (Camayenne 27.0 °C, Freetown 26.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Camayenne 32.0 °C (December), Freetown 30.8 °C (December). Coldest-month night lows: Camayenne 20.9 °C, Freetown 22.4 °C.
  • Camayenne gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 3,521 mm against 3,359 mm.
  • Camayenne gets about 98 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,185 h against 2,088 h; about 1961–1990).
