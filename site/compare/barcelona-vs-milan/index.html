How the climate of Barcelona, Spain compares with Milan, Italy: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Barcelona is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 14.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Barcelona is 4.8 °C warmer (Barcelona 9.6 °C, Milan 4.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Barcelona 28.1 °C (August), Milan 30.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Barcelona 4.7 °C, Milan 1.3 °C.
  • Milan gets 55% more rain and snow a year: 981 mm against 634 mm.
  • Barcelona gets about 452 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,385 h against 1,933 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Barcelona 0 cm, Milan 28 cm (ERA5-Land).
