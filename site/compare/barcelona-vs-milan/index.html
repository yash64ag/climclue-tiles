Barcelona vs Milan climate

How the climate of Barcelona, Spain compares with Milan, Italy, 726 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Barcelona is 2.5 °C (4.6 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 13.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Barcelona is 6.8 °C warmer (Barcelona 10.4 °C, Milan 3.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Barcelona 28.0 °C (August), Milan 30.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Barcelona 5.5 °C, Milan -0.6 °C.
  • Milan gets 55% more rain and snow a year: 981 mm against 634 mm.
  • Barcelona gets about 452 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,385 h against 1,933 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Barcelona 0 cm, Milan 28 cm (ERA5-Land).
