How the climate of Cape Town, South Africa compares with Soweto, South Africa: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Cape Town is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 16.2 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Cape Town is 4.6 °C warmer (Cape Town 13.8 °C, Soweto 9.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cape Town 24.6 °C (February), Soweto 25.8 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Cape Town 8.8 °C, Soweto 1.1 °C.
  • Cape Town gets 40% more rain and snow a year: 948 mm against 678 mm.
  • Soweto gets about 247 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,205 h against 2,958 h; about 1961–1990).
