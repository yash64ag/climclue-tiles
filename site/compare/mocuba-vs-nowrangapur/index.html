Mocuba vs Nowrangapur climate

How the climate of Mocuba, Mozambique compares with Nowrangapur, India, 6,394 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mocuba is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 24.6 °C against 24.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Mocuba is 8.2 °C warmer (Mocuba 27.0 °C, Nowrangapur 18.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mocuba 33.3 °C (November), Nowrangapur 36.3 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Mocuba 14.9 °C, Nowrangapur 11.5 °C.
  • Nowrangapur gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 1,528 mm against 1,252 mm.
  • Mocuba gets about 270 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,804 h against 2,533 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
