How the climate of Rio de Janeiro, Brazil compares with Santa Clara, Cuba: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Santa Clara is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 24.2 °C against 24.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Rio de Janeiro is 5.8 °C warmer (Rio de Janeiro 26.8 °C, Santa Clara 20.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rio de Janeiro 31.5 °C (February), Santa Clara 31.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Rio de Janeiro 16.6 °C, Santa Clara 15.6 °C.
  • Santa Clara gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 1,670 mm against 1,310 mm.
  • Santa Clara gets about 457 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,547 h against 2,091 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
