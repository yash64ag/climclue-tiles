Bandar Lampung vs South Tangerang climate

How the climate of Bandar Lampung, Indonesia compares with South Tangerang, Indonesia, 187 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • South Tangerang is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 26.7 °C against 26.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when South Tangerang is 1.1 °C warmer (Bandar Lampung 26.1 °C, South Tangerang 27.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bandar Lampung 29.6 °C (October), South Tangerang 31.5 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Bandar Lampung 22.7 °C, South Tangerang 22.1 °C.
  • South Tangerang gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 2,884 mm against 2,377 mm.
  • South Tangerang gets about 173 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,641 h against 2,468 h; about 1961–1990).
