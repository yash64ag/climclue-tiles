Ho Chi Minh City vs Phnom Penh climate

How the climate of Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam compares with Phnom Penh, Cambodia, 204 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Phnom Penh is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 28.2 °C against 27.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Phnom Penh is 1.1 °C warmer (Ho Chi Minh City 27.6 °C, Phnom Penh 28.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ho Chi Minh City 34.2 °C (April), Phnom Penh 34.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Ho Chi Minh City 21.6 °C, Phnom Penh 22.1 °C.
  • Ho Chi Minh City gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 1,845 mm against 1,391 mm.
  • Phnom Penh gets about 800 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,549 h against 1,749 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
