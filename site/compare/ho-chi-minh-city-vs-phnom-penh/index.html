How the climate of Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam compares with Phnom Penh, Cambodia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Phnom Penh is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 28.2 °C against 27.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Phnom Penh is 1.1 °C warmer (Ho Chi Minh City 27.3 °C, Phnom Penh 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ho Chi Minh City 34.6 °C (April), Phnom Penh 35.6 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Ho Chi Minh City 21.4 °C, Phnom Penh 21.6 °C.
  • Ho Chi Minh City gets 32% more rain and snow a year: 1,840 mm against 1,395 mm.
  • Phnom Penh gets about 800 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,549 h against 1,749 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
