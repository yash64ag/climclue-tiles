Budapest vs Petržalka climate

How the climate of Budapest, Hungary compares with Petržalka, Slovakia, 159 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Budapest and Petržalka have the same yearly average temperature (10.7 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Petržalka is 0.4 °C warmer (Budapest 0.2 °C, Petržalka 0.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Budapest 27.2 °C (July), Petržalka 26.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Budapest -3.3 °C, Petržalka -3.0 °C.
  • Budapest gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 618 mm against 598 mm.
  • Budapest gets about 125 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,899 h against 1,774 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Budapest 55 cm, Petržalka 64 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Budapest in 7 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Budapest in 10 of 12 months
