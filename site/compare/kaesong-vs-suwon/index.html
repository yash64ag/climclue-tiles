How the climate of Kaesŏng, North Korea compares with Suwon, South Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Suwon is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 12.2 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Suwon is 1.5 °C warmer (Kaesŏng -4.0 °C, Suwon -2.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kaesŏng 29.2 °C (August), Suwon 29.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kaesŏng -8.6 °C, Suwon -7.5 °C.
  • Kaesŏng gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,376 mm against 1,303 mm.
  • Suwon gets about 19 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,395 h against 2,375 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kaesŏng 29 cm, Suwon 26 cm (ERA5-Land).
