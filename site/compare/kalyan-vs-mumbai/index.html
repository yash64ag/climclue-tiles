How the climate of Kalyān, India compares with Mumbai, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kalyān is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Kalyān is 1.4 °C warmer (Kalyān 29.1 °C, Mumbai 27.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kalyān 34.7 °C (April), Mumbai 32.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kalyān 15.9 °C, Mumbai 17.0 °C.
  • Mumbai gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 2,712 mm against 2,234 mm.
  • Kalyān gets about 39 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,706 h against 2,667 h; about 1961–1990).
