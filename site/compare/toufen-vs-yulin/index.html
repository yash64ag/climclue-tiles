How the climate of Toufen, Taiwan compares with Yulin, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Toufen is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 23.2 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Toufen is 2.3 °C warmer (Toufen 16.3 °C, Yulin 14.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Toufen 32.4 °C (July), Yulin 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Toufen 13.4 °C, Yulin 10.2 °C.
  • Yulin gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,753 mm against 1,718 mm.
  • Yulin gets about 14 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,785 h against 1,771 h; about 1961–1990).
