Toufen vs Yulin climate

How the climate of Toufen, Taiwan compares with Yulin, China, 1,119 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Toufen is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 22.9 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Toufen is 2.1 °C warmer (Toufen 16.1 °C, Yulin 13.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Toufen 30.2 °C (July), Yulin 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Toufen 13.0 °C, Yulin 10.0 °C.
  • Toufen gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,984 mm against 1,754 mm.
  • Yulin gets about 14 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,785 h against 1,771 h; about 1961–1990).
