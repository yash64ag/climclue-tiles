Basirhat City vs Khulna climate

How the climate of Basirhat City, India compares with Khulna, Bangladesh, 75 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Basirhat City is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.1 °C against 25.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Basirhat City is 0.4 °C warmer (Basirhat City 29.8 °C, Khulna 29.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Basirhat City 34.4 °C (April), Khulna 34.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Basirhat City 12.2 °C, Khulna 11.6 °C.
  • Khulna gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,889 mm against 1,804 mm.
  • Khulna gets about 8 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,090 h against 2,082 h; about 1961–1990).
