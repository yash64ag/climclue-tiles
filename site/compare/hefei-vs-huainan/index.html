Hefei vs Huainan climate

How the climate of Hefei, China compares with Huainan, China, 89 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Hefei is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.4 °C against 15.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Hefei is 0.9 °C warmer (Hefei 11.2 °C, Huainan 10.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hefei 32.3 °C (July), Huainan 31.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hefei -0.9 °C, Huainan -2.1 °C.
  • Hefei gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,120 mm against 1,028 mm.
  • Huainan gets about 67 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,111 h against 2,044 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hefei 23 cm, Huainan 19 cm (ERA5-Land).
