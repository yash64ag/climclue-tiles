How the climate of Daegu, South Korea compares with Nanyang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Nanyang is 1.6 °C (3.0 °F) warmer over the year: 15.7 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Nanyang is 2.7 °C warmer (Daegu 23.1 °C, Nanyang 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daegu 31.1 °C (August), Nanyang 31.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Daegu -5.3 °C, Nanyang -2.8 °C.
  • Daegu gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 979 mm against 843 mm.
  • Daegu gets about 475 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,483 h against 2,008 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daegu 13 cm, Nanyang 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
