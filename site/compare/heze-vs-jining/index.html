How the climate of Heze, China compares with Jining, Shandong, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Heze is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 14.6 °C against 14.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Heze is 0.3 °C warmer (Heze 0.0 °C, Jining, Shandong -0.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Heze 31.5 °C (July), Jining, Shandong 31.6 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Heze -4.5 °C, Jining, Shandong -5.4 °C.
  • Jining, Shandong gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 691 mm against 647 mm.
  • Jining, Shandong gets about 45 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,403 h against 2,358 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Heze 11 cm, Jining, Shandong 8.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
