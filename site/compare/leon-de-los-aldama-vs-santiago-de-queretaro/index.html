León de los Aldama vs Santiago de Querétaro climate

How the climate of León de los Aldama, Mexico compares with Santiago de Querétaro, Mexico, 147 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • León de los Aldama is 1.3 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 19.2 °C against 17.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when León de los Aldama is 1.8 °C warmer (León de los Aldama 23.1 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 21.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: León de los Aldama 29.7 °C (May), Santiago de Querétaro 28.1 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: León de los Aldama 8.1 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 7.1 °C.
  • León de los Aldama gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 616 mm against 549 mm.
  • Santiago de Querétaro gets about 266 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,547 h against 2,282 h; about 1961–1990).
