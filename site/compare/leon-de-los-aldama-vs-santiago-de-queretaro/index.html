León de los Aldama vs Santiago de Querétaro climate

How the climate of León de los Aldama, Mexico compares with Santiago de Querétaro, Mexico, 147 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • León de los Aldama is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 19.7 °C against 19.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when León de los Aldama is 1.1 °C warmer (León de los Aldama 23.6 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 22.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: León de los Aldama 32.2 °C (May), Santiago de Querétaro 30.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: León de los Aldama 6.8 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 7.1 °C.
  • León de los Aldama gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 631 mm against 456 mm.
  • Santiago de Querétaro gets about 266 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,547 h against 2,282 h; about 1961–1990).
