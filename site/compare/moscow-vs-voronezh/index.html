Moscow vs Voronezh climate

How the climate of Moscow, Russia compares with Voronezh, Russia, 466 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Voronezh is 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) warmer over the year: 6.9 °C against 5.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Voronezh is 2.3 °C warmer (Moscow 16.8 °C, Voronezh 19.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Moscow 23.9 °C (July), Voronezh 26.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Moscow -11.1 °C, Voronezh -10.1 °C.
  • Moscow gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 691 mm against 637 mm.
  • Voronezh gets about 191 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,891 h against 1,701 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Moscow 185 cm, Voronezh 145 cm (ERA5-Land).
