How the climate of Moscow, Russia compares with Voronezh, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Voronezh is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 7.1 °C against 5.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Voronezh is 2.3 °C warmer (Moscow 17.2 °C, Voronezh 19.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Moscow 24.3 °C (July), Voronezh 26.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Moscow -10.0 °C, Voronezh -9.6 °C.
  • Moscow gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 691 mm against 637 mm.
  • Voronezh gets about 191 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,891 h against 1,701 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Moscow 185 cm, Voronezh 145 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Voronezh in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Voronezh in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Voronezh in 11 of 12 months
