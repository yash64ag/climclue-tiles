How the climate of Patna, India compares with Varanasi, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Patna and Varanasi have the same yearly average temperature (25.7 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Varanasi is 1.1 °C warmer (Patna 32.2 °C, Varanasi 33.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Patna 38.8 °C (May), Varanasi 41.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Patna 9.0 °C, Varanasi 8.2 °C.
  • Varanasi gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,052 mm against 1,023 mm.
  • Varanasi gets about 249 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,482 h against 2,233 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Patna in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Varanasi in 12 of 12 months
