Patna vs Varanasi climate

How the climate of Patna, India compares with Varanasi, India, 216 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Varanasi is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 25.8 °C against 25.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Varanasi is 1.1 °C warmer (Patna 31.8 °C, Varanasi 32.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Patna 38.0 °C (May), Varanasi 39.5 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Patna 10.4 °C, Varanasi 10.0 °C.
  • Varanasi gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,060 mm against 1,013 mm.
  • Varanasi gets about 249 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,482 h against 2,233 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Patna in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Varanasi in 12 of 12 months
