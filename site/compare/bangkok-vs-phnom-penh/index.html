Bangkok vs Phnom Penh climate

How the climate of Bangkok, Thailand compares with Phnom Penh, Cambodia, 537 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bangkok is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 28.5 °C against 28.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Bangkok is 0.9 °C warmer (Bangkok 29.6 °C, Phnom Penh 28.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bangkok 34.8 °C (April), Phnom Penh 34.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Bangkok 20.8 °C, Phnom Penh 22.1 °C.
  • Bangkok gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,490 mm against 1,391 mm.
  • Phnom Penh gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,549 h against 2,541 h; about 1961–1990).
