Perpignan vs Rome climate

How the climate of Perpignan, France compares with Rome, Italy, 796 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rome is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 15.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Rome is 1.0 °C warmer (Perpignan 11.3 °C, Rome 12.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Perpignan 30.3 °C (July), Rome 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Perpignan 2.7 °C, Rome 2.7 °C.
  • Rome gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 770 mm against 693 mm.
  • Rome gets about 168 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,424 h against 2,256 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Perpignan 3.1 cm, Rome 0.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
