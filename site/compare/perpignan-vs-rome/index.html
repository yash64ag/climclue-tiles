Perpignan vs Rome climate

How the climate of Perpignan, France compares with Rome, Italy, 796 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rome is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Rome is 1.3 °C warmer (Perpignan 11.2 °C, Rome 12.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Perpignan 29.0 °C (July), Rome 29.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Perpignan 3.2 °C, Rome 3.7 °C.
  • Rome gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 836 mm against 710 mm.
  • Rome gets about 168 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,424 h against 2,256 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Perpignan 3.1 cm, Rome 0.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
