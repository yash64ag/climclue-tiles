How the climate of Norman, United States compares with Wuhu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Wuhu is 0.6 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.7 °C against 16.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Wuhu is 1.7 °C warmer (Norman 16.6 °C, Wuhu 18.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Norman 33.8 °C (August), Wuhu 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Norman -2.1 °C, Wuhu -0.4 °C.
  • Wuhu gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 1,354 mm against 979 mm.
  • Norman gets about 999 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,003 h against 2,004 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Norman 13 cm, Wuhu 22 cm (ERA5-Land).
