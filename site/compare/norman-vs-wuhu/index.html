Norman vs Wuhu climate

How the climate of Norman, United States compares with Wuhu, China, 11,718 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Wuhu is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 16.7 °C against 16.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Wuhu is 1.5 °C warmer (Norman 16.8 °C, Wuhu 18.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Norman 33.5 °C (July), Wuhu 32.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Norman 0.0 °C, Wuhu -0.7 °C.
  • Wuhu gets 40% more rain and snow a year: 1,341 mm against 958 mm.
  • Norman gets about 999 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,003 h against 2,004 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Norman 22 cm, Wuhu 24 cm (ERA5-Land).
