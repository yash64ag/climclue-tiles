Daegu vs Jining, Shandong climate

How the climate of Daegu, South Korea compares with Jining, Shandong, China, 1,087 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jining, Shandong is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 14.5 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Jining, Shandong is 2.5 °C warmer (Daegu 23.1 °C, Jining, Shandong 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daegu 29.8 °C (August), Jining, Shandong 30.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Daegu -4.8 °C, Jining, Shandong -5.0 °C.
  • Daegu gets 42% more rain and snow a year: 979 mm against 691 mm.
  • Daegu gets about 80 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,483 h against 2,403 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daegu 13 cm, Jining, Shandong 8.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
