How the climate of Changsha, Hunan, China compares with Zhuzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zhuzhou is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 18.6 °C against 18.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Zhuzhou is 0.4 °C warmer (Changsha, Hunan 29.9 °C, Zhuzhou 30.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changsha, Hunan 33.9 °C (July), Zhuzhou 34.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changsha, Hunan 2.9 °C, Zhuzhou 2.9 °C.
  • Changsha, Hunan gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,689 mm against 1,637 mm.
  • Zhuzhou gets about 16 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,605 h against 1,589 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changsha, Hunan 9.1 cm, Zhuzhou 6.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
