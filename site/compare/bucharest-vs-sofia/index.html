Bucharest vs Sofia climate

How the climate of Bucharest, Romania compares with Sofia, Bulgaria, 296 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bucharest is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 11.6 °C against 10.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Bucharest is 2.5 °C warmer (Bucharest 21.4 °C, Sofia 18.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bucharest 27.9 °C (July), Sofia 25.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bucharest -4.5 °C, Sofia -5.5 °C.
  • Sofia gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 716 mm against 611 mm.
  • Bucharest gets about 29 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,075 h against 2,045 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bucharest 80 cm, Sofia 160 cm (ERA5-Land).
