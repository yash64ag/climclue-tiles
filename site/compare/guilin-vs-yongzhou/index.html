How the climate of Guilin, China compares with Yongzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Guilin is 1.0 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 19.9 °C against 18.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Guilin is 2.3 °C warmer (Guilin 11.4 °C, Yongzhou 9.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guilin 33.0 °C (August), Yongzhou 34.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Guilin 5.8 °C, Yongzhou 4.0 °C.
  • Guilin gets 47% more rain and snow a year: 2,144 mm against 1,454 mm.
  • Yongzhou gets about 15 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,580 h against 1,565 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Guilin 0 cm, Yongzhou 6.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
