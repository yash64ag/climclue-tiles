How the climate of Jieyang, China compares with Ma On Shan, Hong Kong: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jieyang is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 22.4 °C against 22.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Jieyang is 1.3 °C warmer (Jieyang 29.0 °C, Ma On Shan 27.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jieyang 32.4 °C (July), Ma On Shan 30.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jieyang 10.3 °C, Ma On Shan 11.9 °C.
  • Ma On Shan gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 2,395 mm against 1,731 mm.
  • Jieyang gets about 15 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,979 h against 1,964 h; about 1961–1990).
