Qingyang vs Tianshui climate

How the climate of Qingyang, China compares with Tianshui, China, 214 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tianshui is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 11.0 °C against 9.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Tianshui is 2.6 °C warmer (Qingyang -3.1 °C, Tianshui -0.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qingyang 27.2 °C (July), Tianshui 26.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Qingyang -9.8 °C, Tianshui -7.4 °C.
  • Qingyang gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 618 mm against 577 mm.
  • Qingyang gets about 244 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,270 h against 2,026 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Qingyang 47 cm, Tianshui 79 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Sunnier: Qingyang in 12 of 12 months
