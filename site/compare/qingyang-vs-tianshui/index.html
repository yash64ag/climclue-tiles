How the climate of Qingyang, Gansu, China compares with Tianshui, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tianshui is 2.3 °C (4.2 °F) warmer over the year: 11.8 °C against 9.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Tianshui is 4.2 °C warmer (Qingyang, Gansu -3.4 °C, Tianshui 0.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qingyang, Gansu 27.3 °C (July), Tianshui 28.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Qingyang, Gansu -9.6 °C, Tianshui -5.7 °C.
  • Qingyang, Gansu gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 643 mm against 499 mm.
  • Qingyang, Gansu gets about 244 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,270 h against 2,026 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Qingyang, Gansu 34 cm, Tianshui 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
