Main differences

  • Tangerang is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Tangerang is 0.3 °C warmer (South Tangerang 26.9 °C, Tangerang 27.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: South Tangerang 32.3 °C (September), Tangerang 32.9 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: South Tangerang 21.4 °C, Tangerang 21.5 °C.
  • South Tangerang gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 2,884 mm against 2,445 mm.
  • South Tangerang gets about 19 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,641 h against 2,623 h; about 1961–1990).
