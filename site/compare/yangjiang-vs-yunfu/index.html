Yangjiang vs Yunfu climate

How the climate of Yangjiang, China compares with Yunfu, China, 119 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yangjiang is 1.1 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 22.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Yangjiang is 1.9 °C warmer (Yangjiang 15.6 °C, Yunfu 13.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Yangjiang 31.0 °C (August), Yunfu 31.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Yangjiang 11.6 °C, Yunfu 9.5 °C.
  • Yangjiang gets 43% more rain and snow a year: 2,715 mm against 1,905 mm.
  • Yangjiang gets about 102 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,929 h against 1,827 h; about 1961–1990).
