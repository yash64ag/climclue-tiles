How the climate of Yangjiang, China compares with Yunfu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yangjiang is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 23.2 °C against 22.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Yangjiang is 2.0 °C warmer (Yangjiang 15.9 °C, Yunfu 13.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Yangjiang 31.8 °C (August), Yunfu 32.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Yangjiang 12.0 °C, Yunfu 9.6 °C.
  • Yangjiang gets 42% more rain and snow a year: 2,689 mm against 1,900 mm.
  • Yangjiang gets about 102 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,929 h against 1,827 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Yangjiang in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: Yunfu in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Yangjiang in 12 of 12 months
