How the climate of San Juan, Puerto Rico compares with Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • San Juan and Santo Domingo have the same yearly average temperature (26.4 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Santo Domingo is 0.6 °C warmer (San Juan 24.6 °C, Santo Domingo 25.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: San Juan 32.3 °C (September), Santo Domingo 32.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: San Juan 19.8 °C, Santo Domingo 20.0 °C.
  • Santo Domingo gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,688 mm against 1,654 mm.
  • San Juan gets about 155 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,930 h against 2,775 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: San Juan in 11 of 12 months
