How the climate of Dar es Salaam, Tanzania compares with Nairobi, Kenya: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Dar es Salaam is 7.2 °C (13.0 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 18.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Dar es Salaam is 8.4 °C warmer (Dar es Salaam 27.3 °C, Nairobi 18.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dar es Salaam 32.5 °C (February), Nairobi 27.4 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Dar es Salaam 18.9 °C, Nairobi 11.5 °C.
  • Dar es Salaam gets 37% more rain and snow a year: 1,166 mm against 850 mm.
  • Dar es Salaam gets about 267 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,731 h against 2,464 h; about 1961–1990).

  • Sunnier: Dar es Salaam in 7 of 12 months
