Dar es Salaam vs Nairobi climate

How the climate of Dar es Salaam, Tanzania compares with Nairobi, Kenya, 673 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dar es Salaam is 7.1 °C (12.8 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 18.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Dar es Salaam is 8.4 °C warmer (Dar es Salaam 27.3 °C, Nairobi 18.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dar es Salaam 31.0 °C (February), Nairobi 26.4 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Dar es Salaam 21.7 °C, Nairobi 12.2 °C.
  • Dar es Salaam gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 1,142 mm against 860 mm.
  • Dar es Salaam gets about 267 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,731 h against 2,464 h; about 1961–1990).
