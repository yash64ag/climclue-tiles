Main differences

  • New York City is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 12.3 °C against 12.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when New York City is 0.2 °C warmer (Manhattan, New York -0.6 °C, New York City -0.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manhattan, New York 29.0 °C (July), New York City 29.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Manhattan, New York -4.0 °C, New York City -4.2 °C.
  • Manhattan, New York gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,232 mm against 1,193 mm.
  • Manhattan, New York gets about 30 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,529 h against 2,499 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Manhattan, New York 81 cm, New York City 80 cm (ERA5-Land).
