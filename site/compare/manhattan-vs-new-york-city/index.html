Manhattan vs New York City climate

How the climate of Manhattan, United States compares with New York City, United States, 8 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Manhattan and New York City have the same yearly average temperature (12.2 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when New York City is 0.3 °C warmer (Manhattan 16.3 °C, New York City 16.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manhattan 28.2 °C (July), New York City 28.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Manhattan -3.9 °C, New York City -4.2 °C.
  • Manhattan gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,212 mm against 1,181 mm.
  • Manhattan gets about 30 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,529 h against 2,499 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Manhattan 86 cm, New York City 92 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: New York City in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Manhattan in 10 of 12 months (1 equal)
