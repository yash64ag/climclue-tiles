Main differences

  • New York City is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 12.7 °C against 12.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when New York City is 0.2 °C warmer (Manhattan, New York 0.0 °C, New York City 0.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manhattan, New York 29.1 °C (July), New York City 29.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Manhattan, New York -3.4 °C, New York City -3.6 °C.
  • Manhattan, New York gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,232 mm against 1,193 mm.

  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Manhattan, New York 59 cm, New York City 57 cm (ERA5-Land).
