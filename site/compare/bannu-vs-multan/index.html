Bannu vs Multan climate

How the climate of Bannu, Pakistan compares with Multan, Pakistan, 321 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Multan is 2.3 °C (4.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 23.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Multan is 3.4 °C warmer (Bannu 30.3 °C, Multan 33.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bannu 39.6 °C (June), Multan 41.3 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Bannu 4.9 °C, Multan 7.4 °C.
  • Bannu gets 150% more rain and snow a year: 562 mm against 225 mm.
  • Multan gets about 96 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,083 h against 2,987 h; about 1961–1990).
