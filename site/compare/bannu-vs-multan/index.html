How the climate of Bannu, Pakistan compares with Multan, Pakistan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Multan is 2.6 °C (4.6 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 23.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Multan is 3.6 °C warmer (Bannu 30.0 °C, Multan 33.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bannu 41.8 °C (June), Multan 42.4 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Bannu 4.1 °C, Multan 5.3 °C.
  • Bannu gets 150% more rain and snow a year: 576 mm against 230 mm.
  • Multan gets about 96 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,083 h against 2,987 h; about 1961–1990).
