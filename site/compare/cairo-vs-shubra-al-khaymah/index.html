Cairo vs Shubrā al Khaymah climate

How the climate of Cairo, Egypt compares with Shubrā al Khaymah, Egypt, 7 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Cairo is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 21.9 °C against 21.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Cairo is 0.2 °C warmer (Cairo 21.5 °C, Shubrā al Khaymah 21.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cairo 35.6 °C (July), Shubrā al Khaymah 35.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Cairo 8.2 °C, Shubrā al Khaymah 8.2 °C.
  • Shubrā al Khaymah gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 20 mm against 20 mm.
  • Cairo gets about 1 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,330 h against 3,329 h; about 1961–1990).
