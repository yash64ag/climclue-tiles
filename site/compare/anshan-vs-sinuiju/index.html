Anshan vs Sinŭiju climate

How the climate of Anshan, China compares with Sinŭiju, North Korea, 165 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Sinŭiju is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 9.9 °C against 9.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Sinŭiju is 2.5 °C warmer (Anshan -9.8 °C, Sinŭiju -7.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Anshan 28.8 °C (July), Sinŭiju 27.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Anshan -15.7 °C, Sinŭiju -13.5 °C.
  • Sinŭiju gets 36% more rain and snow a year: 1,025 mm against 754 mm.
  • Anshan gets about 69 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,577 h against 2,508 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Anshan 39 cm, Sinŭiju 33 cm (ERA5-Land).
