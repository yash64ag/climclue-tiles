Campinas vs Goiânia climate

How the climate of Campinas, Brazil compares with Goiânia, Brazil, 729 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Goiânia is 2.4 °C (4.4 °F) warmer over the year: 23.5 °C against 21.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Goiânia is 4.9 °C warmer (Campinas 20.3 °C, Goiânia 25.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Campinas 27.8 °C (February), Goiânia 30.8 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Campinas 12.4 °C, Goiânia 16.1 °C.
  • Campinas gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,607 mm against 1,478 mm.
  • Goiânia gets about 29 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,501 h against 2,472 h; about 1961–1990).
