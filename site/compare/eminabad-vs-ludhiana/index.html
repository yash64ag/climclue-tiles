Eminabad vs Ludhiana climate

How the climate of Eminabad, Pakistan compares with Ludhiana, India, 197 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Eminabad is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 23.6 °C against 23.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Eminabad is 1.1 °C warmer (Eminabad 31.1 °C, Ludhiana 30.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Eminabad 39.2 °C (June), Ludhiana 38.3 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Eminabad 5.9 °C, Ludhiana 6.3 °C.
  • Eminabad gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 916 mm against 821 mm.
  • Eminabad gets about 194 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,962 h against 2,768 h; about 1961–1990).
