Ecatepec de Morelos vs Mérida climate

How the climate of Ecatepec de Morelos, Mexico compares with Mérida, Mexico, 995 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mérida is 9.4 °C (16.9 °F) warmer over the year: 26.4 °C against 17.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Mérida is 10.1 °C warmer (Ecatepec de Morelos 17.9 °C, Mérida 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ecatepec de Morelos 25.8 °C (April), Mérida 35.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Ecatepec de Morelos 6.3 °C, Mérida 18.9 °C.
  • Mérida gets 77% more rain and snow a year: 1,021 mm against 576 mm.
  • Mérida gets about 487 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,635 h against 2,148 h; about 1961–1990).
