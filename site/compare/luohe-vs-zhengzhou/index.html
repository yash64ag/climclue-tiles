Luohe vs Zhengzhou climate

How the climate of Luohe, China compares with Zhengzhou, China, 136 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Luohe is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 15.4 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Luohe is 0.8 °C warmer (Luohe 1.6 °C, Zhengzhou 0.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Luohe 31.3 °C (July), Zhengzhou 31.6 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Luohe -3.4 °C, Zhengzhou -4.0 °C.
  • Luohe gets 42% more rain and snow a year: 899 mm against 632 mm.
  • Zhengzhou gets about 106 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,257 h against 2,151 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Luohe 31 cm, Zhengzhou 34 cm (ERA5-Land).
