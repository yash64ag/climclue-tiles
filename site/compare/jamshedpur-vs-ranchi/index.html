Jamshedpur vs Ranchi climate

How the climate of Jamshedpur, India compares with Ranchi, India, 108 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jamshedpur is 2.5 °C (4.5 °F) warmer over the year: 25.7 °C against 23.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Jamshedpur is 3.0 °C warmer (Jamshedpur 26.1 °C, Ranchi 23.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jamshedpur 37.8 °C (May), Ranchi 35.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Jamshedpur 11.4 °C, Ranchi 10.0 °C.
  • Jamshedpur gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,543 mm against 1,418 mm.
  • Ranchi gets about 14 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,243 h against 2,228 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Ranchi in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Ranchi in 7 of 12 months
