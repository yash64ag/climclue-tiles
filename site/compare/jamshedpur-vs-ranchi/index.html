How the climate of Jamshedpur, India compares with Ranchi, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jamshedpur is 2.7 °C (4.8 °F) warmer over the year: 25.9 °C against 23.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Jamshedpur is 3.3 °C warmer (Jamshedpur 22.6 °C, Ranchi 19.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jamshedpur 38.3 °C (May), Ranchi 36.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Jamshedpur 10.8 °C, Ranchi 9.0 °C.
  • Jamshedpur gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,468 mm against 1,413 mm.
  • Ranchi gets about 14 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,243 h against 2,228 h; about 1961–1990).
