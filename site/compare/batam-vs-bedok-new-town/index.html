Batam vs Bedok New Town climate

How the climate of Batam, Indonesia compares with Bedok New Town, Singapore, 22 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Batam is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Batam is 0.2 °C warmer (Batam 27.4 °C, Bedok New Town 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Batam 28.6 °C (May), Bedok New Town 29.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Batam 25.4 °C, Bedok New Town 24.5 °C.
  • Batam gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 2,597 mm against 2,480 mm.
  • Bedok New Town gets about 14 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,051 h against 2,037 h; about 1961–1990).
