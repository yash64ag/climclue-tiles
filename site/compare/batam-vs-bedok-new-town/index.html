Batam vs Bedok New Town climate

How the climate of Batam, Indonesia compares with Bedok New Town, Singapore, 22 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Batam is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Batam is 0.2 °C warmer (Batam 27.4 °C, Bedok New Town 27.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Batam 31.6 °C (May), Bedok New Town 31.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Batam 22.1 °C, Bedok New Town 22.6 °C.
  • Batam gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 2,545 mm against 2,473 mm.
  • Bedok New Town gets about 14 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,051 h against 2,037 h; about 1961–1990).
