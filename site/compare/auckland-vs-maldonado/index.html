Auckland vs Maldonado climate

How the climate of Auckland, New Zealand compares with Maldonado, Uruguay, 10,526 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Maldonado is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.3 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Maldonado is 2.0 °C warmer (Auckland 19.6 °C, Maldonado 21.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Auckland 21.9 °C (February), Maldonado 24.1 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Auckland 9.9 °C, Maldonado 9.2 °C.
  • Maldonado gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,222 mm against 1,197 mm.
  • Maldonado gets about 319 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,341 h against 2,022 h; about 1961–1990).
