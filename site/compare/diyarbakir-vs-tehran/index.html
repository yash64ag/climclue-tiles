Diyarbakır vs Tehran climate

How the climate of Diyarbakır, Turkey compares with Tehran, Iran, 1,027 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tehran is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 16.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Tehran is 2.2 °C warmer (Diyarbakır 19.6 °C, Tehran 21.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Diyarbakır 37.7 °C (July), Tehran 35.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Diyarbakır -2.1 °C, Tehran -4.5 °C.
  • Diyarbakır gets 48% more rain and snow a year: 567 mm against 384 mm.
  • Diyarbakır gets about 147 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,017 h against 2,870 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Diyarbakır 59 cm, Tehran 84 cm (ERA5-Land).
