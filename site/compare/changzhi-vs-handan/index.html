Main differences

  • Handan is 4.7 °C (8.5 °F) warmer over the year: 14.6 °C against 9.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Handan is 5.8 °C warmer (Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 20.8 °C, Handan 26.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 27.1 °C (July), Handan 32.0 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) -10.0 °C, Handan -5.9 °C.
  • Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 638 mm against 530 mm.
  • Handan gets about 67 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,454 h against 2,387 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 35 cm, Handan 8.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
