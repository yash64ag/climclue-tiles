How the climate of Budapest, Hungary compares with Timişoara, Romania: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Budapest is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 11.9 °C against 11.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Budapest is 0.4 °C warmer (Budapest 0.6 °C, Timişoara 0.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Budapest 28.0 °C (July), Timişoara 28.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Budapest -1.9 °C, Timişoara -3.3 °C.
  • Budapest gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 618 mm against 602 mm.
  • Timişoara gets about 179 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,078 h against 1,899 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Budapest 55 cm, Timişoara 40 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Drier: Budapest in 7 of 12 months
