Jakarta vs Manacapuru climate

How the climate of Jakarta, Indonesia compares with Manacapuru, Brazil, 18,267 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jakarta and Manacapuru have the same yearly average temperature (26.8 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Jakarta is 0.7 °C warmer (Jakarta 27.1 °C, Manacapuru 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jakarta 31.3 °C (September), Manacapuru 30.4 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Jakarta 22.8 °C, Manacapuru 23.8 °C.
  • Jakarta gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 2,513 mm against 2,489 mm.
  • Jakarta gets about 869 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,648 h against 1,779 h; about 1961–1990).
