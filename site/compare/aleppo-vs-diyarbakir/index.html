Aleppo vs Diyarbakır climate

How the climate of Aleppo, Syria compares with Diyarbakır, Turkey, 331 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Aleppo is 1.9 °C (3.3 °F) warmer over the year: 18.1 °C against 16.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Aleppo is 4.3 °C warmer (Aleppo 6.8 °C, Diyarbakır 2.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aleppo 35.9 °C (July), Diyarbakır 39.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Aleppo 2.8 °C, Diyarbakır -1.6 °C.
  • Diyarbakır gets 52% more rain and snow a year: 558 mm against 366 mm.
  • Diyarbakır gets about 19 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,017 h against 2,999 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Aleppo 2.4 cm, Diyarbakır 39 cm (ERA5-Land).
