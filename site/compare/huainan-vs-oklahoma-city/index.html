Huainan vs Oklahoma City climate

How the climate of Huainan, China compares with Oklahoma City, United States, 11,633 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Oklahoma City is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 15.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Oklahoma City is 1.2 °C warmer (Huainan 9.4 °C, Oklahoma City 10.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huainan 31.9 °C (July), Oklahoma City 34.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Huainan -2.1 °C, Oklahoma City -2.8 °C.
  • Huainan gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,028 mm against 906 mm.
  • Oklahoma City gets about 921 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,031 h against 2,111 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Huainan 19 cm, Oklahoma City 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
