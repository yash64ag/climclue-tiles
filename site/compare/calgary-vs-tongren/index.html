Calgary vs Tongren, Qinghai climate

How the climate of Calgary, Canada compares with Tongren, Qinghai, China, 9,763 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Calgary and Tongren, Qinghai have the same yearly average temperature (4.5 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Calgary is 1.7 °C warmer (Calgary 16.5 °C, Tongren, Qinghai 14.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Calgary 23.3 °C (July), Tongren, Qinghai 21.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Calgary -13.1 °C, Tongren, Qinghai -16.0 °C.
  • Tongren, Qinghai gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 475 mm against 441 mm.
  • Tongren, Qinghai gets about 263 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,561 h against 2,298 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Calgary 72 cm, Tongren, Qinghai 32 cm (ERA5-Land).
