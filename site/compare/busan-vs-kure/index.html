How the climate of Busan, South Korea compares with Kure, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kure is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 14.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Kure is 1.5 °C warmer (Busan 6.5 °C, Kure 8.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Busan 29.0 °C (August), Kure 29.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Busan -0.1 °C, Kure 1.5 °C.
  • Busan gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,638 mm against 1,534 mm.
  • Busan gets about 374 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,289 h against 1,915 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Busan 6.1 cm, Kure 9.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
