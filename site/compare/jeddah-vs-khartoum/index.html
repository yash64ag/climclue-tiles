How the climate of Jeddah, Saudi Arabia compares with Khartoum, Sudan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Khartoum is 1.4 °C (2.6 °F) warmer over the year: 29.6 °C against 28.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Khartoum is 4.6 °C warmer (Jeddah 29.6 °C, Khartoum 34.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jeddah 37.8 °C (July), Khartoum 42.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Jeddah 18.3 °C, Khartoum 14.6 °C.
  • Khartoum gets 55% more rain and snow a year: 107 mm against 69 mm.
  • Khartoum gets about 195 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,512 h against 3,317 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Khartoum in 9 of 12 months
