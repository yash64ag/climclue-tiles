Jeddah vs Khartoum climate

How the climate of Jeddah, Saudi Arabia compares with Khartoum, Sudan, 963 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Khartoum is 1.6 °C (2.9 °F) warmer over the year: 29.7 °C against 28.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Khartoum is 4.9 °C warmer (Jeddah 29.4 °C, Khartoum 34.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jeddah 33.9 °C (August), Khartoum 40.5 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Jeddah 20.9 °C, Khartoum 15.2 °C.
  • Khartoum gets 59% more rain and snow a year: 109 mm against 69 mm.
  • Khartoum gets about 195 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,512 h against 3,317 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Khartoum in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
