How the climate of Guigang, China compares with Lạng Sơn, Vietnam: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Guigang is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 22.4 °C against 21.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Guigang is 1.7 °C warmer (Guigang 28.9 °C, Lạng Sơn 27.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guigang 32.8 °C (July), Lạng Sơn 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Guigang 9.6 °C, Lạng Sơn 10.1 °C.
  • Guigang gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,689 mm against 1,491 mm.
  • Guigang gets about 90 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,708 h against 1,618 h; about 1961–1990).
