Birmingham vs Dublin climate

How the climate of Birmingham, United Kingdom compares with Dublin, Ireland, 307 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dublin is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 10.4 °C against 9.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Dublin is 1.8 °C warmer (Birmingham 4.5 °C, Dublin 6.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Birmingham 20.1 °C (July), Dublin 18.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Birmingham 1.1 °C, Dublin 3.3 °C.
  • Dublin gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 941 mm against 737 mm.
  • Dublin gets about 29 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,392 h against 1,363 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Birmingham 6.3 cm, Dublin 4.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
