Barranquilla vs Bogotá climate

How the climate of Barranquilla, Colombia compares with Bogotá, Colombia, 711 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Barranquilla is 14.0 °C (25.1 °F) warmer over the year: 27.1 °C against 13.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Barranquilla is 15.0 °C warmer (Barranquilla 27.6 °C, Bogotá 12.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Barranquilla 29.1 °C (June), Bogotá 18.6 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Barranquilla 25.2 °C, Bogotá 8.8 °C.
  • Bogotá gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 1,046 mm against 849 mm.
  • Barranquilla gets about 948 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,481 h against 1,532 h; about 1961–1990).
