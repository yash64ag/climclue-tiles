Barranquilla vs Bogotá climate

How the climate of Barranquilla, Colombia compares with Bogotá, Colombia, 711 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Barranquilla is 13.0 °C (23.4 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Barranquilla is 13.8 °C warmer (Barranquilla 27.6 °C, Bogotá 13.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Barranquilla 28.9 °C (June), Bogotá 19.4 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Barranquilla 25.2 °C, Bogotá 9.8 °C.
  • Bogotá gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 917 mm against 857 mm.
  • Barranquilla gets about 948 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,481 h against 1,532 h; about 1961–1990).
