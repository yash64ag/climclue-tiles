Bogor vs Jakarta climate

How the climate of Bogor, Indonesia compares with Jakarta, Indonesia, 43 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jakarta is 1.3 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Jakarta is 1.6 °C warmer (Bogor 25.8 °C, Jakarta 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bogor 31.5 °C (September), Jakarta 32.4 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Bogor 19.9 °C, Jakarta 21.7 °C.
  • Bogor gets 43% more rain and snow a year: 3,591 mm against 2,513 mm.
  • Jakarta gets about 23 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,648 h against 2,625 h; about 1961–1990).
