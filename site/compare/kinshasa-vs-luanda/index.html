Kinshasa vs Luanda climate

How the climate of Kinshasa, Democratic Republic of the Congo compares with Luanda, Angola, 551 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kinshasa is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 25.6 °C against 25.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Kinshasa is 3.1 °C warmer (Kinshasa 24.8 °C, Luanda 21.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kinshasa 31.3 °C (September), Luanda 31.7 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Kinshasa 19.6 °C, Luanda 18.2 °C.
  • Kinshasa gets 217% more rain and snow a year: 1,458 mm against 459 mm.
  • Luanda gets about 506 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,306 h against 1,801 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Luanda in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Luanda in 11 of 12 months
