How the climate of Kinshasa, Democratic Republic of the Congo compares with Luanda, Angola: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kinshasa is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 25.6 °C against 24.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Kinshasa is 3.1 °C warmer (Kinshasa 24.6 °C, Luanda 21.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kinshasa 31.1 °C (March), Luanda 30.5 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Kinshasa 19.2 °C, Luanda 18.7 °C.
  • Kinshasa gets 234% more rain and snow a year: 1,463 mm against 437 mm.
  • Luanda gets about 506 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,306 h against 1,801 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
