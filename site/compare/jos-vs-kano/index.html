Jos vs Kano climate

How the climate of Jos, Nigeria compares with Kano, Nigeria, 234 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kano is 4.7 °C (8.4 °F) warmer over the year: 26.1 °C against 21.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Kano is 7.8 °C warmer (Jos 23.1 °C, Kano 30.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jos 31.4 °C (April), Kano 38.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Jos 12.9 °C, Kano 12.9 °C.
  • Jos gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 1,300 mm against 1,037 mm.
  • Kano gets about 279 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,924 h against 2,645 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Kano in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Kano in 8 of 12 months
