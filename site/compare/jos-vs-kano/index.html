How the climate of Jos, Nigeria compares with Kano, Nigeria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kano is 4.4 °C (7.9 °F) warmer over the year: 25.9 °C against 21.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Kano is 7.5 °C warmer (Jos 23.3 °C, Kano 30.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jos 31.1 °C (April), Kano 38.1 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Jos 11.9 °C, Kano 12.5 °C.
  • Jos gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,201 mm against 1,095 mm.
  • Kano gets about 279 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,924 h against 2,645 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
