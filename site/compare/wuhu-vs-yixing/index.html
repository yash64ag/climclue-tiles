Wuhu vs Yixing climate

How the climate of Wuhu, China compares with Yixing, China, 132 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yixing is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 16.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Yixing is 0.6 °C warmer (Wuhu 11.8 °C, Yixing 12.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Wuhu 32.0 °C (July), Yixing 31.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Wuhu -0.7 °C, Yixing 0.7 °C.
  • Wuhu gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,341 mm against 1,234 mm.
  • Wuhu gets about 22 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,004 h against 1,982 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Wuhu 24 cm, Yixing 21 cm (ERA5-Land).
