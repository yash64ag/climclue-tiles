How the climate of Wuhu, China compares with Yixing, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Wuhu and Yixing have the same yearly average temperature (16.7 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Yixing is 0.5 °C warmer (Wuhu 11.8 °C, Yixing 12.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Wuhu 32.5 °C (July), Yixing 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Wuhu -0.4 °C, Yixing -0.1 °C.
  • Wuhu gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,354 mm against 1,213 mm.
  • Wuhu gets about 22 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,004 h against 1,982 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Wuhu 22 cm, Yixing 16 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Yixing in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Yixing in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Wuhu in 9 of 12 months
