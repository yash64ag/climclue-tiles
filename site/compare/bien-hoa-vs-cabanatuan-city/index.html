How the climate of Biên Hòa, Vietnam compares with Cabanatuan City, Philippines: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Biên Hòa is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 27.4 °C against 27.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Biên Hòa is 1.4 °C warmer (Biên Hòa 27.4 °C, Cabanatuan City 26.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Biên Hòa 34.6 °C (April), Cabanatuan City 35.1 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Biên Hòa 21.5 °C, Cabanatuan City 19.9 °C.
  • Cabanatuan City gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 2,097 mm against 1,949 mm.
  • Cabanatuan City gets about 766 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,507 h against 1,741 h; about 1961–1990).
