Biên Hòa vs Cabanatuan City climate

How the climate of Biên Hòa, Vietnam compares with Cabanatuan City, Philippines, 1,611 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Biên Hòa is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 27.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Biên Hòa is 1.3 °C warmer (Biên Hòa 27.4 °C, Cabanatuan City 26.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Biên Hòa 34.0 °C (April), Cabanatuan City 33.7 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Biên Hòa 21.7 °C, Cabanatuan City 20.3 °C.
  • Cabanatuan City gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 2,062 mm against 1,976 mm.
  • Cabanatuan City gets about 766 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,507 h against 1,741 h; about 1961–1990).
