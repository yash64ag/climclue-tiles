Kano vs Raipur, Chhattisgarh climate

How the climate of Kano, Nigeria compares with Raipur, Chhattisgarh, India, 7,797 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Raipur, Chhattisgarh is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 25.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Raipur, Chhattisgarh is 4.1 °C warmer (Kano 30.8 °C, Raipur, Chhattisgarh 34.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kano 38.1 °C (April), Raipur, Chhattisgarh 41.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kano 12.5 °C, Raipur, Chhattisgarh 12.6 °C.
  • Raipur, Chhattisgarh gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 1,277 mm against 1,095 mm.
  • Kano gets about 301 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,924 h against 2,623 h; about 1961–1990).
