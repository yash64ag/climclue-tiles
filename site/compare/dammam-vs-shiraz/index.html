Dammam vs Shiraz climate

How the climate of Dammam, Saudi Arabia compares with Shiraz, Iran, 426 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Dammam is 9.8 °C (17.6 °F) warmer over the year: 26.1 °C against 16.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Dammam is 12.5 °C warmer (Dammam 16.1 °C, Shiraz 3.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dammam 38.4 °C (July), Shiraz 35.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Dammam 13.0 °C, Shiraz -1.8 °C.
  • Shiraz gets 433% more rain and snow a year: 477 mm against 89 mm.
  • Dammam gets about 16 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,298 h against 3,282 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Dammam 0 cm, Shiraz 36 cm (ERA5-Land).
