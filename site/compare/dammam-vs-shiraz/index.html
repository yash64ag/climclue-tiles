Main differences

  • Dammam is 8.4 °C (15.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.1 °C against 17.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Dammam is 11.2 °C warmer (Dammam 16.1 °C, Shiraz 4.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dammam 40.6 °C (July), Shiraz 38.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Dammam 11.5 °C, Shiraz -1.4 °C.
  • Shiraz gets 373% more rain and snow a year: 423 mm against 90 mm.
  • Dammam gets about 16 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,298 h against 3,282 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Dammam 0 cm, Shiraz 36 cm (ERA5-Land).
