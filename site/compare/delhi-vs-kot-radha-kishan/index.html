How the climate of Delhi, India compares with Kot Radha Kishan, Pakistan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Delhi is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 24.8 °C against 24.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Kot Radha Kishan is 1.5 °C warmer (Delhi 29.6 °C, Kot Radha Kishan 31.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Delhi 39.6 °C (May), Kot Radha Kishan 40.3 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Delhi 6.6 °C, Kot Radha Kishan 5.2 °C.
  • Delhi gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 646 mm against 539 mm.
  • Kot Radha Kishan gets about 254 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,933 h against 2,679 h; about 1961–1990).
