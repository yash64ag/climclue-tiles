How the climate of Lạng Sơn, Vietnam compares with Yunfu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yunfu is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 22.4 °C against 21.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Yunfu is 1.4 °C warmer (Lạng Sơn 27.3 °C, Yunfu 28.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lạng Sơn 31.6 °C (July), Yunfu 32.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lạng Sơn 10.1 °C, Yunfu 9.6 °C.
  • Yunfu gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 1,900 mm against 1,491 mm.
  • Yunfu gets about 208 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,827 h against 1,618 h; about 1961–1990).
