How the climate of Songnim-ni, North Korea compares with Tianjin, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tianjin is 2.0 °C (3.6 °F) warmer over the year: 13.3 °C against 11.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Tianjin is 3.9 °C warmer (Songnim-ni 11.5 °C, Tianjin 15.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Songnim-ni 29.1 °C (August), Tianjin 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Songnim-ni -9.9 °C, Tianjin -8.3 °C.
  • Songnim-ni gets 61% more rain and snow a year: 874 mm against 543 mm.
  • Tianjin gets about 196 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,630 h against 2,434 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Songnim-ni 24 cm, Tianjin 9.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
