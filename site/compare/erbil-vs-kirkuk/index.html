How the climate of Erbil, Iraq compares with Kirkuk, Iraq: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kirkuk is 1.7 °C (3.0 °F) warmer over the year: 21.8 °C against 20.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Kirkuk is 2.3 °C warmer (Erbil 24.4 °C, Kirkuk 26.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Erbil 41.9 °C (July), Kirkuk 43.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Erbil 2.2 °C, Kirkuk 3.4 °C.
  • Erbil gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 625 mm against 498 mm.
  • Kirkuk gets about 127 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,318 h against 3,190 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Erbil 4.6 cm, Kirkuk 0.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
