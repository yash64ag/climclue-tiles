Erbil vs Kirkuk climate

How the climate of Erbil, Iraq compares with Kirkuk, Iraq, 87 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kirkuk is 2.1 °C (3.7 °F) warmer over the year: 22.1 °C against 20.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Kirkuk is 2.8 °C warmer (Erbil 24.4 °C, Kirkuk 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Erbil 41.3 °C (July), Kirkuk 42.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Erbil 1.3 °C, Kirkuk 3.6 °C.
  • Erbil gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 629 mm against 457 mm.
  • Kirkuk gets about 127 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,318 h against 3,190 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Erbil 7.2 cm, Kirkuk 1.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
