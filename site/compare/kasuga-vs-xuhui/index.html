Kasuga vs Xuhui climate

How the climate of Kasuga, Japan compares with Xuhui, China, 885 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kasuga is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 17.0 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Kasuga is 1.7 °C warmer (Kasuga 6.3 °C, Xuhui 4.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kasuga 30.6 °C (August), Xuhui 31.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kasuga 1.9 °C, Xuhui 0.7 °C.
  • Kasuga gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,672 mm against 1,499 mm.
  • Xuhui gets about 173 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,960 h against 1,786 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kasuga 6.6 cm, Xuhui 7.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
