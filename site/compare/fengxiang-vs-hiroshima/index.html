Fengxiang vs Hiroshima climate

How the climate of Fengxiang, China compares with Hiroshima, Japan, 1,100 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fengxiang is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.7 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Fengxiang is 1.1 °C warmer (Fengxiang 27.9 °C, Hiroshima 26.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fengxiang 31.4 °C (August), Hiroshima 31.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Fengxiang 1.6 °C, Hiroshima 2.3 °C.
  • Hiroshima gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,546 mm against 1,409 mm.
  • Fengxiang gets about 41 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,937 h against 1,896 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fengxiang 5.5 cm, Hiroshima 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
