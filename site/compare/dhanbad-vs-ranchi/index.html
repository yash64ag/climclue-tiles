How the climate of Dhanbad, India compares with Ranchi, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Dhanbad is 1.7 °C (3.1 °F) warmer over the year: 24.9 °C against 23.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Dhanbad is 2.4 °C warmer (Dhanbad 28.1 °C, Ranchi 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dhanbad 37.7 °C (May), Ranchi 36.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Dhanbad 9.2 °C, Ranchi 9.0 °C.
  • Dhanbad gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,564 mm against 1,413 mm.
  • Ranchi gets about 57 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,243 h against 2,186 h; about 1961–1990).
