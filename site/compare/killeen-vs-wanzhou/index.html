Main differences

  • Killeen is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 19.5 °C against 19.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Killeen is 0.7 °C warmer (Killeen 15.2 °C, Wanzhou 14.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Killeen 35.9 °C (August), Wanzhou 34.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Killeen 2.6 °C, Wanzhou 5.9 °C.
  • Wanzhou gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 1,225 mm against 914 mm.
