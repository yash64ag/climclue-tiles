Guadalajara vs Santiago de Querétaro climate

How the climate of Guadalajara, Mexico compares with Santiago de Querétaro, Mexico, 308 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Guadalajara is 2.4 °C (4.4 °F) warmer over the year: 20.3 °C against 17.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Guadalajara is 2.9 °C warmer (Guadalajara 24.1 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 21.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guadalajara 31.9 °C (May), Santiago de Querétaro 28.1 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Guadalajara 9.5 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 7.1 °C.
  • Guadalajara gets 59% more rain and snow a year: 875 mm against 549 mm.
  • Guadalajara gets about 91 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,638 h against 2,547 h; about 1961–1990).
