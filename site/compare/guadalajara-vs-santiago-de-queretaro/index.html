How the climate of Guadalajara, Mexico compares with Santiago de Querétaro, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Guadalajara is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 20.5 °C against 19.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Guadalajara is 1.9 °C warmer (Guadalajara 24.4 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 22.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guadalajara 33.4 °C (May), Santiago de Querétaro 30.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Guadalajara 8.1 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 7.1 °C.
  • Guadalajara gets 89% more rain and snow a year: 864 mm against 456 mm.
  • Guadalajara gets about 91 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,638 h against 2,547 h; about 1961–1990).
