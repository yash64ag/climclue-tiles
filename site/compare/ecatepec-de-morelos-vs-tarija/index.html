Ecatepec de Morelos vs Tarija climate

How the climate of Ecatepec de Morelos, Mexico compares with Tarija, Bolivia, 5,903 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ecatepec de Morelos is 0.9 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 17.0 °C against 16.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Ecatepec de Morelos is 6.3 °C warmer (Ecatepec de Morelos 19.3 °C, Tarija 12.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ecatepec de Morelos 25.8 °C (April), Tarija 22.7 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Ecatepec de Morelos 6.3 °C, Tarija 5.9 °C.
  • Ecatepec de Morelos gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 576 mm against 570 mm.
  • Tarija gets about 753 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,901 h against 2,148 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each

  • Sunnier: Tarija in 12 of 12 months
