Agadez vs Omdurman climate

How the climate of Agadez, Niger compares with Omdurman, Sudan, 2,616 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Omdurman is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 29.3 °C against 29.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Omdurman is 1.8 °C warmer (Agadez 25.4 °C, Omdurman 27.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Agadez 40.9 °C (May), Omdurman 40.3 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Agadez 12.9 °C, Omdurman 14.6 °C.
  • Agadez gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 127 mm against 99 mm.
  • Omdurman gets about 324 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,512 h against 3,188 h; about 1961–1990).
