Agadez vs Omdurman climate

How the climate of Agadez, Niger compares with Omdurman, Sudan, 2,616 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Omdurman is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 29.4 °C against 29.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Omdurman is 2.0 °C warmer (Agadez 25.4 °C, Omdurman 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Agadez 41.8 °C (May), Omdurman 41.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Agadez 12.8 °C, Omdurman 14.2 °C.
  • Agadez gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 128 mm against 99 mm.
  • Omdurman gets about 324 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,512 h against 3,188 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Agadez in 6 of 12 months (2 equal)
