How the climate of Maoming, China compares with Xinyi, Guangdong, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Maoming is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 23.6 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Maoming is 2.0 °C warmer (Maoming 16.4 °C, Xinyi, Guangdong 14.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maoming 32.3 °C (July), Xinyi, Guangdong 32.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Maoming 12.6 °C, Xinyi, Guangdong 9.8 °C.
  • Maoming gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 2,055 mm against 1,924 mm.
  • Maoming gets about 76 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,916 h against 1,840 h; about 1961–1990).
