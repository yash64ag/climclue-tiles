Maoming vs Xinyi, Guangdong climate

How the climate of Maoming, China compares with Xinyi, Guangdong, China, 79 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Maoming is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 23.6 °C against 22.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Maoming is 2.0 °C warmer (Maoming 16.4 °C, Xinyi, Guangdong 14.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maoming 31.4 °C (July), Xinyi, Guangdong 30.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Maoming 12.6 °C, Xinyi, Guangdong 10.4 °C.
  • Maoming gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 2,081 mm against 1,970 mm.
  • Maoming gets about 76 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,916 h against 1,840 h; about 1961–1990).
