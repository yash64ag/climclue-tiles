Kinshasa vs Pointe-Noire climate

How the climate of Kinshasa, Democratic Republic of the Congo compares with Pointe-Noire, Republic of the Congo, 386 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kinshasa is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 25.6 °C against 24.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Kinshasa is 2.9 °C warmer (Kinshasa 24.6 °C, Pointe-Noire 21.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kinshasa 31.1 °C (March), Pointe-Noire 30.3 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Kinshasa 19.2 °C, Pointe-Noire 18.3 °C.
  • Kinshasa gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,463 mm against 1,343 mm.
  • Kinshasa gets about 78 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,801 h against 1,722 h; about 1961–1990).

  • Drier: Pointe-Noire in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Pointe-Noire in 7 of 12 months
