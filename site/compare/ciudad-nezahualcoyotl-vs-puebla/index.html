Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl vs Puebla climate

How the climate of Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl, Mexico compares with Puebla, Mexico, 93 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Puebla is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Puebla is 1.2 °C warmer (Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl 14.8 °C, Puebla 16.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl 24.9 °C (April), Puebla 25.5 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl 6.3 °C, Puebla 7.4 °C.
  • Puebla gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 739 mm against 690 mm.
  • Puebla gets about 409 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,426 h against 2,017 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Puebla in 11 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Puebla in 12 of 12 months
