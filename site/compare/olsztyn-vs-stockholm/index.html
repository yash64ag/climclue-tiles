How the climate of Olsztyn, Poland compares with Stockholm, Sweden: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Olsztyn is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 7.6 °C against 7.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Olsztyn is 1.8 °C warmer (Olsztyn 12.8 °C, Stockholm 11.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Olsztyn 22.7 °C (July), Stockholm 22.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Olsztyn -5.0 °C, Stockholm -4.7 °C.
  • Olsztyn gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 653 mm against 587 mm.
  • Olsztyn gets about 100 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,587 h against 1,487 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Olsztyn 85 cm, Stockholm 116 cm (ERA5-Land).
