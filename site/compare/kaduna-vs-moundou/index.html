How the climate of Kaduna, Nigeria compares with Moundou, Chad: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Moundou is 2.0 °C (3.7 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 25.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Moundou is 2.9 °C warmer (Kaduna 23.9 °C, Moundou 26.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kaduna 35.5 °C (March), Moundou 38.2 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Kaduna 15.1 °C, Moundou 16.0 °C.
  • Kaduna gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,204 mm against 999 mm.
  • Moundou gets about 81 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,765 h against 2,685 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Moundou in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Moundou in 10 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Sunnier: Moundou in 7 of 12 months
