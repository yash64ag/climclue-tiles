Kaduna vs Moundou climate

How the climate of Kaduna, Nigeria compares with Moundou, Chad, 972 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Moundou is 1.9 °C (3.5 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 25.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Moundou is 2.7 °C warmer (Kaduna 23.9 °C, Moundou 26.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kaduna 35.5 °C (March), Moundou 37.5 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Kaduna 16.5 °C, Moundou 18.2 °C.
  • Kaduna gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 1,203 mm against 1,007 mm.
  • Moundou gets about 81 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,765 h against 2,685 h; about 1961–1990).
