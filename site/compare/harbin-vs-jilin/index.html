Harbin vs Jilin climate

How the climate of Harbin, China compares with Jilin, China, 212 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jilin is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 5.9 °C against 4.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Jilin is 2.6 °C warmer (Harbin -18.6 °C, Jilin -15.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Harbin 27.0 °C (July), Jilin 26.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Harbin -24.6 °C, Jilin -22.4 °C.
  • Jilin gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 650 mm against 549 mm.
  • Harbin gets about 44 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,550 h against 2,506 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Harbin 58 cm, Jilin 101 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Harbin in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Harbin in 9 of 12 months
