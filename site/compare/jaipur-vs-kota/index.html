Jaipur vs Kota climate

How the climate of Jaipur, India compares with Kota, India, 193 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kota is 1.8 °C (3.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.7 °C against 24.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Kota is 2.8 °C warmer (Jaipur 23.4 °C, Kota 26.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jaipur 39.4 °C (May), Kota 41.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Jaipur 8.0 °C, Kota 10.4 °C.
  • Kota gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 760 mm against 628 mm.
  • Kota gets about 36 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,867 h against 2,831 h; about 1961–1990).
