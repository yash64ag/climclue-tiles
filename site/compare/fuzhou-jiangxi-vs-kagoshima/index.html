Fuzhou, Jiangxi vs Kagoshima climate

How the climate of Fuzhou, Jiangxi, China compares with Kagoshima, Japan, 1,428 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fuzhou, Jiangxi is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 19.1 °C against 18.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Fuzhou, Jiangxi is 3.3 °C warmer (Fuzhou, Jiangxi 23.9 °C, Kagoshima 20.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 34.0 °C (July), Kagoshima 30.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 2.6 °C, Kagoshima 4.7 °C.
  • Kagoshima gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 2,249 mm against 2,005 mm.
  • Kagoshima gets about 36 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,859 h against 1,823 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 6.6 cm, Kagoshima 0.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
