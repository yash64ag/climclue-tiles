Fuzhou, Jiangxi vs Kagoshima climate

How the climate of Fuzhou, Jiangxi, China compares with Kagoshima, Japan, 1,428 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fuzhou, Jiangxi is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 19.1 °C against 18.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Fuzhou, Jiangxi is 3.2 °C warmer (Fuzhou, Jiangxi 23.9 °C, Kagoshima 20.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 34.9 °C (July), Kagoshima 32.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 3.1 °C, Kagoshima 4.2 °C.
  • Kagoshima gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 2,100 mm against 1,977 mm.
  • Kagoshima gets about 36 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,859 h against 1,823 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 6.6 cm, Kagoshima 0.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
