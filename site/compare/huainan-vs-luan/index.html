How the climate of Huainan, China compares with Lu’an, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lu’an is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 16.0 °C against 15.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Lu’an is 0.6 °C warmer (Huainan 2.2 °C, Lu’an 2.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huainan 31.9 °C (July), Lu’an 32.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Huainan -2.1 °C, Lu’an -1.5 °C.
  • Lu’an gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 1,301 mm against 1,028 mm.
  • Huainan gets about 73 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,111 h against 2,037 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Huainan 19 cm, Lu’an 29 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Sunnier: Huainan in 10 of 12 months
