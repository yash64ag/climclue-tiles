How the climate of Wuhan, China compares with Xinyang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Wuhan is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 17.5 °C against 16.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Wuhan is 1.9 °C warmer (Wuhan 24.4 °C, Xinyang 22.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Wuhan 33.1 °C (July), Xinyang 31.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Wuhan 0.3 °C, Xinyang -1.2 °C.
  • Wuhan gets 36% more rain and snow a year: 1,550 mm against 1,143 mm.
  • Xinyang gets about 63 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,028 h against 1,964 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Wuhan 14 cm, Xinyang 28 cm (ERA5-Land).
