Wuhan vs Xinyang climate

How the climate of Wuhan, China compares with Xinyang, China, 172 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Wuhan is 1.9 °C (3.4 °F) warmer over the year: 17.5 °C against 15.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Wuhan is 2.3 °C warmer (Wuhan 24.4 °C, Xinyang 22.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Wuhan 32.1 °C (July), Xinyang 30.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Wuhan 0.7 °C, Xinyang -2.1 °C.
  • Wuhan gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 1,544 mm against 1,150 mm.
  • Xinyang gets about 63 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,028 h against 1,964 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Wuhan 16 cm, Xinyang 42 cm (ERA5-Land).
