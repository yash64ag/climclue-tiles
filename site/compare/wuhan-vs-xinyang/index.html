Wuhan vs Xinyang climate

How the climate of Wuhan, China compares with Xinyang, China, 172 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Wuhan is 1.7 °C (3.0 °F) warmer over the year: 17.4 °C against 15.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Wuhan is 2.1 °C warmer (Wuhan 6.5 °C, Xinyang 4.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Wuhan 33.2 °C (July), Xinyang 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Wuhan 0.1 °C, Xinyang -1.9 °C.
  • Wuhan gets 36% more rain and snow a year: 1,550 mm against 1,143 mm.
  • Xinyang gets about 63 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,028 h against 1,964 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Wuhan 14 cm, Xinyang 28 cm (ERA5-Land).
