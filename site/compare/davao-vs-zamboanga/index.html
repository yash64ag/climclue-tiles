Davao vs Zamboanga climate

How the climate of Davao, Philippines compares with Zamboanga, Philippines, 391 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Davao is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 27.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Davao is 0.7 °C warmer (Davao 27.6 °C, Zamboanga 26.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Davao 32.7 °C (April), Zamboanga 32.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Davao 22.0 °C, Zamboanga 22.0 °C.
  • Davao gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,741 mm against 1,695 mm.
  • Davao gets about 77 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,288 h against 2,211 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Davao in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: Davao in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Davao in 11 of 12 months
