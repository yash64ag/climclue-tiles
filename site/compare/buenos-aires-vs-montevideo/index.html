How the climate of Buenos Aires, Argentina compares with Montevideo, Uruguay: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Buenos Aires is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 17.6 °C against 16.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Buenos Aires is 2.5 °C warmer (Buenos Aires 22.8 °C, Montevideo 20.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Buenos Aires 29.3 °C (January), Montevideo 27.2 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Buenos Aires 7.0 °C, Montevideo 6.6 °C.
  • Buenos Aires gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,189 mm against 1,075 mm.
  • Montevideo gets about 53 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,486 h against 2,433 h; about 1961–1990).
