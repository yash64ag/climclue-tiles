How the climate of Pilsen, Czechia compares with Warsaw, Poland: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Pilsen is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 8.9 °C against 8.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Pilsen is 1.5 °C warmer (Pilsen 4.2 °C, Warsaw 2.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pilsen 24.4 °C (July), Warsaw 24.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Pilsen -3.8 °C, Warsaw -4.6 °C.
  • Warsaw gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 582 mm against 518 mm.
  • Warsaw gets about 13 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,587 h against 1,574 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Pilsen 48 cm, Warsaw 61 cm (ERA5-Land).
