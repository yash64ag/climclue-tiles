Pilsen vs Warsaw climate

How the climate of Pilsen, Czechia compares with Warsaw, Poland, 601 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Pilsen is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 8.7 °C against 8.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Pilsen is 1.1 °C warmer (Pilsen 4.0 °C, Warsaw 3.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pilsen 23.2 °C (July), Warsaw 23.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Pilsen -4.0 °C, Warsaw -4.2 °C.
  • Warsaw gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 560 mm against 531 mm.
  • Warsaw gets about 13 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,587 h against 1,574 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Pilsen 88 cm, Warsaw 91 cm (ERA5-Land).
