Ciudad Juárez vs San Antonio climate

How the climate of Ciudad Juárez, Mexico compares with San Antonio, United States, 804 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • San Antonio is 2.0 °C (3.6 °F) warmer over the year: 20.5 °C against 18.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when San Antonio is 4.3 °C warmer (Ciudad Juárez 7.3 °C, San Antonio 11.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ciudad Juárez 35.3 °C (June), San Antonio 34.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Ciudad Juárez 1.4 °C, San Antonio 7.0 °C.
  • San Antonio gets 244% more rain and snow a year: 822 mm against 239 mm.
  • Ciudad Juárez gets about 1,100 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,671 h against 2,571 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ciudad Juárez 3.0 cm, San Antonio 0.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
