How the climate of Ciudad Juárez, Mexico compares with San Antonio, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • San Antonio is 2.5 °C (4.5 °F) warmer over the year: 21.0 °C against 18.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when San Antonio is 4.3 °C warmer (Ciudad Juárez 12.2 °C, San Antonio 16.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ciudad Juárez 36.5 °C (June), San Antonio 35.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Ciudad Juárez 0.2 °C, San Antonio 5.2 °C.
  • San Antonio gets 226% more rain and snow a year: 803 mm against 246 mm.
  • Ciudad Juárez gets about 1,100 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,671 h against 2,571 h; about 1961–1990).
