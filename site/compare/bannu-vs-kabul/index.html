Bannu vs Kabul climate

How the climate of Bannu, Pakistan compares with Kabul, Afghanistan, 217 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bannu is 11.5 °C (20.7 °F) warmer over the year: 23.4 °C against 11.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Bannu is 15.0 °C warmer (Bannu 14.2 °C, Kabul -0.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bannu 41.8 °C (June), Kabul 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bannu 4.1 °C, Kabul -8.4 °C.
  • Bannu gets 66% more rain and snow a year: 576 mm against 346 mm.
  • Kabul gets about 45 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,031 h against 2,987 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bannu 0 cm, Kabul 128 cm (ERA5-Land).
