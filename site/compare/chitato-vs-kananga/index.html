Chitato vs Kananga climate

How the climate of Chitato, Angola compares with Kananga, Democratic Republic of the Congo, 243 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kananga is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 24.3 °C against 24.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Chitato is 0.7 °C warmer (Chitato 25.4 °C, Kananga 24.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chitato 33.2 °C (July), Kananga 31.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chitato 17.8 °C, Kananga 19.4 °C.
  • Chitato gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,509 mm against 1,484 mm.
  • Chitato gets about 194 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,299 h against 2,105 h; about 1961–1990).
