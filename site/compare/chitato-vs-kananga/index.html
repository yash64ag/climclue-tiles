How the climate of Chitato, Angola compares with Kananga, Democratic Republic of the Congo: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kananga is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 24.1 °C against 23.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Chitato is 0.8 °C warmer (Chitato 25.2 °C, Kananga 24.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chitato 33.8 °C (July), Kananga 33.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chitato 16.2 °C, Kananga 15.9 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,538 mm and 1,542 mm).
  • Chitato gets about 194 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,299 h against 2,105 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Kananga in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Chitato in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Chitato in 11 of 12 months
