Kano vs Sokoto climate

How the climate of Kano, Nigeria compares with Sokoto, Nigeria, 375 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Sokoto is 2.8 °C (5.0 °F) warmer over the year: 28.9 °C against 26.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Sokoto is 3.7 °C warmer (Kano 23.6 °C, Sokoto 27.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kano 38.3 °C (April), Sokoto 40.5 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Kano 12.9 °C, Sokoto 17.2 °C.
  • Kano gets 80% more rain and snow a year: 1,037 mm against 576 mm.
  • Kano gets about 135 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,924 h against 2,789 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Sokoto in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Sokoto in 7 of 12 months (4 equal)
  • Sunnier: Kano in 9 of 12 months
