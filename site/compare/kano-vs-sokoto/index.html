How the climate of Kano, Nigeria compares with Sokoto, Nigeria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Sokoto is 2.9 °C (5.3 °F) warmer over the year: 28.9 °C against 25.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Sokoto is 3.8 °C warmer (Kano 23.3 °C, Sokoto 27.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kano 38.1 °C (April), Sokoto 41.0 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Kano 12.5 °C, Sokoto 16.1 °C.
  • Kano gets 93% more rain and snow a year: 1,095 mm against 567 mm.
  • Kano gets about 135 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,924 h against 2,789 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Sokoto in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Sokoto in 8 of 12 months (3 equal)
  • Sunnier: Kano in 9 of 12 months
