Bengaluru vs Kallakurichi climate

How the climate of Bengaluru, India compares with Kallakurichi, India, 202 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kallakurichi is 4.7 °C (8.4 °F) warmer over the year: 28.1 °C against 23.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Kallakurichi is 6.9 °C warmer (Bengaluru 23.1 °C, Kallakurichi 29.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bengaluru 32.8 °C (April), Kallakurichi 37.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Bengaluru 14.8 °C, Kallakurichi 18.9 °C.
  • Kallakurichi gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,016 mm against 895 mm.
  • Kallakurichi gets about 153 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,528 h against 2,374 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Kallakurichi in 9 of 12 months
