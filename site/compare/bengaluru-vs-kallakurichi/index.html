Bengaluru vs Kallakurichi climate

How the climate of Bengaluru, India compares with Kallakurichi, India, 202 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kallakurichi is 4.9 °C (8.9 °F) warmer over the year: 28.1 °C against 23.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Kallakurichi is 7.1 °C warmer (Bengaluru 22.9 °C, Kallakurichi 30.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bengaluru 33.2 °C (April), Kallakurichi 37.5 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Bengaluru 14.6 °C, Kallakurichi 19.1 °C.
  • Kallakurichi gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,028 mm against 914 mm.
  • Kallakurichi gets about 153 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,528 h against 2,374 h; about 1961–1990).
