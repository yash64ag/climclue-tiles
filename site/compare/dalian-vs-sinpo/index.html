Dalian vs Sinp’o climate

How the climate of Dalian, China compares with Sinp’o, North Korea, 579 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dalian is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 11.2 °C against 10.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Dalian is 1.9 °C warmer (Dalian 21.2 °C, Sinp’o 19.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dalian 27.3 °C (August), Sinp’o 26.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Dalian -5.5 °C, Sinp’o -7.3 °C.
  • Sinp’o gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 726 mm against 597 mm.
  • Dalian gets about 378 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,662 h against 2,285 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Dalian 17 cm, Sinp’o 57 cm (ERA5-Land).
