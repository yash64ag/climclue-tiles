How the climate of Ahmedabad, India compares with Jodhpur, Rajasthan, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ahmedabad is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 27.1 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Ahmedabad is 3.2 °C warmer (Ahmedabad 19.8 °C, Jodhpur, Rajasthan 16.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ahmedabad 41.1 °C (May), Jodhpur, Rajasthan 41.5 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Ahmedabad 11.6 °C, Jodhpur, Rajasthan 8.5 °C.
  • Ahmedabad gets 133% more rain and snow a year: 876 mm against 375 mm.
  • Jodhpur, Rajasthan gets about 91 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,092 h against 3,001 h; about 1961–1990).
