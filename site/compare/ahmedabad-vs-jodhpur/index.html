Ahmedabad vs Jodhpur climate

How the climate of Ahmedabad, India compares with Jodhpur, India, 363 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ahmedabad is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 27.1 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Ahmedabad is 3.0 °C warmer (Ahmedabad 19.8 °C, Jodhpur 16.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ahmedabad 40.0 °C (May), Jodhpur 40.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Ahmedabad 13.6 °C, Jodhpur 9.9 °C.
  • Ahmedabad gets 136% more rain and snow a year: 874 mm against 370 mm.
  • Jodhpur gets about 91 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,092 h against 3,001 h; about 1961–1990).
