Kyoto vs Osaka climate

How the climate of Kyoto, Japan compares with Osaka, Japan, 43 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Osaka is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Osaka is 1.3 °C warmer (Kyoto 23.7 °C, Osaka 25.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kyoto 31.9 °C (August), Osaka 33.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kyoto 1.0 °C, Osaka 2.0 °C.
  • Kyoto gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,435 mm against 1,418 mm.
  • Osaka gets about 178 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,852 h against 1,674 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kyoto 27 cm, Osaka 4.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
