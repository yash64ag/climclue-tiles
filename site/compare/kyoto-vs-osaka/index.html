Kyoto vs Osaka climate

How the climate of Kyoto, Japan compares with Osaka, Japan, 43 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Osaka is 1.7 °C (3.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 15.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Osaka is 1.9 °C warmer (Kyoto 23.1 °C, Osaka 25.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kyoto 30.4 °C (August), Osaka 32.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kyoto -0.9 °C, Osaka 0.6 °C.
  • Kyoto gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,590 mm against 1,421 mm.
  • Osaka gets about 178 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,852 h against 1,674 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kyoto 46 cm, Osaka 11 cm (ERA5-Land).
