How the climate of Addis Ababa, Ethiopia compares with Mogadishu, Somalia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Mogadishu is 11.2 °C (20.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.3 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Mogadishu is 12.5 °C warmer (Addis Ababa 14.1 °C, Mogadishu 26.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Addis Ababa 23.9 °C (March), Mogadishu 32.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Addis Ababa 5.7 °C, Mogadishu 20.7 °C.
  • Addis Ababa gets 109% more rain and snow a year: 1,142 mm against 548 mm.
  • Mogadishu gets about 270 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,098 h against 2,828 h; about 1961–1990).
