Addis Ababa vs Mogadishu climate

How the climate of Addis Ababa, Ethiopia compares with Mogadishu, Somalia, 1,066 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mogadishu is 11.7 °C (21.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.2 °C against 14.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Mogadishu is 13.1 °C warmer (Addis Ababa 13.3 °C, Mogadishu 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Addis Ababa 22.0 °C (March), Mogadishu 30.3 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Addis Ababa 6.2 °C, Mogadishu 23.1 °C.
  • Addis Ababa gets 113% more rain and snow a year: 1,199 mm against 562 mm.
  • Mogadishu gets about 270 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,098 h against 2,828 h; about 1961–1990).

  • Sunnier: Mogadishu in 9 of 12 months
