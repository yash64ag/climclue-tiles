Changwon vs Ulsan climate

How the climate of Changwon, South Korea compares with Ulsan, South Korea, 67 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ulsan is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 14.8 °C against 14.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Ulsan is 0.9 °C warmer (Changwon 9.8 °C, Ulsan 10.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changwon 28.8 °C (August), Ulsan 28.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Changwon -2.8 °C, Ulsan -2.0 °C.
  • Changwon gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 1,452 mm against 1,186 mm.
  • Changwon gets about 129 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,419 h against 2,290 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changwon 8.6 cm, Ulsan 6.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
