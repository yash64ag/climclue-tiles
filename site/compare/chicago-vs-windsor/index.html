Chicago vs Windsor climate

How the climate of Chicago, United States compares with Windsor, Canada, 386 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Chicago is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 10.3 °C against 10.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Chicago is 1.3 °C warmer (Chicago 19.1 °C, Windsor 17.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chicago 29.8 °C (July), Windsor 28.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chicago -8.4 °C, Windsor -7.3 °C.
  • Chicago gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,073 mm against 952 mm.
  • Chicago gets about 279 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,470 h against 2,191 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chicago 90 cm, Windsor 103 cm (ERA5-Land).
