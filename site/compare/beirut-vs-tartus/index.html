Beirut vs Ţarţūs climate

How the climate of Beirut, Lebanon compares with Ţarţūs, Syria, 116 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Beirut is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 20.0 °C against 19.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Beirut is 0.7 °C warmer (Beirut 21.5 °C, Ţarţūs 20.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Beirut 30.0 °C (August), Ţarţūs 28.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Beirut 9.1 °C, Ţarţūs 10.1 °C.
  • Ţarţūs gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 1,261 mm against 992 mm.
  • Beirut gets about 5 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,942 h against 2,937 h; about 1961–1990).
