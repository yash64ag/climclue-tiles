Changshu vs Nantong climate

How the climate of Changshu, China compares with Nantong, China, 45 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Changshu is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 16.3 °C against 15.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Changshu is 0.6 °C warmer (Changshu 15.2 °C, Nantong 14.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changshu 31.5 °C (July), Nantong 31.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changshu -0.6 °C, Nantong -0.5 °C.
  • Changshu gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,248 mm against 1,220 mm.
  • Nantong gets about 47 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,051 h against 2,004 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changshu 12 cm, Nantong 10 cm (ERA5-Land).
