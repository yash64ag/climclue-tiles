Nizhniy Novgorod vs Vitebsk climate

How the climate of Nizhniy Novgorod, Russia compares with Vitebsk, Belarus, 871 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Vitebsk is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 6.1 °C against 4.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Vitebsk is 3.8 °C warmer (Nizhniy Novgorod -9.2 °C, Vitebsk -5.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nizhniy Novgorod 23.3 °C (July), Vitebsk 22.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nizhniy Novgorod -11.8 °C, Vitebsk -8.9 °C.
  • Vitebsk gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 728 mm against 697 mm.
  • Nizhniy Novgorod gets about 49 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,802 h against 1,753 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nizhniy Novgorod 207 cm, Vitebsk 170 cm (ERA5-Land).
