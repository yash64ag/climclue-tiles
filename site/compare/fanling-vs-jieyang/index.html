Fanling vs Jieyang climate

How the climate of Fanling, Hong Kong compares with Jieyang, China, 256 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fanling is 0.6 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 22.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Fanling is 1.1 °C warmer (Fanling 15.4 °C, Jieyang 14.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fanling 30.2 °C (July), Jieyang 31.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fanling 11.8 °C, Jieyang 9.2 °C.
  • Fanling gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 2,097 mm against 1,731 mm.
  • Jieyang gets about 21 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,979 h against 1,958 h; about 1961–1990).
