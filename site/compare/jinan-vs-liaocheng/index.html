Jinan vs Liaocheng climate

How the climate of Jinan, China compares with Liaocheng, China, 92 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Liaocheng is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 14.4 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Liaocheng is 0.4 °C warmer (Jinan 2.3 °C, Liaocheng 2.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinan 31.3 °C (July), Liaocheng 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinan -5.8 °C, Liaocheng -5.6 °C.
  • Jinan gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 669 mm against 591 mm.
  • Jinan gets about 60 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,554 h against 2,493 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinan 22 cm, Liaocheng 18 cm (ERA5-Land).
