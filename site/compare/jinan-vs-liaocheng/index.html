How the climate of Jinan, China compares with Liaocheng, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jinan is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.6 °C against 14.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Jinan is 1.5 °C warmer (Jinan 8.9 °C, Liaocheng 7.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinan 32.6 °C (July), Liaocheng 32.0 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Jinan -3.8 °C, Liaocheng -5.9 °C.
  • Jinan gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 657 mm against 583 mm.
  • Jinan gets about 60 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,554 h against 2,493 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinan 10 cm, Liaocheng 8.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
