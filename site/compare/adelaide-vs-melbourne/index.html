Adelaide vs Melbourne climate

How the climate of Adelaide, Australia compares with Melbourne, Australia, 654 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Adelaide is 1.7 °C (3.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.2 °C against 14.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Adelaide is 2.1 °C warmer (Adelaide 21.3 °C, Melbourne 19.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Adelaide 27.9 °C (February), Melbourne 25.4 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Adelaide 7.1 °C, Melbourne 5.8 °C.
  • Adelaide gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 705 mm against 629 mm.
  • Adelaide gets about 485 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,682 h against 2,197 h; about 1961–1990).
