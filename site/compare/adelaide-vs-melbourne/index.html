Adelaide vs Melbourne climate

How the climate of Adelaide, Australia compares with Melbourne, Australia, 654 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Adelaide is 1.7 °C (3.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.3 °C against 14.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Adelaide is 2.0 °C warmer (Adelaide 21.1 °C, Melbourne 19.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Adelaide 25.7 °C (February), Melbourne 24.3 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Adelaide 9.2 °C, Melbourne 7.2 °C.
  • Adelaide gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 645 mm against 625 mm.
  • Adelaide gets about 485 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,682 h against 2,197 h; about 1961–1990).
