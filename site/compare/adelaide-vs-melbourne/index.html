How the climate of Adelaide, Australia compares with Melbourne, Australia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Adelaide is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.4 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Adelaide is 1.5 °C warmer (Adelaide 18.4 °C, Melbourne 16.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Adelaide 27.8 °C (February), Melbourne 26.5 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Adelaide 7.6 °C, Melbourne 6.3 °C.
  • Adelaide gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 705 mm against 629 mm.
  • Adelaide gets about 485 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,682 h against 2,197 h; about 1961–1990).
