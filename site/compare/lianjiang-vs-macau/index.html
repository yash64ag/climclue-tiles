Lianjiang vs Macau climate

How the climate of Lianjiang, China compares with Macau, Macao, 342 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lianjiang is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 23.4 °C against 23.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Lianjiang is 1.2 °C warmer (Lianjiang 24.1 °C, Macau 22.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lianjiang 32.0 °C (July), Macau 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lianjiang 12.5 °C, Macau 12.6 °C.
  • Macau gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 2,195 mm against 2,092 mm.
  • Macau gets about 64 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,945 h against 1,881 h; about 1961–1990).
