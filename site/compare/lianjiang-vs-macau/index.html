Lianjiang vs Macau climate

How the climate of Lianjiang, China compares with Macau, Macao, 342 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lianjiang is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 23.5 °C against 23.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Lianjiang is 1.2 °C warmer (Lianjiang 24.1 °C, Macau 22.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lianjiang 31.7 °C (July), Macau 29.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Lianjiang 12.3 °C, Macau 12.9 °C.
  • Macau gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 2,096 mm against 2,073 mm.
  • Macau gets about 64 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,945 h against 1,881 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Lianjiang in 9 of 12 months (3 equal)
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Macau in 7 of 12 months
