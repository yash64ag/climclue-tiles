How the climate of Kaohsiung, Taiwan compares with Tainan, Taiwan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kaohsiung is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 24.6 °C against 24.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Kaohsiung is 1.3 °C warmer (Kaohsiung 22.4 °C, Tainan 21.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kaohsiung 32.3 °C (July), Tainan 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kaohsiung 14.0 °C, Tainan 12.7 °C.
  • Kaohsiung gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,839 mm against 1,795 mm.
  • Kaohsiung gets about 16 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,008 h against 1,992 h; about 1961–1990).
