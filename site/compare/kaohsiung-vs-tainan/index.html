Kaohsiung vs Tainan climate

How the climate of Kaohsiung, Taiwan compares with Tainan, Taiwan, 43 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kaohsiung is 0.4 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 24.5 °C against 24.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Kaohsiung is 1.0 °C warmer (Kaohsiung 22.3 °C, Tainan 21.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kaohsiung 30.5 °C (July), Tainan 30.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kaohsiung 15.2 °C, Tainan 14.6 °C.
  • Kaohsiung gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,962 mm against 1,855 mm.
  • Kaohsiung gets about 16 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,008 h against 1,992 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Kaohsiung in 7 of 12 months (2 equal)
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Kaohsiung in 8 of 12 months
