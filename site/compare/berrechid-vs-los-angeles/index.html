Main differences

  • Berrechid is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 17.9 °C against 17.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Berrechid is 1.0 °C warmer (Berrechid 13.3 °C, Los Angeles 12.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Berrechid 29.2 °C (July), Los Angeles 29.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Berrechid 6.3 °C, Los Angeles 6.6 °C.
  • Los Angeles gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 431 mm against 365 mm.
  • Los Angeles gets about 261 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,193 h against 2,932 h; about 1961–1990).
