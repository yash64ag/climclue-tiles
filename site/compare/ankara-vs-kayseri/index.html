Ankara vs Kayseri climate

How the climate of Ankara, Turkey compares with Kayseri, Turkey, 262 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ankara is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 12.9 °C against 12.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Ankara is 1.6 °C warmer (Ankara 2.6 °C, Kayseri 1.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ankara 32.3 °C (August), Kayseri 34.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Ankara -2.4 °C, Kayseri -5.1 °C.
  • Ankara gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 420 mm against 347 mm.
  • Kayseri gets about 55 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,867 h against 2,811 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ankara 37 cm, Kayseri 47 cm (ERA5-Land).
