Ankara vs Kayseri climate

How the climate of Ankara, Turkey compares with Kayseri, Turkey, 262 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ankara is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 12.7 °C against 12.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Ankara is 1.5 °C warmer (Ankara 2.4 °C, Kayseri 0.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ankara 30.2 °C (August), Kayseri 30.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Ankara -3.6 °C, Kayseri -5.7 °C.
  • Ankara gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 439 mm against 373 mm.
  • Kayseri gets about 55 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,867 h against 2,811 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ankara 37 cm, Kayseri 47 cm (ERA5-Land).
