How the climate of Jakarta, Indonesia compares with Manaus, Brazil: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jakarta is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Jakarta is 0.7 °C warmer (Jakarta 27.1 °C, Manaus 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jakarta 32.4 °C (September), Manaus 32.3 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Jakarta 21.7 °C, Manaus 22.2 °C.
  • Jakarta gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 2,513 mm against 2,429 mm.
  • Jakarta gets about 881 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,648 h against 1,767 h; about 1961–1990).
