Jakarta vs Manaus climate

How the climate of Jakarta, Indonesia compares with Manaus, Brazil, 18,227 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jakarta is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Jakarta is 0.7 °C warmer (Jakarta 27.1 °C, Manaus 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jakarta 31.3 °C (September), Manaus 30.8 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Jakarta 22.8 °C, Manaus 23.4 °C.
  • Jakarta gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 2,513 mm against 2,429 mm.
  • Jakarta gets about 881 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,648 h against 1,767 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each

  • Sunnier: Jakarta in 12 of 12 months
