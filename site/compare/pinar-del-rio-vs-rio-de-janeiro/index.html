How the climate of Pinar del Río, Cuba compares with Rio de Janeiro, Brazil: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Pinar del Río is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 24.7 °C against 24.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Rio de Janeiro is 5.9 °C warmer (Pinar del Río 21.3 °C, Rio de Janeiro 27.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pinar del Río 31.9 °C (July), Rio de Janeiro 31.2 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Pinar del Río 16.3 °C, Rio de Janeiro 18.4 °C.
  • Pinar del Río gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,365 mm against 1,310 mm.
  • Pinar del Río gets about 625 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,715 h against 2,091 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
