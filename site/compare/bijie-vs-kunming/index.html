Bijie vs Kunming climate

How the climate of Bijie, China compares with Kunming, China, 359 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kunming is 4.2 °C (7.5 °F) warmer over the year: 16.4 °C against 12.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Kunming is 7.8 °C warmer (Bijie 2.0 °C, Kunming 9.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bijie 24.1 °C (July), Kunming 23.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Bijie -1.5 °C, Kunming 4.5 °C.
  • Bijie gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,072 mm against 974 mm.
  • Kunming gets about 689 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,203 h against 1,514 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bijie 97 cm, Kunming 0.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
