Bijie vs Kunming climate

How the climate of Bijie, China compares with Kunming, China, 359 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kunming is 3.1 °C (5.6 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 13.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Kunming is 6.7 °C warmer (Bijie 3.4 °C, Kunming 10.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bijie 25.2 °C (July), Kunming 23.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Bijie -0.2 °C, Kunming 4.8 °C.
  • Bijie gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 973 mm against 929 mm.
  • Kunming gets about 689 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,203 h against 1,514 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bijie 31 cm, Kunming 0 cm (ERA5-Land).
