Gaziantep vs Kermanshah climate

How the climate of Gaziantep, Turkey compares with Kermanshah, Iran, 926 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gaziantep is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 15.3 °C against 15.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Gaziantep is 2.1 °C warmer (Gaziantep 3.3 °C, Kermanshah 1.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gaziantep 35.3 °C (July), Kermanshah 39.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gaziantep -0.9 °C, Kermanshah -4.3 °C.
  • Gaziantep gets 31% more rain and snow a year: 689 mm against 526 mm.
  • Gaziantep gets about 259 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,122 h against 2,863 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gaziantep 84 cm, Kermanshah 54 cm (ERA5-Land).
