Catamarca vs Monterrey climate

How the climate of Catamarca, Argentina compares with Monterrey, Mexico, 7,062 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Monterrey is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 20.5 °C against 20.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Catamarca is 12.6 °C warmer (Catamarca 25.9 °C, Monterrey 13.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Catamarca 29.8 °C (December), Monterrey 30.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Catamarca 8.4 °C, Monterrey 8.7 °C.
  • Monterrey gets 40% more rain and snow a year: 688 mm against 493 mm.
  • Catamarca gets about 1,304 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,191 h against 1,887 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Catamarca in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Catamarca in 12 of 12 months
