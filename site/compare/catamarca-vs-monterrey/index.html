How the climate of Catamarca, Argentina compares with Monterrey, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Monterrey is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 21.7 °C against 20.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Monterrey is 14.1 °C warmer (Catamarca 13.4 °C, Monterrey 27.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Catamarca 32.7 °C (January), Monterrey 33.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Catamarca 5.3 °C, Monterrey 8.3 °C.
  • Monterrey gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 627 mm against 467 mm.
  • Catamarca gets about 1,304 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,191 h against 1,887 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
