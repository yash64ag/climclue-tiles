Marand vs Yerevan climate

How the climate of Marand, Iran compares with Yerevan, Armenia, 222 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Marand is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 11.8 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Marand is 0.7 °C warmer (Marand 6.3 °C, Yerevan 5.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Marand 31.2 °C (July), Yerevan 31.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Marand -6.8 °C, Yerevan -7.3 °C.
  • Yerevan gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 483 mm against 372 mm.
  • Marand gets about 205 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,701 h against 2,497 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Marand 183 cm, Yerevan 109 cm (ERA5-Land).
