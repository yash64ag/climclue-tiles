How the climate of Marand, Iran compares with Yerevan, Armenia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yerevan is 1.6 °C (2.8 °F) warmer over the year: 13.2 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Yerevan is 2.3 °C warmer (Marand 24.6 °C, Yerevan 26.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Marand 31.7 °C (July), Yerevan 34.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Marand -6.7 °C, Yerevan -5.6 °C.
  • Yerevan gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 446 mm against 365 mm.
  • Marand gets about 205 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,701 h against 2,497 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Marand 61 cm, Yerevan 92 cm (ERA5-Land).
