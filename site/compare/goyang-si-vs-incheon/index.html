How the climate of Goyang-si, South Korea compares with Incheon, South Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Goyang-si and Incheon have the same yearly average temperature (12.0 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Incheon is 1.5 °C warmer (Goyang-si -0.5 °C, Incheon 0.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Goyang-si 29.4 °C (August), Incheon 28.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Goyang-si -7.7 °C, Incheon -6.3 °C.
  • Goyang-si gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,429 mm against 1,414 mm.
  • Incheon gets about 23 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,354 h against 2,331 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Goyang-si 27 cm, Incheon 24 cm (ERA5-Land).
