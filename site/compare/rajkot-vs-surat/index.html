Rājkot vs Surat climate

How the climate of Rājkot, India compares with Surat, India, 243 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Surat is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Surat is 2.6 °C warmer (Rājkot 19.6 °C, Surat 22.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rājkot 39.2 °C (May), Surat 34.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Rājkot 13.4 °C, Surat 17.2 °C.
  • Surat gets 95% more rain and snow a year: 1,443 mm against 741 mm.
  • Rājkot gets about 210 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,075 h against 2,865 h; about 1961–1990).
