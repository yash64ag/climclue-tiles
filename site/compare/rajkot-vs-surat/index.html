How the climate of Rājkot, India compares with Surat, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Surat is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Surat is 2.5 °C warmer (Rājkot 19.7 °C, Surat 22.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rājkot 39.3 °C (May), Surat 36.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Rājkot 11.4 °C, Surat 13.8 °C.
  • Surat gets 91% more rain and snow a year: 1,412 mm against 738 mm.
  • Rājkot gets about 210 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,075 h against 2,865 h; about 1961–1990).
