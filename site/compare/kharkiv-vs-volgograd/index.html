How the climate of Kharkiv, Ukraine compares with Volgograd, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Volgograd is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 9.0 °C against 8.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Volgograd is 3.0 °C warmer (Kharkiv 21.4 °C, Volgograd 24.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kharkiv 26.4 °C (July), Volgograd 30.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kharkiv -7.1 °C, Volgograd -9.0 °C.
  • Kharkiv gets 36% more rain and snow a year: 526 mm against 387 mm.
  • Volgograd gets about 364 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,116 h against 1,752 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kharkiv 105 cm, Volgograd 99 cm (ERA5-Land).
