Saitama vs Sendai climate

How the climate of Saitama, Japan compares with Sendai, Japan, 283 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Saitama is 2.9 °C (5.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 12.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Saitama is 3.6 °C warmer (Saitama 13.8 °C, Sendai 10.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Saitama 30.3 °C (August), Sendai 26.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Saitama -1.8 °C, Sendai -3.0 °C.
  • Sendai gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,518 mm against 1,327 mm.
  • Saitama gets about 44 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,863 h against 1,819 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Saitama 20 cm, Sendai 75 cm (ERA5-Land).
