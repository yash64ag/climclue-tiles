How the climate of Saitama, Japan compares with Sendai, Miyagi, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Saitama is 3.1 °C (5.6 °F) warmer over the year: 15.5 °C against 12.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Saitama is 3.8 °C warmer (Saitama 8.4 °C, Sendai, Miyagi 4.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Saitama 31.5 °C (August), Sendai, Miyagi 28.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Saitama -0.7 °C, Sendai, Miyagi -2.0 °C.
  • Sendai, Miyagi gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,487 mm against 1,336 mm.
  • Saitama gets about 44 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,863 h against 1,819 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Saitama 20 cm, Sendai, Miyagi 75 cm (ERA5-Land).
