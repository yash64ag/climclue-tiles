Guangzhou vs Zhaoqing climate

How the climate of Guangzhou, China compares with Zhaoqing, China, 81 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Guangzhou is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 22.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Guangzhou is 0.6 °C warmer (Guangzhou 15.8 °C, Zhaoqing 15.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guangzhou 31.7 °C (July), Zhaoqing 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Guangzhou 10.1 °C, Zhaoqing 9.9 °C.
  • Guangzhou gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 2,053 mm against 1,703 mm.
  • Zhaoqing gets about 13 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,814 h against 1,800 h; about 1961–1990).
