Guangzhou vs Zhaoqing climate

How the climate of Guangzhou, China compares with Zhaoqing, China, 81 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Guangzhou is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 22.9 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Guangzhou is 0.6 °C warmer (Guangzhou 18.9 °C, Zhaoqing 18.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guangzhou 31.7 °C (July), Zhaoqing 31.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Guangzhou 10.1 °C, Zhaoqing 9.8 °C.
  • Guangzhou gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 2,108 mm against 1,718 mm.
  • Zhaoqing gets about 13 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,814 h against 1,800 h; about 1961–1990).
