How the climate of Lanzhou, China compares with Wuwei, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lanzhou is 2.0 °C (3.5 °F) warmer over the year: 11.0 °C against 9.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Lanzhou is 3.0 °C warmer (Lanzhou 6.6 °C, Wuwei 3.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lanzhou 29.6 °C (July), Wuwei 29.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lanzhou -9.6 °C, Wuwei -13.2 °C.
  • Lanzhou gets 68% more rain and snow a year: 285 mm against 169 mm.
  • Wuwei gets about 338 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,814 h against 2,476 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lanzhou 7.4 cm, Wuwei 9.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
