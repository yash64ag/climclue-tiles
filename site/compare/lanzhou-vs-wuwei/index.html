Lanzhou vs Wuwei climate

How the climate of Lanzhou, China compares with Wuwei, China, 234 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lanzhou is 2.5 °C (4.5 °F) warmer over the year: 11.5 °C against 9.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Lanzhou is 3.6 °C warmer (Lanzhou 1.3 °C, Wuwei -2.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lanzhou 28.9 °C (July), Wuwei 28.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lanzhou -8.1 °C, Wuwei -12.3 °C.
  • Lanzhou gets 68% more rain and snow a year: 285 mm against 169 mm.
  • Wuwei gets about 338 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,814 h against 2,476 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lanzhou 7.4 cm, Wuwei 9.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
