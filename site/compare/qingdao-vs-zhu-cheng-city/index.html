How the climate of Qingdao, China compares with Zhu Cheng City, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zhu Cheng City is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 13.2 °C against 13.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Zhu Cheng City is 3.7 °C warmer (Qingdao 15.3 °C, Zhu Cheng City 19.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qingdao 28.4 °C (August), Zhu Cheng City 29.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Qingdao -2.5 °C, Zhu Cheng City -5.6 °C.
  • Qingdao gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 728 mm against 702 mm.
  • Qingdao gets about 20 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,470 h against 2,450 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Qingdao 11 cm, Zhu Cheng City 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
