Qingdao vs Zhu Cheng City climate

How the climate of Qingdao, China compares with Zhu Cheng City, China, 88 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Qingdao and Zhu Cheng City have the same yearly average temperature (13.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Zhu Cheng City is 3.3 °C warmer (Qingdao 15.4 °C, Zhu Cheng City 18.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qingdao 26.8 °C (August), Zhu Cheng City 29.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Qingdao -1.8 °C, Zhu Cheng City -5.9 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (726 mm and 723 mm).
  • Qingdao gets about 20 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,470 h against 2,450 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Qingdao 11 cm, Zhu Cheng City 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
