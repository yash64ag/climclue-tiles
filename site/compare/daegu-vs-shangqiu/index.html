Daegu vs Shangqiu climate

How the climate of Daegu, South Korea compares with Shangqiu, China, 1,187 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shangqiu is 1.0 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 15.0 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Shangqiu is 2.8 °C warmer (Daegu 23.1 °C, Shangqiu 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daegu 31.1 °C (August), Shangqiu 31.8 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Daegu -5.3 °C, Shangqiu -4.1 °C.
  • Daegu gets 35% more rain and snow a year: 979 mm against 724 mm.
  • Daegu gets about 206 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,483 h against 2,276 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daegu 13 cm, Shangqiu 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
