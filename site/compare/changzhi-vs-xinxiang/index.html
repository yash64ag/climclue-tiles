Main differences

  • Xinxiang is 4.7 °C (8.4 °F) warmer over the year: 15.0 °C against 10.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Xinxiang is 5.1 °C warmer (Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 21.4 °C, Xinxiang 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 28.0 °C (July), Xinxiang 32.7 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) -10.0 °C, Xinxiang -4.9 °C.
  • Xinxiang gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 606 mm against 571 mm.
  • Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) gets about 84 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,387 h against 2,303 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 35 cm, Xinxiang 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
