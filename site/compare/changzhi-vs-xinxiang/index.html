Main differences

  • Xinxiang is 5.1 °C (9.3 °F) warmer over the year: 15.0 °C against 9.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Xinxiang is 5.7 °C warmer (Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 20.8 °C, Xinxiang 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 27.1 °C (July), Xinxiang 31.8 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) -10.0 °C, Xinxiang -4.5 °C.
  • Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 638 mm against 617 mm.
  • Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) gets about 84 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,387 h against 2,303 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 35 cm, Xinxiang 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
