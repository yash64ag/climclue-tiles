Amman vs Damascus climate

How the climate of Amman, Jordan compares with Damascus, Syria, 176 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Damascus is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 17.5 °C against 16.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Damascus is 2.2 °C warmer (Amman 25.0 °C, Damascus 27.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amman 31.9 °C (August), Damascus 35.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Amman 3.1 °C, Damascus 2.0 °C.
  • Amman gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 326 mm against 283 mm.
  • Amman gets about 232 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,308 h against 3,075 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amman 1.3 cm, Damascus 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
