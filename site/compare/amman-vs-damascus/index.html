Amman vs Damascus climate

How the climate of Amman, Jordan compares with Damascus, Syria, 176 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Amman is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Damascus is 1.5 °C warmer (Amman 24.8 °C, Damascus 26.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amman 31.4 °C (August), Damascus 33.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Amman 2.7 °C, Damascus 1.3 °C.
  • Amman gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 332 mm against 327 mm.
  • Amman gets about 232 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,308 h against 3,075 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amman 2.3 cm, Damascus 20 cm (ERA5-Land).
