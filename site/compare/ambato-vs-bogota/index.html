Ambato vs Bogotá climate

How the climate of Ambato, Ecuador compares with Bogotá, Colombia, 824 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bogotá is 0.9 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 14.0 °C against 13.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Bogotá is 1.9 °C warmer (Ambato 12.3 °C, Bogotá 14.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ambato 18.3 °C (September), Bogotá 19.4 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Ambato 7.8 °C, Bogotá 9.8 °C.
  • Bogotá gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 917 mm against 810 mm.
  • Ambato gets about 201 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,733 h against 1,532 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Bogotá in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Ambato in 8 of 12 months
