How the climate of Neyshābūr, Iran compares with Tashkent, Uzbekistan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tashkent is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 14.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Tashkent is 1.7 °C warmer (Neyshābūr 8.1 °C, Tashkent 9.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Neyshābūr 34.1 °C (July), Tashkent 35.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Neyshābūr -4.0 °C, Tashkent -2.0 °C.
  • Tashkent gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 450 mm against 396 mm.
  • Neyshābūr gets about 84 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,874 h against 2,790 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Neyshābūr 42 cm, Tashkent 58 cm (ERA5-Land).
