Araguaína vs Onitsha climate

How the climate of Araguaína, Brazil compares with Onitsha, Nigeria, 6,278 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Onitsha is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Onitsha is 3.6 °C warmer (Araguaína 25.4 °C, Onitsha 29.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Araguaína 33.8 °C (August), Onitsha 35.0 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Araguaína 21.7 °C, Onitsha 22.8 °C.
  • Araguaína gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,809 mm against 1,753 mm.
  • Araguaína gets about 341 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,118 h against 1,776 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Onitsha in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Onitsha in 7 of 12 months
