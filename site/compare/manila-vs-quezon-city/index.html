Manila vs Quezon City climate

How the climate of Manila, Philippines compares with Quezon City, Philippines, 9 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Manila is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 27.8 °C against 27.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Manila is 0.4 °C warmer (Manila 28.6 °C, Quezon City 28.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manila 34.9 °C (April), Quezon City 34.5 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Manila 21.6 °C, Quezon City 21.2 °C.
  • Quezon City gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 2,315 mm against 2,057 mm.
  • Manila gets about 44 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,404 h against 2,360 h; about 1961–1990).
