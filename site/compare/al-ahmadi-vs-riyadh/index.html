How the climate of Al Aḩmadī, Kuwait compares with Riyadh, Saudi Arabia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Riyadh is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.5 °C against 25.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Riyadh is 2.1 °C warmer (Al Aḩmadī 18.9 °C, Riyadh 21.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Al Aḩmadī 44.6 °C (July), Riyadh 43.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Al Aḩmadī 7.8 °C, Riyadh 8.5 °C.
  • Al Aḩmadī gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 120 mm against 103 mm.
  • Riyadh gets about 200 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,360 h against 3,160 h; about 1961–1990).
