Al Aḩmadī vs Riyadh climate

How the climate of Al Aḩmadī, Kuwait compares with Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, 506 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Riyadh is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 26.2 °C against 25.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Riyadh is 1.8 °C warmer (Al Aḩmadī 19.1 °C, Riyadh 20.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Al Aḩmadī 44.0 °C (July), Riyadh 44.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Al Aḩmadī 8.3 °C, Riyadh 7.6 °C.
  • Al Aḩmadī gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 120 mm against 103 mm.
  • Riyadh gets about 200 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,360 h against 3,160 h; about 1961–1990).
