Khasavyurt vs Tbilisi climate

How the climate of Khasavyurt, Russia compares with Tbilisi, Georgia, 225 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tbilisi is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 12.9 °C against 12.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Tbilisi is 1.5 °C warmer (Khasavyurt 5.0 °C, Tbilisi 6.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Khasavyurt 29.7 °C (July), Tbilisi 31.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Khasavyurt -2.9 °C, Tbilisi -3.4 °C.
  • Tbilisi gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 567 mm against 483 mm.
  • Tbilisi gets about 154 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,047 h against 1,893 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Khasavyurt 60 cm, Tbilisi 41 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Khasavyurt in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Tbilisi in 8 of 12 months
