Khasavyurt vs Tbilisi climate

How the climate of Khasavyurt, Russia compares with Tbilisi, Georgia, 225 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tbilisi is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 12.4 °C against 12.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Tbilisi is 1.1 °C warmer (Khasavyurt 5.0 °C, Tbilisi 6.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Khasavyurt 28.9 °C (July), Tbilisi 29.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Khasavyurt -3.2 °C, Tbilisi -4.8 °C.
  • Tbilisi gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 582 mm against 490 mm.
  • Tbilisi gets about 154 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,047 h against 1,893 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Khasavyurt 60 cm, Tbilisi 41 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Tbilisi in 9 of 12 months (2 equal)
  • Drier: Khasavyurt in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Tbilisi in 8 of 12 months
