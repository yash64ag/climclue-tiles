Moscow vs Nizhniy Novgorod climate

How the climate of Moscow, Russia compares with Nizhniy Novgorod, Russia, 402 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Moscow is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 5.4 °C against 4.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Moscow is 1.8 °C warmer (Moscow -7.3 °C, Nizhniy Novgorod -9.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Moscow 23.0 °C (July), Nizhniy Novgorod 23.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Moscow -10.8 °C, Nizhniy Novgorod -11.8 °C.
  • Moscow gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 691 mm against 678 mm.
  • Nizhniy Novgorod gets about 102 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,802 h against 1,701 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Moscow 193 cm, Nizhniy Novgorod 205 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Moscow in 7 of 12 months (2 equal)
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Nizhniy Novgorod in 12 of 12 months
