How the climate of Moscow, Russia compares with Nizhniy Novgorod, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Moscow is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 5.8 °C against 5.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Moscow is 2.2 °C warmer (Moscow -6.8 °C, Nizhniy Novgorod -8.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Moscow 24.3 °C (July), Nizhniy Novgorod 24.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Moscow -10.0 °C, Nizhniy Novgorod -11.8 °C.
  • Nizhniy Novgorod gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 697 mm against 691 mm.
  • Nizhniy Novgorod gets about 102 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,802 h against 1,701 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Moscow 185 cm, Nizhniy Novgorod 207 cm (ERA5-Land).
