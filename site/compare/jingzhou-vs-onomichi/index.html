Jingzhou vs Onomichi climate

How the climate of Jingzhou, China compares with Onomichi, Japan, 2,020 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jingzhou is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 17.4 °C against 16.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Jingzhou is 3.3 °C warmer (Jingzhou 22.6 °C, Onomichi 19.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jingzhou 31.5 °C (July), Onomichi 30.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Jingzhou 0.5 °C, Onomichi 1.9 °C.
  • Onomichi gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,308 mm against 1,260 mm.
  • Onomichi gets about 278 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,979 h against 1,701 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jingzhou 11 cm, Onomichi 6.6 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Onomichi in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Onomichi in 9 of 12 months
