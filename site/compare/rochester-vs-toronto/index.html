Rochester, New York vs Toronto climate

How the climate of Rochester, New York, United States compares with Toronto, Canada, 153 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rochester, New York is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 9.2 °C against 8.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Rochester, New York is 1.9 °C warmer (Rochester, New York 13.0 °C, Toronto 11.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rochester, New York 27.6 °C (July), Toronto 25.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Rochester, New York -7.6 °C, Toronto -7.2 °C.
  • Rochester, New York gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 979 mm against 875 mm.
  • Rochester, New York gets about 230 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,232 h against 2,002 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Rochester, New York 142 cm, Toronto 117 cm (ERA5-Land).
