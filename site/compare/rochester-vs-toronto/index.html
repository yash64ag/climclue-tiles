How the climate of Rochester, New York, United States compares with Toronto, Canada: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Rochester, New York is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 9.2 °C against 8.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Rochester, New York is 0.7 °C warmer (Rochester, New York 7.9 °C, Toronto 7.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rochester, New York 27.5 °C (July), Toronto 26.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Rochester, New York -7.7 °C, Toronto -7.7 °C.
  • Rochester, New York gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 979 mm against 875 mm.
  • Rochester, New York gets about 230 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,232 h against 2,002 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Rochester, New York 168 cm, Toronto 96 cm (ERA5-Land).
