Rochester, New York vs Toronto climate

How the climate of Rochester, New York, United States compares with Toronto, Canada, 153 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rochester, New York is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 9.2 °C against 8.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Rochester, New York is 2.4 °C warmer (Rochester, New York 13.1 °C, Toronto 10.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rochester, New York 25.9 °C (July), Toronto 24.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Rochester, New York -6.8 °C, Toronto -6.7 °C.
  • Rochester, New York gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 971 mm against 874 mm.
  • Rochester, New York gets about 230 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,232 h against 2,002 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Rochester, New York 142 cm, Toronto 117 cm (ERA5-Land).
