Qingyuan vs Zhubei climate

How the climate of Qingyuan, China compares with Zhubei, Taiwan, 818 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhubei is 1.0 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 22.5 °C against 21.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Zhubei is 3.0 °C warmer (Qingyuan 14.3 °C, Zhubei 17.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qingyuan 30.8 °C (August), Zhubei 30.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Qingyuan 8.4 °C, Zhubei 13.0 °C.
  • Qingyuan gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 2,431 mm against 2,110 mm.
  • Qingyuan gets about 5 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,758 h against 1,753 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Zhubei in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Zhubei in 7 of 12 months
