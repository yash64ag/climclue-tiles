How the climate of Qingyuan, China compares with Zhubei, Taiwan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zhubei is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 22.8 °C against 22.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Zhubei is 2.9 °C warmer (Qingyuan 14.8 °C, Zhubei 17.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qingyuan 33.3 °C (July), Zhubei 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Qingyuan 8.7 °C, Zhubei 13.3 °C.
  • Qingyuan gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 2,101 mm against 1,932 mm.
  • Qingyuan gets about 5 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,758 h against 1,753 h; about 1961–1990).
