How the climate of Bukit Rahman Putra, Malaysia compares with Pekanbaru, Indonesia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bukit Rahman Putra is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Bukit Rahman Putra is 0.5 °C warmer (Bukit Rahman Putra 27.2 °C, Pekanbaru 26.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bukit Rahman Putra 32.1 °C (May), Pekanbaru 32.4 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Bukit Rahman Putra 21.4 °C, Pekanbaru 20.8 °C.
  • Pekanbaru gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 2,786 mm against 2,758 mm.
  • Pekanbaru gets about 14 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,230 h against 2,216 h; about 1961–1990).
