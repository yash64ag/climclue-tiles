Bukit Rahman Putra vs Pekanbaru climate

How the climate of Bukit Rahman Putra, Malaysia compares with Pekanbaru, Indonesia, 301 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bukit Rahman Putra is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.7 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Bukit Rahman Putra is 0.3 °C warmer (Bukit Rahman Putra 27.1 °C, Pekanbaru 26.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bukit Rahman Putra 30.7 °C (August), Pekanbaru 30.6 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Bukit Rahman Putra 21.8 °C, Pekanbaru 22.4 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (2,781 mm and 2,794 mm).
  • Pekanbaru gets about 14 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,230 h against 2,216 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Pekanbaru in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Pekanbaru in 8 of 12 months
