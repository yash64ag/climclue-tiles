Jeddah vs Riyadh climate

How the climate of Jeddah, Saudi Arabia compares with Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, 849 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jeddah is 1.9 °C (3.4 °F) warmer over the year: 28.1 °C against 26.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Jeddah is 9.9 °C warmer (Jeddah 23.6 °C, Riyadh 13.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jeddah 34.0 °C (August), Riyadh 42.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jeddah 20.7 °C, Riyadh 6.6 °C.
  • Riyadh gets 49% more rain and snow a year: 103 mm against 69 mm.
  • Riyadh gets about 43 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,360 h against 3,317 h; about 1961–1990).
