Belgrade vs Budapest climate

How the climate of Belgrade, Serbia compares with Budapest, Hungary, 319 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Belgrade is 1.7 °C (3.1 °F) warmer over the year: 12.6 °C against 10.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Belgrade is 2.1 °C warmer (Belgrade 13.1 °C, Budapest 10.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belgrade 28.2 °C (July), Budapest 26.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Belgrade -2.4 °C, Budapest -3.7 °C.
  • Belgrade gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 684 mm against 588 mm.
  • Belgrade gets about 92 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,991 h against 1,899 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Belgrade 45 cm, Budapest 55 cm (ERA5-Land).
