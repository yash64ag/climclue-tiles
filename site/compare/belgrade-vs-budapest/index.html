How the climate of Belgrade, Serbia compares with Budapest, Hungary: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Belgrade is 1.0 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 12.9 °C against 11.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Belgrade is 1.5 °C warmer (Belgrade 7.5 °C, Budapest 6.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belgrade 28.7 °C (August), Budapest 28.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Belgrade -1.3 °C, Budapest -1.9 °C.
  • Belgrade gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 702 mm against 618 mm.
  • Belgrade gets about 92 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,991 h against 1,899 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Belgrade 45 cm, Budapest 55 cm (ERA5-Land).
