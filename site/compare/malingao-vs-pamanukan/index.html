Malingao vs Pamanukan climate

How the climate of Malingao, Philippines compares with Pamanukan, Indonesia, 2,378 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Malingao is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 28.3 °C against 27.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Malingao is 2.0 °C warmer (Malingao 29.6 °C, Pamanukan 27.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Malingao 33.4 °C (April), Pamanukan 31.7 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Malingao 23.5 °C, Pamanukan 23.9 °C.
  • Pamanukan gets 35% more rain and snow a year: 2,002 mm against 1,486 mm.
  • Pamanukan gets about 491 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,795 h against 2,303 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Malingao in 10 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Pamanukan in 11 of 12 months
