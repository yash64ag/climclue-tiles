Antalya vs İzmir climate

How the climate of Antalya, Turkey compares with İzmir, Turkey, 355 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Antalya is 1.1 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 18.6 °C against 17.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Antalya is 1.5 °C warmer (Antalya 25.1 °C, İzmir 23.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Antalya 35.0 °C (July), İzmir 34.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Antalya 4.8 °C, İzmir 3.2 °C.
  • Antalya gets 40% more rain and snow a year: 1,012 mm against 721 mm.
  • İzmir gets about 25 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,128 h against 3,103 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Antalya 15 cm, İzmir 9.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
