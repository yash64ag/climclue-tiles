Aden vs Sanaa climate

How the climate of Aden, Yemen compares with Sanaa, Yemen, 300 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Aden is 9.3 °C (16.7 °F) warmer over the year: 27.5 °C against 18.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Aden is 10.8 °C warmer (Aden 28.0 °C, Sanaa 17.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aden 31.7 °C (July), Sanaa 26.9 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Aden 23.3 °C, Sanaa 8.1 °C.
  • Sanaa gets 172% more rain and snow a year: 231 mm against 85 mm.
  • Aden gets about 218 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,132 h against 2,915 h; about 1961–1990).
