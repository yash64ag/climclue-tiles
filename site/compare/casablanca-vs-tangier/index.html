Casablanca vs Tangier climate

How the climate of Casablanca, Morocco compares with Tangier, Morocco, 293 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Casablanca is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 18.4 °C against 18.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Casablanca is 1.1 °C warmer (Casablanca 15.8 °C, Tangier 14.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Casablanca 28.5 °C (August), Tangier 29.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Casablanca 8.8 °C, Tangier 9.2 °C.
  • Tangier gets 90% more rain and snow a year: 693 mm against 365 mm.
  • Casablanca gets about 72 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,895 h against 2,823 h; about 1961–1990).
