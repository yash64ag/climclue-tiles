Huế vs Tocoa climate

How the climate of Huế, Vietnam compares with Tocoa, Honduras, 16,142 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tocoa is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 26.3 °C against 26.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Tocoa is 2.3 °C warmer (Huế 21.4 °C, Tocoa 23.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huế 32.5 °C (July), Tocoa 32.6 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Huế 19.0 °C, Tocoa 20.3 °C.
  • Huế gets 54% more rain and snow a year: 2,948 mm against 1,914 mm.
  • Tocoa gets about 238 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,497 h against 2,260 h; about 1961–1990).
