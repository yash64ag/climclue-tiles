Ahmedabad vs Surat climate

How the climate of Ahmedabad, India compares with Surat, India, 205 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ahmedabad is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 27.1 °C against 27.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Ahmedabad is 2.9 °C warmer (Ahmedabad 33.3 °C, Surat 30.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ahmedabad 41.1 °C (May), Surat 36.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Ahmedabad 11.6 °C, Surat 13.8 °C.
  • Surat gets 61% more rain and snow a year: 1,412 mm against 876 mm.
  • Ahmedabad gets about 136 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,001 h against 2,865 h; about 1961–1990).
