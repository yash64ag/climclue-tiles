Anyang vs Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) climate

Main differences

  • Anyang is 4.5 °C (8.1 °F) warmer over the year: 14.8 °C against 10.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Anyang is 5.3 °C warmer (Anyang 26.6 °C, Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 21.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Anyang 32.0 °C (June), Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 27.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Anyang -5.4 °C, Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) -9.8 °C.
  • Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 571 mm against 552 mm.
  • Anyang gets about 20 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,406 h against 2,387 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Anyang 11 cm, Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 35 cm (ERA5-Land).
