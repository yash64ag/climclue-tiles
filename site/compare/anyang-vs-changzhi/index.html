How the climate of Anyang, China compares with Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E), China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Anyang is 4.3 °C (7.7 °F) warmer over the year: 14.6 °C against 10.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Anyang is 4.8 °C warmer (Anyang 15.7 °C, Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 10.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Anyang 32.2 °C (June), Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 28.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Anyang -5.3 °C, Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) -10.0 °C.
  • Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 571 mm against 552 mm.
  • Anyang gets about 20 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,406 h against 2,387 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Anyang 11 cm, Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 35 cm (ERA5-Land).
