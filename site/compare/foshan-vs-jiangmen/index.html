Foshan vs Jiangmen climate

How the climate of Foshan, China compares with Jiangmen, China, 50 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Foshan is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 23.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Foshan is 0.5 °C warmer (Foshan 28.1 °C, Jiangmen 27.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Foshan 31.9 °C (July), Jiangmen 31.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Foshan 10.4 °C, Jiangmen 10.5 °C.
  • Jiangmen gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 2,173 mm against 1,983 mm.
  • Jiangmen gets about 68 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,875 h against 1,807 h; about 1961–1990).
