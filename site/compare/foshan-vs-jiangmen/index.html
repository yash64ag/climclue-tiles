How the climate of Foshan, China compares with Jiangmen, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Foshan and Jiangmen have the same yearly average temperature (23.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Foshan is 0.3 °C warmer (Foshan 28.1 °C, Jiangmen 27.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Foshan 33.3 °C (July), Jiangmen 32.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Foshan 10.5 °C, Jiangmen 11.2 °C.
  • Jiangmen gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 2,139 mm against 1,981 mm.
  • Jiangmen gets about 68 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,875 h against 1,807 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Foshan in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Jiangmen in 12 of 12 months
