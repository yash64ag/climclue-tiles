How the climate of Beijing, China compares with Sariwŏn-si, North Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Beijing is 1.6 °C (2.8 °F) warmer over the year: 13.0 °C against 11.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Beijing is 4.7 °C warmer (Beijing 22.1 °C, Sariwŏn-si 17.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Beijing 32.5 °C (June), Sariwŏn-si 29.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Beijing -9.5 °C, Sariwŏn-si -9.4 °C.
  • Sariwŏn-si gets 50% more rain and snow a year: 846 mm against 563 mm.
  • Beijing gets about 277 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,704 h against 2,428 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Beijing 12 cm, Sariwŏn-si 26 cm (ERA5-Land).
