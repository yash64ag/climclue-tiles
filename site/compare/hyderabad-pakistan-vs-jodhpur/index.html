How the climate of Hyderabad, Pakistan compares with Jodhpur, Rajasthan, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hyderabad is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Hyderabad is 1.2 °C warmer (Hyderabad 23.4 °C, Jodhpur, Rajasthan 22.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hyderabad 40.9 °C (May), Jodhpur, Rajasthan 41.5 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Hyderabad 10.0 °C, Jodhpur, Rajasthan 8.5 °C.
  • Jodhpur, Rajasthan gets 121% more rain and snow a year: 375 mm against 170 mm.
  • Hyderabad gets about 208 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,301 h against 3,092 h; about 1961–1990).
