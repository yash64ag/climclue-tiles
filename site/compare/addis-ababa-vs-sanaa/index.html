How the climate of Addis Ababa, Ethiopia compares with Sanaa, Yemen: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Sanaa is 3.0 °C (5.4 °F) warmer over the year: 18.2 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Sanaa is 6.5 °C warmer (Addis Ababa 14.4 °C, Sanaa 20.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Addis Ababa 23.9 °C (March), Sanaa 28.7 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Addis Ababa 5.7 °C, Sanaa 6.2 °C.
  • Addis Ababa gets 394% more rain and snow a year: 1,142 mm against 231 mm.
  • Sanaa gets about 87 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,915 h against 2,828 h; about 1961–1990).
