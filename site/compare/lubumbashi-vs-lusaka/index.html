Lubumbashi vs Lusaka climate

How the climate of Lubumbashi, Democratic Republic of the Congo compares with Lusaka, Zambia, 426 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lubumbashi is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 21.2 °C against 20.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Lubumbashi is 2.4 °C warmer (Lubumbashi 18.3 °C, Lusaka 15.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lubumbashi 32.8 °C (October), Lusaka 30.6 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Lubumbashi 9.0 °C, Lusaka 8.4 °C.
  • Lubumbashi gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 1,115 mm against 885 mm.
  • Lusaka gets about 155 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,887 h against 2,732 h; about 1961–1990).
