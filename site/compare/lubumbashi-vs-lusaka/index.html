Lubumbashi vs Lusaka climate

How the climate of Lubumbashi, Democratic Republic of the Congo compares with Lusaka, Zambia, 426 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lubumbashi is 1.1 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 21.2 °C against 20.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Lubumbashi is 2.6 °C warmer (Lubumbashi 18.4 °C, Lusaka 15.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lubumbashi 30.9 °C (October), Lusaka 29.9 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Lubumbashi 11.8 °C, Lusaka 10.3 °C.
  • Lubumbashi gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 1,135 mm against 881 mm.
  • Lusaka gets about 155 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,887 h against 2,732 h; about 1961–1990).
