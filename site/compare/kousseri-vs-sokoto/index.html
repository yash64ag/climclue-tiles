Kousséri vs Sokoto climate

How the climate of Kousséri, Cameroon compares with Sokoto, Nigeria, 1,068 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Sokoto is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 28.9 °C against 28.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Sokoto is 0.7 °C warmer (Kousséri 26.6 °C, Sokoto 27.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kousséri 41.3 °C (April), Sokoto 40.5 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Kousséri 16.4 °C, Sokoto 17.2 °C.
  • Sokoto gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 576 mm against 495 mm.
  • Kousséri gets about 373 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,162 h against 2,789 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Kousséri in 7 of 12 months (2 equal)
  • Sunnier: Kousséri in 9 of 12 months
