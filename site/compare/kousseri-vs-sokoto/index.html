How the climate of Kousséri, Cameroon compares with Sokoto, Nigeria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Sokoto is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 28.9 °C against 28.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Sokoto is 0.6 °C warmer (Kousséri 26.5 °C, Sokoto 27.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kousséri 41.4 °C (April), Sokoto 41.0 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Kousséri 15.1 °C, Sokoto 16.1 °C.
  • Sokoto gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 567 mm against 493 mm.
  • Kousséri gets about 373 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,162 h against 2,789 h; about 1961–1990).
