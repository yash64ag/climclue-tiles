Diyarbakır vs Gaziantep climate

How the climate of Diyarbakır, Turkey compares with Gaziantep, Turkey, 268 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Diyarbakır is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.3 °C against 15.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Diyarbakır is 3.8 °C warmer (Diyarbakır 31.3 °C, Gaziantep 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Diyarbakır 37.7 °C (July), Gaziantep 34.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Diyarbakır -2.1 °C, Gaziantep -1.1 °C.
  • Gaziantep gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 705 mm against 567 mm.
  • Gaziantep gets about 105 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,122 h against 3,017 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Diyarbakır 59 cm, Gaziantep 49 cm (ERA5-Land).
