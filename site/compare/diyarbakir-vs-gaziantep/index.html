How the climate of Diyarbakır, Turkey compares with Gaziantep, Turkey: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Diyarbakır is 0.9 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 16.2 °C against 15.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Diyarbakır is 3.4 °C warmer (Diyarbakır 31.1 °C, Gaziantep 27.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Diyarbakır 39.1 °C (July), Gaziantep 35.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Diyarbakır -1.6 °C, Gaziantep -0.9 °C.
  • Gaziantep gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 689 mm against 558 mm.
  • Gaziantep gets about 105 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,122 h against 3,017 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Diyarbakır 39 cm, Gaziantep 84 cm (ERA5-Land).
