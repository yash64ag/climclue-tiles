How the climate of Macau, Macao compares with Yangjiang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yangjiang is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.2 °C against 23.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Yangjiang is 0.4 °C warmer (Macau 25.9 °C, Yangjiang 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Macau 31.4 °C (July), Yangjiang 31.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Macau 12.6 °C, Yangjiang 12.0 °C.
  • Yangjiang gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 2,689 mm against 2,195 mm.
  • Macau gets about 17 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,945 h against 1,929 h; about 1961–1990).
