Macau vs Yangjiang climate

How the climate of Macau, Macao compares with Yangjiang, China, 168 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Macau and Yangjiang have the same yearly average temperature (23.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Yangjiang is 0.5 °C warmer (Macau 22.9 °C, Yangjiang 23.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Macau 29.8 °C (August), Yangjiang 31.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Macau 12.9 °C, Yangjiang 11.6 °C.
  • Yangjiang gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 2,715 mm against 2,096 mm.
  • Macau gets about 17 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,945 h against 1,929 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Macau in 7 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: Macau in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
