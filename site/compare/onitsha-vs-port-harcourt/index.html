Onitsha vs Port Harcourt climate

How the climate of Onitsha, Nigeria compares with Port Harcourt, Nigeria, 155 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Onitsha is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.7 °C against 26.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Onitsha is 1.5 °C warmer (Onitsha 28.9 °C, Port Harcourt 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Onitsha 34.6 °C (February), Port Harcourt 32.6 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Onitsha 21.6 °C, Port Harcourt 21.4 °C.
  • Port Harcourt gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 2,285 mm against 1,803 mm.
  • Onitsha gets about 404 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,776 h against 1,373 h; about 1961–1990).
