How the climate of Jeju City, South Korea compares with Tokyo, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jeju City is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 15.7 °C against 15.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Jeju City is 1.8 °C warmer (Jeju City 6.2 °C, Tokyo 4.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jeju City 29.4 °C (August), Tokyo 30.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Jeju City 2.9 °C, Tokyo -0.2 °C.
  • Jeju City gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,761 mm against 1,516 mm.
  • Jeju City gets about 267 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,105 h against 1,837 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jeju City 12 cm, Tokyo 19 cm (ERA5-Land).
