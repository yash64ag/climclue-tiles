How the climate of Jeju City, South Korea compares with Tokyo, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jeju City and Tokyo have the same yearly average temperature (15.7 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Tokyo is 1.8 °C warmer (Jeju City 16.8 °C, Tokyo 18.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jeju City 29.4 °C (August), Tokyo 31.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Jeju City 2.9 °C, Tokyo 0.8 °C.
  • Jeju City gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,761 mm against 1,516 mm.
  • Jeju City gets about 267 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,105 h against 1,837 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jeju City 12 cm, Tokyo 19 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Tokyo in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Jeju City in 9 of 12 months
