Hengyang vs Yongzhou climate

How the climate of Hengyang, China compares with Yongzhou, China, 113 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Hengyang is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 19.1 °C against 19.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Hengyang is 0.4 °C warmer (Hengyang 30.8 °C, Yongzhou 30.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hengyang 33.9 °C (July), Yongzhou 33.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hengyang 3.1 °C, Yongzhou 2.9 °C.
  • Hengyang gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,568 mm against 1,454 mm.
  • Hengyang gets about 32 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,612 h against 1,580 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hengyang 6.5 cm, Yongzhou 6.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
