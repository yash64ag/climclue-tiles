Kabul vs Shymkent climate

How the climate of Kabul, Afghanistan compares with Shymkent, Kazakhstan, 866 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shymkent is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 12.5 °C against 11.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Shymkent is 2.2 °C warmer (Kabul 21.6 °C, Shymkent 23.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kabul 30.1 °C (July), Shymkent 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kabul -10.4 °C, Shymkent -6.5 °C.
  • Shymkent gets 76% more rain and snow a year: 608 mm against 346 mm.
  • Kabul gets about 219 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,031 h against 2,812 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kabul 128 cm, Shymkent 134 cm (ERA5-Land).
