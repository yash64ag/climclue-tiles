Dublin vs Liverpool climate

How the climate of Dublin, Ireland compares with Liverpool, United Kingdom, 217 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dublin is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 10.4 °C against 10.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Dublin is 0.7 °C warmer (Dublin 5.8 °C, Liverpool 5.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dublin 19.8 °C (July), Liverpool 19.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Dublin 3.3 °C, Liverpool 2.5 °C.
  • Dublin gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 941 mm against 859 mm.
  • Dublin gets about 33 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,392 h against 1,359 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Dublin 4.0 cm, Liverpool 5.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
