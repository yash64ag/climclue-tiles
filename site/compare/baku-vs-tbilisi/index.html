How the climate of Baku, Azerbaijan compares with Tbilisi, Georgia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Baku is 2.1 °C (3.9 °F) warmer over the year: 15.0 °C against 12.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Baku is 6.1 °C warmer (Baku 8.0 °C, Tbilisi 1.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baku 30.2 °C (August), Tbilisi 31.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baku 2.8 °C, Tbilisi -3.4 °C.
  • Tbilisi gets 167% more rain and snow a year: 567 mm against 213 mm.
  • Baku gets about 61 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,108 h against 2,047 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baku 7.3 cm, Tbilisi 41 cm (ERA5-Land).
