Huai'an vs Lianyungang climate

How the climate of Huai'an, China compares with Lianyungang, China, 114 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Huai'an is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.0 °C against 14.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Huai'an is 0.9 °C warmer (Huai'an 3.8 °C, Lianyungang 2.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huai'an 30.8 °C (July), Lianyungang 30.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Huai'an -3.1 °C, Lianyungang -3.6 °C.
  • Huai'an gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 973 mm against 897 mm.
  • Lianyungang gets about 122 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,300 h against 2,179 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Huai'an 13 cm, Lianyungang 9.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
