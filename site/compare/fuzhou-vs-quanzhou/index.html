Fuzhou, Fujian vs Quanzhou climate

How the climate of Fuzhou, Fujian, China compares with Quanzhou, China, 147 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Quanzhou is 1.4 °C (2.6 °F) warmer over the year: 21.5 °C against 20.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Quanzhou is 2.6 °C warmer (Fuzhou, Fujian 11.1 °C, Quanzhou 13.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuzhou, Fujian 33.9 °C (July), Quanzhou 32.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuzhou, Fujian 6.8 °C, Quanzhou 9.5 °C.
  • Fuzhou, Fujian gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,289 mm against 1,162 mm.
  • Quanzhou gets about 195 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,958 h against 1,763 h; about 1961–1990).
