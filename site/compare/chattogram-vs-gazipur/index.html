How the climate of Chattogram, Bangladesh compares with Gazipur, Bangladesh: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Chattogram is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 25.5 °C against 25.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Chattogram is 2.0 °C warmer (Chattogram 19.8 °C, Gazipur 17.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chattogram 31.9 °C (May), Gazipur 33.1 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Chattogram 13.9 °C, Gazipur 11.0 °C.
  • Chattogram gets 45% more rain and snow a year: 3,182 mm against 2,188 mm.
  • Chattogram gets about 85 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,191 h against 2,106 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Gazipur in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Chattogram in 10 of 12 months
