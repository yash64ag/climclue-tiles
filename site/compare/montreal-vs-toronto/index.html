Montréal vs Toronto climate

How the climate of Montréal, Canada compares with Toronto, Canada, 503 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Toronto is 1.8 °C (3.2 °F) warmer over the year: 8.6 °C against 6.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Toronto is 6.2 °C warmer (Montréal -9.8 °C, Toronto -3.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Montréal 25.6 °C (July), Toronto 24.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Montréal -14.2 °C, Toronto -6.7 °C.
  • Montréal gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 1,109 mm against 874 mm.
  • Toronto gets about 33 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,002 h against 1,969 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Montréal 212 cm, Toronto 117 cm (ERA5-Land).
