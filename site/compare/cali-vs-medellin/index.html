Cali vs Medellín climate

How the climate of Cali, Colombia compares with Medellín, Colombia, 330 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Cali is 2.1 °C (3.8 °F) warmer over the year: 22.2 °C against 20.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Cali is 2.4 °C warmer (Cali 22.4 °C, Medellín 20.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cali 28.2 °C (August), Medellín 25.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Cali 16.5 °C, Medellín 14.7 °C.
  • Medellín gets 41% more rain and snow a year: 1,768 mm against 1,251 mm.
  • Medellín gets about 112 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,823 h against 1,711 h; about 1961–1990).
