How the climate of Cali, Colombia compares with Medellín, Colombia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Cali is 3.5 °C (6.3 °F) warmer over the year: 23.5 °C against 20.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Cali is 3.7 °C warmer (Cali 23.7 °C, Medellín 20.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cali 29.9 °C (August), Medellín 25.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Cali 17.9 °C, Medellín 14.7 °C.
  • Medellín gets 41% more rain and snow a year: 1,768 mm against 1,251 mm.
  • Medellín gets about 112 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,823 h against 1,711 h; about 1961–1990).
