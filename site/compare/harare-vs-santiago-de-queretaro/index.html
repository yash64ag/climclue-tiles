How the climate of Harare, Zimbabwe compares with Santiago de Querétaro, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Santiago de Querétaro is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 18.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Santiago de Querétaro is 7.5 °C warmer (Harare 13.9 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 21.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Harare 28.6 °C (October), Santiago de Querétaro 30.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Harare 5.6 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 5.8 °C.
  • Harare gets 75% more rain and snow a year: 800 mm against 456 mm.
  • Harare gets about 397 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,944 h against 2,547 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
