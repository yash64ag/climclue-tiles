How the climate of Haiphong, Vietnam compares with Qinzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Haiphong is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 24.0 °C against 23.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Haiphong is 2.5 °C warmer (Haiphong 17.0 °C, Qinzhou 14.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Haiphong 32.9 °C (July), Qinzhou 32.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Haiphong 13.8 °C, Qinzhou 11.1 °C.
  • Qinzhou gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 2,418 mm against 1,869 mm.
  • Qinzhou gets about 54 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,777 h against 1,723 h; about 1961–1990).
