Giza vs Tabuk climate

How the climate of Giza, Egypt compares with Tabuk, Saudi Arabia, 550 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Giza is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 21.9 °C against 21.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Giza is 3.1 °C warmer (Giza 13.6 °C, Tabuk 10.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Giza 35.9 °C (July), Tabuk 38.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Giza 8.1 °C, Tabuk 3.0 °C.
  • Tabuk gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 26 mm against 20 mm.
  • Tabuk gets about 373 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,703 h against 3,330 h; about 1961–1990).
