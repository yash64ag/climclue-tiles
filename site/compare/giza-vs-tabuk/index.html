Giza vs Tabuk climate

How the climate of Giza, Egypt compares with Tabuk, Saudi Arabia, 550 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Giza is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 22.1 °C against 21.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Giza is 3.2 °C warmer (Giza 13.8 °C, Tabuk 10.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Giza 35.7 °C (July), Tabuk 38.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Giza 8.3 °C, Tabuk 3.6 °C.
  • Tabuk gets 45% more rain and snow a year: 26 mm against 18 mm.
  • Tabuk gets about 373 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,703 h against 3,330 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Giza in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Tabuk in 12 of 12 months
