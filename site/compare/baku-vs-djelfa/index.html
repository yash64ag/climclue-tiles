How the climate of Baku, Azerbaijan compares with Djelfa, Algeria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Djelfa is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 15.5 °C against 15.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Djelfa is 2.6 °C warmer (Baku 7.3 °C, Djelfa 9.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baku 30.2 °C (August), Djelfa 35.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baku 2.8 °C, Djelfa 0.4 °C.
  • Djelfa gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 268 mm against 213 mm.
  • Djelfa gets about 970 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,078 h against 2,108 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baku 7.3 cm, Djelfa 9.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
