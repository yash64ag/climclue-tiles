How the climate of Jabalpur, India compares with Nagpur, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Nagpur is 1.6 °C (3.0 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 25.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Nagpur is 3.3 °C warmer (Jabalpur 17.4 °C, Nagpur 20.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jabalpur 41.5 °C (May), Nagpur 42.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Jabalpur 9.5 °C, Nagpur 12.5 °C.
  • Jabalpur gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,370 mm against 1,279 mm.
  • Nagpur gets about 48 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,764 h against 2,716 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Nagpur in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Jabalpur in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Nagpur in 9 of 12 months
