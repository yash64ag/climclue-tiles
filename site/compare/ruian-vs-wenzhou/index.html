Rui’an vs Wenzhou climate

How the climate of Rui’an, China compares with Wenzhou, China, 25 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rui’an is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 18.8 °C against 18.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Rui’an is 0.5 °C warmer (Rui’an 16.3 °C, Wenzhou 15.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rui’an 31.8 °C (July), Wenzhou 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Rui’an 5.2 °C, Wenzhou 4.8 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,640 mm and 1,634 mm).
  • Rui’an gets about 1 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,795 h against 1,794 h; about 1961–1990).
