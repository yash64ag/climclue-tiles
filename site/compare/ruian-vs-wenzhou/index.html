Rui’an vs Wenzhou climate

How the climate of Rui’an, China compares with Wenzhou, China, 25 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Rui’an is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 18.8 °C against 18.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Rui’an is 0.6 °C warmer (Rui’an 16.4 °C, Wenzhou 15.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rui’an 30.1 °C (July), Wenzhou 30.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Rui’an 4.9 °C, Wenzhou 4.0 °C.
  • Wenzhou gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,809 mm against 1,607 mm.
  • Rui’an gets about 1 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,795 h against 1,794 h; about 1961–1990).
