How the climate of Rui’an, China compares with Wenzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Rui’an is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 19.5 °C against 18.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Rui’an is 1.4 °C warmer (Rui’an 10.6 °C, Wenzhou 9.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rui’an 32.5 °C (July), Wenzhou 32.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Rui’an 6.0 °C, Wenzhou 5.0 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,640 mm and 1,634 mm).
  • Rui’an gets about 1 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,795 h against 1,794 h; about 1961–1990).
