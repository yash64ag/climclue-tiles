Madurai vs Maicao climate

How the climate of Madurai, India compares with Maicao, Colombia, 15,972 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Madurai is 1.6 °C (3.0 °F) warmer over the year: 29.1 °C against 27.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Madurai is 4.1 °C warmer (Madurai 32.1 °C, Maicao 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Madurai 38.1 °C (May), Maicao 32.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Madurai 19.8 °C, Maicao 23.1 °C.
  • Madurai gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 872 mm against 839 mm.
  • Maicao gets about 13 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,452 h against 2,439 h; about 1961–1990).
