Madurai vs Maicao climate

How the climate of Madurai, India compares with Maicao, Colombia, 15,972 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Madurai is 1.5 °C (2.8 °F) warmer over the year: 29.1 °C against 27.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Madurai is 4.0 °C warmer (Madurai 32.1 °C, Maicao 28.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Madurai 36.2 °C (May), Maicao 33.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Madurai 21.8 °C, Maicao 22.0 °C.
  • Madurai gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 877 mm against 794 mm.
  • Maicao gets about 13 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,452 h against 2,439 h; about 1961–1990).
