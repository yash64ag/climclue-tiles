Yao vs Yixing climate

How the climate of Yao, Japan compares with Yixing, China, 1,514 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yao and Yixing have the same yearly average temperature (16.7 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Yao is 1.8 °C warmer (Yao 7.8 °C, Yixing 6.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Yao 32.2 °C (August), Yixing 31.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Yao 0.4 °C, Yixing 0.4 °C.
  • Yao gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,373 mm against 1,213 mm.
  • Yixing gets about 140 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,982 h against 1,842 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Yao 13 cm, Yixing 16 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
