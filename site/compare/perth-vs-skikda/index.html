How the climate of Perth, Australia compares with Skikda, Algeria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Perth is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 18.8 °C against 18.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Perth is 12.3 °C warmer (Perth 24.8 °C, Skikda 12.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Perth 31.4 °C (February), Skikda 30.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Perth 8.6 °C, Skikda 8.2 °C.
  • Perth gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 780 mm against 744 mm.
  • Perth gets about 104 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,908 h against 2,803 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
