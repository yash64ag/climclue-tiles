Shanghai vs Xuhui climate

How the climate of Shanghai, China compares with Xuhui, China, 3 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Xuhui is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.7 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Xuhui is 0.1 °C warmer (Shanghai 28.1 °C, Xuhui 28.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Shanghai 31.1 °C (July), Xuhui 31.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Shanghai 0.9 °C, Xuhui 0.7 °C.
  • Shanghai gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,459 mm against 1,451 mm.
  • Shanghai gets about 10 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,969 h against 1,960 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Shanghai 7.3 cm, Xuhui 7.4 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Xuhui in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Shanghai in 10 of 12 months
