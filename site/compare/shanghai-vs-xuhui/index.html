How the climate of Shanghai, China compares with Xuhui, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Shanghai and Xuhui have the same yearly average temperature (16.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Shanghai 32.1 °C (July), Xuhui 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Shanghai 1.4 °C, Xuhui 1.4 °C.
  • Xuhui gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,499 mm against 1,476 mm.
  • Shanghai gets about 10 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,969 h against 1,960 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Shanghai 7.7 cm, Xuhui 7.5 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Shanghai in 9 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: Shanghai in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Shanghai in 10 of 12 months
