Kunming vs San Salvador de Jujuy climate

How the climate of Kunming, China compares with San Salvador de Jujuy, Argentina, 18,800 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • San Salvador de Jujuy is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 17.4 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when San Salvador de Jujuy is 11.4 °C warmer (Kunming 10.1 °C, San Salvador de Jujuy 21.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kunming 25.9 °C (April), San Salvador de Jujuy 27.6 °C (December). Coldest-month night lows: Kunming 3.3 °C, San Salvador de Jujuy 4.0 °C.
  • San Salvador de Jujuy gets 32% more rain and snow a year: 1,226 mm against 929 mm.
  • San Salvador de Jujuy gets about 143 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,345 h against 2,203 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
