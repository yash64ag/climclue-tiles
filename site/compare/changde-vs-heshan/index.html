Changde vs Heshan climate

How the climate of Changde, China compares with Heshan, China, 82 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Heshan is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 17.8 °C against 17.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Heshan is 0.3 °C warmer (Changde 29.1 °C, Heshan 29.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changde 32.1 °C (July), Heshan 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changde 1.2 °C, Heshan 1.6 °C.
  • Heshan gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,570 mm against 1,507 mm.
  • Heshan gets about 17 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,588 h against 1,572 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changde 14 cm, Heshan 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
