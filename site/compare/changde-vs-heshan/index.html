How the climate of Changde, China compares with Heshan, Hunan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Changde and Heshan, Hunan have the same yearly average temperature (17.9 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Changde is 0.1 °C warmer (Changde 28.6 °C, Heshan, Hunan 28.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changde 33.1 °C (July), Heshan, Hunan 33.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changde 2.3 °C, Heshan, Hunan 2.5 °C.
  • Heshan, Hunan gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,590 mm against 1,530 mm.
  • Heshan, Hunan gets about 17 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,588 h against 1,572 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changde 14 cm, Heshan, Hunan 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
