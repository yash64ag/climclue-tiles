Changde vs Heshan climate

How the climate of Changde, China compares with Heshan, China, 82 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Heshan is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 17.9 °C against 17.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Heshan is 0.3 °C warmer (Changde 29.1 °C, Heshan 29.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changde 32.0 °C (July), Heshan 32.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changde 1.2 °C, Heshan 1.7 °C.
  • Heshan gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,590 mm against 1,530 mm.
  • Heshan gets about 17 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,588 h against 1,572 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changde 14 cm, Heshan 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
