How the climate of Lilongwe, Malawi compares with Lubumbashi, Democratic Republic of the Congo: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lubumbashi is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 21.2 °C against 20.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Lubumbashi is 2.0 °C warmer (Lilongwe 21.4 °C, Lubumbashi 23.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lilongwe 30.6 °C (October), Lubumbashi 32.8 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Lilongwe 9.5 °C, Lubumbashi 9.0 °C.
  • Lubumbashi gets 40% more rain and snow a year: 1,115 mm against 799 mm.
  • Lilongwe gets about 22 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,754 h against 2,732 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Lubumbashi in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Lilongwe in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
