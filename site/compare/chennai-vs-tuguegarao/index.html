How the climate of Chennai, India compares with Tuguegarao, Philippines: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Chennai is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 27.9 °C against 26.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Chennai is 1.6 °C warmer (Chennai 30.7 °C, Tuguegarao 29.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chennai 36.0 °C (May), Tuguegarao 35.6 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Chennai 19.8 °C, Tuguegarao 18.3 °C.
  • Tuguegarao gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,488 mm against 1,373 mm.
  • Chennai gets about 184 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,675 h against 2,491 h; about 1961–1990).
