Longyan vs Tallahassee climate

How the climate of Longyan, China compares with Tallahassee, United States, 13,439 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Longyan is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 19.7 °C against 19.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Longyan is 1.3 °C warmer (Longyan 21.6 °C, Tallahassee 20.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Longyan 30.3 °C (July), Tallahassee 32.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Longyan 5.8 °C, Tallahassee 6.7 °C.
  • Longyan gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,682 mm against 1,561 mm.
  • Tallahassee gets about 1,075 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,922 h against 1,846 h; about 1961–1990).
