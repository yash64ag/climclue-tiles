How the climate of Longyan, China compares with Tallahassee, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Longyan is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 20.7 °C against 19.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Longyan is 2.4 °C warmer (Longyan 22.7 °C, Tallahassee 20.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Longyan 32.5 °C (July), Tallahassee 32.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Longyan 7.5 °C, Tallahassee 3.9 °C.
  • Longyan gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,570 mm against 1,534 mm.
  • Tallahassee gets about 1,075 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,922 h against 1,846 h; about 1961–1990).
