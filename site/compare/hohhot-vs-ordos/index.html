How the climate of Hohhot, China compares with Ordos, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ordos is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 7.5 °C against 7.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Ordos is 2.1 °C warmer (Hohhot -11.9 °C, Ordos -9.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hohhot 29.1 °C (July), Ordos 28.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hohhot -18.1 °C, Ordos -15.8 °C.
  • Hohhot gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 374 mm against 330 mm.
  • Hohhot gets about 25 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,933 h against 2,907 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hohhot 19 cm, Ordos 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
