Amritsar vs Muridke climate

How the climate of Amritsar, India compares with Muridke, Pakistan, 62 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Muridke is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 23.8 °C against 23.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Muridke is 0.9 °C warmer (Amritsar 30.4 °C, Muridke 31.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amritsar 38.6 °C (June), Muridke 39.3 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Amritsar 5.9 °C, Muridke 5.9 °C.
  • Muridke gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 803 mm against 758 mm.
  • Muridke gets about 126 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,956 h against 2,830 h; about 1961–1990).
