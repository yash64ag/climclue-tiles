How the climate of Amritsar, India compares with Muridke, Pakistan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Muridke is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 23.8 °C against 23.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Muridke is 0.9 °C warmer (Amritsar 30.4 °C, Muridke 31.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amritsar 39.9 °C (June), Muridke 40.2 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Amritsar 4.1 °C, Muridke 4.9 °C.
  • Muridke gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 787 mm against 749 mm.
  • Muridke gets about 126 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,956 h against 2,830 h; about 1961–1990).
