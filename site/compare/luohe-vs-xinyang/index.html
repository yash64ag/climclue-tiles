Luohe vs Xinyang climate

How the climate of Luohe, China compares with Xinyang, China, 161 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Xinyang is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 15.8 °C against 15.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Xinyang is 0.9 °C warmer (Luohe 3.5 °C, Xinyang 4.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Luohe 32.1 °C (June), Xinyang 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Luohe -3.3 °C, Xinyang -1.9 °C.
  • Xinyang gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 1,143 mm against 899 mm.
  • Luohe gets about 124 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,151 h against 2,028 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Luohe 20 cm, Xinyang 28 cm (ERA5-Land).
