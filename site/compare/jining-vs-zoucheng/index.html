Main differences

  • Jining, Shandong is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 14.5 °C against 14.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Jining, Shandong is 0.3 °C warmer (Jining, Shandong 25.6 °C, Zoucheng 25.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jining, Shandong 31.0 °C (July), Zoucheng 30.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jining, Shandong -5.0 °C, Zoucheng -4.9 °C.
  • Zoucheng gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 724 mm against 691 mm.
  • Zoucheng gets about 3 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,406 h against 2,403 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jining, Shandong 8.1 cm, Zoucheng 9.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
