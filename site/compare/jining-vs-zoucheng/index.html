How the climate of Jining, Shandong, China compares with Zoucheng, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jining, Shandong is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 14.5 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Jining, Shandong is 0.5 °C warmer (Jining, Shandong 25.6 °C, Zoucheng 25.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jining, Shandong 31.6 °C (June), Zoucheng 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jining, Shandong -5.4 °C, Zoucheng -5.5 °C.
  • Zoucheng gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 729 mm against 691 mm.
  • Zoucheng gets about 3 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,406 h against 2,403 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jining, Shandong 8.1 cm, Zoucheng 9.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
