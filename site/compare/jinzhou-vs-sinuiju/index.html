Main differences

  • Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 10.1 °C against 9.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) is 1.3 °C warmer (Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) 17.1 °C, Sinŭiju 15.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) 29.1 °C (July), Sinŭiju 28.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) -12.8 °C, Sinŭiju -12.4 °C.
  • Sinŭiju gets 76% more rain and snow a year: 1,025 mm against 582 mm.
  • Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) gets about 169 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,677 h against 2,508 h; about 1961–1990).
