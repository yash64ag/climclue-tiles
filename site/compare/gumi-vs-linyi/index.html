Gumi vs Linyi climate

How the climate of Gumi, South Korea compares with Linyi, China, 911 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Linyi is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 13.8 °C against 13.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Linyi is 1.1 °C warmer (Gumi 23.0 °C, Linyi 24.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gumi 29.4 °C (August), Linyi 30.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gumi -5.4 °C, Linyi -5.2 °C.
  • Gumi gets 44% more rain and snow a year: 1,207 mm against 836 mm.
  • Gumi gets about 110 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,472 h against 2,362 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gumi 17 cm, Linyi 13 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Linyi in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Gumi in 7 of 12 months
