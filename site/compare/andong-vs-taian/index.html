Andong vs Tai’an, Shandong climate

How the climate of Andong, South Korea compares with Tai’an, Shandong, China, 1,040 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tai’an, Shandong is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 13.2 °C against 12.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Tai’an, Shandong is 2.1 °C warmer (Andong 22.3 °C, Tai’an, Shandong 24.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Andong 30.3 °C (August), Tai’an, Shandong 30.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Andong -6.7 °C, Tai’an, Shandong -6.2 °C.
  • Andong gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 1,021 mm against 809 mm.
  • Andong gets about 17 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,513 h against 2,496 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Andong 21 cm, Tai’an, Shandong 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
