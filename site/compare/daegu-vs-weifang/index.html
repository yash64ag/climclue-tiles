How the climate of Daegu, South Korea compares with Weifang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Daegu is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 14.0 °C against 13.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Daegu is 2.1 °C warmer (Daegu 0.3 °C, Weifang -1.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daegu 31.1 °C (August), Weifang 31.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Daegu -5.3 °C, Weifang -6.8 °C.
  • Daegu gets 74% more rain and snow a year: 979 mm against 564 mm.
  • Weifang gets about 43 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,525 h against 2,483 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daegu 13 cm, Weifang 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
