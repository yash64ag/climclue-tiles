Adana vs Khenifra climate

How the climate of Adana, Turkey compares with Khenifra, Morocco, 3,733 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Adana is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 18.9 °C against 18.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Adana is 2.7 °C warmer (Adana 21.7 °C, Khenifra 19.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Adana 34.2 °C (August), Khenifra 36.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Adana 3.7 °C, Khenifra 3.1 °C.
  • Adana gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 687 mm against 655 mm.
  • Khenifra gets about 5 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,997 h against 2,992 h; about 1961–1990).
