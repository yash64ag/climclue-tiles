How the climate of Sariwŏn-si, North Korea compares with Tianjin, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tianjin is 1.9 °C (3.5 °F) warmer over the year: 13.4 °C against 11.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Tianjin is 3.6 °C warmer (Sariwŏn-si 11.5 °C, Tianjin 15.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Sariwŏn-si 29.2 °C (August), Tianjin 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Sariwŏn-si -9.4 °C, Tianjin -7.7 °C.
  • Sariwŏn-si gets 56% more rain and snow a year: 846 mm against 543 mm.
  • Tianjin gets about 202 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,630 h against 2,428 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Sariwŏn-si 26 cm, Tianjin 9.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
