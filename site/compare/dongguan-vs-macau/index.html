Dongguan vs Macau climate

How the climate of Dongguan, China compares with Macau, Macao, 93 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Macau is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 23.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Macau is 1.0 °C warmer (Dongguan 16.1 °C, Macau 17.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dongguan 31.4 °C (August), Macau 29.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Dongguan 10.1 °C, Macau 12.9 °C.
  • Dongguan gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 2,121 mm against 2,096 mm.
  • Macau gets about 95 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,945 h against 1,850 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each

  • Sunnier: Macau in 10 of 12 months
