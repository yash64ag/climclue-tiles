How the climate of Dongguan, China compares with Macau, Macao: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Dongguan and Macau have the same yearly average temperature (23.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Macau is 1.0 °C warmer (Dongguan 16.3 °C, Macau 17.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dongguan 33.0 °C (July), Macau 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Dongguan 10.6 °C, Macau 12.6 °C.
  • Macau gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 2,195 mm against 2,151 mm.
  • Macau gets about 95 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,945 h against 1,850 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Dongguan in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Macau in 10 of 12 months
