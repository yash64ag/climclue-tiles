How the climate of Guangzhou, China compares with Qingyuan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Guangzhou is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 23.2 °C against 22.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Guangzhou is 1.6 °C warmer (Guangzhou 14.7 °C, Qingyuan 13.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guangzhou 33.3 °C (July), Qingyuan 33.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Guangzhou 10.7 °C, Qingyuan 8.7 °C.
  • Qingyuan gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 2,101 mm against 2,053 mm.
  • Guangzhou gets about 43 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,800 h against 1,758 h; about 1961–1990).
