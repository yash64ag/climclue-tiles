How the climate of Bursa, Turkey compares with İzmir, Turkey: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • İzmir is 2.6 °C (4.7 °F) warmer over the year: 17.7 °C against 15.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when İzmir is 3.9 °C warmer (Bursa 24.5 °C, İzmir 28.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bursa 31.1 °C (August), İzmir 34.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bursa 1.8 °C, İzmir 4.5 °C.
  • Bursa gets 62% more rain and snow a year: 1,076 mm against 664 mm.
  • İzmir gets about 356 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,128 h against 2,772 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bursa 27 cm, İzmir 2.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
