How the climate of Linz, Austria compares with Munich, Germany: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Linz is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 10.1 °C against 8.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Linz is 2.4 °C warmer (Linz 15.4 °C, Munich 13.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Linz 25.7 °C (July), Munich 23.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Linz -3.5 °C, Munich -4.2 °C.
  • Munich gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 1,006 mm against 796 mm.
  • Munich gets about 2 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,655 h against 1,653 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Linz 126 cm, Munich 97 cm (ERA5-Land).
