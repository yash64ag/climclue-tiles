Main differences

  • Plano is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 18.4 °C against 18.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Plano is 1.8 °C warmer (Plano 13.7 °C, Yichun, Jiangxi 12.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Plano 34.9 °C (August), Yichun, Jiangxi 34.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Plano 1.6 °C, Yichun, Jiangxi 2.3 °C.
  • Yichun, Jiangxi gets 63% more rain and snow a year: 1,797 mm against 1,102 mm.
  • Plano gets about 1,083 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,804 h against 1,721 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Plano 2.0 cm, Yichun, Jiangxi 7.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
