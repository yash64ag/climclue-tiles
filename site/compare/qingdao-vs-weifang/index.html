Qingdao vs Weifang climate

How the climate of Qingdao, China compares with Weifang, China, 135 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Weifang is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 13.5 °C against 13.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Weifang is 4.6 °C warmer (Qingdao 15.3 °C, Weifang 19.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qingdao 28.4 °C (August), Weifang 31.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Qingdao -2.5 °C, Weifang -6.8 °C.
  • Qingdao gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 728 mm against 564 mm.
  • Weifang gets about 56 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,525 h against 2,470 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Qingdao 11 cm, Weifang 12 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Sunnier: Weifang in 9 of 12 months (1 equal)
