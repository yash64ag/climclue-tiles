Khartoum vs Khartoum North climate

How the climate of Khartoum, Sudan compares with Khartoum North, Sudan, 11 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Khartoum is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 29.7 °C against 29.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Khartoum is 0.3 °C warmer (Khartoum 22.4 °C, Khartoum North 22.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Khartoum 40.5 °C (May), Khartoum North 40.5 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Khartoum 15.2 °C, Khartoum North 14.9 °C.
  • Khartoum gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 109 mm against 102 mm.
  • Khartoum North gets about 5 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,517 h against 3,512 h; about 1961–1990).
