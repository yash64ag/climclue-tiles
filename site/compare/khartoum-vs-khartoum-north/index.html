Khartoum vs Khartoum North climate

How the climate of Khartoum, Sudan compares with Khartoum North, Sudan, 11 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Khartoum is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 29.6 °C against 29.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Khartoum is 0.2 °C warmer (Khartoum 27.9 °C, Khartoum North 27.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Khartoum 42.0 °C (May), Khartoum North 41.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Khartoum 14.6 °C, Khartoum North 14.4 °C.
  • Khartoum gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 107 mm against 101 mm.
  • Khartoum North gets about 5 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,517 h against 3,512 h; about 1961–1990).
