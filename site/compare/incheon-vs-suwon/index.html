Incheon vs Suwon climate

How the climate of Incheon, South Korea compares with Suwon, South Korea, 35 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Suwon is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 12.3 °C against 12.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Suwon is 1.3 °C warmer (Incheon 16.1 °C, Suwon 17.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Incheon 27.3 °C (August), Suwon 28.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Incheon -6.8 °C, Suwon -7.7 °C.
  • Incheon gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,411 mm against 1,297 mm.
  • Suwon gets about 41 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,395 h against 2,354 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Incheon 24 cm, Suwon 26 cm (ERA5-Land).
