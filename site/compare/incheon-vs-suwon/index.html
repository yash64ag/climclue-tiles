How the climate of Incheon, South Korea compares with Suwon, South Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Suwon is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 12.2 °C against 12.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Suwon is 1.3 °C warmer (Incheon 15.9 °C, Suwon 17.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Incheon 28.9 °C (August), Suwon 29.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Incheon -6.3 °C, Suwon -7.5 °C.
  • Incheon gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,414 mm against 1,303 mm.
  • Suwon gets about 41 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,395 h against 2,354 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Incheon 24 cm, Suwon 26 cm (ERA5-Land).
