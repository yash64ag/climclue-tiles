How the climate of Greater Sudbury, Canada compares with Ufa, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Greater Sudbury is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 4.5 °C against 3.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Greater Sudbury is 3.6 °C warmer (Greater Sudbury -0.8 °C, Ufa -4.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Greater Sudbury 25.7 °C (July), Ufa 25.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Greater Sudbury -17.7 °C, Ufa -16.9 °C.
  • Greater Sudbury gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 908 mm against 684 mm.
  • Ufa gets about 40 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,944 h against 1,904 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Greater Sudbury 233 cm, Ufa 229 cm (ERA5-Land).
