Greater Sudbury vs Ufa climate

How the climate of Greater Sudbury, Canada compares with Ufa, Russia, 8,055 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Greater Sudbury is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 4.6 °C against 4.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Greater Sudbury is 3.6 °C warmer (Greater Sudbury -0.6 °C, Ufa -4.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Greater Sudbury 24.8 °C (July), Ufa 25.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Greater Sudbury -17.7 °C, Ufa -16.6 °C.
  • Greater Sudbury gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 908 mm against 684 mm.
  • Ufa gets about 40 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,944 h against 1,904 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Greater Sudbury 226 cm, Ufa 229 cm (ERA5-Land).
