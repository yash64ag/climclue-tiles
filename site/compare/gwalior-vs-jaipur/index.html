How the climate of Gwalior, India compares with Jaipur, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Gwalior is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 25.7 °C against 24.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Gwalior is 1.2 °C warmer (Gwalior 30.5 °C, Jaipur 29.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gwalior 41.9 °C (May), Jaipur 40.5 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Gwalior 7.4 °C, Jaipur 6.9 °C.
  • Gwalior gets 32% more rain and snow a year: 833 mm against 634 mm.
  • Jaipur gets about 94 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,831 h against 2,737 h; about 1961–1990).
