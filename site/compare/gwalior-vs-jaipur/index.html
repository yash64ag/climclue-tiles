Gwalior vs Jaipur climate

How the climate of Gwalior, India compares with Jaipur, India, 249 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gwalior is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 25.7 °C against 24.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Gwalior is 1.3 °C warmer (Gwalior 34.4 °C, Jaipur 33.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gwalior 40.7 °C (May), Jaipur 39.4 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Gwalior 9.0 °C, Jaipur 8.0 °C.
  • Gwalior gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 811 mm against 628 mm.
  • Jaipur gets about 94 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,831 h against 2,737 h; about 1961–1990).
