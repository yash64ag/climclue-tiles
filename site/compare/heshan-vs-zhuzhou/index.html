Heshan, Hunan vs Zhuzhou climate

How the climate of Heshan, Hunan, China compares with Zhuzhou, China, 114 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zhuzhou is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 18.6 °C against 17.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Zhuzhou is 0.9 °C warmer (Heshan, Hunan 24.3 °C, Zhuzhou 25.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Heshan, Hunan 33.2 °C (July), Zhuzhou 34.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Heshan, Hunan 2.5 °C, Zhuzhou 2.9 °C.
  • Zhuzhou gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,637 mm against 1,590 mm.
  • Zhuzhou gets about 17 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,605 h against 1,588 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Heshan, Hunan 14 cm, Zhuzhou 6.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
