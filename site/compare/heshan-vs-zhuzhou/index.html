Heshan vs Zhuzhou climate

How the climate of Heshan, China compares with Zhuzhou, China, 114 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhuzhou is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 17.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Zhuzhou is 1.0 °C warmer (Heshan 18.8 °C, Zhuzhou 19.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Heshan 32.3 °C (July), Zhuzhou 33.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Heshan 1.6 °C, Zhuzhou 2.3 °C.
  • Zhuzhou gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,662 mm against 1,570 mm.
  • Zhuzhou gets about 17 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,605 h against 1,588 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Heshan 25 cm, Zhuzhou 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
