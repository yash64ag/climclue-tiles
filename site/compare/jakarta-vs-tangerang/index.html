Jakarta vs Tangerang climate

How the climate of Jakarta, Indonesia compares with Tangerang, Indonesia, 24 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tangerang is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Tangerang is 0.3 °C warmer (Jakarta 26.1 °C, Tangerang 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jakarta 31.5 °C (September), Tangerang 31.3 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Jakarta 22.5 °C, Tangerang 23.1 °C.
  • Jakarta gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 2,701 mm against 2,341 mm.
  • Jakarta gets about 25 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,648 h against 2,623 h; about 1961–1990).
