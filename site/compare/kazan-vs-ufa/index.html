Kazan vs Ufa climate

How the climate of Kazan, Russia compares with Ufa, Russia, 449 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kazan is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 4.4 °C against 4.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Kazan is 1.4 °C warmer (Kazan -8.9 °C, Ufa -10.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kazan 23.9 °C (July), Ufa 24.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kazan -13.3 °C, Ufa -15.2 °C.
  • Ufa gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 651 mm against 544 mm.
  • Ufa gets about 59 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,944 h against 1,885 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kazan 175 cm, Ufa 229 cm (ERA5-Land).
