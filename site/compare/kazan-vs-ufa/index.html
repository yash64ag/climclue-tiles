How the climate of Kazan, Russia compares with Ufa, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kazan is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 5.0 °C against 3.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Kazan is 2.6 °C warmer (Kazan -8.4 °C, Ufa -11.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kazan 25.6 °C (July), Ufa 25.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kazan -13.5 °C, Ufa -17.1 °C.
  • Ufa gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 684 mm against 536 mm.
  • Ufa gets about 59 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,944 h against 1,885 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kazan 175 cm, Ufa 229 cm (ERA5-Land).
