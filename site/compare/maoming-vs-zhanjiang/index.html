Maoming vs Zhanjiang climate

How the climate of Maoming, China compares with Zhanjiang, China, 73 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Zhanjiang is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 23.7 °C against 23.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Zhanjiang is 0.4 °C warmer (Maoming 20.3 °C, Zhanjiang 20.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maoming 31.4 °C (July), Zhanjiang 31.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Maoming 12.6 °C, Zhanjiang 13.3 °C.
  • Maoming gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 2,081 mm against 1,988 mm.
  • Zhanjiang gets about 4 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,920 h against 1,916 h; about 1961–1990).
