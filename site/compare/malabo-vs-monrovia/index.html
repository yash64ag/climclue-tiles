Malabo vs Monrovia climate

How the climate of Malabo, Equatorial Guinea compares with Monrovia, Liberia, 2,187 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Malabo is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 25.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Malabo is 0.4 °C warmer (Malabo 26.5 °C, Monrovia 26.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Malabo 31.3 °C (February), Monrovia 32.1 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Malabo 21.8 °C, Monrovia 21.1 °C.
  • Monrovia gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 3,199 mm against 2,527 mm.
  • Monrovia gets about 263 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,767 h against 1,504 h; about 1961–1990).
