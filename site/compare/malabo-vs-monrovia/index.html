Malabo vs Monrovia climate

How the climate of Malabo, Equatorial Guinea compares with Monrovia, Liberia, 2,187 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Monrovia is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 25.8 °C against 25.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Monrovia is 0.5 °C warmer (Malabo 24.4 °C, Monrovia 24.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Malabo 27.9 °C (April), Monrovia 29.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Malabo 23.6 °C, Monrovia 23.3 °C.
  • Monrovia gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 3,226 mm against 2,885 mm.
  • Monrovia gets about 263 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,767 h against 1,504 h; about 1961–1990).
