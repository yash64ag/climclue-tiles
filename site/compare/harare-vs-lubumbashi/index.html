How the climate of Harare, Zimbabwe compares with Lubumbashi, Democratic Republic of the Congo: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lubumbashi is 2.7 °C (4.9 °F) warmer over the year: 21.2 °C against 18.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Lubumbashi is 4.5 °C warmer (Harare 16.0 °C, Lubumbashi 20.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Harare 28.6 °C (October), Lubumbashi 32.8 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Harare 5.6 °C, Lubumbashi 9.0 °C.
  • Lubumbashi gets 39% more rain and snow a year: 1,115 mm against 800 mm.
  • Harare gets about 212 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,944 h against 2,732 h; about 1961–1990).
