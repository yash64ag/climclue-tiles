How the climate of Wuxi, Jiangsu, China compares with Yixing, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yixing is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.7 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Yixing is 0.5 °C warmer (Wuxi, Jiangsu 15.6 °C, Yixing 16.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Wuxi, Jiangsu 32.2 °C (July), Yixing 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Wuxi, Jiangsu -0.1 °C, Yixing -0.1 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,210 mm and 1,213 mm).
  • Wuxi, Jiangsu gets about 17 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,999 h against 1,982 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Wuxi, Jiangsu 13 cm, Yixing 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
