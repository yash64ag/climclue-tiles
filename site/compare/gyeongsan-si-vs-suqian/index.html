How the climate of Gyeongsan-si, South Korea compares with Suqian, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Suqian is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 14.8 °C against 13.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Suqian is 1.9 °C warmer (Gyeongsan-si 22.8 °C, Suqian 24.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gyeongsan-si 30.7 °C (August), Suqian 31.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gyeongsan-si -5.1 °C, Suqian -3.7 °C.
  • Gyeongsan-si gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,120 mm against 963 mm.
  • Gyeongsan-si gets about 227 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,452 h against 2,225 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gyeongsan-si 12 cm, Suqian 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
