Shanghai vs Yangpu climate

How the climate of Shanghai, China compares with Yangpu, China, 7 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shanghai and Yangpu have the same yearly average temperature (16.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Yangpu is 0.3 °C warmer (Shanghai 7.2 °C, Yangpu 7.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Shanghai 31.1 °C (July), Yangpu 30.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Shanghai 0.9 °C, Yangpu 1.5 °C.
  • Shanghai gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,459 mm against 1,431 mm.
  • Yangpu gets about 3 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,973 h against 1,969 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Shanghai 7.7 cm, Yangpu 6.4 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 5 months each
  • Drier: Yangpu in 11 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Sunnier: Yangpu in 8 of 12 months (1 equal)
