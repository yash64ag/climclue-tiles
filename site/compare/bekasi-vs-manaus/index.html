Bekasi vs Manaus climate

How the climate of Bekasi, Indonesia compares with Manaus, Brazil, 18,238 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bekasi and Manaus have the same yearly average temperature (26.8 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Bekasi is 0.7 °C warmer (Bekasi 27.1 °C, Manaus 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bekasi 31.4 °C (September), Manaus 30.8 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Bekasi 22.5 °C, Manaus 23.4 °C.
  • Bekasi gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 2,606 mm against 2,429 mm.
  • Bekasi gets about 884 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,651 h against 1,767 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Manaus in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Bekasi in 12 of 12 months
