Baoding, Hebei vs Tianjin climate

How the climate of Baoding, Hebei, China compares with Tianjin, China, 151 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Baoding, Hebei is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 13.8 °C against 13.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Baoding, Hebei is 1.4 °C warmer (Baoding, Hebei 26.6 °C, Tianjin 25.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoding, Hebei 33.1 °C (June), Tianjin 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baoding, Hebei -8.4 °C, Tianjin -8.3 °C.
  • Tianjin gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 543 mm against 516 mm.
  • Tianjin gets about 21 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,630 h against 2,609 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoding, Hebei 10 cm, Tianjin 9.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
