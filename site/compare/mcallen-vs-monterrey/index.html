How the climate of McAllen, United States compares with Monterrey, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • McAllen is 2.0 °C (3.6 °F) warmer over the year: 24.0 °C against 22.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when McAllen is 2.8 °C warmer (McAllen 24.9 °C, Monterrey 22.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: McAllen 36.5 °C (August), Monterrey 35.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: McAllen 10.0 °C, Monterrey 7.7 °C.
  • Monterrey gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 627 mm against 571 mm.
  • McAllen gets about 502 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,389 h against 1,887 h; about 1961–1990).
