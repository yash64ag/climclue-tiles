How the climate of Lethbridge, Canada compares with Xining, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Xining is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 7.1 °C against 6.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Xining is 3.0 °C warmer (Lethbridge -0.1 °C, Xining 3.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lethbridge 26.4 °C (July), Xining 24.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lethbridge -12.1 °C, Xining -13.7 °C.
  • Xining gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 410 mm against 393 mm.
  • Xining gets about 452 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,734 h against 2,283 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lethbridge 86 cm, Xining 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
