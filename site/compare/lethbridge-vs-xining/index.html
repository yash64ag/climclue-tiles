How the climate of Lethbridge, Canada compares with Xining, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lethbridge is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 6.9 °C against 6.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Lethbridge is 2.1 °C warmer (Lethbridge 19.3 °C, Xining 17.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lethbridge 26.9 °C (July), Xining 23.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lethbridge -10.8 °C, Xining -13.6 °C.
  • Xining gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 410 mm against 393 mm.
  • Xining gets about 452 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,734 h against 2,283 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lethbridge 65 cm, Xining 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
