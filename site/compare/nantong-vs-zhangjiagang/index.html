Nantong vs Zhangjiagang climate

How the climate of Nantong, China compares with Zhangjiagang, China, 37 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhangjiagang is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 15.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Zhangjiagang is 0.5 °C warmer (Nantong 14.6 °C, Zhangjiagang 15.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nantong 31.1 °C (July), Zhangjiagang 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nantong -0.5 °C, Zhangjiagang -0.8 °C.
  • Nantong gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,220 mm against 1,183 mm.
  • Nantong gets about 26 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,051 h against 2,026 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nantong 12 cm, Zhangjiagang 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
