Nantong vs Zhangjiagang climate

How the climate of Nantong, China compares with Zhangjiagang, China, 37 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhangjiagang is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 15.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Zhangjiagang is 0.4 °C warmer (Nantong 14.6 °C, Zhangjiagang 15.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nantong 31.0 °C (July), Zhangjiagang 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nantong -0.4 °C, Zhangjiagang -0.8 °C.
  • Nantong gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,240 mm against 1,192 mm.
  • Nantong gets about 26 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,051 h against 2,026 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nantong 10 cm, Zhangjiagang 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
