Main differences

  • Yancheng is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 15.0 °C against 13.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Yancheng is 1.9 °C warmer (Gwangju, Gwangju 8.5 °C, Yancheng 10.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gwangju, Gwangju 30.4 °C (August), Yancheng 30.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gwangju, Gwangju -4.1 °C, Yancheng -2.2 °C.
  • Gwangju, Gwangju gets 47% more rain and snow a year: 1,550 mm against 1,052 mm.
  • Gwangju, Gwangju gets about 104 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,279 h against 2,176 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gwangju, Gwangju 38 cm, Yancheng 10 cm (ERA5-Land).
