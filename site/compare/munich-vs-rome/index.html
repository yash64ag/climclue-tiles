How the climate of Munich, Germany compares with Rome, Italy: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Rome is 6.9 °C (12.5 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 9.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Rome is 8.6 °C warmer (Munich 3.8 °C, Rome 12.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Munich 23.7 °C (July), Rome 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Munich -3.5 °C, Rome 2.7 °C.
  • Munich gets 31% more rain and snow a year: 1,006 mm against 770 mm.
  • Rome gets about 769 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,424 h against 1,655 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Munich 97 cm, Rome 0.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
