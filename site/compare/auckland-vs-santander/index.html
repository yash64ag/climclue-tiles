Auckland vs Santander climate

How the climate of Auckland, New Zealand compares with Santander, Spain, 19,269 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Auckland is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.5 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Auckland is 9.7 °C warmer (Auckland 19.9 °C, Santander 10.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Auckland 21.8 °C (February), Santander 22.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Auckland 9.6 °C, Santander 6.7 °C.
  • Santander gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 1,444 mm against 1,235 mm.
  • Auckland gets about 289 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,022 h against 1,733 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
