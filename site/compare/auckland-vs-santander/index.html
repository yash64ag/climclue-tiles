How the climate of Auckland, New Zealand compares with Santander, Spain: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Auckland is 1.0 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 15.7 °C against 14.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Auckland is 9.9 °C warmer (Auckland 20.1 °C, Santander 10.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Auckland 24.0 °C (February), Santander 23.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Auckland 8.2 °C, Santander 6.9 °C.
  • Santander gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 1,474 mm against 1,197 mm.
  • Auckland gets about 289 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,022 h against 1,733 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
