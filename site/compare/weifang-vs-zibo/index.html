Weifang vs Zibo climate

How the climate of Weifang, China compares with Zibo, China, 93 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zibo is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 14.0 °C against 13.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Zibo is 1.1 °C warmer (Weifang 24.4 °C, Zibo 25.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Weifang 31.0 °C (July), Zibo 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Weifang -6.8 °C, Zibo -6.8 °C.
  • Zibo gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 608 mm against 564 mm.
  • Zibo gets about 11 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,536 h against 2,525 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Weifang 12 cm, Zibo 12 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Sunnier: Zibo in 7 of 12 months (1 equal)
