How the climate of Kobe, Japan compares with Nagoya, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kobe is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 16.9 °C against 15.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Kobe is 2.0 °C warmer (Kobe 8.8 °C, Nagoya 6.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kobe 32.3 °C (August), Nagoya 32.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kobe 2.7 °C, Nagoya 0.4 °C.
  • Nagoya gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 1,689 mm against 1,368 mm.
  • Nagoya gets about 123 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,969 h against 1,846 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kobe 5.6 cm, Nagoya 7.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
