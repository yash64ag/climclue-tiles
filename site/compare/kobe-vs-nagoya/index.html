Kobe vs Nagoya climate

How the climate of Kobe, Japan compares with Nagoya, Japan, 166 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kobe is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 16.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Kobe is 1.8 °C warmer (Kobe 8.7 °C, Nagoya 6.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kobe 29.7 °C (August), Nagoya 31.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kobe 1.9 °C, Nagoya -1.2 °C.
  • Nagoya gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 1,644 mm against 1,378 mm.
  • Nagoya gets about 123 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,969 h against 1,846 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kobe 8.2 cm, Nagoya 9.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
