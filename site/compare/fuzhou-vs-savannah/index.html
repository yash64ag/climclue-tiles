How the climate of Fuzhou, Fujian, China compares with Savannah, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Fuzhou, Fujian is 1.3 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 20.8 °C against 19.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Fuzhou, Fujian is 3.0 °C warmer (Fuzhou, Fujian 18.6 °C, Savannah 15.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuzhou, Fujian 34.3 °C (July), Savannah 33.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuzhou, Fujian 8.1 °C, Savannah 4.5 °C.
  • Fuzhou, Fujian gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,289 mm against 1,220 mm.
  • Savannah gets about 966 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,728 h against 1,763 h; about 1961–1990).
