Fuzhou, Fujian vs Savannah climate

How the climate of Fuzhou, Fujian, China compares with Savannah, United States, 13,197 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Fuzhou, Fujian is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 20.1 °C against 19.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Fuzhou, Fujian is 2.3 °C warmer (Fuzhou, Fujian 29.1 °C, Savannah 26.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuzhou, Fujian 33.9 °C (July), Savannah 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuzhou, Fujian 6.8 °C, Savannah 4.4 °C.
  • Fuzhou, Fujian gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,289 mm against 1,220 mm.
  • Savannah gets about 966 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,728 h against 1,763 h; about 1961–1990).
