Fuzhou, Fujian vs Savannah climate

How the climate of Fuzhou, Fujian, China compares with Savannah, United States, 13,197 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Fuzhou, Fujian is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 19.7 °C against 19.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Fuzhou, Fujian is 1.6 °C warmer (Fuzhou, Fujian 17.4 °C, Savannah 15.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuzhou, Fujian 31.8 °C (July), Savannah 31.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuzhou, Fujian 6.2 °C, Savannah 7.3 °C.
  • Fuzhou, Fujian gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,350 mm against 1,225 mm.
  • Savannah gets about 966 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,728 h against 1,763 h; about 1961–1990).
