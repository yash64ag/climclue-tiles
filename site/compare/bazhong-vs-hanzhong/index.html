Bazhong vs Hanzhong climate

How the climate of Bazhong, China compares with Hanzhong, China, 137 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bazhong is 1.8 °C (3.2 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 15.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Bazhong is 2.3 °C warmer (Bazhong 7.6 °C, Hanzhong 5.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bazhong 31.3 °C (July), Hanzhong 30.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bazhong 3.0 °C, Hanzhong 0.1 °C.
  • Bazhong gets 32% more rain and snow a year: 1,298 mm against 981 mm.
  • Hanzhong gets about 213 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,669 h against 1,456 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bazhong 1.6 cm, Hanzhong 4.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
