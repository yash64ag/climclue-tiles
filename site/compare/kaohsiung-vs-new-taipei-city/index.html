How the climate of Kaohsiung, Taiwan compares with New Taipei City, Taiwan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kaohsiung is 2.0 °C (3.5 °F) warmer over the year: 24.6 °C against 22.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Kaohsiung is 4.4 °C warmer (Kaohsiung 22.4 °C, New Taipei City 17.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kaohsiung 32.3 °C (July), New Taipei City 33.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kaohsiung 14.0 °C, New Taipei City 12.6 °C.
  • New Taipei City gets 75% more rain and snow a year: 3,215 mm against 1,839 mm.
  • Kaohsiung gets about 284 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,008 h against 1,724 h; about 1961–1990).
