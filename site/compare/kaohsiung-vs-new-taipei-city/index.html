Kaohsiung vs New Taipei City climate

How the climate of Kaohsiung, Taiwan compares with New Taipei City, Taiwan, 296 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kaohsiung is 2.0 °C (3.6 °F) warmer over the year: 24.5 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Kaohsiung is 4.4 °C warmer (Kaohsiung 22.3 °C, New Taipei City 17.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kaohsiung 30.5 °C (July), New Taipei City 31.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kaohsiung 15.2 °C, New Taipei City 12.3 °C.
  • New Taipei City gets 68% more rain and snow a year: 3,293 mm against 1,962 mm.
  • Kaohsiung gets about 284 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,008 h against 1,724 h; about 1961–1990).
