How the climate of Gyeongju, South Korea compares with Hanzhong, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hanzhong is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.3 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Hanzhong is 2.8 °C warmer (Gyeongju 3.4 °C, Hanzhong 6.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gyeongju 30.5 °C (August), Hanzhong 30.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gyeongju -3.9 °C, Hanzhong -0.2 °C.
  • Gyeongju gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,098 mm against 981 mm.
  • Gyeongju gets about 681 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,350 h against 1,669 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gyeongju 12 cm, Hanzhong 4.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
