How the climate of Gyeongsan-si, South Korea compares with Xuzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Xuzhou is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.1 °C against 13.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Xuzhou is 2.6 °C warmer (Gyeongsan-si 22.8 °C, Xuzhou 25.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gyeongsan-si 30.7 °C (August), Xuzhou 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gyeongsan-si -5.1 °C, Xuzhou -3.3 °C.
  • Gyeongsan-si gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 1,120 mm against 886 mm.
  • Gyeongsan-si gets about 190 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,452 h against 2,262 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gyeongsan-si 12 cm, Xuzhou 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
