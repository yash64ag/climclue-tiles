How the climate of Caloocan, Philippines compares with Taguig, Philippines: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Caloocan is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 27.8 °C against 27.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Caloocan is 0.5 °C warmer (Caloocan 28.0 °C, Taguig 27.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Caloocan 34.6 °C (April), Taguig 34.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Caloocan 22.0 °C, Taguig 21.2 °C.
  • Caloocan gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 2,143 mm against 1,901 mm.
  • Caloocan gets about 42 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,404 h against 2,362 h; about 1961–1990).
