Datong vs Sioux Falls climate

How the climate of Datong, China compares with Sioux Falls, United States, 10,240 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Datong and Sioux Falls have the same yearly average temperature (8.0 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Sioux Falls is 1.8 °C warmer (Datong -9.9 °C, Sioux Falls -8.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Datong 28.4 °C (July), Sioux Falls 28.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Datong -16.8 °C, Sioux Falls -12.0 °C.
  • Sioux Falls gets 83% more rain and snow a year: 711 mm against 388 mm.
  • Datong gets about 62 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,809 h against 2,747 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Datong 15 cm, Sioux Falls 73 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each

  • Sunnier: Datong in 8 of 12 months
