Main differences

  • Sioux Falls is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 8.0 °C against 7.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Sioux Falls is 1.8 °C warmer (Datong, Shanxi -9.9 °C, Sioux Falls -8.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Datong, Shanxi 28.8 °C (July), Sioux Falls 29.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Datong, Shanxi -16.4 °C, Sioux Falls -13.7 °C.
  • Sioux Falls gets 86% more rain and snow a year: 708 mm against 380 mm.
  • Datong, Shanxi gets about 62 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,809 h against 2,747 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Datong, Shanxi 15 cm, Sioux Falls 86 cm (ERA5-Land).
