Datong vs Sioux Falls climate

How the climate of Datong, China compares with Sioux Falls, United States, 10,240 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Sioux Falls is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 8.0 °C against 7.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Sioux Falls is 2.0 °C warmer (Datong -10.1 °C, Sioux Falls -8.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Datong 28.3 °C (July), Sioux Falls 28.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Datong -17.0 °C, Sioux Falls -12.1 °C.
  • Sioux Falls gets 86% more rain and snow a year: 708 mm against 380 mm.
  • Datong gets about 62 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,809 h against 2,747 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Datong 15 cm, Sioux Falls 73 cm (ERA5-Land).
