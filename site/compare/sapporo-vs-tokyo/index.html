How the climate of Sapporo, Japan compares with Tokyo, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tokyo is 7.0 °C (12.6 °F) warmer over the year: 15.3 °C against 8.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Tokyo is 9.3 °C warmer (Sapporo -4.8 °C, Tokyo 4.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Sapporo 25.4 °C (August), Tokyo 30.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Sapporo -8.5 °C, Tokyo -0.2 °C.
  • Sapporo gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,573 mm against 1,516 mm.
  • Tokyo gets about 160 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,837 h against 1,677 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Sapporo 417 cm, Tokyo 19 cm (ERA5-Land).
