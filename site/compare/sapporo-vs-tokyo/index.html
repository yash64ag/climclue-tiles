Sapporo vs Tokyo climate

How the climate of Sapporo, Japan compares with Tokyo, Japan, 833 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tokyo is 7.3 °C (13.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.4 °C against 8.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Tokyo is 9.8 °C warmer (Sapporo -5.2 °C, Tokyo 4.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Sapporo 24.3 °C (August), Tokyo 29.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Sapporo -9.9 °C, Tokyo 0.0 °C.
  • Sapporo gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,620 mm against 1,486 mm.
  • Tokyo gets about 160 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,837 h against 1,677 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Sapporo 417 cm, Tokyo 19 cm (ERA5-Land).
