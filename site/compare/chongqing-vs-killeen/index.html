How the climate of Chongqing, China compares with Killeen, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Chongqing is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 19.1 °C against 18.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Chongqing is 0.8 °C warmer (Chongqing 14.8 °C, Killeen 13.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chongqing 33.3 °C (August), Killeen 35.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Chongqing 6.5 °C, Killeen 1.5 °C.
  • Chongqing gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,104 mm against 914 mm.
  • Killeen gets about 1,513 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,712 h against 1,198 h; about 1961–1990).
