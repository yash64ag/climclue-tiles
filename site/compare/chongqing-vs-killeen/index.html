Chongqing vs Killeen climate

How the climate of Chongqing, China compares with Killeen, United States, 12,796 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Killeen is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 19.5 °C against 19.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Killeen is 1.2 °C warmer (Chongqing 25.9 °C, Killeen 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chongqing 33.4 °C (August), Killeen 35.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Chongqing 6.6 °C, Killeen 2.6 °C.
  • Chongqing gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,104 mm against 914 mm.
  • Killeen gets about 1,513 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,712 h against 1,198 h; about 1961–1990).
