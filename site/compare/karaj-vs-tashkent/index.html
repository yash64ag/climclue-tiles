Karaj vs Tashkent climate

How the climate of Karaj, Iran compares with Tashkent, Uzbekistan, 1,692 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Karaj is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 14.4 °C against 13.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Karaj is 2.9 °C warmer (Karaj 23.1 °C, Tashkent 20.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Karaj 35.3 °C (July), Tashkent 35.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Karaj -4.8 °C, Tashkent -4.5 °C.
  • Karaj gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 478 mm against 450 mm.
  • Tashkent gets about 16 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,790 h against 2,774 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Karaj 78 cm, Tashkent 58 cm (ERA5-Land).
