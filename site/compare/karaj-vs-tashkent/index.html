Karaj vs Tashkent climate

How the climate of Karaj, Iran compares with Tashkent, Uzbekistan, 1,692 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Karaj is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 14.2 °C against 13.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Karaj is 2.6 °C warmer (Karaj 22.9 °C, Tashkent 20.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Karaj 33.8 °C (July), Tashkent 33.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Karaj -5.6 °C, Tashkent -5.5 °C.
  • Karaj gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 484 mm against 457 mm.
  • Tashkent gets about 16 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,790 h against 2,774 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Karaj 98 cm, Tashkent 80 cm (ERA5-Land).
