How the climate of Dazhou, China compares with Wanzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Wanzhou is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 19.4 °C against 18.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Wanzhou is 1.4 °C warmer (Dazhou 8.6 °C, Wanzhou 10.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dazhou 33.1 °C (August), Wanzhou 34.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Dazhou 4.4 °C, Wanzhou 5.9 °C.
  • Dazhou gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,286 mm against 1,225 mm.
  • Wanzhou gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,401 h against 1,394 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Dazhou 1.3 cm, Wanzhou 0.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
