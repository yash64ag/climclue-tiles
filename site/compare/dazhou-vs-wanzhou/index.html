Dazhou vs Wanzhou climate

How the climate of Dazhou, China compares with Wanzhou, China, 102 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Wanzhou is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 18.2 °C against 17.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Wanzhou is 1.1 °C warmer (Dazhou 7.8 °C, Wanzhou 8.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dazhou 30.4 °C (August), Wanzhou 31.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Dazhou 2.0 °C, Wanzhou 3.4 °C.
  • Dazhou gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,501 mm against 1,391 mm.
  • Wanzhou gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,401 h against 1,394 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Dazhou 6.5 cm, Wanzhou 3.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
