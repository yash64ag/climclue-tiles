How the climate of Antalya, Turkey compares with Latakia, Syria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Antalya is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 19.4 °C against 19.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Antalya is 4.0 °C warmer (Antalya 30.0 °C, Latakia 26.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Antalya 36.3 °C (July), Latakia 29.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Antalya 5.7 °C, Latakia 8.2 °C.
  • Antalya gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,032 mm against 943 mm.
  • Antalya gets about 255 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,103 h against 2,848 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Latakia in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Antalya in 9 of 12 months
