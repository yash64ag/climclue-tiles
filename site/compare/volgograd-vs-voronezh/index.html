How the climate of Volgograd, Russia compares with Voronezh, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Volgograd is 2.0 °C (3.6 °F) warmer over the year: 9.1 °C against 7.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Volgograd is 3.8 °C warmer (Volgograd 23.2 °C, Voronezh 19.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Volgograd 30.1 °C (July), Voronezh 26.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Volgograd -9.1 °C, Voronezh -9.6 °C.
  • Voronezh gets 64% more rain and snow a year: 637 mm against 387 mm.
  • Volgograd gets about 225 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,116 h against 1,891 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Volgograd 99 cm, Voronezh 145 cm (ERA5-Land).
