How the climate of Kunshan, China compares with Qingpu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Qingpu is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Qingpu is 0.3 °C warmer (Kunshan 4.1 °C, Qingpu 4.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kunshan 31.9 °C (July), Qingpu 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kunshan 0.3 °C, Qingpu 0.7 °C.
  • Qingpu gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,403 mm against 1,325 mm.
  • Kunshan gets about 20 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,978 h against 1,958 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kunshan 11 cm, Qingpu 10 cm (ERA5-Land).
