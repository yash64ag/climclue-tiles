Nizhniy Novgorod vs Samara climate

How the climate of Nizhniy Novgorod, Russia compares with Samara, Russia, 524 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Samara is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 5.5 °C against 4.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Samara is 2.3 °C warmer (Nizhniy Novgorod 16.9 °C, Samara 19.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nizhniy Novgorod 23.5 °C (July), Samara 25.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nizhniy Novgorod -11.8 °C, Samara -13.1 °C.
  • Nizhniy Novgorod gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 678 mm against 511 mm.
  • Samara gets about 252 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,055 h against 1,802 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nizhniy Novgorod 205 cm, Samara 182 cm (ERA5-Land).
