How the climate of Nizhniy Novgorod, Russia compares with Samara, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Samara is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 6.0 °C against 5.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Samara is 2.4 °C warmer (Nizhniy Novgorod 17.6 °C, Samara 20.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nizhniy Novgorod 24.6 °C (July), Samara 26.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nizhniy Novgorod -11.8 °C, Samara -13.0 °C.
  • Nizhniy Novgorod gets 37% more rain and snow a year: 697 mm against 510 mm.
  • Samara gets about 252 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,055 h against 1,802 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nizhniy Novgorod 207 cm, Samara 167 cm (ERA5-Land).
