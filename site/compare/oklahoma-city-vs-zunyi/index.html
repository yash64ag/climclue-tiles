Oklahoma City vs Zunyi climate

Main differences

  • Oklahoma City is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 15.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Oklahoma City is 2.8 °C warmer (Oklahoma City 28.1 °C, Zunyi 25.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Oklahoma City 34.4 °C (July), Zunyi 29.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Oklahoma City -2.8 °C, Zunyi 2.0 °C.
  • Zunyi gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 1,082 mm against 906 mm.
  • Oklahoma City gets about 1,749 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,031 h against 1,282 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Oklahoma City 17 cm, Zunyi 7.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
