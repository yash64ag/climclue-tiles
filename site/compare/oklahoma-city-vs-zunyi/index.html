How the climate of Oklahoma City, United States compares with Zunyi, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Oklahoma City and Zunyi have the same yearly average temperature (16.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Zunyi is 2.7 °C warmer (Oklahoma City 4.4 °C, Zunyi 7.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Oklahoma City 34.3 °C (July), Zunyi 29.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Oklahoma City -2.3 °C, Zunyi 2.6 °C.
  • Zunyi gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 1,082 mm against 906 mm.
  • Oklahoma City gets about 1,749 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,031 h against 1,282 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Oklahoma City 13 cm, Zunyi 7.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
