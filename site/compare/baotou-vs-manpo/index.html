How the climate of Baotou, China compares with Manp’o, North Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Manp’o is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 8.0 °C against 7.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Manp’o is 2.4 °C warmer (Baotou 21.4 °C, Manp’o 23.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baotou 30.1 °C (July), Manp’o 28.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baotou -17.4 °C, Manp’o -19.2 °C.
  • Manp’o gets 152% more rain and snow a year: 808 mm against 321 mm.
  • Baotou gets about 534 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,964 h against 2,430 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baotou 15 cm, Manp’o 61 cm (ERA5-Land).
