Baotou vs Manp’o climate

How the climate of Baotou, China compares with Manp’o, North Korea, 1,381 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Baotou is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 7.6 °C against 7.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Baotou is 1.7 °C warmer (Baotou -11.2 °C, Manp’o -12.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baotou 29.6 °C (July), Manp’o 26.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baotou -17.3 °C, Manp’o -21.9 °C.
  • Manp’o gets 160% more rain and snow a year: 849 mm against 327 mm.
  • Baotou gets about 534 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,964 h against 2,430 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baotou 15 cm, Manp’o 61 cm (ERA5-Land).
