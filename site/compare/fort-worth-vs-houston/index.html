How the climate of Fort Worth, United States compares with Houston, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Houston is 2.4 °C (4.3 °F) warmer over the year: 21.3 °C against 18.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Houston is 4.6 °C warmer (Fort Worth 8.4 °C, Houston 13.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fort Worth 35.7 °C (August), Houston 33.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Fort Worth 1.8 °C, Houston 7.2 °C.
  • Houston gets 53% more rain and snow a year: 1,372 mm against 895 mm.
  • Fort Worth gets about 262 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,838 h against 2,576 h; about 1961–1990).
