How the climate of Fairfield, California, United States compares with Santiago, Chile: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Fairfield, California is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Fairfield, California is 14.4 °C warmer (Fairfield, California 23.0 °C, Santiago 8.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fairfield, California 32.2 °C (July), Santiago 28.3 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Fairfield, California 3.6 °C, Santiago 3.7 °C.
  • Santiago gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 618 mm against 548 mm.
  • Fairfield, California gets about 731 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,269 h against 2,538 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
