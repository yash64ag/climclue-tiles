Fairfield vs Santiago climate

How the climate of Fairfield, United States compares with Santiago, Chile, 9,577 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fairfield is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.8 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Santiago is 13.2 °C warmer (Fairfield 9.3 °C, Santiago 22.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fairfield 31.1 °C (July), Santiago 29.2 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Fairfield 4.9 °C, Santiago 3.7 °C.
  • Santiago gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 603 mm against 563 mm.
  • Fairfield gets about 731 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,269 h against 2,538 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Sunnier: Fairfield in 8 of 12 months
