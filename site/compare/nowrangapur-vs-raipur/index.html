Nowrangapur vs Raipur climate

How the climate of Nowrangapur, India compares with Raipur, India, 242 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Raipur is 2.2 °C (4.0 °F) warmer over the year: 26.7 °C against 24.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Raipur is 5.0 °C warmer (Nowrangapur 29.9 °C, Raipur 35.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nowrangapur 36.1 °C (May), Raipur 41.1 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Nowrangapur 12.6 °C, Raipur 13.3 °C.
  • Nowrangapur gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,545 mm against 1,280 mm.
  • Raipur gets about 89 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,623 h against 2,533 h; about 1961–1990).
