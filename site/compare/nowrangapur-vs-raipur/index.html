Nowrangapur vs Raipur, Chhattisgarh climate

Main differences

  • Raipur, Chhattisgarh is 2.1 °C (3.9 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 24.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Raipur, Chhattisgarh is 5.0 °C warmer (Nowrangapur 29.9 °C, Raipur, Chhattisgarh 34.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nowrangapur 36.3 °C (May), Raipur, Chhattisgarh 41.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Nowrangapur 11.5 °C, Raipur, Chhattisgarh 12.6 °C.
  • Nowrangapur gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 1,528 mm against 1,277 mm.
  • Raipur, Chhattisgarh gets about 89 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,623 h against 2,533 h; about 1961–1990).
