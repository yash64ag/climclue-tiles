Fushun vs Manp’o climate

How the climate of Fushun, China compares with Manp’o, North Korea, 212 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fushun is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 8.5 °C against 7.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Fushun is 1.8 °C warmer (Fushun -8.2 °C, Manp’o -9.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fushun 28.1 °C (July), Manp’o 26.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fushun -18.6 °C, Manp’o -21.9 °C.
  • Manp’o gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 849 mm against 765 mm.
  • Fushun gets about 104 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,534 h against 2,430 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fushun 72 cm, Manp’o 135 cm (ERA5-Land).
