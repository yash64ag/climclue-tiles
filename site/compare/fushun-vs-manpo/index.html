How the climate of Fushun, China compares with Manp’o, North Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Fushun is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 8.5 °C against 8.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Fushun is 1.3 °C warmer (Fushun -8.2 °C, Manp’o -9.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fushun 29.1 °C (July), Manp’o 28.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fushun -18.2 °C, Manp’o -19.2 °C.
  • Manp’o gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 808 mm against 772 mm.
  • Fushun gets about 104 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,534 h against 2,430 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fushun 42 cm, Manp’o 61 cm (ERA5-Land).
