Chaozhou vs Ma On Shan climate

How the climate of Chaozhou, China compares with Ma On Shan, Hong Kong, 280 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ma On Shan is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 22.4 °C against 22.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Ma On Shan is 0.9 °C warmer (Chaozhou 17.8 °C, Ma On Shan 18.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chaozhou 31.0 °C (July), Ma On Shan 29.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chaozhou 9.4 °C, Ma On Shan 12.0 °C.
  • Ma On Shan gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 2,244 mm against 1,809 mm.
  • Chaozhou gets about 22 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,986 h against 1,964 h; about 1961–1990).
