How the climate of Chaozhou, China compares with Ma On Shan, Hong Kong: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ma On Shan is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 22.8 °C against 22.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Ma On Shan is 1.6 °C warmer (Chaozhou 14.3 °C, Ma On Shan 15.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chaozhou 32.2 °C (July), Ma On Shan 30.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chaozhou 10.5 °C, Ma On Shan 13.1 °C.
  • Ma On Shan gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 2,395 mm against 1,803 mm.
  • Chaozhou gets about 22 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,986 h against 1,964 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Chaozhou in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Chaozhou in 7 of 12 months
