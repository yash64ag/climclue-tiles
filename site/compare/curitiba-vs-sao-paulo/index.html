Curitiba vs São Paulo climate

How the climate of Curitiba, Brazil compares with São Paulo, Brazil, 339 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • São Paulo is 2.6 °C (4.7 °F) warmer over the year: 20.0 °C against 17.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when São Paulo is 3.2 °C warmer (Curitiba 15.3 °C, São Paulo 18.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Curitiba 24.9 °C (February), São Paulo 26.9 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Curitiba 9.8 °C, São Paulo 12.3 °C.
  • Curitiba gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,535 mm against 1,459 mm.
  • São Paulo gets about 127 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,780 h against 1,653 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: São Paulo in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: São Paulo in 11 of 12 months
