How the climate of Karbala, Iraq compares with Sadr City, Iraq: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Sadr City is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 25.3 °C against 24.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Sadr City is 1.2 °C warmer (Karbala 37.0 °C, Sadr City 38.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Karbala 45.4 °C (July), Sadr City 47.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Karbala 5.5 °C, Sadr City 5.2 °C.
  • Sadr City gets 54% more rain and snow a year: 167 mm against 108 mm.
  • Karbala gets about 101 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,581 h against 3,480 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Sadr City in 10 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: Karbala in 10 of 12 months (2 equal)
  • Sunnier: Karbala in 12 of 12 months
