Bekasi vs Manacapuru climate

How the climate of Bekasi, Indonesia compares with Manacapuru, Brazil, 18,278 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bekasi and Manacapuru have the same yearly average temperature (26.8 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Bekasi is 0.6 °C warmer (Bekasi 27.1 °C, Manacapuru 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bekasi 31.5 °C (September), Manacapuru 30.2 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Bekasi 22.5 °C, Manacapuru 23.9 °C.
  • Bekasi gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 2,659 mm against 2,612 mm.
  • Bekasi gets about 872 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,651 h against 1,779 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Manacapuru in 6 of 12 months (2 equal)
  • Drier: Manacapuru in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Bekasi in 12 of 12 months
