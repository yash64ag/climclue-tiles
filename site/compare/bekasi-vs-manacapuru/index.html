How the climate of Bekasi, Indonesia compares with Manacapuru, Brazil: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bekasi and Manacapuru have the same yearly average temperature (26.8 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Bekasi is 0.6 °C warmer (Bekasi 27.1 °C, Manacapuru 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bekasi 32.3 °C (September), Manacapuru 32.4 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Bekasi 21.7 °C, Manacapuru 22.3 °C.
  • Bekasi gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 2,606 mm against 2,489 mm.
  • Bekasi gets about 872 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,651 h against 1,779 h; about 1961–1990).
