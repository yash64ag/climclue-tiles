How the climate of Dalian, China compares with Panjin, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Dalian is 1.9 °C (3.4 °F) warmer over the year: 11.7 °C against 9.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Dalian is 6.1 °C warmer (Dalian -2.8 °C, Panjin -8.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dalian 27.6 °C (August), Panjin 29.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Dalian -6.1 °C, Panjin -14.3 °C.
  • Panjin gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 669 mm against 597 mm.
  • Dalian gets about 34 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,662 h against 2,628 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Dalian 17 cm, Panjin 24 cm (ERA5-Land).
