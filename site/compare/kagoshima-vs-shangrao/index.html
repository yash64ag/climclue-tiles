How the climate of Kagoshima, Japan compares with Shangrao, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Shangrao is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 19.0 °C against 18.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Shangrao is 2.8 °C warmer (Kagoshima 20.6 °C, Shangrao 23.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kagoshima 32.1 °C (August), Shangrao 34.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kagoshima 4.2 °C, Shangrao 2.4 °C.
  • Kagoshima gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 2,100 mm against 2,027 mm.
  • Shangrao gets about 19 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,878 h against 1,859 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kagoshima 0.1 cm, Shangrao 6.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
