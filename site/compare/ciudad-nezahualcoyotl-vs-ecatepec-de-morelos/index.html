How the climate of Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl, Mexico compares with Ecatepec de Morelos, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 16.3 °C against 15.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl is 0.8 °C warmer (Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl 16.5 °C, Ecatepec de Morelos 15.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl 27.7 °C (May), Ecatepec de Morelos 27.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl 3.5 °C, Ecatepec de Morelos 2.2 °C.
  • Ciudad Nezahualcoyotl gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 710 mm against 654 mm.
  • Ecatepec de Morelos gets about 131 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,148 h against 2,017 h; about 1961–1990).
