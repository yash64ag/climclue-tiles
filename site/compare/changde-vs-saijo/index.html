How the climate of Changde, China compares with Saijō, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Changde is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 17.9 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Changde is 3.0 °C warmer (Changde 22.9 °C, Saijō 19.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changde 33.1 °C (July), Saijō 32.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Changde 2.3 °C, Saijō 2.6 °C.
  • Changde gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,530 mm against 1,496 mm.
  • Saijō gets about 395 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,967 h against 1,572 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changde 14 cm, Saijō 2.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
