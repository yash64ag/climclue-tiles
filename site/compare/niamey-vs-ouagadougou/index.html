Niamey vs Ouagadougou climate

How the climate of Niamey, Niger compares with Ouagadougou, Burkina Faso, 415 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Niamey is 1.1 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 29.3 °C against 28.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Niamey is 2.8 °C warmer (Niamey 32.4 °C, Ouagadougou 29.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Niamey 40.5 °C (April), Ouagadougou 39.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Niamey 15.9 °C, Ouagadougou 17.2 °C.
  • Ouagadougou gets 58% more rain and snow a year: 728 mm against 462 mm.
  • Ouagadougou gets about 44 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,948 h against 2,904 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Sunnier: Ouagadougou in 7 of 12 months
