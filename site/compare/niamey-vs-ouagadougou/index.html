How the climate of Niamey, Niger compares with Ouagadougou, Burkina Faso: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Niamey is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 29.4 °C against 28.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Niamey is 2.9 °C warmer (Niamey 32.4 °C, Ouagadougou 29.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Niamey 40.9 °C (April), Ouagadougou 39.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Niamey 16.1 °C, Ouagadougou 16.5 °C.
  • Ouagadougou gets 61% more rain and snow a year: 722 mm against 447 mm.
  • Ouagadougou gets about 44 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,948 h against 2,904 h; about 1961–1990).
