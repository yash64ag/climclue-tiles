Jabalpur vs Nepalgunj climate

How the climate of Jabalpur, India compares with Nepalgunj, Nepal, 568 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jabalpur is 1.1 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 25.3 °C against 24.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Jabalpur is 3.2 °C warmer (Jabalpur 17.4 °C, Nepalgunj 14.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jabalpur 41.5 °C (May), Nepalgunj 38.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Jabalpur 9.5 °C, Nepalgunj 7.2 °C.
  • Nepalgunj gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 1,606 mm against 1,370 mm.
  • Jabalpur gets about 221 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,716 h against 2,496 h; about 1961–1990).
