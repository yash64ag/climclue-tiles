Lucknow vs Varanasi climate

How the climate of Lucknow, India compares with Varanasi, India, 268 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Varanasi is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 25.8 °C against 25.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Varanasi is 1.1 °C warmer (Lucknow 15.9 °C, Varanasi 17.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lucknow 40.2 °C (May), Varanasi 41.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Lucknow 7.5 °C, Varanasi 8.2 °C.
  • Varanasi gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 1,052 mm against 900 mm.
  • Lucknow gets about 129 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,611 h against 2,482 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Varanasi in 11 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Lucknow in 12 of 12 months
