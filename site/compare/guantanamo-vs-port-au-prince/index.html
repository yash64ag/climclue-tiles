How the climate of Guantánamo, Cuba compares with Port-au-Prince, Haiti: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Port-au-Prince is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Port-au-Prince is 1.2 °C warmer (Guantánamo 25.2 °C, Port-au-Prince 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guantánamo 34.5 °C (July), Port-au-Prince 34.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Guantánamo 18.7 °C, Port-au-Prince 20.0 °C.
  • Port-au-Prince gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,242 mm against 1,139 mm.
  • Port-au-Prince gets about 414 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,795 h against 2,382 h; about 1961–1990).

  • Sunnier: Port-au-Prince in 12 of 12 months
