How the climate of Porto Alegre, Brazil compares with Salto, Uruguay: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Porto Alegre is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 19.7 °C against 18.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Porto Alegre is 2.0 °C warmer (Porto Alegre 14.1 °C, Salto 12.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Porto Alegre 29.6 °C (January), Salto 32.5 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Porto Alegre 9.7 °C, Salto 7.0 °C.
  • Porto Alegre gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,419 mm against 1,291 mm.
  • Salto gets about 356 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,574 h against 2,217 h; about 1961–1990).
