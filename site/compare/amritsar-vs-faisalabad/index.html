Amritsar vs Faisalabad climate

How the climate of Amritsar, India compares with Faisalabad, Pakistan, 171 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Faisalabad is 1.6 °C (2.9 °F) warmer over the year: 25.0 °C against 23.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Faisalabad is 2.5 °C warmer (Amritsar 28.1 °C, Faisalabad 30.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amritsar 38.6 °C (June), Faisalabad 40.0 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Amritsar 5.9 °C, Faisalabad 6.4 °C.
  • Amritsar gets 80% more rain and snow a year: 758 mm against 421 mm.
  • Faisalabad gets about 149 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,978 h against 2,830 h; about 1961–1990).
