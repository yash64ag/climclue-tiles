Ahmedabad vs Vadodara climate

How the climate of Ahmedabad, India compares with Vadodara, India, 103 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Vadodara is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 27.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Vadodara is 1.2 °C warmer (Ahmedabad 19.8 °C, Vadodara 21.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ahmedabad 41.1 °C (May), Vadodara 39.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Ahmedabad 11.6 °C, Vadodara 12.7 °C.
  • Vadodara gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 1,053 mm against 876 mm.
  • Ahmedabad gets about 85 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,001 h against 2,916 h; about 1961–1990).
