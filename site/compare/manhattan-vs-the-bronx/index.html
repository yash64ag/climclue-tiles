How the climate of Manhattan, New York, United States compares with The Bronx, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Manhattan, New York and The Bronx have the same yearly average temperature (12.7 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Manhattan, New York is 0.1 °C warmer (Manhattan, New York 5.6 °C, The Bronx 5.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manhattan, New York 29.4 °C (July), The Bronx 29.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Manhattan, New York -3.4 °C, The Bronx -3.5 °C.
  • Manhattan, New York gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,232 mm against 1,216 mm.
  • Manhattan, New York gets about 10 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,529 h against 2,519 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Manhattan, New York 59 cm, The Bronx 61 cm (ERA5-Land).
