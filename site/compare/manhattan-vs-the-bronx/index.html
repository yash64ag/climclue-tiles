Manhattan vs The Bronx climate

How the climate of Manhattan, United States compares with The Bronx, United States, 11 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Manhattan is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 12.2 °C against 12.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Manhattan is 0.2 °C warmer (Manhattan 10.7 °C, The Bronx 10.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manhattan 28.2 °C (July), The Bronx 28.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Manhattan -3.9 °C, The Bronx -4.2 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,212 mm and 1,213 mm).
  • Manhattan gets about 10 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,529 h against 2,519 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Manhattan 86 cm, The Bronx 90 cm (ERA5-Land).
