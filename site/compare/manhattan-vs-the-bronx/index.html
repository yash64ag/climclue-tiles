Main differences

  • Manhattan, New York is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 12.1 °C against 12.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Manhattan, New York is 0.2 °C warmer (Manhattan, New York 10.7 °C, The Bronx 10.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manhattan, New York 29.0 °C (July), The Bronx 29.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Manhattan, New York -4.0 °C, The Bronx -4.9 °C.
  • Manhattan, New York gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,232 mm against 1,216 mm.
  • Manhattan, New York gets about 10 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,529 h against 2,519 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Manhattan, New York 81 cm, The Bronx 83 cm (ERA5-Land).
