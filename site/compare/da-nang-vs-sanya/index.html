Da Nang vs Sanya climate

How the climate of Da Nang, Vietnam compares with Sanya, China, 279 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Da Nang is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 24.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Da Nang is 1.7 °C warmer (Da Nang 29.6 °C, Sanya 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Da Nang 32.0 °C (June), Sanya 29.5 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Da Nang 20.2 °C, Sanya 18.2 °C.
  • Da Nang gets 48% more rain and snow a year: 2,448 mm against 1,651 mm.
  • Da Nang gets about 94 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,232 h against 2,138 h; about 1961–1990).
