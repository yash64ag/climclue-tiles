Da Nang vs Sanya climate

How the climate of Da Nang, Vietnam compares with Sanya, China, 279 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Da Nang is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 25.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Da Nang is 1.3 °C warmer (Da Nang 29.4 °C, Sanya 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Da Nang 33.4 °C (June), Sanya 31.3 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Da Nang 18.8 °C, Sanya 16.8 °C.
  • Da Nang gets 47% more rain and snow a year: 2,367 mm against 1,615 mm.
  • Da Nang gets about 94 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,232 h against 2,138 h; about 1961–1990).
