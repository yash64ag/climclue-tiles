How the climate of Arica, Chile compares with Trujillo, Peru: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Trujillo is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 19.7 °C against 19.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Trujillo is 1.9 °C warmer (Arica 15.8 °C, Trujillo 17.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Arica 28.4 °C (February), Trujillo 27.6 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Arica 11.2 °C, Trujillo 13.9 °C.
  • Trujillo gets 171% more rain and snow a year: 76 mm against 28 mm.
  • Arica gets about 780 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,713 h against 1,933 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Trujillo in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Arica in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Arica in 12 of 12 months
