How the climate of Kagoshima, Japan compares with Wenzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Wenzhou is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 18.8 °C against 18.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Wenzhou is 1.2 °C warmer (Kagoshima 24.0 °C, Wenzhou 25.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kagoshima 32.1 °C (August), Wenzhou 31.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kagoshima 4.2 °C, Wenzhou 5.3 °C.
  • Kagoshima gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 2,100 mm against 1,634 mm.
  • Kagoshima gets about 65 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,859 h against 1,794 h; about 1961–1990).
