Kagoshima vs Wenzhou climate

How the climate of Kagoshima, Japan compares with Wenzhou, China, 1,032 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kagoshima and Wenzhou have the same yearly average temperature (18.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Wenzhou is 1.0 °C warmer (Kagoshima 24.0 °C, Wenzhou 25.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kagoshima 32.1 °C (August), Wenzhou 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kagoshima 4.2 °C, Wenzhou 4.8 °C.
  • Kagoshima gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 2,100 mm against 1,634 mm.
  • Kagoshima gets about 65 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,859 h against 1,794 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Wenzhou in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Kagoshima in 7 of 12 months
