Jinzhong vs Taiyuan climate

How the climate of Jinzhong, China compares with Taiyuan, China, 27 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jinzhong is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 11.4 °C against 11.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Jinzhong is 0.3 °C warmer (Jinzhong -2.3 °C, Taiyuan -2.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinzhong 30.0 °C (July), Taiyuan 30.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinzhong -9.7 °C, Taiyuan -10.2 °C.
  • Jinzhong gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 463 mm against 422 mm.
  • Taiyuan gets about 24 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,563 h against 2,539 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinzhong 16 cm, Taiyuan 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
