León de los Aldama vs Monterrey climate

How the climate of León de los Aldama, Mexico compares with Monterrey, Mexico, 526 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Monterrey is 2.0 °C (3.6 °F) warmer over the year: 21.7 °C against 19.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Monterrey is 6.7 °C warmer (León de los Aldama 20.9 °C, Monterrey 27.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: León de los Aldama 30.2 °C (May), Monterrey 32.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: León de los Aldama 8.3 °C, Monterrey 9.4 °C.
  • León de los Aldama gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 631 mm against 627 mm.
  • León de los Aldama gets about 395 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,282 h against 1,887 h; about 1961–1990).
