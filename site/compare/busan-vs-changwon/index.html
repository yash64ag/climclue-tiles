Busan vs Changwon climate

How the climate of Busan, South Korea compares with Changwon, South Korea, 35 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Busan is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 14.9 °C against 14.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Busan is 2.5 °C warmer (Busan 6.5 °C, Changwon 4.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Busan 29.0 °C (August), Changwon 30.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Busan -0.1 °C, Changwon -3.2 °C.
  • Busan gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,638 mm against 1,452 mm.
  • Changwon gets about 131 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,419 h against 2,289 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Busan 6.1 cm, Changwon 8.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
