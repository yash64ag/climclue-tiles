How the climate of Iizuka, Japan compares with Nanchang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Nanchang is 1.3 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 18.4 °C against 17.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Nanchang is 3.2 °C warmer (Iizuka 20.1 °C, Nanchang 23.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iizuka 32.6 °C (August), Nanchang 33.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Iizuka 2.1 °C, Nanchang 2.7 °C.
  • Nanchang gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,796 mm against 1,669 mm.
  • Nanchang gets about 80 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,839 h against 1,759 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Iizuka 14 cm, Nanchang 6.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
