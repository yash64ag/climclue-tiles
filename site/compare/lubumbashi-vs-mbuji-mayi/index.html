Lubumbashi vs Mbuji-Mayi climate

How the climate of Lubumbashi, Democratic Republic of the Congo compares with Mbuji-Mayi, Democratic Republic of the Congo, 748 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mbuji-Mayi is 3.1 °C (5.6 °F) warmer over the year: 24.3 °C against 21.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Mbuji-Mayi is 6.6 °C warmer (Lubumbashi 18.1 °C, Mbuji-Mayi 24.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lubumbashi 30.9 °C (October), Mbuji-Mayi 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lubumbashi 11.8 °C, Mbuji-Mayi 18.1 °C.
  • Mbuji-Mayi gets 31% more rain and snow a year: 1,482 mm against 1,135 mm.
  • Lubumbashi gets about 477 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,732 h against 2,255 h; about 1961–1990).
