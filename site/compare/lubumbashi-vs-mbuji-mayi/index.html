How the climate of Lubumbashi, Democratic Republic of the Congo compares with Mbuji-Mayi, Democratic Republic of the Congo: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Mbuji-Mayi is 3.3 °C (5.9 °F) warmer over the year: 24.4 °C against 21.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Mbuji-Mayi is 6.8 °C warmer (Lubumbashi 17.9 °C, Mbuji-Mayi 24.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lubumbashi 32.8 °C (October), Mbuji-Mayi 32.1 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Lubumbashi 9.0 °C, Mbuji-Mayi 17.1 °C.
  • Mbuji-Mayi gets 32% more rain and snow a year: 1,473 mm against 1,115 mm.
  • Lubumbashi gets about 477 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,732 h against 2,255 h; about 1961–1990).
