Chaozhou vs Wong Tai Sin climate

How the climate of Chaozhou, China compares with Wong Tai Sin, Hong Kong, 289 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Wong Tai Sin is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 22.5 °C against 22.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Wong Tai Sin is 1.2 °C warmer (Chaozhou 14.3 °C, Wong Tai Sin 15.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chaozhou 32.1 °C (July), Wong Tai Sin 30.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chaozhou 10.4 °C, Wong Tai Sin 12.2 °C.
  • Wong Tai Sin gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 2,274 mm against 1,803 mm.
  • Chaozhou gets about 24 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,986 h against 1,963 h; about 1961–1990).
