Gyeongsan-si vs Luohe climate

How the climate of Gyeongsan-si, South Korea compares with Luohe, China, 1,366 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Luohe is 1.6 °C (2.8 °F) warmer over the year: 15.4 °C against 13.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Luohe is 3.3 °C warmer (Gyeongsan-si 22.8 °C, Luohe 26.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gyeongsan-si 30.7 °C (August), Luohe 32.1 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Gyeongsan-si -5.1 °C, Luohe -3.3 °C.
  • Gyeongsan-si gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 1,120 mm against 899 mm.
  • Gyeongsan-si gets about 301 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,452 h against 2,151 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gyeongsan-si 12 cm, Luohe 20 cm (ERA5-Land).
