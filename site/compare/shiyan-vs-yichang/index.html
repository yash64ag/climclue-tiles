Shiyan vs Yichang climate

How the climate of Shiyan, China compares with Yichang, China, 220 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yichang is 1.6 °C (2.9 °F) warmer over the year: 17.1 °C against 15.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Yichang is 2.5 °C warmer (Shiyan 4.5 °C, Yichang 7.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Shiyan 30.4 °C (July), Yichang 30.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Shiyan -2.1 °C, Yichang 0.0 °C.
  • Yichang gets 40% more rain and snow a year: 1,326 mm against 950 mm.
  • Shiyan gets about 170 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,798 h against 1,628 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Shiyan 54 cm, Yichang 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
