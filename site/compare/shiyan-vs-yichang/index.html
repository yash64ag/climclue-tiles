How the climate of Shiyan, Hubei, China compares with Yichang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yichang is 1.9 °C (3.4 °F) warmer over the year: 17.7 °C against 15.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Yichang is 3.0 °C warmer (Shiyan, Hubei 4.7 °C, Yichang 7.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Shiyan, Hubei 32.1 °C (July), Yichang 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Shiyan, Hubei -2.1 °C, Yichang 2.1 °C.
  • Yichang gets 37% more rain and snow a year: 1,225 mm against 892 mm.
  • Shiyan, Hubei gets about 170 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,798 h against 1,628 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Shiyan, Hubei 18 cm, Yichang 8.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
