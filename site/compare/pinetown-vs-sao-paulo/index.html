How the climate of Pinetown, South Africa compares with São Paulo, Brazil: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • São Paulo is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 20.0 °C against 19.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when São Paulo is 1.1 °C warmer (Pinetown 15.8 °C, São Paulo 17.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pinetown 27.3 °C (February), São Paulo 27.5 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Pinetown 9.6 °C, São Paulo 11.4 °C.
  • São Paulo gets 54% more rain and snow a year: 1,470 mm against 957 mm.
  • Pinetown gets about 680 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,460 h against 1,780 h; about 1961–1990).
