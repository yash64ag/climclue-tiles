How the climate of Bekasi, Indonesia compares with South Tangerang, Indonesia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bekasi is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Bekasi is 0.2 °C warmer (Bekasi 27.1 °C, South Tangerang 26.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bekasi 32.3 °C (September), South Tangerang 32.3 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Bekasi 21.7 °C, South Tangerang 21.4 °C.
  • South Tangerang gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 2,884 mm against 2,606 mm.
  • Bekasi gets about 9 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,651 h against 2,641 h; about 1961–1990).
