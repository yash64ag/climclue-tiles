Bekasi vs South Tangerang climate

How the climate of Bekasi, Indonesia compares with South Tangerang, Indonesia, 31 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bekasi and South Tangerang have the same yearly average temperature (26.8 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Bekasi is 0.1 °C warmer (Bekasi 26.3 °C, South Tangerang 26.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bekasi 31.5 °C (September), South Tangerang 31.5 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Bekasi 22.5 °C, South Tangerang 22.4 °C.
  • South Tangerang gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 2,702 mm against 2,659 mm.
  • Bekasi gets about 9 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,651 h against 2,641 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Bekasi in 5 of 12 months (6 equal)
  • Drier: Bekasi in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Bekasi in 9 of 12 months
