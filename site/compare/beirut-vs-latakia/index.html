Beirut vs Latakia climate

How the climate of Beirut, Lebanon compares with Latakia, Syria, 184 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Latakia is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 19.5 °C against 19.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Latakia is 1.3 °C warmer (Beirut 11.6 °C, Latakia 12.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Beirut 30.2 °C (August), Latakia 28.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Beirut 7.1 °C, Latakia 10.1 °C.
  • Beirut gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,081 mm against 956 mm.
  • Beirut gets about 94 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,942 h against 2,848 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Latakia in 8 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Beirut in 11 of 12 months
