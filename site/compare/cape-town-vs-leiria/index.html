Cape Town vs Leiria climate

How the climate of Cape Town, South Africa compares with Leiria, Portugal, 8,657 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Cape Town is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 16.2 °C against 15.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Cape Town is 9.3 °C warmer (Cape Town 19.4 °C, Leiria 10.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cape Town 24.6 °C (February), Leiria 26.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Cape Town 8.8 °C, Leiria 6.4 °C.
  • Cape Town gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 948 mm against 804 mm.
  • Cape Town gets about 351 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,958 h against 2,607 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
