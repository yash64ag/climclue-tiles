How the climate of San Diego, United States compares with Tijuana, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • San Diego is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 16.9 °C against 16.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when San Diego is 0.8 °C warmer (San Diego 15.6 °C, Tijuana 14.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: San Diego 25.2 °C (September), Tijuana 25.0 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: San Diego 8.0 °C, Tijuana 7.3 °C.
  • Tijuana gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 296 mm against 256 mm.
  • Tijuana gets about 5 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,067 h against 3,061 h; about 1961–1990).
