San Diego vs Tijuana climate

How the climate of San Diego, United States compares with Tijuana, Mexico, 28 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • San Diego is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 16.9 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when San Diego is 0.8 °C warmer (San Diego 18.1 °C, Tijuana 17.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: San Diego 25.3 °C (August), Tijuana 23.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: San Diego 9.5 °C, Tijuana 11.0 °C.
  • Tijuana gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 285 mm against 273 mm.
  • Tijuana gets about 5 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,067 h against 3,061 h; about 1961–1990).
