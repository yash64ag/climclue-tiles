Belgrade vs Sofia climate

How the climate of Belgrade, Serbia compares with Sofia, Bulgaria, 328 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Belgrade is 2.1 °C (3.8 °F) warmer over the year: 12.6 °C against 10.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Belgrade is 2.9 °C warmer (Belgrade 18.4 °C, Sofia 15.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belgrade 28.2 °C (July), Sofia 25.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Belgrade -2.4 °C, Sofia -5.5 °C.
  • Sofia gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 716 mm against 684 mm.
  • Sofia gets about 54 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,045 h against 1,991 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Belgrade 61 cm, Sofia 160 cm (ERA5-Land).
