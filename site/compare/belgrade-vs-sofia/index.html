How the climate of Belgrade, Serbia compares with Sofia, Bulgaria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Belgrade is 1.8 °C (3.2 °F) warmer over the year: 12.9 °C against 11.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Belgrade is 2.2 °C warmer (Belgrade 18.3 °C, Sofia 16.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belgrade 28.7 °C (August), Sofia 27.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Belgrade -1.3 °C, Sofia -3.7 °C.
  • Belgrade gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 702 mm against 612 mm.
  • Sofia gets about 54 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,045 h against 1,991 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Belgrade 45 cm, Sofia 95 cm (ERA5-Land).
