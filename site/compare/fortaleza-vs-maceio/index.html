Fortaleza vs Maceió climate

How the climate of Fortaleza, Brazil compares with Maceió, Brazil, 731 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fortaleza is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 25.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Fortaleza is 2.6 °C warmer (Fortaleza 26.6 °C, Maceió 23.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fortaleza 30.2 °C (November), Maceió 27.9 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Fortaleza 24.1 °C, Maceió 22.5 °C.
  • Maceió gets 37% more rain and snow a year: 1,798 mm against 1,314 mm.
  • Fortaleza gets about 96 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,643 h against 2,547 h; about 1961–1990).
