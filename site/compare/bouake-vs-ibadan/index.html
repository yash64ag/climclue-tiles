Bouaké vs Ibadan climate

How the climate of Bouaké, Ivory Coast compares with Ibadan, Nigeria, 986 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bouaké is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 25.9 °C against 25.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Ibadan is 0.7 °C warmer (Bouaké 25.8 °C, Ibadan 26.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bouaké 33.8 °C (February), Ibadan 33.6 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Bouaké 19.8 °C, Ibadan 20.7 °C.
  • Ibadan gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,397 mm against 1,281 mm.
  • Bouaké gets about 105 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,036 h against 1,931 h; about 1961–1990).
