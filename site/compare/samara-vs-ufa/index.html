Samara vs Ufa climate

How the climate of Samara, Russia compares with Ufa, Russia, 418 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Samara is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 5.3 °C against 4.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Samara is 1.8 °C warmer (Samara -10.3 °C, Ufa -12.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Samara 26.3 °C (July), Ufa 25.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Samara -13.8 °C, Ufa -16.6 °C.
  • Ufa gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 684 mm against 510 mm.
  • Samara gets about 111 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,055 h against 1,944 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Samara 167 cm, Ufa 229 cm (ERA5-Land).
