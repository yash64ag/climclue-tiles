Samara vs Ufa climate

How the climate of Samara, Russia compares with Ufa, Russia, 418 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Samara is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 5.5 °C against 4.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Samara is 1.8 °C warmer (Samara -10.3 °C, Ufa -12.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Samara 25.7 °C (July), Ufa 24.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Samara -13.1 °C, Ufa -15.2 °C.
  • Ufa gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 651 mm against 511 mm.
  • Samara gets about 111 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,055 h against 1,944 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Samara 167 cm, Ufa 229 cm (ERA5-Land).
