How the climate of Samara, Russia compares with Ufa, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Samara is 2.3 °C (4.2 °F) warmer over the year: 6.1 °C against 3.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Samara is 2.7 °C warmer (Samara 19.8 °C, Ufa 17.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Samara 27.0 °C (July), Ufa 25.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Samara -13.0 °C, Ufa -16.9 °C.
  • Ufa gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 684 mm against 510 mm.
  • Samara gets about 111 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,055 h against 1,944 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Samara 167 cm, Ufa 229 cm (ERA5-Land).
