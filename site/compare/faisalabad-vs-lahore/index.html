Faisalabad vs Lahore climate

How the climate of Faisalabad, Pakistan compares with Lahore, Pakistan, 121 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Faisalabad is 1.1 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 25.0 °C against 23.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Faisalabad is 1.7 °C warmer (Faisalabad 30.6 °C, Lahore 28.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Faisalabad 40.0 °C (June), Lahore 39.1 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Faisalabad 6.4 °C, Lahore 6.0 °C.
  • Lahore gets 62% more rain and snow a year: 680 mm against 421 mm.
  • Faisalabad gets about 41 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,978 h against 2,937 h; about 1961–1990).
