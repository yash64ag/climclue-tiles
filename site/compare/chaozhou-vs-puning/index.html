Chaozhou vs Puning climate

How the climate of Chaozhou, China compares with Puning, China, 60 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Chaozhou and Puning have the same yearly average temperature (22.3 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Chaozhou is 0.1 °C warmer (Chaozhou 27.3 °C, Puning 27.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chaozhou 32.1 °C (July), Puning 31.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chaozhou 10.4 °C, Puning 10.3 °C.
  • Chaozhou gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,803 mm against 1,639 mm.
  • Chaozhou gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,986 h against 1,980 h; about 1961–1990).
