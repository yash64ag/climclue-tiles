Hanumāngarh vs Multan climate

How the climate of Hanumāngarh, India compares with Multan, Pakistan, 283 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Multan is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 25.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Multan is 1.6 °C warmer (Hanumāngarh 32.8 °C, Multan 34.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hanumāngarh 40.7 °C (June), Multan 42.4 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Hanumāngarh 5.3 °C, Multan 5.3 °C.
  • Hanumāngarh gets 35% more rain and snow a year: 311 mm against 230 mm.
  • Multan gets about 188 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,083 h against 2,895 h; about 1961–1990).
