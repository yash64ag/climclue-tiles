Makkah vs Sanaa climate

How the climate of Makkah, Saudi Arabia compares with Sanaa, Yemen, 818 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Makkah is 11.2 °C (20.1 °F) warmer over the year: 29.1 °C against 17.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Makkah is 13.5 °C warmer (Makkah 34.0 °C, Sanaa 20.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Makkah 40.6 °C (June), Sanaa 26.9 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Makkah 15.4 °C, Sanaa 7.5 °C.
  • Sanaa gets 65% more rain and snow a year: 216 mm against 131 mm.
  • Makkah gets about 353 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,268 h against 2,915 h; about 1961–1990).
