How the climate of Makkah, Saudi Arabia compares with Sanaa, Yemen: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Makkah is 11.0 °C (19.9 °F) warmer over the year: 29.2 °C against 18.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Makkah is 13.6 °C warmer (Makkah 34.2 °C, Sanaa 20.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Makkah 41.2 °C (June), Sanaa 28.7 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Makkah 16.6 °C, Sanaa 6.2 °C.
  • Sanaa gets 79% more rain and snow a year: 231 mm against 129 mm.
  • Makkah gets about 353 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,268 h against 2,915 h; about 1961–1990).
