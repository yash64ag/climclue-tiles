Faisalabad vs Gujranwala climate

How the climate of Faisalabad, Pakistan compares with Gujranwala, Pakistan, 132 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Faisalabad is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 24.9 °C against 23.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Faisalabad is 2.3 °C warmer (Faisalabad 30.6 °C, Gujranwala 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Faisalabad 40.9 °C (June), Gujranwala 40.2 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Faisalabad 4.8 °C, Gujranwala 4.8 °C.
  • Gujranwala gets 121% more rain and snow a year: 937 mm against 424 mm.
  • Faisalabad gets about 9 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,978 h against 2,969 h; about 1961–1990).
