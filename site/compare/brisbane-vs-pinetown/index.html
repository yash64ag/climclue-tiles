Brisbane vs Pinetown climate

How the climate of Brisbane, Australia compares with Pinetown, South Africa, 11,162 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Brisbane is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 20.0 °C against 19.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Brisbane is 1.7 °C warmer (Brisbane 23.8 °C, Pinetown 22.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brisbane 29.0 °C (January), Pinetown 27.3 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Brisbane 8.8 °C, Pinetown 9.6 °C.
  • Brisbane gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,042 mm against 957 mm.
  • Brisbane gets about 373 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,833 h against 2,460 h; about 1961–1990).
