New York City vs The Bronx climate

How the climate of New York City, United States compares with The Bronx, United States, 19 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • New York City is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 12.3 °C against 12.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when New York City is 0.5 °C warmer (New York City 10.9 °C, The Bronx 10.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: New York City 28.6 °C (July), The Bronx 28.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: New York City -4.2 °C, The Bronx -4.2 °C.
  • The Bronx gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,213 mm against 1,181 mm.
  • The Bronx gets about 20 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,519 h against 2,499 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: New York City 92 cm, The Bronx 90 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Drier: New York City in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: The Bronx in 10 of 12 months
