Caloocan vs Quezon City climate

How the climate of Caloocan, Philippines compares with Quezon City, Philippines, 9 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Caloocan is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 27.8 °C against 27.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Caloocan is 0.4 °C warmer (Caloocan 26.8 °C, Quezon City 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Caloocan 34.6 °C (April), Quezon City 34.5 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Caloocan 22.0 °C, Quezon City 21.2 °C.
  • Quezon City gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 2,315 mm against 2,143 mm.
  • Caloocan gets about 44 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,404 h against 2,360 h; about 1961–1990).
