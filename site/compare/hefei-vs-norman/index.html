Hefei vs Norman climate

How the climate of Hefei, China compares with Norman, United States, 11,722 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Hefei is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 16.4 °C against 16.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Hefei is 1.0 °C warmer (Hefei 21.9 °C, Norman 20.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hefei 31.5 °C (July), Norman 33.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hefei -1.3 °C, Norman 0.0 °C.
  • Hefei gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 1,140 mm against 958 mm.
  • Norman gets about 959 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,003 h against 2,044 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hefei 23 cm, Norman 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
