How the climate of Hefei, China compares with Norman, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hefei is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 16.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Hefei is 1.5 °C warmer (Hefei 18.1 °C, Norman 16.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hefei 32.2 °C (July), Norman 33.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Hefei -0.4 °C, Norman -2.1 °C.
  • Hefei gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,120 mm against 979 mm.
  • Norman gets about 959 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,003 h against 2,044 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hefei 23 cm, Norman 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
