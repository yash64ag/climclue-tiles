How the climate of Iğdır, Turkey compares with Tabriz, Iran: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Iğdır is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 12.7 °C against 12.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Iğdır is 1.0 °C warmer (Iğdır 12.7 °C, Tabriz 11.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iğdır 33.7 °C (July), Tabriz 32.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Iğdır -6.5 °C, Tabriz -5.6 °C.
  • Iğdır gets 31% more rain and snow a year: 310 mm against 236 mm.
  • Tabriz gets about 72 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,707 h against 2,636 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Iğdır 31 cm, Tabriz 50 cm (ERA5-Land).
