Gyeongsan-si vs Zoucheng climate

How the climate of Gyeongsan-si, South Korea compares with Zoucheng, China, 1,065 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zoucheng is 0.4 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 14.2 °C against 13.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Zoucheng is 2.4 °C warmer (Gyeongsan-si 22.8 °C, Zoucheng 25.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gyeongsan-si 29.6 °C (August), Zoucheng 30.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gyeongsan-si -4.9 °C, Zoucheng -4.9 °C.
  • Gyeongsan-si gets 54% more rain and snow a year: 1,120 mm against 729 mm.
  • Gyeongsan-si gets about 46 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,452 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gyeongsan-si 12 cm, Zoucheng 9.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
