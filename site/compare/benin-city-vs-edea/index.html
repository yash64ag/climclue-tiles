Benin City vs Edéa climate

How the climate of Benin City, Nigeria compares with Edéa, Cameroon, 573 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Edéa is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 25.9 °C against 25.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Edéa is 0.6 °C warmer (Benin City 24.4 °C, Edéa 24.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Benin City 32.7 °C (February), Edéa 31.5 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Benin City 20.9 °C, Edéa 21.8 °C.
  • Edéa gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 2,709 mm against 2,278 mm.
  • Benin City gets about 40 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,755 h against 1,715 h; about 1961–1990).
