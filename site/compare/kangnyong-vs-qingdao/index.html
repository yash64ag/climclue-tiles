Kangnyŏng vs Qingdao climate

How the climate of Kangnyŏng, North Korea compares with Qingdao, China, 500 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Qingdao is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 13.1 °C against 11.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Qingdao is 2.5 °C warmer (Kangnyŏng -1.6 °C, Qingdao 1.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kangnyŏng 26.4 °C (August), Qingdao 26.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kangnyŏng -4.9 °C, Qingdao -1.5 °C.
  • Kangnyŏng gets 43% more rain and snow a year: 1,044 mm against 728 mm.
  • Qingdao gets about 30 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,470 h against 2,439 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kangnyŏng 24 cm, Qingdao 11 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
