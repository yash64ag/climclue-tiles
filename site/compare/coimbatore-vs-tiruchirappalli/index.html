How the climate of Coimbatore, India compares with Tiruchirappalli, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tiruchirappalli is 3.2 °C (5.8 °F) warmer over the year: 29.1 °C against 25.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Tiruchirappalli is 5.8 °C warmer (Coimbatore 25.6 °C, Tiruchirappalli 31.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Coimbatore 35.4 °C (April), Tiruchirappalli 37.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Coimbatore 18.6 °C, Tiruchirappalli 21.3 °C.
  • Tiruchirappalli gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 819 mm against 783 mm.
  • Tiruchirappalli gets about 87 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,493 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Tiruchirappalli in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Tiruchirappalli in 8 of 12 months
