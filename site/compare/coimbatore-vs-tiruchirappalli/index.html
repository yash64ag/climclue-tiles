Coimbatore vs Tiruchirappalli climate

How the climate of Coimbatore, India compares with Tiruchirappalli, India, 190 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tiruchirappalli is 3.4 °C (6.1 °F) warmer over the year: 29.1 °C against 25.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Tiruchirappalli is 5.7 °C warmer (Coimbatore 25.6 °C, Tiruchirappalli 31.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Coimbatore 35.0 °C (April), Tiruchirappalli 38.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Coimbatore 18.3 °C, Tiruchirappalli 19.9 °C.
  • Coimbatore gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 820 mm against 807 mm.
  • Tiruchirappalli gets about 87 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,493 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).
