Montevideo vs Porto Alegre climate

How the climate of Montevideo, Uruguay compares with Porto Alegre, Brazil, 714 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Porto Alegre is 3.3 °C (6.0 °F) warmer over the year: 19.7 °C against 16.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Porto Alegre is 4.1 °C warmer (Montevideo 12.7 °C, Porto Alegre 16.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Montevideo 24.7 °C (January), Porto Alegre 29.4 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Montevideo 8.8 °C, Porto Alegre 10.2 °C.
  • Porto Alegre gets 32% more rain and snow a year: 1,419 mm against 1,075 mm.
  • Montevideo gets about 268 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,486 h against 2,217 h; about 1961–1990).
