How the climate of Handan, China compares with Shijiazhuang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Handan is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 14.6 °C against 14.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Handan is 0.7 °C warmer (Handan 16.7 °C, Shijiazhuang 16.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Handan 33.0 °C (June), Shijiazhuang 32.3 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Handan -6.4 °C, Shijiazhuang -6.2 °C.
  • Handan gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 522 mm against 502 mm.
  • Shijiazhuang gets about 112 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,566 h against 2,454 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Handan 8.9 cm, Shijiazhuang 12 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Handan in 10 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Shijiazhuang in 12 of 12 months
