Handan vs Shijiazhuang climate

How the climate of Handan, China compares with Shijiazhuang, China, 159 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Handan is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 14.6 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Handan is 1.2 °C warmer (Handan 7.3 °C, Shijiazhuang 6.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Handan 33.0 °C (June), Shijiazhuang 33.0 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Handan -6.4 °C, Shijiazhuang -7.7 °C.
  • Handan gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 522 mm against 502 mm.
  • Shijiazhuang gets about 112 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,566 h against 2,454 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Handan 8.9 cm, Shijiazhuang 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
