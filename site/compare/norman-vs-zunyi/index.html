How the climate of Norman, United States compares with Zunyi, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zunyi is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.2 °C against 16.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Zunyi is 2.8 °C warmer (Norman 4.5 °C, Zunyi 7.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Norman 33.8 °C (August), Zunyi 30.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Norman -2.1 °C, Zunyi 2.7 °C.
  • Zunyi gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,082 mm against 979 mm.
  • Norman gets about 1,721 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,003 h against 1,282 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Norman 13 cm, Zunyi 7.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
