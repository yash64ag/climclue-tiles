Bissau vs Virār climate

How the climate of Bissau, Guinea-Bissau compares with Virār, India, 9,407 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bissau is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 27.1 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Bissau is 2.6 °C warmer (Bissau 26.6 °C, Virār 24.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bissau 34.1 °C (March), Virār 33.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Bissau 20.4 °C, Virār 18.1 °C.
  • Virār gets 55% more rain and snow a year: 2,454 mm against 1,583 mm.
  • Virār gets about 29 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,703 h against 2,675 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Bissau in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Virār in 8 of 12 months
