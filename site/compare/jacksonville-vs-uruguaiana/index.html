Jacksonville vs Uruguaiana climate

How the climate of Jacksonville, United States compares with Uruguaiana, Brazil, 7,170 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jacksonville is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 20.3 °C against 20.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Uruguaiana is 14.3 °C warmer (Jacksonville 12.2 °C, Uruguaiana 26.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jacksonville 31.8 °C (July), Uruguaiana 31.2 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Jacksonville 8.5 °C, Uruguaiana 9.9 °C.
  • Uruguaiana gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,479 mm against 1,459 mm.
  • Jacksonville gets about 296 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,845 h against 2,549 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
