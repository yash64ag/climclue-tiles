How the climate of Maicao, Colombia compares with Maracaibo, Venezuela: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Maracaibo is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 28.7 °C against 27.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Maracaibo is 1.9 °C warmer (Maicao 26.7 °C, Maracaibo 28.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maicao 33.5 °C (August), Maracaibo 33.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Maicao 22.0 °C, Maracaibo 23.5 °C.
  • Maicao gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 794 mm against 663 mm.
  • Maracaibo gets about 128 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,580 h against 2,452 h; about 1961–1990).
