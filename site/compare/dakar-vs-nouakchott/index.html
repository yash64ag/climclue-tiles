Dakar vs Nouakchott climate

How the climate of Dakar, Senegal compares with Nouakchott, Mauritania, 408 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Nouakchott is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 25.2 °C against 24.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Nouakchott is 2.9 °C warmer (Dakar 22.4 °C, Nouakchott 25.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dakar 28.4 °C (October), Nouakchott 33.2 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Dakar 19.9 °C, Nouakchott 16.2 °C.
  • Dakar gets 499% more rain and snow a year: 393 mm against 66 mm.
  • Dakar gets about 63 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,972 h against 2,908 h; about 1961–1990).
