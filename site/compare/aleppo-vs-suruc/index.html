Aleppo vs Suruç climate

How the climate of Aleppo, Syria compares with Suruç, Turkey, 142 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Suruç is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 18.6 °C against 17.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Suruç is 3.8 °C warmer (Aleppo 28.6 °C, Suruç 32.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aleppo 35.8 °C (July), Suruç 38.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Aleppo 2.1 °C, Suruç 1.1 °C.
  • Aleppo gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 400 mm against 371 mm.
  • Suruç gets about 219 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,217 h against 2,999 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Aleppo 2.4 cm, Suruç 9.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
