Aleppo vs Suruç climate

How the climate of Aleppo, Syria compares with Suruç, Turkey, 142 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Suruç is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 18.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Suruç is 3.7 °C warmer (Aleppo 28.8 °C, Suruç 32.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aleppo 35.9 °C (July), Suruç 40.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Aleppo 2.8 °C, Suruç 1.1 °C.
  • Suruç gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 379 mm against 366 mm.
  • Suruç gets about 219 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,217 h against 2,999 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Aleppo 2.4 cm, Suruç 9.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
