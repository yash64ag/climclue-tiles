Mashhad vs Navoiy climate

How the climate of Mashhad, Iran compares with Navoiy, Uzbekistan, 657 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Navoiy is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 15.1 °C against 14.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Navoiy is 1.6 °C warmer (Mashhad 19.9 °C, Navoiy 21.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mashhad 33.4 °C (July), Navoiy 35.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Mashhad -3.6 °C, Navoiy -2.8 °C.
  • Mashhad gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 265 mm against 219 mm.
  • Navoiy gets about 78 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,956 h against 2,878 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Mashhad 33 cm, Navoiy 25 cm (ERA5-Land).
