How the climate of Mashhad, Iran compares with Navoiy, Uzbekistan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Navoiy is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 15.3 °C against 14.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Navoiy is 2.0 °C warmer (Mashhad 25.1 °C, Navoiy 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mashhad 34.8 °C (July), Navoiy 36.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Mashhad -3.1 °C, Navoiy -2.9 °C.
  • Mashhad gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 261 mm against 205 mm.
  • Navoiy gets about 78 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,956 h against 2,878 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Mashhad 33 cm, Navoiy 25 cm (ERA5-Land).
