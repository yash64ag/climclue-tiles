How the climate of Mandalay, Myanmar compares with Yangon, Myanmar: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Mandalay is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 27.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Yangon is 3.1 °C warmer (Mandalay 21.1 °C, Yangon 24.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mandalay 39.1 °C (April), Yangon 37.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Mandalay 13.5 °C, Yangon 17.1 °C.
  • Yangon gets 174% more rain and snow a year: 2,796 mm against 1,019 mm.
  • Yangon gets about 198 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,574 h against 2,377 h; about 1961–1990).
