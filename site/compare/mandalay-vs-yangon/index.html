Mandalay vs Yangon climate

How the climate of Mandalay, Myanmar compares with Yangon, Myanmar, 575 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mandalay is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 27.5 °C against 27.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Yangon is 3.1 °C warmer (Mandalay 21.1 °C, Yangon 24.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mandalay 38.0 °C (April), Yangon 37.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Mandalay 15.0 °C, Yangon 17.7 °C.
  • Yangon gets 184% more rain and snow a year: 2,761 mm against 973 mm.
  • Yangon gets about 198 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,574 h against 2,377 h; about 1961–1990).
