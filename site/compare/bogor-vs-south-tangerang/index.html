How the climate of Bogor, Indonesia compares with South Tangerang, Indonesia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • South Tangerang is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.7 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when South Tangerang is 1.4 °C warmer (Bogor 25.8 °C, South Tangerang 27.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bogor 31.5 °C (September), South Tangerang 32.3 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Bogor 19.9 °C, South Tangerang 21.4 °C.
  • Bogor gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 3,591 mm against 2,884 mm.
  • South Tangerang gets about 17 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,641 h against 2,625 h; about 1961–1990).
