Bogor vs South Tangerang climate

How the climate of Bogor, Indonesia compares with South Tangerang, Indonesia, 35 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • South Tangerang is 0.9 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 26.7 °C against 25.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when South Tangerang is 1.1 °C warmer (Bogor 26.1 °C, South Tangerang 27.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bogor 30.9 °C (September), South Tangerang 31.5 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Bogor 20.9 °C, South Tangerang 22.4 °C.
  • Bogor gets 32% more rain and snow a year: 3,567 mm against 2,702 mm.
  • South Tangerang gets about 17 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,641 h against 2,625 h; about 1961–1990).

  • Drier: South Tangerang in 11 of 12 months
