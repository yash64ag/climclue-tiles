Kaifeng vs Zhengzhou climate

How the climate of Kaifeng, China compares with Zhengzhou, China, 60 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhengzhou is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 15.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Zhengzhou is 0.2 °C warmer (Kaifeng 9.3 °C, Zhengzhou 9.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kaifeng 31.4 °C (June), Zhengzhou 31.6 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Kaifeng -4.2 °C, Zhengzhou -4.0 °C.
  • Kaifeng gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 657 mm against 632 mm.
  • Kaifeng gets about 23 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,280 h against 2,257 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kaifeng 14 cm, Zhengzhou 15 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Zhengzhou in 9 of 12 months (2 equal)
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Kaifeng in 10 of 12 months
