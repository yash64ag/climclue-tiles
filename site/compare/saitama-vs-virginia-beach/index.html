Saitama vs Virginia Beach climate

Main differences

  • Virginia Beach is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.4 °C against 15.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Saitama is 2.0 °C warmer (Saitama 27.1 °C, Virginia Beach 25.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Saitama 31.1 °C (August), Virginia Beach 30.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Saitama -1.3 °C, Virginia Beach 0.2 °C.
  • Saitama gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,336 mm against 1,219 mm.
  • Virginia Beach gets about 803 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,666 h against 1,863 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Saitama 20 cm, Virginia Beach 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
