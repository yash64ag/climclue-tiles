How the climate of Baoshan, Shanghai, China compares with Putuo, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Baoshan, Shanghai and Putuo have the same yearly average temperature (16.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Putuo is 0.2 °C warmer (Baoshan, Shanghai 14.8 °C, Putuo 14.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoshan, Shanghai 31.4 °C (July), Putuo 31.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baoshan, Shanghai 1.1 °C, Putuo 1.0 °C.
  • Putuo gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,466 mm against 1,400 mm.
  • Baoshan, Shanghai gets about 18 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,986 h against 1,969 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoshan, Shanghai 7.1 cm, Putuo 8.9 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
