Baoshan, Shanghai vs Putuo climate

How the climate of Baoshan, Shanghai, China compares with Putuo, China, 20 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Baoshan, Shanghai and Putuo have the same yearly average temperature (16.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Putuo is 0.2 °C warmer (Baoshan, Shanghai 9.4 °C, Putuo 9.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoshan, Shanghai 30.9 °C (July), Putuo 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baoshan, Shanghai 1.3 °C, Putuo 0.4 °C.
  • Putuo gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,454 mm against 1,358 mm.
  • Baoshan, Shanghai gets about 18 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,986 h against 1,969 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoshan, Shanghai 7.1 cm, Putuo 8.9 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Putuo in 6 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: Baoshan, Shanghai in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Baoshan, Shanghai in 10 of 12 months
