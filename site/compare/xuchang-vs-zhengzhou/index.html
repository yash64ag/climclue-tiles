Xuchang vs Zhengzhou climate

How the climate of Xuchang, China compares with Zhengzhou, China, 83 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Xuchang is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.3 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Xuchang is 0.4 °C warmer (Xuchang 1.1 °C, Zhengzhou 0.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Xuchang 31.6 °C (June), Zhengzhou 31.6 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Xuchang -3.7 °C, Zhengzhou -4.0 °C.
  • Xuchang gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 756 mm against 632 mm.
  • Zhengzhou gets about 64 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,257 h against 2,193 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Xuchang 18 cm, Zhengzhou 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
