Brooklyn vs Philadelphia climate

How the climate of Brooklyn, United States compares with Philadelphia, United States, 129 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Philadelphia is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 13.1 °C against 12.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Philadelphia is 1.7 °C warmer (Brooklyn 10.4 °C, Philadelphia 12.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brooklyn 27.7 °C (July), Philadelphia 29.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Brooklyn -3.3 °C, Philadelphia -3.3 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,218 mm and 1,215 mm).
  • Brooklyn gets about 55 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,518 h against 2,463 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Brooklyn 77 cm, Philadelphia 66 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Drier: Brooklyn in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Brooklyn in 9 of 12 months
