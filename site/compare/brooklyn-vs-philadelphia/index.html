How the climate of Brooklyn, United States compares with Philadelphia, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Philadelphia is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 13.3 °C against 12.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Philadelphia is 1.2 °C warmer (Brooklyn 11.2 °C, Philadelphia 12.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brooklyn 29.0 °C (July), Philadelphia 30.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Brooklyn -3.0 °C, Philadelphia -3.5 °C.
  • Brooklyn gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,240 mm against 1,220 mm.
  • Brooklyn gets about 55 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,518 h against 2,463 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Brooklyn 56 cm, Philadelphia 47 cm (ERA5-Land).
