Córdoba vs Montevideo climate

How the climate of Córdoba, Argentina compares with Montevideo, Uruguay, 840 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Córdoba is 0.9 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 16.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Córdoba is 3.3 °C warmer (Córdoba 21.2 °C, Montevideo 17.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Córdoba 28.8 °C (January), Montevideo 24.7 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Córdoba 5.2 °C, Montevideo 8.8 °C.
  • Montevideo gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 1,075 mm against 878 mm.
  • Córdoba gets about 107 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,592 h against 2,486 h; about 1961–1990).
