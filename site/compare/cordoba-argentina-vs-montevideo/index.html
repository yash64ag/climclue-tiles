How the climate of Córdoba, Argentina compares with Montevideo, Uruguay: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Córdoba is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 17.7 °C against 16.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Córdoba is 3.5 °C warmer (Córdoba 18.9 °C, Montevideo 15.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Córdoba 29.9 °C (January), Montevideo 27.2 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Córdoba 3.8 °C, Montevideo 6.6 °C.
  • Montevideo gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 1,075 mm against 878 mm.
  • Córdoba gets about 107 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,592 h against 2,486 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Córdoba in 10 of 12 months
  • Drier: Córdoba in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Córdoba in 9 of 12 months
