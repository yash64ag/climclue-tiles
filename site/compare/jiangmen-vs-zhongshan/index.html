How the climate of Jiangmen, China compares with Zhongshan, Guangdong, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zhongshan, Guangdong is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 23.3 °C against 23.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Zhongshan, Guangdong is 0.4 °C warmer (Jiangmen 14.8 °C, Zhongshan, Guangdong 15.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiangmen 32.7 °C (July), Zhongshan, Guangdong 32.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jiangmen 11.2 °C, Zhongshan, Guangdong 11.5 °C.
  • Jiangmen gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 2,139 mm against 2,037 mm.
  • Zhongshan, Guangdong gets about 13 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,887 h against 1,875 h; about 1961–1990).
