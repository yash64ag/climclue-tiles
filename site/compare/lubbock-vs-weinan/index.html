Lubbock vs Weinan climate

How the climate of Lubbock, United States compares with Weinan, China, 11,769 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Weinan is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 15.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Lubbock is 2.8 °C warmer (Lubbock 4.7 °C, Weinan 1.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lubbock 33.2 °C (July), Weinan 34.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lubbock -3.2 °C, Weinan -3.3 °C.
  • Lubbock gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 516 mm against 495 mm.
  • Lubbock gets about 1,137 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,154 h against 2,017 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lubbock 11 cm, Weinan 5.8 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Sunnier: Lubbock in 12 of 12 months
