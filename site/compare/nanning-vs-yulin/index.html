Nanning vs Yulin climate

How the climate of Nanning, China compares with Yulin, China, 189 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yulin is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 22.5 °C against 22.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Yulin is 0.6 °C warmer (Nanning 19.7 °C, Yulin 20.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nanning 32.8 °C (July), Yulin 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nanning 9.6 °C, Yulin 10.2 °C.
  • Yulin gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 1,753 mm against 1,426 mm.
  • Yulin gets about 101 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,785 h against 1,684 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
