Belém vs Talisay, Western Visayas climate

How the climate of Belém, Brazil compares with Talisay, Western Visayas, Philippines, 18,618 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Talisay, Western Visayas is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 27.2 °C against 26.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Talisay, Western Visayas is 2.1 °C warmer (Belém 26.3 °C, Talisay, Western Visayas 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belém 31.5 °C (October), Talisay, Western Visayas 30.5 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Belém 24.1 °C, Talisay, Western Visayas 24.1 °C.
  • Belém gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 2,708 mm against 2,421 mm.
  • Talisay, Western Visayas gets about 116 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,326 h against 2,210 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
