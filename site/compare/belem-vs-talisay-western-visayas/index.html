How the climate of Belém, Pará, Brazil compares with Talisay, Western Visayas, Philippines: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Talisay, Western Visayas is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 27.2 °C against 26.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Talisay, Western Visayas is 2.1 °C warmer (Belém, Pará 26.3 °C, Talisay, Western Visayas 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belém, Pará 32.9 °C (October), Talisay, Western Visayas 32.7 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Belém, Pará 22.0 °C, Talisay, Western Visayas 22.7 °C.
  • Belém, Pará gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 2,804 mm against 2,390 mm.
  • Talisay, Western Visayas gets about 116 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,326 h against 2,210 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
