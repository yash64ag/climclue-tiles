How the climate of Chengdu, China compares with Zigong, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zigong is 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 17.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Zigong is 2.0 °C warmer (Chengdu 6.3 °C, Zigong 8.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chengdu 29.8 °C (July), Zigong 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chengdu 2.9 °C, Zigong 5.8 °C.
  • Zigong gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,098 mm against 995 mm.
  • Zigong gets about 72 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,368 h against 1,296 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Zigong in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Chengdu in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Zigong in 10 of 12 months
