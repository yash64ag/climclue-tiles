Chengdu vs Zigong climate

How the climate of Chengdu, China compares with Zigong, China, 162 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zigong is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 17.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Zigong is 1.8 °C warmer (Chengdu 6.5 °C, Zigong 8.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chengdu 30.1 °C (July), Zigong 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chengdu 2.9 °C, Zigong 5.8 °C.
  • Zigong gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,098 mm against 995 mm.
  • Zigong gets about 72 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,368 h against 1,296 h; about 1961–1990).
