Chengdu vs Zigong climate

How the climate of Chengdu, China compares with Zigong, China, 162 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zigong is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Zigong is 1.6 °C warmer (Chengdu 7.8 °C, Zigong 9.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chengdu 29.7 °C (July), Zigong 30.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chengdu 2.4 °C, Zigong 4.5 °C.
  • Zigong gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,118 mm against 1,007 mm.
  • Zigong gets about 72 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,368 h against 1,296 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chengdu 2.1 cm, Zigong 1.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
