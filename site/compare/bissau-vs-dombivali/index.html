How the climate of Bissau, Guinea-Bissau compares with Dombivali, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bissau is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 27.1 °C against 26.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Bissau is 2.2 °C warmer (Bissau 26.6 °C, Dombivali 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bissau 35.2 °C (March), Dombivali 34.5 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Bissau 17.9 °C, Dombivali 15.9 °C.
  • Dombivali gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 2,159 mm against 1,606 mm.
  • Dombivali gets about 25 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,700 h against 2,675 h; about 1961–1990).
