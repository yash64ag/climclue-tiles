Kazan vs Samara climate

How the climate of Kazan, Russia compares with Samara, Russia, 294 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Samara is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 5.5 °C against 4.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Samara is 1.7 °C warmer (Kazan 5.0 °C, Samara 6.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kazan 23.9 °C (July), Samara 25.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kazan -13.3 °C, Samara -13.1 °C.
  • Kazan gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 544 mm against 511 mm.
  • Samara gets about 169 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,055 h against 1,885 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kazan 219 cm, Samara 182 cm (ERA5-Land).
