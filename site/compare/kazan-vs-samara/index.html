Kazan vs Samara climate

How the climate of Kazan, Russia compares with Samara, Russia, 294 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Samara is 1.0 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 5.3 °C against 4.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Samara is 1.5 °C warmer (Kazan 5.0 °C, Samara 6.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kazan 25.1 °C (July), Samara 26.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kazan -14.2 °C, Samara -13.8 °C.
  • Kazan gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 536 mm against 510 mm.
  • Samara gets about 169 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,055 h against 1,885 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kazan 175 cm, Samara 167 cm (ERA5-Land).
