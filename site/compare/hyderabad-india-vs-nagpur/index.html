Hyderabad vs Nagpur climate

How the climate of Hyderabad, India compares with Nagpur, India, 423 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Nagpur is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Nagpur is 3.0 °C warmer (Hyderabad 32.4 °C, Nagpur 35.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hyderabad 37.7 °C (May), Nagpur 41.3 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Hyderabad 15.8 °C, Nagpur 14.0 °C.
  • Nagpur gets 53% more rain and snow a year: 1,283 mm against 837 mm.
  • Nagpur gets about 73 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,764 h against 2,691 h; about 1961–1990).
