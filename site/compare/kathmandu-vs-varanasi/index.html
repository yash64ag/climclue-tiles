Kathmandu vs Varanasi climate

How the climate of Kathmandu, Nepal compares with Varanasi, India, 351 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Varanasi is 8.1 °C (14.6 °F) warmer over the year: 25.8 °C against 17.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Varanasi is 12.4 °C warmer (Kathmandu 20.9 °C, Varanasi 33.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kathmandu 25.4 °C (June), Varanasi 39.5 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kathmandu 5.0 °C, Varanasi 10.0 °C.
  • Kathmandu gets 87% more rain and snow a year: 1,984 mm against 1,060 mm.
  • Varanasi gets about 491 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,482 h against 1,990 h; about 1961–1990).
