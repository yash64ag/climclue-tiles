Main differences

  • Jining, Shandong is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 14.5 °C against 14.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Jining, Shandong is 0.7 °C warmer (Jining, Shandong 1.7 °C, Liaocheng 1.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jining, Shandong 30.9 °C (July), Liaocheng 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jining, Shandong -5.0 °C, Liaocheng -5.6 °C.
  • Jining, Shandong gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 691 mm against 583 mm.
  • Liaocheng gets about 90 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,493 h against 2,403 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jining, Shandong 8.1 cm, Liaocheng 8.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
