Bamako vs Koudougou climate

How the climate of Bamako, Mali compares with Koudougou, Burkina Faso, 610 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Koudougou is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 28.0 °C against 27.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Koudougou is 0.9 °C warmer (Bamako 27.4 °C, Koudougou 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bamako 39.1 °C (April), Koudougou 38.7 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Bamako 17.1 °C, Koudougou 17.6 °C.
  • Bamako gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 893 mm against 826 mm.
  • Koudougou gets about 28 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,961 h against 2,933 h; about 1961–1990).
