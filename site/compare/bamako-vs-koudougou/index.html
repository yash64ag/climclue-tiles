How the climate of Bamako, Mali compares with Koudougou, Burkina Faso: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Koudougou is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 28.0 °C against 28.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Koudougou is 0.8 °C warmer (Bamako 27.5 °C, Koudougou 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bamako 39.6 °C (April), Koudougou 38.8 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Bamako 16.8 °C, Koudougou 16.6 °C.
  • Bamako gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 861 mm against 834 mm.
  • Koudougou gets about 28 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,961 h against 2,933 h; about 1961–1990).
