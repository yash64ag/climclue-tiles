Main differences

  • Lüliang is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 10.7 °C against 10.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Lüliang is 1.4 °C warmer (Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 23.2 °C, Lüliang 24.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 28.0 °C (July), Lüliang 30.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) -10.0 °C, Lüliang -12.0 °C.
  • Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 571 mm against 480 mm.
  • Lüliang gets about 212 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,599 h against 2,387 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 35 cm, Lüliang 19 cm (ERA5-Land).
