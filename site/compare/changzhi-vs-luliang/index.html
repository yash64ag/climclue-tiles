Main differences

  • Lüliang is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 10.3 °C against 9.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Lüliang is 1.6 °C warmer (Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 20.8 °C, Lüliang 22.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 27.1 °C (July), Lüliang 29.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) -10.0 °C, Lüliang -11.4 °C.
  • Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 638 mm against 499 mm.
  • Lüliang gets about 212 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,599 h against 2,387 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) 54 cm, Lüliang 48 cm (ERA5-Land).
