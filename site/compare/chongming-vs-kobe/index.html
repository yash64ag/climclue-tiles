How the climate of Chongming, China compares with Kobe, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Chongming is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 16.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Chongming is 1.3 °C warmer (Chongming 19.6 °C, Kobe 18.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chongming 30.7 °C (August), Kobe 31.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Chongming 1.8 °C, Kobe 2.3 °C.
  • Kobe gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,368 mm against 1,313 mm.
  • Chongming gets about 163 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,009 h against 1,846 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chongming 4.9 cm, Kobe 5.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
