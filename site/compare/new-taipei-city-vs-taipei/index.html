New Taipei City vs Taipei climate

How the climate of New Taipei City, Taiwan compares with Taipei, Taiwan, 7 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • New Taipei City and Taipei have the same yearly average temperature (22.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when New Taipei City is 0.1 °C warmer (New Taipei City 24.9 °C, Taipei 24.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: New Taipei City 31.2 °C (July), Taipei 31.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: New Taipei City 12.4 °C, Taipei 12.5 °C.
  • New Taipei City gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 3,215 mm against 3,040 mm.
  • New Taipei City gets about 21 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,724 h against 1,702 h; about 1961–1990).
