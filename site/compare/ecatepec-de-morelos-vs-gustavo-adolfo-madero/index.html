Ecatepec de Morelos vs Gustavo Adolfo Madero climate

How the climate of Ecatepec de Morelos, Mexico compares with Gustavo Adolfo Madero, Mexico, 13 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Ecatepec de Morelos is 0.9 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 17.0 °C against 16.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Ecatepec de Morelos is 1.5 °C warmer (Ecatepec de Morelos 19.1 °C, Gustavo Adolfo Madero 17.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ecatepec de Morelos 25.8 °C (April), Gustavo Adolfo Madero 24.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Ecatepec de Morelos 6.3 °C, Gustavo Adolfo Madero 5.7 °C.
  • Gustavo Adolfo Madero gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 719 mm against 576 mm.
  • Ecatepec de Morelos gets about 132 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,148 h against 2,016 h; about 1961–1990).
