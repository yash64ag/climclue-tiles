Ecatepec de Morelos vs Gustavo Adolfo Madero climate

How the climate of Ecatepec de Morelos, Mexico compares with Gustavo Adolfo Madero, Mexico, 13 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ecatepec de Morelos is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 17.0 °C against 16.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Ecatepec de Morelos is 0.9 °C warmer (Ecatepec de Morelos 19.2 °C, Gustavo Adolfo Madero 18.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ecatepec de Morelos 28.8 °C (April), Gustavo Adolfo Madero 27.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Ecatepec de Morelos 4.1 °C, Gustavo Adolfo Madero 4.0 °C.
  • Gustavo Adolfo Madero gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 670 mm against 654 mm.
  • Ecatepec de Morelos gets about 132 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,148 h against 2,016 h; about 1961–1990).
