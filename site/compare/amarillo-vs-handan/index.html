How the climate of Amarillo, United States compares with Handan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Handan is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 14.6 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Amarillo is 3.7 °C warmer (Amarillo 2.8 °C, Handan -0.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amarillo 33.0 °C (July), Handan 33.0 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Amarillo -4.9 °C, Handan -6.4 °C.
  • Amarillo gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 550 mm against 522 mm.
  • Amarillo gets about 789 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 2,454 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amarillo 33 cm, Handan 8.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
