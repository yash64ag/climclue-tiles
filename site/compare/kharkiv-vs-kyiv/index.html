Kharkiv vs Kyiv climate

How the climate of Kharkiv, Ukraine compares with Kyiv, Ukraine, 411 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kyiv is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 8.2 °C against 8.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Kyiv is 1.3 °C warmer (Kharkiv -4.5 °C, Kyiv -3.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kharkiv 25.9 °C (July), Kyiv 24.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kharkiv -7.8 °C, Kyiv -6.2 °C.
  • Kyiv gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 614 mm against 528 mm.
  • Kharkiv gets about 23 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,752 h against 1,729 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kharkiv 140 cm, Kyiv 125 cm (ERA5-Land).
