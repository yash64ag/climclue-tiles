How the climate of Kharkiv, Ukraine compares with Kyiv, Ukraine: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kyiv is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 8.3 °C against 8.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Kyiv is 1.5 °C warmer (Kharkiv -4.5 °C, Kyiv -3.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kharkiv 26.1 °C (July), Kyiv 25.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kharkiv -7.4 °C, Kyiv -6.0 °C.
  • Kyiv gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 632 mm against 526 mm.
  • Kharkiv gets about 23 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,752 h against 1,729 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kharkiv 105 cm, Kyiv 103 cm (ERA5-Land).
