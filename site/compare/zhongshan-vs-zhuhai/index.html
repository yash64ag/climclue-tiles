Zhongshan vs Zhuhai climate

How the climate of Zhongshan, China compares with Zhuhai, China, 34 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhongshan is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 23.3 °C against 23.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Zhongshan is 0.8 °C warmer (Zhongshan 29.4 °C, Zhuhai 28.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Zhongshan 31.2 °C (July), Zhuhai 29.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Zhongshan 11.2 °C, Zhuhai 12.9 °C.
  • Zhuhai gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 2,075 mm against 2,037 mm.
  • Zhuhai gets about 58 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,945 h against 1,887 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
