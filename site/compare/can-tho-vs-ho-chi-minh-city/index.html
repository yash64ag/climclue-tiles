Cần Thơ vs Ho Chi Minh City climate

How the climate of Cần Thơ, Vietnam compares with Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, 127 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ho Chi Minh City is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 27.4 °C against 27.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Ho Chi Minh City is 0.9 °C warmer (Cần Thơ 26.4 °C, Ho Chi Minh City 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cần Thơ 33.4 °C (April), Ho Chi Minh City 34.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Cần Thơ 21.8 °C, Ho Chi Minh City 21.6 °C.
  • Ho Chi Minh City gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,845 mm against 1,720 mm.
  • Cần Thơ gets about 268 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,017 h against 1,749 h; about 1961–1990).
