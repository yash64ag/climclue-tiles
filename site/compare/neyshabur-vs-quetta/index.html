Neyshābūr vs Quetta climate

How the climate of Neyshābūr, Iran compares with Quetta, Pakistan, 1,015 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Quetta is 3.1 °C (5.6 °F) warmer over the year: 17.5 °C against 14.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Quetta is 4.4 °C warmer (Neyshābūr 19.6 °C, Quetta 24.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Neyshābūr 34.1 °C (July), Quetta 36.8 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Neyshābūr -4.0 °C, Quetta -2.0 °C.
  • Neyshābūr gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 396 mm against 317 mm.
  • Quetta gets about 458 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,333 h against 2,874 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Neyshābūr 42 cm, Quetta 82 cm (ERA5-Land).
