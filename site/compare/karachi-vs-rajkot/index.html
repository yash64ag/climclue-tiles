Karachi vs Rājkot climate

How the climate of Karachi, Pakistan compares with Rājkot, India, 480 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rājkot is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 26.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Rājkot is 2.0 °C warmer (Karachi 29.7 °C, Rājkot 31.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Karachi 34.2 °C (October), Rājkot 39.3 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Karachi 12.2 °C, Rājkot 11.4 °C.
  • Rājkot gets 309% more rain and snow a year: 738 mm against 180 mm.
  • Rājkot gets about 31 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,075 h against 3,044 h; about 1961–1990).
