Wenzhou vs Yiwu climate

How the climate of Wenzhou, China compares with Yiwu, China, 157 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Wenzhou is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 18.4 °C against 17.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Wenzhou is 2.9 °C warmer (Wenzhou 8.3 °C, Yiwu 5.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Wenzhou 30.1 °C (July), Yiwu 33.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Wenzhou 4.0 °C, Yiwu 0.7 °C.
  • Wenzhou gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 1,809 mm against 1,517 mm.
  • Yiwu gets about 72 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,866 h against 1,794 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Wenzhou 5.8 cm, Yiwu 28 cm (ERA5-Land).
