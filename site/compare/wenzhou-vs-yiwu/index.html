Wenzhou vs Yiwu climate

How the climate of Wenzhou, China compares with Yiwu, China, 157 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Wenzhou is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 17.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Wenzhou is 2.9 °C warmer (Wenzhou 10.5 °C, Yiwu 7.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Wenzhou 31.3 °C (July), Yiwu 34.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Wenzhou 4.8 °C, Yiwu 1.4 °C.
  • Wenzhou gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,634 mm against 1,413 mm.
  • Yiwu gets about 72 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,866 h against 1,794 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Wenzhou 0.2 cm, Yiwu 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
