Cape Town vs Johannesburg climate

How the climate of Cape Town, South Africa compares with Johannesburg, South Africa, 1,262 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Cape Town is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 16.2 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Cape Town is 5.4 °C warmer (Cape Town 13.8 °C, Johannesburg 8.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cape Town 24.6 °C (February), Johannesburg 24.7 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Cape Town 8.8 °C, Johannesburg 1.1 °C.
  • Cape Town gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 948 mm against 821 mm.
  • Johannesburg gets about 211 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,169 h against 2,958 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Cape Town in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Johannesburg in 7 of 12 months
