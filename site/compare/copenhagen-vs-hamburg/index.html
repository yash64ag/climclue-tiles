How the climate of Copenhagen, Denmark compares with Hamburg, Germany: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hamburg is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 9.5 °C against 9.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Hamburg is 1.6 °C warmer (Copenhagen 3.4 °C, Hamburg 4.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Copenhagen 21.8 °C (July), Hamburg 22.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Copenhagen -1.0 °C, Hamburg -0.8 °C.
  • Hamburg gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 813 mm against 660 mm.
  • Copenhagen gets about 117 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,652 h against 1,535 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Copenhagen 41 cm, Hamburg 40 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Hamburg in 10 of 12 months
  • Drier: Copenhagen in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Copenhagen in 8 of 12 months
