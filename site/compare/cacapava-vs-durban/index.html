How the climate of Caçapava, Brazil compares with Durban, South Africa: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Caçapava is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 21.3 °C against 21.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Caçapava is 1.3 °C warmer (Caçapava 21.7 °C, Durban 20.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Caçapava 29.0 °C (February), Durban 28.4 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Caçapava 11.3 °C, Durban 12.0 °C.
  • Caçapava gets 49% more rain and snow a year: 1,364 mm against 914 mm.
  • Durban gets about 713 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,513 h against 1,800 h; about 1961–1990).
