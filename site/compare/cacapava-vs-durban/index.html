Caçapava vs Durban climate

How the climate of Caçapava, Brazil compares with Durban, South Africa, 7,535 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Caçapava is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 21.2 °C against 21.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Caçapava is 1.1 °C warmer (Caçapava 21.4 °C, Durban 20.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Caçapava 28.3 °C (February), Durban 26.7 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Caçapava 12.4 °C, Durban 13.8 °C.
  • Caçapava gets 55% more rain and snow a year: 1,410 mm against 909 mm.
  • Durban gets about 713 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,513 h against 1,800 h; about 1961–1990).
