Amritsar vs Lahore climate

How the climate of Amritsar, India compares with Lahore, Pakistan, 50 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lahore is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 23.9 °C against 23.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Lahore is 0.8 °C warmer (Amritsar 30.4 °C, Lahore 31.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amritsar 38.6 °C (June), Lahore 39.1 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Amritsar 5.9 °C, Lahore 6.0 °C.
  • Amritsar gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 758 mm against 680 mm.
  • Lahore gets about 107 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,937 h against 2,830 h; about 1961–1990).
