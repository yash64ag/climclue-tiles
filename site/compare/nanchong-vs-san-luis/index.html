Nanchong vs San Luis climate

How the climate of Nanchong, China compares with San Luis, Argentina, 19,248 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Nanchong is 1.0 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Nanchong is 18.4 °C warmer (Nanchong 28.1 °C, San Luis 9.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nanchong 32.2 °C (August), San Luis 31.2 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Nanchong 5.1 °C, San Luis 2.7 °C.
  • Nanchong gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,119 mm against 1,110 mm.
  • San Luis gets about 1,729 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,021 h against 1,292 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: San Luis in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: San Luis in 12 of 12 months
