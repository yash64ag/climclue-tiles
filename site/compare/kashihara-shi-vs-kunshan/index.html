Kashihara-shi vs Kunshan climate

How the climate of Kashihara-shi, Japan compares with Kunshan, China, 1,426 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kunshan is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 16.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Kunshan is 1.3 °C warmer (Kashihara-shi 19.3 °C, Kunshan 20.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kashihara-shi 32.9 °C (August), Kunshan 31.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kashihara-shi 0.6 °C, Kunshan 0.3 °C.
  • Kashihara-shi gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,439 mm against 1,325 mm.
  • Kunshan gets about 143 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,978 h against 1,835 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kashihara-shi 7.7 cm, Kunshan 11 cm (ERA5-Land).
