Fuzhou, Fujian vs Taipei climate

How the climate of Fuzhou, Fujian, China compares with Taipei, Taiwan, 249 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Taipei is 2.5 °C (4.4 °F) warmer over the year: 22.6 °C against 20.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Taipei is 5.1 °C warmer (Fuzhou, Fujian 10.6 °C, Taipei 15.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuzhou, Fujian 33.9 °C (July), Taipei 33.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuzhou, Fujian 6.8 °C, Taipei 12.6 °C.
  • Taipei gets 136% more rain and snow a year: 3,040 mm against 1,289 mm.
  • Fuzhou, Fujian gets about 60 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,763 h against 1,702 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Fuzhou, Fujian in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Taipei in 7 of 12 months
