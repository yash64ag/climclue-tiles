Ilorin vs Parakou climate

How the climate of Ilorin, Nigeria compares with Parakou, Benin, 230 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ilorin is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Ilorin is 1.0 °C warmer (Ilorin 26.9 °C, Parakou 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ilorin 36.3 °C (March), Parakou 36.5 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Ilorin 20.4 °C, Parakou 18.8 °C.
  • Parakou gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,191 mm against 1,085 mm.
  • Parakou gets about 272 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,491 h against 2,219 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Parakou in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Parakou in 12 of 12 months
