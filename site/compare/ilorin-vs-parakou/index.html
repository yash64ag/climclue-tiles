Ilorin vs Parakou climate

How the climate of Ilorin, Nigeria compares with Parakou, Benin, 230 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ilorin is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 26.7 °C against 26.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Ilorin is 1.1 °C warmer (Ilorin 27.0 °C, Parakou 25.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ilorin 36.5 °C (March), Parakou 36.4 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Ilorin 20.3 °C, Parakou 18.7 °C.
  • Parakou gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,225 mm against 1,075 mm.
  • Parakou gets about 272 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,491 h against 2,219 h; about 1961–1990).
