Jiujiang vs Kurume climate

How the climate of Jiujiang, China compares with Kurume, Japan, 1,432 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jiujiang is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 17.7 °C against 16.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Jiujiang is 3.2 °C warmer (Jiujiang 22.9 °C, Kurume 19.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiujiang 32.1 °C (July), Kurume 30.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Jiujiang 1.7 °C, Kurume 1.2 °C.
  • Kurume gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,893 mm against 1,820 mm.
  • Jiujiang gets about 121 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,905 h against 1,785 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jiujiang 19 cm, Kurume 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
