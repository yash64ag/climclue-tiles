How the climate of Jiujiang, China compares with Kurume, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jiujiang is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 17.6 °C against 16.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Jiujiang is 3.1 °C warmer (Jiujiang 22.8 °C, Kurume 19.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiujiang 33.7 °C (July), Kurume 32.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Jiujiang 0.7 °C, Kurume 1.8 °C.
  • Jiujiang gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,879 mm against 1,854 mm.
  • Jiujiang gets about 121 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,905 h against 1,785 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jiujiang 16 cm, Kurume 6.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
