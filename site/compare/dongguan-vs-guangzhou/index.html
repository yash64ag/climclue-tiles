Dongguan vs Guangzhou climate

How the climate of Dongguan, China compares with Guangzhou, China, 52 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dongguan is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 22.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Dongguan is 0.4 °C warmer (Dongguan 14.8 °C, Guangzhou 14.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dongguan 31.4 °C (August), Guangzhou 31.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Dongguan 10.1 °C, Guangzhou 10.1 °C.
  • Dongguan gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 2,121 mm against 2,108 mm.
  • Dongguan gets about 50 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,850 h against 1,800 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Dongguan in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Dongguan in 11 of 12 months
