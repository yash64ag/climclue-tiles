How the climate of Dongguan, China compares with Guangzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Dongguan and Guangzhou have the same yearly average temperature (23.0 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Dongguan is 0.3 °C warmer (Dongguan 14.9 °C, Guangzhou 14.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dongguan 33.0 °C (July), Guangzhou 33.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Dongguan 10.4 °C, Guangzhou 10.4 °C.
  • Dongguan gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 2,151 mm against 2,053 mm.
  • Dongguan gets about 50 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,850 h against 1,800 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Guangzhou in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Dongguan in 11 of 12 months
