Caloocan vs Pasuruan climate

How the climate of Caloocan, Philippines compares with Pasuruan, Indonesia, 2,634 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Caloocan is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 27.8 °C against 27.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Caloocan is 1.9 °C warmer (Caloocan 29.4 °C, Pasuruan 27.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Caloocan 32.3 °C (April), Pasuruan 32.2 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Caloocan 23.4 °C, Pasuruan 23.1 °C.
  • Caloocan gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 2,138 mm against 1,803 mm.
  • Pasuruan gets about 936 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,340 h against 2,404 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
