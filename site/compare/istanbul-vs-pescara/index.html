Istanbul vs Pescara climate

How the climate of Istanbul, Turkey compares with Pescara, Italy, 1,233 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Pescara is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Pescara is 2.6 °C warmer (Istanbul 8.1 °C, Pescara 10.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Istanbul 28.4 °C (August), Pescara 30.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Istanbul 3.4 °C, Pescara 3.7 °C.
  • Istanbul gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 783 mm against 740 mm.
  • Istanbul gets about 574 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,763 h against 2,188 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Istanbul 16 cm, Pescara 3.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
