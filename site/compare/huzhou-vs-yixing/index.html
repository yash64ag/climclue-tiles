Huzhou vs Yixing climate

How the climate of Huzhou, China compares with Yixing, China, 60 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Huzhou is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.9 °C against 16.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Huzhou is 0.4 °C warmer (Huzhou 4.3 °C, Yixing 3.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huzhou 32.8 °C (July), Yixing 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Huzhou 0.5 °C, Yixing -0.1 °C.
  • Huzhou gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,292 mm against 1,213 mm.
  • Yixing gets about 48 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,982 h against 1,934 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Huzhou 17 cm, Yixing 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
