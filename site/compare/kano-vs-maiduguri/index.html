Kano vs Maiduguri climate

How the climate of Kano, Nigeria compares with Maiduguri, Nigeria, 505 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Maiduguri is 1.6 °C (3.0 °F) warmer over the year: 27.6 °C against 25.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Maiduguri is 2.0 °C warmer (Kano 24.1 °C, Maiduguri 26.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kano 38.1 °C (April), Maiduguri 40.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Kano 12.5 °C, Maiduguri 13.1 °C.
  • Kano gets 90% more rain and snow a year: 1,095 mm against 576 mm.
  • Maiduguri gets about 78 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,002 h against 2,924 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Maiduguri in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Maiduguri in 8 of 12 months (4 equal)
  • Sunnier: Maiduguri in 8 of 12 months
