Dubai vs Sharjah climate

How the climate of Dubai, United Arab Emirates compares with Sharjah, United Arab Emirates, 30 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dubai is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 27.7 °C against 27.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Dubai is 0.8 °C warmer (Dubai 31.3 °C, Sharjah 30.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dubai 42.9 °C (August), Sharjah 40.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Dubai 13.5 °C, Sharjah 15.3 °C.
  • Sharjah gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 118 mm against 113 mm.
  • Dubai gets about 30 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,449 h against 3,420 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Dubai in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Dubai in 12 of 12 months
