Iizuka vs Jiujiang climate

How the climate of Iizuka, Japan compares with Jiujiang, China, 1,455 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jiujiang is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 17.6 °C against 17.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Jiujiang is 2.8 °C warmer (Iizuka 20.1 °C, Jiujiang 22.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iizuka 32.6 °C (August), Jiujiang 33.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Iizuka 2.1 °C, Jiujiang 0.7 °C.
  • Jiujiang gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,879 mm against 1,669 mm.
  • Jiujiang gets about 146 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,905 h against 1,759 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Iizuka 14 cm, Jiujiang 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
