Jeddah vs Omdurman climate

How the climate of Jeddah, Saudi Arabia compares with Omdurman, Sudan, 960 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Omdurman is 1.3 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 29.3 °C against 28.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Omdurman is 4.6 °C warmer (Jeddah 29.4 °C, Omdurman 34.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jeddah 33.9 °C (August), Omdurman 40.3 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Jeddah 20.9 °C, Omdurman 14.6 °C.
  • Omdurman gets 45% more rain and snow a year: 99 mm against 69 mm.
  • Omdurman gets about 195 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,512 h against 3,317 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Omdurman in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
