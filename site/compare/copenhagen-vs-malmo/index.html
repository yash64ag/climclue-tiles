Copenhagen vs Malmö climate

How the climate of Copenhagen, Denmark compares with Malmö, Sweden, 28 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Copenhagen and Malmö have the same yearly average temperature (8.4 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Malmö is 0.4 °C warmer (Copenhagen 11.3 °C, Malmö 11.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Copenhagen 20.6 °C (July), Malmö 21.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Copenhagen -1.3 °C, Malmö -1.9 °C.
  • Copenhagen gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 660 mm against 655 mm.
  • Copenhagen gets about 66 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,652 h against 1,586 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Copenhagen 41 cm, Malmö 57 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Copenhagen in 6 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: Malmö in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Copenhagen in 12 of 12 months
