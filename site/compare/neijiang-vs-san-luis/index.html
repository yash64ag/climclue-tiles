Neijiang vs San Luis climate

How the climate of Neijiang, China compares with San Luis, Argentina, 19,100 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Neijiang is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 17.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Neijiang is 17.5 °C warmer (Neijiang 27.4 °C, San Luis 9.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Neijiang 30.7 °C (July), San Luis 29.8 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Neijiang 4.4 °C, San Luis 5.3 °C.
  • San Luis gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,133 mm against 1,091 mm.
  • San Luis gets about 1,716 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,021 h against 1,305 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each

  • Sunnier: San Luis in 12 of 12 months
