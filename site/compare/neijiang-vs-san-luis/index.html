How the climate of Neijiang, China compares with San Luis, Argentina: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Neijiang is 1.0 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Neijiang is 17.8 °C warmer (Neijiang 27.5 °C, San Luis 9.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Neijiang 31.4 °C (July), San Luis 31.2 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Neijiang 5.6 °C, San Luis 2.7 °C.
  • San Luis gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,110 mm against 1,088 mm.
  • San Luis gets about 1,716 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,021 h against 1,305 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
