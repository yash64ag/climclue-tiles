Monterrey vs Santiago del Estero climate

How the climate of Monterrey, Mexico compares with Santiago del Estero, Argentina, 7,088 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Monterrey is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 21.7 °C against 21.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Santiago del Estero is 13.4 °C warmer (Monterrey 14.1 °C, Santiago del Estero 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Monterrey 33.6 °C (August), Santiago del Estero 34.3 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Monterrey 8.3 °C, Santiago del Estero 6.6 °C.
  • Monterrey gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 627 mm against 616 mm.
  • Santiago del Estero gets about 530 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,417 h against 1,887 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Sunnier: Santiago del Estero in 9 of 12 months
