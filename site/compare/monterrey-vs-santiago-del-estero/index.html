How the climate of Monterrey, Mexico compares with Santiago del Estero, Argentina: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Monterrey is 0.9 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 22.0 °C against 21.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Monterrey is 14.8 °C warmer (Monterrey 28.0 °C, Santiago del Estero 13.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Monterrey 35.0 °C (August), Santiago del Estero 34.0 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Monterrey 7.7 °C, Santiago del Estero 5.6 °C.
  • Monterrey gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 627 mm against 616 mm.
  • Santiago del Estero gets about 530 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,417 h against 1,887 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
