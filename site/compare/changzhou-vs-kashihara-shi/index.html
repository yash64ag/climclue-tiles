How the climate of Changzhou, China compares with Kashihara-shi, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Changzhou is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.3 °C against 15.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Changzhou is 2.3 °C warmer (Changzhou 21.0 °C, Kashihara-shi 18.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changzhou 32.0 °C (July), Kashihara-shi 32.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Changzhou -0.6 °C, Kashihara-shi 0.5 °C.
  • Kashihara-shi gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,439 mm against 1,236 mm.
  • Changzhou gets about 182 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,017 h against 1,835 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changzhou 17 cm, Kashihara-shi 7.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
