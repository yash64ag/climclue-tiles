How the climate of Dombivali, India compares with Pimpri, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Dombivali is 2.3 °C (4.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 24.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Dombivali is 3.1 °C warmer (Dombivali 26.2 °C, Pimpri 23.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dombivali 34.5 °C (April), Pimpri 36.6 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Dombivali 15.9 °C, Pimpri 12.1 °C.
  • Dombivali gets 90% more rain and snow a year: 2,159 mm against 1,135 mm.
  • Pimpri gets about 89 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,789 h against 2,700 h; about 1961–1990).
