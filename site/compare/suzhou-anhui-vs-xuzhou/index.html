Main differences

  • Suzhou, Anhui is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Suzhou, Anhui is 0.6 °C warmer (Suzhou, Anhui 3.3 °C, Xuzhou 2.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Suzhou, Anhui 31.6 °C (July), Xuzhou 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Suzhou, Anhui -3.4 °C, Xuzhou -4.0 °C.
  • Suzhou, Anhui gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 904 mm against 886 mm.
  • Xuzhou gets about 61 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,262 h against 2,201 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Suzhou, Anhui 17 cm, Xuzhou 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
