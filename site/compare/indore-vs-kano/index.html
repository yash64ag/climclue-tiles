Indore vs Kano climate

How the climate of Indore, India compares with Kano, Nigeria, 7,188 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kano is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 26.1 °C against 25.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Kano is 2.2 °C warmer (Indore 21.4 °C, Kano 23.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Indore 39.1 °C (May), Kano 38.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Indore 12.5 °C, Kano 12.9 °C.
  • Kano gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,037 mm against 920 mm.
  • Kano gets about 81 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,924 h against 2,843 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Kano in 7 of 12 months
