How the climate of Indore, India compares with Kano, Nigeria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kano is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 25.9 °C against 25.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Indore is 2.0 °C warmer (Indore 32.8 °C, Kano 30.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Indore 40.6 °C (May), Kano 38.1 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Indore 10.4 °C, Kano 12.5 °C.
  • Kano gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 1,095 mm against 926 mm.
  • Kano gets about 81 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,924 h against 2,843 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
