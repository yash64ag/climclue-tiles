Jiading vs Putuo climate

How the climate of Jiading, China compares with Putuo, China, 20 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jiading and Putuo have the same yearly average temperature (16.5 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Putuo is 0.2 °C warmer (Jiading 12.8 °C, Putuo 13.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiading 31.4 °C (July), Putuo 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jiading 0.4 °C, Putuo 0.4 °C.
  • Putuo gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,454 mm against 1,411 mm.
  • Jiading gets about 10 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,979 h against 1,969 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jiading 9.2 cm, Putuo 8.5 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Putuo in 6 of 12 months (4 equal)
  • Drier: Jiading in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Jiading in 10 of 12 months
