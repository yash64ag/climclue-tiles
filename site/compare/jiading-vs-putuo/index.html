Jiading vs Putuo climate

How the climate of Jiading, China compares with Putuo, China, 20 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Putuo is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Putuo is 0.2 °C warmer (Jiading 4.3 °C, Putuo 4.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiading 31.7 °C (July), Putuo 31.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jiading 0.8 °C, Putuo 1.0 °C.
  • Putuo gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,466 mm against 1,422 mm.
  • Jiading gets about 10 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,979 h against 1,969 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jiading 9.5 cm, Putuo 8.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
