Daegu vs Zibo climate

How the climate of Daegu, South Korea compares with Zibo, China, 949 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Daegu and Zibo have the same yearly average temperature (14.0 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Zibo is 2.3 °C warmer (Daegu 23.1 °C, Zibo 25.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daegu 31.1 °C (August), Zibo 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Daegu -5.3 °C, Zibo -6.8 °C.
  • Daegu gets 61% more rain and snow a year: 979 mm against 608 mm.
  • Zibo gets about 54 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,536 h against 2,483 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daegu 13 cm, Zibo 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
