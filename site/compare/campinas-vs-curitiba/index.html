Campinas, São Paulo vs Curitiba climate

How the climate of Campinas, São Paulo, Brazil compares with Curitiba, Brazil, 359 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Campinas, São Paulo is 3.7 °C (6.6 °F) warmer over the year: 21.1 °C against 17.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Campinas, São Paulo is 4.9 °C warmer (Campinas, São Paulo 20.3 °C, Curitiba 15.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Campinas, São Paulo 28.7 °C (January), Curitiba 25.8 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Campinas, São Paulo 10.8 °C, Curitiba 7.8 °C.
  • Campinas, São Paulo gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,607 mm against 1,510 mm.
  • Campinas, São Paulo gets about 820 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,472 h against 1,653 h; about 1961–1990).
