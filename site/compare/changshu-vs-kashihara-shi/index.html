How the climate of Changshu, China compares with Kashihara-shi, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Changshu is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.3 °C against 16.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Changshu is 1.3 °C warmer (Changshu 20.6 °C, Kashihara-shi 19.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changshu 31.8 °C (July), Kashihara-shi 32.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Changshu 0.0 °C, Kashihara-shi 0.6 °C.
  • Kashihara-shi gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,439 mm against 1,268 mm.
  • Changshu gets about 169 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,004 h against 1,835 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changshu 12 cm, Kashihara-shi 7.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
