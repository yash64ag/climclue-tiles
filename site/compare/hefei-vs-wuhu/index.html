Hefei vs Wuhu climate

How the climate of Hefei, China compares with Wuhu, China, 123 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Wuhu is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 16.7 °C against 16.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Wuhu is 0.6 °C warmer (Hefei 3.0 °C, Wuhu 3.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hefei 31.5 °C (July), Wuhu 32.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hefei -1.2 °C, Wuhu -0.7 °C.
  • Wuhu gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,354 mm against 1,120 mm.
  • Hefei gets about 40 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,044 h against 2,004 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hefei 23 cm, Wuhu 22 cm (ERA5-Land).
