How the climate of Hefei, China compares with Wuhu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hefei and Wuhu have the same yearly average temperature (16.7 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Hefei is 0.3 °C warmer (Hefei 22.1 °C, Wuhu 21.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hefei 32.3 °C (July), Wuhu 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hefei -0.4 °C, Wuhu -0.4 °C.
  • Wuhu gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,354 mm against 1,120 mm.
  • Hefei gets about 40 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,044 h against 2,004 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hefei 23 cm, Wuhu 22 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Hefei in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Hefei in 9 of 12 months
