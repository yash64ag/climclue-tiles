Paech’ŏn-ŭp vs Suwon climate

How the climate of Paech’ŏn-ŭp, North Korea compares with Suwon, South Korea, 102 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Suwon is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 12.3 °C against 11.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Suwon is 0.9 °C warmer (Paech’ŏn-ŭp -3.3 °C, Suwon -2.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Paech’ŏn-ŭp 27.9 °C (August), Suwon 28.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Paech’ŏn-ŭp -8.4 °C, Suwon -7.7 °C.
  • Suwon gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,297 mm against 1,278 mm.
  • Paech’ŏn-ŭp gets about 6 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,401 h against 2,395 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Paech’ŏn-ŭp 24 cm, Suwon 26 cm (ERA5-Land).
