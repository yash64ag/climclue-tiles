Paech’ŏn-ŭp vs Suwon climate

How the climate of Paech’ŏn-ŭp, North Korea compares with Suwon, South Korea, 102 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Suwon is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 12.2 °C against 11.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Suwon is 1.1 °C warmer (Paech’ŏn-ŭp -3.5 °C, Suwon -2.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Paech’ŏn-ŭp 28.8 °C (August), Suwon 29.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Paech’ŏn-ŭp -8.2 °C, Suwon -7.5 °C.
  • Suwon gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,303 mm against 1,291 mm.
  • Paech’ŏn-ŭp gets about 6 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,401 h against 2,395 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Paech’ŏn-ŭp 24 cm, Suwon 26 cm (ERA5-Land).
