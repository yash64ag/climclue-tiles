Jinjiang vs Tainan climate

How the climate of Jinjiang, China compares with Tainan, Taiwan, 262 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tainan is 2.7 °C (4.9 °F) warmer over the year: 24.1 °C against 21.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Tainan is 5.3 °C warmer (Jinjiang 15.9 °C, Tainan 21.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinjiang 31.0 °C (July), Tainan 30.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinjiang 9.2 °C, Tainan 14.6 °C.
  • Tainan gets 48% more rain and snow a year: 1,855 mm against 1,252 mm.
  • Tainan gets about 25 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,992 h against 1,967 h; about 1961–1990).
