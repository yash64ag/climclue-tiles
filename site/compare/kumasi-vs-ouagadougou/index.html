Kumasi vs Ouagadougou climate

How the climate of Kumasi, Ghana compares with Ouagadougou, Burkina Faso, 631 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ouagadougou is 2.1 °C (3.8 °F) warmer over the year: 28.2 °C against 26.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Ouagadougou is 5.5 °C warmer (Kumasi 27.4 °C, Ouagadougou 32.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kumasi 33.5 °C (February), Ouagadougou 39.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Kumasi 21.7 °C, Ouagadougou 17.2 °C.
  • Kumasi gets 83% more rain and snow a year: 1,335 mm against 728 mm.
  • Ouagadougou gets about 1,011 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,948 h against 1,937 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Ouagadougou in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Ouagadougou in 12 of 12 months
