Beirut vs Damascus climate

How the climate of Beirut, Lebanon compares with Damascus, Syria, 85 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Beirut is 2.5 °C (4.4 °F) warmer over the year: 20.0 °C against 17.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Beirut is 5.8 °C warmer (Beirut 14.4 °C, Damascus 8.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Beirut 30.0 °C (August), Damascus 34.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Beirut 9.1 °C, Damascus 2.1 °C.
  • Beirut gets 251% more rain and snow a year: 992 mm against 283 mm.
  • Damascus gets about 134 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,075 h against 2,942 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Beirut 0 cm, Damascus 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
