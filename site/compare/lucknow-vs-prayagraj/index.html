How the climate of Lucknow, India compares with Prayagraj, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Prayagraj is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 25.7 °C against 25.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Prayagraj is 0.9 °C warmer (Lucknow 14.6 °C, Prayagraj 15.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lucknow 40.2 °C (May), Prayagraj 41.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Lucknow 7.5 °C, Prayagraj 8.1 °C.
  • Prayagraj gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 916 mm against 900 mm.
  • Prayagraj gets about 55 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,666 h against 2,611 h; about 1961–1990).
