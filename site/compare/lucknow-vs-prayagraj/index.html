Lucknow vs Prayagraj climate

How the climate of Lucknow, India compares with Prayagraj, India, 180 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Prayagraj is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 25.7 °C against 25.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Prayagraj is 0.9 °C warmer (Lucknow 15.9 °C, Prayagraj 16.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lucknow 39.0 °C (May), Prayagraj 40.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Lucknow 8.8 °C, Prayagraj 9.8 °C.
  • Prayagraj gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 907 mm against 897 mm.
  • Prayagraj gets about 55 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,666 h against 2,611 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Prayagraj in 11 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: Prayagraj in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Prayagraj in 9 of 12 months
