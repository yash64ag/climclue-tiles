Davao vs Tawau climate

How the climate of Davao, Philippines compares with Tawau, Malaysia, 910 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Davao is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Davao is 0.4 °C warmer (Davao 27.4 °C, Tawau 26.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Davao 29.7 °C (April), Tawau 29.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Davao 24.3 °C, Tawau 24.2 °C.
  • Tawau gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 2,008 mm against 1,817 mm.
  • Tawau gets about 6 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,294 h against 2,288 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Davao in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: Davao in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
