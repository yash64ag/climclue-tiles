How the climate of Davao, Philippines compares with Tawau, Malaysia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Davao is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 27.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Davao is 0.5 °C warmer (Davao 27.6 °C, Tawau 27.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Davao 32.7 °C (April), Tawau 31.6 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Davao 22.0 °C, Tawau 22.7 °C.
  • Tawau gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,954 mm against 1,741 mm.
  • Tawau gets about 6 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,294 h against 2,288 h; about 1961–1990).
