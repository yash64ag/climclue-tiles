How the climate of Curitiba, Brazil compares with Wollongong, Australia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Wollongong is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 17.7 °C against 17.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Wollongong is 1.2 °C warmer (Curitiba 15.0 °C, Wollongong 16.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Curitiba 26.1 °C (January), Wollongong 25.3 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Curitiba 8.2 °C, Wollongong 9.0 °C.
  • Curitiba gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 1,510 mm against 1,165 mm.
  • Wollongong gets about 783 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,436 h against 1,653 h; about 1961–1990).
