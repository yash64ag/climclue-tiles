Taizhou, Jiangsu vs Yangzhou climate

How the climate of Taizhou, Jiangsu, China compares with Yangzhou, China, 46 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yangzhou is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 15.7 °C against 15.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Yangzhou is 0.4 °C warmer (Taizhou, Jiangsu 14.8 °C, Yangzhou 15.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Taizhou, Jiangsu 31.1 °C (July), Yangzhou 31.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Taizhou, Jiangsu -2.2 °C, Yangzhou -1.8 °C.
  • Taizhou, Jiangsu gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,115 mm against 1,098 mm.
  • Taizhou, Jiangsu gets about 5 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,078 h against 2,074 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Taizhou, Jiangsu 14 cm, Yangzhou 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
