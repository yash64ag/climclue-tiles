How the climate of Puerto Plata, Dominican Republic compares with Salvador, Brazil: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Puerto Plata is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 26.2 °C against 25.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Puerto Plata is 3.9 °C warmer (Puerto Plata 27.7 °C, Salvador 23.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Puerto Plata 33.7 °C (September), Salvador 30.4 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Puerto Plata 18.9 °C, Salvador 21.3 °C.
  • Salvador gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,717 mm against 1,658 mm.
  • Puerto Plata gets about 172 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,503 h against 2,331 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Puerto Plata in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Puerto Plata in 7 of 12 months
