Puerto Plata vs Salvador climate

How the climate of Puerto Plata, Dominican Republic compares with Salvador, Brazil, 5,069 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Puerto Plata is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 25.4 °C against 25.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Puerto Plata is 3.5 °C warmer (Puerto Plata 27.1 °C, Salvador 23.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Puerto Plata 30.2 °C (August), Salvador 27.6 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Puerto Plata 21.3 °C, Salvador 22.6 °C.
  • Salvador gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,761 mm against 1,707 mm.
  • Puerto Plata gets about 172 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,503 h against 2,331 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
