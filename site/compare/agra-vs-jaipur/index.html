Agra vs Jaipur climate

How the climate of Agra, India compares with Jaipur, India, 223 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Agra is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 25.2 °C against 24.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Agra is 1.4 °C warmer (Agra 29.4 °C, Jaipur 28.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Agra 39.9 °C (May), Jaipur 39.4 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Agra 7.9 °C, Jaipur 8.0 °C.
  • Agra gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 719 mm against 634 mm.
  • Jaipur gets about 124 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,831 h against 2,707 h; about 1961–1990).
