How the climate of Bao'an, China compares with Zhuhai, Guangdong, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zhuhai, Guangdong is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 22.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Zhuhai, Guangdong is 0.3 °C warmer (Bao'an 27.6 °C, Zhuhai, Guangdong 27.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bao'an 31.7 °C (July), Zhuhai, Guangdong 31.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bao'an 11.5 °C, Zhuhai, Guangdong 12.5 °C.
  • Bao'an gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 2,140 mm against 2,075 mm.
  • Zhuhai, Guangdong gets about 19 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,945 h against 1,927 h; about 1961–1990).
