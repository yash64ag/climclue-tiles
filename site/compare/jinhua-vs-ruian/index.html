Jinhua vs Rui’an climate

How the climate of Jinhua, China compares with Rui’an, China, 178 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rui’an is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 18.8 °C against 18.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Rui’an is 3.0 °C warmer (Jinhua 8.0 °C, Rui’an 10.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinhua 33.1 °C (July), Rui’an 30.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinhua 1.1 °C, Rui’an 4.9 °C.
  • Jinhua gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,620 mm against 1,607 mm.
  • Jinhua gets about 90 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,885 h against 1,795 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinhua 12 cm, Rui’an 0.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
