Luohe vs Xuchang climate

How the climate of Luohe, China compares with Xuchang, China, 53 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Luohe is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.4 °C against 15.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Luohe is 0.5 °C warmer (Luohe 9.6 °C, Xuchang 9.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Luohe 31.3 °C (July), Xuchang 31.6 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Luohe -3.4 °C, Xuchang -3.7 °C.
  • Luohe gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 899 mm against 756 mm.
  • Xuchang gets about 42 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,193 h against 2,151 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Luohe 20 cm, Xuchang 18 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Xuchang in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Xuchang in 10 of 12 months
