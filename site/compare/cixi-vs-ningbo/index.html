Cixi vs Ningbo climate

How the climate of Cixi, China compares with Ningbo, China, 44 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Cixi and Ningbo have the same yearly average temperature (17.4 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Ningbo is 0.6 °C warmer (Cixi 7.9 °C, Ningbo 8.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cixi 32.9 °C (July), Ningbo 32.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Cixi 2.0 °C, Ningbo 2.6 °C.
  • Ningbo gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,653 mm against 1,491 mm.
  • Cixi gets about 12 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,883 h against 1,871 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Cixi 8.8 cm, Ningbo 7.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
