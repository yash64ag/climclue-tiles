Kashihara-shi vs Putuo climate

How the climate of Kashihara-shi, Japan compares with Putuo, China, 1,392 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Putuo is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 16.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Putuo is 1.2 °C warmer (Kashihara-shi 27.1 °C, Putuo 28.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kashihara-shi 32.9 °C (August), Putuo 31.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kashihara-shi 0.6 °C, Putuo 1.0 °C.
  • Putuo gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,466 mm against 1,439 mm.
  • Putuo gets about 133 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,969 h against 1,835 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kashihara-shi 7.7 cm, Putuo 8.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
