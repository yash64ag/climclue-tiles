Astrakhan vs Yinchuan climate

How the climate of Astrakhan, Russia compares with Yinchuan, China, 4,751 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Astrakhan is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 10.5 °C against 9.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Astrakhan is 2.6 °C warmer (Astrakhan -3.8 °C, Yinchuan -6.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Astrakhan 31.2 °C (July), Yinchuan 30.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Astrakhan -6.6 °C, Yinchuan -12.1 °C.
  • Astrakhan gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 236 mm against 191 mm.
  • Yinchuan gets about 495 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,845 h against 2,350 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Astrakhan 53 cm, Yinchuan 19 cm (ERA5-Land).
