How the climate of Astrakhan, Russia compares with Yinchuan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Astrakhan is 0.9 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 10.8 °C against 9.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Astrakhan is 3.1 °C warmer (Astrakhan -3.5 °C, Yinchuan -6.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Astrakhan 31.9 °C (July), Yinchuan 29.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Astrakhan -7.4 °C, Yinchuan -12.9 °C.
  • Astrakhan gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 236 mm against 191 mm.
  • Yinchuan gets about 495 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,845 h against 2,350 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Astrakhan 39 cm, Yinchuan 8.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
