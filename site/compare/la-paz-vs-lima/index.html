La Paz vs Lima climate

How the climate of La Paz, Bolivia compares with Lima, Peru, 1,077 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lima is 12.6 °C (22.8 °F) warmer over the year: 18.9 °C against 6.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Lima is 15.4 °C warmer (La Paz 7.2 °C, Lima 22.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: La Paz 12.7 °C (November), Lima 24.8 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: La Paz -2.9 °C, Lima 14.8 °C.
  • La Paz gets 3,820% more rain and snow a year: 1,356 mm against 35 mm.
  • La Paz gets about 844 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,349 h against 1,505 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: La Paz 20 cm, Lima 0 cm (ERA5-Land).
