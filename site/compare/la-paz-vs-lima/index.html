La Paz vs Lima climate

How the climate of La Paz, Bolivia compares with Lima, Peru, 1,077 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lima is 10.7 °C (19.2 °F) warmer over the year: 19.0 °C against 8.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Lima is 13.4 °C warmer (La Paz 9.2 °C, Lima 22.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: La Paz 16.6 °C (November), Lima 26.5 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: La Paz -1.6 °C, Lima 13.4 °C.
  • La Paz gets 2,687% more rain and snow a year: 1,064 mm against 38 mm.
  • La Paz gets about 844 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,349 h against 1,505 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: La Paz 5.6 cm, Lima 0 cm (ERA5-Land).
