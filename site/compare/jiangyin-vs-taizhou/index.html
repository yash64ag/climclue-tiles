How the climate of Jiangyin, China compares with Taizhou, Jiangsu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jiangyin is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 16.0 °C against 15.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Jiangyin is 0.6 °C warmer (Jiangyin 5.5 °C, Taizhou, Jiangsu 4.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiangyin 31.8 °C (July), Taizhou, Jiangsu 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jiangyin -0.7 °C, Taizhou, Jiangsu -1.5 °C.
  • Jiangyin gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,166 mm against 1,115 mm.
  • Taizhou, Jiangsu gets about 48 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,078 h against 2,031 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jiangyin 14 cm, Taizhou, Jiangsu 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
