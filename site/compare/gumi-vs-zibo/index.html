Gumi vs Zibo climate

How the climate of Gumi, South Korea compares with Zibo, China, 921 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zibo is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 13.8 °C against 13.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Zibo is 2.2 °C warmer (Gumi 23.1 °C, Zibo 25.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gumi 29.5 °C (August), Zibo 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gumi -5.3 °C, Zibo -6.4 °C.
  • Gumi gets 75% more rain and snow a year: 1,077 mm against 615 mm.
  • Zibo gets about 64 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,536 h against 2,472 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gumi 17 cm, Zibo 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
