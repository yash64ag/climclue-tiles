Gumi vs Zibo climate

How the climate of Gumi, South Korea compares with Zibo, China, 921 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zibo is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 14.0 °C against 13.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Zibo is 2.5 °C warmer (Gumi 23.0 °C, Zibo 25.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gumi 30.8 °C (August), Zibo 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gumi -6.0 °C, Zibo -6.8 °C.
  • Gumi gets 98% more rain and snow a year: 1,207 mm against 608 mm.
  • Zibo gets about 64 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,536 h against 2,472 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gumi 17 cm, Zibo 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
