Main differences

  • Chengtangcun is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 14.4 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Chengtangcun is 0.3 °C warmer (Chengtangcun 1.9 °C, Zoucheng 1.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chengtangcun 31.1 °C (July), Zoucheng 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chengtangcun -5.0 °C, Zoucheng -5.5 °C.
  • Chengtangcun gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 735 mm against 729 mm.
  • Zoucheng gets about 40 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,406 h against 2,366 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chengtangcun 11 cm, Zoucheng 9.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
