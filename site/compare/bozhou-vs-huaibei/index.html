Bozhou vs Huaibei climate

How the climate of Bozhou, China compares with Huaibei, China, 95 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bozhou is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 14.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Bozhou is 0.5 °C warmer (Bozhou 9.2 °C, Huaibei 8.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bozhou 31.1 °C (July), Huaibei 31.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bozhou -3.6 °C, Huaibei -3.8 °C.
  • Huaibei gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 907 mm against 811 mm.
  • Huaibei gets about 9 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,229 h against 2,220 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bozhou 17 cm, Huaibei 18 cm (ERA5-Land).
