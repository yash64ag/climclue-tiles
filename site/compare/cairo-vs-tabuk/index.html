Cairo vs Tabuk climate

How the climate of Cairo, Egypt compares with Tabuk, Saudi Arabia, 548 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Cairo is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 21.9 °C against 21.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Cairo is 3.1 °C warmer (Cairo 13.7 °C, Tabuk 10.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cairo 36.0 °C (July), Tabuk 38.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Cairo 8.2 °C, Tabuk 3.1 °C.
  • Tabuk gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 26 mm against 20 mm.
  • Tabuk gets about 373 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,703 h against 3,330 h; about 1961–1990).
