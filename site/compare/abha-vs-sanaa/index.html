How the climate of Abha, Saudi Arabia compares with Sanaa, Yemen: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Sanaa is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 18.2 °C against 17.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Sanaa is 2.0 °C warmer (Abha 12.9 °C, Sanaa 14.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abha 29.4 °C (June), Sanaa 28.7 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Abha 6.2 °C, Sanaa 6.2 °C.
  • Abha gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 320 mm against 231 mm.
  • Abha gets about 11 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,926 h against 2,915 h; about 1961–1990).
