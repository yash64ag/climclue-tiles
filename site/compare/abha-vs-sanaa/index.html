Abha vs Sanaa climate

How the climate of Abha, Saudi Arabia compares with Sanaa, Yemen, 366 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Abha is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 18.4 °C against 17.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Abha is 1.5 °C warmer (Abha 22.4 °C, Sanaa 20.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abha 27.4 °C (June), Sanaa 26.9 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Abha 9.1 °C, Sanaa 7.5 °C.
  • Abha gets 43% more rain and snow a year: 309 mm against 216 mm.
  • Abha gets about 11 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,926 h against 2,915 h; about 1961–1990).
