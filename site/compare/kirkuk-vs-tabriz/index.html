Kirkuk vs Tabriz climate

How the climate of Kirkuk, Iraq compares with Tabriz, Iran, 336 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kirkuk is 10.3 °C (18.5 °F) warmer over the year: 21.8 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Kirkuk is 11.4 °C warmer (Kirkuk 35.8 °C, Tabriz 24.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kirkuk 43.7 °C (July), Tabriz 31.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kirkuk 3.4 °C, Tabriz -5.9 °C.
  • Kirkuk gets 111% more rain and snow a year: 498 mm against 236 mm.
  • Kirkuk gets about 610 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,318 h against 2,707 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kirkuk 0.2 cm, Tabriz 50 cm (ERA5-Land).
