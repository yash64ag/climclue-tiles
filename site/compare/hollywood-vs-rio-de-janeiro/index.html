How the climate of Hollywood, United States compares with Rio de Janeiro, Brazil: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hollywood is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 24.8 °C against 24.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Hollywood is 7.4 °C warmer (Hollywood 28.4 °C, Rio de Janeiro 21.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hollywood 32.4 °C (August), Rio de Janeiro 31.5 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Hollywood 15.2 °C, Rio de Janeiro 16.6 °C.
  • Hollywood gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 1,559 mm against 1,310 mm.
  • Hollywood gets about 988 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,078 h against 2,091 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
