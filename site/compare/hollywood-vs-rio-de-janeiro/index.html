Hollywood vs Rio de Janeiro climate

How the climate of Hollywood, United States compares with Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, 6,740 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Hollywood is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 24.3 °C against 23.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Hollywood is 6.8 °C warmer (Hollywood 27.6 °C, Rio de Janeiro 20.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hollywood 29.9 °C (August), Rio de Janeiro 30.1 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Hollywood 17.9 °C, Rio de Janeiro 17.4 °C.
  • Hollywood gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,555 mm against 1,368 mm.
  • Hollywood gets about 988 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,078 h against 2,091 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
