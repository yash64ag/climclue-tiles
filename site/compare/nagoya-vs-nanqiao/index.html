How the climate of Nagoya, Japan compares with Nanqiao, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Nanqiao is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 16.9 °C against 15.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Nanqiao is 1.8 °C warmer (Nagoya 11.9 °C, Nanqiao 13.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nagoya 32.0 °C (August), Nanqiao 31.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nagoya 0.4 °C, Nanqiao 1.7 °C.
  • Nagoya gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 1,689 mm against 1,466 mm.
  • Nagoya gets about 28 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,969 h against 1,940 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nagoya 7.4 cm, Nanqiao 6.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
