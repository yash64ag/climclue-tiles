Andong vs Zhu Cheng City climate

How the climate of Andong, South Korea compares with Zhu Cheng City, China, 838 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhu Cheng City is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 13.2 °C against 12.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Zhu Cheng City is 0.9 °C warmer (Andong 25.0 °C, Zhu Cheng City 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Andong 28.7 °C (August), Zhu Cheng City 29.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Andong -6.5 °C, Zhu Cheng City -5.8 °C.
  • Andong gets 45% more rain and snow a year: 1,021 mm against 702 mm.
  • Andong gets about 63 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,513 h against 2,450 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Andong 21 cm, Zhu Cheng City 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
