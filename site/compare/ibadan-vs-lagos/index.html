Ibadan vs Lagos climate

How the climate of Ibadan, Nigeria compares with Lagos, Nigeria, 117 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lagos is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 25.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Lagos is 1.3 °C warmer (Ibadan 26.0 °C, Lagos 27.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ibadan 34.2 °C (February), Lagos 29.9 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Ibadan 21.4 °C, Lagos 23.7 °C.
  • Lagos gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,627 mm against 1,397 mm.
  • Ibadan gets about 52 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,931 h against 1,879 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Lagos in 11 of 12 months
  • Drier: Ibadan in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Ibadan in 9 of 12 months
