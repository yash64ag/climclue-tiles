Metro vs Zamboanga climate

How the climate of Metro, Indonesia compares with Zamboanga, Philippines, 2,291 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Metro is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 26.5 °C against 26.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Metro is 1.1 °C warmer (Metro 27.1 °C, Zamboanga 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Metro 31.4 °C (October), Zamboanga 28.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Metro 22.3 °C, Zamboanga 24.5 °C.
  • Metro gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 2,281 mm against 2,000 mm.
  • Metro gets about 277 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,488 h against 2,211 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Metro in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Metro in 8 of 12 months
