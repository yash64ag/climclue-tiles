Metro vs Zamboanga climate

How the climate of Metro, Indonesia compares with Zamboanga, Philippines, 2,291 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zamboanga is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Zamboanga is 0.8 °C warmer (Metro 26.8 °C, Zamboanga 27.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Metro 31.4 °C (October), Zamboanga 29.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Metro 22.2 °C, Zamboanga 25.5 °C.
  • Metro gets 36% more rain and snow a year: 2,304 mm against 1,695 mm.
  • Metro gets about 277 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,488 h against 2,211 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Zamboanga in 11 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Metro in 8 of 12 months
