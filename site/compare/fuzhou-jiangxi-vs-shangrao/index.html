How the climate of Fuzhou, Jiangxi, China compares with Shangrao, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Fuzhou, Jiangxi is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 19.1 °C against 19.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Fuzhou, Jiangxi is 0.6 °C warmer (Fuzhou, Jiangxi 27.1 °C, Shangrao 26.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 34.9 °C (July), Shangrao 34.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 3.1 °C, Shangrao 2.4 °C.
  • Shangrao gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 2,027 mm against 1,977 mm.
  • Shangrao gets about 55 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,878 h against 1,823 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 6.6 cm, Shangrao 6.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
