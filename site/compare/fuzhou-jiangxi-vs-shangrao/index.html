Fuzhou, Jiangxi vs Shangrao climate

How the climate of Fuzhou, Jiangxi, China compares with Shangrao, China, 167 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Fuzhou, Jiangxi is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 19.1 °C against 18.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Fuzhou, Jiangxi is 0.6 °C warmer (Fuzhou, Jiangxi 27.1 °C, Shangrao 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 34.0 °C (July), Shangrao 33.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 2.6 °C, Shangrao 2.2 °C.
  • Shangrao gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 2,045 mm against 2,005 mm.
  • Shangrao gets about 55 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,878 h against 1,823 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 6.6 cm, Shangrao 6.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
