How the climate of Mar del Plata, Argentina compares with Melbourne, Australia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Melbourne is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 14.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Melbourne is 2.1 °C warmer (Mar del Plata 10.6 °C, Melbourne 12.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mar del Plata 26.1 °C (January), Melbourne 26.5 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Mar del Plata 3.5 °C, Melbourne 6.3 °C.
  • Mar del Plata gets 50% more rain and snow a year: 943 mm against 629 mm.
  • Mar del Plata gets about 446 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,642 h against 2,197 h; about 1961–1990).
