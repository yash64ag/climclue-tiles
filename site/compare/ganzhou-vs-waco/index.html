Ganzhou vs Waco climate

How the climate of Ganzhou, China compares with Waco, United States, 12,781 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ganzhou is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 20.0 °C against 19.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Ganzhou is 1.3 °C warmer (Ganzhou 21.7 °C, Waco 20.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ganzhou 35.0 °C (July), Waco 36.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Ganzhou 4.4 °C, Waco 3.1 °C.
  • Ganzhou gets 68% more rain and snow a year: 1,635 mm against 971 mm.
  • Waco gets about 922 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,724 h against 1,802 h; about 1961–1990).
