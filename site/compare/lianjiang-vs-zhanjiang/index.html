Lianjiang vs Zhanjiang climate

How the climate of Lianjiang, China compares with Zhanjiang, China, 47 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhanjiang is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 23.8 °C against 23.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Zhanjiang is 0.6 °C warmer (Lianjiang 17.4 °C, Zhanjiang 18.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lianjiang 32.0 °C (July), Zhanjiang 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lianjiang 12.5 °C, Zhanjiang 13.3 °C.
  • Lianjiang gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 2,092 mm against 1,982 mm.
  • Zhanjiang gets about 39 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,920 h against 1,881 h; about 1961–1990).
