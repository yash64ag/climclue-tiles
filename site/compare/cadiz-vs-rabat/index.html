Cadiz vs Rabat climate

How the climate of Cadiz, Spain compares with Rabat, Morocco, 284 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rabat is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 18.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Rabat is 1.1 °C warmer (Cadiz 16.1 °C, Rabat 17.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cadiz 27.7 °C (July), Rabat 27.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Cadiz 9.8 °C, Rabat 10.0 °C.
  • Cadiz gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 605 mm against 485 mm.
  • Cadiz gets about 76 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,945 h against 2,869 h; about 1961–1990).
