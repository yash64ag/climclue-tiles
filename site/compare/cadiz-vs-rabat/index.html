How the climate of Cadiz, Spain compares with Rabat, Morocco: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Rabat is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 18.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Rabat is 1.2 °C warmer (Cadiz 13.5 °C, Rabat 14.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cadiz 28.9 °C (August), Rabat 28.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Cadiz 8.3 °C, Rabat 8.6 °C.
  • Cadiz gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 605 mm against 485 mm.
  • Cadiz gets about 76 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,945 h against 2,869 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Rabat in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: Rabat in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Rabat in 7 of 12 months
