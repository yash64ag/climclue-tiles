Guiyang vs Santa Rosa climate

How the climate of Guiyang, China compares with Santa Rosa, Argentina, 18,613 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Guiyang is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.6 °C against 15.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Santa Rosa is 18.1 °C warmer (Guiyang 5.3 °C, Santa Rosa 23.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guiyang 26.7 °C (August), Santa Rosa 29.7 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Guiyang 1.5 °C, Santa Rosa 3.9 °C.
  • Guiyang gets 44% more rain and snow a year: 1,065 mm against 742 mm.
  • Santa Rosa gets about 1,314 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,621 h against 1,307 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Guiyang 8.2 cm, Santa Rosa 0.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
