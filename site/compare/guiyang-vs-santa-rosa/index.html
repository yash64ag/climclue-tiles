How the climate of Guiyang, China compares with Santa Rosa, La Pampa, Argentina: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Guiyang is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 15.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Santa Rosa, La Pampa is 18.1 °C warmer (Guiyang 5.5 °C, Santa Rosa, La Pampa 23.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guiyang 28.3 °C (August), Santa Rosa, La Pampa 31.0 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Guiyang 2.5 °C, Santa Rosa, La Pampa 1.7 °C.
  • Guiyang gets 37% more rain and snow a year: 1,011 mm against 740 mm.
  • Santa Rosa, La Pampa gets about 1,314 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,621 h against 1,307 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Guiyang 4.4 cm, Santa Rosa, La Pampa 0.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
