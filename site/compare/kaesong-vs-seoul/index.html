Kaesŏng vs Seoul climate

How the climate of Kaesŏng, North Korea compares with Seoul, South Korea, 58 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Seoul is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 12.0 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Seoul is 0.7 °C warmer (Kaesŏng -4.0 °C, Seoul -3.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kaesŏng 28.3 °C (August), Seoul 28.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kaesŏng -9.7 °C, Seoul -9.0 °C.
  • Seoul gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,405 mm against 1,377 mm.
  • Kaesŏng gets about 55 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,375 h against 2,320 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kaesŏng 35 cm, Seoul 33 cm (ERA5-Land).
