How the climate of Foshan, China compares with Zhaoqing, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Foshan is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 22.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Foshan is 0.6 °C warmer (Foshan 15.9 °C, Zhaoqing 15.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Foshan 33.2 °C (July), Zhaoqing 33.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Foshan 10.8 °C, Zhaoqing 10.0 °C.
  • Foshan gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,981 mm against 1,703 mm.
  • Zhaoqing gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,814 h against 1,807 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Foshan in 12 of 12 months
