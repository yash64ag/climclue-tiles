Xuhui vs Yangpu climate

How the climate of Xuhui, China compares with Yangpu, China, 10 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Xuhui is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.7 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Xuhui is 0.3 °C warmer (Xuhui 28.3 °C, Yangpu 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Xuhui 31.2 °C (July), Yangpu 30.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Xuhui 0.7 °C, Yangpu 1.5 °C.
  • Xuhui gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,451 mm against 1,431 mm.
  • Yangpu gets about 13 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,973 h against 1,960 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Xuhui 7.4 cm, Yangpu 6.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
