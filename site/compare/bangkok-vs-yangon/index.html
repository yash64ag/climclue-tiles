Bangkok vs Yangon climate

How the climate of Bangkok, Thailand compares with Yangon, Myanmar, 576 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bangkok is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 28.5 °C against 27.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Bangkok is 2.1 °C warmer (Bangkok 26.4 °C, Yangon 24.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bangkok 34.8 °C (April), Yangon 37.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Bangkok 20.8 °C, Yangon 17.7 °C.
  • Yangon gets 85% more rain and snow a year: 2,761 mm against 1,490 mm.
  • Yangon gets about 33 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,574 h against 2,541 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Bangkok in 11 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Bangkok in 7 of 12 months
