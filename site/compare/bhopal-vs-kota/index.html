Bhopal vs Kota climate

How the climate of Bhopal, India compares with Kota, India, 267 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kota is 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 25.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Kota is 3.4 °C warmer (Bhopal 31.0 °C, Kota 34.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bhopal 41.2 °C (May), Kota 42.5 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Bhopal 10.4 °C, Kota 9.7 °C.
  • Bhopal gets 62% more rain and snow a year: 1,214 mm against 751 mm.
  • Kota gets about 69 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,867 h against 2,798 h; about 1961–1990).
