Gwalior vs Nepalgunj climate

How the climate of Gwalior, India compares with Nepalgunj, Nepal, 396 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gwalior is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 25.7 °C against 24.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Gwalior is 3.5 °C warmer (Gwalior 34.2 °C, Nepalgunj 30.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gwalior 41.9 °C (May), Nepalgunj 38.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Gwalior 7.4 °C, Nepalgunj 7.2 °C.
  • Nepalgunj gets 93% more rain and snow a year: 1,606 mm against 833 mm.
  • Gwalior gets about 242 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,737 h against 2,496 h; about 1961–1990).
