How the climate of Delhi, India compares with Ghāziābād, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Delhi is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 24.8 °C against 24.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Delhi is 0.2 °C warmer (Delhi 25.3 °C, Ghāziābād 25.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Delhi 39.6 °C (May), Ghāziābād 39.6 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Delhi 6.6 °C, Ghāziābād 6.6 °C.
  • Ghāziābād gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 692 mm against 646 mm.
  • Delhi gets about 6 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,679 h against 2,674 h; about 1961–1990).
