Karaj vs Malatya climate

How the climate of Karaj, Iran compares with Malatya, Turkey, 1,158 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Karaj is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 14.4 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Karaj is 1.8 °C warmer (Karaj 25.6 °C, Malatya 23.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Karaj 35.3 °C (July), Malatya 34.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Karaj -4.8 °C, Malatya -2.3 °C.
  • Karaj gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 478 mm against 448 mm.
  • Malatya gets about 245 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,019 h against 2,774 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Karaj 78 cm, Malatya 100 cm (ERA5-Land).
