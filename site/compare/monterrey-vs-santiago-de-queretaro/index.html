Monterrey vs Santiago de Querétaro climate

How the climate of Monterrey, Mexico compares with Santiago de Querétaro, Mexico, 567 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Monterrey is 2.5 °C (4.6 °F) warmer over the year: 21.7 °C against 19.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Monterrey is 7.2 °C warmer (Monterrey 27.6 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 20.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Monterrey 33.6 °C (August), Santiago de Querétaro 30.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Monterrey 8.3 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 7.1 °C.
  • Monterrey gets 37% more rain and snow a year: 627 mm against 456 mm.
  • Santiago de Querétaro gets about 661 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,547 h against 1,887 h; about 1961–1990).
