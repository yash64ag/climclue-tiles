How the climate of Monterrey, Mexico compares with Santiago de Querétaro, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Monterrey is 3.5 °C (6.3 °F) warmer over the year: 22.0 °C against 18.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Monterrey is 7.9 °C warmer (Monterrey 28.1 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 20.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Monterrey 34.7 °C (August), Santiago de Querétaro 30.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Monterrey 7.5 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 5.8 °C.
  • Monterrey gets 37% more rain and snow a year: 627 mm against 456 mm.
  • Santiago de Querétaro gets about 661 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,547 h against 1,887 h; about 1961–1990).
