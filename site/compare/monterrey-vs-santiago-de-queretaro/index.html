Monterrey vs Santiago de Querétaro climate

How the climate of Monterrey, Mexico compares with Santiago de Querétaro, Mexico, 567 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Monterrey is 2.6 °C (4.7 °F) warmer over the year: 20.5 °C against 17.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Monterrey is 6.8 °C warmer (Monterrey 25.9 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 19.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Monterrey 30.8 °C (August), Santiago de Querétaro 28.1 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Monterrey 8.7 °C, Santiago de Querétaro 7.1 °C.
  • Monterrey gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 688 mm against 549 mm.
  • Santiago de Querétaro gets about 661 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,547 h against 1,887 h; about 1961–1990).

  • Drier: Santiago de Querétaro in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Santiago de Querétaro in 11 of 12 months
