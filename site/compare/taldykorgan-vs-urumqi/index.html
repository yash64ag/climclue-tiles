Taldykorgan vs Ürümqi climate

How the climate of Taldykorgan, Kazakhstan compares with Ürümqi, China, 745 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Taldykorgan is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 8.0 °C against 7.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Taldykorgan is 2.0 °C warmer (Taldykorgan -9.2 °C, Ürümqi -11.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Taldykorgan 29.3 °C (July), Ürümqi 29.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Taldykorgan -13.0 °C, Ürümqi -13.9 °C.
  • Taldykorgan gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 439 mm against 317 mm.
  • Ürümqi gets about 106 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,777 h against 2,671 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Taldykorgan 123 cm, Ürümqi 103 cm (ERA5-Land).
