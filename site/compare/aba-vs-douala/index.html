Aba vs Douala climate

How the climate of Aba, Nigeria compares with Douala, Cameroon, 285 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Douala is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 25.9 °C against 25.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Douala is 0.7 °C warmer (Aba 24.9 °C, Douala 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aba 33.2 °C (February), Douala 30.6 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Aba 21.7 °C, Douala 22.7 °C.
  • Douala gets 32% more rain and snow a year: 3,195 mm against 2,429 mm.
  • Douala gets about 206 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,704 h against 1,498 h; about 1961–1990).
