How the climate of Aba, Nigeria compares with Douala, Cameroon: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Douala is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 25.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Douala is 0.7 °C warmer (Aba 24.9 °C, Douala 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aba 32.8 °C (February), Douala 31.6 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Aba 20.8 °C, Douala 21.9 °C.
  • Douala gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 3,194 mm against 2,485 mm.
  • Douala gets about 206 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,704 h against 1,498 h; about 1961–1990).
