Gyeongsan-si vs Lianyungang climate

How the climate of Gyeongsan-si, South Korea compares with Lianyungang, China, 875 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lianyungang is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 14.4 °C against 13.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Lianyungang is 1.2 °C warmer (Gyeongsan-si 22.8 °C, Lianyungang 24.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gyeongsan-si 30.7 °C (August), Lianyungang 30.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gyeongsan-si -5.1 °C, Lianyungang -3.7 °C.
  • Gyeongsan-si gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 1,120 mm against 878 mm.
  • Gyeongsan-si gets about 151 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,452 h against 2,300 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gyeongsan-si 12 cm, Lianyungang 9.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
