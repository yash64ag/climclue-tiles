Guadalajara vs León de los Aldama climate

How the climate of Guadalajara, Mexico compares with León de los Aldama, Mexico, 180 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Guadalajara is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 20.5 °C against 19.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Guadalajara is 1.3 °C warmer (Guadalajara 16.8 °C, León de los Aldama 15.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guadalajara 33.4 °C (May), León de los Aldama 32.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Guadalajara 8.1 °C, León de los Aldama 6.8 °C.
  • Guadalajara gets 37% more rain and snow a year: 864 mm against 631 mm.
  • Guadalajara gets about 357 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,638 h against 2,282 h; about 1961–1990).
