Guadalajara vs León de los Aldama climate

How the climate of Guadalajara, Mexico compares with León de los Aldama, Mexico, 180 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Guadalajara is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 20.3 °C against 19.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Guadalajara is 1.5 °C warmer (Guadalajara 16.6 °C, León de los Aldama 15.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guadalajara 31.9 °C (May), León de los Aldama 29.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Guadalajara 9.5 °C, León de los Aldama 8.1 °C.
  • Guadalajara gets 42% more rain and snow a year: 875 mm against 616 mm.
  • Guadalajara gets about 357 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,638 h against 2,282 h; about 1961–1990).
