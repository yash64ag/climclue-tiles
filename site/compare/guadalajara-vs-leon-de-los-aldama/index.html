How the climate of Guadalajara, Mexico compares with León de los Aldama, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Guadalajara is 1.6 °C (2.8 °F) warmer over the year: 20.6 °C against 19.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Guadalajara is 1.8 °C warmer (Guadalajara 18.7 °C, León de los Aldama 16.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guadalajara 33.2 °C (May), León de los Aldama 32.4 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Guadalajara 8.1 °C, León de los Aldama 5.1 °C.
  • Guadalajara gets 37% more rain and snow a year: 864 mm against 631 mm.
  • Guadalajara gets about 357 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,638 h against 2,282 h; about 1961–1990).
