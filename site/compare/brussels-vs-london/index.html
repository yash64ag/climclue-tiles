How the climate of Brussels, Belgium compares with London, United Kingdom: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Brussels and London have the same yearly average temperature (10.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when London is 1.5 °C warmer (Brussels 3.4 °C, London 4.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brussels 23.2 °C (July), London 22.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Brussels 0.7 °C, London 2.0 °C.
  • Brussels gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 851 mm against 661 mm.
  • Brussels gets about 128 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,587 h against 1,459 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Brussels 15 cm, London 5.3 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: London in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Brussels in 11 of 12 months
