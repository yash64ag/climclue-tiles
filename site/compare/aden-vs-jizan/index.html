Aden vs Jizan climate

How the climate of Aden, Yemen compares with Jizan, Saudi Arabia, 529 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jizan is 2.1 °C (3.7 °F) warmer over the year: 29.5 °C against 27.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Jizan is 2.7 °C warmer (Aden 27.9 °C, Jizan 30.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aden 31.8 °C (July), Jizan 34.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Aden 23.2 °C, Jizan 23.8 °C.
  • Jizan gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 117 mm against 84 mm.
  • Aden gets about 111 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,132 h against 3,022 h; about 1961–1990).
