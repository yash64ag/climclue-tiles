How the climate of Aden, Yemen compares with Jizan, Saudi Arabia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jizan is 1.8 °C (3.2 °F) warmer over the year: 29.2 °C against 27.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Jizan is 2.5 °C warmer (Aden 28.0 °C, Jizan 30.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aden 34.5 °C (June), Jizan 36.9 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Aden 21.0 °C, Jizan 20.8 °C.
  • Jizan gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 114 mm against 85 mm.
  • Aden gets about 111 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,132 h against 3,022 h; about 1961–1990).
