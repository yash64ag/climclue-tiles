How the climate of Havana, Cuba compares with Playa del Carmen, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Havana and Playa del Carmen have the same yearly average temperature (25.4 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Havana is 0.6 °C warmer (Havana 28.0 °C, Playa del Carmen 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Havana 32.4 °C (July), Playa del Carmen 32.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Havana 17.8 °C, Playa del Carmen 17.2 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,368 mm and 1,363 mm).
  • Havana gets about 459 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,950 h against 2,491 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Playa del Carmen in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Havana in 12 of 12 months
