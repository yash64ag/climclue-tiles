Zaozhuang vs Zoucheng climate

How the climate of Zaozhuang, China compares with Zoucheng, China, 80 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zoucheng is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 14.2 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Zoucheng is 0.6 °C warmer (Zaozhuang 24.6 °C, Zoucheng 25.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Zaozhuang 30.7 °C (July), Zoucheng 31.2 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Zaozhuang -4.9 °C, Zoucheng -5.6 °C.
  • Zaozhuang gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 738 mm against 729 mm.
  • Zoucheng gets about 69 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,406 h against 2,337 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Zaozhuang 13 cm, Zoucheng 9.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
