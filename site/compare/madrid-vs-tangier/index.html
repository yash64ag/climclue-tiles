How the climate of Madrid, Spain compares with Tangier, Morocco: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tangier is 3.4 °C (6.1 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 14.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Tangier is 8.0 °C warmer (Madrid 6.2 °C, Tangier 14.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Madrid 33.7 °C (July), Tangier 28.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Madrid 0.9 °C, Tangier 9.6 °C.
  • Tangier gets 64% more rain and snow a year: 655 mm against 400 mm.
  • Tangier gets about 226 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,823 h against 2,597 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Madrid 4.3 cm, Tangier 0 cm (ERA5-Land).
