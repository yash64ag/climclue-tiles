How the climate of Yongzhou, China compares with Zhuzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yongzhou is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 18.9 °C against 18.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Yongzhou is 0.7 °C warmer (Yongzhou 9.1 °C, Zhuzhou 8.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Yongzhou 34.0 °C (July), Zhuzhou 34.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Yongzhou 4.0 °C, Zhuzhou 2.9 °C.
  • Zhuzhou gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,637 mm against 1,454 mm.
  • Zhuzhou gets about 26 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,605 h against 1,580 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Yongzhou 6.2 cm, Zhuzhou 6.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
