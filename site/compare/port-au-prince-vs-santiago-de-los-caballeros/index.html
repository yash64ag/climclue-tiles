Port-au-Prince vs Santiago de los Caballeros climate

How the climate of Port-au-Prince, Haiti compares with Santiago de los Caballeros, Dominican Republic, 200 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Port-au-Prince is 1.9 °C (3.5 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 25.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Port-au-Prince is 2.8 °C warmer (Port-au-Prince 25.9 °C, Santiago de los Caballeros 23.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Port-au-Prince 34.2 °C (July), Santiago de los Caballeros 32.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Port-au-Prince 20.0 °C, Santiago de los Caballeros 17.4 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,242 mm and 1,242 mm).
  • Port-au-Prince gets about 306 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,795 h against 2,489 h; about 1961–1990).
