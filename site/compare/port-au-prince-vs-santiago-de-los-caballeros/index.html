Port-au-Prince vs Santiago de los Caballeros climate

How the climate of Port-au-Prince, Haiti compares with Santiago de los Caballeros, Dominican Republic, 200 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Port-au-Prince is 0.9 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 25.9 °C against 24.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Port-au-Prince is 1.7 °C warmer (Port-au-Prince 24.6 °C, Santiago de los Caballeros 22.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Port-au-Prince 31.5 °C (July), Santiago de los Caballeros 32.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Port-au-Prince 19.9 °C, Santiago de los Caballeros 18.7 °C.
  • Port-au-Prince gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,402 mm against 1,304 mm.
  • Port-au-Prince gets about 306 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,795 h against 2,489 h; about 1961–1990).
