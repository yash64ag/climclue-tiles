How the climate of Bhopal, India compares with Indore, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bhopal and Indore have the same yearly average temperature (25.4 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Bhopal is 1.2 °C warmer (Bhopal 31.0 °C, Indore 29.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bhopal 41.2 °C (May), Indore 40.6 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Bhopal 10.4 °C, Indore 10.4 °C.
  • Bhopal gets 31% more rain and snow a year: 1,214 mm against 926 mm.
  • Indore gets about 45 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,843 h against 2,798 h; about 1961–1990).
