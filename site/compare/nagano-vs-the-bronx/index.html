Nagano vs The Bronx climate

How the climate of Nagano, Japan compares with The Bronx, United States, 10,799 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • The Bronx is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 12.1 °C against 11.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when The Bronx is 2.1 °C warmer (Nagano 19.4 °C, The Bronx 21.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nagano 28.0 °C (August), The Bronx 28.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nagano -6.3 °C, The Bronx -4.2 °C.
  • Nagano gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 1,391 mm against 1,213 mm.
  • The Bronx gets about 716 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,519 h against 1,803 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nagano 466 cm, The Bronx 90 cm (ERA5-Land).
