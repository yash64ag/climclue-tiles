Nagano vs The Bronx climate

How the climate of Nagano, Japan compares with The Bronx, United States, 10,799 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Nagano is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 12.4 °C against 12.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Nagano is 1.7 °C warmer (Nagano 25.3 °C, The Bronx 23.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nagano 29.1 °C (August), The Bronx 28.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nagano -5.3 °C, The Bronx -4.3 °C.
  • Nagano gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,237 mm against 1,216 mm.
  • The Bronx gets about 716 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,519 h against 1,803 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nagano 194 cm, The Bronx 83 cm (ERA5-Land).
