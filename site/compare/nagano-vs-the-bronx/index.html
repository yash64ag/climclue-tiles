How the climate of Nagano, Japan compares with The Bronx, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Nagano and The Bronx have the same yearly average temperature (12.4 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Nagano is 1.3 °C warmer (Nagano 25.3 °C, The Bronx 23.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nagano 30.0 °C (August), The Bronx 29.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nagano -4.7 °C, The Bronx -4.2 °C.
  • Nagano gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,237 mm against 1,216 mm.
  • The Bronx gets about 716 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,519 h against 1,803 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nagano 194 cm, The Bronx 61 cm (ERA5-Land).
