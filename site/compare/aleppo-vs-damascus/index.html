Aleppo vs Damascus climate

How the climate of Aleppo, Syria compares with Damascus, Syria, 310 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Aleppo is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 17.8 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Aleppo is 2.4 °C warmer (Aleppo 28.6 °C, Damascus 26.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aleppo 35.8 °C (July), Damascus 33.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Aleppo 2.1 °C, Damascus 1.3 °C.
  • Aleppo gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 400 mm against 327 mm.
  • Damascus gets about 77 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,075 h against 2,999 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Aleppo 5.2 cm, Damascus 20 cm (ERA5-Land).
