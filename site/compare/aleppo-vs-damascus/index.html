How the climate of Aleppo, Syria compares with Damascus, Syria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Aleppo is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 18.1 °C against 17.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Aleppo is 1.5 °C warmer (Aleppo 28.8 °C, Damascus 27.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aleppo 35.9 °C (July), Damascus 35.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Aleppo 2.8 °C, Damascus 2.0 °C.
  • Aleppo gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 366 mm against 283 mm.
  • Damascus gets about 77 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,075 h against 2,999 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Aleppo 2.4 cm, Damascus 13 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Drier: Damascus in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Damascus in 8 of 12 months
