Sham Shui Po vs Tainan climate

How the climate of Sham Shui Po, Hong Kong compares with Tainan, Taiwan, 625 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tainan is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 24.0 °C against 23.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Tainan is 2.0 °C warmer (Sham Shui Po 15.9 °C, Tainan 18.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Sham Shui Po 29.8 °C (July), Tainan 30.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Sham Shui Po 12.9 °C, Tainan 14.9 °C.
  • Sham Shui Po gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 2,067 mm against 1,795 mm.
  • Tainan gets about 30 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,992 h against 1,963 h; about 1961–1990).
