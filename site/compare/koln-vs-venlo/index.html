Köln vs Venlo climate

How the climate of Köln, Germany compares with Venlo, The Netherlands, 73 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Köln and Venlo have the same yearly average temperature (10.5 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Köln is 0.3 °C warmer (Köln 18.6 °C, Venlo 18.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Köln 23.9 °C (July), Venlo 23.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Köln 0.1 °C, Venlo 0.2 °C.
  • Köln gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 806 mm against 802 mm.
  • Köln gets about 75 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,455 h against 1,380 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Köln 23 cm, Venlo 19 cm (ERA5-Land).
