Cali vs Njeru climate

How the climate of Cali, Colombia compares with Njeru, Uganda, 12,191 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Cali is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 22.2 °C against 22.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Cali is 1.1 °C warmer (Cali 22.3 °C, Njeru 21.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cali 28.2 °C (August), Njeru 29.8 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Cali 16.5 °C, Njeru 15.5 °C.
  • Njeru gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,423 mm against 1,251 mm.
  • Njeru gets about 864 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,575 h against 1,711 h; about 1961–1990).
