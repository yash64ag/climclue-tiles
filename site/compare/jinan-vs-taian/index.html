How the climate of Jinan, China compares with Tai’an, Shandong, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jinan is 2.7 °C (4.9 °F) warmer over the year: 16.0 °C against 13.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Jinan is 3.3 °C warmer (Jinan 17.4 °C, Tai’an, Shandong 14.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinan 33.0 °C (July), Tai’an, Shandong 30.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinan -3.5 °C, Tai’an, Shandong -6.2 °C.
  • Tai’an, Shandong gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 809 mm against 657 mm.
  • Jinan gets about 58 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,554 h against 2,496 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinan 10 cm, Tai’an, Shandong 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
