Nanjing vs Yangzhou, Jiangsu climate

How the climate of Nanjing, China compares with Yangzhou, Jiangsu, China, 72 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Nanjing is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 16.0 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Nanjing is 0.5 °C warmer (Nanjing 15.7 °C, Yangzhou, Jiangsu 15.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nanjing 31.9 °C (July), Yangzhou, Jiangsu 31.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nanjing -1.4 °C, Yangzhou, Jiangsu -1.6 °C.
  • Nanjing gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,180 mm against 1,098 mm.
  • Yangzhou, Jiangsu gets about 19 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,074 h against 2,055 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nanjing 20 cm, Yangzhou, Jiangsu 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
