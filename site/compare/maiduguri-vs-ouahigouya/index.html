Maiduguri vs Ouahigouya climate

How the climate of Maiduguri, Nigeria compares with Ouahigouya, Burkina Faso, 1,700 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ouahigouya is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 28.3 °C against 27.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Ouahigouya is 1.2 °C warmer (Maiduguri 25.1 °C, Ouahigouya 26.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maiduguri 39.9 °C (April), Ouahigouya 39.8 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Maiduguri 14.2 °C, Ouahigouya 15.8 °C.
  • Ouahigouya gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 642 mm against 574 mm.
  • Ouahigouya gets about 21 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,023 h against 3,002 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Maiduguri in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Maiduguri in 7 of 12 months
