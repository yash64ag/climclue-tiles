How the climate of Maiduguri, Nigeria compares with Ouahigouya, Burkina Faso: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ouahigouya is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 28.4 °C against 27.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Ouahigouya is 1.2 °C warmer (Maiduguri 25.1 °C, Ouahigouya 26.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maiduguri 40.4 °C (April), Ouahigouya 39.8 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Maiduguri 13.1 °C, Ouahigouya 15.6 °C.
  • Ouahigouya gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 620 mm against 576 mm.
  • Ouahigouya gets about 21 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,023 h against 3,002 h; about 1961–1990).
