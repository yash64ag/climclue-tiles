Breda vs Brussels climate

How the climate of Breda, The Netherlands compares with Brussels, Belgium, 87 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Brussels is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 10.6 °C against 10.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Brussels is 0.3 °C warmer (Breda 6.5 °C, Brussels 6.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Breda 22.9 °C (July), Brussels 23.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Breda 0.4 °C, Brussels 0.7 °C.
  • Breda gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 882 mm against 851 mm.
  • Brussels gets about 123 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,587 h against 1,464 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Breda 14 cm, Brussels 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
