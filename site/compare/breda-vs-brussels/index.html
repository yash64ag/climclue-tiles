Breda vs Brussels climate

How the climate of Breda, The Netherlands compares with Brussels, Belgium, 87 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Breda and Brussels have the same yearly average temperature (10.4 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Breda is 0.1 °C warmer (Breda 7.0 °C, Brussels 6.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Breda 22.2 °C (July), Brussels 22.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Breda 0.7 °C, Brussels 0.6 °C.
  • Breda gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 877 mm against 863 mm.
  • Brussels gets about 123 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,587 h against 1,464 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Breda 14 cm, Brussels 15 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Brussels in 3 of 12 months (7 equal)
  • Drier: Breda in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Brussels in 12 of 12 months
