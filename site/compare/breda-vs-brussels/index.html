How the climate of Breda, The Netherlands compares with Brussels, Belgium: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Brussels is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 10.8 °C against 10.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Brussels is 0.8 °C warmer (Breda 3.6 °C, Brussels 4.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Breda 23.1 °C (July), Brussels 23.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Breda 0.1 °C, Brussels 0.6 °C.
  • Breda gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 882 mm against 851 mm.
  • Brussels gets about 123 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,587 h against 1,464 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Breda 14 cm, Brussels 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
