How the climate of Kalyān, India compares with Virār, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kalyān is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Kalyān is 0.8 °C warmer (Kalyān 29.1 °C, Virār 28.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kalyān 34.7 °C (April), Virār 33.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Kalyān 15.9 °C, Virār 16.0 °C.
  • Virār gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 2,434 mm against 2,234 mm.
  • Kalyān gets about 3 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,706 h against 2,703 h; about 1961–1990).
