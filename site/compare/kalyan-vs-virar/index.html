Kalyān vs Virār climate

How the climate of Kalyān, India compares with Virār, India, 41 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kalyān is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Kalyān is 0.8 °C warmer (Kalyān 29.1 °C, Virār 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kalyān 35.8 °C (April), Virār 33.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Kalyān 16.7 °C, Virār 18.1 °C.
  • Virār gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 2,454 mm against 2,265 mm.
  • Kalyān gets about 3 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,706 h against 2,703 h; about 1961–1990).
