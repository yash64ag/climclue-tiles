How the climate of Arequipa, Peru compares with La Paz, Bolivia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Arequipa is 6.9 °C (12.3 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 8.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Arequipa is 8.2 °C warmer (Arequipa 14.1 °C, La Paz 5.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Arequipa 24.7 °C (October), La Paz 16.6 °C (November). Coldest-month night lows: Arequipa 5.5 °C, La Paz -1.6 °C.
  • La Paz gets 534% more rain and snow a year: 1,064 mm against 168 mm.
  • Arequipa gets about 829 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,178 h against 2,349 h; about 1961–1990).
