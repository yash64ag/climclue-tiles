Arequipa vs La Paz climate

How the climate of Arequipa, Peru compares with La Paz, Bolivia, 361 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Arequipa is 7.7 °C (13.9 °F) warmer over the year: 14.0 °C against 6.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Arequipa is 9.1 °C warmer (Arequipa 12.8 °C, La Paz 3.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Arequipa 20.8 °C (October), La Paz 12.7 °C (November). Coldest-month night lows: Arequipa 6.6 °C, La Paz -2.9 °C.
  • La Paz gets 551% more rain and snow a year: 1,356 mm against 208 mm.
  • Arequipa gets about 829 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,178 h against 2,349 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Arequipa 0 cm, La Paz 20 cm (ERA5-Land).
