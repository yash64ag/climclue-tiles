Xinxiang vs Zhengzhou climate

How the climate of Xinxiang, China compares with Zhengzhou, China, 50 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhengzhou is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 15.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Zhengzhou is 0.6 °C warmer (Xinxiang 2.1 °C, Zhengzhou 2.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Xinxiang 31.8 °C (June), Zhengzhou 31.6 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Xinxiang -4.5 °C, Zhengzhou -4.0 °C.
  • Zhengzhou gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 632 mm against 617 mm.
  • Xinxiang gets about 46 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,303 h against 2,257 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Xinxiang 23 cm, Zhengzhou 34 cm (ERA5-Land).
