How the climate of Mumbai, India compares with Thāne, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Thāne is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.7 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Thāne is 0.8 °C warmer (Mumbai 29.0 °C, Thāne 29.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mumbai 32.7 °C (May), Thāne 33.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Mumbai 17.0 °C, Thāne 16.0 °C.
  • Mumbai gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 2,712 mm against 2,597 mm.
  • Thāne gets about 8 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,675 h against 2,667 h; about 1961–1990).
