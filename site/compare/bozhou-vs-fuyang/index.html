Bozhou vs Fuyang, Anhui climate

How the climate of Bozhou, China compares with Fuyang, Anhui, China, 109 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Fuyang, Anhui is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 15.7 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Fuyang, Anhui is 1.0 °C warmer (Bozhou 3.1 °C, Fuyang, Anhui 4.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bozhou 31.8 °C (July), Fuyang, Anhui 31.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bozhou -3.6 °C, Fuyang, Anhui -2.5 °C.
  • Fuyang, Anhui gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 1,013 mm against 811 mm.
  • Bozhou gets about 85 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,220 h against 2,134 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bozhou 17 cm, Fuyang, Anhui 23 cm (ERA5-Land).
