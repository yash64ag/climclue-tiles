Manhattan vs Nagano climate

How the climate of Manhattan, United States compares with Nagano, Japan, 10,802 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Manhattan is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 12.2 °C against 11.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Manhattan is 2.2 °C warmer (Manhattan 21.6 °C, Nagano 19.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manhattan 28.2 °C (July), Nagano 28.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Manhattan -3.9 °C, Nagano -6.3 °C.
  • Nagano gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 1,391 mm against 1,212 mm.
  • Manhattan gets about 726 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,529 h against 1,803 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Manhattan 81 cm, Nagano 194 cm (ERA5-Land).
