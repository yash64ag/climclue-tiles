Manhattan, New York vs Nagano climate

How the climate of Manhattan, New York, United States compares with Nagano, Japan, 10,802 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Nagano is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 12.4 °C against 12.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Nagano is 1.7 °C warmer (Manhattan, New York 23.6 °C, Nagano 25.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manhattan, New York 29.0 °C (July), Nagano 30.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Manhattan, New York -4.0 °C, Nagano -4.7 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,232 mm and 1,237 mm).
  • Manhattan, New York gets about 726 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,529 h against 1,803 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Manhattan, New York 81 cm, Nagano 194 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Manhattan, New York in 12 of 12 months
