How the climate of El Eulma, Algeria compares with Madrid, Spain: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • El Eulma is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 15.1 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when El Eulma is 1.3 °C warmer (El Eulma 16.3 °C, Madrid 15.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: El Eulma 33.0 °C (July), Madrid 32.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: El Eulma 1.8 °C, Madrid 1.5 °C.
  • El Eulma gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 420 mm against 400 mm.
  • El Eulma gets about 281 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,878 h against 2,597 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: El Eulma 12 cm, Madrid 4.3 cm (ERA5-Land).
