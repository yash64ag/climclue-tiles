How the climate of Ḩawallī, Kuwait compares with Riyadh, Saudi Arabia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Riyadh is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 26.5 °C against 25.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Riyadh is 2.5 °C warmer (Ḩawallī 18.6 °C, Riyadh 21.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ḩawallī 44.5 °C (July), Riyadh 43.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ḩawallī 8.0 °C, Riyadh 8.5 °C.
  • Ḩawallī gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 111 mm against 103 mm.
  • Riyadh gets about 222 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,360 h against 3,138 h; about 1961–1990).
