Caracas vs Maracaibo climate

How the climate of Caracas, Venezuela compares with Maracaibo, Venezuela, 518 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Maracaibo is 6.9 °C (12.5 °F) warmer over the year: 28.5 °C against 21.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Maracaibo is 7.7 °C warmer (Caracas 20.6 °C, Maracaibo 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Caracas 26.8 °C (April), Maracaibo 33.8 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Caracas 16.8 °C, Maracaibo 23.9 °C.
  • Caracas gets 100% more rain and snow a year: 1,347 mm against 673 mm.
  • Maracaibo gets about 195 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,580 h against 2,385 h; about 1961–1990).
