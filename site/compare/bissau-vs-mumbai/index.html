Bissau vs Mumbai climate

How the climate of Bissau, Guinea-Bissau compares with Mumbai, India, 9,423 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bissau is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 27.1 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Bissau is 2.2 °C warmer (Bissau 26.6 °C, Mumbai 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bissau 35.2 °C (March), Mumbai 32.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Bissau 17.9 °C, Mumbai 17.0 °C.
  • Mumbai gets 69% more rain and snow a year: 2,712 mm against 1,606 mm.
  • Bissau gets about 8 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,675 h against 2,667 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Mumbai in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Bissau in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Mumbai in 8 of 12 months
