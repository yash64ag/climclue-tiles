How the climate of Erbil, Iraq compares with Mosul, Iraq: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Mosul is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 21.0 °C against 20.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Mosul is 1.1 °C warmer (Erbil 30.8 °C, Mosul 31.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Erbil 41.9 °C (July), Mosul 44.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Erbil 2.2 °C, Mosul 2.3 °C.
  • Erbil gets 44% more rain and snow a year: 625 mm against 435 mm.
  • Mosul gets about 5 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,196 h against 3,190 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Erbil 4.6 cm, Mosul 1.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
