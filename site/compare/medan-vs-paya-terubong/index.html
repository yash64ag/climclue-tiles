Medan vs Paya Terubong climate

How the climate of Medan, Indonesia compares with Paya Terubong, Malaysia, 268 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Medan and Paya Terubong have the same yearly average temperature (26.5 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Paya Terubong is 0.2 °C warmer (Medan 26.4 °C, Paya Terubong 26.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Medan 32.2 °C (April), Paya Terubong 31.6 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Medan 21.1 °C, Paya Terubong 21.5 °C.
  • Paya Terubong gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 3,200 mm against 2,911 mm.
  • Paya Terubong gets about 265 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,407 h against 2,142 h; about 1961–1990).
