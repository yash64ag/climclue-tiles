Istanbul vs İzmir climate

How the climate of Istanbul, Turkey compares with İzmir, Turkey, 328 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • İzmir is 2.8 °C (5.0 °F) warmer over the year: 17.5 °C against 14.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when İzmir is 4.5 °C warmer (Istanbul 23.8 °C, İzmir 28.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Istanbul 26.5 °C (August), İzmir 34.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Istanbul 3.9 °C, İzmir 3.2 °C.
  • Istanbul gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 813 mm against 721 mm.
  • İzmir gets about 366 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,128 h against 2,763 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Istanbul 28 cm, İzmir 9.0 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: İzmir in 12 of 12 months
