How the climate of Fortaleza, Brazil compares with Jolo, Philippines: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jolo is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Fortaleza is 0.8 °C warmer (Fortaleza 27.1 °C, Jolo 26.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fortaleza 30.4 °C (February), Jolo 31.5 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Fortaleza 22.4 °C, Jolo 21.4 °C.
  • Fortaleza gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,353 mm against 1,227 mm.
  • Fortaleza gets about 409 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,643 h against 2,234 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
