How the climate of Haikou, Hainan, China compares with Zhanjiang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Haikou, Hainan is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 24.9 °C against 23.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Haikou, Hainan is 2.0 °C warmer (Haikou, Hainan 19.7 °C, Zhanjiang 17.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Haikou, Hainan 33.0 °C (July), Zhanjiang 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Haikou, Hainan 15.8 °C, Zhanjiang 13.3 °C.
  • Zhanjiang gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,982 mm against 1,800 mm.
  • Haikou, Hainan gets about 172 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,092 h against 1,920 h; about 1961–1990).
