Havana vs Jacksonville, Florida climate

How the climate of Havana, Cuba compares with Jacksonville, Florida, United States, 804 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Havana is 5.1 °C (9.2 °F) warmer over the year: 25.4 °C against 20.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Havana is 10.0 °C warmer (Havana 22.1 °C, Jacksonville, Florida 12.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Havana 32.4 °C (July), Jacksonville, Florida 32.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Havana 17.8 °C, Jacksonville, Florida 6.0 °C.
  • Jacksonville, Florida gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,459 mm against 1,368 mm.
  • Havana gets about 104 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,950 h against 2,845 h; about 1961–1990).
