How the climate of Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia compares with Medan, Indonesia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kuala Lumpur is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.7 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Kuala Lumpur is 0.3 °C warmer (Kuala Lumpur 26.7 °C, Medan 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kuala Lumpur 32.0 °C (March), Medan 32.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Kuala Lumpur 21.4 °C, Medan 21.1 °C.
  • Kuala Lumpur gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 2,940 mm against 2,911 mm.
  • Kuala Lumpur gets about 73 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,214 h against 2,142 h; about 1961–1990).
