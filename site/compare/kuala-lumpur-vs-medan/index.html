Kuala Lumpur vs Medan climate

How the climate of Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia compares with Medan, Indonesia, 339 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kuala Lumpur is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 26.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Kuala Lumpur is 0.4 °C warmer (Kuala Lumpur 26.6 °C, Medan 26.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kuala Lumpur 30.6 °C (March), Medan 30.7 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Kuala Lumpur 21.7 °C, Medan 21.3 °C.
  • Kuala Lumpur gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 2,938 mm against 2,910 mm.
  • Kuala Lumpur gets about 73 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,214 h against 2,142 h; about 1961–1990).
