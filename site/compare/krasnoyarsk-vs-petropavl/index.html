How the climate of Krasnoyarsk, Russia compares with Petropavl, Kazakhstan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Krasnoyarsk and Petropavl have the same yearly average temperature (2.3 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Krasnoyarsk is 2.6 °C warmer (Krasnoyarsk -5.1 °C, Petropavl -7.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Krasnoyarsk 25.4 °C (July), Petropavl 25.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Krasnoyarsk -19.7 °C, Petropavl -19.4 °C.
  • Krasnoyarsk gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 451 mm against 399 mm.
  • Petropavl gets about 333 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,162 h against 1,829 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Krasnoyarsk 122 cm, Petropavl 122 cm (ERA5-Land).
