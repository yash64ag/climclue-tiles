Krasnoyarsk vs Petropavl climate

How the climate of Krasnoyarsk, Russia compares with Petropavl, Kazakhstan, 1,497 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Petropavl is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 2.5 °C against 1.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Petropavl is 1.9 °C warmer (Krasnoyarsk -17.3 °C, Petropavl -15.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Krasnoyarsk 24.0 °C (July), Petropavl 23.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Krasnoyarsk -21.8 °C, Petropavl -17.8 °C.
  • Krasnoyarsk gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 461 mm against 391 mm.
  • Petropavl gets about 333 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,162 h against 1,829 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Krasnoyarsk 184 cm, Petropavl 148 cm (ERA5-Land).
