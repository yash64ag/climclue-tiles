Brazzaville vs Pointe-Noire climate

How the climate of Brazzaville, Republic of the Congo compares with Pointe-Noire, Republic of the Congo, 383 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Brazzaville is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 25.3 °C against 24.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Brazzaville is 2.6 °C warmer (Brazzaville 24.4 °C, Pointe-Noire 21.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brazzaville 30.9 °C (September), Pointe-Noire 28.8 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Brazzaville 19.2 °C, Pointe-Noire 19.6 °C.
  • Brazzaville gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,557 mm against 1,457 mm.
  • Brazzaville gets about 196 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,918 h against 1,722 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Pointe-Noire in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Brazzaville in 7 of 12 months
