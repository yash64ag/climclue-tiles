How the climate of Brazzaville, Republic of the Congo compares with Pointe-Noire, Republic of the Congo: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Brazzaville is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 25.5 °C against 24.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Brazzaville is 2.8 °C warmer (Brazzaville 24.6 °C, Pointe-Noire 21.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brazzaville 31.1 °C (April), Pointe-Noire 30.3 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Brazzaville 19.1 °C, Pointe-Noire 18.3 °C.
  • Brazzaville gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,561 mm against 1,343 mm.
  • Brazzaville gets about 196 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,918 h against 1,722 h; about 1961–1990).
