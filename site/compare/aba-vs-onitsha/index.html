Aba vs Onitsha climate

How the climate of Aba, Nigeria compares with Onitsha, Nigeria, 133 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Onitsha is 1.1 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.7 °C against 25.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Onitsha is 1.6 °C warmer (Aba 27.4 °C, Onitsha 28.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aba 33.1 °C (February), Onitsha 34.9 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Aba 21.8 °C, Onitsha 22.7 °C.
  • Aba gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 2,485 mm against 1,803 mm.
  • Onitsha gets about 279 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,776 h against 1,498 h; about 1961–1990).
