Abidjan vs Accra climate

How the climate of Abidjan, Ivory Coast compares with Accra, Ghana, 422 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Accra is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 26.3 °C against 25.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Accra is 0.9 °C warmer (Abidjan 26.1 °C, Accra 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abidjan 29.2 °C (March), Accra 29.7 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Abidjan 22.7 °C, Accra 22.9 °C.
  • Abidjan gets 125% more rain and snow a year: 1,908 mm against 847 mm.
  • Accra gets about 151 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,268 h against 2,118 h; about 1961–1990).
