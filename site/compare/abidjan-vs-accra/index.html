How the climate of Abidjan, Ivory Coast compares with Accra, Ghana: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Accra is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 26.2 °C against 25.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Accra is 0.8 °C warmer (Abidjan 26.2 °C, Accra 27.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abidjan 30.9 °C (March), Accra 31.7 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Abidjan 21.3 °C, Accra 21.0 °C.
  • Abidjan gets 124% more rain and snow a year: 1,884 mm against 840 mm.
  • Accra gets about 151 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,268 h against 2,118 h; about 1961–1990).
