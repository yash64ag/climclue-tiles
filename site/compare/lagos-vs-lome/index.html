Lagos vs Lomé climate

How the climate of Lagos, Nigeria compares with Lomé, Togo, 243 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lagos and Lomé have the same yearly average temperature (26.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Lagos is 0.3 °C warmer (Lagos 24.8 °C, Lomé 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lagos 30.0 °C (February), Lomé 30.4 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Lagos 23.7 °C, Lomé 23.1 °C.
  • Lagos gets 82% more rain and snow a year: 1,605 mm against 884 mm.
  • Lomé gets about 451 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,330 h against 1,879 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Lomé in 7 of 12 months (2 equal)
  • Drier: Lomé in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Lomé in 12 of 12 months
