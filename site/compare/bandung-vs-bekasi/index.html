Bandung vs Bekasi climate

How the climate of Bandung, Indonesia compares with Bekasi, Indonesia, 102 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bekasi is 3.1 °C (5.5 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 23.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Bekasi is 3.4 °C warmer (Bandung 23.4 °C, Bekasi 26.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bandung 28.2 °C (September), Bekasi 31.5 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Bandung 18.9 °C, Bekasi 22.5 °C.
  • Bekasi gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 2,659 mm against 2,391 mm.
  • Bekasi gets about 197 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,651 h against 2,454 h; about 1961–1990).
