How the climate of Bandung, Indonesia compares with Bekasi, Indonesia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bekasi is 3.4 °C (6.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 23.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Bekasi is 3.7 °C warmer (Bandung 23.5 °C, Bekasi 27.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bandung 29.3 °C (September), Bekasi 32.3 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Bandung 17.3 °C, Bekasi 21.7 °C.
  • Bekasi gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 2,606 mm against 2,449 mm.
  • Bekasi gets about 197 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,651 h against 2,454 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
