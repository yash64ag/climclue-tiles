Changde vs Changsha, Hunan climate

How the climate of Changde, China compares with Changsha, Hunan, China, 155 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Changsha, Hunan is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 17.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Changsha, Hunan is 0.8 °C warmer (Changde 29.1 °C, Changsha, Hunan 29.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changde 32.0 °C (July), Changsha, Hunan 32.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changde 1.2 °C, Changsha, Hunan 2.0 °C.
  • Changsha, Hunan gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,689 mm against 1,530 mm.
  • Changsha, Hunan gets about 17 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,589 h against 1,572 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changde 14 cm, Changsha, Hunan 9.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
