Istanbul vs Rome climate

How the climate of Istanbul, Turkey compares with Rome, Italy, 1,371 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rome is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Rome is 2.8 °C warmer (Istanbul 8.1 °C, Rome 10.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Istanbul 26.5 °C (August), Rome 30.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Istanbul 4.1 °C, Rome 3.5 °C.
  • Istanbul gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 783 mm against 770 mm.
  • Istanbul gets about 339 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,763 h against 2,424 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Istanbul 16 cm, Rome 0.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
