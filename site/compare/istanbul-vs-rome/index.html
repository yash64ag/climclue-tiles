How the climate of Istanbul, Turkey compares with Rome, Italy: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Rome is 1.1 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Rome is 2.6 °C warmer (Istanbul 8.1 °C, Rome 10.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Istanbul 28.4 °C (July), Rome 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Istanbul 3.2 °C, Rome 2.5 °C.
  • Istanbul gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 783 mm against 770 mm.
  • Istanbul gets about 339 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,763 h against 2,424 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Istanbul 16 cm, Rome 0.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
