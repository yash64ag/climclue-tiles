How the climate of Abobo, Ivory Coast compares with Monrovia, Liberia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Monrovia is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 25.9 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Monrovia is 1.0 °C warmer (Abobo 23.9 °C, Monrovia 24.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abobo 30.6 °C (March), Monrovia 32.1 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Abobo 21.2 °C, Monrovia 21.1 °C.
  • Monrovia gets 52% more rain and snow a year: 3,199 mm against 2,100 mm.
  • Abobo gets about 346 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,113 h against 1,767 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Monrovia in 10 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Abobo in 12 of 12 months
