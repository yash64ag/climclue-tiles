Gaziantep vs Kayseri climate

How the climate of Gaziantep, Turkey compares with Kayseri, Turkey, 250 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gaziantep is 2.9 °C (5.3 °F) warmer over the year: 15.3 °C against 12.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Gaziantep is 3.6 °C warmer (Gaziantep 24.1 °C, Kayseri 20.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gaziantep 35.3 °C (July), Kayseri 34.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Gaziantep -0.9 °C, Kayseri -5.1 °C.
  • Gaziantep gets 99% more rain and snow a year: 689 mm against 347 mm.
  • Gaziantep gets about 255 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,122 h against 2,867 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gaziantep 84 cm, Kayseri 47 cm (ERA5-Land).
