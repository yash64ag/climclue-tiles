How the climate of Chongqing, China compares with Zunyi, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Chongqing is 2.9 °C (5.2 °F) warmer over the year: 19.1 °C against 16.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Chongqing is 3.5 °C warmer (Chongqing 10.6 °C, Zunyi 7.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chongqing 33.3 °C (August), Zunyi 30.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Chongqing 6.5 °C, Zunyi 2.7 °C.
  • Chongqing gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,104 mm against 1,082 mm.
  • Zunyi gets about 84 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,282 h against 1,198 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chongqing 0 cm, Zunyi 7.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
