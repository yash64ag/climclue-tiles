How the climate of Kunming, China compares with Vanderbijlpark, South Africa: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Vanderbijlpark is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.4 °C against 16.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Kunming is 11.3 °C warmer (Kunming 20.9 °C, Vanderbijlpark 9.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kunming 24.7 °C (May), Vanderbijlpark 27.2 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Kunming 3.5 °C, Vanderbijlpark 0.3 °C.
  • Kunming gets 36% more rain and snow a year: 929 mm against 685 mm.
  • Vanderbijlpark gets about 1,027 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,230 h against 2,203 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
