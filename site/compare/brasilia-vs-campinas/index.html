Brasília vs Campinas climate

How the climate of Brasília, Brazil compares with Campinas, Brazil, 798 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Brasília is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 21.2 °C against 21.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Brasília is 2.4 °C warmer (Brasília 22.6 °C, Campinas 20.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brasília 28.1 °C (September), Campinas 27.8 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Brasília 13.9 °C, Campinas 12.4 °C.
  • Campinas gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,607 mm against 1,544 mm.
  • Campinas gets about 118 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,472 h against 2,354 h; about 1961–1990).
