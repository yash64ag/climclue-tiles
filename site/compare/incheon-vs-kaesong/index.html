How the climate of Incheon, South Korea compares with Kaesŏng, North Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Incheon is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 12.3 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Incheon is 1.9 °C warmer (Incheon 0.8 °C, Kaesŏng -1.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Incheon 29.0 °C (August), Kaesŏng 29.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Incheon -6.1 °C, Kaesŏng -8.6 °C.
  • Incheon gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,414 mm against 1,376 mm.
  • Kaesŏng gets about 21 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,375 h against 2,354 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Incheon 24 cm, Kaesŏng 29 cm (ERA5-Land).
