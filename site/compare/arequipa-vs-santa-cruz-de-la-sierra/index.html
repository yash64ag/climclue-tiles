How the climate of Arequipa, Peru compares with Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Santa Cruz de la Sierra is 8.3 °C (14.9 °F) warmer over the year: 23.5 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Santa Cruz de la Sierra is 10.6 °C warmer (Arequipa 14.9 °C, Santa Cruz de la Sierra 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Arequipa 24.7 °C (October), Santa Cruz de la Sierra 31.1 °C (November). Coldest-month night lows: Arequipa 5.5 °C, Santa Cruz de la Sierra 14.4 °C.
  • Santa Cruz de la Sierra gets 781% more rain and snow a year: 1,479 mm against 168 mm.
  • Arequipa gets about 513 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,178 h against 2,665 h; about 1961–1990).
