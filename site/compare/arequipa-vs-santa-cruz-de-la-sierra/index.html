Arequipa vs Santa Cruz de la Sierra climate

How the climate of Arequipa, Peru compares with Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia, 901 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Santa Cruz de la Sierra is 9.5 °C (17.1 °F) warmer over the year: 23.5 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Santa Cruz de la Sierra is 11.9 °C warmer (Arequipa 13.8 °C, Santa Cruz de la Sierra 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Arequipa 20.8 °C (October), Santa Cruz de la Sierra 29.6 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Arequipa 6.6 °C, Santa Cruz de la Sierra 16.3 °C.
  • Santa Cruz de la Sierra gets 620% more rain and snow a year: 1,501 mm against 208 mm.
  • Arequipa gets about 513 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,178 h against 2,665 h; about 1961–1990).
