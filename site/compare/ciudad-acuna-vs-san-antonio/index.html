Ciudad Acuña vs San Antonio climate

How the climate of Ciudad Acuña, Mexico compares with San Antonio, United States, 238 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Ciudad Acuña is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 21.7 °C against 20.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Ciudad Acuña is 2.0 °C warmer (Ciudad Acuña 26.4 °C, San Antonio 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ciudad Acuña 35.7 °C (August), San Antonio 34.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Ciudad Acuña 7.0 °C, San Antonio 7.0 °C.
  • San Antonio gets 61% more rain and snow a year: 822 mm against 509 mm.
  • San Antonio gets about 19 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,571 h against 2,553 h; about 1961–1990).
