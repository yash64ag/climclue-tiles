How the climate of Ciudad Acuña, Mexico compares with San Antonio, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ciudad Acuña is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 21.4 °C against 20.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Ciudad Acuña is 1.1 °C warmer (Ciudad Acuña 26.2 °C, San Antonio 25.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ciudad Acuña 36.4 °C (August), San Antonio 35.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Ciudad Acuña 4.6 °C, San Antonio 5.0 °C.
  • San Antonio gets 56% more rain and snow a year: 803 mm against 515 mm.
  • San Antonio gets about 19 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,571 h against 2,553 h; about 1961–1990).
