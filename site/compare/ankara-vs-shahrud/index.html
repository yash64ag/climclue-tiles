Ankara vs Shahrud climate

How the climate of Ankara, Turkey compares with Shahrud, Iran, 1,967 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shahrud is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 13.0 °C against 12.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Shahrud is 1.5 °C warmer (Ankara 20.8 °C, Shahrud 22.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ankara 30.2 °C (August), Shahrud 29.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ankara -3.6 °C, Shahrud -3.4 °C.
  • Ankara gets 56% more rain and snow a year: 439 mm against 282 mm.
  • Shahrud gets about 149 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,960 h against 2,811 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ankara 91 cm, Shahrud 104 cm (ERA5-Land).
