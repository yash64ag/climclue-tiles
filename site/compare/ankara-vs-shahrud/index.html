How the climate of Ankara, Turkey compares with Shahrud, Iran: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Shahrud is 1.0 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 14.0 °C against 12.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Shahrud is 2.4 °C warmer (Ankara 21.0 °C, Shahrud 23.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ankara 32.3 °C (August), Shahrud 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ankara -2.4 °C, Shahrud -3.4 °C.
  • Ankara gets 76% more rain and snow a year: 420 mm against 238 mm.
  • Shahrud gets about 149 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,960 h against 2,811 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ankara 37 cm, Shahrud 50 cm (ERA5-Land).
