Belo Horizonte vs Guarulhos climate

How the climate of Belo Horizonte, Brazil compares with Guarulhos, Brazil, 476 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Belo Horizonte is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 20.3 °C against 19.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Belo Horizonte is 1.5 °C warmer (Belo Horizonte 20.1 °C, Guarulhos 18.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belo Horizonte 26.7 °C (February), Guarulhos 26.9 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Belo Horizonte 11.4 °C, Guarulhos 12.1 °C.
  • Belo Horizonte gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,697 mm against 1,466 mm.
  • Belo Horizonte gets about 601 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,383 h against 1,782 h; about 1961–1990).
