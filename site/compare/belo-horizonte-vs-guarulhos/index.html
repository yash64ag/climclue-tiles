How the climate of Belo Horizonte, Brazil compares with Guarulhos, Brazil: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Belo Horizonte is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 21.0 °C against 20.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Belo Horizonte is 2.1 °C warmer (Belo Horizonte 20.8 °C, Guarulhos 18.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belo Horizonte 28.4 °C (February), Guarulhos 27.6 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Belo Horizonte 11.7 °C, Guarulhos 11.2 °C.
  • Belo Horizonte gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,615 mm against 1,425 mm.
  • Belo Horizonte gets about 601 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,383 h against 1,782 h; about 1961–1990).
