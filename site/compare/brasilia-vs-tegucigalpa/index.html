How the climate of Brasília, Brazil compares with Tegucigalpa, Honduras: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tegucigalpa is 1.3 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 22.6 °C against 21.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Tegucigalpa is 3.9 °C warmer (Brasília 19.2 °C, Tegucigalpa 23.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brasília 28.9 °C (September), Tegucigalpa 31.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Brasília 12.9 °C, Tegucigalpa 15.2 °C.
  • Brasília gets 46% more rain and snow a year: 1,544 mm against 1,055 mm.
  • Brasília gets about 22 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,354 h against 2,332 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
