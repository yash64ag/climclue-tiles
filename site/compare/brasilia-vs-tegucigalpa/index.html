Brasília vs Tegucigalpa climate

How the climate of Brasília, Brazil compares with Tegucigalpa, Honduras, 5,446 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Brasília is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 21.6 °C against 21.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Brasília is 2.3 °C warmer (Brasília 21.9 °C, Tegucigalpa 19.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brasília 28.4 °C (September), Tegucigalpa 29.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Brasília 14.3 °C, Tegucigalpa 14.9 °C.
  • Brasília gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,507 mm against 1,372 mm.
  • Brasília gets about 22 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,354 h against 2,332 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
