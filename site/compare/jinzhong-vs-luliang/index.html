Jinzhong vs Lüliang climate

How the climate of Jinzhong, China compares with Lüliang, China, 143 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jinzhong is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 11.4 °C against 10.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Jinzhong is 1.6 °C warmer (Jinzhong -3.9 °C, Lüliang -5.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinzhong 30.4 °C (July), Lüliang 30.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinzhong -10.6 °C, Lüliang -12.0 °C.
  • Lüliang gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 480 mm against 463 mm.
  • Lüliang gets about 60 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,599 h against 2,539 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinzhong 16 cm, Lüliang 19 cm (ERA5-Land).
