Jinzhong vs Lüliang climate

How the climate of Jinzhong, China compares with Lüliang, China, 143 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jinzhong is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 11.5 °C against 10.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Jinzhong is 2.1 °C warmer (Jinzhong -3.9 °C, Lüliang -6.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinzhong 30.1 °C (July), Lüliang 29.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinzhong -9.6 °C, Lüliang -11.4 °C.
  • Lüliang gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 499 mm against 467 mm.
  • Lüliang gets about 60 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,599 h against 2,539 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinzhong 33 cm, Lüliang 48 cm (ERA5-Land).
