How the climate of Amiens, France compares with Birmingham, United Kingdom: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Amiens is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 10.7 °C against 9.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Amiens is 1.6 °C warmer (Amiens 18.1 °C, Birmingham 16.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amiens 22.5 °C (July), Birmingham 21.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Amiens 1.5 °C, Birmingham 1.1 °C.
  • Birmingham gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 737 mm against 702 mm.
  • Amiens gets about 317 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,680 h against 1,363 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amiens 9.6 cm, Birmingham 6.3 cm (ERA5-Land).
