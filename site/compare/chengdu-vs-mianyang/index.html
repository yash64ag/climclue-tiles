Chengdu vs Mianyang climate

How the climate of Chengdu, China compares with Mianyang, China, 107 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mianyang is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 17.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Mianyang is 0.3 °C warmer (Chengdu 26.3 °C, Mianyang 26.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chengdu 29.6 °C (July), Mianyang 29.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chengdu 2.3 °C, Mianyang 2.0 °C.
  • Chengdu gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 995 mm against 919 mm.
  • Mianyang gets about 98 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,394 h against 1,296 h; about 1961–1990).
