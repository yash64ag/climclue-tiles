Chengdu vs Mianyang climate

How the climate of Chengdu, China compares with Mianyang, China, 107 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Chengdu is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 17.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Mianyang is 0.2 °C warmer (Chengdu 25.6 °C, Mianyang 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chengdu 29.7 °C (July), Mianyang 29.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chengdu 2.4 °C, Mianyang 2.0 °C.
  • Chengdu gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,007 mm against 956 mm.
  • Mianyang gets about 98 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,394 h against 1,296 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chengdu 2.1 cm, Mianyang 2.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
