Main differences

  • Mianyang, Sichuan is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 17.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Mianyang, Sichuan is 0.7 °C warmer (Chengdu 24.5 °C, Mianyang, Sichuan 25.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chengdu 29.8 °C (July), Mianyang, Sichuan 30.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chengdu 2.9 °C, Mianyang, Sichuan 2.6 °C.
