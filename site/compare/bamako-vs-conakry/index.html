Bamako vs Conakry climate

How the climate of Bamako, Mali compares with Conakry, Guinea, 710 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bamako is 1.7 °C (3.1 °F) warmer over the year: 27.9 °C against 26.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Bamako is 6.3 °C warmer (Bamako 32.8 °C, Conakry 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bamako 39.1 °C (April), Conakry 28.4 °C (December). Coldest-month night lows: Bamako 17.1 °C, Conakry 24.2 °C.
  • Conakry gets 299% more rain and snow a year: 3,562 mm against 893 mm.
  • Bamako gets about 748 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,933 h against 2,185 h; about 1961–1990).
