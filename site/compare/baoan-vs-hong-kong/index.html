How the climate of Bao'an, China compares with Hong Kong, Hong Kong: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hong Kong is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 22.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Hong Kong is 0.5 °C warmer (Bao'an 15.6 °C, Hong Kong 16.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bao'an 31.7 °C (July), Hong Kong 30.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bao'an 11.5 °C, Hong Kong 13.4 °C.
  • Hong Kong gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 2,341 mm against 2,140 mm.
  • Hong Kong gets about 67 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,994 h against 1,927 h; about 1961–1990).
