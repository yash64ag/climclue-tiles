Fuzhou, Fujian vs Jinjiang climate

How the climate of Fuzhou, Fujian, China compares with Jinjiang, China, 156 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jinjiang is 1.7 °C (3.0 °F) warmer over the year: 21.4 °C against 19.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Jinjiang is 2.7 °C warmer (Fuzhou, Fujian 10.4 °C, Jinjiang 13.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuzhou, Fujian 31.8 °C (July), Jinjiang 31.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuzhou, Fujian 6.2 °C, Jinjiang 9.2 °C.
  • Fuzhou, Fujian gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,350 mm against 1,252 mm.
  • Jinjiang gets about 205 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,967 h against 1,763 h; about 1961–1990).
