Main differences

  • Jinjiang, Fujian is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 21.4 °C against 20.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Jinjiang, Fujian is 2.6 °C warmer (Fuzhou, Fujian 10.6 °C, Jinjiang, Fujian 13.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuzhou, Fujian 33.9 °C (July), Jinjiang, Fujian 32.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuzhou, Fujian 6.8 °C, Jinjiang, Fujian 9.7 °C.
  • Fuzhou, Fujian gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,289 mm against 1,228 mm.
  • Jinjiang, Fujian gets about 205 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,967 h against 1,763 h; about 1961–1990).
