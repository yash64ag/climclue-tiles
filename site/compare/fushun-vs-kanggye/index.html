Fushun vs Kanggye climate

How the climate of Fushun, China compares with Kanggye, North Korea, 243 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fushun is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 8.5 °C against 8.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Fushun is 1.3 °C warmer (Fushun 22.4 °C, Kanggye 21.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fushun 29.1 °C (July), Kanggye 28.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fushun -18.2 °C, Kanggye -18.1 °C.
  • Kanggye gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 787 mm against 772 mm.
  • Fushun gets about 128 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,534 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fushun 42 cm, Kanggye 65 cm (ERA5-Land).
