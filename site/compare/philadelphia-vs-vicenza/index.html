Philadelphia vs Vicenza climate

How the climate of Philadelphia, United States compares with Vicenza, Italy, 6,751 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Vicenza is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 13.6 °C against 13.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Vicenza is 2.8 °C warmer (Philadelphia 6.3 °C, Vicenza 9.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Philadelphia 29.9 °C (July), Vicenza 28.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Philadelphia -3.3 °C, Vicenza -1.6 °C.
  • Philadelphia gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,215 mm against 1,045 mm.
  • Philadelphia gets about 430 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,463 h against 2,033 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Philadelphia 66 cm, Vicenza 21 cm (ERA5-Land).
