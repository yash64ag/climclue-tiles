How the climate of Philadelphia, United States compares with Vicenza, Italy: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Vicenza is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 13.6 °C against 13.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Vicenza is 2.6 °C warmer (Philadelphia 6.5 °C, Vicenza 9.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Philadelphia 30.8 °C (July), Vicenza 30.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Philadelphia -3.5 °C, Vicenza -1.3 °C.
  • Philadelphia gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 1,220 mm against 1,030 mm.
  • Philadelphia gets about 430 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,463 h against 2,033 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Philadelphia 47 cm, Vicenza 21 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Vicenza in 9 of 12 months
