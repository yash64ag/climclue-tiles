Lahore vs Multan climate

How the climate of Lahore, Pakistan compares with Multan, Pakistan, 313 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Multan is 2.1 °C (3.8 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 23.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Multan is 3.2 °C warmer (Lahore 30.3 °C, Multan 33.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lahore 39.9 °C (June), Multan 42.4 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Lahore 4.9 °C, Multan 5.3 °C.
  • Lahore gets 197% more rain and snow a year: 684 mm against 230 mm.
  • Multan gets about 146 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,083 h against 2,937 h; about 1961–1990).
