How the climate of Janakpur, Nepal compares with Patna, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Patna is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 25.7 °C against 25.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Patna is 1.4 °C warmer (Janakpur 30.4 °C, Patna 31.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Janakpur 37.3 °C (April), Patna 38.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Janakpur 9.0 °C, Patna 9.0 °C.
  • Janakpur gets 43% more rain and snow a year: 1,459 mm against 1,023 mm.
  • Patna gets about 78 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,233 h against 2,154 h; about 1961–1990).
