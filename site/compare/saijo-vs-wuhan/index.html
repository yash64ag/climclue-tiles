Saijō vs Wuhan climate

How the climate of Saijō, Japan compares with Wuhan, China, 1,814 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Wuhan is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 17.5 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Wuhan is 3.0 °C warmer (Saijō 19.9 °C, Wuhan 22.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Saijō 30.5 °C (August), Wuhan 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Saijō 3.4 °C, Wuhan 0.7 °C.
  • Wuhan gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,544 mm against 1,481 mm.
  • Saijō gets about 3 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,967 h against 1,964 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Saijō 2.8 cm, Wuhan 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
