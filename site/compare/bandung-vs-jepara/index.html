Bandung vs Jepara climate

How the climate of Bandung, Indonesia compares with Jepara, Indonesia, 340 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jepara is 3.3 °C (5.9 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 23.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Jepara is 3.6 °C warmer (Bandung 23.9 °C, Jepara 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bandung 28.2 °C (September), Jepara 31.0 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Bandung 18.9 °C, Jepara 24.0 °C.
  • Jepara gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 2,506 mm against 2,391 mm.
  • Jepara gets about 309 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,762 h against 2,454 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Jepara in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Jepara in 9 of 12 months
