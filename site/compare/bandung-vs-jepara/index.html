How the climate of Bandung, Indonesia compares with Jepara, Indonesia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jepara is 3.6 °C (6.6 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 23.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Jepara is 4.0 °C warmer (Bandung 23.5 °C, Jepara 27.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bandung 29.3 °C (September), Jepara 33.5 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Bandung 17.3 °C, Jepara 21.3 °C.
  • Jepara gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 2,502 mm against 2,449 mm.
  • Jepara gets about 309 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,762 h against 2,454 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Jepara in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Jepara in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Jepara in 9 of 12 months
