How the climate of Calgary, Canada compares with Edmonton, Canada: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Calgary is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 4.8 °C against 3.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Calgary is 4.4 °C warmer (Calgary -6.5 °C, Edmonton -10.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Calgary 23.2 °C (July), Edmonton 23.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Calgary -12.0 °C, Edmonton -15.8 °C.
  • Edmonton gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 448 mm against 441 mm.
  • Calgary gets about 101 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,298 h against 2,198 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Calgary 95 cm, Edmonton 100 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Sunnier: Calgary in 10 of 12 months
