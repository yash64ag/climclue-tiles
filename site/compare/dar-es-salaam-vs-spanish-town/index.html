Dar es Salaam vs Spanish Town climate

How the climate of Dar es Salaam, Tanzania compares with Spanish Town, Jamaica, 13,010 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Spanish Town is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 26.3 °C against 26.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Spanish Town is 3.7 °C warmer (Dar es Salaam 24.1 °C, Spanish Town 27.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dar es Salaam 31.0 °C (February), Spanish Town 30.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Dar es Salaam 21.7 °C, Spanish Town 22.7 °C.
  • Spanish Town gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,164 mm against 1,142 mm.
  • Dar es Salaam gets about 163 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,731 h against 2,568 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
