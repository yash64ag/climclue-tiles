How the climate of Dar es Salaam, Tanzania compares with Spanish Town, Jamaica: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Spanish Town is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 26.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Spanish Town is 4.6 °C warmer (Dar es Salaam 24.2 °C, Spanish Town 28.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dar es Salaam 32.5 °C (February), Spanish Town 33.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Dar es Salaam 18.9 °C, Spanish Town 20.9 °C.
  • Dar es Salaam gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,166 mm against 1,114 mm.
  • Dar es Salaam gets about 163 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,731 h against 2,568 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Spanish Town in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Dar es Salaam in 9 of 12 months
