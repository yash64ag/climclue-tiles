How the climate of Bucharest, Romania compares with Odesa, Ukraine: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bucharest is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 11.4 °C against 10.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Bucharest is 2.3 °C warmer (Bucharest 5.9 °C, Odesa 3.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bucharest 29.9 °C (July), Odesa 26.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bucharest -4.7 °C, Odesa -2.9 °C.
  • Bucharest gets 35% more rain and snow a year: 619 mm against 458 mm.
  • Odesa gets about 49 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,124 h against 2,075 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bucharest 68 cm, Odesa 62 cm (ERA5-Land).
