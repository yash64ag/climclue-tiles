Bucharest vs Odesa climate

How the climate of Bucharest, Romania compares with Odesa, Ukraine, 428 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bucharest is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 11.6 °C against 11.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Bucharest is 2.3 °C warmer (Bucharest 6.0 °C, Odesa 3.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bucharest 27.9 °C (July), Odesa 26.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bucharest -4.5 °C, Odesa -2.8 °C.
  • Bucharest gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 611 mm against 474 mm.
  • Odesa gets about 49 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,124 h against 2,075 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bucharest 68 cm, Odesa 62 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Drier: Odesa in 10 of 12 months
