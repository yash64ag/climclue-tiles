Batam vs Singapore climate

How the climate of Batam, Indonesia compares with Singapore, Singapore, 25 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Batam is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Batam is 0.1 °C warmer (Batam 27.4 °C, Singapore 27.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Batam 31.6 °C (May), Singapore 31.3 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Batam 22.1 °C, Singapore 22.3 °C.
  • Batam gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 2,545 mm against 2,424 mm.
  • Singapore gets about 18 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,055 h against 2,037 h; about 1961–1990).
