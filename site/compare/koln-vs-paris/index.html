Köln vs Paris climate

How the climate of Köln, Germany compares with Paris, France, 403 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Paris is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 11.2 °C against 10.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Paris is 1.3 °C warmer (Köln 2.6 °C, Paris 4.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Köln 23.1 °C (July), Paris 23.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Köln 0.1 °C, Paris 1.2 °C.
  • Köln gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 819 mm against 662 mm.
  • Paris gets about 290 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,744 h against 1,455 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Köln 23 cm, Paris 9.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
