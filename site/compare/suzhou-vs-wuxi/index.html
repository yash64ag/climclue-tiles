Suzhou, Jiangsu vs Wuxi climate

How the climate of Suzhou, Jiangsu, China compares with Wuxi, China, 41 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Suzhou, Jiangsu is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Suzhou, Jiangsu is 0.3 °C warmer (Suzhou, Jiangsu 12.6 °C, Wuxi 12.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Suzhou, Jiangsu 31.6 °C (July), Wuxi 31.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Suzhou, Jiangsu 0.2 °C, Wuxi 0.1 °C.
  • Suzhou, Jiangsu gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,203 mm against 1,183 mm.
  • Wuxi gets about 22 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,999 h against 1,977 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Suzhou, Jiangsu 11 cm, Wuxi 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
