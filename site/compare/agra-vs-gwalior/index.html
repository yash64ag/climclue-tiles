Agra vs Gwalior climate

How the climate of Agra, India compares with Gwalior, India, 107 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gwalior is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 25.7 °C against 25.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Gwalior is 1.2 °C warmer (Agra 23.4 °C, Gwalior 24.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Agra 40.9 °C (May), Gwalior 41.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Agra 6.7 °C, Gwalior 7.4 °C.
  • Gwalior gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 833 mm against 719 mm.
  • Gwalior gets about 31 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,737 h against 2,707 h; about 1961–1990).
