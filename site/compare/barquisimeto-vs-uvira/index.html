Barquisimeto vs Uvira climate

How the climate of Barquisimeto, Venezuela compares with Uvira, Democratic Republic of the Congo, 11,003 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Barquisimeto is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 24.7 °C against 24.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Barquisimeto is 0.7 °C warmer (Barquisimeto 25.4 °C, Uvira 24.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Barquisimeto 30.9 °C (March), Uvira 31.0 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Barquisimeto 18.5 °C, Uvira 17.6 °C.
  • Uvira gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 935 mm against 700 mm.
  • Barquisimeto gets about 435 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,600 h against 2,166 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
