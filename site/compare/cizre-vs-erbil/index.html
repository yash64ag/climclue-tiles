How the climate of Cizre, Turkey compares with Erbil, Iraq: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Erbil is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 20.2 °C against 18.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Erbil is 1.9 °C warmer (Cizre 22.5 °C, Erbil 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cizre 41.9 °C (July), Erbil 41.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Cizre 0.5 °C, Erbil 2.2 °C.
  • Cizre gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 704 mm against 625 mm.
  • Erbil gets about 96 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,190 h against 3,094 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Cizre 25 cm, Erbil 4.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
