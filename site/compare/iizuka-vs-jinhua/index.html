Iizuka vs Jinhua, Zhejiang climate

How the climate of Iizuka, Japan compares with Jinhua, Zhejiang, China, 1,162 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jinhua, Zhejiang is 1.3 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 18.4 °C against 17.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Jinhua, Zhejiang is 2.7 °C warmer (Iizuka 20.1 °C, Jinhua, Zhejiang 22.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iizuka 32.6 °C (August), Jinhua, Zhejiang 34.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Iizuka 2.1 °C, Jinhua, Zhejiang 2.0 °C.
  • Iizuka gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,669 mm against 1,534 mm.
  • Jinhua, Zhejiang gets about 126 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,885 h against 1,759 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Iizuka 14 cm, Jinhua, Zhejiang 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
