How the climate of Gqeberha, South Africa compares with Soweto, South Africa: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Gqeberha is 2.5 °C (4.5 °F) warmer over the year: 18.2 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Gqeberha is 6.8 °C warmer (Gqeberha 16.0 °C, Soweto 9.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gqeberha 25.8 °C (February), Soweto 25.8 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Gqeberha 9.3 °C, Soweto 1.1 °C.
  • Soweto gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 678 mm against 558 mm.
  • Soweto gets about 467 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,205 h against 2,739 h; about 1961–1990).
