Gqeberha vs Soweto climate

How the climate of Gqeberha, South Africa compares with Soweto, South Africa, 882 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gqeberha is 2.6 °C (4.7 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Gqeberha is 7.0 °C warmer (Gqeberha 16.1 °C, Soweto 9.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gqeberha 23.3 °C (February), Soweto 24.9 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Gqeberha 12.9 °C, Soweto 2.8 °C.
  • Soweto gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 703 mm against 558 mm.
  • Soweto gets about 467 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,205 h against 2,739 h; about 1961–1990).
