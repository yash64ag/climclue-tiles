Kigali vs Kisangani climate

How the climate of Kigali, Rwanda compares with Kisangani, Democratic Republic of the Congo, 607 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kisangani is 4.2 °C (7.6 °F) warmer over the year: 24.7 °C against 20.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Kisangani is 5.1 °C warmer (Kigali 20.1 °C, Kisangani 25.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kigali 26.3 °C (August), Kisangani 31.0 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Kigali 14.6 °C, Kisangani 21.6 °C.
  • Kisangani gets 54% more rain and snow a year: 1,649 mm against 1,069 mm.
  • Kigali gets about 19 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,990 h against 1,971 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Kisangani in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Kigali in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Kisangani in 7 of 12 months
