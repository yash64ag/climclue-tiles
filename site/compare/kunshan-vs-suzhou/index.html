How the climate of Kunshan, China compares with Suzhou, Jiangsu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Suzhou, Jiangsu is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Suzhou, Jiangsu is 0.4 °C warmer (Kunshan 15.2 °C, Suzhou, Jiangsu 15.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kunshan 31.9 °C (July), Suzhou, Jiangsu 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kunshan 0.3 °C, Suzhou, Jiangsu 0.3 °C.
  • Kunshan gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,325 mm against 1,208 mm.
  • Kunshan gets about 1 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,978 h against 1,977 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kunshan 11 cm, Suzhou, Jiangsu 11 cm (ERA5-Land).
