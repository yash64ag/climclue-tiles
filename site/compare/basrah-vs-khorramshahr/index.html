Basrah vs Khorramshahr climate

How the climate of Basrah, Iraq compares with Khorramshahr, Iran, 39 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Basrah is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Basrah is 0.4 °C warmer (Basrah 34.9 °C, Khorramshahr 34.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Basrah 46.5 °C (July), Khorramshahr 46.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Basrah 7.3 °C, Khorramshahr 7.5 °C.
  • Khorramshahr gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 164 mm against 151 mm.
  • Basrah gets about 109 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,037 h against 2,928 h; about 1961–1990).
