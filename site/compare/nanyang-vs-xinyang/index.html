Nanyang vs Xinyang climate

How the climate of Nanyang, China compares with Xinyang, China, 173 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Xinyang is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.8 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Nanyang is 0.5 °C warmer (Nanyang 25.9 °C, Xinyang 25.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nanyang 31.7 °C (July), Xinyang 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nanyang -2.8 °C, Xinyang -1.9 °C.
  • Xinyang gets 36% more rain and snow a year: 1,143 mm against 843 mm.
  • Xinyang gets about 20 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,028 h against 2,008 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nanyang 16 cm, Xinyang 28 cm (ERA5-Land).
