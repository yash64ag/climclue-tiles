How the climate of Almaty, Kazakhstan compares with Davenport, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Davenport is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 10.5 °C against 10.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Davenport is 1.5 °C warmer (Almaty 3.2 °C, Davenport 4.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Almaty 30.4 °C (July), Davenport 29.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Almaty -9.1 °C, Davenport -9.4 °C.
  • Almaty gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 926 mm against 907 mm.
  • Davenport gets about 31 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,492 h against 2,460 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Almaty 145 cm, Davenport 63 cm (ERA5-Land).
