How the climate of Oslo, Norway compares with Stockholm, Sweden: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Oslo is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 6.7 °C against 6.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Stockholm is 2.0 °C warmer (Oslo -4.0 °C, Stockholm -2.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Oslo 22.9 °C (July), Stockholm 21.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Oslo -7.1 °C, Stockholm -4.7 °C.
  • Oslo gets 57% more rain and snow a year: 922 mm against 587 mm.
  • Oslo gets about 92 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,579 h against 1,487 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Oslo 246 cm, Stockholm 116 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Warmer: Oslo in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Stockholm in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Oslo in 10 of 12 months
