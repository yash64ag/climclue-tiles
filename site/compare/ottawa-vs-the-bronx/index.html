How the climate of Ottawa, Canada compares with The Bronx, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • The Bronx is 6.1 °C (11.0 °F) warmer over the year: 12.5 °C against 6.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when The Bronx is 10.4 °C warmer (Ottawa -10.5 °C, The Bronx -0.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ottawa 26.4 °C (July), The Bronx 29.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ottawa -15.1 °C, The Bronx -4.2 °C.
  • The Bronx gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 1,216 mm against 960 mm.
  • The Bronx gets about 526 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,519 h against 1,993 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ottawa 175 cm, The Bronx 61 cm (ERA5-Land).
