Ottawa vs The Bronx climate

How the climate of Ottawa, Canada compares with The Bronx, United States, 528 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • The Bronx is 5.5 °C (9.9 °F) warmer over the year: 12.1 °C against 6.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when The Bronx is 9.5 °C warmer (Ottawa -10.2 °C, The Bronx -0.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ottawa 26.0 °C (July), The Bronx 28.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ottawa -14.6 °C, The Bronx -4.2 °C.
  • The Bronx gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 1,213 mm against 974 mm.
  • The Bronx gets about 526 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,519 h against 1,993 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ottawa 211 cm, The Bronx 90 cm (ERA5-Land).
