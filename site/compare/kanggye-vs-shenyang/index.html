How the climate of Kanggye, North Korea compares with Shenyang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Shenyang is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 8.9 °C against 8.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Shenyang is 1.4 °C warmer (Kanggye -8.9 °C, Shenyang -7.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kanggye 28.6 °C (July), Shenyang 29.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kanggye -18.1 °C, Shenyang -16.6 °C.
  • Kanggye gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 787 mm against 715 mm.
  • Shenyang gets about 134 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,540 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kanggye 65 cm, Shenyang 37 cm (ERA5-Land).
