How the climate of Daegu, South Korea compares with Xuzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Xuzhou is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 15.1 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Xuzhou is 2.3 °C warmer (Daegu 23.1 °C, Xuzhou 25.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daegu 31.1 °C (August), Xuzhou 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Daegu -5.3 °C, Xuzhou -3.3 °C.
  • Daegu gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 979 mm against 886 mm.
  • Daegu gets about 221 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,483 h against 2,262 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daegu 13 cm, Xuzhou 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
