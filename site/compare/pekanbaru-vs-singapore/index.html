Pekanbaru vs Singapore climate

How the climate of Pekanbaru, Indonesia compares with Singapore, Singapore, 281 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Singapore is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Singapore is 0.7 °C warmer (Pekanbaru 27.1 °C, Singapore 27.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pekanbaru 30.6 °C (May), Singapore 28.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Pekanbaru 22.3 °C, Singapore 24.9 °C.
  • Pekanbaru gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 2,786 mm against 2,424 mm.
  • Pekanbaru gets about 175 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,230 h against 2,055 h; about 1961–1990).
