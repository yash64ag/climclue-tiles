Jiangmen vs Yunfu climate

How the climate of Jiangmen, China compares with Yunfu, China, 114 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jiangmen is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 22.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Jiangmen is 1.1 °C warmer (Jiangmen 16.2 °C, Yunfu 15.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiangmen 32.7 °C (July), Yunfu 32.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jiangmen 11.2 °C, Yunfu 9.6 °C.
  • Jiangmen gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 2,139 mm against 1,900 mm.
  • Jiangmen gets about 48 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,875 h against 1,827 h; about 1961–1990).
