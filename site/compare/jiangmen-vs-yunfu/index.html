Jiangmen vs Yunfu climate

How the climate of Jiangmen, China compares with Yunfu, China, 114 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jiangmen is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 22.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Jiangmen is 1.3 °C warmer (Jiangmen 19.3 °C, Yunfu 17.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiangmen 31.5 °C (July), Yunfu 31.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jiangmen 10.5 °C, Yunfu 9.5 °C.
  • Jiangmen gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 2,173 mm against 1,905 mm.
  • Jiangmen gets about 48 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,875 h against 1,827 h; about 1961–1990).
