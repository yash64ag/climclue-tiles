Mombasa vs Tanga climate

How the climate of Mombasa, Kenya compares with Tanga, Tanzania, 129 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Mombasa is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.2 °C against 26.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Mombasa is 0.5 °C warmer (Mombasa 24.9 °C, Tanga 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mombasa 31.9 °C (February), Tanga 32.7 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Mombasa 21.9 °C, Tanga 20.7 °C.
  • Tanga gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,169 mm against 1,092 mm.
  • Mombasa gets about 1 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,940 h against 2,939 h; about 1961–1990).
