Mombasa vs Tanga climate

How the climate of Mombasa, Kenya compares with Tanga, Tanzania, 129 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Mombasa is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.2 °C against 26.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Tanga is 0.4 °C warmer (Mombasa 27.8 °C, Tanga 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mombasa 31.6 °C (February), Tanga 32.1 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Mombasa 22.1 °C, Tanga 21.2 °C.
  • Tanga gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,168 mm against 1,110 mm.
  • Mombasa gets about 1 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,940 h against 2,939 h; about 1961–1990).
