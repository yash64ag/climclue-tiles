Ghāziābād vs Kasur climate

How the climate of Ghāziābād, India compares with Kasur, Pakistan, 397 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ghāziābād is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 24.7 °C against 24.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Ghāziābād is 1.7 °C warmer (Ghāziābād 22.4 °C, Kasur 20.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ghāziābād 39.6 °C (May), Kasur 40.0 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Ghāziābād 6.6 °C, Kasur 4.8 °C.
  • Ghāziābād gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 692 mm against 576 mm.
  • Kasur gets about 229 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,903 h against 2,674 h; about 1961–1990).
