How the climate of Baliuag, Philippines compares with Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ho Chi Minh City is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 27.5 °C against 27.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Ho Chi Minh City is 1.1 °C warmer (Baliuag 26.2 °C, Ho Chi Minh City 27.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baliuag 34.6 °C (April), Ho Chi Minh City 34.6 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Baliuag 21.0 °C, Ho Chi Minh City 21.4 °C.
  • Baliuag gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,921 mm against 1,840 mm.
  • Baliuag gets about 682 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,431 h against 1,749 h; about 1961–1990).
