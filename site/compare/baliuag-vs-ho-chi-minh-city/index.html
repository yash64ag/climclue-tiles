Baliuag vs Ho Chi Minh City climate

How the climate of Baliuag, Philippines compares with Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, 1,613 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Baliuag and Ho Chi Minh City have the same yearly average temperature (27.4 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Ho Chi Minh City is 1.1 °C warmer (Baliuag 26.3 °C, Ho Chi Minh City 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baliuag 32.7 °C (April), Ho Chi Minh City 34.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Baliuag 22.1 °C, Ho Chi Minh City 21.6 °C.
  • Baliuag gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,896 mm against 1,845 mm.
  • Baliuag gets about 682 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,431 h against 1,749 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Baliuag in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Baliuag in 12 of 12 months
