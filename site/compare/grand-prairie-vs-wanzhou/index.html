How the climate of Grand Prairie, United States compares with Wanzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Wanzhou is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 19.4 °C against 19.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Wanzhou is 1.4 °C warmer (Grand Prairie 8.6 °C, Wanzhou 10.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Grand Prairie 35.4 °C (August), Wanzhou 34.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Grand Prairie 2.5 °C, Wanzhou 5.9 °C.
  • Wanzhou gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 1,225 mm against 952 mm.
  • Grand Prairie gets about 1,396 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,797 h against 1,401 h; about 1961–1990).
