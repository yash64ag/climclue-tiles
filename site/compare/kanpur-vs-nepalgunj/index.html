How the climate of Kanpur, India compares with Nepalgunj, Nepal: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kanpur is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 25.3 °C against 24.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Kanpur is 2.4 °C warmer (Kanpur 33.1 °C, Nepalgunj 30.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kanpur 40.7 °C (May), Nepalgunj 38.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kanpur 7.2 °C, Nepalgunj 7.2 °C.
  • Nepalgunj gets 82% more rain and snow a year: 1,606 mm against 882 mm.
  • Kanpur gets about 167 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,663 h against 2,496 h; about 1961–1990).
