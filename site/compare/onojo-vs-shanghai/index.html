How the climate of Ōnojō, Japan compares with Shanghai, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ōnojō and Shanghai have the same yearly average temperature (17.0 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Shanghai is 1.5 °C warmer (Ōnojō 27.2 °C, Shanghai 28.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ōnojō 32.0 °C (August), Shanghai 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ōnojō 2.8 °C, Shanghai 1.6 °C.
  • Ōnojō gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,658 mm against 1,476 mm.
  • Shanghai gets about 183 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,969 h against 1,786 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ōnojō 6.6 cm, Shanghai 7.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
