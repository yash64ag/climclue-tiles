How the climate of Saint Petersburg, Russia compares with Vitebsk, Belarus: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Vitebsk is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 6.1 °C against 5.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Vitebsk is 1.6 °C warmer (Saint Petersburg 11.3 °C, Vitebsk 12.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Saint Petersburg 22.6 °C (July), Vitebsk 23.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Saint Petersburg -8.7 °C, Vitebsk -9.0 °C.
  • Vitebsk gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 728 mm against 690 mm.
  • Vitebsk gets about 132 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,753 h against 1,621 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Saint Petersburg 179 cm, Vitebsk 170 cm (ERA5-Land).
