Rawalpindi vs Srinagar climate

How the climate of Rawalpindi, Pakistan compares with Srinagar, India, 171 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Rawalpindi is 8.7 °C (15.6 °F) warmer over the year: 21.4 °C against 12.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Rawalpindi is 10.5 °C warmer (Rawalpindi 28.0 °C, Srinagar 17.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rawalpindi 38.5 °C (June), Srinagar 29.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Rawalpindi 2.8 °C, Srinagar -3.6 °C.
  • Rawalpindi gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 1,338 mm against 1,009 mm.
  • Rawalpindi gets about 142 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,889 h against 2,747 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Rawalpindi 0 cm, Srinagar 72 cm (ERA5-Land).
