Rawalpindi vs Srinagar climate

How the climate of Rawalpindi, Pakistan compares with Srinagar, India, 171 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rawalpindi is 9.1 °C (16.4 °F) warmer over the year: 21.4 °C against 12.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Rawalpindi is 11.2 °C warmer (Rawalpindi 27.9 °C, Srinagar 16.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rawalpindi 36.7 °C (June), Srinagar 26.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Rawalpindi 3.9 °C, Srinagar -4.8 °C.
  • Rawalpindi gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 1,329 mm against 1,119 mm.
  • Rawalpindi gets about 142 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,889 h against 2,747 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Rawalpindi 0.1 cm, Srinagar 374 cm (ERA5-Land).
