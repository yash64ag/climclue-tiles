How the climate of Kaifeng, China compares with Xuchang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Xuchang is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 15.3 °C against 15.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Xuchang is 0.6 °C warmer (Kaifeng 2.5 °C, Xuchang 3.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kaifeng 32.5 °C (June), Xuchang 32.6 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Kaifeng -4.4 °C, Xuchang -3.8 °C.
  • Xuchang gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 762 mm against 658 mm.
  • Kaifeng gets about 87 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,280 h against 2,193 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kaifeng 14 cm, Xuchang 18 cm (ERA5-Land).
