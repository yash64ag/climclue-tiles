Dhaka vs Gazipur climate

How the climate of Dhaka, Bangladesh compares with Gazipur, Bangladesh, 32 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dhaka is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 25.5 °C against 25.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Dhaka is 0.4 °C warmer (Dhaka 25.9 °C, Gazipur 25.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dhaka 33.3 °C (April), Gazipur 33.1 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Dhaka 11.2 °C, Gazipur 11.0 °C.
  • Gazipur gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 2,188 mm against 2,134 mm.
  • Gazipur gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,106 h against 2,099 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Dhaka in 11 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Gazipur in 7 of 12 months
