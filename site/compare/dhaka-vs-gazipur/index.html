Dhaka vs Gazipur climate

How the climate of Dhaka, Bangladesh compares with Gazipur, Bangladesh, 32 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dhaka is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 25.5 °C against 25.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Dhaka is 0.3 °C warmer (Dhaka 25.9 °C, Gazipur 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dhaka 32.6 °C (April), Gazipur 32.6 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Dhaka 12.8 °C, Gazipur 12.3 °C.
  • Gazipur gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 2,184 mm against 2,161 mm.
  • Gazipur gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,106 h against 2,099 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Dhaka in 9 of 12 months (3 equal)
  • Drier: Gazipur in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Gazipur in 7 of 12 months
