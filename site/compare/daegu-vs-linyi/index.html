How the climate of Daegu, South Korea compares with Linyi, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Daegu is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 14.0 °C against 13.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Daegu is 0.9 °C warmer (Daegu 2.3 °C, Linyi 1.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daegu 31.1 °C (August), Linyi 30.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Daegu -5.3 °C, Linyi -5.2 °C.
  • Daegu gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 979 mm against 836 mm.
  • Daegu gets about 120 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,483 h against 2,362 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daegu 13 cm, Linyi 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
