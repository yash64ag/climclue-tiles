How the climate of Belém, Pará, Brazil compares with Jayapura, Indonesia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Belém, Pará is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Belém, Pará is 0.9 °C warmer (Belém, Pará 27.5 °C, Jayapura 26.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belém, Pará 32.9 °C (October), Jayapura 31.4 °C (November). Coldest-month night lows: Belém, Pará 22.0 °C, Jayapura 22.4 °C.
  • Belém, Pará gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 2,804 mm against 2,382 mm.
  • Belém, Pará gets about 462 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,210 h against 1,749 h; about 1961–1990).
