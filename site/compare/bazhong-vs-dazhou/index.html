Bazhong vs Dazhou climate

How the climate of Bazhong, China compares with Dazhou, China, 100 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Dazhou is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 17.4 °C against 17.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Dazhou is 0.8 °C warmer (Bazhong 26.6 °C, Dazhou 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bazhong 29.8 °C (July), Dazhou 30.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bazhong 1.1 °C, Dazhou 2.0 °C.
  • Dazhou gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 1,501 mm against 1,308 mm.
  • Bazhong gets about 62 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,456 h against 1,394 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bazhong 1.6 cm, Dazhou 1.3 cm (ERA5-Land).
