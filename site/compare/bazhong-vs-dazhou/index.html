How the climate of Bazhong, China compares with Dazhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Dazhou is 1.0 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Dazhou is 1.5 °C warmer (Bazhong 26.9 °C, Dazhou 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bazhong 31.3 °C (July), Dazhou 33.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bazhong 3.0 °C, Dazhou 4.4 °C.
  • Bazhong gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,298 mm against 1,286 mm.
  • Bazhong gets about 62 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,456 h against 1,394 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bazhong 1.6 cm, Dazhou 1.3 cm (ERA5-Land).
