Da Nang vs Huế climate

How the climate of Da Nang, Vietnam compares with Huế, Vietnam, 80 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Da Nang and Huế have the same yearly average temperature (26.0 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Da Nang is 0.6 °C warmer (Da Nang 22.5 °C, Huế 21.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Da Nang 33.4 °C (June), Huế 33.8 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Da Nang 18.8 °C, Huế 18.3 °C.
  • Huế gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 2,948 mm against 2,367 mm.
  • Huế gets about 27 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,260 h against 2,232 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Huế in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Da Nang in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Huế in 8 of 12 months
