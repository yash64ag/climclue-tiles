Lianyungang vs Linyi climate

How the climate of Lianyungang, China compares with Linyi, China, 95 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lianyungang is 0.6 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 14.4 °C against 13.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Lianyungang is 1.4 °C warmer (Lianyungang 2.8 °C, Linyi 1.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lianyungang 30.0 °C (July), Linyi 30.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lianyungang -3.7 °C, Linyi -5.2 °C.
  • Lianyungang gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 878 mm against 836 mm.
  • Linyi gets about 62 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,362 h against 2,300 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lianyungang 9.4 cm, Linyi 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
