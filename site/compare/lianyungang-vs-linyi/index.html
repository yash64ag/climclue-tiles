Lianyungang vs Linyi climate

How the climate of Lianyungang, China compares with Linyi, China, 95 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lianyungang is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 14.4 °C against 13.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Lianyungang is 1.4 °C warmer (Lianyungang 9.2 °C, Linyi 7.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lianyungang 30.4 °C (July), Linyi 30.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lianyungang -3.6 °C, Linyi -5.1 °C.
  • Lianyungang gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 897 mm against 835 mm.
  • Linyi gets about 62 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,362 h against 2,300 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lianyungang 9.4 cm, Linyi 13 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Drier: Linyi in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Linyi in 11 of 12 months (1 equal)
