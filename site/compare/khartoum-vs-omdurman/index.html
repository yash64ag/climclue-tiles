Main differences

  • Khartoum is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 29.6 °C against 29.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Khartoum is 0.4 °C warmer (Khartoum 27.7 °C, Omdurman 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Khartoum 40.5 °C (May), Omdurman 40.4 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Khartoum 15.1 °C, Omdurman 14.7 °C.
  • Khartoum gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 107 mm against 99 mm.
  • Omdurman gets about 0 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,512 h against 3,512 h; about 1961–1990).
