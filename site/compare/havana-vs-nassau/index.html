How the climate of Havana, Cuba compares with Nassau, Bahamas: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Havana is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 25.4 °C against 25.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Havana is 1.2 °C warmer (Havana 24.9 °C, Nassau 23.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Havana 32.4 °C (July), Nassau 32.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Havana 17.8 °C, Nassau 17.7 °C.
  • Havana gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,368 mm against 1,269 mm.
  • Havana gets about 116 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,950 h against 2,834 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Havana in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: Nassau in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
