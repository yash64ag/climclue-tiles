Havana vs Nassau climate

How the climate of Havana, Cuba compares with Nassau, Bahamas, 555 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Havana is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 25.4 °C against 24.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Havana is 1.6 °C warmer (Havana 24.9 °C, Nassau 23.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Havana 31.4 °C (August), Nassau 28.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Havana 19.9 °C, Nassau 21.5 °C.
  • Havana gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,368 mm against 1,269 mm.
  • Havana gets about 116 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,950 h against 2,834 h; about 1961–1990).
