Catacamas vs Valencia climate

How the climate of Catacamas, Honduras compares with Valencia, Venezuela, 2,010 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Catacamas is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 24.8 °C against 24.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Catacamas is 1.3 °C warmer (Catacamas 26.6 °C, Valencia 25.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Catacamas 32.3 °C (April), Valencia 31.4 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Catacamas 18.2 °C, Valencia 16.1 °C.
  • Valencia gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,379 mm against 1,357 mm.
  • Valencia gets about 166 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,706 h against 2,540 h; about 1961–1990).
