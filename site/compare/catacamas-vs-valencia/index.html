Catacamas vs Valencia climate

How the climate of Catacamas, Honduras compares with Valencia, Venezuela, 2,010 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Catacamas is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 25.0 °C against 24.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Catacamas is 1.3 °C warmer (Catacamas 26.8 °C, Valencia 25.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Catacamas 33.2 °C (April), Valencia 28.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Catacamas 18.5 °C, Valencia 20.0 °C.
  • Catacamas gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,284 mm against 1,267 mm.
  • Valencia gets about 166 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,706 h against 2,540 h; about 1961–1990).
