How the climate of Catacamas, Honduras compares with Valencia, Venezuela: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Catacamas is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 24.9 °C against 24.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Catacamas is 1.1 °C warmer (Catacamas 26.6 °C, Valencia 25.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Catacamas 32.1 °C (May), Valencia 31.6 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Catacamas 18.1 °C, Valencia 16.3 °C.
  • Valencia gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,379 mm against 1,357 mm.
  • Valencia gets about 166 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,706 h against 2,540 h; about 1961–1990).
