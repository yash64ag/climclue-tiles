Tangshan vs Tianjin climate

How the climate of Tangshan, China compares with Tianjin, China, 103 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tianjin is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 13.3 °C against 12.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Tianjin is 1.4 °C warmer (Tangshan 4.0 °C, Tianjin 5.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Tangshan 30.5 °C (July), Tianjin 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Tangshan -10.3 °C, Tianjin -8.3 °C.
  • Tangshan gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 630 mm against 543 mm.
  • Tangshan gets about 42 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,672 h against 2,630 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Tangshan 14 cm, Tianjin 9.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
