Tai’an vs Zibo climate

How the climate of Tai’an, China compares with Zibo, China, 108 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zibo is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 13.8 °C against 13.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Zibo is 0.9 °C warmer (Tai’an 19.8 °C, Zibo 20.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Tai’an 30.4 °C (July), Zibo 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Tai’an -6.5 °C, Zibo -6.4 °C.
  • Tai’an gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 826 mm against 615 mm.
  • Zibo gets about 40 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,536 h against 2,496 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Tai’an 15 cm, Zibo 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
