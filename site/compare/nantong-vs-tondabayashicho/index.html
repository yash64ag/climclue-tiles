Nantong, Jiangsu vs Tondabayashichō climate

How the climate of Nantong, Jiangsu, China compares with Tondabayashichō, Japan, 1,395 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tondabayashichō is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 16.4 °C against 15.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Tondabayashichō is 1.7 °C warmer (Nantong, Jiangsu 3.5 °C, Tondabayashichō 5.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nantong, Jiangsu 31.3 °C (July), Tondabayashichō 32.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Nantong, Jiangsu -0.2 °C, Tondabayashichō 1.3 °C.
  • Tondabayashichō gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,419 mm against 1,240 mm.
  • Nantong, Jiangsu gets about 197 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,051 h against 1,855 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nantong, Jiangsu 10 cm, Tondabayashichō 9.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
