How the climate of Guiyang, China compares with Liupanshui, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Guiyang is 3.4 °C (6.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 13.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Guiyang is 4.8 °C warmer (Guiyang 24.8 °C, Liupanshui 20.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guiyang 28.9 °C (August), Liupanshui 24.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Guiyang 3.1 °C, Liupanshui -0.4 °C.
  • Liupanshui gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 1,243 mm against 1,011 mm.
  • Liupanshui gets about 342 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,649 h against 1,307 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Guiyang 4.4 cm, Liupanshui 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
