Guiyang vs Liupanshui climate

How the climate of Guiyang, China compares with Liupanshui, China, 187 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Guiyang is 2.7 °C (4.9 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 13.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Guiyang is 4.0 °C warmer (Guiyang 24.0 °C, Liupanshui 20.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guiyang 26.9 °C (August), Liupanshui 23.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Guiyang 1.7 °C, Liupanshui 0.1 °C.
  • Liupanshui gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 1,243 mm against 1,011 mm.
  • Liupanshui gets about 342 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,649 h against 1,307 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Guiyang 4.4 cm, Liupanshui 14 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Guiyang in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Liupanshui in 9 of 12 months
