Guiyang vs Liupanshui climate

How the climate of Guiyang, China compares with Liupanshui, China, 187 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Guiyang is 3.1 °C (5.6 °F) warmer over the year: 15.6 °C against 12.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Guiyang is 4.4 °C warmer (Guiyang 23.8 °C, Liupanshui 19.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guiyang 26.7 °C (August), Liupanshui 22.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Guiyang 1.5 °C, Liupanshui -0.5 °C.
  • Liupanshui gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 1,327 mm against 1,065 mm.
  • Liupanshui gets about 342 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,649 h against 1,307 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Guiyang 4.4 cm, Liupanshui 14 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Guiyang in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Liupanshui in 9 of 12 months
