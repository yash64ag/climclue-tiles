Gujranwala vs Rawalpindi climate

How the climate of Gujranwala, Pakistan compares with Rawalpindi, Pakistan, 192 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gujranwala is 2.2 °C (3.9 °F) warmer over the year: 23.6 °C against 21.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Gujranwala is 3.4 °C warmer (Gujranwala 31.4 °C, Rawalpindi 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gujranwala 39.2 °C (June), Rawalpindi 36.7 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Gujranwala 5.9 °C, Rawalpindi 3.9 °C.
  • Rawalpindi gets 44% more rain and snow a year: 1,329 mm against 923 mm.
  • Gujranwala gets about 81 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,969 h against 2,889 h; about 1961–1990).
