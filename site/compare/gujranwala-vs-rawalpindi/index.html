Gujranwala vs Rawalpindi climate

How the climate of Gujranwala, Pakistan compares with Rawalpindi, Pakistan, 192 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gujranwala is 2.1 °C (3.7 °F) warmer over the year: 23.5 °C against 21.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Gujranwala is 3.3 °C warmer (Gujranwala 31.3 °C, Rawalpindi 28.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gujranwala 40.2 °C (June), Rawalpindi 38.5 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Gujranwala 4.8 °C, Rawalpindi 2.8 °C.
  • Rawalpindi gets 43% more rain and snow a year: 1,338 mm against 937 mm.
  • Gujranwala gets about 81 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,969 h against 2,889 h; about 1961–1990).
