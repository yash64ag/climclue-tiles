Haiphong vs Nanning climate

How the climate of Haiphong, Vietnam compares with Nanning, China, 275 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Haiphong is 1.5 °C (2.8 °F) warmer over the year: 24.0 °C against 22.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Haiphong is 3.6 °C warmer (Haiphong 17.0 °C, Nanning 13.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Haiphong 31.4 °C (July), Nanning 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Haiphong 14.1 °C, Nanning 9.7 °C.
  • Haiphong gets 31% more rain and snow a year: 1,869 mm against 1,426 mm.
  • Haiphong gets about 39 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,723 h against 1,684 h; about 1961–1990).
