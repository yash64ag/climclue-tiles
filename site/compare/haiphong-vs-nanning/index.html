Haiphong vs Nanning climate

How the climate of Haiphong, Vietnam compares with Nanning, China, 275 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Haiphong is 1.7 °C (3.0 °F) warmer over the year: 24.0 °C against 22.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Haiphong is 3.7 °C warmer (Haiphong 17.1 °C, Nanning 13.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Haiphong 31.5 °C (July), Nanning 31.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Haiphong 14.2 °C, Nanning 9.6 °C.
  • Haiphong gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 1,852 mm against 1,479 mm.
  • Haiphong gets about 39 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,723 h against 1,684 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Haiphong in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Haiphong in 9 of 12 months
