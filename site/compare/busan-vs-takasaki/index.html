Busan vs Takasaki climate

How the climate of Busan, South Korea compares with Takasaki, Japan, 912 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Takasaki is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 14.8 °C against 14.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Takasaki is 1.1 °C warmer (Busan 16.9 °C, Takasaki 17.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Busan 26.9 °C (August), Takasaki 28.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Busan -0.4 °C, Takasaki -1.4 °C.
  • Busan gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 1,594 mm against 1,347 mm.
  • Busan gets about 374 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,289 h against 1,914 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Busan 7.0 cm, Takasaki 30 cm (ERA5-Land).
