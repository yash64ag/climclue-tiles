Busan vs Takasaki climate

How the climate of Busan, South Korea compares with Takasaki, Japan, 912 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Busan is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 14.9 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Busan is 1.4 °C warmer (Busan 9.1 °C, Takasaki 7.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Busan 27.0 °C (August), Takasaki 28.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Busan 0.8 °C, Takasaki -1.4 °C.
  • Busan gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,638 mm against 1,356 mm.
  • Busan gets about 374 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,289 h against 1,914 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Busan 6.1 cm, Takasaki 13 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Takasaki in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Busan in 12 of 12 months
