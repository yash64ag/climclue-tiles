Oklahoma City vs Shiyan climate

How the climate of Oklahoma City, United States compares with Shiyan, China, 11,887 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Oklahoma City is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 15.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Oklahoma City is 1.6 °C warmer (Oklahoma City 27.6 °C, Shiyan 26.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Oklahoma City 33.6 °C (July), Shiyan 30.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Oklahoma City -0.4 °C, Shiyan -2.1 °C.
  • Shiyan gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 950 mm against 905 mm.
  • Oklahoma City gets about 1,233 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,031 h against 1,798 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Oklahoma City 25 cm, Shiyan 54 cm (ERA5-Land).
