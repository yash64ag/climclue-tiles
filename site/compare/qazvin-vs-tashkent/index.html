Qazvin vs Tashkent climate

How the climate of Qazvin, Iran compares with Tashkent, Uzbekistan, 1,751 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tashkent is 0.6 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 13.7 °C against 13.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Tashkent is 2.5 °C warmer (Qazvin 22.7 °C, Tashkent 25.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qazvin 32.7 °C (July), Tashkent 33.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Qazvin -5.5 °C, Tashkent -5.6 °C.
  • Qazvin gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 493 mm against 450 mm.
  • Tashkent gets about 348 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,790 h against 2,442 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Qazvin 87 cm, Tashkent 58 cm (ERA5-Land).
