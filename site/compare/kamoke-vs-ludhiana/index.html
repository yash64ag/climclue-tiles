How the climate of Kamoke, Pakistan compares with Ludhiana, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kamoke is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 23.6 °C against 23.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Kamoke is 1.2 °C warmer (Kamoke 31.3 °C, Ludhiana 30.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kamoke 40.3 °C (June), Ludhiana 39.4 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kamoke 4.9 °C, Ludhiana 4.9 °C.
  • Kamoke gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 858 mm against 821 mm.
  • Kamoke gets about 188 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,956 h against 2,768 h; about 1961–1990).
