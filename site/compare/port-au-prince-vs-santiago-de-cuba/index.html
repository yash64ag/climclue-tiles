How the climate of Port-au-Prince, Haiti compares with Santiago de Cuba, Cuba: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Port-au-Prince is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Port-au-Prince is 1.5 °C warmer (Port-au-Prince 26.4 °C, Santiago de Cuba 24.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Port-au-Prince 34.2 °C (July), Santiago de Cuba 33.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Port-au-Prince 20.0 °C, Santiago de Cuba 19.1 °C.
  • Port-au-Prince gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,242 mm against 1,119 mm.
  • Port-au-Prince gets about 428 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,795 h against 2,367 h; about 1961–1990).

  • Sunnier: Port-au-Prince in 12 of 12 months
