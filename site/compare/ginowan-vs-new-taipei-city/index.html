How the climate of Ginowan, Japan compares with New Taipei City, Taiwan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ginowan is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 22.7 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Ginowan is 1.1 °C warmer (Ginowan 18.2 °C, New Taipei City 17.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ginowan 31.2 °C (July), New Taipei City 33.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ginowan 13.7 °C, New Taipei City 12.8 °C.
  • New Taipei City gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 3,215 mm against 2,661 mm.
  • Ginowan gets about 109 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,833 h against 1,724 h; about 1961–1990).
