Shymkent vs Tashkent climate

How the climate of Shymkent, Kazakhstan compares with Tashkent, Uzbekistan, 120 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tashkent is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 13.7 °C against 12.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Tashkent is 1.6 °C warmer (Shymkent 0.5 °C, Tashkent 2.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Shymkent 34.1 °C (July), Tashkent 35.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Shymkent -5.9 °C, Tashkent -4.5 °C.
  • Shymkent gets 35% more rain and snow a year: 608 mm against 450 mm.
  • Shymkent gets about 22 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,812 h against 2,790 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Shymkent 134 cm, Tashkent 58 cm (ERA5-Land).
