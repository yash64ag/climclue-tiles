Shymkent vs Tashkent climate

How the climate of Shymkent, Kazakhstan compares with Tashkent, Uzbekistan, 120 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tashkent is 1.5 °C (2.8 °F) warmer over the year: 13.8 °C against 12.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Tashkent is 2.0 °C warmer (Shymkent 0.1 °C, Tashkent 2.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Shymkent 32.3 °C (July), Tashkent 33.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Shymkent -6.7 °C, Tashkent -5.5 °C.
  • Shymkent gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 631 mm against 457 mm.
  • Shymkent gets about 22 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,812 h against 2,790 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Shymkent 134 cm, Tashkent 58 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Tashkent in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Tashkent in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Shymkent in 7 of 12 months
