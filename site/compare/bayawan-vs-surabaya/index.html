Bayawan vs Surabaya climate

How the climate of Bayawan, Philippines compares with Surabaya, Indonesia, 2,157 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Surabaya is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 27.8 °C against 27.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Surabaya is 1.8 °C warmer (Bayawan 27.1 °C, Surabaya 28.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bayawan 32.7 °C (April), Surabaya 33.2 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Bayawan 23.0 °C, Surabaya 23.3 °C.
  • Bayawan gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 2,046 mm against 1,841 mm.
  • Surabaya gets about 1,022 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 2,221 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
