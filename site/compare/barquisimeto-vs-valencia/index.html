How the climate of Barquisimeto, Venezuela compares with Valencia, Venezuela: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Barquisimeto is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 24.7 °C against 24.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Barquisimeto is 0.9 °C warmer (Barquisimeto 25.1 °C, Valencia 24.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Barquisimeto 30.9 °C (March), Valencia 31.4 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Barquisimeto 18.5 °C, Valencia 16.1 °C.
  • Valencia gets 97% more rain and snow a year: 1,379 mm against 700 mm.
  • Valencia gets about 106 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,706 h against 2,600 h; about 1961–1990).
