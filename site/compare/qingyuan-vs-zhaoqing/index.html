Qingyuan vs Zhaoqing climate

How the climate of Qingyuan, China compares with Zhaoqing, China, 93 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhaoqing is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 22.7 °C against 22.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Zhaoqing is 0.9 °C warmer (Qingyuan 13.2 °C, Zhaoqing 14.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qingyuan 31.6 °C (August), Zhaoqing 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Qingyuan 8.8 °C, Zhaoqing 9.9 °C.
  • Qingyuan gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 2,101 mm against 1,703 mm.
  • Zhaoqing gets about 56 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,814 h against 1,758 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Zhaoqing in 11 of 12 months
