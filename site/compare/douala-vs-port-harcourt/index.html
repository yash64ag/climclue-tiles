How the climate of Douala, Cameroon compares with Port Harcourt, Nigeria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Port Harcourt is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.1 °C against 26.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Port Harcourt is 0.3 °C warmer (Douala 27.2 °C, Port Harcourt 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Douala 31.6 °C (February), Port Harcourt 32.6 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Douala 21.9 °C, Port Harcourt 21.4 °C.
  • Douala gets 40% more rain and snow a year: 3,194 mm against 2,285 mm.
  • Douala gets about 331 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,704 h against 1,373 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
