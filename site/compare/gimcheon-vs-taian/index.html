Gimcheon vs Tai’an climate

How the climate of Gimcheon, South Korea compares with Tai’an, China, 987 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gimcheon is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 13.4 °C against 13.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Tai’an is 1.5 °C warmer (Gimcheon 22.9 °C, Tai’an 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gimcheon 29.2 °C (August), Tai’an 30.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gimcheon -5.7 °C, Tai’an -6.5 °C.
  • Gimcheon gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 1,063 mm against 826 mm.
  • Tai’an gets about 65 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,496 h against 2,431 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gimcheon 21 cm, Tai’an 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
