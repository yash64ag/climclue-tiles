How the climate of Gimcheon, South Korea compares with Tai’an, Shandong, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Gimcheon is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 13.4 °C against 13.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Gimcheon is 1.5 °C warmer (Gimcheon 1.6 °C, Tai’an, Shandong 0.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gimcheon 30.5 °C (August), Tai’an, Shandong 30.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gimcheon -5.8 °C, Tai’an, Shandong -6.2 °C.
  • Gimcheon gets 36% more rain and snow a year: 1,097 mm against 809 mm.
  • Tai’an, Shandong gets about 65 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,496 h against 2,431 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gimcheon 21 cm, Tai’an, Shandong 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
