How the climate of Adelaide, Australia compares with Coimbra, Portugal: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Adelaide is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 16.4 °C against 16.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Adelaide is 12.0 °C warmer (Adelaide 21.8 °C, Coimbra 9.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Adelaide 27.8 °C (February), Coimbra 28.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Adelaide 7.6 °C, Coimbra 5.1 °C.
  • Coimbra gets 41% more rain and snow a year: 996 mm against 705 mm.
  • Adelaide gets about 132 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,682 h against 2,551 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
