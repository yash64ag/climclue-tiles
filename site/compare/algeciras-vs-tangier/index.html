Algeciras vs Tangier climate

How the climate of Algeciras, Spain compares with Tangier, Morocco, 51 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tangier is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 17.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Tangier is 1.0 °C warmer (Algeciras 22.7 °C, Tangier 23.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Algeciras 26.9 °C (August), Tangier 28.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Algeciras 10.3 °C, Tangier 9.6 °C.
  • Algeciras gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 784 mm against 655 mm.
  • Tangier gets about 98 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,823 h against 2,725 h; about 1961–1990).
