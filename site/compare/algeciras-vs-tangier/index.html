Algeciras vs Tangier climate

How the climate of Algeciras, Spain compares with Tangier, Morocco, 51 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tangier is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 18.0 °C against 17.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Tangier is 0.9 °C warmer (Algeciras 20.6 °C, Tangier 21.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Algeciras 26.3 °C (August), Tangier 29.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Algeciras 10.7 °C, Tangier 9.2 °C.
  • Algeciras gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 787 mm against 693 mm.
  • Tangier gets about 98 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,823 h against 2,725 h; about 1961–1990).
