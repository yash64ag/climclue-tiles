How the climate of Nepalgunj, Nepal compares with Prayagraj, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Prayagraj is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 25.7 °C against 24.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Prayagraj is 2.7 °C warmer (Nepalgunj 30.8 °C, Prayagraj 33.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nepalgunj 38.0 °C (May), Prayagraj 41.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Nepalgunj 7.2 °C, Prayagraj 8.1 °C.
  • Nepalgunj gets 75% more rain and snow a year: 1,606 mm against 916 mm.
  • Prayagraj gets about 170 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,666 h against 2,496 h; about 1961–1990).
