How the climate of Amarillo, United States compares with Xinxiang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Xinxiang is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 15.0 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Xinxiang is 2.9 °C warmer (Amarillo 18.8 °C, Xinxiang 21.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amarillo 33.0 °C (July), Xinxiang 32.7 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Amarillo -4.9 °C, Xinxiang -4.9 °C.
  • Xinxiang gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 606 mm against 550 mm.
  • Amarillo gets about 940 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 2,303 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amarillo 33 cm, Xinxiang 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
