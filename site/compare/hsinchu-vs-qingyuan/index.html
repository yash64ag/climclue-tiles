Hsinchu vs Qingyuan climate

How the climate of Hsinchu, Taiwan compares with Qingyuan, China, 814 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Hsinchu is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 22.9 °C against 22.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Hsinchu is 2.9 °C warmer (Hsinchu 17.7 °C, Qingyuan 14.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hsinchu 30.1 °C (July), Qingyuan 31.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Hsinchu 13.5 °C, Qingyuan 8.8 °C.
  • Qingyuan gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 2,101 mm against 1,936 mm.
  • Hsinchu gets about 26 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,784 h against 1,758 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
