Jaffna vs Tirunelveli climate

How the climate of Jaffna, Sri Lanka compares with Tirunelveli, India, 276 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tirunelveli is 1.1 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 28.7 °C against 27.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Tirunelveli is 2.0 °C warmer (Jaffna 27.9 °C, Tirunelveli 29.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jaffna 30.8 °C (May), Tirunelveli 36.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Jaffna 24.4 °C, Tirunelveli 20.6 °C.
  • Jaffna gets 50% more rain and snow a year: 1,265 mm against 842 mm.
  • Jaffna gets about 113 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,514 h against 2,401 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Tirunelveli in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Jaffna in 9 of 12 months
