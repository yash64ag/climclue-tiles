Jaffna vs Tirunelveli climate

How the climate of Jaffna, Sri Lanka compares with Tirunelveli, India, 276 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tirunelveli is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 28.9 °C against 27.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Tirunelveli is 2.1 °C warmer (Jaffna 27.8 °C, Tirunelveli 29.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jaffna 30.4 °C (April), Tirunelveli 37.1 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Jaffna 24.4 °C, Tirunelveli 20.7 °C.
  • Jaffna gets 54% more rain and snow a year: 1,268 mm against 823 mm.
  • Jaffna gets about 113 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,514 h against 2,401 h; about 1961–1990).
