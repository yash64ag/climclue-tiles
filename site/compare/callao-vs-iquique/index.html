How the climate of Callao, Peru compares with Iquique, Chile: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Callao is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 17.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Callao is 1.6 °C warmer (Callao 16.9 °C, Iquique 15.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Callao 26.2 °C (February), Iquique 26.7 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Callao 12.8 °C, Iquique 10.8 °C.
  • Iquique gets 42% more rain and snow a year: 30 mm against 21 mm.
  • Iquique gets about 1,308 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,813 h against 1,505 h; about 1961–1990).
