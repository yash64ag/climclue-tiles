How the climate of Gyeongju, South Korea compares with Huai'an, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Huai'an is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 15.0 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Huai'an is 2.2 °C warmer (Gyeongju 22.3 °C, Huai'an 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gyeongju 30.5 °C (August), Huai'an 30.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gyeongju -3.9 °C, Huai'an -3.0 °C.
  • Gyeongju gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,098 mm against 973 mm.
  • Gyeongju gets about 171 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,350 h against 2,179 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gyeongju 12 cm, Huai'an 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
