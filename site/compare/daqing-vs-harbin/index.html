Daqing vs Harbin climate

How the climate of Daqing, China compares with Harbin, China, 157 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Harbin is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 4.6 °C against 4.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Harbin is 0.7 °C warmer (Daqing 7.0 °C, Harbin 7.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daqing 28.4 °C (July), Harbin 27.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Daqing -24.6 °C, Harbin -24.4 °C.
  • Harbin gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 551 mm against 460 mm.
  • Daqing gets about 103 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,653 h against 2,550 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daqing 19 cm, Harbin 33 cm (ERA5-Land).
