Jiangyin vs Wuxi, Jiangsu climate

How the climate of Jiangyin, China compares with Wuxi, Jiangsu, China, 38 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Wuxi, Jiangsu is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 16.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Wuxi, Jiangsu is 0.7 °C warmer (Jiangyin 11.6 °C, Wuxi, Jiangsu 12.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiangyin 31.8 °C (July), Wuxi, Jiangsu 32.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jiangyin -0.7 °C, Wuxi, Jiangsu -0.1 °C.
  • Wuxi, Jiangsu gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,210 mm against 1,166 mm.
  • Jiangyin gets about 32 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,031 h against 1,999 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jiangyin 14 cm, Wuxi, Jiangsu 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
