Addis Ababa vs Quillacollo climate

How the climate of Addis Ababa, Ethiopia compares with Quillacollo, Bolivia, 11,890 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Addis Ababa is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 14.5 °C against 14.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Addis Ababa is 3.1 °C warmer (Addis Ababa 16.1 °C, Quillacollo 13.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Addis Ababa 22.0 °C (March), Quillacollo 20.3 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Addis Ababa 6.2 °C, Quillacollo 4.9 °C.
  • Addis Ababa gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 1,199 mm against 952 mm.
  • Addis Ababa gets about 357 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,828 h against 2,471 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
