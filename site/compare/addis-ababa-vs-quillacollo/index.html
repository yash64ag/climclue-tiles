Addis Ababa vs Quillacollo climate

How the climate of Addis Ababa, Ethiopia compares with Quillacollo, Bolivia, 11,890 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Addis Ababa is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 15.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Addis Ababa is 2.8 °C warmer (Addis Ababa 15.5 °C, Quillacollo 12.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Addis Ababa 23.9 °C (March), Quillacollo 25.1 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Addis Ababa 5.7 °C, Quillacollo 1.7 °C.
  • Addis Ababa gets 48% more rain and snow a year: 1,142 mm against 773 mm.
  • Addis Ababa gets about 357 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,828 h against 2,471 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
