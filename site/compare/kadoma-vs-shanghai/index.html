Kadoma vs Shanghai climate

How the climate of Kadoma, Japan compares with Shanghai, China, 1,372 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kadoma is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.7 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Kadoma is 1.0 °C warmer (Kadoma 5.7 °C, Shanghai 4.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kadoma 31.9 °C (August), Shanghai 31.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kadoma 0.7 °C, Shanghai 0.9 °C.
  • Kadoma gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,466 mm against 1,459 mm.
  • Shanghai gets about 141 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,969 h against 1,828 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kadoma 5.1 cm, Shanghai 7.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
