Cixi vs Shaoxing climate

How the climate of Cixi, China compares with Shaoxing, China, 67 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shaoxing is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 17.5 °C against 17.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Shaoxing is 0.9 °C warmer (Cixi 15.8 °C, Shaoxing 16.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cixi 32.9 °C (July), Shaoxing 34.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Cixi 2.0 °C, Shaoxing 1.2 °C.
  • Cixi gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,491 mm against 1,452 mm.
  • Cixi gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,883 h against 1,876 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Cixi 8.8 cm, Shaoxing 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
