Barranquilla vs La Concepción climate

How the climate of Barranquilla, Colombia compares with La Concepción, Venezuela, 324 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • La Concepción is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 28.0 °C against 27.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when La Concepción is 1.7 °C warmer (Barranquilla 26.6 °C, La Concepción 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Barranquilla 31.7 °C (June), La Concepción 33.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Barranquilla 22.6 °C, La Concepción 22.4 °C.
  • La Concepción gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 881 mm against 857 mm.
  • La Concepción gets about 53 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,534 h against 2,481 h; about 1961–1990).
