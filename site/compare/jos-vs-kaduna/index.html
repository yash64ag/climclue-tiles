Jos vs Kaduna climate

How the climate of Jos, Nigeria compares with Kaduna, Nigeria, 172 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kaduna is 3.4 °C (6.2 °F) warmer over the year: 25.0 °C against 21.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Kaduna is 4.1 °C warmer (Jos 24.2 °C, Kaduna 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jos 31.1 °C (April), Kaduna 35.5 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Jos 11.9 °C, Kaduna 15.1 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,201 mm and 1,204 mm).
  • Kaduna gets about 40 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,685 h against 2,645 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Kaduna in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Kaduna in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Kaduna in 7 of 12 months
