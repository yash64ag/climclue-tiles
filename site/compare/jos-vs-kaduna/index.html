Jos vs Kaduna climate

How the climate of Jos, Nigeria compares with Kaduna, Nigeria, 172 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kaduna is 3.6 °C (6.5 °F) warmer over the year: 25.0 °C against 21.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Kaduna is 4.3 °C warmer (Jos 24.1 °C, Kaduna 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jos 31.4 °C (April), Kaduna 35.5 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Jos 12.9 °C, Kaduna 16.5 °C.
  • Jos gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,300 mm against 1,203 mm.
  • Kaduna gets about 40 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,685 h against 2,645 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Kaduna in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Kaduna in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Kaduna in 7 of 12 months
