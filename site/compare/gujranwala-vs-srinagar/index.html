Gujranwala vs Srinagar climate

How the climate of Gujranwala, Pakistan compares with Srinagar, India, 222 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gujranwala is 11.3 °C (20.4 °F) warmer over the year: 23.6 °C against 12.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Gujranwala is 14.6 °C warmer (Gujranwala 31.4 °C, Srinagar 16.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gujranwala 39.2 °C (June), Srinagar 26.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gujranwala 5.9 °C, Srinagar -4.8 °C.
  • Srinagar gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,119 mm against 923 mm.
  • Gujranwala gets about 223 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,969 h against 2,747 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gujranwala 0 cm, Srinagar 374 cm (ERA5-Land).
