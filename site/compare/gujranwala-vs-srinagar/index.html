How the climate of Gujranwala, Pakistan compares with Srinagar, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Gujranwala is 10.8 °C (19.4 °F) warmer over the year: 23.5 °C against 12.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Gujranwala is 13.8 °C warmer (Gujranwala 31.3 °C, Srinagar 17.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gujranwala 40.2 °C (June), Srinagar 29.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gujranwala 4.8 °C, Srinagar -3.6 °C.
  • Srinagar gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,009 mm against 937 mm.
  • Gujranwala gets about 223 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,969 h against 2,747 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gujranwala 0 cm, Srinagar 72 cm (ERA5-Land).
