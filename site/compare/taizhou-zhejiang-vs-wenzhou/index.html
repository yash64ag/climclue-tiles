Taizhou, Zhejiang vs Wenzhou climate

How the climate of Taizhou, Zhejiang, China compares with Wenzhou, China, 105 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Taizhou, Zhejiang and Wenzhou have the same yearly average temperature (18.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Taizhou, Zhejiang is 1.0 °C warmer (Taizhou, Zhejiang 29.2 °C, Wenzhou 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Taizhou, Zhejiang 31.9 °C (July), Wenzhou 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Taizhou, Zhejiang 4.5 °C, Wenzhou 4.8 °C.
  • Taizhou, Zhejiang gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,707 mm against 1,634 mm.
  • Taizhou, Zhejiang gets about 17 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,811 h against 1,794 h; about 1961–1990).
