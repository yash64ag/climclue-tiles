How the climate of Taizhou, Zhejiang, China compares with Wenzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Wenzhou is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 18.7 °C against 18.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Wenzhou is 1.1 °C warmer (Taizhou, Zhejiang 7.5 °C, Wenzhou 8.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Taizhou, Zhejiang 31.9 °C (July), Wenzhou 32.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Taizhou, Zhejiang 4.5 °C, Wenzhou 5.0 °C.
  • Taizhou, Zhejiang gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,707 mm against 1,634 mm.
  • Taizhou, Zhejiang gets about 17 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,811 h against 1,794 h; about 1961–1990).
