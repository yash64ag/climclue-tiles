Taizhou, Zhejiang vs Wenzhou climate

How the climate of Taizhou, Zhejiang, China compares with Wenzhou, China, 105 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Taizhou, Zhejiang is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 18.6 °C against 18.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Taizhou, Zhejiang is 1.2 °C warmer (Taizhou, Zhejiang 29.3 °C, Wenzhou 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Taizhou, Zhejiang 32.2 °C (July), Wenzhou 30.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Taizhou, Zhejiang 3.3 °C, Wenzhou 4.0 °C.
  • Wenzhou gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,809 mm against 1,688 mm.
  • Taizhou, Zhejiang gets about 17 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,811 h against 1,794 h; about 1961–1990).
