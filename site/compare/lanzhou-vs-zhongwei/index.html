How the climate of Lanzhou, China compares with Zhongwei, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lanzhou and Zhongwei have the same yearly average temperature (11.5 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Lanzhou is 2.3 °C warmer (Lanzhou -2.5 °C, Zhongwei -4.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lanzhou 29.8 °C (July), Zhongwei 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lanzhou -8.8 °C, Zhongwei -11.5 °C.
  • Lanzhou gets 52% more rain and snow a year: 285 mm against 188 mm.
  • Zhongwei gets about 221 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,697 h against 2,476 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lanzhou 7.4 cm, Zhongwei 5.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
