Lanzhou vs Zhongwei climate

How the climate of Lanzhou, China compares with Zhongwei, China, 202 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhongwei is 0.6 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 11.4 °C against 10.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Zhongwei is 2.6 °C warmer (Lanzhou 21.1 °C, Zhongwei 23.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lanzhou 28.3 °C (July), Zhongwei 31.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lanzhou -8.8 °C, Zhongwei -11.4 °C.
  • Lanzhou gets 68% more rain and snow a year: 311 mm against 185 mm.
  • Zhongwei gets about 221 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,697 h against 2,476 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lanzhou 7.4 cm, Zhongwei 5.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
