Havana vs Mérida climate

How the climate of Havana, Cuba compares with Mérida, Mexico, 784 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mérida is 1.0 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 26.4 °C against 25.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Mérida is 2.9 °C warmer (Havana 24.9 °C, Mérida 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Havana 31.7 °C (August), Mérida 35.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Havana 19.8 °C, Mérida 18.9 °C.
  • Havana gets 35% more rain and snow a year: 1,383 mm against 1,021 mm.
  • Havana gets about 315 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,950 h against 2,635 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Mérida in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Havana in 11 of 12 months
