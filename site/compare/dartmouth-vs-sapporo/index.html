How the climate of Dartmouth, Canada compares with Sapporo, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Sapporo is 1.6 °C (2.9 °F) warmer over the year: 8.7 °C against 7.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Sapporo is 3.4 °C warmer (Dartmouth 18.7 °C, Sapporo 22.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dartmouth 23.4 °C (July), Sapporo 26.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Dartmouth -9.4 °C, Sapporo -7.5 °C.
  • Sapporo gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,573 mm against 1,448 mm.
  • Dartmouth gets about 158 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,835 h against 1,677 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Dartmouth 164 cm, Sapporo 417 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Sapporo in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Dartmouth in 8 of 12 months
