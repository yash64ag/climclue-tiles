Bandar Lampung vs Tangerang climate

How the climate of Bandar Lampung, Indonesia compares with Tangerang, Indonesia, 173 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tangerang is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 25.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Tangerang is 2.0 °C warmer (Bandar Lampung 25.6 °C, Tangerang 27.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bandar Lampung 29.1 °C (October), Tangerang 31.3 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Bandar Lampung 22.3 °C, Tangerang 23.1 °C.
  • Bandar Lampung gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 2,593 mm against 2,341 mm.
  • Tangerang gets about 154 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,623 h against 2,468 h; about 1961–1990).
