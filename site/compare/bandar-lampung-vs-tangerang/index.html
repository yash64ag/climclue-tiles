Bandar Lampung vs Tangerang climate

How the climate of Bandar Lampung, Indonesia compares with Tangerang, Indonesia, 173 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tangerang is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Tangerang is 1.4 °C warmer (Bandar Lampung 26.1 °C, Tangerang 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bandar Lampung 31.6 °C (May), Tangerang 32.9 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Bandar Lampung 20.6 °C, Tangerang 21.5 °C.
  • Tangerang gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 2,445 mm against 2,377 mm.
  • Tangerang gets about 154 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,623 h against 2,468 h; about 1961–1990).
