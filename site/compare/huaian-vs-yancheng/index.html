Huai'an vs Yancheng climate

How the climate of Huai'an, China compares with Yancheng, China, 109 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Huai'an and Yancheng have the same yearly average temperature (15.0 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Huai'an is 0.7 °C warmer (Huai'an 24.4 °C, Yancheng 23.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huai'an 30.8 °C (July), Yancheng 30.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Huai'an -3.2 °C, Yancheng -2.7 °C.
  • Yancheng gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,052 mm against 973 mm.
  • Huai'an gets about 3 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,179 h against 2,176 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Huai'an 13 cm, Yancheng 10 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Huai'an in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Huai'an in 8 of 12 months
