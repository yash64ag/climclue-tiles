Guilin vs Liuzhou climate

How the climate of Guilin, China compares with Liuzhou, China, 139 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Liuzhou is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 21.4 °C against 20.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Liuzhou is 2.0 °C warmer (Guilin 11.4 °C, Liuzhou 13.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guilin 31.5 °C (August), Liuzhou 31.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Guilin 5.6 °C, Liuzhou 7.5 °C.
  • Guilin gets 43% more rain and snow a year: 2,144 mm against 1,503 mm.
  • Liuzhou gets about 4 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,568 h against 1,565 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Liuzhou in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Liuzhou in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Liuzhou in 7 of 12 months (1 equal)
