Daegu vs Suqian climate

How the climate of Daegu, South Korea compares with Suqian, China, 963 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Suqian is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 14.8 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Suqian is 1.6 °C warmer (Daegu 23.1 °C, Suqian 24.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daegu 31.1 °C (August), Suqian 31.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Daegu -5.3 °C, Suqian -3.7 °C.
  • Daegu gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 979 mm against 963 mm.
  • Daegu gets about 258 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,483 h against 2,225 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daegu 13 cm, Suqian 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
