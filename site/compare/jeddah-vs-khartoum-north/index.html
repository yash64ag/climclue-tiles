Jeddah vs Khartoum North climate

How the climate of Jeddah, Saudi Arabia compares with Khartoum North, Sudan, 955 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Khartoum North is 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) warmer over the year: 29.5 °C against 28.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Khartoum North is 4.8 °C warmer (Jeddah 29.4 °C, Khartoum North 34.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jeddah 33.9 °C (August), Khartoum North 40.5 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Jeddah 20.9 °C, Khartoum North 14.9 °C.
  • Khartoum North gets 48% more rain and snow a year: 102 mm against 69 mm.
  • Khartoum North gets about 200 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,517 h against 3,317 h; about 1961–1990).

  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
