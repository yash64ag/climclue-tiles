How the climate of Jiading, China compares with Shanghai, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Shanghai is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Shanghai is 0.4 °C warmer (Jiading 6.8 °C, Shanghai 7.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiading 31.7 °C (July), Shanghai 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jiading 0.8 °C, Shanghai 1.1 °C.
  • Shanghai gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,476 mm against 1,422 mm.
  • Jiading gets about 10 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,979 h against 1,969 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jiading 9.5 cm, Shanghai 7.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
