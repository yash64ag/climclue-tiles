Semarang vs Surabaya climate

How the climate of Semarang, Indonesia compares with Surabaya, Indonesia, 259 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Surabaya is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 27.8 °C against 27.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Surabaya is 1.0 °C warmer (Semarang 27.4 °C, Surabaya 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Semarang 33.1 °C (September), Surabaya 32.5 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Semarang 22.9 °C, Surabaya 23.8 °C.
  • Semarang gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 2,415 mm against 1,854 mm.
  • Surabaya gets about 407 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 2,835 h; about 1961–1990).
