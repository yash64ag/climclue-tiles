Semarang vs Surabaya climate

How the climate of Semarang, Indonesia compares with Surabaya, Indonesia, 259 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Surabaya is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 27.8 °C against 27.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Surabaya is 1.2 °C warmer (Semarang 27.3 °C, Surabaya 28.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Semarang 34.1 °C (September), Surabaya 33.2 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Semarang 21.6 °C, Surabaya 23.3 °C.
  • Semarang gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 2,475 mm against 1,841 mm.
  • Surabaya gets about 407 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 2,835 h; about 1961–1990).
