How the climate of Ashgabat, Turkmenistan compares with Bokhtar, Tajikistan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bokhtar is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 17.7 °C against 17.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Bokhtar is 1.5 °C warmer (Ashgabat 10.6 °C, Bokhtar 12.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ashgabat 38.9 °C (July), Bokhtar 39.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ashgabat -1.1 °C, Bokhtar -0.2 °C.
  • Bokhtar gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 315 mm against 274 mm.
  • Ashgabat gets about 4 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,708 h against 2,704 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ashgabat 13 cm, Bokhtar 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
