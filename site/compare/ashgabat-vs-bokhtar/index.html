Ashgabat vs Bokhtar climate

How the climate of Ashgabat, Turkmenistan compares with Bokhtar, Tajikistan, 912 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bokhtar is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 17.7 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Bokhtar is 1.8 °C warmer (Ashgabat 10.4 °C, Bokhtar 12.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ashgabat 36.3 °C (July), Bokhtar 37.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ashgabat -0.9 °C, Bokhtar -1.3 °C.
  • Bokhtar gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 312 mm against 278 mm.
  • Ashgabat gets about 4 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,708 h against 2,704 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ashgabat 28 cm, Bokhtar 29 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
