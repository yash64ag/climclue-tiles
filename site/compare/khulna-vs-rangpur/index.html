Khulna vs Rangpur climate

How the climate of Khulna, Bangladesh compares with Rangpur, Bangladesh, 328 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Khulna is 1.1 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 25.9 °C against 24.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Khulna is 2.0 °C warmer (Khulna 29.4 °C, Rangpur 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Khulna 34.1 °C (April), Rangpur 32.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Khulna 13.2 °C, Rangpur 11.7 °C.
  • Rangpur gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 2,428 mm against 1,886 mm.
  • Rangpur gets about 104 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,194 h against 2,090 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
