Bogor vs Iquitos climate

How the climate of Bogor, Indonesia compares with Iquitos, Peru, 18,865 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bogor is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 25.8 °C against 25.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Bogor is 0.9 °C warmer (Bogor 25.9 °C, Iquitos 24.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bogor 30.9 °C (September), Iquitos 29.7 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Bogor 20.9 °C, Iquitos 22.3 °C.
  • Bogor gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 3,567 mm against 3,280 mm.
  • Bogor gets about 562 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,625 h against 2,063 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Bogor in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Bogor in 10 of 12 months
