Bogor vs Iquitos climate

How the climate of Bogor, Indonesia compares with Iquitos, Peru, 18,865 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Iquitos is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 25.7 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Iquitos is 1.2 °C warmer (Bogor 24.9 °C, Iquitos 26.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bogor 31.5 °C (September), Iquitos 31.2 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Bogor 19.9 °C, Iquitos 19.7 °C.
  • Bogor gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 3,591 mm against 3,275 mm.
  • Bogor gets about 562 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,625 h against 2,063 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Iquitos in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Bogor in 10 of 12 months
