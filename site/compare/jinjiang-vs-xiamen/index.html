Jinjiang vs Xiamen climate

How the climate of Jinjiang, China compares with Xiamen, China, 62 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jinjiang is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 21.4 °C against 21.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Jinjiang is 0.6 °C warmer (Jinjiang 28.6 °C, Xiamen 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinjiang 31.0 °C (July), Xiamen 30.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinjiang 9.2 °C, Xiamen 9.6 °C.
  • Xiamen gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,395 mm against 1,252 mm.
  • Xiamen gets about 99 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,067 h against 1,967 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
