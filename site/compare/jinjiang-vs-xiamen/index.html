Jinjiang, Fujian vs Xiamen climate

How the climate of Jinjiang, Fujian, China compares with Xiamen, China, 62 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jinjiang, Fujian and Xiamen have the same yearly average temperature (21.4 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Jinjiang, Fujian is 0.4 °C warmer (Jinjiang, Fujian 28.7 °C, Xiamen 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinjiang, Fujian 32.4 °C (July), Xiamen 31.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinjiang, Fujian 9.7 °C, Xiamen 9.9 °C.
  • Xiamen gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,343 mm against 1,228 mm.
  • Xiamen gets about 99 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,067 h against 1,967 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
