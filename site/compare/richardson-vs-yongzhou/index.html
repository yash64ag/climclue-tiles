How the climate of Richardson, United States compares with Yongzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yongzhou is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 19.1 °C against 18.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Richardson is 1.2 °C warmer (Richardson 14.0 °C, Yongzhou 12.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Richardson 35.2 °C (August), Yongzhou 34.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Richardson 2.0 °C, Yongzhou 3.9 °C.
  • Yongzhou gets 37% more rain and snow a year: 1,454 mm against 1,063 mm.
  • Richardson gets about 1,218 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,798 h against 1,580 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Richardson 0.8 cm, Yongzhou 6.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
