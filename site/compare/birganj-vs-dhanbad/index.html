Birgañj vs Dhanbad climate

How the climate of Birgañj, Nepal compares with Dhanbad, India, 390 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Birgañj is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 25.0 °C against 24.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Birgañj is 1.1 °C warmer (Birgañj 28.9 °C, Dhanbad 27.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Birgañj 36.2 °C (April), Dhanbad 37.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Birgañj 9.9 °C, Dhanbad 10.2 °C.
  • Birgañj gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,734 mm against 1,564 mm.
  • Dhanbad gets about 2 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,186 h against 2,184 h; about 1961–1990).
