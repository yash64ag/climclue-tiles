Main differences

  • Lexington-Fayette is 0.4 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 13.1 °C against 12.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Lexington-Fayette is 1.6 °C warmer (Lexington-Fayette 22.6 °C, Srinagar 20.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lexington-Fayette 29.5 °C (July), Srinagar 26.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lexington-Fayette -2.7 °C, Srinagar -4.8 °C.
  • Lexington-Fayette gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 1,226 mm against 1,009 mm.
  • Srinagar gets about 568 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,747 h against 2,178 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lexington-Fayette 45 cm, Srinagar 72 cm (ERA5-Land).
