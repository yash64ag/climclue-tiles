Gumi vs Jinan climate

How the climate of Gumi, South Korea compares with Jinan, China, 1,016 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jinan is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 14.3 °C against 13.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Jinan is 2.9 °C warmer (Gumi 23.0 °C, Jinan 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gumi 30.8 °C (August), Jinan 31.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gumi -6.0 °C, Jinan -5.7 °C.
  • Gumi gets 84% more rain and snow a year: 1,207 mm against 657 mm.
  • Jinan gets about 82 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,554 h against 2,472 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gumi 17 cm, Jinan 10 cm (ERA5-Land).
