Gumi vs Jinan climate

How the climate of Gumi, South Korea compares with Jinan, China, 1,016 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jinan is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 14.2 °C against 13.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Jinan is 2.7 °C warmer (Gumi 23.1 °C, Jinan 25.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gumi 29.5 °C (August), Jinan 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gumi -5.3 °C, Jinan -5.8 °C.
  • Gumi gets 61% more rain and snow a year: 1,077 mm against 669 mm.
  • Jinan gets about 82 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,554 h against 2,472 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gumi 17 cm, Jinan 10 cm (ERA5-Land).
