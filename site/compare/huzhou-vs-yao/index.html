How the climate of Huzhou, China compares with Yao, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Huzhou is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.9 °C against 16.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Huzhou is 1.7 °C warmer (Huzhou 21.3 °C, Yao 19.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huzhou 32.8 °C (July), Yao 33.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Huzhou 0.5 °C, Yao 1.7 °C.
  • Yao gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,373 mm against 1,292 mm.
  • Huzhou gets about 92 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,934 h against 1,842 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Huzhou 17 cm, Yao 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
