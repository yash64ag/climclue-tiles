Algiers vs Tripoli climate

How the climate of Algiers, Algeria compares with Tripoli, Libya, 1,016 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tripoli is 2.4 °C (4.3 °F) warmer over the year: 20.4 °C against 18.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Tripoli is 3.5 °C warmer (Algiers 19.9 °C, Tripoli 23.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Algiers 30.4 °C (August), Tripoli 31.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Algiers 8.0 °C, Tripoli 10.2 °C.
  • Algiers gets 158% more rain and snow a year: 749 mm against 290 mm.
  • Tripoli gets about 333 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,219 h against 2,886 h; about 1961–1990).
