Huế vs Santiago climate

How the climate of Huế, Vietnam compares with Santiago, Philippines, 1,487 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Santiago is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 26.3 °C against 25.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Santiago is 1.9 °C warmer (Huế 21.1 °C, Santiago 23.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huế 32.5 °C (July), Santiago 32.4 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Huế 18.7 °C, Santiago 19.4 °C.
  • Huế gets 57% more rain and snow a year: 3,151 mm against 2,010 mm.
  • Santiago gets about 248 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,508 h against 2,260 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Santiago in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: Huế in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Santiago in 9 of 12 months
