Huế vs Santiago climate

How the climate of Huế, Vietnam compares with Santiago, Philippines, 1,487 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Santiago is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 26.4 °C against 26.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Santiago is 1.8 °C warmer (Huế 21.4 °C, Santiago 23.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huế 33.8 °C (June), Santiago 34.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Huế 18.3 °C, Santiago 18.5 °C.
  • Huế gets 55% more rain and snow a year: 2,948 mm against 1,899 mm.
  • Santiago gets about 248 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,508 h against 2,260 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Santiago in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: Huế in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Santiago in 9 of 12 months
