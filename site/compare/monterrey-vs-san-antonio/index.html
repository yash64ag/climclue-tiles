Monterrey vs San Antonio climate

How the climate of Monterrey, Mexico compares with San Antonio, United States, 453 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Monterrey and San Antonio have the same yearly average temperature (20.5 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when San Antonio is 3.0 °C warmer (Monterrey 26.1 °C, San Antonio 29.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Monterrey 30.8 °C (August), San Antonio 34.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Monterrey 8.7 °C, San Antonio 7.0 °C.
  • San Antonio gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 822 mm against 688 mm.
  • San Antonio gets about 685 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,571 h against 1,887 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Monterrey in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: San Antonio in 12 of 12 months
