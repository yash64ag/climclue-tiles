How the climate of Kunming, China compares with Liupanshui, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kunming is 3.1 °C (5.6 °F) warmer over the year: 16.3 °C against 13.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Kunming is 5.6 °C warmer (Kunming 9.8 °C, Liupanshui 4.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kunming 24.8 °C (May), Liupanshui 24.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kunming 3.4 °C, Liupanshui -0.4 °C.
  • Liupanshui gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 1,243 mm against 929 mm.
  • Kunming gets about 553 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,203 h against 1,649 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kunming 0 cm, Liupanshui 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
