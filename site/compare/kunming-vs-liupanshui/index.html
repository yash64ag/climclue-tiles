Kunming vs Liupanshui climate

How the climate of Kunming, China compares with Liupanshui, China, 273 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Kunming is 3.8 °C (6.9 °F) warmer over the year: 16.4 °C against 12.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Kunming is 6.4 °C warmer (Kunming 12.1 °C, Liupanshui 5.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kunming 23.7 °C (May), Liupanshui 22.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kunming 4.5 °C, Liupanshui -0.5 °C.
  • Liupanshui gets 36% more rain and snow a year: 1,327 mm against 974 mm.
  • Kunming gets about 553 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,203 h against 1,649 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kunming 0 cm, Liupanshui 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
