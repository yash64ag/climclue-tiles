How the climate of Pudong, China compares with Xuhui, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Pudong and Xuhui have the same yearly average temperature (16.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Pudong is 0.2 °C warmer (Pudong 13.3 °C, Xuhui 13.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pudong 31.4 °C (July), Xuhui 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Pudong 1.3 °C, Xuhui 1.1 °C.
  • Xuhui gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,499 mm against 1,413 mm.
  • Pudong gets about 13 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,973 h against 1,960 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Pudong 6.4 cm, Xuhui 7.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
