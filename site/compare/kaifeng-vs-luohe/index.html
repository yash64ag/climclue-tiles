How the climate of Kaifeng, China compares with Luohe, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Luohe is 0.4 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 15.4 °C against 15.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Luohe is 1.0 °C warmer (Kaifeng 2.5 °C, Luohe 3.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kaifeng 32.5 °C (June), Luohe 32.1 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Kaifeng -4.4 °C, Luohe -3.3 °C.
  • Luohe gets 37% more rain and snow a year: 899 mm against 658 mm.
  • Kaifeng gets about 129 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,280 h against 2,151 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kaifeng 14 cm, Luohe 20 cm (ERA5-Land).
