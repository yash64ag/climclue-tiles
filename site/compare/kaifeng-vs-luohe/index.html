Kaifeng vs Luohe climate

How the climate of Kaifeng, China compares with Luohe, China, 139 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Luohe is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 15.4 °C against 15.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Luohe is 1.0 °C warmer (Kaifeng 0.6 °C, Luohe 1.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kaifeng 31.4 °C (June), Luohe 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kaifeng -4.2 °C, Luohe -3.4 °C.
  • Luohe gets 37% more rain and snow a year: 899 mm against 657 mm.
  • Kaifeng gets about 129 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,280 h against 2,151 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kaifeng 14 cm, Luohe 20 cm (ERA5-Land).
