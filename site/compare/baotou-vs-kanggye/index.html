Baotou vs Kanggye climate

How the climate of Baotou, China compares with Kanggye, North Korea, 1,407 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kanggye is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 8.1 °C against 7.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Kanggye is 2.3 °C warmer (Baotou 21.4 °C, Kanggye 23.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baotou 30.1 °C (July), Kanggye 28.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baotou -17.4 °C, Kanggye -18.1 °C.
  • Kanggye gets 145% more rain and snow a year: 787 mm against 321 mm.
  • Baotou gets about 558 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,964 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baotou 15 cm, Kanggye 65 cm (ERA5-Land).
