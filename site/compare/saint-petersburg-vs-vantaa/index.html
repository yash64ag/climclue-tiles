How the climate of Saint Petersburg, Russia compares with Vantaa, Finland: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Saint Petersburg is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 5.8 °C against 5.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Saint Petersburg is 1.1 °C warmer (Saint Petersburg 5.3 °C, Vantaa 4.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Saint Petersburg 22.6 °C (July), Vantaa 21.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Saint Petersburg -8.7 °C, Vantaa -8.7 °C.
  • Vantaa gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 726 mm against 690 mm.
  • Saint Petersburg gets about 51 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,621 h against 1,570 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Saint Petersburg 179 cm, Vantaa 179 cm (ERA5-Land).
