Amagasaki vs Fengxiang climate

How the climate of Amagasaki, Japan compares with Fengxiang, China, 1,371 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Amagasaki is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 16.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Amagasaki is 0.8 °C warmer (Amagasaki 28.8 °C, Fengxiang 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amagasaki 31.6 °C (August), Fengxiang 30.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Amagasaki 1.0 °C, Fengxiang 1.8 °C.
  • Fengxiang gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,406 mm against 1,389 mm.
  • Fengxiang gets about 98 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,937 h against 1,839 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amagasaki 13 cm, Fengxiang 6.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
