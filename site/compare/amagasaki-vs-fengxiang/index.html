How the climate of Amagasaki, Japan compares with Fengxiang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Amagasaki is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.9 °C against 16.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Amagasaki is 0.9 °C warmer (Amagasaki 28.9 °C, Fengxiang 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amagasaki 32.9 °C (August), Fengxiang 31.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Amagasaki 2.3 °C, Fengxiang 1.6 °C.
  • Fengxiang gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,409 mm against 1,358 mm.
  • Fengxiang gets about 98 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,937 h against 1,839 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amagasaki 4.2 cm, Fengxiang 5.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
