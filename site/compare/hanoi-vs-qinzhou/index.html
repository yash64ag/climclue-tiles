Hanoi vs Qinzhou climate

How the climate of Hanoi, Vietnam compares with Qinzhou, China, 310 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Hanoi is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 24.1 °C against 22.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Hanoi is 2.4 °C warmer (Hanoi 16.9 °C, Qinzhou 14.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hanoi 33.1 °C (July), Qinzhou 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hanoi 14.1 °C, Qinzhou 10.8 °C.
  • Qinzhou gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 2,418 mm against 1,891 mm.
  • Qinzhou gets about 132 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,777 h against 1,645 h; about 1961–1990).
