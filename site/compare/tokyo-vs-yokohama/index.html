How the climate of Tokyo, Japan compares with Yokohama, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yokohama is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 15.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Yokohama is 1.5 °C warmer (Tokyo 4.5 °C, Yokohama 5.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Tokyo 30.3 °C (August), Yokohama 30.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Tokyo -0.2 °C, Yokohama 2.0 °C.
  • Yokohama gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,530 mm against 1,516 mm.
  • Tokyo gets about 10 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,837 h against 1,827 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Tokyo 19 cm, Yokohama 11 cm (ERA5-Land).
