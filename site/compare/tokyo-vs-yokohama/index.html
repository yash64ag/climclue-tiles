Tokyo vs Yokohama climate

How the climate of Tokyo, Japan compares with Yokohama, Japan, 29 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yokohama is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 15.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Yokohama is 1.6 °C warmer (Tokyo 4.7 °C, Yokohama 6.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Tokyo 29.2 °C (August), Yokohama 27.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Tokyo 0.0 °C, Yokohama 2.7 °C.
  • Yokohama gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,537 mm against 1,486 mm.
  • Tokyo gets about 10 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,837 h against 1,827 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Tokyo 33 cm, Yokohama 20 cm (ERA5-Land).
