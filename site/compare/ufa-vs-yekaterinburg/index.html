How the climate of Ufa, Russia compares with Yekaterinburg, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ufa is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 3.7 °C against 3.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Yekaterinburg is 1.5 °C warmer (Ufa -5.2 °C, Yekaterinburg -3.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ufa 25.7 °C (July), Yekaterinburg 24.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ufa -17.1 °C, Yekaterinburg -16.0 °C.
  • Ufa gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 684 mm against 546 mm.
  • Ufa gets about 68 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,944 h against 1,876 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ufa 229 cm, Yekaterinburg 152 cm (ERA5-Land).
