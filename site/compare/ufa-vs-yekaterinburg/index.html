Ufa vs Yekaterinburg climate

How the climate of Ufa, Russia compares with Yekaterinburg, Russia, 374 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ufa is 1.6 °C (2.9 °F) warmer over the year: 4.1 °C against 2.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Ufa is 2.4 °C warmer (Ufa 13.2 °C, Yekaterinburg 10.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ufa 25.9 °C (July), Yekaterinburg 23.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ufa -16.6 °C, Yekaterinburg -17.3 °C.
  • Ufa gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 684 mm against 546 mm.
  • Ufa gets about 68 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,944 h against 1,876 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ufa 229 cm, Yekaterinburg 152 cm (ERA5-Land).
