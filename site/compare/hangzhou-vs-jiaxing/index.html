Hangzhou vs Jiaxing climate

How the climate of Hangzhou, China compares with Jiaxing, China, 76 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Hangzhou is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 17.1 °C against 16.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Hangzhou is 0.8 °C warmer (Hangzhou 16.4 °C, Jiaxing 15.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hangzhou 32.8 °C (July), Jiaxing 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hangzhou 0.1 °C, Jiaxing 0.3 °C.
  • Hangzhou gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,569 mm against 1,447 mm.
  • Jiaxing gets about 41 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,922 h against 1,882 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hangzhou 24 cm, Jiaxing 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
