How the climate of Hangzhou, China compares with Jiaxing, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hangzhou is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 17.4 °C against 16.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Hangzhou is 1.1 °C warmer (Hangzhou 16.6 °C, Jiaxing 15.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hangzhou 33.5 °C (July), Jiaxing 32.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hangzhou 1.6 °C, Jiaxing 0.8 °C.
  • Hangzhou gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,499 mm against 1,455 mm.
  • Jiaxing gets about 41 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,922 h against 1,882 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hangzhou 24 cm, Jiaxing 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
