Hyderabad vs Iguala de la Independencia climate

How the climate of Hyderabad, India compares with Iguala de la Independencia, Mexico, 16,036 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Iguala de la Independencia is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Hyderabad is 2.5 °C warmer (Hyderabad 32.4 °C, Iguala de la Independencia 29.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hyderabad 37.7 °C (May), Iguala de la Independencia 36.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Hyderabad 15.8 °C, Iguala de la Independencia 17.0 °C.
  • Iguala de la Independencia gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 908 mm against 837 mm.
  • Hyderabad gets about 524 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,691 h against 2,167 h; about 1961–1990).
