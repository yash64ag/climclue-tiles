Main differences

  • Hyderabad is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 26.5 °C against 26.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Hyderabad is 2.7 °C warmer (Hyderabad 32.4 °C, Iguala de la Independencia 29.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hyderabad 38.8 °C (May), Iguala de la Independencia 37.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Hyderabad 15.1 °C, Iguala de la Independencia 15.2 °C.
