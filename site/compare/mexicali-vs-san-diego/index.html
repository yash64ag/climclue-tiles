Mexicali vs San Diego climate

How the climate of Mexicali, Mexico compares with San Diego, United States, 160 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mexicali is 7.6 °C (13.7 °F) warmer over the year: 24.5 °C against 16.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Mexicali is 14.9 °C warmer (Mexicali 34.8 °C, San Diego 19.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mexicali 42.5 °C (July), San Diego 24.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Mexicali 8.4 °C, San Diego 10.2 °C.
  • San Diego gets 227% more rain and snow a year: 256 mm against 78 mm.
  • Mexicali gets about 591 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,652 h against 3,061 h; about 1961–1990).
