Ankara vs Kerch climate

How the climate of Ankara, Turkey compares with Kerch, Ukraine, 673 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ankara is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 12.9 °C against 12.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Ankara is 2.0 °C warmer (Ankara 6.7 °C, Kerch 4.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ankara 30.4 °C (August), Kerch 26.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ankara -3.4 °C, Kerch -0.2 °C.
  • Kerch gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 446 mm against 420 mm.
  • Ankara gets about 569 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,811 h against 2,242 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ankara 37 cm, Kerch 43 cm (ERA5-Land).
