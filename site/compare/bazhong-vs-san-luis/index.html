Bazhong vs San Luis climate

How the climate of Bazhong, China compares with San Luis, Argentina, 19,347 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • San Luis is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 17.5 °C against 17.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when San Luis is 18.3 °C warmer (Bazhong 6.2 °C, San Luis 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bazhong 29.8 °C (July), San Luis 29.8 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Bazhong 1.1 °C, San Luis 5.3 °C.
  • Bazhong gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 1,308 mm against 1,133 mm.
  • San Luis gets about 1,565 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,021 h against 1,456 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bazhong 5.0 cm, San Luis 0.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each

  • Sunnier: San Luis in 12 of 12 months
