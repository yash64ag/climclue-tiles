How the climate of Bazhong, China compares with San Luis, Argentina: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bazhong and San Luis have the same yearly average temperature (17.3 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when San Luis is 17.7 °C warmer (Bazhong 6.4 °C, San Luis 24.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bazhong 31.3 °C (July), San Luis 31.2 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Bazhong 3.0 °C, San Luis 2.7 °C.
  • Bazhong gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 1,298 mm against 1,110 mm.
  • San Luis gets about 1,565 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,021 h against 1,456 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bazhong 1.6 cm, San Luis 0 cm (ERA5-Land).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
