Adelaide vs Leiria climate

How the climate of Adelaide, Australia compares with Leiria, Portugal, 17,101 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Adelaide is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 16.2 °C against 15.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Adelaide is 11.2 °C warmer (Adelaide 21.3 °C, Leiria 10.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Adelaide 27.9 °C (February), Leiria 26.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Adelaide 7.1 °C, Leiria 6.4 °C.
  • Leiria gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 804 mm against 705 mm.
  • Adelaide gets about 75 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,682 h against 2,607 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
