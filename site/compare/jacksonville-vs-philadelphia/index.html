Main differences

  • Jacksonville, Florida is 7.2 °C (13.0 °F) warmer over the year: 20.3 °C against 13.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Jacksonville, Florida is 11.8 °C warmer (Jacksonville, Florida 13.9 °C, Philadelphia 2.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jacksonville, Florida 32.6 °C (July), Philadelphia 30.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jacksonville, Florida 6.0 °C, Philadelphia -3.9 °C.
