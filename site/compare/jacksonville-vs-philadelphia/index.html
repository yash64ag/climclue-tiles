Main differences

  • Jacksonville is 7.2 °C (12.9 °F) warmer over the year: 20.3 °C against 13.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Jacksonville is 11.8 °C warmer (Jacksonville 13.8 °C, Philadelphia 2.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jacksonville 31.8 °C (July), Philadelphia 29.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jacksonville 8.6 °C, Philadelphia -3.3 °C.
  • Jacksonville gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 1,457 mm against 1,215 mm.
  • Jacksonville gets about 382 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,845 h against 2,463 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jacksonville 0 cm, Philadelphia 73 cm (ERA5-Land).
