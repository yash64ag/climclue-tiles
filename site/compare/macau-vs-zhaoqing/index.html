Macau vs Zhaoqing climate

How the climate of Macau, Macao compares with Zhaoqing, China, 146 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Macau is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Macau is 1.8 °C warmer (Macau 15.8 °C, Zhaoqing 13.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Macau 29.8 °C (August), Zhaoqing 31.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Macau 12.9 °C, Zhaoqing 9.8 °C.
  • Macau gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 2,096 mm against 1,718 mm.
  • Macau gets about 132 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,945 h against 1,814 h; about 1961–1990).
