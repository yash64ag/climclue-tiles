How the climate of Macau, Macao compares with Zhaoqing, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Macau is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 22.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Macau is 1.8 °C warmer (Macau 15.9 °C, Zhaoqing 14.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Macau 31.4 °C (July), Zhaoqing 33.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Macau 12.6 °C, Zhaoqing 10.0 °C.
  • Macau gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 2,195 mm against 1,703 mm.
  • Macau gets about 132 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,945 h against 1,814 h; about 1961–1990).
