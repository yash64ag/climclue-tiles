Kabul vs Marāgheh, Razavi Khorasan climate

How the climate of Kabul, Afghanistan compares with Marāgheh, Razavi Khorasan, Iran, 879 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Marāgheh, Razavi Khorasan is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 12.1 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Marāgheh, Razavi Khorasan is 2.3 °C warmer (Kabul -3.3 °C, Marāgheh, Razavi Khorasan -0.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kabul 30.0 °C (July), Marāgheh, Razavi Khorasan 29.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kabul -10.7 °C, Marāgheh, Razavi Khorasan -5.8 °C.
  • Kabul gets 39% more rain and snow a year: 386 mm against 279 mm.
  • Kabul gets about 107 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,031 h against 2,924 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kabul 213 cm, Marāgheh, Razavi Khorasan 113 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Marāgheh, Razavi Khorasan in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Kabul in 8 of 12 months
