How the climate of Kabul, Afghanistan compares with Marāgheh, Razavi Khorasan, Iran: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kabul is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 11.9 °C against 11.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Marāgheh, Razavi Khorasan is 1.7 °C warmer (Kabul -2.9 °C, Marāgheh, Razavi Khorasan -1.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kabul 31.4 °C (July), Marāgheh, Razavi Khorasan 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kabul -8.4 °C, Marāgheh, Razavi Khorasan -7.1 °C.
  • Kabul gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 346 mm against 280 mm.
  • Kabul gets about 107 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,031 h against 2,924 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kabul 128 cm, Marāgheh, Razavi Khorasan 65 cm (ERA5-Land).
