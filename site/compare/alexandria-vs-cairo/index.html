Alexandria vs Cairo climate

How the climate of Alexandria, Egypt compares with Cairo, Egypt, 180 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Cairo is 1.5 °C (2.8 °F) warmer over the year: 21.9 °C against 20.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Cairo is 4.2 °C warmer (Alexandria 20.8 °C, Cairo 24.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Alexandria 30.1 °C (August), Cairo 35.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Alexandria 9.6 °C, Cairo 8.2 °C.
  • Alexandria gets 874% more rain and snow a year: 196 mm against 20 mm.
  • Cairo gets about 74 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,330 h against 3,256 h; about 1961–1990).
