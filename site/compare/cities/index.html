City climate comparisons

Two towns side by side, month by month. Each town of 1,000,000+ people is paired with its three nearest towns of that size (within 1,500 km, any country) and with its closest climate twin in another country.

  • Africa · 221 comparisons, e.g. Ibadan vs Lagos, Kinshasa vs Luanda, Brazzaville vs Kinshasa
  • Asia · 1,025 comparisons, e.g. Beijing vs Tianjin, Pudong vs Shanghai, Dongguan vs Guangzhou
  • Europe · 109 comparisons, e.g. Bucharest vs Istanbul, Moscow vs Saint Petersburg, Minsk vs Moscow
  • North America · 140 comparisons, e.g. Arlington, Texas vs Chongqing, Iztapalapa vs Mexico City, Mexico City vs Puebla
  • Oceania · 9 comparisons, e.g. Melbourne vs Sydney, Brisbane vs Sydney, Brisbane vs Melbourne
  • South America · 108 comparisons, e.g. Rio de Janeiro vs São Paulo, Curitiba vs São Paulo, Guarulhos vs São Paulo
