Rabat vs Tangier climate

How the climate of Rabat, Morocco compares with Tangier, Morocco, 217 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rabat is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 18.6 °C against 18.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Rabat is 1.4 °C warmer (Rabat 17.3 °C, Tangier 15.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rabat 27.6 °C (August), Tangier 29.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Rabat 10.1 °C, Tangier 9.2 °C.
  • Tangier gets 43% more rain and snow a year: 693 mm against 484 mm.
  • Rabat gets about 46 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,869 h against 2,823 h; about 1961–1990).
