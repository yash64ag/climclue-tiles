How the climate of Rabat, Morocco compares with Tangier, Morocco: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Rabat is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 18.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Rabat is 1.0 °C warmer (Rabat 17.2 °C, Tangier 16.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rabat 28.4 °C (August), Tangier 27.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Rabat 8.6 °C, Tangier 9.7 °C.
  • Tangier gets 35% more rain and snow a year: 655 mm against 485 mm.
  • Rabat gets about 46 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,869 h against 2,823 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
