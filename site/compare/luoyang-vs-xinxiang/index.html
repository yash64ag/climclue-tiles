Luoyang, Henan vs Xinxiang climate

How the climate of Luoyang, Henan, China compares with Xinxiang, China, 137 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Luoyang, Henan is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 15.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Luoyang, Henan is 0.4 °C warmer (Luoyang, Henan 4.2 °C, Xinxiang 3.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Luoyang, Henan 32.6 °C (June), Xinxiang 32.7 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Luoyang, Henan -4.4 °C, Xinxiang -4.9 °C.
  • Xinxiang gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 606 mm against 566 mm.
  • Xinxiang gets about 121 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,303 h against 2,182 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Luoyang, Henan 14 cm, Xinxiang 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
