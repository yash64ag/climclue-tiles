How the climate of Liupanshui, China compares with Qujing, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Qujing is 2.3 °C (4.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.4 °C against 13.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Qujing is 4.4 °C warmer (Liupanshui 6.3 °C, Qujing 10.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Liupanshui 24.5 °C (July), Qujing 24.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Liupanshui -0.4 °C, Qujing 2.3 °C.
  • Liupanshui gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 1,243 mm against 1,007 mm.
  • Qujing gets about 284 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,933 h against 1,649 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Liupanshui 14 cm, Qujing 0 cm (ERA5-Land).
