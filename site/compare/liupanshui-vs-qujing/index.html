Liupanshui vs Qujing climate

How the climate of Liupanshui, China compares with Qujing, China, 162 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Qujing is 2.7 °C (4.8 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 12.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Qujing is 4.8 °C warmer (Liupanshui 5.7 °C, Qujing 10.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Liupanshui 22.7 °C (July), Qujing 23.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Liupanshui -0.5 °C, Qujing 3.0 °C.
  • Liupanshui gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 1,327 mm against 1,039 mm.
  • Qujing gets about 284 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,933 h against 1,649 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Liupanshui 14 cm, Qujing 0 cm (ERA5-Land).
