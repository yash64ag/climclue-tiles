How the climate of Lüliang, China compares with Songnim-ni, North Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Songnim-ni is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 11.3 °C against 10.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Songnim-ni is 2.3 °C warmer (Lüliang 17.6 °C, Songnim-ni 19.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lüliang 30.6 °C (July), Songnim-ni 29.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Lüliang -12.0 °C, Songnim-ni -9.9 °C.
  • Songnim-ni gets 82% more rain and snow a year: 874 mm against 480 mm.
  • Lüliang gets about 165 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,599 h against 2,434 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lüliang 19 cm, Songnim-ni 24 cm (ERA5-Land).
