Anshan vs Panjin climate

How the climate of Anshan, China compares with Panjin, China, 77 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Panjin is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 9.8 °C against 9.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Panjin is 0.7 °C warmer (Anshan -9.7 °C, Panjin -8.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Anshan 28.9 °C (July), Panjin 28.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Anshan -15.5 °C, Panjin -14.6 °C.
  • Anshan gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 745 mm against 661 mm.
  • Panjin gets about 52 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,628 h against 2,577 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Anshan 39 cm, Panjin 24 cm (ERA5-Land).
