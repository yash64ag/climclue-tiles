Hadejia vs Vadodara climate

How the climate of Hadejia, Nigeria compares with Vadodara, India, 6,748 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Hadejia is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 27.5 °C against 27.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Hadejia is 1.4 °C warmer (Hadejia 24.6 °C, Vadodara 23.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hadejia 39.9 °C (April), Vadodara 39.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Hadejia 13.6 °C, Vadodara 12.7 °C.
  • Vadodara gets 101% more rain and snow a year: 1,053 mm against 524 mm.
  • Hadejia gets about 82 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,998 h against 2,916 h; about 1961–1990).
