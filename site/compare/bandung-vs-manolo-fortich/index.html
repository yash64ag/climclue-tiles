Bandung vs Manolo Fortich climate

How the climate of Bandung, Indonesia compares with Manolo Fortich, Philippines, 2,559 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Manolo Fortich is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 24.3 °C against 23.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Manolo Fortich is 1.1 °C warmer (Bandung 24.1 °C, Manolo Fortich 25.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bandung 28.2 °C (September), Manolo Fortich 28.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Bandung 18.9 °C, Manolo Fortich 19.7 °C.
  • Manolo Fortich gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 2,689 mm against 2,391 mm.
  • Bandung gets about 259 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,454 h against 2,195 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Manolo Fortich in 11 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Bandung in 9 of 12 months
