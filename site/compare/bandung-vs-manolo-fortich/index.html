How the climate of Bandung, Indonesia compares with Manolo Fortich, Philippines: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Manolo Fortich is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 24.7 °C against 23.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Manolo Fortich is 1.8 °C warmer (Bandung 23.8 °C, Manolo Fortich 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bandung 29.3 °C (September), Manolo Fortich 31.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Bandung 17.3 °C, Manolo Fortich 19.1 °C.
  • Manolo Fortich gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 2,624 mm against 2,449 mm.
  • Bandung gets about 259 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,454 h against 2,195 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
