How the climate of Fanling, Hong Kong compares with Puning, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Fanling is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 22.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Fanling is 1.2 °C warmer (Fanling 15.4 °C, Puning 14.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fanling 31.7 °C (July), Puning 31.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fanling 12.1 °C, Puning 10.3 °C.
  • Fanling gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 2,097 mm against 1,639 mm.
  • Puning gets about 22 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,980 h against 1,958 h; about 1961–1990).
