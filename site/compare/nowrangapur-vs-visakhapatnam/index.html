Nowrangapur vs Visakhapatnam climate

How the climate of Nowrangapur, India compares with Visakhapatnam, India, 186 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Visakhapatnam is 2.6 °C (4.8 °F) warmer over the year: 27.1 °C against 24.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Visakhapatnam is 4.6 °C warmer (Nowrangapur 18.9 °C, Visakhapatnam 23.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nowrangapur 36.1 °C (May), Visakhapatnam 33.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Nowrangapur 12.6 °C, Visakhapatnam 20.5 °C.
  • Nowrangapur gets 35% more rain and snow a year: 1,545 mm against 1,147 mm.
  • Visakhapatnam gets about 21 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,555 h against 2,533 h; about 1961–1990).
