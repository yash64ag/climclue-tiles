Abidjan vs Twifu Praso climate

How the climate of Abidjan, Ivory Coast compares with Twifu Praso, Ghana, 273 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Abidjan and Twifu Praso have the same yearly average temperature (25.8 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Twifu Praso is 0.5 °C warmer (Abidjan 23.9 °C, Twifu Praso 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abidjan 29.2 °C (March), Twifu Praso 32.5 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Abidjan 22.7 °C, Twifu Praso 21.8 °C.
  • Abidjan gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 1,908 mm against 1,513 mm.
  • Abidjan gets about 178 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,118 h against 1,940 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Abidjan in 5 of 12 months (4 equal)
  • Drier: Twifu Praso in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Abidjan in 11 of 12 months
