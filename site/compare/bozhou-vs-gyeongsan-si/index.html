How the climate of Bozhou, China compares with Gyeongsan-si, South Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bozhou is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 13.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Bozhou is 3.0 °C warmer (Bozhou 25.9 °C, Gyeongsan-si 22.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bozhou 31.8 °C (July), Gyeongsan-si 30.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bozhou -3.6 °C, Gyeongsan-si -5.1 °C.
  • Gyeongsan-si gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 1,120 mm against 811 mm.
  • Gyeongsan-si gets about 232 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,452 h against 2,220 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bozhou 17 cm, Gyeongsan-si 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
