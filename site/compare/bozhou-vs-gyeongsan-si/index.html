Bozhou vs Gyeongsan-si climate

How the climate of Bozhou, China compares with Gyeongsan-si, South Korea, 1,202 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bozhou is 1.4 °C (2.6 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 13.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Bozhou is 3.1 °C warmer (Bozhou 25.9 °C, Gyeongsan-si 22.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bozhou 31.1 °C (July), Gyeongsan-si 29.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bozhou -3.5 °C, Gyeongsan-si -4.9 °C.
  • Gyeongsan-si gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 1,043 mm against 816 mm.
  • Gyeongsan-si gets about 232 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,452 h against 2,220 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bozhou 17 cm, Gyeongsan-si 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
