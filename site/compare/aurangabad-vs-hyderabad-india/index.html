Aurangabad vs Hyderabad climate

How the climate of Aurangabad, India compares with Hyderabad, India, 430 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Hyderabad is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 26.5 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Hyderabad is 2.3 °C warmer (Aurangabad 23.1 °C, Hyderabad 25.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aurangabad 38.5 °C (May), Hyderabad 37.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Aurangabad 14.6 °C, Hyderabad 15.8 °C.
  • Aurangabad gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 973 mm against 837 mm.
  • Aurangabad gets about 94 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,785 h against 2,691 h; about 1961–1990).
