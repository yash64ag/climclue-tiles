How the climate of Dallas, Texas, United States compares with Houston, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Houston is 1.6 °C (2.8 °F) warmer over the year: 20.6 °C against 19.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Houston is 3.9 °C warmer (Dallas, Texas 8.6 °C, Houston 12.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dallas, Texas 35.4 °C (August), Houston 32.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Dallas, Texas 2.1 °C, Houston 7.1 °C.
  • Houston gets 46% more rain and snow a year: 1,372 mm against 939 mm.
  • Dallas, Texas gets about 218 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,794 h against 2,576 h; about 1961–1990).
