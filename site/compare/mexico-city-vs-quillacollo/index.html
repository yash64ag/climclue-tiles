Mexico City vs Quillacollo climate

How the climate of Mexico City, Mexico compares with Quillacollo, Bolivia, 5,444 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mexico City is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 15.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Mexico City is 5.5 °C warmer (Mexico City 18.3 °C, Quillacollo 12.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mexico City 24.3 °C (April), Quillacollo 21.7 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Mexico City 5.8 °C, Quillacollo 5.4 °C.
  • Quillacollo gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 773 mm against 728 mm.
  • Quillacollo gets about 454 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,471 h against 2,016 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Mexico City in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Quillacollo in 9 of 12 months
