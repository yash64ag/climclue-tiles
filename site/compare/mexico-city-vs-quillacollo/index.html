How the climate of Mexico City, Mexico compares with Quillacollo, Bolivia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Mexico City is 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 15.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Mexico City is 6.0 °C warmer (Mexico City 18.8 °C, Quillacollo 12.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mexico City 27.2 °C (April), Quillacollo 25.1 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Mexico City 4.3 °C, Quillacollo 1.7 °C.
  • Quillacollo gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 773 mm against 728 mm.
  • Quillacollo gets about 454 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,471 h against 2,016 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
