How the climate of Weifang, China compares with Yantai, Shandong, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Weifang is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 13.5 °C against 12.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Weifang is 3.4 °C warmer (Weifang 14.3 °C, Yantai, Shandong 10.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Weifang 31.0 °C (July), Yantai, Shandong 28.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Weifang -6.8 °C, Yantai, Shandong -4.3 °C.
  • Yantai, Shandong gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 690 mm against 564 mm.
  • Yantai, Shandong gets about 35 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,560 h against 2,525 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Weifang 12 cm, Yantai, Shandong 28 cm (ERA5-Land).
