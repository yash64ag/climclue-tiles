How the climate of Nice, France compares with Rome, Italy: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Rome is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 15.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Rome is 1.5 °C warmer (Nice 23.9 °C, Rome 25.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nice 27.6 °C (August), Rome 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nice 4.9 °C, Rome 2.5 °C.
  • Nice gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 826 mm against 770 mm.
  • Nice gets about 102 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,525 h against 2,424 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nice 4.9 cm, Rome 0.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
