How the climate of Coimbatore, India compares with Palo Negro, Venezuela: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Coimbatore and Palo Negro have the same yearly average temperature (25.9 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Coimbatore is 1.6 °C warmer (Coimbatore 28.4 °C, Palo Negro 26.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Coimbatore 35.4 °C (April), Palo Negro 33.6 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Coimbatore 18.6 °C, Palo Negro 18.2 °C.
  • Palo Negro gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 1,010 mm against 783 mm.
  • Palo Negro gets about 248 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,654 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Palo Negro in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: Coimbatore in 7 of 12 months
