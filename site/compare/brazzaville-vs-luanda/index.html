How the climate of Brazzaville, Republic of the Congo compares with Luanda, Angola: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Brazzaville is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 25.5 °C against 24.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Brazzaville is 3.0 °C warmer (Brazzaville 24.6 °C, Luanda 21.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brazzaville 31.1 °C (April), Luanda 30.5 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Brazzaville 19.1 °C, Luanda 18.7 °C.
  • Brazzaville gets 257% more rain and snow a year: 1,561 mm against 437 mm.
  • Luanda gets about 388 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,306 h against 1,918 h; about 1961–1990).
