Brazzaville vs Luanda climate

How the climate of Brazzaville, Republic of the Congo compares with Luanda, Angola, 556 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Brazzaville is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 25.3 °C against 25.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Brazzaville is 2.7 °C warmer (Brazzaville 24.4 °C, Luanda 21.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brazzaville 30.9 °C (September), Luanda 31.7 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Brazzaville 19.2 °C, Luanda 18.2 °C.
  • Brazzaville gets 239% more rain and snow a year: 1,557 mm against 459 mm.
  • Luanda gets about 388 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,306 h against 1,918 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Luanda in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Luanda in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Luanda in 11 of 12 months
