How the climate of Funtua, Nigeria compares with Pune, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Funtua and Pune have the same yearly average temperature (24.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Funtua is 1.1 °C warmer (Funtua 29.4 °C, Pune 28.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Funtua 36.4 °C (April), Pune 37.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Funtua 13.0 °C, Pune 12.0 °C.
  • Funtua gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,120 mm against 999 mm.
  • Pune gets about 10 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,793 h against 2,783 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
