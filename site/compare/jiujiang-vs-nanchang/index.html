How the climate of Jiujiang, China compares with Nanchang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Nanchang is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 17.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Nanchang is 1.3 °C warmer (Jiujiang 6.8 °C, Nanchang 8.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiujiang 33.7 °C (July), Nanchang 34.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jiujiang 0.7 °C, Nanchang 2.4 °C.
  • Jiujiang gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,879 mm against 1,796 mm.
  • Jiujiang gets about 66 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,905 h against 1,839 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jiujiang 16 cm, Nanchang 6.5 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Nanchang in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Jiujiang in 9 of 12 months
