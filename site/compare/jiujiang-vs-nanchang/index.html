Jiujiang vs Nanchang climate

How the climate of Jiujiang, China compares with Nanchang, China, 114 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Nanchang is 0.9 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 17.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Nanchang is 1.4 °C warmer (Jiujiang 6.8 °C, Nanchang 8.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiujiang 32.1 °C (July), Nanchang 33.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jiujiang 1.7 °C, Nanchang 2.2 °C.
  • Jiujiang gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,820 mm against 1,801 mm.
  • Jiujiang gets about 66 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,905 h against 1,839 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jiujiang 16 cm, Nanchang 6.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
