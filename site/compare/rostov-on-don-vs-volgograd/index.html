How the climate of Rostov-on-Don, Russia compares with Volgograd, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Rostov-on-Don is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 10.1 °C against 8.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Rostov-on-Don is 3.3 °C warmer (Rostov-on-Don -2.8 °C, Volgograd -6.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rostov-on-Don 29.3 °C (July), Volgograd 30.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Rostov-on-Don -6.0 °C, Volgograd -9.6 °C.
  • Rostov-on-Don gets 59% more rain and snow a year: 616 mm against 387 mm.
  • Volgograd gets about 30 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,116 h against 2,086 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Rostov-on-Don 98 cm, Volgograd 99 cm (ERA5-Land).
