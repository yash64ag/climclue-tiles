How the climate of Moscow, Russia compares with Vitebsk, Belarus: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Vitebsk is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 6.1 °C against 5.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Vitebsk is 1.4 °C warmer (Moscow -6.8 °C, Vitebsk -5.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Moscow 24.3 °C (July), Vitebsk 23.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Moscow -10.0 °C, Vitebsk -9.0 °C.
  • Vitebsk gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 728 mm against 691 mm.
  • Vitebsk gets about 53 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,753 h against 1,701 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Moscow 185 cm, Vitebsk 170 cm (ERA5-Land).
