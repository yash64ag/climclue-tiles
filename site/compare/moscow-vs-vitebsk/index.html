Moscow vs Vitebsk climate

How the climate of Moscow, Russia compares with Vitebsk, Belarus, 471 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Vitebsk is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 6.0 °C against 5.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Vitebsk is 2.0 °C warmer (Moscow -7.3 °C, Vitebsk -5.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Moscow 23.0 °C (July), Vitebsk 22.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Moscow -10.8 °C, Vitebsk -8.9 °C.
  • Vitebsk gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 738 mm against 691 mm.
  • Vitebsk gets about 53 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,753 h against 1,701 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Moscow 193 cm, Vitebsk 191 cm (ERA5-Land).
