Goiânia vs Kon Tum climate

How the climate of Goiânia, Brazil compares with Kon Tum, Vietnam, 17,567 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kon Tum is 0.6 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 24.1 °C against 23.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Kon Tum is 3.5 °C warmer (Goiânia 21.5 °C, Kon Tum 25.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Goiânia 32.0 °C (September), Kon Tum 30.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Goiânia 14.0 °C, Kon Tum 17.7 °C.
  • Kon Tum gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,660 mm against 1,478 mm.
  • Goiânia gets about 357 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,501 h against 2,144 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
