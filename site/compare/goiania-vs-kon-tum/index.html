Goiânia vs Kon Tum climate

How the climate of Goiânia, Brazil compares with Kon Tum, Vietnam, 17,567 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kon Tum is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.8 °C against 23.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Kon Tum is 3.0 °C warmer (Goiânia 21.6 °C, Kon Tum 24.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Goiânia 31.1 °C (September), Kon Tum 31.7 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Goiânia 16.4 °C, Kon Tum 15.9 °C.
  • Kon Tum gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 1,713 mm against 1,465 mm.
  • Goiânia gets about 357 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,501 h against 2,144 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
