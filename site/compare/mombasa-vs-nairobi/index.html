Mombasa vs Nairobi climate

How the climate of Mombasa, Kenya compares with Nairobi, Kenya, 442 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mombasa is 7.4 °C (13.4 °F) warmer over the year: 26.2 °C against 18.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Mombasa is 8.6 °C warmer (Mombasa 27.4 °C, Nairobi 18.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mombasa 32.5 °C (March), Nairobi 27.4 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Mombasa 20.3 °C, Nairobi 11.5 °C.
  • Mombasa gets 31% more rain and snow a year: 1,110 mm against 850 mm.
  • Mombasa gets about 475 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,940 h against 2,464 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Mombasa in 11 of 12 months
