Caloocan vs Zamboanga climate

How the climate of Caloocan, Philippines compares with Zamboanga, Philippines, 869 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Caloocan is 1.7 °C (3.1 °F) warmer over the year: 27.8 °C against 26.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Caloocan is 3.0 °C warmer (Caloocan 29.4 °C, Zamboanga 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Caloocan 32.3 °C (April), Zamboanga 28.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Caloocan 23.4 °C, Zamboanga 24.5 °C.
  • Caloocan gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 2,138 mm against 2,000 mm.
  • Caloocan gets about 194 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,404 h against 2,211 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Caloocan in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Caloocan in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Caloocan in 9 of 12 months
