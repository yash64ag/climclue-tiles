How the climate of Caloocan, Philippines compares with Zamboanga, Philippines: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Caloocan is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 27.8 °C against 27.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Caloocan is 2.1 °C warmer (Caloocan 29.4 °C, Zamboanga 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Caloocan 34.6 °C (April), Zamboanga 32.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Caloocan 22.0 °C, Zamboanga 22.0 °C.
  • Caloocan gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 2,143 mm against 1,695 mm.
  • Caloocan gets about 194 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,404 h against 2,211 h; about 1961–1990).
