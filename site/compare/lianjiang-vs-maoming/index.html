Lianjiang vs Maoming climate

How the climate of Lianjiang, China compares with Maoming, China, 65 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lianjiang and Maoming have the same yearly average temperature (23.5 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Maoming is 0.3 °C warmer (Lianjiang 16.1 °C, Maoming 16.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lianjiang 31.7 °C (July), Maoming 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lianjiang 12.3 °C, Maoming 12.6 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (2,073 mm and 2,081 mm).
  • Maoming gets about 35 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,916 h against 1,881 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Maoming in 6 of 12 months (4 equal)
  • Drier: Maoming in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Maoming in 9 of 12 months
