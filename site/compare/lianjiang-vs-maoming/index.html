Lianjiang vs Maoming climate

How the climate of Lianjiang, China compares with Maoming, China, 65 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Maoming is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.6 °C against 23.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Maoming is 0.3 °C warmer (Lianjiang 16.0 °C, Maoming 16.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lianjiang 32.0 °C (July), Maoming 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lianjiang 12.5 °C, Maoming 12.6 °C.
  • Lianjiang gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 2,092 mm against 2,055 mm.
  • Maoming gets about 35 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,916 h against 1,881 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Maoming in 10 of 12 months
