How the climate of Foshan, China compares with Guangzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Foshan and Guangzhou have the same yearly average temperature (23.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Guangzhou is 0.1 °C warmer (Foshan 25.2 °C, Guangzhou 25.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Foshan 33.3 °C (July), Guangzhou 33.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Foshan 10.5 °C, Guangzhou 10.7 °C.
  • Guangzhou gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 2,053 mm against 1,981 mm.
  • Foshan gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,807 h against 1,800 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Guangzhou in 8 of 12 months
