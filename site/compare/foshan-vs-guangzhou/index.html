Foshan vs Guangzhou climate

How the climate of Foshan, China compares with Guangzhou, China, 16 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Foshan is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 22.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Foshan is 0.2 °C warmer (Foshan 23.4 °C, Guangzhou 23.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Foshan 31.9 °C (July), Guangzhou 31.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Foshan 10.4 °C, Guangzhou 10.1 °C.
  • Guangzhou gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 2,108 mm against 1,983 mm.
  • Foshan gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,807 h against 1,800 h; about 1961–1990).
