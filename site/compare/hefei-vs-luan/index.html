Hefei vs Lu’an climate

How the climate of Hefei, China compares with Lu’an, China, 74 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Hefei is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 16.4 °C against 16.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Hefei is 0.5 °C warmer (Hefei 27.8 °C, Lu’an 27.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hefei 31.5 °C (July), Lu’an 31.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hefei -1.3 °C, Lu’an -1.8 °C.
  • Lu’an gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,288 mm against 1,140 mm.
  • Hefei gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,044 h against 2,037 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hefei 26 cm, Lu’an 32 cm (ERA5-Land).
