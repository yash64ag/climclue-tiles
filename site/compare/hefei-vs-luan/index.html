Hefei vs Lu’an climate

How the climate of Hefei, China compares with Lu’an, China, 74 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Hefei is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 16.4 °C against 16.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Hefei is 0.6 °C warmer (Hefei 23.4 °C, Lu’an 22.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hefei 32.3 °C (July), Lu’an 32.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hefei -0.9 °C, Lu’an -1.5 °C.
  • Lu’an gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,301 mm against 1,120 mm.
  • Hefei gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,044 h against 2,037 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hefei 23 cm, Lu’an 29 cm (ERA5-Land).
