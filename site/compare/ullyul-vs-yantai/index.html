Main differences

  • Yantai, Shandong is 1.1 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 12.5 °C against 11.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Yantai, Shandong is 2.3 °C warmer (Ŭllyul -0.3 °C, Yantai, Shandong 2.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ŭllyul 28.6 °C (August), Yantai, Shandong 28.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Ŭllyul -7.9 °C, Yantai, Shandong -4.3 °C.
  • Ŭllyul gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 786 mm against 690 mm.
  • Yantai, Shandong gets about 97 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,560 h against 2,463 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ŭllyul 25 cm, Yantai, Shandong 28 cm (ERA5-Land).
