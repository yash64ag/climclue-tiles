Ŭllyul vs Yantai climate

How the climate of Ŭllyul, North Korea compares with Yantai, China, 348 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yantai is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 12.5 °C against 11.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Yantai is 1.7 °C warmer (Ŭllyul 0.1 °C, Yantai 1.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ŭllyul 27.3 °C (August), Yantai 27.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ŭllyul -7.1 °C, Yantai -4.0 °C.
  • Ŭllyul gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 813 mm against 663 mm.
  • Yantai gets about 97 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,560 h against 2,463 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ŭllyul 25 cm, Yantai 28 cm (ERA5-Land).
