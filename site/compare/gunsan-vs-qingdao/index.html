How the climate of Gunsan, South Korea compares with Qingdao, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Gunsan and Qingdao have the same yearly average temperature (13.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Qingdao is 0.8 °C warmer (Gunsan 16.2 °C, Qingdao 17.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gunsan 30.2 °C (August), Qingdao 28.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Gunsan -3.4 °C, Qingdao -2.6 °C.
  • Gunsan gets 78% more rain and snow a year: 1,296 mm against 728 mm.
  • Qingdao gets about 26 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,470 h against 2,443 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gunsan 28 cm, Qingdao 11 cm (ERA5-Land).
