How the climate of Songnim-ni, North Korea compares with Tangshan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tangshan is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 12.2 °C against 11.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Tangshan is 2.7 °C warmer (Songnim-ni 17.3 °C, Tangshan 20.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Songnim-ni 29.1 °C (August), Tangshan 30.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Songnim-ni -9.9 °C, Tangshan -10.3 °C.
  • Songnim-ni gets 39% more rain and snow a year: 874 mm against 630 mm.
  • Tangshan gets about 238 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,672 h against 2,434 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Songnim-ni 24 cm, Tangshan 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
