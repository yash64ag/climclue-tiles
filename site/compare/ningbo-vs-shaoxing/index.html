Ningbo vs Shaoxing climate

How the climate of Ningbo, China compares with Shaoxing, China, 94 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shaoxing is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 17.6 °C against 17.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Ningbo is 1.4 °C warmer (Ningbo 8.6 °C, Shaoxing 7.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ningbo 31.9 °C (July), Shaoxing 33.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ningbo 2.4 °C, Shaoxing 0.6 °C.
  • Ningbo gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,656 mm against 1,422 mm.
  • Shaoxing gets about 6 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,876 h against 1,871 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ningbo 7.1 cm, Shaoxing 16 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Shaoxing in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Shaoxing in 8 of 12 months
