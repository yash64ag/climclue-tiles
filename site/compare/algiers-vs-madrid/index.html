Algiers vs Madrid climate

How the climate of Algiers, Algeria compares with Madrid, Spain, 718 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Algiers is 3.5 °C (6.3 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 14.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Algiers is 6.7 °C warmer (Algiers 12.9 °C, Madrid 6.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Algiers 32.4 °C (August), Madrid 33.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Algiers 6.7 °C, Madrid 0.9 °C.
  • Algiers gets 71% more rain and snow a year: 682 mm against 400 mm.
  • Algiers gets about 289 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,886 h against 2,597 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Algiers 0 cm, Madrid 4.3 cm (ERA5-Land).
