Algiers vs Madrid climate

How the climate of Algiers, Algeria compares with Madrid, Spain, 718 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Algiers is 3.2 °C (5.8 °F) warmer over the year: 18.0 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Algiers is 6.4 °C warmer (Algiers 12.6 °C, Madrid 6.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Algiers 30.4 °C (August), Madrid 31.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Algiers 8.0 °C, Madrid 0.6 °C.
  • Algiers gets 70% more rain and snow a year: 749 mm against 439 mm.
  • Algiers gets about 289 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,886 h against 2,597 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Algiers 0.3 cm, Madrid 11 cm (ERA5-Land).
