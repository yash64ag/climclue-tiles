How the climate of Bukavu, Democratic Republic of the Congo compares with Kigali, Rwanda: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bukavu and Kigali have the same yearly average temperature (20.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Bukavu is 0.4 °C warmer (Bukavu 20.6 °C, Kigali 20.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bukavu 27.8 °C (August), Kigali 28.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bukavu 13.8 °C, Kigali 13.7 °C.
  • Bukavu gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 1,428 mm against 1,069 mm.
  • Kigali gets about 3 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,990 h against 1,987 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Bukavu in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: Kigali in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
