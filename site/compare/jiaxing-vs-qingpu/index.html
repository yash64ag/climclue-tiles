Jiaxing vs Qingpu climate

How the climate of Jiaxing, China compares with Qingpu, China, 57 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jiaxing is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Jiaxing is 0.3 °C warmer (Jiaxing 15.6 °C, Qingpu 15.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiaxing 32.1 °C (July), Qingpu 31.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jiaxing 0.3 °C, Qingpu -0.1 °C.
  • Jiaxing gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,447 mm against 1,399 mm.
  • Qingpu gets about 36 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,958 h against 1,922 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jiaxing 13 cm, Qingpu 10 cm (ERA5-Land).
