How the climate of Saitama, Japan compares with Ulsan, South Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Saitama is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 15.3 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Saitama is 1.6 °C warmer (Saitama 6.5 °C, Ulsan 4.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Saitama 31.1 °C (August), Ulsan 30.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Saitama -1.3 °C, Ulsan -2.2 °C.
  • Saitama gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,336 mm against 1,186 mm.
  • Ulsan gets about 427 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,290 h against 1,863 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Saitama 20 cm, Ulsan 6.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
