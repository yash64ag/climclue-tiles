Coimbatore vs Madurai climate

How the climate of Coimbatore, India compares with Madurai, India, 175 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Madurai is 3.2 °C (5.8 °F) warmer over the year: 29.1 °C against 25.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Madurai is 5.6 °C warmer (Coimbatore 24.9 °C, Madurai 30.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Coimbatore 35.4 °C (April), Madurai 36.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Coimbatore 18.6 °C, Madurai 21.8 °C.
  • Madurai gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 877 mm against 783 mm.
  • Madurai gets about 33 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,439 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).
