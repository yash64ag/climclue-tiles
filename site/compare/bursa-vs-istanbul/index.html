Bursa vs Istanbul climate

How the climate of Bursa, Turkey compares with Istanbul, Turkey, 91 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Istanbul is 3.4 °C (6.1 °F) warmer over the year: 14.7 °C against 11.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Istanbul is 4.6 °C warmer (Bursa 4.0 °C, Istanbul 8.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bursa 25.2 °C (August), Istanbul 26.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bursa -2.9 °C, Istanbul 3.9 °C.
  • Bursa gets 73% more rain and snow a year: 1,406 mm against 813 mm.
  • Bursa gets about 9 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,772 h against 2,763 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bursa 160 cm, Istanbul 28 cm (ERA5-Land).
