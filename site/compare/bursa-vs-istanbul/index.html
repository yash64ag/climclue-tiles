How the climate of Bursa, Turkey compares with Istanbul, Turkey: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bursa is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 15.1 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Bursa is 1.5 °C warmer (Bursa 13.8 °C, Istanbul 12.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bursa 31.1 °C (August), Istanbul 28.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bursa 1.8 °C, Istanbul 3.2 °C.
  • Bursa gets 37% more rain and snow a year: 1,076 mm against 783 mm.
  • Bursa gets about 9 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,772 h against 2,763 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bursa 27 cm, Istanbul 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
