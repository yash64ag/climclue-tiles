Benin City vs Port Harcourt climate

How the climate of Benin City, Nigeria compares with Port Harcourt, Nigeria, 232 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Port Harcourt is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 26.1 °C against 25.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Port Harcourt is 0.7 °C warmer (Benin City 25.9 °C, Port Harcourt 26.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Benin City 33.3 °C (February), Port Harcourt 31.7 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Benin City 21.9 °C, Port Harcourt 22.8 °C.
  • Port Harcourt gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 2,261 mm against 2,246 mm.
  • Benin City gets about 382 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,755 h against 1,373 h; about 1961–1990).
