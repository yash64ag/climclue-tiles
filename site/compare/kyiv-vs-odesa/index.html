Kyiv vs Odesa climate

How the climate of Kyiv, Ukraine compares with Odesa, Ukraine, 442 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Odesa is 2.7 °C (4.9 °F) warmer over the year: 11.0 °C against 8.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Odesa is 4.1 °C warmer (Kyiv 1.8 °C, Odesa 5.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kyiv 24.5 °C (July), Odesa 26.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kyiv -6.2 °C, Odesa -2.8 °C.
  • Kyiv gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 614 mm against 474 mm.
  • Odesa gets about 395 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,124 h against 1,729 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kyiv 103 cm, Odesa 62 cm (ERA5-Land).
