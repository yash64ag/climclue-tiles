How the climate of Zapopan, Mexico compares with Zomba, Malawi: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zomba is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 20.6 °C against 20.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Zapopan is 6.7 °C warmer (Zapopan 23.6 °C, Zomba 16.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Zapopan 32.9 °C (May), Zomba 29.0 °C (November). Coldest-month night lows: Zapopan 8.5 °C, Zomba 11.1 °C.
  • Zomba gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 1,117 mm against 901 mm.
  • Zapopan gets about 5 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,647 h against 2,642 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
