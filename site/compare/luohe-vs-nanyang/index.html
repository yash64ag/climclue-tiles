Luohe vs Nanyang climate

How the climate of Luohe, China compares with Nanyang, China, 152 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Nanyang is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 15.7 °C against 15.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Nanyang is 0.7 °C warmer (Luohe 1.6 °C, Nanyang 2.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Luohe 32.1 °C (June), Nanyang 31.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Luohe -3.3 °C, Nanyang -2.8 °C.
  • Luohe gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 899 mm against 843 mm.
  • Luohe gets about 144 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,151 h against 2,008 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Luohe 20 cm, Nanyang 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
