Aba vs Port Harcourt climate

How the climate of Aba, Nigeria compares with Port Harcourt, Nigeria, 54 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Port Harcourt is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 26.1 °C against 25.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Port Harcourt is 0.7 °C warmer (Aba 24.9 °C, Port Harcourt 25.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aba 32.8 °C (February), Port Harcourt 32.6 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Aba 20.8 °C, Port Harcourt 21.4 °C.
  • Aba gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 2,485 mm against 2,285 mm.
  • Aba gets about 125 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,498 h against 1,373 h; about 1961–1990).
