Diyarbakır vs Mosul climate

How the climate of Diyarbakır, Turkey compares with Mosul, Iraq, 311 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mosul is 4.8 °C (8.7 °F) warmer over the year: 21.0 °C against 16.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Mosul is 6.0 °C warmer (Diyarbakır 19.6 °C, Mosul 25.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Diyarbakır 39.1 °C (July), Mosul 44.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Diyarbakır -1.6 °C, Mosul 2.3 °C.
  • Diyarbakır gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 558 mm against 435 mm.
  • Mosul gets about 179 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,196 h against 3,017 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Diyarbakır 39 cm, Mosul 1.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
