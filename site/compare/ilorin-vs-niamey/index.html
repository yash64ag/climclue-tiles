Ilorin vs Niamey climate

How the climate of Ilorin, Nigeria compares with Niamey, Niger, 618 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Niamey is 2.7 °C (4.9 °F) warmer over the year: 29.3 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Niamey is 7.1 °C warmer (Ilorin 27.3 °C, Niamey 34.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ilorin 36.3 °C (March), Niamey 40.5 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Ilorin 20.4 °C, Niamey 15.9 °C.
  • Ilorin gets 135% more rain and snow a year: 1,085 mm against 462 mm.
  • Niamey gets about 685 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,904 h against 2,219 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
