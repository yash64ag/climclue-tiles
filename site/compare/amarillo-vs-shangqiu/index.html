Amarillo vs Shangqiu climate

How the climate of Amarillo, United States compares with Shangqiu, China, 11,348 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shangqiu is 0.9 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 15.0 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Shangqiu is 2.5 °C warmer (Amarillo 23.4 °C, Shangqiu 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amarillo 32.2 °C (July), Shangqiu 31.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Amarillo -1.5 °C, Shangqiu -4.0 °C.
  • Shangqiu gets 32% more rain and snow a year: 724 mm against 550 mm.
  • Amarillo gets about 967 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 2,276 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amarillo 23 cm, Shangqiu 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
