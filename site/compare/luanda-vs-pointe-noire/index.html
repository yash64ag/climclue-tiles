Luanda vs Pointe-Noire climate

How the climate of Luanda, Angola compares with Pointe-Noire, Republic of the Congo, 476 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Luanda is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 24.9 °C against 24.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Luanda is 0.7 °C warmer (Luanda 27.2 °C, Pointe-Noire 26.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Luanda 30.5 °C (March), Pointe-Noire 30.3 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Luanda 18.7 °C, Pointe-Noire 18.3 °C.
  • Pointe-Noire gets 207% more rain and snow a year: 1,343 mm against 437 mm.
  • Luanda gets about 584 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,306 h against 1,722 h; about 1961–1990).
