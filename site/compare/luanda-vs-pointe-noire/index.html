Luanda vs Pointe-Noire climate

How the climate of Luanda, Angola compares with Pointe-Noire, Republic of the Congo, 476 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Luanda is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 25.0 °C against 24.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Luanda is 0.9 °C warmer (Luanda 27.3 °C, Pointe-Noire 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Luanda 31.7 °C (February), Pointe-Noire 28.8 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Luanda 18.2 °C, Pointe-Noire 19.6 °C.
  • Pointe-Noire gets 217% more rain and snow a year: 1,457 mm against 459 mm.
  • Luanda gets about 584 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,306 h against 1,722 h; about 1961–1990).
