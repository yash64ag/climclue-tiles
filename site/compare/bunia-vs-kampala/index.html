Bunia vs Kampala climate

How the climate of Bunia, Democratic Republic of the Congo compares with Kampala, Uganda, 294 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kampala is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 21.3 °C against 21.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Kampala is 0.6 °C warmer (Bunia 20.4 °C, Kampala 21.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bunia 27.8 °C (February), Kampala 25.4 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Bunia 16.4 °C, Kampala 17.2 °C.
  • Kampala gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,395 mm against 1,238 mm.
  • Kampala gets about 319 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,454 h against 2,135 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Kampala in 7 of 12 months (3 equal)
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Kampala in 12 of 12 months
