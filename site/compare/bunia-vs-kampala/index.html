Bunia vs Kampala climate

How the climate of Bunia, Democratic Republic of the Congo compares with Kampala, Uganda, 294 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kampala is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 21.5 °C against 21.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Kampala is 0.8 °C warmer (Bunia 20.5 °C, Kampala 21.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bunia 27.8 °C (February), Kampala 25.1 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Bunia 16.3 °C, Kampala 17.8 °C.
  • Kampala gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,388 mm against 1,261 mm.
  • Kampala gets about 319 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,454 h against 2,135 h; about 1961–1990).
