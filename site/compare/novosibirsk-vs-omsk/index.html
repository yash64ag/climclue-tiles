Novosibirsk vs Omsk climate

How the climate of Novosibirsk, Russia compares with Omsk, Russia, 609 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Omsk is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 2.1 °C against 1.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Omsk is 1.4 °C warmer (Novosibirsk 2.3 °C, Omsk 3.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Novosibirsk 25.5 °C (July), Omsk 25.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Novosibirsk -21.1 °C, Omsk -20.8 °C.
  • Novosibirsk gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 463 mm against 391 mm.
  • Omsk gets about 64 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,171 h against 2,107 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Novosibirsk 163 cm, Omsk 118 cm (ERA5-Land).
