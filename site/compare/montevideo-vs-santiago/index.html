Montevideo vs Santiago climate

How the climate of Montevideo, Uruguay compares with Santiago, Chile, 1,339 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Montevideo is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.4 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Montevideo is 2.2 °C warmer (Montevideo 14.4 °C, Santiago 12.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Montevideo 27.2 °C (January), Santiago 28.3 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Montevideo 6.6 °C, Santiago 3.7 °C.
  • Montevideo gets 74% more rain and snow a year: 1,075 mm against 618 mm.
  • Santiago gets about 52 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,538 h against 2,486 h; about 1961–1990).
