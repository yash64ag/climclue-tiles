Shenzhen vs Zhuhai, Guangdong climate

How the climate of Shenzhen, China compares with Zhuhai, Guangdong, China, 59 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shenzhen and Zhuhai, Guangdong have the same yearly average temperature (23.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Zhuhai, Guangdong is 0.3 °C warmer (Shenzhen 15.4 °C, Zhuhai, Guangdong 15.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Shenzhen 32.2 °C (July), Zhuhai, Guangdong 31.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Shenzhen 11.2 °C, Zhuhai, Guangdong 12.5 °C.
  • Shenzhen gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 2,176 mm against 2,075 mm.
  • Zhuhai, Guangdong gets about 5 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,945 h against 1,940 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
