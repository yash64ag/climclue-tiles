Johannesburg vs Maseru climate

How the climate of Johannesburg, South Africa compares with Maseru, Lesotho, 351 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Johannesburg is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 15.1 °C against 14.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Johannesburg is 1.9 °C warmer (Johannesburg 8.6 °C, Maseru 6.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Johannesburg 24.3 °C (January), Maseru 26.8 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Johannesburg 2.1 °C, Maseru -0.5 °C.
  • Johannesburg gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 792 mm against 662 mm.
  • Johannesburg gets about 25 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,169 h against 3,144 h; about 1961–1990).
