Camayenne vs Touba climate

How the climate of Camayenne, Guinea compares with Touba, Senegal, 638 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Touba is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 26.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Touba is 3.2 °C warmer (Camayenne 25.4 °C, Touba 28.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Camayenne 28.4 °C (December), Touba 37.5 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Camayenne 24.2 °C, Touba 16.8 °C.
  • Camayenne gets 659% more rain and snow a year: 3,562 mm against 469 mm.
  • Touba gets about 847 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,032 h against 2,185 h; about 1961–1990).
