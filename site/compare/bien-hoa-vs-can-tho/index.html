How the climate of Biên Hòa, Vietnam compares with Cần Thơ, Vietnam: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Biên Hòa is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 27.4 °C against 27.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Biên Hòa is 1.0 °C warmer (Biên Hòa 26.6 °C, Cần Thơ 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Biên Hòa 34.6 °C (April), Cần Thơ 33.8 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Biên Hòa 21.5 °C, Cần Thơ 21.3 °C.
  • Biên Hòa gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,949 mm against 1,717 mm.
  • Cần Thơ gets about 276 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,017 h against 1,741 h; about 1961–1990).
