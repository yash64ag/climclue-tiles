Biên Hòa vs Cần Thơ climate

How the climate of Biên Hòa, Vietnam compares with Cần Thơ, Vietnam, 152 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Biên Hòa is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 27.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Biên Hòa is 0.9 °C warmer (Biên Hòa 27.4 °C, Cần Thơ 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Biên Hòa 34.0 °C (April), Cần Thơ 33.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Biên Hòa 21.7 °C, Cần Thơ 21.8 °C.
  • Biên Hòa gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 1,976 mm against 1,720 mm.
  • Cần Thơ gets about 276 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,017 h against 1,741 h; about 1961–1990).
