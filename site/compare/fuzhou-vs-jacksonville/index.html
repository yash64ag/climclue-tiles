Main differences

  • Jacksonville, Florida is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 20.9 °C against 20.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Jacksonville, Florida is 2.5 °C warmer (Fuzhou, Fujian 14.6 °C, Jacksonville, Florida 17.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuzhou, Fujian 34.3 °C (July), Jacksonville, Florida 33.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuzhou, Fujian 8.1 °C, Jacksonville, Florida 6.6 °C.
