Bandar Lampung vs Palembang climate

How the climate of Bandar Lampung, Indonesia compares with Palembang, Indonesia, 285 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Palembang is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 26.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Palembang is 1.0 °C warmer (Bandar Lampung 25.8 °C, Palembang 26.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bandar Lampung 29.6 °C (October), Palembang 31.6 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Bandar Lampung 22.7 °C, Palembang 22.9 °C.
  • Palembang gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 3,044 mm against 2,377 mm.
  • Bandar Lampung gets about 46 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,468 h against 2,422 h; about 1961–1990).
