Jinan vs Zibo climate

How the climate of Jinan, China compares with Zibo, China, 96 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jinan is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 14.3 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Jinan is 0.6 °C warmer (Jinan 2.3 °C, Zibo 1.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinan 31.7 °C (July), Zibo 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinan -5.7 °C, Zibo -6.8 °C.
  • Jinan gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 657 mm against 608 mm.
  • Jinan gets about 17 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,554 h against 2,536 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinan 10 cm, Zibo 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
