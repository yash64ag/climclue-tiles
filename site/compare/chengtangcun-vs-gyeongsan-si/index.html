How the climate of Chengtangcun, China compares with Gyeongsan-si, South Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Chengtangcun is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 14.4 °C against 13.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Chengtangcun is 2.2 °C warmer (Chengtangcun 25.1 °C, Gyeongsan-si 22.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chengtangcun 31.1 °C (July), Gyeongsan-si 30.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Chengtangcun -5.0 °C, Gyeongsan-si -5.1 °C.
  • Gyeongsan-si gets 52% more rain and snow a year: 1,120 mm against 735 mm.
  • Gyeongsan-si gets about 86 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,452 h against 2,366 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chengtangcun 11 cm, Gyeongsan-si 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
