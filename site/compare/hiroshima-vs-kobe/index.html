How the climate of Hiroshima, Japan compares with Kobe, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kobe is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.9 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Kobe is 0.5 °C warmer (Hiroshima 24.4 °C, Kobe 24.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hiroshima 31.9 °C (August), Kobe 32.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Hiroshima 2.3 °C, Kobe 2.7 °C.
  • Hiroshima gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,546 mm against 1,368 mm.
  • Hiroshima gets about 50 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,896 h against 1,846 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hiroshima 14 cm, Kobe 5.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
