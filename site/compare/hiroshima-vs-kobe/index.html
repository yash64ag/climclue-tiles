Hiroshima vs Kobe climate

How the climate of Hiroshima, Japan compares with Kobe, Japan, 252 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kobe is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 15.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Kobe is 1.3 °C warmer (Hiroshima 12.3 °C, Kobe 13.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hiroshima 30.0 °C (August), Kobe 29.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Hiroshima 0.7 °C, Kobe 1.9 °C.
  • Hiroshima gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,604 mm against 1,378 mm.
  • Hiroshima gets about 50 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,896 h against 1,846 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hiroshima 38 cm, Kobe 8.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
