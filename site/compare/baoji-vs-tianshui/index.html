Baoji vs Tianshui climate

How the climate of Baoji, China compares with Tianshui, China, 139 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Baoji is 2.1 °C (3.7 °F) warmer over the year: 14.4 °C against 12.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Baoji is 2.4 °C warmer (Baoji 24.2 °C, Tianshui 21.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoji 30.4 °C (July), Tianshui 28.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baoji -3.9 °C, Tianshui -6.1 °C.
  • Baoji gets 31% more rain and snow a year: 653 mm against 499 mm.
  • Tianshui gets about 87 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,026 h against 1,939 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoji 6.0 cm, Tianshui 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
