How the climate of Baoji, China compares with Tianshui, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Baoji is 2.6 °C (4.7 °F) warmer over the year: 14.4 °C against 11.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Baoji is 3.1 °C warmer (Baoji 24.2 °C, Tianshui 21.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoji 31.3 °C (July), Tianshui 28.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baoji -3.0 °C, Tianshui -5.7 °C.
  • Baoji gets 31% more rain and snow a year: 653 mm against 499 mm.
  • Tianshui gets about 87 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,026 h against 1,939 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoji 6.0 cm, Tianshui 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
