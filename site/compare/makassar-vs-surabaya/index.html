Makassar vs Surabaya climate

How the climate of Makassar, Indonesia compares with Surabaya, Indonesia, 775 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Surabaya is 1.0 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 27.8 °C against 26.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Surabaya is 1.5 °C warmer (Makassar 26.9 °C, Surabaya 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Makassar 29.7 °C (September), Surabaya 32.5 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Makassar 23.9 °C, Surabaya 23.8 °C.
  • Makassar gets 80% more rain and snow a year: 3,336 mm against 1,854 mm.
  • Surabaya gets about 391 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 2,852 h; about 1961–1990).
