Brasília vs Goiânia climate

How the climate of Brasília, Brazil compares with Goiânia, Brazil, 173 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Goiânia is 2.1 °C (3.8 °F) warmer over the year: 23.7 °C against 21.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Goiânia is 2.5 °C warmer (Brasília 21.3 °C, Goiânia 23.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brasília 28.4 °C (September), Goiânia 31.1 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Brasília 14.3 °C, Goiânia 16.4 °C.
  • Brasília gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,507 mm against 1,465 mm.
  • Goiânia gets about 147 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,501 h against 2,354 h; about 1961–1990).
