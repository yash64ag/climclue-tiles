How the climate of Brasília, Brazil compares with Goiânia, Brazil: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Goiânia is 2.2 °C (4.0 °F) warmer over the year: 23.5 °C against 21.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Goiânia is 2.6 °C warmer (Brasília 20.9 °C, Goiânia 23.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brasília 28.9 °C (September), Goiânia 32.0 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Brasília 12.9 °C, Goiânia 14.0 °C.
  • Brasília gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,544 mm against 1,478 mm.
  • Goiânia gets about 147 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,501 h against 2,354 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
