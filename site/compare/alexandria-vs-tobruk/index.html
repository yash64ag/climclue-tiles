Alexandria vs Tobruk climate

How the climate of Alexandria, Egypt compares with Tobruk, Libya, 573 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Alexandria is 0.4 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 20.4 °C against 20.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Alexandria is 0.9 °C warmer (Alexandria 16.4 °C, Tobruk 15.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Alexandria 30.1 °C (August), Tobruk 30.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Alexandria 9.6 °C, Tobruk 9.6 °C.
  • Alexandria gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 196 mm against 161 mm.
  • Alexandria gets about 89 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,256 h against 3,167 h; about 1961–1990).
