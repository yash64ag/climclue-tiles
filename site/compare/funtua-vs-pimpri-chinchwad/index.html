Funtua vs Pimpri-Chinchwad climate

How the climate of Funtua, Nigeria compares with Pimpri-Chinchwad, India, 7,146 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Funtua is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 24.7 °C against 24.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Funtua is 1.3 °C warmer (Funtua 29.6 °C, Pimpri-Chinchwad 28.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Funtua 36.4 °C (April), Pimpri-Chinchwad 36.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Funtua 14.6 °C, Pimpri-Chinchwad 14.7 °C.
  • Funtua gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,120 mm against 1,061 mm.
  • Pimpri-Chinchwad gets about 5 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,789 h against 2,783 h; about 1961–1990).
