How the climate of Hohhot, China compares with Kanggye, North Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kanggye is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 8.1 °C against 7.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Kanggye is 2.9 °C warmer (Hohhot 20.8 °C, Kanggye 23.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hohhot 29.1 °C (July), Kanggye 28.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hohhot -18.1 °C, Kanggye -18.1 °C.
  • Kanggye gets 111% more rain and snow a year: 787 mm against 374 mm.
  • Hohhot gets about 527 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,933 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hohhot 19 cm, Kanggye 65 cm (ERA5-Land).
