Hohhot vs Kanggye climate

How the climate of Hohhot, China compares with Kanggye, North Korea, 1,254 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kanggye is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 6.9 °C against 6.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Kanggye is 1.8 °C warmer (Hohhot 20.6 °C, Kanggye 22.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hohhot 27.9 °C (July), Kanggye 26.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Hohhot -18.5 °C, Kanggye -22.2 °C.
  • Kanggye gets 135% more rain and snow a year: 885 mm against 376 mm.
  • Hohhot gets about 527 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,933 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hohhot 19 cm, Kanggye 65 cm (ERA5-Land).
