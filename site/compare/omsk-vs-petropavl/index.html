Omsk vs Petropavl climate

How the climate of Omsk, Russia compares with Petropavl, Kazakhstan, 270 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Petropavl is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 2.4 °C against 2.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Petropavl is 1.1 °C warmer (Omsk -15.4 °C, Petropavl -14.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Omsk 25.5 °C (July), Petropavl 25.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Omsk -20.8 °C, Petropavl -19.7 °C.
  • Petropavl gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 399 mm against 391 mm.
  • Omsk gets about 9 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,171 h against 2,162 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Omsk 118 cm, Petropavl 122 cm (ERA5-Land).
