How the climate of Berlin, Germany compares with Prague, Czechia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Prague is 0.9 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 10.5 °C against 9.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Prague is 1.5 °C warmer (Berlin 16.8 °C, Prague 18.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Berlin 24.5 °C (July), Prague 25.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Berlin -2.0 °C, Prague -1.8 °C.
  • Berlin gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 594 mm against 525 mm.
  • Prague gets about 26 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,640 h against 1,614 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Berlin 51 cm, Prague 42 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Prague in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Prague in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Prague in 9 of 12 months (1 equal)
