Jalālābād vs Peshawar climate

How the climate of Jalālābād, Afghanistan compares with Peshawar, Pakistan, 113 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Peshawar is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 22.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Peshawar is 1.3 °C warmer (Jalālābād 12.3 °C, Peshawar 13.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jalālābād 41.1 °C (June), Peshawar 41.4 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Jalālābād 3.9 °C, Peshawar 4.1 °C.
  • Peshawar gets 46% more rain and snow a year: 583 mm against 399 mm.
  • Jalālābād gets about 75 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,923 h against 2,849 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Peshawar in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: Jalālābād in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Jalālābād in 7 of 12 months
