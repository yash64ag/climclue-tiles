Jalālābād vs Peshawar climate

How the climate of Jalālābād, Afghanistan compares with Peshawar, Pakistan, 113 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Peshawar is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 23.3 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Peshawar is 1.8 °C warmer (Jalālābād 11.9 °C, Peshawar 13.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jalālābād 38.0 °C (June), Peshawar 39.8 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Jalālābād 3.5 °C, Peshawar 4.9 °C.
  • Peshawar gets 48% more rain and snow a year: 590 mm against 399 mm.
  • Jalālābād gets about 75 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,923 h against 2,849 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Jalālābād in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Jalālābād in 7 of 12 months
