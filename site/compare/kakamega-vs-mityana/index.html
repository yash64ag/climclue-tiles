Kakamega vs Mityana climate

How the climate of Kakamega, Kenya compares with Mityana, Uganda, 304 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mityana is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 20.6 °C against 20.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Mityana is 0.7 °C warmer (Kakamega 19.3 °C, Mityana 19.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kakamega 27.8 °C (February), Mityana 26.7 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Kakamega 14.7 °C, Mityana 15.7 °C.
  • Kakamega gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,729 mm against 1,430 mm.
  • Kakamega gets about 434 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,755 h against 2,321 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Mityana in 10 of 12 months (2 equal)
  • Drier: Mityana in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Kakamega in 12 of 12 months
