Kakamega vs Mityana climate

How the climate of Kakamega, Kenya compares with Mityana, Uganda, 304 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mityana is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 20.9 °C against 20.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Mityana is 1.2 °C warmer (Kakamega 19.0 °C, Mityana 20.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kakamega 29.6 °C (February), Mityana 28.2 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Kakamega 12.3 °C, Mityana 14.6 °C.
  • Kakamega gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 1,674 mm against 1,319 mm.
  • Kakamega gets about 434 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,755 h against 2,321 h; about 1961–1990).
