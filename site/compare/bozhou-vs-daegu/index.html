Bozhou vs Daegu climate

How the climate of Bozhou, China compares with Daegu, South Korea, 1,190 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bozhou is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Bozhou is 2.7 °C warmer (Bozhou 25.9 °C, Daegu 23.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bozhou 31.8 °C (July), Daegu 31.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bozhou -3.6 °C, Daegu -5.3 °C.
  • Daegu gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 979 mm against 811 mm.
  • Daegu gets about 263 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,483 h against 2,220 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bozhou 17 cm, Daegu 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
