Nepalgunj vs Ranchi climate

How the climate of Nepalgunj, Nepal compares with Ranchi, India, 641 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Nepalgunj is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 24.3 °C against 23.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Nepalgunj is 3.5 °C warmer (Nepalgunj 29.1 °C, Ranchi 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nepalgunj 38.0 °C (May), Ranchi 36.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Nepalgunj 7.2 °C, Ranchi 9.0 °C.
  • Nepalgunj gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,606 mm against 1,413 mm.
  • Nepalgunj gets about 253 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,496 h against 2,243 h; about 1961–1990).
