Nanyang vs Shiyan, Hubei climate

How the climate of Nanyang, China compares with Shiyan, Hubei, China, 170 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shiyan, Hubei is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 15.8 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Shiyan, Hubei is 0.8 °C warmer (Nanyang 2.2 °C, Shiyan, Hubei 3.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nanyang 31.7 °C (July), Shiyan, Hubei 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nanyang -2.8 °C, Shiyan, Hubei -2.1 °C.
  • Shiyan, Hubei gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 892 mm against 843 mm.
  • Nanyang gets about 209 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,008 h against 1,798 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nanyang 16 cm, Shiyan, Hubei 18 cm (ERA5-Land).
