Gulu vs Yaoundé climate

How the climate of Gulu, Uganda compares with Yaoundé, Cameroon, 2,310 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gulu is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 23.5 °C against 23.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Gulu is 1.9 °C warmer (Gulu 25.4 °C, Yaoundé 23.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gulu 32.0 °C (February), Yaoundé 29.9 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Gulu 17.6 °C, Yaoundé 19.7 °C.
  • Yaoundé gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,729 mm against 1,629 mm.
  • Gulu gets about 811 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,575 h against 1,763 h; about 1961–1990).
