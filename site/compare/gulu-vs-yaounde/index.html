Gulu vs Yaoundé climate

How the climate of Gulu, Uganda compares with Yaoundé, Cameroon, 2,310 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gulu is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 23.4 °C against 23.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Gulu is 2.0 °C warmer (Gulu 25.5 °C, Yaoundé 23.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gulu 31.9 °C (February), Yaoundé 29.7 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Gulu 17.4 °C, Yaoundé 19.6 °C.
  • Yaoundé gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,794 mm against 1,594 mm.
  • Gulu gets about 811 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,575 h against 1,763 h; about 1961–1990).
