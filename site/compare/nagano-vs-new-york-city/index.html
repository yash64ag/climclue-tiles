Nagano vs New York City climate

How the climate of Nagano, Japan compares with New York City, United States, 10,808 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Nagano is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 12.4 °C against 12.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Nagano is 1.5 °C warmer (Nagano 25.3 °C, New York City 23.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nagano 29.1 °C (August), New York City 28.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nagano -5.3 °C, New York City -3.9 °C.
  • Nagano gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,237 mm against 1,193 mm.
  • New York City gets about 696 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,499 h against 1,803 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nagano 194 cm, New York City 80 cm (ERA5-Land).
