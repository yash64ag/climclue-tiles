How the climate of Greeley, United States compares with Lanzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lanzhou is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 11.0 °C against 10.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Lanzhou is 3.3 °C warmer (Greeley 9.7 °C, Lanzhou 13.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Greeley 32.2 °C (July), Lanzhou 29.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Greeley -9.2 °C, Lanzhou -9.6 °C.
  • Greeley gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 357 mm against 285 mm.
  • Greeley gets about 545 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,021 h against 2,476 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Greeley 76 cm, Lanzhou 7.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
