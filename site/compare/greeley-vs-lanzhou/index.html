Greeley vs Lanzhou climate

How the climate of Greeley, United States compares with Lanzhou, China, 11,025 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lanzhou is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 11.5 °C against 10.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Lanzhou is 3.8 °C warmer (Greeley 9.6 °C, Lanzhou 13.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Greeley 31.9 °C (July), Lanzhou 28.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Greeley -7.2 °C, Lanzhou -8.1 °C.
  • Greeley gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 357 mm against 285 mm.
  • Greeley gets about 545 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,021 h against 2,476 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Greeley 42 cm, Lanzhou 7.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
