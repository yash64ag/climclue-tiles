Kathmandu vs Prayagraj climate

How the climate of Kathmandu, Nepal compares with Prayagraj, India, 427 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Prayagraj is 8.0 °C (14.4 °F) warmer over the year: 25.7 °C against 17.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Prayagraj is 12.7 °C warmer (Kathmandu 20.9 °C, Prayagraj 33.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kathmandu 25.4 °C (June), Prayagraj 40.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kathmandu 5.0 °C, Prayagraj 9.8 °C.
  • Kathmandu gets 119% more rain and snow a year: 1,984 mm against 907 mm.
  • Prayagraj gets about 675 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,666 h against 1,990 h; about 1961–1990).
