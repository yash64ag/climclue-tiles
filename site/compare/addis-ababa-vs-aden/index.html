Addis Ababa vs Aden climate

How the climate of Addis Ababa, Ethiopia compares with Aden, Yemen, 804 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Aden is 12.9 °C (23.3 °F) warmer over the year: 27.4 °C against 14.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Aden is 16.4 °C warmer (Addis Ababa 13.8 °C, Aden 30.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Addis Ababa 22.0 °C (March), Aden 31.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Addis Ababa 6.2 °C, Aden 23.2 °C.
  • Addis Ababa gets 1,322% more rain and snow a year: 1,199 mm against 84 mm.
  • Aden gets about 305 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,132 h against 2,828 h; about 1961–1990).
