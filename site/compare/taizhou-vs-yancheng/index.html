Taizhou, Jiangsu vs Yancheng climate

How the climate of Taizhou, Jiangsu, China compares with Yancheng, China, 99 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Taizhou, Jiangsu is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.5 °C against 15.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Taizhou, Jiangsu is 0.8 °C warmer (Taizhou, Jiangsu 4.5 °C, Yancheng 3.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Taizhou, Jiangsu 31.3 °C (July), Yancheng 30.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Taizhou, Jiangsu -1.5 °C, Yancheng -2.2 °C.
  • Taizhou, Jiangsu gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,115 mm against 1,052 mm.
  • Yancheng gets about 97 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,176 h against 2,078 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Taizhou, Jiangsu 14 cm, Yancheng 10 cm (ERA5-Land).
