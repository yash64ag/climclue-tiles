Taizhou, Jiangsu vs Yancheng climate

How the climate of Taizhou, Jiangsu, China compares with Yancheng, China, 99 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Taizhou, Jiangsu is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.6 °C against 15.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Taizhou, Jiangsu is 0.9 °C warmer (Taizhou, Jiangsu 4.7 °C, Yancheng 3.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Taizhou, Jiangsu 31.1 °C (July), Yancheng 30.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Taizhou, Jiangsu -2.1 °C, Yancheng -2.7 °C.
  • Taizhou, Jiangsu gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,116 mm against 1,049 mm.
  • Yancheng gets about 97 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,176 h against 2,078 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Taizhou, Jiangsu 14 cm, Yancheng 10 cm (ERA5-Land).
