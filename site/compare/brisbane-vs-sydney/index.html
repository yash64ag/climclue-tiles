Brisbane vs Sydney climate

How the climate of Brisbane, Australia compares with Sydney, Australia, 733 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Brisbane is 2.3 °C (4.1 °F) warmer over the year: 20.0 °C against 17.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Brisbane is 3.2 °C warmer (Brisbane 22.1 °C, Sydney 18.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brisbane 28.3 °C (January), Sydney 23.9 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Brisbane 10.2 °C, Sydney 10.4 °C.
  • Sydney gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,097 mm against 1,042 mm.
  • Brisbane gets about 345 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,833 h against 2,488 h; about 1961–1990).
