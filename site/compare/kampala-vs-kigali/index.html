Kampala vs Kigali climate

How the climate of Kampala, Uganda compares with Kigali, Rwanda, 377 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kampala is 1.0 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 21.5 °C against 20.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Kampala is 1.7 °C warmer (Kampala 22.2 °C, Kigali 20.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kampala 25.1 °C (February), Kigali 26.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kampala 17.8 °C, Kigali 14.6 °C.
  • Kampala gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 1,388 mm against 1,069 mm.
  • Kampala gets about 464 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,454 h against 1,990 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
