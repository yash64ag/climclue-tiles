How the climate of Guangzhou, China compares with Jiangmen, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Guangzhou is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 23.2 °C against 23.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Guangzhou is 0.4 °C warmer (Guangzhou 29.5 °C, Jiangmen 29.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guangzhou 33.3 °C (July), Jiangmen 32.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Guangzhou 10.7 °C, Jiangmen 11.2 °C.
  • Jiangmen gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 2,139 mm against 2,053 mm.
  • Jiangmen gets about 74 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,875 h against 1,800 h; about 1961–1990).
