Guangzhou vs Jiangmen climate

How the climate of Guangzhou, China compares with Jiangmen, China, 62 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jiangmen is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 22.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Jiangmen is 0.4 °C warmer (Guangzhou 15.8 °C, Jiangmen 16.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guangzhou 31.7 °C (July), Jiangmen 31.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Guangzhou 10.1 °C, Jiangmen 10.5 °C.
  • Jiangmen gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 2,173 mm against 2,108 mm.
  • Jiangmen gets about 74 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,875 h against 1,800 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Jiangmen in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Jiangmen in 12 of 12 months
