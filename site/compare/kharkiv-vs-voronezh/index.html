Kharkiv vs Voronezh climate

How the climate of Kharkiv, Ukraine compares with Voronezh, Russia, 279 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kharkiv is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 8.1 °C against 6.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Kharkiv is 1.9 °C warmer (Kharkiv -4.5 °C, Voronezh -6.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kharkiv 25.9 °C (July), Voronezh 25.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kharkiv -7.8 °C, Voronezh -9.6 °C.
  • Voronezh gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 616 mm against 528 mm.
  • Voronezh gets about 139 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,891 h against 1,752 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kharkiv 140 cm, Voronezh 161 cm (ERA5-Land).
