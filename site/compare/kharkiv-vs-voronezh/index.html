How the climate of Kharkiv, Ukraine compares with Voronezh, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kharkiv is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 8.3 °C against 7.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Kharkiv is 2.1 °C warmer (Kharkiv -4.3 °C, Voronezh -6.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kharkiv 26.4 °C (July), Voronezh 26.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kharkiv -7.1 °C, Voronezh -9.7 °C.
  • Voronezh gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 637 mm against 526 mm.
  • Voronezh gets about 139 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,891 h against 1,752 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kharkiv 105 cm, Voronezh 145 cm (ERA5-Land).
