How the climate of Daegu, South Korea compares with Zoucheng, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zoucheng is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 14.2 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Zoucheng is 2.0 °C warmer (Daegu 23.1 °C, Zoucheng 25.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daegu 31.1 °C (August), Zoucheng 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Daegu -5.3 °C, Zoucheng -5.5 °C.
  • Daegu gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 979 mm against 729 mm.
  • Daegu gets about 76 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,483 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daegu 13 cm, Zoucheng 9.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
