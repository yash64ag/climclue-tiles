How the climate of Jiaxing, China compares with Suzhou, Jiangsu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jiaxing is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Jiaxing is 0.5 °C warmer (Jiaxing 4.5 °C, Suzhou, Jiangsu 4.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiaxing 32.6 °C (July), Suzhou, Jiangsu 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jiaxing 0.8 °C, Suzhou, Jiangsu 0.3 °C.
  • Jiaxing gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 1,455 mm against 1,208 mm.
  • Suzhou, Jiangsu gets about 54 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,977 h against 1,922 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jiaxing 13 cm, Suzhou, Jiangsu 11 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Warmer: Jiaxing in 9 of 12 months
