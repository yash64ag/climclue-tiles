How the climate of Hangzhou, China compares with Shaoxing, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hangzhou is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 17.6 °C against 17.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Hangzhou is 0.3 °C warmer (Hangzhou 7.5 °C, Shaoxing 7.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hangzhou 33.7 °C (July), Shaoxing 34.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hangzhou 1.7 °C, Shaoxing 1.2 °C.
  • Hangzhou gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,499 mm against 1,452 mm.
  • Hangzhou gets about 6 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,882 h against 1,876 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hangzhou 24 cm, Shaoxing 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
