Hangzhou vs Shaoxing climate

How the climate of Hangzhou, China compares with Shaoxing, China, 52 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shaoxing is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 17.6 °C against 17.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Shaoxing is 0.5 °C warmer (Hangzhou 24.1 °C, Shaoxing 24.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hangzhou 32.8 °C (July), Shaoxing 33.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hangzhou 0.1 °C, Shaoxing 0.6 °C.
  • Hangzhou gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,569 mm against 1,422 mm.
  • Hangzhou gets about 6 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,882 h against 1,876 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hangzhou 27 cm, Shaoxing 22 cm (ERA5-Land).
