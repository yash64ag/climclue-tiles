Hangzhou vs Shaoxing climate

How the climate of Hangzhou, China compares with Shaoxing, China, 52 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shaoxing is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 17.5 °C against 17.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Shaoxing is 0.5 °C warmer (Hangzhou 12.8 °C, Shaoxing 13.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hangzhou 33.5 °C (July), Shaoxing 34.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hangzhou 0.9 °C, Shaoxing 1.2 °C.
  • Hangzhou gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,499 mm against 1,452 mm.
  • Hangzhou gets about 6 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,882 h against 1,876 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hangzhou 24 cm, Shaoxing 16 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Shaoxing in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Hangzhou in 7 of 12 months (1 equal)
