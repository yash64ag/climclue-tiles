Main differences

  • Longyan is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 20.7 °C against 20.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Longyan is 1.3 °C warmer (Gainesville, Florida 26.7 °C, Longyan 28.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gainesville, Florida 32.2 °C (July), Longyan 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gainesville, Florida 5.7 °C, Longyan 7.5 °C.
  • Longyan gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 1,570 mm against 1,303 mm.
  • Gainesville, Florida gets about 1,036 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,882 h against 1,846 h; about 1961–1990).
