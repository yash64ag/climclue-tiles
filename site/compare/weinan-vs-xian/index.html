Weinan vs Xi’an climate

How the climate of Weinan, China compares with Xi’an, China, 60 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Weinan is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 15.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Weinan is 1.3 °C warmer (Weinan 27.3 °C, Xi’an 26.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Weinan 34.0 °C (July), Xi’an 32.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Weinan -3.3 °C, Xi’an -3.1 °C.
  • Xi’an gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 664 mm against 495 mm.
  • Weinan gets about 108 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,017 h against 1,909 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Weinan 5.8 cm, Xi’an 10 cm (ERA5-Land).
