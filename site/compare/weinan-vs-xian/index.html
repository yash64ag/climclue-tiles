Weinan vs Xi’an climate

How the climate of Weinan, China compares with Xi’an, China, 60 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Weinan is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 16.2 °C against 15.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Weinan is 1.5 °C warmer (Weinan 27.4 °C, Xi’an 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Weinan 33.4 °C (July), Xi’an 31.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Weinan -2.9 °C, Xi’an -3.5 °C.
  • Xi’an gets 42% more rain and snow a year: 674 mm against 475 mm.
  • Weinan gets about 108 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,017 h against 1,909 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Weinan 30 cm, Xi’an 38 cm (ERA5-Land).
