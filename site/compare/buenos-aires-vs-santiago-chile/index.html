Buenos Aires vs Santiago climate

How the climate of Buenos Aires, Argentina compares with Santiago, Chile, 1,137 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Buenos Aires is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.9 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Buenos Aires is 1.7 °C warmer (Buenos Aires 13.9 °C, Santiago 12.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Buenos Aires 27.1 °C (January), Santiago 29.2 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Buenos Aires 7.8 °C, Santiago 3.7 °C.
  • Buenos Aires gets 89% more rain and snow a year: 1,142 mm against 603 mm.
  • Santiago gets about 105 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,538 h against 2,433 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Buenos Aires in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Santiago in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
