Bangkok vs Cần Thơ climate

How the climate of Bangkok, Thailand compares with Cần Thơ, Vietnam, 708 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bangkok is 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) warmer over the year: 28.5 °C against 27.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Bangkok is 2.2 °C warmer (Bangkok 29.3 °C, Cần Thơ 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bangkok 35.2 °C (April), Cần Thơ 33.8 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Bangkok 20.8 °C, Cần Thơ 21.3 °C.
  • Cần Thơ gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 1,717 mm against 1,459 mm.
  • Bangkok gets about 525 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,541 h against 2,017 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Bangkok in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Bangkok in 11 of 12 months
