How the climate of Habikino, Japan compares with Suzhou, Jiangsu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Suzhou, Jiangsu is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Suzhou, Jiangsu is 1.5 °C warmer (Habikino 19.5 °C, Suzhou, Jiangsu 20.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Habikino 33.0 °C (August), Suzhou, Jiangsu 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Habikino 1.5 °C, Suzhou, Jiangsu 0.3 °C.
  • Habikino gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,371 mm against 1,208 mm.
  • Suzhou, Jiangsu gets about 112 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,977 h against 1,864 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Habikino 6.6 cm, Suzhou, Jiangsu 11 cm (ERA5-Land).
