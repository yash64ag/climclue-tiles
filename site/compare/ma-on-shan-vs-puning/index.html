Ma On Shan vs Puning climate

How the climate of Ma On Shan, Hong Kong compares with Puning, China, 222 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Puning is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 22.3 °C against 22.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Puning is 1.2 °C warmer (Ma On Shan 27.6 °C, Puning 28.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ma On Shan 30.4 °C (July), Puning 31.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ma On Shan 11.9 °C, Puning 10.3 °C.
  • Ma On Shan gets 46% more rain and snow a year: 2,395 mm against 1,639 mm.
  • Puning gets about 16 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,980 h against 1,964 h; about 1961–1990).
