Al Ḩasakah vs Mosul climate

How the climate of Al Ḩasakah, Syria compares with Mosul, Iraq, 213 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mosul is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 21.0 °C against 19.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Mosul is 1.4 °C warmer (Al Ḩasakah 30.4 °C, Mosul 31.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Al Ḩasakah 40.5 °C (July), Mosul 42.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Al Ḩasakah 1.8 °C, Mosul 2.6 °C.
  • Mosul gets 40% more rain and snow a year: 430 mm against 306 mm.
  • Mosul gets about 190 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,196 h against 3,005 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Al Ḩasakah 4.1 cm, Mosul 3.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
