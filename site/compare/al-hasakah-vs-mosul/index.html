Al Ḩasakah vs Mosul climate

How the climate of Al Ḩasakah, Syria compares with Mosul, Iraq, 213 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Mosul is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 21.0 °C against 20.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Mosul is 1.4 °C warmer (Al Ḩasakah 34.0 °C, Mosul 35.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Al Ḩasakah 42.8 °C (July), Mosul 44.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Al Ḩasakah 1.6 °C, Mosul 2.3 °C.
  • Mosul gets 52% more rain and snow a year: 435 mm against 287 mm.
  • Mosul gets about 190 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,196 h against 3,005 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Al Ḩasakah 1.5 cm, Mosul 1.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
