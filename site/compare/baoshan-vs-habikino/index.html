Baoshan, Shanghai vs Habikino climate

How the climate of Baoshan, Shanghai, China compares with Habikino, Japan, 1,360 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Baoshan, Shanghai and Habikino have the same yearly average temperature (16.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Habikino is 0.9 °C warmer (Baoshan, Shanghai 4.5 °C, Habikino 5.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoshan, Shanghai 30.9 °C (July), Habikino 32.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Baoshan, Shanghai 1.3 °C, Habikino 0.3 °C.
  • Habikino gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,390 mm against 1,358 mm.
  • Baoshan, Shanghai gets about 122 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,986 h against 1,864 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoshan, Shanghai 7.1 cm, Habikino 6.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
