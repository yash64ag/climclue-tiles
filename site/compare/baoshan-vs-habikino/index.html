Baoshan, Shanghai vs Habikino climate

How the climate of Baoshan, Shanghai, China compares with Habikino, Japan, 1,360 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Baoshan, Shanghai is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Habikino is 0.9 °C warmer (Baoshan, Shanghai 27.8 °C, Habikino 28.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoshan, Shanghai 31.4 °C (July), Habikino 33.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Baoshan, Shanghai 1.1 °C, Habikino 1.5 °C.
  • Baoshan, Shanghai gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,400 mm against 1,371 mm.
  • Baoshan, Shanghai gets about 122 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,986 h against 1,864 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoshan, Shanghai 7.1 cm, Habikino 6.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
