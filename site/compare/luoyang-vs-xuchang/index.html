How the climate of Luoyang, Henan, China compares with Xuchang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Xuchang is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 15.3 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Xuchang is 0.6 °C warmer (Luoyang, Henan 2.6 °C, Xuchang 3.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Luoyang, Henan 32.6 °C (June), Xuchang 32.6 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Luoyang, Henan -4.4 °C, Xuchang -3.8 °C.
  • Xuchang gets 35% more rain and snow a year: 762 mm against 566 mm.
  • Xuchang gets about 11 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,193 h against 2,182 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Luoyang, Henan 14 cm, Xuchang 18 cm (ERA5-Land).
