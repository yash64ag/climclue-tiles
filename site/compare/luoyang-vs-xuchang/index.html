Luoyang, Henan vs Xuchang climate

How the climate of Luoyang, Henan, China compares with Xuchang, China, 149 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Xuchang is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.3 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Xuchang is 0.5 °C warmer (Luoyang, Henan 2.6 °C, Xuchang 3.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Luoyang, Henan 32.1 °C (June), Xuchang 31.6 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Luoyang, Henan -4.1 °C, Xuchang -3.7 °C.
  • Xuchang gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 756 mm against 582 mm.
  • Xuchang gets about 11 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,193 h against 2,182 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Luoyang, Henan 42 cm, Xuchang 31 cm (ERA5-Land).
