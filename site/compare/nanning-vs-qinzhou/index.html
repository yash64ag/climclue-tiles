How the climate of Nanning, China compares with Qinzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Qinzhou is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 22.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Qinzhou is 1.2 °C warmer (Nanning 15.5 °C, Qinzhou 16.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nanning 32.9 °C (July), Qinzhou 32.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Nanning 9.8 °C, Qinzhou 11.1 °C.
  • Qinzhou gets 70% more rain and snow a year: 2,418 mm against 1,426 mm.
  • Qinzhou gets about 93 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,777 h against 1,684 h; about 1961–1990).
