Bojnūrd vs Konya climate

How the climate of Bojnūrd, Iran compares with Konya, Turkey, 2,181 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Konya is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 13.1 °C against 12.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Konya is 1.6 °C warmer (Bojnūrd -0.3 °C, Konya 1.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bojnūrd 31.4 °C (July), Konya 30.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bojnūrd -5.2 °C, Konya -3.3 °C.
  • Konya gets 40% more rain and snow a year: 389 mm against 278 mm.
  • Konya gets about 86 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,920 h against 2,833 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bojnūrd 51 cm, Konya 48 cm (ERA5-Land).
