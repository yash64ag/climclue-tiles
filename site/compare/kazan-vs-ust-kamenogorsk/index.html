Kazan vs Ust-Kamenogorsk climate

How the climate of Kazan, Russia compares with Ust-Kamenogorsk, Kazakhstan, 2,313 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kazan is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 4.4 °C against 4.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Kazan is 3.3 °C warmer (Kazan -10.8 °C, Ust-Kamenogorsk -14.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kazan 25.1 °C (July), Ust-Kamenogorsk 28.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kazan -14.2 °C, Ust-Kamenogorsk -19.0 °C.
  • Kazan gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 536 mm against 472 mm.
  • Ust-Kamenogorsk gets about 584 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,469 h against 1,885 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kazan 175 cm, Ust-Kamenogorsk 162 cm (ERA5-Land).
