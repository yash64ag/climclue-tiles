Lüliang vs Sunch’ŏn climate

How the climate of Lüliang, China compares with Sunch’ŏn, North Korea, 1,303 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lüliang is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 10.7 °C against 10.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Sunch’ŏn is 1.9 °C warmer (Lüliang 17.6 °C, Sunch’ŏn 19.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lüliang 30.6 °C (July), Sunch’ŏn 28.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Lüliang -12.0 °C, Sunch’ŏn -12.4 °C.
  • Sunch’ŏn gets 108% more rain and snow a year: 999 mm against 480 mm.
  • Lüliang gets about 181 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,599 h against 2,418 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lüliang 19 cm, Sunch’ŏn 32 cm (ERA5-Land).
