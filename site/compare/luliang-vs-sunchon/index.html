Lüliang vs Sunch’ŏn climate

How the climate of Lüliang, China compares with Sunch’ŏn, North Korea, 1,303 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Sunch’ŏn is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 10.5 °C against 10.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Sunch’ŏn is 2.3 °C warmer (Lüliang 22.3 °C, Sunch’ŏn 24.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lüliang 29.0 °C (July), Sunch’ŏn 27.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Lüliang -11.4 °C, Sunch’ŏn -14.3 °C.
  • Sunch’ŏn gets 102% more rain and snow a year: 1,008 mm against 499 mm.
  • Lüliang gets about 181 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,599 h against 2,418 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lüliang 48 cm, Sunch’ŏn 65 cm (ERA5-Land).
