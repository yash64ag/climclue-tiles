Dar es Salaam vs Mwanza climate

How the climate of Dar es Salaam, Tanzania compares with Mwanza, Tanzania, 853 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dar es Salaam is 3.3 °C (5.9 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 22.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Dar es Salaam is 5.0 °C warmer (Dar es Salaam 27.4 °C, Mwanza 22.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dar es Salaam 31.0 °C (February), Mwanza 25.8 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Dar es Salaam 21.7 °C, Mwanza 18.8 °C.
  • Dar es Salaam gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,142 mm against 1,049 mm.
  • Mwanza gets about 58 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,789 h against 2,731 h; about 1961–1990).
