Dar es Salaam vs Mwanza climate

How the climate of Dar es Salaam, Tanzania compares with Mwanza, Tanzania, 853 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dar es Salaam is 3.0 °C (5.5 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 23.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Dar es Salaam is 4.8 °C warmer (Dar es Salaam 27.5 °C, Mwanza 22.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dar es Salaam 32.5 °C (February), Mwanza 28.9 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Dar es Salaam 18.9 °C, Mwanza 16.5 °C.
  • Dar es Salaam gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 1,166 mm against 988 mm.
  • Mwanza gets about 58 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,789 h against 2,731 h; about 1961–1990).
