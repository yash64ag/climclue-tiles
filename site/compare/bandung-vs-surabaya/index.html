Bandung vs Surabaya climate

How the climate of Bandung, Indonesia compares with Surabaya, Indonesia, 569 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Surabaya is 4.4 °C (7.9 °F) warmer over the year: 27.8 °C against 23.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Surabaya is 5.2 °C warmer (Bandung 23.7 °C, Surabaya 28.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bandung 29.3 °C (September), Surabaya 33.2 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Bandung 17.3 °C, Surabaya 23.3 °C.
  • Bandung gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 2,449 mm against 1,841 mm.
  • Surabaya gets about 789 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 2,454 h; about 1961–1990).
