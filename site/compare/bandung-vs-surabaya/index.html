Bandung vs Surabaya climate

How the climate of Bandung, Indonesia compares with Surabaya, Indonesia, 569 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Surabaya is 4.1 °C (7.4 °F) warmer over the year: 27.8 °C against 23.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Surabaya is 4.9 °C warmer (Bandung 24.1 °C, Surabaya 28.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bandung 28.2 °C (September), Surabaya 32.5 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Bandung 18.9 °C, Surabaya 23.8 °C.
  • Bandung gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 2,391 mm against 1,854 mm.
  • Surabaya gets about 789 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 2,454 h; about 1961–1990).
