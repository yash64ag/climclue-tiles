Manaus vs Semarang climate

How the climate of Manaus, Brazil compares with Semarang, Indonesia, 18,471 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Semarang is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 27.2 °C against 26.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Semarang is 1.0 °C warmer (Manaus 26.4 °C, Semarang 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manaus 32.3 °C (September), Semarang 34.1 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Manaus 22.2 °C, Semarang 21.6 °C.
  • Semarang gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 2,475 mm against 2,429 mm.
  • Semarang gets about 1,068 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,835 h against 1,767 h; about 1961–1990).
