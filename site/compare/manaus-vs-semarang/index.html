Manaus vs Semarang climate

How the climate of Manaus, Brazil compares with Semarang, Indonesia, 18,471 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Semarang is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 26.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Semarang is 1.0 °C warmer (Manaus 26.6 °C, Semarang 27.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manaus 30.9 °C (October), Semarang 33.1 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Manaus 23.8 °C, Semarang 22.9 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (2,406 mm and 2,415 mm).
  • Semarang gets about 1,068 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,835 h against 1,767 h; about 1961–1990).
