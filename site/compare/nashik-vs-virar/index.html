Nashik vs Virār climate

How the climate of Nashik, India compares with Virār, India, 119 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Virār is 2.8 °C (5.0 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 23.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Virār is 4.0 °C warmer (Nashik 22.4 °C, Virār 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nashik 34.9 °C (April), Virār 33.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Nashik 13.9 °C, Virār 18.1 °C.
  • Virār gets 74% more rain and snow a year: 2,454 mm against 1,409 mm.
  • Nashik gets about 54 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,758 h against 2,703 h; about 1961–1990).
