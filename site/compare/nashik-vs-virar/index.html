Nashik vs Virār climate

How the climate of Nashik, India compares with Virār, India, 119 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Virār is 2.6 °C (4.7 °F) warmer over the year: 26.7 °C against 24.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Virār is 4.1 °C warmer (Nashik 20.3 °C, Virār 24.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nashik 36.1 °C (April), Virār 33.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Nashik 11.0 °C, Virār 16.0 °C.
  • Virār gets 106% more rain and snow a year: 2,434 mm against 1,180 mm.
  • Nashik gets about 54 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,758 h against 2,703 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Virār in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Nashik in 11 of 12 months
