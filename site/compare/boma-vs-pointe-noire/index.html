How the climate of Boma, Democratic Republic of the Congo compares with Pointe-Noire, Republic of the Congo: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Boma is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 25.0 °C against 24.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Boma is 0.5 °C warmer (Boma 26.1 °C, Pointe-Noire 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Boma 31.0 °C (March), Pointe-Noire 30.3 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Boma 18.4 °C, Pointe-Noire 18.3 °C.
  • Pointe-Noire gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 1,343 mm against 1,101 mm.
  • Pointe-Noire gets about 281 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,722 h against 1,441 h; about 1961–1990).
