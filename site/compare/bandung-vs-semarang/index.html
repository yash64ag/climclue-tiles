How the climate of Bandung, Indonesia compares with Semarang, Indonesia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Semarang is 3.8 °C (6.8 °F) warmer over the year: 27.2 °C against 23.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Semarang is 4.6 °C warmer (Bandung 23.5 °C, Semarang 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bandung 29.3 °C (September), Semarang 34.1 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Bandung 17.3 °C, Semarang 21.6 °C.
  • Semarang gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 2,475 mm against 2,449 mm.
  • Semarang gets about 382 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,835 h against 2,454 h; about 1961–1990).
