Jeddah vs Makkah climate

How the climate of Jeddah, Saudi Arabia compares with Makkah, Saudi Arabia, 67 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Makkah is 1.0 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 29.1 °C against 28.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Makkah is 3.8 °C warmer (Jeddah 30.3 °C, Makkah 34.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jeddah 33.9 °C (August), Makkah 40.6 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Jeddah 20.9 °C, Makkah 15.4 °C.
  • Makkah gets 91% more rain and snow a year: 131 mm against 69 mm.
  • Jeddah gets about 50 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,317 h against 3,268 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Makkah in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: Jeddah in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Jeddah in 9 of 12 months
