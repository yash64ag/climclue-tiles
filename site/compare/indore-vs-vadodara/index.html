Indore vs Vadodara climate

How the climate of Indore, India compares with Vadodara, India, 274 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Vadodara is 2.0 °C (3.5 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 25.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Vadodara is 2.9 °C warmer (Indore 25.6 °C, Vadodara 28.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Indore 40.6 °C (May), Vadodara 39.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Indore 10.4 °C, Vadodara 12.7 °C.
  • Vadodara gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,053 mm against 926 mm.
  • Vadodara gets about 73 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,916 h against 2,843 h; about 1961–1990).
