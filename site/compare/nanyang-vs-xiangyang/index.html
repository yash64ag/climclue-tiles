Nanyang vs Xiangyang climate

How the climate of Nanyang, China compares with Xiangyang, China, 114 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Xiangyang is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Xiangyang is 1.3 °C warmer (Nanyang 4.0 °C, Xiangyang 5.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nanyang 30.9 °C (July), Xiangyang 31.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nanyang -2.8 °C, Xiangyang -1.6 °C.
  • Xiangyang gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 942 mm against 843 mm.
  • Nanyang gets about 143 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,008 h against 1,864 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nanyang 16 cm, Xiangyang 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
