How the climate of Amarillo, United States compares with Anyang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Anyang is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 14.6 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Amarillo is 3.3 °C warmer (Amarillo 2.8 °C, Anyang -0.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amarillo 33.1 °C (July), Anyang 32.2 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Amarillo -4.8 °C, Anyang -5.3 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (550 mm and 552 mm).
  • Amarillo gets about 837 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amarillo 33 cm, Anyang 11 cm (ERA5-Land).
