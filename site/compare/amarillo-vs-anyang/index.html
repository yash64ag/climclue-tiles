Amarillo vs Anyang climate

How the climate of Amarillo, United States compares with Anyang, China, 11,244 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Anyang is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 14.8 °C against 14.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Amarillo is 3.5 °C warmer (Amarillo 2.9 °C, Anyang -0.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amarillo 32.5 °C (July), Anyang 32.0 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Amarillo -1.4 °C, Anyang -5.4 °C.
  • Anyang gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 571 mm against 542 mm.
  • Amarillo gets about 837 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amarillo 23 cm, Anyang 11 cm (ERA5-Land).
