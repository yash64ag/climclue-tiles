How the climate of Petržalka, Slovakia compares with Vienna, Austria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Vienna is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 10.9 °C against 10.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Vienna is 0.9 °C warmer (Petržalka -0.5 °C, Vienna 0.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Petržalka 26.9 °C (July), Vienna 26.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Petržalka -3.0 °C, Vienna -2.1 °C.
  • Vienna gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 626 mm against 598 mm.
  • Petržalka gets about 35 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,774 h against 1,739 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Petržalka 64 cm, Vienna 63 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Drier: Petržalka in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Petržalka in 9 of 12 months
