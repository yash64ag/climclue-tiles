How the climate of Jammu, India compares with Rawalpindi, Pakistan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jammu is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 22.2 °C against 21.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Jammu is 1.7 °C warmer (Jammu 24.5 °C, Rawalpindi 22.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jammu 38.7 °C (June), Rawalpindi 38.5 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Jammu 5.0 °C, Rawalpindi 2.8 °C.
  • Jammu gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,504 mm against 1,338 mm.
  • Jammu gets about 23 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,912 h against 2,889 h; about 1961–1990).
