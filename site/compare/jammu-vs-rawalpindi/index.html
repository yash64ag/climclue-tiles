Jammu vs Rawalpindi climate

How the climate of Jammu, India compares with Rawalpindi, Pakistan, 194 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jammu is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 22.2 °C against 21.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Jammu is 1.7 °C warmer (Jammu 24.6 °C, Rawalpindi 22.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jammu 37.4 °C (June), Rawalpindi 36.7 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Jammu 4.3 °C, Rawalpindi 3.9 °C.
  • Jammu gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,503 mm against 1,329 mm.
  • Jammu gets about 23 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,912 h against 2,889 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Jammu in 9 of 12 months (2 equal)
  • Drier: Rawalpindi in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
