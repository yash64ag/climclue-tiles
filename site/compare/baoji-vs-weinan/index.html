Baoji vs Weinan climate

How the climate of Baoji, China compares with Weinan, China, 209 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Weinan is 1.7 °C (3.0 °F) warmer over the year: 16.2 °C against 14.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Weinan is 3.2 °C warmer (Baoji 24.3 °C, Weinan 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoji 30.4 °C (July), Weinan 33.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baoji -3.9 °C, Weinan -2.9 °C.
  • Baoji gets 41% more rain and snow a year: 670 mm against 475 mm.
  • Weinan gets about 78 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,017 h against 1,939 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoji 6.0 cm, Weinan 5.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
