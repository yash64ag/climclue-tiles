How the climate of Baoji, China compares with Weinan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Weinan is 1.7 °C (3.0 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 14.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Weinan is 3.1 °C warmer (Baoji 24.2 °C, Weinan 27.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoji 31.3 °C (July), Weinan 34.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baoji -3.0 °C, Weinan -3.3 °C.
  • Baoji gets 32% more rain and snow a year: 653 mm against 495 mm.
  • Weinan gets about 78 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,017 h against 1,939 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoji 6.0 cm, Weinan 5.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
