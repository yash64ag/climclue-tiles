Al Ain City vs Madinah climate

How the climate of Al Ain City, United Arab Emirates compares with Madinah, Saudi Arabia, 1,635 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Al Ain City is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 28.0 °C against 27.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Al Ain City is 1.2 °C warmer (Al Ain City 36.0 °C, Madinah 34.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Al Ain City 42.9 °C (July), Madinah 42.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Al Ain City 11.8 °C, Madinah 11.7 °C.
  • Al Ain City gets 56% more rain and snow a year: 98 mm against 63 mm.
  • Madinah gets about 144 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,599 h against 3,455 h; about 1961–1990).
