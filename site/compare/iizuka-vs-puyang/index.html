Iizuka vs Puyang, Zhejiang climate

How the climate of Iizuka, Japan compares with Puyang, Zhejiang, China, 1,123 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Puyang, Zhejiang is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 17.7 °C against 17.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Puyang, Zhejiang is 2.2 °C warmer (Iizuka 27.4 °C, Puyang, Zhejiang 29.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iizuka 32.6 °C (August), Puyang, Zhejiang 34.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Iizuka 2.1 °C, Puyang, Zhejiang 1.2 °C.
  • Iizuka gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,669 mm against 1,592 mm.
  • Puyang, Zhejiang gets about 109 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,868 h against 1,759 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Iizuka 14 cm, Puyang, Zhejiang 26 cm (ERA5-Land).
