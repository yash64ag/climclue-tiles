How the climate of Foshan, China compares with Macau, Macao: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Foshan and Macau have the same yearly average temperature (23.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Macau is 1.2 °C warmer (Foshan 14.7 °C, Macau 15.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Foshan 33.3 °C (July), Macau 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Foshan 10.5 °C, Macau 12.6 °C.
  • Macau gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 2,195 mm against 1,981 mm.
  • Macau gets about 138 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,945 h against 1,807 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Foshan in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Macau in 11 of 12 months
