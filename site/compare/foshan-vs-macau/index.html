Foshan vs Macau climate

How the climate of Foshan, China compares with Macau, Macao, 101 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Foshan and Macau have the same yearly average temperature (23.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Macau is 1.1 °C warmer (Foshan 14.7 °C, Macau 15.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Foshan 31.9 °C (July), Macau 29.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Foshan 10.4 °C, Macau 12.9 °C.
  • Macau gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 2,096 mm against 1,983 mm.
  • Macau gets about 138 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,945 h against 1,807 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Foshan in 6 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Macau in 11 of 12 months
