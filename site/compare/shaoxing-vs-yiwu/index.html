How the climate of Shaoxing, China compares with Yiwu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yiwu is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 17.9 °C against 17.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Yiwu is 0.6 °C warmer (Shaoxing 6.9 °C, Yiwu 7.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Shaoxing 34.0 °C (July), Yiwu 34.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Shaoxing 1.2 °C, Yiwu 1.4 °C.
  • Shaoxing gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,452 mm against 1,413 mm.
  • Shaoxing gets about 10 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,876 h against 1,866 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Shaoxing 16 cm, Yiwu 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
