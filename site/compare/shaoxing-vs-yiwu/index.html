Shaoxing vs Yiwu climate

How the climate of Shaoxing, China compares with Yiwu, China, 90 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yiwu is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 17.8 °C against 17.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Yiwu is 0.5 °C warmer (Shaoxing 5.0 °C, Yiwu 5.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Shaoxing 33.5 °C (July), Yiwu 33.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Shaoxing 0.6 °C, Yiwu 0.7 °C.
  • Yiwu gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,517 mm against 1,422 mm.
  • Shaoxing gets about 10 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,876 h against 1,866 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Shaoxing 16 cm, Yiwu 14 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Drier: Shaoxing in 8 of 12 months
