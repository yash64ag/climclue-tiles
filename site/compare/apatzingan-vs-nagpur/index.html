How the climate of Apatzingán, Mexico compares with Nagpur, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Apatzingán and Nagpur have the same yearly average temperature (26.9 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Nagpur is 5.2 °C warmer (Apatzingán 30.3 °C, Nagpur 35.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Apatzingán 38.7 °C (May), Nagpur 42.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Apatzingán 16.2 °C, Nagpur 12.5 °C.
  • Nagpur gets 57% more rain and snow a year: 1,279 mm against 815 mm.
  • Nagpur gets about 418 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,764 h against 2,346 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
