Apatzingán vs Nagpur climate

How the climate of Apatzingán, Mexico compares with Nagpur, India, 15,538 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Apatzingán is 1.0 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 27.9 °C against 26.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Apatzingán is 4.8 °C warmer (Apatzingán 25.1 °C, Nagpur 20.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Apatzingán 38.8 °C (May), Nagpur 41.3 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Apatzingán 17.1 °C, Nagpur 14.0 °C.
  • Nagpur gets 66% more rain and snow a year: 1,283 mm against 773 mm.
  • Nagpur gets about 418 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,764 h against 2,346 h; about 1961–1990).
