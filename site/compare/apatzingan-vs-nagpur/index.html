Apatzingán vs Nagpur climate

How the climate of Apatzingán, Mexico compares with Nagpur, India, 15,538 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Apatzingán is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 27.7 °C against 27.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Apatzingán is 4.5 °C warmer (Apatzingán 24.9 °C, Nagpur 20.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Apatzingán 39.2 °C (April), Nagpur 42.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Apatzingán 16.4 °C, Nagpur 12.5 °C.
  • Nagpur gets 57% more rain and snow a year: 1,279 mm against 815 mm.
  • Nagpur gets about 418 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,764 h against 2,346 h; about 1961–1990).
