Kallakurichi vs Tiruchirappalli climate

How the climate of Kallakurichi, India compares with Tiruchirappalli, India, 106 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tiruchirappalli is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 29.1 °C against 28.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Tiruchirappalli is 1.4 °C warmer (Kallakurichi 28.4 °C, Tiruchirappalli 29.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kallakurichi 37.5 °C (May), Tiruchirappalli 37.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kallakurichi 19.1 °C, Tiruchirappalli 21.3 °C.
  • Kallakurichi gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 1,028 mm against 819 mm.
  • Kallakurichi gets about 35 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,528 h against 2,493 h; about 1961–1990).
