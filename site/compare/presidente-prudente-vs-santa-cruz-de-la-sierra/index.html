Presidente Prudente vs Santa Cruz de la Sierra climate

How the climate of Presidente Prudente, Brazil compares with Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia, 1,323 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Santa Cruz de la Sierra is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 23.5 °C against 23.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Santa Cruz de la Sierra is 0.9 °C warmer (Presidente Prudente 24.3 °C, Santa Cruz de la Sierra 25.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Presidente Prudente 29.6 °C (November), Santa Cruz de la Sierra 29.6 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Presidente Prudente 14.7 °C, Santa Cruz de la Sierra 16.3 °C.
  • Santa Cruz de la Sierra gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,501 mm against 1,438 mm.
  • Santa Cruz de la Sierra gets about 485 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,665 h against 2,180 h; about 1961–1990).
