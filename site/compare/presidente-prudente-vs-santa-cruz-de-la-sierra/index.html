How the climate of Presidente Prudente, Brazil compares with Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Santa Cruz de la Sierra is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 23.5 °C against 23.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Santa Cruz de la Sierra is 1.0 °C warmer (Presidente Prudente 24.2 °C, Santa Cruz de la Sierra 25.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Presidente Prudente 30.4 °C (February), Santa Cruz de la Sierra 31.1 °C (November). Coldest-month night lows: Presidente Prudente 12.9 °C, Santa Cruz de la Sierra 14.4 °C.
  • Santa Cruz de la Sierra gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,479 mm against 1,447 mm.
  • Santa Cruz de la Sierra gets about 485 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,665 h against 2,180 h; about 1961–1990).
