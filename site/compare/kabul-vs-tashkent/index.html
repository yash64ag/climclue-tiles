How the climate of Kabul, Afghanistan compares with Tashkent, Uzbekistan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tashkent is 3.0 °C (5.4 °F) warmer over the year: 14.9 °C against 11.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Tashkent is 4.9 °C warmer (Kabul -2.9 °C, Tashkent 2.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kabul 31.4 °C (July), Tashkent 35.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kabul -8.4 °C, Tashkent -2.2 °C.
  • Tashkent gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 450 mm against 346 mm.
  • Kabul gets about 241 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,031 h against 2,790 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kabul 128 cm, Tashkent 58 cm (ERA5-Land).
