Kabul vs Tashkent climate

How the climate of Kabul, Afghanistan compares with Tashkent, Uzbekistan, 749 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tashkent is 2.2 °C (3.9 °F) warmer over the year: 13.8 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Tashkent is 4.0 °C warmer (Kabul 21.3 °C, Tashkent 25.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kabul 30.0 °C (July), Tashkent 33.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kabul -10.7 °C, Tashkent -5.5 °C.
  • Tashkent gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 457 mm against 386 mm.
  • Kabul gets about 241 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,031 h against 2,790 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kabul 213 cm, Tashkent 80 cm (ERA5-Land).
