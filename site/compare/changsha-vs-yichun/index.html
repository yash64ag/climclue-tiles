How the climate of Changsha, Hunan, China compares with Yichun, Jiangxi, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Changsha, Hunan and Yichun, Jiangxi have the same yearly average temperature (18.3 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Changsha, Hunan is 0.4 °C warmer (Changsha, Hunan 29.1 °C, Yichun, Jiangxi 28.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changsha, Hunan 33.9 °C (July), Yichun, Jiangxi 34.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changsha, Hunan 2.9 °C, Yichun, Jiangxi 2.3 °C.
  • Yichun, Jiangxi gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,797 mm against 1,689 mm.
  • Yichun, Jiangxi gets about 131 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,721 h against 1,589 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changsha, Hunan 9.1 cm, Yichun, Jiangxi 7.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
