How the climate of Lianyungang, China compares with Suqian, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Suqian is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 14.8 °C against 14.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Suqian is 0.6 °C warmer (Lianyungang 14.3 °C, Suqian 14.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lianyungang 30.1 °C (July), Suqian 31.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lianyungang -3.6 °C, Suqian -3.7 °C.
  • Suqian gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 963 mm against 878 mm.
  • Lianyungang gets about 76 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,300 h against 2,225 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lianyungang 9.4 cm, Suqian 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
