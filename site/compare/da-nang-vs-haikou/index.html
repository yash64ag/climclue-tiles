How the climate of Da Nang, Vietnam compares with Haikou, Hainan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Da Nang is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 24.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Da Nang is 3.4 °C warmer (Da Nang 21.8 °C, Haikou, Hainan 18.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Da Nang 33.4 °C (June), Haikou, Hainan 33.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Da Nang 18.8 °C, Haikou, Hainan 15.8 °C.
  • Da Nang gets 32% more rain and snow a year: 2,367 mm against 1,800 mm.
  • Da Nang gets about 140 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,232 h against 2,092 h; about 1961–1990).
