Girón vs Kisangani climate

How the climate of Girón, Colombia compares with Kisangani, Democratic Republic of the Congo, 10,923 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kisangani is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 24.7 °C against 23.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Kisangani is 2.4 °C warmer (Girón 23.5 °C, Kisangani 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Girón 27.3 °C (March), Kisangani 31.0 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Girón 19.3 °C, Kisangani 21.6 °C.
  • Kisangani gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,649 mm against 1,620 mm.
  • Kisangani gets about 213 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,971 h against 1,758 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
