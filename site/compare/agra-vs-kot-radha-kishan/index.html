Agra vs Kot Radha Kishan climate

How the climate of Agra, India compares with Kot Radha Kishan, Pakistan, 584 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Agra is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 25.2 °C against 24.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Agra is 2.3 °C warmer (Agra 23.4 °C, Kot Radha Kishan 21.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Agra 40.9 °C (May), Kot Radha Kishan 40.3 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Agra 6.7 °C, Kot Radha Kishan 5.2 °C.
  • Agra gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 719 mm against 539 mm.
  • Kot Radha Kishan gets about 227 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,933 h against 2,707 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Agra in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Kot Radha Kishan in 7 of 12 months
