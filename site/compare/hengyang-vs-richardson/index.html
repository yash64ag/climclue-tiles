How the climate of Hengyang, China compares with Richardson, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hengyang is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 19.1 °C against 18.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Hengyang is 1.5 °C warmer (Hengyang 30.8 °C, Richardson 29.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hengyang 35.0 °C (July), Richardson 35.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Hengyang 3.4 °C, Richardson 2.0 °C.
  • Hengyang gets 49% more rain and snow a year: 1,588 mm against 1,063 mm.
  • Richardson gets about 1,186 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,798 h against 1,612 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hengyang 6.5 cm, Richardson 0.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
