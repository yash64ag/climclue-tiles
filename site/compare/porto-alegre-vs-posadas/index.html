How the climate of Porto Alegre, Brazil compares with Posadas, Argentina: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Posadas is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 21.1 °C against 19.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Posadas is 1.9 °C warmer (Porto Alegre 19.5 °C, Posadas 21.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Porto Alegre 30.2 °C (January), Posadas 32.0 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Porto Alegre 10.4 °C, Posadas 10.5 °C.
  • Posadas gets 37% more rain and snow a year: 1,939 mm against 1,419 mm.
  • Posadas gets about 76 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,293 h against 2,217 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Posadas in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Porto Alegre in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Posadas in 8 of 12 months
