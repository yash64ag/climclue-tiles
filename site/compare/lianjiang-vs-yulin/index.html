Lianjiang vs Yulin climate

How the climate of Lianjiang, China compares with Yulin, China, 110 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lianjiang is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 23.5 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Lianjiang is 2.1 °C warmer (Lianjiang 16.1 °C, Yulin 13.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lianjiang 31.7 °C (July), Yulin 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lianjiang 12.3 °C, Yulin 10.0 °C.
  • Lianjiang gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 2,073 mm against 1,754 mm.
  • Lianjiang gets about 96 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,881 h against 1,785 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Sunnier: Lianjiang in 11 of 12 months
