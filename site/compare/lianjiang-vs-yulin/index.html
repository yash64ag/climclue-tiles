How the climate of Lianjiang, China compares with Yulin, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lianjiang is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 23.4 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Lianjiang is 2.0 °C warmer (Lianjiang 16.0 °C, Yulin 14.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lianjiang 32.0 °C (July), Yulin 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lianjiang 12.5 °C, Yulin 10.2 °C.
  • Lianjiang gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 2,092 mm against 1,753 mm.
  • Lianjiang gets about 96 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,881 h against 1,785 h; about 1961–1990).
