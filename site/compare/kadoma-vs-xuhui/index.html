Kadoma vs Xuhui climate

How the climate of Kadoma, Japan compares with Xuhui, China, 1,374 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Kadoma is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.7 °C against 16.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Kadoma is 0.9 °C warmer (Kadoma 28.9 °C, Xuhui 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kadoma 31.9 °C (August), Xuhui 31.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kadoma 0.7 °C, Xuhui 0.7 °C.
  • Kadoma gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,466 mm against 1,451 mm.
  • Xuhui gets about 131 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,960 h against 1,828 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kadoma 12 cm, Xuhui 7.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
