Kadoma vs Xuhui climate

How the climate of Kadoma, Japan compares with Xuhui, China, 1,374 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kadoma is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Kadoma is 1.1 °C warmer (Kadoma 5.7 °C, Xuhui 4.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kadoma 33.2 °C (August), Xuhui 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kadoma 1.9 °C, Xuhui 1.1 °C.
  • Xuhui gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,499 mm against 1,461 mm.
  • Xuhui gets about 131 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,960 h against 1,828 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kadoma 5.1 cm, Xuhui 7.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
