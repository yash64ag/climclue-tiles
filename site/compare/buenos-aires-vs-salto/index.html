How the climate of Buenos Aires, Argentina compares with Salto, Uruguay: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Salto is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 18.9 °C against 17.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Salto is 1.7 °C warmer (Buenos Aires 20.1 °C, Salto 21.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Buenos Aires 29.3 °C (January), Salto 32.5 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Buenos Aires 7.0 °C, Salto 7.0 °C.
  • Salto gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,291 mm against 1,189 mm.
  • Salto gets about 141 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,574 h against 2,433 h; about 1961–1990).
