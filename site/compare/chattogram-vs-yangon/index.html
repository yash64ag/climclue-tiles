Chattogram vs Yangon climate

How the climate of Chattogram, Bangladesh compares with Yangon, Myanmar, 764 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yangon is 1.8 °C (3.2 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Yangon is 4.3 °C warmer (Chattogram 19.8 °C, Yangon 24.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chattogram 31.9 °C (May), Yangon 37.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Chattogram 13.9 °C, Yangon 17.1 °C.
  • Chattogram gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 3,182 mm against 2,796 mm.
  • Yangon gets about 384 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,574 h against 2,191 h; about 1961–1990).
