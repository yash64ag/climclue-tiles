Chattogram vs Yangon climate

How the climate of Chattogram, Bangladesh compares with Yangon, Myanmar, 764 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yangon is 1.9 °C (3.4 °F) warmer over the year: 27.4 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Yangon is 4.5 °C warmer (Chattogram 19.8 °C, Yangon 24.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chattogram 30.4 °C (May), Yangon 37.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Chattogram 16.0 °C, Yangon 17.7 °C.
  • Chattogram gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 3,146 mm against 2,761 mm.
  • Yangon gets about 384 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,574 h against 2,191 h; about 1961–1990).
