How the climate of Girardot City, Colombia compares with Kisangani, Democratic Republic of the Congo: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Girardot City is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 24.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Girardot City is 1.7 °C warmer (Girardot City 25.9 °C, Kisangani 24.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Girardot City 32.5 °C (February), Kisangani 31.9 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Girardot City 18.9 °C, Kisangani 18.8 °C.
  • Girardot City gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,795 mm against 1,649 mm.
  • Girardot City gets about 78 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,049 h against 1,971 h; about 1961–1990).
