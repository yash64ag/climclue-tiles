Mérida vs Puebla, Puebla climate

How the climate of Mérida, Mexico compares with Puebla, Puebla, Mexico, 922 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mérida is 9.1 °C (16.4 °F) warmer over the year: 26.3 °C against 17.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Mérida is 10.0 °C warmer (Mérida 28.1 °C, Puebla, Puebla 18.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mérida 35.7 °C (May), Puebla, Puebla 27.0 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Mérida 17.2 °C, Puebla, Puebla 6.6 °C.
  • Mérida gets 40% more rain and snow a year: 1,016 mm against 724 mm.
  • Mérida gets about 209 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,635 h against 2,426 h; about 1961–1990).
