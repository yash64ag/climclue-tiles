How the climate of Houston, United States compares with San Antonio, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • San Antonio is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 20.6 °C against 20.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when San Antonio is 1.0 °C warmer (Houston 28.2 °C, San Antonio 29.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Houston 32.7 °C (August), San Antonio 35.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Houston 7.1 °C, San Antonio 4.6 °C.
  • Houston gets 71% more rain and snow a year: 1,372 mm against 803 mm.
  • Houston gets about 4 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,576 h against 2,571 h; about 1961–1990).
