Nanchang vs Yichun, Jiangxi climate

How the climate of Nanchang, China compares with Yichun, Jiangxi, China, 171 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Nanchang is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 18.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Nanchang is 0.7 °C warmer (Nanchang 29.4 °C, Yichun, Jiangxi 28.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nanchang 33.0 °C (July), Yichun, Jiangxi 33.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nanchang 2.2 °C, Yichun, Jiangxi 2.3 °C.
  • Nanchang gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,801 mm against 1,777 mm.
  • Nanchang gets about 118 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,839 h against 1,721 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nanchang 12 cm, Yichun, Jiangxi 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
