How the climate of Nanchang, China compares with Yichun, Jiangxi, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Nanchang is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 18.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Nanchang is 0.6 °C warmer (Nanchang 29.3 °C, Yichun, Jiangxi 28.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nanchang 34.1 °C (July), Yichun, Jiangxi 34.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nanchang 2.4 °C, Yichun, Jiangxi 2.3 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,796 mm and 1,797 mm).
  • Nanchang gets about 118 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,839 h against 1,721 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nanchang 6.5 cm, Yichun, Jiangxi 7.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
