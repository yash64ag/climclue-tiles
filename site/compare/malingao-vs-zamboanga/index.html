How the climate of Malingao, Philippines compares with Zamboanga, Philippines: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Malingao is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 28.3 °C against 27.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Malingao is 1.3 °C warmer (Malingao 29.8 °C, Zamboanga 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Malingao 35.0 °C (April), Zamboanga 32.8 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Malingao 23.1 °C, Zamboanga 23.1 °C.
  • Zamboanga gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 1,695 mm against 1,453 mm.
  • Malingao gets about 92 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,303 h against 2,211 h; about 1961–1990).
