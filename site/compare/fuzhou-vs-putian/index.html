Fuzhou, Fujian vs Putian climate

How the climate of Fuzhou, Fujian, China compares with Putian, China, 75 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Putian is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 20.5 °C against 19.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Putian is 1.2 °C warmer (Fuzhou, Fujian 12.4 °C, Putian 13.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuzhou, Fujian 31.8 °C (July), Putian 31.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuzhou, Fujian 6.2 °C, Putian 7.6 °C.
  • Fuzhou, Fujian gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,350 mm against 1,324 mm.
  • Putian gets about 96 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,859 h against 1,763 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Putian in 11 of 12 months
  • Drier: Putian in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Putian in 12 of 12 months
