How the climate of Fuzhou, Fujian, China compares with Putian, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Fuzhou, Fujian is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 20.8 °C against 20.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Fuzhou, Fujian is 1.1 °C warmer (Fuzhou, Fujian 30.0 °C, Putian 28.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuzhou, Fujian 34.3 °C (July), Putian 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuzhou, Fujian 8.1 °C, Putian 8.3 °C.
  • Putian gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,434 mm against 1,289 mm.
  • Putian gets about 96 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,859 h against 1,763 h; about 1961–1990).
