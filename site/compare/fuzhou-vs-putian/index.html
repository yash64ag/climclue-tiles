Fuzhou, Fujian vs Putian climate

How the climate of Fuzhou, Fujian, China compares with Putian, China, 75 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Putian is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 20.5 °C against 20.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Putian is 1.1 °C warmer (Fuzhou, Fujian 11.1 °C, Putian 12.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuzhou, Fujian 33.9 °C (July), Putian 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuzhou, Fujian 6.8 °C, Putian 8.3 °C.
  • Putian gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,434 mm against 1,289 mm.
  • Putian gets about 96 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,859 h against 1,763 h; about 1961–1990).
