How the climate of Antalya, Turkey compares with Konya, Turkey: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Antalya is 6.4 °C (11.5 °F) warmer over the year: 19.4 °C against 13.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Antalya is 8.8 °C warmer (Antalya 10.1 °C, Konya 1.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Antalya 36.3 °C (July), Konya 32.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Antalya 5.7 °C, Konya -2.8 °C.
  • Antalya gets 165% more rain and snow a year: 1,032 mm against 389 mm.
  • Antalya gets about 183 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,103 h against 2,920 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Antalya 0.8 cm, Konya 48 cm (ERA5-Land).
