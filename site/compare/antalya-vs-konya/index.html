Antalya vs Konya climate

How the climate of Antalya, Turkey compares with Konya, Turkey, 191 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Antalya is 5.7 °C (10.3 °F) warmer over the year: 18.6 °C against 12.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Antalya is 7.8 °C warmer (Antalya 8.9 °C, Konya 1.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Antalya 35.0 °C (July), Konya 30.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Antalya 4.8 °C, Konya -3.5 °C.
  • Antalya gets 147% more rain and snow a year: 1,012 mm against 410 mm.
  • Antalya gets about 183 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,103 h against 2,920 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Antalya 0.8 cm, Konya 48 cm (ERA5-Land).
