How the climate of Stockholm, Sweden compares with Tallinn, Estonia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Stockholm is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 7.2 °C against 6.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Stockholm is 2.5 °C warmer (Stockholm -2.1 °C, Tallinn -4.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Stockholm 22.7 °C (July), Tallinn 21.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Stockholm -4.7 °C, Tallinn -7.5 °C.
  • Tallinn gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 685 mm against 587 mm.
  • Tallinn gets about 206 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,693 h against 1,487 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Stockholm 116 cm, Tallinn 152 cm (ERA5-Land).
