Lucknow vs Nepalgunj climate

How the climate of Lucknow, India compares with Nepalgunj, Nepal, 151 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lucknow is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 25.1 °C against 24.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Lucknow is 1.9 °C warmer (Lucknow 32.6 °C, Nepalgunj 30.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lucknow 39.0 °C (May), Nepalgunj 37.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Lucknow 8.8 °C, Nepalgunj 8.2 °C.
  • Nepalgunj gets 80% more rain and snow a year: 1,612 mm against 897 mm.
  • Lucknow gets about 115 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,611 h against 2,496 h; about 1961–1990).
