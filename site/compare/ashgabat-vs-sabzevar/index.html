How the climate of Ashgabat, Turkmenistan compares with Sabzevar, Iran: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Sabzevar is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 17.4 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Sabzevar is 1.6 °C warmer (Ashgabat 16.6 °C, Sabzevar 18.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ashgabat 38.6 °C (July), Sabzevar 37.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ashgabat -0.2 °C, Sabzevar -1.5 °C.
  • Ashgabat gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 274 mm against 211 mm.
  • Sabzevar gets about 157 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,865 h against 2,708 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ashgabat 13 cm, Sabzevar 18 cm (ERA5-Land).
