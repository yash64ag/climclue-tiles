Chiang Mai vs Mandalay climate

How the climate of Chiang Mai, Thailand compares with Mandalay, Myanmar, 466 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mandalay is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 26.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Mandalay is 1.4 °C warmer (Chiang Mai 29.9 °C, Mandalay 31.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chiang Mai 38.3 °C (April), Mandalay 39.1 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Chiang Mai 14.2 °C, Mandalay 13.5 °C.
  • Mandalay gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,019 mm against 989 mm.
  • Chiang Mai gets about 279 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,656 h against 2,377 h; about 1961–1990).
