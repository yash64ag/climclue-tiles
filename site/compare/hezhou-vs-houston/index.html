Hezhou vs Houston climate

How the climate of Hezhou, China compares with Houston, United States, 13,341 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Hezhou is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 20.7 °C against 20.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Houston is 1.5 °C warmer (Hezhou 10.1 °C, Houston 11.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hezhou 33.4 °C (July), Houston 32.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Hezhou 6.0 °C, Houston 7.1 °C.
  • Hezhou gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,592 mm against 1,372 mm.
  • Houston gets about 873 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,576 h against 1,703 h; about 1961–1990).
