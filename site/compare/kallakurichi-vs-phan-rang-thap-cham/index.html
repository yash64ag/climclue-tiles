Kallakurichi vs Phan Rang-Tháp Chàm climate

How the climate of Kallakurichi, India compares with Phan Rang-Tháp Chàm, Vietnam, 3,269 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Kallakurichi is 1.4 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 28.1 °C against 26.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Kallakurichi is 3.4 °C warmer (Kallakurichi 31.9 °C, Phan Rang-Tháp Chàm 28.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kallakurichi 37.7 °C (May), Phan Rang-Tháp Chàm 31.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kallakurichi 18.9 °C, Phan Rang-Tháp Chàm 20.6 °C.
  • Kallakurichi gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,016 mm against 978 mm.
  • Kallakurichi gets about 563 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,528 h against 1,965 h; about 1961–1990).
