Kallakurichi vs Phan Rang-Tháp Chàm climate

How the climate of Kallakurichi, India compares with Phan Rang-Tháp Chàm, Vietnam, 3,269 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kallakurichi is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 28.1 °C against 26.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Kallakurichi is 3.5 °C warmer (Kallakurichi 32.0 °C, Phan Rang-Tháp Chàm 28.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kallakurichi 37.5 °C (May), Phan Rang-Tháp Chàm 32.2 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Kallakurichi 19.1 °C, Phan Rang-Tháp Chàm 21.0 °C.
  • Kallakurichi gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,028 mm against 976 mm.
  • Kallakurichi gets about 563 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,528 h against 1,965 h; about 1961–1990).
