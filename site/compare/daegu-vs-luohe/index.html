How the climate of Daegu, South Korea compares with Luohe, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Luohe is 1.4 °C (2.6 °F) warmer over the year: 15.4 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Luohe is 3.0 °C warmer (Daegu 23.1 °C, Luohe 26.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daegu 31.1 °C (August), Luohe 32.1 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Daegu -5.3 °C, Luohe -3.3 °C.
  • Daegu gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 979 mm against 899 mm.
  • Daegu gets about 331 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,483 h against 2,151 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daegu 13 cm, Luohe 20 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Luohe in 11 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Daegu in 9 of 12 months
