How the climate of Ankara, Turkey compares with Istanbul, Turkey: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Istanbul is 1.9 °C (3.3 °F) warmer over the year: 14.8 °C against 12.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Istanbul is 5.1 °C warmer (Ankara 1.3 °C, Istanbul 6.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ankara 32.3 °C (August), Istanbul 28.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ankara -2.4 °C, Istanbul 3.2 °C.
  • Istanbul gets 87% more rain and snow a year: 783 mm against 420 mm.
  • Ankara gets about 48 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,811 h against 2,763 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ankara 37 cm, Istanbul 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
