Aba vs Edéa climate

How the climate of Aba, Nigeria compares with Edéa, Cameroon, 339 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Edéa is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 25.9 °C against 25.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Edéa is 0.6 °C warmer (Aba 24.9 °C, Edéa 25.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aba 33.1 °C (February), Edéa 31.1 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Aba 21.8 °C, Edéa 22.4 °C.
  • Edéa gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 2,709 mm against 2,485 mm.
  • Edéa gets about 217 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,715 h against 1,498 h; about 1961–1990).
