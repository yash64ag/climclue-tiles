Barranquilla vs Medellín climate

How the climate of Barranquilla, Colombia compares with Medellín, Colombia, 532 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Barranquilla is 9.4 °C (16.9 °F) warmer over the year: 27.1 °C against 17.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Barranquilla is 9.9 °C warmer (Barranquilla 27.6 °C, Medellín 17.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Barranquilla 29.1 °C (June), Medellín 23.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Barranquilla 25.2 °C, Medellín 12.6 °C.
  • Medellín gets 137% more rain and snow a year: 2,015 mm against 849 mm.
  • Barranquilla gets about 658 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,481 h against 1,823 h; about 1961–1990).
