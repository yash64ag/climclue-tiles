Barranquilla vs Medellín climate

How the climate of Barranquilla, Colombia compares with Medellín, Colombia, 532 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Barranquilla is 7.0 °C (12.5 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 20.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Barranquilla is 7.6 °C warmer (Barranquilla 27.6 °C, Medellín 20.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Barranquilla 28.9 °C (June), Medellín 25.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Barranquilla 25.2 °C, Medellín 14.8 °C.
  • Medellín gets 106% more rain and snow a year: 1,768 mm against 857 mm.
  • Barranquilla gets about 658 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,481 h against 1,823 h; about 1961–1990).
