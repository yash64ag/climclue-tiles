Almaty vs Ürümqi climate

How the climate of Almaty, Kazakhstan compares with Ürümqi, China, 863 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Almaty is 1.3 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 9.1 °C against 7.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Almaty is 4.9 °C warmer (Almaty -6.3 °C, Ürümqi -11.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Almaty 27.2 °C (July), Ürümqi 29.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Almaty -9.7 °C, Ürümqi -13.9 °C.
  • Almaty gets 217% more rain and snow a year: 1,006 mm against 317 mm.
  • Ürümqi gets about 317 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,777 h against 2,460 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Almaty 145 cm, Ürümqi 72 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Almaty in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Ürümqi in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Ürümqi in 11 of 12 months
