Chaozhou vs Shantou climate

How the climate of Chaozhou, China compares with Shantou, China, 34 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Chaozhou is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 22.3 °C against 22.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Shantou is 0.5 °C warmer (Chaozhou 15.8 °C, Shantou 16.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chaozhou 32.1 °C (July), Shantou 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chaozhou 10.4 °C, Shantou 11.1 °C.
  • Chaozhou gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 1,803 mm against 1,567 mm.
  • Shantou gets about 21 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,008 h against 1,986 h; about 1961–1990).
