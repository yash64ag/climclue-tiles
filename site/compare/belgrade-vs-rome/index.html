How the climate of Belgrade, Serbia compares with Rome, Italy: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Rome is 3.0 °C (5.4 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 12.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Rome is 5.8 °C warmer (Belgrade 1.5 °C, Rome 7.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belgrade 28.7 °C (August), Rome 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Belgrade -1.3 °C, Rome 2.5 °C.
  • Rome gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 770 mm against 702 mm.
  • Rome gets about 432 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,424 h against 1,991 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Belgrade 45 cm, Rome 0.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
