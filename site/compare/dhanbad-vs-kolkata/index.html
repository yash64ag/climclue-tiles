Dhanbad vs Kolkata climate

How the climate of Dhanbad, India compares with Kolkata, India, 241 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kolkata is 1.0 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 26.1 °C against 25.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Kolkata is 2.2 °C warmer (Dhanbad 17.4 °C, Kolkata 19.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dhanbad 37.4 °C (May), Kolkata 35.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Dhanbad 10.5 °C, Kolkata 13.1 °C.
  • Kolkata gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 1,824 mm against 1,500 mm.
  • Dhanbad gets about 109 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,186 h against 2,078 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
