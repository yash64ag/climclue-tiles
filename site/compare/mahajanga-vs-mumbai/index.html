How the climate of Mahajanga, Madagascar compares with Mumbai, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Mahajanga is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Mahajanga is 3.4 °C warmer (Mahajanga 27.1 °C, Mumbai 23.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mahajanga 32.7 °C (April), Mumbai 32.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Mahajanga 18.7 °C, Mumbai 17.0 °C.
  • Mumbai gets 82% more rain and snow a year: 2,712 mm against 1,486 mm.
  • Mahajanga gets about 508 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,175 h against 2,667 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
