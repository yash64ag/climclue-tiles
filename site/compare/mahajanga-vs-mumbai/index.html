Mahajanga vs Mumbai climate

How the climate of Mahajanga, Madagascar compares with Mumbai, India, 4,839 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mahajanga is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Mahajanga is 3.3 °C warmer (Mahajanga 27.1 °C, Mumbai 23.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mahajanga 32.5 °C (October), Mumbai 30.4 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Mahajanga 20.6 °C, Mumbai 20.8 °C.
  • Mumbai gets 81% more rain and snow a year: 2,706 mm against 1,496 mm.
  • Mahajanga gets about 508 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,175 h against 2,667 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
