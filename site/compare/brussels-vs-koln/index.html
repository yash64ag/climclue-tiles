Brussels vs Köln climate

How the climate of Brussels, Belgium compares with Köln, Germany, 183 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Köln is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 10.5 °C against 10.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Köln is 0.8 °C warmer (Brussels 15.9 °C, Köln 16.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brussels 22.3 °C (July), Köln 23.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Brussels 0.6 °C, Köln 0.1 °C.
  • Brussels gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 863 mm against 819 mm.
  • Brussels gets about 132 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,587 h against 1,455 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Brussels 15 cm, Köln 23 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Köln in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Brussels in 12 of 12 months
