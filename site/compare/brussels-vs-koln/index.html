How the climate of Brussels, Belgium compares with Köln, Germany: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Brussels and Köln have the same yearly average temperature (10.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Brussels is 0.7 °C warmer (Brussels 3.4 °C, Köln 2.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brussels 23.2 °C (July), Köln 23.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Brussels 0.7 °C, Köln 0.1 °C.
  • Brussels gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 851 mm against 806 mm.
  • Brussels gets about 132 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,587 h against 1,455 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Brussels 15 cm, Köln 23 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Köln in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Brussels in 12 of 12 months
