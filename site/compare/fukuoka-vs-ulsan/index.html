Fukuoka vs Ulsan climate

How the climate of Fukuoka, Japan compares with Ulsan, South Korea, 238 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fukuoka is 2.5 °C (4.6 °F) warmer over the year: 17.2 °C against 14.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Fukuoka is 4.0 °C warmer (Fukuoka 6.7 °C, Ulsan 2.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fukuoka 30.7 °C (August), Ulsan 28.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Fukuoka 2.7 °C, Ulsan -1.8 °C.
  • Fukuoka gets 53% more rain and snow a year: 1,824 mm against 1,192 mm.
  • Ulsan gets about 501 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,290 h against 1,789 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fukuoka 7.5 cm, Ulsan 6.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
