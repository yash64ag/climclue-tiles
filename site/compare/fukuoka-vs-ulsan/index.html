Fukuoka vs Ulsan climate

How the climate of Fukuoka, Japan compares with Ulsan, South Korea, 238 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fukuoka is 2.5 °C (4.5 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Fukuoka is 4.2 °C warmer (Fukuoka 9.1 °C, Ulsan 4.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fukuoka 32.2 °C (August), Ulsan 30.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Fukuoka 3.3 °C, Ulsan -2.2 °C.
  • Fukuoka gets 46% more rain and snow a year: 1,726 mm against 1,186 mm.
  • Ulsan gets about 501 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,290 h against 1,789 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fukuoka 7.5 cm, Ulsan 6.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
