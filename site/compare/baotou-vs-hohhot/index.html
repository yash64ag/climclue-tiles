Baotou vs Hohhot climate

How the climate of Baotou, China compares with Hohhot, China, 153 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Baotou is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 7.6 °C against 7.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Baotou is 1.0 °C warmer (Baotou -6.2 °C, Hohhot -7.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baotou 30.1 °C (July), Hohhot 29.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baotou -17.4 °C, Hohhot -18.1 °C.
  • Hohhot gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 374 mm against 321 mm.
  • Baotou gets about 32 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,964 h against 2,933 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baotou 15 cm, Hohhot 19 cm (ERA5-Land).
