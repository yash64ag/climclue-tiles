Baotou vs Hohhot climate

How the climate of Baotou, China compares with Hohhot, China, 153 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Baotou is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 7.6 °C against 6.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Baotou is 1.3 °C warmer (Baotou -6.2 °C, Hohhot -7.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baotou 29.6 °C (July), Hohhot 27.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baotou -17.3 °C, Hohhot -18.5 °C.
  • Hohhot gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 376 mm against 327 mm.
  • Baotou gets about 32 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,964 h against 2,933 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baotou 15 cm, Hohhot 19 cm (ERA5-Land).
