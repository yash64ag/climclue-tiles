Alexandria vs Giza climate

How the climate of Alexandria, Egypt compares with Giza, Egypt, 181 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Giza is 1.7 °C (3.0 °F) warmer over the year: 22.1 °C against 20.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Giza is 4.4 °C warmer (Alexandria 20.8 °C, Giza 25.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Alexandria 30.1 °C (August), Giza 35.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Alexandria 9.6 °C, Giza 8.3 °C.
  • Alexandria gets 987% more rain and snow a year: 196 mm against 18 mm.
  • Giza gets about 74 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,330 h against 3,256 h; about 1961–1990).
