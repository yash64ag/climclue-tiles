Panjin vs Shenyang climate

How the climate of Panjin, China compares with Shenyang, China, 136 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Panjin is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 9.8 °C against 8.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Panjin is 2.3 °C warmer (Panjin -8.9 °C, Shenyang -11.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Panjin 28.8 °C (July), Shenyang 28.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Panjin -14.6 °C, Shenyang -17.6 °C.
  • Shenyang gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 717 mm against 661 mm.
  • Panjin gets about 89 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,628 h against 2,540 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Panjin 24 cm, Shenyang 37 cm (ERA5-Land).
