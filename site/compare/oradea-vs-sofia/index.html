How the climate of Oradea, Romania compares with Sofia, Bulgaria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Oradea and Sofia have the same yearly average temperature (11.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Oradea is 0.9 °C warmer (Oradea 17.1 °C, Sofia 16.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Oradea 27.9 °C (July), Sofia 27.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Oradea -3.8 °C, Sofia -3.7 °C.
  • Oradea gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 625 mm against 612 mm.
  • Sofia gets about 61 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,045 h against 1,985 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Oradea 53 cm, Sofia 95 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Oradea in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Sofia in 8 of 12 months
