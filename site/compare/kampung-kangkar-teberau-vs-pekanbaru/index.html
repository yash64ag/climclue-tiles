Main differences

  • Kampung Kangkar Teberau is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Kampung Kangkar Teberau is 0.2 °C warmer (Kampung Kangkar Teberau 27.1 °C, Pekanbaru 26.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kampung Kangkar Teberau 31.1 °C (May), Pekanbaru 32.4 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kampung Kangkar Teberau 22.4 °C, Pekanbaru 20.8 °C.

  • Pekanbaru gets about 149 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,230 h against 2,081 h; about 1961–1990).
