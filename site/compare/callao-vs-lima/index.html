Callao vs Lima climate

How the climate of Callao, Peru compares with Lima, Peru, 12 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lima is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 19.0 °C against 18.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Lima is 0.7 °C warmer (Callao 16.4 °C, Lima 17.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Callao 26.2 °C (February), Lima 26.5 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Callao 12.8 °C, Lima 13.4 °C.
  • Lima gets 83% more rain and snow a year: 38 mm against 21 mm.
