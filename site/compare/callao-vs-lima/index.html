Callao vs Lima climate

How the climate of Callao, Peru compares with Lima, Peru, 12 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Lima is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 18.9 °C against 18.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Lima is 0.5 °C warmer (Callao 15.8 °C, Lima 16.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Callao 24.2 °C (February), Lima 24.8 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Callao 14.8 °C, Lima 14.8 °C.
  • Lima gets 52% more rain and snow a year: 35 mm against 23 mm.
