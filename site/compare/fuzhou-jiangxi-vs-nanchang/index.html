Fuzhou, Jiangxi vs Nanchang climate

How the climate of Fuzhou, Jiangxi, China compares with Nanchang, China, 93 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Fuzhou, Jiangxi is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 19.1 °C against 18.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Fuzhou, Jiangxi is 0.9 °C warmer (Fuzhou, Jiangxi 6.8 °C, Nanchang 5.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 34.0 °C (July), Nanchang 33.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 2.6 °C, Nanchang 2.2 °C.
  • Fuzhou, Jiangxi gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 2,005 mm against 1,801 mm.
  • Nanchang gets about 16 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,839 h against 1,823 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 6.6 cm, Nanchang 6.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
