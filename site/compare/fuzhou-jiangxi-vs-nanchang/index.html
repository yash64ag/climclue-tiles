How the climate of Fuzhou, Jiangxi, China compares with Nanchang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Fuzhou, Jiangxi is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 19.1 °C against 18.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Fuzhou, Jiangxi is 1.1 °C warmer (Fuzhou, Jiangxi 12.9 °C, Nanchang 11.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 34.9 °C (July), Nanchang 33.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 3.1 °C, Nanchang 2.7 °C.
  • Fuzhou, Jiangxi gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,977 mm against 1,796 mm.
  • Nanchang gets about 16 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,839 h against 1,823 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 6.6 cm, Nanchang 6.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
