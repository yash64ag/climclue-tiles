Arequipa vs Callao climate

How the climate of Arequipa, Peru compares with Callao, Peru, 773 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Callao is 3.3 °C (6.0 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Callao is 7.4 °C warmer (Arequipa 14.9 °C, Callao 22.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Arequipa 24.7 °C (October), Callao 26.2 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Arequipa 5.5 °C, Callao 12.8 °C.
  • Arequipa gets 704% more rain and snow a year: 168 mm against 21 mm.
  • Arequipa gets about 1,673 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,178 h against 1,505 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
