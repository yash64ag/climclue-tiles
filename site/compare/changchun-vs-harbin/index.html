Changchun vs Harbin climate

How the climate of Changchun, China compares with Harbin, China, 233 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Changchun is 1.6 °C (2.9 °F) warmer over the year: 6.2 °C against 4.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Changchun is 3.4 °C warmer (Changchun -9.7 °C, Harbin -13.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changchun 28.0 °C (July), Harbin 27.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changchun -20.9 °C, Harbin -24.4 °C.
  • Changchun gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 635 mm against 551 mm.
  • Changchun gets about 20 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,570 h against 2,550 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changchun 33 cm, Harbin 33 cm (ERA5-Land).
