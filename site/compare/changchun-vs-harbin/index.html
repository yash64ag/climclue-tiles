Changchun vs Harbin climate

How the climate of Changchun, China compares with Harbin, China, 233 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Changchun is 1.7 °C (3.0 °F) warmer over the year: 6.3 °C against 4.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Changchun is 3.4 °C warmer (Changchun -15.2 °C, Harbin -18.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changchun 27.1 °C (July), Harbin 27.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changchun -20.9 °C, Harbin -24.6 °C.
  • Changchun gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 616 mm against 549 mm.
  • Changchun gets about 20 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,570 h against 2,550 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changchun 33 cm, Harbin 33 cm (ERA5-Land).
