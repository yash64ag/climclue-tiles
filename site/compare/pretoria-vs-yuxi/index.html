How the climate of Pretoria, South Africa compares with Yuxi, Yunnan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Pretoria is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 19.0 °C against 18.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Pretoria is 11.3 °C warmer (Pretoria 23.5 °C, Yuxi, Yunnan 12.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pretoria 29.5 °C (January), Yuxi, Yunnan 27.6 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Pretoria 4.4 °C, Yuxi, Yunnan 5.3 °C.
  • Yuxi, Yunnan gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 762 mm against 695 mm.
  • Pretoria gets about 1,120 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,202 h against 2,081 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each

  • Sunnier: Pretoria in 12 of 12 months
