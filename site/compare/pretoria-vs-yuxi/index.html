Pretoria vs Yuxi climate

How the climate of Pretoria, South Africa compares with Yuxi, China, 9,739 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yuxi is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 18.0 °C against 17.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Yuxi is 11.3 °C warmer (Pretoria 11.3 °C, Yuxi 22.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pretoria 27.2 °C (January), Yuxi 25.5 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Pretoria 4.8 °C, Yuxi 6.4 °C.
  • Yuxi gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 806 mm against 707 mm.
  • Pretoria gets about 1,120 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,202 h against 2,081 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Yuxi in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Pretoria in 12 of 12 months
