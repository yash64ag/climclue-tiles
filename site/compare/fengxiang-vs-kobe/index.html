Fengxiang vs Kobe climate

How the climate of Fengxiang, China compares with Kobe, Japan, 1,350 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fengxiang is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.7 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Fengxiang is 1.2 °C warmer (Fengxiang 27.9 °C, Kobe 26.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fengxiang 30.1 °C (July), Kobe 29.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Fengxiang 1.8 °C, Kobe 1.9 °C.
  • Fengxiang gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,406 mm against 1,378 mm.
  • Fengxiang gets about 91 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,937 h against 1,846 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fengxiang 5.5 cm, Kobe 5.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
