How the climate of Fengxiang, China compares with Kobe, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kobe is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.9 °C against 16.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Kobe is 1.0 °C warmer (Fengxiang 5.2 °C, Kobe 6.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fengxiang 31.5 °C (July), Kobe 32.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Fengxiang 1.7 °C, Kobe 2.7 °C.
  • Fengxiang gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,409 mm against 1,368 mm.
  • Fengxiang gets about 91 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,937 h against 1,846 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fengxiang 5.5 cm, Kobe 5.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
