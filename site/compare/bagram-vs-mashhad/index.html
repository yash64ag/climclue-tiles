How the climate of Bagrām, Afghanistan compares with Mashhad, Iran: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Mashhad is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 14.6 °C against 14.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Mashhad is 1.5 °C warmer (Bagrām 0.1 °C, Mashhad 1.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bagrām 32.7 °C (July), Mashhad 35.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bagrām -4.6 °C, Mashhad -4.2 °C.
  • Bagrām gets 47% more rain and snow a year: 384 mm against 261 mm.
  • Bagrām gets about 98 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,977 h against 2,878 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bagrām 62 cm, Mashhad 33 cm (ERA5-Land).
