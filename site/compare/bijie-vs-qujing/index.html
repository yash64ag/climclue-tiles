Bijie vs Qujing climate

How the climate of Bijie, China compares with Qujing, China, 252 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Qujing is 1.9 °C (3.5 °F) warmer over the year: 15.4 °C against 13.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Qujing is 5.1 °C warmer (Bijie 3.4 °C, Qujing 8.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bijie 26.8 °C (July), Qujing 24.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bijie 0.1 °C, Qujing 2.3 °C.
  • Qujing gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,007 mm against 973 mm.
  • Qujing gets about 419 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,933 h against 1,514 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bijie 31 cm, Qujing 0 cm (ERA5-Land).
