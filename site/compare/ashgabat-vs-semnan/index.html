How the climate of Ashgabat, Turkmenistan compares with Semnan, Iran: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ashgabat is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 16.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Ashgabat is 1.7 °C warmer (Ashgabat 28.7 °C, Semnan 27.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ashgabat 38.6 °C (July), Semnan 36.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ashgabat -0.2 °C, Semnan -1.3 °C.
  • Ashgabat gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 274 mm against 214 mm.
  • Semnan gets about 229 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,937 h against 2,708 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ashgabat 13 cm, Semnan 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
