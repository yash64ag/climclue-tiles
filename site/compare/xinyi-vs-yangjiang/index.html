How the climate of Xinyi, Guangdong, China compares with Yangjiang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yangjiang is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 23.2 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Yangjiang is 1.5 °C warmer (Xinyi, Guangdong 14.3 °C, Yangjiang 15.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Xinyi, Guangdong 32.4 °C (July), Yangjiang 31.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Xinyi, Guangdong 9.8 °C, Yangjiang 12.0 °C.
  • Yangjiang gets 40% more rain and snow a year: 2,689 mm against 1,924 mm.
  • Yangjiang gets about 88 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,929 h against 1,840 h; about 1961–1990).
