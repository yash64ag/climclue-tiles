Hamburg vs Köln climate

How the climate of Hamburg, Germany compares with Köln, Germany, 357 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Köln is 1.4 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 10.5 °C against 9.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Köln is 2.0 °C warmer (Hamburg 4.5 °C, Köln 6.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hamburg 22.0 °C (July), Köln 23.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hamburg -0.9 °C, Köln 0.1 °C.
  • Köln gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 819 mm against 812 mm.
  • Hamburg gets about 80 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,535 h against 1,455 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hamburg 40 cm, Köln 23 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Hamburg in 6 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Sunnier: Hamburg in 8 of 12 months
