Maputo vs Teófilo Otoni climate

How the climate of Maputo, Mozambique compares with Teófilo Otoni, Brazil, 7,601 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Teófilo Otoni is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 22.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Teófilo Otoni is 1.0 °C warmer (Maputo 22.6 °C, Teófilo Otoni 23.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maputo 28.6 °C (February), Teófilo Otoni 29.8 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Maputo 15.7 °C, Teófilo Otoni 15.7 °C.
  • Teófilo Otoni gets 43% more rain and snow a year: 1,115 mm against 780 mm.
  • Maputo gets about 883 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,827 h against 1,944 h; about 1961–1990).
