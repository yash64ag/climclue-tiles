Maputo vs Teófilo Otoni climate

How the climate of Maputo, Mozambique compares with Teófilo Otoni, Brazil, 7,601 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Teófilo Otoni is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 23.8 °C against 22.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Teófilo Otoni is 1.9 °C warmer (Maputo 22.4 °C, Teófilo Otoni 24.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maputo 28.1 °C (January), Teófilo Otoni 30.5 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Maputo 15.9 °C, Teófilo Otoni 16.5 °C.
  • Teófilo Otoni gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 969 mm against 785 mm.
  • Maputo gets about 883 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,827 h against 1,944 h; about 1961–1990).
