Bao'an vs Dongguan climate

How the climate of Bao'an, China compares with Dongguan, China, 54 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dongguan is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 22.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Dongguan is 0.9 °C warmer (Bao'an 28.4 °C, Dongguan 29.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bao'an 31.7 °C (July), Dongguan 33.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bao'an 11.5 °C, Dongguan 10.4 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (2,140 mm and 2,151 mm).
  • Bao'an gets about 76 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,927 h against 1,850 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
