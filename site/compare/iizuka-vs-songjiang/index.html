How the climate of Iizuka, Japan compares with Songjiang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Songjiang is 0.6 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 16.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Songjiang is 1.9 °C warmer (Iizuka 22.6 °C, Songjiang 24.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iizuka 32.4 °C (August), Songjiang 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Iizuka 1.4 °C, Songjiang 1.0 °C.
  • Iizuka gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 1,669 mm against 1,427 mm.
  • Songjiang gets about 193 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,952 h against 1,759 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Iizuka 14 cm, Songjiang 9.8 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Songjiang in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: Songjiang in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Songjiang in 8 of 12 months
