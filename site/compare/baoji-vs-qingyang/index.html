How the climate of Baoji, China compares with Qingyang, Gansu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Baoji is 4.9 °C (8.9 °F) warmer over the year: 14.4 °C against 9.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Baoji is 6.2 °C warmer (Baoji 1.6 °C, Qingyang, Gansu -4.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoji 31.3 °C (July), Qingyang, Gansu 27.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baoji -3.0 °C, Qingyang, Gansu -9.6 °C.
  • Baoji gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 653 mm against 643 mm.
  • Qingyang, Gansu gets about 331 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,270 h against 1,939 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoji 6.0 cm, Qingyang, Gansu 34 cm (ERA5-Land).
