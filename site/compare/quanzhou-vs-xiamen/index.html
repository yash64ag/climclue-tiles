How the climate of Quanzhou, China compares with Xiamen, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Quanzhou is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 21.5 °C against 21.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Xiamen is 0.5 °C warmer (Quanzhou 15.0 °C, Xiamen 15.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Quanzhou 32.9 °C (July), Xiamen 32.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Quanzhou 9.5 °C, Xiamen 10.0 °C.
  • Xiamen gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,343 mm against 1,162 mm.
  • Xiamen gets about 109 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,067 h against 1,958 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Quanzhou in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Quanzhou in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Xiamen in 12 of 12 months
