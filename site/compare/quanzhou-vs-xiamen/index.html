Quanzhou vs Xiamen climate

How the climate of Quanzhou, China compares with Xiamen, China, 70 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Quanzhou is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 21.4 °C against 21.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Quanzhou is 0.7 °C warmer (Quanzhou 28.9 °C, Xiamen 28.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Quanzhou 31.6 °C (July), Xiamen 30.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Quanzhou 8.3 °C, Xiamen 9.6 °C.
  • Xiamen gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,395 mm against 1,251 mm.
  • Xiamen gets about 109 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,067 h against 1,958 h; about 1961–1990).
