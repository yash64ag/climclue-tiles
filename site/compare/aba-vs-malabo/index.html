Aba vs Malabo climate

How the climate of Aba, Nigeria compares with Malabo, Equatorial Guinea, 217 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Malabo is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 25.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Malabo is 0.8 °C warmer (Aba 24.9 °C, Malabo 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aba 33.1 °C (February), Malabo 28.0 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Aba 21.8 °C, Malabo 24.1 °C.
  • Malabo gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 2,527 mm against 2,485 mm.
  • Malabo gets about 6 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,504 h against 1,498 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Malabo in 10 of 12 months
  • Drier: Aba in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
