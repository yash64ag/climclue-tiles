How the climate of Huaibei, China compares with Shangqiu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Shangqiu is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.0 °C against 14.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Shangqiu is 0.5 °C warmer (Huaibei 25.5 °C, Shangqiu 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huaibei 31.5 °C (July), Shangqiu 31.8 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Huaibei -3.8 °C, Shangqiu -4.1 °C.
  • Huaibei gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 907 mm against 724 mm.
  • Shangqiu gets about 47 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,276 h against 2,229 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Huaibei 18 cm, Shangqiu 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
