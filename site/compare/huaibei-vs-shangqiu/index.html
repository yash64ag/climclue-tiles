Huaibei vs Shangqiu climate

How the climate of Huaibei, China compares with Shangqiu, China, 115 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Huaibei and Shangqiu have the same yearly average temperature (15.0 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Shangqiu is 0.4 °C warmer (Huaibei 15.4 °C, Shangqiu 15.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huaibei 31.1 °C (July), Shangqiu 31.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Huaibei -3.7 °C, Shangqiu -4.0 °C.
  • Huaibei gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 869 mm against 719 mm.
  • Shangqiu gets about 47 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,276 h against 2,229 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Huaibei 18 cm, Shangqiu 15 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 5 months each
  • Drier: Shangqiu in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Shangqiu in 12 of 12 months
