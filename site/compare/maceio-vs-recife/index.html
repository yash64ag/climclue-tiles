Maceió vs Recife climate

How the climate of Maceió, Brazil compares with Recife, Brazil, 202 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Recife is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Recife is 0.8 °C warmer (Maceió 25.7 °C, Recife 26.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maceió 30.6 °C (February), Recife 30.9 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Maceió 20.5 °C, Recife 21.0 °C.
  • Recife gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,844 mm against 1,744 mm.
  • Recife gets about 30 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,577 h against 2,547 h; about 1961–1990).
