Amman vs Cairo climate

How the climate of Amman, Jordan compares with Cairo, Egypt, 494 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Cairo is 5.0 °C (9.0 °F) warmer over the year: 21.9 °C against 16.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Cairo is 6.5 °C warmer (Amman 8.2 °C, Cairo 14.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amman 31.6 °C (August), Cairo 36.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Amman 2.9 °C, Cairo 8.2 °C.
  • Amman gets 1,522% more rain and snow a year: 326 mm against 20 mm.
  • Cairo gets about 22 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,330 h against 3,308 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amman 1.3 cm, Cairo 0 cm (ERA5-Land).
