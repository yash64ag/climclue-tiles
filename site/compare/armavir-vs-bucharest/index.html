Armavir vs Bucharest climate

How the climate of Armavir, Russia compares with Bucharest, Romania, 1,186 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bucharest is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 11.7 °C against 11.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Bucharest is 1.3 °C warmer (Armavir 4.7 °C, Bucharest 6.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Armavir 28.1 °C (July), Bucharest 27.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Armavir -4.4 °C, Bucharest -4.5 °C.
  • Armavir gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 648 mm against 619 mm.
  • Armavir gets about 139 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,214 h against 2,075 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Armavir 70 cm, Bucharest 68 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Bucharest in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Armavir in 11 of 12 months
