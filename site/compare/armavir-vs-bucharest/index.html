How the climate of Armavir, Russia compares with Bucharest, Romania: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Armavir is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 11.6 °C against 11.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Armavir is 0.8 °C warmer (Armavir 1.1 °C, Bucharest 0.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Armavir 30.1 °C (July), Bucharest 30.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Armavir -4.1 °C, Bucharest -4.7 °C.
  • Armavir gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 648 mm against 619 mm.
  • Armavir gets about 139 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,214 h against 2,075 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Armavir 70 cm, Bucharest 68 cm (ERA5-Land).
