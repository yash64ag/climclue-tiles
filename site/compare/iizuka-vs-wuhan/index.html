Iizuka vs Wuhan climate

How the climate of Iizuka, Japan compares with Wuhan, China, 1,582 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Wuhan is 0.4 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 17.4 °C against 17.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Wuhan is 2.7 °C warmer (Iizuka 20.1 °C, Wuhan 22.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iizuka 32.6 °C (August), Wuhan 33.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Iizuka 2.1 °C, Wuhan 0.1 °C.
  • Iizuka gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,669 mm against 1,550 mm.
  • Wuhan gets about 205 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,964 h against 1,759 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Iizuka 14 cm, Wuhan 14 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
