Idlib vs İzmir climate

How the climate of Idlib, Syria compares with İzmir, Turkey, 885 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • İzmir is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 17.5 °C against 17.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Idlib is 1.5 °C warmer (Idlib 19.9 °C, İzmir 18.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Idlib 33.9 °C (August), İzmir 34.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Idlib 2.2 °C, İzmir 3.2 °C.
  • İzmir gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 721 mm against 523 mm.
  • İzmir gets about 163 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,128 h against 2,965 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Idlib 4.7 cm, İzmir 9.0 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Sunnier: İzmir in 10 of 12 months
