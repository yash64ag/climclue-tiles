Idlib vs İzmir climate

How the climate of Idlib, Syria compares with İzmir, Turkey, 885 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • İzmir is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 17.7 °C against 17.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when İzmir is 1.8 °C warmer (Idlib 7.9 °C, İzmir 9.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Idlib 32.9 °C (August), İzmir 34.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Idlib 2.9 °C, İzmir 4.5 °C.
  • İzmir gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 664 mm against 543 mm.
  • İzmir gets about 163 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,128 h against 2,965 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Idlib 2.5 cm, İzmir 2.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
