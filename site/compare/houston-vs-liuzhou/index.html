How the climate of Houston, United States compares with Liuzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Liuzhou is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 21.4 °C against 21.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Liuzhou is 1.4 °C warmer (Houston 22.1 °C, Liuzhou 23.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Houston 34.1 °C (August), Liuzhou 33.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Houston 7.1 °C, Liuzhou 7.3 °C.
  • Liuzhou gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,503 mm against 1,372 mm.
  • Houston gets about 1,007 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,576 h against 1,568 h; about 1961–1990).
