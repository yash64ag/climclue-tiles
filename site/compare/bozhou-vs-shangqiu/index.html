How the climate of Bozhou, China compares with Shangqiu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bozhou is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 15.5 °C against 15.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Bozhou is 0.8 °C warmer (Bozhou 9.6 °C, Shangqiu 8.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bozhou 31.9 °C (July), Shangqiu 31.8 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Bozhou -3.0 °C, Shangqiu -4.1 °C.
  • Bozhou gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 811 mm against 724 mm.
  • Shangqiu gets about 57 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,276 h against 2,220 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bozhou 17 cm, Shangqiu 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
