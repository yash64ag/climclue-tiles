How the climate of Krasnoyarsk, Russia compares with Omsk, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Omsk is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 2.4 °C against 2.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Krasnoyarsk is 2.1 °C warmer (Krasnoyarsk -12.9 °C, Omsk -15.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Krasnoyarsk 25.2 °C (July), Omsk 25.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Krasnoyarsk -19.9 °C, Omsk -20.6 °C.
  • Krasnoyarsk gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 451 mm against 391 mm.
  • Omsk gets about 342 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,171 h against 1,829 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Krasnoyarsk 122 cm, Omsk 118 cm (ERA5-Land).
