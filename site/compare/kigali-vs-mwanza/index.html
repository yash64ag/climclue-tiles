Kigali vs Mwanza climate

How the climate of Kigali, Rwanda compares with Mwanza, Tanzania, 322 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mwanza is 2.5 °C (4.5 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 20.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Mwanza is 2.9 °C warmer (Kigali 20.2 °C, Mwanza 23.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kigali 26.3 °C (August), Mwanza 25.7 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Kigali 14.6 °C, Mwanza 19.3 °C.
  • Kigali gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,069 mm against 988 mm.
  • Mwanza gets about 799 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,789 h against 1,990 h; about 1961–1990).
