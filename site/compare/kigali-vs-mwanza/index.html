Kigali vs Mwanza climate

How the climate of Kigali, Rwanda compares with Mwanza, Tanzania, 322 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mwanza is 2.6 °C (4.6 °F) warmer over the year: 22.7 °C against 20.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Mwanza is 3.0 °C warmer (Kigali 19.9 °C, Mwanza 22.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kigali 26.0 °C (August), Mwanza 25.8 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Kigali 14.3 °C, Mwanza 18.8 °C.
  • Mwanza gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,049 mm against 1,044 mm.
  • Mwanza gets about 799 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,789 h against 1,990 h; about 1961–1990).
