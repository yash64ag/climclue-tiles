How the climate of Almaty, Kazakhstan compares with Cedar Rapids, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Almaty is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 10.5 °C against 9.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Almaty is 2.0 °C warmer (Almaty -4.1 °C, Cedar Rapids -6.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Almaty 30.2 °C (July), Cedar Rapids 29.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Almaty -8.8 °C, Cedar Rapids -10.8 °C.
  • Cedar Rapids gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 952 mm against 926 mm.
  • Cedar Rapids gets about 184 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,644 h against 2,460 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Almaty 145 cm, Cedar Rapids 75 cm (ERA5-Land).
