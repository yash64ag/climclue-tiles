Jining, Shandong vs Tai’an, Shandong climate

Main differences

  • Jining, Shandong is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 14.5 °C against 13.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Jining, Shandong is 1.6 °C warmer (Jining, Shandong 8.0 °C, Tai’an, Shandong 6.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jining, Shandong 31.6 °C (June), Tai’an, Shandong 30.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jining, Shandong -5.4 °C, Tai’an, Shandong -6.2 °C.
  • Tai’an, Shandong gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 809 mm against 691 mm.
  • Tai’an, Shandong gets about 93 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,496 h against 2,403 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jining, Shandong 8.1 cm, Tai’an, Shandong 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
