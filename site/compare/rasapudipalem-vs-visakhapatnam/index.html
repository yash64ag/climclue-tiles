Main differences

  • Visakhapatnam is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 27.4 °C against 27.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Visakhapatnam is 0.3 °C warmer (Rasapūdipalem 30.3 °C, Visakhapatnam 30.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rasapūdipalem 34.6 °C (May), Visakhapatnam 34.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Rasapūdipalem 18.0 °C, Visakhapatnam 18.1 °C.
  • Rasapūdipalem gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,139 mm against 1,087 mm.
  • Visakhapatnam gets about 3 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,555 h against 2,552 h; about 1961–1990).
