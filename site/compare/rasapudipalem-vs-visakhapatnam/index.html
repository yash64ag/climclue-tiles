Rasapūdipalem vs Visakhapatnam climate

How the climate of Rasapūdipalem, India compares with Visakhapatnam, India, 13 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rasapūdipalem and Visakhapatnam have the same yearly average temperature (27.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Visakhapatnam is 0.1 °C warmer (Rasapūdipalem 23.1 °C, Visakhapatnam 23.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rasapūdipalem 33.1 °C (May), Visakhapatnam 33.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Rasapūdipalem 20.3 °C, Visakhapatnam 20.5 °C.
  • Rasapūdipalem gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,230 mm against 1,147 mm.
  • Visakhapatnam gets about 3 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,555 h against 2,552 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 3 months each
  • Drier: Visakhapatnam in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Visakhapatnam in 8 of 12 months
