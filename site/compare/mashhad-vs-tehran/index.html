Mashhad vs Tehran climate

How the climate of Mashhad, Iran compares with Tehran, Iran, 739 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tehran is 2.0 °C (3.5 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 14.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Tehran is 4.5 °C warmer (Mashhad 25.8 °C, Tehran 30.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mashhad 33.4 °C (July), Tehran 35.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Mashhad -3.6 °C, Tehran -4.5 °C.
  • Tehran gets 45% more rain and snow a year: 384 mm against 265 mm.
  • Mashhad gets about 8 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,878 h against 2,870 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Mashhad 33 cm, Tehran 33 cm (ERA5-Land).
