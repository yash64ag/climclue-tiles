How the climate of Mashhad, Iran compares with Tehran, Iran: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tehran is 2.4 °C (4.4 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 14.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Tehran is 4.6 °C warmer (Mashhad 21.1 °C, Tehran 25.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mashhad 34.8 °C (July), Tehran 37.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Mashhad -3.1 °C, Tehran -0.6 °C.
  • Tehran gets 52% more rain and snow a year: 397 mm against 261 mm.
  • Mashhad gets about 8 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,878 h against 2,870 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Mashhad 33 cm, Tehran 33 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Tehran in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Mashhad in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
