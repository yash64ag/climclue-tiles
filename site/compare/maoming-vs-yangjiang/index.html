Maoming vs Yangjiang climate

How the climate of Maoming, China compares with Yangjiang, China, 110 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Maoming is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 23.6 °C against 23.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Maoming is 0.9 °C warmer (Maoming 17.6 °C, Yangjiang 16.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maoming 31.4 °C (July), Yangjiang 31.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Maoming 12.6 °C, Yangjiang 11.6 °C.
  • Yangjiang gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 2,715 mm against 2,081 mm.
  • Yangjiang gets about 13 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,929 h against 1,916 h; about 1961–1990).
