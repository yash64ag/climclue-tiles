How the climate of Wuhu, China compares with Yao, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yao is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.7 °C against 16.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Wuhu is 2.1 °C warmer (Wuhu 21.8 °C, Yao 19.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Wuhu 32.5 °C (July), Yao 33.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Wuhu -0.4 °C, Yao 1.7 °C.
  • Yao gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,373 mm against 1,354 mm.
  • Wuhu gets about 162 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,004 h against 1,842 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Wuhu 22 cm, Yao 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
