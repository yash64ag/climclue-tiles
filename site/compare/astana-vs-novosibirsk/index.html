Astana vs Novosibirsk climate

How the climate of Astana, Kazakhstan compares with Novosibirsk, Russia, 876 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Astana is 1.7 °C (3.1 °F) warmer over the year: 3.4 °C against 1.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Astana is 2.8 °C warmer (Astana 5.0 °C, Novosibirsk 2.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Astana 26.8 °C (July), Novosibirsk 25.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Astana -18.8 °C, Novosibirsk -21.1 °C.
  • Novosibirsk gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 463 mm against 334 mm.
  • Astana gets about 343 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,450 h against 2,107 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Astana 100 cm, Novosibirsk 163 cm (ERA5-Land).
