How the climate of Algiers, Algeria compares with Palermo, Italy: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Palermo is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 18.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Palermo is 0.7 °C warmer (Algiers 15.9 °C, Palermo 16.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Algiers 32.4 °C (August), Palermo 30.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Algiers 6.7 °C, Palermo 9.1 °C.
  • Palermo gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 700 mm against 682 mm.
  • Algiers gets about 296 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,886 h against 2,590 h; about 1961–1990).
