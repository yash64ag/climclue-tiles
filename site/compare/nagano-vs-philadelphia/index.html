Nagano vs Philadelphia climate

How the climate of Nagano, Japan compares with Philadelphia, United States, 10,841 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Philadelphia is 0.6 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 13.1 °C against 12.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Philadelphia is 2.1 °C warmer (Nagano 20.5 °C, Philadelphia 22.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nagano 29.1 °C (August), Philadelphia 29.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nagano -5.3 °C, Philadelphia -3.3 °C.
  • Nagano gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,237 mm against 1,220 mm.
  • Philadelphia gets about 660 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,463 h against 1,803 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nagano 194 cm, Philadelphia 66 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Philadelphia in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Philadelphia in 12 of 12 months
