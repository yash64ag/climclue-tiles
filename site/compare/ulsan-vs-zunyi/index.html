How the climate of Ulsan, South Korea compares with Zunyi, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zunyi is 1.3 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Zunyi is 2.6 °C warmer (Ulsan 13.9 °C, Zunyi 16.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ulsan 30.3 °C (August), Zunyi 29.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Ulsan -2.2 °C, Zunyi 2.6 °C.
  • Ulsan gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,186 mm against 1,082 mm.
  • Ulsan gets about 1,008 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,290 h against 1,282 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ulsan 6.9 cm, Zunyi 7.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
