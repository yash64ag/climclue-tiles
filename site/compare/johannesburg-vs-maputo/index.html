How the climate of Johannesburg, South Africa compares with Maputo, Mozambique: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Maputo is 8.0 °C (14.4 °F) warmer over the year: 22.8 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Maputo is 11.3 °C warmer (Johannesburg 8.3 °C, Maputo 19.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Johannesburg 24.7 °C (January), Maputo 30.0 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Johannesburg 1.1 °C, Maputo 13.4 °C.
  • Johannesburg gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 821 mm against 785 mm.
  • Johannesburg gets about 342 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,169 h against 2,827 h; about 1961–1990).
