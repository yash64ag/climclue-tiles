Johannesburg vs Maputo climate

How the climate of Johannesburg, South Africa compares with Maputo, Mozambique, 454 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Maputo is 7.9 °C (14.1 °F) warmer over the year: 22.9 °C against 15.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Maputo is 11.1 °C warmer (Johannesburg 8.6 °C, Maputo 19.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Johannesburg 24.3 °C (January), Maputo 28.6 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Johannesburg 2.1 °C, Maputo 15.7 °C.
  • Johannesburg gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 792 mm against 780 mm.
  • Johannesburg gets about 342 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,169 h against 2,827 h; about 1961–1990).

  • Drier: Johannesburg in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Johannesburg in 12 of 12 months
