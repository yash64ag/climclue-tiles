How the climate of Kano, Nigeria compares with N'Djamena, Chad: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • N'Djamena is 2.9 °C (5.2 °F) warmer over the year: 28.8 °C against 25.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when N'Djamena is 3.7 °C warmer (Kano 24.1 °C, N'Djamena 27.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kano 38.1 °C (April), N'Djamena 41.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Kano 12.5 °C, N'Djamena 15.1 °C.
  • Kano gets 125% more rain and snow a year: 1,095 mm against 487 mm.
  • N'Djamena gets about 242 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,166 h against 2,924 h; about 1961–1990).
