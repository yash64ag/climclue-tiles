Kano vs N'Djamena climate

How the climate of Kano, Nigeria compares with N'Djamena, Chad, 710 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • N'Djamena is 2.7 °C (4.8 °F) warmer over the year: 28.8 °C against 26.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when N'Djamena is 3.4 °C warmer (Kano 24.4 °C, N'Djamena 27.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kano 38.3 °C (April), N'Djamena 41.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Kano 12.9 °C, N'Djamena 16.3 °C.
  • Kano gets 116% more rain and snow a year: 1,037 mm against 480 mm.
  • N'Djamena gets about 242 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,166 h against 2,924 h; about 1961–1990).

  • Sunnier: N'Djamena in 8 of 12 months
