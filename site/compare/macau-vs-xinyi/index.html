Macau vs Xinyi, Guangdong climate

How the climate of Macau, Macao compares with Xinyi, Guangdong, China, 268 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Macau is 0.6 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Macau is 1.6 °C warmer (Macau 15.9 °C, Xinyi, Guangdong 14.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Macau 29.6 °C (August), Xinyi, Guangdong 30.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Macau 13.2 °C, Xinyi, Guangdong 10.3 °C.
  • Macau gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 2,195 mm against 1,924 mm.
  • Macau gets about 105 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,945 h against 1,840 h; about 1961–1990).
