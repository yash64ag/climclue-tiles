Faridabad vs Najafgarh climate

How the climate of Faridabad, India compares with Najafgarh, India, 39 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Najafgarh is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 24.9 °C against 24.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Najafgarh is 0.3 °C warmer (Faridabad 28.8 °C, Najafgarh 29.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Faridabad 39.4 °C (May), Najafgarh 39.6 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Faridabad 7.8 °C, Najafgarh 7.4 °C.
  • Faridabad gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 693 mm against 597 mm.
  • Najafgarh gets about 21 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,702 h against 2,681 h; about 1961–1990).
