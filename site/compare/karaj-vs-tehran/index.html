Karaj vs Tehran climate

How the climate of Karaj, Iran compares with Tehran, Iran, 42 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Tehran is 2.4 °C (4.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Tehran is 2.9 °C warmer (Karaj 25.4 °C, Tehran 28.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Karaj 33.8 °C (July), Tehran 35.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Karaj -5.6 °C, Tehran -4.5 °C.
  • Karaj gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 484 mm against 384 mm.
  • Tehran gets about 96 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,870 h against 2,774 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Karaj 98 cm, Tehran 84 cm (ERA5-Land).
