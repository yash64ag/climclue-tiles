Karaj vs Tehran climate

How the climate of Karaj, Iran compares with Tehran, Iran, 42 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tehran is 1.7 °C (3.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 14.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Tehran is 2.2 °C warmer (Karaj 25.6 °C, Tehran 27.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Karaj 33.9 °C (July), Tehran 35.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Karaj -5.6 °C, Tehran -5.2 °C.
  • Karaj gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 478 mm against 397 mm.
  • Tehran gets about 96 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,870 h against 2,774 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Karaj 78 cm, Tehran 33 cm (ERA5-Land).
