How the climate of Karaj, Iran compares with Tehran, Iran: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tehran is 2.7 °C (4.8 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 14.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Tehran is 3.3 °C warmer (Karaj 2.0 °C, Tehran 5.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Karaj 35.2 °C (July), Tehran 37.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Karaj -4.1 °C, Tehran -0.6 °C.
  • Karaj gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 478 mm against 397 mm.
  • Tehran gets about 96 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,870 h against 2,774 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Karaj 78 cm, Tehran 33 cm (ERA5-Land).
