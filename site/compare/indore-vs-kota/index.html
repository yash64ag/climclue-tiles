Indore vs Kota climate

How the climate of Indore, India compares with Kota, India, 274 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kota is 1.5 °C (2.8 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 25.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Kota is 4.6 °C warmer (Indore 29.9 °C, Kota 34.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Indore 40.6 °C (May), Kota 42.5 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Indore 10.4 °C, Kota 9.7 °C.
  • Indore gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 926 mm against 751 mm.
  • Kota gets about 24 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,867 h against 2,843 h; about 1961–1990).
