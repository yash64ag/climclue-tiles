How the climate of Camayenne, Guinea compares with Mariveles, Philippines: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Mariveles is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Mariveles is 1.6 °C warmer (Camayenne 26.4 °C, Mariveles 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Camayenne 32.0 °C (December), Mariveles 33.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Camayenne 20.9 °C, Mariveles 20.9 °C.
  • Camayenne gets 31% more rain and snow a year: 3,521 mm against 2,677 mm.
  • Mariveles gets about 255 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,440 h against 2,185 h; about 1961–1990).
