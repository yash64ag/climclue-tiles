Camayenne vs Mariveles climate

How the climate of Camayenne, Guinea compares with Mariveles, Philippines, 14,303 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Camayenne is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 26.1 °C against 25.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Camayenne is 1.9 °C warmer (Camayenne 26.8 °C, Mariveles 24.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Camayenne 28.4 °C (December), Mariveles 28.6 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Camayenne 24.2 °C, Mariveles 23.0 °C.
  • Camayenne gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 3,562 mm against 2,967 mm.
  • Mariveles gets about 255 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,440 h against 2,185 h; about 1961–1990).
