Baoji vs Xianyang climate

How the climate of Baoji, China compares with Xianyang, China, 135 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Xianyang is 1.0 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 15.4 °C against 14.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Xianyang is 1.9 °C warmer (Baoji 24.2 °C, Xianyang 26.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoji 31.3 °C (July), Xianyang 32.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baoji -3.0 °C, Xianyang -3.1 °C.
  • Baoji gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 653 mm against 578 mm.
  • Baoji gets about 8 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,939 h against 1,931 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoji 6.0 cm, Xianyang 8.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
