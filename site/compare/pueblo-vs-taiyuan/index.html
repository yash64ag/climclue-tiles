Pueblo vs Taiyuan climate

How the climate of Pueblo, United States compares with Taiyuan, China, 10,735 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Pueblo is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 11.8 °C against 11.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Pueblo is 3.5 °C warmer (Pueblo -0.7 °C, Taiyuan -4.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pueblo 32.8 °C (July), Taiyuan 30.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Pueblo -6.5 °C, Taiyuan -10.1 °C.
  • Taiyuan gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 432 mm against 364 mm.
  • Pueblo gets about 808 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,371 h against 2,563 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Pueblo 109 cm, Taiyuan 38 cm (ERA5-Land).
