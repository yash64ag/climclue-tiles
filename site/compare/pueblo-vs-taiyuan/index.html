How the climate of Pueblo, United States compares with Taiyuan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Pueblo is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 11.4 °C against 10.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Pueblo is 3.8 °C warmer (Pueblo -0.7 °C, Taiyuan -4.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pueblo 34.0 °C (July), Taiyuan 29.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Pueblo -9.8 °C, Taiyuan -11.0 °C.
  • Taiyuan gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 422 mm against 342 mm.
  • Pueblo gets about 808 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,371 h against 2,563 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Pueblo 53 cm, Taiyuan 14 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Drier: Taiyuan in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Pueblo in 12 of 12 months
