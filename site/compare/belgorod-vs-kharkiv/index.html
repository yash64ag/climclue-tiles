How the climate of Belgorod, Russia compares with Kharkiv, Ukraine: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kharkiv is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 8.1 °C against 7.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Kharkiv is 0.7 °C warmer (Belgorod -5.3 °C, Kharkiv -4.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belgorod 25.9 °C (July), Kharkiv 26.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Belgorod -8.5 °C, Kharkiv -7.7 °C.
  • Belgorod gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 582 mm against 526 mm.
  • Belgorod gets about 37 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,789 h against 1,752 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Belgorod 138 cm, Kharkiv 105 cm (ERA5-Land).
