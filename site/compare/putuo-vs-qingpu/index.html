Putuo vs Qingpu climate

How the climate of Putuo, China compares with Qingpu, China, 28 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Putuo and Qingpu have the same yearly average temperature (16.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Qingpu is 0.3 °C warmer (Putuo 14.9 °C, Qingpu 15.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Putuo 31.4 °C (July), Qingpu 31.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Putuo 0.4 °C, Qingpu -0.1 °C.
  • Putuo gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,454 mm against 1,399 mm.
  • Putuo gets about 11 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,969 h against 1,958 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Putuo 8.5 cm, Qingpu 10 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Qingpu in 6 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: Qingpu in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Putuo in 11 of 12 months
