Putuo vs Qingpu climate

How the climate of Putuo, China compares with Qingpu, China, 28 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Putuo and Qingpu have the same yearly average temperature (16.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Putuo is 0.3 °C warmer (Putuo 7.0 °C, Qingpu 6.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Putuo 31.7 °C (July), Qingpu 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Putuo 1.0 °C, Qingpu 0.7 °C.
  • Putuo gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,466 mm against 1,403 mm.
  • Putuo gets about 11 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,969 h against 1,958 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Putuo 8.9 cm, Qingpu 10 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Qingpu in 6 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: Qingpu in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Putuo in 11 of 12 months
