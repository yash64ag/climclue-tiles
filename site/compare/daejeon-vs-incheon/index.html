How the climate of Daejeon, South Korea compares with Incheon, South Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Daejeon is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 13.3 °C against 12.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Daejeon is 2.7 °C warmer (Daejeon 18.6 °C, Incheon 15.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daejeon 30.6 °C (August), Incheon 28.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Daejeon -6.2 °C, Incheon -6.3 °C.
  • Incheon gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,414 mm against 1,223 mm.
  • Daejeon gets about 71 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,425 h against 2,354 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daejeon 27 cm, Incheon 24 cm (ERA5-Land).
