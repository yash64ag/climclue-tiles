Kano vs Nandurbar climate

How the climate of Kano, Nigeria compares with Nandurbar, India, 7,032 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Nandurbar is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.3 °C against 26.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Nandurbar is 0.8 °C warmer (Kano 26.3 °C, Nandurbar 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kano 38.3 °C (April), Nandurbar 37.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kano 12.9 °C, Nandurbar 14.7 °C.
  • Kano gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 1,037 mm against 900 mm.
  • Kano gets about 74 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,924 h against 2,850 h; about 1961–1990).
