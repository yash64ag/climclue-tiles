Kano vs Nandurbar climate

How the climate of Kano, Nigeria compares with Nandurbar, India, 7,032 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Nandurbar is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 26.4 °C against 25.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Nandurbar is 1.1 °C warmer (Kano 28.6 °C, Nandurbar 29.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kano 38.1 °C (April), Nandurbar 37.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kano 12.6 °C, Nandurbar 14.8 °C.
  • Kano gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,095 mm against 908 mm.
  • Kano gets about 74 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,924 h against 2,850 h; about 1961–1990).
