Oklahoma City vs Xinyang climate

How the climate of Oklahoma City, United States compares with Xinyang, China, 11,809 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Oklahoma City is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 15.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Oklahoma City is 1.0 °C warmer (Oklahoma City 27.7 °C, Xinyang 26.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Oklahoma City 34.4 °C (July), Xinyang 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Oklahoma City -2.8 °C, Xinyang -1.9 °C.
  • Xinyang gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 1,143 mm against 906 mm.
  • Oklahoma City gets about 1,004 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,031 h against 2,028 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Oklahoma City 17 cm, Xinyang 28 cm (ERA5-Land).
