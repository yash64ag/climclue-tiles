Changsha, Hunan vs McKinney climate

How the climate of Changsha, Hunan, China compares with McKinney, United States, 12,509 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • McKinney is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 18.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when McKinney is 1.6 °C warmer (Changsha, Hunan 11.9 °C, McKinney 13.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changsha, Hunan 32.9 °C (July), McKinney 34.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Changsha, Hunan 2.0 °C, McKinney 3.4 °C.
  • Changsha, Hunan gets 52% more rain and snow a year: 1,681 mm against 1,108 mm.
  • McKinney gets about 1,219 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,808 h against 1,589 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changsha, Hunan 16 cm, McKinney 6.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
