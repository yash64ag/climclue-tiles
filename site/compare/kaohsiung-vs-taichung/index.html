Kaohsiung vs Taichung climate

How the climate of Kaohsiung, Taiwan compares with Taichung, Taiwan, 174 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kaohsiung is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 24.5 °C against 23.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Kaohsiung is 2.5 °C warmer (Kaohsiung 22.3 °C, Taichung 19.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kaohsiung 30.5 °C (July), Taichung 29.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kaohsiung 15.2 °C, Taichung 13.5 °C.
  • Kaohsiung gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,962 mm against 1,949 mm.
  • Kaohsiung gets about 171 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,008 h against 1,837 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Kaohsiung in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Kaohsiung in 10 of 12 months
