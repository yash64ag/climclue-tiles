Kaohsiung vs Taichung climate

How the climate of Kaohsiung, Taiwan compares with Taichung, Taiwan, 174 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kaohsiung is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 24.6 °C against 23.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Kaohsiung is 2.5 °C warmer (Kaohsiung 22.4 °C, Taichung 19.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kaohsiung 30.4 °C (July), Taichung 29.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kaohsiung 15.5 °C, Taichung 13.3 °C.
  • Taichung gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 2,055 mm against 1,839 mm.
  • Kaohsiung gets about 171 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,008 h against 1,837 h; about 1961–1990).
