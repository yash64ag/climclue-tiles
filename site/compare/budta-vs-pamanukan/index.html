Budta vs Pamanukan climate

How the climate of Budta, Philippines compares with Pamanukan, Indonesia, 2,377 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Budta is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 28.2 °C against 27.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Budta is 1.9 °C warmer (Budta 29.1 °C, Pamanukan 27.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Budta 33.3 °C (April), Pamanukan 32.0 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Budta 23.5 °C, Pamanukan 23.5 °C.
  • Pamanukan gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 2,016 mm against 1,503 mm.
  • Pamanukan gets about 504 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,795 h against 2,291 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Budta in 10 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Pamanukan in 11 of 12 months
