Budta vs Pamanukan climate

How the climate of Budta, Philippines compares with Pamanukan, Indonesia, 2,377 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Budta is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 28.2 °C against 27.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Budta is 1.9 °C warmer (Budta 29.6 °C, Pamanukan 27.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Budta 33.3 °C (April), Pamanukan 31.7 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Budta 23.5 °C, Pamanukan 23.9 °C.
  • Pamanukan gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 2,002 mm against 1,540 mm.
  • Pamanukan gets about 504 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,795 h against 2,291 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Budta in 10 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Pamanukan in 11 of 12 months
