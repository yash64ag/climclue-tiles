Algiers vs Fes climate

How the climate of Algiers, Algeria compares with Fes, Morocco, 792 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fes is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 18.6 °C against 18.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Fes is 1.2 °C warmer (Algiers 13.7 °C, Fes 14.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Algiers 31.2 °C (August), Fes 34.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Algiers 8.0 °C, Fes 5.1 °C.
  • Algiers gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 682 mm against 546 mm.
  • Algiers gets about 37 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,886 h against 2,849 h; about 1961–1990).
