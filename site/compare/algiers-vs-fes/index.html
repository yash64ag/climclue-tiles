How the climate of Algiers, Algeria compares with Fes, Morocco: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Fes is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 18.6 °C against 17.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Fes is 1.9 °C warmer (Algiers 25.2 °C, Fes 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Algiers 31.9 °C (August), Fes 34.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Algiers 6.1 °C, Fes 5.4 °C.
  • Algiers gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 682 mm against 546 mm.
  • Algiers gets about 37 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,886 h against 2,849 h; about 1961–1990).
