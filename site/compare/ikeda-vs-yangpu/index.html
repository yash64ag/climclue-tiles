Ikeda vs Yangpu climate

How the climate of Ikeda, Japan compares with Yangpu, China, 1,354 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yangpu is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Yangpu is 1.0 °C warmer (Ikeda 27.1 °C, Yangpu 28.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ikeda 31.1 °C (August), Yangpu 30.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ikeda 0.7 °C, Yangpu 1.3 °C.
  • Ikeda gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,449 mm against 1,430 mm.
  • Yangpu gets about 200 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,973 h against 1,773 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ikeda 15 cm, Yangpu 6.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
