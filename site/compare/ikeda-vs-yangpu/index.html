How the climate of Ikeda, Japan compares with Yangpu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yangpu is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 17.1 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Yangpu is 1.6 °C warmer (Ikeda 27.1 °C, Yangpu 28.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ikeda 32.4 °C (August), Yangpu 31.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ikeda 1.8 °C, Yangpu 1.9 °C.
  • Ikeda gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,449 mm against 1,430 mm.
  • Yangpu gets about 200 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,973 h against 1,773 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ikeda 15 cm, Yangpu 6.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
