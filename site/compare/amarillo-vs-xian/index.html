Amarillo vs Xi’an climate

How the climate of Amarillo, United States compares with Xi’an, China, 11,654 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Xi’an is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 15.3 °C against 14.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Xi’an is 2.7 °C warmer (Amarillo 14.1 °C, Xi’an 16.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amarillo 32.5 °C (July), Xi’an 31.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Amarillo -1.4 °C, Xi’an -3.5 °C.
  • Xi’an gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 674 mm against 542 mm.
  • Amarillo gets about 1,334 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 1,909 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amarillo 23 cm, Xi’an 10 cm (ERA5-Land).
