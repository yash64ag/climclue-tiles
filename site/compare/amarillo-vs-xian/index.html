Amarillo vs Xi’an climate

How the climate of Amarillo, United States compares with Xi’an, China, 11,654 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Xi’an is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.4 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Xi’an is 2.9 °C warmer (Amarillo 13.9 °C, Xi’an 16.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amarillo 33.0 °C (July), Xi’an 32.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Amarillo -5.1 °C, Xi’an -3.1 °C.
  • Xi’an gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 664 mm against 550 mm.
  • Amarillo gets about 1,334 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 1,909 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amarillo 23 cm, Xi’an 10 cm (ERA5-Land).
