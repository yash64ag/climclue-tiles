Budapest vs Prague climate

How the climate of Budapest, Hungary compares with Prague, Czechia, 444 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Budapest is 1.4 °C (2.6 °F) warmer over the year: 10.9 °C against 9.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Budapest is 2.5 °C warmer (Budapest 19.6 °C, Prague 17.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Budapest 26.7 °C (July), Prague 23.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Budapest -3.7 °C, Prague -3.5 °C.
  • Budapest gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 588 mm against 541 mm.
  • Budapest gets about 260 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,899 h against 1,640 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Budapest 55 cm, Prague 42 cm (ERA5-Land).
