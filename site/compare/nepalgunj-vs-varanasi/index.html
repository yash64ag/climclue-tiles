How the climate of Nepalgunj, Nepal compares with Varanasi, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Varanasi is 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) warmer over the year: 25.8 °C against 24.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Varanasi is 2.6 °C warmer (Nepalgunj 30.8 °C, Varanasi 33.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nepalgunj 38.0 °C (May), Varanasi 41.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Nepalgunj 7.2 °C, Varanasi 8.2 °C.
  • Nepalgunj gets 53% more rain and snow a year: 1,606 mm against 1,052 mm.
  • Nepalgunj gets about 14 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,496 h against 2,482 h; about 1961–1990).
