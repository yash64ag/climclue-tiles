Oslo vs Turku climate

How the climate of Oslo, Norway compares with Turku, Finland, 639 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Oslo is 1.1 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 6.7 °C against 5.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Oslo is 2.2 °C warmer (Oslo 0.7 °C, Turku -1.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Oslo 21.6 °C (July), Turku 21.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Oslo -7.7 °C, Turku -8.4 °C.
  • Oslo gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 922 mm against 755 mm.
  • Turku gets about 91 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,669 h against 1,579 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Oslo 246 cm, Turku 164 cm (ERA5-Land).
