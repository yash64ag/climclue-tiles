Oslo vs Turku climate

How the climate of Oslo, Norway compares with Turku, Finland, 639 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Oslo is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 6.0 °C against 5.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Oslo is 1.6 °C warmer (Oslo 0.1 °C, Turku -1.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Oslo 20.8 °C (July), Turku 21.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Oslo -8.1 °C, Turku -8.3 °C.
  • Oslo gets 36% more rain and snow a year: 1,019 mm against 751 mm.
  • Turku gets about 91 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,669 h against 1,579 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Oslo 246 cm, Turku 164 cm (ERA5-Land).
