Maroua vs Ouagadougou climate

How the climate of Maroua, Cameroon compares with Ouagadougou, Burkina Faso, 1,738 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ouagadougou is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 28.3 °C against 27.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Ouagadougou is 1.1 °C warmer (Maroua 26.4 °C, Ouagadougou 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maroua 39.9 °C (April), Ouagadougou 39.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Maroua 16.2 °C, Ouagadougou 16.5 °C.
  • Maroua gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 815 mm against 722 mm.
  • Maroua gets about 25 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,973 h against 2,948 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Ouagadougou in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Maroua in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
