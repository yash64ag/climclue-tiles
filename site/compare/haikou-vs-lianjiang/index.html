How the climate of Haikou, Hainan, China compares with Lianjiang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Haikou, Hainan is 1.4 °C (2.6 °F) warmer over the year: 24.9 °C against 23.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Haikou, Hainan is 2.4 °C warmer (Haikou, Hainan 18.4 °C, Lianjiang 16.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Haikou, Hainan 33.0 °C (July), Lianjiang 32.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Haikou, Hainan 15.8 °C, Lianjiang 12.5 °C.
  • Lianjiang gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 2,092 mm against 1,800 mm.
  • Haikou, Hainan gets about 211 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,092 h against 1,881 h; about 1961–1990).
