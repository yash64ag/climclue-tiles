Qingyang vs Wuzhong climate

How the climate of Qingyang, China compares with Wuzhong, China, 284 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Wuzhong is 1.7 °C (3.0 °F) warmer over the year: 11.2 °C against 9.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Wuzhong is 3.3 °C warmer (Qingyang 22.2 °C, Wuzhong 25.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qingyang 26.8 °C (July), Wuzhong 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Qingyang -10.1 °C, Wuzhong -11.6 °C.
  • Qingyang gets 248% more rain and snow a year: 643 mm against 185 mm.
  • Wuzhong gets about 482 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,752 h against 2,270 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Qingyang 34 cm, Wuzhong 6.3 cm (ERA5-Land).
