Guarulhos vs São Paulo climate

Main differences

  • Guarulhos is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 20.0 °C against 20.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Guarulhos is 0.2 °C warmer (Guarulhos 18.6 °C, São Paulo 18.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guarulhos 27.6 °C (February), São Paulo 27.5 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Guarulhos 11.2 °C, São Paulo 11.4 °C.
  • São Paulo gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,470 mm against 1,425 mm.
  • Guarulhos gets about 2 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,782 h against 1,780 h; about 1961–1990).
