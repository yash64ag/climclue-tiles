Shantou vs Wong Tai Sin climate

How the climate of Shantou, China compares with Wong Tai Sin, Hong Kong, 279 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Wong Tai Sin is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 22.5 °C against 22.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Wong Tai Sin is 1.4 °C warmer (Shantou 17.6 °C, Wong Tai Sin 18.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Shantou 29.9 °C (July), Wong Tai Sin 29.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Shantou 11.4 °C, Wong Tai Sin 12.4 °C.
  • Wong Tai Sin gets 45% more rain and snow a year: 2,274 mm against 1,567 mm.
  • Shantou gets about 45 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,008 h against 1,963 h; about 1961–1990).
