Astana vs Rubtsovsk climate

How the climate of Astana, Kazakhstan compares with Rubtsovsk, Russia, 678 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rubtsovsk is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 3.9 °C against 3.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Rubtsovsk is 0.9 °C warmer (Astana -5.8 °C, Rubtsovsk -5.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Astana 26.8 °C (July), Rubtsovsk 27.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Astana -18.8 °C, Rubtsovsk -19.5 °C.
  • Rubtsovsk gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 361 mm against 334 mm.
  • Astana gets about 66 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,450 h against 2,383 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Astana 100 cm, Rubtsovsk 97 cm (ERA5-Land).
