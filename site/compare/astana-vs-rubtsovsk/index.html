How the climate of Astana, Kazakhstan compares with Rubtsovsk, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Rubtsovsk is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 3.8 °C against 3.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Astana is 1.3 °C warmer (Astana -13.8 °C, Rubtsovsk -15.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Astana 26.7 °C (July), Rubtsovsk 27.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Astana -18.6 °C, Rubtsovsk -19.7 °C.
  • Rubtsovsk gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 361 mm against 334 mm.
  • Astana gets about 66 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,450 h against 2,383 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Astana 100 cm, Rubtsovsk 97 cm (ERA5-Land).
