Shijiazhuang vs Taiyuan climate

How the climate of Shijiazhuang, China compares with Taiyuan, China, 169 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shijiazhuang is 2.7 °C (4.9 °F) warmer over the year: 14.0 °C against 11.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Shijiazhuang is 3.4 °C warmer (Shijiazhuang 16.4 °C, Taiyuan 13.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Shijiazhuang 32.1 °C (June), Taiyuan 30.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Shijiazhuang -6.9 °C, Taiyuan -10.2 °C.
  • Shijiazhuang gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 502 mm against 422 mm.
  • Shijiazhuang gets about 3 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,566 h against 2,563 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Shijiazhuang 12 cm, Taiyuan 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
