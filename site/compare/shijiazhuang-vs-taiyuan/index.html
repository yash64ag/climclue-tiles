How the climate of Shijiazhuang, China compares with Taiyuan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Shijiazhuang is 3.4 °C (6.1 °F) warmer over the year: 14.3 °C against 10.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Shijiazhuang is 4.0 °C warmer (Shijiazhuang 15.2 °C, Taiyuan 11.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Shijiazhuang 32.3 °C (June), Taiyuan 29.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Shijiazhuang -6.2 °C, Taiyuan -11.0 °C.
  • Shijiazhuang gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 502 mm against 422 mm.
  • Shijiazhuang gets about 3 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,566 h against 2,563 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Shijiazhuang 12 cm, Taiyuan 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
