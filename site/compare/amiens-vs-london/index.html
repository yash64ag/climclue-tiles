How the climate of Amiens, France compares with London, United Kingdom: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Amiens is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 10.7 °C against 10.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when London is 0.8 °C warmer (Amiens 3.9 °C, London 4.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amiens 22.5 °C (July), London 22.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Amiens 1.5 °C, London 1.8 °C.
  • Amiens gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 702 mm against 661 mm.
  • Amiens gets about 221 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,680 h against 1,459 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amiens 9.6 cm, London 5.3 cm (ERA5-Land).
