Perth vs Tangier climate

How the climate of Perth, Australia compares with Tangier, Morocco, 14,691 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Perth is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 18.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Perth is 11.8 °C warmer (Perth 24.3 °C, Tangier 12.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Perth 30.4 °C (February), Tangier 29.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Perth 9.8 °C, Tangier 9.2 °C.
  • Perth gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 777 mm against 693 mm.
  • Perth gets about 85 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,908 h against 2,823 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
