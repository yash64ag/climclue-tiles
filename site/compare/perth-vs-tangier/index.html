Perth vs Tangier climate

How the climate of Perth, Australia compares with Tangier, Morocco, 14,691 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Perth is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 18.6 °C against 18.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Perth is 11.4 °C warmer (Perth 24.3 °C, Tangier 13.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Perth 31.1 °C (February), Tangier 28.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Perth 8.8 °C, Tangier 9.6 °C.
  • Perth gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 780 mm against 655 mm.
  • Perth gets about 85 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,908 h against 2,823 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
