Main differences

  • Jinhua, Zhejiang is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 18.4 °C against 17.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Jinhua, Zhejiang is 0.9 °C warmer (Jinhua, Zhejiang 6.1 °C, Puyang, Zhejiang 5.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinhua, Zhejiang 34.7 °C (July), Puyang, Zhejiang 34.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinhua, Zhejiang 2.0 °C, Puyang, Zhejiang 1.2 °C.
  • Puyang, Zhejiang gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,592 mm against 1,534 mm.
  • Jinhua, Zhejiang gets about 17 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,885 h against 1,868 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinhua, Zhejiang 12 cm, Puyang, Zhejiang 26 cm (ERA5-Land).
