Callao vs Trujillo climate

How the climate of Callao, Peru compares with Trujillo, Peru, 484 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Trujillo is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 19.7 °C against 18.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Trujillo is 1.7 °C warmer (Callao 16.9 °C, Trujillo 18.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Callao 26.2 °C (February), Trujillo 27.6 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Callao 12.8 °C, Trujillo 13.9 °C.
  • Trujillo gets 263% more rain and snow a year: 76 mm against 21 mm.
  • Trujillo gets about 427 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,933 h against 1,505 h; about 1961–1990).
