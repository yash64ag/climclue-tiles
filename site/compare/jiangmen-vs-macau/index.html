How the climate of Jiangmen, China compares with Macau, Macao: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jiangmen and Macau have the same yearly average temperature (23.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Macau is 1.0 °C warmer (Jiangmen 14.8 °C, Macau 15.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiangmen 32.7 °C (July), Macau 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jiangmen 11.2 °C, Macau 12.6 °C.
  • Macau gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 2,195 mm against 2,139 mm.
  • Macau gets about 71 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,945 h against 1,875 h; about 1961–1990).
