How the climate of Jiaxing, China compares with Shaoxing, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Shaoxing is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 17.5 °C against 16.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Shaoxing is 1.2 °C warmer (Jiaxing 15.6 °C, Shaoxing 16.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiaxing 32.6 °C (July), Shaoxing 34.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jiaxing 0.8 °C, Shaoxing 1.2 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,455 mm and 1,452 mm).
  • Jiaxing gets about 46 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,922 h against 1,876 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jiaxing 13 cm, Shaoxing 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
