Jiaxing vs Shaoxing climate

How the climate of Jiaxing, China compares with Shaoxing, China, 85 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shaoxing is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 17.6 °C against 16.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Shaoxing is 1.2 °C warmer (Jiaxing 15.6 °C, Shaoxing 16.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiaxing 32.1 °C (July), Shaoxing 33.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jiaxing 0.3 °C, Shaoxing 0.6 °C.
  • Jiaxing gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,447 mm against 1,422 mm.
  • Jiaxing gets about 46 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,922 h against 1,876 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jiaxing 13 cm, Shaoxing 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
