How the climate of Amarillo, United States compares with Xuchang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Xuchang is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.3 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Xuchang is 3.1 °C warmer (Amarillo 18.8 °C, Xuchang 21.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amarillo 33.1 °C (July), Xuchang 32.6 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Amarillo -4.8 °C, Xuchang -3.8 °C.
  • Xuchang gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 762 mm against 550 mm.
  • Amarillo gets about 1,050 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 2,193 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amarillo 33 cm, Xuchang 18 cm (ERA5-Land).
