Main differences

  • Chaeryŏng-ni is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 11.4 °C against 11.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Chaeryŏng-ni is 2.1 °C warmer (Chaeryŏng-ni 10.1 °C, Dalian 8.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chaeryŏng-ni 28.5 °C (August), Dalian 27.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Chaeryŏng-ni -7.9 °C, Dalian -5.5 °C.
  • Chaeryŏng-ni gets 36% more rain and snow a year: 812 mm against 597 mm.
  • Dalian gets about 206 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,662 h against 2,457 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chaeryŏng-ni 26 cm, Dalian 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
