Bhopal vs Nagpur climate

How the climate of Bhopal, India compares with Nagpur, India, 292 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Nagpur is 1.5 °C (2.6 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 25.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Nagpur is 2.9 °C warmer (Bhopal 20.8 °C, Nagpur 23.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bhopal 39.9 °C (May), Nagpur 41.3 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Bhopal 11.7 °C, Nagpur 14.0 °C.
  • Nagpur gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,283 mm against 1,184 mm.
  • Bhopal gets about 34 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,798 h against 2,764 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Nagpur in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Bhopal in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Bhopal in 7 of 12 months (1 equal)
