Milan vs Rome climate

How the climate of Milan, Italy compares with Rome, Italy, 479 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rome is 2.3 °C (4.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 13.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Rome is 5.3 °C warmer (Milan 3.6 °C, Rome 8.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Milan 29.3 °C (July), Rome 29.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Milan -0.9 °C, Rome 3.7 °C.
  • Milan gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 985 mm against 836 mm.
  • Rome gets about 491 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,424 h against 1,933 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Milan 28 cm, Rome 0.7 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Rome in 12 of 12 months
