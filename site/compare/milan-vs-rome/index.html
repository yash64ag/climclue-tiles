Milan vs Rome climate

How the climate of Milan, Italy compares with Rome, Italy, 479 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rome is 2.4 °C (4.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 13.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Rome is 5.2 °C warmer (Milan 3.6 °C, Rome 8.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Milan 30.4 °C (July), Rome 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Milan -0.6 °C, Rome 2.7 °C.
  • Milan gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 981 mm against 770 mm.
  • Rome gets about 491 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,424 h against 1,933 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Milan 28 cm, Rome 0.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
