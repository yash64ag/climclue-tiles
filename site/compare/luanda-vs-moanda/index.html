How the climate of Luanda, Angola compares with Moanda, Democratic Republic of the Congo: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Luanda is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 25.9 °C against 24.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Luanda is 2.3 °C warmer (Luanda 28.8 °C, Moanda 26.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Luanda 30.9 °C (February), Moanda 30.5 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Luanda 19.3 °C, Moanda 17.7 °C.
  • Moanda gets 103% more rain and snow a year: 889 mm against 437 mm.
  • Luanda gets about 679 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,306 h against 1,628 h; about 1961–1990).
