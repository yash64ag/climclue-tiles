Gujranwala vs Lahore climate

How the climate of Gujranwala, Pakistan compares with Lahore, Pakistan, 68 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lahore is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 23.9 °C against 23.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Lahore is 0.7 °C warmer (Gujranwala 26.1 °C, Lahore 26.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gujranwala 39.1 °C (June), Lahore 39.1 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Gujranwala 5.8 °C, Lahore 6.0 °C.
  • Gujranwala gets 37% more rain and snow a year: 937 mm against 684 mm.
  • Gujranwala gets about 32 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,969 h against 2,937 h; about 1961–1990).
