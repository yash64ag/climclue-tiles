How the climate of Basrah, Iraq compares with Shiraz, Iran: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Basrah is 9.2 °C (16.5 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 17.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Basrah is 11.0 °C warmer (Basrah 33.4 °C, Shiraz 22.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Basrah 47.2 °C (August), Shiraz 38.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Basrah 6.9 °C, Shiraz -0.5 °C.
  • Shiraz gets 181% more rain and snow a year: 423 mm against 151 mm.
  • Shiraz gets about 244 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,282 h against 3,037 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Basrah 0 cm, Shiraz 36 cm (ERA5-Land).
