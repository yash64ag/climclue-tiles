Lạng Sơn vs Xiamen climate

How the climate of Lạng Sơn, Vietnam compares with Xiamen, China, 1,193 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Lạng Sơn is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 21.6 °C against 21.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Lạng Sơn is 2.4 °C warmer (Lạng Sơn 22.4 °C, Xiamen 20.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lạng Sơn 29.9 °C (July), Xiamen 30.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lạng Sơn 9.7 °C, Xiamen 9.6 °C.
  • Lạng Sơn gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,508 mm against 1,395 mm.
  • Xiamen gets about 449 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,067 h against 1,618 h; about 1961–1990).
