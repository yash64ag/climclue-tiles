How the climate of Habikino, Japan compares with Wuxi, Jiangsu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Habikino and Wuxi, Jiangsu have the same yearly average temperature (16.5 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Habikino is 1.6 °C warmer (Habikino 5.4 °C, Wuxi, Jiangsu 3.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Habikino 33.0 °C (August), Wuxi, Jiangsu 32.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Habikino 1.5 °C, Wuxi, Jiangsu -0.1 °C.
  • Habikino gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,371 mm against 1,210 mm.
  • Wuxi, Jiangsu gets about 134 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,999 h against 1,864 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Habikino 6.6 cm, Wuxi, Jiangsu 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
