How the climate of Jeddah, Saudi Arabia compares with Sanaa, Yemen: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jeddah is 9.9 °C (17.9 °F) warmer over the year: 28.1 °C against 18.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Jeddah is 12.6 °C warmer (Jeddah 29.8 °C, Sanaa 17.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jeddah 38.0 °C (July), Sanaa 28.7 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Jeddah 17.9 °C, Sanaa 6.2 °C.
  • Sanaa gets 235% more rain and snow a year: 231 mm against 69 mm.
  • Jeddah gets about 402 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,317 h against 2,915 h; about 1961–1990).
