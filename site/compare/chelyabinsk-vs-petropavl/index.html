Chelyabinsk vs Petropavl climate

How the climate of Chelyabinsk, Russia compares with Petropavl, Kazakhstan, 493 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Chelyabinsk is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 3.1 °C against 2.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Chelyabinsk is 1.5 °C warmer (Chelyabinsk -13.8 °C, Petropavl -15.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chelyabinsk 25.1 °C (July), Petropavl 25.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chelyabinsk -18.1 °C, Petropavl -19.7 °C.
  • Chelyabinsk gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 460 mm against 399 mm.
  • Petropavl gets about 78 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,162 h against 2,084 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chelyabinsk 123 cm, Petropavl 122 cm (ERA5-Land).
