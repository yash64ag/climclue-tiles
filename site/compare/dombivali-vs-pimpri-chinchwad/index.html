Dombivali vs Pimpri-Chinchwad climate

How the climate of Dombivali, India compares with Pimpri-Chinchwad, India, 101 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Dombivali is 2.3 °C (4.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 24.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Dombivali is 3.2 °C warmer (Dombivali 26.3 °C, Pimpri-Chinchwad 23.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dombivali 34.9 °C (April), Pimpri-Chinchwad 36.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Dombivali 17.4 °C, Pimpri-Chinchwad 14.7 °C.
  • Dombivali gets 102% more rain and snow a year: 2,138 mm against 1,061 mm.
  • Pimpri-Chinchwad gets about 89 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,789 h against 2,700 h; about 1961–1990).
