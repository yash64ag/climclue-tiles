How the climate of Abuja, Nigeria compares with Kano, Nigeria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Abuja is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 26.2 °C against 25.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Abuja is 6.4 °C warmer (Abuja 27.0 °C, Kano 20.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abuja 35.7 °C (February), Kano 38.1 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Abuja 19.3 °C, Kano 12.5 °C.
  • Abuja gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 1,467 mm against 1,095 mm.
  • Kano gets about 452 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,924 h against 2,472 h; about 1961–1990).
