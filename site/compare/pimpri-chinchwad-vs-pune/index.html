Main differences

  • Pune is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 24.6 °C against 24.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Pune is 0.3 °C warmer (Pimpri-Chinchwad 26.1 °C, Pune 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pimpri-Chinchwad 36.6 °C (April), Pune 37.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Pimpri-Chinchwad 12.1 °C, Pune 12.0 °C.
  • Pimpri-Chinchwad gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,135 mm against 999 mm.
  • Pune gets about 4 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,793 h against 2,789 h; about 1961–1990).
