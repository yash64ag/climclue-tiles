How the climate of Bijie, China compares with Guiyang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Guiyang is 1.0 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 15.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Guiyang is 1.2 °C warmer (Bijie 15.5 °C, Guiyang 16.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bijie 28.0 °C (July), Guiyang 28.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bijie 1.7 °C, Guiyang 2.5 °C.
  • Guiyang gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,011 mm against 973 mm.
  • Bijie gets about 207 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,514 h against 1,307 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bijie 31 cm, Guiyang 4.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
