Bijie vs Guiyang climate

How the climate of Bijie, China compares with Guiyang, China, 163 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Guiyang is 3.4 °C (6.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.6 °C against 12.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Guiyang is 3.7 °C warmer (Bijie 12.7 °C, Guiyang 16.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bijie 24.1 °C (July), Guiyang 26.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bijie -1.5 °C, Guiyang 1.5 °C.
  • Bijie gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,072 mm against 1,065 mm.
  • Bijie gets about 207 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,514 h against 1,307 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bijie 97 cm, Guiyang 8.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
