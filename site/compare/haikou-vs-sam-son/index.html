Main differences

  • Haikou, Hainan is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 24.9 °C against 24.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Haikou, Hainan is 1.5 °C warmer (Haikou, Hainan 22.4 °C, Sầm Sơn 20.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Haikou, Hainan 33.0 °C (July), Sầm Sơn 33.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Haikou, Hainan 15.8 °C, Sầm Sơn 14.1 °C.

  • Haikou, Hainan gets about 202 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,092 h against 1,890 h; about 1961–1990).
