Haikou, Hainan vs Sầm Sơn climate

How the climate of Haikou, Hainan, China compares with Sầm Sơn, Vietnam, 466 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Haikou, Hainan and Sầm Sơn have the same yearly average temperature (24.3 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Sầm Sơn is 1.2 °C warmer (Haikou, Hainan 28.3 °C, Sầm Sơn 29.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Haikou, Hainan 32.4 °C (July), Sầm Sơn 33.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Haikou, Hainan 15.3 °C, Sầm Sơn 14.1 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,800 mm and 1,799 mm).
  • Haikou, Hainan gets about 202 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,092 h against 1,890 h; about 1961–1990).
