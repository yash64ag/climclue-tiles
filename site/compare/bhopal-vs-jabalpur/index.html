Bhopal vs Jabalpur climate

How the climate of Bhopal, India compares with Jabalpur, India, 260 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bhopal and Jabalpur have the same yearly average temperature (25.4 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Jabalpur is 0.7 °C warmer (Bhopal 25.6 °C, Jabalpur 26.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bhopal 39.9 °C (May), Jabalpur 40.4 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Bhopal 11.7 °C, Jabalpur 11.3 °C.
  • Jabalpur gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,351 mm against 1,184 mm.
  • Bhopal gets about 82 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,798 h against 2,716 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Bhopal in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Bhopal in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Bhopal in 10 of 12 months
