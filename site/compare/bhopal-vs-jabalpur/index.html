How the climate of Bhopal, India compares with Jabalpur, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bhopal and Jabalpur have the same yearly average temperature (25.4 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Jabalpur is 0.7 °C warmer (Bhopal 25.5 °C, Jabalpur 26.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bhopal 41.2 °C (May), Jabalpur 41.5 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Bhopal 10.4 °C, Jabalpur 9.5 °C.
  • Jabalpur gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,370 mm against 1,214 mm.
  • Bhopal gets about 82 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,798 h against 2,716 h; about 1961–1990).
