Erbil vs Sadr City climate

How the climate of Erbil, Iraq compares with Sadr City, Iraq, 314 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Sadr City is 5.1 °C (9.1 °F) warmer over the year: 25.3 °C against 20.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Sadr City is 6.6 °C warmer (Erbil 24.4 °C, Sadr City 31.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Erbil 41.4 °C (July), Sadr City 44.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Erbil 1.4 °C, Sadr City 5.7 °C.
  • Erbil gets 274% more rain and snow a year: 625 mm against 167 mm.
  • Sadr City gets about 289 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,480 h against 3,190 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Erbil 4.6 cm, Sadr City 0 cm (ERA5-Land).
