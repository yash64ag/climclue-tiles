Oklahoma City vs Xiangyang climate

How the climate of Oklahoma City, United States compares with Xiangyang, China, 11,896 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Xiangyang is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 15.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Xiangyang is 1.6 °C warmer (Oklahoma City 20.6 °C, Xiangyang 22.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Oklahoma City 34.4 °C (July), Xiangyang 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Oklahoma City -2.8 °C, Xiangyang -1.3 °C.
  • Xiangyang gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 942 mm against 906 mm.
  • Oklahoma City gets about 1,167 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,031 h against 1,864 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Oklahoma City 17 cm, Xiangyang 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
