Adelaide vs Cape Town climate

How the climate of Adelaide, Australia compares with Cape Town, South Africa, 10,150 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Adelaide and Cape Town have the same yearly average temperature (16.2 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Cape Town is 2.1 °C warmer (Adelaide 11.1 °C, Cape Town 13.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Adelaide 27.9 °C (February), Cape Town 24.6 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Adelaide 7.1 °C, Cape Town 8.8 °C.
  • Cape Town gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 948 mm against 705 mm.
  • Cape Town gets about 276 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,958 h against 2,682 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Adelaide in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Cape Town in 12 of 12 months
