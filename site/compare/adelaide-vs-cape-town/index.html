How the climate of Adelaide, Australia compares with Cape Town, South Africa: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Adelaide is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 16.7 °C against 16.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Adelaide is 3.1 °C warmer (Adelaide 22.5 °C, Cape Town 19.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Adelaide 28.9 °C (February), Cape Town 24.6 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Adelaide 6.9 °C, Cape Town 8.8 °C.
  • Cape Town gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 948 mm against 705 mm.
  • Cape Town gets about 276 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,958 h against 2,682 h; about 1961–1990).
