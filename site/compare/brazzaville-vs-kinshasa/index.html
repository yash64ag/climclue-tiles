Brazzaville vs Kinshasa climate

How the climate of Brazzaville, Republic of the Congo compares with Kinshasa, Democratic Republic of the Congo, 8 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kinshasa is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 25.6 °C against 25.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Kinshasa is 0.4 °C warmer (Brazzaville 25.6 °C, Kinshasa 26.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brazzaville 30.9 °C (September), Kinshasa 31.3 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Brazzaville 19.2 °C, Kinshasa 19.6 °C.
  • Brazzaville gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,557 mm against 1,458 mm.
  • Brazzaville gets about 118 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,918 h against 1,801 h; about 1961–1990).
