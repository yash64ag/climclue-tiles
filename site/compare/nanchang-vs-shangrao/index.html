Nanchang vs Shangrao climate

How the climate of Nanchang, China compares with Shangrao, China, 206 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shangrao is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 19.0 °C against 18.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Shangrao is 1.1 °C warmer (Nanchang 5.8 °C, Shangrao 6.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nanchang 34.1 °C (July), Shangrao 34.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nanchang 2.4 °C, Shangrao 2.4 °C.
  • Shangrao gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 2,027 mm against 1,796 mm.
  • Shangrao gets about 39 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,878 h against 1,839 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nanchang 6.5 cm, Shangrao 6.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
