Harare vs Lilongwe climate

How the climate of Harare, Zimbabwe compares with Lilongwe, Malawi, 519 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lilongwe is 2.5 °C (4.5 °F) warmer over the year: 20.9 °C against 18.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Lilongwe is 3.4 °C warmer (Harare 13.9 °C, Lilongwe 17.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Harare 28.6 °C (October), Lilongwe 30.6 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Harare 5.6 °C, Lilongwe 9.5 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (800 mm and 799 mm).
  • Harare gets about 190 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,944 h against 2,754 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Lilongwe in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Harare in 10 of 12 months
