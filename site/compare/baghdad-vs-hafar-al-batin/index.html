Baghdad vs Hafar Al-Batin climate

How the climate of Baghdad, Iraq compares with Hafar Al-Batin, Saudi Arabia, 566 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Baghdad is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 25.2 °C against 24.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Baghdad is 1.9 °C warmer (Baghdad 38.1 °C, Hafar Al-Batin 36.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baghdad 47.1 °C (July), Hafar Al-Batin 44.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baghdad 5.3 °C, Hafar Al-Batin 5.3 °C.
  • Baghdad gets 41% more rain and snow a year: 160 mm against 113 mm.
  • Baghdad gets about 11 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,480 h against 3,469 h; about 1961–1990).
