Baghdad vs Hafar Al-Batin climate

How the climate of Baghdad, Iraq compares with Hafar Al-Batin, Saudi Arabia, 566 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Baghdad is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 25.3 °C against 24.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Baghdad is 2.1 °C warmer (Baghdad 38.1 °C, Hafar Al-Batin 36.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baghdad 44.6 °C (July), Hafar Al-Batin 43.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Baghdad 5.7 °C, Hafar Al-Batin 4.9 °C.
  • Baghdad gets 40% more rain and snow a year: 164 mm against 117 mm.
  • Baghdad gets about 11 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,480 h against 3,469 h; about 1961–1990).
