La Plata vs Montevideo climate

How the climate of La Plata, Argentina compares with Montevideo, Uruguay, 161 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • La Plata is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 16.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when La Plata is 1.5 °C warmer (La Plata 23.4 °C, Montevideo 21.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: La Plata 27.8 °C (January), Montevideo 24.6 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: La Plata 7.1 °C, Montevideo 8.8 °C.
  • La Plata gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,115 mm against 1,079 mm.
  • Montevideo gets about 166 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,486 h against 2,319 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: La Plata in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Montevideo in 10 of 12 months
