Lilongwe vs Zapopan climate

How the climate of Lilongwe, Malawi compares with Zapopan, Mexico, 15,421 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zapopan is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 21.1 °C against 20.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Zapopan is 6.9 °C warmer (Lilongwe 17.1 °C, Zapopan 24.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lilongwe 29.4 °C (November), Zapopan 32.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Lilongwe 10.9 °C, Zapopan 10.1 °C.
  • Zapopan gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 864 mm against 810 mm.
  • Lilongwe gets about 107 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,754 h against 2,647 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
