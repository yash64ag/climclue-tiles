How the climate of Lilongwe, Malawi compares with Zapopan, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lilongwe is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 20.9 °C against 20.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Lilongwe is 6.0 °C warmer (Lilongwe 22.9 °C, Zapopan 16.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lilongwe 30.6 °C (October), Zapopan 33.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Lilongwe 9.5 °C, Zapopan 7.9 °C.
  • Zapopan gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 901 mm against 799 mm.
  • Lilongwe gets about 107 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,754 h against 2,647 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
