Guangzhou vs Shaoguan climate

How the climate of Guangzhou, China compares with Shaoguan, China, 190 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Guangzhou is 1.9 °C (3.4 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 21.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Guangzhou is 3.9 °C warmer (Guangzhou 14.6 °C, Shaoguan 10.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guangzhou 33.2 °C (July), Shaoguan 34.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Guangzhou 10.3 °C, Shaoguan 6.3 °C.
  • Guangzhou gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 2,053 mm against 1,701 mm.
  • Guangzhou gets about 52 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,800 h against 1,749 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Guangzhou in 10 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Guangzhou in 7 of 12 months
