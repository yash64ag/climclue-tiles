Córdoba vs Santiago climate

How the climate of Córdoba, Argentina compares with Santiago, Chile, 648 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Córdoba is 1.6 °C (2.9 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Córdoba is 3.0 °C warmer (Córdoba 18.6 °C, Santiago 15.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Córdoba 30.2 °C (January), Santiago 28.3 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Córdoba 3.1 °C, Santiago 3.7 °C.
  • Córdoba gets 42% more rain and snow a year: 878 mm against 618 mm.
  • Córdoba gets about 54 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,592 h against 2,538 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Córdoba in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Santiago in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
