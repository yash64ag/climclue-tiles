How the climate of Bamako, Mali compares with Camayenne, Guinea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bamako is 1.7 °C (3.1 °F) warmer over the year: 28.0 °C against 26.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Bamako is 6.5 °C warmer (Bamako 32.8 °C, Camayenne 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bamako 39.6 °C (April), Camayenne 32.0 °C (December). Coldest-month night lows: Bamako 16.8 °C, Camayenne 20.9 °C.
  • Camayenne gets 309% more rain and snow a year: 3,521 mm against 861 mm.
  • Bamako gets about 748 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,933 h against 2,185 h; about 1961–1990).
