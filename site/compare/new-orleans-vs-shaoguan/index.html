New Orleans vs Shaoguan climate

How the climate of New Orleans, United States compares with Shaoguan, China, 13,424 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shaoguan is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 21.1 °C against 20.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Shaoguan is 1.9 °C warmer (New Orleans 21.4 °C, Shaoguan 23.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: New Orleans 31.0 °C (July), Shaoguan 32.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: New Orleans 9.2 °C, Shaoguan 5.9 °C.
  • New Orleans gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 2,005 mm against 1,701 mm.
  • New Orleans gets about 900 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,648 h against 1,749 h; about 1961–1990).
