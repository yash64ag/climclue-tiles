Weifang vs Yishui climate

How the climate of Weifang, China compares with Yishui, China, 111 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Weifang is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 13.5 °C against 12.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Weifang is 1.1 °C warmer (Weifang 24.4 °C, Yishui 23.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Weifang 31.0 °C (July), Yishui 29.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Weifang -6.8 °C, Yishui -6.4 °C.
  • Yishui gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 688 mm against 564 mm.
  • Weifang gets about 89 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,525 h against 2,437 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Weifang 12 cm, Yishui 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
