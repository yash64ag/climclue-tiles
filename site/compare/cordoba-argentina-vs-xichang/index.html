Córdoba vs Xichang, Sichuan climate

How the climate of Córdoba, Argentina compares with Xichang, Sichuan, China, 18,650 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Córdoba is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 17.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Córdoba is 14.6 °C warmer (Córdoba 23.7 °C, Xichang, Sichuan 9.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Córdoba 30.2 °C (January), Xichang, Sichuan 27.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Córdoba 3.1 °C, Xichang, Sichuan 2.6 °C.
  • Xichang, Sichuan gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 1,090 mm against 878 mm.
  • Córdoba gets about 300 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,592 h against 2,292 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
