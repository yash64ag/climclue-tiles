Batam vs Kuala Lumpur climate

How the climate of Batam, Indonesia compares with Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, 341 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Batam is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Batam is 0.6 °C warmer (Batam 27.8 °C, Kuala Lumpur 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Batam 28.6 °C (May), Kuala Lumpur 30.6 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Batam 25.4 °C, Kuala Lumpur 21.7 °C.
  • Kuala Lumpur gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 2,938 mm against 2,597 mm.
  • Kuala Lumpur gets about 177 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,214 h against 2,037 h; about 1961–1990).
