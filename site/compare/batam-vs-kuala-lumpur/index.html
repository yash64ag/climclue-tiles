How the climate of Batam, Indonesia compares with Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Batam is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Batam is 0.7 °C warmer (Batam 27.8 °C, Kuala Lumpur 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Batam 31.6 °C (May), Kuala Lumpur 32.0 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Batam 22.1 °C, Kuala Lumpur 21.4 °C.
  • Kuala Lumpur gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 2,940 mm against 2,545 mm.
  • Kuala Lumpur gets about 177 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,214 h against 2,037 h; about 1961–1990).
