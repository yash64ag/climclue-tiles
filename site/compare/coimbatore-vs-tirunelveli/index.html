Coimbatore vs Tirunelveli climate

How the climate of Coimbatore, India compares with Tirunelveli, India, 265 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tirunelveli is 3.0 °C (5.3 °F) warmer over the year: 28.7 °C against 25.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Tirunelveli is 4.5 °C warmer (Coimbatore 24.9 °C, Tirunelveli 29.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Coimbatore 35.0 °C (April), Tirunelveli 36.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Coimbatore 18.3 °C, Tirunelveli 20.6 °C.
  • Tirunelveli gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 842 mm against 820 mm.
  • Coimbatore gets about 4 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,406 h against 2,401 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Tirunelveli in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Coimbatore in 8 of 12 months
