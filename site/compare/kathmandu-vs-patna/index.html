Kathmandu vs Patna climate

How the climate of Kathmandu, Nepal compares with Patna, India, 235 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Patna is 8.1 °C (14.5 °F) warmer over the year: 25.7 °C against 17.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Patna is 11.5 °C warmer (Kathmandu 19.1 °C, Patna 30.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kathmandu 25.4 °C (June), Patna 38.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kathmandu 5.0 °C, Patna 10.4 °C.
  • Kathmandu gets 96% more rain and snow a year: 1,984 mm against 1,013 mm.
  • Patna gets about 242 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,233 h against 1,990 h; about 1961–1990).
