How the climate of Kathmandu, Nepal compares with Patna, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Patna is 7.1 °C (12.7 °F) warmer over the year: 25.7 °C against 18.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Patna is 10.7 °C warmer (Kathmandu 19.8 °C, Patna 30.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kathmandu 28.8 °C (May), Patna 38.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kathmandu 2.4 °C, Patna 9.0 °C.
  • Kathmandu gets 60% more rain and snow a year: 1,634 mm against 1,023 mm.
  • Patna gets about 242 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,233 h against 1,990 h; about 1961–1990).
