Brussels vs Paris climate

How the climate of Brussels, Belgium compares with Paris, France, 264 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Paris is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 11.3 °C against 10.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Paris is 1.1 °C warmer (Brussels 17.9 °C, Paris 19.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brussels 23.2 °C (July), Paris 24.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Brussels 0.7 °C, Paris 1.6 °C.
  • Brussels gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 851 mm against 658 mm.
  • Paris gets about 158 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,744 h against 1,587 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Brussels 15 cm, Paris 9.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
