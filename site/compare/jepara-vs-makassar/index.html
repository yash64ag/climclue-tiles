Jepara vs Makassar climate

How the climate of Jepara, Indonesia compares with Makassar, Indonesia, 982 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jepara is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Jepara is 0.3 °C warmer (Jepara 27.3 °C, Makassar 27.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jepara 33.5 °C (September), Makassar 32.3 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Jepara 21.3 °C, Makassar 21.7 °C.
  • Makassar gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 3,326 mm against 2,502 mm.
  • Makassar gets about 90 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,852 h against 2,762 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Jepara in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Makassar in 10 of 12 months
