How the climate of Dongguan, China compares with Huizhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Dongguan is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Dongguan is 0.7 °C warmer (Dongguan 14.9 °C, Huizhou 14.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dongguan 33.0 °C (July), Huizhou 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Dongguan 10.4 °C, Huizhou 9.9 °C.
  • Dongguan gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 2,151 mm against 1,962 mm.
  • Huizhou gets about 47 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,898 h against 1,850 h; about 1961–1990).
