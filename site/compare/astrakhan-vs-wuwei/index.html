Astrakhan vs Wuwei climate

How the climate of Astrakhan, Russia compares with Wuwei, China, 4,505 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Astrakhan is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 10.5 °C against 9.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Astrakhan is 3.2 °C warmer (Astrakhan 25.6 °C, Wuwei 22.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Astrakhan 31.2 °C (July), Wuwei 28.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Astrakhan -6.6 °C, Wuwei -12.3 °C.
  • Astrakhan gets 46% more rain and snow a year: 236 mm against 161 mm.
  • Wuwei gets about 463 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,814 h against 2,350 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Astrakhan 39 cm, Wuwei 9.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
