Agadez vs Khartoum North climate

How the climate of Agadez, Niger compares with Khartoum North, Sudan, 2,622 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Khartoum North is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 29.5 °C against 29.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Khartoum North is 2.1 °C warmer (Agadez 25.4 °C, Khartoum North 27.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Agadez 40.9 °C (May), Khartoum North 40.5 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Agadez 12.9 °C, Khartoum North 14.9 °C.
  • Agadez gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 127 mm against 102 mm.
  • Khartoum North gets about 329 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,517 h against 3,188 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Agadez in 6 of 12 months (2 equal)
  • Sunnier: Khartoum North in 11 of 12 months
