How the climate of Jiading, China compares with Ōnojō, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jiading is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 17.0 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Jiading is 1.7 °C warmer (Jiading 24.6 °C, Ōnojō 22.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiading 32.2 °C (July), Ōnojō 32.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Jiading 1.4 °C, Ōnojō 2.6 °C.
  • Ōnojō gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 1,658 mm against 1,422 mm.
  • Jiading gets about 193 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,979 h against 1,786 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jiading 9.5 cm, Ōnojō 6.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
