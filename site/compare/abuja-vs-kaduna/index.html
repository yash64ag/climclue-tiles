Abuja vs Kaduna climate

How the climate of Abuja, Nigeria compares with Kaduna, Nigeria, 163 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Abuja is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.2 °C against 25.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Abuja is 3.9 °C warmer (Abuja 27.0 °C, Kaduna 23.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abuja 35.7 °C (February), Kaduna 35.5 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Abuja 19.3 °C, Kaduna 15.1 °C.
  • Abuja gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 1,467 mm against 1,204 mm.
  • Kaduna gets about 212 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,685 h against 2,472 h; about 1961–1990).
