How the climate of Manhattan, New York, United States compares with Philadelphia, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Philadelphia is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 13.3 °C against 12.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Philadelphia is 1.1 °C warmer (Manhattan, New York 5.4 °C, Philadelphia 6.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manhattan, New York 29.1 °C (July), Philadelphia 30.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Manhattan, New York -3.4 °C, Philadelphia -3.5 °C.
  • Manhattan, New York gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,232 mm against 1,220 mm.
  • Manhattan, New York gets about 66 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,529 h against 2,463 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Manhattan, New York 59 cm, Philadelphia 47 cm (ERA5-Land).
