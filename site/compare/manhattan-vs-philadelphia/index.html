Manhattan vs Philadelphia climate

How the climate of Manhattan, United States compares with Philadelphia, United States, 137 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Philadelphia is 0.9 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 13.1 °C against 12.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Philadelphia is 1.6 °C warmer (Manhattan 4.8 °C, Philadelphia 6.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manhattan 28.2 °C (July), Philadelphia 29.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Manhattan -3.9 °C, Philadelphia -3.3 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,212 mm and 1,215 mm).
  • Manhattan gets about 66 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,529 h against 2,463 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Manhattan 81 cm, Philadelphia 66 cm (ERA5-Land).
