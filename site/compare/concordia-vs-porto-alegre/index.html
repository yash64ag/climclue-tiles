Concordia vs Porto Alegre climate

How the climate of Concordia, Argentina compares with Porto Alegre, Brazil, 666 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Porto Alegre is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 19.6 °C against 18.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Porto Alegre is 2.0 °C warmer (Concordia 12.1 °C, Porto Alegre 14.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Concordia 30.9 °C (January), Porto Alegre 29.3 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Concordia 8.5 °C, Porto Alegre 10.3 °C.
  • Porto Alegre gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,428 mm against 1,329 mm.
  • Concordia gets about 368 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,585 h against 2,217 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Porto Alegre in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Concordia in 11 of 12 months
