Belém vs Manaus climate

How the climate of Belém, Brazil compares with Manaus, Brazil, 1,293 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Belém is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Belém is 0.6 °C warmer (Belém 27.3 °C, Manaus 26.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belém 31.5 °C (October), Manaus 30.8 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Belém 23.9 °C, Manaus 23.4 °C.
  • Belém gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 2,804 mm against 2,429 mm.
  • Belém gets about 443 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,210 h against 1,767 h; about 1961–1990).
