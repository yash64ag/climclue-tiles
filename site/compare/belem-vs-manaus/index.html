How the climate of Belém, Pará, Brazil compares with Manaus, Brazil: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Belém, Pará is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Belém, Pará is 0.6 °C warmer (Belém, Pará 27.3 °C, Manaus 26.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belém, Pará 32.9 °C (October), Manaus 32.3 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Belém, Pará 22.0 °C, Manaus 22.2 °C.
  • Belém, Pará gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 2,804 mm against 2,429 mm.
  • Belém, Pará gets about 443 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,210 h against 1,767 h; about 1961–1990).
