Kakamega vs Nairobi climate

How the climate of Kakamega, Kenya compares with Nairobi, Kenya, 288 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kakamega is 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) warmer over the year: 20.4 °C against 18.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Kakamega is 2.4 °C warmer (Kakamega 19.6 °C, Nairobi 17.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kakamega 27.8 °C (February), Nairobi 26.4 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Kakamega 14.7 °C, Nairobi 12.2 °C.
  • Kakamega gets 101% more rain and snow a year: 1,729 mm against 860 mm.
  • Kakamega gets about 291 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,755 h against 2,464 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
