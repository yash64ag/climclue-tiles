How the climate of Kakamega, Kenya compares with Nairobi, Kenya: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kakamega is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 20.2 °C against 18.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Kakamega is 2.2 °C warmer (Kakamega 19.0 °C, Nairobi 16.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kakamega 29.6 °C (February), Nairobi 27.4 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Kakamega 12.3 °C, Nairobi 11.5 °C.
  • Kakamega gets 97% more rain and snow a year: 1,674 mm against 850 mm.
  • Kakamega gets about 291 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,755 h against 2,464 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
