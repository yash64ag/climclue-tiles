Cixi vs Fengxiang climate

How the climate of Cixi, China compares with Fengxiang, China, 79 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Cixi is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 17.4 °C against 16.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Cixi is 1.4 °C warmer (Cixi 15.8 °C, Fengxiang 14.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cixi 32.7 °C (July), Fengxiang 30.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Cixi 1.5 °C, Fengxiang 1.8 °C.
  • Cixi gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,507 mm against 1,406 mm.
  • Fengxiang gets about 54 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,937 h against 1,883 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Cixi 11 cm, Fengxiang 6.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
