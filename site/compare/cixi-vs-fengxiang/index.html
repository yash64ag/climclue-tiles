How the climate of Cixi, China compares with Fengxiang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Cixi is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 17.4 °C against 16.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Cixi is 1.4 °C warmer (Cixi 15.8 °C, Fengxiang 14.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cixi 32.9 °C (July), Fengxiang 31.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Cixi 2.0 °C, Fengxiang 1.6 °C.
  • Cixi gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,491 mm against 1,409 mm.
  • Fengxiang gets about 54 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,937 h against 1,883 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Cixi 8.8 cm, Fengxiang 5.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
