Bandar Lampung vs NIA Valencia climate

How the climate of Bandar Lampung, Indonesia compares with NIA Valencia, Philippines, 2,653 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bandar Lampung and NIA Valencia have the same yearly average temperature (26.2 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when NIA Valencia is 0.8 °C warmer (Bandar Lampung 26.6 °C, NIA Valencia 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bandar Lampung 29.6 °C (October), NIA Valencia 30.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Bandar Lampung 22.7 °C, NIA Valencia 21.4 °C.
  • Bandar Lampung gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 2,377 mm against 2,094 mm.
  • Bandar Lampung gets about 248 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,468 h against 2,220 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

  • Sunnier: Bandar Lampung in 7 of 12 months
