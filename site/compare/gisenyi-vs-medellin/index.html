Gisenyi vs Medellín climate

How the climate of Gisenyi, Rwanda compares with Medellín, Colombia, 11,667 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gisenyi is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 20.6 °C against 20.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Gisenyi is 0.9 °C warmer (Gisenyi 20.9 °C, Medellín 20.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gisenyi 26.6 °C (August), Medellín 25.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Gisenyi 14.1 °C, Medellín 14.7 °C.
  • Gisenyi gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,805 mm against 1,768 mm.
  • Gisenyi gets about 172 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,995 h against 1,823 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
