Maceió vs Salvador climate

Main differences

  • Maceió is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 25.5 °C against 25.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Maceió is 0.5 °C warmer (Maceió 24.2 °C, Salvador 23.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maceió 27.9 °C (March), Salvador 27.5 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Maceió 22.7 °C, Salvador 22.7 °C.
  • Maceió gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,744 mm against 1,717 mm.
  • Maceió gets about 217 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,547 h against 2,331 h; about 1961–1990).
