Main differences

  • Maceió is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 25.4 °C against 25.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Maceió is 0.4 °C warmer (Maceió 24.4 °C, Salvador 24.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maceió 27.9 °C (February), Salvador 27.6 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Maceió 22.5 °C, Salvador 22.6 °C.
  • Maceió gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,798 mm against 1,761 mm.
  • Maceió gets about 217 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,547 h against 2,331 h; about 1961–1990).
