How the climate of Heshan, Hunan, China compares with Iizuka, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Heshan, Hunan is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 17.9 °C against 17.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Heshan, Hunan is 2.7 °C warmer (Heshan, Hunan 22.8 °C, Iizuka 20.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Heshan, Hunan 33.2 °C (July), Iizuka 32.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Heshan, Hunan 2.5 °C, Iizuka 2.1 °C.
  • Iizuka gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,669 mm against 1,590 mm.
  • Iizuka gets about 171 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,759 h against 1,588 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Heshan, Hunan 14 cm, Iizuka 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
