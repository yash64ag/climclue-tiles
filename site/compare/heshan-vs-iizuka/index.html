Heshan vs Iizuka climate

How the climate of Heshan, China compares with Iizuka, Japan, 1,832 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Heshan is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 17.9 °C against 17.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Heshan is 2.7 °C warmer (Heshan 22.8 °C, Iizuka 20.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Heshan 32.2 °C (July), Iizuka 31.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Heshan 1.7 °C, Iizuka 1.6 °C.
  • Iizuka gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,669 mm against 1,590 mm.
  • Iizuka gets about 171 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,759 h against 1,588 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Heshan 14 cm, Iizuka 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
