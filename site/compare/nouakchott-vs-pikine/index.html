Nouakchott vs Pikine climate

How the climate of Nouakchott, Mauritania compares with Pikine, Senegal, 399 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Nouakchott is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 25.4 °C against 24.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Nouakchott is 2.9 °C warmer (Nouakchott 25.9 °C, Pikine 22.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nouakchott 33.9 °C (October), Pikine 29.4 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Nouakchott 16.1 °C, Pikine 19.6 °C.
  • Pikine gets 454% more rain and snow a year: 391 mm against 71 mm.
  • Pikine gets about 69 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,977 h against 2,908 h; about 1961–1990).
