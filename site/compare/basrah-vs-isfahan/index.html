Basrah vs Isfahan climate

How the climate of Basrah, Iraq compares with Isfahan, Iran, 439 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Basrah is 10.4 °C (18.8 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 16.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Basrah is 11.4 °C warmer (Basrah 33.4 °C, Isfahan 21.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Basrah 47.2 °C (August), Isfahan 37.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Basrah 6.9 °C, Isfahan -3.8 °C.
  • Basrah gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 151 mm against 144 mm.
  • Isfahan gets about 147 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,185 h against 3,037 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Basrah 0 cm, Isfahan 19 cm (ERA5-Land).
