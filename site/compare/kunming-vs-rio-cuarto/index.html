How the climate of Kunming, China compares with Río Cuarto, Argentina: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Río Cuarto is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 16.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Río Cuarto is 13.5 °C warmer (Kunming 9.8 °C, Río Cuarto 23.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kunming 24.8 °C (May), Río Cuarto 29.7 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Kunming 3.4 °C, Río Cuarto 2.7 °C.
  • Kunming gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 929 mm against 887 mm.
  • Río Cuarto gets about 520 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,722 h against 2,203 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
