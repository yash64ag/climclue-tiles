Adana vs Córdoba climate

How the climate of Adana, Turkey compares with Córdoba, Spain, 3,514 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Córdoba is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 19.0 °C against 18.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Córdoba is 1.8 °C warmer (Adana 9.7 °C, Córdoba 11.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Adana 34.2 °C (August), Córdoba 38.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Adana 3.7 °C, Córdoba 4.6 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (687 mm and 687 mm).
  • Adana gets about 231 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,992 h against 2,761 h; about 1961–1990).
