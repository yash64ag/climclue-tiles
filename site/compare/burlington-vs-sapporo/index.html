How the climate of Burlington, Canada compares with Sapporo, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Burlington is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 8.5 °C against 8.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Burlington is 2.5 °C warmer (Burlington 18.4 °C, Sapporo 15.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Burlington 27.0 °C (July), Sapporo 25.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Burlington -8.3 °C, Sapporo -8.5 °C.
  • Sapporo gets 48% more rain and snow a year: 1,573 mm against 1,065 mm.
  • Burlington gets about 318 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,995 h against 1,677 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Burlington 122 cm, Sapporo 417 cm (ERA5-Land).
