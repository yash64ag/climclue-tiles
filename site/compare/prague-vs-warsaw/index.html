Prague vs Warsaw climate

How the climate of Prague, Czechia compares with Warsaw, Poland, 517 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Prague is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 9.6 °C against 8.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Prague is 2.1 °C warmer (Prague 4.9 °C, Warsaw 2.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Prague 25.4 °C (July), Warsaw 24.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Prague -3.2 °C, Warsaw -4.4 °C.
  • Warsaw gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 582 mm against 525 mm.
  • Prague gets about 53 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,640 h against 1,587 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Prague 42 cm, Warsaw 61 cm (ERA5-Land).
