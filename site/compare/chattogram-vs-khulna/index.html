Chattogram vs Khulna climate

How the climate of Chattogram, Bangladesh compares with Khulna, Bangladesh, 239 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Khulna is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 25.9 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Khulna is 1.4 °C warmer (Chattogram 27.6 °C, Khulna 29.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chattogram 30.4 °C (May), Khulna 34.1 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Chattogram 16.0 °C, Khulna 13.2 °C.
  • Chattogram gets 67% more rain and snow a year: 3,146 mm against 1,886 mm.
  • Chattogram gets about 101 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,191 h against 2,090 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Khulna in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Chattogram in 9 of 12 months
