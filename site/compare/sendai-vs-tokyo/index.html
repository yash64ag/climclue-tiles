How the climate of Sendai, Miyagi, Japan compares with Tokyo, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tokyo is 2.8 °C (5.1 °F) warmer over the year: 15.3 °C against 12.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Tokyo is 3.4 °C warmer (Sendai, Miyagi 5.0 °C, Tokyo 8.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Sendai, Miyagi 27.7 °C (August), Tokyo 30.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Sendai, Miyagi -1.9 °C, Tokyo -0.2 °C.
  • Tokyo gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,516 mm against 1,487 mm.
  • Tokyo gets about 18 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,837 h against 1,819 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Sendai, Miyagi 75 cm, Tokyo 19 cm (ERA5-Land).
