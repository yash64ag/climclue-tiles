Huizhou vs Ma On Shan climate

How the climate of Huizhou, China compares with Ma On Shan, Hong Kong, 80 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Huizhou is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 22.5 °C against 22.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Ma On Shan is 0.9 °C warmer (Huizhou 14.2 °C, Ma On Shan 15.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huizhou 31.3 °C (August), Ma On Shan 29.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Huizhou 8.9 °C, Ma On Shan 12.0 °C.
  • Ma On Shan gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 2,244 mm against 1,884 mm.
  • Ma On Shan gets about 66 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,964 h against 1,898 h; about 1961–1990).
