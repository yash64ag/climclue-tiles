Longyan vs Xiamen climate

How the climate of Longyan, China compares with Xiamen, China, 126 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Xiamen is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 21.4 °C against 20.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Xiamen is 2.2 °C warmer (Longyan 13.0 °C, Xiamen 15.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Longyan 32.5 °C (July), Xiamen 31.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Longyan 7.5 °C, Xiamen 9.9 °C.
  • Longyan gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 1,570 mm against 1,343 mm.
  • Xiamen gets about 221 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,067 h against 1,846 h; about 1961–1990).
