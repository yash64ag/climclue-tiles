How the climate of Dhanbad, India compares with Patna, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Patna is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 25.7 °C against 24.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Patna is 2.0 °C warmer (Dhanbad 29.8 °C, Patna 31.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dhanbad 37.7 °C (May), Patna 38.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Dhanbad 9.2 °C, Patna 9.0 °C.
  • Dhanbad gets 53% more rain and snow a year: 1,564 mm against 1,023 mm.
  • Patna gets about 47 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,233 h against 2,186 h; about 1961–1990).
