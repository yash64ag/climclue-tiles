How the climate of Heze, China compares with Shangqiu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Shangqiu is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 15.0 °C against 14.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Shangqiu is 0.9 °C warmer (Heze 1.9 °C, Shangqiu 2.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Heze 31.5 °C (July), Shangqiu 31.8 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Heze -4.5 °C, Shangqiu -4.1 °C.
  • Shangqiu gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 724 mm against 647 mm.
  • Heze gets about 82 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,358 h against 2,276 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Heze 11 cm, Shangqiu 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
