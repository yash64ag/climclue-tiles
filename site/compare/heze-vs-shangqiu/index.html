Heze vs Shangqiu climate

How the climate of Heze, China compares with Shangqiu, China, 93 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shangqiu is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 15.0 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Shangqiu is 0.6 °C warmer (Heze 2.0 °C, Shangqiu 2.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Heze 31.3 °C (June), Shangqiu 31.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Heze -4.7 °C, Shangqiu -4.0 °C.
  • Shangqiu gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 719 mm against 642 mm.
  • Heze gets about 82 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,358 h against 2,276 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Heze 11 cm, Shangqiu 15 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Shangqiu in 7 of 12 months (3 equal)
  • Drier: Heze in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Heze in 11 of 12 months
