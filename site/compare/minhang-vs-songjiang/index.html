Minhang vs Songjiang climate

How the climate of Minhang, China compares with Songjiang, China, 17 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Minhang and Songjiang have the same yearly average temperature (16.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Songjiang is 0.1 °C warmer (Minhang 24.1 °C, Songjiang 24.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Minhang 31.6 °C (July), Songjiang 31.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Minhang 0.3 °C, Songjiang 0.0 °C.
  • Minhang gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,461 mm against 1,440 mm.
  • Minhang gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,959 h against 1,952 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Minhang 8.6 cm, Songjiang 9.7 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Songjiang in 5 of 12 months (3 equal)
  • Drier: Songjiang in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Minhang in 11 of 12 months
