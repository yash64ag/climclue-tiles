How the climate of Minhang, China compares with Songjiang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Minhang is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 17.0 °C against 16.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Minhang is 0.3 °C warmer (Minhang 7.3 °C, Songjiang 7.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Minhang 32.3 °C (July), Songjiang 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Minhang 1.5 °C, Songjiang 1.0 °C.
  • Minhang gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,467 mm against 1,427 mm.
  • Minhang gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,959 h against 1,952 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Minhang 8.6 cm, Songjiang 9.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
