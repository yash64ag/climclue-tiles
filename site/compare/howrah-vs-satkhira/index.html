Howrah vs Sātkhira climate

How the climate of Howrah, India compares with Sātkhira, Bangladesh, 79 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Howrah and Sātkhira have the same yearly average temperature (26.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Howrah is 0.5 °C warmer (Howrah 30.1 °C, Sātkhira 29.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Howrah 35.2 °C (April), Sātkhira 34.1 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Howrah 13.0 °C, Sātkhira 13.5 °C.
  • Sātkhira gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,824 mm against 1,804 mm.
  • Sātkhira gets about 6 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,084 h against 2,078 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Sātkhira in 6 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: Howrah in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
