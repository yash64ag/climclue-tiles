Karbala vs Kirkuk climate

How the climate of Karbala, Iraq compares with Kirkuk, Iraq, 319 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Karbala is 2.9 °C (5.3 °F) warmer over the year: 24.8 °C against 21.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Karbala is 4.4 °C warmer (Karbala 24.6 °C, Kirkuk 20.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Karbala 43.5 °C (July), Kirkuk 42.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Karbala 6.0 °C, Kirkuk 3.4 °C.
  • Kirkuk gets 359% more rain and snow a year: 498 mm against 108 mm.
  • Karbala gets about 263 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,581 h against 3,318 h; about 1961–1990).
