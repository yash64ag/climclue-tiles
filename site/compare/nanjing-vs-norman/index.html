Main differences

  • Norman is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 16.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Norman is 1.4 °C warmer (Nanjing 9.6 °C, Norman 10.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nanjing 31.4 °C (July), Norman 33.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nanjing -1.6 °C, Norman 0.0 °C.
  • Nanjing gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 1,180 mm against 979 mm.
  • Norman gets about 948 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,003 h against 2,055 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nanjing 20 cm, Norman 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
