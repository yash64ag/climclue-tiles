Changchun vs Kanggye climate

How the climate of Changchun, China compares with Kanggye, North Korea, 340 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kanggye is 1.9 °C (3.4 °F) warmer over the year: 8.1 °C against 6.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Kanggye is 3.6 °C warmer (Changchun -3.3 °C, Kanggye 0.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changchun 28.0 °C (July), Kanggye 28.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changchun -20.9 °C, Kanggye -18.1 °C.
  • Kanggye gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 787 mm against 635 mm.
  • Changchun gets about 164 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,570 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changchun 33 cm, Kanggye 65 cm (ERA5-Land).
