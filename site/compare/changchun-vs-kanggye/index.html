Changchun vs Kanggye climate

How the climate of Changchun, China compares with Kanggye, North Korea, 340 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kanggye is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 6.9 °C against 6.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Kanggye is 2.5 °C warmer (Changchun -15.2 °C, Kanggye -12.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changchun 27.1 °C (July), Kanggye 26.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Changchun -20.9 °C, Kanggye -22.2 °C.
  • Kanggye gets 44% more rain and snow a year: 885 mm against 616 mm.
  • Changchun gets about 164 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,570 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changchun 33 cm, Kanggye 65 cm (ERA5-Land).
