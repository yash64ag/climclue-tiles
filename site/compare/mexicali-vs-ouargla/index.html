Mexicali vs Ouargla climate

How the climate of Mexicali, Mexico compares with Ouargla, Algeria, 10,520 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Mexicali is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 24.5 °C against 23.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Mexicali is 4.0 °C warmer (Mexicali 15.2 °C, Ouargla 11.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mexicali 43.7 °C (July), Ouargla 43.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Mexicali 6.2 °C, Ouargla 4.5 °C.
  • Mexicali gets 104% more rain and snow a year: 78 mm against 38 mm.
  • Mexicali gets about 197 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,652 h against 3,455 h; about 1961–1990).
