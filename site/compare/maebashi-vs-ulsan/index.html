How the climate of Maebashi, Japan compares with Ulsan, South Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ulsan is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 14.8 °C against 14.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Maebashi is 1.5 °C warmer (Maebashi 6.4 °C, Ulsan 4.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maebashi 30.4 °C (August), Ulsan 30.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Maebashi -1.7 °C, Ulsan -2.2 °C.
  • Maebashi gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,309 mm against 1,186 mm.
  • Ulsan gets about 380 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,290 h against 1,910 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Maebashi 22 cm, Ulsan 6.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
