How the climate of Casablanca, Morocco compares with Fes, Morocco: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Fes is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 18.6 °C against 18.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Fes is 3.9 °C warmer (Casablanca 23.2 °C, Fes 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Casablanca 27.5 °C (August), Fes 34.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Casablanca 8.3 °C, Fes 5.4 °C.
  • Fes gets 50% more rain and snow a year: 546 mm against 363 mm.
  • Casablanca gets about 46 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,895 h against 2,849 h; about 1961–1990).
