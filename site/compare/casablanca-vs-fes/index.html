Casablanca vs Fes climate

How the climate of Casablanca, Morocco compares with Fes, Morocco, 246 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fes is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 19.0 °C against 18.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Fes is 4.1 °C warmer (Casablanca 23.4 °C, Fes 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Casablanca 28.5 °C (August), Fes 34.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Casablanca 8.8 °C, Fes 5.5 °C.
  • Fes gets 51% more rain and snow a year: 551 mm against 365 mm.
  • Casablanca gets about 46 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,895 h against 2,849 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each

  • Sunnier: Casablanca in 9 of 12 months
