Tabriz vs Tehran climate

How the climate of Tabriz, Iran compares with Tehran, Iran, 528 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tehran is 4.5 °C (8.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Tehran is 6.8 °C warmer (Tabriz 21.0 °C, Tehran 27.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Tabriz 31.1 °C (July), Tehran 37.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Tabriz -5.9 °C, Tehran -3.8 °C.
  • Tehran gets 68% more rain and snow a year: 397 mm against 236 mm.
  • Tehran gets about 163 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,870 h against 2,707 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Tabriz 50 cm, Tehran 33 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Tehran in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Tehran in 8 of 12 months
