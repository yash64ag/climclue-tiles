How the climate of Chililabombwe, Zambia compares with Lubumbashi, Democratic Republic of the Congo: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Chililabombwe and Lubumbashi have the same yearly average temperature (21.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Chililabombwe is 0.2 °C warmer (Chililabombwe 23.1 °C, Lubumbashi 22.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chililabombwe 34.0 °C (September), Lubumbashi 32.8 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Chililabombwe 7.0 °C, Lubumbashi 9.0 °C.
  • Chililabombwe gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,262 mm against 1,115 mm.
  • Chililabombwe gets about 21 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,753 h against 2,732 h; about 1961–1990).
