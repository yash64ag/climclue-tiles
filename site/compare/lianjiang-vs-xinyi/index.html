How the climate of Lianjiang, China compares with Xinyi, Guangdong, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lianjiang is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 23.4 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Lianjiang is 1.8 °C warmer (Lianjiang 17.4 °C, Xinyi, Guangdong 15.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lianjiang 32.0 °C (July), Xinyi, Guangdong 32.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lianjiang 12.5 °C, Xinyi, Guangdong 9.8 °C.
  • Lianjiang gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 2,092 mm against 1,924 mm.
  • Lianjiang gets about 41 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,881 h against 1,840 h; about 1961–1990).
