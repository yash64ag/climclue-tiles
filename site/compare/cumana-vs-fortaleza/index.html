Cumaná vs Fortaleza climate

How the climate of Cumaná, Venezuela compares with Fortaleza, Brazil, 3,247 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fortaleza is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Fortaleza is 1.9 °C warmer (Cumaná 25.3 °C, Fortaleza 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cumaná 29.5 °C (May), Fortaleza 30.2 °C (November). Coldest-month night lows: Cumaná 23.0 °C, Fortaleza 24.1 °C.
  • Fortaleza gets 47% more rain and snow a year: 1,314 mm against 895 mm.
  • Cumaná gets about 159 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,803 h against 2,643 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
