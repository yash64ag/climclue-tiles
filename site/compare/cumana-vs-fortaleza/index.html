How the climate of Cumaná, Venezuela compares with Fortaleza, Brazil: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Fortaleza is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Fortaleza is 1.8 °C warmer (Cumaná 25.3 °C, Fortaleza 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cumaná 32.2 °C (September), Fortaleza 30.4 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Cumaná 19.9 °C, Fortaleza 22.4 °C.
  • Fortaleza gets 79% more rain and snow a year: 1,353 mm against 756 mm.
  • Cumaná gets about 159 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,803 h against 2,643 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
