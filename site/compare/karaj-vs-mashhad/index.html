Karaj vs Mashhad climate

How the climate of Karaj, Iran compares with Mashhad, Iran, 776 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mashhad is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 14.6 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Mashhad is 2.1 °C warmer (Karaj -0.6 °C, Mashhad 1.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Karaj 33.8 °C (July), Mashhad 33.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Karaj -5.6 °C, Mashhad -3.6 °C.
  • Karaj gets 83% more rain and snow a year: 484 mm against 265 mm.
  • Mashhad gets about 104 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,878 h against 2,774 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Karaj 78 cm, Mashhad 33 cm (ERA5-Land).
