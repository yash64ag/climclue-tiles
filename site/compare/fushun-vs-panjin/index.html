How the climate of Fushun, China compares with Panjin, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Panjin is 1.3 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 9.8 °C against 8.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Panjin is 2.9 °C warmer (Fushun -11.8 °C, Panjin -8.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fushun 29.1 °C (July), Panjin 29.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fushun -18.2 °C, Panjin -14.3 °C.
  • Fushun gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 772 mm against 669 mm.
  • Panjin gets about 94 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,628 h against 2,534 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fushun 42 cm, Panjin 24 cm (ERA5-Land).
