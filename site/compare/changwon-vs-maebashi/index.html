Changwon vs Maebashi climate

How the climate of Changwon, South Korea compares with Maebashi, Japan, 946 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Maebashi is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 14.6 °C against 14.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Maebashi is 2.4 °C warmer (Changwon 4.0 °C, Maebashi 6.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changwon 28.8 °C (August), Maebashi 28.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Changwon -2.8 °C, Maebashi -1.7 °C.
  • Changwon gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,452 mm against 1,309 mm.
  • Changwon gets about 509 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,419 h against 1,910 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changwon 8.6 cm, Maebashi 22 cm (ERA5-Land).
