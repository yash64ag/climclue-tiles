How the climate of Changwon, South Korea compares with Maebashi, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Changwon and Maebashi have the same yearly average temperature (14.5 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Maebashi is 2.0 °C warmer (Changwon 4.0 °C, Maebashi 6.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changwon 30.4 °C (August), Maebashi 30.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Changwon -3.2 °C, Maebashi -1.0 °C.
  • Changwon gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,452 mm against 1,309 mm.
  • Changwon gets about 509 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,419 h against 1,910 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changwon 8.6 cm, Maebashi 22 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Sunnier: Changwon in 12 of 12 months
