Qingdao vs Ŭllyul climate

How the climate of Qingdao, China compares with Ŭllyul, North Korea, 505 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Qingdao is 1.6 °C (2.9 °F) warmer over the year: 13.1 °C against 11.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Qingdao is 3.8 °C warmer (Qingdao 0.8 °C, Ŭllyul -3.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qingdao 26.8 °C (August), Ŭllyul 27.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Qingdao -1.8 °C, Ŭllyul -7.1 °C.
  • Ŭllyul gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 813 mm against 726 mm.
  • Qingdao gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,470 h against 2,463 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Qingdao 11 cm, Ŭllyul 25 cm (ERA5-Land).
