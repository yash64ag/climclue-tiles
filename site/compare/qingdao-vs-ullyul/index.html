How the climate of Qingdao, China compares with Ŭllyul, North Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Qingdao is 1.7 °C (3.0 °F) warmer over the year: 13.1 °C against 11.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Qingdao is 4.3 °C warmer (Qingdao 1.0 °C, Ŭllyul -3.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qingdao 28.4 °C (August), Ŭllyul 28.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Qingdao -2.5 °C, Ŭllyul -7.9 °C.
  • Ŭllyul gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 786 mm against 728 mm.
  • Qingdao gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,470 h against 2,463 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Qingdao 11 cm, Ŭllyul 25 cm (ERA5-Land).
