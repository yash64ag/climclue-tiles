How the climate of Iztapalapa, Mexico compares with Quillacollo, Bolivia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Iztapalapa is 1.4 °C (2.6 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 15.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Iztapalapa is 5.8 °C warmer (Iztapalapa 18.6 °C, Quillacollo 12.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iztapalapa 27.2 °C (May), Quillacollo 25.1 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Iztapalapa 4.4 °C, Quillacollo 1.7 °C.
  • Iztapalapa gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 779 mm against 773 mm.
  • Quillacollo gets about 457 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,471 h against 2,013 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
