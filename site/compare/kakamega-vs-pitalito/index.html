Kakamega vs Pitalito climate

How the climate of Kakamega, Kenya compares with Pitalito, Colombia, 12,318 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kakamega is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 20.2 °C against 20.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Kakamega is 0.9 °C warmer (Kakamega 21.4 °C, Pitalito 20.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kakamega 29.6 °C (February), Pitalito 25.8 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Kakamega 12.3 °C, Pitalito 13.4 °C.
  • Kakamega gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,674 mm against 1,619 mm.
  • Kakamega gets about 1,306 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,755 h against 1,449 h; about 1961–1990).
