How the climate of Pueblo, United States compares with Zhongwei, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zhongwei is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 11.6 °C against 11.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Pueblo is 4.2 °C warmer (Pueblo -0.7 °C, Zhongwei -4.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pueblo 34.0 °C (July), Zhongwei 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Pueblo -9.8 °C, Zhongwei -11.5 °C.
  • Pueblo gets 82% more rain and snow a year: 342 mm against 188 mm.
  • Pueblo gets about 674 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,371 h against 2,697 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Pueblo 53 cm, Zhongwei 5.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
