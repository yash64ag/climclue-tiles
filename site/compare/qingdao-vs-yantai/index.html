Qingdao vs Yantai, Shandong climate

How the climate of Qingdao, China compares with Yantai, Shandong, China, 183 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Qingdao is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 13.1 °C against 12.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Qingdao is 2.2 °C warmer (Qingdao 1.0 °C, Yantai, Shandong -1.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qingdao 28.4 °C (August), Yantai, Shandong 28.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Qingdao -2.5 °C, Yantai, Shandong -4.3 °C.
  • Qingdao gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 728 mm against 690 mm.
  • Yantai, Shandong gets about 90 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,560 h against 2,470 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Qingdao 11 cm, Yantai, Shandong 28 cm (ERA5-Land).
