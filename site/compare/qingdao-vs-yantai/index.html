Qingdao vs Yantai climate

How the climate of Qingdao, China compares with Yantai, China, 183 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Qingdao is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 13.1 °C against 12.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Qingdao is 2.1 °C warmer (Qingdao 0.8 °C, Yantai -1.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qingdao 26.8 °C (August), Yantai 27.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Qingdao -1.8 °C, Yantai -4.0 °C.
  • Qingdao gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 726 mm against 663 mm.
  • Yantai gets about 90 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,560 h against 2,470 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Qingdao 11 cm, Yantai 28 cm (ERA5-Land).
