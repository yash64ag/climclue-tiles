Dombivali vs Nashik climate

How the climate of Dombivali, India compares with Nashik, India, 114 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dombivali is 2.7 °C (4.8 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 24.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Dombivali is 3.7 °C warmer (Dombivali 24.0 °C, Nashik 20.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dombivali 34.5 °C (April), Nashik 36.1 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Dombivali 15.9 °C, Nashik 11.0 °C.
  • Dombivali gets 83% more rain and snow a year: 2,159 mm against 1,180 mm.
  • Nashik gets about 58 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,758 h against 2,700 h; about 1961–1990).
