Santiago de Querétaro vs Zapopan climate

How the climate of Santiago de Querétaro, Mexico compares with Zapopan, Mexico, 312 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Zapopan is 3.2 °C (5.7 °F) warmer over the year: 21.1 °C against 17.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Zapopan is 3.7 °C warmer (Santiago de Querétaro 21.3 °C, Zapopan 24.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Santiago de Querétaro 28.1 °C (May), Zapopan 32.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Santiago de Querétaro 7.1 °C, Zapopan 10.1 °C.
  • Zapopan gets 57% more rain and snow a year: 864 mm against 549 mm.
  • Zapopan gets about 100 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,647 h against 2,547 h; about 1961–1990).
