Santiago de Querétaro vs Zapopan climate

How the climate of Santiago de Querétaro, Mexico compares with Zapopan, Mexico, 312 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zapopan is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 20.4 °C against 19.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Zapopan is 1.7 °C warmer (Santiago de Querétaro 22.5 °C, Zapopan 24.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Santiago de Querétaro 29.3 °C (May), Zapopan 32.0 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Santiago de Querétaro 8.3 °C, Zapopan 9.3 °C.
  • Zapopan gets 98% more rain and snow a year: 901 mm against 456 mm.
  • Zapopan gets about 100 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,647 h against 2,547 h; about 1961–1990).
