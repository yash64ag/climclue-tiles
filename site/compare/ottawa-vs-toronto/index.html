Ottawa vs Toronto climate

How the climate of Ottawa, Canada compares with Toronto, Canada, 351 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Toronto is 2.0 °C (3.7 °F) warmer over the year: 8.6 °C against 6.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Toronto is 6.6 °C warmer (Ottawa -10.2 °C, Toronto -3.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ottawa 26.0 °C (July), Toronto 24.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Ottawa -14.6 °C, Toronto -6.7 °C.
  • Ottawa gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 974 mm against 874 mm.
  • Toronto gets about 8 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,002 h against 1,993 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ottawa 185 cm, Toronto 117 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Toronto in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: Toronto in 10 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Sunnier: Toronto in 7 of 12 months
