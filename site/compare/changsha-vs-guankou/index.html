How the climate of Changsha, Hunan, China compares with Guankou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Changsha, Hunan and Guankou have the same yearly average temperature (18.3 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Guankou is 0.3 °C warmer (Changsha, Hunan 5.7 °C, Guankou 6.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changsha, Hunan 33.8 °C (July), Guankou 34.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changsha, Hunan 2.8 °C, Guankou 2.3 °C.
  • Guankou gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,774 mm against 1,689 mm.
  • Guankou gets about 62 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,652 h against 1,589 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changsha, Hunan 9.1 cm, Guankou 6.8 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Changsha, Hunan in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Guankou in 12 of 12 months
