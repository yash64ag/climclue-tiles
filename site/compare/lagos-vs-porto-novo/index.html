How the climate of Lagos, Nigeria compares with Porto-Novo, Benin: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lagos is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Lagos is 0.2 °C warmer (Lagos 27.1 °C, Porto-Novo 26.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lagos 32.2 °C (February), Porto-Novo 31.5 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Lagos 21.7 °C, Porto-Novo 22.1 °C.
  • Lagos gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 1,627 mm against 1,295 mm.
  • Porto-Novo gets about 271 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,150 h against 1,879 h; about 1961–1990).
