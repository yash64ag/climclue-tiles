Lagos vs Porto-Novo climate

How the climate of Lagos, Nigeria compares with Porto-Novo, Benin, 88 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lagos and Porto-Novo have the same yearly average temperature (26.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Lagos is 0.1 °C warmer (Lagos 26.9 °C, Porto-Novo 26.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lagos 30.0 °C (February), Porto-Novo 30.1 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Lagos 23.7 °C, Porto-Novo 23.4 °C.
  • Lagos gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 1,605 mm against 1,282 mm.
  • Porto-Novo gets about 271 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,150 h against 1,879 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Lagos in 6 of 12 months (6 equal)
  • Drier: Porto-Novo in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Porto-Novo in 12 of 12 months
