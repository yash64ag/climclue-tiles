How the climate of Bijie, China compares with Bolzano, Italy: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bijie is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 13.5 °C against 13.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Bijie is 2.0 °C warmer (Bijie 5.1 °C, Bolzano 3.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bijie 26.8 °C (July), Bolzano 29.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bijie 0.1 °C, Bolzano -2.2 °C.
  • Bijie gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 973 mm against 779 mm.
  • Bolzano gets about 356 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,870 h against 1,514 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bijie 31 cm, Bolzano 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
