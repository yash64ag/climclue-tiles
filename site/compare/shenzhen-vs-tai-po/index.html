Shenzhen vs Tai Po climate

How the climate of Shenzhen, China compares with Tai Po, Hong Kong, 15 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shenzhen is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 23.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Shenzhen is 0.2 °C warmer (Shenzhen 22.9 °C, Tai Po 22.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Shenzhen 30.5 °C (August), Tai Po 30.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Shenzhen 11.6 °C, Tai Po 12.1 °C.
  • Tai Po gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 2,220 mm against 2,176 mm.
  • Tai Po gets about 18 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,958 h against 1,940 h; about 1961–1990).
