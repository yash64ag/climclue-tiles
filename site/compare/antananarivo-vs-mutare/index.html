Antananarivo vs Mutare climate

How the climate of Antananarivo, Madagascar compares with Mutare, Zimbabwe, 1,563 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mutare is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 18.6 °C against 18.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Mutare is 1.8 °C warmer (Antananarivo 16.6 °C, Mutare 18.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Antananarivo 25.7 °C (November), Mutare 26.3 °C (November). Coldest-month night lows: Antananarivo 9.8 °C, Mutare 8.3 °C.
  • Antananarivo gets 45% more rain and snow a year: 1,436 mm against 993 mm.
  • Mutare gets about 58 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,592 h against 2,533 h; about 1961–1990).
