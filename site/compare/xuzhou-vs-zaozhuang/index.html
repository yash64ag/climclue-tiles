How the climate of Xuzhou, China compares with Zaozhuang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Xuzhou is 1.0 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 15.1 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Xuzhou is 1.3 °C warmer (Xuzhou 3.1 °C, Zaozhuang 1.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Xuzhou 31.4 °C (July), Zaozhuang 30.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Xuzhou -3.3 °C, Zaozhuang -4.9 °C.
  • Xuzhou gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 886 mm against 738 mm.
  • Zaozhuang gets about 75 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,337 h against 2,262 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Xuzhou 17 cm, Zaozhuang 13 cm (ERA5-Land).
