Xuzhou vs Zaozhuang climate

How the climate of Xuzhou, China compares with Zaozhuang, China, 78 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Xuzhou is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 14.8 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Xuzhou is 0.8 °C warmer (Xuzhou 0.7 °C, Zaozhuang -0.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Xuzhou 30.9 °C (July), Zaozhuang 30.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Xuzhou -4.0 °C, Zaozhuang -4.5 °C.
  • Xuzhou gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 888 mm against 761 mm.
  • Zaozhuang gets about 75 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,337 h against 2,262 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Xuzhou 23 cm, Zaozhuang 22 cm (ERA5-Land).
