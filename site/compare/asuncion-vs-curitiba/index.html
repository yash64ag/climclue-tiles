Asunción vs Curitiba climate

How the climate of Asunción, Paraguay compares with Curitiba, Brazil, 841 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Asunción is 5.4 °C (9.8 °F) warmer over the year: 22.8 °C against 17.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Asunción is 6.9 °C warmer (Asunción 27.8 °C, Curitiba 20.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Asunción 32.3 °C (January), Curitiba 24.9 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Asunción 13.5 °C, Curitiba 9.8 °C.
  • Curitiba gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,535 mm against 1,426 mm.
  • Asunción gets about 1,112 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,764 h against 1,653 h; about 1961–1990).
