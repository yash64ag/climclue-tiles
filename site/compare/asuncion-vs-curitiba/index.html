How the climate of Asunción, Paraguay compares with Curitiba, Brazil: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Asunción is 5.9 °C (10.6 °F) warmer over the year: 22.9 °C against 17.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Asunción is 7.3 °C warmer (Asunción 27.7 °C, Curitiba 20.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Asunción 33.0 °C (January), Curitiba 26.1 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Asunción 13.1 °C, Curitiba 8.2 °C.
  • Curitiba gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,510 mm against 1,439 mm.
  • Asunción gets about 1,112 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,764 h against 1,653 h; about 1961–1990).
