How the climate of Guigang, China compares with Liuzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Guigang is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 22.4 °C against 21.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Guigang is 2.0 °C warmer (Guigang 13.1 °C, Liuzhou 11.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guigang 32.8 °C (July), Liuzhou 33.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Guigang 9.6 °C, Liuzhou 7.3 °C.
  • Guigang gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,689 mm against 1,503 mm.
  • Guigang gets about 140 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,708 h against 1,568 h; about 1961–1990).
