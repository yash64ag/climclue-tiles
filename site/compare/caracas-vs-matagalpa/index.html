Caracas vs Matagalpa climate

How the climate of Caracas, Venezuela compares with Matagalpa, Nicaragua, 2,090 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Matagalpa is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 22.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Matagalpa is 1.7 °C warmer (Caracas 23.0 °C, Matagalpa 24.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Caracas 28.8 °C (May), Matagalpa 30.5 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Caracas 15.6 °C, Matagalpa 16.5 °C.
  • Matagalpa gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 1,458 mm against 1,121 mm.
  • Caracas gets about 68 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,385 h against 2,318 h; about 1961–1990).
