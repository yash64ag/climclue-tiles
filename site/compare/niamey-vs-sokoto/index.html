Niamey vs Sokoto climate

How the climate of Niamey, Niger compares with Sokoto, Nigeria, 343 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Niamey is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 29.3 °C against 28.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Niamey is 1.5 °C warmer (Niamey 29.6 °C, Sokoto 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Niamey 40.5 °C (April), Sokoto 40.5 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Niamey 15.9 °C, Sokoto 17.2 °C.
  • Sokoto gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 576 mm against 462 mm.
  • Niamey gets about 115 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,904 h against 2,789 h; about 1961–1990).
