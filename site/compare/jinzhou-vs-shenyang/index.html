Jinzhou, Liaoning vs Shenyang climate

How the climate of Jinzhou, Liaoning, China compares with Shenyang, China, 206 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Jinzhou, Liaoning is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 10.0 °C against 8.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Jinzhou, Liaoning is 3.7 °C warmer (Jinzhou, Liaoning -7.5 °C, Shenyang -11.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinzhou, Liaoning 28.2 °C (July), Shenyang 28.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinzhou, Liaoning -12.6 °C, Shenyang -17.6 °C.
  • Shenyang gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 717 mm against 584 mm.
  • Jinzhou, Liaoning gets about 137 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,677 h against 2,540 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinzhou, Liaoning 33 cm, Shenyang 56 cm (ERA5-Land).
