Main differences

  • Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 10.1 °C against 8.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) is 4.0 °C warmer (Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) -7.2 °C, Shenyang -11.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) 27.9 °C (July), Shenyang 28.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) -12.0 °C, Shenyang -17.6 °C.
  • Shenyang gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 715 mm against 582 mm.
  • Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) gets about 137 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,677 h against 2,540 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) 17 cm, Shenyang 37 cm (ERA5-Land).
