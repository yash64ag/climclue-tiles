Datong, Shanxi vs Kanggye climate

How the climate of Datong, Shanxi, China compares with Kanggye, North Korea, 1,127 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kanggye is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 8.1 °C against 7.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Kanggye is 2.4 °C warmer (Datong, Shanxi 21.2 °C, Kanggye 23.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Datong, Shanxi 29.1 °C (July), Kanggye 28.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Datong, Shanxi -16.7 °C, Kanggye -18.1 °C.
  • Kanggye gets 107% more rain and snow a year: 787 mm against 380 mm.
  • Datong, Shanxi gets about 403 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,809 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Datong, Shanxi 15 cm, Kanggye 65 cm (ERA5-Land).
