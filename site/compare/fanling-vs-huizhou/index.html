Fanling vs Huizhou climate

How the climate of Fanling, Hong Kong compares with Huizhou, China, 74 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fanling is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Fanling is 1.2 °C warmer (Fanling 16.8 °C, Huizhou 15.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fanling 31.7 °C (July), Huizhou 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fanling 12.1 °C, Huizhou 9.9 °C.
  • Fanling gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 2,097 mm against 1,962 mm.
  • Fanling gets about 60 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,958 h against 1,898 h; about 1961–1990).
