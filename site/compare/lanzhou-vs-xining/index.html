How the climate of Lanzhou, China compares with Xining, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lanzhou is 4.8 °C (8.6 °F) warmer over the year: 11.5 °C against 6.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Lanzhou is 6.5 °C warmer (Lanzhou 23.7 °C, Xining 17.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lanzhou 29.8 °C (July), Xining 23.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lanzhou -8.8 °C, Xining -13.6 °C.
  • Xining gets 44% more rain and snow a year: 410 mm against 285 mm.
  • Xining gets about 259 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,734 h against 2,476 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lanzhou 7.4 cm, Xining 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
