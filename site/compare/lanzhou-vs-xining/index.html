Lanzhou vs Xining climate

How the climate of Lanzhou, China compares with Xining, China, 197 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lanzhou is 4.5 °C (8.1 °F) warmer over the year: 10.8 °C against 6.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Lanzhou is 6.1 °C warmer (Lanzhou 22.9 °C, Xining 16.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lanzhou 28.3 °C (July), Xining 22.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lanzhou -8.8 °C, Xining -14.3 °C.
  • Xining gets 39% more rain and snow a year: 433 mm against 311 mm.
  • Xining gets about 259 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,734 h against 2,476 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lanzhou 53 cm, Xining 80 cm (ERA5-Land).
