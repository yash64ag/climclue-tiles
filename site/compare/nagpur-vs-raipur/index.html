Nagpur vs Raipur, Chhattisgarh climate

How the climate of Nagpur, India compares with Raipur, Chhattisgarh, India, 264 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Nagpur is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Nagpur is 0.8 °C warmer (Nagpur 20.4 °C, Raipur, Chhattisgarh 19.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nagpur 42.8 °C (May), Raipur, Chhattisgarh 41.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Nagpur 12.5 °C, Raipur, Chhattisgarh 12.6 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,279 mm and 1,277 mm).
  • Nagpur gets about 141 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,764 h against 2,623 h; about 1961–1990).
