How the climate of Ciudad Juárez, Mexico compares with Phoenix, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Phoenix is 4.7 °C (8.5 °F) warmer over the year: 23.3 °C against 18.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Phoenix is 6.5 °C warmer (Ciudad Juárez 27.4 °C, Phoenix 34.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ciudad Juárez 37.8 °C (June), Phoenix 42.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ciudad Juárez -0.5 °C, Phoenix 4.2 °C.
  • Ciudad Juárez gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 246 mm against 217 mm.
  • Phoenix gets about 144 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,815 h against 3,671 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Phoenix in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Phoenix in 10 of 12 months
