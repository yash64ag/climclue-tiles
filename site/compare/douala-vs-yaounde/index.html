Douala vs Yaoundé climate

How the climate of Douala, Cameroon compares with Yaoundé, Cameroon, 202 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Douala is 3.0 °C (5.4 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 23.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Douala is 3.4 °C warmer (Douala 26.4 °C, Yaoundé 23.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Douala 31.6 °C (February), Yaoundé 29.8 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Douala 21.9 °C, Yaoundé 18.0 °C.
  • Douala gets 78% more rain and snow a year: 3,194 mm against 1,794 mm.
  • Yaoundé gets about 59 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,763 h against 1,704 h; about 1961–1990).
