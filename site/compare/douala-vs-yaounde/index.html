Douala vs Yaoundé climate

How the climate of Douala, Cameroon compares with Yaoundé, Cameroon, 202 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Douala is 2.9 °C (5.3 °F) warmer over the year: 25.9 °C against 23.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Douala is 3.3 °C warmer (Douala 26.4 °C, Yaoundé 23.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Douala 30.6 °C (February), Yaoundé 29.9 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Douala 22.7 °C, Yaoundé 19.7 °C.
  • Douala gets 85% more rain and snow a year: 3,195 mm against 1,729 mm.
  • Yaoundé gets about 59 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,763 h against 1,704 h; about 1961–1990).
