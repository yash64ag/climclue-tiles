How the climate of Douala, Cameroon compares with Yaoundé, Cameroon: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Douala is 3.0 °C (5.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 23.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Douala is 3.7 °C warmer (Douala 28.1 °C, Yaoundé 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Douala 32.7 °C (February), Yaoundé 31.0 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Douala 22.7 °C, Yaoundé 15.5 °C.
  • Douala gets 78% more rain and snow a year: 3,194 mm against 1,794 mm.
  • Yaoundé gets about 59 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,763 h against 1,704 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Douala in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Yaoundé in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Yaoundé in 6 of 12 months (1 equal)
