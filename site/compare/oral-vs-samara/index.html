How the climate of Oral, Kazakhstan compares with Samara, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Oral is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 6.3 °C against 6.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Oral is 1.2 °C warmer (Oral 20.9 °C, Samara 19.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Oral 29.7 °C (July), Samara 26.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Oral -14.8 °C, Samara -13.0 °C.
  • Samara gets 43% more rain and snow a year: 510 mm against 357 mm.
  • Oral gets about 220 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,274 h against 2,055 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Oral 105 cm, Samara 167 cm (ERA5-Land).
