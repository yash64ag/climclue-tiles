Puning vs Shantou climate

How the climate of Puning, China compares with Shantou, China, 52 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Puning is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 22.3 °C against 22.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Shantou is 0.5 °C warmer (Puning 15.7 °C, Shantou 16.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Puning 31.2 °C (July), Shantou 29.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Puning 9.3 °C, Shantou 11.4 °C.
  • Puning gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,639 mm against 1,567 mm.
  • Shantou gets about 28 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,008 h against 1,980 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Shantou in 11 of 12 months
