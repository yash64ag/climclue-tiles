How the climate of Guankou, China compares with McKinney, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Guankou is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 18.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when McKinney is 1.2 °C warmer (Guankou 12.2 °C, McKinney 13.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guankou 34.2 °C (July), McKinney 35.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Guankou 2.3 °C, McKinney 0.8 °C.
  • Guankou gets 66% more rain and snow a year: 1,774 mm against 1,067 mm.
  • McKinney gets about 1,156 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,808 h against 1,652 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Guankou 6.8 cm, McKinney 2.3 cm (ERA5-Land).
