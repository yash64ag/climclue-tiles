Adana vs Kayseri climate

How the climate of Adana, Turkey compares with Kayseri, Turkey, 195 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Adana is 6.7 °C (12.1 °F) warmer over the year: 18.9 °C against 12.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Adana is 9.0 °C warmer (Adana 8.8 °C, Kayseri -0.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Adana 33.8 °C (August), Kayseri 30.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Adana 4.3 °C, Kayseri -5.7 °C.
  • Adana gets 92% more rain and snow a year: 714 mm against 373 mm.
  • Adana gets about 125 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,992 h against 2,867 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Adana 1.7 cm, Kayseri 152 cm (ERA5-Land).
