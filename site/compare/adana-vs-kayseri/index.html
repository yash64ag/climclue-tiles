How the climate of Adana, Turkey compares with Kayseri, Turkey: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Adana is 6.5 °C (11.7 °F) warmer over the year: 18.9 °C against 12.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Adana is 8.7 °C warmer (Adana 9.7 °C, Kayseri 1.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Adana 34.2 °C (August), Kayseri 34.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Adana 3.7 °C, Kayseri -5.1 °C.
  • Adana gets 98% more rain and snow a year: 687 mm against 347 mm.
  • Adana gets about 125 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,992 h against 2,867 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Adana 0.2 cm, Kayseri 47 cm (ERA5-Land).
