Ruse vs Tbilisi climate

How the climate of Ruse, Bulgaria compares with Tbilisi, Georgia, 1,556 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tbilisi is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 12.9 °C against 12.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Tbilisi is 1.2 °C warmer (Ruse 19.1 °C, Tbilisi 20.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ruse 30.8 °C (July), Tbilisi 31.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ruse -3.7 °C, Tbilisi -3.4 °C.
  • Ruse gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 592 mm against 567 mm.
  • Ruse gets about 65 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,112 h against 2,047 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ruse 67 cm, Tbilisi 41 cm (ERA5-Land).
