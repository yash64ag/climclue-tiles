How the climate of Ruse, Bulgaria compares with Tbilisi, Georgia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tbilisi is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 13.6 °C against 12.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Tbilisi is 2.5 °C warmer (Ruse -0.1 °C, Tbilisi 2.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ruse 30.8 °C (July), Tbilisi 31.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ruse -3.7 °C, Tbilisi -1.3 °C.
  • Ruse gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 592 mm against 567 mm.
  • Ruse gets about 65 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,112 h against 2,047 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ruse 67 cm, Tbilisi 41 cm (ERA5-Land).
