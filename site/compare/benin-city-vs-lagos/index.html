Benin City vs Lagos climate

How the climate of Benin City, Nigeria compares with Lagos, Nigeria, 247 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lagos is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 25.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Lagos is 1.4 °C warmer (Benin City 26.4 °C, Lagos 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Benin City 32.7 °C (February), Lagos 32.2 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Benin City 20.9 °C, Lagos 21.7 °C.
  • Benin City gets 40% more rain and snow a year: 2,278 mm against 1,627 mm.
  • Lagos gets about 124 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,879 h against 1,755 h; about 1961–1990).
