How the climate of Minhang, China compares with Shanghai, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Minhang and Shanghai have the same yearly average temperature (17.0 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Shanghai is 0.2 °C warmer (Minhang 7.3 °C, Shanghai 7.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Minhang 32.3 °C (July), Shanghai 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Minhang 1.5 °C, Shanghai 1.6 °C.
  • Shanghai gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,476 mm against 1,467 mm.
  • Shanghai gets about 10 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,969 h against 1,959 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Minhang 8.6 cm, Shanghai 7.7 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Shanghai in 6 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: Minhang in 10 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Sunnier: Shanghai in 10 of 12 months
