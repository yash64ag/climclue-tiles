How the climate of Barcelona, Spain compares with Toulon, France: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Barcelona is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 15.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Barcelona is 0.7 °C warmer (Barcelona 18.0 °C, Toulon 17.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Barcelona 28.0 °C (August), Toulon 28.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Barcelona 5.5 °C, Toulon 5.7 °C.
  • Barcelona gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 634 mm against 622 mm.
  • Toulon gets about 379 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,764 h against 2,385 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
