Ciudad Juárez vs Mexicali climate

How the climate of Ciudad Juárez, Mexico compares with Mexicali, Mexico, 852 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mexicali is 5.9 °C (10.5 °F) warmer over the year: 24.5 °C against 18.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Mexicali is 7.7 °C warmer (Ciudad Juárez 7.4 °C, Mexicali 15.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ciudad Juárez 37.8 °C (June), Mexicali 43.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ciudad Juárez -0.5 °C, Mexicali 6.2 °C.
  • Ciudad Juárez gets 215% more rain and snow a year: 246 mm against 78 mm.
  • Ciudad Juárez gets about 19 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,671 h against 3,652 h; about 1961–1990).
