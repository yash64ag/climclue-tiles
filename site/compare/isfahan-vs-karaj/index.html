Isfahan vs Karaj climate

How the climate of Isfahan, Iran compares with Karaj, Iran, 359 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Isfahan is 2.0 °C (3.7 °F) warmer over the year: 16.4 °C against 14.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Isfahan is 3.5 °C warmer (Isfahan 4.8 °C, Karaj 1.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Isfahan 36.7 °C (July), Karaj 33.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Isfahan -4.2 °C, Karaj -5.6 °C.
  • Karaj gets 233% more rain and snow a year: 478 mm against 144 mm.
  • Isfahan gets about 411 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,185 h against 2,774 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Isfahan 19 cm, Karaj 78 cm (ERA5-Land).
