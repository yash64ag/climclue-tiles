Isfahan vs Karaj climate

How the climate of Isfahan, Iran compares with Karaj, Iran, 359 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Isfahan is 2.1 °C (3.8 °F) warmer over the year: 16.3 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Isfahan is 3.4 °C warmer (Isfahan 4.7 °C, Karaj 1.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Isfahan 36.6 °C (July), Karaj 33.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Isfahan -4.4 °C, Karaj -5.6 °C.
  • Karaj gets 221% more rain and snow a year: 484 mm against 151 mm.
  • Isfahan gets about 411 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,185 h against 2,774 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Isfahan 18 cm, Karaj 98 cm (ERA5-Land).
