Ashgabat vs Karaj climate

How the climate of Ashgabat, Turkmenistan compares with Karaj, Iran, 698 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ashgabat is 3.1 °C (5.6 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Ashgabat is 4.4 °C warmer (Ashgabat 17.4 °C, Karaj 13.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ashgabat 36.3 °C (July), Karaj 33.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ashgabat -0.9 °C, Karaj -5.6 °C.
  • Karaj gets 74% more rain and snow a year: 484 mm against 278 mm.
  • Karaj gets about 66 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,774 h against 2,708 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ashgabat 28 cm, Karaj 98 cm (ERA5-Land).
