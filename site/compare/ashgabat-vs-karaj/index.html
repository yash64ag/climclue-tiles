Ashgabat vs Karaj climate

How the climate of Ashgabat, Turkmenistan compares with Karaj, Iran, 698 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ashgabat is 3.1 °C (5.5 °F) warmer over the year: 17.4 °C against 14.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Ashgabat is 4.3 °C warmer (Ashgabat 17.6 °C, Karaj 13.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ashgabat 36.5 °C (July), Karaj 33.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ashgabat -0.8 °C, Karaj -5.6 °C.
  • Karaj gets 74% more rain and snow a year: 478 mm against 274 mm.
  • Karaj gets about 66 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,774 h against 2,708 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ashgabat 13 cm, Karaj 78 cm (ERA5-Land).
