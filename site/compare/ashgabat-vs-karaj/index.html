How the climate of Ashgabat, Turkmenistan compares with Karaj, Iran: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ashgabat is 2.9 °C (5.3 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 14.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Ashgabat is 4.6 °C warmer (Ashgabat 4.1 °C, Karaj -0.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ashgabat 38.6 °C (July), Karaj 35.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ashgabat -0.2 °C, Karaj -4.8 °C.
  • Karaj gets 74% more rain and snow a year: 478 mm against 274 mm.
  • Karaj gets about 66 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,774 h against 2,708 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ashgabat 13 cm, Karaj 78 cm (ERA5-Land).
