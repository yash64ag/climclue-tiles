Main differences

  • McKinney is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 18.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when McKinney is 1.6 °C warmer (McKinney 13.6 °C, Yichun, Jiangxi 11.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: McKinney 34.0 °C (August), Yichun, Jiangxi 33.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: McKinney 3.4 °C, Yichun, Jiangxi 2.3 °C.
  • Yichun, Jiangxi gets 60% more rain and snow a year: 1,777 mm against 1,108 mm.
  • McKinney gets about 1,087 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,808 h against 1,721 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: McKinney 2.3 cm, Yichun, Jiangxi 7.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
