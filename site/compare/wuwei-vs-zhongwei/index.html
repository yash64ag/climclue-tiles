How the climate of Wuwei, China compares with Zhongwei, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zhongwei is 2.5 °C (4.6 °F) warmer over the year: 11.6 °C against 9.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Zhongwei is 3.4 °C warmer (Wuwei 22.2 °C, Zhongwei 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Wuwei 29.1 °C (July), Zhongwei 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Wuwei -13.2 °C, Zhongwei -11.5 °C.
  • Zhongwei gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 188 mm against 169 mm.
  • Wuwei gets about 117 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,814 h against 2,697 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Wuwei 9.6 cm, Zhongwei 5.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
