Kot Radha Kishan vs Najafgarh climate

How the climate of Kot Radha Kishan, Pakistan compares with Najafgarh, India, 398 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Najafgarh is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 24.9 °C against 24.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Najafgarh is 1.5 °C warmer (Kot Radha Kishan 21.1 °C, Najafgarh 22.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kot Radha Kishan 40.3 °C (June), Najafgarh 39.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kot Radha Kishan 5.2 °C, Najafgarh 6.4 °C.
  • Najafgarh gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 604 mm against 539 mm.
  • Kot Radha Kishan gets about 231 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,933 h against 2,702 h; about 1961–1990).
