Kobe vs Osaka climate

How the climate of Kobe, Japan compares with Osaka, Japan, 29 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Osaka is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Osaka is 0.9 °C warmer (Kobe 28.1 °C, Osaka 28.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kobe 29.7 °C (August), Osaka 32.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kobe 1.9 °C, Osaka 0.6 °C.
  • Osaka gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,421 mm against 1,378 mm.
  • Osaka gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,852 h against 1,846 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kobe 8.2 cm, Osaka 11 cm (ERA5-Land).
