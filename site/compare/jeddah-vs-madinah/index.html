Jeddah vs Madinah climate

How the climate of Jeddah, Saudi Arabia compares with Madinah, Saudi Arabia, 334 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jeddah is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 28.1 °C against 27.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Jeddah is 5.9 °C warmer (Jeddah 23.6 °C, Madinah 17.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jeddah 37.4 °C (July), Madinah 42.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Jeddah 18.4 °C, Madinah 11.7 °C.
  • Jeddah gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 69 mm against 63 mm.
  • Madinah gets about 282 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,599 h against 3,317 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Jeddah in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Madinah in 11 of 12 months
