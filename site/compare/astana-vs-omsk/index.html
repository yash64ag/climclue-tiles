Astana vs Omsk climate

How the climate of Astana, Kazakhstan compares with Omsk, Russia, 443 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Astana is 1.3 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 3.4 °C against 2.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Astana is 2.2 °C warmer (Astana -14.3 °C, Omsk -16.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Astana 26.8 °C (July), Omsk 25.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Astana -18.8 °C, Omsk -20.8 °C.
  • Omsk gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 391 mm against 334 mm.
  • Astana gets about 279 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,450 h against 2,171 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Astana 100 cm, Omsk 118 cm (ERA5-Land).
