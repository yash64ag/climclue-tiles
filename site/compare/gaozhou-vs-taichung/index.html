Gaozhou vs Taichung climate

How the climate of Gaozhou, China compares with Taichung, Taiwan, 1,035 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gaozhou is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.4 °C against 23.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Taichung is 1.0 °C warmer (Gaozhou 17.5 °C, Taichung 18.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gaozhou 32.3 °C (July), Taichung 31.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gaozhou 12.0 °C, Taichung 13.3 °C.
  • Gaozhou gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 2,245 mm against 2,055 mm.
  • Gaozhou gets about 50 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,887 h against 1,837 h; about 1961–1990).
