Gaozhou vs Taichung climate

How the climate of Gaozhou, China compares with Taichung, Taiwan, 1,035 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gaozhou is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.3 °C against 23.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Taichung is 1.0 °C warmer (Gaozhou 17.4 °C, Taichung 18.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gaozhou 31.4 °C (July), Taichung 29.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gaozhou 12.0 °C, Taichung 13.5 °C.
  • Gaozhou gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 2,209 mm against 1,949 mm.
  • Gaozhou gets about 50 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,887 h against 1,837 h; about 1961–1990).
