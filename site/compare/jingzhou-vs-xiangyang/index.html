Jingzhou vs Xiangyang climate

How the climate of Jingzhou, China compares with Xiangyang, China, 188 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jingzhou is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 17.4 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Jingzhou is 1.6 °C warmer (Jingzhou 7.0 °C, Xiangyang 5.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jingzhou 31.4 °C (July), Xiangyang 31.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jingzhou 0.5 °C, Xiangyang -1.6 °C.
  • Jingzhou gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 1,262 mm against 942 mm.
  • Xiangyang gets about 163 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,864 h against 1,701 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jingzhou 11 cm, Xiangyang 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
