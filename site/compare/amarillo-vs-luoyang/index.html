Amarillo vs Luoyang, Henan climate

How the climate of Amarillo, United States compares with Luoyang, Henan, China, 11,467 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Luoyang, Henan is 1.0 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Luoyang, Henan is 3.0 °C warmer (Amarillo 23.4 °C, Luoyang, Henan 26.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amarillo 33.0 °C (July), Luoyang, Henan 32.6 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Amarillo -5.1 °C, Luoyang, Henan -4.4 °C.
  • Luoyang, Henan gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 566 mm against 550 mm.
  • Amarillo gets about 1,061 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 2,182 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amarillo 23 cm, Luoyang, Henan 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
