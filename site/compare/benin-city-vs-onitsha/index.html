Benin City vs Onitsha climate

How the climate of Benin City, Nigeria compares with Onitsha, Nigeria, 130 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Onitsha is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 25.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Onitsha is 1.7 °C warmer (Benin City 27.4 °C, Onitsha 29.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Benin City 33.3 °C (February), Onitsha 35.0 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Benin City 21.9 °C, Onitsha 22.8 °C.
  • Benin City gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 2,246 mm against 1,753 mm.
  • Onitsha gets about 22 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,776 h against 1,755 h; about 1961–1990).
