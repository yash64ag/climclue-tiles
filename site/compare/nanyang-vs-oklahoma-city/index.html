Nanyang vs Oklahoma City climate

How the climate of Nanyang, China compares with Oklahoma City, United States, 11,783 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Oklahoma City is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Oklahoma City is 1.4 °C warmer (Nanyang 26.3 °C, Oklahoma City 27.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nanyang 31.7 °C (July), Oklahoma City 34.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nanyang -2.8 °C, Oklahoma City -2.8 °C.
  • Oklahoma City gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 906 mm against 843 mm.
  • Oklahoma City gets about 1,024 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,031 h against 2,008 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nanyang 16 cm, Oklahoma City 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
