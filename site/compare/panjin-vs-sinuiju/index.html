How the climate of Panjin, China compares with Sinŭiju, North Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Sinŭiju is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 9.9 °C against 9.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Panjin is 2.2 °C warmer (Panjin 18.1 °C, Sinŭiju 15.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Panjin 29.0 °C (July), Sinŭiju 28.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Panjin -14.3 °C, Sinŭiju -12.4 °C.
  • Sinŭiju gets 53% more rain and snow a year: 1,025 mm against 669 mm.
  • Panjin gets about 120 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,628 h against 2,508 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Panjin 24 cm, Sinŭiju 33 cm (ERA5-Land).
