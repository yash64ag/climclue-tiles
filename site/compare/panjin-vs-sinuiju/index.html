Panjin vs Sinŭiju climate

How the climate of Panjin, China compares with Sinŭiju, North Korea, 227 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Panjin is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 9.8 °C against 9.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Panjin is 2.4 °C warmer (Panjin 18.1 °C, Sinŭiju 15.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Panjin 28.8 °C (July), Sinŭiju 27.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Panjin -14.6 °C, Sinŭiju -14.0 °C.
  • Sinŭiju gets 59% more rain and snow a year: 1,050 mm against 661 mm.
  • Panjin gets about 120 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,628 h against 2,508 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Panjin 40 cm, Sinŭiju 49 cm (ERA5-Land).
