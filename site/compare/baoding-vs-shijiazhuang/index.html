Baoding vs Shijiazhuang climate

How the climate of Baoding, China compares with Shijiazhuang, China, 126 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shijiazhuang is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 14.0 °C against 13.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Shijiazhuang is 0.8 °C warmer (Baoding 5.3 °C, Shijiazhuang 6.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoding 32.1 °C (June), Shijiazhuang 32.1 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Baoding -7.5 °C, Shijiazhuang -6.9 °C.
  • Baoding gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 516 mm against 504 mm.
  • Baoding gets about 43 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,609 h against 2,566 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoding 10 cm, Shijiazhuang 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
