Main differences

  • Hezhou is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 20.7 °C against 20.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Hezhou is 1.6 °C warmer (Cypress 21.3 °C, Hezhou 22.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cypress 33.8 °C (August), Hezhou 31.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Cypress 7.4 °C, Hezhou 5.8 °C.
  • Hezhou gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 1,592 mm against 1,222 mm.
  • Cypress gets about 866 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,569 h against 1,703 h; about 1961–1990).
