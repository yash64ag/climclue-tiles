How the climate of Cypress, Texas, United States compares with Hezhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Cypress, Texas and Hezhou have the same yearly average temperature (20.7 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Cypress, Texas is 1.5 °C warmer (Cypress, Texas 11.6 °C, Hezhou 10.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cypress, Texas 34.6 °C (August), Hezhou 33.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Cypress, Texas 6.0 °C, Hezhou 6.0 °C.
  • Hezhou gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 1,592 mm against 1,222 mm.
  • Cypress, Texas gets about 866 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,569 h against 1,703 h; about 1961–1990).
