Main differences

  • Hezhou is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 20.7 °C against 20.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Hezhou is 1.6 °C warmer (Cypress, Texas 21.3 °C, Hezhou 22.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cypress, Texas 34.0 °C (August), Hezhou 33.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Cypress, Texas 5.9 °C, Hezhou 6.0 °C.
