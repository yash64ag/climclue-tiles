Cypress vs Hezhou climate

How the climate of Cypress, United States compares with Hezhou, China, 13,306 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Hezhou is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 20.5 °C against 20.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Cypress is 1.4 °C warmer (Cypress 13.1 °C, Hezhou 11.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cypress 33.7 °C (August), Hezhou 31.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Cypress 7.4 °C, Hezhou 5.6 °C.
  • Hezhou gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 1,620 mm against 1,254 mm.
  • Cypress gets about 866 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,569 h against 1,703 h; about 1961–1990).
