Baku vs Yerevan climate

How the climate of Baku, Azerbaijan compares with Yerevan, Armenia, 457 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Baku is 3.3 °C (6.0 °F) warmer over the year: 14.9 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Baku is 8.3 °C warmer (Baku 5.7 °C, Yerevan -2.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baku 27.6 °C (August), Yerevan 31.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baku 4.0 °C, Yerevan -7.3 °C.
  • Yerevan gets 105% more rain and snow a year: 483 mm against 236 mm.
  • Yerevan gets about 388 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,497 h against 2,108 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baku 11 cm, Yerevan 109 cm (ERA5-Land).
