Baku vs Yerevan climate

How the climate of Baku, Azerbaijan compares with Yerevan, Armenia, 457 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Baku is 2.5 °C (4.6 °F) warmer over the year: 15.0 °C against 12.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Baku is 7.8 °C warmer (Baku 6.0 °C, Yerevan -1.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baku 30.2 °C (August), Yerevan 33.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baku 2.8 °C, Yerevan -6.4 °C.
  • Yerevan gets 110% more rain and snow a year: 446 mm against 213 mm.
  • Yerevan gets about 388 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,497 h against 2,108 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baku 7.3 cm, Yerevan 92 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Baku in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Baku in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Yerevan in 10 of 12 months
