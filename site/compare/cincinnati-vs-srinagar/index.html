Cincinnati vs Srinagar climate

How the climate of Cincinnati, United States compares with Srinagar, India, 11,600 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Srinagar is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 12.7 °C against 12.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Cincinnati is 1.6 °C warmer (Cincinnati 22.6 °C, Srinagar 20.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cincinnati 29.4 °C (July), Srinagar 26.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Cincinnati -3.9 °C, Srinagar -4.8 °C.
  • Srinagar gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,009 mm against 997 mm.
  • Srinagar gets about 431 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,747 h against 2,315 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Cincinnati 56 cm, Srinagar 72 cm (ERA5-Land).
