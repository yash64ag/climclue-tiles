How the climate of Cincinnati, United States compares with Srinagar, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Srinagar is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 12.7 °C against 12.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Srinagar is 1.5 °C warmer (Cincinnati 1.3 °C, Srinagar 2.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cincinnati 30.3 °C (July), Srinagar 29.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Cincinnati -5.0 °C, Srinagar -3.6 °C.
  • Srinagar gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,009 mm against 997 mm.
  • Srinagar gets about 431 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,747 h against 2,315 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Cincinnati 37 cm, Srinagar 72 cm (ERA5-Land).
