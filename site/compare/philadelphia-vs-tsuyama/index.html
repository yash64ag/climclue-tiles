How the climate of Philadelphia, United States compares with Tsuyama, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tsuyama is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 13.8 °C against 13.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Tsuyama is 1.8 °C warmer (Philadelphia 0.7 °C, Tsuyama 2.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Philadelphia 30.8 °C (July), Tsuyama 31.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Philadelphia -3.5 °C, Tsuyama -1.4 °C.
  • Tsuyama gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,383 mm against 1,220 mm.
  • Philadelphia gets about 661 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,463 h against 1,802 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Philadelphia 47 cm, Tsuyama 35 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Tsuyama in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Philadelphia in 12 of 12 months
