Jodhpur vs Kunri climate

How the climate of Jodhpur, India compares with Kunri, Pakistan, 365 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kunri is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Kunri is 1.3 °C warmer (Jodhpur 29.0 °C, Kunri 30.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jodhpur 40.0 °C (May), Kunri 40.6 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Jodhpur 9.7 °C, Kunri 10.8 °C.
  • Jodhpur gets 44% more rain and snow a year: 375 mm against 261 mm.
  • Kunri gets about 380 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,472 h against 3,092 h; about 1961–1990).
