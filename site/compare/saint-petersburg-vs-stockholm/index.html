Saint Petersburg vs Stockholm climate

How the climate of Saint Petersburg, Russia compares with Stockholm, Sweden, 691 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Stockholm is 1.8 °C (3.2 °F) warmer over the year: 6.6 °C against 4.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Stockholm is 4.4 °C warmer (Saint Petersburg -6.8 °C, Stockholm -2.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Saint Petersburg 20.9 °C (July), Stockholm 20.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Saint Petersburg -9.3 °C, Stockholm -4.8 °C.
  • Saint Petersburg gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 699 mm against 586 mm.
  • Saint Petersburg gets about 134 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,621 h against 1,487 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Saint Petersburg 191 cm, Stockholm 126 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Stockholm in 9 of 12 months (2 equal)
  • Drier: Stockholm in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Saint Petersburg in 8 of 12 months
