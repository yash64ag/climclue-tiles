Saint Petersburg vs Stockholm climate

How the climate of Saint Petersburg, Russia compares with Stockholm, Sweden, 691 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Stockholm is 1.8 °C (3.2 °F) warmer over the year: 6.6 °C against 4.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Stockholm is 4.3 °C warmer (Saint Petersburg -6.7 °C, Stockholm -2.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Saint Petersburg 21.3 °C (July), Stockholm 21.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Saint Petersburg -9.6 °C, Stockholm -4.7 °C.
  • Saint Petersburg gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 690 mm against 587 mm.
  • Saint Petersburg gets about 134 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,621 h against 1,487 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Saint Petersburg 179 cm, Stockholm 116 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Stockholm in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Saint Petersburg in 8 of 12 months
