Yancheng vs Yangzhou climate

How the climate of Yancheng, China compares with Yangzhou, China, 126 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yangzhou is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 15.7 °C against 15.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Yangzhou is 1.0 °C warmer (Yancheng 8.0 °C, Yangzhou 9.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Yancheng 30.8 °C (July), Yangzhou 31.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Yancheng -2.7 °C, Yangzhou -1.8 °C.
  • Yangzhou gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,098 mm against 1,052 mm.
  • Yancheng gets about 102 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,176 h against 2,074 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Yancheng 10 cm, Yangzhou 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
