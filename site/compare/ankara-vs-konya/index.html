Ankara vs Konya climate

How the climate of Ankara, Turkey compares with Konya, Turkey, 230 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Konya is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 13.1 °C against 12.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Konya is 0.4 °C warmer (Ankara 21.0 °C, Konya 21.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ankara 32.3 °C (August), Konya 32.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Ankara -2.4 °C, Konya -2.8 °C.
  • Ankara gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 420 mm against 389 mm.
  • Konya gets about 109 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,920 h against 2,811 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ankara 37 cm, Konya 48 cm (ERA5-Land).
