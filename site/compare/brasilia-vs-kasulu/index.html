Brasília vs Kasulu climate

How the climate of Brasília, Brazil compares with Kasulu, Tanzania, 8,590 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Brasília and Kasulu have the same yearly average temperature (21.2 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Kasulu is 1.6 °C warmer (Brasília 19.2 °C, Kasulu 20.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brasília 28.1 °C (September), Kasulu 29.6 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Brasília 13.9 °C, Kasulu 15.1 °C.
  • Brasília gets 39% more rain and snow a year: 1,544 mm against 1,114 mm.
  • Kasulu gets about 133 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,487 h against 2,354 h; about 1961–1990).
