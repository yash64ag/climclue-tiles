Kalyān vs Thāne climate

How the climate of Kalyān, India compares with Thāne, India, 19 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kalyān is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Kalyān is 0.7 °C warmer (Kalyān 29.1 °C, Thāne 28.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kalyān 34.7 °C (April), Thāne 33.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kalyān 15.9 °C, Thāne 16.0 °C.
  • Thāne gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 2,597 mm against 2,234 mm.
  • Kalyān gets about 31 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,706 h against 2,675 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Kalyān in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Kalyān in 12 of 12 months
