Changzhou vs Jiangyin climate

How the climate of Changzhou, China compares with Jiangyin, China, 33 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Changzhou is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 16.3 °C against 16.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Changzhou is 0.4 °C warmer (Changzhou 15.6 °C, Jiangyin 15.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changzhou 32.0 °C (July), Jiangyin 31.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changzhou -0.6 °C, Jiangyin -0.7 °C.
  • Changzhou gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,236 mm against 1,166 mm.
  • Jiangyin gets about 13 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,031 h against 2,017 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changzhou 17 cm, Jiangyin 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
