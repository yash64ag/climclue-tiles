Kagoshima vs Taizhou, Zhejiang climate

How the climate of Kagoshima, Japan compares with Taizhou, Zhejiang, China, 934 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Taizhou, Zhejiang is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 18.6 °C against 18.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Taizhou, Zhejiang is 1.6 °C warmer (Kagoshima 27.6 °C, Taizhou, Zhejiang 29.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kagoshima 30.3 °C (August), Taizhou, Zhejiang 32.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kagoshima 4.7 °C, Taizhou, Zhejiang 3.3 °C.
  • Kagoshima gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 2,249 mm against 1,688 mm.
  • Kagoshima gets about 48 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,859 h against 1,811 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kagoshima 1.2 cm, Taizhou, Zhejiang 7.3 cm (ERA5-Land).
