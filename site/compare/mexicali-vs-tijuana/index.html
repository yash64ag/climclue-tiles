Mexicali vs Tijuana climate

How the climate of Mexicali, Mexico compares with Tijuana, Mexico, 146 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mexicali is 8.0 °C (14.5 °F) warmer over the year: 24.5 °C against 16.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Mexicali is 15.1 °C warmer (Mexicali 34.8 °C, Tijuana 19.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mexicali 43.7 °C (July), Tijuana 25.0 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Mexicali 6.2 °C, Tijuana 7.3 °C.
  • Tijuana gets 278% more rain and snow a year: 296 mm against 78 mm.
  • Mexicali gets about 585 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,652 h against 3,067 h; about 1961–1990).
