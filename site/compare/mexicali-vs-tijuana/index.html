How the climate of Mexicali, Mexico compares with Tijuana, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Mexicali is 6.6 °C (11.8 °F) warmer over the year: 23.7 °C against 17.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Mexicali is 13.3 °C warmer (Mexicali 34.2 °C, Tijuana 20.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mexicali 42.6 °C (July), Tijuana 26.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Mexicali 6.3 °C, Tijuana 7.4 °C.
  • Tijuana gets 278% more rain and snow a year: 296 mm against 78 mm.
  • Mexicali gets about 585 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,652 h against 3,067 h; about 1961–1990).
