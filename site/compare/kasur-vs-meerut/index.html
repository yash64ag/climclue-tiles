Kasur vs Meerut climate

How the climate of Kasur, Pakistan compares with Meerut, India, 393 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Meerut is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 24.4 °C against 24.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Meerut is 1.6 °C warmer (Kasur 20.7 °C, Meerut 22.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kasur 40.0 °C (June), Meerut 39.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kasur 4.8 °C, Meerut 6.5 °C.
  • Meerut gets 52% more rain and snow a year: 875 mm against 576 mm.
  • Kasur gets about 244 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,903 h against 2,659 h; about 1961–1990).
