Kasur vs Meerut climate

How the climate of Kasur, Pakistan compares with Meerut, India, 393 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Meerut is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 24.4 °C against 24.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Meerut is 1.8 °C warmer (Kasur 20.6 °C, Meerut 22.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kasur 39.1 °C (June), Meerut 38.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kasur 6.1 °C, Meerut 7.8 °C.
  • Meerut gets 53% more rain and snow a year: 887 mm against 578 mm.
  • Kasur gets about 244 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,903 h against 2,659 h; about 1961–1990).
