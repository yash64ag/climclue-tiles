Cali vs Quito climate

How the climate of Cali, Colombia compares with Quito, Ecuador, 464 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Cali is 9.7 °C (17.4 °F) warmer over the year: 20.9 °C against 11.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Cali is 9.9 °C warmer (Cali 21.1 °C, Quito 11.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cali 24.9 °C (August), Quito 16.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Cali 16.6 °C, Quito 7.8 °C.
  • Quito gets 66% more rain and snow a year: 2,262 mm against 1,365 mm.
  • Quito gets about 164 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,875 h against 1,711 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
