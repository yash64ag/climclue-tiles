How the climate of Cali, Colombia compares with Quito, Ecuador: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Cali is 9.8 °C (17.6 °F) warmer over the year: 22.2 °C against 12.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Cali is 10.0 °C warmer (Cali 22.3 °C, Quito 12.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cali 28.2 °C (August), Quito 22.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Cali 16.5 °C, Quito 3.0 °C.
  • Quito gets 66% more rain and snow a year: 2,072 mm against 1,251 mm.
  • Quito gets about 164 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,875 h against 1,711 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
