How the climate of Changsha, Hunan, China compares with Hengyang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hengyang is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 19.1 °C against 18.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Hengyang is 0.9 °C warmer (Changsha, Hunan 5.7 °C, Hengyang 6.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changsha, Hunan 33.8 °C (July), Hengyang 35.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changsha, Hunan 2.8 °C, Hengyang 3.4 °C.
  • Changsha, Hunan gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,689 mm against 1,588 mm.
  • Hengyang gets about 23 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,612 h against 1,589 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changsha, Hunan 9.1 cm, Hengyang 6.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
