How the climate of Montréal, Canada compares with Ottawa, Canada: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Montréal is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 6.5 °C against 6.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Montréal is 0.7 °C warmer (Montréal -9.8 °C, Ottawa -10.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Montréal 25.5 °C (July), Ottawa 26.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Montréal -13.9 °C, Ottawa -15.1 °C.
  • Montréal gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 1,207 mm against 960 mm.
  • Ottawa gets about 25 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,993 h against 1,969 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Montréal 186 cm, Ottawa 175 cm (ERA5-Land).
