Montréal vs Ottawa climate

How the climate of Montréal, Canada compares with Ottawa, Canada, 165 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Montréal is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 6.8 °C against 6.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Montréal is 0.5 °C warmer (Montréal 8.4 °C, Ottawa 8.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Montréal 25.6 °C (July), Ottawa 26.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Montréal -14.2 °C, Ottawa -14.6 °C.
  • Montréal gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,109 mm against 974 mm.
  • Ottawa gets about 25 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,993 h against 1,969 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Montréal 217 cm, Ottawa 211 cm (ERA5-Land).
