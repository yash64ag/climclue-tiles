Montréal vs Ottawa climate

How the climate of Montréal, Canada compares with Ottawa, Canada, 165 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Montréal and Ottawa have the same yearly average temperature (6.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Ottawa is 0.6 °C warmer (Montréal 6.0 °C, Ottawa 6.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Montréal 26.0 °C (July), Ottawa 26.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Montréal -13.9 °C, Ottawa -14.6 °C.
  • Montréal gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 1,207 mm against 960 mm.
  • Ottawa gets about 25 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,993 h against 1,969 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Montréal 212 cm, Ottawa 185 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Ottawa in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Ottawa in 8 of 12 months
