How the climate of Cần Thơ, Vietnam compares with Gapan, Philippines: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Gapan is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 27.1 °C against 27.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Gapan is 0.7 °C warmer (Cần Thơ 28.4 °C, Gapan 29.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cần Thơ 33.8 °C (April), Gapan 34.8 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Cần Thơ 21.3 °C, Gapan 20.1 °C.
  • Gapan gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 2,010 mm against 1,717 mm.
  • Gapan gets about 479 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,495 h against 2,017 h; about 1961–1990).
