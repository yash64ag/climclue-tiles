Cần Thơ vs Gapan climate

How the climate of Cần Thơ, Vietnam compares with Gapan, Philippines, 1,745 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Cần Thơ and Gapan have the same yearly average temperature (27.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Gapan is 0.7 °C warmer (Cần Thơ 28.4 °C, Gapan 29.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cần Thơ 33.4 °C (April), Gapan 33.6 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Cần Thơ 21.8 °C, Gapan 20.3 °C.
  • Gapan gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 2,024 mm against 1,720 mm.
  • Gapan gets about 479 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,495 h against 2,017 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Gapan in 6 of 12 months (2 equal)
  • Drier: Cần Thơ in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Gapan in 12 of 12 months
