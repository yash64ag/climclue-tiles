Baotou vs Ordos climate

How the climate of Baotou, China compares with Ordos, China, 116 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Baotou is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 7.6 °C against 7.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Baotou is 1.4 °C warmer (Baotou 23.9 °C, Ordos 22.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baotou 29.6 °C (July), Ordos 27.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baotou -17.3 °C, Ordos -15.3 °C.
  • Ordos gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 333 mm against 327 mm.
  • Baotou gets about 57 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,964 h against 2,907 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baotou 30 cm, Ordos 34 cm (ERA5-Land).
