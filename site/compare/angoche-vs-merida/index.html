Angoche vs Mérida climate

How the climate of Angoche, Mozambique compares with Mérida, Mexico, 14,691 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mérida is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 26.4 °C against 25.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Mérida is 5.5 °C warmer (Angoche 22.6 °C, Mérida 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Angoche 30.6 °C (December), Mérida 35.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Angoche 19.9 °C, Mérida 18.9 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,018 mm and 1,021 mm).
  • Angoche gets about 423 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,058 h against 2,635 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
