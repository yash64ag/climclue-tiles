Angoche vs Mérida climate

How the climate of Angoche, Mozambique compares with Mérida, Mexico, 14,691 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mérida is 1.0 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 26.3 °C against 25.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Mérida is 5.5 °C warmer (Angoche 22.5 °C, Mérida 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Angoche 31.3 °C (December), Mérida 35.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Angoche 18.0 °C, Mérida 17.2 °C.
  • Mérida gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,016 mm against 1,007 mm.
  • Angoche gets about 423 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,058 h against 2,635 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
