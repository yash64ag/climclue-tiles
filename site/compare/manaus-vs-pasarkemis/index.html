Manaus vs Pasarkemis climate

How the climate of Manaus, Brazil compares with Pasarkemis, Indonesia, 18,201 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Pasarkemis is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Pasarkemis is 0.9 °C warmer (Manaus 26.4 °C, Pasarkemis 27.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manaus 32.3 °C (September), Pasarkemis 32.9 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Manaus 22.2 °C, Pasarkemis 21.5 °C.
  • Pasarkemis gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 2,522 mm against 2,429 mm.
  • Pasarkemis gets about 856 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,623 h against 1,767 h; about 1961–1990).
