How the climate of Queens, United States compares with The Bronx, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Queens is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 12.3 °C against 12.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Queens is 0.7 °C warmer (Queens 0.1 °C, The Bronx -0.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Queens 28.8 °C (July), The Bronx 29.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Queens -3.3 °C, The Bronx -4.9 °C.
  • The Bronx gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,216 mm against 1,199 mm.
  • Queens gets about 4 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,523 h against 2,519 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Queens 73 cm, The Bronx 83 cm (ERA5-Land).
