How the climate of Giá Rai, Vietnam compares with Quezon City, Philippines: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Quezon City is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 27.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Quezon City is 1.0 °C warmer (Giá Rai 28.4 °C, Quezon City 29.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Giá Rai 33.2 °C (April), Quezon City 34.7 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Giá Rai 21.8 °C, Quezon City 20.4 °C.
  • Quezon City gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 2,315 mm against 2,182 mm.
  • Quezon City gets about 291 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,360 h against 2,069 h; about 1961–1990).
