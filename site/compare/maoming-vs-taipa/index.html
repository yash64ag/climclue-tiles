Maoming vs Taipa climate

How the climate of Maoming, China compares with Taipa, Macao, 278 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Maoming is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 23.6 °C against 23.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Maoming is 1.3 °C warmer (Maoming 24.1 °C, Taipa 22.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maoming 31.5 °C (July), Taipa 29.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Maoming 12.5 °C, Taipa 13.7 °C.
  • Taipa gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 2,110 mm against 2,055 mm.
  • Taipa gets about 29 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,945 h against 1,916 h; about 1961–1990).
