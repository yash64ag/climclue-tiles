How the climate of Jinhua, Zhejiang, China compares with Yiwu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jinhua, Zhejiang is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 18.4 °C against 17.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Jinhua, Zhejiang is 0.6 °C warmer (Jinhua, Zhejiang 8.3 °C, Yiwu 7.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinhua, Zhejiang 34.7 °C (July), Yiwu 34.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinhua, Zhejiang 2.0 °C, Yiwu 1.4 °C.
  • Jinhua, Zhejiang gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,534 mm against 1,413 mm.
  • Jinhua, Zhejiang gets about 19 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,885 h against 1,866 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinhua, Zhejiang 12 cm, Yiwu 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
