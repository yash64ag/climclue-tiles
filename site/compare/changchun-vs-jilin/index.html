How the climate of Changchun, China compares with Jilin, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Changchun is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 6.5 °C against 5.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Changchun is 1.5 °C warmer (Changchun -14.4 °C, Jilin -15.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changchun 27.8 °C (July), Jilin 28.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changchun -19.4 °C, Jilin -22.7 °C.
  • Jilin gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 649 mm against 635 mm.
  • Changchun gets about 64 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,570 h against 2,506 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changchun 33 cm, Jilin 43 cm (ERA5-Land).
