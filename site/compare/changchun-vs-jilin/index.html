Changchun vs Jilin climate

How the climate of Changchun, China compares with Jilin, China, 99 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Changchun is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 6.3 °C against 5.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Changchun is 0.9 °C warmer (Changchun -9.7 °C, Jilin -10.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changchun 27.1 °C (July), Jilin 26.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changchun -20.9 °C, Jilin -22.4 °C.
  • Jilin gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 650 mm against 616 mm.
  • Changchun gets about 64 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,570 h against 2,506 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changchun 60 cm, Jilin 101 cm (ERA5-Land).
