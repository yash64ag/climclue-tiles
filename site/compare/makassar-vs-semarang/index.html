How the climate of Makassar, Indonesia compares with Semarang, Indonesia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Semarang is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 27.2 °C against 26.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Semarang is 0.8 °C warmer (Makassar 27.3 °C, Semarang 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Makassar 32.3 °C (September), Semarang 34.1 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Makassar 21.7 °C, Semarang 21.6 °C.
  • Makassar gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 3,326 mm against 2,475 mm.
  • Makassar gets about 17 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,852 h against 2,835 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Semarang in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: Makassar in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
