Changde vs Yichang climate

How the climate of Changde, China compares with Yichang, China, 191 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Changde is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 17.8 °C against 17.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Changde is 1.1 °C warmer (Changde 29.1 °C, Yichang 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changde 32.1 °C (July), Yichang 30.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changde 1.2 °C, Yichang 0.0 °C.
  • Changde gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,507 mm against 1,326 mm.
  • Yichang gets about 56 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,628 h against 1,572 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changde 14 cm, Yichang 8.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
