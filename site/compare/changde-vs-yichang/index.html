Changde vs Yichang climate

How the climate of Changde, China compares with Yichang, China, 191 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Changde is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 17.8 °C against 17.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Changde is 0.7 °C warmer (Changde 29.1 °C, Yichang 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changde 33.1 °C (July), Yichang 32.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changde 2.0 °C, Yichang 1.5 °C.
  • Changde gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 1,530 mm against 1,225 mm.
  • Yichang gets about 56 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,628 h against 1,572 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changde 14 cm, Yichang 8.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
