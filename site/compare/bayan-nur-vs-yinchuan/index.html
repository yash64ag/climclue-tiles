How the climate of Bayan Nur, China compares with Yinchuan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yinchuan is 1.5 °C (2.6 °F) warmer over the year: 9.9 °C against 8.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Yinchuan is 3.4 °C warmer (Bayan Nur -10.1 °C, Yinchuan -6.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bayan Nur 31.5 °C (July), Yinchuan 29.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bayan Nur -16.4 °C, Yinchuan -12.9 °C.
  • Yinchuan gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 191 mm against 164 mm.
  • Bayan Nur gets about 160 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,006 h against 2,845 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bayan Nur 6.4 cm, Yinchuan 8.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
