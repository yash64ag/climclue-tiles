Brussels vs Tilburg climate

How the climate of Brussels, Belgium compares with Tilburg, The Netherlands, 94 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Brussels is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 10.6 °C against 10.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Brussels is 0.4 °C warmer (Brussels 6.7 °C, Tilburg 6.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brussels 23.2 °C (July), Tilburg 23.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Brussels 0.7 °C, Tilburg 0.1 °C.
  • Tilburg gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 863 mm against 851 mm.
  • Brussels gets about 175 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,587 h against 1,411 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Brussels 15 cm, Tilburg 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
