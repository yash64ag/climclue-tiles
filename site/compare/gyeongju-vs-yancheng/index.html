Gyeongju vs Yancheng climate

How the climate of Gyeongju, South Korea compares with Yancheng, China, 873 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yancheng is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 15.0 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Yancheng is 1.5 °C warmer (Gyeongju 22.3 °C, Yancheng 23.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gyeongju 30.5 °C (August), Yancheng 30.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gyeongju -3.9 °C, Yancheng -2.2 °C.
  • Gyeongju gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,098 mm against 1,052 mm.
  • Gyeongju gets about 174 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,350 h against 2,176 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gyeongju 12 cm, Yancheng 10 cm (ERA5-Land).
