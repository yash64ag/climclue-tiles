Jinjiang vs Putian climate

How the climate of Jinjiang, China compares with Putian, China, 82 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jinjiang is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 21.4 °C against 20.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Jinjiang is 1.6 °C warmer (Jinjiang 13.2 °C, Putian 11.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinjiang 30.7 °C (July), Putian 31.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinjiang 9.5 °C, Putian 7.4 °C.
  • Putian gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 1,434 mm against 1,228 mm.
  • Jinjiang gets about 109 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,967 h against 1,859 h; about 1961–1990).
