Surat vs Vadodara climate

How the climate of Surat, India compares with Vadodara, India, 129 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Vadodara is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 27.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Vadodara is 2.5 °C warmer (Surat 30.4 °C, Vadodara 32.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Surat 36.3 °C (April), Vadodara 39.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Surat 13.8 °C, Vadodara 12.7 °C.
  • Surat gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 1,412 mm against 1,053 mm.
  • Vadodara gets about 51 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,916 h against 2,865 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Vadodara in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Vadodara in 11 of 12 months
