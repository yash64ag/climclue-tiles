Budta vs Zamboanga climate

How the climate of Budta, Philippines compares with Zamboanga, Philippines, 263 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Budta is 2.1 °C (3.8 °F) warmer over the year: 28.2 °C against 26.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Budta is 3.0 °C warmer (Budta 29.6 °C, Zamboanga 26.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Budta 33.3 °C (April), Zamboanga 28.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Budta 23.5 °C, Zamboanga 24.5 °C.
  • Zamboanga gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 2,000 mm against 1,540 mm.
  • Budta gets about 80 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,291 h against 2,211 h; about 1961–1990).
