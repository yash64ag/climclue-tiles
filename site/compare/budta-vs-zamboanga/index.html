Budta vs Zamboanga climate

How the climate of Budta, Philippines compares with Zamboanga, Philippines, 263 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Budta is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 28.2 °C against 27.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Budta is 2.0 °C warmer (Budta 29.6 °C, Zamboanga 27.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Budta 34.8 °C (April), Zamboanga 32.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Budta 23.0 °C, Zamboanga 22.0 °C.
  • Zamboanga gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,695 mm against 1,503 mm.
  • Budta gets about 80 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,291 h against 2,211 h; about 1961–1990).
