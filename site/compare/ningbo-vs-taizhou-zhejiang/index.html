Ningbo vs Taizhou, Zhejiang climate

How the climate of Ningbo, China compares with Taizhou, Zhejiang, China, 136 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Taizhou, Zhejiang is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 18.6 °C against 17.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Taizhou, Zhejiang is 1.8 °C warmer (Ningbo 7.2 °C, Taizhou, Zhejiang 8.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ningbo 31.9 °C (July), Taizhou, Zhejiang 32.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ningbo 2.4 °C, Taizhou, Zhejiang 3.3 °C.
  • Taizhou, Zhejiang gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,688 mm against 1,656 mm.
  • Ningbo gets about 60 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,871 h against 1,811 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ningbo 7.1 cm, Taizhou, Zhejiang 0.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
