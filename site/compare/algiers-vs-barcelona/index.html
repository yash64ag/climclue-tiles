Algiers vs Barcelona climate

How the climate of Algiers, Algeria compares with Barcelona, Spain, 524 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Algiers is 1.8 °C (3.2 °F) warmer over the year: 18.0 °C against 16.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Algiers is 2.1 °C warmer (Algiers 25.6 °C, Barcelona 23.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Algiers 30.4 °C (August), Barcelona 26.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Algiers 8.0 °C, Barcelona 7.0 °C.
  • Algiers gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 749 mm against 603 mm.
  • Algiers gets about 501 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,886 h against 2,385 h; about 1961–1990).
