Algiers vs Barcelona climate

How the climate of Algiers, Algeria compares with Barcelona, Spain, 524 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Algiers is 2.2 °C (4.0 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 16.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Algiers is 2.5 °C warmer (Algiers 26.4 °C, Barcelona 24.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Algiers 32.4 °C (August), Barcelona 28.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Algiers 6.7 °C, Barcelona 5.5 °C.
  • Algiers gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 682 mm against 634 mm.
  • Algiers gets about 501 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,886 h against 2,385 h; about 1961–1990).
