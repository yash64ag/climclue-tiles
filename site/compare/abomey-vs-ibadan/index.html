Abomey vs Ibadan climate

How the climate of Abomey, Benin compares with Ibadan, Nigeria, 212 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Abomey is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 26.3 °C against 25.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Abomey is 0.8 °C warmer (Abomey 27.6 °C, Ibadan 26.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abomey 34.6 °C (February), Ibadan 33.6 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Abomey 20.4 °C, Ibadan 20.7 °C.
  • Ibadan gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,397 mm against 1,236 mm.
  • Abomey gets about 243 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,173 h against 1,931 h; about 1961–1990).
