How the climate of Ashgabat, Turkmenistan compares with Mashhad, Iran: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ashgabat is 2.3 °C (4.2 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 15.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Ashgabat is 3.9 °C warmer (Ashgabat 31.3 °C, Mashhad 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ashgabat 38.7 °C (July), Mashhad 35.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ashgabat -0.3 °C, Mashhad -2.9 °C.
  • Ashgabat gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 274 mm against 261 mm.
  • Mashhad gets about 171 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,878 h against 2,708 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ashgabat 13 cm, Mashhad 33 cm (ERA5-Land).
