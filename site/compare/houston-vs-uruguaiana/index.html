How the climate of Houston, United States compares with Uruguaiana, Brazil: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Houston is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 21.2 °C against 20.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Houston is 15.4 °C warmer (Houston 29.0 °C, Uruguaiana 13.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Houston 34.1 °C (August), Uruguaiana 32.6 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Houston 7.1 °C, Uruguaiana 8.9 °C.
  • Uruguaiana gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,479 mm against 1,372 mm.
  • Houston gets about 26 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,576 h against 2,549 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
