Houston vs Uruguaiana climate

How the climate of Houston, United States compares with Uruguaiana, Brazil, 7,762 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Houston is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 20.6 °C against 20.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Uruguaiana is 14.8 °C warmer (Houston 11.7 °C, Uruguaiana 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Houston 33.3 °C (August), Uruguaiana 31.3 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Houston 8.0 °C, Uruguaiana 10.0 °C.
  • Uruguaiana gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,473 mm against 1,378 mm.
  • Houston gets about 26 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,576 h against 2,549 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
