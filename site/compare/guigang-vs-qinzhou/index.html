Guigang vs Qinzhou climate

How the climate of Guigang, China compares with Qinzhou, China, 159 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Qinzhou is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 22.9 °C against 21.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Qinzhou is 1.9 °C warmer (Guigang 12.6 °C, Qinzhou 14.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guigang 30.8 °C (August), Qinzhou 31.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Guigang 8.7 °C, Qinzhou 11.0 °C.
  • Qinzhou gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 2,420 mm against 1,960 mm.
  • Qinzhou gets about 69 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,777 h against 1,708 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Qinzhou in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Qinzhou in 9 of 12 months
