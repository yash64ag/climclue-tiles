How the climate of Suqian, China compares with Xuzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Xuzhou is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 15.1 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Xuzhou is 0.7 °C warmer (Suqian 24.8 °C, Xuzhou 25.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Suqian 31.0 °C (July), Xuzhou 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Suqian -3.7 °C, Xuzhou -3.3 °C.
  • Suqian gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 963 mm against 886 mm.
  • Xuzhou gets about 37 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,262 h against 2,225 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Suqian 15 cm, Xuzhou 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
