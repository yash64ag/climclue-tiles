Suqian vs Xuzhou climate

How the climate of Suqian, China compares with Xuzhou, China, 97 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Suqian and Xuzhou have the same yearly average temperature (14.8 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Xuzhou is 0.4 °C warmer (Suqian 14.9 °C, Xuzhou 15.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Suqian 30.8 °C (July), Xuzhou 30.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Suqian -3.8 °C, Xuzhou -4.0 °C.
  • Suqian gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 955 mm against 888 mm.
  • Xuzhou gets about 37 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,262 h against 2,225 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Suqian 21 cm, Xuzhou 23 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Xuzhou in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Xuzhou in 9 of 12 months
