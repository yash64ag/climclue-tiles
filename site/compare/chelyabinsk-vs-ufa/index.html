Chelyabinsk vs Ufa climate

How the climate of Chelyabinsk, Russia compares with Ufa, Russia, 352 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ufa is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 4.3 °C against 3.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Ufa is 1.9 °C warmer (Chelyabinsk -6.0 °C, Ufa -4.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chelyabinsk 23.2 °C (July), Ufa 24.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chelyabinsk -17.1 °C, Ufa -15.2 °C.
  • Ufa gets 42% more rain and snow a year: 651 mm against 460 mm.
  • Chelyabinsk gets about 140 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,084 h against 1,944 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chelyabinsk 123 cm, Ufa 229 cm (ERA5-Land).
