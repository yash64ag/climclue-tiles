Kabul vs Rawalpindi climate

How the climate of Kabul, Afghanistan compares with Rawalpindi, Pakistan, 372 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rawalpindi is 9.5 °C (17.1 °F) warmer over the year: 21.4 °C against 11.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Rawalpindi is 13.7 °C warmer (Kabul -0.8 °C, Rawalpindi 12.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kabul 31.4 °C (July), Rawalpindi 38.5 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Kabul -8.4 °C, Rawalpindi 2.8 °C.
  • Rawalpindi gets 286% more rain and snow a year: 1,338 mm against 346 mm.
  • Kabul gets about 142 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,031 h against 2,889 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kabul 128 cm, Rawalpindi 0 cm (ERA5-Land).
