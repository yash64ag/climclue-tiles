Kabul vs Rawalpindi climate

How the climate of Kabul, Afghanistan compares with Rawalpindi, Pakistan, 372 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rawalpindi is 9.7 °C (17.5 °F) warmer over the year: 21.4 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Rawalpindi is 14.0 °C warmer (Kabul -1.1 °C, Rawalpindi 12.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kabul 30.0 °C (July), Rawalpindi 36.7 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Kabul -10.7 °C, Rawalpindi 3.9 °C.
  • Rawalpindi gets 244% more rain and snow a year: 1,329 mm against 386 mm.
  • Kabul gets about 142 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,031 h against 2,889 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kabul 213 cm, Rawalpindi 0.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
