How the climate of Guangzhou, China compares with Macau, Macao: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Guangzhou is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.2 °C against 23.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Guangzhou is 1.2 °C warmer (Guangzhou 29.7 °C, Macau 28.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guangzhou 33.3 °C (July), Macau 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Guangzhou 10.7 °C, Macau 12.6 °C.
  • Macau gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 2,195 mm against 2,053 mm.
  • Macau gets about 145 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,945 h against 1,800 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Guangzhou in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Guangzhou in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Macau in 12 of 12 months
