Guangzhou vs Macau climate

How the climate of Guangzhou, China compares with Macau, Macao, 106 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Macau is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 22.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Macau is 1.3 °C warmer (Guangzhou 14.4 °C, Macau 15.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guangzhou 31.7 °C (July), Macau 29.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Guangzhou 10.1 °C, Macau 12.9 °C.
  • Guangzhou gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 2,108 mm against 2,096 mm.
  • Macau gets about 145 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,945 h against 1,800 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Macau in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Macau in 12 of 12 months
