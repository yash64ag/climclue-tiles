Bengaluru vs Tiruchirappalli climate

How the climate of Bengaluru, India compares with Tiruchirappalli, India, 268 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tiruchirappalli is 5.9 °C (10.6 °F) warmer over the year: 29.1 °C against 23.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Tiruchirappalli is 8.0 °C warmer (Bengaluru 22.6 °C, Tiruchirappalli 30.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bengaluru 32.6 °C (April), Tiruchirappalli 38.6 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Bengaluru 14.5 °C, Tiruchirappalli 19.9 °C.
  • Bengaluru gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 914 mm against 819 mm.
  • Tiruchirappalli gets about 118 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,493 h against 2,374 h; about 1961–1990).
