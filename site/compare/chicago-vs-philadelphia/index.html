How the climate of Chicago, United States compares with Philadelphia, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Philadelphia is 2.8 °C (5.0 °F) warmer over the year: 13.1 °C against 10.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Philadelphia is 4.7 °C warmer (Chicago -2.7 °C, Philadelphia 2.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chicago 29.8 °C (July), Philadelphia 30.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Chicago -8.4 °C, Philadelphia -3.9 °C.
  • Philadelphia gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,220 mm against 1,073 mm.
  • Chicago gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,470 h against 2,463 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chicago 81 cm, Philadelphia 47 cm (ERA5-Land).
