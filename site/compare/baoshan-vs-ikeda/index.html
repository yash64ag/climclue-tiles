Baoshan, Shanghai vs Ikeda climate

How the climate of Baoshan, Shanghai, China compares with Ikeda, Japan, 1,351 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Baoshan, Shanghai is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Baoshan, Shanghai is 1.0 °C warmer (Baoshan, Shanghai 28.1 °C, Ikeda 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoshan, Shanghai 31.4 °C (July), Ikeda 32.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Baoshan, Shanghai 1.1 °C, Ikeda 1.8 °C.
  • Ikeda gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,449 mm against 1,400 mm.
  • Baoshan, Shanghai gets about 213 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,986 h against 1,773 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoshan, Shanghai 7.1 cm, Ikeda 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
