Edmonton vs Yekaterinburg climate

How the climate of Edmonton, Canada compares with Yekaterinburg, Russia, 7,727 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Edmonton is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 3.2 °C against 2.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Edmonton is 3.0 °C warmer (Edmonton -9.3 °C, Yekaterinburg -12.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Edmonton 23.2 °C (July), Yekaterinburg 23.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Edmonton -17.0 °C, Yekaterinburg -17.3 °C.
  • Yekaterinburg gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 546 mm against 448 mm.
  • Edmonton gets about 322 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,198 h against 1,876 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Edmonton 89 cm, Yekaterinburg 152 cm (ERA5-Land).
