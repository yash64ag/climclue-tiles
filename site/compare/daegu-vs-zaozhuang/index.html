Daegu vs Zaozhuang climate

How the climate of Daegu, South Korea compares with Zaozhuang, China, 1,007 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zaozhuang is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 14.2 °C against 14.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Zaozhuang is 1.5 °C warmer (Daegu 23.1 °C, Zaozhuang 24.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daegu 29.8 °C (August), Zaozhuang 30.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Daegu -4.8 °C, Zaozhuang -4.5 °C.
  • Daegu gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 979 mm against 738 mm.
  • Daegu gets about 145 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,483 h against 2,337 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daegu 13 cm, Zaozhuang 13 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Zaozhuang in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Daegu in 8 of 12 months
