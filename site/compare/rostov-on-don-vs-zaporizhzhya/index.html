Rostov-on-Don vs Zaporizhzhya climate

How the climate of Rostov-on-Don, Russia compares with Zaporizhzhya, Ukraine, 352 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rostov-on-Don and Zaporizhzhya have the same yearly average temperature (10.0 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Rostov-on-Don is 0.8 °C warmer (Rostov-on-Don 23.4 °C, Zaporizhzhya 22.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rostov-on-Don 28.3 °C (July), Zaporizhzhya 26.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Rostov-on-Don -6.0 °C, Zaporizhzhya -4.7 °C.
  • Rostov-on-Don gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 620 mm against 501 mm.
  • Rostov-on-Don gets about 71 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,086 h against 2,015 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Rostov-on-Don 90 cm, Zaporizhzhya 92 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Zaporizhzhya in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Rostov-on-Don in 8 of 12 months
