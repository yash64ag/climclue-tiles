Rostov-on-Don vs Zaporizhzhya climate

How the climate of Rostov-on-Don, Russia compares with Zaporizhzhya, Ukraine, 352 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rostov-on-Don is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 10.1 °C against 9.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Rostov-on-Don is 1.0 °C warmer (Rostov-on-Don 23.6 °C, Zaporizhzhya 22.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Rostov-on-Don 29.3 °C (July), Zaporizhzhya 28.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Rostov-on-Don -6.0 °C, Zaporizhzhya -5.4 °C.
  • Rostov-on-Don gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 616 mm against 490 mm.
  • Rostov-on-Don gets about 71 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,086 h against 2,015 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Rostov-on-Don 98 cm, Zaporizhzhya 80 cm (ERA5-Land).
