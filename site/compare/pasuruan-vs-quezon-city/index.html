Pasuruan vs Quezon City climate

How the climate of Pasuruan, Indonesia compares with Quezon City, Philippines, 2,637 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Pasuruan is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 27.5 °C against 27.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Quezon City is 1.6 °C warmer (Pasuruan 27.5 °C, Quezon City 29.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pasuruan 33.4 °C (October), Quezon City 34.5 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Pasuruan 22.6 °C, Quezon City 21.2 °C.
  • Quezon City gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 2,315 mm against 1,828 mm.
  • Pasuruan gets about 980 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,340 h against 2,360 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
