Pasuruan vs Quezon City climate

How the climate of Pasuruan, Indonesia compares with Quezon City, Philippines, 2,637 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Pasuruan and Quezon City have the same yearly average temperature (27.5 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Quezon City is 1.6 °C warmer (Pasuruan 27.6 °C, Quezon City 29.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pasuruan 32.2 °C (October), Quezon City 32.1 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Pasuruan 23.1 °C, Quezon City 23.0 °C.
  • Quezon City gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 2,291 mm against 1,803 mm.
  • Pasuruan gets about 980 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,340 h against 2,360 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Pasuruan in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Pasuruan in 12 of 12 months
