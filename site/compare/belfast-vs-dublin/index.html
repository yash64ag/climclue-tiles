Belfast vs Dublin climate

How the climate of Belfast, United Kingdom compares with Dublin, Ireland, 142 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dublin is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 10.4 °C against 10.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Dublin is 0.8 °C warmer (Belfast 15.1 °C, Dublin 15.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belfast 19.0 °C (July), Dublin 19.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Belfast 3.1 °C, Dublin 3.3 °C.
  • Belfast gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,016 mm against 941 mm.
  • Dublin gets about 83 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,392 h against 1,309 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Belfast 5.2 cm, Dublin 4.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
