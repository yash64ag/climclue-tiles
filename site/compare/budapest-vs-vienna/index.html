How the climate of Budapest, Hungary compares with Vienna, Austria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Budapest is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 10.7 °C against 10.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Budapest is 0.8 °C warmer (Budapest 16.6 °C, Vienna 15.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Budapest 27.2 °C (July), Vienna 26.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Budapest -3.3 °C, Vienna -2.7 °C.
  • Vienna gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 626 mm against 618 mm.
  • Budapest gets about 161 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,899 h against 1,739 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Budapest 55 cm, Vienna 63 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Drier: Vienna in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Budapest in 10 of 12 months
