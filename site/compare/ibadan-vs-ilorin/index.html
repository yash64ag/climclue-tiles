Ibadan vs Ilorin climate

How the climate of Ibadan, Nigeria compares with Ilorin, Nigeria, 143 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ilorin is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 26.7 °C against 25.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Ilorin is 1.9 °C warmer (Ibadan 26.8 °C, Ilorin 28.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ibadan 34.2 °C (February), Ilorin 36.5 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Ibadan 21.4 °C, Ilorin 20.3 °C.
  • Ibadan gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 1,397 mm against 1,075 mm.
  • Ilorin gets about 288 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,219 h against 1,931 h; about 1961–1990).
