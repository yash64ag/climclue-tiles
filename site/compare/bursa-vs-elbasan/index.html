How the climate of Bursa, Turkey compares with Elbasan, Albania: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Elbasan is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 15.6 °C against 15.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Elbasan is 1.7 °C warmer (Bursa 9.0 °C, Elbasan 10.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bursa 31.1 °C (August), Elbasan 32.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bursa 1.8 °C, Elbasan 1.3 °C.
  • Elbasan gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,228 mm against 1,076 mm.
  • Bursa gets about 477 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,772 h against 2,295 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bursa 27 cm, Elbasan 8.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
