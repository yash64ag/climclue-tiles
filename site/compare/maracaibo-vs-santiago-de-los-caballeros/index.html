How the climate of Maracaibo, Venezuela compares with Santiago de los Caballeros, Dominican Republic: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Maracaibo is 3.6 °C (6.4 °F) warmer over the year: 28.7 °C against 25.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Maracaibo is 5.4 °C warmer (Maracaibo 28.6 °C, Santiago de los Caballeros 23.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maracaibo 33.9 °C (July), Santiago de los Caballeros 32.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Maracaibo 23.5 °C, Santiago de los Caballeros 17.4 °C.
  • Santiago de los Caballeros gets 87% more rain and snow a year: 1,242 mm against 663 mm.
  • Maracaibo gets about 91 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,580 h against 2,489 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Maracaibo in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Maracaibo in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Santiago de los Caballeros in 7 of 12 months
