Maracaibo vs Santiago de los Caballeros climate

How the climate of Maracaibo, Venezuela compares with Santiago de los Caballeros, Dominican Republic, 984 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Maracaibo is 3.6 °C (6.4 °F) warmer over the year: 28.5 °C against 24.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Maracaibo is 5.4 °C warmer (Maracaibo 28.4 °C, Santiago de los Caballeros 22.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maracaibo 33.8 °C (March), Santiago de los Caballeros 32.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Maracaibo 23.9 °C, Santiago de los Caballeros 18.7 °C.
  • Santiago de los Caballeros gets 94% more rain and snow a year: 1,304 mm against 673 mm.
  • Maracaibo gets about 91 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,580 h against 2,489 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Maracaibo in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Santiago de los Caballeros in 7 of 12 months
