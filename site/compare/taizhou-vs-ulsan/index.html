Taizhou, Jiangsu vs Ulsan climate

How the climate of Taizhou, Jiangsu, China compares with Ulsan, South Korea, 930 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Taizhou, Jiangsu is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 15.5 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Taizhou, Jiangsu is 2.7 °C warmer (Taizhou, Jiangsu 24.2 °C, Ulsan 21.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Taizhou, Jiangsu 31.3 °C (July), Ulsan 30.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Taizhou, Jiangsu -1.5 °C, Ulsan -2.2 °C.
  • Ulsan gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,186 mm against 1,115 mm.
  • Ulsan gets about 212 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,290 h against 2,078 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Taizhou, Jiangsu 14 cm, Ulsan 6.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
