Accra vs Kumasi climate

How the climate of Accra, Ghana compares with Kumasi, Ghana, 202 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Accra is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 26.3 °C against 26.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Accra is 1.1 °C warmer (Accra 26.9 °C, Kumasi 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Accra 29.7 °C (March), Kumasi 33.5 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Accra 22.9 °C, Kumasi 21.7 °C.
  • Kumasi gets 58% more rain and snow a year: 1,335 mm against 847 mm.
  • Accra gets about 331 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,268 h against 1,937 h; about 1961–1990).
