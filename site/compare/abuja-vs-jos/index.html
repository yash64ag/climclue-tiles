Abuja vs Jos climate

How the climate of Abuja, Nigeria compares with Jos, Nigeria, 181 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Abuja is 4.6 °C (8.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.2 °C against 21.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Abuja is 7.7 °C warmer (Abuja 27.0 °C, Jos 19.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abuja 35.1 °C (March), Jos 31.5 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Abuja 20.7 °C, Jos 12.7 °C.
  • Abuja gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 1,467 mm against 1,201 mm.
  • Jos gets about 172 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,645 h against 2,472 h; about 1961–1990).
