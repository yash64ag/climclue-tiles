Dombivali vs Thāne climate

How the climate of Dombivali, India compares with Thāne, India, 13 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Dombivali is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Dombivali is 0.7 °C warmer (Dombivali 27.1 °C, Thāne 26.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dombivali 34.9 °C (April), Thāne 32.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Dombivali 17.4 °C, Thāne 18.8 °C.
  • Thāne gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 2,629 mm against 2,138 mm.
  • Dombivali gets about 25 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,700 h against 2,675 h; about 1961–1990).
