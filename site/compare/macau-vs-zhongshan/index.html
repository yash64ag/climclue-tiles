Macau vs Zhongshan climate

How the climate of Macau, Macao compares with Zhongshan, China, 40 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhongshan is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 23.3 °C against 23.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Zhongshan is 0.8 °C warmer (Macau 28.6 °C, Zhongshan 29.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Macau 29.8 °C (August), Zhongshan 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Macau 12.9 °C, Zhongshan 11.1 °C.
  • Macau gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 2,096 mm against 2,016 mm.
  • Macau gets about 58 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,945 h against 1,887 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
