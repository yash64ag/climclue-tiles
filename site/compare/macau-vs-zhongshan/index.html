Macau vs Zhongshan, Guangdong climate

How the climate of Macau, Macao compares with Zhongshan, Guangdong, China, 40 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhongshan, Guangdong is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 23.3 °C against 23.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Zhongshan, Guangdong is 0.9 °C warmer (Macau 28.6 °C, Zhongshan, Guangdong 29.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Macau 31.4 °C (July), Zhongshan, Guangdong 32.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Macau 12.6 °C, Zhongshan, Guangdong 11.5 °C.
  • Macau gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 2,195 mm against 2,037 mm.
  • Macau gets about 58 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,945 h against 1,887 h; about 1961–1990).
