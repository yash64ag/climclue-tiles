How the climate of Munich, Germany compares with Vienna, Austria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Vienna is 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) warmer over the year: 10.5 °C against 9.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Vienna is 2.7 °C warmer (Munich 18.4 °C, Vienna 21.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Munich 23.7 °C (July), Vienna 26.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Munich -3.5 °C, Vienna -2.7 °C.
  • Munich gets 61% more rain and snow a year: 1,006 mm against 626 mm.
  • Vienna gets about 84 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,739 h against 1,655 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Munich 97 cm, Vienna 63 cm (ERA5-Land).
