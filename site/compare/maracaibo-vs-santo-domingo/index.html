Maracaibo vs Santo Domingo climate

How the climate of Maracaibo, Venezuela compares with Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, 890 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Maracaibo is 3.1 °C (5.6 °F) warmer over the year: 28.7 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Maracaibo is 4.4 °C warmer (Maracaibo 28.6 °C, Santo Domingo 24.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maracaibo 33.9 °C (July), Santo Domingo 31.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Maracaibo 23.5 °C, Santo Domingo 18.9 °C.
  • Santo Domingo gets 155% more rain and snow a year: 1,688 mm against 663 mm.
  • Santo Domingo gets about 195 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,775 h against 2,580 h; about 1961–1990).
