Maracaibo vs Santo Domingo climate

How the climate of Maracaibo, Venezuela compares with Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, 890 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Maracaibo is 3.0 °C (5.3 °F) warmer over the year: 28.5 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Maracaibo is 4.3 °C warmer (Maracaibo 28.9 °C, Santo Domingo 24.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maracaibo 33.8 °C (March), Santo Domingo 29.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Maracaibo 23.9 °C, Santo Domingo 21.3 °C.
  • Santo Domingo gets 154% more rain and snow a year: 1,711 mm against 673 mm.
  • Santo Domingo gets about 195 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,775 h against 2,580 h; about 1961–1990).
