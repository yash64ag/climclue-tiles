Dakar vs Pikine climate

How the climate of Dakar, Senegal compares with Pikine, Senegal, 10 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Pikine is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 24.4 °C against 24.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Pikine is 0.4 °C warmer (Dakar 21.0 °C, Pikine 21.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dakar 31.0 °C (October), Pikine 31.1 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Dakar 16.1 °C, Pikine 16.5 °C.
  • Dakar gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 393 mm against 385 mm.
  • Pikine gets about 5 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,977 h against 2,972 h; about 1961–1990).
