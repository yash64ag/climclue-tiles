Dakar vs Pikine climate

How the climate of Dakar, Senegal compares with Pikine, Senegal, 10 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Pikine is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 24.6 °C against 24.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Pikine is 0.9 °C warmer (Dakar 20.9 °C, Pikine 21.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dakar 28.4 °C (October), Pikine 29.4 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Dakar 19.9 °C, Pikine 19.6 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (390 mm and 391 mm).
  • Pikine gets about 5 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,977 h against 2,972 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Pikine in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Dakar in 6 of 12 months (5 equal)
  • Sunnier: Pikine in 11 of 12 months (1 equal)
