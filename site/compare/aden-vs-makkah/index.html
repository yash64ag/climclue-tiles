Aden vs Makkah climate

How the climate of Aden, Yemen compares with Makkah, Saudi Arabia, 1,109 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Makkah is 1.7 °C (3.0 °F) warmer over the year: 29.1 °C against 27.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Makkah is 4.7 °C warmer (Aden 29.4 °C, Makkah 34.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aden 31.8 °C (July), Makkah 40.6 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Aden 23.2 °C, Makkah 15.4 °C.
  • Makkah gets 55% more rain and snow a year: 131 mm against 84 mm.
  • Makkah gets about 135 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,268 h against 3,132 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Makkah in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Makkah in 9 of 12 months
