Bannu vs Rawalpindi climate

How the climate of Bannu, Pakistan compares with Rawalpindi, Pakistan, 237 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bannu is 2.3 °C (4.1 °F) warmer over the year: 23.7 °C against 21.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Bannu is 3.5 °C warmer (Bannu 34.5 °C, Rawalpindi 30.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bannu 39.6 °C (June), Rawalpindi 36.7 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Bannu 4.9 °C, Rawalpindi 3.9 °C.
  • Rawalpindi gets 136% more rain and snow a year: 1,329 mm against 562 mm.
  • Bannu gets about 98 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,987 h against 2,889 h; about 1961–1990).
