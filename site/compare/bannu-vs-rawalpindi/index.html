Bannu vs Rawalpindi climate

How the climate of Bannu, Pakistan compares with Rawalpindi, Pakistan, 237 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bannu is 2.0 °C (3.6 °F) warmer over the year: 23.4 °C against 21.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Bannu is 3.2 °C warmer (Bannu 34.2 °C, Rawalpindi 31.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bannu 41.8 °C (June), Rawalpindi 38.5 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Bannu 4.1 °C, Rawalpindi 2.8 °C.
  • Rawalpindi gets 132% more rain and snow a year: 1,338 mm against 576 mm.
  • Bannu gets about 98 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,987 h against 2,889 h; about 1961–1990).
