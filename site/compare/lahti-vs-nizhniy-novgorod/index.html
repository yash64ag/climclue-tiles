Lahti vs Nizhniy Novgorod climate

How the climate of Lahti, Finland compares with Nizhniy Novgorod, Russia, 1,175 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Nizhniy Novgorod is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 4.8 °C against 4.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Lahti is 3.1 °C warmer (Lahti -4.5 °C, Nizhniy Novgorod -7.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lahti 21.2 °C (July), Nizhniy Novgorod 23.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lahti -9.7 °C, Nizhniy Novgorod -11.8 °C.
  • Nizhniy Novgorod gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 678 mm against 671 mm.
  • Nizhniy Novgorod gets about 365 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,802 h against 1,437 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lahti 204 cm, Nizhniy Novgorod 205 cm (ERA5-Land).
