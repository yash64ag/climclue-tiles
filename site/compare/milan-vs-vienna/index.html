Milan vs Vienna climate

How the climate of Milan, Italy compares with Vienna, Austria, 625 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Milan is 3.1 °C (5.6 °F) warmer over the year: 13.6 °C against 10.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Milan is 3.8 °C warmer (Milan 9.4 °C, Vienna 5.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Milan 29.2 °C (July), Vienna 25.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Milan -0.9 °C, Vienna -3.6 °C.
  • Milan gets 57% more rain and snow a year: 981 mm against 626 mm.
  • Milan gets about 194 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,933 h against 1,739 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Milan 28 cm, Vienna 63 cm (ERA5-Land).
