Amarillo vs Heze climate

How the climate of Amarillo, United States compares with Heze, China, 11,277 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Heze is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 14.8 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Heze is 2.6 °C warmer (Amarillo 23.4 °C, Heze 26.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amarillo 33.0 °C (July), Heze 31.9 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Amarillo -5.1 °C, Heze -4.8 °C.
  • Heze gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 647 mm against 550 mm.
  • Amarillo gets about 885 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 2,358 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amarillo 23 cm, Heze 11 cm (ERA5-Land).
