How the climate of Putian, China compares with Quanzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Quanzhou is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 21.5 °C against 20.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Quanzhou is 1.5 °C warmer (Putian 12.1 °C, Quanzhou 13.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Putian 32.5 °C (July), Quanzhou 32.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Putian 8.3 °C, Quanzhou 9.5 °C.
  • Putian gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 1,434 mm against 1,162 mm.
  • Quanzhou gets about 99 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,958 h against 1,859 h; about 1961–1990).
