Putian vs Quanzhou climate

How the climate of Putian, China compares with Quanzhou, China, 72 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Quanzhou is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 21.4 °C against 20.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Quanzhou is 1.5 °C warmer (Putian 12.1 °C, Quanzhou 13.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Putian 31.0 °C (July), Quanzhou 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Putian 7.6 °C, Quanzhou 8.3 °C.
  • Putian gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,324 mm against 1,251 mm.
  • Quanzhou gets about 99 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,958 h against 1,859 h; about 1961–1990).
