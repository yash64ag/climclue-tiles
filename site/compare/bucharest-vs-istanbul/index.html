Bucharest vs Istanbul climate

How the climate of Bucharest, Romania compares with Istanbul, Turkey, 445 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Istanbul is 3.2 °C (5.7 °F) warmer over the year: 14.8 °C against 11.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Istanbul is 8.5 °C warmer (Bucharest 0.3 °C, Istanbul 8.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bucharest 27.9 °C (July), Istanbul 26.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bucharest -4.5 °C, Istanbul 4.1 °C.
  • Istanbul gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 783 mm against 619 mm.
  • Istanbul gets about 688 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,763 h against 2,075 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bucharest 68 cm, Istanbul 16 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Istanbul in 10 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Istanbul in 12 of 12 months
