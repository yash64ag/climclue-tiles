Bucharest vs Istanbul climate

How the climate of Bucharest, Romania compares with Istanbul, Turkey, 445 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Istanbul is 3.1 °C (5.6 °F) warmer over the year: 14.7 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Istanbul is 8.3 °C warmer (Bucharest 0.3 °C, Istanbul 8.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bucharest 27.9 °C (July), Istanbul 26.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bucharest -4.5 °C, Istanbul 3.9 °C.
  • Istanbul gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 813 mm against 611 mm.
  • Istanbul gets about 688 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,763 h against 2,075 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bucharest 68 cm, Istanbul 16 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Istanbul in 9 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Istanbul in 12 of 12 months
