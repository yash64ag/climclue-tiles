How the climate of Moscow, Russia compares with Saint Petersburg, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Moscow is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 5.8 °C against 5.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Moscow is 1.9 °C warmer (Moscow 13.2 °C, Saint Petersburg 11.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Moscow 24.3 °C (July), Saint Petersburg 22.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Moscow -10.0 °C, Saint Petersburg -8.7 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (691 mm and 690 mm).
  • Moscow gets about 80 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,701 h against 1,621 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Moscow 185 cm, Saint Petersburg 179 cm (ERA5-Land).
