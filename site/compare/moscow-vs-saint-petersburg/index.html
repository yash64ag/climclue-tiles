Moscow vs Saint Petersburg climate

How the climate of Moscow, Russia compares with Saint Petersburg, Russia, 635 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Moscow is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 5.4 °C against 4.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Moscow is 2.8 °C warmer (Moscow 13.1 °C, Saint Petersburg 10.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Moscow 23.0 °C (July), Saint Petersburg 20.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Moscow -10.8 °C, Saint Petersburg -9.3 °C.
  • Saint Petersburg gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 699 mm against 691 mm.
  • Moscow gets about 80 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,701 h against 1,621 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Moscow 185 cm, Saint Petersburg 179 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Moscow in 7 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Moscow in 10 of 12 months
