Barquisimeto vs Maracaibo climate

How the climate of Barquisimeto, Venezuela compares with Maracaibo, Venezuela, 255 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Maracaibo is 4.0 °C (7.2 °F) warmer over the year: 28.7 °C against 24.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Maracaibo is 5.2 °C warmer (Barquisimeto 24.1 °C, Maracaibo 29.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Barquisimeto 30.9 °C (March), Maracaibo 33.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Barquisimeto 18.5 °C, Maracaibo 23.5 °C.
  • Barquisimeto gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 700 mm against 663 mm.
  • Barquisimeto gets about 21 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,600 h against 2,580 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
