How the climate of Barquisimeto, Venezuela compares with Maracaibo, Venezuela: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Maracaibo is 3.8 °C (6.8 °F) warmer over the year: 28.8 °C against 25.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Maracaibo is 5.5 °C warmer (Barquisimeto 24.1 °C, Maracaibo 29.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Barquisimeto 32.1 °C (March), Maracaibo 34.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Barquisimeto 19.5 °C, Maracaibo 23.7 °C.
  • Barquisimeto gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 700 mm against 663 mm.
  • Barquisimeto gets about 21 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,600 h against 2,580 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Maracaibo in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Maracaibo in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Maracaibo in 8 of 12 months
