Wuwei vs Xining climate

How the climate of Wuwei, China compares with Xining, China, 164 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Wuwei is 2.9 °C (5.2 °F) warmer over the year: 9.1 °C against 6.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Wuwei is 5.6 °C warmer (Wuwei 22.4 °C, Xining 16.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Wuwei 28.7 °C (July), Xining 22.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Wuwei -12.3 °C, Xining -14.3 °C.
  • Xining gets 169% more rain and snow a year: 433 mm against 161 mm.
  • Wuwei gets about 79 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,814 h against 2,734 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Wuwei 9.6 cm, Xining 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
