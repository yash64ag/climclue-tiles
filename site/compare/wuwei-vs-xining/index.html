Wuwei vs Xining climate

How the climate of Wuwei, China compares with Xining, China, 164 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Wuwei is 2.3 °C (4.1 °F) warmer over the year: 9.0 °C against 6.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Wuwei is 5.1 °C warmer (Wuwei 22.2 °C, Xining 17.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Wuwei 29.1 °C (July), Xining 23.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Wuwei -13.2 °C, Xining -13.6 °C.
  • Xining gets 142% more rain and snow a year: 410 mm against 169 mm.
  • Wuwei gets about 79 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,814 h against 2,734 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Wuwei 9.6 cm, Xining 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
