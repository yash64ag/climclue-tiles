Diyarbakır vs Khorramabad climate

How the climate of Diyarbakır, Turkey compares with Khorramabad, Iran, 884 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Diyarbakır is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 16.3 °C against 16.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Diyarbakır is 1.4 °C warmer (Diyarbakır 31.3 °C, Khorramabad 29.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Diyarbakır 37.7 °C (July), Khorramabad 37.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Diyarbakır -2.1 °C, Khorramabad -4.1 °C.
  • Diyarbakır gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 567 mm against 537 mm.
  • Diyarbakır gets about 113 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,017 h against 2,904 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Diyarbakır 59 cm, Khorramabad 188 cm (ERA5-Land).
