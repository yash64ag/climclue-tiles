Diyarbakır vs Khorramabad climate

How the climate of Diyarbakır, Turkey compares with Khorramabad, Iran, 884 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Diyarbakır and Khorramabad have the same yearly average temperature (16.2 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Khorramabad is 1.3 °C warmer (Diyarbakır 9.6 °C, Khorramabad 10.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Diyarbakır 39.1 °C (July), Khorramabad 40.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Diyarbakır -1.6 °C, Khorramabad -3.4 °C.
  • Khorramabad gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 561 mm against 558 mm.
  • Diyarbakır gets about 113 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,017 h against 2,904 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Diyarbakır 39 cm, Khorramabad 36 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Khorramabad in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Diyarbakır in 7 of 12 months
