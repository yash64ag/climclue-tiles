Main differences

  • Sariwŏn-si is 0.4 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 11.4 °C against 11.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Sariwŏn-si is 2.3 °C warmer (Jinzhong 17.8 °C, Sariwŏn-si 20.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinzhong 30.0 °C (July), Sariwŏn-si 29.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Jinzhong -10.7 °C, Sariwŏn-si -9.4 °C.
