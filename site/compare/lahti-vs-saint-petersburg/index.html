Lahti vs Saint Petersburg climate

How the climate of Lahti, Finland compares with Saint Petersburg, Russia, 280 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Saint Petersburg is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 4.8 °C against 4.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Saint Petersburg is 0.7 °C warmer (Lahti 3.6 °C, Saint Petersburg 4.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lahti 21.0 °C (July), Saint Petersburg 20.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lahti -9.7 °C, Saint Petersburg -9.2 °C.
  • Saint Petersburg gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 690 mm against 667 mm.
  • Saint Petersburg gets about 184 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,621 h against 1,437 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lahti 189 cm, Saint Petersburg 179 cm (ERA5-Land).
