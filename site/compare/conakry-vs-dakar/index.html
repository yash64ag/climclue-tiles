How the climate of Conakry, Guinea compares with Dakar, Senegal: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Conakry is 2.0 °C (3.7 °F) warmer over the year: 26.2 °C against 24.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Conakry is 5.2 °C warmer (Conakry 26.4 °C, Dakar 21.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Conakry 32.0 °C (December), Dakar 31.0 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Conakry 20.9 °C, Dakar 16.1 °C.
  • Conakry gets 794% more rain and snow a year: 3,517 mm against 393 mm.
  • Dakar gets about 786 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,972 h against 2,185 h; about 1961–1990).
