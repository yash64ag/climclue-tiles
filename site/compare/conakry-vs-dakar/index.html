Conakry vs Dakar climate

How the climate of Conakry, Guinea compares with Dakar, Senegal, 704 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Conakry is 2.0 °C (3.5 °F) warmer over the year: 26.1 °C against 24.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Conakry is 5.3 °C warmer (Conakry 26.4 °C, Dakar 21.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Conakry 28.4 °C (December), Dakar 28.4 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Conakry 24.2 °C, Dakar 19.9 °C.
  • Conakry gets 814% more rain and snow a year: 3,562 mm against 390 mm.
  • Dakar gets about 786 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,972 h against 2,185 h; about 1961–1990).
