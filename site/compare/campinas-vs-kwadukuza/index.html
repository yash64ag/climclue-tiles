Campinas, São Paulo vs KwaDukuza climate

How the climate of Campinas, São Paulo, Brazil compares with KwaDukuza, South Africa, 7,710 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Campinas, São Paulo and KwaDukuza have the same yearly average temperature (21.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Campinas, São Paulo is 1.3 °C warmer (Campinas, São Paulo 21.7 °C, KwaDukuza 20.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Campinas, São Paulo 28.7 °C (January), KwaDukuza 28.7 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Campinas, São Paulo 10.8 °C, KwaDukuza 11.0 °C.
  • Campinas, São Paulo gets 54% more rain and snow a year: 1,607 mm against 1,041 mm.
  • KwaDukuza gets about 145 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,617 h against 2,472 h; about 1961–1990).
