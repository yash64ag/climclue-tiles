How the climate of Aleppo, Syria compares with Gaziantep, Turkey: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Aleppo is 2.8 °C (5.0 °F) warmer over the year: 18.1 °C against 15.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Aleppo is 3.7 °C warmer (Aleppo 8.1 °C, Gaziantep 4.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aleppo 35.9 °C (July), Gaziantep 35.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Aleppo 2.8 °C, Gaziantep -0.9 °C.
  • Gaziantep gets 88% more rain and snow a year: 689 mm against 366 mm.
  • Gaziantep gets about 123 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,122 h against 2,999 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Aleppo 2.4 cm, Gaziantep 84 cm (ERA5-Land).
