Aleppo vs Gaziantep climate

How the climate of Aleppo, Syria compares with Gaziantep, Turkey, 97 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Aleppo is 2.8 °C (5.1 °F) warmer over the year: 17.8 °C against 15.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Aleppo is 3.7 °C warmer (Aleppo 7.8 °C, Gaziantep 4.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aleppo 35.8 °C (July), Gaziantep 34.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Aleppo 2.1 °C, Gaziantep -1.1 °C.
  • Gaziantep gets 76% more rain and snow a year: 705 mm against 400 mm.
  • Gaziantep gets about 123 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,122 h against 2,999 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Aleppo 5.2 cm, Gaziantep 49 cm (ERA5-Land).
