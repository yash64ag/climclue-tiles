Philadelphia vs Toronto climate

How the climate of Philadelphia, United States compares with Toronto, Canada, 540 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Philadelphia is 4.5 °C (8.1 °F) warmer over the year: 13.1 °C against 8.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Philadelphia is 7.0 °C warmer (Philadelphia 12.2 °C, Toronto 5.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Philadelphia 29.9 °C (July), Toronto 24.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Philadelphia -3.3 °C, Toronto -6.7 °C.
  • Philadelphia gets 39% more rain and snow a year: 1,215 mm against 874 mm.
  • Philadelphia gets about 461 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,463 h against 2,002 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Philadelphia 66 cm, Toronto 117 cm (ERA5-Land).
