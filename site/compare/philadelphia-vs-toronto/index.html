How the climate of Philadelphia, United States compares with Toronto, Canada: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Philadelphia is 4.7 °C (8.5 °F) warmer over the year: 13.6 °C against 8.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Philadelphia is 6.0 °C warmer (Philadelphia 6.8 °C, Toronto 0.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Philadelphia 30.8 °C (July), Toronto 26.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Philadelphia -3.0 °C, Toronto -7.7 °C.
  • Philadelphia gets 39% more rain and snow a year: 1,220 mm against 875 mm.
  • Philadelphia gets about 461 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,463 h against 2,002 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Philadelphia 47 cm, Toronto 96 cm (ERA5-Land).
