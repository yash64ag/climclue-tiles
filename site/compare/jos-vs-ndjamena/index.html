Jos vs N'Djamena climate

How the climate of Jos, Nigeria compares with N'Djamena, Chad, 714 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • N'Djamena is 7.2 °C (13.0 °F) warmer over the year: 28.8 °C against 21.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when N'Djamena is 10.3 °C warmer (Jos 23.3 °C, N'Djamena 33.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jos 31.1 °C (April), N'Djamena 41.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Jos 11.9 °C, N'Djamena 15.1 °C.
  • Jos gets 146% more rain and snow a year: 1,201 mm against 487 mm.
  • N'Djamena gets about 522 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,166 h against 2,645 h; about 1961–1990).
