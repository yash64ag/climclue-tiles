Hyderabad vs Rājkot climate

How the climate of Hyderabad, Pakistan compares with Rājkot, India, 424 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Hyderabad is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Hyderabad is 2.9 °C warmer (Hyderabad 31.4 °C, Rājkot 28.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hyderabad 40.5 °C (May), Rājkot 39.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Hyderabad 11.2 °C, Rājkot 13.4 °C.
  • Rājkot gets 335% more rain and snow a year: 741 mm against 171 mm.
  • Hyderabad gets about 226 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,301 h against 3,075 h; about 1961–1990).
