Hyderabad vs Rājkot climate

How the climate of Hyderabad, Pakistan compares with Rājkot, India, 424 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hyderabad is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Hyderabad is 3.0 °C warmer (Hyderabad 31.5 °C, Rājkot 28.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hyderabad 40.9 °C (May), Rājkot 39.3 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Hyderabad 10.0 °C, Rājkot 11.4 °C.
  • Rājkot gets 334% more rain and snow a year: 738 mm against 170 mm.
  • Hyderabad gets about 226 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,301 h against 3,075 h; about 1961–1990).
