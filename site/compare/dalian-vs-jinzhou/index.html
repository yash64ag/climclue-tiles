How the climate of Dalian, China compares with Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E), China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Dalian is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 11.7 °C against 10.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Dalian is 4.5 °C warmer (Dalian 0.3 °C, Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) -4.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dalian 27.6 °C (August), Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) 28.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Dalian -6.1 °C, Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) -11.9 °C.
  • Dalian gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 597 mm against 582 mm.
  • Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) gets about 14 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,677 h against 2,662 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Dalian 17 cm, Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
