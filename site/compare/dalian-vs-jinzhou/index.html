Dalian vs Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) climate

Main differences

  • Dalian is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 11.1 °C against 10.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Dalian is 6.0 °C warmer (Dalian 1.1 °C, Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) -4.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dalian 25.5 °C (August), Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) 28.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Dalian -4.4 °C, Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) -12.6 °C.
  • Dalian gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 591 mm against 584 mm.
  • Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) gets about 14 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,677 h against 2,662 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Dalian 17 cm, Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
