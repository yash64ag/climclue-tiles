Kabul vs Quetta climate

How the climate of Kabul, Afghanistan compares with Quetta, Pakistan, 524 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Quetta is 5.6 °C (10.0 °F) warmer over the year: 17.5 °C against 11.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Quetta is 7.9 °C warmer (Kabul -2.9 °C, Quetta 5.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kabul 31.4 °C (July), Quetta 36.8 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Kabul -8.4 °C, Quetta -2.0 °C.
  • Kabul gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 346 mm against 317 mm.
  • Quetta gets about 302 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,333 h against 3,031 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kabul 128 cm, Quetta 82 cm (ERA5-Land).
