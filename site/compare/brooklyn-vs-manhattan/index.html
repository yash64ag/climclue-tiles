Brooklyn vs Manhattan climate

How the climate of Brooklyn, United States compares with Manhattan, United States, 15 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Brooklyn and Manhattan have the same yearly average temperature (12.3 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Brooklyn is 0.4 °C warmer (Brooklyn -0.1 °C, Manhattan -0.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brooklyn 27.7 °C (July), Manhattan 28.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Brooklyn -3.3 °C, Manhattan -3.9 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,218 mm and 1,212 mm).
  • Manhattan gets about 12 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,529 h against 2,518 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Brooklyn 83 cm, Manhattan 86 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Brooklyn in 6 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: Manhattan in 9 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Sunnier: Manhattan in 8 of 12 months
