Main differences

  • Brooklyn is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 12.8 °C against 12.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Brooklyn is 0.5 °C warmer (Brooklyn 0.5 °C, Manhattan, New York 0.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brooklyn 29.0 °C (July), Manhattan, New York 29.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Brooklyn -3.0 °C, Manhattan, New York -3.4 °C.
  • Brooklyn gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,240 mm against 1,232 mm.
  • Manhattan, New York gets about 12 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,529 h against 2,518 h; about 1961–1990).
