How the climate of Liaocheng, China compares with Tai’an, Shandong, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Liaocheng is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 14.4 °C against 13.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Liaocheng is 1.7 °C warmer (Liaocheng 26.1 °C, Tai’an, Shandong 24.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Liaocheng 32.0 °C (June), Tai’an, Shandong 30.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Liaocheng -5.9 °C, Tai’an, Shandong -6.2 °C.
  • Tai’an, Shandong gets 39% more rain and snow a year: 809 mm against 583 mm.
  • Tai’an, Shandong gets about 3 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,496 h against 2,493 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Liaocheng 8.4 cm, Tai’an, Shandong 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
