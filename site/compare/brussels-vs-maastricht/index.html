How the climate of Brussels, Belgium compares with Maastricht, The Netherlands: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Brussels is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 10.8 °C against 10.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Brussels is 0.7 °C warmer (Brussels 4.4 °C, Maastricht 3.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brussels 23.3 °C (July), Maastricht 23.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Brussels 0.6 °C, Maastricht 0.3 °C.
  • Brussels gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 851 mm against 841 mm.
  • Brussels gets about 166 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,587 h against 1,420 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Brussels 15 cm, Maastricht 23 cm (ERA5-Land).
