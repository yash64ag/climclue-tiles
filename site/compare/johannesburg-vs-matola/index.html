How the climate of Johannesburg, South Africa compares with Matola, Mozambique: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Matola is 8.1 °C (14.6 °F) warmer over the year: 22.9 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Matola is 11.1 °C warmer (Johannesburg 8.3 °C, Matola 19.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Johannesburg 24.7 °C (January), Matola 30.5 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Johannesburg 1.1 °C, Matola 12.7 °C.
  • Johannesburg gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 821 mm against 759 mm.
  • Johannesburg gets about 360 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,169 h against 2,808 h; about 1961–1990).
