Johannesburg vs Matola climate

How the climate of Johannesburg, South Africa compares with Matola, Mozambique, 442 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Matola is 7.9 °C (14.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 15.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Matola is 10.9 °C warmer (Johannesburg 8.6 °C, Matola 19.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Johannesburg 24.3 °C (January), Matola 29.3 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Johannesburg 2.1 °C, Matola 14.9 °C.
  • Johannesburg gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 792 mm against 764 mm.
  • Johannesburg gets about 360 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,169 h against 2,808 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Matola in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Johannesburg in 12 of 12 months
