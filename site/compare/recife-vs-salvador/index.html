Recife vs Salvador climate

How the climate of Recife, Brazil compares with Salvador, Brazil, 675 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Recife is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 26.1 °C against 25.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Recife is 1.1 °C warmer (Recife 25.1 °C, Salvador 24.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Recife 29.6 °C (February), Salvador 27.6 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Recife 22.4 °C, Salvador 22.6 °C.
  • Recife gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,853 mm against 1,761 mm.
  • Recife gets about 246 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,577 h against 2,331 h; about 1961–1990).
