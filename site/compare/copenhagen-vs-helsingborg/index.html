How the climate of Copenhagen, Denmark compares with Helsingborg, Sweden: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Copenhagen is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 9.0 °C against 8.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Copenhagen is 0.7 °C warmer (Copenhagen 1.5 °C, Helsingborg 0.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Copenhagen 21.5 °C (July), Helsingborg 21.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Copenhagen -1.0 °C, Helsingborg -1.4 °C.
  • Helsingborg gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 679 mm against 660 mm.
  • Copenhagen gets about 61 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,652 h against 1,591 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Copenhagen 41 cm, Helsingborg 54 cm (ERA5-Land).
