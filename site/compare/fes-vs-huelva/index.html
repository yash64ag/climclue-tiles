Fes vs Huelva climate

How the climate of Fes, Morocco compares with Huelva, Spain, 400 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Huelva is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 18.7 °C against 18.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Huelva is 0.6 °C warmer (Fes 10.7 °C, Huelva 11.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fes 34.7 °C (July), Huelva 32.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fes 5.4 °C, Huelva 6.9 °C.
  • Huelva gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 592 mm against 546 mm.
  • Huelva gets about 80 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,929 h against 2,849 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Huelva in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Fes in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
