How the climate of Bao'an, China compares with Shenzhen, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Shenzhen is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 22.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Shenzhen is 0.5 °C warmer (Bao'an 28.4 °C, Shenzhen 28.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bao'an 31.7 °C (July), Shenzhen 32.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bao'an 11.5 °C, Shenzhen 11.2 °C.
  • Shenzhen gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 2,176 mm against 2,140 mm.
  • Shenzhen gets about 13 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,940 h against 1,927 h; about 1961–1990).
