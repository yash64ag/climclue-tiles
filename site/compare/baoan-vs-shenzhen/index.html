Bao'an vs Shenzhen climate

How the climate of Bao'an, China compares with Shenzhen, China, 19 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bao'an and Shenzhen have the same yearly average temperature (22.9 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Bao'an is 0.3 °C warmer (Bao'an 16.9 °C, Shenzhen 16.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bao'an 30.2 °C (August), Shenzhen 30.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bao'an 12.4 °C, Shenzhen 11.5 °C.
  • Shenzhen gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 2,314 mm against 2,163 mm.
  • Shenzhen gets about 13 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,940 h against 1,927 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Shenzhen in 6 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Shenzhen in 11 of 12 months
