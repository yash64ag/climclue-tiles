How the climate of Manzanillo, Cuba compares with Mérida, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Manzanillo and Mérida have the same yearly average temperature (26.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Mérida is 1.6 °C warmer (Manzanillo 27.3 °C, Mérida 28.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manzanillo 33.9 °C (July), Mérida 36.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Manzanillo 18.7 °C, Mérida 16.3 °C.
  • Manzanillo gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,225 mm against 1,016 mm.
  • Mérida gets about 70 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,635 h against 2,565 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Manzanillo in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Mérida in 8 of 12 months
