How the climate of Kathmandu, Nepal compares with San Salvador de Jujuy, Argentina: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kathmandu is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 18.7 °C against 17.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Kathmandu is 11.7 °C warmer (Kathmandu 24.0 °C, San Salvador de Jujuy 12.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kathmandu 28.8 °C (May), San Salvador de Jujuy 27.6 °C (December). Coldest-month night lows: Kathmandu 2.4 °C, San Salvador de Jujuy 4.0 °C.
  • Kathmandu gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 1,634 mm against 1,226 mm.
  • San Salvador de Jujuy gets about 355 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,345 h against 1,990 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Kathmandu in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: San Salvador de Jujuy in 9 of 12 months
