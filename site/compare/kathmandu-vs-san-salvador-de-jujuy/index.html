Kathmandu vs San Salvador de Jujuy climate

How the climate of Kathmandu, Nepal compares with San Salvador de Jujuy, Argentina, 17,058 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kathmandu is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 17.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Kathmandu is 11.0 °C warmer (Kathmandu 23.3 °C, San Salvador de Jujuy 12.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kathmandu 26.0 °C (June), San Salvador de Jujuy 24.6 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Kathmandu 6.0 °C, San Salvador de Jujuy 8.3 °C.
  • Kathmandu gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 1,634 mm against 1,226 mm.
  • San Salvador de Jujuy gets about 355 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,345 h against 1,990 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Kathmandu in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: San Salvador de Jujuy in 9 of 12 months
