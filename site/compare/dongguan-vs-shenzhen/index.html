Dongguan vs Shenzhen climate

How the climate of Dongguan, China compares with Shenzhen, China, 62 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dongguan is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 22.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Dongguan is 0.7 °C warmer (Dongguan 29.3 °C, Shenzhen 28.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dongguan 31.4 °C (August), Shenzhen 30.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Dongguan 10.1 °C, Shenzhen 11.5 °C.
  • Shenzhen gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 2,314 mm against 2,121 mm.
  • Shenzhen gets about 90 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,940 h against 1,850 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Dongguan in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Shenzhen in 11 of 12 months
