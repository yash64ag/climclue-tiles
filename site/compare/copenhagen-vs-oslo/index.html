Copenhagen vs Oslo climate

How the climate of Copenhagen, Denmark compares with Oslo, Norway, 483 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Copenhagen is 2.5 °C (4.5 °F) warmer over the year: 8.5 °C against 6.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Copenhagen is 5.6 °C warmer (Copenhagen 2.1 °C, Oslo -3.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Copenhagen 20.3 °C (July), Oslo 20.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Copenhagen -1.1 °C, Oslo -8.1 °C.
  • Oslo gets 56% more rain and snow a year: 1,019 mm against 652 mm.
  • Copenhagen gets about 73 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,652 h against 1,579 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Copenhagen 69 cm, Oslo 255 cm (ERA5-Land).
