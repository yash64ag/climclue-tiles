Copenhagen vs Oslo climate

How the climate of Copenhagen, Denmark compares with Oslo, Norway, 483 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Copenhagen is 1.8 °C (3.2 °F) warmer over the year: 8.4 °C against 6.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Copenhagen is 4.9 °C warmer (Copenhagen 1.9 °C, Oslo -2.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Copenhagen 21.3 °C (July), Oslo 22.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Copenhagen -1.5 °C, Oslo -7.1 °C.
  • Oslo gets 40% more rain and snow a year: 922 mm against 660 mm.
  • Copenhagen gets about 73 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,652 h against 1,579 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Copenhagen 41 cm, Oslo 246 cm (ERA5-Land).
