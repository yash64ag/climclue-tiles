Saitama vs Tokyo climate

How the climate of Saitama, Japan compares with Tokyo, Japan, 24 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Saitama and Tokyo have the same yearly average temperature (15.3 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Tokyo is 0.6 °C warmer (Saitama 6.5 °C, Tokyo 7.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Saitama 31.1 °C (August), Tokyo 30.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Saitama -1.3 °C, Tokyo -0.2 °C.
  • Tokyo gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 1,516 mm against 1,336 mm.
  • Saitama gets about 26 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,863 h against 1,837 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Saitama 20 cm, Tokyo 19 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Saitama in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Saitama in 8 of 12 months
