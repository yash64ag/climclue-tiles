How the climate of Odesa, Ukraine compares with Tiraspol, Moldova: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Odesa is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 10.8 °C against 10.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Odesa is 1.4 °C warmer (Odesa 1.1 °C, Tiraspol -0.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Odesa 26.9 °C (July), Tiraspol 27.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Odesa -2.9 °C, Tiraspol -4.6 °C.
  • Tiraspol gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 514 mm against 458 mm.
  • Odesa gets about 28 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,124 h against 2,096 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Odesa 62 cm, Tiraspol 70 cm (ERA5-Land).
