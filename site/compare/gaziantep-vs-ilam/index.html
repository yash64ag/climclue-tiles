How the climate of Gaziantep, Turkey compares with Īlām, Iran: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Īlām is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 15.5 °C against 15.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Īlām is 1.4 °C warmer (Gaziantep 24.1 °C, Īlām 25.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gaziantep 35.3 °C (July), Īlām 38.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Gaziantep -0.9 °C, Īlām -3.3 °C.
  • Īlām gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 699 mm against 689 mm.
  • Gaziantep gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,122 h against 3,115 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gaziantep 84 cm, Īlām 97 cm (ERA5-Land).
