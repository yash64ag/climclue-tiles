How the climate of Bukhara, Uzbekistan compares with Isfahan, Iran: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Isfahan is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.4 °C against 16.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Isfahan is 2.5 °C warmer (Bukhara 14.8 °C, Isfahan 17.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bukhara 37.9 °C (July), Isfahan 37.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bukhara -2.7 °C, Isfahan -3.8 °C.
  • Bukhara gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 147 mm against 144 mm.
  • Isfahan gets about 204 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,185 h against 2,981 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bukhara 12 cm, Isfahan 19 cm (ERA5-Land).
