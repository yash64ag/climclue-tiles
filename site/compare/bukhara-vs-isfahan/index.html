Bukhara vs Isfahan climate

How the climate of Bukhara, Uzbekistan compares with Isfahan, Iran, 1,389 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bukhara and Isfahan have the same yearly average temperature (16.2 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Isfahan is 2.3 °C warmer (Bukhara 14.9 °C, Isfahan 17.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bukhara 37.0 °C (July), Isfahan 36.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bukhara -2.3 °C, Isfahan -4.4 °C.
  • Isfahan gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 151 mm against 147 mm.
  • Isfahan gets about 204 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,185 h against 2,981 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bukhara 12 cm, Isfahan 19 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Isfahan in 7 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Sunnier: Isfahan in 7 of 12 months
