How the climate of Bandar-e Māhshahr, Iran compares with Basrah, Iraq: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Basrah is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Basrah is 1.1 °C warmer (Bandar-e Māhshahr 37.8 °C, Basrah 38.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bandar-e Māhshahr 46.1 °C (July), Basrah 47.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bandar-e Māhshahr 7.6 °C, Basrah 6.9 °C.
  • Bandar-e Māhshahr gets 39% more rain and snow a year: 210 mm against 151 mm.
  • Basrah gets about 151 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,037 h against 2,887 h; about 1961–1990).
