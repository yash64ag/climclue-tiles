How the climate of Los Angeles, United States compares with Tijuana, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Los Angeles is 1.4 °C (2.6 °F) warmer over the year: 17.9 °C against 16.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Los Angeles is 3.6 °C warmer (Los Angeles 23.2 °C, Tijuana 19.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Los Angeles 29.8 °C (August), Tijuana 25.0 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Los Angeles 6.6 °C, Tijuana 7.3 °C.
  • Los Angeles gets 46% more rain and snow a year: 431 mm against 296 mm.
  • Los Angeles gets about 126 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,193 h against 3,067 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
