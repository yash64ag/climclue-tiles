Nashik vs Surat climate

How the climate of Nashik, India compares with Surat, India, 167 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Surat is 2.9 °C (5.1 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 24.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Surat is 3.9 °C warmer (Nashik 24.1 °C, Surat 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nashik 35.2 °C (April), Surat 33.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Nashik 14.0 °C, Surat 17.3 °C.
  • Surat gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 1,412 mm against 1,180 mm.
  • Surat gets about 108 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,865 h against 2,758 h; about 1961–1990).
