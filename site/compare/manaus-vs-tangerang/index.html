Manaus vs Tangerang climate

How the climate of Manaus, Brazil compares with Tangerang, Indonesia, 18,209 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tangerang is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 27.0 °C against 26.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Tangerang is 0.7 °C warmer (Manaus 26.6 °C, Tangerang 27.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manaus 30.9 °C (October), Tangerang 31.3 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Manaus 23.8 °C, Tangerang 23.1 °C.
  • Manaus gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 2,406 mm against 2,341 mm.
  • Tangerang gets about 855 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,623 h against 1,767 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Tangerang in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Tangerang in 12 of 12 months
