Dammam vs Manama climate

How the climate of Dammam, Saudi Arabia compares with Manama, Bahrain, 53 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dammam is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 26.1 °C against 25.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Dammam is 1.6 °C warmer (Dammam 33.0 °C, Manama 31.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dammam 38.4 °C (July), Manama 34.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Dammam 13.0 °C, Manama 16.1 °C.
  • Dammam gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 89 mm against 82 mm.
  • Manama gets about 14 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,311 h against 3,298 h; about 1961–1990).
