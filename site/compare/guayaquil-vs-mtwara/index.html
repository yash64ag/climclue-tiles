How the climate of Guayaquil, Ecuador compares with Mtwara, Tanzania: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Guayaquil and Mtwara have the same yearly average temperature (25.8 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Mtwara is 0.9 °C warmer (Guayaquil 25.3 °C, Mtwara 26.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guayaquil 31.9 °C (April), Mtwara 30.9 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Guayaquil 16.8 °C, Mtwara 18.7 °C.
  • Guayaquil gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 1,314 mm against 950 mm.
  • Mtwara gets about 1,829 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,912 h against 1,084 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Guayaquil in 7 of 12 months
