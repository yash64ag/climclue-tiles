Songjiang vs Xuhui climate

How the climate of Songjiang, China compares with Xuhui, China, 28 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Songjiang and Xuhui have the same yearly average temperature (16.7 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Songjiang is 0.4 °C warmer (Songjiang 15.3 °C, Xuhui 14.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Songjiang 32.0 °C (July), Xuhui 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Songjiang 0.8 °C, Xuhui 1.1 °C.
  • Xuhui gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,499 mm against 1,427 mm.
  • Xuhui gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,960 h against 1,952 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Songjiang 9.8 cm, Xuhui 7.5 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Songjiang in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Songjiang in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Xuhui in 10 of 12 months (2 equal)
