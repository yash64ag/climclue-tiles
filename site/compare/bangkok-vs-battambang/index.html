How the climate of Bangkok, Thailand compares with Battambang, Cambodia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bangkok is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 28.5 °C against 28.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Bangkok is 0.8 °C warmer (Bangkok 27.3 °C, Battambang 26.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bangkok 35.2 °C (April), Battambang 36.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Bangkok 20.8 °C, Battambang 20.5 °C.
  • Bangkok gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,459 mm against 1,360 mm.
  • Battambang gets about 10 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,551 h against 2,541 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Bangkok in 10 of 12 months

  • Sunnier: Bangkok in 8 of 12 months
