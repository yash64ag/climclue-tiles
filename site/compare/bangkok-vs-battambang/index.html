Bangkok vs Battambang climate

How the climate of Bangkok, Thailand compares with Battambang, Cambodia, 301 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bangkok is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 28.5 °C against 28.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Bangkok is 0.7 °C warmer (Bangkok 27.9 °C, Battambang 27.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bangkok 34.8 °C (April), Battambang 35.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Bangkok 20.8 °C, Battambang 20.5 °C.
  • Bangkok gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,490 mm against 1,360 mm.
  • Battambang gets about 10 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,551 h against 2,541 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Battambang in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Bangkok in 8 of 12 months
