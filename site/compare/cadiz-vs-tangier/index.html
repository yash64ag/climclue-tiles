Cadiz vs Tangier climate

How the climate of Cadiz, Spain compares with Tangier, Morocco, 95 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Cadiz and Tangier have the same yearly average temperature (18.3 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Cadiz is 0.6 °C warmer (Cadiz 24.3 °C, Tangier 23.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cadiz 27.7 °C (July), Tangier 28.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Cadiz 9.8 °C, Tangier 9.8 °C.
  • Tangier gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 655 mm against 605 mm.
  • Cadiz gets about 122 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,945 h against 2,823 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Cadiz in 7 of 12 months
