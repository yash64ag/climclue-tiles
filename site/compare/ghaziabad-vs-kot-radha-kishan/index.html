Ghāziābād vs Kot Radha Kishan climate

How the climate of Ghāziābād, India compares with Kot Radha Kishan, Pakistan, 426 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Ghāziābād is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 24.7 °C against 24.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Kot Radha Kishan is 1.5 °C warmer (Ghāziābād 29.6 °C, Kot Radha Kishan 31.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ghāziābād 39.2 °C (May), Kot Radha Kishan 39.5 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Ghāziābād 7.6 °C, Kot Radha Kishan 6.4 °C.
  • Ghāziābād gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 698 mm against 542 mm.
  • Kot Radha Kishan gets about 260 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,933 h against 2,674 h; about 1961–1990).
