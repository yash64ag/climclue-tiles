Fuzhou, Jiangxi vs Jiujiang climate

How the climate of Fuzhou, Jiangxi, China compares with Jiujiang, China, 197 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Fuzhou, Jiangxi is 1.6 °C (2.8 °F) warmer over the year: 19.1 °C against 17.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Fuzhou, Jiangxi is 2.3 °C warmer (Fuzhou, Jiangxi 9.2 °C, Jiujiang 6.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 34.9 °C (July), Jiujiang 33.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 3.1 °C, Jiujiang 0.7 °C.
  • Fuzhou, Jiangxi gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,977 mm against 1,879 mm.
  • Jiujiang gets about 82 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,905 h against 1,823 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fuzhou, Jiangxi 6.6 cm, Jiujiang 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
