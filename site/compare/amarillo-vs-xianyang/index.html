How the climate of Amarillo, United States compares with Xianyang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Xianyang is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.4 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Xianyang is 3.1 °C warmer (Amarillo 13.7 °C, Xianyang 16.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amarillo 33.0 °C (July), Xianyang 32.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Amarillo -4.9 °C, Xianyang -3.0 °C.
  • Xianyang gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 578 mm against 550 mm.
  • Amarillo gets about 1,313 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 1,931 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amarillo 33 cm, Xianyang 8.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
