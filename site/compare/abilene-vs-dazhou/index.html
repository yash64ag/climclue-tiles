How the climate of Abilene, United States compares with Dazhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Abilene and Dazhou have the same yearly average temperature (18.3 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Dazhou is 1.1 °C warmer (Abilene 7.5 °C, Dazhou 8.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abilene 34.9 °C (July), Dazhou 33.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Abilene 0.6 °C, Dazhou 4.4 °C.
  • Dazhou gets 102% more rain and snow a year: 1,286 mm against 636 mm.
  • Abilene gets about 1,694 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,088 h against 1,394 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Abilene 0.9 cm, Dazhou 1.3 cm (ERA5-Land).
