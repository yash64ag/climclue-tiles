Abilene vs Dazhou climate

Main differences

  • Abilene is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 18.4 °C against 18.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Abilene is 1.2 °C warmer (Abilene 26.9 °C, Dazhou 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abilene 35.3 °C (July), Dazhou 33.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Abilene 0.3 °C, Dazhou 4.4 °C.
  • Dazhou gets 102% more rain and snow a year: 1,286 mm against 636 mm.
  • Abilene gets about 1,694 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,088 h against 1,394 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Abilene 0.5 cm, Dazhou 1.3 cm (ERA5-Land).
