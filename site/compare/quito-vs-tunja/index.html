Quito vs Tunja climate

How the climate of Quito, Ecuador compares with Tunja, Colombia, 861 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tunja is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 12.5 °C against 12.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Quito is 1.1 °C warmer (Quito 12.8 °C, Tunja 11.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Quito 17.6 °C (August), Tunja 17.9 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Quito 8.8 °C, Tunja 7.4 °C.
  • Quito gets 56% more rain and snow a year: 2,072 mm against 1,332 mm.
  • Tunja gets about 25 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,900 h against 1,875 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Tunja in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: Tunja in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
