Amarillo vs Zhengzhou climate

How the climate of Amarillo, United States compares with Zhengzhou, China, 11,406 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhengzhou is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 14.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Zhengzhou is 2.9 °C warmer (Amarillo 19.1 °C, Zhengzhou 21.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amarillo 32.5 °C (July), Zhengzhou 31.6 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Amarillo -1.4 °C, Zhengzhou -4.0 °C.
  • Zhengzhou gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 632 mm against 542 mm.
  • Amarillo gets about 986 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 2,257 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amarillo 55 cm, Zhengzhou 34 cm (ERA5-Land).
