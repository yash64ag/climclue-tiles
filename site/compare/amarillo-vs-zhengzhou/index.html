How the climate of Amarillo, United States compares with Zhengzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zhengzhou is 1.0 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 15.2 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Zhengzhou is 3.1 °C warmer (Amarillo 18.9 °C, Zhengzhou 21.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amarillo 33.0 °C (July), Zhengzhou 32.6 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Amarillo -5.1 °C, Zhengzhou -4.3 °C.
  • Zhengzhou gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 632 mm against 550 mm.
  • Amarillo gets about 986 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 2,257 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amarillo 33 cm, Zhengzhou 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
