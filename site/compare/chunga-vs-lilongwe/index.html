How the climate of Chunga, Zambia compares with Lilongwe, Malawi: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lilongwe is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 20.9 °C against 20.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Lilongwe is 1.1 °C warmer (Chunga 16.3 °C, Lilongwe 17.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chunga 31.5 °C (October), Lilongwe 30.6 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Chunga 8.2 °C, Lilongwe 9.5 °C.
  • Chunga gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 807 mm against 799 mm.
  • Chunga gets about 143 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,897 h against 2,754 h; about 1961–1990).
