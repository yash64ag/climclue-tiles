Jieyang vs Shantou climate

How the climate of Jieyang, China compares with Shantou, China, 38 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jieyang is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 22.4 °C against 22.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Jieyang is 0.7 °C warmer (Jieyang 22.0 °C, Shantou 21.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jieyang 31.5 °C (July), Shantou 29.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jieyang 9.2 °C, Shantou 11.4 °C.
  • Jieyang gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,731 mm against 1,567 mm.
  • Shantou gets about 29 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,008 h against 1,979 h; about 1961–1990).
