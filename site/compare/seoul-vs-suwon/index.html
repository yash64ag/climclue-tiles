How the climate of Seoul, South Korea compares with Suwon, South Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Suwon is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 12.2 °C against 11.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Suwon is 0.9 °C warmer (Seoul -3.3 °C, Suwon -2.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Seoul 29.2 °C (August), Suwon 29.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Seoul -7.7 °C, Suwon -7.5 °C.
  • Seoul gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,443 mm against 1,303 mm.
  • Suwon gets about 74 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,395 h against 2,320 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Seoul 27 cm, Suwon 26 cm (ERA5-Land).
