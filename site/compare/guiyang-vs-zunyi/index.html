Guiyang vs Zunyi climate

How the climate of Guiyang, China compares with Zunyi, China, 124 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zunyi is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 15.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Zunyi is 1.5 °C warmer (Guiyang 23.8 °C, Zunyi 25.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guiyang 26.7 °C (August), Zunyi 28.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Guiyang 1.5 °C, Zunyi 1.3 °C.
  • Guiyang gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,065 mm against 1,060 mm.
  • Guiyang gets about 26 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,307 h against 1,282 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Guiyang 4.4 cm, Zunyi 7.0 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Guiyang in 9 of 12 months
