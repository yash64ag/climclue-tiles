How the climate of Guiyang, China compares with Zunyi, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Guiyang is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 15.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Zunyi is 1.1 °C warmer (Guiyang 24.0 °C, Zunyi 25.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guiyang 28.2 °C (July), Zunyi 29.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Guiyang 2.2 °C, Zunyi 2.0 °C.
  • Zunyi gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,082 mm against 1,011 mm.
  • Guiyang gets about 26 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,307 h against 1,282 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Guiyang 4.4 cm, Zunyi 7.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
