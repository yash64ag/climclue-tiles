Howrah vs Jamshedpur climate

How the climate of Howrah, India compares with Jamshedpur, India, 220 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Howrah is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 26.1 °C against 25.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Jamshedpur is 1.3 °C warmer (Howrah 30.1 °C, Jamshedpur 31.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Howrah 35.2 °C (May), Jamshedpur 38.3 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Howrah 12.3 °C, Jamshedpur 10.8 °C.
  • Howrah gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 1,804 mm against 1,468 mm.
  • Jamshedpur gets about 151 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,228 h against 2,078 h; about 1961–1990).
