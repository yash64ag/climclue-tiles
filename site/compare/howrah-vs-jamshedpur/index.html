Howrah vs Jamshedpur climate

How the climate of Howrah, India compares with Jamshedpur, India, 220 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Howrah is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 26.1 °C against 25.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Howrah is 1.3 °C warmer (Howrah 23.6 °C, Jamshedpur 22.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Howrah 35.2 °C (April), Jamshedpur 37.8 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Howrah 13.1 °C, Jamshedpur 11.4 °C.
  • Howrah gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 1,824 mm against 1,543 mm.
  • Jamshedpur gets about 151 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,228 h against 2,078 h; about 1961–1990).

  • Drier: Jamshedpur in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Jamshedpur in 12 of 12 months
