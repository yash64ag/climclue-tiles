How the climate of Khulna, Bangladesh compares with Kolkata, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kolkata is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 26.1 °C against 25.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Kolkata is 0.8 °C warmer (Khulna 29.4 °C, Kolkata 30.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Khulna 34.4 °C (April), Kolkata 35.0 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Khulna 11.6 °C, Kolkata 12.2 °C.
  • Khulna gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,889 mm against 1,827 mm.
  • Khulna gets about 12 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,090 h against 2,078 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Kolkata in 9 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
