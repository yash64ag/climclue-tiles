Antalya vs Córdoba climate

How the climate of Antalya, Turkey compares with Córdoba, Spain, 3,116 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Antalya is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 19.4 °C against 19.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Córdoba is 1.6 °C warmer (Antalya 13.2 °C, Córdoba 14.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Antalya 36.3 °C (July), Córdoba 38.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Antalya 5.7 °C, Córdoba 4.6 °C.
  • Antalya gets 50% more rain and snow a year: 1,032 mm against 687 mm.
  • Antalya gets about 342 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,103 h against 2,761 h; about 1961–1990).
