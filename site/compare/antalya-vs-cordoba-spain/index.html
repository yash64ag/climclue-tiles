Antalya vs Córdoba climate

How the climate of Antalya, Turkey compares with Córdoba, Spain, 3,116 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Córdoba is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 18.7 °C against 18.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Córdoba is 2.2 °C warmer (Antalya 12.3 °C, Córdoba 14.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Antalya 35.0 °C (July), Córdoba 35.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Antalya 4.8 °C, Córdoba 4.3 °C.
  • Antalya gets 61% more rain and snow a year: 1,012 mm against 627 mm.
  • Antalya gets about 342 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,103 h against 2,761 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Antalya 15 cm, Córdoba 0.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
