Guilin vs Hezhou climate

How the climate of Guilin, China compares with Hezhou, China, 161 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Hezhou is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 20.5 °C against 20.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Hezhou is 0.9 °C warmer (Guilin 21.8 °C, Hezhou 22.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guilin 31.5 °C (August), Hezhou 31.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Guilin 5.7 °C, Hezhou 5.6 °C.
  • Guilin gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 2,178 mm against 1,620 mm.
  • Hezhou gets about 138 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,703 h against 1,565 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Guilin 1.7 cm, Hezhou 0.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
