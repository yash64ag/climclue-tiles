How the climate of Guilin, China compares with Hezhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hezhou is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 20.7 °C against 19.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Hezhou is 1.3 °C warmer (Guilin 14.1 °C, Hezhou 15.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guilin 33.0 °C (August), Hezhou 33.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Guilin 5.8 °C, Hezhou 6.0 °C.
  • Guilin gets 35% more rain and snow a year: 2,144 mm against 1,592 mm.
  • Hezhou gets about 138 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,703 h against 1,565 h; about 1961–1990).
