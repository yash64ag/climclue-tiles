How the climate of Katsina, Nigeria compares with Rājkot, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Rājkot is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 26.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Rājkot is 1.7 °C warmer (Katsina 25.8 °C, Rājkot 27.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Katsina 38.6 °C (April), Rājkot 39.3 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Katsina 12.8 °C, Rājkot 11.4 °C.
  • Rājkot gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 738 mm against 579 mm.
  • Rājkot gets about 162 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,075 h against 2,913 h; about 1961–1990).
