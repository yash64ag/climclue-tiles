Katsina vs Rājkot climate

How the climate of Katsina, Nigeria compares with Rājkot, India, 6,731 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rājkot is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.5 °C against 26.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Katsina is 1.7 °C warmer (Katsina 23.6 °C, Rājkot 21.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Katsina 38.2 °C (April), Rājkot 39.2 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Katsina 13.6 °C, Rājkot 13.4 °C.
  • Rājkot gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 741 mm against 578 mm.
  • Rājkot gets about 162 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,075 h against 2,913 h; about 1961–1990).
