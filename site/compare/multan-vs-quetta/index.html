Multan vs Quetta climate

How the climate of Multan, Pakistan compares with Quetta, Pakistan, 430 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Multan is 11.3 °C (20.4 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 14.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Multan is 12.7 °C warmer (Multan 28.4 °C, Quetta 15.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Multan 41.3 °C (June), Quetta 29.9 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Multan 7.4 °C, Quetta -2.9 °C.
  • Quetta gets 87% more rain and snow a year: 420 mm against 225 mm.
  • Quetta gets about 250 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,333 h against 3,083 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Multan 0 cm, Quetta 45 cm (ERA5-Land).
