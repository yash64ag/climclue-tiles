How the climate of Multan, Pakistan compares with Quetta, Pakistan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Multan is 8.5 °C (15.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 17.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Multan is 9.9 °C warmer (Multan 28.5 °C, Quetta 18.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Multan 42.4 °C (June), Quetta 36.8 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Multan 5.3 °C, Quetta -2.0 °C.
  • Quetta gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 317 mm against 230 mm.
  • Quetta gets about 250 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,333 h against 3,083 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Multan 0 cm, Quetta 82 cm (ERA5-Land).
