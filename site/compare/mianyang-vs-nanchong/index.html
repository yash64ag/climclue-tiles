Mianyang, Sichuan vs Nanchong climate

How the climate of Mianyang, Sichuan, China compares with Nanchong, China, 153 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Nanchong is 0.9 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Nanchong is 2.0 °C warmer (Mianyang, Sichuan 25.9 °C, Nanchong 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mianyang, Sichuan 30.5 °C (July), Nanchong 32.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Mianyang, Sichuan 2.6 °C, Nanchong 5.1 °C.
  • Nanchong gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 1,119 mm against 919 mm.
  • Mianyang, Sichuan gets about 102 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,394 h against 1,292 h; about 1961–1990).
