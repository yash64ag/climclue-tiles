Baoding vs Tangshan climate

How the climate of Baoding, China compares with Tangshan, China, 249 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Baoding is 1.6 °C (2.9 °F) warmer over the year: 13.8 °C against 12.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Baoding is 2.5 °C warmer (Baoding 26.6 °C, Tangshan 24.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoding 32.1 °C (June), Tangshan 30.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baoding -7.6 °C, Tangshan -9.6 °C.
  • Tangshan gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 630 mm against 516 mm.
  • Tangshan gets about 63 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,672 h against 2,609 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoding 10 cm, Tangshan 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
