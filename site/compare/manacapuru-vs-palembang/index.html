Manacapuru vs Palembang climate

How the climate of Manacapuru, Brazil compares with Palembang, Indonesia, 18,248 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Manacapuru is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Manacapuru is 0.8 °C warmer (Manacapuru 27.4 °C, Palembang 26.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manacapuru 32.4 °C (September), Palembang 31.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Manacapuru 22.3 °C, Palembang 21.8 °C.
  • Palembang gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 3,044 mm against 2,489 mm.
  • Palembang gets about 643 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,422 h against 1,779 h; about 1961–1990).
