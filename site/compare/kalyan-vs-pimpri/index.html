Kalyān vs Pimpri climate

How the climate of Kalyān, India compares with Pimpri, India, 99 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kalyān is 2.3 °C (4.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 24.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Kalyān is 3.1 °C warmer (Kalyān 26.1 °C, Pimpri 23.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kalyān 35.8 °C (April), Pimpri 36.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Kalyān 16.7 °C, Pimpri 14.7 °C.
  • Kalyān gets 113% more rain and snow a year: 2,265 mm against 1,061 mm.
  • Pimpri gets about 83 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,789 h against 2,706 h; about 1961–1990).
