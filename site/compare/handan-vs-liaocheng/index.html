Handan vs Liaocheng climate

How the climate of Handan, China compares with Liaocheng, China, 136 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Handan is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 14.6 °C against 14.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Handan is 0.9 °C warmer (Handan 16.7 °C, Liaocheng 15.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Handan 32.1 °C (June), Liaocheng 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Handan -5.8 °C, Liaocheng -5.6 °C.
  • Liaocheng gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 583 mm against 522 mm.
  • Liaocheng gets about 39 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,493 h against 2,454 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Handan 8.9 cm, Liaocheng 8.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
