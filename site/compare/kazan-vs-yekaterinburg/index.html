How the climate of Kazan, Russia compares with Yekaterinburg, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kazan is 1.9 °C (3.5 °F) warmer over the year: 4.4 °C against 2.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Kazan is 2.9 °C warmer (Kazan -8.9 °C, Yekaterinburg -11.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kazan 25.1 °C (July), Yekaterinburg 23.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kazan -14.2 °C, Yekaterinburg -17.3 °C.
  • Yekaterinburg gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 546 mm against 536 mm.
  • Kazan gets about 9 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,885 h against 1,876 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kazan 175 cm, Yekaterinburg 152 cm (ERA5-Land).
