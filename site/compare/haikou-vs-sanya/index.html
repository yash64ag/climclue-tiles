Haikou, Hainan vs Sanya climate

How the climate of Haikou, Hainan, China compares with Sanya, China, 217 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Sanya is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 24.9 °C against 24.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Sanya is 2.1 °C warmer (Haikou, Hainan 18.4 °C, Sanya 20.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Haikou, Hainan 30.8 °C (July), Sanya 29.5 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Haikou, Hainan 17.1 °C, Sanya 18.2 °C.
  • Haikou, Hainan gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,824 mm against 1,651 mm.
  • Sanya gets about 46 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,138 h against 2,092 h; about 1961–1990).
