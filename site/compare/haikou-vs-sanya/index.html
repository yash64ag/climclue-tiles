How the climate of Haikou, Hainan, China compares with Sanya, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Sanya is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 25.2 °C against 24.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Sanya is 2.2 °C warmer (Haikou, Hainan 18.4 °C, Sanya 20.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Haikou, Hainan 33.0 °C (July), Sanya 31.3 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Haikou, Hainan 15.8 °C, Sanya 16.8 °C.
  • Haikou, Hainan gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,800 mm against 1,615 mm.
  • Sanya gets about 46 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,138 h against 2,092 h; about 1961–1990).
