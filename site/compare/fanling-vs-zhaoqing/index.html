How the climate of Fanling, Hong Kong compares with Zhaoqing, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Fanling is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 23.6 °C against 22.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Fanling is 2.1 °C warmer (Fanling 16.2 °C, Zhaoqing 14.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fanling 32.0 °C (July), Zhaoqing 33.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fanling 13.2 °C, Zhaoqing 10.0 °C.
  • Fanling gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 2,097 mm against 1,703 mm.
  • Fanling gets about 144 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,958 h against 1,814 h; about 1961–1990).
