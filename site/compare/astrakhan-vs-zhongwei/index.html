Astrakhan vs Zhongwei climate

How the climate of Astrakhan, Russia compares with Zhongwei, China, 4,723 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhongwei is 1.1 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 11.6 °C against 10.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Zhongwei is 4.5 °C warmer (Astrakhan 2.1 °C, Zhongwei 6.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Astrakhan 31.9 °C (July), Zhongwei 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Astrakhan -7.7 °C, Zhongwei -11.5 °C.
  • Astrakhan gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 236 mm against 188 mm.
  • Zhongwei gets about 346 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,697 h against 2,350 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Astrakhan 39 cm, Zhongwei 5.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
