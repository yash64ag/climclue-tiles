Jiading vs Kunshan climate

How the climate of Jiading, China compares with Kunshan, China, 28 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jiading is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Jiading is 0.3 °C warmer (Jiading 12.9 °C, Kunshan 12.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiading 31.7 °C (July), Kunshan 31.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jiading 0.8 °C, Kunshan 0.3 °C.
  • Jiading gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,422 mm against 1,325 mm.
  • Jiading gets about 1 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,979 h against 1,978 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jiading 9.5 cm, Kunshan 11 cm (ERA5-Land).
