How the climate of George, South Africa compares with Melbourne, Australia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • George is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when George is 2.7 °C warmer (George 12.8 °C, Melbourne 10.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: George 25.7 °C (February), Melbourne 26.5 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: George 7.3 °C, Melbourne 6.3 °C.
  • George gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 653 mm against 629 mm.
  • George gets about 542 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,739 h against 2,197 h; about 1961–1990).
