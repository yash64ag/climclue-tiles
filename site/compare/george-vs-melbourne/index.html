George vs Melbourne climate

How the climate of George, South Africa compares with Melbourne, Australia, 10,068 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • George is 1.4 °C (2.5 °F) warmer over the year: 16.0 °C against 14.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when George is 2.3 °C warmer (George 12.2 °C, Melbourne 9.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: George 24.0 °C (February), Melbourne 24.3 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: George 8.2 °C, Melbourne 7.2 °C.
  • George gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 664 mm against 625 mm.
  • George gets about 542 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,739 h against 2,197 h; about 1961–1990).
