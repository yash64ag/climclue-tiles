How the climate of Tokyo, Japan compares with Virginia Beach, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Virginia Beach is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 15.6 °C against 15.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Virginia Beach is 2.2 °C warmer (Tokyo 21.5 °C, Virginia Beach 23.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Tokyo 30.3 °C (August), Virginia Beach 30.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Tokyo -0.2 °C, Virginia Beach 0.1 °C.
  • Tokyo gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 1,516 mm against 1,219 mm.
  • Virginia Beach gets about 829 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,666 h against 1,837 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Tokyo 19 cm, Virginia Beach 9.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
