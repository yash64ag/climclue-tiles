Tokyo vs Virginia Beach climate

How the climate of Tokyo, Japan compares with Virginia Beach, United States, 11,148 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tokyo and Virginia Beach have the same yearly average temperature (15.3 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Tokyo is 1.6 °C warmer (Tokyo 26.7 °C, Virginia Beach 25.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Tokyo 29.4 °C (August), Virginia Beach 27.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Tokyo -0.3 °C, Virginia Beach 3.0 °C.
  • Tokyo gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 1,516 mm against 1,219 mm.
  • Virginia Beach gets about 829 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,666 h against 1,837 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Tokyo 19 cm, Virginia Beach 12 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Virginia Beach in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Virginia Beach in 10 of 12 months
