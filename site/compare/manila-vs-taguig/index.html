How the climate of Manila, Philippines compares with Taguig, Philippines: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Manila is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 27.8 °C against 27.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Manila is 0.2 °C warmer (Manila 29.5 °C, Taguig 29.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manila 35.2 °C (April), Taguig 34.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Manila 20.8 °C, Taguig 20.9 °C.
  • Manila gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 2,057 mm against 1,901 mm.
  • Manila gets about 42 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,404 h against 2,362 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
