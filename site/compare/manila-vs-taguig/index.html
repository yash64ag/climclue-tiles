Manila vs Taguig climate

How the climate of Manila, Philippines compares with Taguig, Philippines, 14 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Manila is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 27.8 °C against 27.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Manila is 0.4 °C warmer (Manila 28.1 °C, Taguig 27.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Manila 32.3 °C (April), Taguig 32.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Manila 23.4 °C, Taguig 23.3 °C.
  • Manila gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 2,138 mm against 2,013 mm.
  • Manila gets about 42 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,404 h against 2,362 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Manila in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Taguig in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Manila in 11 of 12 months
