How the climate of Tabriz, Iran compares with Yerevan, Armenia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yerevan is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 13.2 °C against 12.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Yerevan is 1.7 °C warmer (Tabriz 11.7 °C, Yerevan 13.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Tabriz 32.4 °C (July), Yerevan 34.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Tabriz -5.6 °C, Yerevan -5.6 °C.
  • Yerevan gets 89% more rain and snow a year: 446 mm against 236 mm.
  • Tabriz gets about 211 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,707 h against 2,497 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Tabriz 50 cm, Yerevan 92 cm (ERA5-Land).
