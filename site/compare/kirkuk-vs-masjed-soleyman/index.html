Kirkuk vs Masjed Soleymān climate

How the climate of Kirkuk, Iraq compares with Masjed Soleymān, Iran, 600 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Masjed Soleymān is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 23.4 °C against 22.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Masjed Soleymān is 2.0 °C warmer (Kirkuk 10.1 °C, Masjed Soleymān 12.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kirkuk 42.6 °C (July), Masjed Soleymān 43.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kirkuk 3.6 °C, Masjed Soleymān 4.5 °C.
  • Masjed Soleymān gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 583 mm against 457 mm.
  • Kirkuk gets about 492 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,318 h against 2,826 h; about 1961–1990).
