How the climate of Kirkuk, Iraq compares with Masjed Soleymān, Iran: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Masjed Soleymān is 3.1 °C (5.5 °F) warmer over the year: 24.9 °C against 21.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Masjed Soleymān is 3.7 °C warmer (Kirkuk 26.7 °C, Masjed Soleymān 30.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kirkuk 43.7 °C (July), Masjed Soleymān 46.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kirkuk 3.4 °C, Masjed Soleymān 5.8 °C.
  • Masjed Soleymān gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 522 mm against 498 mm.
  • Kirkuk gets about 492 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,318 h against 2,826 h; about 1961–1990).
