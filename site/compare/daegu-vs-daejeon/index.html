Daegu vs Daejeon climate

How the climate of Daegu, South Korea compares with Daejeon, South Korea, 122 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Daegu is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 14.0 °C against 13.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Daegu is 1.3 °C warmer (Daegu 2.8 °C, Daejeon 1.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daegu 31.1 °C (August), Daejeon 30.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Daegu -5.3 °C, Daejeon -6.2 °C.
  • Daejeon gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 1,223 mm against 979 mm.
  • Daegu gets about 58 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,483 h against 2,425 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daegu 13 cm, Daejeon 27 cm (ERA5-Land).
