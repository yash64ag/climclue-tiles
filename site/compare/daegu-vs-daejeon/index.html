Daegu vs Daejeon climate

How the climate of Daegu, South Korea compares with Daejeon, South Korea, 122 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Daegu is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 13.6 °C against 13.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Daegu is 1.1 °C warmer (Daegu 2.5 °C, Daejeon 1.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daegu 29.4 °C (August), Daejeon 29.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Daegu -5.0 °C, Daejeon -5.8 °C.
  • Daejeon gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 1,332 mm against 1,062 mm.
  • Daegu gets about 58 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,483 h against 2,425 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daegu 20 cm, Daejeon 33 cm (ERA5-Land).
