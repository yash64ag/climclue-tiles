How the climate of Iztapalapa, Mexico compares with Mexico City, Mexico: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Mexico City is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Mexico City is 0.2 °C warmer (Iztapalapa 17.6 °C, Mexico City 17.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iztapalapa 27.2 °C (May), Mexico City 27.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Iztapalapa 4.4 °C, Mexico City 4.3 °C.
  • Iztapalapa gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 779 mm against 728 mm.
