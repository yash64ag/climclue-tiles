Iztapalapa vs Mexico City climate

How the climate of Iztapalapa, Mexico compares with Mexico City, Mexico, 11 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Mexico City is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.0 °C against 15.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Mexico City is 0.4 °C warmer (Iztapalapa 16.8 °C, Mexico City 17.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iztapalapa 23.9 °C (May), Mexico City 24.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Iztapalapa 5.7 °C, Mexico City 5.7 °C.
  • Iztapalapa gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 777 mm against 719 mm.
  • Mexico City gets about 3 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,016 h against 2,013 h; about 1961–1990).
