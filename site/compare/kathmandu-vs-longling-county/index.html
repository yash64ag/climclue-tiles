How the climate of Kathmandu, Nepal compares with Longling County, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kathmandu is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 18.7 °C against 17.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Kathmandu is 2.9 °C warmer (Kathmandu 24.0 °C, Longling County 21.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kathmandu 28.8 °C (May), Longling County 25.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Kathmandu 2.4 °C, Longling County 3.2 °C.
  • Longling County gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 2,123 mm against 1,634 mm.
  • Longling County gets about 131 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,121 h against 1,990 h; about 1961–1990).
