Chattogram vs Mandalay climate

How the climate of Chattogram, Bangladesh compares with Mandalay, Myanmar, 440 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mandalay is 2.0 °C (3.6 °F) warmer over the year: 27.5 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Mandalay is 4.6 °C warmer (Chattogram 27.6 °C, Mandalay 32.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chattogram 30.4 °C (May), Mandalay 38.0 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Chattogram 16.0 °C, Mandalay 15.0 °C.
  • Chattogram gets 223% more rain and snow a year: 3,146 mm against 973 mm.
  • Mandalay gets about 186 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,377 h against 2,191 h; about 1961–1990).
