Chattogram vs Mandalay climate

How the climate of Chattogram, Bangladesh compares with Mandalay, Myanmar, 440 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mandalay is 1.9 °C (3.4 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Mandalay is 4.4 °C warmer (Chattogram 27.5 °C, Mandalay 31.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chattogram 31.9 °C (May), Mandalay 39.1 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Chattogram 13.9 °C, Mandalay 13.5 °C.
  • Chattogram gets 212% more rain and snow a year: 3,182 mm against 1,019 mm.
  • Mandalay gets about 186 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,377 h against 2,191 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
