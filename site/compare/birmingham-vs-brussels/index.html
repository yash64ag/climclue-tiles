How the climate of Birmingham, United Kingdom compares with Brussels, Belgium: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Brussels is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 10.9 °C against 9.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Brussels is 2.5 °C warmer (Birmingham 11.4 °C, Brussels 13.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Birmingham 21.3 °C (July), Brussels 23.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Birmingham 0.9 °C, Brussels 0.7 °C.
  • Brussels gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 851 mm against 737 mm.
  • Brussels gets about 224 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,587 h against 1,363 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Birmingham 6.3 cm, Brussels 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
