Birmingham vs Brussels climate

How the climate of Birmingham, United Kingdom compares with Brussels, Belgium, 467 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Brussels is 0.6 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 10.4 °C against 9.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Brussels is 1.9 °C warmer (Birmingham 11.4 °C, Brussels 13.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Birmingham 20.2 °C (July), Brussels 22.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Birmingham 1.2 °C, Brussels 0.6 °C.
  • Brussels gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 863 mm against 748 mm.
  • Brussels gets about 224 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,587 h against 1,363 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Birmingham 6.3 cm, Brussels 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
