Asahikawa vs Montréal climate

How the climate of Asahikawa, Japan compares with Montréal, Canada, 9,473 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Asahikawa is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 7.4 °C against 6.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Asahikawa is 2.9 °C warmer (Asahikawa -6.9 °C, Montréal -9.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Asahikawa 24.9 °C (August), Montréal 25.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Asahikawa -11.8 °C, Montréal -14.1 °C.
  • Asahikawa gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,269 mm against 1,207 mm.
  • Montréal gets about 323 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,969 h against 1,645 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Asahikawa 371 cm, Montréal 212 cm (ERA5-Land).
