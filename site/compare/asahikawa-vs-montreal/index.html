Asahikawa vs Montréal climate

How the climate of Asahikawa, Japan compares with Montréal, Canada, 9,473 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Asahikawa is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 7.2 °C against 6.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Asahikawa is 2.7 °C warmer (Asahikawa -7.0 °C, Montréal -9.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Asahikawa 24.7 °C (August), Montréal 25.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Asahikawa -12.0 °C, Montréal -14.2 °C.
  • Asahikawa gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 1,348 mm against 1,109 mm.
  • Montréal gets about 323 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,969 h against 1,645 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Asahikawa 406 cm, Montréal 217 cm (ERA5-Land).
