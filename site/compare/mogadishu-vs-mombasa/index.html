Mogadishu vs Mombasa climate

How the climate of Mogadishu, Somalia compares with Mombasa, Kenya, 926 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mogadishu and Mombasa have the same yearly average temperature (26.2 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Mombasa is 1.2 °C warmer (Mogadishu 26.6 °C, Mombasa 27.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mogadishu 30.3 °C (March), Mombasa 31.9 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Mogadishu 23.1 °C, Mombasa 21.9 °C.
  • Mombasa gets 94% more rain and snow a year: 1,092 mm against 562 mm.
  • Mogadishu gets about 158 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,098 h against 2,940 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Mogadishu in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Mogadishu in 12 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Mogadishu in 10 of 12 months
