Mogadishu vs Mombasa climate

How the climate of Mogadishu, Somalia compares with Mombasa, Kenya, 926 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mogadishu is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.3 °C against 26.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Mombasa is 1.2 °C warmer (Mogadishu 26.6 °C, Mombasa 27.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Mogadishu 32.9 °C (April), Mombasa 32.5 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Mogadishu 20.7 °C, Mombasa 20.3 °C.
  • Mombasa gets 103% more rain and snow a year: 1,110 mm against 548 mm.
  • Mogadishu gets about 158 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,098 h against 2,940 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
