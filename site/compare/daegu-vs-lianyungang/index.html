Daegu vs Lianyungang climate

How the climate of Daegu, South Korea compares with Lianyungang, China, 864 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Lianyungang is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 14.4 °C against 13.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Lianyungang is 1.4 °C warmer (Daegu 22.6 °C, Lianyungang 24.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Daegu 29.4 °C (August), Lianyungang 30.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Daegu -5.0 °C, Lianyungang -3.6 °C.
  • Daegu gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 1,062 mm against 897 mm.
  • Daegu gets about 182 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,483 h against 2,300 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Daegu 20 cm, Lianyungang 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
