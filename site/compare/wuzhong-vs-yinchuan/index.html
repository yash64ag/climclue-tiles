Wuzhong vs Yinchuan climate

How the climate of Wuzhong, China compares with Yinchuan, China, 54 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Wuzhong is 1.3 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 11.3 °C against 9.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Wuzhong is 1.9 °C warmer (Wuzhong 6.0 °C, Yinchuan 4.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Wuzhong 31.4 °C (July), Yinchuan 30.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Wuzhong -11.6 °C, Yinchuan -12.1 °C.
  • Yinchuan gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 191 mm against 187 mm.
  • Yinchuan gets about 93 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,845 h against 2,752 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Wuzhong 6.3 cm, Yinchuan 8.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
