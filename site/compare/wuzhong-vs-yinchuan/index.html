How the climate of Wuzhong, China compares with Yinchuan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Wuzhong is 1.3 °C (2.3 °F) warmer over the year: 11.2 °C against 9.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Wuzhong is 1.6 °C warmer (Wuzhong 23.6 °C, Yinchuan 21.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Wuzhong 31.7 °C (July), Yinchuan 29.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Wuzhong -12.2 °C, Yinchuan -12.9 °C.
  • Yinchuan gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 191 mm against 185 mm.
  • Yinchuan gets about 93 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,845 h against 2,752 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Wuzhong 6.3 cm, Yinchuan 8.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
