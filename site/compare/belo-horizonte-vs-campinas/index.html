Belo Horizonte vs Campinas climate

How the climate of Belo Horizonte, Brazil compares with Campinas, Brazil, 463 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Belo Horizonte and Campinas have the same yearly average temperature (21.0 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Campinas is 0.7 °C warmer (Belo Horizonte 22.3 °C, Campinas 23.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belo Horizonte 27.3 °C (February), Campinas 27.8 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Belo Horizonte 12.1 °C, Campinas 12.4 °C.
  • Belo Horizonte gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,615 mm against 1,607 mm.
  • Campinas gets about 89 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,472 h against 2,383 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Belo Horizonte in 8 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Campinas in 8 of 12 months
