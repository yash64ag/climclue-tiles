How the climate of Lahore, Pakistan compares with Meerut, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Lahore is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 24.7 °C against 24.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Lahore is 1.9 °C warmer (Lahore 29.9 °C, Meerut 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lahore 40.4 °C (June), Meerut 39.7 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Lahore 6.7 °C, Meerut 6.5 °C.
  • Meerut gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 875 mm against 684 mm.
  • Lahore gets about 278 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,937 h against 2,659 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Lahore in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Lahore in 10 of 12 months
