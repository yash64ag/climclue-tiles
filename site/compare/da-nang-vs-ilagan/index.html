Da Nang vs Ilagan climate

How the climate of Da Nang, Vietnam compares with Ilagan, Philippines, 1,461 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ilagan is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Ilagan is 1.8 °C warmer (Da Nang 24.1 °C, Ilagan 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Da Nang 32.0 °C (June), Ilagan 33.1 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Da Nang 20.2 °C, Ilagan 20.1 °C.
  • Da Nang gets 35% more rain and snow a year: 2,448 mm against 1,817 mm.
  • Ilagan gets about 259 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,491 h against 2,232 h; about 1961–1990).
