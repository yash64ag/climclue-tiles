Da Nang vs Ilagan climate

How the climate of Da Nang, Vietnam compares with Ilagan, Philippines, 1,461 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ilagan is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Ilagan is 1.9 °C warmer (Da Nang 24.1 °C, Ilagan 26.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Da Nang 33.4 °C (June), Ilagan 35.1 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Da Nang 18.8 °C, Ilagan 19.0 °C.
  • Da Nang gets 31% more rain and snow a year: 2,367 mm against 1,803 mm.
  • Ilagan gets about 259 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,491 h against 2,232 h; about 1961–1990).
