How the climate of Metairie, United States compares with Shaoguan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Shaoguan is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 21.2 °C against 21.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Metairie is 1.4 °C warmer (Metairie 17.2 °C, Shaoguan 15.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Metairie 33.1 °C (July), Shaoguan 34.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Metairie 7.3 °C, Shaoguan 6.8 °C.
  • Metairie gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,876 mm against 1,701 mm.
  • Metairie gets about 894 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,643 h against 1,749 h; about 1961–1990).
