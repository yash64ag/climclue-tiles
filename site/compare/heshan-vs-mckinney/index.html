Main differences

  • McKinney is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 17.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when McKinney is 1.8 °C warmer (Heshan, Hunan 11.7 °C, McKinney 13.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Heshan, Hunan 33.2 °C (July), McKinney 35.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Heshan, Hunan 2.5 °C, McKinney 1.2 °C.
  • Heshan, Hunan gets 49% more rain and snow a year: 1,590 mm against 1,067 mm.
