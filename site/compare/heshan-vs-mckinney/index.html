Heshan vs McKinney climate

How the climate of Heshan, China compares with McKinney, United States, 12,499 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • McKinney is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 17.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when McKinney is 1.8 °C warmer (Heshan 11.7 °C, McKinney 13.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Heshan 32.2 °C (July), McKinney 34.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Heshan 1.7 °C, McKinney 3.3 °C.
  • Heshan gets 49% more rain and snow a year: 1,590 mm against 1,067 mm.
  • McKinney gets about 1,220 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,808 h against 1,588 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Heshan 14 cm, McKinney 2.3 cm (ERA5-Land).
