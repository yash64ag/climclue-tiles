How the climate of Cabudare, Venezuela compares with Coimbatore, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Coimbatore is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 25.9 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Coimbatore is 2.6 °C warmer (Cabudare 26.5 °C, Coimbatore 29.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cabudare 32.8 °C (March), Coimbatore 35.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Cabudare 20.0 °C, Coimbatore 18.6 °C.
  • Cabudare gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 861 mm against 783 mm.
  • Cabudare gets about 216 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,622 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).
