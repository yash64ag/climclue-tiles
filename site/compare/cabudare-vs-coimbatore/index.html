Cabudare vs Coimbatore climate

Main differences

  • Coimbatore is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 25.8 °C against 25.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Coimbatore is 3.0 °C warmer (Cabudare 25.9 °C, Coimbatore 28.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Cabudare 31.3 °C (March), Coimbatore 35.0 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Cabudare 20.4 °C, Coimbatore 18.3 °C.
  • Cabudare gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 826 mm against 820 mm.
  • Cabudare gets about 216 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,622 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).
