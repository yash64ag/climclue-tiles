How the climate of Brussels, Belgium compares with Dublin, Ireland: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Brussels is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 10.9 °C against 10.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Brussels is 2.9 °C warmer (Brussels 18.8 °C, Dublin 15.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brussels 23.3 °C (July), Dublin 20.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Brussels 0.7 °C, Dublin 2.2 °C.
  • Dublin gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 941 mm against 851 mm.
  • Brussels gets about 195 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,587 h against 1,392 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Brussels 15 cm, Dublin 4.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
