Brussels vs Dublin climate

How the climate of Brussels, Belgium compares with Dublin, Ireland, 774 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Brussels is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 10.6 °C against 10.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Dublin is 2.6 °C warmer (Brussels 3.4 °C, Dublin 6.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brussels 23.2 °C (July), Dublin 19.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Brussels 0.7 °C, Dublin 3.3 °C.
  • Dublin gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 941 mm against 851 mm.
  • Brussels gets about 195 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,587 h against 1,392 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Brussels 15 cm, Dublin 4.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
