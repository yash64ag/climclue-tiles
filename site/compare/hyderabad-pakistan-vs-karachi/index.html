How the climate of Hyderabad, Pakistan compares with Karachi, Pakistan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hyderabad is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 26.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Hyderabad is 3.3 °C warmer (Hyderabad 33.0 °C, Karachi 29.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hyderabad 40.9 °C (May), Karachi 34.2 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Hyderabad 10.0 °C, Karachi 12.2 °C.
  • Karachi gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 180 mm against 170 mm.
  • Hyderabad gets about 256 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,301 h against 3,044 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Hyderabad in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Hyderabad in 12 of 12 months
