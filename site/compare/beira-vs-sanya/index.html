How the climate of Beira, Mozambique compares with Sanya, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Sanya is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 25.2 °C against 24.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Sanya is 7.2 °C warmer (Beira 20.9 °C, Sanya 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Beira 31.1 °C (January), Sanya 31.3 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Beira 15.8 °C, Sanya 16.8 °C.
  • Sanya gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,615 mm against 1,463 mm.
  • Beira gets about 846 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,985 h against 2,138 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Sanya in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Beira in 12 of 12 months
