Beira vs Sanya climate

How the climate of Beira, Mozambique compares with Sanya, China, 9,178 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Sanya is 0.4 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 24.9 °C against 24.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Sanya is 6.9 °C warmer (Beira 20.9 °C, Sanya 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Beira 29.9 °C (January), Sanya 29.5 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Beira 17.7 °C, Sanya 18.2 °C.
  • Sanya gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,651 mm against 1,482 mm.
  • Beira gets about 846 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,985 h against 2,138 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Sanya in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Beira in 12 of 12 months
