How the climate of Isfahan, Iran compares with Tehran, Iran: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Isfahan is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 16.4 °C against 16.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Isfahan is 2.5 °C warmer (Isfahan 4.8 °C, Tehran 2.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Isfahan 37.7 °C (July), Tehran 37.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Isfahan -3.8 °C, Tehran -3.8 °C.
  • Tehran gets 176% more rain and snow a year: 397 mm against 144 mm.
  • Isfahan gets about 314 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,185 h against 2,870 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Isfahan 19 cm, Tehran 33 cm (ERA5-Land).
