Baghdad vs Kirkuk climate

How the climate of Baghdad, Iraq compares with Kirkuk, Iraq, 237 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Baghdad is 3.1 °C (5.6 °F) warmer over the year: 25.3 °C against 22.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Baghdad is 4.4 °C warmer (Baghdad 24.9 °C, Kirkuk 20.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baghdad 44.6 °C (July), Kirkuk 42.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baghdad 5.7 °C, Kirkuk 3.6 °C.
  • Kirkuk gets 179% more rain and snow a year: 457 mm against 164 mm.
  • Baghdad gets about 162 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,480 h against 3,318 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baghdad 0.1 cm, Kirkuk 1.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
