Baghdad vs Kirkuk climate

How the climate of Baghdad, Iraq compares with Kirkuk, Iraq, 237 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Baghdad is 3.4 °C (6.1 °F) warmer over the year: 25.2 °C against 21.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Baghdad is 4.6 °C warmer (Baghdad 24.8 °C, Kirkuk 20.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baghdad 47.1 °C (July), Kirkuk 43.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baghdad 5.3 °C, Kirkuk 3.4 °C.
  • Kirkuk gets 212% more rain and snow a year: 498 mm against 160 mm.
  • Baghdad gets about 162 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,480 h against 3,318 h; about 1961–1990).
