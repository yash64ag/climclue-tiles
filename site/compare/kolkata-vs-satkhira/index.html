Kolkata vs Sātkhira climate

How the climate of Kolkata, India compares with Sātkhira, Bangladesh, 75 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kolkata and Sātkhira have the same yearly average temperature (26.1 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Kolkata is 0.5 °C warmer (Kolkata 30.1 °C, Sātkhira 29.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kolkata 35.0 °C (April), Sātkhira 34.7 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Kolkata 12.2 °C, Sātkhira 12.0 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,827 mm and 1,824 mm).
  • Sātkhira gets about 6 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,084 h against 2,078 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Sātkhira in 6 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
