Aurangabad vs Iguala de la Independencia climate

How the climate of Aurangabad, India compares with Iguala de la Independencia, Mexico, 15,728 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Iguala de la Independencia is 1.2 °C (2.1 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Iguala de la Independencia is 3.7 °C warmer (Aurangabad 20.4 °C, Iguala de la Independencia 24.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aurangabad 38.5 °C (May), Iguala de la Independencia 36.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Aurangabad 14.6 °C, Iguala de la Independencia 17.0 °C.
  • Aurangabad gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 973 mm against 908 mm.
  • Aurangabad gets about 618 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,785 h against 2,167 h; about 1961–1990).
