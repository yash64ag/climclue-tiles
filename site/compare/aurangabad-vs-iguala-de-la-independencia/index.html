Aurangabad vs Iguala de la Independencia climate

How the climate of Aurangabad, India compares with Iguala de la Independencia, Mexico, 15,728 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Iguala de la Independencia is 1.8 °C (3.3 °F) warmer over the year: 27.4 °C against 25.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Iguala de la Independencia is 4.4 °C warmer (Aurangabad 20.6 °C, Iguala de la Independencia 25.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aurangabad 38.6 °C (May), Iguala de la Independencia 37.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Aurangabad 14.7 °C, Iguala de la Independencia 17.7 °C.
  • Aurangabad gets 13% more rain and snow a year: 955 mm against 842 mm.
  • Aurangabad gets about 618 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,785 h against 2,167 h; about 1961–1990).
