How the climate of Regina, Canada compares with Yekaterinburg, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yekaterinburg is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 3.2 °C against 3.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Yekaterinburg is 1.9 °C warmer (Regina -13.0 °C, Yekaterinburg -11.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Regina 25.6 °C (July), Yekaterinburg 24.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Regina -20.1 °C, Yekaterinburg -16.1 °C.
  • Yekaterinburg gets 35% more rain and snow a year: 546 mm against 404 mm.
  • Regina gets about 433 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,309 h against 1,876 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Regina 81 cm, Yekaterinburg 152 cm (ERA5-Land).
