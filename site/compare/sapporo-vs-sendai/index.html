Sapporo vs Sendai, Miyagi climate

How the climate of Sapporo, Japan compares with Sendai, Miyagi, Japan, 535 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Sendai, Miyagi is 4.2 °C (7.5 °F) warmer over the year: 12.5 °C against 8.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Sendai, Miyagi is 6.6 °C warmer (Sapporo -4.8 °C, Sendai, Miyagi 1.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Sapporo 25.4 °C (August), Sendai, Miyagi 27.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Sapporo -8.5 °C, Sendai, Miyagi -1.9 °C.
  • Sapporo gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,573 mm against 1,487 mm.
  • Sendai, Miyagi gets about 142 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,819 h against 1,677 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Sapporo 417 cm, Sendai, Miyagi 75 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Sendai, Miyagi in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Sendai, Miyagi in 8 of 12 months
