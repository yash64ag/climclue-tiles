Sapporo vs Sendai climate

How the climate of Sapporo, Japan compares with Sendai, Japan, 535 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Sendai is 4.2 °C (7.6 °F) warmer over the year: 12.3 °C against 8.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Sendai is 6.7 °C warmer (Sapporo -5.2 °C, Sendai 1.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Sapporo 24.3 °C (August), Sendai 26.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Sapporo -9.9 °C, Sendai -3.0 °C.
  • Sapporo gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 1,620 mm against 1,518 mm.
  • Sendai gets about 142 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,819 h against 1,677 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Sapporo 417 cm, Sendai 75 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Sendai in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Sendai in 8 of 12 months
