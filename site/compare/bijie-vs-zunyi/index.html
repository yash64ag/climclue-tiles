Bijie vs Zunyi climate

How the climate of Bijie, China compares with Zunyi, China, 166 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zunyi is 3.7 °C (6.7 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 12.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Zunyi is 4.8 °C warmer (Bijie 20.4 °C, Zunyi 25.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bijie 24.1 °C (July), Zunyi 28.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bijie -1.5 °C, Zunyi 1.3 °C.
  • Bijie gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,072 mm against 1,060 mm.
  • Bijie gets about 232 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,514 h against 1,282 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bijie 97 cm, Zunyi 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
