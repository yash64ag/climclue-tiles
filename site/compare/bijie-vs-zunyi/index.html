How the climate of Bijie, China compares with Zunyi, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zunyi is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.2 °C against 15.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Zunyi is 2.4 °C warmer (Bijie 23.2 °C, Zunyi 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bijie 28.0 °C (July), Zunyi 30.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bijie 1.7 °C, Zunyi 2.7 °C.
  • Zunyi gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 1,082 mm against 973 mm.
  • Bijie gets about 232 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,514 h against 1,282 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bijie 31 cm, Zunyi 7.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
