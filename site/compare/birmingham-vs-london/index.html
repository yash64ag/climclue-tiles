Birmingham vs London climate

How the climate of Birmingham, United Kingdom compares with London, United Kingdom, 163 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • London is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 10.6 °C against 9.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when London is 1.1 °C warmer (Birmingham 16.2 °C, London 17.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Birmingham 20.1 °C (July), London 21.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Birmingham 1.1 °C, London 1.8 °C.
  • Birmingham gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 737 mm against 661 mm.
  • London gets about 96 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,459 h against 1,363 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Birmingham 6.3 cm, London 5.3 cm (ERA5-Land).
