How the climate of Gyeongsan-si, South Korea compares with Jining, Shandong, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jining, Shandong is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 14.5 °C against 13.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Jining, Shandong is 2.8 °C warmer (Gyeongsan-si 22.8 °C, Jining, Shandong 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Gyeongsan-si 30.7 °C (August), Jining, Shandong 31.6 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Gyeongsan-si -5.1 °C, Jining, Shandong -5.4 °C.
  • Gyeongsan-si gets 62% more rain and snow a year: 1,120 mm against 691 mm.
  • Gyeongsan-si gets about 49 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,452 h against 2,403 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Gyeongsan-si 12 cm, Jining, Shandong 8.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
