Changshu vs Kunshan climate

How the climate of Changshu, China compares with Kunshan, China, 36 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kunshan is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 16.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Kunshan is 0.4 °C warmer (Changshu 3.8 °C, Kunshan 4.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changshu 31.5 °C (July), Kunshan 31.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changshu -0.6 °C, Kunshan -0.3 °C.
  • Kunshan gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,298 mm against 1,248 mm.
  • Changshu gets about 26 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,004 h against 1,978 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changshu 14 cm, Kunshan 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
