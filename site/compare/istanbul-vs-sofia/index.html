Istanbul vs Sofia climate

How the climate of Istanbul, Turkey compares with Sofia, Bulgaria, 502 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Istanbul is 3.7 °C (6.7 °F) warmer over the year: 14.8 °C against 11.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Istanbul is 7.7 °C warmer (Istanbul 8.8 °C, Sofia 1.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Istanbul 28.4 °C (August), Sofia 27.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Istanbul 3.4 °C, Sofia -3.7 °C.
  • Istanbul gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 783 mm against 612 mm.
  • Istanbul gets about 717 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,763 h against 2,045 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Istanbul 16 cm, Sofia 95 cm (ERA5-Land).
