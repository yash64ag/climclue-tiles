How the climate of Ankara, Turkey compares with West Valley City, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ankara is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 12.9 °C against 12.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Ankara is 2.9 °C warmer (Ankara 3.2 °C, West Valley City 0.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ankara 32.3 °C (August), West Valley City 33.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ankara -2.4 °C, West Valley City -4.3 °C.
  • West Valley City gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 439 mm against 420 mm.
  • West Valley City gets about 171 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,982 h against 2,811 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Ankara 37 cm, West Valley City 104 cm (ERA5-Land).
