Tainan vs Tinsukia climate

How the climate of Tainan, Taiwan compares with Tinsukia, India, 2,544 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tainan is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 24.1 °C against 24.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Tainan is 1.0 °C warmer (Tainan 19.4 °C, Tinsukia 18.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Tainan 30.5 °C (July), Tinsukia 31.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Tainan 14.6 °C, Tinsukia 12.6 °C.
  • Tinsukia gets 32% more rain and snow a year: 2,443 mm against 1,855 mm.
  • Tinsukia gets about 395 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,387 h against 1,992 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 6 months each
  • Drier: Tainan in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Tinsukia in 8 of 12 months
