Abidjan vs Kumasi climate

How the climate of Abidjan, Ivory Coast compares with Kumasi, Ghana, 302 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kumasi is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 25.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Kumasi is 1.3 °C warmer (Abidjan 26.9 °C, Kumasi 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abidjan 30.9 °C (March), Kumasi 33.9 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Abidjan 21.3 °C, Kumasi 20.9 °C.
  • Abidjan gets 38% more rain and snow a year: 1,884 mm against 1,364 mm.
  • Abidjan gets about 180 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,118 h against 1,937 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Kumasi in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Abidjan in 10 of 12 months
