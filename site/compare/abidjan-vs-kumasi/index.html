Abidjan vs Kumasi climate

How the climate of Abidjan, Ivory Coast compares with Kumasi, Ghana, 302 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kumasi is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 26.1 °C against 25.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Kumasi is 1.3 °C warmer (Abidjan 26.9 °C, Kumasi 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abidjan 29.2 °C (March), Kumasi 33.5 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Abidjan 22.7 °C, Kumasi 21.7 °C.
  • Abidjan gets 43% more rain and snow a year: 1,908 mm against 1,335 mm.
  • Abidjan gets about 180 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,118 h against 1,937 h; about 1961–1990).
