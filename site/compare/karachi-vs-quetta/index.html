Karachi vs Quetta climate

How the climate of Karachi, Pakistan compares with Quetta, Pakistan, 592 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Karachi is 11.5 °C (20.6 °F) warmer over the year: 26.1 °C against 14.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Karachi is 17.4 °C warmer (Karachi 19.6 °C, Quetta 2.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Karachi 33.4 °C (May), Quetta 29.9 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Karachi 14.9 °C, Quetta -2.9 °C.
  • Quetta gets 130% more rain and snow a year: 420 mm against 183 mm.
  • Quetta gets about 288 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,333 h against 3,044 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Karachi 0 cm, Quetta 45 cm (ERA5-Land).
