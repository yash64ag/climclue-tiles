Abobo vs Twifu Praso climate

How the climate of Abobo, Ivory Coast compares with Twifu Praso, Ghana, 274 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Twifu Praso is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 25.7 °C against 25.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Twifu Praso is 0.6 °C warmer (Abobo 26.6 °C, Twifu Praso 27.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abobo 30.6 °C (March), Twifu Praso 32.0 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Abobo 21.2 °C, Twifu Praso 21.4 °C.
  • Abobo gets 36% more rain and snow a year: 2,100 mm against 1,548 mm.
  • Abobo gets about 173 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,113 h against 1,940 h; about 1961–1990).
