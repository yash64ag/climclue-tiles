Berlin vs Hamburg climate

How the climate of Berlin, Germany compares with Hamburg, Germany, 255 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Berlin is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 9.5 °C against 9.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Berlin is 1.3 °C warmer (Berlin 19.1 °C, Hamburg 17.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Berlin 24.1 °C (July), Hamburg 22.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Berlin -2.0 °C, Hamburg -1.1 °C.
  • Hamburg gets 37% more rain and snow a year: 813 mm against 594 mm.
  • Berlin gets about 79 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,614 h against 1,535 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Berlin 51 cm, Hamburg 40 cm (ERA5-Land).
