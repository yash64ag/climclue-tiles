How the climate of Berlin, Germany compares with Hamburg, Germany: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hamburg is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 9.6 °C against 9.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Hamburg is 1.3 °C warmer (Berlin 0.4 °C, Hamburg 1.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Berlin 24.4 °C (July), Hamburg 22.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Berlin -2.1 °C, Hamburg -0.7 °C.
  • Hamburg gets 37% more rain and snow a year: 813 mm against 594 mm.
  • Berlin gets about 79 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,614 h against 1,535 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Berlin 51 cm, Hamburg 40 cm (ERA5-Land).
