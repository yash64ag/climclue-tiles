Longyan vs Quanzhou climate

How the climate of Longyan, China compares with Quanzhou, China, 159 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Quanzhou is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 21.5 °C against 20.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Quanzhou is 2.0 °C warmer (Longyan 13.0 °C, Quanzhou 15.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Longyan 32.5 °C (July), Quanzhou 32.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Longyan 7.5 °C, Quanzhou 9.5 °C.
  • Longyan gets 35% more rain and snow a year: 1,570 mm against 1,162 mm.
  • Quanzhou gets about 112 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,958 h against 1,846 h; about 1961–1990).
