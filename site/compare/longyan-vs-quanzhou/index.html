Longyan vs Quanzhou climate

How the climate of Longyan, China compares with Quanzhou, China, 159 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Quanzhou is 1.7 °C (3.1 °F) warmer over the year: 21.4 °C against 19.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Quanzhou is 2.6 °C warmer (Longyan 16.9 °C, Quanzhou 19.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Longyan 30.3 °C (July), Quanzhou 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Longyan 5.8 °C, Quanzhou 8.3 °C.
  • Longyan gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 1,682 mm against 1,251 mm.
  • Quanzhou gets about 112 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,958 h against 1,846 h; about 1961–1990).
