Camayenne vs Monrovia climate

How the climate of Camayenne, Guinea compares with Monrovia, Liberia, 480 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Camayenne is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 26.2 °C against 25.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Camayenne is 1.1 °C warmer (Camayenne 27.0 °C, Monrovia 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Camayenne 28.2 °C (December), Monrovia 28.8 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Camayenne 24.4 °C, Monrovia 23.6 °C.
  • Camayenne gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 3,521 mm against 3,199 mm.
  • Camayenne gets about 418 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,185 h against 1,767 h; about 1961–1990).
