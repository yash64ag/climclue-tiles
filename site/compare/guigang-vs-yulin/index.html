How the climate of Guigang, China compares with Yulin, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yulin is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 22.5 °C against 22.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Yulin is 0.8 °C warmer (Guigang 13.1 °C, Yulin 14.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guigang 32.8 °C (July), Yulin 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Guigang 9.6 °C, Yulin 10.2 °C.
  • Yulin gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,753 mm against 1,689 mm.
  • Yulin gets about 77 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,785 h against 1,708 h; about 1961–1990).
