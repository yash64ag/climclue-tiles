Guigang vs Yulin climate

How the climate of Guigang, China compares with Yulin, China, 78 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yulin is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 22.5 °C against 21.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Yulin is 1.4 °C warmer (Guigang 12.6 °C, Yulin 13.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guigang 30.8 °C (August), Yulin 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Guigang 8.7 °C, Yulin 10.0 °C.
  • Guigang gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,960 mm against 1,754 mm.
  • Yulin gets about 77 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,785 h against 1,708 h; about 1961–1990).
