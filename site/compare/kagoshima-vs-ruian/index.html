How the climate of Kagoshima, Japan compares with Rui’an, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Rui’an is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 18.8 °C against 18.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Rui’an is 1.2 °C warmer (Kagoshima 24.0 °C, Rui’an 25.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kagoshima 32.1 °C (August), Rui’an 31.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kagoshima 4.2 °C, Rui’an 5.2 °C.
  • Kagoshima gets 28% more rain and snow a year: 2,100 mm against 1,640 mm.
  • Kagoshima gets about 64 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,859 h against 1,795 h; about 1961–1990).
