Kagoshima vs Rui’an climate

How the climate of Kagoshima, Japan compares with Rui’an, China, 1,044 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Rui’an is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 18.8 °C against 18.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Rui’an is 1.4 °C warmer (Kagoshima 23.9 °C, Rui’an 25.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kagoshima 30.3 °C (August), Rui’an 30.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kagoshima 4.7 °C, Rui’an 4.9 °C.
  • Kagoshima gets 40% more rain and snow a year: 2,249 mm against 1,607 mm.
  • Kagoshima gets about 64 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,859 h against 1,795 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kagoshima 1.2 cm, Rui’an 4.1 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Sunnier: Kagoshima in 7 of 12 months
