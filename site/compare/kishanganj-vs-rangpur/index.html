How the climate of Kishanganj, India compares with Rangpur, Bangladesh: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kishanganj is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 25.0 °C against 24.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Kishanganj is 1.4 °C warmer (Kishanganj 28.6 °C, Rangpur 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kishanganj 35.3 °C (April), Rangpur 33.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Kishanganj 9.9 °C, Rangpur 10.2 °C.
  • Rangpur gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 2,429 mm against 2,313 mm.
  • Rangpur gets about 10 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,194 h against 2,184 h; about 1961–1990).
