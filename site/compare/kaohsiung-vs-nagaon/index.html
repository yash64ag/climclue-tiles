Kaohsiung vs Nagaon climate

How the climate of Kaohsiung, Taiwan compares with Nagaon, India, 2,823 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kaohsiung is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 24.6 °C against 24.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Kaohsiung is 1.8 °C warmer (Kaohsiung 19.0 °C, Nagaon 17.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kaohsiung 32.3 °C (July), Nagaon 32.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kaohsiung 14.0 °C, Nagaon 10.8 °C.
  • Nagaon gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 2,324 mm against 1,839 mm.
  • Nagaon gets about 320 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,328 h against 2,008 h; about 1961–1990).
