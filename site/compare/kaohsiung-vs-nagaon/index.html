Kaohsiung vs Nagaon climate

How the climate of Kaohsiung, Taiwan compares with Nagaon, India, 2,823 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kaohsiung is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 24.5 °C against 24.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Kaohsiung is 1.6 °C warmer (Kaohsiung 19.9 °C, Nagaon 18.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kaohsiung 30.5 °C (July), Nagaon 31.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kaohsiung 15.2 °C, Nagaon 12.7 °C.
  • Nagaon gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 2,316 mm against 1,962 mm.
  • Nagaon gets about 320 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,328 h against 2,008 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 5 months each
  • Drier: Kaohsiung in 7 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Sunnier: Nagaon in 8 of 12 months
