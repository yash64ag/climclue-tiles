How the climate of Qingyuan, China compares with Shaoguan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Qingyuan is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 22.2 °C against 21.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Qingyuan is 2.2 °C warmer (Qingyuan 13.2 °C, Shaoguan 10.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Qingyuan 33.3 °C (July), Shaoguan 34.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Qingyuan 8.7 °C, Shaoguan 6.8 °C.
  • Qingyuan gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 2,101 mm against 1,701 mm.
  • Qingyuan gets about 9 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,758 h against 1,749 h; about 1961–1990).
