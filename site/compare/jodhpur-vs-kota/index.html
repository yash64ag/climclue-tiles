Jodhpur vs Kota climate

How the climate of Jodhpur, India compares with Kota, India, 308 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kota is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 26.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Kota is 1.6 °C warmer (Jodhpur 34.1 °C, Kota 35.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jodhpur 40.0 °C (May), Kota 41.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Jodhpur 9.7 °C, Kota 10.4 °C.
  • Kota gets 100% more rain and snow a year: 751 mm against 375 mm.
  • Jodhpur gets about 225 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,092 h against 2,867 h; about 1961–1990).
