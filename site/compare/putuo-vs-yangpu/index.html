How the climate of Putuo, China compares with Yangpu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Putuo and Yangpu have the same yearly average temperature (17.0 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Yangpu is 0.4 °C warmer (Putuo 7.4 °C, Yangpu 7.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Putuo 32.2 °C (July), Yangpu 31.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Putuo 1.5 °C, Yangpu 1.9 °C.
  • Putuo gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,466 mm against 1,430 mm.
  • Yangpu gets about 4 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,973 h against 1,969 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Putuo 8.9 cm, Yangpu 6.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
