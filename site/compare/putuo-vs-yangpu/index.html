Putuo vs Yangpu climate

How the climate of Putuo, China compares with Yangpu, China, 12 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Putuo and Yangpu have the same yearly average temperature (16.6 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Yangpu is 0.3 °C warmer (Putuo 7.0 °C, Yangpu 7.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Putuo 31.3 °C (July), Yangpu 30.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Putuo 0.6 °C, Yangpu 1.3 °C.
  • Putuo gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,466 mm against 1,430 mm.
  • Yangpu gets about 4 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,973 h against 1,969 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Putuo 8.9 cm, Yangpu 6.4 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: even, 5 months each
  • Drier: Yangpu in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Yangpu in 8 of 12 months (1 equal)
