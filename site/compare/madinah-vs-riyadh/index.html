Madinah vs Riyadh climate

How the climate of Madinah, Saudi Arabia compares with Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, 719 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Madinah is 1.6 °C (2.9 °F) warmer over the year: 27.8 °C against 26.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Madinah is 4.1 °C warmer (Madinah 19.5 °C, Riyadh 15.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Madinah 42.2 °C (August), Riyadh 44.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Madinah 11.7 °C, Riyadh 7.6 °C.
  • Riyadh gets 64% more rain and snow a year: 103 mm against 63 mm.
  • Madinah gets about 239 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,599 h against 3,360 h; about 1961–1990).
