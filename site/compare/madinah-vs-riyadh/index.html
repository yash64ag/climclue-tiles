Madinah vs Riyadh climate

How the climate of Madinah, Saudi Arabia compares with Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, 719 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Madinah is 1.4 °C (2.6 °F) warmer over the year: 27.7 °C against 26.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Madinah is 3.8 °C warmer (Madinah 19.4 °C, Riyadh 15.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Madinah 42.2 °C (August), Riyadh 42.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Madinah 9.3 °C, Riyadh 6.7 °C.
  • Riyadh gets 54% more rain and snow a year: 102 mm against 66 mm.
  • Madinah gets about 239 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,599 h against 3,360 h; about 1961–1990).
