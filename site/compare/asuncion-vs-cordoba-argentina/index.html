Asunción vs Córdoba climate

How the climate of Asunción, Paraguay compares with Córdoba, Argentina, 934 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Asunción is 5.6 °C (10.1 °F) warmer over the year: 22.8 °C against 17.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Asunción is 7.6 °C warmer (Asunción 17.3 °C, Córdoba 9.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Asunción 32.3 °C (January), Córdoba 28.9 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Asunción 13.5 °C, Córdoba 5.2 °C.
  • Asunción gets 63% more rain and snow a year: 1,426 mm against 877 mm.
  • Asunción gets about 172 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,764 h against 2,592 h; about 1961–1990).
