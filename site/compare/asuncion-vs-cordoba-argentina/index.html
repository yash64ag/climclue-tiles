Asunción vs Córdoba climate

How the climate of Asunción, Paraguay compares with Córdoba, Argentina, 934 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Asunción is 5.3 °C (9.6 °F) warmer over the year: 22.6 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Asunción is 7.4 °C warmer (Asunción 17.1 °C, Córdoba 9.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Asunción 32.0 °C (January), Córdoba 28.8 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Asunción 13.4 °C, Córdoba 5.2 °C.
  • Asunción gets 64% more rain and snow a year: 1,439 mm against 878 mm.
  • Asunción gets about 172 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,764 h against 2,592 h; about 1961–1990).
