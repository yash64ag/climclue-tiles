How the climate of Dallas, Texas, United States compares with San Antonio, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • San Antonio is 1.6 °C (3.0 °F) warmer over the year: 20.6 °C against 19.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when San Antonio is 3.1 °C warmer (Dallas, Texas 7.8 °C, San Antonio 10.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dallas, Texas 35.4 °C (August), San Antonio 35.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Dallas, Texas 2.1 °C, San Antonio 4.6 °C.
  • Dallas, Texas gets 17% more rain and snow a year: 939 mm against 803 mm.
  • Dallas, Texas gets about 223 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,794 h against 2,571 h; about 1961–1990).
