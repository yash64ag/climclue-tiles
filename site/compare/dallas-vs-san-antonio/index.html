Dallas vs San Antonio climate

How the climate of Dallas, United States compares with San Antonio, United States, 407 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • San Antonio is 1.6 °C (2.9 °F) warmer over the year: 20.5 °C against 18.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when San Antonio is 3.0 °C warmer (Dallas 7.8 °C, San Antonio 10.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dallas 34.6 °C (August), San Antonio 34.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Dallas 4.0 °C, San Antonio 7.0 °C.
  • Dallas gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 983 mm against 822 mm.
  • Dallas gets about 223 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,794 h against 2,571 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Dallas 4.8 cm, San Antonio 0.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
