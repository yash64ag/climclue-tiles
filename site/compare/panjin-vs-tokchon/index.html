Panjin vs Tŏkch’ŏn climate

How the climate of Panjin, China compares with Tŏkch’ŏn, North Korea, 385 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Panjin is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 9.8 °C against 9.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Panjin is 1.7 °C warmer (Panjin 18.1 °C, Tŏkch’ŏn 16.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Panjin 29.0 °C (July), Tŏkch’ŏn 28.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Panjin -14.3 °C, Tŏkch’ŏn -14.9 °C.
  • Tŏkch’ŏn gets 62% more rain and snow a year: 1,082 mm against 669 mm.
  • Panjin gets about 226 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,628 h against 2,402 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Panjin 24 cm, Tŏkch’ŏn 49 cm (ERA5-Land).
