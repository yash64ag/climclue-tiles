Tbilisi vs Yerevan climate

How the climate of Tbilisi, Georgia compares with Yerevan, Armenia, 170 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tbilisi is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 12.4 °C against 11.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Tbilisi is 2.8 °C warmer (Tbilisi 0.1 °C, Yerevan -2.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Tbilisi 29.8 °C (July), Yerevan 31.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Tbilisi -4.8 °C, Yerevan -7.3 °C.
  • Tbilisi gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 582 mm against 483 mm.
  • Yerevan gets about 450 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,497 h against 2,047 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Tbilisi 93 cm, Yerevan 109 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Tbilisi in 8 of 12 months (1 equal)
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Yerevan in 12 of 12 months
