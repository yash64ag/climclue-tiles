Hyesan vs Jilin climate

How the climate of Hyesan, North Korea compares with Jilin, China, 302 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jilin is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 5.9 °C against 5.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Jilin is 2.7 °C warmer (Hyesan 18.1 °C, Jilin 20.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hyesan 26.1 °C (July), Jilin 28.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hyesan -19.8 °C, Jilin -22.7 °C.
  • Jilin gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 649 mm against 618 mm.
  • Jilin gets about 177 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,506 h against 2,329 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hyesan 87 cm, Jilin 43 cm (ERA5-Land).
