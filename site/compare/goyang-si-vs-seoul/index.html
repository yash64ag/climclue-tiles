How the climate of Goyang-si, South Korea compares with Seoul, South Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Goyang-si is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 12.1 °C against 12.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Goyang-si is 0.3 °C warmer (Goyang-si 25.8 °C, Seoul 25.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Goyang-si 29.4 °C (August), Seoul 29.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Goyang-si -7.6 °C, Seoul -7.7 °C.
  • Seoul gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,443 mm against 1,429 mm.
  • Goyang-si gets about 11 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,331 h against 2,320 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Goyang-si 27 cm, Seoul 27 cm (ERA5-Land).
