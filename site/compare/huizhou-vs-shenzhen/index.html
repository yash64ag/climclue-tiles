How the climate of Huizhou, China compares with Shenzhen, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Shenzhen is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Shenzhen is 1.3 °C warmer (Huizhou 15.6 °C, Shenzhen 16.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huizhou 32.5 °C (July), Shenzhen 32.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Huizhou 9.9 °C, Shenzhen 11.2 °C.
  • Shenzhen gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 2,176 mm against 1,962 mm.
  • Shenzhen gets about 42 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,940 h against 1,898 h; about 1961–1990).
