Huizhou vs Shenzhen climate

How the climate of Huizhou, China compares with Shenzhen, China, 72 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shenzhen is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 22.8 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Shenzhen is 1.2 °C warmer (Huizhou 14.2 °C, Shenzhen 15.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huizhou 31.3 °C (August), Shenzhen 30.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Huizhou 8.9 °C, Shenzhen 11.5 °C.
  • Shenzhen gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 2,314 mm against 1,884 mm.
  • Shenzhen gets about 42 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,940 h against 1,898 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Sunnier: Shenzhen in 9 of 12 months
