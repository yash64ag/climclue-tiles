Kakamega vs Mwanza climate

How the climate of Kakamega, Kenya compares with Mwanza, Tanzania, 373 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mwanza is 2.3 °C (4.2 °F) warmer over the year: 22.7 °C against 20.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Mwanza is 3.3 °C warmer (Kakamega 19.3 °C, Mwanza 22.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kakamega 27.8 °C (February), Mwanza 25.8 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Kakamega 14.7 °C, Mwanza 18.8 °C.
  • Kakamega gets 65% more rain and snow a year: 1,729 mm against 1,049 mm.
  • Mwanza gets about 33 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,789 h against 2,755 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Mwanza in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Mwanza in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Kakamega in 7 of 12 months
