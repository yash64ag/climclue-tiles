Kakamega vs Mwanza climate

How the climate of Kakamega, Kenya compares with Mwanza, Tanzania, 373 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Mwanza is 2.8 °C (5.1 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 20.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Mwanza is 3.8 °C warmer (Kakamega 19.3 °C, Mwanza 23.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kakamega 27.9 °C (February), Mwanza 25.7 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Kakamega 14.2 °C, Mwanza 19.3 °C.
  • Kakamega gets 69% more rain and snow a year: 1,674 mm against 988 mm.
  • Mwanza gets about 33 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,789 h against 2,755 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Mwanza in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Mwanza in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Kakamega in 7 of 12 months
