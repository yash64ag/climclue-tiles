Busan vs Fukuoka climate

How the climate of Busan, South Korea compares with Fukuoka, Japan, 210 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fukuoka is 2.3 °C (4.2 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 14.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Fukuoka is 3.3 °C warmer (Busan 24.0 °C, Fukuoka 27.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Busan 29.0 °C (August), Fukuoka 32.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Busan -0.1 °C, Fukuoka 3.3 °C.
  • Fukuoka gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,726 mm against 1,638 mm.
  • Busan gets about 500 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,289 h against 1,789 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Busan 6.1 cm, Fukuoka 7.5 cm (ERA5-Land).
