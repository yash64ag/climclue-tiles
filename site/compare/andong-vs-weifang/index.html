How the climate of Andong, South Korea compares with Weifang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Weifang is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 13.4 °C against 12.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Weifang is 2.0 °C warmer (Andong 22.3 °C, Weifang 24.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Andong 30.3 °C (August), Weifang 31.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Andong -6.7 °C, Weifang -7.1 °C.
  • Andong gets 81% more rain and snow a year: 1,021 mm against 564 mm.
  • Weifang gets about 12 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,525 h against 2,513 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Andong 21 cm, Weifang 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
