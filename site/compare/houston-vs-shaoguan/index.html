Houston vs Shaoguan climate

How the climate of Houston, United States compares with Shaoguan, China, 13,207 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shaoguan is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 21.1 °C against 20.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Shaoguan is 1.9 °C warmer (Houston 21.4 °C, Shaoguan 23.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Houston 32.7 °C (August), Shaoguan 34.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Houston 7.1 °C, Shaoguan 6.3 °C.
  • Shaoguan gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 1,701 mm against 1,372 mm.
  • Houston gets about 827 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,576 h against 1,749 h; about 1961–1990).
