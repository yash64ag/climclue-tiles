Changde vs Jingzhou climate

How the climate of Changde, China compares with Jingzhou, China, 154 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Changde is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 17.8 °C against 17.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Changde is 0.8 °C warmer (Changde 7.7 °C, Jingzhou 7.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changde 32.0 °C (July), Jingzhou 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Changde 1.2 °C, Jingzhou 0.5 °C.
  • Changde gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,530 mm against 1,262 mm.
  • Jingzhou gets about 129 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,701 h against 1,572 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changde 14 cm, Jingzhou 11 cm (ERA5-Land).
