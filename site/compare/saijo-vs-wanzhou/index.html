Saijō vs Wanzhou climate

How the climate of Saijō, Japan compares with Wanzhou, China, 2,349 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Wanzhou is 2.1 °C (3.8 °F) warmer over the year: 19.4 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Wanzhou is 4.2 °C warmer (Saijō 10.3 °C, Wanzhou 14.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Saijō 32.1 °C (August), Wanzhou 34.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Saijō 2.6 °C, Wanzhou 5.9 °C.
  • Saijō gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 1,496 mm against 1,225 mm.
  • Saijō gets about 566 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,967 h against 1,401 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Saijō 2.8 cm, Wanzhou 0.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
