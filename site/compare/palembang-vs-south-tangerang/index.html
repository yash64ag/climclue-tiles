Palembang vs South Tangerang climate

How the climate of Palembang, Indonesia compares with South Tangerang, Indonesia, 434 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • South Tangerang is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 26.7 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when South Tangerang is 0.3 °C warmer (Palembang 27.1 °C, South Tangerang 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Palembang 31.6 °C (September), South Tangerang 31.5 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Palembang 22.9 °C, South Tangerang 22.4 °C.
  • Palembang gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 3,004 mm against 2,702 mm.
  • South Tangerang gets about 220 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,641 h against 2,422 h; about 1961–1990).
