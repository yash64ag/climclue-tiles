Fes vs Sevilla climate

How the climate of Fes, Morocco compares with Sevilla, Spain, 383 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fes is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 19.0 °C against 18.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Fes is 0.8 °C warmer (Fes 15.6 °C, Sevilla 14.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fes 34.5 °C (July), Sevilla 34.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fes 5.5 °C, Sevilla 5.8 °C.
  • Sevilla gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 585 mm against 551 mm.
  • Fes gets about 20 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,849 h against 2,829 h; about 1961–1990).
