How the climate of Fes, Morocco compares with Sevilla, Spain: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Sevilla is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 18.9 °C against 18.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Sevilla is 1.2 °C warmer (Fes 23.7 °C, Sevilla 24.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fes 34.7 °C (July), Sevilla 36.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fes 5.4 °C, Sevilla 5.6 °C.
  • Sevilla gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 551 mm against 546 mm.
  • Fes gets about 20 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,849 h against 2,829 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Sevilla in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Fes in 8 of 12 months
