How the climate of Bao'an, China compares with Tin Shui Wai, Hong Kong: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Tin Shui Wai is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 23.6 °C against 23.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Tin Shui Wai is 0.7 °C warmer (Bao'an 25.9 °C, Tin Shui Wai 26.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bao'an 31.7 °C (July), Tin Shui Wai 31.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bao'an 11.7 °C, Tin Shui Wai 13.4 °C.
  • Tin Shui Wai gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 2,242 mm against 2,140 mm.
  • Tin Shui Wai gets about 29 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,955 h against 1,927 h; about 1961–1990).
