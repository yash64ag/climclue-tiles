Xinyi, Guangdong vs Yulin climate

Main differences

  • Yulin is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 22.5 °C against 22.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Yulin is 0.4 °C warmer (Xinyi, Guangdong 24.4 °C, Yulin 24.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Xinyi, Guangdong 32.4 °C (July), Yulin 32.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Xinyi, Guangdong 9.8 °C, Yulin 10.2 °C.
  • Xinyi, Guangdong gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,924 mm against 1,753 mm.
  • Xinyi, Guangdong gets about 55 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,840 h against 1,785 h; about 1961–1990).
