Xinyi, Guangdong vs Yulin climate

How the climate of Xinyi, Guangdong, China compares with Yulin, China, 87 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Yulin is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 22.5 °C against 22.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Xinyi, Guangdong is 0.4 °C warmer (Xinyi, Guangdong 14.3 °C, Yulin 13.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Xinyi, Guangdong 30.8 °C (August), Yulin 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Xinyi, Guangdong 10.4 °C, Yulin 10.0 °C.
  • Xinyi, Guangdong gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,970 mm against 1,754 mm.
  • Xinyi, Guangdong gets about 55 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,840 h against 1,785 h; about 1961–1990).
