Amritsar vs Gujranwala climate

How the climate of Amritsar, India compares with Gujranwala, Pakistan, 88 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gujranwala is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 23.6 °C against 23.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Gujranwala is 0.7 °C warmer (Amritsar 30.4 °C, Gujranwala 31.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amritsar 38.6 °C (June), Gujranwala 39.2 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Amritsar 5.9 °C, Gujranwala 5.9 °C.
  • Gujranwala gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 923 mm against 758 mm.
  • Gujranwala gets about 140 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,969 h against 2,830 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Gujranwala in 9 of 12 months (2 equal)
  • Drier: Amritsar in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Gujranwala in 11 of 12 months
