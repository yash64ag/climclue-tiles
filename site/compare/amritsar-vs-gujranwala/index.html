Amritsar vs Gujranwala climate

How the climate of Amritsar, India compares with Gujranwala, Pakistan, 88 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Gujranwala is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 23.5 °C against 23.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Gujranwala is 0.7 °C warmer (Amritsar 30.4 °C, Gujranwala 31.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amritsar 39.9 °C (June), Gujranwala 40.2 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Amritsar 4.1 °C, Gujranwala 4.8 °C.
  • Gujranwala gets 25% more rain and snow a year: 937 mm against 749 mm.
  • Gujranwala gets about 140 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,969 h against 2,830 h; about 1961–1990).
