Aurangabad vs Nashik climate

How the climate of Aurangabad, India compares with Nashik, India, 163 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Aurangabad is 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) warmer over the year: 25.6 °C against 24.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Aurangabad is 3.7 °C warmer (Aurangabad 32.0 °C, Nashik 28.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Aurangabad 38.6 °C (May), Nashik 35.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Aurangabad 14.7 °C, Nashik 14.0 °C.
  • Nashik gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 1,180 mm against 955 mm.
  • Aurangabad gets about 28 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,785 h against 2,758 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Aurangabad in 12 of 12 months

  • Sunnier: Aurangabad in 8 of 12 months
