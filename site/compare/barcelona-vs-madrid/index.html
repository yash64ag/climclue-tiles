Barcelona vs Madrid climate

How the climate of Barcelona, Spain compares with Madrid, Spain, 504 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Barcelona is 1.4 °C (2.6 °F) warmer over the year: 16.2 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Barcelona is 4.5 °C warmer (Barcelona 10.7 °C, Madrid 6.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Barcelona 26.1 °C (August), Madrid 31.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Barcelona 7.0 °C, Madrid 0.6 °C.
  • Barcelona gets 37% more rain and snow a year: 603 mm against 439 mm.
  • Madrid gets about 212 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,597 h against 2,385 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Barcelona 0 cm, Madrid 4.3 cm (ERA5-Land).
