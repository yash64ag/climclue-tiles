Barcelona vs Madrid climate

How the climate of Barcelona, Spain compares with Madrid, Spain, 504 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Barcelona is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 14.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Barcelona is 4.2 °C warmer (Barcelona 10.4 °C, Madrid 6.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Barcelona 28.0 °C (August), Madrid 33.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Barcelona 5.5 °C, Madrid 0.9 °C.
  • Barcelona gets 58% more rain and snow a year: 634 mm against 400 mm.
  • Madrid gets about 212 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,597 h against 2,385 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Barcelona 0 cm, Madrid 4.3 cm (ERA5-Land).
