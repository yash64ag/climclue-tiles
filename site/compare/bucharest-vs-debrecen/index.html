How the climate of Bucharest, Romania compares with Debrecen, Hungary: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bucharest is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 11.5 °C against 10.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Bucharest is 1.5 °C warmer (Bucharest 22.7 °C, Debrecen 21.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bucharest 30.0 °C (July), Debrecen 27.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bucharest -4.7 °C, Debrecen -4.3 °C.
  • Bucharest gets 7% more rain and snow a year: 619 mm against 576 mm.
  • Bucharest gets about 117 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,075 h against 1,958 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bucharest 68 cm, Debrecen 49 cm (ERA5-Land).
