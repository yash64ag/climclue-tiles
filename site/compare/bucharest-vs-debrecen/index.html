Bucharest vs Debrecen climate

How the climate of Bucharest, Romania compares with Debrecen, Hungary, 488 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bucharest is 0.5 °C (0.9 °F) warmer over the year: 11.6 °C against 11.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Bucharest is 1.3 °C warmer (Bucharest 23.1 °C, Debrecen 21.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bucharest 27.9 °C (July), Debrecen 27.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bucharest -4.5 °C, Debrecen -4.0 °C.
  • Bucharest gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 611 mm against 581 mm.
  • Bucharest gets about 117 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,075 h against 1,958 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bucharest 68 cm, Debrecen 49 cm (ERA5-Land).
