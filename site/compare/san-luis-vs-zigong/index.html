San Luis vs Zigong climate

How the climate of San Luis, Argentina compares with Zigong, China, 19,063 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zigong is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 17.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Zigong is 17.4 °C warmer (San Luis 9.9 °C, Zigong 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: San Luis 29.8 °C (January), Zigong 30.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: San Luis 5.3 °C, Zigong 4.5 °C.
  • San Luis gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,133 mm against 1,118 mm.
  • San Luis gets about 1,653 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,021 h against 1,368 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
