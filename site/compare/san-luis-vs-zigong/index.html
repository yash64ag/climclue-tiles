How the climate of San Luis, Argentina compares with Zigong, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zigong is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 18.5 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Zigong is 17.9 °C warmer (San Luis 9.7 °C, Zigong 27.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: San Luis 31.2 °C (January), Zigong 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: San Luis 2.7 °C, Zigong 5.8 °C.
  • San Luis gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,110 mm against 1,098 mm.
  • San Luis gets about 1,653 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,021 h against 1,368 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Zigong in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: San Luis in 12 of 12 months
