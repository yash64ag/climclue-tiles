Huai'an vs Suqian climate

How the climate of Huai'an, China compares with Suqian, China, 78 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Huai'an is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 15.0 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Huai'an is 0.7 °C warmer (Huai'an 3.6 °C, Suqian 3.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Huai'an 30.8 °C (July), Suqian 30.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Huai'an -3.1 °C, Suqian -3.8 °C.
  • Huai'an gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 973 mm against 955 mm.
  • Suqian gets about 46 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,225 h against 2,179 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Huai'an 18 cm, Suqian 21 cm (ERA5-Land).
