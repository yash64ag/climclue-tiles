How the climate of Kazan, Russia compares with Nizhniy Novgorod, Russia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Nizhniy Novgorod is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 5.3 °C against 4.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Nizhniy Novgorod is 1.6 °C warmer (Kazan -10.2 °C, Nizhniy Novgorod -8.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kazan 25.6 °C (July), Nizhniy Novgorod 24.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kazan -13.7 °C, Nizhniy Novgorod -11.8 °C.
  • Nizhniy Novgorod gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 697 mm against 536 mm.
  • Kazan gets about 83 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,885 h against 1,802 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kazan 175 cm, Nizhniy Novgorod 207 cm (ERA5-Land).
