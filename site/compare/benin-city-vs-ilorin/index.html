Benin City vs Ilorin climate

How the climate of Benin City, Nigeria compares with Ilorin, Nigeria, 268 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Ilorin is 0.9 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 26.6 °C against 25.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Ilorin is 2.2 °C warmer (Benin City 26.9 °C, Ilorin 29.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Benin City 33.3 °C (February), Ilorin 36.3 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Benin City 21.9 °C, Ilorin 20.4 °C.
  • Benin City gets 107% more rain and snow a year: 2,246 mm against 1,085 mm.
  • Ilorin gets about 464 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,219 h against 1,755 h; about 1961–1990).
