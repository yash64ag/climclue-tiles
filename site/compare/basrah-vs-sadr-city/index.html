Basrah vs Sadr City climate

How the climate of Basrah, Iraq compares with Sadr City, Iraq, 448 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Basrah is 1.6 °C (2.9 °F) warmer over the year: 26.9 °C against 25.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Basrah is 2.3 °C warmer (Basrah 33.4 °C, Sadr City 31.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Basrah 46.5 °C (July), Sadr City 44.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Basrah 7.3 °C, Sadr City 5.7 °C.
  • Sadr City gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 167 mm against 151 mm.
  • Sadr City gets about 443 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,480 h against 3,037 h; about 1961–1990).
