Puyang, Zhejiang vs Shaoxing climate

How the climate of Puyang, Zhejiang, China compares with Shaoxing, China, 90 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shaoxing is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 17.6 °C against 17.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Shaoxing is 0.8 °C warmer (Puyang, Zhejiang 28.9 °C, Shaoxing 29.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Puyang, Zhejiang 32.6 °C (July), Shaoxing 33.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Puyang, Zhejiang 0.4 °C, Shaoxing 0.6 °C.
  • Puyang, Zhejiang gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 1,764 mm against 1,422 mm.
  • Shaoxing gets about 8 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,876 h against 1,868 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Puyang, Zhejiang 26 cm, Shaoxing 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
