Chongming vs Kyoto climate

How the climate of Chongming, China compares with Kyoto, Japan, 1,359 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Chongming is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.3 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Chongming is 1.8 °C warmer (Chongming 19.4 °C, Kyoto 17.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chongming 28.8 °C (July), Kyoto 30.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Chongming 2.9 °C, Kyoto -0.4 °C.
  • Kyoto gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,435 mm against 1,313 mm.
  • Chongming gets about 334 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,009 h against 1,674 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chongming 4.9 cm, Kyoto 27 cm (ERA5-Land).
