Nanjing vs Wuhu climate

How the climate of Nanjing, China compares with Wuhu, China, 85 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Wuhu is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.7 °C against 16.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Wuhu is 0.8 °C warmer (Nanjing 2.9 °C, Wuhu 3.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nanjing 31.5 °C (July), Wuhu 32.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nanjing -1.6 °C, Wuhu -0.7 °C.
  • Wuhu gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 1,341 mm against 1,167 mm.
  • Nanjing gets about 51 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,055 h against 2,004 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nanjing 24 cm, Wuhu 24 cm (ERA5-Land).
