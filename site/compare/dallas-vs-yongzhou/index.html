How the climate of Dallas, Texas, United States compares with Yongzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Dallas, Texas is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 19.3 °C against 18.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Dallas, Texas is 1.8 °C warmer (Dallas, Texas 14.4 °C, Yongzhou 12.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dallas, Texas 35.7 °C (August), Yongzhou 34.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Dallas, Texas 2.5 °C, Yongzhou 4.0 °C.
  • Yongzhou gets 55% more rain and snow a year: 1,454 mm against 939 mm.
  • Dallas, Texas gets about 1,214 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,794 h against 1,580 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Dallas, Texas 0.5 cm, Yongzhou 6.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
