Dallas vs Yongzhou climate

How the climate of Dallas, United States compares with Yongzhou, China, 12,777 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yongzhou is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 19.1 °C against 19.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Dallas is 1.5 °C warmer (Dallas 14.3 °C, Yongzhou 12.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dallas 34.7 °C (August), Yongzhou 33.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Dallas 3.9 °C, Yongzhou 3.0 °C.
  • Yongzhou gets 55% more rain and snow a year: 1,454 mm against 939 mm.
  • Dallas gets about 1,214 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,794 h against 1,580 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Dallas 0.4 cm, Yongzhou 6.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
