Main differences

  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Īẕeh is 1.4 °C warmer (Erbil 24.4 °C, Īẕeh 25.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Erbil 41.9 °C (July), Īẕeh 42.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Erbil 2.2 °C, Īẕeh 0.9 °C.
  • Īẕeh gets 30% more rain and snow a year: 812 mm against 625 mm.
