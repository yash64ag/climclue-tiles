Erbil vs Īẕeh climate

How the climate of Erbil, Iraq compares with Īẕeh, Iran, 726 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Īẕeh is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 20.4 °C against 20.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Īẕeh is 1.2 °C warmer (Erbil 24.4 °C, Īẕeh 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Erbil 41.3 °C (July), Īẕeh 39.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Erbil 1.3 °C, Īẕeh 0.2 °C.
  • Īẕeh gets 29% more rain and snow a year: 813 mm against 629 mm.
  • Erbil gets about 242 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,190 h against 2,948 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Erbil 7.2 cm, Īẕeh 25 cm (ERA5-Land).
