Lüliang vs Taiyuan climate

How the climate of Lüliang, China compares with Taiyuan, China, 131 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Taiyuan is 0.6 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 11.3 °C against 10.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Taiyuan is 1.3 °C warmer (Lüliang -5.5 °C, Taiyuan -4.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lüliang 30.6 °C (July), Taiyuan 30.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lüliang -12.0 °C, Taiyuan -11.0 °C.
  • Lüliang gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 480 mm against 422 mm.
  • Lüliang gets about 36 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,599 h against 2,563 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lüliang 19 cm, Taiyuan 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
