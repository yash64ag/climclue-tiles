Lüliang vs Taiyuan climate

How the climate of Lüliang, China compares with Taiyuan, China, 131 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Taiyuan is 1.1 °C (1.9 °F) warmer over the year: 11.3 °C against 10.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Taiyuan is 1.8 °C warmer (Lüliang -6.0 °C, Taiyuan -4.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lüliang 29.0 °C (July), Taiyuan 30.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lüliang -11.4 °C, Taiyuan -10.1 °C.
  • Lüliang gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 499 mm against 432 mm.
  • Lüliang gets about 36 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,599 h against 2,563 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lüliang 48 cm, Taiyuan 38 cm (ERA5-Land).
