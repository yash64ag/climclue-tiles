How the climate of Lüliang, China compares with Taiyuan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Taiyuan is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 10.9 °C against 10.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Taiyuan is 1.0 °C warmer (Lüliang -3.8 °C, Taiyuan -2.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Lüliang 30.6 °C (July), Taiyuan 29.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Lüliang -12.0 °C, Taiyuan -11.0 °C.
  • Lüliang gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 480 mm against 422 mm.
  • Lüliang gets about 36 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,599 h against 2,563 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Lüliang 19 cm, Taiyuan 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
