Kananga vs Kinshasa climate

How the climate of Kananga, Democratic Republic of the Congo compares with Kinshasa, Democratic Republic of the Congo, 806 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kinshasa is 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) warmer over the year: 25.6 °C against 24.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Kinshasa is 2.6 °C warmer (Kananga 23.6 °C, Kinshasa 26.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kananga 31.6 °C (July), Kinshasa 31.3 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Kananga 19.1 °C, Kinshasa 19.5 °C.
  • Kananga gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,542 mm against 1,463 mm.
  • Kananga gets about 304 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,105 h against 1,801 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Kinshasa in 11 of 12 months
  • Drier: Kinshasa in 7 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Kananga in 8 of 12 months
