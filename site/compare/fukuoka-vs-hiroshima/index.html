Fukuoka vs Hiroshima climate

How the climate of Fukuoka, Japan compares with Hiroshima, Japan, 207 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fukuoka is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Fukuoka is 1.1 °C warmer (Fukuoka 7.7 °C, Hiroshima 6.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fukuoka 32.2 °C (August), Hiroshima 31.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Fukuoka 3.3 °C, Hiroshima 2.3 °C.
  • Fukuoka gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,726 mm against 1,546 mm.
  • Hiroshima gets about 107 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,896 h against 1,789 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fukuoka 7.5 cm, Hiroshima 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
