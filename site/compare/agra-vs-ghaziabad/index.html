Agra vs Ghāziābād climate

How the climate of Agra, India compares with Ghāziābād, India, 174 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Agra is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 25.2 °C against 24.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Agra is 1.0 °C warmer (Agra 23.4 °C, Ghāziābād 22.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Agra 40.9 °C (May), Ghāziābād 39.6 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Agra 6.7 °C, Ghāziābād 6.6 °C.
  • Agra gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 719 mm against 692 mm.
  • Agra gets about 33 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,707 h against 2,674 h; about 1961–1990).
