Fortaleza vs Salvador climate

How the climate of Fortaleza, Brazil compares with Salvador, Brazil, 1,030 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fortaleza is 1.5 °C (2.8 °F) warmer over the year: 26.8 °C against 25.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Fortaleza is 2.8 °C warmer (Fortaleza 26.4 °C, Salvador 23.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fortaleza 29.7 °C (November), Salvador 27.5 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Fortaleza 24.2 °C, Salvador 22.7 °C.
  • Salvador gets 27% more rain and snow a year: 1,717 mm against 1,353 mm.
  • Fortaleza gets about 313 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,643 h against 2,331 h; about 1961–1990).
