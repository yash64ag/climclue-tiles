How the climate of Khartoum North, Sudan compares with Omdurman, Sudan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Khartoum North is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 29.5 °C against 29.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Khartoum North is 0.2 °C warmer (Khartoum North 27.6 °C, Omdurman 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Khartoum North 41.9 °C (May), Omdurman 41.9 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Khartoum North 14.4 °C, Omdurman 14.2 °C.
  • Khartoum North gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 101 mm against 99 mm.
  • Khartoum North gets about 5 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,517 h against 3,512 h; about 1961–1990).
