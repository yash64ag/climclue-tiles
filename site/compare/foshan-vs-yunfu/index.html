How the climate of Foshan, China compares with Yunfu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Foshan is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 22.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Foshan is 0.9 °C warmer (Foshan 28.1 °C, Yunfu 27.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Foshan 33.3 °C (July), Yunfu 32.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Foshan 10.5 °C, Yunfu 9.6 °C.
  • Foshan gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,981 mm against 1,900 mm.
  • Yunfu gets about 20 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,827 h against 1,807 h; about 1961–1990).
