How the climate of Jinhua, Zhejiang, China compares with Shangrao, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Shangrao is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 19.0 °C against 18.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Shangrao is 1.1 °C warmer (Jinhua, Zhejiang 17.7 °C, Shangrao 18.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinhua, Zhejiang 34.7 °C (July), Shangrao 34.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinhua, Zhejiang 2.0 °C, Shangrao 2.4 °C.
  • Shangrao gets 32% more rain and snow a year: 2,027 mm against 1,534 mm.
  • Jinhua, Zhejiang gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,885 h against 1,878 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinhua, Zhejiang 12 cm, Shangrao 6.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
