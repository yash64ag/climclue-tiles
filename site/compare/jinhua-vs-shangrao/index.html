Jinhua vs Shangrao climate

How the climate of Jinhua, China compares with Shangrao, China, 181 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Shangrao is 1.0 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 18.9 °C against 18.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Shangrao is 1.5 °C warmer (Jinhua 17.3 °C, Shangrao 18.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinhua 33.1 °C (July), Shangrao 33.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinhua 1.1 °C, Shangrao 2.2 °C.
  • Shangrao gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 2,045 mm against 1,620 mm.
  • Jinhua gets about 7 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,885 h against 1,878 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinhua 24 cm, Shangrao 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
