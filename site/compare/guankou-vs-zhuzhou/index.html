How the climate of Guankou, China compares with Zhuzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zhuzhou is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 18.6 °C against 18.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Zhuzhou is 0.5 °C warmer (Guankou 29.7 °C, Zhuzhou 30.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Guankou 34.2 °C (July), Zhuzhou 34.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Guankou 2.3 °C, Zhuzhou 2.9 °C.
  • Guankou gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,774 mm against 1,637 mm.
  • Guankou gets about 46 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,652 h against 1,605 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Guankou 6.8 cm, Zhuzhou 6.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
