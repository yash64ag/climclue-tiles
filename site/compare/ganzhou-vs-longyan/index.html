Ganzhou vs Longyan climate

How the climate of Ganzhou, China compares with Longyan, China, 226 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Ganzhou is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 20.0 °C against 19.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Ganzhou is 3.6 °C warmer (Ganzhou 30.4 °C, Longyan 26.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ganzhou 33.8 °C (July), Longyan 30.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ganzhou 3.7 °C, Longyan 5.8 °C.
  • Longyan gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,682 mm against 1,637 mm.
  • Longyan gets about 44 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,846 h against 1,802 h; about 1961–1990).
