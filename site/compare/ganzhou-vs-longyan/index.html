How the climate of Ganzhou, China compares with Longyan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Longyan is 0.4 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 20.7 °C against 20.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Longyan is 3.3 °C warmer (Ganzhou 8.7 °C, Longyan 11.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ganzhou 34.6 °C (July), Longyan 32.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Ganzhou 5.2 °C, Longyan 7.5 °C.
  • Ganzhou gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,635 mm against 1,570 mm.
  • Longyan gets about 44 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,846 h against 1,802 h; about 1961–1990).
