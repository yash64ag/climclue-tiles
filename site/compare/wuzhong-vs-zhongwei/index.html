Wuzhong vs Zhongwei climate

How the climate of Wuzhong, China compares with Zhongwei, China, 103 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhongwei is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 11.6 °C against 11.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Zhongwei is 0.8 °C warmer (Wuzhong -0.9 °C, Zhongwei -0.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Wuzhong 31.7 °C (July), Zhongwei 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Wuzhong -12.2 °C, Zhongwei -11.5 °C.
  • Zhongwei gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 188 mm against 185 mm.
  • Wuzhong gets about 55 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,752 h against 2,697 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Wuzhong 6.3 cm, Zhongwei 5.6 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Sunnier: Wuzhong in 12 of 12 months
