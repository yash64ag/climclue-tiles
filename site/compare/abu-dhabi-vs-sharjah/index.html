How the climate of Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates compares with Sharjah, United Arab Emirates: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Abu Dhabi is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 27.6 °C against 27.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Abu Dhabi is 0.4 °C warmer (Abu Dhabi 25.7 °C, Sharjah 25.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Abu Dhabi 40.4 °C (August), Sharjah 40.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Abu Dhabi 14.3 °C, Sharjah 14.0 °C.
  • Sharjah gets 67% more rain and snow a year: 118 mm against 71 mm.
  • Abu Dhabi gets about 23 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,443 h against 3,420 h; about 1961–1990).
