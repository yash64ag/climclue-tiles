Jinhua vs Shaoxing climate

How the climate of Jinhua, China compares with Shaoxing, China, 134 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jinhua is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 18.4 °C against 17.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Jinhua is 1.2 °C warmer (Jinhua 8.1 °C, Shaoxing 6.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinhua 33.6 °C (July), Shaoxing 33.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Jinhua 1.3 °C, Shaoxing 0.5 °C.
  • Jinhua gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,534 mm against 1,452 mm.
  • Jinhua gets about 9 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,885 h against 1,876 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinhua 12 cm, Shaoxing 16 cm (ERA5-Land).
