How the climate of Phnom Penh, Cambodia compares with Tân Châu, Vietnam: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Phnom Penh is 0.5 °C (0.8 °F) warmer over the year: 28.2 °C against 27.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Phnom Penh is 0.9 °C warmer (Phnom Penh 28.3 °C, Tân Châu 27.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Phnom Penh 35.6 °C (April), Tân Châu 35.0 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Phnom Penh 21.6 °C, Tân Châu 21.5 °C.
  • Tân Châu gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,459 mm against 1,395 mm.
  • Phnom Penh gets about 308 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,549 h against 2,241 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
