Agadez vs Khartoum climate

How the climate of Agadez, Niger compares with Khartoum, Sudan, 2,623 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Khartoum is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 29.6 °C against 29.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Khartoum is 2.4 °C warmer (Agadez 25.4 °C, Khartoum 27.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Agadez 40.7 °C (May), Khartoum 40.5 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: Agadez 12.9 °C, Khartoum 15.1 °C.
  • Agadez gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 128 mm against 107 mm.
  • Khartoum gets about 323 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,512 h against 3,188 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Khartoum in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Agadez in 6 of 12 months (2 equal)
  • Sunnier: Khartoum in 11 of 12 months
