How the climate of Paniqui, Philippines compares with Yangon, Myanmar: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Paniqui is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 27.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Yangon is 1.8 °C warmer (Paniqui 29.3 °C, Yangon 31.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Paniqui 35.0 °C (April), Yangon 37.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Paniqui 20.2 °C, Yangon 17.1 °C.
  • Yangon gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 2,796 mm against 2,087 mm.
  • Paniqui gets about 4 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,578 h against 2,574 h; about 1961–1990).
