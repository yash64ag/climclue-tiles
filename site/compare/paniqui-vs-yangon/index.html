Paniqui vs Yangon climate

How the climate of Paniqui, Philippines compares with Yangon, Myanmar, 2,609 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Yangon is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 27.4 °C against 27.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Yangon is 2.0 °C warmer (Paniqui 29.3 °C, Yangon 31.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Paniqui 34.1 °C (April), Yangon 37.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Paniqui 20.3 °C, Yangon 17.7 °C.
  • Yangon gets 31% more rain and snow a year: 2,761 mm against 2,114 mm.
  • Paniqui gets about 4 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,578 h against 2,574 h; about 1961–1990).
