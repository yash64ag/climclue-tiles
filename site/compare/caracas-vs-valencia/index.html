Caracas vs Valencia climate

How the climate of Caracas, Venezuela compares with Valencia, Venezuela, 128 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Valencia is 2.2 °C (3.9 °F) warmer over the year: 24.6 °C against 22.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Valencia is 2.4 °C warmer (Caracas 23.3 °C, Valencia 25.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Caracas 28.8 °C (May), Valencia 31.4 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Caracas 15.6 °C, Valencia 16.1 °C.
  • Valencia gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 1,379 mm against 1,121 mm.
  • Valencia gets about 321 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,706 h against 2,385 h; about 1961–1990).
