How the climate of Fuyang, Anhui, China compares with Suzhou, Anhui, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Fuyang, Anhui is 0.5 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 15.7 °C against 15.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Fuyang, Anhui is 0.9 °C warmer (Fuyang, Anhui 4.2 °C, Suzhou, Anhui 3.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fuyang, Anhui 31.9 °C (July), Suzhou, Anhui 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Fuyang, Anhui -2.5 °C, Suzhou, Anhui -3.4 °C.
  • Fuyang, Anhui gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 1,013 mm against 904 mm.
  • Suzhou, Anhui gets about 67 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,201 h against 2,134 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fuyang, Anhui 23 cm, Suzhou, Anhui 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
