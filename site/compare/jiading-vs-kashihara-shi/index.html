Jiading vs Kashihara-shi climate

How the climate of Jiading, China compares with Kashihara-shi, Japan, 1,400 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jiading is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 16.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Jiading is 1.1 °C warmer (Jiading 28.3 °C, Kashihara-shi 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiading 31.4 °C (July), Kashihara-shi 31.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Jiading 0.4 °C, Kashihara-shi -0.2 °C.
  • Kashihara-shi gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,488 mm against 1,411 mm.
  • Jiading gets about 144 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,979 h against 1,835 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jiading 9.5 cm, Kashihara-shi 7.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
