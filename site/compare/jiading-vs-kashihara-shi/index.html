How the climate of Jiading, China compares with Kashihara-shi, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jiading is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 16.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Jiading is 1.4 °C warmer (Jiading 28.4 °C, Kashihara-shi 27.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jiading 31.8 °C (July), Kashihara-shi 32.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Jiading 1.1 °C, Kashihara-shi 0.6 °C.
  • Kashihara-shi gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,439 mm against 1,422 mm.
  • Jiading gets about 144 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,979 h against 1,835 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jiading 9.5 cm, Kashihara-shi 7.7 cm (ERA5-Land).
