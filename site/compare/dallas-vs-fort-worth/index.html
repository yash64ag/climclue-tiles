Dallas vs Fort Worth climate

How the climate of Dallas, United States compares with Fort Worth, United States, 48 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Dallas and Fort Worth have the same yearly average temperature (18.9 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Dallas is 0.3 °C warmer (Dallas 8.6 °C, Fort Worth 8.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dallas 34.6 °C (August), Fort Worth 34.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Dallas 4.0 °C, Fort Worth 3.8 °C.
  • Dallas gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 983 mm against 880 mm.
  • Fort Worth gets about 44 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,838 h against 2,794 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Dallas 4.8 cm, Fort Worth 5.4 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Sunnier: Fort Worth in 12 of 12 months
