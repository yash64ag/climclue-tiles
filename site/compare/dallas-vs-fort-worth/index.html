Main differences

  • Dallas, Texas is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 19.0 °C against 18.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Dallas, Texas is 0.2 °C warmer (Dallas, Texas 8.6 °C, Fort Worth 8.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Dallas, Texas 35.4 °C (August), Fort Worth 35.8 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Dallas, Texas 2.1 °C, Fort Worth 1.5 °C.
  • Dallas, Texas gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 939 mm against 895 mm.
  • Fort Worth gets about 44 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,838 h against 2,794 h; about 1961–1990).
