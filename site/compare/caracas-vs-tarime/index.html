Caracas vs Tarime climate

How the climate of Caracas, Venezuela compares with Tarime, Tanzania, 11,264 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Caracas is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 22.4 °C against 21.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Caracas is 2.1 °C warmer (Caracas 23.3 °C, Tarime 21.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Caracas 27.4 °C (April), Tarime 28.6 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Caracas 17.7 °C, Tarime 15.8 °C.
  • Caracas gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,121 mm against 1,106 mm.
  • Tarime gets about 524 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,909 h against 2,385 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
