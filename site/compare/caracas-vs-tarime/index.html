Caracas vs Tarime climate

How the climate of Caracas, Venezuela compares with Tarime, Tanzania, 11,264 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Caracas is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 21.6 °C against 21.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Caracas is 1.3 °C warmer (Caracas 22.4 °C, Tarime 21.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Caracas 26.8 °C (April), Tarime 28.7 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Caracas 16.8 °C, Tarime 15.8 °C.
  • Caracas gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 1,347 mm against 1,094 mm.
  • Tarime gets about 524 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,909 h against 2,385 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
