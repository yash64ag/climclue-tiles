How the climate of Hohhot, China compares with Hyesan, North Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Hohhot is 1.7 °C (3.0 °F) warmer over the year: 7.0 °C against 5.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Hohhot is 3.0 °C warmer (Hohhot 21.0 °C, Hyesan 18.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Hohhot 29.1 °C (July), Hyesan 26.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Hohhot -18.1 °C, Hyesan -19.8 °C.
  • Hyesan gets 65% more rain and snow a year: 618 mm against 374 mm.
  • Hohhot gets about 604 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,933 h against 2,329 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Hohhot 19 cm, Hyesan 87 cm (ERA5-Land).
