Iztapalapa vs Puebla climate

How the climate of Iztapalapa, Mexico compares with Puebla, Mexico, 96 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Puebla is 1.5 °C (2.6 °F) warmer over the year: 17.3 °C against 15.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Puebla is 1.8 °C warmer (Iztapalapa 14.3 °C, Puebla 16.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iztapalapa 23.9 °C (May), Puebla 25.5 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Iztapalapa 5.7 °C, Puebla 7.4 °C.
  • Iztapalapa gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 777 mm against 739 mm.
  • Puebla gets about 413 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,426 h against 2,013 h; about 1961–1990).
