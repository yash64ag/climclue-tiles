How the climate of Baoding, Hebei, China compares with Datong, Shanxi, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Baoding, Hebei is 5.9 °C (10.6 °F) warmer over the year: 13.8 °C against 7.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Baoding, Hebei is 7.2 °C warmer (Baoding, Hebei -2.6 °C, Datong, Shanxi -9.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoding, Hebei 33.1 °C (June), Datong, Shanxi 29.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Baoding, Hebei -8.4 °C, Datong, Shanxi -16.4 °C.
  • Baoding, Hebei gets 36% more rain and snow a year: 516 mm against 380 mm.
  • Datong, Shanxi gets about 200 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,809 h against 2,609 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoding, Hebei 10 cm, Datong, Shanxi 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
