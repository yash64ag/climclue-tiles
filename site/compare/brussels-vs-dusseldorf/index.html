How the climate of Brussels, Belgium compares with Düsseldorf, Germany: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Düsseldorf is 0.1 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 10.9 °C against 10.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Düsseldorf is 0.7 °C warmer (Brussels 18.7 °C, Düsseldorf 19.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brussels 23.3 °C (July), Düsseldorf 24.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Brussels 0.6 °C, Düsseldorf 0.5 °C.
  • Düsseldorf gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 867 mm against 851 mm.
  • Brussels gets about 163 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,587 h against 1,424 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Brussels 15 cm, Düsseldorf 23 cm (ERA5-Land).
