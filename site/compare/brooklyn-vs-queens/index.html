How the climate of Brooklyn, United States compares with Queens, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Brooklyn and Queens have the same yearly average temperature (12.3 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Queens is 0.1 °C warmer (Brooklyn -0.1 °C, Queens 0.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brooklyn 28.8 °C (July), Queens 28.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Brooklyn -3.6 °C, Queens -3.3 °C.
  • Brooklyn gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,240 mm against 1,199 mm.
  • Queens gets about 6 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,523 h against 2,518 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Brooklyn 77 cm, Queens 73 cm (ERA5-Land).
