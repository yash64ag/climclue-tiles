How the climate of Baoding, Hebei, China compares with Songnim-ni, North Korea: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Baoding, Hebei is 2.5 °C (4.5 °F) warmer over the year: 13.8 °C against 11.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Baoding, Hebei is 4.9 °C warmer (Baoding, Hebei 22.3 °C, Songnim-ni 17.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Baoding, Hebei 33.1 °C (June), Songnim-ni 29.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Baoding, Hebei -8.4 °C, Songnim-ni -9.9 °C.
  • Songnim-ni gets 69% more rain and snow a year: 874 mm against 516 mm.
  • Baoding, Hebei gets about 175 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,609 h against 2,434 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Baoding, Hebei 10 cm, Songnim-ni 24 cm (ERA5-Land).
