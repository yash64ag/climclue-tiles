Adana vs Gaziantep climate

How the climate of Adana, Turkey compares with Gaziantep, Turkey, 183 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Adana is 3.9 °C (7.0 °F) warmer over the year: 18.9 °C against 15.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Adana is 5.7 °C warmer (Adana 8.8 °C, Gaziantep 3.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Adana 33.8 °C (August), Gaziantep 34.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Adana 4.3 °C, Gaziantep -1.1 °C.
  • Adana gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 714 mm against 705 mm.
  • Gaziantep gets about 130 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,122 h against 2,992 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Adana 1.7 cm, Gaziantep 49 cm (ERA5-Land).
