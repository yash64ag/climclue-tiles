Adana vs Gaziantep climate

How the climate of Adana, Turkey compares with Gaziantep, Turkey, 183 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Adana is 3.6 °C (6.4 °F) warmer over the year: 18.9 °C against 15.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Adana is 5.3 °C warmer (Adana 9.7 °C, Gaziantep 4.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Adana 34.2 °C (August), Gaziantep 35.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Adana 3.7 °C, Gaziantep -0.9 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (687 mm and 689 mm).
  • Gaziantep gets about 130 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,122 h against 2,992 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Adana 0.2 cm, Gaziantep 84 cm (ERA5-Land).
