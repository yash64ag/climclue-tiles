Nagoya vs Pudong climate

How the climate of Nagoya, Japan compares with Pudong, China, 1,497 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Pudong is 0.7 °C (1.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 15.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Pudong is 1.6 °C warmer (Nagoya 26.5 °C, Pudong 28.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nagoya 32.0 °C (August), Pudong 31.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nagoya 0.4 °C, Pudong 1.3 °C.
  • Nagoya gets 20% more rain and snow a year: 1,689 mm against 1,413 mm.
  • Pudong gets about 4 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,973 h against 1,969 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nagoya 7.4 cm, Pudong 6.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
