Nagoya vs Pudong climate

How the climate of Nagoya, Japan compares with Pudong, China, 1,497 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Pudong is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 16.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Pudong is 1.5 °C warmer (Nagoya 11.9 °C, Pudong 13.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nagoya 31.0 °C (August), Pudong 30.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Nagoya -1.2 °C, Pudong 1.5 °C.
  • Nagoya gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 1,644 mm against 1,431 mm.
  • Pudong gets about 4 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,973 h against 1,969 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nagoya 7.4 cm, Pudong 6.4 cm (ERA5-Land).
