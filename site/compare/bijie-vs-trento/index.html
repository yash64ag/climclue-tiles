Bijie vs Trento climate

How the climate of Bijie, China compares with Trento, Italy, 8,163 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Bijie is 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 12.2 °C against 11.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Bijie is 2.1 °C warmer (Bijie 3.8 °C, Trento 1.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bijie 24.1 °C (July), Trento 25.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bijie -1.5 °C, Trento -6.0 °C.
  • Bijie gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,072 mm against 922 mm.
  • Trento gets about 408 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,922 h against 1,514 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bijie 97 cm, Trento 232 cm (ERA5-Land).
