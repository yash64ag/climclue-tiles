How the climate of Bijie, China compares with Trento, Italy: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Bijie is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 13.5 °C against 13.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Bijie is 1.8 °C warmer (Bijie 5.1 °C, Trento 3.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bijie 26.8 °C (July), Trento 29.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Bijie 0.1 °C, Trento -2.5 °C.
  • Bijie gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 973 mm against 836 mm.
  • Trento gets about 408 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,922 h against 1,514 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bijie 31 cm, Trento 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
