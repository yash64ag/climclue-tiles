Fengxiang vs Kawanishi climate

How the climate of Fengxiang, China compares with Kawanishi, Japan, 1,374 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Fengxiang is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.7 °C against 16.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Fengxiang is 0.9 °C warmer (Fengxiang 19.5 °C, Kawanishi 18.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fengxiang 29.9 °C (July), Kawanishi 31.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Fengxiang 1.8 °C, Kawanishi 0.8 °C.
  • Kawanishi gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,445 mm against 1,409 mm.
  • Fengxiang gets about 163 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,937 h against 1,773 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fengxiang 5.5 cm, Kawanishi 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
