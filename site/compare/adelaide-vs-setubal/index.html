Adelaide vs Setúbal climate

How the climate of Adelaide, Australia compares with Setúbal, Portugal, 17,105 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Setúbal is 0.4 °C (0.7 °F) warmer over the year: 16.6 °C against 16.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Setúbal is 10.3 °C warmer (Adelaide 11.1 °C, Setúbal 21.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Adelaide 27.9 °C (February), Setúbal 27.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Adelaide 7.1 °C, Setúbal 8.4 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (705 mm and 707 mm).
  • Setúbal gets about 70 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,752 h against 2,682 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
