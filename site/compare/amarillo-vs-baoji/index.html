Amarillo vs Baoji climate

How the climate of Amarillo, United States compares with Baoji, China, 11,708 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Baoji is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 14.4 °C against 14.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Baoji is 1.6 °C warmer (Amarillo 13.9 °C, Baoji 15.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amarillo 33.0 °C (July), Baoji 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Amarillo -5.1 °C, Baoji -3.0 °C.
  • Baoji gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 653 mm against 550 mm.
  • Amarillo gets about 1,304 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 1,939 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amarillo 23 cm, Baoji 6.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
