Amarillo vs Baoji climate

How the climate of Amarillo, United States compares with Baoji, China, 11,708 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Baoji is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 14.5 °C against 14.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Baoji is 1.6 °C warmer (Amarillo 14.1 °C, Baoji 15.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Amarillo 32.5 °C (July), Baoji 30.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Amarillo -1.4 °C, Baoji -3.9 °C.
  • Baoji gets 24% more rain and snow a year: 670 mm against 542 mm.
  • Amarillo gets about 1,304 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 1,939 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Amarillo 55 cm, Baoji 85 cm (ERA5-Land).
