Onomichi vs Suzhou, Jiangsu climate

How the climate of Onomichi, Japan compares with Suzhou, Jiangsu, China, 1,226 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Onomichi is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.8 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Onomichi is 2.1 °C warmer (Onomichi 8.4 °C, Suzhou, Jiangsu 6.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Onomichi 30.7 °C (August), Suzhou, Jiangsu 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Onomichi 1.9 °C, Suzhou, Jiangsu 0.2 °C.
  • Onomichi gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,308 mm against 1,203 mm.
  • Onomichi gets about 3 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,979 h against 1,977 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Onomichi 12 cm, Suzhou, Jiangsu 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
