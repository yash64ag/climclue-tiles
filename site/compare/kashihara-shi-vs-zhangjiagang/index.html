How the climate of Kashihara-shi, Japan compares with Zhangjiagang, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zhangjiagang is 0.6 °C (1.0 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 15.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Zhangjiagang is 1.8 °C warmer (Kashihara-shi 18.7 °C, Zhangjiagang 20.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kashihara-shi 32.6 °C (August), Zhangjiagang 31.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kashihara-shi 0.5 °C, Zhangjiagang -0.4 °C.
  • Kashihara-shi gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,439 mm against 1,192 mm.
  • Zhangjiagang gets about 191 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,026 h against 1,835 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kashihara-shi 7.7 cm, Zhangjiagang 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
