Kashihara-shi vs Zhangjiagang climate

How the climate of Kashihara-shi, Japan compares with Zhangjiagang, China, 1,448 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Kashihara-shi is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.1 °C against 16.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Kashihara-shi is 1.5 °C warmer (Kashihara-shi 4.9 °C, Zhangjiagang 3.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kashihara-shi 31.6 °C (August), Zhangjiagang 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kashihara-shi -0.4 °C, Zhangjiagang -0.8 °C.
  • Kashihara-shi gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,439 mm against 1,192 mm.
  • Zhangjiagang gets about 191 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,026 h against 1,835 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kashihara-shi 7.7 cm, Zhangjiagang 12 cm (ERA5-Land).
