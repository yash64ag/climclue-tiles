Kashihara-shi vs Zhangjiagang climate

How the climate of Kashihara-shi, Japan compares with Zhangjiagang, China, 1,448 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Kashihara-shi is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 16.3 °C against 16.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Kashihara-shi is 1.6 °C warmer (Kashihara-shi 5.0 °C, Zhangjiagang 3.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kashihara-shi 31.8 °C (August), Zhangjiagang 31.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kashihara-shi -0.2 °C, Zhangjiagang -0.8 °C.
  • Kashihara-shi gets 26% more rain and snow a year: 1,488 mm against 1,183 mm.
  • Zhangjiagang gets about 191 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,026 h against 1,835 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kashihara-shi 28 cm, Zhangjiagang 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
