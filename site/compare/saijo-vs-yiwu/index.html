How the climate of Saijō, Japan compares with Yiwu, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Yiwu is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 17.9 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Yiwu is 2.5 °C warmer (Saijō 19.8 °C, Yiwu 22.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Saijō 32.1 °C (August), Yiwu 34.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Saijō 2.6 °C, Yiwu 1.4 °C.
  • Saijō gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,496 mm against 1,413 mm.
  • Saijō gets about 101 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,967 h against 1,866 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Saijō 2.8 cm, Yiwu 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
