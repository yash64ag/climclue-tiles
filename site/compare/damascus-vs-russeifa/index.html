Damascus vs Russeifa climate

How the climate of Damascus, Syria compares with Russeifa, Jordan, 167 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Russeifa is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 17.8 °C against 17.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Russeifa is 1.2 °C warmer (Damascus 7.9 °C, Russeifa 9.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Damascus 34.9 °C (July), Russeifa 32.9 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Damascus 2.1 °C, Russeifa 3.6 °C.
  • Damascus gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 283 mm against 232 mm.
  • Russeifa gets about 223 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,299 h against 3,075 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Damascus 13 cm, Russeifa 0.2 cm (ERA5-Land).
