How the climate of Arad, Romania compares with Sofia, Bulgaria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Arad is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 11.3 °C against 11.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Arad is 0.8 °C warmer (Arad 16.9 °C, Sofia 16.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Arad 28.8 °C (July), Sofia 27.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Arad -3.8 °C, Sofia -3.7 °C.
  • Arad gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 619 mm against 612 mm.
  • Sofia gets about 6 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,045 h against 2,040 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Arad 43 cm, Sofia 95 cm (ERA5-Land).
