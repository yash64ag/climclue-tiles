How the climate of Bazhong, China compares with Nanchong, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Nanchong is 0.9 °C (1.7 °F) warmer over the year: 18.3 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Nanchong is 1.3 °C warmer (Bazhong 7.6 °C, Nanchong 8.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Bazhong 31.3 °C (July), Nanchong 32.2 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Bazhong 3.0 °C, Nanchong 5.1 °C.
  • Bazhong gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 1,298 mm against 1,119 mm.
  • Bazhong gets about 164 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,456 h against 1,292 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Bazhong 1.6 cm, Nanchong 0.1 cm (ERA5-Land).
