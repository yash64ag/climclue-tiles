Medan vs Seremban climate

How the climate of Medan, Indonesia compares with Seremban, Malaysia, 375 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Medan and Seremban have the same yearly average temperature (26.5 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Seremban is 0.4 °C warmer (Medan 26.4 °C, Seremban 26.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Medan 32.2 °C (April), Seremban 32.1 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Medan 21.1 °C, Seremban 21.2 °C.
  • Medan gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 2,911 mm against 2,564 mm.
  • Seremban gets about 75 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,217 h against 2,142 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Drier: Seremban in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Seremban in 9 of 12 months
