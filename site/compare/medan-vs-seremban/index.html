Medan vs Seremban climate

How the climate of Medan, Indonesia compares with Seremban, Malaysia, 375 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Medan is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.4 °C against 26.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in July, when Medan is 0.4 °C warmer (Medan 26.6 °C, Seremban 26.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Medan 30.7 °C (June), Seremban 30.5 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Medan 21.3 °C, Seremban 21.5 °C.
  • Medan gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 2,910 mm against 2,635 mm.
  • Seremban gets about 75 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,217 h against 2,142 h; about 1961–1990).
