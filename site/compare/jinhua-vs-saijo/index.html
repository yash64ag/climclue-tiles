Jinhua vs Saijō climate

How the climate of Jinhua, China compares with Saijō, Japan, 1,389 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jinhua is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 18.0 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Jinhua is 2.4 °C warmer (Jinhua 22.3 °C, Saijō 19.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinhua 33.1 °C (July), Saijō 30.5 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Jinhua 1.1 °C, Saijō 3.4 °C.
  • Jinhua gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,620 mm against 1,481 mm.
  • Saijō gets about 82 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,967 h against 1,885 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinhua 24 cm, Saijō 17 cm (ERA5-Land).
