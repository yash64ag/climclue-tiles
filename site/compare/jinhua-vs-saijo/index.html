Jinhua vs Saijō climate

How the climate of Jinhua, China compares with Saijō, Japan, 1,389 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jinhua is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 18.4 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Jinhua is 2.9 °C warmer (Jinhua 22.7 °C, Saijō 19.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinhua 33.6 °C (July), Saijō 30.4 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Jinhua 1.3 °C, Saijō 3.4 °C.
  • Jinhua gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,534 mm against 1,496 mm.
  • Saijō gets about 82 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,967 h against 1,885 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinhua 12 cm, Saijō 2.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
