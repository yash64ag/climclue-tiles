How the climate of Jinhua, Zhejiang, China compares with Saijō, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Jinhua, Zhejiang is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 18.4 °C against 17.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Jinhua, Zhejiang is 2.9 °C warmer (Jinhua, Zhejiang 22.7 °C, Saijō 19.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jinhua, Zhejiang 34.7 °C (July), Saijō 32.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Jinhua, Zhejiang 2.0 °C, Saijō 2.6 °C.
  • Jinhua, Zhejiang gets 3% more rain and snow a year: 1,534 mm against 1,496 mm.
  • Saijō gets about 82 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,967 h against 1,885 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Jinhua, Zhejiang 12 cm, Saijō 2.8 cm (ERA5-Land).
