Quito vs Zipaquirá climate

How the climate of Quito, Ecuador compares with Zipaquirá, Colombia, 770 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zipaquirá is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 11.9 °C against 11.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Zipaquirá is 1.1 °C warmer (Quito 11.1 °C, Zipaquirá 12.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Quito 16.3 °C (August), Zipaquirá 17.3 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Quito 7.8 °C, Zipaquirá 7.2 °C.
  • Quito gets 34% more rain and snow a year: 2,262 mm against 1,695 mm.
  • Quito gets about 150 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,875 h against 1,725 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
