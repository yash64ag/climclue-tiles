How the climate of Quito, Ecuador compares with Zipaquirá, Colombia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Zipaquirá is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 13.9 °C against 13.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Zipaquirá is 0.8 °C warmer (Quito 13.5 °C, Zipaquirá 14.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Quito 22.1 °C (August), Zipaquirá 20.4 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Quito 3.0 °C, Zipaquirá 7.7 °C.
  • Quito gets 49% more rain and snow a year: 2,072 mm against 1,392 mm.
  • Quito gets about 150 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,875 h against 1,725 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

  • Sunnier: Quito in 8 of 12 months
