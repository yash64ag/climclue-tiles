How the climate of Ilorin, Nigeria compares with Lagos, Nigeria: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Ilorin is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 26.7 °C against 26.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Ilorin is 1.4 °C warmer (Ilorin 29.3 °C, Lagos 27.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Ilorin 35.7 °C (February), Lagos 32.2 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: Ilorin 19.7 °C, Lagos 21.7 °C.
  • Lagos gets 51% more rain and snow a year: 1,627 mm against 1,075 mm.
  • Ilorin gets about 340 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,219 h against 1,879 h; about 1961–1990).
