How the climate of Chongming, China compares with Saitama, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Chongming is 1.2 °C (2.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 15.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Chongming is 2.2 °C warmer (Chongming 13.8 °C, Saitama 11.7 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Chongming 30.7 °C (August), Saitama 31.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Chongming 1.8 °C, Saitama -1.3 °C.
  • Saitama gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 1,336 mm against 1,313 mm.
  • Chongming gets about 146 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,009 h against 1,863 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Chongming 4.9 cm, Saitama 20 cm (ERA5-Land).
