Fort Worth vs Wansheng climate

How the climate of Fort Worth, United States compares with Wansheng, China, 12,694 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Fort Worth is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 18.8 °C against 18.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Fort Worth is 1.5 °C warmer (Fort Worth 26.9 °C, Wansheng 25.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fort Worth 35.8 °C (August), Wansheng 32.7 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Fort Worth 1.5 °C, Wansheng 5.6 °C.
  • Wansheng gets 21% more rain and snow a year: 1,085 mm against 895 mm.
  • Fort Worth gets about 1,610 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,838 h against 1,228 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Fort Worth 0.4 cm, Wansheng 2.0 cm (ERA5-Land).
