Main differences

  • La Ceiba, Atlántida Department is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 25.7 °C against 25.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when La Ceiba, Atlántida Department is 2.9 °C warmer (La Ceiba, Atlántida Department 26.9 °C, Maceió 23.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: La Ceiba, Atlántida Department 29.3 °C (June), Maceió 27.9 °C (February). Coldest-month night lows: La Ceiba, Atlántida Department 21.6 °C, Maceió 22.5 °C.
  • La Ceiba, Atlántida Department gets 16% more rain and snow a year: 2,084 mm against 1,798 mm.
  • La Ceiba, Atlántida Department gets about 66 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,613 h against 2,547 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
