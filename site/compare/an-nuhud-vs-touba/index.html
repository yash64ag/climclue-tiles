An Nuhūd vs Touba climate

How the climate of An Nuhūd, Sudan compares with Touba, Senegal, 4,784 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Touba is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 27.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when An Nuhūd is 2.2 °C warmer (An Nuhūd 30.6 °C, Touba 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: An Nuhūd 38.2 °C (April), Touba 37.5 °C (May). Coldest-month night lows: An Nuhūd 14.9 °C, Touba 16.8 °C.
  • Touba gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 469 mm against 352 mm.
  • An Nuhūd gets about 303 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,335 h against 3,032 h; about 1961–1990).
