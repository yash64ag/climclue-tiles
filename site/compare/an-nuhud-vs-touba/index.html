How the climate of An Nuhūd, Sudan compares with Touba, Senegal: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Touba is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 27.3 °C against 27.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when An Nuhūd is 2.5 °C warmer (An Nuhūd 30.8 °C, Touba 28.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: An Nuhūd 39.7 °C (April), Touba 37.9 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: An Nuhūd 13.1 °C, Touba 15.1 °C.
  • Touba gets 33% more rain and snow a year: 468 mm against 352 mm.
  • An Nuhūd gets about 303 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,335 h against 3,032 h; about 1961–1990).
