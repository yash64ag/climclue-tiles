Jepara vs Surabaya climate

How the climate of Jepara, Indonesia compares with Surabaya, Indonesia, 241 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Surabaya is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 27.8 °C against 27.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in October, when Surabaya is 1.2 °C warmer (Jepara 27.7 °C, Surabaya 28.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jepara 31.3 °C (September), Surabaya 32.4 °C (October). Coldest-month night lows: Jepara 23.8 °C, Surabaya 23.8 °C.
  • Jepara gets 36% more rain and snow a year: 2,502 mm against 1,841 mm.
  • Surabaya gets about 480 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,243 h against 2,762 h; about 1961–1990).
