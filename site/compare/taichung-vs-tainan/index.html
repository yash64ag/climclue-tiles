Taichung vs Tainan climate

How the climate of Taichung, Taiwan compares with Tainan, Taiwan, 137 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Tainan is 0.9 °C (1.5 °F) warmer over the year: 24.1 °C against 23.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Tainan is 1.5 °C warmer (Taichung 19.8 °C, Tainan 21.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Taichung 29.9 °C (July), Tainan 30.5 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Taichung 13.5 °C, Tainan 14.6 °C.
  • Taichung gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 1,949 mm against 1,855 mm.
  • Tainan gets about 155 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,992 h against 1,837 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier
