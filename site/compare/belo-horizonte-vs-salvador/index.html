Belo Horizonte vs Salvador climate

How the climate of Belo Horizonte, Brazil compares with Salvador, Brazil, 966 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Salvador is 4.2 °C (7.6 °F) warmer over the year: 25.2 °C against 21.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Salvador is 6.5 °C warmer (Belo Horizonte 18.0 °C, Salvador 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belo Horizonte 27.3 °C (February), Salvador 27.5 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Belo Horizonte 12.1 °C, Salvador 22.7 °C.
  • Salvador gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 1,717 mm against 1,615 mm.
  • Belo Horizonte gets about 53 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,383 h against 2,331 h; about 1961–1990).
