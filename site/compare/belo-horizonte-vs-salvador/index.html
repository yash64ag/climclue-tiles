Belo Horizonte vs Salvador climate

How the climate of Belo Horizonte, Brazil compares with Salvador, Brazil, 966 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Salvador is 4.9 °C (8.8 °F) warmer over the year: 25.2 °C against 20.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Salvador is 7.2 °C warmer (Belo Horizonte 17.3 °C, Salvador 24.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Belo Horizonte 26.7 °C (February), Salvador 27.6 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Belo Horizonte 11.4 °C, Salvador 22.6 °C.
  • Salvador gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,761 mm against 1,697 mm.
  • Belo Horizonte gets about 53 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,383 h against 2,331 h; about 1961–1990).
