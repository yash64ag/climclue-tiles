How the climate of Prague, Czechia compares with Wrocław, Poland: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Prague is 1.1 °C (2.0 °F) warmer over the year: 10.5 °C against 9.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Prague is 1.5 °C warmer (Prague 5.8 °C, Wrocław 4.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Prague 25.3 °C (July), Wrocław 25.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Prague -1.8 °C, Wrocław -3.6 °C.
  • Wrocław gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 576 mm against 525 mm.
  • Prague gets about 162 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,640 h against 1,477 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Prague 42 cm, Wrocław 46 cm (ERA5-Land).
