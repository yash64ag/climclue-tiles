Prague vs Wrocław climate

How the climate of Prague, Czechia compares with Wrocław, Poland, 216 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Prague and Wrocław have the same yearly average temperature (9.4 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Prague is 0.4 °C warmer (Prague 4.7 °C, Wrocław 4.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Prague 23.9 °C (July), Wrocław 23.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Prague -3.5 °C, Wrocław -3.5 °C.
  • Wrocław gets 5% more rain and snow a year: 571 mm against 541 mm.
  • Prague gets about 162 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,640 h against 1,477 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Prague 42 cm, Wrocław 46 cm (ERA5-Land).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Prague in 4 of 12 months (5 equal)
  • Drier: Prague in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Prague in 12 of 12 months
