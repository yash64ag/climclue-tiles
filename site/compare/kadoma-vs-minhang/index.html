Kadoma vs Minhang climate

How the climate of Kadoma, Japan compares with Minhang, China, 1,384 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Kadoma is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 16.7 °C against 16.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Kadoma is 1.1 °C warmer (Kadoma 5.7 °C, Minhang 4.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kadoma 31.9 °C (August), Minhang 31.6 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Kadoma 0.7 °C, Minhang 0.3 °C.
  • Both get about the same rain and snow a year (1,466 mm and 1,461 mm).
  • Minhang gets about 131 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,959 h against 1,828 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kadoma 5.1 cm, Minhang 8.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
