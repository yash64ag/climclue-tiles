Durban vs Johannesburg climate

How the climate of Durban, South Africa compares with Johannesburg, South Africa, 501 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Durban is 6.1 °C (10.9 °F) warmer over the year: 21.1 °C against 15.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Durban is 9.4 °C warmer (Durban 17.9 °C, Johannesburg 8.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Durban 26.7 °C (February), Johannesburg 24.3 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Durban 13.8 °C, Johannesburg 2.1 °C.
  • Durban gets 15% more rain and snow a year: 909 mm against 792 mm.
  • Johannesburg gets about 656 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,169 h against 2,513 h; about 1961–1990).
