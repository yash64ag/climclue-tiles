Durban vs Johannesburg climate

How the climate of Durban, South Africa compares with Johannesburg, South Africa, 501 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Durban is 6.4 °C (11.6 °F) warmer over the year: 21.2 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Durban is 9.8 °C warmer (Durban 18.1 °C, Johannesburg 8.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Durban 26.7 °C (February), Johannesburg 24.0 °C (January). Coldest-month night lows: Durban 14.0 °C, Johannesburg 1.8 °C.
  • Durban gets 11% more rain and snow a year: 914 mm against 821 mm.
  • Johannesburg gets about 656 more hours of bright sunshine a year (3,169 h against 2,513 h; about 1961–1990).
