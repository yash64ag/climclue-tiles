Shiyan vs Xiangyang climate

How the climate of Shiyan, China compares with Xiangyang, China, 145 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Xiangyang is 1.0 °C (1.8 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 15.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Xiangyang is 1.3 °C warmer (Shiyan 21.1 °C, Xiangyang 22.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Shiyan 30.4 °C (July), Xiangyang 31.2 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Shiyan -2.1 °C, Xiangyang -1.6 °C.
  • Shiyan gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 950 mm against 943 mm.
  • Xiangyang gets about 66 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,864 h against 1,798 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Shiyan 54 cm, Xiangyang 20 cm (ERA5-Land).
