Shiyan, Hubei vs Xiangyang climate

How the climate of Shiyan, Hubei, China compares with Xiangyang, China, 145 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Xiangyang is 0.6 °C (1.1 °F) warmer over the year: 16.5 °C against 15.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Xiangyang is 0.8 °C warmer (Shiyan, Hubei 21.4 °C, Xiangyang 22.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Shiyan, Hubei 32.1 °C (July), Xiangyang 32.1 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Shiyan, Hubei -2.1 °C, Xiangyang -1.3 °C.
  • Xiangyang gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 942 mm against 892 mm.
  • Xiangyang gets about 66 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,864 h against 1,798 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Shiyan, Hubei 18 cm, Xiangyang 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
