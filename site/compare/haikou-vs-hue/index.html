Haikou, Hainan vs Huế climate

How the climate of Haikou, Hainan, China compares with Huế, Vietnam, 492 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Huế is 1.7 °C (3.0 °F) warmer over the year: 26.0 °C against 24.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Huế is 3.0 °C warmer (Haikou, Hainan 18.4 °C, Huế 21.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Haikou, Hainan 32.4 °C (July), Huế 33.8 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Haikou, Hainan 15.3 °C, Huế 18.3 °C.
  • Huế gets 64% more rain and snow a year: 2,948 mm against 1,800 mm.
  • Huế gets about 168 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,260 h against 2,092 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Huế in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: Huế in 7 of 12 months
