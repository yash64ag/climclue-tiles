How the climate of Anyang, China compares with Handan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Anyang is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 14.8 °C against 14.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Anyang is 0.4 °C warmer (Anyang 7.7 °C, Handan 7.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Anyang 32.9 °C (June), Handan 33.0 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Anyang -5.8 °C, Handan -6.4 °C.
  • Anyang gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 552 mm against 522 mm.
  • Handan gets about 48 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,454 h against 2,406 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Anyang 11 cm, Handan 8.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
