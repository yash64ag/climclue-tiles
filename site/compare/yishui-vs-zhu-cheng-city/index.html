Yishui vs Zhu Cheng City climate

How the climate of Yishui, China compares with Zhu Cheng City, China, 74 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Zhu Cheng City is 0.3 °C (0.5 °F) warmer over the year: 13.2 °C against 12.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in November, when Zhu Cheng City is 0.9 °C warmer (Yishui 6.7 °C, Zhu Cheng City 7.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Yishui 29.7 °C (July), Zhu Cheng City 29.9 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Yishui -6.2 °C, Zhu Cheng City -5.8 °C.
  • Zhu Cheng City gets 2% more rain and snow a year: 702 mm against 688 mm.
  • Zhu Cheng City gets about 13 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,450 h against 2,437 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Yishui 16 cm, Zhu Cheng City 15 cm (ERA5-Land).
