Pikine vs Sullana climate

How the climate of Pikine, Senegal compares with Sullana, Peru, 7,308 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Pikine is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 24.4 °C against 24.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Sullana is 6.0 °C warmer (Pikine 21.2 °C, Sullana 27.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pikine 31.1 °C (October), Sullana 33.3 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Pikine 16.5 °C, Sullana 15.5 °C.
  • Pikine gets 77% more rain and snow a year: 385 mm against 218 mm.
  • Pikine gets about 448 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,977 h against 2,529 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
