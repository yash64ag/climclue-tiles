Pikine vs Sullana climate

How the climate of Pikine, Senegal compares with Sullana, Peru, 7,308 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Pikine is 0.3 °C (0.6 °F) warmer over the year: 24.6 °C against 24.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Sullana is 5.7 °C warmer (Pikine 21.6 °C, Sullana 27.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Pikine 29.4 °C (October), Sullana 33.0 °C (March). Coldest-month night lows: Pikine 19.6 °C, Sullana 16.8 °C.
  • Pikine gets 78% more rain and snow a year: 391 mm against 220 mm.
  • Pikine gets about 448 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,977 h against 2,529 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
