Copenhagen vs Stockholm climate

How the climate of Copenhagen, Denmark compares with Stockholm, Sweden, 522 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Copenhagen is 1.9 °C (3.3 °F) warmer over the year: 8.4 °C against 6.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Copenhagen is 3.1 °C warmer (Copenhagen 0.8 °C, Stockholm -2.3 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Copenhagen 21.3 °C (July), Stockholm 21.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Copenhagen -1.5 °C, Stockholm -4.7 °C.
  • Copenhagen gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 660 mm against 587 mm.
  • Copenhagen gets about 165 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,652 h against 1,487 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Copenhagen 41 cm, Stockholm 116 cm (ERA5-Land).
