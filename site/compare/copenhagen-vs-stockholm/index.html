How the climate of Copenhagen, Denmark compares with Stockholm, Sweden: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Copenhagen is 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) warmer over the year: 9.0 °C against 7.5 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Copenhagen is 3.2 °C warmer (Copenhagen 1.5 °C, Stockholm -1.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Copenhagen 21.5 °C (July), Stockholm 23.0 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Copenhagen -1.0 °C, Stockholm -4.3 °C.
  • Copenhagen gets 12% more rain and snow a year: 660 mm against 587 mm.
  • Copenhagen gets about 165 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,652 h against 1,487 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Copenhagen 41 cm, Stockholm 116 cm (ERA5-Land).
