How the climate of Fortaleza, Brazil compares with Puducherry, India: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Puducherry is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 27.7 °C against 26.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Puducherry is 3.7 °C warmer (Fortaleza 26.3 °C, Puducherry 30.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Fortaleza 30.9 °C (January), Puducherry 35.1 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Fortaleza 22.2 °C, Puducherry 20.8 °C.
  • Fortaleza gets 8% more rain and snow a year: 1,353 mm against 1,252 mm.
  • Fortaleza gets about 56 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,643 h against 2,587 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Puducherry in 8 of 12 months
  • Drier: even, 6 months each
  • Sunnier: even, 6 months each
