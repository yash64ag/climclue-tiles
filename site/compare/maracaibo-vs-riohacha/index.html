How the climate of Maracaibo, Venezuela compares with Riohacha, Colombia: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Maracaibo is 1.3 °C (2.4 °F) warmer over the year: 28.7 °C against 27.3 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in March, when Maracaibo is 2.1 °C warmer (Maracaibo 29.0 °C, Riohacha 26.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Maracaibo 33.9 °C (July), Riohacha 33.4 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Maracaibo 23.5 °C, Riohacha 21.4 °C.
  • Riohacha gets 23% more rain and snow a year: 815 mm against 663 mm.
  • Maracaibo gets about 77 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,580 h against 2,503 h; about 1961–1990).
