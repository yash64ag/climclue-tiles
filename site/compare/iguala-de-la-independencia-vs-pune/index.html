Main differences

  • Iguala de la Independencia is 1.7 °C (3.0 °F) warmer over the year: 26.3 °C against 24.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in August, when Iguala de la Independencia is 2.3 °C warmer (Iguala de la Independencia 26.2 °C, Pune 23.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Iguala de la Independencia 37.9 °C (April), Pune 37.3 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Iguala de la Independencia 15.2 °C, Pune 12.0 °C.
  • Pune gets 19% more rain and snow a year: 999 mm against 842 mm.
  • Pune gets about 626 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,793 h against 2,167 h; about 1961–1990).
