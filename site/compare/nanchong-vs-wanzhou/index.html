How the climate of Nanchong, China compares with Wanzhou, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Wanzhou is 1.4 °C (2.6 °F) warmer over the year: 19.4 °C against 18.0 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Wanzhou is 1.8 °C warmer (Nanchong 7.0 °C, Wanzhou 8.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nanchong 32.1 °C (August), Wanzhou 34.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Nanchong 4.6 °C, Wanzhou 5.9 °C.
  • Wanzhou gets 10% more rain and snow a year: 1,225 mm against 1,119 mm.
  • Wanzhou gets about 109 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,401 h against 1,292 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Wanzhou in 12 of 12 months
  • Drier: Nanchong in 9 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Wanzhou in 10 of 12 months
