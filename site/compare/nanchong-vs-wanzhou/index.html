Nanchong vs Wanzhou climate

How the climate of Nanchong, China compares with Wanzhou, China, 221 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Main differences

  • Wanzhou is 0.1 °C (0.2 °F) warmer over the year: 18.2 °C against 18.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Wanzhou is 0.4 °C warmer (Nanchong 23.6 °C, Wanzhou 23.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Nanchong 30.8 °C (July), Wanzhou 31.1 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Nanchong 3.4 °C, Wanzhou 3.4 °C.
  • Wanzhou gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 1,391 mm against 1,139 mm.
  • Wanzhou gets about 109 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,401 h against 1,292 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Nanchong 3.2 cm, Wanzhou 3.6 cm (ERA5-Land).
