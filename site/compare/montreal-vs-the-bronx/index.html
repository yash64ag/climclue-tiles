Montréal vs The Bronx climate

How the climate of Montréal, Canada compares with The Bronx, United States, 519 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • The Bronx is 5.5 °C (9.9 °F) warmer over the year: 12.1 °C against 6.6 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when The Bronx is 9.1 °C warmer (Montréal -9.7 °C, The Bronx -0.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Montréal 26.0 °C (July), The Bronx 29.8 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Montréal -13.9 °C, The Bronx -4.9 °C.
  • The Bronx gets 1% more rain and snow a year: 1,216 mm against 1,207 mm.
  • The Bronx gets about 551 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,519 h against 1,969 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Montréal 212 cm, The Bronx 83 cm (ERA5-Land).
