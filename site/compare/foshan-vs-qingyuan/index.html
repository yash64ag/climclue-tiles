How the climate of Foshan, China compares with Qingyuan, China: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Foshan is 0.9 °C (1.6 °F) warmer over the year: 23.1 °C against 22.2 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in January, when Foshan is 1.5 °C warmer (Foshan 14.7 °C, Qingyuan 13.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Foshan 33.3 °C (July), Qingyuan 33.3 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Foshan 10.5 °C, Qingyuan 8.7 °C.
  • Qingyuan gets 6% more rain and snow a year: 2,101 mm against 1,981 mm.
  • Foshan gets about 49 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,807 h against 1,758 h; about 1961–1990).
