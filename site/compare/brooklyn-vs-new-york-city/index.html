How the climate of Brooklyn, United States compares with New York City, United States: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • New York City is 0.1 °C (0.1 °F) warmer over the year: 12.9 °C against 12.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when New York City is 0.3 °C warmer (Brooklyn 11.3 °C, New York City 11.5 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Brooklyn 29.1 °C (July), New York City 29.7 °C (July). Coldest-month night lows: Brooklyn -3.0 °C, New York City -3.2 °C.
  • Brooklyn gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,240 mm against 1,193 mm.
  • Brooklyn gets about 18 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,518 h against 2,499 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Brooklyn 56 cm, New York City 57 cm (ERA5-Land).
