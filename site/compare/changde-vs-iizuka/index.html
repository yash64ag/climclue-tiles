How the climate of Changde, China compares with Iizuka, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Changde is 0.8 °C (1.4 °F) warmer over the year: 17.9 °C against 17.1 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in May, when Changde is 2.8 °C warmer (Changde 22.8 °C, Iizuka 20.1 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Changde 33.0 °C (July), Iizuka 32.6 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Changde 2.3 °C, Iizuka 2.1 °C.
  • Iizuka gets 9% more rain and snow a year: 1,669 mm against 1,530 mm.
  • Iizuka gets about 187 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,759 h against 1,572 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Changde 14 cm, Iizuka 14 cm (ERA5-Land).
