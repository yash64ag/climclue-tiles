How the climate of Kyoto, Japan compares with Nagoya, Japan: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Nagoya is 0.2 °C (0.4 °F) warmer over the year: 15.9 °C against 15.7 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in September, when Nagoya is 0.5 °C warmer (Kyoto 23.7 °C, Nagoya 24.2 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Kyoto 31.9 °C (August), Nagoya 32.0 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Kyoto 1.0 °C, Nagoya 0.4 °C.
  • Nagoya gets 18% more rain and snow a year: 1,689 mm against 1,435 mm.
  • Nagoya gets about 294 more hours of bright sunshine a year (1,969 h against 1,674 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Kyoto 27 cm, Nagoya 7.4 cm (ERA5-Land).

  • Drier: Kyoto in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Nagoya in 12 of 12 months
