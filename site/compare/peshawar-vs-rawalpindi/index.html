Peshawar vs Rawalpindi climate

How the climate of Peshawar, Pakistan compares with Rawalpindi, Pakistan, 143 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Peshawar is 1.9 °C (3.4 °F) warmer over the year: 23.3 °C against 21.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Peshawar is 3.2 °C warmer (Peshawar 34.1 °C, Rawalpindi 30.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Peshawar 39.8 °C (June), Rawalpindi 36.7 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Peshawar 4.9 °C, Rawalpindi 3.9 °C.
  • Rawalpindi gets 125% more rain and snow a year: 1,329 mm against 590 mm.
  • Rawalpindi gets about 40 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,889 h against 2,849 h; about 1961–1990).
