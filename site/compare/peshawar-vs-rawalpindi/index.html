Peshawar vs Rawalpindi climate

How the climate of Peshawar, Pakistan compares with Rawalpindi, Pakistan, 143 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Peshawar is 1.6 °C (3.0 °F) warmer over the year: 23.0 °C against 21.4 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Peshawar is 2.8 °C warmer (Peshawar 33.9 °C, Rawalpindi 31.0 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Peshawar 41.4 °C (June), Rawalpindi 38.5 °C (June). Coldest-month night lows: Peshawar 4.1 °C, Rawalpindi 2.8 °C.
  • Rawalpindi gets 130% more rain and snow a year: 1,338 mm against 583 mm.
  • Rawalpindi gets about 40 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,889 h against 2,849 h; about 1961–1990).
