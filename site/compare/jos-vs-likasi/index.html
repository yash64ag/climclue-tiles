Jos vs Likasi climate

How the climate of Jos, Nigeria compares with Likasi, Democratic Republic of the Congo, 3,050 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Jos is 0.7 °C (1.2 °F) warmer over the year: 21.5 °C against 20.9 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in April, when Jos is 4.4 °C warmer (Jos 25.1 °C, Likasi 20.8 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Jos 31.1 °C (April), Likasi 32.3 °C (September). Coldest-month night lows: Jos 11.9 °C, Likasi 9.3 °C.
  • Likasi gets 4% more rain and snow a year: 1,253 mm against 1,201 mm.
  • Likasi gets about 11 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,656 h against 2,645 h; about 1961–1990).
  • They are in different hemispheres, so their seasons are reversed.
