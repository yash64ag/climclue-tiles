Quezon City vs Taguig climate

How the climate of Quezon City, Philippines compares with Taguig, Philippines, 14 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for the ~10 km area around each town.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Quezon City and Taguig have the same yearly average temperature (27.5 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in February, when Taguig is 0.1 °C warmer (Quezon City 26.4 °C, Taguig 26.6 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Quezon City 32.1 °C (April), Taguig 32.2 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Quezon City 23.0 °C, Taguig 23.3 °C.
  • Quezon City gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 2,291 mm against 2,013 mm.
  • Taguig gets about 2 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,362 h against 2,360 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Taguig in 6 of 12 months (3 equal)
  • Drier: Taguig in 11 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Taguig in 7 of 12 months
