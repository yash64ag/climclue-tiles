How the climate of Quezon City, Philippines compares with Taguig, Philippines: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Main differences

  • Quezon City and Taguig have the same yearly average temperature (27.4 °C).
  • The biggest monthly difference is in June, when Taguig is 0.2 °C warmer (Quezon City 28.2 °C, Taguig 28.4 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Quezon City 34.5 °C (April), Taguig 34.4 °C (April). Coldest-month night lows: Quezon City 21.2 °C, Taguig 21.2 °C.
  • Quezon City gets 22% more rain and snow a year: 2,315 mm against 1,901 mm.
  • Taguig gets about 2 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,362 h against 2,360 h; about 1961–1990).

Month by month: which is warmer, drier, sunnier

  • Warmer: Quezon City in 7 of 12 months
  • Drier: Taguig in 10 of 12 months
  • Sunnier: Taguig in 7 of 12 months
