Isesaki vs Ulsan climate

How the climate of Isesaki, Japan compares with Ulsan, South Korea, 894 km apart: month-by-month averages from modelled 1981–2010 data for each town’s own location.

Compare them on the globe ›

Main differences

  • Isesaki is 0.2 °C (0.3 °F) warmer over the year: 14.9 °C against 14.8 °C.
  • The biggest monthly difference is in December, when Isesaki is 1.6 °C warmer (Isesaki 6.5 °C, Ulsan 4.9 °C).
  • Hottest-month day highs: Isesaki 30.8 °C (August), Ulsan 30.3 °C (August). Coldest-month night lows: Isesaki -1.7 °C, Ulsan -2.2 °C.
  • Isesaki gets 14% more rain and snow a year: 1,356 mm against 1,186 mm.
  • Ulsan gets about 382 more hours of bright sunshine a year (2,290 h against 1,908 h; about 1961–1990).
  • Fresh snow in a typical year: Isesaki 16 cm, Ulsan 6.9 cm (ERA5-Land).
