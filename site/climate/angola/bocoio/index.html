Bocoio at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Angola, Bocoio is the 4th wettest (1,578 mm a year). Compared with Lobito, the nearest covered town with different numbers (66 km away), Bocoio is 5.7 °C cooler in April and 2.0 °C cooler in July, with 1,133 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (20.9 °C), averages 2.7 °C more than its coolest, July (18.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.7 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 69 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bocoio, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bocoio has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 69 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
