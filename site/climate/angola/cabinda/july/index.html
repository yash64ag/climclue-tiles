How July compares in Cabinda

July is the coolest month, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Cabinda. Compared with June: 1.6 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. August is 0.1 °C warmer than July on average.

Cabinda in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cabinda in July?
In July, Cabinda averages a day high of 23.9 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 99 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Cabinda?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Cabinda’s mild months (June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cabinda in July?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is November (156 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Cabinda in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cabinda get in July?
About 99 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cabinda in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
