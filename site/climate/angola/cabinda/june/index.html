How June compares in Cabinda

June is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Cabinda. Compared with May: 2.3 °C cooler, 43 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. July is 1.5 °C cooler than June on average.

Cabinda in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cabinda in June?
In June, Cabinda averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 103 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Cabinda?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Cabinda’s mild months (June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cabinda in June?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (161 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Cabinda in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cabinda get in June?
About 103 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cabinda in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
