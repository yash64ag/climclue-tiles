How December compares in Cabinda

December is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Cabinda. Compared with November: about as warm, 13 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. January is 0.3 °C warmer than December on average.

Cabinda in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cabinda in December?
In December, Cabinda averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 109 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Cabinda?
December is not one of Cabinda’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cabinda in December?
149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (161 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Cabinda in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cabinda get in December?
About 109 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Cabinda in December?
About 25.8 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (28.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Cabinda in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
