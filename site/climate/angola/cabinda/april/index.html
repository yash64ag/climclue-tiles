Cabinda weather in April

Average April weather in Cabinda, Angola: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Cabinda averages 29.9 °C (86 °F) days and 22.8 °C (73 °F) nights, with 140 mm (5.5 in) of rain over the month and about 148 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Cabinda

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Cabinda. Compared with March: 0.3 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. May is 1.1 °C cooler than April on average.

Cabinda in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cabinda in April?
In April, Cabinda averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 140 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Cabinda?
April is not one of Cabinda’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cabinda in April?
140 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (161 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Cabinda in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cabinda get in April?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Cabinda in April?
About 27.3 °C (81 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (28.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Cabinda in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
