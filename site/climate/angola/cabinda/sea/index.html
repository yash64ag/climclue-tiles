Cabinda sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Angola

The sea off Cabinda averages 28.1 °C (83 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 21.5 °C (71 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from October to May.

Cabinda sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Cabinda?
In March, at about 28.1 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Cabinda?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from October to May. The coolest month is August, at 21.5 °C (71 °F).
How warm is the sea in Cabinda in August?
About 21.5 °C (71 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Cabinda?
About 25.1 °C (77 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
