How August compares in Cabinda

August is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Cabinda. Compared with July: 0.1 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. September is 1.4 °C warmer than August on average.

Cabinda in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cabinda in August?
In August, Cabinda averages a day high of 25.0 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 18.0 °C (64 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 72 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Cabinda?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Cabinda’s mild months (June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cabinda in August?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (161 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Cabinda in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cabinda get in August?
About 72 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Cabinda in August?
About 21.5 °C (71 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is March (28.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Cabinda in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
