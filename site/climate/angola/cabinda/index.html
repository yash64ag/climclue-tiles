Cabinda at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Angola, Cabinda is the least sunny (about 1,268 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Soyo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Cabinda is 0.3 °C cooler in March and 0.3 °C warmer in July, with 265 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (26.8 °C), averages 5.3 °C more than its coolest, July (21.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.8 °C (68 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cabinda, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Cabinda

For mild weather, the best months to visit Cabinda are June, July, August, September and October.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July, August, September and October.
  • Warmest (average temperature): March (26.8 °C), February (26.4 °C) and April (26.4 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): July (2 mm), June (2 mm) and August (3 mm).

Why Cabinda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
