How May compares in Cabinda

May is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Cabinda. Compared with April: 1.1 °C cooler, 93 mm less precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. June is 2.3 °C cooler than May on average.

Cabinda in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cabinda in May?
In May, Cabinda averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 110 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Cabinda?
May is not one of Cabinda’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cabinda in May?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (156 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Cabinda in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cabinda get in May?
About 110 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cabinda in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
