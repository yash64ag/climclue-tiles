How November compares in Cabinda

November is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Cabinda. Compared with October: 0.8 °C warmer, 99 mm more precipitation and 38 hours more sunshine. December is about as warm as November on average.

Cabinda in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cabinda in November?
In November, Cabinda averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 24.0 °C (75 °F), with 156 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 103 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Cabinda?
November is not one of Cabinda’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cabinda in November?
156 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is November (156 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Cabinda in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cabinda get in November?
About 103 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cabinda in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
