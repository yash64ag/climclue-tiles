Sumbe at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Angola, Sumbe is 35th warmest (23.6 °C year average), 41st sunniest (about 2,125 hours of sunshine a year) and 21st wettest (615 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gabela, the nearest covered town with different numbers (71 km away), Sumbe is 4.6 °C warmer in March and 1.4 °C warmer in August, with 1,929 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (26.1 °C), averages 6.2 °C more than its coolest, August (19.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.6 °C (65 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sumbe, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sumbe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
