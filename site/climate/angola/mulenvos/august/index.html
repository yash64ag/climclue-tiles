Mulenvos weather in August

Average August weather in Mulenvos, Angola: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Mulenvos averages 24.8 °C (77 °F) days and 19.0 °C (66 °F) nights, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of rain over the month and about 153 hours of sunshine.

Mulenvos in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mulenvos in August?
In August, Mulenvos averages a day high of 24.8 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 19.0 °C (66 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Mulenvos?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Mulenvos’s mild months (June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mulenvos in August?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (144 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mulenvos in August?

How many hours of sunshine does Mulenvos get in August?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
