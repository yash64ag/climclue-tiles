Mulenvos in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mulenvos in October?
In October, Mulenvos averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Mulenvos?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Mulenvos’s mild months (June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mulenvos in October?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (142 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mulenvos in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mulenvos get in October?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mulenvos in October?
