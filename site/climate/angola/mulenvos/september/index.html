Mulenvos weather in September

Average September weather in Mulenvos, Angola: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Mulenvos averages 26.2 °C (79 °F) days and 20.4 °C (69 °F) nights, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of rain over the month and about 148 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Mulenvos

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Mulenvos. Compared with August: 1.5 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. October is 1.5 °C warmer than September on average.

Mulenvos in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mulenvos in September?
In September, Mulenvos averages a day high of 26.2 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Mulenvos?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Mulenvos’s mild months (June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mulenvos in September?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (144 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mulenvos in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mulenvos get in September?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Mulenvos in September?
About 21.8 °C (71 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is March (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Mulenvos in September?
