Mulenvos in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mulenvos in November?
In November, Mulenvos averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Mulenvos?
November is not one of Mulenvos’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mulenvos in November?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (142 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mulenvos in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mulenvos get in November?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mulenvos in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
