Mulenvos in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mulenvos in April?
In April, Mulenvos averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.7 °C (75 °F), with 134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Mulenvos?
April is not one of Mulenvos’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mulenvos in April?
134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (144 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mulenvos in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mulenvos get in April?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Mulenvos in April?
About 27.1 °C (81 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Mulenvos in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
