Caxito at a glance

Among the 136 towns ClimaClue covers in Angola, Caxito is the 3rd warmest (25.6 °C year average). Compared with Mabubas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Caxito is 0.4 °C warmer in February and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 90 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.9 °C), averages 5.9 °C more than its coolest, July (22.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.6 °C (65 °F), in July. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Caxito, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Caxito has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Caxito itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (8.578°S, 13.664°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-925-1936), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
