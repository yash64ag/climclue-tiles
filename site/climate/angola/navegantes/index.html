Navegantes at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Angola, Navegantes is the 3rd driest (283 mm a year). Compared with Benguela, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Navegantes is 0.1 °C warmer in April and 0.1 °C cooler in August, with 56 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (27.1 °C), averages 7.2 °C more than its coolest, August (19.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.7 °C (64 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Navegantes, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Navegantes has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
