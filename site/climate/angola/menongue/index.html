Menongue at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Angola, Menongue is the sunniest (about 3,104 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Cuíto, the nearest covered town with different numbers (266 km away), Menongue is 2.3 °C warmer in September and 0.7 °C warmer in July, with 464 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (23.8 °C), averages 6.3 °C more than its coolest, July (17.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.9 °C (48 °F), in July. The area lies about 517 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Menongue, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Menongue has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 517 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
