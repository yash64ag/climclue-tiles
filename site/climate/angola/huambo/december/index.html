How December compares in Huambo

December is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Huambo. Compared with November: 0.3 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Huambo in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huambo in December?
In December, Huambo averages a day high of 24.1 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 216 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation and about 139 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Huambo?
December is not one of Huambo’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huambo in December?
216 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (220 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Huambo in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huambo get in December?
About 139 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huambo in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
