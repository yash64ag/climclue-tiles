Huambo weather in August

Average August weather in Huambo, Angola: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Huambo averages 27.9 °C (82 °F) days and 11.0 °C (52 °F) nights, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of rain over the month and about 251 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Huambo

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Huambo. Compared with July: 2.7 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. September is 1.7 °C warmer than August on average.

Huambo in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huambo in August?
In August, Huambo averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 11.0 °C (52 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Huambo?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Huambo’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huambo in August?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (226 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Huambo in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huambo get in August?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huambo in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
