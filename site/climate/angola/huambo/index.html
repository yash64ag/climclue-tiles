Huambo at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Angola, Huambo is the 5th coolest (18.8 °C year average). Compared with Caála, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Huambo is 0.8 °C warmer in September and 0.3 °C warmer in June, with 194 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (21.4 °C), averages 4.6 °C more than its coolest, June (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.6 °C (49 °F), in July. The area lies about 241 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Huambo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Huambo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 241 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
