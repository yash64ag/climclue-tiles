How September compares in Huambo

September is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Huambo. Compared with August: 1.7 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 52 hours less sunshine. October is 1.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Huambo in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huambo in September?
In September, Huambo averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 199 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Huambo?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Huambo’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huambo in September?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (220 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Huambo in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huambo get in September?
About 199 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huambo in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
