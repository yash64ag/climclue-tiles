How April compares in Huambo

April is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Huambo. Compared with March: 0.2 °C cooler, 89 mm less precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. May is 0.5 °C cooler than April on average.

Huambo in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huambo in April?
In April, Huambo averages a day high of 24.2 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 122 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Huambo?
April is not one of Huambo’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huambo in April?
122 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (220 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Huambo in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huambo get in April?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huambo in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
