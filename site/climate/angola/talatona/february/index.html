Talatona in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Talatona in February?
In February, Talatona averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Talatona?
February is not one of Talatona’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Talatona in February?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (113 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Talatona in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Talatona get in February?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Talatona in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
