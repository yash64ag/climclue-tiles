Talatona in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Talatona in March?
In March, Talatona averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 25.0 °C (77 °F), with 113 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Talatona?
March is not one of Talatona’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Talatona in March?
113 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (113 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Talatona in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Talatona get in March?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Talatona in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
