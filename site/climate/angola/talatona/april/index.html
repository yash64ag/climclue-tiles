April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Talatona. Compared with March: 0.4 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. May is 1.1 °C cooler than April on average.

Talatona in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Talatona in April?
In April, Talatona averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Talatona?
April is not one of Talatona’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Talatona in April?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (116 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Talatona in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Talatona get in April?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Talatona in April?
About 27.1 °C (81 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Talatona in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
