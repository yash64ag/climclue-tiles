Golfe in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Golfe in August?
In August, Golfe averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 18.3 °C (65 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Golfe?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Golfe’s mild months (June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Golfe in August?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (132 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Golfe in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Golfe get in August?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Golfe in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
