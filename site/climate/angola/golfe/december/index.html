How December compares in Golfe

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Golfe. Compared with November: 0.4 °C warmer, 12 mm less precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. January is 0.3 °C warmer than December on average.

Golfe in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Golfe in December?
In December, Golfe averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.7 °C (75 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Golfe?
December is not one of Golfe’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Golfe in December?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (132 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Golfe in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Golfe get in December?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Golfe in December?
About 25.8 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Golfe in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
