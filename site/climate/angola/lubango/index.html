Lubango at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Angola, Lubango is the coolest (17.8 °C year average) and the 3rd sunniest (about 2,671 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Mossamedes, the nearest covered town with different numbers (147 km away), Lubango is 1.8 °C warmer in September and 4.5 °C cooler in June, with 1,157 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (19.7 °C), averages 4.1 °C more than its coolest, June (15.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.3 °C (49 °F), in July. The area lies about 136 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lubango, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lubango has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 136 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.1 °C. The 10 km area around Lubango, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 16.0 °C a year, 1.8 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Lubango itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.917°S, 13.492°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-989-1934), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
