How November compares in Lubango

November is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Lubango. Compared with October: 1.0 °C cooler, 79 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. December is 0.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Lubango in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lubango in November?
In November, Lubango averages a day high of 22.6 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 141 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Lubango?
November is not one of Lubango’s mild months; those are May, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lubango in November?
141 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (322 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Lubango in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lubango get in November?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lubango in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
