How March compares in Lubango

March is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Lubango. Compared with February: 0.2 °C warmer, 131 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. April is 0.2 °C cooler than March on average.

Lubango in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lubango in March?
In March, Lubango averages a day high of 21.2 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 322 mm (12.7 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Lubango?
March is not one of Lubango’s mild months; those are May, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lubango in March?
322 mm (12.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (322 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Lubango in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lubango get in March?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lubango in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
