How February compares in Lubango

February is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Lubango. Compared with January: about as warm, 25 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. March is 0.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Lubango in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lubango in February?
In February, Lubango averages a day high of 21.0 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 12.2 °C (54 °F), with 191 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Lubango?
February is not one of Lubango’s mild months; those are May, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lubango in February?
191 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (322 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Lubango in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lubango get in February?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lubango in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
