How October compares in Viana

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Viana. Compared with September: 1.4 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. November is 1.2 °C warmer than October on average.

Viana in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Viana in October?
In October, Viana averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Viana?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Viana’s mild months (June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Viana in October?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (151 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Viana in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Viana get in October?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Viana in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
