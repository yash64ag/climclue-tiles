Viana at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Angola, Viana is 8th warmest (25.1 °C year average), 28th sunniest (about 2,297 hours of sunshine a year) and 24th wettest (567 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mulenvos, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Viana is 0.1 °C cooler in February and 0.2 °C warmer in July, with 36 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.1 °C), averages 5.3 °C more than its coolest, July (21.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.6 °C (64 °F), in July. The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Viana, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Viana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
