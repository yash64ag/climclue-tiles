Why Viana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Viana climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Viana?
February, with an average of 27.1 °C (81 °F), day highs around 33.0 °C (91 °F) and night lows around 23.3 °C (74 °F).
What is the coldest month in Viana?
July, with an average of 21.9 °C (71 °F), day highs around 29.0 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 17.6 °C (64 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Viana?
March, with 151 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 567 mm (22.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Viana?
June, with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Viana?
May, with about 229 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Viana?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Viana?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July, August, September and October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Viana’s GeoNames location (8.906°S, 13.375°E; ClimaClue area sq-929-1933), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 865,863 (GeoNames).
