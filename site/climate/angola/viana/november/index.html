How November compares in Viana

November is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Viana. Compared with October: 1.1 °C warmer, 51 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. December is 0.3 °C warmer than November on average.

Viana in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Viana in November?
In November, Viana averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Viana?
November is not one of Viana’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Viana in November?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (153 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Viana in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Viana get in November?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Viana in November?
About 25.7 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Viana in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
