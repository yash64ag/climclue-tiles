Soyo at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Angola, Soyo is the 3rd least sunny (about 1,772 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Cabinda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Soyo is 0.3 °C warmer in March and 0.5 °C cooler in August, with 265 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (27.1 °C), averages 6.0 °C more than its coolest, August (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.4 °C (69 °F), in July. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Soyo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Soyo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
