How December compares in Benguela

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Benguela. Compared with November: 0.2 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. January is 0.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Benguela in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benguela in December?
In December, Benguela averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Benguela?
December is not one of Benguela’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benguela in December?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (124 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Benguela in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benguela get in December?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benguela in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
