How May compares in Benguela

May is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Benguela. Compared with April: 1.8 °C cooler, 63 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. June is 3.2 °C cooler than May on average.

Benguela in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benguela in May?
In May, Benguela averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Benguela?
May is not one of Benguela’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benguela in May?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (124 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Benguela in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benguela get in May?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benguela in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
