How July compares in Benguela

July is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Benguela. Compared with June: 1.9 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. August is 0.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Benguela in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benguela in July?
In July, Benguela averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 16.6 °C (62 °F), with 1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 127 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Benguela?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Benguela’s mild months (June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benguela in July?
1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (109 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Benguela in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benguela get in July?
About 127 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Benguela in July?
About 21.3 °C (70 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is March (27.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Benguela in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
