How October compares in Benguela

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Benguela. Compared with September: 2.0 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. November is 1.7 °C warmer than October on average.

Benguela in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benguela in October?
In October, Benguela averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Benguela?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Benguela’s mild months (June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benguela in October?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (124 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Benguela in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benguela get in October?
About 145 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benguela in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
