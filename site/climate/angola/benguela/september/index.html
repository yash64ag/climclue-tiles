How September compares in Benguela

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Benguela. Compared with August: 1.6 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 35 hours more sunshine. October is 2.0 °C warmer than September on average.

Benguela in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benguela in September?
In September, Benguela averages a day high of 25.0 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 18.1 °C (65 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Benguela?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Benguela’s mild months (June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benguela in September?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (109 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Benguela in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benguela get in September?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Benguela in September?
About 21.2 °C (70 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is March (27.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Benguela in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
