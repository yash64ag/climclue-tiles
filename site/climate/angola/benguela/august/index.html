How August compares in Benguela

August is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Benguela. Compared with July: 0.1 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. September is 1.7 °C warmer than August on average.

Benguela in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benguela in August?
In August, Benguela averages a day high of 23.2 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 17.6 °C (64 °F), with 1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 122 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Benguela?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Benguela’s mild months (June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benguela in August?
1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (124 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Benguela in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benguela get in August?
About 122 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benguela in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
