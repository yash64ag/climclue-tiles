How January compares in Benguela

January is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Benguela. Compared with December: 0.2 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. February is 0.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Benguela in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benguela in January?
In January, Benguela averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Benguela?
January is not one of Benguela’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benguela in January?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (124 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Benguela in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benguela get in January?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benguela in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
