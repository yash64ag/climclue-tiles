Benguela sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Angola

The sea off Benguela averages 27.8 °C (82 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 20.6 °C (69 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from November to May.

Benguela sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Benguela?
In March, at about 27.8 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Benguela?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from November to May. The coolest month is August, at 20.6 °C (69 °F).
How warm is the sea in Benguela in August?
About 20.6 °C (69 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Benguela?
About 24.5 °C (76 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
