How February compares in Benguela

February is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Benguela. Compared with January: 0.8 °C warmer, 29 mm more precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. March is 0.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Benguela in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benguela in February?
In February, Benguela averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Benguela?
February is not one of Benguela’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benguela in February?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (124 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Benguela in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benguela get in February?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benguela in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
