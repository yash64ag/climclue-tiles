How September compares in Hoji ya Henda

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Hoji ya Henda. Compared with August: 1.4 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. October is 1.6 °C warmer than September on average.

Hoji ya Henda in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hoji ya Henda in September?
In September, Hoji ya Henda averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Hoji ya Henda?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Hoji ya Henda’s mild months (June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hoji ya Henda in September?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (128 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Hoji ya Henda in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hoji ya Henda get in September?
About 145 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Hoji ya Henda in September?
About 21.8 °C (71 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is March (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Hoji ya Henda in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
