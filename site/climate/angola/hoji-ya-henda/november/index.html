Hoji ya Henda in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hoji ya Henda in November?
In November, Hoji ya Henda averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Hoji ya Henda?
November is not one of Hoji ya Henda’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hoji ya Henda in November?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (127 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Hoji ya Henda in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hoji ya Henda get in November?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hoji ya Henda in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
