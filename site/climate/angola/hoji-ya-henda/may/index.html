Hoji ya Henda in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hoji ya Henda in May?
In May, Hoji ya Henda averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Hoji ya Henda?
May is not one of Hoji ya Henda’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hoji ya Henda in May?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (128 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Hoji ya Henda in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hoji ya Henda get in May?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Hoji ya Henda in May?
About 25.3 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Hoji ya Henda in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
