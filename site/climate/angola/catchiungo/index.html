Catchiungo at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Angola, Catchiungo is the 2nd coolest (18.2 °C year average). Compared with Huambo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Catchiungo is 0.6 °C cooler in September and 0.4 °C cooler in June, with 188 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (20.9 °C), averages 4.4 °C more than its coolest, June (16.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.0 °C (50 °F), in June. The area lies about 288 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Catchiungo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Catchiungo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 288 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
