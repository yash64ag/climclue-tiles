Dundo at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Angola, Dundo is 27th warmest (24.4 °C year average), 9th sunniest (about 2,312 hours of sunshine a year) and 11th wettest (1,390 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chitato, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Dundo is 0.3 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C warmer in November, with 119 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.6 °C), averages 2.3 °C more than its coolest, November (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.1 °C (65 °F), in July. The area lies about 843 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dundo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dundo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 843 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
