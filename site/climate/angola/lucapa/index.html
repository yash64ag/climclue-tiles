Lucapa at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Angola, Lucapa is the 5th sunniest (about 2,468 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Dundo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (117 km away), Lucapa is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 0.8 °C cooler in December, with 61 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.4 °C), averages 2.8 °C more than its coolest, December (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.4 °C (63 °F), in July. The area lies about 816 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Lucapa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 816 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
