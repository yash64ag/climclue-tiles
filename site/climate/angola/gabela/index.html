Gabela at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Angola, Gabela is the wettest (2,545 mm a year). Compared with Sumbe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (71 km away), Gabela is 4.3 °C cooler in April and 1.9 °C cooler in July, with 1,929 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (21.8 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, July (18.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.4 °C (58 °F), in July. The area lies about 66 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gabela, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gabela has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 66 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
