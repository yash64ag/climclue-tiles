How March compares in Luanda

March is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Luanda. Compared with February: 0.2 °C warmer, 65 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. April is 0.5 °C cooler than March on average.

Luanda in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Luanda in March?
In March, Luanda averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 122 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 209 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Luanda?
March is not one of Luanda’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Luanda in March?
122 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (122 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Luanda in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Luanda get in March?
About 209 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Luanda in March?
About 28.0 °C (82 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Luanda in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
