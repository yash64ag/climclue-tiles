Luanda weather in January

Average January weather in Luanda, Angola: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Luanda averages 29.4 °C (85 °F) days and 23.6 °C (74 °F) nights, with 47 mm (1.9 in) of rain over the month and about 214 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Luanda

January is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Luanda. Compared with December: 0.3 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. February is 0.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Luanda in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Luanda in January?
In January, Luanda averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Luanda?
January is not one of Luanda’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Luanda in January?
47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (122 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Luanda in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Luanda get in January?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Luanda in January?
About 26.2 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Luanda in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
