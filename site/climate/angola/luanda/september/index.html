How September compares in Luanda

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Luanda. Compared with August: 1.4 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. October is 1.6 °C warmer than September on average.

Luanda in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Luanda in September?
In September, Luanda averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Luanda?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Luanda’s mild months (June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Luanda in September?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (127 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Luanda in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Luanda get in September?
About 145 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Luanda in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
