Mbanza Kongo at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Angola, Mbanza Kongo is the 2nd least sunny (about 1,698 hours of sunshine a year) and the 5th wettest (1,549 mm a year). Compared with Uíge, the nearest covered town with different numbers (175 km away), Mbanza Kongo is 1.9 °C warmer in March and 0.3 °C warmer in July, with 394 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (24.9 °C), averages 3.5 °C more than its coolest, July (21.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.6 °C (62 °F), in July. The area lies about 188 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mbanza Kongo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mbanza Kongo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 188 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
