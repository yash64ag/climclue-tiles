How October compares in Calumbo

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Calumbo. Compared with September: 1.4 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. November is 1.2 °C warmer than October on average.

Calumbo in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Calumbo in October?
In October, Calumbo averages a day high of 27.8 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 160 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Calumbo?
October is not one of Calumbo’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Calumbo in October?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (138 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Calumbo in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Calumbo get in October?
About 160 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Calumbo in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
