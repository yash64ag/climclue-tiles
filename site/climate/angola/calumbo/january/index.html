How January compares in Calumbo

January is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Calumbo. Compared with December: 0.4 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. February is 0.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Calumbo in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Calumbo in January?
In January, Calumbo averages a day high of 33.5 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Calumbo?
January is not one of Calumbo’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Calumbo in January?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (145 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Calumbo in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Calumbo get in January?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Calumbo in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
