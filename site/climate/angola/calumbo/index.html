Calumbo at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Angola, Calumbo is the warmest (25.3 °C year average). Compared with Zango, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Calumbo is 0.4 °C warmer in February and 0.2 °C cooler in July, with 26 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.6 °C), averages 5.9 °C more than its coolest, July (21.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.2 °C (63 °F), in July. The area lies about 49 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Calumbo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Calumbo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 49 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
