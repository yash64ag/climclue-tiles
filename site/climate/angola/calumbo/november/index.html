Calumbo weather in November

Average November weather in Calumbo, Angola: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Calumbo averages 29.1 °C (84 °F) days and 23.4 °C (74 °F) nights, with 59 mm (2.3 in) of rain over the month and about 187 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Calumbo

November is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Calumbo. Compared with October: 1.2 °C warmer, 47 mm more precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. December is 0.4 °C warmer than November on average.

Calumbo in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Calumbo in November?
In November, Calumbo averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Calumbo?
November is not one of Calumbo’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Calumbo in November?
59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (138 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Calumbo in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Calumbo get in November?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Calumbo in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
