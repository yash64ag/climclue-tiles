Uíge at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Angola, Uíge is the 2nd wettest (1,943 mm a year). Compared with Mbanza Kongo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (175 km away), Uíge is 1.9 °C cooler in March and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 394 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (23.1 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, July (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.9 °C (59 °F), in July. The area lies about 209 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Uíge, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Uíge has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 209 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
