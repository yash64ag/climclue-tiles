Kikolo weather in November

Average November weather in Kikolo, Angola: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Kikolo averages 28.8 °C (84 °F) days and 23.2 °C (74 °F) nights, with 51 mm (2.0 in) of rain over the month and about 193 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Kikolo

November is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kikolo. Compared with October: 1.3 °C warmer, 41 mm more precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. December is 0.4 °C warmer than November on average.

Kikolo in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kikolo in November?
In November, Kikolo averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kikolo?
November is not one of Kikolo’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kikolo in November?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (131 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Kikolo in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kikolo get in November?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kikolo in November?
About 25.5 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (27.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kikolo in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
