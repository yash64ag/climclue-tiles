How October compares in Kikolo

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Kikolo. Compared with September: 1.6 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. November is 1.3 °C warmer than October on average.

Kikolo in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kikolo in October?
In October, Kikolo averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kikolo?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Kikolo’s mild months (June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kikolo in October?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (130 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Kikolo in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kikolo get in October?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kikolo in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
