How June compares in Kikolo

June is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Kikolo. Compared with May: 2.8 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. July is 1.8 °C cooler than June on average.

Kikolo in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kikolo in June?
In June, Kikolo averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 20.5 °C (69 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Kikolo?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Kikolo’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kikolo in June?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the driest month of the year. The wettest month is March (131 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Kikolo in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kikolo get in June?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kikolo in June?
About 22.5 °C (73 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is March (27.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kikolo in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
