Maianga in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maianga in July?
In July, Maianga averages a day high of 26.9 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Maianga?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Maianga’s mild months (June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maianga in July?

Does it snow in Maianga in July?
