How August compares in Maianga

August is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Maianga. Compared with July: 0.1 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. September is 1.4 °C warmer than August on average.

Maianga in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maianga in August?
In August, Maianga averages a day high of 24.3 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 18.8 °C (66 °F), with 1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Maianga?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Maianga’s mild months (June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maianga in August?
1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (123 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Maianga in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maianga get in August?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Maianga in August?
About 20.5 °C (69 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is March (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Maianga in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
