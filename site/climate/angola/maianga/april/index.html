How April compares in Maianga

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Maianga. Compared with March: 0.5 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. May is 1.0 °C cooler than April on average.

Maianga in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maianga in April?
In April, Maianga averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 24.4 °C (76 °F), with 117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 196 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Maianga?
April is not one of Maianga’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maianga in April?
117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (127 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Maianga in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maianga get in April?
About 196 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maianga in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
