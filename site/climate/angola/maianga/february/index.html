Maianga weather in February

Average February weather in Maianga, Angola: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Maianga averages 30.2 °C (86 °F) days and 24.2 °C (76 °F) nights, with 58 mm (2.3 in) of rain over the month and about 203 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Maianga

February is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Maianga. Compared with January: 0.6 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. March is 0.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Maianga in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maianga in February?
In February, Maianga averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Maianga?
February is not one of Maianga’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maianga in February?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (123 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Maianga in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maianga get in February?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Maianga in February?
About 27.6 °C (82 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Maianga in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
