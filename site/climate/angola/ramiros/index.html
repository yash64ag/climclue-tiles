Ramiros at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Angola, Ramiros is the 5th driest (362 mm a year). Compared with Benfica, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Ramiros is 0.2 °C cooler in March and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 32 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (27.1 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, July (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.2 °C (67 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ramiros, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ramiros has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
