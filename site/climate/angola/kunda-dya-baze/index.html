Kunda dya Baze at a glance

Among the 136 towns ClimaClue covers in Angola, Kunda dya Baze is 31st warmest (24.7 °C year average), 13th sunniest (about 2,610 hours of sunshine a year) and 79th wettest (1,120 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cafunfo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (105 km away), Kunda dya Baze is 1.6 °C warmer in September and 1.0 °C warmer in December, with 523 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (26.2 °C), averages 2.3 °C more than its coolest, December (23.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.5 °C (55 °F), in June. The area lies about 418 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kunda dya Baze, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kunda dya Baze has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 418 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-929-1970), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
