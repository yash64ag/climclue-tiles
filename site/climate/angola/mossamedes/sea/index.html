Mossamedes sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Namibe · Angola

The sea off Mossamedes averages 25.5 °C (78 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 18.4 °C (65 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from February to April.

Mossamedes sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Mossamedes?
In March, at about 25.5 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Mossamedes?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from February to April. The coolest month is August, at 18.4 °C (65 °F).
How warm is the sea in Mossamedes in August?
About 18.4 °C (65 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Mossamedes?
About 22.1 °C (72 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Mossamedes nearby?
In March: Baía Farta (27.5 °C, 309 km), Navegantes (27.8 °C, 319 km) and Benguela (27.8 °C, 321 km).
