How September compares in Camama

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Camama. Compared with August: 1.4 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. October is 1.5 °C warmer than September on average.

Camama in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Camama in September?
In September, Camama averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Camama?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Camama’s mild months (June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Camama in September?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (133 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Camama in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Camama get in September?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Camama in September?
About 21.7 °C (71 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is March (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Camama in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
