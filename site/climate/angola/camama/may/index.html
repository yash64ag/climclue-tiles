How May compares in Camama

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Camama. Compared with April: 1.0 °C cooler, 111 mm less precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. June is 2.6 °C cooler than May on average.

Camama in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Camama in May?
In May, Camama averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Camama?
May is not one of Camama’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Camama in May?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (131 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Camama in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Camama get in May?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Camama in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
