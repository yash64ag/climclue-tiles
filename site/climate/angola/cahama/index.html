Cahama at a glance

Among the 136 towns ClimaClue covers in Angola, Cahama is 72nd warmest (22.4 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 2,845 hours of sunshine a year) and 127th wettest (391 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Humbe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (77 km away), Cahama is 1.3 °C cooler in October and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 67 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (24.9 °C), averages 6.5 °C more than its coolest, July (18.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.0 °C (50 °F), in July. The area lies about 268 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cahama, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cahama has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 268 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Cahama itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (16.286°S, 14.310°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1002-1943), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
