Žytkavičy at a glance

Among the 57 towns ClimaClue covers in Belarus, Žytkavičy is the 5th wettest (733 mm a year). Compared with Soligorsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Žytkavičy is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 79 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.3 °C), averages 23.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 540 km from the sea.

Why Žytkavičy has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Žytkavičy itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (52.217°N, 27.856°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-317-2078), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
