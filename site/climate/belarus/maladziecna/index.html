Maladziečna at a glance

Among the 57 towns ClimaClue covers in Belarus, Maladziečna is 33rd warmest (6.5 °C year average), 37th sunniest (about 1,722 hours of sunshine a year) and 29th wettest (657 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vileyka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Maladziečna is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. The area lies about 354 km from the sea.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Maladziečna itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (54.317°N, 26.854°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-296-2068), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
