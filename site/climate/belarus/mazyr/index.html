Mazyr at a glance

Among the 13 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Belarus, Mazyr is the 3rd warmest (7.4 °C year average) and the 3rd sunniest (about 1,779 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Bobruysk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (123 km away), Mazyr is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 41 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.4 °C), averages 23.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 625 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mazyr, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mazyr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 625 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
