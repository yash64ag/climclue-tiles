Vitebsk at a glance

Among the 13 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Belarus, Vitebsk is the coolest (6.0 °C year average) and the wettest (738 mm a year). Compared with Orsha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (76 km away), Vitebsk is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in February, with 82 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.1 °C), averages 23.7 °C more than its coolest, February (-5.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 438 km from the sea.

Why Vitebsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 438 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
