Bobruysk at a glance

Among the 13 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Belarus, Bobruysk is the 2nd sunniest (about 1,787 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (634 mm a year). Compared with Mahilyow, the nearest covered town with different numbers (113 km away), Bobruysk is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 17 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.9 °C), averages 23.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 553 km from the sea.

Why Bobruysk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 553 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C.
