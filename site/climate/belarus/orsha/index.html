Orsha at a glance

Among the 13 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Belarus, Orsha is the 2nd coolest (6.0 °C year average). Compared with Mahilyow, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Orsha is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in February, with 5 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.1 °C), averages 23.7 °C more than its coolest, February (-5.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 489 km from the sea.

Why Orsha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 489 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
