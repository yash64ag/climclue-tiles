Astraviec at a glance

Among the 57 towns ClimaClue covers in Belarus, Astraviec is 34th warmest (6.5 °C year average), 40th sunniest (about 1,708 hours of sunshine a year) and 22nd wettest (678 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Oshmyany, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Astraviec is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.9 °C), averages 22.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 297 km from the sea.

Why Astraviec has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Astraviec itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (54.614°N, 25.955°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-293-2059), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
