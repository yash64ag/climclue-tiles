Pastavy at a glance

Among the 57 towns ClimaClue covers in Belarus, Pastavy is 36th warmest (6.4 °C year average), 41st sunniest (about 1,706 hours of sunshine a year) and 35th wettest (650 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hlybokaye, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Pastavy is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in February, with 32 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.1 °C), averages 22.7 °C more than its coolest, February (-4.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 281 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pastavy, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pastavy has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 281 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.7 °C.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pastavy itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (55.117°N, 26.833°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-288-2068), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
