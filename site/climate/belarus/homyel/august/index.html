Homyel' in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Homyel' in August?
In August, Homyel' averages a day high of 22.7 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Homyel'?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Homyel'’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Homyel' in August?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (92 mm), the driest February (35 mm).
Does it snow in Homyel' in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Homyel' get in August?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Homyel' in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
