Homyel' in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Homyel' in April?
In April, Homyel' averages a day high of 12.4 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 2.7 °C (37 °F), with 42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Homyel'?
April is not one of Homyel'’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Homyel' in April?
42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (92 mm), the driest February (35 mm).
Does it snow in Homyel' in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.3 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Homyel' get in April?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Homyel' in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
