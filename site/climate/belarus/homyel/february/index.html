Homyel' in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Homyel' in February?
In February, Homyel' averages a day high of -2.0 °C (28 °F) and a night low of -7.5 °C (19 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 72 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Homyel'?
February is not one of Homyel'’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Homyel' in February?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (92 mm), the driest February (35 mm).
Does it snow in Homyel' in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 30 cm (11.9 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 16 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Homyel' get in February?
About 72 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Homyel' in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
