Homyel' at a glance

Among the 13 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Belarus, Homyel' is the sunniest (about 1,795 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Mazyr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (127 km away), Homyel' is about as warm in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 20 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.4 °C), averages 24.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 656 km from the sea (deep inland).
