Homyel' weather in September

Average September weather in Homyel', Belarus: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

Homyel' in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Homyel' in September?
In September, Homyel' averages a day high of 17.5 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 7.6 °C (46 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Homyel'?
September is not one of Homyel'’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Homyel' in September?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (92 mm), the driest February (36 mm).
Does it snow in Homyel' in September?

How many hours of sunshine does Homyel' get in September?
