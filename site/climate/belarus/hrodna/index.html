Hrodna at a glance

Among the 13 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Belarus, Hrodna is the 3rd least sunny (about 1,681 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (612 mm a year). Compared with Lida, the nearest covered town with different numbers (99 km away), Hrodna is about as warm in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 88 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.1 °C), averages 21.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 234 km from the sea.

Why Hrodna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 234 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
