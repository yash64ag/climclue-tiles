Minsk weather in February

Average February weather in Minsk, Belarus: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Minsk averages -1.7 °C (29 °F) days and -8.2 °C (17 °F) nights, with 36 mm (1.4 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 71 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Minsk

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Minsk. Compared with January: 0.1 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. March is 4.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Minsk in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Minsk in February?
In February, Minsk averages a day high of -1.7 °C (29 °F) and a night low of -8.2 °C (17 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 71 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Minsk?
February is not one of Minsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Minsk in February?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (90 mm), the driest February (36 mm).
Does it snow in Minsk in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 28 cm (11.0 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 9.8 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Minsk get in February?
About 71 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Minsk in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
