How July compares in Minsk

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Minsk. Compared with June: 2.1 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. August is 1.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Minsk in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Minsk in July?
In July, Minsk averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 13.7 °C (57 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 253 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Minsk?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Minsk’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Minsk in July?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (92 mm), the driest February (37 mm).
Does it snow in Minsk in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Minsk get in July?
About 253 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Minsk in July?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
