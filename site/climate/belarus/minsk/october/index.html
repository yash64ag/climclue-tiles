How October compares in Minsk

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Minsk. Compared with September: 5.3 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 65 hours less sunshine. November is 6.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Minsk in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Minsk in October?
In October, Minsk averages a day high of 10.0 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 3.6 °C (38 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 98 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Minsk?
October is not one of Minsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Minsk in October?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (90 mm), the driest February (36 mm).
Does it snow in Minsk in October?
Yes, on average: about 3.4 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in October (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Minsk get in October?
About 98 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Minsk in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
