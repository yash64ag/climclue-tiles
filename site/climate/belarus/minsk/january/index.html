How January compares in Minsk

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Minsk. Compared with December: 1.2 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. February is about as warm as January on average.

Minsk in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Minsk in January?
In January, Minsk averages a day high of -3.0 °C (27 °F) and a night low of -7.5 °C (19 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 48 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Minsk?
January is not one of Minsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Minsk in January?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (92 mm), the driest February (37 mm).
Does it snow in Minsk in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 36 cm (14.2 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 16 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Minsk get in January?
About 48 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Minsk in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
