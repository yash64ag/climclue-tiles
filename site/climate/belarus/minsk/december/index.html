How December compares in Minsk

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Minsk. Compared with November: 4.1 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. January is 1.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Minsk in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Minsk in December?
In December, Minsk averages a day high of -1.5 °C (29 °F) and a night low of -6.2 °C (21 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 29 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Minsk?
December is not one of Minsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Minsk in December?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (90 mm), the driest February (36 mm).
Does it snow in Minsk in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 33 cm (13.1 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Minsk get in December?
About 29 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Minsk in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
