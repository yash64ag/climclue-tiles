Minsk in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Minsk in September?
In September, Minsk averages a day high of 16.1 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 7.2 °C (45 °F), with 57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Minsk?
September is not one of Minsk’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Minsk in September?
57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (90 mm), the driest February (36 mm).
Does it snow in Minsk in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Minsk get in September?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Minsk in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
