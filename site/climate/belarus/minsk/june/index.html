How June compares in Minsk

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the sunniest month in Minsk. Compared with May: 3.1 °C warmer, 26 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. July is 2.0 °C warmer than June on average.

Minsk in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Minsk in June?
In June, Minsk averages a day high of 21.4 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 11.5 °C (53 °F), with 90 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Minsk?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Minsk’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Minsk in June?
90 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (90 mm), the driest February (36 mm).
Does it snow in Minsk in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Minsk get in June?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Minsk in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
