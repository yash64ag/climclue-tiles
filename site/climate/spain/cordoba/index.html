Córdoba at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Córdoba is the 3rd warmest (18.7 °C year average). Compared with Jaén, the nearest covered town with different numbers (87 km away), Córdoba is 2.4 °C warmer in July and 2.0 °C warmer in January, with 106 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 19.4 °C more than its coolest, January (9.4 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 138 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 8 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 104% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Córdoba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 138 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
