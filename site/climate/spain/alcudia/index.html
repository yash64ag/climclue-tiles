Alcúdia at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Alcúdia is 140th warmest (18.1 °C year average), 358th sunniest (about 2,536 hours of sunshine a year) and 185th wettest (624 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pollença, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Alcúdia is 0.3 °C warmer in August and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 162 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.9 °C), averages 14.0 °C more than its coolest, January (11.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.8 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Alcúdia, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Alcúdia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Alcúdia itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (39.853°N, 3.121°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-441-1831), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
