Los Realejos at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Los Realejos is 184th warmest (17.7 °C year average), 231st sunniest (about 2,648 hours of sunshine a year) and 429th wettest (417 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Realejo Alto, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Los Realejos is 0.4 °C warmer in September and 0.7 °C warmer in February, with 18 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (20.8 °C), averages 5.6 °C more than its coolest, February (15.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.1 °C (54 °F), in February. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Los Realejos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.6 °C. The 10 km area around Los Realejos, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 14.4 °C a year, 3.4 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
