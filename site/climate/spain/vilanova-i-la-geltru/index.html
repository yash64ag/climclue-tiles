Vilanova i la Geltrú at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Vilanova i la Geltrú is 266th warmest (16.3 °C year average), 385th sunniest (about 2,449 hours of sunshine a year) and 286th wettest (548 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sant Pere de Ribes, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Vilanova i la Geltrú is 0.3 °C warmer in August and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 19 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.3 °C), averages 14.9 °C more than its coolest, January (9.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.2 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Vilanova i la Geltrú has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
