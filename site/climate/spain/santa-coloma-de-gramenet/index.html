Santa Coloma de Gramenet at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Santa Coloma de Gramenet is 32nd warmest (15.7 °C year average), 48th sunniest (about 2,364 hours of sunshine a year) and 24th wettest (597 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Barcelona, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Santa Coloma de Gramenet is 0.4 °C cooler in August and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 5 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.6 °C), averages 14.8 °C more than its coolest, January (8.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.6 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Santa Coloma de Gramenet has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
