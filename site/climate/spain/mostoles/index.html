Móstoles at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Móstoles is 46th warmest (14.6 °C year average), 28th sunniest (about 2,605 hours of sunshine a year) and 47th wettest (453 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fuenlabrada, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Móstoles is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 18 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.8 °C), averages 20.6 °C more than its coolest, January (5.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 323 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 9 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 99% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Móstoles has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 323 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
