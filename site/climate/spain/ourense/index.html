Why Ourense has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 90 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Ourense climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Ourense?
August, with an average of 20.3 °C (68 °F), day highs around 26.1 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 14.6 °C (58 °F).
What is the coldest month in Ourense?
January, with an average of 7.3 °C (45 °F), day highs around 10.7 °C (51 °F) and night lows around 3.0 °C (37 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Ourense?
December, with 142 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 930 mm (36.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Ourense?
July, with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Ourense?
July, with about 277 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Ourense?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 5.7 cm (2.2 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Ourense?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Ourense have?

Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest).
