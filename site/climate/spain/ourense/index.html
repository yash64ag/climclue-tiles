Ourense at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Ourense is 56th warmest (13.7 °C year average), 58th sunniest (about 2,032 hours of sunshine a year) and 11th wettest (930 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vigo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (72 km away), Ourense is 1.1 °C warmer in August and 2.6 °C cooler in January, with 936 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (20.3 °C), averages 12.9 °C more than its coolest, January (7.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 90 km from the sea.

Why Ourense has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 90 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
