Esplugues de Llobregat at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Esplugues de Llobregat is 328th warmest (15.7 °C year average), 393rd sunniest (about 2,395 hours of sunshine a year) and 229th wettest (586 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sant Just Desvern, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Esplugues de Llobregat is about as warm in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 8 mm more precipitation a year. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Esplugues de Llobregat has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
