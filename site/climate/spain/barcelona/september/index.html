How September compares in Barcelona

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Barcelona. Compared with August: 2.6 °C cooler, 24 mm more precipitation and 62 hours less sunshine. October is 3.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Barcelona in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barcelona in September?
In September, Barcelona averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Barcelona?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Barcelona’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barcelona in September?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (90 mm), the driest July (22 mm).
Does it snow in Barcelona in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barcelona get in September?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Barcelona in September?
About 23.8 °C (75 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Barcelona in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
