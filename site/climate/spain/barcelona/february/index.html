Barcelona weather in February

Average February weather in Barcelona, Spain: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Barcelona averages 14.1 °C (57 °F) days and 6.0 °C (43 °F) nights, with 43 mm (1.7 in) of rain over the month and about 150 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Barcelona

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Barcelona. Compared with January: 0.5 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. March is 1.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Barcelona in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barcelona in February?
In February, Barcelona averages a day high of 14.1 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 6.0 °C (43 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Barcelona?
February is not one of Barcelona’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barcelona in February?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (90 mm), the driest July (22 mm).
Does it snow in Barcelona in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barcelona get in February?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Barcelona in February?
About 13.0 °C (55 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Barcelona in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
