Why Barcelona has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Barcelona climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Barcelona?
August, with an average of 24.1 °C (75 °F), day highs around 26.1 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 21.5 °C (71 °F).
What is the coldest month in Barcelona?
January, with an average of 9.7 °C (49 °F), day highs around 12.2 °C (54 °F) and night lows around 7.0 °C (45 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Barcelona?
October, with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 603 mm (23.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Barcelona?
July, with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Barcelona?
July, with about 300 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Barcelona?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Barcelona?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August, September and October.
What type of climate does Barcelona have?
Mediterranean, hot summer (Köppen Csa). Hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.4 × 11 km, that contains Barcelona’s GeoNames location (41.389°N, 2.159°E; ClimaClue area sq-426-1821), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,686,208 (GeoNames).
