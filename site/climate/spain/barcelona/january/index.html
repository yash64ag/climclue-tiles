How January compares in Barcelona

January is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Barcelona. Compared with December: 0.9 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. February is 0.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Barcelona in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barcelona in January?
In January, Barcelona averages a day high of 13.5 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 5.1 °C (41 °F), with 50 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Barcelona?
January is not one of Barcelona’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barcelona in January?
50 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (90 mm), the driest July (22 mm).
Does it snow in Barcelona in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barcelona get in January?
About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Barcelona in January?
About 13.5 °C (56 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Barcelona in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
