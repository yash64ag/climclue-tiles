How August compares in Barcelona

August is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Barcelona. Compared with July: 0.5 °C warmer, 32 mm more precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. September is 2.5 °C cooler than August on average.

Barcelona in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barcelona in August?
In August, Barcelona averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Barcelona?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Barcelona’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barcelona in August?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (90 mm), the driest July (22 mm).
Does it snow in Barcelona in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barcelona get in August?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Barcelona in August?
About 25.2 °C (77 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (25.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Barcelona in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
