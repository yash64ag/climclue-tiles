How April compares in Barcelona

April is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Barcelona. Compared with March: 1.8 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. May is 3.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Barcelona in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barcelona in April?
In April, Barcelona averages a day high of 16.0 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 10.7 °C (51 °F), with 47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Barcelona?
April is not one of Barcelona’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barcelona in April?
47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (86 mm), the driest July (21 mm).
Does it snow in Barcelona in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barcelona get in April?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Barcelona in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
