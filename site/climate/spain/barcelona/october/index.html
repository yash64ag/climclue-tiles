Barcelona weather in October

Average October weather in Barcelona, Spain: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Barcelona averages 22.0 °C (72 °F) days and 13.9 °C (57 °F) nights, with 90 mm (3.5 in) of rain over the month and about 176 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Barcelona

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Barcelona. Compared with September: 3.5 °C cooler, 12 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. November is 4.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Barcelona in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barcelona in October?
In October, Barcelona averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 13.9 °C (57 °F), with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Barcelona?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Barcelona’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barcelona in October?
90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is October (90 mm), the driest July (22 mm).
Does it snow in Barcelona in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barcelona get in October?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Barcelona in October?
About 20.8 °C (69 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Barcelona in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
