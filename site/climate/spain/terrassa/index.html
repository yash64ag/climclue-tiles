Terrassa at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Terrassa is 50th warmest (14.6 °C year average), 50th sunniest (about 2,333 hours of sunshine a year) and 20th wettest (619 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sabadell, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Terrassa is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 33 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.1 °C), averages 16.3 °C more than its coolest, January (6.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Terrassa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Terrassa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
