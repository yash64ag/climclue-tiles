Tomares at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Tomares is 69th warmest (18.5 °C year average), 25th sunniest (about 2,844 hours of sunshine a year) and 161st wettest (642 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Castilleja de la Cuesta, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Tomares is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 8 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.2 °C), averages 16.8 °C more than its coolest, January (10.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.4 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 69 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 16 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.0 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 114% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Tomares has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 69 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tomares itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.373°N, 6.046°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-466-1739), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
