Guadix at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Guadix is 381st warmest (15.0 °C year average), 101st sunniest (about 2,773 hours of sunshine a year) and 456th wettest (382 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Baza, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Guadix is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.5 °C), averages 19.2 °C more than its coolest, January (6.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 67 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Guadix, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Guadix has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 67 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-467-1768), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
