Adeje sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Canary Islands · Spain

The sea off Adeje averages 20.8 °C (69 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 16.3 °C (61 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to October.

Adeje sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Adeje?
In September, at about 20.8 °C (69 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Adeje?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to October. The coolest month is February, at 16.3 °C (61 °F).
How warm is the sea in Adeje in February?
About 16.3 °C (61 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Adeje?
About 18.4 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Adeje nearby?
In September: San Miguel De Abona (22.4 °C, 11 km), Granadilla de Abona (21.9 °C, 15 km) and San Isidro (21.9 °C, 17 km).
