How February compares in Madrid

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Madrid. Compared with January: 1.8 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. March is 3.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Madrid in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Madrid in February?
In February, Madrid averages a day high of 11.7 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 1.3 °C (34 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Madrid?
February is not one of Madrid’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Madrid in February?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (60 mm), the driest July (11 mm).
Does it snow in Madrid in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.7 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Madrid get in February?
About 145 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Madrid in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
