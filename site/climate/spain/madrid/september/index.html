How September compares in Madrid

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Madrid. Compared with August: 4.5 °C cooler, 13 mm more precipitation and 89 hours less sunshine. October is 5.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Madrid in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Madrid in September?
In September, Madrid averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 14.2 °C (58 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Madrid?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Madrid’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Madrid in September?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (60 mm), the driest July (11 mm).
Does it snow in Madrid in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Madrid get in September?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Madrid in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
