How June compares in Madrid

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Madrid. Compared with May: 5.7 °C warmer, 27 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. July is 3.6 °C warmer than June on average.

Madrid in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Madrid in June?
In June, Madrid averages a day high of 27.8 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 14.6 °C (58 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 289 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Madrid?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Madrid’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Madrid in June?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (60 mm), the driest July (11 mm).
Does it snow in Madrid in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Madrid get in June?
About 289 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Madrid in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
