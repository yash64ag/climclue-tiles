Madrid weather in August

Average August weather in Madrid, Spain: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Madrid averages 33.0 °C (91 °F) days and 17.9 °C (64 °F) nights, with 11 mm (0.4 in) of rain over the month and about 330 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Madrid

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Madrid. Compared with July: 0.6 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. September is 4.5 °C cooler than August on average.

Madrid in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Madrid in August?
In August, Madrid averages a day high of 33.0 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 330 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Madrid?
August is not one of Madrid’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Madrid in August?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (54 mm), the driest July (10 mm).
Does it snow in Madrid in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Madrid get in August?
About 330 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Madrid in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
