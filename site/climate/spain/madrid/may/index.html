Madrid weather in May

Average May weather in Madrid, Spain: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Madrid averages 23.0 °C (73 °F) days and 10.3 °C (51 °F) nights, with 45 mm (1.8 in) of rain over the month and about 262 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Madrid

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Madrid. Compared with April: 4.1 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 54 hours more sunshine. June is 5.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Madrid in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Madrid in May?
In May, Madrid averages a day high of 23.0 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 10.3 °C (51 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 262 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Madrid?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Madrid’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Madrid in May?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (54 mm), the driest July (10 mm).
Does it snow in Madrid in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Madrid get in May?
About 262 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Madrid in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
