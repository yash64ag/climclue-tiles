How November compares in Madrid

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Madrid. Compared with October: 5.6 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 53 hours less sunshine. December is 3.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Madrid in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Madrid in November?
In November, Madrid averages a day high of 13.7 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 4.6 °C (40 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Madrid?
November is not one of Madrid’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Madrid in November?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (54 mm), the driest July (10 mm).
Does it snow in Madrid in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Madrid get in November?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Madrid in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
