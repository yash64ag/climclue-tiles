How January compares in Madrid

January is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Madrid. Compared with December: 0.9 °C cooler, 22 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. February is 1.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Madrid in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Madrid in January?
In January, Madrid averages a day high of 9.6 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 0.6 °C (33 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Madrid?
January is not one of Madrid’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Madrid in January?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (60 mm), the driest July (11 mm).
Does it snow in Madrid in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.2 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Madrid get in January?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Madrid in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
