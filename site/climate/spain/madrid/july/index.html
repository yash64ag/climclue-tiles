How July compares in Madrid

July is the warmest month, the driest month and the sunniest month in Madrid. Compared with June: 3.6 °C warmer, 12 mm less precipitation and 61 hours more sunshine. August is 0.6 °C cooler than July on average.

Madrid in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Madrid in July?
In July, Madrid averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 17.8 °C (64 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 349 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Madrid?
July is not one of Madrid’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Madrid in July?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is October (60 mm), the driest July (11 mm).
Does it snow in Madrid in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Madrid get in July?
About 349 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Madrid in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
