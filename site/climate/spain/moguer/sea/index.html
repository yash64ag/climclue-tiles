Moguer sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Andalusia · Spain

The sea off Moguer averages 22.5 °C (73 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 15.0 °C (59 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to October.

Moguer sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Moguer?
In August, at about 22.5 °C (73 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Moguer?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to October. The coolest month is February, at 15.0 °C (59 °F).
How warm is the sea in Moguer in February?
About 15.0 °C (59 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Moguer?
About 18.4 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
