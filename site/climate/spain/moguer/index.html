Moguer at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Moguer is 131st warmest (18.1 °C year average), 8th sunniest (about 2,924 hours of sunshine a year) and 195th wettest (617 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Huelva, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Moguer is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 25 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.9 °C), averages 15.0 °C more than its coolest, January (10.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.9 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.0 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 117% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Moguer has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Moguer itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.276°N, 6.839°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-467-1731), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
