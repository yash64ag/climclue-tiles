Illescas at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Illescas is 360th warmest (15.3 °C year average), 240th sunniest (about 2,636 hours of sunshine a year) and 404th wettest (445 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Seseña, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Illescas is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Why Illescas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 317 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Illescas itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.122°N, 3.847°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-438-1761), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
