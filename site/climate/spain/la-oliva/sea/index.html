La Oliva sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Canary Islands · Spain

The sea off La Oliva averages 22.8 °C (73 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 18.2 °C (65 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to December.

La Oliva sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in La Oliva?
In September, at about 22.8 °C (73 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in La Oliva?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to December. The coolest month is February, at 18.2 °C (65 °F).
How warm is the sea in La Oliva in February?
About 18.2 °C (65 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in La Oliva?
About 20.3 °C (69 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
