Tolosa at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Tolosa is 496th warmest (13.6 °C year average), 509th sunniest (about 1,758 hours of sunshine a year) and 19th wettest (1,783 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Oria, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Tolosa is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 78 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (19.8 °C), averages 12.0 °C more than its coolest, January (7.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 19 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 116% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Tolosa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.0 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tolosa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (43.135°N, 2.078°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-408-1779), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
