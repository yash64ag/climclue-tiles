Santa Cruz de Tenerife at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Santa Cruz de Tenerife is 11th warmest (18.4 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 2,750 hours of sunshine a year) and 54th wettest (427 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with La Laguna, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Santa Cruz de Tenerife is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 2.0 °C warmer in February, with 34 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.4 °C), averages 5.6 °C more than its coolest, February (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.2 °C (59 °F), in February. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Santa Cruz de Tenerife has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
