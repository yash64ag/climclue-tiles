Oviedo at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Oviedo is 61st warmest (12.9 °C year average), 61st sunniest (about 1,761 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (1,104 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gijón, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Oviedo is 1.0 °C cooler in August and 1.9 °C cooler in January, with 15 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (18.4 °C), averages 10.6 °C more than its coolest, January (7.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 37 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 109% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Oviedo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 37 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
