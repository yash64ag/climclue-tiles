L'Hospitalet de Llobregat at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, L'Hospitalet de Llobregat is 287th warmest (16.1 °C year average), 407th sunniest (about 2,385 hours of sunshine a year) and 243rd wettest (573 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cornellà de Llobregat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), L'Hospitalet de Llobregat is about as warm in August and about as warm in January, with 20 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.2 °C), averages 15.0 °C more than its coolest, January (9.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why L'Hospitalet de Llobregat has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
