L'Hospitalet de Llobregat at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, L'Hospitalet de Llobregat is 28th warmest (16.2 °C year average), 46th sunniest (about 2,385 hours of sunshine a year) and 22nd wettest (603 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Santa Coloma de Gramenet, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), L'Hospitalet de Llobregat is 0.4 °C warmer in August and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 5 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.1 °C), averages 14.4 °C more than its coolest, January (9.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.0 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why L'Hospitalet de Llobregat has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
