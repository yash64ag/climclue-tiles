Logroño at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Logroño is 59th warmest (13.4 °C year average), 56th sunniest (about 2,182 hours of sunshine a year) and 16th wettest (663 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gasteiz / Vitoria, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Logroño is 2.4 °C warmer in August and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 315 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.4 °C), averages 15.3 °C more than its coolest, January (6.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 111 km from the sea.

Why Logroño has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 111 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
