Las Palmas de Gran Canaria at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Las Palmas de Gran Canaria is the 3rd driest (282 mm a year). Compared with Telde, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Las Palmas de Gran Canaria is 0.3 °C warmer in September and 1.4 °C warmer in February, with 42 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (21.4 °C), averages 5.1 °C more than its coolest, February (16.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.0 °C (59 °F), in February. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Las Palmas de Gran Canaria has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
