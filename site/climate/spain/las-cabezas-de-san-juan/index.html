Las Cabezas de San Juan at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Las Cabezas de San Juan is 59th warmest (18.6 °C year average), 20th sunniest (about 2,865 hours of sunshine a year) and 148th wettest (655 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lebrija, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Las Cabezas de San Juan is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 39 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.8 °C), averages 16.1 °C more than its coolest, January (10.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.0 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 54 km from the sea.

Why Las Cabezas de San Juan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 54 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
