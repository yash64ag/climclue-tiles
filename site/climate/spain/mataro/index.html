Mataró at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Mataró is 34th warmest (15.8 °C year average), 52nd sunniest (about 2,298 hours of sunshine a year) and 17th wettest (645 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Badalona, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Mataró is 0.4 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 70 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.6 °C), averages 14.2 °C more than its coolest, January (9.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.8 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mataró, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mataró has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
