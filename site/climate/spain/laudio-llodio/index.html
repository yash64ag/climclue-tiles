Laudio / Llodio at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Laudio / Llodio is 499th warmest (13.6 °C year average), 528th sunniest (about 1,634 hours of sunshine a year) and 57th wettest (1,131 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Basauri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Laudio / Llodio is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 22 mm more precipitation a year. Why Laudio / Llodio has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 34 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Laudio / Llodio itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (43.143°N, 2.962°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-408-1770), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
