Dos Hermanas at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Dos Hermanas is the 5th warmest (18.7 °C year average) and the 4th sunniest (about 2,838 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Sevilla, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Dos Hermanas is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 56 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.3 °C), averages 16.7 °C more than its coolest, January (10.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.8 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 69 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 15 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.0 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 114% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Dos Hermanas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 69 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
