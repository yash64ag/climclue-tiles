Sabadell at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Sabadell is 40th warmest (15.0 °C year average), 51st sunniest (about 2,331 hours of sunshine a year) and 22nd wettest (603 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Terrassa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Sabadell is 0.6 °C warmer in August and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 26 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.4 °C), averages 16.1 °C more than its coolest, January (7.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 27 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sabadell, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sabadell has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 27 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sabadell itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.543°N, 2.109°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-424-1821), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
