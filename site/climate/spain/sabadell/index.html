Sabadell at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Sabadell is 35th warmest (15.2 °C year average), 51st sunniest (about 2,331 hours of sunshine a year) and 27th wettest (587 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Terrassa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Sabadell is 0.5 °C warmer in August and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 33 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.6 °C), averages 16.0 °C more than its coolest, January (7.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 27 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sabadell, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sabadell has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 27 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
