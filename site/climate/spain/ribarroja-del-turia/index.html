Ribarroja del Turia at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Ribarroja del Turia is 201st warmest (17.6 °C year average), 287th sunniest (about 2,597 hours of sunshine a year) and 339th wettest (510 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Ribarroja del Turia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 28 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
