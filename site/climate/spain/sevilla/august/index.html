How August compares in Sevilla

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Sevilla. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. September is 2.8 °C cooler than August on average.

Sevilla in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sevilla in August?
In August, Sevilla averages a day high of 35.8 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 19.0 °C (66 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 337 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Sevilla?
August is not one of Sevilla’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sevilla in August?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (104 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Sevilla in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sevilla get in August?
About 337 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sevilla in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
