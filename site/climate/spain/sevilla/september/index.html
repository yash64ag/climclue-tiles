How September compares in Sevilla

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Sevilla. Compared with August: 2.8 °C cooler, 22 mm more precipitation and 86 hours less sunshine. October is 4.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Sevilla in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sevilla in September?
In September, Sevilla averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 18.3 °C (65 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 252 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Sevilla?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Sevilla’s mild months (March, April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sevilla in September?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (109 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Sevilla in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sevilla get in September?
About 252 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sevilla in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
