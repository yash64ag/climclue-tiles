How June compares in Sevilla

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Sevilla. Compared with May: 4.5 °C warmer, 24 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. July is 2.8 °C warmer than June on average.

Sevilla in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sevilla in June?
In June, Sevilla averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 17.8 °C (64 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 317 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Sevilla?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Sevilla’s mild months (March, April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sevilla in June?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (109 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Sevilla in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sevilla get in June?
About 317 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sevilla in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
