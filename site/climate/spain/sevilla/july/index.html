How July compares in Sevilla

July is the warmest month, the driest month and the sunniest month in Sevilla. Compared with June: 2.8 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Sevilla in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sevilla in July?
In July, Sevilla averages a day high of 34.6 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 362 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Sevilla?
July is not one of Sevilla’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sevilla in July?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (109 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Sevilla in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sevilla get in July?
About 362 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sevilla in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
