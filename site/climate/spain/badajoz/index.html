Why Badajoz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 178 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

July, with an average of 26.1 °C (79 °F), day highs around 33.1 °C (92 °F) and night lows around 18.8 °C (66 °F).
What is the coldest month in Badajoz?
January, with an average of 8.7 °C (48 °F), day highs around 13.2 °C (56 °F) and night lows around 4.4 °C (40 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Badajoz?

What is the driest month in Badajoz?

What is the sunniest month in Badajoz?
July, with about 362 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Badajoz?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Badajoz?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, September and October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.7 × 11 km, that contains Badajoz’s GeoNames location (38.878°N, 6.971°W; ClimaClue area sq-451-1730), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 150,530 (GeoNames).
