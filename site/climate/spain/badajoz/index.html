Badajoz at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Badajoz is 21st warmest (17.0 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 2,757 hours of sunshine a year) and 35th wettest (535 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Huelva, the nearest covered town with different numbers (179 km away), Badajoz is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 2.8 °C cooler in January, with 51 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.1 °C), averages 17.5 °C more than its coolest, January (8.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 178 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 12 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.2 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 119% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Badajoz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 178 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
