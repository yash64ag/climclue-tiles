Palamós sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Catalonia · Spain

The sea off Palamós averages 23.6 °C (74 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.9 °C (55 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Palamós sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Palamós?
In August, at about 23.6 °C (74 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Palamós?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 12.9 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in Palamós in February?
About 12.9 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Palamós?
About 17.3 °C (63 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Palamós nearby?
In August: Lloret de Mar (24.7 °C, 29 km), Blanes (24.7 °C, 34 km) and Pineda de Mar (24.7 °C, 44 km).
