Vilafranca del Penedès weather by month and climate

Vilafranca del Penedès at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Vilafranca del Penedès is 375th warmest (15.1 °C year average), 388th sunniest (about 2,437 hours of sunshine a year) and 256th wettest (567 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sant Pere de Ribes, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Vilafranca del Penedès is 0.5 °C cooler in August and 1.5 °C cooler in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.3 °C), averages 15.6 °C more than its coolest, January (7.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Vilafranca del Penedès has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
