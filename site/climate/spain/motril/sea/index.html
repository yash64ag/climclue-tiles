Motril sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Andalusia · Spain

The sea off Motril averages 22.4 °C (72 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.9 °C (57 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Motril sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Motril?
In August, at about 22.4 °C (72 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Motril?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 13.9 °C (57 °F).
How warm is the sea in Motril in February?
About 13.9 °C (57 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Motril?
About 17.4 °C (63 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Motril nearby?
In August: Adra (23.2 °C, 44 km), Berja (23.2 °C, 52 km) and El Ejido (23.8 °C, 63 km).
