Rivas-Vaciamadrid at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Rivas-Vaciamadrid is 357th warmest (15.3 °C year average), 266th sunniest (about 2,609 hours of sunshine a year) and 448th wettest (398 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Arganda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Rivas-Vaciamadrid is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 26 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.2 °C), averages 20.1 °C more than its coolest, January (6.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 299 km from the sea.

Why Rivas-Vaciamadrid has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 299 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Rivas-Vaciamadrid itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.326°N, 3.511°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-436-1764), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
