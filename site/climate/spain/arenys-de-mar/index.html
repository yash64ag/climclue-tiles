Arenys de Mar at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Arenys de Mar is 313th warmest (15.9 °C year average), 444th sunniest (about 2,282 hours of sunshine a year) and 133rd wettest (686 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Arenys de Mar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
