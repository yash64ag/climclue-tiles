Alhaurín de la Torre sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Andalusia · Spain

The sea off Alhaurín de la Torre averages 22.5 °C (73 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 15.3 °C (60 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Alhaurín de la Torre sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Alhaurín de la Torre?
In August, at about 22.5 °C (73 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Alhaurín de la Torre?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 15.3 °C (60 °F).
How warm is the sea in Alhaurín de la Torre in February?
About 15.3 °C (60 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Alhaurín de la Torre?
About 18.3 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
