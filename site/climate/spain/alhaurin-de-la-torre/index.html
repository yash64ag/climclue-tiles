Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Alhaurín de la Torre is 175th warmest (17.8 °C year average), 97th sunniest (about 2,776 hours of sunshine a year) and 180th wettest (627 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Alhaurín de la Torre has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.1 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
