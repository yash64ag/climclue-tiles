How November compares in Málaga

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Málaga. Compared with October: 3.9 °C cooler, 36 mm more precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. December is 2.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Málaga in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Málaga in November?
In November, Málaga averages a day high of 17.4 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 11.4 °C (53 °F), with 95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Málaga?
November is not one of Málaga’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Málaga in November?
95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (105 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Málaga in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Málaga get in November?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Málaga in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
