How October compares in Málaga

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Málaga. Compared with September: 3.3 °C cooler, 32 mm more precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. November is 3.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Málaga in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Málaga in October?
In October, Málaga averages a day high of 24.0 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Málaga?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Málaga’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Málaga in October?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (99 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Málaga in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Málaga get in October?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Málaga in October?
About 18.8 °C (66 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (22.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Málaga in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
