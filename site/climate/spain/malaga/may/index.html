How May compares in Málaga

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Málaga. Compared with April: 2.8 °C warmer, 21 mm less precipitation and 71 hours more sunshine. June is 3.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Málaga in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Málaga in May?
In May, Málaga averages a day high of 21.3 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 13.7 °C (57 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 286 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Málaga?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Málaga’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Málaga in May?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (105 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Málaga in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Málaga get in May?
About 286 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Málaga in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
