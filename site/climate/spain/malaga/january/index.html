How January compares in Málaga

January is the coolest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Málaga. Compared with December: 1.0 °C cooler, 36 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. February is 0.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Málaga in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Málaga in January?
In January, Málaga averages a day high of 14.0 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 7.6 °C (46 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Málaga?
January is not one of Málaga’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Málaga in January?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (105 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Málaga in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Málaga get in January?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Málaga in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
