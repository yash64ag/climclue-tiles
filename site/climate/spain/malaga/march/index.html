How March compares in Málaga

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Málaga. Compared with February: 1.7 °C warmer, 12 mm less precipitation and 48 hours more sunshine. April is 1.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Málaga in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Málaga in March?
In March, Málaga averages a day high of 19.5 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 9.8 °C (50 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Málaga?
March is not one of Málaga’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Málaga in March?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (99 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Málaga in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Málaga get in March?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Málaga in March?
About 14.6 °C (58 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (22.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Málaga in March?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
