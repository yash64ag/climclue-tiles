Málaga weather in June

Average June weather in Málaga, Spain: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Málaga averages 27.5 °C (82 °F) days and 16.9 °C (62 °F) nights, with 8 mm (0.3 in) of rain over the month and about 314 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Málaga

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Málaga. Compared with May: 3.4 °C warmer, 17 mm less precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. July is 2.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Málaga in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Málaga in June?
In June, Málaga averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 314 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Málaga?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Málaga’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Málaga in June?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (99 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Málaga in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Málaga get in June?
About 314 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Málaga in June?
About 18.8 °C (66 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (22.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Málaga in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
