How August compares in Málaga

August is the warmest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Málaga. Compared with July: 0.4 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. September is 2.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Málaga in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Málaga in August?
In August, Málaga averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 322 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Málaga?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Málaga’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Málaga in August?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (99 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Málaga in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Málaga get in August?
About 322 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Málaga in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
