How December compares in Málaga

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Málaga. Compared with November: 2.5 °C cooler, 10 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. January is 1.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Málaga in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Málaga in December?
In December, Málaga averages a day high of 14.8 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 9.0 °C (48 °F), with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Málaga?
December is not one of Málaga’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Málaga in December?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (105 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Málaga in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Málaga get in December?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Málaga in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
