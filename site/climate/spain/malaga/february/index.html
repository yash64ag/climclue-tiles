How February compares in Málaga

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Málaga. Compared with January: 0.9 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. March is 1.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Málaga in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Málaga in February?
In February, Málaga averages a day high of 14.9 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 8.2 °C (47 °F), with 64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 160 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Málaga?
February is not one of Málaga’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Málaga in February?
64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (105 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Málaga in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Málaga get in February?
About 160 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Málaga in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
