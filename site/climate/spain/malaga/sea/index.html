Málaga sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Andalusia · Spain

The sea off Málaga averages 22.1 °C (72 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.2 °C (58 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Málaga sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Málaga?
In August, at about 22.1 °C (72 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Málaga?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 14.2 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in Málaga in February?
About 14.2 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Málaga?
About 17.4 °C (63 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
