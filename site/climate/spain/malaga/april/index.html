How April compares in Málaga

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Málaga. Compared with March: 1.5 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. May is 2.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Málaga in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Málaga in April?
In April, Málaga averages a day high of 21.1 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 11.3 °C (52 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Málaga?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Málaga’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Málaga in April?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (99 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Málaga in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Málaga get in April?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Málaga in April?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
