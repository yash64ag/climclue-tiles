Ayamonte sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Andalusia · Spain

The sea off Ayamonte averages 21.6 °C (71 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 15.4 °C (60 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to October.

Ayamonte sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ayamonte?
In August, at about 21.6 °C (71 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ayamonte?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to October. The coolest month is February, at 15.4 °C (60 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ayamonte in February?
About 15.4 °C (60 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Ayamonte?
About 18.3 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Ayamonte nearby?
In August: Aljaraque (22.3 °C, 35 km), Huelva (22.3 °C, 42 km) and Moguer (22.5 °C, 51 km).
