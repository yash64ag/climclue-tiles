Why La Laguna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

La Laguna climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in La Laguna?
August, with an average of 21.4 °C (71 °F), day highs around 22.4 °C (72 °F) and night lows around 20.7 °C (69 °F).
What is the coldest month in La Laguna?
February, with an average of 13.8 °C (57 °F), day highs around 14.7 °C (58 °F) and night lows around 13.0 °C (55 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in La Laguna?
December, with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 461 mm (18.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in La Laguna?
July, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in La Laguna?
July, with about 303 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in La Laguna?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in La Laguna?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.8 × 11 km, that contains La Laguna’s GeoNames location (28.485°N, 16.320°W; ClimaClue area sq-555-1636), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 150,661 (GeoNames).
