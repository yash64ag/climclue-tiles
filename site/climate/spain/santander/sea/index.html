Santander sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Cantabria · Spain

The sea off Santander averages 21.5 °C (71 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.5 °C (55 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Santander sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Santander?
In August, at about 21.5 °C (71 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Santander?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 12.5 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in Santander in February?
About 12.5 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Santander?
About 16.2 °C (61 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
