Why Burgos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 129 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Burgos climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Burgos?
August, with an average of 19.4 °C (67 °F), day highs around 25.2 °C (77 °F) and night lows around 13.1 °C (56 °F).
What is the coldest month in Burgos?
January, with an average of 4.0 °C (39 °F), day highs around 6.8 °C (44 °F) and night lows around 0.1 °C (32 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Burgos?
December, with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 581 mm (22.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Burgos?
August, with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Burgos?
July, with about 320 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Burgos?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 35 cm (13.9 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Burgos?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Burgos have?
Mediterranean, warm summer (Köppen Csb). Warm, dry summers and mild, wet winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.2 × 11 km, that contains Burgos’s GeoNames location (42.341°N, 3.702°W; ClimaClue area sq-416-1762), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 176,418 (GeoNames).
