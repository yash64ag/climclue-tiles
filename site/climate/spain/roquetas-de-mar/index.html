Roquetas de Mar at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Roquetas de Mar is 29th warmest (18.9 °C year average), 39th sunniest (about 2,819 hours of sunshine a year) and 526th wettest (273 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vícar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Roquetas de Mar is 0.6 °C warmer in August and 2.2 °C warmer in January, with 70 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.8 °C), averages 12.5 °C more than its coolest, January (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.1 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 25 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 131% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern. Why Roquetas de Mar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Roquetas de Mar itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.764°N, 2.615°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-472-1773), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
