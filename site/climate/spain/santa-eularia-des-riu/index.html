Santa Eulària des Riu weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Santa Eulària des Riu, Spain (Balearic Islands), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Santa Eulària des Riu is warmest in August, with 31.0 °C (88 °F) days and 22.4 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 16.6 °C (62 °F) days and 9.1 °C nights. Most rain falls around October (68 mm); July is driest (5 mm).

Santa Eulària des Riu at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Santa Eulària des Riu is 19th warmest (18.9 °C year average), 178th sunniest (about 2,717 hours of sunshine a year) and 364th wettest (483 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ibiza, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Santa Eulària des Riu is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 23 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.6 °C), averages 13.8 °C more than its coolest, January (12.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.1 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Santa Eulària des Riu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Santa Eulària des Riu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.985°N, 1.534°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-450-1815), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
