How May compares in Zaragoza

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Zaragoza. Compared with April: 4.2 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 52 hours more sunshine. June is 4.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Zaragoza in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zaragoza in May?
In May, Zaragoza averages a day high of 24.3 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 12.1 °C (54 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 270 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Zaragoza?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Zaragoza’s mild months (May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zaragoza in May?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is May (45 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Zaragoza in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zaragoza get in May?
About 270 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zaragoza in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
