How November compares in Zaragoza

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Zaragoza. Compared with October: 5.6 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 52 hours less sunshine. December is 3.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Zaragoza in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zaragoza in November?
In November, Zaragoza averages a day high of 14.9 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 6.8 °C (44 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Zaragoza?
November is not one of Zaragoza’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zaragoza in November?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (45 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Zaragoza in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zaragoza get in November?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zaragoza in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
