How February compares in Zaragoza

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Zaragoza. Compared with January: 1.6 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. March is 3.6 °C warmer than February on average.

Zaragoza in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zaragoza in February?
In February, Zaragoza averages a day high of 13.1 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 3.8 °C (39 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Zaragoza?
February is not one of Zaragoza’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zaragoza in February?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (45 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Zaragoza in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.7 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Zaragoza get in February?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zaragoza in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
