How September compares in Zaragoza

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Zaragoza. Compared with August: 3.9 °C cooler, 15 mm more precipitation and 81 hours less sunshine. October is 5.0 °C cooler than September on average.

Zaragoza in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zaragoza in September?
In September, Zaragoza averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 15.6 °C (60 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Zaragoza?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Zaragoza’s mild months (May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zaragoza in September?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (45 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Zaragoza in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zaragoza get in September?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zaragoza in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
