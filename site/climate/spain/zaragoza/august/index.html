How August compares in Zaragoza

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Zaragoza. Compared with July: 0.5 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. September is 3.9 °C cooler than August on average.

Zaragoza in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zaragoza in August?
In August, Zaragoza averages a day high of 32.4 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 18.9 °C (66 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 311 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Zaragoza?
August is not one of Zaragoza’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zaragoza in August?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (44 mm), the driest July (15 mm).
Does it snow in Zaragoza in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zaragoza get in August?
About 311 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zaragoza in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
