How March compares in Zaragoza

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Zaragoza. Compared with February: 3.6 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. April is 2.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Zaragoza in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zaragoza in March?
In March, Zaragoza averages a day high of 17.4 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 6.2 °C (43 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Zaragoza?
March is not one of Zaragoza’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zaragoza in March?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (45 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Zaragoza in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zaragoza get in March?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zaragoza in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
