Bilbao sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Basque Country · Spain

The sea off Bilbao averages 21.3 °C (70 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.2 °C (54 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Bilbao sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Bilbao?
In August, at about 21.3 °C (70 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Bilbao?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 12.2 °C (54 °F).
How warm is the sea in Bilbao in February?
About 12.2 °C (54 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Bilbao?
About 15.9 °C (61 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
