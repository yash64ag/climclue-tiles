Bilbao at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Bilbao is the least sunny (about 1,569 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th wettest (1,173 mm a year). Compared with Gasteiz / Vitoria, the nearest covered town with different numbers (50 km away), Bilbao is 0.5 °C warmer in August and 3.2 °C warmer in January, with 195 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (19.4 °C), averages 11.0 °C more than its coolest, January (8.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.6 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 99% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Bilbao has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
