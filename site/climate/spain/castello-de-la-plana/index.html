Castelló de la Plana at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Castelló de la Plana is 19th warmest (17.5 °C year average), 25th sunniest (about 2,626 hours of sunshine a year) and 39th wettest (495 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Valencia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (64 km away), Castelló de la Plana is 1.0 °C cooler in August and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 30 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.3 °C), averages 14.5 °C more than its coolest, January (10.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.8 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 11 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 101% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Castelló de la Plana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
