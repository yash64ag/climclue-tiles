How May compares in Valencia

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Valencia. Compared with April: 3.1 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 39 hours more sunshine. June is 3.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Valencia in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Valencia in May?
In May, Valencia averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 15.3 °C (60 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Valencia?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Valencia’s mild months (April, May, June, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Valencia in May?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (86 mm), the driest July (9 mm).
Does it snow in Valencia in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Valencia get in May?
About 254 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Valencia in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
