How June compares in Valencia

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Valencia. Compared with May: 3.9 °C warmer, 20 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. July is 2.6 °C warmer than June on average.

Valencia in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Valencia in June?
In June, Valencia averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 19.1 °C (66 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 272 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Valencia?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Valencia’s mild months (April, May, June, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Valencia in June?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (85 mm), the driest July (9 mm).
Does it snow in Valencia in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Valencia get in June?
About 272 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Valencia in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
