How October compares in Valencia

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Valencia. Compared with September: 3.8 °C cooler, 9 mm more precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. November is 4.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Valencia in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Valencia in October?
In October, Valencia averages a day high of 22.3 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 16.1 °C (61 °F), with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 196 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Valencia?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Valencia’s mild months (April, May, June, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Valencia in October?
86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is October (86 mm), the driest July (9 mm).
Does it snow in Valencia in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Valencia get in October?
About 196 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Valencia in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
