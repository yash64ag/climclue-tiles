Why Valencia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Valencia climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Valencia?
August, with an average of 26.3 °C (79 °F), day highs around 29.0 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 22.4 °C (72 °F).
What is the coldest month in Valencia?
January, with an average of 11.1 °C (52 °F), day highs around 13.9 °C (57 °F) and night lows around 7.5 °C (46 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Valencia?
October, with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 524 mm (20.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Valencia?
July, with 9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Valencia?
July, with about 307 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Valencia?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Valencia?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, September, October and November.
What type of climate does Valencia have?
Mediterranean, hot summer (Köppen Csa). Hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.6 × 11 km, that contains Valencia’s GeoNames location (39.474°N, 0.380°W; ClimaClue area sq-445-1796), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 824,340 (GeoNames).
