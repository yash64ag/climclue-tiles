How March compares in Valencia

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Valencia. Compared with February: 2.2 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. April is 2.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Valencia in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Valencia in March?
In March, Valencia averages a day high of 19.3 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 8.6 °C (47 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Valencia?
March is not one of Valencia’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Valencia in March?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (85 mm), the driest July (9 mm).
Does it snow in Valencia in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Valencia get in March?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Valencia in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
