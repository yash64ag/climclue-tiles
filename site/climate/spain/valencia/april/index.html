How April compares in Valencia

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Valencia. Compared with March: 2.3 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. May is 3.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Valencia in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Valencia in April?
In April, Valencia averages a day high of 19.3 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Valencia?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Valencia’s mild months (April, May, June, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Valencia in April?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (86 mm), the driest July (9 mm).
Does it snow in Valencia in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Valencia get in April?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Valencia in April?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
