How December compares in Valencia

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Valencia. Compared with November: 3.1 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. January is 1.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Of the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Valencia ranks 14th warmest (average temperature), 16th sunniest and 46th wettest in December.

Valencia in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Valencia in December?
In December, Valencia averages a day high of 14.7 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Valencia?
December is not one of Valencia’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Valencia in December?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (86 mm), the driest July (9 mm).
Does it snow in Valencia in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Valencia get in December?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Valencia in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
