How February compares in Valencia

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Valencia. Compared with January: 0.8 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. March is 2.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Valencia in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Valencia in February?
In February, Valencia averages a day high of 16.8 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 6.8 °C (44 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 160 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Valencia?
February is not one of Valencia’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Valencia in February?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (85 mm), the driest July (9 mm).
Does it snow in Valencia in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Valencia get in February?
About 160 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Valencia in February?
About 13.4 °C (56 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (26.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Valencia in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
