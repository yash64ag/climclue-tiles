Arucas sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Canary Islands · Spain

The sea off Arucas averages 22.6 °C (73 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 18.1 °C (65 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to November.

Arucas sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Arucas?
In September, at about 22.6 °C (73 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Arucas?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to November. The coolest month is February, at 18.1 °C (65 °F).
How warm is the sea in Arucas in February?
About 18.1 °C (65 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Arucas?
About 20.1 °C (68 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
