Sant Joan Despí weather by month and climate

Sant Joan Despí at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Sant Joan Despí is 300th warmest (16.1 °C year average), 393rd sunniest (about 2,395 hours of sunshine a year) and 273rd wettest (556 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sant Feliu de Llobregat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Sant Joan Despí is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.0 °C), averages 14.8 °C more than its coolest, January (9.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.3 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Sant Joan Despí has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sant Joan Despí itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.367°N, 2.057°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-426-1820), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
