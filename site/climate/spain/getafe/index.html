Getafe at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Getafe is 41st warmest (14.9 °C year average), 30th sunniest (about 2,605 hours of sunshine a year) and 52nd wettest (431 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fuenlabrada, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Getafe is 0.3 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.1 °C), averages 20.8 °C more than its coolest, January (5.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 315 km from the sea.

Why Getafe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 315 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
