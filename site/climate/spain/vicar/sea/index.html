Vícar sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Andalusia · Spain

The sea off Vícar averages 23.8 °C (75 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.8 °C (59 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to October.

Vícar sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Vícar?
In August, at about 23.8 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Vícar?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to October. The coolest month is February, at 14.8 °C (59 °F).
How warm is the sea in Vícar in February?
About 14.8 °C (59 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Vícar?
About 18.5 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Vícar nearby?
In August: Águilas (25.4 °C, 114 km).
