Lleida at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Lleida is 31st warmest (16.1 °C year average), 23rd sunniest (about 2,651 hours of sunshine a year) and 56th wettest (385 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Reus, the nearest covered town with different numbers (65 km away), Lleida is 1.9 °C warmer in July and 2.2 °C cooler in January, with 165 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.6 °C), averages 20.1 °C more than its coolest, January (6.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 83 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 104% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Lleida has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 83 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
