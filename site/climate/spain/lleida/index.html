Why Lleida has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 83 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Lleida climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Lleida?
July, with an average of 26.6 °C (80 °F), day highs around 33.0 °C (91 °F) and night lows around 19.3 °C (67 °F).
What is the coldest month in Lleida?
January, with an average of 6.5 °C (44 °F), day highs around 11.0 °C (52 °F) and night lows around 1.6 °C (35 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Lleida?
October, with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 385 mm (15.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Lleida?
July, with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Lleida?
July, with about 341 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Lleida?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 4.4 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Lleida?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, September and October.
What type of climate does Lleida have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.3 × 11 km, that contains Lleida’s GeoNames location (41.617°N, 0.622°E; ClimaClue area sq-423-1806), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 140,797 (GeoNames).
