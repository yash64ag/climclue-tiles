Redondela sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Galicia · Spain

The sea off Redondela averages 16.8 °C (62 °F) in July, its warmest month, and 12.8 °C (55 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Redondela sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Redondela?
In July, at about 16.8 °C (62 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Redondela?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 12.8 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in Redondela in February?
About 12.8 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Redondela?
About 14.9 °C (59 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
