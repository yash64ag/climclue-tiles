Aranjuez at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Aranjuez is 314th warmest (15.9 °C year average), 241st sunniest (about 2,633 hours of sunshine a year) and 463rd wettest (373 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Seseña, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Aranjuez is 0.7 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 75 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.5 °C), averages 21.5 °C more than its coolest, January (6.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 298 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Aranjuez, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Aranjuez has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 298 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Aranjuez itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.031°N, 3.602°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-439-1763), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
