Mogán at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Mogán is 111th warmest (18.2 °C year average), 98th sunniest (about 2,775 hours of sunshine a year) and 453rd wettest (390 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Bartolomé de Tirajana, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Mogán is 0.5 °C cooler in August and 3.0 °C warmer in January, with 42 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.7 °C), averages 6.7 °C more than its coolest, January (15.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.9 °C (53 °F), in February. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mogán, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mogán has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.7 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Mogán itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (27.884°N, 15.725°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-561-1642), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
