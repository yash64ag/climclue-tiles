Pineda de Mar sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Catalonia · Spain

The sea off Pineda de Mar averages 24.7 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.9 °C (55 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Pineda de Mar sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Pineda de Mar?
In August, at about 24.7 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Pineda de Mar?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 12.9 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in Pineda de Mar in February?
About 12.9 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Pineda de Mar?
About 17.8 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
