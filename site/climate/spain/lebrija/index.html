Lebrija at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Lebrija is 64th warmest (18.5 °C year average), 19th sunniest (about 2,894 hours of sunshine a year) and 197th wettest (616 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Las Cabezas de San Juan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Lebrija is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 39 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.7 °C), averages 15.9 °C more than its coolest, January (10.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.1 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 19 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 104% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Lebrija has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Lebrija itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.921°N, 6.075°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-470-1739), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
