Utrera at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Utrera is 42nd warmest (18.7 °C year average), 35th sunniest (about 2,828 hours of sunshine a year) and 139th wettest (675 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Los Palacios y Villafranca, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Utrera is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 28 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 16.9 °C more than its coolest, January (10.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.4 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 78 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 15 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 119% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Utrera has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 78 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Utrera itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.185°N, 5.781°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-468-1742), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
