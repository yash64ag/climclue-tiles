Alcobendas at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Alcobendas is 51st warmest (14.5 °C year average), 40th sunniest (about 2,551 hours of sunshine a year) and 40th wettest (482 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chamartín, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Alcobendas is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 26 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.6 °C), averages 20.4 °C more than its coolest, January (5.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 314 km from the sea.

Why Alcobendas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 314 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
