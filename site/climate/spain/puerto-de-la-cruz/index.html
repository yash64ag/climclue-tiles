Puerto de la Cruz at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Puerto de la Cruz is 58th warmest (18.6 °C year average), 194th sunniest (about 2,698 hours of sunshine a year) and 468th wettest (362 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with La Orotava, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Puerto de la Cruz is 1.1 °C warmer in September and 2.1 °C warmer in February, with 57 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (21.4 °C), averages 4.9 °C more than its coolest, February (16.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.4 °C (56 °F), in February. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: a NOAA weather station 13 km from Puerto de la Cruz measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from these (temperature by up to 12.4 °C (22.4 °F) in one month). One station can differ from modelled values (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Puerto de la Cruz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Puerto de la Cruz itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (28.417°N, 16.551°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-555-1634), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
