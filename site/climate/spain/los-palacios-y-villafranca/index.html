Los Palacios y Villafranca at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Los Palacios y Villafranca is 43rd warmest (18.6 °C year average), 24th sunniest (about 2,849 hours of sunshine a year) and 154th wettest (647 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Utrera, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Los Palacios y Villafranca is about as warm in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 28 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.1 °C), averages 16.5 °C more than its coolest, January (10.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.4 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 63 km from the sea.

Why Los Palacios y Villafranca has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 63 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
