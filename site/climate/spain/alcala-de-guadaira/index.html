Alcalá de Guadaira at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Alcalá de Guadaira is 50th warmest (18.6 °C year average), 38th sunniest (about 2,825 hours of sunshine a year) and 188th wettest (623 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Alcalá de Guadaira has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 83 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
