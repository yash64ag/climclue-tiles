Pilar de la Horadada at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Pilar de la Horadada is 85th warmest (18.4 °C year average), 325th sunniest (about 2,573 hours of sunshine a year) and 485th wettest (319 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Pedro del Pinatar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Pilar de la Horadada is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.0 °C), averages 14.0 °C more than its coolest, January (11.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.7 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Pilar de la Horadada has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
