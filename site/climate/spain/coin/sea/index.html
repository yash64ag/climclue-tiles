Coín sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Andalusia · Spain

The sea off Coín averages 20.5 °C (69 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.4 °C (58 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August.

Coín sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Coín?
In August, at about 20.5 °C (69 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Coín?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 14.4 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in Coín in February?
About 14.4 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Coín?
About 17.0 °C (63 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Coín nearby?
In August: Alhaurín el Grande (22.5 °C, 6 km), Mijas (22.5 °C, 13 km) and Alhaurín de la Torre (22.5 °C, 17 km).
