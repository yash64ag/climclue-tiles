Elche at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Elche is the 2nd driest (278 mm a year). Compared with Alicante, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Elche is 0.7 °C warmer in August and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 43 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.8 °C), averages 15.8 °C more than its coolest, January (10.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.2 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 26 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Elche, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Elche has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 26 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
