Chiclana de la Frontera at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Chiclana de la Frontera is 90th warmest (18.3 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 2,919 hours of sunshine a year) and 170th wettest (635 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Fernando, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Chiclana de la Frontera is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 29 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.6 °C), averages 12.1 °C more than its coolest, January (12.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.0 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Chiclana de la Frontera has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
