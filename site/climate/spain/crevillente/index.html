Crevillente at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Crevillente is 119th warmest (18.2 °C year average), 161st sunniest (about 2,733 hours of sunshine a year) and 507th wettest (298 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Elche, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Crevillente is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 24 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.6 °C), averages 16.1 °C more than its coolest, January (10.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.2 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 34 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Crevillente, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Crevillente has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 34 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.1 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Crevillente itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.250°N, 0.810°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-457-1791), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
