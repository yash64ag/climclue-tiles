Pozuelo de Alarcón at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Pozuelo de Alarcón is 442nd warmest (14.6 °C year average), 283rd sunniest (about 2,597 hours of sunshine a year) and 351st wettest (497 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Boadilla del Monte, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Pozuelo de Alarcón is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 15 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.2 °C), averages 19.5 °C more than its coolest, January (5.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 326 km from the sea.

Why Pozuelo de Alarcón has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 326 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pozuelo de Alarcón itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.433°N, 3.813°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
