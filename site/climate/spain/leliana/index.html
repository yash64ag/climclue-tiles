L'Eliana at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, L'Eliana is 190th warmest (17.7 °C year average), 287th sunniest (about 2,597 hours of sunshine a year) and 381st wettest (465 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with La Pobla de Vallbona, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), L'Eliana is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 9 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.7 °C), averages 15.5 °C more than its coolest, January (10.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.1 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 28 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of L'Eliana, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why L'Eliana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 28 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for L'Eliana itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (39.567°N, 0.533°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-444-1794), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
