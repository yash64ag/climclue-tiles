Mairena del Aljarafe at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Mairena del Aljarafe is 42nd warmest (18.7 °C year average), 25th sunniest (about 2,844 hours of sunshine a year) and 169th wettest (637 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Juan de Aznalfarache, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Mairena del Aljarafe is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.3 °C), averages 16.8 °C more than its coolest, January (10.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.5 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 69 km from the sea.

Why Mairena del Aljarafe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 69 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
