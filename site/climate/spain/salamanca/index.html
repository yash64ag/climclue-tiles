Why Salamanca has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 260 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Salamanca climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Salamanca?
July, with an average of 22.1 °C (72 °F), day highs around 28.4 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 14.5 °C (58 °F).
What is the coldest month in Salamanca?
January, with an average of 4.5 °C (40 °F), day highs around 8.3 °C (47 °F) and night lows around 0.4 °C (33 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Salamanca?
December, with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 471 mm (18.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Salamanca?
July, with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Salamanca?
July, with about 362 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Salamanca?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 14 cm (5.5 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Salamanca?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Salamanca have?
Mediterranean, hot summer (Köppen Csa). Hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.4 × 11 km, that contains Salamanca’s GeoNames location (40.969°N, 5.664°W; ClimaClue area sq-430-1743), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 144,825 (GeoNames).
