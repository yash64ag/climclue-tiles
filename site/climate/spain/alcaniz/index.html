Alcañiz at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Alcañiz is 287th warmest (16.2 °C year average), 262nd sunniest (about 2,618 hours of sunshine a year) and 452nd wettest (392 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tortosa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Alcañiz is 1.2 °C warmer in July and 1.6 °C cooler in January, with 86 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.9 °C), averages 18.3 °C more than its coolest, January (7.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 89 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Alcañiz, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Alcañiz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 89 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Alcañiz itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.050°N, 0.133°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-429-1798), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
