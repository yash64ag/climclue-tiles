Huelva at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Huelva is the 2nd warmest (18.8 °C year average) and the 2nd sunniest (about 2,929 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Sevilla, the nearest covered town with different numbers (86 km away), Huelva is 0.9 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.6 °C), averages 15.2 °C more than its coolest, January (11.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.8 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 111% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Huelva has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
