Valdepeñas at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Valdepeñas is 350th warmest (15.4 °C year average), 183rd sunniest (about 2,710 hours of sunshine a year) and 454th wettest (389 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with La Solana, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Valdepeñas is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 53 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.4 °C), averages 20.3 °C more than its coolest, January (6.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 222 km from the sea.

Why Valdepeñas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 222 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
