Zafra at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Zafra is 321st warmest (15.8 °C year average), 105th sunniest (about 2,770 hours of sunshine a year) and 331st wettest (516 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Almendralejo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Zafra is 1.2 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 23 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.1 °C), averages 17.6 °C more than its coolest, January (7.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 154 km from the sea.

Why Zafra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 154 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-455-1735), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
