Totana at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Totana is 222nd warmest (17.4 °C year average), 133rd sunniest (about 2,751 hours of sunshine a year) and 508th wettest (298 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Alhama de Murcia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Totana is 0.3 °C cooler in August and about as warm in January, with 18 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.1 °C), averages 16.6 °C more than its coolest, January (9.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 38 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Totana, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Totana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Totana itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.769°N, 1.502°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-462-1784), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
