Atamaría sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Murcia · Spain

The sea off Atamaría averages 25.5 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.1 °C (57 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Atamaría sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Atamaría?
In August, at about 25.5 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Atamaría?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 14.1 °C (57 °F).
How warm is the sea in Atamaría in February?
About 14.1 °C (57 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Atamaría?
About 19.0 °C (66 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Atamaría nearby?
In August: Torrevieja (26.2 °C, 44 km), Rojales (26.2 °C, 55 km) and Guardamar del Segura (26.2 °C, 56 km).
