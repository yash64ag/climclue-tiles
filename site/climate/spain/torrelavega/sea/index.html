Torrelavega sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Cantabria · Spain

The sea off Torrelavega averages 20.8 °C (69 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.2 °C (54 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August.

Torrelavega sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Torrelavega?
In August, at about 20.8 °C (69 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Torrelavega?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 12.2 °C (54 °F).
How warm is the sea in Torrelavega in February?
About 12.2 °C (54 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Torrelavega?
About 15.7 °C (60 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
