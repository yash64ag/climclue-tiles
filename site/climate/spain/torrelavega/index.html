Torrelavega at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Torrelavega is 420th warmest (14.7 °C year average), 506th sunniest (about 1,798 hours of sunshine a year) and 44th wettest (1,302 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Camargo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Torrelavega is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 159 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (20.4 °C), averages 10.9 °C more than its coolest, January (9.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.2 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 20 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Torrelavega has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.9 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
