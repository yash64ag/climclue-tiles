Parla at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Parla is 38th warmest (15.1 °C year average), 26th sunniest (about 2,620 hours of sunshine a year) and 50th wettest (435 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Getafe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Parla is 0.3 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.4 °C), averages 21.1 °C more than its coolest, January (5.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 312 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 8 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 112% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Parla has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 312 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
