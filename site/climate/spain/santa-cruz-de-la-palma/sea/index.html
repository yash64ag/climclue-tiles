Santa Cruz de la Palma sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Canary Islands · Spain

The sea off Santa Cruz de la Palma averages 22.1 °C (72 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 17.4 °C (63 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to November.

Santa Cruz de la Palma sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Santa Cruz de la Palma?
In September, at about 22.1 °C (72 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Santa Cruz de la Palma?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to November. The coolest month is February, at 17.4 °C (63 °F).
How warm is the sea in Santa Cruz de la Palma in February?
About 17.4 °C (63 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Santa Cruz de la Palma?
About 19.5 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Santa Cruz de la Palma nearby?
In September: Los Llanos de Aridane (22.7 °C, 15 km).
