Cangas do Morrazo at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Cangas do Morrazo is 433rd warmest (14.6 °C year average), 464th sunniest (about 2,197 hours of sunshine a year) and 10th wettest (1,915 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Moaña, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Cangas do Morrazo is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 66 mm less precipitation a year. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Cangas do Morrazo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
