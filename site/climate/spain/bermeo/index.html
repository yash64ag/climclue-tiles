Bermeo weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Bermeo, Spain (Basque Country), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Bermeo is warmest in August, with 24.8 °C (77 °F) days and 15.4 °C nights, and coolest in February, with 14.1 °C (57 °F) days and 6.2 °C nights. Most rain falls around November (168 mm); July is driest (54 mm).

Bermeo at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Bermeo is the 3rd least sunny (about 1,544 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Gernika-Lumo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Bermeo is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.4 °C warmer in February, with 17 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (20.1 °C), averages 9.9 °C more than its coolest, February (10.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.2 °C (43 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Bermeo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bermeo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (43.421°N, 2.722°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-405-1772), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
