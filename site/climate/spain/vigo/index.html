Vigo at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Vigo is the 2nd wettest (1,866 mm a year). Compared with Ourense, the nearest covered town with different numbers (72 km away), Vigo is 1.1 °C cooler in August and 2.6 °C warmer in January, with 936 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (19.1 °C), averages 9.2 °C more than its coolest, January (9.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.3 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 8 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 103% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Vigo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
