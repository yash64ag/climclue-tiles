Azuqueca de Henares at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Azuqueca de Henares is 469th warmest (14.2 °C year average), 383rd sunniest (about 2,461 hours of sunshine a year) and 411th wettest (438 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Guadalajara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Azuqueca de Henares is 0.9 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 27 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.8 °C), averages 19.5 °C more than its coolest, January (5.3 °C). Why Azuqueca de Henares has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 282 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Azuqueca de Henares itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.566°N, 3.268°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-434-1767), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
