Why Donostia / San Sebastián has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Donostia / San Sebastián climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Donostia / San Sebastián?
August, with an average of 20.1 °C (68 °F), day highs around 22.5 °C (73 °F) and night lows around 17.6 °C (64 °F).
What is the coldest month in Donostia / San Sebastián?
January, with an average of 9.2 °C (48 °F), day highs around 11.2 °C (52 °F) and night lows around 6.8 °C (44 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Donostia / San Sebastián?

What is the driest month in Donostia / San Sebastián?

What is the sunniest month in Donostia / San Sebastián?

Does it snow in Donostia / San Sebastián?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 2.3 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Donostia / San Sebastián?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.1 × 11 km, that contains Donostia / San Sebastián’s GeoNames location (43.313°N, 1.975°W; ClimaClue area sq-406-1780), averaged over the area’s land. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 185,357 (GeoNames).
