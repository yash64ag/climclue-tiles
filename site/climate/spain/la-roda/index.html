La Roda at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, La Roda is 392nd warmest (14.9 °C year average), 234th sunniest (about 2,644 hours of sunshine a year) and 430th wettest (417 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Albacete, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), La Roda is about as warm in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 42 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.8 °C), averages 20.0 °C more than its coolest, January (5.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 164 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of La Roda, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why La Roda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 164 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for La Roda itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (39.207°N, 2.157°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-447-1778), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
