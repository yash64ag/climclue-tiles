Marratxí at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Marratxí is 162nd warmest (18.0 °C year average), 257th sunniest (about 2,621 hours of sunshine a year) and 298th wettest (543 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Palma, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Marratxí is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 31 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.0 °C), averages 14.6 °C more than its coolest, January (11.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.0 °C (45 °F), in February. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Marratxí has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Marratxí itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (39.621°N, 2.725°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-443-1827), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
