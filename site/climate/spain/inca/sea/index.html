Inca sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Balearic Islands · Spain

The sea off Inca averages 25.9 °C (79 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.6 °C (56 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Inca sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Inca?
In August, at about 25.9 °C (79 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Inca?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 13.6 °C (56 °F).
How warm is the sea in Inca in February?
About 13.6 °C (56 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Inca?
About 19.0 °C (66 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
