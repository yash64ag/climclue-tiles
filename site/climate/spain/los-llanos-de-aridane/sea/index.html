Los Llanos de Aridane sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Canary Islands · Spain

The sea off Los Llanos de Aridane averages 22.7 °C (73 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 18.0 °C (64 °F) in March. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to November.

Los Llanos de Aridane sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Los Llanos de Aridane?
In September, at about 22.7 °C (73 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Los Llanos de Aridane?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to November. The coolest month is March, at 18.0 °C (64 °F).
How warm is the sea in Los Llanos de Aridane in March?
About 18.0 °C (64 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Los Llanos de Aridane?
About 20.2 °C (68 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
