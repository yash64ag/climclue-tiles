San Vicent del Raspeig sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Valencia · Spain

The sea off San Vicent del Raspeig averages 25.5 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.4 °C (56 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

San Vicent del Raspeig sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in San Vicent del Raspeig?
In August, at about 25.5 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in San Vicent del Raspeig?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 13.4 °C (56 °F).
How warm is the sea in San Vicent del Raspeig in February?
About 13.4 °C (56 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in San Vicent del Raspeig?
About 18.6 °C (66 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in San Vicent del Raspeig nearby?
In August: Villajoyosa (26.1 °C, 28 km), Guardamar del Segura (26.2 °C, 36 km) and Benidorm (26.1 °C, 38 km).
