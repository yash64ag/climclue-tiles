San Vicent del Raspeig at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, San Vicent del Raspeig is 132nd warmest (18.1 °C year average), 121st sunniest (about 2,760 hours of sunshine a year) and 489th wettest (317 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Alicante, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), San Vicent del Raspeig is about as warm in August and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 9 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.1 °C), averages 15.0 °C more than its coolest, January (11.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.0 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Why San Vicent del Raspeig has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
