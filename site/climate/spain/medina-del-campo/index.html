Medina del Campo at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Medina del Campo is 501st warmest (13.4 °C year average), 346th sunniest (about 2,550 hours of sunshine a year) and 400th wettest (449 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Laguna de Duero, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Medina del Campo is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.3 °C), averages 18.4 °C more than its coolest, January (4.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 236 km from the sea.

Why Medina del Campo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 236 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Medina del Campo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.312°N, 4.914°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-426-1750), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
