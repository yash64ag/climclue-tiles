Mejorada del Campo at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Mejorada del Campo is 358th warmest (15.3 °C year average), 328th sunniest (about 2,572 hours of sunshine a year) and 442nd wettest (403 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Fernando de Henares, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Mejorada del Campo is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.6 °C), averages 21.0 °C more than its coolest, January (5.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 291 km from the sea.

Why Mejorada del Campo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 291 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
