Mutxamel sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Valencia · Spain

The sea off Mutxamel averages 25.5 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.4 °C (56 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Mutxamel sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Mutxamel?
In August, at about 25.5 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Mutxamel?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 13.4 °C (56 °F).
How warm is the sea in Mutxamel in February?
About 13.4 °C (56 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Mutxamel?
About 18.6 °C (66 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Mutxamel nearby?
In August: Villajoyosa (26.1 °C, 21 km), Benidorm (26.1 °C, 31 km) and la Nucia (26.1 °C, 35 km).
