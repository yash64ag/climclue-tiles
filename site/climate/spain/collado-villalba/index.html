Collado-Villalba at a glance

Among the 544 towns ClimaClue covers in Spain, Collado-Villalba is 503rd warmest (13.5 °C year average), 377th sunniest (about 2,482 hours of sunshine a year) and 119th wettest (715 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Galapagar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Collado-Villalba is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 8 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.2 °C), averages 19.6 °C more than its coolest, January (4.6 °C). Why Collado-Villalba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 312 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-433-1759), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
