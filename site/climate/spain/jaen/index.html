Jaén at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Jaén is the 5th sunniest (about 2,830 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Granada, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Jaén is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.4 °C), averages 19.0 °C more than its coolest, January (7.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 122 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 103% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Jaén has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 122 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
