Torrejón de Ardoz at a glance

Among the 66 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Spain, Torrejón de Ardoz is 34th warmest (15.2 °C year average), 38th sunniest (about 2,566 hours of sunshine a year) and 55th wettest (415 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Alcalá de Henares, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Torrejón de Ardoz is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 39 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.4 °C), averages 20.9 °C more than its coolest, January (5.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 295 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 105% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Torrejón de Ardoz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 295 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
