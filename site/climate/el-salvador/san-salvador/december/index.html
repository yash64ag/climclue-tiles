How December compares in San Salvador

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in San Salvador. Compared with November: 0.1 °C cooler, 35 mm less precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

San Salvador in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Salvador in December?
In December, San Salvador averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 18.5 °C (65 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit San Salvador?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of San Salvador’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Salvador in December?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (420 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in San Salvador in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Salvador get in December?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Salvador in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
