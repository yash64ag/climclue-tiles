San Salvador weather in April

Average April weather in San Salvador, El Salvador: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, San Salvador averages 32.7 °C (91 °F) days and 17.8 °C (64 °F) nights, with 43 mm (1.7 in) of rain over the month and about 251 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in San Salvador

April is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in San Salvador. Compared with March: 0.6 °C warmer, 31 mm more precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. May is 0.4 °C cooler than April on average.

San Salvador in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Salvador in April?
In April, San Salvador averages a day high of 32.7 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 17.8 °C (64 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit San Salvador?
April is not one of San Salvador’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Salvador in April?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (391 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in San Salvador in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Salvador get in April?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Salvador in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
