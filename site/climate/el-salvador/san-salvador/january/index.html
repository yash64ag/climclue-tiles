How January compares in San Salvador

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in San Salvador. Compared with December: 0.1 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. February is 0.6 °C warmer than January on average.

San Salvador in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in San Salvador in January?
In January, San Salvador averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 262 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit San Salvador?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of San Salvador’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in San Salvador in January?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (420 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in San Salvador in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does San Salvador get in January?
About 262 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for San Salvador in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
