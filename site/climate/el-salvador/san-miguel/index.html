San Miguel at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in El Salvador, San Miguel is the warmest (28.1 °C year average) and the sunniest (about 2,898 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Soyapango, the nearest covered town with different numbers (107 km away), San Miguel is 4.0 °C warmer in April and 2.6 °C warmer in October, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.1 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, October (26.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.5 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of San Miguel, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why San Miguel has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
