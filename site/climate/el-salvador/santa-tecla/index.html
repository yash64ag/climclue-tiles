Why Santa Tecla has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Santa Tecla climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Santa Tecla?
April, with an average of 23.6 °C (75 °F), day highs around 28.5 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 19.7 °C (67 °F).
What is the coldest month in Santa Tecla?
January, with an average of 21.9 °C (72 °F), day highs around 27.7 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 17.4 °C (63 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Santa Tecla?
September, with 454 mm (17.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,272 mm (89.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Santa Tecla?
January, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Santa Tecla?
March, with about 273 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Santa Tecla?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Santa Tecla?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, April, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Santa Tecla’s GeoNames location (13.677°N, 89.283°W; ClimaClue area sq-703-907), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 124,694 (GeoNames).
