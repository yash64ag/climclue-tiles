Cojutepeque at a glance

Among the 35 towns ClimaClue covers in El Salvador, Cojutepeque is the least sunny (about 2,604 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with San Martín, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Cojutepeque is 1.6 °C cooler in April and 1.4 °C cooler in January, with 192 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (24.3 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, January (22.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.9 °C (59 °F), in January. The area lies about 49 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cojutepeque, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cojutepeque has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 49 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C. The 10 km area around Cojutepeque, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 24.2 °C a year, 1.0 °C warmer than the town itself, because it takes in lower ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Cojutepeque itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (13.720°N, 88.933°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-702-910), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
