Antiguo Cuscatlán at a glance

Among the 35 towns ClimaClue covers in El Salvador, Antiguo Cuscatlán is the 2nd coolest (22.4 °C year average) and the 5th wettest (2,265 mm a year). Compared with Santa Tecla, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Antiguo Cuscatlán is about as warm in April and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 255 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (23.4 °C), averages 1.7 °C more than its coolest, January (21.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.8 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Antiguo Cuscatlán, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Antiguo Cuscatlán has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
