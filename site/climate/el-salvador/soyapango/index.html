Soyapango at a glance

Among the 7 towns of 100,000+ people covered in El Salvador, Soyapango is the 3rd warmest (24.8 °C year average), the 3rd least sunny (about 2,718 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (1,499 mm a year). Compared with San Salvador, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Soyapango is 1.6 °C warmer in April and 1.3 °C warmer in January, with 610 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (26.1 °C), averages 2.1 °C more than its coolest, January (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.6 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Soyapango, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Soyapango has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
