Ma On Shan at a glance

Among the 136 towns ClimaClue covers in Hong Kong, Ma On Shan is the coolest (22.2 °C year average). Compared with Tai Shui Hang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Ma On Shan is 0.9 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 51 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 12.6 °C more than its coolest, January (15.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.9 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ma On Shan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ma On Shan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ma On Shan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.406°N, 114.241°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-615-2942), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
