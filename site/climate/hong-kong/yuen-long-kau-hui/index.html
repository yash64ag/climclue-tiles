Yuen Long Kau Hui at a glance

Among the 136 towns ClimaClue covers in Hong Kong, Yuen Long Kau Hui is 10th warmest (23.1 °C year average), 120th sunniest (about 1,955 hours of sunshine a year) and 70th wettest (2,201 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yeung Uk Tsuen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Yuen Long Kau Hui is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.8 °C), averages 13.0 °C more than its coolest, January (15.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.4 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 23 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Yuen Long Kau Hui has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Yuen Long Kau Hui itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.448°N, 114.032°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-615-2940), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
