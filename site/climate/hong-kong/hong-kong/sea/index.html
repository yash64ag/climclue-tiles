Hong Kong sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Hong Kong

The sea off Hong Kong averages 28.7 °C (84 °F) in July, its warmest month, and 19.3 °C (67 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

Hong Kong sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Hong Kong?
In July, at about 28.7 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Hong Kong?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is January, at 19.3 °C (67 °F).
How warm is the sea in Hong Kong in January?
About 19.3 °C (67 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Hong Kong?
About 24.4 °C (76 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
