How July compares in Hong Kong

July is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Hong Kong. Compared with June: 0.8 °C warmer, 75 mm less precipitation and 63 hours more sunshine. August is 0.2 °C cooler than July on average.

Hong Kong in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hong Kong in July?
In July, Hong Kong averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 25.7 °C (78 °F), with 389 mm (15.3 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Hong Kong?
July is not one of Hong Kong’s mild months; those are January, February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hong Kong in July?
389 mm (15.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (464 mm), the driest January (23 mm).
Does it snow in Hong Kong in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hong Kong get in July?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hong Kong in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
