Best time to visit Hong Kong

For mild weather, the best months to visit Hong Kong are January, February, March, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, March, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (28.3 °C), August (28.2 °C) and June (27.5 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): January (23 mm), December (26 mm) and November (41 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (227 h), August (205 h) and October (197 h).

Why Hong Kong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hong Kong itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.278°N, 114.175°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-617-2941), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
