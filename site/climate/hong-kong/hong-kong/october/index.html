How October compares in Hong Kong

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Hong Kong. Compared with September: 2.2 °C cooler, 239 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. November is 3.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Hong Kong in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hong Kong in October?
In October, Hong Kong averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Hong Kong?
October is not one of Hong Kong’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hong Kong in October?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (460 mm), the driest January (23 mm).
Does it snow in Hong Kong in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hong Kong get in October?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hong Kong in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
