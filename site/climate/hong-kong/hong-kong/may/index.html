How May compares in Hong Kong

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Hong Kong. Compared with April: 2.9 °C warmer, 160 mm more precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. June is 1.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Hong Kong in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hong Kong in May?
In May, Hong Kong averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 328 mm (12.9 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Hong Kong?
May is not one of Hong Kong’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hong Kong in May?
328 mm (12.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (460 mm), the driest January (23 mm).
Does it snow in Hong Kong in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hong Kong get in May?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Hong Kong in May?
About 25.7 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is July (28.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Hong Kong in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
