Mei Foo Sun Chuen at a glance

Among the 136 towns ClimaClue covers in Hong Kong, Mei Foo Sun Chuen is 32nd warmest (23.1 °C year average), 56th sunniest (about 1,963 hours of sunshine a year) and 120th wettest (2,068 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hoi Lai Estate, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Mei Foo Sun Chuen is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 18 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 12.8 °C more than its coolest, January (15.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.5 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 23 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Mei Foo Sun Chuen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
