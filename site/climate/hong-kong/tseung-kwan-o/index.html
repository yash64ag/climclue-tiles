Tseung Kwan O at a glance

Among the 22 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Hong Kong, Tseung Kwan O is the 5th coolest (22.4 °C year average), the 5th sunniest (about 1,974 hours of sunshine a year) and the 5th wettest (2,369 mm a year). Compared with Kowloon City, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Tseung Kwan O is 0.2 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 24 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 12.4 °C more than its coolest, January (15.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.6 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tseung Kwan O, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tseung Kwan O has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
