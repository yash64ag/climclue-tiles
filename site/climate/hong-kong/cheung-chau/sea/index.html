Cheung Chau sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Islands · Hong Kong

The sea off Cheung Chau averages 28.8 °C (84 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 18.9 °C (66 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

Cheung Chau sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Cheung Chau?
In August, at about 28.8 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Cheung Chau?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is January, at 18.9 °C (66 °F).
How warm is the sea in Cheung Chau in January?
About 18.9 °C (66 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Cheung Chau?
About 24.3 °C (76 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
