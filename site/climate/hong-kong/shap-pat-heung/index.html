Shap Pat Heung at a glance

Among the 136 towns ClimaClue covers in Hong Kong, Shap Pat Heung is 37th warmest (23.1 °C year average), 120th sunniest (about 1,955 hours of sunshine a year) and 51st wettest (2,261 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yuen Long San Hui, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Shap Pat Heung is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 27 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.7 °C), averages 13.0 °C more than its coolest, January (15.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.3 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 23 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Shap Pat Heung has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
