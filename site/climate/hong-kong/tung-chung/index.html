Tung Chung at a glance

Among the 22 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Hong Kong, Tung Chung is the 4th wettest (2,401 mm a year). Compared with San Tung Chung Hang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Tung Chung is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 128 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.3 °C), averages 12.4 °C more than its coolest, January (15.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.8 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Tung Chung has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.4 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
