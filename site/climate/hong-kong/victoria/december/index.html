How December compares in Victoria

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Victoria. Compared with November: 3.8 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. January is 1.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Victoria in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Victoria in December?
In December, Victoria averages a day high of 19.2 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Victoria?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Victoria’s mild months (January, February, March, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Victoria in December?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (464 mm), the driest January (23 mm).
Does it snow in Victoria in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Victoria get in December?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Victoria in December?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
