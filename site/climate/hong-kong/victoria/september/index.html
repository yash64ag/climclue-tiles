Victoria weather in September

Average September weather in Victoria, Hong Kong: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Victoria averages 29.6 °C (85 °F) days and 24.6 °C (76 °F) nights, with 279 mm (11.0 in) of rain over the month and about 185 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Victoria

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Victoria. Compared with August: 0.9 °C cooler, 108 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. October is 2.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Victoria in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Victoria in September?
In September, Victoria averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 279 mm (11.0 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Victoria?
September is not one of Victoria’s mild months; those are January, February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Victoria in September?
279 mm (11.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (433 mm), the driest January (21 mm).
Does it snow in Victoria in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Victoria get in September?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Victoria in September?
About 28.2 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is July (28.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Victoria in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
