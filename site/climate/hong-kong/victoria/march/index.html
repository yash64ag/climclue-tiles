How March compares in Victoria

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Victoria. Compared with February: 2.3 °C warmer, 24 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. April is 3.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Victoria in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Victoria in March?
In March, Victoria averages a day high of 21.2 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 17.1 °C (63 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 108 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Victoria?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Victoria’s mild months (January, February, March, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Victoria in March?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (464 mm), the driest January (23 mm).
Does it snow in Victoria in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Victoria get in March?
About 108 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Victoria in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
