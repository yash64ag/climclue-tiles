Victoria weather in April

Average April weather in Victoria, Hong Kong: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Victoria averages 25.1 °C (77 °F) days and 20.2 °C (68 °F) nights, with 157 mm (6.2 in) of rain over the month and about 117 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Victoria

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Victoria. Compared with March: 3.3 °C warmer, 85 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. May is 2.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Victoria in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Victoria in April?
In April, Victoria averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 117 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Victoria?
April is not one of Victoria’s mild months; those are January, February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Victoria in April?
157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (433 mm), the driest January (21 mm).
Does it snow in Victoria in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Victoria get in April?
About 117 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Victoria in April?
About 22.8 °C (73 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is July (28.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Victoria in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
