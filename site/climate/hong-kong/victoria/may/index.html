How May compares in Victoria

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Victoria. Compared with April: 2.8 °C warmer, 161 mm more precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. June is 1.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Victoria in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Victoria in May?
In May, Victoria averages a day high of 26.5 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 330 mm (13.0 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Victoria?
May is not one of Victoria’s mild months; those are January, February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Victoria in May?
330 mm (13.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (464 mm), the driest January (23 mm).
Does it snow in Victoria in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Victoria get in May?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Victoria in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
