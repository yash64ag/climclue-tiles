Tuen Mun in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tuen Mun in April?
In April, Tuen Mun averages a day high of 24.7 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 178 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation and about 111 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Tuen Mun?
April is not one of Tuen Mun’s mild months; those are January, February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tuen Mun in April?
178 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (443 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Tuen Mun in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tuen Mun get in April?
About 111 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tuen Mun in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
