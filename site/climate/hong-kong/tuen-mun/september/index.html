How September compares in Tuen Mun

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Tuen Mun. Compared with August: 1.0 °C cooler, 113 mm less precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. October is 2.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Tuen Mun in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tuen Mun in September?
In September, Tuen Mun averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 298 mm (11.7 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Tuen Mun?
September is not one of Tuen Mun’s mild months; those are January, February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tuen Mun in September?
298 mm (11.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (443 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Tuen Mun in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tuen Mun get in September?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tuen Mun in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
