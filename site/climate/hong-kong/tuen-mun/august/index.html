Tuen Mun in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tuen Mun in August?
In August, Tuen Mun averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 25.4 °C (78 °F), with 411 mm (16.2 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Tuen Mun?
August is not one of Tuen Mun’s mild months; those are January, February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tuen Mun in August?
411 mm (16.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (443 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Tuen Mun in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tuen Mun get in August?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tuen Mun in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
