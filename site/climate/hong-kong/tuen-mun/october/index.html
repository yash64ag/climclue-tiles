How October compares in Tuen Mun

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Tuen Mun. Compared with September: 2.1 °C cooler, 228 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. November is 3.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Tuen Mun in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tuen Mun in October?
In October, Tuen Mun averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Tuen Mun?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Tuen Mun’s mild months (January, February, March, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tuen Mun in October?
70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (443 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Tuen Mun in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tuen Mun get in October?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tuen Mun in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
