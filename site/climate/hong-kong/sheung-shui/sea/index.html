Sheung Shui sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · North District · Hong Kong

The sea off Sheung Shui averages 28.7 °C (84 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 17.5 °C (64 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

Sheung Shui sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Sheung Shui?
In August, at about 28.7 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Sheung Shui?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is January, at 17.5 °C (64 °F).
How warm is the sea in Sheung Shui in January?
About 17.5 °C (64 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Sheung Shui?
About 23.7 °C (75 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
