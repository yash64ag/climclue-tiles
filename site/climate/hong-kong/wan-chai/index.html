Wan Chai at a glance

Among the 22 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Hong Kong, Wan Chai is the sunniest (about 1,994 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Sham Shui Po, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Wan Chai is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.8 °C), averages 12.0 °C more than its coolest, January (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.0 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Wan Chai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Wan Chai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
