Sau Mau Ping at a glance

Among the 136 towns ClimaClue covers in Hong Kong, Sau Mau Ping is 130th warmest (22.5 °C year average), 34th sunniest (about 1,974 hours of sunshine a year) and 53rd wettest (2,248 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lam Tin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Sau Mau Ping is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 37 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 12.2 °C more than its coolest, January (15.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.0 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Sau Mau Ping has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
