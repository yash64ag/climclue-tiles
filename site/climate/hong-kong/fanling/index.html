Fanling at a glance

Among the 22 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Hong Kong, Fanling is the 3rd coolest (22.3 °C year average) and the 3rd wettest (2,398 mm a year). Compared with Yuen Long Kau Hui, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Fanling is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 161 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 12.8 °C more than its coolest, January (15.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.7 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 23 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Fanling, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Fanling has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
