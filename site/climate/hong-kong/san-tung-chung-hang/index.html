San Tung Chung Hang at a glance

Among the 136 towns ClimaClue covers in Hong Kong, San Tung Chung Hang is the wettest (2,529 mm a year). Compared with Tung Chung, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), San Tung Chung Hang is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 128 mm more precipitation a year. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why San Tung Chung Hang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for San Tung Chung Hang itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.279°N, 113.943°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-617-2939), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
