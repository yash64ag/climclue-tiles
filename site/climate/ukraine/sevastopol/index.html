Sevastopol at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Sevastopol is the warmest (12.5 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 2,201 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th wettest (689 mm a year). Compared with Simferopol, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Sevastopol is 1.8 °C warmer in August and 3.2 °C warmer in February, with 75 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.3 °C), averages 20.2 °C more than its coolest, February (3.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 0.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sevastopol, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Sevastopol

For mild weather, the best months to visit Sevastopol are May, June, July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May, June, July, August and September.

Why Sevastopol has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
