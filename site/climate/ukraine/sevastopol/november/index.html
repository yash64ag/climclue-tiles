How November compares in Sevastopol

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Sevastopol. Compared with October: 5.0 °C cooler, 14 mm more precipitation and 79 hours less sunshine. December is 3.8 °C cooler than November on average.

Sevastopol in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sevastopol in November?
In November, Sevastopol averages a day high of 10.9 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 6.3 °C (43 °F), with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 94 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Sevastopol?
November is not one of Sevastopol’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sevastopol in November?
81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is November (81 mm), the driest May (33 mm).
Does it snow in Sevastopol in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sevastopol get in November?
About 94 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sevastopol in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
