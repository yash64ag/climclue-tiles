How April compares in Sevastopol

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Sevastopol. Compared with March: 4.5 °C warmer, 13 mm less precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. May is 4.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Sevastopol in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sevastopol in April?
In April, Sevastopol averages a day high of 14.8 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 5.6 °C (42 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Sevastopol?
April is not one of Sevastopol’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sevastopol in April?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (82 mm), the driest May (32 mm).
Does it snow in Sevastopol in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sevastopol get in April?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Sevastopol in April?
About 9.5 °C (49 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (24.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Sevastopol in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
