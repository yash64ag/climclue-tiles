How October compares in Sevastopol

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Sevastopol. Compared with September: 5.1 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 59 hours less sunshine. November is 5.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Sevastopol in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sevastopol in October?
In October, Sevastopol averages a day high of 16.3 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Sevastopol?
October is not one of Sevastopol’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sevastopol in October?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (81 mm), the driest May (33 mm).
Does it snow in Sevastopol in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sevastopol get in October?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sevastopol in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
