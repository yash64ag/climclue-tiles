How July compares in Sevastopol

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Sevastopol. Compared with June: 3.2 °C warmer, 15 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C warmer than July on average.

Sevastopol in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sevastopol in July?
In July, Sevastopol averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 309 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Sevastopol?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Sevastopol’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sevastopol in July?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (82 mm), the driest May (32 mm).
Does it snow in Sevastopol in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sevastopol get in July?
About 309 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sevastopol in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
