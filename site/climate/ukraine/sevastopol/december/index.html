How December compares in Sevastopol

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Sevastopol. Compared with November: 3.8 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. January is 1.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Sevastopol in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sevastopol in December?
In December, Sevastopol averages a day high of 6.8 °C (44 °F) and a night low of 2.7 °C (37 °F), with 80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 65 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Sevastopol?
December is not one of Sevastopol’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sevastopol in December?
80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (81 mm), the driest May (33 mm).
Does it snow in Sevastopol in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.1 cm (3.2 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Sevastopol get in December?
About 65 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sevastopol in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
