Pyatykhatky at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Pyatykhatky is 95th warmest (8.8 °C year average), 78th sunniest (about 1,995 hours of sunshine a year) and 184th wettest (553 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zhovti Vody, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Pyatykhatky is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.4 °C), averages 25.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 264 km from the sea.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pyatykhatky itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.408°N, 33.709°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-355-2137), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
