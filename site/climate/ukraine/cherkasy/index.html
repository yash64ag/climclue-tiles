Cherkasy at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Cherkasy is 20th warmest (8.9 °C year average), 28th sunniest (about 1,799 hours of sunshine a year) and 21st wettest (585 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kropyvnytskyi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (105 km away), Cherkasy is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 72 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.4 °C), averages 24.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 326 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cherkasy, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cherkasy has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 326 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
