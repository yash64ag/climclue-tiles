Slovyansk at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Slovyansk is 24th warmest (8.7 °C year average), 24th sunniest (about 1,932 hours of sunshine a year) and 28th wettest (534 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kramatorsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Slovyansk is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.9 °C), averages 26.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 207 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Slovyansk, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Slovyansk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 207 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
