Vinnytsya at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Vinnytsya is 33rd warmest (8.2 °C year average), 27th sunniest (about 1,836 hours of sunshine a year) and 10th wettest (629 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Khmelnytskyi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (110 km away), Vinnytsya is 0.6 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 30 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.6 °C), averages 23.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 357 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 10 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 106% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Vinnytsya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 357 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
