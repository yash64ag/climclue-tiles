Debaltseve at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Debaltseve is 250th warmest (7.8 °C year average), 70th sunniest (about 2,005 hours of sunshine a year) and 112th wettest (610 mm a year), near the middle on each. The area lies about 141 km from the sea.

Why Debaltseve has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Debaltseve itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.336°N, 38.403°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-356-2184), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
