Sheptytskyi at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Sheptytskyi is 126th warmest (8.5 °C year average), 256th sunniest (about 1,663 hours of sunshine a year) and 53rd wettest (649 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sokal, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Sheptytskyi is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 21.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 558 km from the sea.

Why Sheptytskyi has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sheptytskyi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (50.394°N, 24.239°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-336-2042), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
