Kostyantynivka weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Kostyantynivka, Ukraine (Donetsk), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Kostyantynivka at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Kostyantynivka is 92nd warmest (8.8 °C year average), 109th sunniest (about 1,959 hours of sunshine a year) and 244th wettest (488 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Druzhkivka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Kostyantynivka is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. The area lies about 171 km from the sea.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kostyantynivka itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.527°N, 37.706°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-354-2177), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
