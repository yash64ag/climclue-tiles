Shakhtarsk weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Shakhtarsk, Ukraine (Donetsk), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Shakhtarsk at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Shakhtarsk is 139th warmest (8.4 °C year average), 58th sunniest (about 2,019 hours of sunshine a year) and 154th wettest (580 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Khrestivka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Shakhtarsk is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 9 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.5 °C), averages 25.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 110 km from the sea.

Why Shakhtarsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 110 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.8 °C.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Shakhtarsk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.057°N, 38.438°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-359-2184), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
