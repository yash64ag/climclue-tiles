How February compares in Kyiv

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Kyiv. Compared with January: 0.4 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. March is 4.6 °C warmer than February on average.

Kyiv in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kyiv in February?
In February, Kyiv averages a day high of -0.2 °C (32 °F) and a night low of -5.9 °C (21 °F), with 37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 70 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Kyiv?
February is not one of Kyiv’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kyiv in February?
37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (85 mm), the driest January (34 mm).
Does it snow in Kyiv in February?
Yes, on average: about 24 cm (9.4 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 5.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kyiv get in February?
About 70 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kyiv in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
