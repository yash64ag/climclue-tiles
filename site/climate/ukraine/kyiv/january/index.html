How January compares in Kyiv

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Kyiv. Compared with December: 1.2 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. February is 0.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Kyiv in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kyiv in January?
In January, Kyiv averages a day high of -1.4 °C (29 °F) and a night low of -6.0 °C (21 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 53 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kyiv?
January is not one of Kyiv’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kyiv in January?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (83 mm), the driest January (33 mm).
Does it snow in Kyiv in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 28 cm (11.0 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 12 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kyiv get in January?
About 53 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kyiv in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
