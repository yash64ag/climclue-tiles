How April compares in Kyiv

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Kyiv. Compared with March: 7.5 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. May is 6.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Kyiv in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kyiv in April?
In April, Kyiv averages a day high of 13.3 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kyiv?
April is not one of Kyiv’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kyiv in April?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (85 mm), the driest January (34 mm).
Does it snow in Kyiv in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kyiv get in April?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kyiv in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
