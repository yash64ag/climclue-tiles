How December compares in Kyiv

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Kyiv. Compared with November: 4.1 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. January is 1.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Kyiv in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kyiv in December?
In December, Kyiv averages a day high of 0.0 °C (32 °F) and a night low of -4.8 °C (23 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 32 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kyiv?
December is not one of Kyiv’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kyiv in December?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (85 mm), the driest January (34 mm).
Does it snow in Kyiv in December?
Yes, on average: about 27 cm (10.6 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kyiv get in December?
About 32 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kyiv in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
