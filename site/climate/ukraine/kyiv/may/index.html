How May compares in Kyiv

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kyiv. Compared with April: 6.3 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 68 hours more sunshine. June is 3.1 °C warmer than May on average.

Kyiv in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kyiv in May?
In May, Kyiv averages a day high of 20.2 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 10.1 °C (50 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kyiv?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Kyiv’s mild months (May, June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kyiv in May?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (85 mm), the driest January (34 mm).
Does it snow in Kyiv in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kyiv get in May?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kyiv in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
