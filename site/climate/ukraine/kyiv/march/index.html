Kyiv weather in March

Average March weather in Kyiv, Ukraine: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Kyiv averages 4.8 °C (41 °F) days and -2.0 °C (28 °F) nights, with 36 mm (1.4 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 118 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Kyiv

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kyiv. Compared with February: 4.7 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 48 hours more sunshine. April is 7.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Kyiv in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kyiv in March?
In March, Kyiv averages a day high of 4.8 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -2.0 °C (28 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 118 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kyiv?
March is not one of Kyiv’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kyiv in March?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (85 mm), the driest January (34 mm).
Does it snow in Kyiv in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 14 cm (5.3 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kyiv get in March?
About 118 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kyiv in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
