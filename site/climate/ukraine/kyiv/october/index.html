Kyiv weather in October

Average October weather in Kyiv, Ukraine: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Kyiv averages 11.8 °C (53 °F) days and 4.0 °C (39 °F) nights, with 43 mm (1.7 in) of rain over the month and about 118 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Kyiv

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kyiv. Compared with September: 5.8 °C cooler, 15 mm less precipitation and 55 hours less sunshine. November is 6.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Kyiv in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kyiv in October?
In October, Kyiv averages a day high of 11.8 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 4.0 °C (39 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 118 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kyiv?
October is not one of Kyiv’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kyiv in October?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (85 mm), the driest January (34 mm).
Does it snow in Kyiv in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kyiv get in October?
About 118 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kyiv in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
