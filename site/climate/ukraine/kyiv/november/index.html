How November compares in Kyiv

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kyiv. Compared with October: 6.3 °C cooler, 6 mm more precipitation and 71 hours less sunshine. December is 4.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Kyiv in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kyiv in November?
In November, Kyiv averages a day high of 4.2 °C (40 °F) and a night low of -0.3 °C (31 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 47 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kyiv?
November is not one of Kyiv’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kyiv in November?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (83 mm), the driest January (33 mm).
Does it snow in Kyiv in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 19 cm (7.3 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kyiv get in November?
About 47 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kyiv in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
