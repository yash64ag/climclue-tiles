Mariupol sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Donetsk · Ukraine

The sea off Mariupol averages 23.2 °C (74 °F) in July, its warmest month, and 3.7 °C (39 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to August.

Mariupol sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Mariupol?
In July, at about 23.2 °C (74 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Mariupol?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to August. The coolest month is February, at 3.7 °C (39 °F).
How warm is the sea in Mariupol in February?
About 3.7 °C (39 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Mariupol?
About 12.5 °C (55 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Mariupol nearby?
In July: Berdyansk (24.4 °C, 68 km), Yeysk (24.1 °C, 72 km) and Primorsko-Akhtarsk (24.6 °C, 126 km).
