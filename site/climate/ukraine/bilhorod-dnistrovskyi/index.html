Bilhorod-Dnistrovskyi at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Bilhorod-Dnistrovskyi is 19th warmest (11.2 °C year average), 19th sunniest (about 2,124 hours of sunshine a year) and 265th wettest (440 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chornomors’k, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Bilhorod-Dnistrovskyi is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 22 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.9 °C), averages 23.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 19 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Bilhorod-Dnistrovskyi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bilhorod-Dnistrovskyi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (46.195°N, 30.348°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-378-2103), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
