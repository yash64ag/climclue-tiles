Dokuchayevsk at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Dokuchayevsk is 78th warmest (8.9 °C year average), 59th sunniest (about 2,017 hours of sunshine a year) and 227th wettest (519 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Volnovakha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Dokuchayevsk is 0.7 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 101 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.1 °C), averages 25.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 89 km from the sea.

Why Dokuchayevsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dokuchayevsk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (47.752°N, 37.678°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-362-2176), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
