Novi Kodaky at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Novi Kodaky is 60th warmest (9.2 °C year average), 90th sunniest (about 1,984 hours of sunshine a year) and 205th wettest (538 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dnipro, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Novi Kodaky is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.1 °C), averages 25.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 222 km from the sea.

Why Novi Kodaky has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Novi Kodaky itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.487°N, 34.956°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-355-2149), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
