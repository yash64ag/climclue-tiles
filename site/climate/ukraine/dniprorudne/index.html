Dniprorudne at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Dniprorudne is 38th warmest (10.1 °C year average), 32nd sunniest (about 2,061 hours of sunshine a year) and 241st wettest (494 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Enerhodar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Dniprorudne is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 22 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.8 °C), averages 25.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 108 km from the sea.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dniprorudne itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (47.390°N, 35.001°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-366-2150), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
