Why Kerch has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Kerch climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Kerch?
July, with an average of 24.1 °C (75 °F), day highs around 26.3 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 21.6 °C (71 °F).
What is the coldest month in Kerch?
February, with an average of 1.6 °C (35 °F), day highs around 3.6 °C (38 °F) and night lows around -0.3 °C (31 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Kerch?
December, with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 464 mm (18.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Kerch?
October, with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Kerch?
July, with about 327 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Kerch?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 47 cm (18.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 2 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Kerch?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Kerch have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.8 × 11 km, that contains Kerch’s GeoNames location (45.357°N, 36.475°E; ClimaClue area sq-386-2164), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 148,932 (GeoNames).
