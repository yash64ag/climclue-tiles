Skadovsk sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Kherson · Ukraine

The sea off Skadovsk averages 23.3 °C (74 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 4.0 °C (39 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to September.

Skadovsk sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Skadovsk?
In August, at about 23.3 °C (74 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Skadovsk?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to September. The coolest month is February, at 4.0 °C (39 °F).
How warm is the sea in Skadovsk in February?
About 4.0 °C (39 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Skadovsk?
About 13.2 °C (56 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Skadovsk nearby?
In August: Yevpatoriya (24.1 °C, 107 km), Saky (24.1 °C, 121 km) and Sevastopol (24.2 °C, 174 km).
