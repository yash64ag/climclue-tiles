Lviv at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Lviv is the 2nd wettest (744 mm a year). Compared with Ivano-Frankivsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (113 km away), Lviv is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 78 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.6 °C), averages 21.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 600 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 103% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Best time to visit Lviv

For mild weather, the best months to visit Lviv are June, July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July and August.

Why Lviv has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 600 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
