How April compares in Lviv

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Lviv. Compared with March: 6.3 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 67 hours more sunshine. May is 5.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Lviv in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lviv in April?
In April, Lviv averages a day high of 12.8 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 3.3 °C (38 °F), with 50 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Lviv?
April is not one of Lviv’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lviv in April?
50 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (99 mm), the driest January (38 mm).
Does it snow in Lviv in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.3 cm (2.9 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Lviv get in April?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lviv in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
