How October compares in Lviv

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Lviv. Compared with September: 4.9 °C cooler, 15 mm less precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. November is 5.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Lviv in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lviv in October?
In October, Lviv averages a day high of 13.0 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 137 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Lviv?
October is not one of Lviv’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lviv in October?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (100 mm), the driest January (38 mm).
Does it snow in Lviv in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lviv get in October?
About 137 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lviv in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
