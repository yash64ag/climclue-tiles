How May compares in Lviv

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Lviv. Compared with April: 5.3 °C warmer, 39 mm more precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. June is 2.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Lviv in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lviv in May?
In May, Lviv averages a day high of 18.2 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 8.9 °C (48 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Lviv?
May is not one of Lviv’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lviv in May?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (99 mm), the driest January (38 mm).
Does it snow in Lviv in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lviv get in May?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lviv in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
