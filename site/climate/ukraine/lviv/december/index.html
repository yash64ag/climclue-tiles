Lviv weather in December

Average December weather in Lviv, Ukraine: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Lviv averages 1.2 °C (34 °F) days and -4.2 °C (24 °F) nights, with 47 mm (1.8 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 36 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Lviv

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Lviv. Compared with November: 4.3 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. January is 1.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Lviv in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lviv in December?
In December, Lviv averages a day high of 1.2 °C (34 °F) and a night low of -4.2 °C (24 °F), with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 36 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Lviv?
December is not one of Lviv’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lviv in December?
47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (100 mm), the driest January (38 mm).
Does it snow in Lviv in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 23 cm (8.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Lviv get in December?
About 36 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lviv in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
