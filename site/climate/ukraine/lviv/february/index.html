How February compares in Lviv

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Lviv. Compared with January: 0.9 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. March is 4.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Lviv in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lviv in February?
In February, Lviv averages a day high of 0.8 °C (33 °F) and a night low of -5.3 °C (22 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 75 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Lviv?
February is not one of Lviv’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lviv in February?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (99 mm), the driest January (38 mm).
Does it snow in Lviv in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 31 cm (12.4 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 12 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Lviv get in February?
About 75 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lviv in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
