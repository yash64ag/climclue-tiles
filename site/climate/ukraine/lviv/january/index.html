How January compares in Lviv

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Lviv. Compared with December: 1.3 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. February is 0.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Lviv in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lviv in January?
In January, Lviv averages a day high of 0.4 °C (33 °F) and a night low of -6.0 °C (21 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 61 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Lviv?
January is not one of Lviv’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lviv in January?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (100 mm), the driest January (38 mm).
Does it snow in Lviv in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 23 cm (9.1 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Lviv get in January?
About 61 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lviv in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
