Khartsyzk at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Khartsyzk is 141st warmest (8.3 °C year average), 68th sunniest (about 2,007 hours of sunshine a year) and 128th wettest (601 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Khanzhonkivskyi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Khartsyzk is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 11 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.3 °C), averages 25.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 111 km from the sea.

Why Khartsyzk has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Khartsyzk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.043°N, 38.140°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-359-2181), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
