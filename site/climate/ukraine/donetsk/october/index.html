How October compares in Donetsk

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Donetsk. Compared with September: 6.5 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 65 hours less sunshine. November is 6.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Donetsk in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Donetsk in October?
In October, Donetsk averages a day high of 12.6 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 5.0 °C (41 °F), with 37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Donetsk?
October is not one of Donetsk’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Donetsk in October?
37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (71 mm), the driest August (34 mm).
Does it snow in Donetsk in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.2 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Donetsk get in October?
About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Donetsk in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
