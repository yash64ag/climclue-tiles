How July compares in Donetsk

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Donetsk. Compared with June: 2.3 °C warmer, 17 mm less precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. August is 0.8 °C cooler than July on average.

Donetsk in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Donetsk in July?
In July, Donetsk averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.4 °C (62 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 294 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Donetsk?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Donetsk’s mild months (May, June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Donetsk in July?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (71 mm), the driest August (35 mm).
Does it snow in Donetsk in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Donetsk get in July?
About 294 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Donetsk in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
