How November compares in Donetsk

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Donetsk. Compared with October: 6.8 °C cooler, 9 mm more precipitation and 84 hours less sunshine. December is 4.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Donetsk in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Donetsk in November?
In November, Donetsk averages a day high of 4.7 °C (40 °F) and a night low of -0.8 °C (31 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 59 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Donetsk?
November is not one of Donetsk’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Donetsk in November?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (71 mm), the driest August (34 mm).
Does it snow in Donetsk in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 11 cm (4.4 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Donetsk get in November?
About 59 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Donetsk in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
