How December compares in Donetsk

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Donetsk. Compared with November: 4.4 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. January is 1.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Donetsk in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Donetsk in December?
In December, Donetsk averages a day high of 0.0 °C (32 °F) and a night low of -5.1 °C (23 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 39 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Donetsk?
December is not one of Donetsk’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Donetsk in December?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (71 mm), the driest August (35 mm).
Does it snow in Donetsk in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 23 cm (9.2 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Donetsk get in December?
About 39 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Donetsk in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
