How August compares in Donetsk

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Donetsk. Compared with July: 0.8 °C cooler, 20 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. September is 5.8 °C cooler than August on average.

Donetsk in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Donetsk in August?
In August, Donetsk averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 276 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Donetsk?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Donetsk’s mild months (May, June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Donetsk in August?
34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (71 mm), the driest August (34 mm).
Does it snow in Donetsk in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Donetsk get in August?
About 276 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Donetsk in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
