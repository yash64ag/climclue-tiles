How March compares in Donetsk

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Donetsk. Compared with February: 5.2 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. April is 7.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Donetsk in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Donetsk in March?
In March, Donetsk averages a day high of 4.8 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -2.2 °C (28 °F), with 37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Donetsk?
March is not one of Donetsk’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Donetsk in March?
37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (71 mm), the driest August (35 mm).
Does it snow in Donetsk in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 19 cm (7.3 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Donetsk get in March?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Donetsk in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
