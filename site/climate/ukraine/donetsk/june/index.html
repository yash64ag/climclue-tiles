How June compares in Donetsk

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Donetsk. Compared with May: 4.0 °C warmer, 26 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Donetsk in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Donetsk in June?
In June, Donetsk averages a day high of 24.7 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 269 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Donetsk?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Donetsk’s mild months (May, June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Donetsk in June?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (71 mm), the driest August (35 mm).
Does it snow in Donetsk in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Donetsk get in June?
About 269 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Donetsk in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
