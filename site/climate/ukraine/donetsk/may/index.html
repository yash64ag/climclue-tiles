How May compares in Donetsk

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Donetsk. Compared with April: 6.0 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 70 hours more sunshine. June is 4.0 °C warmer than May on average.

Donetsk in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Donetsk in May?
In May, Donetsk averages a day high of 20.2 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 9.6 °C (49 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Donetsk?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Donetsk’s mild months (May, June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Donetsk in May?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (71 mm), the driest August (34 mm).
Does it snow in Donetsk in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Donetsk get in May?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Donetsk in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
