Uzhhorod at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Uzhhorod is the 3rd wettest (716 mm a year). Compared with Ivano-Frankivsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (180 km away), Uzhhorod is 1.4 °C warmer in July and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 49 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.6 °C), averages 22.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 651 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 97% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Uzhhorod has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
