How April compares in Kharkiv

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Kharkiv. Compared with March: 8.5 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. May is 6.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Kharkiv in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kharkiv in April?
In April, Kharkiv averages a day high of 14.0 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 4.5 °C (40 °F), with 34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kharkiv?
April is not one of Kharkiv’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kharkiv in April?
34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (62 mm), the driest March (33 mm).
Does it snow in Kharkiv in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kharkiv get in April?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kharkiv in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
