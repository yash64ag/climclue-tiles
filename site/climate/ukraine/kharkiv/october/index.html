How October compares in Kharkiv

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Kharkiv. Compared with September: 6.5 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 60 hours less sunshine. November is 7.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Kharkiv in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kharkiv in October?
In October, Kharkiv averages a day high of 11.5 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 4.6 °C (40 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 119 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kharkiv?
October is not one of Kharkiv’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kharkiv in October?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (62 mm), the driest March (33 mm).
Does it snow in Kharkiv in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.1 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kharkiv get in October?
About 119 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kharkiv in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
