How September compares in Kharkiv

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kharkiv. Compared with August: 5.9 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 62 hours less sunshine. October is 6.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Kharkiv in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kharkiv in September?
In September, Kharkiv averages a day high of 18.8 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 10.2 °C (50 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Kharkiv?
September is not one of Kharkiv’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kharkiv in September?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (62 mm), the driest March (33 mm).
Does it snow in Kharkiv in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kharkiv get in September?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kharkiv in September?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
