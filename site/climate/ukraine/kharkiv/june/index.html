How June compares in Kharkiv

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Kharkiv. Compared with May: 3.6 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. July is 2.0 °C warmer than June on average.

Kharkiv in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kharkiv in June?
In June, Kharkiv averages a day high of 23.8 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 14.0 °C (57 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Kharkiv?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Kharkiv’s mild months (May, June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kharkiv in June?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (62 mm), the driest March (33 mm).
Does it snow in Kharkiv in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kharkiv get in June?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kharkiv in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
