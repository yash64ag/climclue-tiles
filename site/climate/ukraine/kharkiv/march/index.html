How March compares in Kharkiv

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kharkiv. Compared with February: 5.2 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 47 hours more sunshine. April is 8.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Kharkiv in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kharkiv in March?
In March, Kharkiv averages a day high of 4.1 °C (39 °F) and a night low of -2.7 °C (27 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 116 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kharkiv?
March is not one of Kharkiv’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kharkiv in March?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (62 mm), the driest March (33 mm).
Does it snow in Kharkiv in March?
Yes, on average: about 15 cm (5.9 in) of fresh snow in March (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kharkiv get in March?
About 116 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kharkiv in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
