How May compares in Kharkiv

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kharkiv. Compared with April: 6.5 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 70 hours more sunshine. June is 3.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Kharkiv in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kharkiv in May?
In May, Kharkiv averages a day high of 20.2 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 9.8 °C (50 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kharkiv?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Kharkiv’s mild months (May, June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kharkiv in May?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (62 mm), the driest March (33 mm).
Does it snow in Kharkiv in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kharkiv get in May?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kharkiv in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
