Ternopil at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Ternopil is the 5th coolest (7.9 °C year average) and the 2nd least sunny (about 1,617 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Ivano-Frankivsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (95 km away), Ternopil is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 40 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.9 °C), averages 22.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 524 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ternopil, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ternopil has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 524 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
