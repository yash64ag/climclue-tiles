Brovary at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Brovary is 36th warmest (8.1 °C year average), 35th sunniest (about 1,736 hours of sunshine a year) and 14th wettest (622 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kyiv, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Brovary is 0.2 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 8 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.1 °C), averages 23.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 445 km from the sea.

Why Brovary has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 445 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
