Ilovays’k at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Ilovays’k is 112th warmest (8.6 °C year average), 56th sunniest (about 2,022 hours of sunshine a year) and 140th wettest (592 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zuhres, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Ilovays’k is 0.6 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 68 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.8 °C), averages 25.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 100 km from the sea.

Why Ilovays’k has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ilovays’k itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (47.925°N, 38.202°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-360-2182), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
