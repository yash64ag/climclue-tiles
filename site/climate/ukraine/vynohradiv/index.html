Vynohradiv at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Vynohradiv is 40th warmest (10.0 °C year average), 123rd sunniest (about 1,928 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (826 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Khust, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Vynohradiv is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 122 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.9 °C), averages 23.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 573 km from the sea.

Why Vynohradiv has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Vynohradiv itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.141°N, 23.036°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-358-2030), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
