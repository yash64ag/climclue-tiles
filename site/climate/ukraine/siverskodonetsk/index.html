Siverskodonetsk weather by month and climate

Siverskodonetsk at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Siverskodonetsk is 89th warmest (8.8 °C year average), 100th sunniest (about 1,967 hours of sunshine a year) and 234th wettest (507 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lysychansk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Siverskodonetsk is 0.8 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 80 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.3 °C), averages 26.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 205 km from the sea.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Siverskodonetsk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.944°N, 38.488°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-350-2184), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
