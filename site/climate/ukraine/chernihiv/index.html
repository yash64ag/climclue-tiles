Chernihiv weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Chernihiv, Ukraine (Chernihiv Oblast), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Chernihiv is warmest in July, with 24.8 °C (77 °F) days and 14.6 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -1.3 °C (30 °F) days and -7.5 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 35 mm in March to 80 mm in July.

Chernihiv at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Chernihiv is 261st warmest (7.5 °C year average), 185th sunniest (about 1,770 hours of sunshine a year) and 81st wettest (634 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Masany, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Chernihiv is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 14 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.7 °C), averages 24.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 556 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chernihiv, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Chernihiv

For mild weather, the best months to visit Chernihiv are June, July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July and August.

  • Driest (least precipitation): March (35 mm), January (38 mm) and February (40 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (254 h), June (253 h) and May (247 h).

Why Chernihiv has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 556 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Chernihiv itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (51.505°N, 31.287°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-324-2112), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
