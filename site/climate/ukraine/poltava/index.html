Poltava at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Poltava is 28th warmest (8.3 °C year average), 15th sunniest (about 2,002 hours of sunshine a year) and 13th wettest (625 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kremenchuk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (102 km away), Poltava is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 74 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.1 °C), averages 25.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 347 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 127% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Poltava has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 347 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
