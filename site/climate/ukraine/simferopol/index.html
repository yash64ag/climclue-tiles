Simferopol at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Simferopol is the sunniest (about 2,309 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (764 mm a year). Compared with Sevastopol, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Simferopol is 1.3 °C cooler in July and 3.5 °C cooler in January, with 75 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.8 °C), averages 22.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Simferopol, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Simferopol has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
