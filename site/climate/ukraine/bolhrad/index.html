Bolhrad at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Bolhrad is 21st warmest (11.2 °C year average), 25th sunniest (about 2,080 hours of sunshine a year) and 260th wettest (449 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Reni, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Bolhrad is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 25 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.0 °C), averages 23.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 78 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bolhrad, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bolhrad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 78 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bolhrad itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (45.678°N, 28.618°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-383-2086), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
