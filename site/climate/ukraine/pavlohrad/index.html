Pavlohrad at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Pavlohrad is 17th warmest (9.1 °C year average), 22nd sunniest (about 1,950 hours of sunshine a year) and 33rd wettest (515 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dnipro, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Pavlohrad is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 55 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.1 °C), averages 25.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 213 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pavlohrad, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pavlohrad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 213 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
