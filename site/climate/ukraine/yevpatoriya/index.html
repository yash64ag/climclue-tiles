Why Yevpatoriya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Yevpatoriya climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Yevpatoriya?
July, with an average of 23.6 °C (75 °F), day highs around 27.0 °C (81 °F) and night lows around 20.0 °C (68 °F).
What is the coldest month in Yevpatoriya?
January, with an average of 1.9 °C (35 °F), day highs around 4.1 °C (39 °F) and night lows around -0.2 °C (32 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Yevpatoriya?

What is the driest month in Yevpatoriya?

What is the sunniest month in Yevpatoriya?
July, with about 302 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Yevpatoriya?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 37 cm (14.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 3 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Yevpatoriya?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August and September.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.8 × 11 km, that contains Yevpatoriya’s GeoNames location (45.201°N, 33.367°E; ClimaClue area sq-387-2133), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 107,040 (GeoNames).
