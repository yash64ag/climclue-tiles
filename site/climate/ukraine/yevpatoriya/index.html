Yevpatoriya at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Yevpatoriya is the 3rd warmest (12.1 °C year average), the 4th sunniest (about 2,163 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (455 mm a year). Compared with Simferopol, the nearest covered town with different numbers (64 km away), Yevpatoriya is 1.9 °C warmer in July and 2.1 °C warmer in January, with 309 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.6 °C), averages 21.8 °C more than its coolest, January (1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 19 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yevpatoriya, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yevpatoriya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
