Yevpatoriya sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Crimea · Ukraine

The sea off Yevpatoriya averages 24.1 °C (75 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 6.2 °C (43 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Yevpatoriya sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Yevpatoriya?
In August, at about 24.1 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Yevpatoriya?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 6.2 °C (43 °F).
How warm is the sea in Yevpatoriya in February?
About 6.2 °C (43 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Yevpatoriya?
About 14.2 °C (58 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
