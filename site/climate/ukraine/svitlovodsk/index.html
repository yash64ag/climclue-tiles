Svitlovodsk at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Svitlovodsk is 70th warmest (9.0 °C year average), 128th sunniest (about 1,907 hours of sunshine a year) and 163rd wettest (568 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kremenchuk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Svitlovodsk is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 21 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.7 °C), averages 25.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 309 km from the sea.

Why Svitlovodsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 309 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.2 °C.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-349-2132), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
