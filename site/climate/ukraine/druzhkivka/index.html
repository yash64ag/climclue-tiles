Druzhkivka weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Druzhkivka, Ukraine (Donetsk), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Druzhkivka is warmest in July, with 27.5 °C (82 °F) days and 16.8 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -0.9 °C (30 °F) days and -7.1 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 33 mm in August to 63 mm in June.

Druzhkivka at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Druzhkivka is 86th warmest (8.9 °C year average), 115th sunniest (about 1,949 hours of sunshine a year) and 239th wettest (500 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kramatorsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Druzhkivka is 0.2 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 7 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.2 °C), averages 26.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 185 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Druzhkivka, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Druzhkivka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 185 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.1 °C.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Druzhkivka itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.620°N, 37.525°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-353-2175), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
