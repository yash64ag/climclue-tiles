How January compares in Dnipro

January is the coolest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Dnipro. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. February is 0.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Dnipro in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dnipro in January?
In January, Dnipro averages a day high of -0.9 °C (30 °F) and a night low of -5.9 °C (21 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 69 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Dnipro?
January is not one of Dnipro’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dnipro in January?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (65 mm), the driest April (40 mm).
Does it snow in Dnipro in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 28 cm (10.9 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Dnipro get in January?
About 69 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dnipro in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
