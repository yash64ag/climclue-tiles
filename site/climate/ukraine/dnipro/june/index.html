How June compares in Dnipro

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Dnipro. Compared with May: 3.8 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. July is 2.2 °C warmer than June on average.

Dnipro in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dnipro in June?
In June, Dnipro averages a day high of 24.4 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 15.1 °C (59 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Dnipro?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Dnipro’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dnipro in June?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (65 mm), the driest April (40 mm).
Does it snow in Dnipro in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dnipro get in June?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dnipro in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
