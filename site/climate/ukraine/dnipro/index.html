Dnipro at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Dnipro is 16th warmest (9.1 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 1,978 hours of sunshine a year) and 22nd wettest (570 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kamyanske, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Dnipro is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 41 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.1 °C), averages 25.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 220 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dnipro, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dnipro has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 220 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
