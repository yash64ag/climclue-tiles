Bila Tserkva at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Bila Tserkva is 30th warmest (8.2 °C year average), 33rd sunniest (about 1,762 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (630 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kyiv, the nearest covered town with different numbers (79 km away), Bila Tserkva is 0.2 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 16 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.1 °C), averages 23.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 362 km from the sea.
