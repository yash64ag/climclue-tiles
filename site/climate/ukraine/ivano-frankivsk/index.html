Ivano-Frankivsk at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Ivano-Frankivsk is the 4th least sunny (about 1,651 hours of sunshine a year) and the 5th wettest (666 mm a year). Compared with Ternopil, the nearest covered town with different numbers (95 km away), Ivano-Frankivsk is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 40 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.3 °C), averages 22.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 521 km from the sea.

Why Ivano-Frankivsk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 521 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.0 °C.
