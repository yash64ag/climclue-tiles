Berdyansk at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Berdyansk is the 4th warmest (11.0 °C year average). Compared with Mariupol, the nearest covered town with different numbers (68 km away), Berdyansk is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 24 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.9 °C), averages 24.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 13 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Berdyansk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
