Why Berdyansk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Berdyansk climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Berdyansk?
July, with an average of 23.9 °C (75 °F), day highs around 26.2 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 21.3 °C (70 °F).
What is the coldest month in Berdyansk?
January, with an average of -0.7 °C (31 °F), day highs around 0.9 °C (34 °F) and night lows around -2.1 °C (28 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Berdyansk?

What is the driest month in Berdyansk?

What is the sunniest month in Berdyansk?
July, with about 307 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Berdyansk?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 66 cm (25.9 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 4 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Berdyansk?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August and September.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.6 × 11 km, that contains Berdyansk’s GeoNames location (46.756°N, 36.788°E; ClimaClue area sq-372-2167), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 106,311 (GeoNames).
