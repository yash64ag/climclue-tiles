Berdyansk sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Zaporizhzhia · Ukraine

The sea off Berdyansk averages 24.4 °C (76 °F) in July, its warmest month, and 1.3 °C (34 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August.

Berdyansk sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Berdyansk?
In July, at about 24.4 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Berdyansk?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August. The coolest month is February, at 1.3 °C (34 °F).
How warm is the sea in Berdyansk in February?
About 1.3 °C (34 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Berdyansk?
About 11.9 °C (53 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
