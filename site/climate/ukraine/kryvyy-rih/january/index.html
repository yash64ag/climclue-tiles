Kryvyy Rih in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kryvyy Rih in January?
In January, Kryvyy Rih averages a day high of 0.0 °C (32 °F) and a night low of -5.4 °C (22 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 68 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kryvyy Rih?
January is not one of Kryvyy Rih’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kryvyy Rih in January?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (63 mm), the driest March (31 mm).
Does it snow in Kryvyy Rih in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 23 cm (9.0 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 6.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kryvyy Rih get in January?
About 68 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kryvyy Rih in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
