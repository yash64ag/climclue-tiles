Kryvyy Rih weather by month and climate

Kryvyy Rih at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Kryvyy Rih is 54th warmest (9.5 °C year average), 88th sunniest (about 1,985 hours of sunshine a year) and 240th wettest (497 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Inhulets, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Kryvyy Rih is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 30 mm more precipitation a year. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kryvyy Rih itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (47.906°N, 33.394°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-360-2133), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
