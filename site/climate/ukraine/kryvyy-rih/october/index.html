Kryvyy Rih in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kryvyy Rih in October?
In October, Kryvyy Rih averages a day high of 13.7 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 6.1 °C (43 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kryvyy Rih?
October is not one of Kryvyy Rih’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kryvyy Rih in October?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (63 mm), the driest March (31 mm).
Does it snow in Kryvyy Rih in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kryvyy Rih get in October?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kryvyy Rih in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
