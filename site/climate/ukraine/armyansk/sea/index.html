Armyansk sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Crimea · Ukraine

The sea off Armyansk averages 23.7 °C (75 °F) in July, its warmest month, and 3.5 °C (38 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to September.

Armyansk sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Armyansk?
In July, at about 23.7 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Armyansk?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to September. The coolest month is February, at 3.5 °C (38 °F).
How warm is the sea in Armyansk in February?
About 3.5 °C (38 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Armyansk?
About 13.1 °C (56 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
