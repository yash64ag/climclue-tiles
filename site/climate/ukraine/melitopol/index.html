Melitopol at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Melitopol is 9th warmest (10.4 °C year average), 8th sunniest (about 2,099 hours of sunshine a year) and 37th wettest (481 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Berdyansk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (107 km away), Melitopol is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 54 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.3 °C), averages 25.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 51 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Melitopol, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Melitopol has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 51 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
