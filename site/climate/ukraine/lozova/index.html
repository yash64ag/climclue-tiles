Lozova at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Lozova is 145th warmest (8.3 °C year average), 126th sunniest (about 1,918 hours of sunshine a year) and 136th wettest (592 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ternivka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Lozova is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 53 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.2 °C), averages 25.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 229 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lozova, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lozova has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 229 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Lozova itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.891°N, 36.314°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-351-2163), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
