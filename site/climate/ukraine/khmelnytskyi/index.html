Khmelnytskyi weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Khmelnytskyi, Ukraine, from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Khmelnytskyi is warmest in July, with 25.0 °C (77 °F) days and 13.4 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -0.4 °C (31 °F) days and -6.6 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (106 mm); January is driest (32 mm).

Khmelnytskyi at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Khmelnytskyi is 231st warmest (7.9 °C year average), 177th sunniest (about 1,781 hours of sunshine a year) and 64th wettest (642 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Krasyliv, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Khmelnytskyi is 0.2 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 43 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.2 °C), averages 22.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 442 km from the sea.

Why Khmelnytskyi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Khmelnytskyi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (49.418°N, 26.979°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-345-2069), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
