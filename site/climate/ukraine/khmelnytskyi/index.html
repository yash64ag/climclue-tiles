Khmelnytskyi at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Khmelnytskyi is the 4th coolest (7.8 °C year average). Compared with Ternopil, the nearest covered town with different numbers (101 km away), Khmelnytskyi is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 32 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 22.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 442 km from the sea.

Why Khmelnytskyi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 442 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.6 °C.
