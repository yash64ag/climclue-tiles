Pelahiivka at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Pelahiivka is 220th warmest (8.0 °C year average), 54th sunniest (about 2,024 hours of sunshine a year) and 48th wettest (653 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chystyakove, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Pelahiivka is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 41 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.0 °C), averages 25.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 115 km from the sea.

Why Pelahiivka has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pelahiivka itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.108°N, 38.613°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-358-2186), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
