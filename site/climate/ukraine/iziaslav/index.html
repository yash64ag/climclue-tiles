Iziaslav weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Iziaslav, Ukraine (Khmelnytskyi), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Iziaslav at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Iziaslav is 226th warmest (8.0 °C year average), 243rd sunniest (about 1,706 hours of sunshine a year) and 36th wettest (673 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shepetivka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Iziaslav is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 19 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 22.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 506 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 17 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 112% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Iziaslav has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 506 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Iziaslav itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (50.120°N, 26.825°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-338-2068), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
