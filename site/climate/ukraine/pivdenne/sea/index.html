Pivdenne sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Odesa · Ukraine

The sea off Pivdenne averages 23.3 °C (74 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 2.3 °C (36 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to August.

Pivdenne sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Pivdenne?
In August, at about 23.3 °C (74 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Pivdenne?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to August. The coolest month is February, at 2.3 °C (36 °F).
How warm is the sea in Pivdenne in February?
About 2.3 °C (36 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Pivdenne?
About 12.4 °C (54 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Pivdenne nearby?
In August: Yevpatoriya (24.1 °C, 236 km), Saky (24.1 °C, 255 km) and Sevastopol (24.2 °C, 293 km).
