Odesa sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Ukraine

The sea off Odesa averages 23.4 °C (74 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 2.2 °C (36 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to August.

Odesa sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Odesa?
In August, at about 23.4 °C (74 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Odesa?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to August. The coolest month is February, at 2.2 °C (36 °F).
How warm is the sea in Odesa in February?
About 2.2 °C (36 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Odesa?
About 12.4 °C (54 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Odesa nearby?
In August: Yevpatoriya (24.1 °C, 248 km), Saky (24.1 °C, 268 km) and Năvodari (24.3 °C, 294 km).
