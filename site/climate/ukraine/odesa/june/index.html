How June compares in Odesa

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Odesa. Compared with May: 4.4 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. July is 2.6 °C warmer than June on average.

Odesa in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Odesa in June?
In June, Odesa averages a day high of 24.6 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 16.2 °C (61 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 285 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Odesa?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Odesa’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Odesa in June?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (51 mm), the driest April (29 mm).
Does it snow in Odesa in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Odesa get in June?
About 285 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Odesa in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
