Odesa at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Odesa is the 5th warmest (11.0 °C year average), the 5th sunniest (about 2,124 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th driest (474 mm a year). Compared with Mykolayiv, the nearest covered town with different numbers (110 km away), Odesa is about as warm in July and 1.3 °C warmer in January, with 38 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.1 °C), averages 23.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 8 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 9 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 107% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Odesa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
