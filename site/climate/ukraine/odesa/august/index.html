Odesa weather in August

Average August weather in Odesa, Ukraine: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Odesa averages 26.9 °C (80 °F) days and 18.6 °C (65 °F) nights, with 39 mm (1.5 in) of rain over the month and about 292 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Odesa

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Odesa. Compared with July: 0.5 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. September is 5.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Odesa in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Odesa in August?
In August, Odesa averages a day high of 26.9 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 18.6 °C (65 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 292 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Odesa?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Odesa’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Odesa in August?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (51 mm), the driest April (29 mm).
Does it snow in Odesa in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Odesa get in August?
About 292 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Odesa in August?
About 23.4 °C (74 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (23.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Odesa in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
