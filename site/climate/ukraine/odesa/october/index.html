How October compares in Odesa

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Odesa. Compared with September: 5.6 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 68 hours less sunshine. November is 5.9 °C cooler than October on average.

Odesa in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Odesa in October?
In October, Odesa averages a day high of 15.1 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 8.7 °C (48 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Odesa?
October is not one of Odesa’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Odesa in October?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (51 mm), the driest April (29 mm).
Does it snow in Odesa in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Odesa get in October?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Odesa in October?
About 14.7 °C (58 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (23.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Odesa in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
