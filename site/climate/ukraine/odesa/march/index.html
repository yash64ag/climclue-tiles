Odesa weather in March

Average March weather in Odesa, Ukraine: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Odesa averages 6.5 °C (44 °F) days and 1.0 °C (34 °F) nights, with 32 mm (1.3 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 123 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Odesa

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Odesa. Compared with February: 3.9 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. April is 6.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Odesa in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Odesa in March?
In March, Odesa averages a day high of 6.5 °C (44 °F) and a night low of 1.0 °C (34 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 123 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Odesa?
March is not one of Odesa’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Odesa in March?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (51 mm), the driest April (29 mm).
Does it snow in Odesa in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.2 cm (3.6 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Odesa get in March?
About 123 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Odesa in March?
About 3.3 °C (38 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (23.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Odesa in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
