How November compares in Odesa

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Odesa. Compared with October: 5.8 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 88 hours less sunshine. December is 4.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Odesa in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Odesa in November?
In November, Odesa averages a day high of 8.3 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 3.5 °C (38 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 74 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Odesa?
November is not one of Odesa’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Odesa in November?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (52 mm), the driest April (30 mm).
Does it snow in Odesa in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.3 cm (2.1 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Odesa get in November?
About 74 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Odesa in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
