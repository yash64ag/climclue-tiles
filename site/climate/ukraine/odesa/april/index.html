How April compares in Odesa

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Odesa. Compared with March: 6.2 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 59 hours more sunshine. May is 6.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Odesa in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Odesa in April?
In April, Odesa averages a day high of 13.7 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 5.7 °C (42 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Odesa?
April is not one of Odesa’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Odesa in April?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (51 mm), the driest April (29 mm).
Does it snow in Odesa in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Odesa get in April?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Odesa in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
