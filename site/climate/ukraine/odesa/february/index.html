How February compares in Odesa

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Odesa. Compared with January: 0.2 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. March is 3.6 °C warmer than February on average.

Odesa in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Odesa in February?
In February, Odesa averages a day high of 2.7 °C (37 °F) and a night low of -2.6 °C (27 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 78 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Odesa?
February is not one of Odesa’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Odesa in February?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (51 mm), the driest April (29 mm).
Does it snow in Odesa in February?
Yes, on average: about 18 cm (6.9 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Odesa get in February?
About 78 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Odesa in February?
About 2.2 °C (36 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (23.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Odesa in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
