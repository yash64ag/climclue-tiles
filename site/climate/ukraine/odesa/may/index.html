How May compares in Odesa

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Odesa. Compared with April: 6.2 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 77 hours more sunshine. June is 4.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Odesa in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Odesa in May?
In May, Odesa averages a day high of 19.7 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 12.2 °C (54 °F), with 37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 260 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Odesa?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Odesa’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Odesa in May?
37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (51 mm), the driest April (29 mm).
Does it snow in Odesa in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Odesa get in May?
About 260 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Odesa in May?
About 14.9 °C (59 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (23.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Odesa in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
