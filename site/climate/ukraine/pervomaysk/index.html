Pervomaysk at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Pervomaysk is 53rd warmest (9.5 °C year average), 132nd sunniest (about 1,904 hours of sunshine a year) and 210th wettest (536 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pivdennoukrainsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Pervomaysk is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 27 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.9 °C), averages 24.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 167 km from the sea.

Why Pervomaysk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pervomaysk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.045°N, 30.848°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-359-2108), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
