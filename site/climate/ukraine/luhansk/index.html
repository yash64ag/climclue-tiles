Luhansk at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Luhansk is 21st warmest (8.8 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 2,020 hours of sunshine a year) and 26th wettest (543 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Alchevsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Luhansk is 0.9 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in February, with 45 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.3 °C), averages 26.5 °C more than its coolest, February (-4.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 156 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 111% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Luhansk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 156 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
