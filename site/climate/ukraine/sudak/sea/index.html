Sudak sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Crimea · Ukraine

The sea off Sudak averages 23.3 °C (74 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 6.6 °C (44 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Sudak sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Sudak?
In August, at about 23.3 °C (74 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Sudak?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 6.6 °C (44 °F).
How warm is the sea in Sudak in February?
About 6.6 °C (44 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Sudak?
About 14.0 °C (57 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Sudak nearby?
In August: Feodosiya (24.4 °C, 38 km), Saky (24.1 °C, 113 km) and Balaklava (23.9 °C, 115 km).
