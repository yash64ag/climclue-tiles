Yalta at a glance

Among the 272 towns ClimaClue covers in Ukraine, Yalta is the 4th warmest (12.4 °C year average) and the 4th sunniest (about 2,273 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Alushta, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Yalta is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.5 °C warmer in February, with 158 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.6 °C), averages 21.1 °C more than its coolest, February (2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: a NOAA weather station 8 km from Yalta measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from these (temperature by up to 7.7 °C (13.9 °F) in one month). One station can differ from modelled values (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Yalta has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.1 °C. The 10 km area around Yalta, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 8.7 °C a year, 3.8 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Yalta itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (44.502°N, 34.166°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-394-2141), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
