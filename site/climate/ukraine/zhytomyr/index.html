Zhytomyr at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Zhytomyr is the 3rd coolest (7.8 °C year average) and the 5th least sunny (about 1,680 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Bila Tserkva, the nearest covered town with different numbers (115 km away), Zhytomyr is 0.8 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.3 °C), averages 23.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 448 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Zhytomyr, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Zhytomyr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 448 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
