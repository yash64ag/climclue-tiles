How March compares in Zaporizhzhya

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Zaporizhzhya. Compared with February: 4.8 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 47 hours more sunshine. April is 7.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Zaporizhzhya in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zaporizhzhya in March?
In March, Zaporizhzhya averages a day high of 6.0 °C (43 °F) and a night low of -0.7 °C (31 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Zaporizhzhya?
March is not one of Zaporizhzhya’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zaporizhzhya in March?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (58 mm), the driest August (33 mm).
Does it snow in Zaporizhzhya in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 16 cm (6.3 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.8 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Zaporizhzhya get in March?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zaporizhzhya in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
