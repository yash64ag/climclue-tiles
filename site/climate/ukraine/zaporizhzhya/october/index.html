Zaporizhzhya weather in October

Average October weather in Zaporizhzhya, Ukraine: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Zaporizhzhya averages 14.4 °C (58 °F) days and 5.6 °C (42 °F) nights, with 35 mm (1.4 in) of rain over the month and about 147 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Zaporizhzhya

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Zaporizhzhya. Compared with September: 6.5 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 63 hours less sunshine. November is 6.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Zaporizhzhya in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zaporizhzhya in October?
In October, Zaporizhzhya averages a day high of 14.4 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 5.6 °C (42 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Zaporizhzhya?
October is not one of Zaporizhzhya’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zaporizhzhya in October?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (57 mm), the driest August (32 mm).
Does it snow in Zaporizhzhya in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zaporizhzhya get in October?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zaporizhzhya in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
