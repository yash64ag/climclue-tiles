How December compares in Zaporizhzhya

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Zaporizhzhya. Compared with November: 4.4 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Zaporizhzhya in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zaporizhzhya in December?
In December, Zaporizhzhya averages a day high of 1.5 °C (35 °F) and a night low of -3.6 °C (26 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 44 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Zaporizhzhya?
December is not one of Zaporizhzhya’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zaporizhzhya in December?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (57 mm), the driest August (32 mm).
Does it snow in Zaporizhzhya in December?
Yes, on average: about 17 cm (6.8 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Zaporizhzhya get in December?
About 44 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zaporizhzhya in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
