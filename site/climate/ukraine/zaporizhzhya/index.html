Zaporizhzhya at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Zaporizhzhya is 13th warmest (9.9 °C year average), 13th sunniest (about 2,015 hours of sunshine a year) and 37th wettest (490 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nikopol, the nearest covered town with different numbers (63 km away), Zaporizhzhya is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.6 °C), averages 25.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 154 km from the sea.

Why Zaporizhzhya has this climate

About these numbers

Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-361-2151), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
