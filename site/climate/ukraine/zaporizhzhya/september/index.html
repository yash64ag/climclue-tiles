How September compares in Zaporizhzhya

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Zaporizhzhya. Compared with August: 5.5 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 67 hours less sunshine. October is 6.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Zaporizhzhya in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zaporizhzhya in September?
In September, Zaporizhzhya averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 11.1 °C (52 °F), with 37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Zaporizhzhya?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Zaporizhzhya’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zaporizhzhya in September?
37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (57 mm), the driest August (32 mm).
Does it snow in Zaporizhzhya in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zaporizhzhya get in September?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zaporizhzhya in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
