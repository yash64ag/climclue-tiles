Mykolayiv at a glance

Among the 43 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ukraine, Mykolayiv is the driest (436 mm a year). Compared with Odesa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (110 km away), Mykolayiv is about as warm in July and 1.3 °C cooler in January, with 38 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.1 °C), averages 24.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 59 km from the sea.

Why Mykolayiv has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 59 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
