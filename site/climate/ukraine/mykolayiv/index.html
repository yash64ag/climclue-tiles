Why Mykolayiv has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 59 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Mykolayiv climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Mykolayiv?
July, with an average of 23.1 °C (73 °F), day highs around 27.9 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 18.2 °C (65 °F).
What is the coldest month in Mykolayiv?
January, with an average of -1.8 °C (29 °F), day highs around 1.4 °C (35 °F) and night lows around -4.2 °C (24 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Mykolayiv?
June, with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 436 mm (17.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Mykolayiv?
March, with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Mykolayiv?
July, with about 295 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Mykolayiv?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 62 cm (24.3 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 4 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Mykolayiv?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Mykolayiv have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.6 × 11 km, that contains Mykolayiv’s GeoNames location (46.976°N, 31.993°E; ClimaClue area sq-370-2119), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 470,011 (GeoNames).
