Beau Bassin-Rose Hill weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Beau Bassin-Rose Hill, Mauritius (Plaines Wilhems), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Beau Bassin-Rose Hill at a glance

Among the 14 towns ClimaClue covers in Mauritius, Beau Bassin-Rose Hill is 10th warmest (22.8 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 2,589 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (1,709 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Quatre Bornes, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Beau Bassin-Rose Hill is 0.5 °C warmer in February and 0.7 °C warmer in August, with 94 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (25.4 °C), averages 5.3 °C more than its coolest, August (20.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.2 °C (61 °F), in August. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 8 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 84% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Beau Bassin-Rose Hill has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
