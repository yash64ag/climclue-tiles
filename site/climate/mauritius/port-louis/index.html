Port Louis at a glance

Among the 3 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Mauritius, Port Louis is the warmest (23.8 °C year average), the sunniest (about 2,589 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (1,504 mm a year). Compared with Beau Bassin-Rose Hill, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Port Louis is 0.9 °C warmer in February and 1.2 °C warmer in August, with 154 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.3 °C), averages 5.0 °C more than its coolest, August (21.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.7 °C (67 °F), in August. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: a NOAA weather station 15 km from Port Louis measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 2.7 °C (4.8 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Port Louis has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
