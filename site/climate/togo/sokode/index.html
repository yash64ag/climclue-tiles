Sokodé at a glance

Among the 3 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Togo, Sokodé is the 3rd warmest (26.2 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 2,442 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (1,267 mm a year). Compared with Kara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (63 km away), Sokodé is 1.0 °C cooler in March and 0.4 °C cooler in August, with 33 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (28.9 °C), averages 4.8 °C more than its coolest, August (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.1 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 314 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sokodé, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sokodé has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 314 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
