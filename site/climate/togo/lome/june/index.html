How June compares in Lomé

June is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Lomé. Compared with May: 1.2 °C cooler, 37 mm more precipitation and 65 hours less sunshine. July is 1.1 °C cooler than June on average.

Lomé in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lomé in June?
In June, Lomé averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 187 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Lomé?
No month in Lomé is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lomé in June?
187 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (187 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Lomé in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lomé get in June?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Lomé in June?
About 27.6 °C (82 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (28.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Lomé in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
