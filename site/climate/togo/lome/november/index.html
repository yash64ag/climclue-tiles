How November compares in Lomé

November is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Lomé. Compared with October: 1.0 °C warmer, 55 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. December is 0.1 °C warmer than November on average.

Lomé in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lomé in November?
In November, Lomé averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Lomé?
November is not one of Lomé’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lomé in November?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (187 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Lomé in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lomé get in November?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Lomé in November?
About 28.5 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (28.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Lomé in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
