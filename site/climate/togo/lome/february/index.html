How February compares in Lomé

February is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Lomé. Compared with January: 0.8 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. March is 0.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Lomé in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lomé in February?
In February, Lomé averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 25.9 °C (79 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Lomé?
February is not one of Lomé’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lomé in February?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (187 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Lomé in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lomé get in February?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lomé in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
