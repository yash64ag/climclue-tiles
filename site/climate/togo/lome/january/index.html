How January compares in Lomé

January is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Lomé. Compared with December: 0.3 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. February is 0.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Lomé in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lomé in January?
In January, Lomé averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Lomé?
January is not one of Lomé’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lomé in January?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (189 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Lomé in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lomé get in January?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lomé in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
