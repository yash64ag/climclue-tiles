How April compares in Lomé

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Lomé. Compared with March: 0.1 °C cooler, 45 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. May is 0.6 °C cooler than April on average.

Lomé in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lomé in April?
In April, Lomé averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 26.2 °C (79 °F), with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Lomé?
April is not one of Lomé’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lomé in April?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (189 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Lomé in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lomé get in April?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lomé in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
