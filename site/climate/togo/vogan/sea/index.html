Vogan sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Maritime · Togo

The sea off Vogan averages 28.9 °C (84 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 24.2 °C (76 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Vogan sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Vogan?
In May, at about 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Vogan?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 24.2 °C (76 °F).
How warm is the sea in Vogan in August?
About 24.2 °C (76 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Vogan?
About 27.3 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
