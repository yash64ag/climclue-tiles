Bayamón at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Puerto Rico, Bayamón is the 2nd warmest (25.7 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 2,921 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (1,785 mm a year). Compared with San Juan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Bayamón is 0.4 °C cooler in August and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 121 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.3 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, January (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.0 °C (70 °F), in February. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 11 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 103% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Bayamón has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
