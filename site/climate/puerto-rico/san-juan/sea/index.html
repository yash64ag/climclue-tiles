San Juan sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Puerto Rico

The sea off San Juan averages 29.0 °C (84 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 26.1 °C (79 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

San Juan sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in San Juan?
In September, at about 29.0 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in San Juan?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is March, at 26.1 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in San Juan in March?
About 26.1 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in San Juan?
About 27.6 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
