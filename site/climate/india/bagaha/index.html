Bagaha at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Bagaha is 380th warmest (25.0 °C year average), 437th sunniest (about 2,262 hours of sunshine a year) and 102nd wettest (1,612 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kushinagar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Bagaha is 0.7 °C cooler in May and about as warm in January, with 302 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.9 °C), averages 15.6 °C more than its coolest, January (15.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.6 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 697 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bagaha, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bagaha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 697 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
