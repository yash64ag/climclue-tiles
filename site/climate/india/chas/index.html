Chas at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Chas is 370th warmest (25.1 °C year average), 460th sunniest (about 2,204 hours of sunshine a year) and 116th wettest (1,540 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Phusro, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Chas is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 99 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.3 °C), averages 14.6 °C more than its coolest, January (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.2 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 265 km from the sea.

Why Chas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 265 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
