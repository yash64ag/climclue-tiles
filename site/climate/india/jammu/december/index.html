How December compares in Jammu

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Jammu. Compared with November: 4.1 °C cooler, 28 mm more precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. January is 1.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Jammu in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jammu in December?
In December, Jammu averages a day high of 19.1 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 6.1 °C (43 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jammu?
December is not one of Jammu’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jammu in December?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (447 mm), the driest November (16 mm).
Does it snow in Jammu in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jammu get in December?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jammu in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
