How July compares in Jammu

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Jammu. Compared with June: 1.9 °C cooler, 363 mm more precipitation and 45 hours less sunshine. August is 0.9 °C cooler than July on average.

Jammu in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jammu in July?
In July, Jammu averages a day high of 32.8 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 24.7 °C (76 °F), with 448 mm (17.6 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Jammu?
July is not one of Jammu’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jammu in July?
448 mm (17.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (448 mm), the driest November (16 mm).
Does it snow in Jammu in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jammu get in July?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jammu in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
