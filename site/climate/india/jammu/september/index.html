Jammu weather in September

Average September weather in Jammu, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Jammu averages 32.8 °C (91 °F) days and 20.8 °C (69 °F) nights, with 139 mm (5.5 in) of rain over the month and about 255 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Jammu

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Jammu. Compared with August: 1.9 °C cooler, 233 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. October is 4.6 °C cooler than September on average.

What to pack for Jammu in September

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses.

Jammu in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jammu in September?
In September, Jammu averages a day high of 32.8 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Jammu?
September is not one of Jammu’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jammu in September?
139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (448 mm), the driest November (16 mm).
Does it snow in Jammu in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jammu get in September?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jammu in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
