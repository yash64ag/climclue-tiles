How November compares in Jammu

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Jammu. Compared with October: 5.5 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. December is 4.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Jammu in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jammu in November?
In November, Jammu averages a day high of 24.0 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 9.7 °C (49 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Jammu?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Jammu’s mild months (March, April, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jammu in November?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (447 mm), the driest November (16 mm).
Does it snow in Jammu in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jammu get in November?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jammu in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
