How June compares in Jammu

June is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Jammu. Compared with May: 2.1 °C warmer, 43 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. July is 1.8 °C cooler than June on average.

Jammu in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jammu in June?
In June, Jammu averages a day high of 37.4 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 23.8 °C (75 °F), with 84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 283 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Jammu?
June is not one of Jammu’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jammu in June?
84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (447 mm), the driest November (16 mm).
Does it snow in Jammu in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jammu get in June?
About 283 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jammu in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
