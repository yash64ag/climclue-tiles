Jammu at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Jammu is 513th warmest (22.2 °C year average), 39th sunniest (about 2,912 hours of sunshine a year) and 123rd wettest (1,503 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pathānkot, the nearest covered town with different numbers (90 km away), Jammu is 0.3 °C cooler in June and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 169 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (31.4 °C), averages 20.0 °C more than its coolest, January (11.4 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 1,158 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jammu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Jammu

For mild weather, the best months to visit Jammu are March, April, October and November.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): March, April, October and November.
  • Warmest (average temperature): June (31.4 °C), July (29.6 °C) and May (29.4 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): November (16 mm), October (25 mm) and May (41 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): May (301 h), June (283 h) and October (279 h).

Why Jammu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,158 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
