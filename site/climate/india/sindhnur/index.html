Sindhnūr at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Sindhnūr is 384th warmest (27.9 °C year average), 1625th sunniest (about 2,670 hours of sunshine a year) and 3277th wettest (660 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Siruguppa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Sindhnūr is about as warm in April and 0.2 °C cooler in December, with 16 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (32.6 °C), averages 8.6 °C more than its coolest, December (24.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.8 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 300 km from the sea.

Why Sindhnūr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 300 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
