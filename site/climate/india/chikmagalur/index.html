Chikmagalūr at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Chikmagalūr is 515th warmest (21.8 °C year average), 384th sunniest (about 2,459 hours of sunshine a year) and 133rd wettest (1,457 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hassan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (49 km away), Chikmagalūr is 0.8 °C cooler in April and 0.5 °C cooler in December, with 557 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (24.7 °C), averages 5.0 °C more than its coolest, December (19.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.9 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 119 km from the sea.

Why Chikmagalūr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 119 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.0 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
