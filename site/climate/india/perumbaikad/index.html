Perumbaikad at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Perumbaikad is 1042nd warmest (26.9 °C year average), 2782nd sunniest (about 2,441 hours of sunshine a year) and 186th wettest (3,264 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Aymanam, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Perumbaikad is about as warm in March and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 42 mm more precipitation a year. Why Perumbaikad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Perumbaikad itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (9.618°N, 76.531°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-743-2565), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
