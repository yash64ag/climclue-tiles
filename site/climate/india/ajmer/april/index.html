How April compares in Ajmer

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Ajmer. Compared with March: 5.5 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. May is 3.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Ajmer in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ajmer in April?
In April, Ajmer averages a day high of 36.2 °C (97 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 285 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ajmer?
April is not one of Ajmer’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ajmer in April?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (171 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ajmer in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ajmer get in April?
About 285 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ajmer in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
