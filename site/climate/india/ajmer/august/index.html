How August compares in Ajmer

August is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Ajmer. Compared with July: 1.6 °C cooler, 28 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. September is 0.3 °C warmer than August on average.

Ajmer in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ajmer in August?
In August, Ajmer averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.7 °C (75 °F), with 141 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Ajmer?
August is not one of Ajmer’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ajmer in August?
141 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (169 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ajmer in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ajmer get in August?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ajmer in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
