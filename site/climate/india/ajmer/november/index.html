How November compares in Ajmer

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Ajmer. Compared with October: 5.1 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. December is 4.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Ajmer in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ajmer in November?
In November, Ajmer averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 14.5 °C (58 °F), with 6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 265 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Ajmer?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Ajmer’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ajmer in November?
6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (169 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ajmer in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ajmer get in November?
About 265 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ajmer in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
