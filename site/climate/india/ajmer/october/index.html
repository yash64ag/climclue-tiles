How October compares in Ajmer

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Ajmer. Compared with September: 1.8 °C cooler, 53 mm less precipitation and 50 hours more sunshine. November is 5.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Ajmer in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ajmer in October?
In October, Ajmer averages a day high of 33.7 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 279 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Ajmer?
October is not one of Ajmer’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ajmer in October?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (171 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ajmer in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ajmer get in October?
About 279 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ajmer in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
