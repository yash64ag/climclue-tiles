Ajmer weather in May

Average May weather in Ajmer, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Ajmer averages 39.9 °C (104 °F) days and 26.2 °C (79 °F) nights, with 12 mm (0.5 in) of rain over the month and about 298 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Ajmer

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Ajmer. Compared with April: 3.3 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. June is 0.5 °C cooler than May on average.

Ajmer in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ajmer in May?
In May, Ajmer averages a day high of 39.9 °C (104 °F) and a night low of 26.2 °C (79 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 298 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Ajmer?
May is not one of Ajmer’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ajmer in May?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (171 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ajmer in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ajmer get in May?
About 298 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ajmer in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
