How January compares in Ajmer

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Ajmer. Compared with December: 1.2 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. February is 3.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Ajmer in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ajmer in January?
In January, Ajmer averages a day high of 22.1 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 8.6 °C (47 °F), with 6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Ajmer?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Ajmer’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ajmer in January?
6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (169 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ajmer in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ajmer get in January?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ajmer in January?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
