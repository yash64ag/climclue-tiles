How December compares in Ajmer

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Ajmer. Compared with November: 4.4 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. January is 1.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Ajmer in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ajmer in December?
In December, Ajmer averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 10.2 °C (50 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ajmer?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Ajmer’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ajmer in December?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (169 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ajmer in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ajmer get in December?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ajmer in December?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
