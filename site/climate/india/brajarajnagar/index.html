Brajarajnagar at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Brajarajnagar is 1522nd warmest (26.5 °C year average), 2743rd sunniest (about 2,452 hours of sunshine a year) and 758th wettest (1,698 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Belpahar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Brajarajnagar is about as warm in May and about as warm in January, with 17 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.1 °C), averages 13.1 °C more than its coolest, January (20.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.7 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 275 km from the sea.

Why Brajarajnagar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 275 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-621-2639), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
