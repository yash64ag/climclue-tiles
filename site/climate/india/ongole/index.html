Ongole at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Ongole is 21st warmest (28.4 °C year average), 243rd sunniest (about 2,675 hours of sunshine a year) and 299th wettest (995 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chilakalūrupet, the nearest covered town with different numbers (66 km away), Ongole is 0.6 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 59 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.6 °C), averages 8.4 °C more than its coolest, January (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.8 °C (68 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ongole, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ongole has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-684-2600), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
