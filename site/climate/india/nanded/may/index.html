How May compares in Nanded

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Nanded. Compared with April: 1.7 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. June is 4.0 °C cooler than May on average.

Nanded in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanded in May?
In May, Nanded averages a day high of 40.3 °C (105 °F) and a night low of 28.2 °C (83 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 296 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Nanded?
May is not one of Nanded’s mild months; those are January, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanded in May?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (254 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Nanded in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanded get in May?
About 296 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanded in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
