How November compares in Nanded

November is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Nanded. Compared with October: 2.3 °C cooler, 60 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. December is 1.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Nanded in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanded in November?
In November, Nanded averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 17.3 °C (63 °F), with 14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 258 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Nanded?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Nanded’s mild months (January, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanded in November?
14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (251 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Nanded in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanded get in November?
About 258 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanded in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
