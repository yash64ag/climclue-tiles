Nanded weather in January

Average January weather in Nanded, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Nanded averages 30.3 °C (87 °F) days and 15.5 °C (60 °F) nights, with 11 mm (0.4 in) of rain over the month and about 281 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Nanded

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Nanded. Compared with December: 0.8 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. February is 2.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Nanded in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanded in January?
In January, Nanded averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 281 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Nanded?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Nanded’s mild months (January, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanded in January?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (251 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Nanded in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanded get in January?
About 281 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanded in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
