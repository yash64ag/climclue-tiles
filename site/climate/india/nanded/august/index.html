Nanded weather in August

Average August weather in Nanded, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Nanded averages 30.5 °C (87 °F) days and 22.6 °C (73 °F) nights, with 251 mm (9.9 in) of rain over the month and about 115 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Nanded

August is the 6th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Nanded. Compared with July: 1.0 °C cooler, 13 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. September is 0.3 °C warmer than August on average.

Nanded in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanded in August?
In August, Nanded averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 251 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation and about 115 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Nanded?
August is not one of Nanded’s mild months; those are January, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanded in August?
251 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (251 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Nanded in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanded get in August?
About 115 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanded in August?
