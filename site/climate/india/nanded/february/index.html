How February compares in Nanded

February is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Nanded. Compared with January: 2.7 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. March is 3.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Nanded in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanded in February?
In February, Nanded averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 18.5 °C (65 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 270 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Nanded?
February is not one of Nanded’s mild months; those are January, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanded in February?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (254 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Nanded in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanded get in February?
About 270 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanded in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
