How October compares in Nanded

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Nanded. Compared with September: 0.7 °C cooler, 119 mm less precipitation and 71 hours more sunshine. November is 2.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Nanded in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanded in October?
In October, Nanded averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Nanded?
October is not one of Nanded’s mild months; those are January, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanded in October?
75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (254 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Nanded in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanded get in October?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanded in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
