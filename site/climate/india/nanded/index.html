Nanded at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Nanded is 74th warmest (27.4 °C year average), 110th sunniest (about 2,771 hours of sunshine a year) and 289th wettest (1,014 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Parbhani, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Nanded is 0.7 °C warmer in May and 0.7 °C warmer in December, with 8 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.6 °C), averages 12.6 °C more than its coolest, December (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.9 °C (61 °F), in December. The area lies about 474 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nanded, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nanded has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 474 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
