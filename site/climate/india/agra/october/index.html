How October compares in Agra

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Agra. Compared with September: 3.0 °C cooler, 118 mm less precipitation and 60 hours more sunshine. November is 5.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Agra in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Agra in October?
In October, Agra averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Agra?
October is not one of Agra’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Agra in October?
24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (205 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Agra in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Agra get in October?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Agra in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
