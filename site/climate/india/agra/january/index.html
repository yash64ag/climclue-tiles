How January compares in Agra

January is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Agra. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. February is 3.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Agra in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Agra in January?
In January, Agra averages a day high of 20.8 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Agra?
January is not one of Agra’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Agra in January?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (206 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Agra in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Agra get in January?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Agra in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
