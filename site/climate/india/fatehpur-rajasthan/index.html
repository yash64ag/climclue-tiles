Fatehpur, Rajasthan weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Fatehpur, Rajasthan, India, from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Fatehpur, Rajasthan is warmest in June, with 40.3 °C (105 °F) days and 28.1 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 22.3 °C (72 °F) days and 6.5 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (133 mm); December is driest (3 mm).

Fatehpur, Rajasthan at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Fatehpur, Rajasthan is 2002nd warmest (25.9 °C year average), 324th sunniest (about 2,880 hours of sunshine a year) and 3681st wettest (425 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rāmgarh, Rajasthan (28.2°N 75.0°E), the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Fatehpur, Rajasthan is 0.2 °C cooler in June and about as warm in January, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.2 °C), averages 19.9 °C more than its coolest, January (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.5 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 692 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Fatehpur, Rajasthan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 692 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Fatehpur itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (27.995°N, 74.956°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-560-2549), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
