How May compares in Salem

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Salem. Compared with April: 0.4 °C cooler, 43 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. June is 2.0 °C cooler than May on average.

Salem in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Salem in May?
In May, Salem averages a day high of 35.9 °C (97 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Salem?
May is not one of Salem’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Salem in May?
100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (196 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Salem in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Salem get in May?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Salem in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
