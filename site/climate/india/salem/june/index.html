How June compares in Salem

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Salem. Compared with May: 1.9 °C cooler, 36 mm less precipitation and 83 hours less sunshine. July is 0.8 °C cooler than June on average.

Salem in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Salem in June?
In June, Salem averages a day high of 33.8 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Salem?
June is not one of Salem’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Salem in June?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (188 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Salem in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Salem get in June?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Salem in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
