How April compares in Salem

April is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Salem. Compared with March: 1.5 °C warmer, 39 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. May is 0.4 °C cooler than April on average.

Salem in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Salem in April?
In April, Salem averages a day high of 36.4 °C (98 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Salem?
April is not one of Salem’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Salem in April?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (196 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Salem in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Salem get in April?
About 254 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Salem in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
