Edappāl at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Edappāl is 535th warmest (27.5 °C year average), 2479th sunniest (about 2,497 hours of sunshine a year) and 408th wettest (2,825 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kāladi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Edappāl is about as warm in March and about as warm in July, with 10 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (29.6 °C), averages 3.7 °C more than its coolest, July (26.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.0 °C (73 °F), in October. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Edappāl has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
