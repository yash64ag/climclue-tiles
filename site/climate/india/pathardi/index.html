Pāthardi weather by month and climate

Pāthardi at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Pāthardi is 1785th warmest (26.2 °C year average), 736th sunniest (about 2,780 hours of sunshine a year) and 3236th wettest (675 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Paithan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (40 km away), Pāthardi is 0.3 °C cooler in May and about as warm in December, with 47 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.1 °C), averages 10.7 °C more than its coolest, December (21.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.4 °C (56 °F), in December. The area lies about 244 km from the sea.

Why Pāthardi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 244 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pāthardi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.173°N, 75.174°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-648-2551), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
