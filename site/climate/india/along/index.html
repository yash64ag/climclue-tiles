Along at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Along is 3556th warmest (23.3 °C year average), 2874th sunniest (about 2,431 hours of sunshine a year) and 27th wettest (4,253 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jining, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Along is 0.4 °C warmer in August and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 100 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.2 °C), averages 11.3 °C more than its coolest, January (17.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.2 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 700 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Along has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 700 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.3 °C. The 10 km area around Along, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 21.4 °C a year, 1.9 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Along itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (28.170°N, 94.801°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-558-2748), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
