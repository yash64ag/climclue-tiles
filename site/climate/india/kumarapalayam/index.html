Why Kumarapalayam has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 220 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

What is the coldest month in Kumarapalayam?
December, with an average of 25.1 °C (77 °F), day highs around 29.6 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 19.9 °C (68 °F).

What is the driest month in Kumarapalayam?

What is the sunniest month in Kumarapalayam?

Does it snow in Kumarapalayam?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Kumarapalayam?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Kumarapalayam’s GeoNames location (11.445°N, 77.711°E; ClimaClue area sq-725-2577), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 195,071 (GeoNames).
