Nalgonda at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Nalgonda is 363rd warmest (27.9 °C year average), 1429th sunniest (about 2,688 hours of sunshine a year) and 2330th wettest (942 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nakrekal, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Nalgonda is 0.2 °C cooler in May and 0.3 °C cooler in December, with 11 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.6 °C), averages 10.8 °C more than its coolest, December (22.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.7 °C (62 °F), in December. The area lies about 193 km from the sea.

Why Nalgonda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 193 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nalgonda itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (17.054°N, 79.267°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-669-2592), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
