Nalgonda at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Nalgonda is 38th warmest (28.0 °C year average), 214th sunniest (about 2,688 hours of sunshine a year) and 338th wettest (916 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Miryalaguda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Nalgonda is 0.4 °C cooler in May and 0.9 °C cooler in December, with 69 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.8 °C), averages 10.8 °C more than its coolest, December (22.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.1 °C (63 °F), in December. The area lies about 193 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nalgonda, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nalgonda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 193 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
