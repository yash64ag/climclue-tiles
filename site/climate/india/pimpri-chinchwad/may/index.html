Pimpri-Chinchwad in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pimpri-Chinchwad in May?
In May, Pimpri-Chinchwad averages a day high of 35.1 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 306 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Pimpri-Chinchwad?
May is not one of Pimpri-Chinchwad’s mild months; those are January, February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pimpri-Chinchwad in May?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (333 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Pimpri-Chinchwad in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pimpri-Chinchwad get in May?
About 306 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
