Pimpri-Chinchwad in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pimpri-Chinchwad in June?
In June, Pimpri-Chinchwad averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 199 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Pimpri-Chinchwad?
June is not one of Pimpri-Chinchwad’s mild months; those are January, February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pimpri-Chinchwad in June?
199 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (366 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Pimpri-Chinchwad in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pimpri-Chinchwad get in June?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pimpri-Chinchwad in June?
