Pimpri-Chinchwad in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pimpri-Chinchwad in April?
In April, Pimpri-Chinchwad averages a day high of 36.3 °C (97 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 292 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Pimpri-Chinchwad?
April is not one of Pimpri-Chinchwad’s mild months; those are January, February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pimpri-Chinchwad in April?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (333 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Pimpri-Chinchwad in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pimpri-Chinchwad get in April?
About 292 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pimpri-Chinchwad in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
