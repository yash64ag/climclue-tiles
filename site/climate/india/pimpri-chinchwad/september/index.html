Pimpri-Chinchwad in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pimpri-Chinchwad in September?
In September, Pimpri-Chinchwad averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 166 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Pimpri-Chinchwad?
September is not one of Pimpri-Chinchwad’s mild months; those are January, February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
