Hāflong at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Hāflong is 3643rd warmest (21.6 °C year average), 3376th sunniest (about 2,247 hours of sunshine a year) and 158th wettest (3,450 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Silchar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Hāflong is 2.9 °C cooler in August and 3.0 °C cooler in January, with 323 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.6 °C), averages 10.8 °C more than its coolest, January (14.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.6 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 322 km from the sea.

Why Hāflong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 322 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C. The 10 km area around Hāflong, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 20.4 °C a year, 1.2 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hāflong itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.165°N, 93.017°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-588-2730), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
