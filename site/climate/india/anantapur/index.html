Anantapur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Anantapur is 54th warmest (27.8 °C year average), 322nd sunniest (about 2,597 hours of sunshine a year) and 487th wettest (604 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dharmavaram, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Anantapur is 0.1 °C warmer in April and about as warm in December, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (32.5 °C), averages 9.0 °C more than its coolest, December (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.3 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 270 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Anantapur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Anantapur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 270 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
