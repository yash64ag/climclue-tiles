Purnia at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Purnia is 347th warmest (25.3 °C year average), 476th sunniest (about 2,165 hours of sunshine a year) and 78th wettest (1,710 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Katihar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Purnia is about as warm in April and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 98 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.4 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, January (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.9 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 448 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Purnia, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Purnia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 448 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
