Why Purnia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 448 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Purnia climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Purnia?
April, with an average of 29.4 °C (85 °F), day highs around 35.5 °C (96 °F) and night lows around 22.5 °C (73 °F).
What is the coldest month in Purnia?
January, with an average of 16.6 °C (62 °F), day highs around 22.9 °C (73 °F) and night lows around 10.9 °C (52 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Purnia?
July, with 418 mm (16.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,710 mm (67.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Purnia?
December, with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Purnia?
April, with about 244 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Purnia?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Purnia?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, November and December.
What type of climate does Purnia have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Purnia’s GeoNames location (25.779°N, 87.474°E; ClimaClue area sq-582-2674), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 282,248 (GeoNames).
