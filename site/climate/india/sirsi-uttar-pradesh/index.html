Sirsi, Uttar Pradesh at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Sirsi, Uttar Pradesh is 3160th warmest (24.5 °C year average), 1891st sunniest (about 2,635 hours of sunshine a year) and 1977th wettest (1,045 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sambhal, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Sirsi, Uttar Pradesh is about as warm in June and about as warm in January, with 39 mm more precipitation a year. Why Sirsi, Uttar Pradesh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 954 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sirsi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (28.639°N, 78.643°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-553-2586), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
