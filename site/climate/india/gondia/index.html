Gondiā at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Gondiā is 194th warmest (26.2 °C year average), 172nd sunniest (about 2,709 hours of sunshine a year) and 129th wettest (1,478 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rāj-Nāndgaon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (96 km away), Gondiā is 0.7 °C cooler in May and 0.3 °C cooler in December, with 229 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.3 °C), averages 14.7 °C more than its coolest, December (19.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.5 °C (55 °F), in December. The area lies about 534 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gondiā, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gondiā has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 534 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
