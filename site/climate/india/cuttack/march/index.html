Cuttack in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuttack in March?
In March, Cuttack averages a day high of 35.1 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Cuttack?
March is not one of Cuttack’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuttack in March?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (368 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Cuttack in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuttack get in March?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cuttack in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
