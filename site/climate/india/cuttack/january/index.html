How January compares in Cuttack

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Cuttack. Compared with December: 0.3 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. February is 3.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Cuttack in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuttack in January?
In January, Cuttack averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 247 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Cuttack?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Cuttack’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuttack in January?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (368 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Cuttack in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuttack get in January?
About 247 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cuttack in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
