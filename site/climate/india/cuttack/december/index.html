How December compares in Cuttack

December is the coolest month, the driest month and the sunniest month in Cuttack. Compared with November: 2.9 °C cooler, 40 mm less precipitation and 35 hours more sunshine. January is 0.3 °C warmer than December on average.

Cuttack in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuttack in December?
In December, Cuttack averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Cuttack?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Cuttack’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuttack in December?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (368 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Cuttack in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuttack get in December?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cuttack in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
