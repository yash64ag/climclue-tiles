How August compares in Cuttack

August is the 6th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Cuttack. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, 19 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. September is 0.1 °C cooler than August on average.

Cuttack in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuttack in August?
In August, Cuttack averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 25.6 °C (78 °F), with 368 mm (14.5 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Cuttack?
August is not one of Cuttack’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuttack in August?
368 mm (14.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (368 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Cuttack in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuttack get in August?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cuttack in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
