How November compares in Cuttack

November is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Cuttack. Compared with October: 2.8 °C cooler, 88 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. December is 2.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Cuttack in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuttack in November?
In November, Cuttack averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 19.1 °C (66 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Cuttack?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Cuttack’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuttack in November?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (368 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Cuttack in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuttack get in November?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cuttack in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
