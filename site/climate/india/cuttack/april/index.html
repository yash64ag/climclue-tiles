Cuttack in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuttack in April?
In April, Cuttack averages a day high of 36.6 °C (98 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Cuttack?
April is not one of Cuttack’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuttack in April?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (376 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Cuttack in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuttack get in April?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cuttack in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
