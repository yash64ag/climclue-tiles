Dharashiv at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Dharashiv is 206th warmest (26.1 °C year average), 129th sunniest (about 2,748 hours of sunshine a year) and 267th wettest (1,046 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Barshi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Dharashiv is 0.6 °C cooler in May and 0.9 °C cooler in December, with 269 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.3 °C), averages 10.5 °C more than its coolest, December (21.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.2 °C (61 °F), in December. The area lies about 320 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dharashiv, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dharashiv has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 320 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
