Baharampur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Baharampur is 287th warmest (25.7 °C year average), 492nd sunniest (about 2,114 hours of sunshine a year) and 121st wettest (1,508 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Navadwīp, the nearest covered town with different numbers (79 km away), Baharampur is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 19 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.9 °C), averages 12.5 °C more than its coolest, January (17.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.8 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 269 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Baharampur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Baharampur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 269 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
