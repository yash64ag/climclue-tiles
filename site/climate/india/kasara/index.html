Kasāra at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Kasāra is 2355th warmest (25.5 °C year average), 998th sunniest (about 2,744 hours of sunshine a year) and 184th wettest (3,267 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Igatpuri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Kasāra is 1.3 °C warmer in May and 2.0 °C warmer in January, with 226 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.0 °C), averages 6.6 °C more than its coolest, January (22.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.9 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 84 km from the sea.

Why Kasāra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 84 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kasāra itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.645°N, 73.474°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-643-2534), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
