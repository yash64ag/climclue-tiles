Byndoor sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Karnataka · India

The sea off Byndoor averages 29.9 °C (86 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 26.9 °C (80 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Byndoor sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Byndoor?
In May, at about 29.9 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Byndoor?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 26.9 °C (80 °F).
How warm is the sea in Byndoor in August?
About 26.9 °C (80 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Byndoor?
About 28.4 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
