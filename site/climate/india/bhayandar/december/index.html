Bhayandar weather in December

Average December weather in Bhayandar, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Bhayandar averages 31.4 °C (89 °F) days and 17.5 °C (64 °F) nights, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of rain over the month and about 261 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Bhayandar

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Bhayandar. Compared with November: 2.2 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. January is 1.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Bhayandar in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhayandar in December?
In December, Bhayandar averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Bhayandar?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Bhayandar’s mild months (January, February and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhayandar in December?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (866 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Bhayandar in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhayandar get in December?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Bhayandar in December?
About 26.3 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (29.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Bhayandar in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
