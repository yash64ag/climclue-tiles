Bhayandar at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Bhayandar is 134th warmest (26.8 °C year average), 189th sunniest (about 2,700 hours of sunshine a year) and 36th wettest (2,402 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Borivli, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Bhayandar is 0.2 °C warmer in May and about as warm in January, with 92 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.6 °C), averages 6.2 °C more than its coolest, January (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.0 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bhayandar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bhayandar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
