Bhayandar in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhayandar in April?
In April, Bhayandar averages a day high of 32.7 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 24.4 °C (76 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 291 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Bhayandar?
April is not one of Bhayandar’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
