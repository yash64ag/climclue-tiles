How October compares in Bhayandar

October is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Bhayandar. Compared with September: 0.7 °C warmer, 294 mm less precipitation and 72 hours more sunshine. November is 1.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Bhayandar in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhayandar in October?
In October, Bhayandar averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Bhayandar?
October is not one of Bhayandar’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhayandar in October?
85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (870 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Bhayandar in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhayandar get in October?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bhayandar in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
