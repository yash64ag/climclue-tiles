Khali Kachigam sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu · India

The sea off Khali Kachigam averages 29.6 °C (85 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 23.5 °C (74 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December.

Khali Kachigam sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Khali Kachigam?
In June, at about 29.6 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Khali Kachigam?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December. The coolest month is January, at 23.5 °C (74 °F).
How warm is the sea in Khali Kachigam in January?
About 23.5 °C (74 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Khali Kachigam?
About 27.1 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
