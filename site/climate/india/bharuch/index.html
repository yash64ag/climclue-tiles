Bharūch at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Bharūch is 88th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 44th sunniest (about 2,898 hours of sunshine a year) and 215th wettest (1,169 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Surat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Bharūch is 1.1 °C warmer in May and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 274 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.6 °C), averages 10.0 °C more than its coolest, January (21.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.3 °C (60 °F), in January. The area lies about 43 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bharūch, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bharūch has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 43 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
