Why Balasore has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Balasore climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Balasore?
May, with an average of 30.4 °C (87 °F), day highs around 33.5 °C (92 °F) and night lows around 27.8 °C (82 °F).
What is the coldest month in Balasore?
January, with an average of 20.4 °C (69 °F), day highs around 24.2 °C (76 °F) and night lows around 16.5 °C (62 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Balasore?
August, with 317 mm (12.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,762 mm (69.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Balasore?
December, with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Balasore?
April, with about 238 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Balasore?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Balasore?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Balasore’s GeoNames location (21.493°N, 86.933°E; ClimaClue area sq-625-2669), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 144,373 (GeoNames).
