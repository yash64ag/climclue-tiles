Farrukhnagar at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Farrukhnagar is 2342nd warmest (25.5 °C year average), 1575th sunniest (about 2,675 hours of sunshine a year) and 2307th wettest (949 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Srīrāmnagar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Farrukhnagar is 0.9 °C cooler in May and 0.4 °C cooler in December, with 74 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.2 °C), averages 10.1 °C more than its coolest, December (21.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.7 °C (58 °F), in December. The area lies about 273 km from the sea.

Why Farrukhnagar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 273 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
