Ooty at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Ooty is 521st warmest (15.1 °C year average), 455th sunniest (about 2,218 hours of sunshine a year) and 109th wettest (1,585 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Coimbatore, the nearest covered town with different numbers (54 km away), Ooty is 11.5 °C cooler in April and 10.4 °C cooler in December, with 764 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (17.4 °C), averages 4.3 °C more than its coolest, December (13.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.2 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 104 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ooty, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ooty has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 104 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 8.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
