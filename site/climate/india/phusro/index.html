Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Phusro is 2767th warmest (25.0 °C year average), 3492nd sunniest (about 2,204 hours of sunshine a year) and 1138th wettest (1,379 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Phusro has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 280 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
