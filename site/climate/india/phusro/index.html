Phusro at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Phusro is 394th warmest (24.9 °C year average), 459th sunniest (about 2,204 hours of sunshine a year) and 139th wettest (1,441 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bokāro, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Phusro is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 99 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.1 °C), averages 14.8 °C more than its coolest, January (16.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.0 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 280 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Phusro, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Phusro has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 280 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
