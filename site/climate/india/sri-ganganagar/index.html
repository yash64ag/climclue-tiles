Sri Ganganagar weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Sri Ganganagar, India (Rajasthan), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Sri Ganganagar is warmest in June, with 40.7 °C (105 °F) days and 27.4 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 20.6 °C (69 °F) days and 5.2 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (92 mm); November is driest (3 mm).

Sri Ganganagar at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Sri Ganganagar is 2671st warmest (25.2 °C year average), 239th sunniest (about 2,927 hours of sunshine a year) and 3723rd wettest (325 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sadulshahar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Sri Ganganagar is about as warm in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 24 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.0 °C), averages 21.1 °C more than its coolest, January (12.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.2 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 867 km from the sea (deep inland).

Best time to visit Sri Ganganagar

For mild weather, the best months to visit Sri Ganganagar are February, March and November.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): February, March and November.
  • Warmest (average temperature): June (34.0 °C), July (32.8 °C) and May (32.5 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): November (3 mm), December (4 mm) and October (7 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): May (284 h), October (277 h) and April (273 h).

Why Sri Ganganagar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 867 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sri Ganganagar itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (29.920°N, 73.875°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-540-2538), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
