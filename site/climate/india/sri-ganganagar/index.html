Sri Ganganagar at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Sri Ganganagar is the 3rd driest (325 mm a year). Compared with Abohar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (40 km away), Sri Ganganagar is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 61 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.0 °C), averages 21.1 °C more than its coolest, January (12.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.5 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 867 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sri Ganganagar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Sri Ganganagar

For mild weather, the best months to visit Sri Ganganagar are February, March and November.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): February, March and November.
  • Warmest (average temperature): June (34.0 °C), July (32.9 °C) and May (32.5 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): November (3 mm), December (4 mm) and October (7 mm).

Why Sri Ganganagar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 867 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
