Latur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Latur is 253rd warmest (25.9 °C year average), 134th sunniest (about 2,746 hours of sunshine a year) and 350th wettest (882 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Udgīr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Latur is 0.3 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 158 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.5 °C), averages 11.4 °C more than its coolest, December (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.1 °C (59 °F), in December. The area lies about 376 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Latur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Latur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 376 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
