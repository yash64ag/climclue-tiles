Tirkadavūr at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Tirkadavūr is 944th warmest (27.0 °C year average), 2959th sunniest (about 2,415 hours of sunshine a year) and 482nd wettest (2,469 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Karuva, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Tirkadavūr is 0.1 °C cooler in April and 0.1 °C cooler in August, with 21 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.5 °C), averages 2.5 °C more than its coolest, August (26.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.5 °C (73 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Tirkadavūr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
