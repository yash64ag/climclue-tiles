Bhālswa Jahangirpur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Bhālswa Jahangirpur is 414th warmest (24.8 °C year average), 219th sunniest (about 2,686 hours of sunshine a year) and 485th wettest (609 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sultan Pur Majra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Bhālswa Jahangirpur is 0.1 °C cooler in June and about as warm in January, with 14 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.4 °C), averages 19.9 °C more than its coolest, January (13.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.5 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 872 km from the sea (deep inland).
