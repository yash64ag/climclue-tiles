Udgīr at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Udgīr is 225th warmest (26.0 °C year average), 142nd sunniest (about 2,739 hours of sunshine a year) and 274th wettest (1,040 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Latur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Udgīr is 0.3 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 158 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.8 °C), averages 11.8 °C more than its coolest, December (20.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.0 °C (59 °F), in December. The area lies about 439 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Udgīr, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Udgīr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 439 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
