Valabhīpur weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Valabhīpur, India (Gujarat), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Valabhīpur at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Valabhīpur is 133rd warmest (28.5 °C year average), 171st sunniest (about 2,967 hours of sunshine a year) and 2959th wettest (774 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sihor, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Valabhīpur is 0.7 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.8 °C), averages 13.3 °C more than its coolest, January (21.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.5 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 41 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Valabhīpur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Valabhīpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 41 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Valabhīpur itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.889°N, 71.879°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-621-2518), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
