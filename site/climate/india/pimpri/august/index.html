How August compares in Pimpri

August is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Pimpri. Compared with July: 0.5 °C cooler, 127 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. September is 0.4 °C warmer than August on average.

Pimpri in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pimpri in August?
In August, Pimpri averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 20.7 °C (69 °F), with 239 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation and about 102 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Pimpri?
August is not one of Pimpri’s mild months; those are January, February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pimpri in August?
239 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (366 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Pimpri in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pimpri get in August?
About 102 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pimpri in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
