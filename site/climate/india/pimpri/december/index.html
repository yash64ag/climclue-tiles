How December compares in Pimpri

December is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Pimpri. Compared with November: 2.0 °C cooler, 21 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. January is 0.1 °C warmer than December on average.

Pimpri in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pimpri in December?
In December, Pimpri averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 15.1 °C (59 °F), with 6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 281 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Pimpri?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Pimpri’s mild months (January, February, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pimpri in December?
6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (333 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Pimpri in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pimpri get in December?
About 281 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pimpri in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
