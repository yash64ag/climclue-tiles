Pimpri at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Pimpri is 451st warmest (24.5 °C year average), 94th sunniest (about 2,789 hours of sunshine a year) and 262nd wettest (1,061 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pune, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Pimpri is about as warm in April and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 50 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.3 °C), averages 7.2 °C more than its coolest, December (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.7 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 106 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pimpri, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pimpri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 106 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
