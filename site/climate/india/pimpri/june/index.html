How June compares in Pimpri

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Pimpri. Compared with May: 2.2 °C cooler, 160 mm more precipitation and 132 hours less sunshine. July is 1.7 °C cooler than June on average.

Pimpri in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pimpri in June?
In June, Pimpri averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 188 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Pimpri?
June is not one of Pimpri’s mild months; those are January, February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pimpri in June?
188 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (333 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Pimpri in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pimpri get in June?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pimpri in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
