Bakhtiyārpur at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Bakhtiyārpur is 2068th warmest (25.8 °C year average), 3482nd sunniest (about 2,211 hours of sunshine a year) and 1964th wettest (1,048 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bakhtiarpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Bakhtiyārpur is about as warm in May and about as warm in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.1 °C), averages 16.0 °C more than its coolest, January (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.7 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 474 km from the sea.

Best time to visit Bakhtiyārpur

For mild weather, the best months to visit Bakhtiyārpur are January, February, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): May (32.1 °C), June (31.6 °C) and April (30.6 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): November (6 mm), December (7 mm) and March (8 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): April (259 h), March (238 h) and May (222 h).

Why Bakhtiyārpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 474 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bakhtiyārpur itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.462°N, 85.532°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-585-2655), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
