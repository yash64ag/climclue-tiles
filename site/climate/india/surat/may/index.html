How May compares in Surat

May is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Surat. Compared with April: 0.9 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. June is 0.7 °C cooler than May on average.

Surat in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surat in May?
In May, Surat averages a day high of 34.0 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 27.1 °C (81 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 320 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Surat?
May is not one of Surat’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surat in May?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (602 mm), the driest March (0 mm).
Does it snow in Surat in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Surat get in May?
About 320 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Surat in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
