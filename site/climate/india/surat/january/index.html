How January compares in Surat

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Surat. Compared with December: 1.2 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. February is 1.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Surat in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surat in January?
In January, Surat averages a day high of 27.8 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 17.2 °C (63 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 282 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Surat?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Surat’s mild months (January, February and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surat in January?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (602 mm), the driest March (0 mm).
Does it snow in Surat in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Surat get in January?
About 282 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Surat in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
