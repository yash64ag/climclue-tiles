How November compares in Surat

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Surat. Compared with October: 2.1 °C cooler, 36 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. December is 2.8 °C cooler than November on average.

Surat in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surat in November?
In November, Surat averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 21.2 °C (70 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Surat?
November is not one of Surat’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surat in November?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (602 mm), the driest March (0 mm).
Does it snow in Surat in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Surat get in November?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Surat in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
