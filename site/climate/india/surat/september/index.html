How September compares in Surat

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Surat. Compared with August: 0.5 °C warmer, 125 mm less precipitation and 97 hours more sunshine. October is 0.5 °C warmer than September on average.

Surat in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Surat in September?
In September, Surat averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 25.4 °C (78 °F), with 215 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Surat?
September is not one of Surat’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Surat in September?
215 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (602 mm), the driest March (0 mm).
Does it snow in Surat in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Surat get in September?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Surat in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
