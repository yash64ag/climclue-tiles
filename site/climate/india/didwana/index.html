Dīdwāna weather by month and climate

Dīdwāna at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Dīdwāna is 2006th warmest (25.9 °C year average), 248th sunniest (about 2,921 hours of sunshine a year) and 3721st wettest (334 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lādnūn, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Dīdwāna is 0.2 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 5 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.9 °C), averages 19.1 °C more than its coolest, January (14.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.6 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 626 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Dīdwāna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 626 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dīdwāna itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (27.401°N, 74.575°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-565-2545), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
