Neyveli at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Neyveli is 29th warmest (28.2 °C year average), 342nd sunniest (about 2,554 hours of sunshine a year) and 168th wettest (1,293 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cuddalore, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Neyveli is 1.4 °C warmer in May and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 21 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.1 °C), averages 8.0 °C more than its coolest, January (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.5 °C (67 °F), in January. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Neyveli, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Neyveli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
