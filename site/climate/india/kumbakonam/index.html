Kumbakonam at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Kumbakonam is 7th warmest (28.7 °C year average), 354th sunniest (about 2,529 hours of sunshine a year) and 219th wettest (1,165 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Thanjavur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Kumbakonam is 0.2 °C warmer in May and about as warm in January, with 114 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.1 °C), averages 7.4 °C more than its coolest, January (24.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.3 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 66 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kumbakonam, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kumbakonam has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 66 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
