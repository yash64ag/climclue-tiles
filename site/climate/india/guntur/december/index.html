How December compares in Guntur

December is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Guntur. Compared with November: 1.5 °C cooler, 41 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. January is 0.4 °C warmer than December on average.

Guntur in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guntur in December?
In December, Guntur averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 19.1 °C (66 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Guntur?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Guntur’s mild months (January and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guntur in December?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (189 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Guntur in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guntur get in December?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guntur in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
