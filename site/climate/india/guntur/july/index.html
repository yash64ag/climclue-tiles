How July compares in Guntur

July is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Guntur. Compared with June: 2.1 °C cooler, 70 mm more precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. August is 0.6 °C cooler than July on average.

Guntur in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guntur in July?
In July, Guntur averages a day high of 33.1 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 26.1 °C (79 °F), with 187 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Guntur?
July is not one of Guntur’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guntur in July?
187 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (189 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Guntur in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guntur get in July?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guntur in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
