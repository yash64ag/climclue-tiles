How February compares in Guntur

February is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Guntur. Compared with January: 2.1 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. March is 2.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Guntur in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guntur in February?
In February, Guntur averages a day high of 32.4 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 20.7 °C (69 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Guntur?
February is not one of Guntur’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guntur in February?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (186 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Guntur in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guntur get in February?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guntur in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
