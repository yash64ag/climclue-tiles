Guntur weather in May

Average May weather in Guntur, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Guntur averages 38.7 °C (102 °F) days and 27.6 °C (82 °F) nights, with 56 mm (2.2 in) of rain over the month and about 273 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Guntur

May is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Guntur. Compared with April: 2.0 °C warmer, 37 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. June is 1.2 °C cooler than May on average.

Guntur in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guntur in May?
In May, Guntur averages a day high of 38.7 °C (102 °F) and a night low of 27.6 °C (82 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 273 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Guntur?
May is not one of Guntur’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guntur in May?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (189 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Guntur in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guntur get in May?
About 273 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guntur in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
