How November compares in Guntur

November is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Guntur. Compared with October: 2.0 °C cooler, 125 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. December is 1.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Guntur in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guntur in November?
In November, Guntur averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Guntur?
November is not one of Guntur’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guntur in November?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (186 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Guntur in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guntur get in November?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guntur in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
