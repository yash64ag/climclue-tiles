Why Guntur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 67 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Guntur climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Guntur?
May, with an average of 33.3 °C (92 °F), day highs around 38.9 °C (102 °F) and night lows around 28.1 °C (83 °F).
What is the coldest month in Guntur?
December, with an average of 24.1 °C (75 °F), day highs around 29.0 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 19.0 °C (66 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Guntur?
August, with 186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,003 mm (39.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Guntur?
January, with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Guntur?
April, with about 274 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Guntur?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Guntur?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January and December.
What type of climate does Guntur have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Guntur’s GeoNames location (16.300°N, 80.457°E; ClimaClue area sq-676-2604), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 670,073 (GeoNames).
