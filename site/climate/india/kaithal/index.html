Kaithal at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Kaithal is 468th warmest (24.1 °C year average), 148th sunniest (about 2,735 hours of sunshine a year) and 449th wettest (680 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Thānesar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Kaithal is 0.3 °C warmer in June and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 165 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (32.8 °C), averages 20.3 °C more than its coolest, January (12.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.8 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 945 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kaithal, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kaithal has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 945 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
