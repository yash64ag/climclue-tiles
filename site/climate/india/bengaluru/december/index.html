How December compares in Bengaluru

December is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Bengaluru. Compared with November: 0.9 °C cooler, 37 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. January is 0.6 °C warmer than December on average.

Bengaluru in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bengaluru in December?
In December, Bengaluru averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Bengaluru?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Bengaluru’s mild months (January, February, June, July, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bengaluru in December?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (174 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Bengaluru in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bengaluru get in December?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bengaluru in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
