How March compares in Bengaluru

March is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Bengaluru. Compared with February: 2.3 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. April is 1.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Bengaluru in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bengaluru in March?
In March, Bengaluru averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 18.6 °C (65 °F), with 14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 269 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bengaluru?
March is not one of Bengaluru’s mild months; those are January, February, June, July, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bengaluru in March?
14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (174 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Bengaluru in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bengaluru get in March?
About 269 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bengaluru in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
