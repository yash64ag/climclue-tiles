How April compares in Bengaluru

April is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Bengaluru. Compared with March: 1.1 °C warmer, 39 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. May is 0.8 °C cooler than April on average.

Bengaluru in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bengaluru in April?
In April, Bengaluru averages a day high of 32.8 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 20.7 °C (69 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 257 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Bengaluru?
April is not one of Bengaluru’s mild months; those are January, February, June, July, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bengaluru in April?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (174 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Bengaluru in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bengaluru get in April?
About 257 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bengaluru in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
