Raurkela Industrial Township at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Raurkela Industrial Township is 234th warmest (26.0 °C year average), 420th sunniest (about 2,361 hours of sunshine a year) and 135th wettest (1,452 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sambalpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (123 km away), Raurkela Industrial Township is 1.5 °C cooler in May and 2.3 °C cooler in January, with 226 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.0 °C), averages 12.9 °C more than its coolest, January (19.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.5 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 244 km from the sea.

Why Raurkela Industrial Township has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 244 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
