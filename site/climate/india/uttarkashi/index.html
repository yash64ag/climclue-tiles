Uttarkāshi at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Uttarkāshi is 3689th warmest (17.2 °C year average), 2125th sunniest (about 2,581 hours of sunshine a year) and 643rd wettest (1,895 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tehri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Uttarkāshi is 3.8 °C cooler in July and 3.9 °C cooler in January, with 317 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.5 °C), averages 15.1 °C more than its coolest, January (8.4 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 1,127 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Uttarkāshi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Uttarkāshi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,127 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.1 °C. The 10 km area around Uttarkāshi, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 14.7 °C a year, 2.4 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Uttarkāshi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (30.730°N, 78.443°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-532-2584), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
