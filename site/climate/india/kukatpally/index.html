Kukatpally at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Kukatpally is 197th warmest (26.2 °C year average), 197th sunniest (about 2,694 hours of sunshine a year) and 337th wettest (919 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Quthbullapur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Kukatpally is 0.3 °C warmer in May and 0.3 °C warmer in December, with 110 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.1 °C), averages 10.8 °C more than its coolest, December (21.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.5 °C (60 °F), in December. The area lies about 285 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kukatpally, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kukatpally has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 285 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
