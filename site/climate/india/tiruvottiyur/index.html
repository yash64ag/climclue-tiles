Tiruvottiyūr at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Tiruvottiyūr is 45th warmest (28.0 °C year average), 248th sunniest (about 2,674 hours of sunshine a year) and 150th wettest (1,372 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Madhavaram, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Tiruvottiyūr is 0.3 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 15 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.6 °C), averages 5.8 °C more than its coolest, January (24.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.4 °C (72 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tiruvottiyūr, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tiruvottiyūr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
