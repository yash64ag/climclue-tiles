Malappuram at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Malappuram is 123rd warmest (26.9 °C year average), 364th sunniest (about 2,505 hours of sunshine a year) and 13th wettest (3,126 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ponnāni, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Malappuram is 0.4 °C cooler in March and 0.5 °C cooler in July, with 272 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (29.1 °C), averages 3.6 °C more than its coolest, July (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.6 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Malappuram, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Malappuram has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
