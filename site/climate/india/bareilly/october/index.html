Bareilly in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bareilly in October?
In October, Bareilly averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 18.6 °C (65 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 253 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Bareilly?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Bareilly’s mild months (February, March, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bareilly in October?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (353 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Bareilly in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bareilly get in October?
About 253 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bareilly in October?
