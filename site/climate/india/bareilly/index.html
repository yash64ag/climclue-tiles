Bareilly at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Bareilly is 440th warmest (24.6 °C year average), 313th sunniest (about 2,614 hours of sunshine a year) and 245th wettest (1,090 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pīlibhīt, the nearest covered town with different numbers (47 km away), Bareilly is 0.9 °C warmer in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 520 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (32.1 °C), averages 18.0 °C more than its coolest, January (14.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.4 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 985 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bareilly, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bareilly has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 985 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
