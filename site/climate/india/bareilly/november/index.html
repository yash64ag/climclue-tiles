How November compares in Bareilly

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Bareilly. Compared with October: 4.9 °C cooler, 35 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. December is 4.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Bareilly in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bareilly in November?
In November, Bareilly averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 13.7 °C (57 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Bareilly?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Bareilly’s mild months (February, March, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bareilly in November?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (351 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Bareilly in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bareilly get in November?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bareilly in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
