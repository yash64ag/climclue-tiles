How March compares in Mumbai

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Mumbai. Compared with February: 1.9 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. April is 1.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Mumbai in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mumbai in March?
In March, Mumbai averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 279 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Mumbai?
March is not one of Mumbai’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mumbai in March?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (985 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mumbai in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mumbai get in March?
About 279 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mumbai in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
