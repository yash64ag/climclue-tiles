How October compares in Mumbai

October is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Mumbai. Compared with September: 0.9 °C warmer, 324 mm less precipitation and 71 hours more sunshine. November is 1.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Mumbai in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mumbai in October?
In October, Mumbai averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 25.4 °C (78 °F), with 85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Mumbai?
October is not one of Mumbai’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mumbai in October?
85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (981 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mumbai in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mumbai get in October?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mumbai in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
