Mumbai at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Mumbai is 165th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 260th sunniest (about 2,667 hours of sunshine a year) and 23rd wettest (2,706 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Airoli, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Mumbai is 0.6 °C cooler in May and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 77 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.9 °C), averages 5.0 °C more than its coolest, January (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.8 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mumbai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Mumbai

For mild weather, the best months to visit Mumbai are January, February and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): May (28.9 °C), June (28.1 °C) and October (27.8 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): February (1 mm), March (1 mm) and April (1 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): May (304 h), April (290 h) and March (279 h).

Why Mumbai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
