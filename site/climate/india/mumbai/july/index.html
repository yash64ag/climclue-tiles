How July compares in Mumbai

July is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Mumbai. Compared with June: 1.3 °C cooler, 441 mm more precipitation and 76 hours less sunshine. August is 0.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Mumbai in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mumbai in July?
In July, Mumbai averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.4 °C (76 °F), with 985 mm (38.8 in) of precipitation and about 90 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Mumbai?
July is not one of Mumbai’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mumbai in July?
985 mm (38.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (985 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Mumbai in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mumbai get in July?
About 90 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Mumbai in July?
About 28.3 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (29.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Mumbai in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
