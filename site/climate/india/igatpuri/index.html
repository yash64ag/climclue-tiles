Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Igatpuri is 3421st warmest (23.8 °C year average), 992nd sunniest (about 2,745 hours of sunshine a year) and 275th wettest (3,042 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Igatpuri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 94 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
