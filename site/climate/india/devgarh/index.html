Devgarh at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Devgarh is 3226th warmest (24.4 °C year average), 153rd sunniest (about 2,982 hours of sunshine a year) and 3344th wettest (646 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Amet, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Devgarh is 0.4 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 26 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.0 °C), averages 16.4 °C more than its coolest, January (15.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.0 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 408 km from the sea.

Why Devgarh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 408 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Devgarh itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.525°N, 73.908°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-584-2539), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
