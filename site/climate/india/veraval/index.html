Verāval at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Verāval is 190th warmest (26.3 °C year average), 25th sunniest (about 2,971 hours of sunshine a year) and 294th wettest (1,010 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jūnāgadh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (69 km away), Verāval is 0.9 °C cooler in June and 2.3 °C warmer in January, with 51 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.1 °C), averages 7.0 °C more than its coolest, January (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.9 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Verāval, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Verāval has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
