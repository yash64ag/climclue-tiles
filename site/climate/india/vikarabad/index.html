Vikārābād at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Vikārābād is 2325th warmest (25.6 °C year average), 1393rd sunniest (about 2,691 hours of sunshine a year) and 1813th wettest (1,097 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Singāpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Vikārābād is 0.4 °C cooler in May and about as warm in December, with 116 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.5 °C), averages 10.5 °C more than its coolest, December (21.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.9 °C (59 °F), in December. The area lies about 318 km from the sea.

Why Vikārābād has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 318 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
