Bicholim at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Bicholim is 1496th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 643rd sunniest (about 2,791 hours of sunshine a year) and 81st wettest (3,779 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Bicholim has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
