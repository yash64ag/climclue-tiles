Kadapa at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Kadapa is 13th warmest (28.6 °C year average), 314th sunniest (about 2,614 hours of sunshine a year) and 454th wettest (676 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Proddatūr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Kadapa is 0.5 °C cooler in May and 0.7 °C cooler in December, with 33 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.4 °C), averages 9.8 °C more than its coolest, December (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.6 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 147 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kadapa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kadapa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 147 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
