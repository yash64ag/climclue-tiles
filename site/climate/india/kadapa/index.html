Why Kadapa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 147 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Kadapa climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Kadapa?
May, with an average of 33.4 °C (92 °F), day highs around 38.9 °C (102 °F) and night lows around 27.8 °C (82 °F).
What is the coldest month in Kadapa?
December, with an average of 23.6 °C (74 °F), day highs around 28.3 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 18.3 °C (65 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Kadapa?
October, with 145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 676 mm (26.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Kadapa?
February, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Kadapa?
March, with about 277 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Kadapa?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Kadapa?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January and December.
What type of climate does Kadapa have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Kadapa’s GeoNames location (14.480°N, 78.823°E; ClimaClue area sq-695-2588), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 344,893 (GeoNames).
