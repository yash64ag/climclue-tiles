Bihār Sharīf at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Bihār Sharīf is 272nd warmest (25.7 °C year average), 456th sunniest (about 2,217 hours of sunshine a year) and 275th wettest (1,035 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jahānābād, the nearest covered town with different numbers (54 km away), Bihār Sharīf is 0.5 °C cooler in May and about as warm in January, with 16 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.4 °C), averages 16.4 °C more than its coolest, January (15.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.3 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 453 km from the sea.

Why Bihār Sharīf has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 453 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
