How November compares in Amravati

November is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Amravati. Compared with October: 3.1 °C cooler, 43 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. December is 2.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Amravati in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amravati in November?
In November, Amravati averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 16.6 °C (62 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Amravati?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Amravati’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amravati in November?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (273 mm), the driest April (4 mm).
Does it snow in Amravati in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Amravati get in November?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amravati in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
