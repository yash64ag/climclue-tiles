How May compares in Amravati

May is the warmest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Amravati. Compared with April: 2.2 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. June is 3.5 °C cooler than May on average.

Amravati in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amravati in May?
In May, Amravati averages a day high of 40.8 °C (105 °F) and a night low of 28.2 °C (83 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 300 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Amravati?
May is not one of Amravati’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amravati in May?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (273 mm), the driest April (4 mm).
Does it snow in Amravati in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Amravati get in May?
About 300 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amravati in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
