How October compares in Amravati

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Amravati. Compared with September: 0.9 °C cooler, 116 mm less precipitation and 78 hours more sunshine. November is 3.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Amravati in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amravati in October?
In October, Amravati averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Amravati?
October is not one of Amravati’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amravati in October?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (273 mm), the driest April (4 mm).
Does it snow in Amravati in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Amravati get in October?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amravati in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
