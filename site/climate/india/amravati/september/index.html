How September compares in Amravati

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Amravati. Compared with August: 0.6 °C warmer, 37 mm less precipitation and 69 hours more sunshine. October is 0.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Amravati in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amravati in September?
In September, Amravati averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 177 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Amravati?
September is not one of Amravati’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amravati in September?
177 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (273 mm), the driest April (4 mm).
Does it snow in Amravati in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Amravati get in September?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amravati in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
