Trāl weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Trāl, India (Jammu and Kashmir), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Trāl is warmest in July, with 27.7 °C (82 °F) days and 16.3 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 4.1 °C (39 °F) days and -4.2 °C nights. Most rain falls around March (147 mm); November is driest (39 mm).

Why Trāl has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,272 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.0 °C. The 10 km area around Trāl, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 8.4 °C a year, 3.1 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Trāl itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.927°N, 75.116°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-500-2551), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
