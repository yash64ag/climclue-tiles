Bānda at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Bānda is 254th warmest (25.9 °C year average), 170th sunniest (about 2,710 hours of sunshine a year) and 315th wettest (964 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fatehpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (69 km away), Bānda is 0.8 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 100 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.4 °C), averages 18.9 °C more than its coolest, January (15.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.3 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 815 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bānda, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bānda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 815 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
