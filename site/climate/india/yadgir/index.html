Yadgir at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Yadgir is 446th warmest (27.7 °C year average), 1263rd sunniest (about 2,708 hours of sunshine a year) and 2825th wettest (817 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gurmatkāl, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Yadgir is 1.8 °C warmer in May and 1.8 °C warmer in December, with 196 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.4 °C), averages 9.8 °C more than its coolest, December (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.3 °C (63 °F), in December. The area lies about 361 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yadgir, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yadgir has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 361 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Yadgir itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (16.770°N, 77.138°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-672-2571), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
