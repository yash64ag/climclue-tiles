How September compares in Visakhapatnam

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Visakhapatnam. Compared with August: 0.1 °C cooler, 62 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. October is 0.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Visakhapatnam in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Visakhapatnam in September?
In September, Visakhapatnam averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 26.0 °C (79 °F), with 219 mm (8.6 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Visakhapatnam?
September is not one of Visakhapatnam’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Visakhapatnam in September?
219 mm (8.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (248 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Visakhapatnam in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Visakhapatnam get in September?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Visakhapatnam in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
