How August compares in Visakhapatnam

August is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Visakhapatnam. Compared with July: 0.4 °C cooler, 11 mm more precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. September is 0.1 °C cooler than August on average.

Visakhapatnam in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Visakhapatnam in August?
In August, Visakhapatnam averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 26.3 °C (79 °F), with 163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Visakhapatnam?
August is not one of Visakhapatnam’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Visakhapatnam in August?
163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (268 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Visakhapatnam in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Visakhapatnam get in August?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Visakhapatnam in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
