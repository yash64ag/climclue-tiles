Visakhapatnam at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Visakhapatnam is 98th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 341st sunniest (about 2,555 hours of sunshine a year) and 222nd wettest (1,147 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gajuwaka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Visakhapatnam is 0.3 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 11 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.1 °C), averages 6.9 °C more than its coolest, January (23.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.5 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Visakhapatnam, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Visakhapatnam

For mild weather, the best months to visit Visakhapatnam are January and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): May (30.1 °C), June (29.9 °C) and April (28.9 °C).

Why Visakhapatnam has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
