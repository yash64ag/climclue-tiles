How October compares in Visakhapatnam

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Visakhapatnam. Compared with September: 0.8 °C cooler, 37 mm more precipitation and 53 hours more sunshine. November is 1.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Visakhapatnam in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Visakhapatnam in October?
In October, Visakhapatnam averages a day high of 29.2 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 268 mm (10.6 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Visakhapatnam?
October is not one of Visakhapatnam’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Visakhapatnam in October?
268 mm (10.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is October (268 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Visakhapatnam in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Visakhapatnam get in October?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Visakhapatnam in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
