How November compares in Visakhapatnam

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Visakhapatnam. Compared with October: 1.8 °C cooler, 186 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. December is 1.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Visakhapatnam in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Visakhapatnam in November?
In November, Visakhapatnam averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Visakhapatnam?
November is not one of Visakhapatnam’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Visakhapatnam in November?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (268 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Visakhapatnam in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Visakhapatnam get in November?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Visakhapatnam in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
