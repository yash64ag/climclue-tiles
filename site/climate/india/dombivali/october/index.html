How October compares in Dombivali

October is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Dombivali. Compared with September: 0.6 °C warmer, 254 mm less precipitation and 72 hours more sunshine. November is 1.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Dombivali in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dombivali in October?
In October, Dombivali averages a day high of 32.9 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 247 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Dombivali?
October is not one of Dombivali’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dombivali in October?
88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (776 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Dombivali in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dombivali get in October?
About 247 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dombivali in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
