Hassan at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Hassan is 512th warmest (22.3 °C year average), 387th sunniest (about 2,445 hours of sunshine a year) and 308th wettest (976 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chikmagalūr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (49 km away), Hassan is 1.5 °C warmer in April and 1.2 °C warmer in December, with 599 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (25.4 °C), averages 5.4 °C more than its coolest, December (20.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.1 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 137 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hassan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hassan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 137 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
