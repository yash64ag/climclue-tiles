Thiruvananthapuram weather in January

Average January weather in Thiruvananthapuram, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Thiruvananthapuram averages 30.8 °C (87 °F) days and 21.5 °C (71 °F) nights, with 24 mm (1.0 in) of rain over the month and about 261 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Thiruvananthapuram

January is the 5th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Thiruvananthapuram. Compared with December: 0.2 °C cooler, 37 mm less precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. February is 0.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Thiruvananthapuram in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Thiruvananthapuram in January?
In January, Thiruvananthapuram averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Thiruvananthapuram?
No month in Thiruvananthapuram is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Thiruvananthapuram in January?
24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (314 mm), the driest January (24 mm).
Does it snow in Thiruvananthapuram in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Thiruvananthapuram get in January?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Thiruvananthapuram in January?
About 28.3 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is April (30.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Thiruvananthapuram in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
