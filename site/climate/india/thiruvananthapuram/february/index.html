How February compares in Thiruvananthapuram

February is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Thiruvananthapuram. Compared with January: 0.4 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. March is 0.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Thiruvananthapuram in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Thiruvananthapuram in February?
In February, Thiruvananthapuram averages a day high of 29.2 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Thiruvananthapuram?
No month in Thiruvananthapuram is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Thiruvananthapuram in February?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (314 mm), the driest January (24 mm).
Does it snow in Thiruvananthapuram in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Thiruvananthapuram get in February?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Thiruvananthapuram in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
