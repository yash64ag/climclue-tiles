How April compares in Thiruvananthapuram

April is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Thiruvananthapuram. Compared with March: 0.5 °C warmer, 85 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. May is 0.2 °C cooler than April on average.

Thiruvananthapuram in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Thiruvananthapuram in April?
In April, Thiruvananthapuram averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 25.4 °C (78 °F), with 144 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Thiruvananthapuram?
No month in Thiruvananthapuram is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Thiruvananthapuram in April?
144 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (314 mm), the driest January (24 mm).
Does it snow in Thiruvananthapuram in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Thiruvananthapuram get in April?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Thiruvananthapuram in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
