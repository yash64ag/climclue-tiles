How November compares in Thiruvananthapuram

November is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Thiruvananthapuram. Compared with October: 0.1 °C warmer, 61 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. December is about as warm as November on average.

Thiruvananthapuram in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Thiruvananthapuram in November?
In November, Thiruvananthapuram averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 249 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Thiruvananthapuram?
No month in Thiruvananthapuram is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Thiruvananthapuram in November?
249 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (310 mm), the driest January (24 mm).
Does it snow in Thiruvananthapuram in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Thiruvananthapuram get in November?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Thiruvananthapuram in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
