How March compares in Thiruvananthapuram

March is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Thiruvananthapuram. Compared with February: 0.8 °C warmer, 25 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. April is 0.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Thiruvananthapuram in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Thiruvananthapuram in March?
In March, Thiruvananthapuram averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Thiruvananthapuram?
No month in Thiruvananthapuram is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Thiruvananthapuram in March?
57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (310 mm), the driest January (24 mm).
Does it snow in Thiruvananthapuram in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Thiruvananthapuram get in March?
About 254 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Thiruvananthapuram in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
