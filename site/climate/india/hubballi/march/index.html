Hubballi in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hubballi in March?
In March, Hubballi averages a day high of 34.8 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 20.1 °C (68 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 280 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Hubballi?
March is not one of Hubballi’s mild months; those are January, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
