Hubballi in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hubballi in August?
In August, Hubballi averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 116 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Hubballi?
August is not one of Hubballi’s mild months; those are January, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hubballi in August?
123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (169 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Hubballi in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hubballi get in August?
About 116 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hubballi in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
