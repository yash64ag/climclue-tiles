Hubballi in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hubballi in April?
In April, Hubballi averages a day high of 35.6 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 276 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Hubballi?
April is not one of Hubballi’s mild months; those are January, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hubballi in April?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (166 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Hubballi in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hubballi get in April?
About 276 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hubballi in April?
