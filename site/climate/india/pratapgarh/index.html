Pratāpgarh at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Pratāpgarh is 2722nd warmest (25.1 °C year average), 269th sunniest (about 2,909 hours of sunshine a year) and 2232nd wettest (969 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Panth Pīplia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Pratāpgarh is 1.0 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 112 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.4 °C), averages 14.6 °C more than its coolest, January (17.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.2 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 316 km from the sea.

Why Pratāpgarh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 316 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-599-2547), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
