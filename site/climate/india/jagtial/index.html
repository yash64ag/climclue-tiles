Why Jagtiāl has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 370 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Jagtiāl climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Jagtiāl?
May, with an average of 34.1 °C (93 °F), day highs around 39.5 °C (103 °F) and night lows around 28.3 °C (83 °F).
What is the coldest month in Jagtiāl?
December, with an average of 22.1 °C (72 °F), day highs around 28.0 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 15.8 °C (60 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Jagtiāl?
July, with 273 mm (10.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,063 mm (41.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Jagtiāl?
December, with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Jagtiāl?
May, with about 286 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Jagtiāl?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Jagtiāl?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Jagtiāl’s GeoNames location (18.795°N, 78.917°E; ClimaClue area sq-652-2589), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 103,930 (GeoNames).
