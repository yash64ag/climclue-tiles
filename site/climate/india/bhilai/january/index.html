How January compares in Bhilai

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Bhilai. Compared with December: 0.4 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. February is 3.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Bhilai in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhilai in January?
In January, Bhilai averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 13.3 °C (56 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 253 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Bhilai?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Bhilai’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhilai in January?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (363 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Bhilai in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhilai get in January?
About 253 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bhilai in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
