How October compares in Bhilai

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Bhilai. Compared with September: 1.4 °C cooler, 164 mm less precipitation and 73 hours more sunshine. November is 3.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Bhilai in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhilai in October?
In October, Bhilai averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 20.5 °C (69 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 239 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Bhilai?
October is not one of Bhilai’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhilai in October?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (365 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Bhilai in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhilai get in October?
About 239 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bhilai in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
