How April compares in Bhilai

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Bhilai. Compared with March: 4.6 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. May is 2.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Bhilai in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhilai in April?
In April, Bhilai averages a day high of 39.3 °C (103 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 272 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Bhilai?
April is not one of Bhilai’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhilai in April?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (363 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Bhilai in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhilai get in April?
About 272 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bhilai in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
