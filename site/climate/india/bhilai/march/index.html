Bhilai weather in March

Average March weather in Bhilai, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Bhilai averages 35.4 °C (96 °F) days and 20.4 °C (69 °F) nights, with 18 mm (0.7 in) of rain over the month and about 265 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Bhilai

March is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Bhilai. Compared with February: 4.8 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. April is 4.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Bhilai in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhilai in March?
In March, Bhilai averages a day high of 35.4 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 265 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bhilai?
March is not one of Bhilai’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhilai in March?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (365 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Bhilai in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhilai get in March?
About 265 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bhilai in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
