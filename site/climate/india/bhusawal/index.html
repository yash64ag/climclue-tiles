Bhusawal at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Bhusawal is 100th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 66th sunniest (about 2,839 hours of sunshine a year) and 384th wettest (825 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jalgaon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Bhusawal is 0.2 °C warmer in May and about as warm in December, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.9 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, December (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.8 °C (59 °F), in January. The area lies about 303 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bhusawal, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bhusawal has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 303 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
