Dārjiling at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Dārjiling is 519th warmest (16.0 °C year average), 458th sunniest (about 2,214 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (3,552 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Siliguri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Dārjiling is 6.9 °C cooler in August and 8.1 °C cooler in January, with 467 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (20.9 °C), averages 12.0 °C more than its coolest, January (8.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 565 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dārjiling, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dārjiling has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 565 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 11.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
