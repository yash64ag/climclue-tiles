Aizawl at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Aizawl is 515th warmest (21.0 °C year average), 466th sunniest (about 2,191 hours of sunshine a year) and 25th wettest (2,636 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Silchar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (122 km away), Aizawl is 3.8 °C cooler in June and 3.6 °C cooler in January, with 1,165 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (24.4 °C), averages 10.1 °C more than its coolest, January (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.8 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 181 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Aizawl, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Aizawl has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 181 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
