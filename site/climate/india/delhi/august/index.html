How August compares in Delhi

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Delhi. Compared with July: 1.1 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. September is 0.9 °C cooler than August on average.

Delhi in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Delhi in August?
In August, Delhi averages a day high of 32.5 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 25.9 °C (79 °F), with 169 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Delhi?
August is not one of Delhi’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Delhi in August?
169 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (173 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Delhi in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Delhi get in August?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Delhi in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
