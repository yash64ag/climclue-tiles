Delhi weather in July

Average July weather in Delhi, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Delhi averages 34.8 °C (95 °F) days and 26.5 °C (80 °F) nights, with 173 mm (6.8 in) of rain over the month and about 167 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Delhi

July is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Delhi. Compared with June: 2.6 °C cooler, 110 mm more precipitation and 39 hours less sunshine. August is 1.0 °C cooler than July on average.

Delhi in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Delhi in July?
In July, Delhi averages a day high of 34.8 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 26.5 °C (80 °F), with 173 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Delhi?
July is not one of Delhi’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Delhi in July?
173 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (173 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Delhi in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Delhi get in July?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Delhi in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
