Ulhasnagar in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulhasnagar in July?
In July, Ulhasnagar averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 818 mm (32.2 in) of precipitation and about 94 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Ulhasnagar?
July is not one of Ulhasnagar’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulhasnagar in July?
818 mm (32.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (818 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Ulhasnagar in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ulhasnagar get in July?
About 94 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulhasnagar in July?
