How December compares in Ulhasnagar

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ulhasnagar. Compared with November: 2.2 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. January is 0.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Ulhasnagar in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulhasnagar in December?
In December, Ulhasnagar averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 262 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ulhasnagar?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Ulhasnagar’s mild months (January, February and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulhasnagar in December?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (818 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Ulhasnagar in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ulhasnagar get in December?
About 262 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulhasnagar in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
