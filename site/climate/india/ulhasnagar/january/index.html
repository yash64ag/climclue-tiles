How January compares in Ulhasnagar

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Ulhasnagar. Compared with December: 0.7 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. February is 1.2 °C warmer than January on average.
