How September compares in Guwahati

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Guwahati. Compared with August: 0.9 °C cooler, 59 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. October is 1.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Guwahati in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guwahati in September?
In September, Guwahati averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 258 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation and about 139 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Guwahati?
September is not one of Guwahati’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guwahati in September?
258 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (396 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Guwahati in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guwahati get in September?
About 139 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guwahati in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
