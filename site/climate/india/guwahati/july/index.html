How July compares in Guwahati

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Guwahati. Compared with June: 0.2 °C warmer, 30 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. August is 0.2 °C warmer than July on average.

Guwahati in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guwahati in July?
In July, Guwahati averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 358 mm (14.1 in) of precipitation and about 122 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Guwahati?
July is not one of Guwahati’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guwahati in July?
358 mm (14.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (389 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Guwahati in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guwahati get in July?
About 122 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guwahati in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
