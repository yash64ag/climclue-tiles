How June compares in Guwahati

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Guwahati. Compared with May: 1.1 °C warmer, 64 mm more precipitation and 58 hours less sunshine. July is 0.2 °C warmer than June on average.

Guwahati in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guwahati in June?
In June, Guwahati averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 389 mm (15.3 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Guwahati?
June is not one of Guwahati’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guwahati in June?
389 mm (15.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (389 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Guwahati in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guwahati get in June?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guwahati in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
