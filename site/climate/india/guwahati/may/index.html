How May compares in Guwahati

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Guwahati. Compared with April: 1.1 °C warmer, 103 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. June is 1.1 °C warmer than May on average.

Guwahati in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guwahati in May?
In May, Guwahati averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 324 mm (12.8 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Guwahati?
May is not one of Guwahati’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guwahati in May?
324 mm (12.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (389 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Guwahati in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guwahati get in May?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guwahati in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
