Māler Kotla at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Māler Kotla is 484th warmest (23.7 °C year average), 114th sunniest (about 2,768 hours of sunshine a year) and 480th wettest (625 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Barnāla, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Māler Kotla is 0.4 °C cooler in June and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 96 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (32.8 °C), averages 20.8 °C more than its coolest, January (11.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.2 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 993 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Māler Kotla, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Māler Kotla has this climate
