Jaleshwar weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Jaleshwar, India (Odisha), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Jaleshwar is warmest in May, with 35.6 °C (96 °F) days and 25.8 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 25.4 °C (78 °F) days and 13.3 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (339 mm); December is driest (11 mm).

Jaleshwar at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Jaleshwar is 1718th warmest (26.3 °C year average), 3519th sunniest (about 2,194 hours of sunshine a year) and 741st wettest (1,718 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Egra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Jaleshwar is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.7 °C), averages 11.3 °C more than its coolest, January (19.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.3 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Why Jaleshwar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jaleshwar itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.802°N, 87.222°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-621-2672), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
