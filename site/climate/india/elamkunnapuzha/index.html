Elamkunnapuzha at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Elamkunnapuzha is 1475th warmest (26.6 °C year average), 2584th sunniest (about 2,473 hours of sunshine a year) and 343rd wettest (2,961 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Narakal, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Elamkunnapuzha is about as warm in April and about as warm in August, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.1 °C), averages 2.6 °C more than its coolest, August (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.7 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Elamkunnapuzha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
