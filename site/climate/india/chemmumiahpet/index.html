Chemmumiahpet at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Chemmumiahpet is 58th warmest (28.9 °C year average), 1996th sunniest (about 2,614 hours of sunshine a year) and 3283rd wettest (658 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kadapa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Chemmumiahpet is about as warm in May and about as warm in December, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.7 °C), averages 9.8 °C more than its coolest, December (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.5 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 147 km from the sea.

Why Chemmumiahpet has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 147 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
