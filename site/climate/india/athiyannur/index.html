Athiyannur at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Athiyannur is 1618th warmest (26.4 °C year average), 3122nd sunniest (about 2,380 hours of sunshine a year) and 670th wettest (1,831 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Neyyāttinkara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Athiyannur is 0.2 °C cooler in April and 0.2 °C cooler in August, with 65 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (27.8 °C), averages 2.1 °C more than its coolest, August (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.6 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Athiyannur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Athiyannur itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (8.393°N, 77.064°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-756-2570), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
