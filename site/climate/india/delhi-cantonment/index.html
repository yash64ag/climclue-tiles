Why Delhi Cantonment has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 863 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Delhi Cantonment climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Delhi Cantonment?
June, with an average of 33.4 °C (92 °F), day highs around 38.5 °C (101 °F) and night lows around 27.6 °C (82 °F).
What is the coldest month in Delhi Cantonment?
January, with an average of 13.4 °C (56 °F), day highs around 20.1 °C (68 °F) and night lows around 7.6 °C (46 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Delhi Cantonment?
August, with 164 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 615 mm (24.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Delhi Cantonment?
November, with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Delhi Cantonment?
May, with about 269 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Delhi Cantonment?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Delhi Cantonment?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March and November.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.8 × 11 km, that contains Delhi Cantonment’s GeoNames location (28.600°N, 77.133°E; ClimaClue area sq-553-2571), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 110,351 (GeoNames).
