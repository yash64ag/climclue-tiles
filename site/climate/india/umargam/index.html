Umargām weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Umargām, India (Gujarat), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Umargām at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Umargām is 1434th warmest (26.6 °C year average), 752nd sunniest (about 2,778 hours of sunshine a year) and 563rd wettest (2,155 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Solsumba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Umargām is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 100 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.3 °C), averages 6.5 °C more than its coolest, January (22.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.7 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Umargām has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Umargām itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.197°N, 72.750°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-638-2527), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
