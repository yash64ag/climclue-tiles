Ranchi at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Ranchi is 505th warmest (23.2 °C year average), 442nd sunniest (about 2,243 hours of sunshine a year) and 146th wettest (1,418 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hazāribāgh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (72 km away), Ranchi is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 1.1 °C warmer in January, with 9 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.4 °C), averages 13.9 °C more than its coolest, January (15.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.0 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 281 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ranchi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ranchi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 281 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
