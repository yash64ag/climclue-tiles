How August compares in Ranchi

August is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Ranchi. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 44 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. September is 0.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Ranchi in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ranchi in August?
In August, Ranchi averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 320 mm (12.6 in) of precipitation and about 111 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Ranchi?
August is not one of Ranchi’s mild months; those are January, February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ranchi in August?
320 mm (12.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (364 mm), the driest November (11 mm).
Does it snow in Ranchi in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ranchi get in August?
About 111 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ranchi in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
