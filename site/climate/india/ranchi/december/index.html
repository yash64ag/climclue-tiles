How December compares in Ranchi

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Ranchi. Compared with November: 3.4 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. January is 0.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Ranchi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ranchi in December?
In December, Ranchi averages a day high of 22.3 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 10.6 °C (51 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ranchi?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Ranchi’s mild months (January, February, March, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ranchi in December?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (364 mm), the driest November (11 mm).
Does it snow in Ranchi in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ranchi get in December?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ranchi in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
