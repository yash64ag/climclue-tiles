How October compares in Ranchi

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Ranchi. Compared with September: 2.0 °C cooler, 175 mm less precipitation and 71 hours more sunshine. November is 3.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Ranchi in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ranchi in October?
In October, Ranchi averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 18.4 °C (65 °F), with 75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Ranchi?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Ranchi’s mild months (January, February, March, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ranchi in October?
75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (364 mm), the driest November (11 mm).
Does it snow in Ranchi in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ranchi get in October?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ranchi in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
