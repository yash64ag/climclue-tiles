How November compares in Ranchi

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Ranchi. Compared with October: 3.8 °C cooler, 64 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. December is 3.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Ranchi in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ranchi in November?
In November, Ranchi averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Ranchi?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Ranchi’s mild months (January, February, March, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ranchi in November?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (363 mm), the driest November (10 mm).
Does it snow in Ranchi in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ranchi get in November?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ranchi in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
