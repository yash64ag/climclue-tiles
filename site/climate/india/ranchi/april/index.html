How April compares in Ranchi

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Ranchi. Compared with March: 4.5 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. May is 1.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Ranchi in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ranchi in April?
In April, Ranchi averages a day high of 35.1 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 21.2 °C (70 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ranchi?
April is not one of Ranchi’s mild months; those are January, February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ranchi in April?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (364 mm), the driest November (11 mm).
Does it snow in Ranchi in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ranchi get in April?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ranchi in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
