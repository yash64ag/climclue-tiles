How September compares in Ranchi

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Ranchi. Compared with August: 0.3 °C cooler, 72 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. October is 1.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Ranchi in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ranchi in September?
In September, Ranchi averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 249 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Ranchi?
September is not one of Ranchi’s mild months; those are January, February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ranchi in September?
249 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (363 mm), the driest November (10 mm).
Does it snow in Ranchi in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ranchi get in September?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ranchi in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
