Sāgar at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Sāgar is 3249th warmest (24.3 °C year average), 2022nd sunniest (about 2,609 hours of sunshine a year) and 292nd wettest (3,022 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sirālkoppa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Sāgar is 0.8 °C cooler in April and 0.3 °C cooler in August, with 1,643 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (26.7 °C), averages 4.1 °C more than its coolest, August (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.5 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 68 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sāgar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sāgar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 68 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sāgar itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.165°N, 75.029°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-698-2550), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
