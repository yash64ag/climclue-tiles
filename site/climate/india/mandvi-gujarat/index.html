Why Māndvi, Gujarat (21.3°N 73.3°E) has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
