Dhāruhera weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Dhāruhera, India (Haryana), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Dhāruhera is warmest in June, with 39.5 °C (103 °F) days and 28.1 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 20.9 °C (70 °F) days and 6.7 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (178 mm); November is driest (5 mm).

Dhāruhera at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Dhāruhera is 2640th warmest (25.2 °C year average), 1146th sunniest (about 2,724 hours of sunshine a year) and 3430th wettest (618 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bhiwadi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Dhāruhera is 0.2 °C warmer in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 19 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.8 °C), averages 20.0 °C more than its coolest, January (13.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.7 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 804 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Dhāruhera has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 804 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dhāruhera itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (28.206°N, 76.797°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-557-2567), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
