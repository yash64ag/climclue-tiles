Deesa at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Deesa is the 5th sunniest (about 3,138 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Pālanpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Deesa is 0.4 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 176 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.0 °C), averages 14.2 °C more than its coolest, January (18.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.3 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 236 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Deesa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Deesa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 236 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
