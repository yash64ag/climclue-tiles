Dabhoi at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Dabhoi is 819th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 267th sunniest (about 2,910 hours of sunshine a year) and 1753rd wettest (1,118 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vāghodia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Dabhoi is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.6 °C), averages 11.6 °C more than its coolest, January (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.6 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 103 km from the sea.

Why Dabhoi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 103 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dabhoi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.183°N, 73.433°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-618-2534), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
