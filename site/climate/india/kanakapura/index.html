Kanakapura weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Kanakapura, India (Karnataka), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Kanakapura is warmest in April, with 34.8 °C (95 °F) days and 21.8 °C nights, and coolest in December, with 27.7 °C (82 °F) days and 16.9 °C nights. Most rain falls around October (179 mm); January is driest (2 mm).

Kanakapura at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Kanakapura is 2801st warmest (25.0 °C year average), 2998th sunniest (about 2,406 hours of sunshine a year) and 2777th wettest (831 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Closepet, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Kanakapura is 0.1 °C warmer in April and 0.2 °C warmer in December, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.3 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, December (22.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.6 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 237 km from the sea.

Why Kanakapura has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 237 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kanakapura itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (12.547°N, 77.420°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-714-2574), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
