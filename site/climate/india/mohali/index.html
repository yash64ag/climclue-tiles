Mohali at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Mohali is 475th warmest (23.9 °C year average), 164th sunniest (about 2,715 hours of sunshine a year) and 324th wettest (950 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chandigarh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Mohali is 0.2 °C warmer in June and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 126 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (32.4 °C), averages 19.5 °C more than its coolest, January (12.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.3 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,037 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mohali, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Mohali

For mild weather, the best months to visit Mohali are February, March, October and November.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): February, March, October and November.

Why Mohali has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,037 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
