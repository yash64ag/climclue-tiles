Mudgal weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Mudgal, India (Karnataka), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Mudgal is warmest in April, with 39.1 °C (102 °F) days and 24.6 °C nights, and coolest in December, with 29.3 °C (85 °F) days and 16.7 °C nights. Most rain falls around September (157 mm); February is driest (1 mm).

Mudgal at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Mudgal is 1054th warmest (26.9 °C year average), 1541st sunniest (about 2,677 hours of sunshine a year) and 3296th wettest (656 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lingsugūr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Mudgal is 0.3 °C cooler in April and 0.2 °C cooler in December, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (31.9 °C), averages 8.9 °C more than its coolest, December (23.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.7 °C (62 °F), in December. The area lies about 289 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mudgal, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mudgal has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 289 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Mudgal itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (16.012°N, 76.442°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-679-2564), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
