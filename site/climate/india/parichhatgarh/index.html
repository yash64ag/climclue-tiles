Parīchhatgarh at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Parīchhatgarh is 3223rd warmest (24.4 °C year average), 1766th sunniest (about 2,655 hours of sunshine a year) and 2168th wettest (992 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kithor, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Parīchhatgarh is 0.1 °C cooler in June and about as warm in January, with 44 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (32.5 °C), averages 18.8 °C more than its coolest, January (13.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.6 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 936 km from the sea (deep inland).

For mild weather, the best months to visit Parīchhatgarh are February, March, October and November.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): February, March, October and November.

Why Parīchhatgarh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 936 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Parīchhatgarh itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (28.978°N, 77.934°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-550-2579), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
