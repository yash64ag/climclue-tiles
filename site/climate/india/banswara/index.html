Bānswāra at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Bānswāra is 162nd warmest (26.6 °C year average), 34th sunniest (about 2,929 hours of sunshine a year) and 299th wettest (997 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ratlām, the nearest covered town with different numbers (65 km away), Bānswāra is 1.2 °C warmer in May and 1.5 °C warmer in January, with 49 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.5 °C), averages 13.7 °C more than its coolest, January (19.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.4 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 257 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bānswāra, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bānswāra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 257 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
