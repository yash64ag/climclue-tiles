Datia at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Datia is 309th warmest (25.5 °C year average), 132nd sunniest (about 2,746 hours of sunshine a year) and 328th wettest (933 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jhānsi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Datia is 0.3 °C cooler in May and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.2 °C), averages 19.1 °C more than its coolest, January (15.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.9 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 723 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Datia, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Datia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 723 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Datia itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.673°N, 78.459°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-583-2584), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
