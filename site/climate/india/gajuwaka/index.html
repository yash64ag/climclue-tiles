Gajuwaka at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Gajuwaka is 101st warmest (27.1 °C year average), 344th sunniest (about 2,552 hours of sunshine a year) and 226th wettest (1,136 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Visakhapatnam, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Gajuwaka is 0.3 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 11 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.4 °C), averages 7.4 °C more than its coolest, January (23.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.4 °C (67 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gajuwaka, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gajuwaka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
