Why Dhule has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 199 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Dhule climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Dhule?
May, with an average of 33.1 °C (92 °F), day highs around 40.0 °C (104 °F) and night lows around 26.0 °C (79 °F).
What is the coldest month in Dhule?
January, with an average of 21.6 °C (71 °F), day highs around 29.1 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 15.3 °C (60 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Dhule?
July, with 209 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 791 mm (31.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Dhule?
January, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Dhule?
May, with about 316 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Dhule?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Dhule?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Dhule’s GeoNames location (20.901°N, 74.777°E; ClimaClue area sq-630-2547), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 375,559 (GeoNames).
