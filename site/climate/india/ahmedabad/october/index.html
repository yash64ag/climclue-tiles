How October compares in Ahmedabad

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Ahmedabad. Compared with September: 0.9 °C cooler, 105 mm less precipitation and 67 hours more sunshine. November is 3.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Ahmedabad in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ahmedabad in October?
In October, Ahmedabad averages a day high of 35.5 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 286 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Ahmedabad?
October is not one of Ahmedabad’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ahmedabad in October?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (358 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Ahmedabad in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ahmedabad get in October?
About 286 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ahmedabad in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
