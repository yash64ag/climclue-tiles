How January compares in Ahmedabad

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ahmedabad. Compared with December: 0.9 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. February is 2.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Ahmedabad in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ahmedabad in January?
In January, Ahmedabad averages a day high of 27.3 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 13.6 °C (56 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 284 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Ahmedabad?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Ahmedabad’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ahmedabad in January?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (358 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Ahmedabad in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ahmedabad get in January?
About 284 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ahmedabad in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
