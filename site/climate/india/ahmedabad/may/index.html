How May compares in Ahmedabad

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Ahmedabad. Compared with April: 2.3 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. June is 0.9 °C cooler than May on average.

Ahmedabad in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ahmedabad in May?
In May, Ahmedabad averages a day high of 41.1 °C (106 °F) and a night low of 25.6 °C (78 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 327 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Ahmedabad?
May is not one of Ahmedabad’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ahmedabad in May?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (358 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Ahmedabad in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ahmedabad get in May?
About 327 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ahmedabad in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
