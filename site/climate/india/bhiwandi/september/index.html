How September compares in Bhiwandi

September is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Bhiwandi. Compared with August: 0.4 °C warmer, 167 mm less precipitation and 86 hours more sunshine. October is 0.6 °C warmer than September on average.

Bhiwandi in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhiwandi in September?
In September, Bhiwandi averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 344 mm (13.5 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Bhiwandi?
September is not one of Bhiwandi’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhiwandi in September?
344 mm (13.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (758 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Bhiwandi in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhiwandi get in September?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bhiwandi in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
