Bhiwandi in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhiwandi in March?
In March, Bhiwandi averages a day high of 34.4 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 281 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bhiwandi?
March is not one of Bhiwandi’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhiwandi in March?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (802 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Bhiwandi in March?
