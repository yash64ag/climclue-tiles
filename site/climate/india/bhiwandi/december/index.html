How December compares in Bhiwandi

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Bhiwandi. Compared with November: 2.3 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. January is 0.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Bhiwandi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhiwandi in December?
In December, Bhiwandi averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 17.8 °C (64 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Bhiwandi?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Bhiwandi’s mild months (January, February and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhiwandi in December?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (802 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Bhiwandi in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhiwandi get in December?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bhiwandi in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
