How January compares in Bhiwandi

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Bhiwandi. Compared with December: 0.8 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. February is 1.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Bhiwandi in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhiwandi in January?
In January, Bhiwandi averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 15.8 °C (60 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 274 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Bhiwandi?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Bhiwandi’s mild months (January, February and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhiwandi in January?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (758 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Bhiwandi in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhiwandi get in January?
About 274 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bhiwandi in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
