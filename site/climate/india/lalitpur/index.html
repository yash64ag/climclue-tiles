Lalitpur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Lalitpur is 344th warmest (25.3 °C year average), 125th sunniest (about 2,754 hours of sunshine a year) and 270th wettest (1,041 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jhānsi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (87 km away), Lalitpur is 0.4 °C cooler in May and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 112 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.0 °C), averages 18.1 °C more than its coolest, January (15.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.0 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 672 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lalitpur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lalitpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 672 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
