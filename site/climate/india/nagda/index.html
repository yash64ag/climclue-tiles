Why Nagda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 340 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Nagda climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Nagda?
May, with an average of 33.1 °C (92 °F), day highs around 39.4 °C (103 °F) and night lows around 25.9 °C (79 °F).
What is the coldest month in Nagda?
January, with an average of 18.3 °C (65 °F), day highs around 25.6 °C (78 °F) and night lows around 11.7 °C (53 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Nagda?
July, with 288 mm (11.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 874 mm (34.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Nagda?
March, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Nagda?
May, with about 316 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Nagda?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Nagda?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, November and December.
What type of climate does Nagda have?
Tropical savanna (Köppen Aw). Hot all year, with a clear wet season and a clear dry season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Nagda’s GeoNames location (23.458°N, 75.418°E; ClimaClue area sq-605-2554), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 103,501 (GeoNames).
