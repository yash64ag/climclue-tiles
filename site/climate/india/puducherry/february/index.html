How February compares in Puducherry

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Puducherry. Compared with January: 0.9 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. March is 1.6 °C warmer than February on average.

Puducherry in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Puducherry in February?
In February, Puducherry averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Puducherry?
February is not one of Puducherry’s mild months; those are January, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Puducherry in February?
24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (317 mm), the driest March (18 mm).
Does it snow in Puducherry in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Puducherry get in February?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Puducherry in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
