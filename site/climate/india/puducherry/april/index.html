How April compares in Puducherry

April is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Puducherry. Compared with March: 1.8 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. May is 1.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Puducherry in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Puducherry in April?
In April, Puducherry averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 27.2 °C (81 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Puducherry?
April is not one of Puducherry’s mild months; those are January, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Puducherry in April?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (317 mm), the driest March (18 mm).
Does it snow in Puducherry in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Puducherry get in April?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Puducherry in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
