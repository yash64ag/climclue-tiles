Mūkondapalli at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Mūkondapalli is 3522nd warmest (23.4 °C year average), 3056th sunniest (about 2,393 hours of sunshine a year) and 2907th wettest (790 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Mūkondapalli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 244 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
