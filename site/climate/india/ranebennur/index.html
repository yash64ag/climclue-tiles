Ranebennur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Ranebennur is 334th warmest (25.3 °C year average), 310th sunniest (about 2,617 hours of sunshine a year) and 433rd wettest (721 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Davangere, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Ranebennur is 0.2 °C cooler in April and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 80 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.9 °C), averages 5.6 °C more than its coolest, December (23.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.1 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 144 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ranebennur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ranebennur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 144 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
