Navadwīp at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Navadwīp is 265th warmest (25.8 °C year average), 495th sunniest (about 2,093 hours of sunshine a year) and 127th wettest (1,489 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Krishnanagar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Navadwīp is about as warm in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 25 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.9 °C), averages 12.2 °C more than its coolest, January (17.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.0 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 193 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Navadwīp, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Navadwīp has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 193 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
