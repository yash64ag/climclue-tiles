Why Puruliya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 227 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Puruliya climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Puruliya?
May, with an average of 30.9 °C (88 °F), day highs around 37.3 °C (99 °F) and night lows around 25.3 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Puruliya?
January, with an average of 16.9 °C (62 °F), day highs around 23.7 °C (75 °F) and night lows around 10.6 °C (51 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Puruliya?
July, with 413 mm (16.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,653 mm (65.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Puruliya?
December, with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Puruliya?
April, with about 244 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Puruliya?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Puruliya?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, November and December.
What type of climate does Puruliya have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Puruliya’s GeoNames location (23.331°N, 86.363°E; ClimaClue area sq-606-2663), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 122,533 (GeoNames).
