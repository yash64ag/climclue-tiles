Sambalpur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Sambalpur is 81st warmest (27.3 °C year average), 378th sunniest (about 2,468 hours of sunshine a year) and 88th wettest (1,678 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Raigarh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (77 km away), Sambalpur is 0.5 °C cooler in May and 1.4 °C warmer in January, with 89 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.5 °C), averages 12.1 °C more than its coolest, January (21.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.0 °C (59 °F), in January. The area lies about 239 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sambalpur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sambalpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 239 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
