Vijayapuri North at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Vijayapuri North is 62nd warmest (28.8 °C year average), 1391st sunniest (about 2,691 hours of sunshine a year) and 3066th wettest (740 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mācherla, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Vijayapuri North is 0.3 °C warmer in May and about as warm in December, with 19 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.1 °C), averages 10.0 °C more than its coolest, December (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.3 °C (65 °F), in December. The area lies about 146 km from the sea.

Why Vijayapuri North has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 146 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Vijayapuri North itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (16.585°N, 79.320°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-674-2593), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
