How September compares in Bhubaneswar

September is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Bhubaneswar. Compared with August: 0.1 °C cooler, 127 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. October is 1.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Bhubaneswar in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhubaneswar in September?
In September, Bhubaneswar averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 280 mm (11.0 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Bhubaneswar?
September is not one of Bhubaneswar’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhubaneswar in September?
280 mm (11.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (407 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Bhubaneswar in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhubaneswar get in September?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bhubaneswar in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
