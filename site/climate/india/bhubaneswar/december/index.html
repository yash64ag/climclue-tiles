Bhubaneswar weather in December

Average December weather in Bhubaneswar, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Bhubaneswar averages 27.2 °C (81 °F) days and 15.0 °C (59 °F) nights, with 6 mm (0.2 in) of rain over the month and about 250 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Bhubaneswar

December is the coolest month, the driest month and the sunniest month in Bhubaneswar. Compared with November: 2.9 °C cooler, 48 mm less precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. January is 0.5 °C warmer than December on average.

Bhubaneswar in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhubaneswar in December?
In December, Bhubaneswar averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 250 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Bhubaneswar?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Bhubaneswar’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhubaneswar in December?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (417 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Bhubaneswar in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhubaneswar get in December?
About 250 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bhubaneswar in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
