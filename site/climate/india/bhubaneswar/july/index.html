How July compares in Bhubaneswar

July is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Bhubaneswar. Compared with June: 1.5 °C cooler, 142 mm more precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Bhubaneswar in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhubaneswar in July?
In July, Bhubaneswar averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 26.1 °C (79 °F), with 380 mm (14.9 in) of precipitation and about 106 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Bhubaneswar?
July is not one of Bhubaneswar’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhubaneswar in July?
380 mm (14.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (407 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Bhubaneswar in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhubaneswar get in July?
About 106 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bhubaneswar in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
