How October compares in Bhubaneswar

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Bhubaneswar. Compared with September: 1.2 °C cooler, 130 mm less precipitation and 70 hours more sunshine. November is 2.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Bhubaneswar in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhubaneswar in October?
In October, Bhubaneswar averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 155 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Bhubaneswar?
October is not one of Bhubaneswar’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhubaneswar in October?
155 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (417 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Bhubaneswar in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhubaneswar get in October?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bhubaneswar in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
