How May compares in Bhubaneswar

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Bhubaneswar. Compared with April: 0.9 °C warmer, 80 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. June is 1.1 °C cooler than May on average.

Bhubaneswar in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhubaneswar in May?
In May, Bhubaneswar averages a day high of 36.5 °C (98 °F) and a night low of 25.5 °C (78 °F), with 116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 239 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Bhubaneswar?
May is not one of Bhubaneswar’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhubaneswar in May?
116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (417 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Bhubaneswar in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhubaneswar get in May?
About 239 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bhubaneswar in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
