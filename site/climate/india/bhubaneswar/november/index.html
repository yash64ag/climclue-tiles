How November compares in Bhubaneswar

November is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Bhubaneswar. Compared with October: 2.9 °C cooler, 100 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. December is 2.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Bhubaneswar in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhubaneswar in November?
In November, Bhubaneswar averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 19.2 °C (67 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Bhubaneswar?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Bhubaneswar’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhubaneswar in November?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (407 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Bhubaneswar in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhubaneswar get in November?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bhubaneswar in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
