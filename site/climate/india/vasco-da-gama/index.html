Vasco da Gama at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Vasco da Gama is 167th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 100th sunniest (about 2,787 hours of sunshine a year) and 10th wettest (3,310 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Belagavi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (89 km away), Vasco da Gama is 2.3 °C warmer in May and 3.5 °C warmer in January, with 2,023 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.6 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, January (25.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.7 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Vasco da Gama, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Vasco da Gama has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
