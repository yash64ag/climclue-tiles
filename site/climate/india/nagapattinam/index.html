Nagapattinam at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Nagapattinam is 55th warmest (27.8 °C year average), 351st sunniest (about 2,535 hours of sunshine a year) and 148th wettest (1,404 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kumbakonam, the nearest covered town with different numbers (54 km away), Nagapattinam is 2.2 °C cooler in May and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 239 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.9 °C), averages 4.6 °C more than its coolest, January (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.9 °C (75 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nagapattinam, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nagapattinam has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
