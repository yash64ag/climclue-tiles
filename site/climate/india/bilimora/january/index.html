How January compares in Bilimora

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Bilimora. Compared with December: 1.2 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. February is 1.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Bilimora in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bilimora in January?
In January, Bilimora averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 17.2 °C (63 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 281 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Bilimora?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Bilimora’s mild months (January, February and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bilimora in January?

Does it snow in Bilimora in January?

How many hours of sunshine does Bilimora get in January?
