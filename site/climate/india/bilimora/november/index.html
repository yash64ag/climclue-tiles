How November compares in Bilimora

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Bilimora. Compared with October: 1.9 °C cooler, 46 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. December is 2.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Bilimora in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bilimora in November?
In November, Bilimora averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 262 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Bilimora?
November is not one of Bilimora’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bilimora in November?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (787 mm), the driest March (1 mm).
Does it snow in Bilimora in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bilimora get in November?
About 262 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bilimora in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
