How May compares in Bilimora

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Bilimora. Compared with April: 1.0 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. June is 0.6 °C cooler than May on average.

Bilimora in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bilimora in May?
In May, Bilimora averages a day high of 32.9 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 26.6 °C (80 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 317 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Bilimora?
May is not one of Bilimora’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bilimora in May?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (773 mm), the driest March (1 mm).
Does it snow in Bilimora in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bilimora get in May?
About 317 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bilimora in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
