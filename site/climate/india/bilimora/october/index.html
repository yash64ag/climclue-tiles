Bilimora in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bilimora in October?
In October, Bilimora averages a day high of 34.5 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Bilimora?
October is not one of Bilimora’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bilimora in October?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (773 mm), the driest March (1 mm).
Does it snow in Bilimora in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bilimora get in October?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bilimora in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
