Dharmābād weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Dharmābād, India (Maharashtra), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Dharmābād is warmest in May, with 41.5 °C (107 °F) days and 26.9 °C nights, and coolest in December, with 29.5 °C (85 °F) days and 14.2 °C nights. Most rain falls around August (300 mm); February is driest (5 mm).

Dharmābād at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Dharmābād is 667th warmest (27.3 °C year average), 905th sunniest (about 2,757 hours of sunshine a year) and 1751st wettest (1,118 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bodhan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Dharmābād is 0.2 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 33 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.2 °C), averages 12.4 °C more than its coolest, December (21.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.2 °C (58 °F), in December. The area lies about 442 km from the sea.

Why Dharmābād has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 442 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dharmābād itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.891°N, 77.849°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-651-2578), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
