Why Bhiwadi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 810 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Bhiwadi climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Bhiwadi?
June, with an average of 33.8 °C (93 °F), day highs around 38.9 °C (102 °F) and night lows around 28.1 °C (83 °F).
What is the coldest month in Bhiwadi?
January, with an average of 13.8 °C (57 °F), day highs around 20.4 °C (69 °F) and night lows around 7.6 °C (46 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Bhiwadi?
July, with 180 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 626 mm (24.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Bhiwadi?
November, with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Bhiwadi?
May, with about 272 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Bhiwadi?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Bhiwadi?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.8 × 11 km, that contains Bhiwadi’s GeoNames location (28.210°N, 76.861°E; ClimaClue area sq-557-2568), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 104,921 (GeoNames).
