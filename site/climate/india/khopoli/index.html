Khopoli at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Khopoli is 1803rd warmest (26.2 °C year average), 1150th sunniest (about 2,723 hours of sunshine a year) and 234th wettest (3,132 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lonavla, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Khopoli is 2.1 °C warmer in May and 3.1 °C warmer in January, with 669 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.0 °C), averages 5.6 °C more than its coolest, January (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.3 °C (60 °F), in January. The area lies about 54 km from the sea.

Why Khopoli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 54 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.6 °C. The 10 km area around Khopoli, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 24.9 °C a year, 1.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Khopoli itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.786°N, 73.346°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-652-2533), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
