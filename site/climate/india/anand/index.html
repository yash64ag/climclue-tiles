Anand at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Anand is 93rd warmest (27.1 °C year average), 27th sunniest (about 2,957 hours of sunshine a year) and 313th wettest (967 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nadiād, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Anand is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 31 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.9 °C), averages 12.4 °C more than its coolest, January (20.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.2 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 68 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Anand, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Anand has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 68 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
