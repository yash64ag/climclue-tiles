Kārakkād at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Kārakkād is 797th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 2496th sunniest (about 2,492 hours of sunshine a year) and 396th wettest (2,841 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Guruvāyūr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Kārakkād is about as warm in March and about as warm in August, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (28.8 °C), averages 3.1 °C more than its coolest, August (25.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.8 °C (73 °F), in October. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Kārakkād has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
