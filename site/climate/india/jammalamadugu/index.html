Jammalamadugu at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Jammalamadugu is 53rd warmest (29.0 °C year average), 1915th sunniest (about 2,631 hours of sunshine a year) and 3138th wettest (710 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Proddatūr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Jammalamadugu is about as warm in May and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.9 °C), averages 9.6 °C more than its coolest, December (24.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.7 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 193 km from the sea.

Why Jammalamadugu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 193 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-691-2583), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
