Bharwāri at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Bharwāri is 2278th warmest (25.6 °C year average), 1475th sunniest (about 2,684 hours of sunshine a year) and 2528th wettest (887 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Manjhanpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Bharwāri is about as warm in May and about as warm in January, with 16 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.5 °C), averages 18.1 °C more than its coolest, January (15.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.9 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 737 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Bharwāri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 737 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-584-2614), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
