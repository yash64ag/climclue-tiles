Sāngli in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sāngli in January?
In January, Sāngli averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 294 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Sāngli?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Sāngli’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sāngli in January?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (151 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Sāngli in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sāngli get in January?
About 294 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
