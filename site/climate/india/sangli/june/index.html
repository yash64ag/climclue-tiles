Sāngli in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sāngli in June?
In June, Sāngli averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 113 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Sāngli?
June is not one of Sāngli’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sāngli in June?
113 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (156 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Sāngli in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sāngli get in June?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sāngli in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
