How July compares in Sāngli

What is the weather like in Sāngli in July?

Is July a good time to visit Sāngli?
July is not one of Sāngli’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
