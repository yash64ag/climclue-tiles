Sāngli in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sāngli in November?
In November, Sāngli averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 18.4 °C (65 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 260 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Sāngli?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Sāngli’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sāngli in November?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (156 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Sāngli in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sāngli get in November?
About 260 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sāngli in November?
