How August compares in Ujjain

August is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Ujjain. Compared with July: 1.2 °C cooler, 23 mm less precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. September is 0.7 °C warmer than August on average.

Ujjain in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ujjain in August?
In August, Ujjain averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 268 mm (10.6 in) of precipitation and about 94 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Ujjain?
August is not one of Ujjain’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ujjain in August?
268 mm (10.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (291 mm), the driest April (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ujjain in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ujjain get in August?
About 94 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ujjain in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
