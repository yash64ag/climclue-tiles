Ujjain at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Ujjain is 339th warmest (25.3 °C year average), 57th sunniest (about 2,855 hours of sunshine a year) and 351st wettest (879 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dewas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Ujjain is 0.3 °C warmer in May and about as warm in January, with 78 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.0 °C), averages 14.8 °C more than its coolest, January (18.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.8 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 356 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ujjain, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ujjain has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 356 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
