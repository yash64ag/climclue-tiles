How October compares in Ujjain

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Ujjain. Compared with September: 0.5 °C cooler, 118 mm less precipitation and 81 hours more sunshine. November is 3.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Ujjain in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ujjain in October?
In October, Ujjain averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 19.3 °C (67 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 269 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Ujjain?
October is not one of Ujjain’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ujjain in October?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (291 mm), the driest April (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ujjain in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ujjain get in October?
About 269 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ujjain in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
