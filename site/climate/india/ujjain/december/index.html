How December compares in Ujjain

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ujjain. Compared with November: 3.2 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. January is 0.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Ujjain in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ujjain in December?
In December, Ujjain averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 11.1 °C (52 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 275 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ujjain?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Ujjain’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ujjain in December?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (287 mm), the driest April (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ujjain in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ujjain get in December?
About 275 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ujjain in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
