How January compares in Ujjain

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Ujjain. Compared with December: 0.7 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. February is 2.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Ujjain in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ujjain in January?
In January, Ujjain averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 11.8 °C (53 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 281 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Ujjain?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Ujjain’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ujjain in January?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (291 mm), the driest April (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ujjain in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ujjain get in January?
About 281 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ujjain in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
