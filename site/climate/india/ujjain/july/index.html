How July compares in Ujjain

July is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Ujjain. Compared with June: 4.0 °C cooler, 190 mm more precipitation and 95 hours less sunshine. August is 1.2 °C cooler than July on average.

Ujjain in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ujjain in July?
In July, Ujjain averages a day high of 29.2 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 291 mm (11.5 in) of precipitation and about 113 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Ujjain?
July is not one of Ujjain’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ujjain in July?
291 mm (11.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (291 mm), the driest April (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ujjain in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ujjain get in July?
About 113 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ujjain in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
