Thāna Bhawan at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Thāna Bhawan is 3331st warmest (24.1 °C year average), 1579th sunniest (about 2,674 hours of sunshine a year) and 2172nd wettest (990 mm a year), near the middle on each. The area lies about 963 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Thāna Bhawan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 963 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
