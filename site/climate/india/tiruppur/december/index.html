Tiruppur weather in December

Average December weather in Tiruppur, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Tiruppur averages 29.3 °C (85 °F) days and 19.8 °C (68 °F) nights, with 39 mm (1.5 in) of rain over the month and about 213 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Tiruppur

December is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Tiruppur. Compared with November: 0.8 °C cooler, 96 mm less precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. January is 0.6 °C warmer than December on average.

Tiruppur in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tiruppur in December?
In December, Tiruppur averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Tiruppur?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Tiruppur’s mild months (December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tiruppur in December?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (169 mm), the driest February (7 mm).
Does it snow in Tiruppur in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tiruppur get in December?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
