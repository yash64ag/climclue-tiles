Tiruppur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Tiruppur is 87th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 389th sunniest (about 2,440 hours of sunshine a year) and 453rd wettest (676 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Coimbatore, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Tiruppur is 1.6 °C warmer in April and 0.9 °C warmer in December, with 144 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.6 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, December (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.0 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 167 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tiruppur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tiruppur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 167 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
