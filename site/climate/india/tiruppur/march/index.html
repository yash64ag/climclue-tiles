How March compares in Tiruppur

March is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Tiruppur. Compared with February: 2.4 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. April is 0.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Tiruppur in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tiruppur in March?
In March, Tiruppur averages a day high of 35.8 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 269 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Tiruppur?
March is not one of Tiruppur’s mild months; those are December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tiruppur in March?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (169 mm), the driest February (7 mm).
Does it snow in Tiruppur in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tiruppur get in March?
About 269 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tiruppur in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
