How September compares in Tiruppur

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Tiruppur. Compared with August: 0.6 °C warmer, 26 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. October is 0.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Tiruppur in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tiruppur in September?
In September, Tiruppur averages a day high of 32.3 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Tiruppur?
September is not one of Tiruppur’s mild months; those are December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tiruppur in September?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (176 mm), the driest February (7 mm).
Does it snow in Tiruppur in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tiruppur get in September?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tiruppur in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
