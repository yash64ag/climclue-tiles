Tiruppur in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tiruppur in August?
In August, Tiruppur averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Tiruppur?
August is not one of Tiruppur’s mild months; those are December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tiruppur in August?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (169 mm), the driest February (7 mm).
Does it snow in Tiruppur in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tiruppur get in August?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
