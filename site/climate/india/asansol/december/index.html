In December, Āsansol averages a day high of 24.6 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 12.4 °C (54 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 209 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Āsansol?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Āsansol’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
