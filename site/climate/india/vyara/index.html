Vyāra at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Vyāra is 1262nd warmest (26.7 °C year average), 401st sunniest (about 2,854 hours of sunshine a year) and 922nd wettest (1,524 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Māndvi, Gujarat (21.3°N 73.3°E), the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Vyāra is 0.6 °C cooler in May and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 36 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.9 °C), averages 8.9 °C more than its coolest, January (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.3 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 66 km from the sea.

Why Vyāra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 66 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Vyāra itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.111°N, 73.394°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-628-2533), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
