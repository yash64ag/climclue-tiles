Pālkonda at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Pālkonda is 725th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 2442nd sunniest (about 2,508 hours of sunshine a year) and 1375th wettest (1,270 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rāzām, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Pālkonda is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.3 °C cooler in December, with 50 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.5 °C), averages 9.3 °C more than its coolest, December (22.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.6 °C (62 °F), in December. The area lies about 59 km from the sea.

Why Pālkonda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 59 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pālkonda itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.604°N, 83.756°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-653-2637), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
