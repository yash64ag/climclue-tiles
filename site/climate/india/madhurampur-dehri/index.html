Madhurampur Dehri at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Madhurampur Dehri is 275th warmest (25.7 °C year average), 423rd sunniest (about 2,356 hours of sunshine a year) and 220th wettest (1,163 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sasarām, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Madhurampur Dehri is about as warm in May and about as warm in January, with 5 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.1 °C), averages 17.2 °C more than its coolest, January (15.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.0 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 495 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Madhurampur Dehri, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Madhurampur Dehri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 495 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
