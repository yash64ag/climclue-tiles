Botad at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Botad is 47th warmest (28.0 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 3,004 hours of sunshine a year) and 402nd wettest (798 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Surendranagar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (62 km away), Botad is 0.6 °C warmer in May and 1.1 °C warmer in January, with 61 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.0 °C), averages 13.1 °C more than its coolest, January (20.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.4 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 66 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Botad, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Botad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 66 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
