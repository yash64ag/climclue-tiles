Why Sahāranpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,006 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Sahāranpur climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Sahāranpur?
June, with an average of 31.6 °C (89 °F), day highs around 36.8 °C (98 °F) and night lows around 25.5 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Sahāranpur?
January, with an average of 12.9 °C (55 °F), day highs around 19.4 °C (67 °F) and night lows around 7.0 °C (45 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Sahāranpur?
July, with 415 mm (16.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,401 mm (55.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Sahāranpur?
November, with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Sahāranpur?
May, with about 269 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Sahāranpur?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Sahāranpur?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March, October and November.
What type of climate does Sahāranpur have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.6 × 11 km, that contains Sahāranpur’s GeoNames location (29.968°N, 77.545°E; ClimaClue area sq-540-2575), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 484,873 (GeoNames).
