Sahāranpur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Sahāranpur is 496th warmest (23.5 °C year average), 257th sunniest (about 2,668 hours of sunshine a year) and 149th wettest (1,401 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yamuna Nagar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Sahāranpur is about as warm in June and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 41 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (31.6 °C), averages 18.6 °C more than its coolest, January (12.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.0 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,006 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sahāranpur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sahāranpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,006 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
