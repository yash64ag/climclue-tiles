How July compares in Jaipur

July is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Jaipur. Compared with June: 3.3 °C cooler, 170 mm more precipitation and 52 hours less sunshine. August is 1.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Of the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Jaipur ranks 160th warmest (average temperature), 110th sunniest and 347th wettest in July.

Jaipur in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jaipur in July?
In July, Jaipur averages a day high of 32.9 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 25.6 °C (78 °F), with 217 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Jaipur?
July is not one of Jaipur’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jaipur in July?
217 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (217 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Jaipur in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jaipur get in July?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jaipur in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
