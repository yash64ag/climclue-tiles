How August compares in Jaipur

August is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Jaipur. Compared with July: 1.5 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. September is 0.1 °C warmer than August on average.

Jaipur in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jaipur in August?
In August, Jaipur averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 203 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Jaipur?
August is not one of Jaipur’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jaipur in August?
203 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (217 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Jaipur in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jaipur get in August?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jaipur in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
