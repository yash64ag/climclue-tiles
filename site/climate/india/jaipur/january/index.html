How January compares in Jaipur

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Jaipur. Compared with December: 1.3 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. February is 3.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Jaipur in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jaipur in January?
In January, Jaipur averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 6.9 °C (44 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 239 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Jaipur?
January is not one of Jaipur’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jaipur in January?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (220 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Jaipur in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jaipur get in January?
About 239 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jaipur in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
