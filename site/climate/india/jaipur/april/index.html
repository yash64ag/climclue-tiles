How April compares in Jaipur

April is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Jaipur. Compared with March: 5.8 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. May is 3.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Jaipur in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jaipur in April?
In April, Jaipur averages a day high of 36.2 °C (97 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 279 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Jaipur?
April is not one of Jaipur’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jaipur in April?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (217 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Jaipur in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jaipur get in April?
About 279 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jaipur in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
