How March compares in Jaipur

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Jaipur. Compared with February: 5.8 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. April is 5.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Jaipur in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jaipur in March?
In March, Jaipur averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 257 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Jaipur?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Jaipur’s mild months (February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jaipur in March?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (217 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Jaipur in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jaipur get in March?
About 257 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jaipur in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
