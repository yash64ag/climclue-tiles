Why Akola has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 426 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Akola climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Akola?
May, with an average of 34.6 °C (94 °F), day highs around 40.6 °C (105 °F) and night lows around 28.0 °C (82 °F).
What is the coldest month in Akola?
December, with an average of 20.6 °C (69 °F), day highs around 27.7 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 14.3 °C (58 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Akola?
July, with 243 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 854 mm (33.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Akola?
April, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Akola?
May, with about 306 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Akola?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Akola?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, November and December.
What type of climate does Akola have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Akola’s GeoNames location (20.710°N, 76.998°E; ClimaClue area sq-632-2569), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 428,857 (GeoNames).
