Hosūr at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Hosūr is 491st warmest (23.6 °C year average), 409th sunniest (about 2,393 hours of sunshine a year) and 410th wettest (775 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bengaluru, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Hosūr is 0.1 °C warmer in April and 0.2 °C cooler in December, with 120 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (27.1 °C), averages 6.7 °C more than its coolest, December (20.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.7 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 244 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hosūr, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hosūr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 244 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
