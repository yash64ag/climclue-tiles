Giridih at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Giridih is 445th warmest (24.5 °C year average), 470th sunniest (about 2,174 hours of sunshine a year) and 132nd wettest (1,469 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dhanbad, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Giridih is 0.6 °C cooler in May and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 31 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.4 °C), averages 14.6 °C more than its coolest, January (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.2 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 311 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Giridih, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Giridih has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 311 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
