Kīratpur weather by month and climate

Kīratpur at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Kīratpur is 3458th warmest (23.7 °C year average), 1869th sunniest (about 2,638 hours of sunshine a year) and 1422nd wettest (1,243 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mandāwar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Kīratpur is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 43 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (31.7 °C), averages 18.3 °C more than its coolest, January (13.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.3 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,007 km from the sea (deep inland).

Best time to visit Kīratpur

For mild weather, the best months to visit Kīratpur are February, March, October and November.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): February, March, October and November.
  • Warmest (average temperature): June (31.7 °C), May (31.2 °C) and July (29.4 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): November (5 mm), December (11 mm) and April (17 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): May (265 h), April (261 h) and October (258 h).

Why Kīratpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,007 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kīratpur itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (29.507°N, 78.206°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-544-2582), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
