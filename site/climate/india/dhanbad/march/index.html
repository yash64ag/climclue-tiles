How March compares in Dhanbad

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Dhanbad. Compared with February: 5.1 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. April is 4.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Dhanbad in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dhanbad in March?
In March, Dhanbad averages a day high of 32.7 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 17.7 °C (64 °F), with 24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Dhanbad?
March is not one of Dhanbad’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dhanbad in March?
24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (378 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Dhanbad in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dhanbad get in March?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dhanbad in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
