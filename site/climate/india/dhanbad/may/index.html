How May compares in Dhanbad

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Dhanbad. Compared with April: 1.3 °C warmer, 45 mm more precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. June is 1.1 °C cooler than May on average.

Dhanbad in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dhanbad in May?
In May, Dhanbad averages a day high of 37.4 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Dhanbad?
May is not one of Dhanbad’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dhanbad in May?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (378 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Dhanbad in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dhanbad get in May?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dhanbad in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
