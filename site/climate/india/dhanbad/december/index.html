How December compares in Dhanbad

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Dhanbad. Compared with November: 4.1 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. January is 0.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Dhanbad in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dhanbad in December?
In December, Dhanbad averages a day high of 24.3 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 11.6 °C (53 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Dhanbad?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Dhanbad’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dhanbad in December?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (378 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Dhanbad in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dhanbad get in December?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dhanbad in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
