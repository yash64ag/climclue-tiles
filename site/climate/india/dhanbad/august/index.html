How August compares in Dhanbad

August is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Dhanbad. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, 48 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. September is 0.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Dhanbad in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dhanbad in August?
In August, Dhanbad averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 331 mm (13.0 in) of precipitation and about 110 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Dhanbad?
August is not one of Dhanbad’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dhanbad in August?
331 mm (13.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (378 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Dhanbad in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dhanbad get in August?
About 110 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dhanbad in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
