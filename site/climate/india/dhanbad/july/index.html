How July compares in Dhanbad

July is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Dhanbad. Compared with June: 1.7 °C cooler, 139 mm more precipitation and 43 hours less sunshine. August is 0.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Dhanbad in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dhanbad in July?
In July, Dhanbad averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 378 mm (14.9 in) of precipitation and about 96 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Dhanbad?
July is not one of Dhanbad’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dhanbad in July?
378 mm (14.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (378 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Dhanbad in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dhanbad get in July?
About 96 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dhanbad in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
