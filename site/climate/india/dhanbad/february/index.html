How February compares in Dhanbad

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Dhanbad. Compared with January: 3.6 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. March is 5.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Dhanbad in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dhanbad in February?
In February, Dhanbad averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 13.3 °C (56 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Dhanbad?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Dhanbad’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dhanbad in February?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (378 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Dhanbad in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dhanbad get in February?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dhanbad in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
