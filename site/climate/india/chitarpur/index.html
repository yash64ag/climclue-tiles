Chitarpur weather by month and climate

Chitarpur at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Chitarpur is 3140th warmest (24.5 °C year average), 3421st sunniest (about 2,232 hours of sunshine a year) and 1079th wettest (1,422 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rāmgarh, Jharkhand, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Chitarpur is 0.5 °C cooler in May and about as warm in January, with 204 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.8 °C), averages 14.4 °C more than its coolest, January (16.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.6 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 280 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chitarpur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chitarpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 280 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Chitarpur itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (23.573°N, 85.654°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-604-2656), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
