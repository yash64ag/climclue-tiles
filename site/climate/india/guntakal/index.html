Guntakal at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Guntakal is 80th warmest (27.3 °C year average), 308th sunniest (about 2,618 hours of sunshine a year) and 462nd wettest (655 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ballari, the nearest covered town with different numbers (47 km away), Guntakal is 0.1 °C warmer in April and 0.2 °C cooler in December, with 108 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (32.0 °C), averages 8.7 °C more than its coolest, December (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.5 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 301 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Guntakal, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Guntakal has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 301 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
