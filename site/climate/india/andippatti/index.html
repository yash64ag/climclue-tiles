Āndippatti at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Āndippatti is 501st warmest (27.6 °C year average), 3088th sunniest (about 2,387 hours of sunshine a year) and 2511th wettest (891 mm a year), near the middle on each. Its warmest month, May (30.4 °C), averages 5.7 °C more than its coolest, December (24.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.3 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 133 km from the sea.

Why Āndippatti has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 133 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.7 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
