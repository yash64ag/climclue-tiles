Lāsalgaon weather by month and climate

Lāsalgaon at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Lāsalgaon is 2836th warmest (24.9 °C year average), 739th sunniest (about 2,780 hours of sunshine a year) and 2810th wettest (822 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chāndor, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Lāsalgaon is 0.9 °C warmer in May and 1.2 °C warmer in January, with 147 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.2 °C), averages 10.0 °C more than its coolest, January (20.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.0 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 160 km from the sea.

Why Lāsalgaon has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 160 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Lāsalgaon itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.143°N, 74.239°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-638-2542), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
