Hatti at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Hatti is 946th warmest (27.0 °C year average), 1515th sunniest (about 2,680 hours of sunshine a year) and 3082nd wettest (735 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lingsugūr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Hatti is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.3 °C cooler in December, with 79 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.0 °C), averages 9.0 °C more than its coolest, December (23.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.7 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 313 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hatti, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hatti has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 313 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hatti itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (16.198°N, 76.660°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-678-2566), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
