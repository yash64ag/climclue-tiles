Deshnoke weather by month and climate

Deshnoke at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Deshnoke is 1418th warmest (26.6 °C year average), 97th sunniest (about 3,032 hours of sunshine a year) and 3736th wettest (282 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bikaner, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Deshnoke is 0.5 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 18 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.8 °C), averages 19.8 °C more than its coolest, January (14.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.3 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 628 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Deshnoke has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 628 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Deshnoke itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (27.798°N, 73.343°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-562-2533), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
