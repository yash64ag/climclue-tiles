Valsād at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Valsād is 1573rd warmest (26.5 °C year average), 513th sunniest (about 2,824 hours of sunshine a year) and 616th wettest (2,028 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pārdi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Valsād is 0.1 °C warmer in May and about as warm in January, with 64 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.3 °C), averages 6.9 °C more than its coolest, January (22.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.0 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Valsād has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
