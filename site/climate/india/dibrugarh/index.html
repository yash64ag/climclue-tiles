Dibrugarh at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Dibrugarh is 3438th warmest (23.8 °C year average), 3027th sunniest (about 2,399 hours of sunshine a year) and 217th wettest (3,173 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Silapathar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Dibrugarh is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 134 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.6 °C), averages 11.6 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.1 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 640 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Dibrugarh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 640 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dibrugarh itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (27.480°N, 94.908°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-565-2749), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
