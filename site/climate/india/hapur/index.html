Hāpur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Hāpur is 446th warmest (24.5 °C year average), 269th sunniest (about 2,662 hours of sunshine a year) and 379th wettest (835 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Modīnagar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Hāpur is about as warm in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 35 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (32.9 °C), averages 19.3 °C more than its coolest, January (13.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.7 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 906 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hāpur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hāpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 906 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
