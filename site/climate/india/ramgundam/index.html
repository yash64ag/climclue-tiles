Rāmgundam at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Rāmgundam is 31st warmest (28.1 °C year average), 156th sunniest (about 2,727 hours of sunshine a year) and 244th wettest (1,093 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ramagundam, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Rāmgundam is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 22 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (35.0 °C), averages 12.4 °C more than its coolest, December (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.4 °C (62 °F), in December. The area lies about 352 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rāmgundam, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Rāmgundam has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 352 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
