Kanayannur in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kanayannur in March?
In March, Kanayannur averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 25.9 °C (79 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kanayannur?
No month in Kanayannur is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kanayannur in March?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (725 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Kanayannur in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kanayannur get in March?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kanayannur in March?
