Kanayannur in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kanayannur in July?
In July, Kanayannur averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 559 mm (22.0 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Kanayannur?
No month in Kanayannur is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kanayannur in July?
559 mm (22.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (727 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Kanayannur in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kanayannur get in July?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
