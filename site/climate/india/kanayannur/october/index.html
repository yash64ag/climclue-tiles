Kanayannur in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kanayannur in October?
In October, Kanayannur averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 345 mm (13.6 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kanayannur?
No month in Kanayannur is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kanayannur in October?
345 mm (13.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (727 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Kanayannur in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kanayannur get in October?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kanayannur in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
