How May compares in Kanayannur

May is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Kanayannur. Compared with April: 0.5 °C cooler, 145 mm more precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. June is 1.5 °C cooler than May on average.

Kanayannur in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kanayannur in May?
In May, Kanayannur averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 26.1 °C (79 °F), with 274 mm (10.8 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kanayannur?
No month in Kanayannur is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kanayannur in May?
274 mm (10.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (725 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Kanayannur in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kanayannur get in May?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kanayannur in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
