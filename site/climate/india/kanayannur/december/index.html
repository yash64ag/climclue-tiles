How December compares in Kanayannur

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kanayannur. Compared with November: about as warm, 123 mm less precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. January is about as warm as December on average.

Kanayannur in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kanayannur in December?
In December, Kanayannur averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kanayannur?
No month in Kanayannur is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kanayannur in December?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (725 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Kanayannur in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kanayannur get in December?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kanayannur in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
