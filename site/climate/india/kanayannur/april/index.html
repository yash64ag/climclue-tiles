How April compares in Kanayannur

April is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kanayannur. Compared with March: 0.2 °C warmer, 83 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. May is 0.5 °C cooler than April on average.

Kanayannur in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kanayannur in April?
In April, Kanayannur averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 26.2 °C (79 °F), with 129 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 242 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kanayannur?
No month in Kanayannur is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kanayannur in April?
129 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (725 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Kanayannur in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kanayannur get in April?
About 242 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kanayannur in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
