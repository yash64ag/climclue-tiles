Azamgarh at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Azamgarh is 309th warmest (25.5 °C year average), 391st sunniest (about 2,436 hours of sunshine a year) and 235th wettest (1,110 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mau, the nearest covered town with different numbers (40 km away), Azamgarh is about as warm in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.5 °C), averages 17.3 °C more than its coolest, January (15.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.4 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 654 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Azamgarh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Azamgarh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 654 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
