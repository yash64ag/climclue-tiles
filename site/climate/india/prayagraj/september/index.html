How September compares in Prayagraj

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Prayagraj. Compared with August: 0.7 °C cooler, 112 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. October is 2.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Prayagraj in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Prayagraj in September?
In September, Prayagraj averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 24.7 °C (76 °F), with 175 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Prayagraj?
September is not one of Prayagraj’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Prayagraj in September?
175 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (287 mm), the driest April (4 mm).
Does it snow in Prayagraj in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Prayagraj get in September?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Prayagraj in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
