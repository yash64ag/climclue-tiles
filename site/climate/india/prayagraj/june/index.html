How June compares in Prayagraj

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Prayagraj. Compared with May: 0.4 °C cooler, 79 mm more precipitation and 81 hours less sunshine. July is 3.2 °C cooler than June on average.

Prayagraj in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Prayagraj in June?
In June, Prayagraj averages a day high of 39.1 °C (102 °F) and a night low of 27.1 °C (81 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Prayagraj?
June is not one of Prayagraj’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Prayagraj in June?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (290 mm), the driest April (4 mm).
Does it snow in Prayagraj in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Prayagraj get in June?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
