Prayagraj at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Prayagraj is 279th warmest (25.7 °C year average), 265th sunniest (about 2,666 hours of sunshine a year) and 339th wettest (907 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gyānpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (64 km away), Prayagraj is about as warm in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 111 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.5 °C), averages 18.0 °C more than its coolest, January (15.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.8 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 699 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Prayagraj, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Prayagraj has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 699 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
