How May compares in Prayagraj

May is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Prayagraj. Compared with April: 3.0 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. June is 0.4 °C cooler than May on average.

Prayagraj in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Prayagraj in May?
In May, Prayagraj averages a day high of 40.0 °C (104 °F) and a night low of 26.6 °C (80 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 276 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Prayagraj?
May is not one of Prayagraj’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Prayagraj in May?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (287 mm), the driest April (4 mm).
Does it snow in Prayagraj in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Prayagraj get in May?
About 276 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Prayagraj in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
