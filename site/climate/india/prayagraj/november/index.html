How November compares in Prayagraj

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Prayagraj. Compared with October: 4.9 °C cooler, 21 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. December is 4.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Prayagraj in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Prayagraj in November?
In November, Prayagraj averages a day high of 27.8 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 247 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Prayagraj?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Prayagraj’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Prayagraj in November?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (287 mm), the driest April (4 mm).
Does it snow in Prayagraj in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Prayagraj get in November?
About 247 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Prayagraj in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
