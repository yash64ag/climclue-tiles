How July compares in Prayagraj

July is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Prayagraj. Compared with June: 3.2 °C cooler, 172 mm more precipitation and 61 hours less sunshine. August is 0.8 °C cooler than July on average.

Prayagraj in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Prayagraj in July?
In July, Prayagraj averages a day high of 32.7 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 26.6 °C (80 °F), with 260 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Prayagraj?
July is not one of Prayagraj’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Prayagraj in July?
260 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (287 mm), the driest April (4 mm).
Does it snow in Prayagraj in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Prayagraj get in July?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Prayagraj in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
