Merta at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Merta is 2091st warmest (25.8 °C year average), 148th sunniest (about 2,984 hours of sunshine a year) and 3695th wettest (392 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Merta Road, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Merta is about as warm in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 18 mm less precipitation a year. Why Merta has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 526 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Merta itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.649°N, 74.034°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-573-2540), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
