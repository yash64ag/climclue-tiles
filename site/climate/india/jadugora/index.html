Jadugora at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Jadugora is 1901st warmest (26.1 °C year average), 3455th sunniest (about 2,219 hours of sunshine a year) and 870th wettest (1,574 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chhota Gobindpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Jadugora is about as warm in May and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 105 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.4 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, January (18.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.4 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 157 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jadugora, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jadugora has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 157 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
