Pātan at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Pātan is 138th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 3,110 hours of sunshine a year) and 431st wettest (729 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mahesāna, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Pātan is about as warm in May and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 51 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.3 °C), averages 14.5 °C more than its coolest, January (18.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.4 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 192 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pātan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pātan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 192 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
