Kapasan at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Kapasan is 2580th warmest (25.3 °C year average), 204th sunniest (about 2,950 hours of sunshine a year) and 3166th wettest (701 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fatehnagar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Kapasan is 0.4 °C warmer in May and about as warm in January, with 62 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.6 °C), averages 17.4 °C more than its coolest, January (16.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.7 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 357 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kapasan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kapasan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 357 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kapasan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (24.889°N, 74.317°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-591-2543), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
