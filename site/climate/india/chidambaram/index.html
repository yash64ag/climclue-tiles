Chidambaram at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Chidambaram is 242nd warmest (28.1 °C year average), 2224th sunniest (about 2,557 hours of sunshine a year) and 1135th wettest (1,382 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Annāmalainagar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Chidambaram is 0.3 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 20 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.1 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, January (24.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.4 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chidambaram, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chidambaram has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Chidambaram itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (11.399°N, 79.691°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-726-2596), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
