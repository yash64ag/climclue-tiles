Sasarām at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Sasarām is 283rd warmest (25.7 °C year average), 419th sunniest (about 2,362 hours of sunshine a year) and 217th wettest (1,168 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Madhurampur Dehri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Sasarām is about as warm in May and about as warm in January, with 5 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.1 °C), averages 17.2 °C more than its coolest, January (15.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.0 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 502 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sasarām, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sasarām has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 502 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
