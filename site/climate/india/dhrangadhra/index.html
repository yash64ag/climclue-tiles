Dhrāngadhra at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Dhrāngadhra is 539th warmest (27.5 °C year average), 50th sunniest (about 3,096 hours of sunshine a year) and 3315th wettest (652 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Dhrāngadhra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 123 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
