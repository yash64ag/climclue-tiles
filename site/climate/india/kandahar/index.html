Kandahār weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Kandahār, India (Maharashtra), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Kandahār at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Kandahār is 753rd warmest (27.2 °C year average), 868th sunniest (about 2,763 hours of sunshine a year) and 2247th wettest (965 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Loha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Kandahār is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 35 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.2 °C), averages 12.5 °C more than its coolest, December (21.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.6 °C (58 °F), in December. The area lies about 450 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kandahār, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Kandahār

For mild weather, the best months to visit Kandahār are January, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): May (34.2 °C), April (32.6 °C) and June (30.3 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): February (3 mm), December (6 mm) and January (11 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): May (295 h), April (285 h) and January (282 h).

Why Kandahār has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 450 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kandahār itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.873°N, 77.192°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-651-2571), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
