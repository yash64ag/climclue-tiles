Rānīganj at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Rānīganj is 316th warmest (25.4 °C year average), 487th sunniest (about 2,148 hours of sunshine a year) and 122nd wettest (1,506 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jāmuria, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Rānīganj is about as warm in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.6 °C), averages 13.6 °C more than its coolest, January (17.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.0 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 227 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rānīganj, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Rānīganj has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 227 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
