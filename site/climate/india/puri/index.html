Puri at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Puri is 92nd warmest (27.1 °C year average), 402nd sunniest (about 2,410 hours of sunshine a year) and 106th wettest (1,609 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bhubaneswar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (53 km away), Puri is 1.1 °C cooler in May and 1.1 °C warmer in January, with 120 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.9 °C), averages 7.2 °C more than its coolest, January (22.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.5 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Puri, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Puri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
