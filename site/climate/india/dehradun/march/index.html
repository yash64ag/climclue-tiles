How March compares in Dehradun

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Dehradun. Compared with February: 4.6 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. April is 5.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Dehradun in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dehradun in March?
In March, Dehradun averages a day high of 26.2 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 11.8 °C (53 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Dehradun?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Dehradun’s mild months (March, April, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dehradun in March?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (729 mm), the driest November (12 mm).
Does it snow in Dehradun in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dehradun get in March?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dehradun in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
