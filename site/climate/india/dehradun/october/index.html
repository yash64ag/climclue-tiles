How October compares in Dehradun

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Dehradun. Compared with September: 3.9 °C cooler, 274 mm less precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. November is 4.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Dehradun in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dehradun in October?
In October, Dehradun averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Dehradun?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Dehradun’s mild months (March, April, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dehradun in October?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (729 mm), the driest November (12 mm).
Does it snow in Dehradun in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dehradun get in October?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dehradun in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
