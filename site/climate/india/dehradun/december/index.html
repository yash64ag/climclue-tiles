How December compares in Dehradun

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Dehradun. Compared with November: 3.8 °C cooler, 12 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. January is 1.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Dehradun in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dehradun in December?
In December, Dehradun averages a day high of 19.1 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 6.3 °C (43 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Dehradun?
December is not one of Dehradun’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dehradun in December?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (790 mm), the driest November (13 mm).
Does it snow in Dehradun in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dehradun get in December?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dehradun in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
