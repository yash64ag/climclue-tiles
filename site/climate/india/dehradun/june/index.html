How June compares in Dehradun

June is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Dehradun. Compared with May: 0.9 °C warmer, 108 mm more precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. July is 1.4 °C cooler than June on average.

Dehradun in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dehradun in June?
In June, Dehradun averages a day high of 34.5 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 167 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Dehradun?
June is not one of Dehradun’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dehradun in June?
167 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (729 mm), the driest November (12 mm).
Does it snow in Dehradun in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dehradun get in June?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dehradun in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
