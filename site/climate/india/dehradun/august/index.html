How August compares in Dehradun

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Dehradun. Compared with July: 0.5 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. September is 1.5 °C cooler than August on average.

Dehradun in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dehradun in August?
In August, Dehradun averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 729 mm (28.7 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Dehradun?
August is not one of Dehradun’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dehradun in August?
729 mm (28.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (729 mm), the driest November (12 mm).
Does it snow in Dehradun in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dehradun get in August?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dehradun in August?
