Khanna at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Khanna is 494th warmest (23.5 °C year average), 136th sunniest (about 2,745 hours of sunshine a year) and 358th wettest (866 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Māler Kotla, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Khanna is 0.5 °C cooler in June and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 241 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (32.3 °C), averages 20.1 °C more than its coolest, January (12.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.7 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,028 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Khanna, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Khanna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,028 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
