Atarra at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Atarra is 1998th warmest (25.9 °C year average), 1248th sunniest (about 2,710 hours of sunshine a year) and 2022nd wettest (1,032 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Naraini, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Atarra is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 58 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.4 °C), averages 18.6 °C more than its coolest, January (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.0 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 785 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Atarra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 785 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
