How March compares in Chennai

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Chennai. Compared with February: 1.8 °C warmer, 11 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. April is 2.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Chennai in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chennai in March?
In March, Chennai averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 286 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Chennai?
March is not one of Chennai’s mild months; those are January, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chennai in March?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is November (350 mm), the driest March (10 mm).
Does it snow in Chennai in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chennai get in March?
About 286 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chennai in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
