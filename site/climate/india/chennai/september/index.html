Chennai weather in September

Average September weather in Chennai, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Chennai averages 33.4 °C (92 °F) days and 24.1 °C (75 °F) nights, with 132 mm (5.2 in) of rain over the month and about 189 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Chennai

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Chennai. Compared with August: 0.5 °C cooler, 11 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. October is 1.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Chennai in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chennai in September?
In September, Chennai averages a day high of 33.4 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Chennai?
September is not one of Chennai’s mild months; those are January, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chennai in September?
132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (366 mm), the driest March (10 mm).
Does it snow in Chennai in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chennai get in September?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Chennai in September?
About 29.0 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is April (29.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Chennai in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
