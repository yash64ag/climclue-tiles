How April compares in Chennai

April is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Chennai. Compared with March: 2.0 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. May is 1.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Chennai in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chennai in April?
In April, Chennai averages a day high of 34.2 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Chennai?
April is not one of Chennai’s mild months; those are January, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chennai in April?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (366 mm), the driest March (10 mm).
Does it snow in Chennai in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chennai get in April?
About 278 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chennai in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
