Panvel at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Panvel is 166th warmest (26.6 °C year average), 215th sunniest (about 2,688 hours of sunshine a year) and 23rd wettest (2,692 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Airoli, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Panvel is 0.3 °C cooler in May and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 310 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.4 °C), averages 5.8 °C more than its coolest, January (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.7 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Panvel, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Panvel has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Panvel itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (18.989°N, 73.110°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-650-2531), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
