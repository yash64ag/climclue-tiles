Robertsonpet at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Robertsonpet is 483rd warmest (23.7 °C year average), 397th sunniest (about 2,420 hours of sunshine a year) and 369th wettest (852 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kolār, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Robertsonpet is 0.2 °C cooler in April and about as warm in December, with 112 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (27.3 °C), averages 7.2 °C more than its coolest, December (20.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.4 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 215 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Robertsonpet, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Robertsonpet has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 215 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
