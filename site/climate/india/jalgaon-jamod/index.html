Jalgaon Jamod at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Jalgaon Jamod is 1583rd warmest (26.5 °C year average), 548th sunniest (about 2,814 hours of sunshine a year) and 2115th wettest (1,007 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nāndūra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Jalgaon Jamod is 0.3 °C cooler in May and 0.6 °C cooler in December, with 171 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.9 °C), averages 13.6 °C more than its coolest, December (20.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.9 °C (53 °F), in December. The area lies about 386 km from the sea.

Why Jalgaon Jamod has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 386 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
