Erode at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Erode is 15th warmest (28.5 °C year average), 381st sunniest (about 2,467 hours of sunshine a year) and 460th wettest (660 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kumarapalayam, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Erode is 0.3 °C warmer in April and 0.4 °C warmer in December, with 70 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (32.1 °C), averages 6.6 °C more than its coolest, December (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.0 °C (68 °F), in January. The area lies about 216 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Erode, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Erode has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 216 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
