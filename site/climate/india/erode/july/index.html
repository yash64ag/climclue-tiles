How July compares in Erode

July is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Erode. Compared with June: 1.0 °C cooler, 24 mm more precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. August is 0.2 °C cooler than July on average.

Erode in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Erode in July?
In July, Erode averages a day high of 33.1 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 24.0 °C (75 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Erode?
No month in Erode is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Erode in July?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (154 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Erode in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Erode get in July?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Erode in July?
