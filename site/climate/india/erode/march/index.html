How March compares in Erode

March is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Erode. Compared with February: 2.5 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. April is 1.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Erode in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Erode in March?
In March, Erode averages a day high of 36.9 °C (98 °F) and a night low of 23.7 °C (75 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 271 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Erode?
No month in Erode is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Erode in March?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (154 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Erode in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Erode get in March?
About 271 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Erode in March?
