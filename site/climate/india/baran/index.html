Bārān at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Bārān is 186th warmest (26.4 °C year average), 72nd sunniest (about 2,833 hours of sunshine a year) and 344th wettest (899 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kota, the nearest covered town with different numbers (69 km away), Bārān is 0.3 °C cooler in May and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 139 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (35.3 °C), averages 18.5 °C more than its coolest, January (16.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.3 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 534 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bārān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bārān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 534 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
