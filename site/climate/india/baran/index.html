Why Bārān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 534 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Bārān climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Bārān?
May, with an average of 35.3 °C (95 °F), day highs around 41.6 °C (107 °F) and night lows around 27.8 °C (82 °F).
What is the coldest month in Bārān?
January, with an average of 16.8 °C (62 °F), day highs around 23.9 °C (75 °F) and night lows around 10.3 °C (51 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Bārān?
July, with 330 mm (13.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 899 mm (35.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Bārān?
April, with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Bārān?
May, with about 300 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Bārān?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Bārān?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Bārān’s GeoNames location (25.100°N, 76.517°E; ClimaClue area sq-588-2565), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 117,992 (GeoNames).
