Pipraich at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Pipraich is 2689th warmest (25.1 °C year average), 3237th sunniest (about 2,332 hours of sunshine a year) and 1182nd wettest (1,352 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gorakhpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Pipraich is 0.1 °C cooler in May and about as warm in January, with 86 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.6 °C), averages 16.5 °C more than its coolest, January (15.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.9 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 703 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Pipraich has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 703 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
