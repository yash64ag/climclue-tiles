Mātābhānga weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Mātābhānga, India (West Bengal), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Mātābhānga is warmest in August, with 31.6 °C (89 °F) days and 25.3 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 23.9 °C (75 °F) days and 11.0 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (788 mm); December is driest (8 mm).

Mātābhānga at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Mātābhānga is 3090th warmest (24.6 °C year average), 3403rd sunniest (about 2,236 hours of sunshine a year) and 127th wettest (3,608 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fālākāta, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Mātābhānga is 0.2 °C warmer in August and about as warm in January, with 212 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.4 °C), averages 11.0 °C more than its coolest, January (17.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.0 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 452 km from the sea.

Why Mātābhānga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 452 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Mātābhānga itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.342°N, 89.216°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-576-2692), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
