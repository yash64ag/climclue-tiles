Why Barshi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 281 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Barshi climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Barshi?
May, with an average of 32.9 °C (91 °F), day highs around 39.0 °C (102 °F) and night lows around 25.7 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Barshi?
December, with an average of 22.6 °C (73 °F), day highs around 28.7 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 17.0 °C (63 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Barshi?
September, with 201 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 777 mm (30.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Barshi?
February, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Barshi?
May, with about 298 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Barshi?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Barshi?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, November and December.
What type of climate does Barshi have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Barshi’s GeoNames location (18.235°N, 75.693°E; ClimaClue area sq-657-2556), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 118,722 (GeoNames).
