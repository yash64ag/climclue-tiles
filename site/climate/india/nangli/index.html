Nangli weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Nangli, India (Punjab), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Nangli is warmest in June, with 40.0 °C (104 °F) days and 25.4 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 19.2 °C (67 °F) days and 4.2 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (242 mm); November is driest (9 mm).

Nangli at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Nangli is 3570th warmest (23.3 °C year average), 496th sunniest (about 2,830 hours of sunshine a year) and 2935th wettest (783 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Amritsar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Nangli is about as warm in June and about as warm in January, with 34 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (32.7 °C), averages 21.0 °C more than its coolest, January (11.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 1,078 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Nangli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,078 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nangli itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (31.686°N, 74.887°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-523-2548), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
