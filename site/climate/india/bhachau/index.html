Bhachāu at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Bhachāu is 521st warmest (27.6 °C year average), 7th sunniest (about 3,224 hours of sunshine a year) and 3559th wettest (542 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gāndhīdhām, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Bhachāu is 1.1 °C warmer in May and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 17 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.5 °C), averages 12.6 °C more than its coolest, January (20.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.0 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bhachāu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bhachāu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bhachāu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (23.299°N, 70.343°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-607-2503), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
