How December compares in Kalaburagi

December is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Kalaburagi. Compared with November: 1.7 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. January is 0.5 °C warmer than December on average.

Kalaburagi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kalaburagi in December?
In December, Kalaburagi averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 16.1 °C (61 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 270 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kalaburagi?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Kalaburagi’s mild months (January, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kalaburagi in December?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (203 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Kalaburagi in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kalaburagi get in December?
About 270 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kalaburagi in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
