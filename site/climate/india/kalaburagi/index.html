Kalaburagi at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Kalaburagi is 85th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 154th sunniest (about 2,728 hours of sunshine a year) and 390th wettest (817 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bidar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (96 km away), Kalaburagi is 1.0 °C warmer in May and 1.7 °C warmer in December, with 169 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.4 °C), averages 10.6 °C more than its coolest, December (22.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.6 °C (62 °F), in December. The area lies about 388 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kalaburagi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kalaburagi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 388 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
