How October compares in Kalaburagi

October is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kalaburagi. Compared with September: 0.5 °C cooler, 116 mm less precipitation and 63 hours more sunshine. November is 1.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Kalaburagi in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kalaburagi in October?
In October, Kalaburagi averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kalaburagi?
October is not one of Kalaburagi’s mild months; those are January, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kalaburagi in October?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (205 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Kalaburagi in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kalaburagi get in October?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kalaburagi in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
