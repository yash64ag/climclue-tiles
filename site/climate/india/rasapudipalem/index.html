Rasapūdipalem at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Rasapūdipalem is 97th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 344th sunniest (about 2,552 hours of sunshine a year) and 192nd wettest (1,230 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gajuwaka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Rasapūdipalem is 0.3 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 94 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.1 °C), averages 7.0 °C more than its coolest, January (23.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.3 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Rasapūdipalem has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
