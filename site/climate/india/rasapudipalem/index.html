Rasapūdipalem at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Rasapūdipalem is 695th warmest (27.3 °C year average), 2237th sunniest (about 2,552 hours of sunshine a year) and 1703rd wettest (1,139 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gajuwaka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Rasapūdipalem is 0.4 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 71 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.3 °C), averages 6.9 °C more than its coolest, January (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.0 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Rasapūdipalem has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Rasapūdipalem itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (17.733°N, 83.316°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
