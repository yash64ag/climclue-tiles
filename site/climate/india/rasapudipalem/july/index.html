Rasapūdipalem in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rasapūdipalem in July?
In July, Rasapūdipalem averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 151 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 129 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Rasapūdipalem?
July is not one of Rasapūdipalem’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rasapūdipalem in July?
151 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (258 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Rasapūdipalem in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rasapūdipalem get in July?
About 129 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Rasapūdipalem in July?
About 28.7 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (29.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Rasapūdipalem in July?
