Kukshi weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Kukshi, India (Madhya Pradesh), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Kukshi is warmest in May, with 41.3 °C (106 °F) days and 26.1 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 29.7 °C (85 °F) days and 12.3 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (252 mm); April is driest (1 mm).

Kukshi at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Kukshi is 726th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 323rd sunniest (about 2,880 hours of sunshine a year) and 2874th wettest (801 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Barwāni, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Kukshi is 0.4 °C cooler in May and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 39 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.7 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, January (21.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.3 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 237 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kukshi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kukshi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 237 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kukshi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.207°N, 74.758°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-617-2547), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
