Jhanjhārpur at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Jhanjhārpur is 2468th warmest (25.4 °C year average), 3611th sunniest (about 2,162 hours of sunshine a year) and 975th wettest (1,488 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ghogardīha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Jhanjhārpur is 0.3 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 101 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.6 °C), averages 14.3 °C more than its coolest, January (16.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.3 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 529 km from the sea.

Why Jhanjhārpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 529 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jhanjhārpur itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.265°N, 86.280°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-577-2662), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
