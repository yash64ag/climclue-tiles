Shrīrangapattana weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Shrīrangapattana, India (Karnataka), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Shrīrangapattana is warmest in April, with 34.7 °C (94 °F) days and 21.7 °C nights, and coolest in December, with 27.8 °C (82 °F) days and 17.0 °C nights. Most rain falls around October (152 mm); February is driest (2 mm).

Shrīrangapattana at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Shrīrangapattana is 2917th warmest (24.8 °C year average), 2909th sunniest (about 2,423 hours of sunshine a year) and 3275th wettest (660 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with French Rocks, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Shrīrangapattana is 0.3 °C warmer in April and 0.3 °C warmer in December, with 36 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.2 °C), averages 5.8 °C more than its coolest, December (22.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.0 °C (63 °F), in December. The area lies about 158 km from the sea.

Why Shrīrangapattana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 158 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Shrīrangapattana itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (12.423°N, 76.684°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-715-2566), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
