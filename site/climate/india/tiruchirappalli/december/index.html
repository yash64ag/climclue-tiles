How December compares in Tiruchirappalli

December is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Tiruchirappalli. Compared with November: 1.1 °C cooler, 91 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. January is 0.1 °C warmer than December on average.

Tiruchirappalli in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tiruchirappalli in December?
In December, Tiruchirappalli averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Tiruchirappalli?
No month in Tiruchirappalli is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tiruchirappalli in December?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (169 mm), the driest January (13 mm).
Does it snow in Tiruchirappalli in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tiruchirappalli get in December?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tiruchirappalli in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
