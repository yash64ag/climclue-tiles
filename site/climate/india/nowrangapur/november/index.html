How November compares in Nowrangapur

November is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Nowrangapur. Compared with October: 2.7 °C cooler, 77 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. December is 2.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Nowrangapur in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nowrangapur in November?
In November, Nowrangapur averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Nowrangapur?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Nowrangapur’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nowrangapur in November?
24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (383 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Nowrangapur in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nowrangapur get in November?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nowrangapur in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
