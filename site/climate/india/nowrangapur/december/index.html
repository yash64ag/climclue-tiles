How December compares in Nowrangapur

December is the coolest month, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Nowrangapur. Compared with November: 2.3 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. January is 1.0 °C warmer than December on average.

Nowrangapur in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nowrangapur in December?
In December, Nowrangapur averages a day high of 26.2 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 11.5 °C (53 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Nowrangapur?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Nowrangapur’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nowrangapur in December?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (383 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Nowrangapur in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nowrangapur get in December?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nowrangapur in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
