Nowrangapur in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nowrangapur in May?
In May, Nowrangapur averages a day high of 36.1 °C (97 °F) and a night low of 24.4 °C (76 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 258 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Nowrangapur?
May is not one of Nowrangapur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nowrangapur in May?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (386 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Nowrangapur in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nowrangapur get in May?
About 258 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
