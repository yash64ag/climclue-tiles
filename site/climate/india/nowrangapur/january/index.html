How January compares in Nowrangapur

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Nowrangapur. Compared with December: 0.9 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. February is 3.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Nowrangapur in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nowrangapur in January?
In January, Nowrangapur averages a day high of 26.5 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 13.1 °C (56 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 256 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Nowrangapur?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Nowrangapur’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nowrangapur in January?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (386 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Nowrangapur in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nowrangapur get in January?
About 256 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nowrangapur in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
