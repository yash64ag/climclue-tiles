Nowrangapur weather in June

Average June weather in Nowrangapur, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Nowrangapur averages 32.0 °C (90 °F) days and 22.6 °C (73 °F) nights, with 215 mm (8.5 in) of rain over the month and about 162 hours of sunshine.
