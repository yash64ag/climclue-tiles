Chitradurga at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Chitradurga is 377th warmest (25.1 °C year average), 350th sunniest (about 2,541 hours of sunshine a year) and 498th wettest (569 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Davangere, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Chitradurga is 0.1 °C warmer in April and 1.0 °C cooler in December, with 72 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.1 °C), averages 7.0 °C more than its coolest, December (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.1 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 212 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chitradurga, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chitradurga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 212 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
