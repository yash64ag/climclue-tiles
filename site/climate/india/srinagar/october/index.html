How October compares in Srinagar

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Srinagar. Compared with September: 6.2 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. November is 5.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Srinagar in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Srinagar in October?
In October, Srinagar averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 6.4 °C (44 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Srinagar?
October is not one of Srinagar’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Srinagar in October?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (141 mm), the driest November (38 mm).
Does it snow in Srinagar in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Srinagar get in October?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Srinagar in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
