How April compares in Srinagar

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Srinagar. Compared with March: 5.8 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. May is 4.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Srinagar in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Srinagar in April?
In April, Srinagar averages a day high of 19.0 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 7.2 °C (45 °F), with 138 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Srinagar?
April is not one of Srinagar’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Srinagar in April?
138 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (141 mm), the driest November (38 mm).
Does it snow in Srinagar in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Srinagar get in April?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Srinagar in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
