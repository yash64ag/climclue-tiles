How August compares in Srinagar

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Srinagar. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. September is 3.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Srinagar in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Srinagar in August?
In August, Srinagar averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Srinagar?
August is not one of Srinagar’s mild months; those are June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Srinagar in August?
101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (155 mm), the driest November (42 mm).
Does it snow in Srinagar in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Srinagar get in August?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Srinagar in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
