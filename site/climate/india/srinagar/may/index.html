How May compares in Srinagar

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Srinagar. Compared with April: 4.2 °C warmer, 33 mm less precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. June is 3.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Srinagar in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Srinagar in May?
In May, Srinagar averages a day high of 21.7 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 9.3 °C (49 °F), with 120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Srinagar?
May is not one of Srinagar’s mild months; those are June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Srinagar in May?
120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (155 mm), the driest November (42 mm).
Does it snow in Srinagar in May?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.9 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in May (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Srinagar get in May?
About 278 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Srinagar in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
