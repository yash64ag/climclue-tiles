How December compares in Srinagar

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Srinagar. Compared with November: 5.0 °C cooler, 11 mm more precipitation and 42 hours less sunshine. January is 2.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Srinagar in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Srinagar in December?
In December, Srinagar averages a day high of 7.7 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -2.0 °C (28 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Srinagar?
December is not one of Srinagar’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Srinagar in December?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (141 mm), the driest November (38 mm).
Does it snow in Srinagar in December?
Yes, on average: about 7.0 cm (2.8 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Srinagar get in December?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Srinagar in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
