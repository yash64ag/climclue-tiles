Jorhat at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Jorhat is 476th warmest (23.8 °C year average), 417th sunniest (about 2,373 hours of sunshine a year) and 24th wettest (2,639 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dimāpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (106 km away), Jorhat is 0.4 °C warmer in August and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 699 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.6 °C), averages 12.0 °C more than its coolest, January (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.7 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 536 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jorhat, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jorhat has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 536 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
