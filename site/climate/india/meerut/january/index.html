How January compares in Meerut

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Meerut. Compared with December: 1.2 °C cooler, 11 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. February is 3.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Meerut in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Meerut in January?
In January, Meerut averages a day high of 20.1 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 7.8 °C (46 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Meerut?
January is not one of Meerut’s mild months; those are February, March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Meerut in January?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (249 mm), the driest November (7 mm).
Does it snow in Meerut in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Meerut get in January?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Meerut in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
