Meerut at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Meerut is 454th warmest (24.4 °C year average), 273rd sunniest (about 2,659 hours of sunshine a year) and 348th wettest (887 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Modīnagar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Meerut is 0.2 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 87 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (32.6 °C), averages 19.1 °C more than its coolest, January (13.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.8 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 924 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Meerut, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Meerut has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 924 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
