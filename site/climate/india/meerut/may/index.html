How May compares in Meerut

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Meerut. Compared with April: 3.4 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. June is 0.4 °C warmer than May on average.

Meerut in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Meerut in May?
In May, Meerut averages a day high of 39.7 °C (103 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 267 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Meerut?
May is not one of Meerut’s mild months; those are February, March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Meerut in May?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (245 mm), the driest November (7 mm).
Does it snow in Meerut in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Meerut get in May?
About 267 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Meerut in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
