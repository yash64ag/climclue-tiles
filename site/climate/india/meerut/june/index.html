How June compares in Meerut

June is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Meerut. Compared with May: 0.4 °C warmer, 30 mm more precipitation and 59 hours less sunshine. July is 2.6 °C cooler than June on average.

Meerut in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Meerut in June?
In June, Meerut averages a day high of 38.5 °C (101 °F) and a night low of 26.7 °C (80 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Meerut?
June is not one of Meerut’s mild months; those are February, March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Meerut in June?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (245 mm), the driest November (7 mm).
Does it snow in Meerut in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Meerut get in June?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Meerut in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
