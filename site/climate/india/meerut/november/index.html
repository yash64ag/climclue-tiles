Meerut weather in November

Average November weather in Meerut, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Meerut averages 27.1 °C (81 °F) days and 11.3 °C (52 °F) nights, with 7 mm (0.3 in) of rain over the month and about 239 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Meerut

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Meerut. Compared with October: 5.3 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. December is 4.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Meerut in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Meerut in November?
In November, Meerut averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 11.3 °C (52 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 239 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Meerut?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Meerut’s mild months (February, March, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Meerut in November?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (245 mm), the driest November (7 mm).
Does it snow in Meerut in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Meerut get in November?
About 239 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Meerut in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
