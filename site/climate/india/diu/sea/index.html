Diu sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu · India

The sea off Diu averages 29.0 °C (84 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 24.0 °C (75 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Diu sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Diu?
In June, at about 29.0 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Diu?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 24.0 °C (75 °F).
How warm is the sea in Diu in January?
About 24.0 °C (75 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Diu?
About 26.8 °C (80 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Diu nearby?
In June: Talāja (29.7 °C, 130 km).
