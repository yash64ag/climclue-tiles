How December compares in Mysuru

December is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Mysuru. Compared with November: 0.7 °C cooler, 41 mm less precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. January is 0.8 °C warmer than December on average.

Mysuru in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mysuru in December?
In December, Mysuru averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 16.6 °C (62 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Mysuru?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Mysuru’s mild months (January, February, June, August, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mysuru in December?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (174 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Mysuru in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mysuru get in December?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mysuru in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
