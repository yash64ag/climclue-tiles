How May compares in Mysuru

May is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Mysuru. Compared with April: 1.0 °C cooler, 53 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. June is 2.2 °C cooler than May on average.

Mysuru in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mysuru in May?
In May, Mysuru averages a day high of 32.3 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 119 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Mysuru?
May is not one of Mysuru’s mild months; those are January, February, June, August, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mysuru in May?
119 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (174 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Mysuru in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mysuru get in May?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mysuru in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
