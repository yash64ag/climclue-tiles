How September compares in Mysuru

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Mysuru. Compared with August: 0.4 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. October is 0.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Mysuru in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mysuru in September?
In September, Mysuru averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 20.1 °C (68 °F), with 110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Mysuru?
September is not one of Mysuru’s mild months; those are January, February, June, August, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mysuru in September?
110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (174 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Mysuru in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mysuru get in September?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mysuru in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
