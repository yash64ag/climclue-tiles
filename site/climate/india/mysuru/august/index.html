How August compares in Mysuru

August is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Mysuru. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. September is 0.4 °C warmer than August on average.

Mysuru in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mysuru in August?
In August, Mysuru averages a day high of 26.9 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 90 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 129 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Mysuru?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Mysuru’s mild months (January, February, June, August, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mysuru in August?
90 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (174 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Mysuru in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mysuru get in August?
About 129 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mysuru in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
