How October compares in Mysuru

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Mysuru. Compared with September: 0.1 °C cooler, 65 mm more precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. November is 0.9 °C cooler than October on average.

Mysuru in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mysuru in October?
In October, Mysuru averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 174 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Mysuru?
October is not one of Mysuru’s mild months; those are January, February, June, August, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mysuru in October?
174 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is October (174 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Mysuru in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mysuru get in October?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mysuru in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
