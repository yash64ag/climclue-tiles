Mysuru at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Mysuru is 461st warmest (24.3 °C year average), 399th sunniest (about 2,417 hours of sunshine a year) and 378th wettest (835 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mandya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Mysuru is 0.9 °C cooler in April and 0.3 °C cooler in December, with 119 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (27.4 °C), averages 5.5 °C more than its coolest, December (21.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.3 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 147 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mysuru, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mysuru has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 147 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
