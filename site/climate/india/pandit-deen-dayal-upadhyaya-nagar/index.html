Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Pandit Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Nagar is 256th warmest (25.8 °C year average), 372nd sunniest (about 2,479 hours of sunshine a year) and 261st wettest (1,066 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Pandit Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Nagar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 588 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
