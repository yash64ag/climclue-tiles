How August compares in Hyderabad

August is the 6th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Hyderabad. Compared with July: 0.7 °C cooler, 11 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. September is 0.3 °C warmer than August on average.

Hyderabad in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hyderabad in August?
In August, Hyderabad averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 183 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation and about 133 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Hyderabad?
August is not one of Hyderabad’s mild months; those are January, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hyderabad in August?
183 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (183 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Hyderabad in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hyderabad get in August?
About 133 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hyderabad in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
