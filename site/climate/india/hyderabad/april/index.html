How April compares in Hyderabad

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Hyderabad. Compared with March: 2.5 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. May is 1.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Hyderabad in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hyderabad in April?
In April, Hyderabad averages a day high of 38.0 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 274 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Hyderabad?
April is not one of Hyderabad’s mild months; those are January, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hyderabad in April?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (191 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Hyderabad in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hyderabad get in April?
About 274 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hyderabad in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
