How November compares in Hyderabad

November is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Hyderabad. Compared with October: 2.0 °C cooler, 61 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. December is 1.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Hyderabad in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hyderabad in November?
In November, Hyderabad averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 18.0 °C (64 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 242 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Hyderabad?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Hyderabad’s mild months (January, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hyderabad in November?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (183 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Hyderabad in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hyderabad get in November?
About 242 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hyderabad in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
