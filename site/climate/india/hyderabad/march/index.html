How March compares in Hyderabad

March is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Hyderabad. Compared with February: 3.4 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. April is 2.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Hyderabad in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hyderabad in March?
In March, Hyderabad averages a day high of 34.7 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 265 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Hyderabad?
March is not one of Hyderabad’s mild months; those are January, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hyderabad in March?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (183 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Hyderabad in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hyderabad get in March?
About 265 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hyderabad in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
