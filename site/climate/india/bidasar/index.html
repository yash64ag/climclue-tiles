Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Bīdāsar is 1779th warmest (26.2 °C year average), 225th sunniest (about 2,932 hours of sunshine a year) and 3720th wettest (337 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Bīdāsar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 662 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
