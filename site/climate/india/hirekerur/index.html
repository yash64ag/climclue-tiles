Hirekerūr weather by month and climate

Hirekerūr at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Hirekerūr is 3036th warmest (24.7 °C year average), 2009th sunniest (about 2,611 hours of sunshine a year) and 1981st wettest (1,044 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sirālkoppa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Hirekerūr is 0.5 °C warmer in April and about as warm in December, with 335 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.0 °C), averages 5.1 °C more than its coolest, December (22.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.9 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 108 km from the sea.

Why Hirekerūr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 108 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hirekerūr itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.455°N, 75.395°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-695-2553), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
