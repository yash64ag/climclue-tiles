Mirzāpur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Mirzāpur is 221st warmest (26.1 °C year average), 339th sunniest (about 2,560 hours of sunshine a year) and 298th wettest (998 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gyānpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Mirzāpur is 0.3 °C warmer in May and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 22 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.8 °C), averages 17.7 °C more than its coolest, January (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.4 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 624 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Mirzāpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 624 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
