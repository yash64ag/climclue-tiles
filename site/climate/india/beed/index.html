Beed at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Beed is 163rd warmest (26.6 °C year average), 109th sunniest (about 2,773 hours of sunshine a year) and 429th wettest (731 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Barshi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (84 km away), Beed is about as warm in May and 0.9 °C cooler in December, with 46 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.9 °C), averages 11.1 °C more than its coolest, December (21.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.8 °C (60 °F), in December. The area lies about 305 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Beed, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Beed has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 305 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
