Gummidipundi at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Gummidipundi is 252nd warmest (28.1 °C year average), 1653rd sunniest (about 2,668 hours of sunshine a year) and 1290th wettest (1,306 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ponneri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Gummidipundi is 0.4 °C warmer in May and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 24 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.7 °C), averages 7.4 °C more than its coolest, January (24.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.4 °C (67 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Gummidipundi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
