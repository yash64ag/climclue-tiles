Yavatmāl at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Yavatmāl is 151st warmest (26.6 °C year average), 108th sunniest (about 2,773 hours of sunshine a year) and 199th wettest (1,203 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Wardha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (62 km away), Yavatmāl is 0.8 °C cooler in May and 0.6 °C cooler in December, with 22 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.8 °C), averages 14.4 °C more than its coolest, December (20.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.3 °C (58 °F), in December. The area lies about 554 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yavatmāl, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yavatmāl has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 554 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
