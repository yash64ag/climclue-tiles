Shahuwadi at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Shahuwadi is 488th warmest (23.6 °C year average), 150th sunniest (about 2,733 hours of sunshine a year) and 28th wettest (2,519 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kolhāpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Shahuwadi is 1.7 °C cooler in April and 0.5 °C cooler in December, with 1,201 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (26.6 °C), averages 5.1 °C more than its coolest, December (21.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.1 °C (59 °F), in January. The area lies about 78 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shahuwadi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shahuwadi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 78 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
