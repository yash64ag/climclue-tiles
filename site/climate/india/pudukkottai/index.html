Pudukkottai at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Pudukkottai is 16th warmest (28.5 °C year average), 371st sunniest (about 2,481 hours of sunshine a year) and 323rd wettest (955 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kāraikkudi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Pudukkottai is 0.2 °C cooler in May and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 15 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.6 °C), averages 6.7 °C more than its coolest, January (24.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.0 °C (68 °F), in January. The area lies about 59 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pudukkottai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pudukkottai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 59 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
