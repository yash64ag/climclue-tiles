Ādilābād at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Ādilābād is 58th warmest (27.8 °C year average), 125th sunniest (about 2,754 hours of sunshine a year) and 252nd wettest (1,073 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chānda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (85 km away), Ādilābād is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.3 °C warmer in December, with 211 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (35.5 °C), averages 13.4 °C more than its coolest, December (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.1 °C (61 °F), in December. The area lies about 478 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ādilābād, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ādilābād has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 478 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
