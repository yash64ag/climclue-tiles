Phagwāra weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Phagwāra, India (Punjab), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Phagwāra is warmest in June, with 39.6 °C (103 °F) days and 25.6 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 19.0 °C (66 °F) days and 5.0 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (234 mm); November is driest (10 mm).

Phagwāra at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Phagwāra is 3516th warmest (23.5 °C year average), 774th sunniest (about 2,775 hours of sunshine a year) and 2796th wettest (825 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gorāya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Phagwāra is 0.1 °C warmer in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (32.5 °C), averages 20.6 °C more than its coolest, January (11.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.0 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,063 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Phagwāra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,063 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Phagwāra itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (31.225°N, 75.774°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-527-2557), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
