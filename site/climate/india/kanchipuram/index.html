Kanchipuram at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Kanchipuram is 35th warmest (28.1 °C year average), 298th sunniest (about 2,635 hours of sunshine a year) and 202nd wettest (1,196 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rānipet, the nearest covered town with different numbers (41 km away), Kanchipuram is about as warm in May and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 110 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.9 °C), averages 8.0 °C more than its coolest, January (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.1 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 64 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kanchipuram, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kanchipuram has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 64 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
