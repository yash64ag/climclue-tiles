Mandsaur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Mandsaur is 276th warmest (25.7 °C year average), 45th sunniest (about 2,896 hours of sunshine a year) and 375th wettest (840 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nimach, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Mandsaur is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 37 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.5 °C), averages 15.5 °C more than its coolest, January (17.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.3 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 340 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mandsaur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mandsaur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 340 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
