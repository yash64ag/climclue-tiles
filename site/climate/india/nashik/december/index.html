How December compares in Nashik

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Nashik. Compared with November: 2.3 °C cooler, 22 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. January is 0.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Nashik in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nashik in December?
In December, Nashik averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 14.5 °C (58 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 273 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Nashik?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Nashik’s mild months (January, February, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nashik in December?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (452 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Nashik in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nashik get in December?
About 273 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nashik in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
