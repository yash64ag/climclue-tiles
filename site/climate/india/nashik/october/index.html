How October compares in Nashik

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Nashik. Compared with September: 0.4 °C warmer, 158 mm less precipitation and 74 hours more sunshine. November is 2.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Nashik in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nashik in October?
In October, Nashik averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 250 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Nashik?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Nashik’s mild months (January, February, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nashik in October?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (452 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Nashik in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nashik get in October?
About 250 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nashik in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
