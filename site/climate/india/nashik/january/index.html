Nashik weather in January

Average January weather in Nashik, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Nashik averages 29.1 °C (84 °F) days and 11.0 °C (52 °F) nights, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of rain over the month and about 280 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Nashik

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Nashik. Compared with December: 0.3 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 1.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Nashik in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nashik in January?
In January, Nashik averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 11.0 °C (52 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 280 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Nashik?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Nashik’s mild months (January, February, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nashik in January?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (372 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Nashik in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nashik get in January?
About 280 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nashik in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
