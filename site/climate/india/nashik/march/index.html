How March compares in Nashik

March is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Nashik. Compared with February: 3.7 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. April is 2.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Nashik in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nashik in March?
In March, Nashik averages a day high of 34.5 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 16.6 °C (62 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 283 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Nashik?
March is not one of Nashik’s mild months; those are January, February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nashik in March?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (372 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Nashik in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nashik get in March?
About 283 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nashik in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
