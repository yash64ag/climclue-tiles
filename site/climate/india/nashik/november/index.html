How November compares in Nashik

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Nashik. Compared with October: 2.0 °C cooler, 57 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. December is 2.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Nashik in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nashik in November?
In November, Nashik averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 14.9 °C (59 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 258 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Nashik?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Nashik’s mild months (January, February, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nashik in November?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (372 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Nashik in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nashik get in November?
About 258 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nashik in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
