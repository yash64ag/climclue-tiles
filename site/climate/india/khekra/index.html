Khekra weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Khekra, India (Uttar Pradesh), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Khekra is warmest in June, with 38.9 °C (102 °F) days and 27.3 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 20.4 °C (69 °F) days and 6.3 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (186 mm); November is driest (6 mm).

Khekra at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Khekra is 3126th warmest (24.6 °C year average), 1517th sunniest (about 2,680 hours of sunshine a year) and 3195th wettest (691 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bāghpat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Khekra is 0.1 °C warmer in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.1 °C), averages 19.7 °C more than its coolest, January (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.3 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 887 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Khekra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 887 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Khekra itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (28.866°N, 77.284°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-551-2572), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
