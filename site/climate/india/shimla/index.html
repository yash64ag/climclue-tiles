Why Shimla has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,104 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Shimla climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Shimla?
June, with an average of 21.6 °C (71 °F), day highs around 26.3 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 15.6 °C (60 °F).
What is the coldest month in Shimla?
January, with an average of 4.8 °C (41 °F), day highs around 11.1 °C (52 °F) and night lows around -1.5 °C (29 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Shimla?
July, with 477 mm (18.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,864 mm (73.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Shimla?
November, with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Shimla?
May, with about 266 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Shimla?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 18 cm (7.0 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Shimla?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April and May.
What type of climate does Shimla have?
Subtropical highland, dry winter (Köppen Cwb). Mild highland climate with wet summers and dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.5 × 11 km, that contains Shimla’s GeoNames location (31.104°N, 77.167°E; ClimaClue area sq-528-2571), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 173,503 (GeoNames).
