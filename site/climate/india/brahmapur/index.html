Why Brahmapur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Brahmapur climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Brahmapur?
May, with an average of 31.1 °C (88 °F), day highs around 36.2 °C (97 °F) and night lows around 26.9 °C (80 °F).
What is the coldest month in Brahmapur?
December, with an average of 21.3 °C (70 °F), day highs around 26.2 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 16.3 °C (61 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Brahmapur?
September, with 243 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,345 mm (52.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Brahmapur?
December, with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Brahmapur?
January, with about 254 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Brahmapur?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Brahmapur?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, November and December.
What type of climate does Brahmapur have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Brahmapur’s GeoNames location (19.312°N, 84.793°E; ClimaClue area sq-646-2647), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 356,598 (GeoNames).
