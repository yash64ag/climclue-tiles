Barwala weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Barwala, India (Gujarat), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Barwala at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Barwala is 85th warmest (28.7 °C year average), 144th sunniest (about 2,987 hours of sunshine a year) and 2815th wettest (820 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dhandhuka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Barwala is 0.7 °C cooler in May and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.4 °C), averages 12.5 °C more than its coolest, January (21.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.8 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 39 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Barwala, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Barwala has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 39 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Barwala itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.220°N, 72.068°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-617-2520), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
