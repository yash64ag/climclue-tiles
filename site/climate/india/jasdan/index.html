Jasdan at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Jasdan is 1120th warmest (26.9 °C year average), 116th sunniest (about 3,016 hours of sunshine a year) and 2720th wettest (841 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bābra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Jasdan is 0.2 °C cooler in May and about as warm in January, with 58 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.3 °C), averages 11.8 °C more than its coolest, January (20.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.3 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 104 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jasdan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jasdan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 104 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-619-2512), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
