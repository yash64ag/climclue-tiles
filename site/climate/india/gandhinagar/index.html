Gandhinagar at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Gandhinagar is 114th warmest (27.0 °C year average), 19th sunniest (about 3,012 hours of sunshine a year) and 365th wettest (858 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kalol, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Gandhinagar is 0.1 °C cooler in May and about as warm in January, with 24 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.4 °C), averages 13.8 °C more than its coolest, January (19.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.4 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 124 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gandhinagar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gandhinagar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 124 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
