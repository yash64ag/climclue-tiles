Alang sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Gujarat · India

The sea off Alang averages 29.7 °C (85 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 22.5 °C (73 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December.

Alang sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Alang?
In June, at about 29.7 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Alang?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December. The coolest month is January, at 22.5 °C (73 °F).
How warm is the sea in Alang in January?
About 22.5 °C (73 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Alang?
About 26.8 °C (80 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
