Begūn at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Begūn is 2297th warmest (25.6 °C year average), 283rd sunniest (about 2,902 hours of sunshine a year) and 3250th wettest (669 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Māndalgarh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Begūn is 0.3 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 19 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.1 °C), averages 17.5 °C more than its coolest, January (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.2 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 409 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Begūn, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Begūn has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 409 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-590-2550), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
