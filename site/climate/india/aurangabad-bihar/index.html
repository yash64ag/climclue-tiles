Aurangābād at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Aurangābād is 264th warmest (25.8 °C year average), 424th sunniest (about 2,338 hours of sunshine a year) and 218th wettest (1,167 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Madhurampur Dehri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Aurangābād is about as warm in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.1 °C), averages 17.0 °C more than its coolest, January (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.0 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 465 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Aurangābād, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Aurangābād has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 465 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
