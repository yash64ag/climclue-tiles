Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Serilingampalle is 1846th warmest (26.1 °C year average), 1348th sunniest (about 2,694 hours of sunshine a year) and 2433rd wettest (911 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Serilingampalle has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 292 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
