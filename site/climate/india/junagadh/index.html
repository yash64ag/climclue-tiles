Jūnāgadh at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Jūnāgadh is 205th warmest (26.1 °C year average), 17th sunniest (about 3,031 hours of sunshine a year) and 317th wettest (959 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jetpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Jūnāgadh is 0.6 °C cooler in May and about as warm in January, with 157 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.6 °C), averages 10.8 °C more than its coolest, January (19.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.3 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 68 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jūnāgadh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jūnāgadh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 68 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
