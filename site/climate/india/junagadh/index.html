Jūnāgadh at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Jūnāgadh is 1856th warmest (26.1 °C year average), 101st sunniest (about 3,031 hours of sunshine a year) and 2126th wettest (1,004 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mendarda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Jūnāgadh is 0.6 °C warmer in May and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 17 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.6 °C), averages 10.7 °C more than its coolest, January (19.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.4 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 68 km from the sea.

Why Jūnāgadh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 68 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.7 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jūnāgadh itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.520°N, 70.460°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-624-2504), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
