Shāhpur, Uttar Pradesh at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Shāhpur, Uttar Pradesh is 3288th warmest (24.2 °C year average), 1687th sunniest (about 2,666 hours of sunshine a year) and 2220th wettest (974 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Budhāna, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Shāhpur, Uttar Pradesh is 0.1 °C cooler in June and about as warm in January, with 67 mm more precipitation a year. Why Shāhpur, Uttar Pradesh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 949 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Shāhpur itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (29.350°N, 77.552°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-546-2575), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
