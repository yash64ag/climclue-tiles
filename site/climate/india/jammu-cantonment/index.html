Jammu Cantonment at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Jammu Cantonment is 3628th warmest (22.4 °C year average), 263rd sunniest (about 2,912 hours of sunshine a year) and 1011th wettest (1,466 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jammu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Jammu Cantonment is 0.3 °C warmer in June and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 38 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (31.7 °C), averages 20.1 °C more than its coolest, January (11.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.2 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,158 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Jammu Cantonment has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,158 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jammu Cantonment itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (32.704°N, 74.861°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-512-2548), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
