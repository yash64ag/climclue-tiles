Borivli in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Borivli in August?
In August, Borivli averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 586 mm (23.1 in) of precipitation and about 87 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Borivli?
August is not one of Borivli’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Borivli in August?
586 mm (23.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (904 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Borivli in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Borivli get in August?
About 87 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Borivli in August?
About 27.6 °C (82 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (29.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Borivli in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
