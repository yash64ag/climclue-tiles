Borivli at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Borivli is 143rd warmest (26.7 °C year average), 224th sunniest (about 2,684 hours of sunshine a year) and 30th wettest (2,494 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bhayandar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Borivli is 0.2 °C cooler in May and about as warm in January, with 92 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.4 °C), averages 6.0 °C more than its coolest, January (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.8 °C (68 °F), in January. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Borivli, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Borivli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
