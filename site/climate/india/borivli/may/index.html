How May compares in Borivli

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Borivli. Compared with April: 1.2 °C warmer, 25 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. June is 0.9 °C cooler than May on average.

Borivli in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Borivli in May?
In May, Borivli averages a day high of 33.4 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 25.4 °C (78 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 306 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Borivli?
May is not one of Borivli’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Borivli in May?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (904 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Borivli in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Borivli get in May?
About 306 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Borivli in May?
About 29.5 °C (85 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (29.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Borivli in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
