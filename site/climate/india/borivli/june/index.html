How June compares in Borivli

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Borivli. Compared with May: 0.9 °C cooler, 461 mm more precipitation and 137 hours less sunshine. July is 1.3 °C cooler than June on average.

Borivli in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Borivli in June?
In June, Borivli averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 26.6 °C (80 °F), with 487 mm (19.2 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Borivli?
June is not one of Borivli’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Borivli in June?
487 mm (19.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (904 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Borivli in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Borivli get in June?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Borivli in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
