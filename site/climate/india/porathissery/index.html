Porathissery at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Porathissery is 691st warmest (27.3 °C year average), 2560th sunniest (about 2,478 hours of sunshine a year) and 395th wettest (2,843 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kattur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Porathissery is 0.2 °C warmer in March and about as warm in July, with 23 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (29.0 °C), averages 3.1 °C more than its coolest, July (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.8 °C (73 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Porathissery has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
