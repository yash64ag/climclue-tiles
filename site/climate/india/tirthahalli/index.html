Tīrthahalli at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Tīrthahalli is 3422nd warmest (23.8 °C year average), 2179th sunniest (about 2,565 hours of sunshine a year) and 143rd wettest (3,550 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Someshwar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Tīrthahalli is 1.7 °C cooler in April and 2.2 °C cooler in August, with 3,618 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (26.2 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, August (22.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.1 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 73 km from the sea.

Why Tīrthahalli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 73 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tīrthahalli itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (13.688°N, 75.245°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-703-2552), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
