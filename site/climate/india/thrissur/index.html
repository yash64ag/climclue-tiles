Thrissur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Thrissur is 84th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 373rd sunniest (about 2,479 hours of sunshine a year) and 22nd wettest (2,768 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ponnāni, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Thrissur is about as warm in March and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 86 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (29.4 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, July (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.4 °C (72 °F), in December. The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Thrissur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Thrissur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
