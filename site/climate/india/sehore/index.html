Sehore at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Sehore is 303rd warmest (25.5 °C year average), 83rd sunniest (about 2,810 hours of sunshine a year) and 246th wettest (1,089 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bhopal, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Sehore is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 95 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.9 °C), averages 15.8 °C more than its coolest, January (18.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.9 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 487 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sehore, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sehore has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 487 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
