Why Vidisha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 575 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Vidisha climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Vidisha?
May, with an average of 34.3 °C (94 °F), day highs around 40.4 °C (105 °F) and night lows around 27.4 °C (81 °F).
What is the coldest month in Vidisha?
January, with an average of 17.3 °C (63 °F), day highs around 24.4 °C (76 °F) and night lows around 10.8 °C (51 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Vidisha?
July, with 372 mm (14.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,133 mm (44.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Vidisha?
April, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Vidisha?
May, with about 299 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Vidisha?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Vidisha?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Vidisha’s GeoNames location (23.526°N, 77.811°E; ClimaClue area sq-604-2578), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 155,951 (GeoNames).
