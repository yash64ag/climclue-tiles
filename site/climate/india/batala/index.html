Batāla at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Batāla is 511th warmest (22.7 °C year average), 79th sunniest (about 2,820 hours of sunshine a year) and 346th wettest (896 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Amritsar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Batāla is 0.9 °C cooler in June and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 138 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (31.9 °C), averages 20.4 °C more than its coolest, January (11.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.5 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,114 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Batāla, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Batāla has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,114 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
