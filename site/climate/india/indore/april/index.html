How April compares in Indore

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Indore. Compared with March: 4.5 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. May is 1.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Indore in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Indore in April?
In April, Indore averages a day high of 37.6 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 297 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Indore?
April is not one of Indore’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Indore in April?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (294 mm), the driest April (2 mm).
Does it snow in Indore in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Indore get in April?
About 297 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Indore in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
