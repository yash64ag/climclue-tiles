How September compares in Indore

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Indore. Compared with August: 0.7 °C warmer, 95 mm less precipitation and 95 hours more sunshine. October is 0.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Indore in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Indore in September?
In September, Indore averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Indore?
September is not one of Indore’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Indore in September?
168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (294 mm), the driest April (2 mm).
Does it snow in Indore in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Indore get in September?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Indore in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
