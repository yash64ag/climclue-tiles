How February compares in Indore

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Indore. Compared with January: 2.6 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. March is 5.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Indore in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Indore in February?
In February, Indore averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 14.3 °C (58 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 268 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Indore?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Indore’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Indore in February?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (294 mm), the driest April (2 mm).
Does it snow in Indore in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Indore get in February?
About 268 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Indore in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
