How March compares in Indore

March is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Indore. Compared with February: 5.1 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. April is 4.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Indore in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Indore in March?
In March, Indore averages a day high of 33.4 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 18.8 °C (66 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 282 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Indore?
March is not one of Indore’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Indore in March?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (294 mm), the driest April (2 mm).
Does it snow in Indore in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Indore get in March?
About 282 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Indore in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
