How January compares in Indore

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Indore. Compared with December: 0.6 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 2.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Indore in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Indore in January?
In January, Indore averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 284 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Indore?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Indore’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Indore in January?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (294 mm), the driest April (2 mm).
Does it snow in Indore in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Indore get in January?
About 284 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Indore in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
