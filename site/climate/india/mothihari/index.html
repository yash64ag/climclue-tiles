Mothīhāri at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Mothīhāri is 321st warmest (25.4 °C year average), 463rd sunniest (about 2,198 hours of sunshine a year) and 133rd wettest (1,464 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bettiah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Mothīhāri is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 49 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.3 °C), averages 15.4 °C more than its coolest, January (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.3 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 620 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mothīhāri, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mothīhāri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 620 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
