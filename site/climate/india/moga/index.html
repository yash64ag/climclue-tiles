Moga at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Moga is 474th warmest (23.9 °C year average), 80th sunniest (about 2,817 hours of sunshine a year) and 506th wettest (521 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Firozpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Moga is 0.4 °C cooler in June and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 10 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.0 °C), averages 21.1 °C more than its coolest, January (11.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.9 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,004 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Moga, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Moga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,004 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
