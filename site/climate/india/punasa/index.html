Punāsa at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Punāsa is 137th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 59th sunniest (about 2,850 hours of sunshine a year) and 248th wettest (1,088 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Khandwa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Punāsa is 0.5 °C warmer in May and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 130 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.8 °C), averages 14.9 °C more than its coolest, January (19.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.7 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 399 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Punāsa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Punāsa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 399 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
