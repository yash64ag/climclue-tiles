Modīnagar at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Modīnagar is 3186th warmest (24.5 °C year average), 1680th sunniest (about 2,666 hours of sunshine a year) and 2861st wettest (805 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Modīnagar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 904 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-551-2575), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
