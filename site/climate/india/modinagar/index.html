Why Modīnagar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 904 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Modīnagar climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Modīnagar?
June, with an average of 32.9 °C (91 °F), day highs around 38.0 °C (100 °F) and night lows around 27.0 °C (81 °F).
What is the coldest month in Modīnagar?
January, with an average of 13.4 °C (56 °F), day highs around 20.1 °C (68 °F) and night lows around 7.6 °C (46 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Modīnagar?
July, with 222 mm (8.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 800 mm (31.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Modīnagar?
November, with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Modīnagar?
May, with about 267 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Modīnagar?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Modīnagar?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March, October and November.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.8 × 11 km, that contains Modīnagar’s GeoNames location (28.831°N, 77.573°E; ClimaClue area sq-551-2575), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 475,843 (GeoNames).
