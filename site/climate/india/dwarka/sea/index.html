Dwārka sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Gujarat · India

The sea off Dwārka averages 29.2 °C (85 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 23.4 °C (74 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December.

Dwārka sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Dwārka?
In June, at about 29.2 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Dwārka?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December. The coolest month is January, at 23.4 °C (74 °F).
How warm is the sea in Dwārka in January?
About 23.4 °C (74 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Dwārka?
About 26.7 °C (80 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Dwārka nearby?
In June: Sikka (29.8 °C, 92 km), Bedi (29.8 °C, 114 km) and Jamnagar (29.8 °C, 116 km).
