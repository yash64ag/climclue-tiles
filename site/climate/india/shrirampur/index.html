Shrīrāmpur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Shrīrāmpur is 228th warmest (26.0 °C year average), 511th sunniest (about 2,079 hours of sunshine a year) and 71st wettest (1,724 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pānihāti, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Shrīrāmpur is about as warm in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 52 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.1 °C), averages 11.7 °C more than its coolest, January (18.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.7 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 119 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shrīrāmpur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shrīrāmpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 119 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
