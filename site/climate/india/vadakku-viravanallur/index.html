Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Vadakku Viravanallur is 187th warmest (28.3 °C year average), 3083rd sunniest (about 2,387 hours of sunshine a year) and 2489th wettest (895 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Vadakku Viravanallur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 65 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
