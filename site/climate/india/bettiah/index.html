Bettiah at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Bettiah is 346th warmest (25.3 °C year average), 454th sunniest (about 2,218 hours of sunshine a year) and 119th wettest (1,513 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mothīhāri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Bettiah is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 49 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.1 °C), averages 15.5 °C more than its coolest, January (15.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.1 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 656 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bettiah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bettiah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 656 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
