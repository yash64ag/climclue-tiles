Chhota Udepur at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Chhota Udepur is 1172nd warmest (26.8 °C year average), 290th sunniest (about 2,898 hours of sunshine a year) and 1727th wettest (1,131 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Alirajpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Chhota Udepur is 0.7 °C warmer in May and 1.2 °C warmer in January, with 129 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.4 °C), averages 11.5 °C more than its coolest, January (21.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.4 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 166 km from the sea.

Why Chhota Udepur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 166 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-616-2540), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
