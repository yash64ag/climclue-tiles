Būndi at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Būndi is 175th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 48th sunniest (about 2,879 hours of sunshine a year) and 426th wettest (738 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kota, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Būndi is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 23 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (35.5 °C), averages 18.9 °C more than its coolest, January (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.6 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 490 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Būndi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Būndi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 490 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
