Dīnānagar weather by month and climate

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Dīnānagar is 3616th warmest (22.6 °C year average), 501st sunniest (about 2,828 hours of sunshine a year) and 1610th wettest (1,175 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gurdaspur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Dīnānagar is 0.1 °C warmer in June and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 82 mm more precipitation a year. Why Dīnānagar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,151 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dīnānagar itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (32.137°N, 75.473°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-518-2554), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
