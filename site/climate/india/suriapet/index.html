Suriāpet at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Suriāpet is 33rd warmest (28.1 °C year average), 218th sunniest (about 2,687 hours of sunshine a year) and 310th wettest (973 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Miryalaguda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Suriāpet is 0.5 °C cooler in May and 0.6 °C cooler in December, with 126 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.6 °C), averages 10.4 °C more than its coolest, December (23.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.7 °C (64 °F), in December. The area lies about 178 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Suriāpet, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Suriāpet has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 178 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
