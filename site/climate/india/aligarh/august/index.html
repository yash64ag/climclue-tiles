How August compares in Alīgarh

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Alīgarh. Compared with July: 1.2 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. September is 0.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Alīgarh in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Alīgarh in August?
In August, Alīgarh averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 25.8 °C (78 °F), with 228 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Alīgarh?
August is not one of Alīgarh’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Alīgarh in August?
228 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (228 mm), the driest November (5 mm).
Does it snow in Alīgarh in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Alīgarh get in August?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Alīgarh in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
