How November compares in Alīgarh

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Alīgarh. Compared with October: 5.4 °C cooler, 15 mm less precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. December is 4.8 °C cooler than November on average.

Alīgarh in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Alīgarh in November?
In November, Alīgarh averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Alīgarh?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Alīgarh’s mild months (February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Alīgarh in November?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (228 mm), the driest November (5 mm).
Does it snow in Alīgarh in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Alīgarh get in November?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Alīgarh in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
