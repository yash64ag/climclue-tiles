Alīgarh at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Alīgarh is 395th warmest (24.9 °C year average), 233rd sunniest (about 2,677 hours of sunshine a year) and 405th wettest (789 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hāthras, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Alīgarh is 0.1 °C cooler in June and about as warm in January, with 32 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.5 °C), averages 19.7 °C more than its coolest, January (13.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.9 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 848 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Alīgarh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Alīgarh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 848 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
