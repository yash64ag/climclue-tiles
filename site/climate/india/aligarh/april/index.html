How April compares in Alīgarh

April is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Alīgarh. Compared with March: 6.4 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. May is 3.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Alīgarh in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Alīgarh in April?
In April, Alīgarh averages a day high of 37.3 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 268 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Alīgarh?
April is not one of Alīgarh’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Alīgarh in April?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (231 mm), the driest November (5 mm).
Does it snow in Alīgarh in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Alīgarh get in April?
About 268 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Alīgarh in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
