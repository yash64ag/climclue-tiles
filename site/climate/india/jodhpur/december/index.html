How December compares in Jodhpur

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Jodhpur. Compared with November: 4.4 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. January is 1.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Jodhpur in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jodhpur in December?
In December, Jodhpur averages a day high of 25.4 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 11.3 °C (52 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jodhpur?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Jodhpur’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jodhpur in December?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (131 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Jodhpur in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jodhpur get in December?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jodhpur in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
