How October compares in Jodhpur

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Jodhpur. Compared with September: 1.9 °C cooler, 39 mm less precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. November is 5.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Jodhpur in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jodhpur in October?
In October, Jodhpur averages a day high of 34.8 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 291 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Jodhpur?
October is not one of Jodhpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jodhpur in October?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (131 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Jodhpur in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jodhpur get in October?
About 291 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jodhpur in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
