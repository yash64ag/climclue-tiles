How March compares in Jodhpur, Rajasthan

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Jodhpur, Rajasthan. Compared with February: 5.8 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. April is 5.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Jodhpur, Rajasthan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jodhpur, Rajasthan in March?
In March, Jodhpur, Rajasthan averages a day high of 33.9 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 17.1 °C (63 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 273 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Jodhpur, Rajasthan?
March is not one of Jodhpur, Rajasthan’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jodhpur, Rajasthan in March?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (133 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Jodhpur, Rajasthan in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jodhpur, Rajasthan get in March?
About 273 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jodhpur, Rajasthan in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
