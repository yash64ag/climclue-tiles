How April compares in Jodhpur

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Jodhpur. Compared with March: 5.3 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. May is 3.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Jodhpur in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jodhpur in April?
In April, Jodhpur averages a day high of 37.6 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 292 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Jodhpur?
April is not one of Jodhpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jodhpur in April?
9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (131 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Jodhpur in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jodhpur get in April?
About 292 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jodhpur in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
