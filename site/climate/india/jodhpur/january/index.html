How January compares in Jodhpur

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Jodhpur. Compared with December: 1.2 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. February is 3.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Jodhpur in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jodhpur in January?
In January, Jodhpur averages a day high of 23.8 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 9.9 °C (50 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Jodhpur?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Jodhpur’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jodhpur in January?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (131 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Jodhpur in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jodhpur get in January?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jodhpur in January?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
