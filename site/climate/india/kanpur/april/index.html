How April compares in Kanpur

April is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Kanpur. Compared with March: 6.0 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. May is 3.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Kanpur in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kanpur in April?
In April, Kanpur averages a day high of 38.2 °C (101 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 273 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kanpur?
April is not one of Kanpur’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kanpur in April?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (248 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Kanpur in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kanpur get in April?
About 273 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kanpur in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
