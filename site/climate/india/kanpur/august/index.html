How August compares in Kanpur

August is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kanpur. Compared with July: 0.9 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. September is 0.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Kanpur in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kanpur in August?
In August, Kanpur averages a day high of 31.8 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 25.8 °C (78 °F), with 247 mm (9.7 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Kanpur?
August is not one of Kanpur’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kanpur in August?
247 mm (9.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (247 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Kanpur in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kanpur get in August?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kanpur in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
