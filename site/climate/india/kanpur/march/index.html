How March compares in Kanpur

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Kanpur. Compared with February: 5.7 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. April is 6.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Kanpur in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kanpur in March?
In March, Kanpur averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 16.4 °C (62 °F), with 9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kanpur?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Kanpur’s mild months (February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kanpur in March?
9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (247 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Kanpur in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kanpur get in March?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kanpur in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
