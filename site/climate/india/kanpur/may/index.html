How May compares in Kanpur

May is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Kanpur. Compared with April: 3.2 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. June is about as warm as May on average.

Kanpur in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kanpur in May?
In May, Kanpur averages a day high of 39.6 °C (103 °F) and a night low of 26.2 °C (79 °F), with 14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 275 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kanpur?
May is not one of Kanpur’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kanpur in May?
14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (247 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Kanpur in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kanpur get in May?
About 275 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kanpur in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
