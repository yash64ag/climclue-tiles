How January compares in Siliguri

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Siliguri. Compared with December: 1.3 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. February is 2.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Siliguri in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Siliguri in January?
In January, Siliguri averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 11.7 °C (53 °F), with 14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Siliguri?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Siliguri’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Siliguri in January?
14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (872 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Siliguri in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Siliguri get in January?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Siliguri in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
