How April compares in Siliguri

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Siliguri. Compared with March: 2.5 °C warmer, 65 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. May is 0.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Siliguri in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Siliguri in April?
In April, Siliguri averages a day high of 31.8 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Siliguri?
April is not one of Siliguri’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Siliguri in April?
99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (872 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Siliguri in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Siliguri get in April?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Siliguri in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
