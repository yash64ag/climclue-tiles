How March compares in Siliguri

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Siliguri. Compared with February: 4.5 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 38 hours more sunshine. April is 2.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Siliguri in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Siliguri in March?
In March, Siliguri averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Siliguri?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Siliguri’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Siliguri in March?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (872 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Siliguri in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Siliguri get in March?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Siliguri in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
