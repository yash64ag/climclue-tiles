Siliguri at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Siliguri is 467th warmest (24.1 °C year average), 449th sunniest (about 2,230 hours of sunshine a year) and 14th wettest (3,085 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jalpāiguri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Siliguri is 0.4 °C cooler in August and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 607 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.8 °C), averages 10.8 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.7 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 526 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Siliguri, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Siliguri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 526 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
