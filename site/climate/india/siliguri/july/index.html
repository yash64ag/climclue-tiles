Siliguri weather in July

Average July weather in Siliguri, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Siliguri averages 30.8 °C (87 °F) days and 24.2 °C (76 °F) nights, with 872 mm (34.3 in) of rain over the month and about 118 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Siliguri

July is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Siliguri. Compared with June: 0.1 °C cooler, 283 mm more precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. August is 0.3 °C warmer than July on average.

Siliguri in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Siliguri in July?
In July, Siliguri averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 872 mm (34.3 in) of precipitation and about 118 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Siliguri?
July is not one of Siliguri’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Siliguri in July?
872 mm (34.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (872 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Siliguri in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Siliguri get in July?
About 118 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
