How October compares in Siliguri

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Siliguri. Compared with September: 2.1 °C cooler, 354 mm less precipitation and 67 hours more sunshine. November is 3.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Siliguri in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Siliguri in October?
In October, Siliguri averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Siliguri?
October is not one of Siliguri’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Siliguri in October?
117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (872 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Siliguri in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Siliguri get in October?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Siliguri in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
