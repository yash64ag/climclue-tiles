Gevrai weather by month and climate

Gevrai at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Gevrai is 1211th warmest (26.8 °C year average), 673rd sunniest (about 2,788 hours of sunshine a year) and 3090th wettest (731 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Beed, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Gevrai is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.5 °C cooler in December, with 11 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.4 °C), averages 11.8 °C more than its coolest, December (21.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.9 °C (57 °F), in December. The area lies about 308 km from the sea.

Why Gevrai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 308 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Gevrai itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.264°N, 75.750°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-647-2557), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
