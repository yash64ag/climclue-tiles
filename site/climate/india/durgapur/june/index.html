How June compares in Durgapur

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Durgapur. Compared with May: 0.7 °C cooler, 122 mm more precipitation and 85 hours less sunshine. July is 1.2 °C cooler than June on average.

Durgapur in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Durgapur in June?
In June, Durgapur averages a day high of 33.9 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 26.3 °C (79 °F), with 238 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation and about 131 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Durgapur?
June is not one of Durgapur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Durgapur in June?
238 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (354 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Durgapur in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Durgapur get in June?
About 131 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Durgapur in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
