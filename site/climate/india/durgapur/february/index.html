How February compares in Durgapur

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Durgapur. Compared with January: 3.6 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. March is 4.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Durgapur in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Durgapur in February?
In February, Durgapur averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 199 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Durgapur?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Durgapur’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Durgapur in February?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (354 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Durgapur in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Durgapur get in February?
About 199 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Durgapur in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
