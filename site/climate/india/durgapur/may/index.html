How May compares in Durgapur

May is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Durgapur. Compared with April: 0.9 °C warmer, 71 mm more precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. June is 0.7 °C cooler than May on average.

Durgapur in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Durgapur in May?
In May, Durgapur averages a day high of 37.2 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 24.0 °C (75 °F), with 112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Durgapur?
May is not one of Durgapur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Durgapur in May?
112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (345 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Durgapur in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Durgapur get in May?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Durgapur in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
