Polavaram at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Polavaram is 389th warmest (27.8 °C year average), 1930th sunniest (about 2,627 hours of sunshine a year) and 1469th wettest (1,221 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gokavaram, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Polavaram is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.4 °C warmer in December, with 5 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.1 °C), averages 8.3 °C more than its coolest, December (23.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.6 °C (65 °F), in December. The area lies about 82 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Polavaram, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Polavaram has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 82 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Polavaram itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (17.248°N, 81.644°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-667-2616), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
