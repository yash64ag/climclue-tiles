Madhapar weather by month and climate

Madhapar at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Madhapar is the 3rd sunniest (about 3,255 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Bhuj, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Madhapar is about as warm in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.0 °C), averages 11.5 °C more than its coolest, January (19.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.6 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 59 km from the sea.

Why Madhapar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 59 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Madhapar itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (23.238°N, 69.699°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-607-2496), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
