Kadayal at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Kadayal is 876th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 3186th sunniest (about 2,368 hours of sunshine a year) and 752nd wettest (1,701 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Triparappu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Kadayal is about as warm in March and about as warm in November, with 42 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (28.9 °C), averages 2.7 °C more than its coolest, November (26.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.2 °C (72 °F), in January. The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Why Kadayal has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.7 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kadayal itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (8.408°N, 77.266°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-755-2572), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
