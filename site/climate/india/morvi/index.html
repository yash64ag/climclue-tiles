Morvi at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Morvi is the 4th sunniest (about 3,139 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Rājkot, the nearest covered town with different numbers (59 km away), Morvi is 0.5 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 113 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.1 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, January (19.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.8 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 61 km from the sea.

Why Morvi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 61 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
