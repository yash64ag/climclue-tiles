Shivamogga at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Shivamogga is 448th warmest (24.5 °C year average), 333rd sunniest (about 2,574 hours of sunshine a year) and 228th wettest (1,124 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bhadrāvati, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Shivamogga is 0.2 °C cooler in April and 0.2 °C warmer in December, with 168 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (27.6 °C), averages 4.7 °C more than its coolest, December (22.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.1 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 110 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shivamogga, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shivamogga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 110 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
