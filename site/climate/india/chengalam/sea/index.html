Chengalam sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Kerala · India

The sea off Chengalam averages 30.0 °C (86 °F) in April, its warmest month, and 26.3 °C (79 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Chengalam sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Chengalam?
In April, at about 30.0 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Chengalam?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 26.3 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in Chengalam in August?
About 26.3 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Chengalam?
About 28.2 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
