How May compares in Patna

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Patna. Compared with April: 1.6 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 39 hours less sunshine. June is 0.5 °C cooler than May on average.

Patna in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Patna in May?
In May, Patna averages a day high of 38.0 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 26.1 °C (79 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Patna?
May is not one of Patna’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Patna in May?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (279 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Patna in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Patna get in May?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Patna in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
