Why Patna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 499 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Patna climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Patna?
May, with an average of 32.3 °C (90 °F), day highs around 38.0 °C (100 °F) and night lows around 26.1 °C (79 °F).
What is the coldest month in Patna?
January, with an average of 15.9 °C (61 °F), day highs around 22.2 °C (72 °F) and night lows around 10.4 °C (51 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Patna?
August, with 279 mm (11.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,013 mm (39.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Patna?
November, with 6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Patna?
April, with about 263 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Patna?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Patna?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, November and December.
What type of climate does Patna have?
Mediterranean, hot summer (Köppen Csa). Hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Patna’s GeoNames location (25.594°N, 85.136°E; ClimaClue area sq-584-2651), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,684,297 (GeoNames).
