How September compares in Patna

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Patna. Compared with August: 0.8 °C cooler, 102 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. October is 2.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Patna in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Patna in September?
In September, Patna averages a day high of 32.3 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 179 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation and about 131 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Patna?
September is not one of Patna’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Patna in September?
179 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (282 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Patna in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Patna get in September?
About 131 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Patna in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
