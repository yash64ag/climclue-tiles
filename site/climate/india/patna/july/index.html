How July compares in Patna

July is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Patna. Compared with June: 2.2 °C cooler, 126 mm more precipitation and 55 hours less sunshine. August is 0.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Patna in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Patna in July?
In July, Patna averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 26.2 °C (79 °F), with 276 mm (10.9 in) of precipitation and about 105 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Patna?
July is not one of Patna’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Patna in July?
276 mm (10.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (279 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Patna in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Patna get in July?
About 105 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Patna in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
