How March compares in Patna

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Patna. Compared with February: 5.8 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. April is 5.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Patna in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Patna in March?
In March, Patna averages a day high of 33.4 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Patna?
March is not one of Patna’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Patna in March?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (282 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Patna in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Patna get in March?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Patna in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
