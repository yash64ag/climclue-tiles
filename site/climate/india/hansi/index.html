Hānsi at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Hānsi is 2733rd warmest (25.1 °C year average), 765th sunniest (about 2,776 hours of sunshine a year) and 3598th wettest (507 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bawāni Khera, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Hānsi is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 21 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.0 °C), averages 20.7 °C more than its coolest, January (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.9 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 854 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Hānsi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 854 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-548-2559), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
