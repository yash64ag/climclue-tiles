Tirunelveli weather in December

Average December weather in Tirunelveli, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Tirunelveli averages 29.7 °C (85 °F) days and 22.2 °C (72 °F) nights, with 97 mm (3.8 in) of rain over the month and about 210 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Tirunelveli

December is the coolest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Tirunelveli. Compared with November: 0.7 °C cooler, 117 mm less precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. January is 0.3 °C warmer than December on average.

Tirunelveli in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tirunelveli in December?
In December, Tirunelveli averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Tirunelveli?
No month in Tirunelveli is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tirunelveli in December?
97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (215 mm), the driest August (22 mm).
Does it snow in Tirunelveli in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tirunelveli get in December?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
