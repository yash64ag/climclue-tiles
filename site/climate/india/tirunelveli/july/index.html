How July compares in Tirunelveli

July is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Tirunelveli. Compared with June: 0.4 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. August is 0.2 °C warmer than July on average.

Tirunelveli in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tirunelveli in July?
In July, Tirunelveli averages a day high of 32.6 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 26.5 °C (80 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Tirunelveli?
No month in Tirunelveli is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tirunelveli in July?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (215 mm), the driest August (22 mm).
Does it snow in Tirunelveli in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tirunelveli get in July?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tirunelveli in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
