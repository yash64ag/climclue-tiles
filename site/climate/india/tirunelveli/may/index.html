How May compares in Tirunelveli

May is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Tirunelveli. Compared with April: 0.3 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. June is 1.6 °C cooler than May on average.

Tirunelveli in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tirunelveli in May?
In May, Tirunelveli averages a day high of 34.9 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 28.1 °C (83 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Tirunelveli?
No month in Tirunelveli is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tirunelveli in May?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (215 mm), the driest August (22 mm).
Does it snow in Tirunelveli in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tirunelveli get in May?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
