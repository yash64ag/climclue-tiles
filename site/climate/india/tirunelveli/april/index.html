How April compares in Tirunelveli

Tirunelveli in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tirunelveli in April?
In April, Tirunelveli averages a day high of 36.9 °C (98 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Tirunelveli?
No month in Tirunelveli is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tirunelveli in April?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (215 mm), the driest August (22 mm).
Does it snow in Tirunelveli in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tirunelveli get in April?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tirunelveli in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
