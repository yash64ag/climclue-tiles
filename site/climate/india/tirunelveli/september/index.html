Tirunelveli in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tirunelveli in September?
In September, Tirunelveli averages a day high of 35.2 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Tirunelveli?
No month in Tirunelveli is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tirunelveli in September?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (216 mm), the driest August (24 mm).
Does it snow in Tirunelveli in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tirunelveli get in September?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tirunelveli in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
