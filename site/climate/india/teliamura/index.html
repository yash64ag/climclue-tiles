Teliamura at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Teliamura is 2916th warmest (24.8 °C year average), 3613th sunniest (about 2,161 hours of sunshine a year) and 541st wettest (2,236 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Āmbāsa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Teliamura is 0.2 °C warmer in August and about as warm in January, with 40 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.8 °C), averages 9.8 °C more than its coolest, January (18.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.6 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 135 km from the sea.

Why Teliamura has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 135 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Teliamura itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (23.842°N, 91.630°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-601-2716), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
