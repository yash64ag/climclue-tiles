How June compares in Thāne

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Thāne. Compared with May: 1.1 °C cooler, 479 mm more precipitation and 138 hours less sunshine. July is 1.4 °C cooler than June on average.

Thāne in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Thāne in June?
In June, Thāne averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 26.3 °C (79 °F), with 506 mm (19.9 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Thāne?
June is not one of Thāne’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Thāne in June?
506 mm (19.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (955 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Thāne in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Thāne get in June?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Thāne in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
