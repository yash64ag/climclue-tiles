How November compares in Thāne

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Thāne. Compared with October: 1.4 °C cooler, 79 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. December is 2.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Thāne in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Thāne in November?
In November, Thāne averages a day high of 32.7 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 19.9 °C (68 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Thāne?
November is not one of Thāne’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Thāne in November?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (939 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Thāne in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Thāne get in November?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Thāne in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
