Thāne at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Thāne is 1345th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 1572nd sunniest (about 2,675 hours of sunshine a year) and 461st wettest (2,597 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Airoli, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Thāne is about as warm in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 215 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.7 °C), averages 6.4 °C more than its coolest, January (23.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.0 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Thāne, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Thāne has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Thāne itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.197°N, 72.964°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-648-2529), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
