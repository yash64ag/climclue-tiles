Thāne at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Thāne is 169th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 241st sunniest (about 2,675 hours of sunshine a year) and 26th wettest (2,629 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Borivli, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Thāne is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 136 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.4 °C), averages 6.2 °C more than its coolest, January (23.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.8 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Thāne, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Thāne has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
