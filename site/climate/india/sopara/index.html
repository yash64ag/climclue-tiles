Sopāra at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Sopāra is 159th warmest (26.6 °C year average), 182nd sunniest (about 2,703 hours of sunshine a year) and 31st wettest (2,454 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bhayandar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Sopāra is about as warm in May and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 52 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.6 °C), averages 6.5 °C more than its coolest, January (23.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.1 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sopāra, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sopāra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
