Sopāra at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Sopāra is 1254th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 1294th sunniest (about 2,703 hours of sunshine a year) and 497th wettest (2,415 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Virār, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Sopāra is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 19 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.4 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, January (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.1 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Sopāra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers
