Karīmnagar at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Karīmnagar is 48th warmest (28.0 °C year average), 160th sunniest (about 2,720 hours of sunshine a year) and 255th wettest (1,070 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jagtiāl, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Karīmnagar is 0.5 °C warmer in May and 0.5 °C warmer in December, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.6 °C), averages 12.1 °C more than its coolest, December (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.6 °C (62 °F), in December. The area lies about 330 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Karīmnagar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Karīmnagar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 330 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
