Chhatarpur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Chhatarpur is 298th warmest (25.6 °C year average), 157th sunniest (about 2,726 hours of sunshine a year) and 207th wettest (1,187 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bānda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (98 km away), Chhatarpur is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 223 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.3 °C), averages 18.5 °C more than its coolest, January (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.8 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 788 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chhatarpur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chhatarpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 788 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
