Sirohi at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Sirohi is 1999th warmest (25.9 °C year average), 62nd sunniest (about 3,081 hours of sunshine a year) and 3512th wettest (588 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pindwāra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Sirohi is 0.6 °C warmer in May and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 73 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.7 °C), averages 15.3 °C more than its coolest, January (17.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.8 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 303 km from the sea.

Why Sirohi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 303 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sirohi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (24.888°N, 72.848°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-591-2528), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
