How October compares in Ludhiana

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Ludhiana. Compared with September: 4.1 °C cooler, 124 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. November is 5.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Ludhiana in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ludhiana in October?
In October, Ludhiana averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 17.6 °C (64 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 265 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Ludhiana?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Ludhiana’s mild months (February, March, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ludhiana in October?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (219 mm), the driest November (9 mm).
Does it snow in Ludhiana in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ludhiana get in October?
About 265 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ludhiana in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
