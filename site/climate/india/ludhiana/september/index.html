How September compares in Ludhiana

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ludhiana. Compared with August: 1.3 °C cooler, 66 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. October is 4.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Ludhiana in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ludhiana in September?
In September, Ludhiana averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 136 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 239 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Ludhiana?
September is not one of Ludhiana’s mild months; those are February, March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ludhiana in September?
136 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (219 mm), the driest November (9 mm).
Does it snow in Ludhiana in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ludhiana get in September?
About 239 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ludhiana in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
