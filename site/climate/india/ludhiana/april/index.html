How April compares in Ludhiana

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Ludhiana. Compared with March: 6.6 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. May is 4.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Ludhiana in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ludhiana in April?
In April, Ludhiana averages a day high of 35.0 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 18.8 °C (66 °F), with 24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ludhiana?
April is not one of Ludhiana’s mild months; those are February, March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ludhiana in April?
24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (219 mm), the driest November (9 mm).
Does it snow in Ludhiana in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ludhiana get in April?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ludhiana in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
