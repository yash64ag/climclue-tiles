Ludhiana at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Ludhiana is 500th warmest (23.4 °C year average), 115th sunniest (about 2,768 hours of sunshine a year) and 391st wettest (815 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Phagwāra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Ludhiana is 0.2 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 11 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (32.4 °C), averages 20.5 °C more than its coolest, January (11.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.4 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,035 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ludhiana, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Ludhiana

For mild weather, the best months to visit Ludhiana are February, March, October and November.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): February, March, October and November.
  • Warmest (average temperature): June (32.4 °C), May (31.3 °C) and July (30.1 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): November (9 mm), October (12 mm) and December (16 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): May (284 h), October (265 h) and April (264 h).

Why Ludhiana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,035 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
