How June compares in Ludhiana

June is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Ludhiana. Compared with May: 1.1 °C warmer, 40 mm more precipitation and 39 hours less sunshine. July is 2.2 °C cooler than June on average.

Ludhiana in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ludhiana in June?
In June, Ludhiana averages a day high of 38.2 °C (101 °F) and a night low of 26.0 °C (79 °F), with 70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 245 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Ludhiana?
June is not one of Ludhiana’s mild months; those are February, March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ludhiana in June?
70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (219 mm), the driest November (9 mm).
Does it snow in Ludhiana in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ludhiana get in June?
About 245 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ludhiana in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
