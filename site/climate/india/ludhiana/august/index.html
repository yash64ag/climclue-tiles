How August compares in Ludhiana

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Ludhiana. Compared with July: 0.9 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. September is 1.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Ludhiana in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ludhiana in August?
In August, Ludhiana averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 202 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Ludhiana?
August is not one of Ludhiana’s mild months; those are February, March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ludhiana in August?
202 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (219 mm), the driest November (9 mm).
Does it snow in Ludhiana in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ludhiana get in August?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ludhiana in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
