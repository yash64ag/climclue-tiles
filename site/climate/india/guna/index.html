Guna at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Guna is 388th warmest (25.0 °C year average), 97th sunniest (about 2,788 hours of sunshine a year) and 278th wettest (1,031 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shivpuri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (93 km away), Guna is 0.2 °C warmer in May and 1.2 °C warmer in January, with 167 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.5 °C), averages 17.2 °C more than its coolest, January (16.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.2 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 572 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Guna, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Guna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 572 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
