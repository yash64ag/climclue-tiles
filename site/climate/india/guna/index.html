Guna at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Guna is 2750th warmest (25.1 °C year average), 666th sunniest (about 2,788 hours of sunshine a year) and 1983rd wettest (1,044 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rāghogarh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Guna is 0.3 °C cooler in May and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 68 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.6 °C), averages 17.2 °C more than its coolest, January (16.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.1 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 572 km from the sea.

Why Guna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 572 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Guna itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (24.647°N, 77.311°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-593-2573), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
