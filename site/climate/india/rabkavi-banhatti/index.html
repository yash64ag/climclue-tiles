Rabkavi-Banhatti at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Rabkavi-Banhatti is 1579th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 1173rd sunniest (about 2,721 hours of sunshine a year) and 3438th wettest (616 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rabkavi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Rabkavi-Banhatti is about as warm in April and about as warm in December, with 5 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (31.0 °C), averages 7.5 °C more than its coolest, December (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.6 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 183 km from the sea.

Why Rabkavi-Banhatti has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 183 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
