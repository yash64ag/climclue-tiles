Nongpoh at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Nongpoh is 3650th warmest (21.4 °C year average), 3363rd sunniest (about 2,253 hours of sunshine a year) and 295th wettest (3,017 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mawlai-Mawïong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Nongpoh is 3.2 °C warmer in July and 3.2 °C warmer in January, with 890 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.5 °C), averages 11.4 °C more than its coolest, January (14.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.0 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 369 km from the sea.

Why Nongpoh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 369 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.4 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nongpoh itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.902°N, 91.877°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-580-2718), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
