How October compares in Faridabad

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Faridabad. Compared with September: 3.3 °C cooler, 121 mm less precipitation and 50 hours more sunshine. November is 5.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Faridabad in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Faridabad in October?
In October, Faridabad averages a day high of 32.3 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 18.6 °C (65 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Faridabad?
October is not one of Faridabad’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Faridabad in October?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (197 mm), the driest November (7 mm).
Does it snow in Faridabad in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Faridabad get in October?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Faridabad in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
