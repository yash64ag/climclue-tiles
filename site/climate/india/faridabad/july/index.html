How July compares in Faridabad

July is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Faridabad. Compared with June: 2.6 °C cooler, 125 mm more precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. August is 1.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Faridabad in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Faridabad in July?
In July, Faridabad averages a day high of 33.9 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 26.8 °C (80 °F), with 193 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Faridabad?
July is not one of Faridabad’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Faridabad in July?
193 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (193 mm), the driest November (7 mm).
Does it snow in Faridabad in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Faridabad get in July?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Faridabad in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
