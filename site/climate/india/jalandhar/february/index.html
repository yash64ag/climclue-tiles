How February compares in Jalandhar

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Jalandhar. Compared with January: 3.1 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. March is 5.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Jalandhar in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jalandhar in February?
In February, Jalandhar averages a day high of 20.6 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 8.1 °C (47 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 196 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Jalandhar?
February is not one of Jalandhar’s mild months; those are March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jalandhar in February?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (237 mm), the driest November (10 mm).
Does it snow in Jalandhar in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jalandhar get in February?
About 196 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jalandhar in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
