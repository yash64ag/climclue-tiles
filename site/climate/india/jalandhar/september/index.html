How September compares in Jalandhar

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Jalandhar. Compared with August: 1.4 °C cooler, 74 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. October is 4.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Jalandhar in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jalandhar in September?
In September, Jalandhar averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Jalandhar?
September is not one of Jalandhar’s mild months; those are March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jalandhar in September?
125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (237 mm), the driest November (10 mm).
Does it snow in Jalandhar in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jalandhar get in September?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jalandhar in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
