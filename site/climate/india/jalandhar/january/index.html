How January compares in Jalandhar

January is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Jalandhar. Compared with December: 1.2 °C cooler, 16 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. February is 3.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Jalandhar in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jalandhar in January?
In January, Jalandhar averages a day high of 18.8 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 4.5 °C (40 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Jalandhar?
January is not one of Jalandhar’s mild months; those are March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jalandhar in January?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (237 mm), the driest November (10 mm).
Does it snow in Jalandhar in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jalandhar get in January?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jalandhar in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
