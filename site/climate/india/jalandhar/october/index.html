October · Punjab · India

How October compares in Jalandhar

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Jalandhar. Compared with September: 4.3 °C cooler, 110 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. November is 5.9 °C cooler than October on average.

Jalandhar in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jalandhar in October?
In October, Jalandhar averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 15.8 °C (60 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Jalandhar?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Jalandhar’s mild months (March, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jalandhar in October?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (237 mm), the driest November (10 mm).
Does it snow in Jalandhar in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jalandhar get in October?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jalandhar in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
