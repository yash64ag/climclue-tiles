How December compares in Jalandhar

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Jalandhar. Compared with November: 4.8 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 36 hours less sunshine. January is 1.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Jalandhar in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jalandhar in December?
In December, Jalandhar averages a day high of 20.5 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 5.2 °C (41 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jalandhar?
December is not one of Jalandhar’s mild months; those are March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jalandhar in December?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (237 mm), the driest November (10 mm).
Does it snow in Jalandhar in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jalandhar get in December?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jalandhar in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
