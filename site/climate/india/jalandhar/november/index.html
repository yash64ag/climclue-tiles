How November compares in Jalandhar

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Jalandhar. Compared with October: 5.9 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. December is 4.8 °C cooler than November on average.

Jalandhar in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jalandhar in November?
In November, Jalandhar averages a day high of 26.2 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 9.2 °C (49 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Jalandhar?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Jalandhar’s mild months (March, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jalandhar in November?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (237 mm), the driest November (10 mm).
Does it snow in Jalandhar in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jalandhar get in November?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jalandhar in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
