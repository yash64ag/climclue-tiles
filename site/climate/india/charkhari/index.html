Charkhāri at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Charkhāri is 2064th warmest (25.8 °C year average), 1177th sunniest (about 2,721 hours of sunshine a year) and 2090th wettest (1,013 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Charkhāri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 828 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
