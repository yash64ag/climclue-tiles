Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Pithorāgarh is 3701st warmest (16.3 °C year average), 2514th sunniest (about 2,487 hours of sunshine a year) and 381st wettest (2,863 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Pithorāgarh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,133 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 8.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
