Bagalkot at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Bagalkot is 147th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 192nd sunniest (about 2,697 hours of sunshine a year) and 497th wettest (575 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vijayapura, the nearest covered town with different numbers (71 km away), Bagalkot is 1.1 °C cooler in April and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 68 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (31.4 °C), averages 8.1 °C more than its coolest, December (23.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.0 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 217 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bagalkot, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bagalkot has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 217 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
