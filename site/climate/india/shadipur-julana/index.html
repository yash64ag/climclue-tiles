Shādīpur Julāna at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Shādīpur Julāna is 2960th warmest (24.8 °C year average), 1060th sunniest (about 2,737 hours of sunshine a year) and 3457th wettest (609 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Maham, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Shādīpur Julāna is 0.3 °C cooler in June and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 49 mm more precipitation a year. Why Shādīpur Julāna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 875 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
