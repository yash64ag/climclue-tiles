How October compares in Narela

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Narela. Compared with September: 3.5 °C cooler, 99 mm less precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. November is 5.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Narela in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Narela in October?
In October, Narela averages a day high of 31.8 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 18.7 °C (66 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Narela?
October is not one of Narela’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Narela in October?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (172 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Narela in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Narela get in October?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Narela in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
