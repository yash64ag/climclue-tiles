How September compares in Narela

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Narela. Compared with August: 0.9 °C cooler, 58 mm less precipitation and 38 hours more sunshine. October is 3.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Narela in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Narela in September?
In September, Narela averages a day high of 33.7 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 23.8 °C (75 °F), with 113 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Narela?
September is not one of Narela’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Narela in September?
113 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (171 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Narela in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Narela get in September?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Narela in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
