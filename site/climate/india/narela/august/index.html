Narela in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Narela in August?
In August, Narela averages a day high of 33.5 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 25.8 °C (78 °F), with 171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Narela?
August is not one of Narela’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Narela in August?
171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (171 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Narela in August?

How many hours of sunshine does Narela get in August?
