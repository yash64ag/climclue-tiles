Narela in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Narela in April?
In April, Narela averages a day high of 36.5 °C (98 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Narela?
April is not one of Narela’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Narela in April?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (171 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Narela in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Narela get in April?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Narela in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
