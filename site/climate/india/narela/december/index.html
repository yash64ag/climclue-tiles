Narela weather in December

Average December weather in Narela, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Narela averages 22.2 °C (72 °F) days and 7.3 °C (45 °F) nights, with 7 mm (0.3 in) of rain over the month and about 212 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Narela

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Narela. Compared with November: 4.9 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Narela in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Narela in December?
In December, Narela averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 7.3 °C (45 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Narela?
December is not one of Narela’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Narela in December?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (171 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Narela in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Narela get in December?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Narela in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
