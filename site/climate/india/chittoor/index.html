Chittoor at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Chittoor is 124th warmest (26.8 °C year average), 336th sunniest (about 2,566 hours of sunshine a year) and 330th wettest (937 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vellore, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Chittoor is 0.6 °C cooler in May and 0.6 °C cooler in December, with 103 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.1 °C), averages 8.7 °C more than its coolest, December (22.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.6 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 143 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chittoor, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chittoor has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 143 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
