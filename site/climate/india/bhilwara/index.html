Bhilwara at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Bhilwara is 304th warmest (25.5 °C year average), 30th sunniest (about 2,936 hours of sunshine a year) and 476th wettest (627 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chittorgarh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), Bhilwara is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 58 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.0 °C), averages 18.1 °C more than its coolest, January (15.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.9 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 420 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bhilwara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bhilwara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 420 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
