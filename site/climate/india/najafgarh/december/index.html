How December compares in Najafgarh

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Najafgarh. Compared with November: 5.0 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. January is 1.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Najafgarh in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Najafgarh in December?
In December, Najafgarh averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 9.0 °C (48 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Najafgarh?
December is not one of Najafgarh’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Najafgarh in December?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (163 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Najafgarh in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Najafgarh get in December?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Najafgarh in December?
