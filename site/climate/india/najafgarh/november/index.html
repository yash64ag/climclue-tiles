Najafgarh in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Najafgarh in November?
In November, Najafgarh averages a day high of 27.3 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 13.6 °C (56 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Najafgarh?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Najafgarh’s mild months (February, March and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Najafgarh in November?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (163 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Najafgarh in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Najafgarh get in November?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Najafgarh in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
