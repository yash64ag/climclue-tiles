Najafgarh in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Najafgarh in March?
In March, Najafgarh averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Najafgarh?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Najafgarh’s mild months (February, March and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Najafgarh in March?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (163 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Najafgarh in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Najafgarh get in March?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Najafgarh in March?
