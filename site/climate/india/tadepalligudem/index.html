Tadepalligudem at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Tadepalligudem is 359th warmest (27.9 °C year average), 1880th sunniest (about 2,636 hours of sunshine a year) and 1585th wettest (1,184 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Attili, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Tadepalligudem is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.2 °C), averages 8.1 °C more than its coolest, December (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.4 °C (67 °F), in January. The area lies about 67 km from the sea.

Why Tadepalligudem has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 67 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
