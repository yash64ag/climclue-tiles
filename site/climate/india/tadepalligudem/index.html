Tadepalligudem at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Tadepalligudem is 52nd warmest (27.9 °C year average), 295th sunniest (about 2,636 hours of sunshine a year) and 200th wettest (1,197 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bhimavaram, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Tadepalligudem is 0.2 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C cooler in December, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.1 °C), averages 8.1 °C more than its coolest, December (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.0 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 67 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tadepalligudem, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tadepalligudem has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 67 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
