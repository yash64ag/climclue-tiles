Dharmavaram weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Dharmavaram, India (Andhra Pradesh), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Dharmavaram is warmest in April, with 39.2 °C (103 °F) days and 25.5 °C nights, and coolest in December, with 29.1 °C (84 °F) days and 17.8 °C nights. Most rain falls around September (133 mm); January is driest (3 mm).

Dharmavaram at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Dharmavaram is 422nd warmest (27.8 °C year average), 2122nd sunniest (about 2,582 hours of sunshine a year) and 3515th wettest (587 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kakkalapalle, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Dharmavaram is 0.3 °C cooler in April and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (32.4 °C), averages 8.9 °C more than its coolest, December (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.8 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 262 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dharmavaram, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dharmavaram has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 262 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dharmavaram itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.414°N, 77.720°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-695-2577), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
