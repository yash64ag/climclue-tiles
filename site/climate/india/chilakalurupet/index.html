Chilakalūrupet at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Chilakalūrupet is 17th warmest (28.5 °C year average), 236th sunniest (about 2,676 hours of sunshine a year) and 332nd wettest (935 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Narasaraopet, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Chilakalūrupet is 0.2 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 9 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.1 °C), averages 9.1 °C more than its coolest, December (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.9 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chilakalūrupet, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chilakalūrupet has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
