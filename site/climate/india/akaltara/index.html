Akaltara at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Akaltara is 1521st warmest (26.5 °C year average), 2174th sunniest (about 2,566 hours of sunshine a year) and 1026th wettest (1,459 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nailā, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Akaltara is about as warm in May and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 21 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.5 °C), averages 14.9 °C more than its coolest, December (19.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.6 °C (55 °F), in December. The area lies about 395 km from the sea.

Why Akaltara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 395 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-619-2624), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
