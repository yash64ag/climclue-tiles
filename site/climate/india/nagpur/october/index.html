How October compares in Nagpur

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Nagpur. Compared with September: 1.1 °C cooler, 152 mm less precipitation and 77 hours more sunshine. November is 3.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Nagpur in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nagpur in October?
In October, Nagpur averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 62 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Nagpur?
October is not one of Nagpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nagpur in October?
62 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (386 mm), the driest April (8 mm).
Does it snow in Nagpur in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nagpur get in October?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nagpur in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
