Nagpur weather in November

Average November weather in Nagpur, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Nagpur averages 30.7 °C (87 °F) days and 15.4 °C (60 °F) nights, with 17 mm (0.7 in) of rain over the month and about 260 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Nagpur

November is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Nagpur. Compared with October: 3.1 °C cooler, 46 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. December is 2.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Nagpur in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nagpur in November?
In November, Nagpur averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 260 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Nagpur?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Nagpur’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nagpur in November?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (385 mm), the driest April (8 mm).
Does it snow in Nagpur in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nagpur get in November?
About 260 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nagpur in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
