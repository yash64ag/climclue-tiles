How July compares in Nagpur

July is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Nagpur. Compared with June: 4.2 °C cooler, 190 mm more precipitation and 70 hours less sunshine. August is 0.8 °C cooler than July on average.

Nagpur in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nagpur in July?
In July, Nagpur averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 386 mm (15.2 in) of precipitation and about 116 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Nagpur?
July is not one of Nagpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nagpur in July?
386 mm (15.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (386 mm), the driest April (8 mm).
Does it snow in Nagpur in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nagpur get in July?
About 116 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nagpur in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
