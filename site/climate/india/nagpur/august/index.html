How August compares in Nagpur

August is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Nagpur. Compared with July: 0.8 °C cooler, 76 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. September is 0.6 °C warmer than August on average.

Nagpur in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nagpur in August?
In August, Nagpur averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.8 °C (75 °F), with 310 mm (12.2 in) of precipitation and about 117 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Nagpur?
August is not one of Nagpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nagpur in August?
310 mm (12.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (386 mm), the driest April (8 mm).
Does it snow in Nagpur in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nagpur get in August?
About 117 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nagpur in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
