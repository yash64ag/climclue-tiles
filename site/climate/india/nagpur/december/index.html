How December compares in Nagpur

December is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Nagpur. Compared with November: 2.6 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. January is 0.3 °C warmer than December on average.

Nagpur in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nagpur in December?
In December, Nagpur averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 14.0 °C (57 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Nagpur?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Nagpur’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nagpur in December?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (386 mm), the driest April (8 mm).
Does it snow in Nagpur in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nagpur get in December?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nagpur in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
