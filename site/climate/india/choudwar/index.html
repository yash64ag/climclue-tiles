Choudwar at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Choudwar is 1161st warmest (26.8 °C year average), 3187th sunniest (about 2,366 hours of sunshine a year) and 831st wettest (1,618 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cuttack, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Choudwar is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 9 mm less precipitation a year. Why Choudwar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 77 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-634-2659), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
