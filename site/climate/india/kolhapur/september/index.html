How September compares in Kolhāpur

September is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Kolhāpur. Compared with August: 0.4 °C warmer, 124 mm less precipitation and 59 hours more sunshine. October is 0.7 °C warmer than September on average.

Kolhāpur in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kolhāpur in September?
In September, Kolhāpur averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Kolhāpur?
September is not one of Kolhāpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kolhāpur in September?
160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (441 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Kolhāpur in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kolhāpur get in September?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kolhāpur in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
