Kolhāpur weather in March

Average March weather in Kolhāpur, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Kolhāpur averages 34.4 °C (94 °F) days and 19.8 °C (68 °F) nights, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of rain over the month and about 285 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Kolhāpur

March is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Kolhāpur. Compared with February: 2.7 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. April is 1.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Kolhāpur in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kolhāpur in March?
In March, Kolhāpur averages a day high of 34.4 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 285 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kolhāpur?
March is not one of Kolhāpur’s mild months; those are January, February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kolhāpur in March?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (443 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Kolhāpur in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kolhāpur get in March?
About 285 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kolhāpur in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
