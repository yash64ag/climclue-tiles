How April compares in Kolhāpur

April is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kolhāpur. Compared with March: 1.4 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. May is 0.4 °C cooler than April on average.

Kolhāpur in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kolhāpur in April?
In April, Kolhāpur averages a day high of 35.6 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 284 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kolhāpur?
April is not one of Kolhāpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kolhāpur in April?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (441 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Kolhāpur in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kolhāpur get in April?
About 284 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kolhāpur in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
