How July compares in Kolhāpur

July is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Kolhāpur. Compared with June: 1.4 °C cooler, 234 mm more precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. August is 0.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Kolhāpur in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kolhāpur in July?
In July, Kolhāpur averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 443 mm (17.4 in) of precipitation and about 105 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Kolhāpur?
July is not one of Kolhāpur’s mild months; those are January, February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kolhāpur in July?
443 mm (17.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (443 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Kolhāpur in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kolhāpur get in July?
About 105 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kolhāpur in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
