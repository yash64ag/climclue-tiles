How December compares in Chandigarh

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Chandigarh. Compared with November: 4.6 °C cooler, 9 mm more precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. January is 1.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Chandigarh in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chandigarh in December?
In December, Chandigarh averages a day high of 21.3 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 6.6 °C (44 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 198 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Chandigarh?
December is not one of Chandigarh’s mild months; those are February, March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chandigarh in December?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (312 mm), the driest November (13 mm).
Does it snow in Chandigarh in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chandigarh get in December?
About 198 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chandigarh in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
