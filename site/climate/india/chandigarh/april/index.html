How April compares in Chandigarh

April is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Chandigarh. Compared with March: 6.4 °C warmer, 13 mm less precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. May is 3.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Chandigarh in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chandigarh in April?
In April, Chandigarh averages a day high of 34.5 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 18.9 °C (66 °F), with 24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Chandigarh?
April is not one of Chandigarh’s mild months; those are February, March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chandigarh in April?
24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (305 mm), the driest November (12 mm).
Does it snow in Chandigarh in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chandigarh get in April?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chandigarh in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
