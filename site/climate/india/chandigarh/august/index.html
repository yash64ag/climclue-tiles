How August compares in Chandigarh

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Chandigarh. Compared with July: 0.9 °C cooler, 27 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. September is 1.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Chandigarh in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chandigarh in August?
In August, Chandigarh averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 277 mm (10.9 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Chandigarh?
August is not one of Chandigarh’s mild months; those are February, March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chandigarh in August?
277 mm (10.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (305 mm), the driest November (12 mm).
Does it snow in Chandigarh in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chandigarh get in August?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chandigarh in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
