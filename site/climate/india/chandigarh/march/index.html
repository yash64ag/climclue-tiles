How March compares in Chandigarh

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Chandigarh. Compared with February: 5.3 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. April is 6.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Chandigarh in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chandigarh in March?
In March, Chandigarh averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 13.7 °C (57 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Chandigarh?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Chandigarh’s mild months (February, March, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chandigarh in March?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (305 mm), the driest November (12 mm).
Does it snow in Chandigarh in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chandigarh get in March?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chandigarh in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
