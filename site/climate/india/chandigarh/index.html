Chandigarh at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Chandigarh is 487th warmest (23.7 °C year average), 166th sunniest (about 2,715 hours of sunshine a year) and 251st wettest (1,077 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Panchkula, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Chandigarh is 0.4 °C cooler in June and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 31 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (32.1 °C), averages 19.5 °C more than its coolest, January (12.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.1 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,047 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chandigarh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Chandigarh

For mild weather, the best months to visit Chandigarh are February, March, October and November.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): February, March, October and November.

Why Chandigarh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,047 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
