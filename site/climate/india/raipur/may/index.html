Raipur, Chhattisgarh weather in May

Average May weather in Raipur, Chhattisgarh, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

How May compares in Raipur, Chhattisgarh
