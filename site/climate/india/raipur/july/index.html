How July compares in Raipur

July is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Raipur. Compared with June: 4.0 °C cooler, 183 mm more precipitation and 56 hours less sunshine. August is 0.6 °C cooler than July on average.

Raipur in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Raipur in July?
In July, Raipur averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 371 mm (14.6 in) of precipitation and about 117 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Raipur?
July is not one of Raipur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Raipur in July?
371 mm (14.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (371 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Raipur in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Raipur get in July?
About 117 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Raipur in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
