Raipur, Chhattisgarh weather in November

Average November weather in Raipur, Chhattisgarh, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Raipur, Chhattisgarh averages 29.0 °C (84 °F) days and 15.7 °C (60 °F) nights, with 10 mm (0.4 in) of rain over the month and about 240 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Raipur, Chhattisgarh

November is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Raipur, Chhattisgarh. Compared with October: 3.4 °C cooler, 45 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. December is 2.7 °C cooler than November on average.
