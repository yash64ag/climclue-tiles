How October compares in Raipur

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Raipur. Compared with September: 1.5 °C cooler, 170 mm less precipitation and 73 hours more sunshine. November is 3.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Raipur in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Raipur in October?
In October, Raipur averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Raipur?
October is not one of Raipur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Raipur in October?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (371 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Raipur in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Raipur get in October?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Raipur in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
