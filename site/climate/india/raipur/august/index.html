Raipur, Chhattisgarh in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Raipur, Chhattisgarh in August?
In August, Raipur, Chhattisgarh averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 342 mm (13.5 in) of precipitation and about 131 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Raipur, Chhattisgarh?
August is not one of Raipur, Chhattisgarh’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Raipur, Chhattisgarh in August?
342 mm (13.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (371 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Raipur, Chhattisgarh in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Raipur, Chhattisgarh get in August?
About 131 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
