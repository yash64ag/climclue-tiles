How December compares in Raipur

December is the coolest month, the driest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Raipur. Compared with November: 2.7 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. January is 0.3 °C warmer than December on average.

Raipur in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Raipur in December?
In December, Raipur averages a day high of 26.2 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 13.3 °C (56 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 253 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Raipur?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Raipur’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Raipur in December?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (371 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Raipur in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Raipur get in December?
About 253 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Raipur in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
