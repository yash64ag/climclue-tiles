How March compares in Raipur

March is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Raipur. Compared with February: 4.7 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. April is 4.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Raipur in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Raipur in March?
In March, Raipur averages a day high of 34.9 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Raipur?
March is not one of Raipur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Raipur in March?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (371 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Raipur in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Raipur get in March?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Raipur in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
