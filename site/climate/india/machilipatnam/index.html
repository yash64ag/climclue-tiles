Machilīpatnam at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Machilīpatnam is 43rd warmest (28.0 °C year average), 282nd sunniest (about 2,652 hours of sunshine a year) and 216th wettest (1,168 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gudivāda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Machilīpatnam is 1.8 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 64 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.4 °C), averages 6.9 °C more than its coolest, January (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.9 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Machilīpatnam, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Machilīpatnam has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
