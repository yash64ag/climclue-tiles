Sherghāti at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Sherghāti is 2180th warmest (25.7 °C year average), 3281st sunniest (about 2,301 hours of sunshine a year) and 1551st wettest (1,194 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Sherghāti has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 423 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
