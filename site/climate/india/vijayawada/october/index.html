How October compares in Vijayawada

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Vijayawada. Compared with September: 1.2 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. November is 1.9 °C cooler than October on average.

Vijayawada in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vijayawada in October?
In October, Vijayawada averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Vijayawada?
October is not one of Vijayawada’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vijayawada in October?
171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (208 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Vijayawada in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vijayawada get in October?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vijayawada in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
