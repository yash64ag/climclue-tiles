Vijayawada in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vijayawada in March?
In March, Vijayawada averages a day high of 35.2 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 271 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Vijayawada?
March is not one of Vijayawada’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vijayawada in March?
14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (208 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Vijayawada in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vijayawada get in March?
About 271 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vijayawada in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
