How June compares in Vijayawada

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Vijayawada. Compared with May: 1.5 °C cooler, 75 mm more precipitation and 96 hours less sunshine. July is 2.2 °C cooler than June on average.

Vijayawada in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vijayawada in June?
In June, Vijayawada averages a day high of 35.6 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 27.6 °C (82 °F), with 129 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Vijayawada?
June is not one of Vijayawada’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vijayawada in June?
129 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (208 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Vijayawada in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vijayawada get in June?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vijayawada in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
