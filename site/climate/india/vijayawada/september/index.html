How September compares in Vijayawada

September is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Vijayawada. Compared with August: 0.1 °C cooler, 28 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. October is 1.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Vijayawada in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vijayawada in September?
In September, Vijayawada averages a day high of 31.8 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 174 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Vijayawada?
September is not one of Vijayawada’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vijayawada in September?
174 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (208 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Vijayawada in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vijayawada get in September?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vijayawada in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
