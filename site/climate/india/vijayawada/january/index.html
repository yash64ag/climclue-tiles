Vijayawada weather in January

Average January weather in Vijayawada, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Vijayawada averages 29.7 °C (85 °F) days and 19.1 °C (66 °F) nights, with 6 mm (0.2 in) of rain over the month and about 269 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Vijayawada

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Vijayawada. Compared with December: 0.3 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. February is 2.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Vijayawada in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vijayawada in January?
In January, Vijayawada averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 19.1 °C (66 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 269 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Vijayawada?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Vijayawada’s mild months (January and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vijayawada in January?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (203 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Vijayawada in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vijayawada get in January?
About 269 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vijayawada in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
