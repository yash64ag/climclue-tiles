How August compares in Virār

August is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Virār. Compared with July: 0.4 °C cooler, 305 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. September is 0.4 °C warmer than August on average.

Virār in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Virār in August?
In August, Virār averages a day high of 27.8 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 581 mm (22.9 in) of precipitation and about 88 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Virār?
August is not one of Virār’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Virār in August?
581 mm (22.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (886 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Virār in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Virār get in August?
About 88 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Virār in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
