How July compares in Virār

July is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Virār. Compared with June: 1.4 °C cooler, 428 mm more precipitation and 79 hours less sunshine. August is 0.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Virār in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Virār in July?
In July, Virār averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 891 mm (35.1 in) of precipitation and about 93 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Virār?
July is not one of Virār’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Virār in July?
891 mm (35.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (891 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Virār in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Virār get in July?
About 93 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Virār in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
