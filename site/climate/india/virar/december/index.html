Virār in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Virār in December?
In December, Virār averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Virār?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Virār’s mild months (January, February and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Virār in December?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (886 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Virār in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Virār get in December?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Virār in December?
About 26.0 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (29.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Virār in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
