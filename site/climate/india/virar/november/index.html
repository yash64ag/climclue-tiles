How November compares in Virār

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Virār. Compared with October: 1.2 °C cooler, 72 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. December is 2.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Virār in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Virār in November?
In November, Virār averages a day high of 33.0 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Virār?
November is not one of Virār’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Virār in November?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (886 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Virār in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Virār get in November?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Virār in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
