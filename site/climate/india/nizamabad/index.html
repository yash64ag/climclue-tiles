Nizāmābād at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Nizāmābād is 111th warmest (27.0 °C year average), 139th sunniest (about 2,740 hours of sunshine a year) and 221st wettest (1,153 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jagtiāl, the nearest covered town with different numbers (87 km away), Nizāmābād is 0.5 °C cooler in May and 0.5 °C cooler in December, with 90 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.6 °C), averages 12.1 °C more than its coolest, December (21.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.8 °C (60 °F), in December. The area lies about 411 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nizāmābād, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nizāmābād has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 411 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
