Pallikunnu at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Pallikunnu is 1145th warmest (26.8 °C year average), 2183rd sunniest (about 2,565 hours of sunshine a year) and 153rd wettest (3,497 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kannur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Pallikunnu is about as warm in April and about as warm in August, with 23 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.7 °C), averages 3.0 °C more than its coolest, August (25.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.5 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Pallikunnu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
