Kālna at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Kālna is 2047th warmest (25.8 °C year average), 3695th sunniest (about 2,089 hours of sunshine a year) and 933rd wettest (1,515 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shāntipur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Kālna is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 25 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.1 °C), averages 12.1 °C more than its coolest, January (18.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.9 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 172 km from the sea.

Why Kālna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 172 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kālna itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (23.219°N, 88.363°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-607-2683), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
