Panchkula at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Panchkula is 465th warmest (24.1 °C year average), 174th sunniest (about 2,707 hours of sunshine a year) and 269th wettest (1,045 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chandigarh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Panchkula is 0.4 °C warmer in June and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 31 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (32.5 °C), averages 19.4 °C more than its coolest, January (13.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.7 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,042 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Panchkula, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Panchkula has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,042 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
