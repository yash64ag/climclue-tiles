How September compares in Aurangabad

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Aurangabad. Compared with August: 0.4 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 77 hours more sunshine. October is 0.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Aurangabad in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aurangabad in September?
In September, Aurangabad averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 211 mm (8.3 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Aurangabad?
September is not one of Aurangabad’s mild months; those are January, February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aurangabad in September?
211 mm (8.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (220 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Aurangabad in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aurangabad get in September?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aurangabad in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
