How July compares in Aurangabad

July is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Aurangabad. Compared with June: 2.6 °C cooler, 39 mm more precipitation and 77 hours less sunshine. August is 0.9 °C cooler than July on average.

Aurangabad in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aurangabad in July?
In July, Aurangabad averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 208 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation and about 107 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Aurangabad?
July is not one of Aurangabad’s mild months; those are January, February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aurangabad in July?
208 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (220 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Aurangabad in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aurangabad get in July?
About 107 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aurangabad in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
