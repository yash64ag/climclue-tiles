How April compares in Aurangabad

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Aurangabad. Compared with March: 3.6 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. May is 1.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Aurangabad in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aurangabad in April?
In April, Aurangabad averages a day high of 37.6 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 292 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Aurangabad?
April is not one of Aurangabad’s mild months; those are January, February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aurangabad in April?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (220 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Aurangabad in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aurangabad get in April?
About 292 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aurangabad in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
