Aurangabad at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Aurangabad is 314th warmest (25.5 °C year average), 102nd sunniest (about 2,785 hours of sunshine a year) and 309th wettest (973 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jālna, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Aurangabad is 1.2 °C cooler in May and about as warm in December, with 144 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.9 °C), averages 11.4 °C more than its coolest, December (20.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.6 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 279 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Aurangabad, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Aurangabad

For mild weather, the best months to visit Aurangabad are January, February, October, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, October, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): May (31.9 °C), April (30.9 °C) and June (28.3 °C).

Why Aurangabad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 279 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
