How May compares in Aurangabad

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Aurangabad. Compared with April: 1.0 °C warmer, 21 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. June is 3.6 °C cooler than May on average.

Aurangabad in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aurangabad in May?
In May, Aurangabad averages a day high of 39.9 °C (104 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 306 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Aurangabad?
May is not one of Aurangabad’s mild months; those are January, February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aurangabad in May?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (218 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Aurangabad in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aurangabad get in May?
About 306 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aurangabad in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
