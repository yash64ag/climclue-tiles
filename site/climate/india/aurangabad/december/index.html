How December compares in Aurangabad

December is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Aurangabad. Compared with November: 2.2 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. January is 0.3 °C warmer than December on average.

Aurangabad in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aurangabad in December?
In December, Aurangabad averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 13.0 °C (55 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Aurangabad?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Aurangabad’s mild months (January, February, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aurangabad in December?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (218 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Aurangabad in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aurangabad get in December?
About 278 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aurangabad in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
