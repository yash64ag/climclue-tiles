How October compares in Aurangabad

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Aurangabad. Compared with September: 0.4 °C cooler, 119 mm less precipitation and 76 hours more sunshine. November is 2.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Aurangabad in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aurangabad in October?
In October, Aurangabad averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 19.4 °C (67 °F), with 93 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Aurangabad?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Aurangabad’s mild months (January, February, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aurangabad in October?
93 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (220 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Aurangabad in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aurangabad get in October?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aurangabad in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
