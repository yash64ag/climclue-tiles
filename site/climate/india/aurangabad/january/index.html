How January compares in Aurangabad

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Aurangabad. Compared with December: 0.4 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. February is 2.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Aurangabad in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aurangabad in January?
In January, Aurangabad averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 14.6 °C (58 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 285 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Aurangabad?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Aurangabad’s mild months (January, February, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aurangabad in January?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (220 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Aurangabad in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aurangabad get in January?
About 285 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aurangabad in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
