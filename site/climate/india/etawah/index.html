Etāwah at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Etāwah is 342nd warmest (25.3 °C year average), 212th sunniest (about 2,689 hours of sunshine a year) and 401st wettest (799 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bhind, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Etāwah is 0.4 °C cooler in June and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 27 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.5 °C), averages 18.9 °C more than its coolest, January (14.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.9 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 841 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Etāwah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Etāwah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 841 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
