Saoner at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Saoner is 1307th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 837th sunniest (about 2,766 hours of sunshine a year) and 1908th wettest (1,068 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Khāpa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Saoner is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (35.1 °C), averages 15.1 °C more than its coolest, December (20.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.4 °C (54 °F), in December. The area lies about 601 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Saoner has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 601 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Saoner itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.385°N, 78.922°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-626-2589), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
