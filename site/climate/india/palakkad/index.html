Palakkad at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Palakkad is 105th warmest (27.0 °C year average), 376th sunniest (about 2,470 hours of sunshine a year) and 63rd wettest (1,781 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kurichchi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (41 km away), Palakkad is 1.6 °C warmer in March and 0.3 °C warmer in July, with 960 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (30.3 °C), averages 5.1 °C more than its coolest, July (25.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.2 °C (70 °F), in January. The area lies about 79 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Palakkad, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Palakkad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 79 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
