Kozhikode in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kozhikode in August?
In August, Kozhikode averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 430 mm (16.9 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Kozhikode?
No month in Kozhikode is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kozhikode in August?
430 mm (16.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (889 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Kozhikode in August?

How many hours of sunshine does Kozhikode get in August?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
