Kozhikode in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kozhikode in December?
In December, Kozhikode averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kozhikode?
No month in Kozhikode is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kozhikode in December?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (889 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Kozhikode in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kozhikode get in December?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
