Lucknow weather in July

Average July weather in Lucknow, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Lucknow averages 34.0 °C (93 °F) days and 25.5 °C (78 °F) nights, with 253 mm (9.9 in) of rain over the month and about 140 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Lucknow

July is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Lucknow. Compared with June: 2.9 °C cooler, 171 mm more precipitation and 58 hours less sunshine. August is 0.6 °C cooler than July on average.

Lucknow in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lucknow in July?
In July, Lucknow averages a day high of 34.0 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 25.5 °C (78 °F), with 253 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Lucknow?
July is not one of Lucknow’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lucknow in July?
253 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (253 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Lucknow in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lucknow get in July?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lucknow in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
