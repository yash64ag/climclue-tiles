How February compares in Lucknow

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Lucknow. Compared with January: 3.6 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. March is 5.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Lucknow in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lucknow in February?
In February, Lucknow averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 11.6 °C (53 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Lucknow?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Lucknow’s mild months (February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lucknow in February?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (251 mm), the driest November (5 mm).
Does it snow in Lucknow in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lucknow get in February?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lucknow in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
