How January compares in Lucknow

January is the coolest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Lucknow. Compared with December: 1.3 °C cooler, 10 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. February is 3.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Lucknow in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lucknow in January?
In January, Lucknow averages a day high of 21.4 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Lucknow?
January is not one of Lucknow’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lucknow in January?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (251 mm), the driest November (5 mm).
Does it snow in Lucknow in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lucknow get in January?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lucknow in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
