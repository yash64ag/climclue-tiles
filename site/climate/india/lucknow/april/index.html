How April compares in Lucknow

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Lucknow. Compared with March: 6.0 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. May is 2.7 °C warmer than April on average.

Lucknow in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lucknow in April?
In April, Lucknow averages a day high of 37.2 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 271 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Lucknow?
April is not one of Lucknow’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lucknow in April?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (251 mm), the driest November (5 mm).
Does it snow in Lucknow in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lucknow get in April?
About 271 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lucknow in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
