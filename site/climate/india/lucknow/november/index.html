How November compares in Lucknow

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Lucknow. Compared with October: 5.1 °C cooler, 49 mm less precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. December is 4.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Lucknow in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lucknow in November?
In November, Lucknow averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 14.3 °C (58 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Lucknow?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Lucknow’s mild months (February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lucknow in November?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (251 mm), the driest November (5 mm).
Does it snow in Lucknow in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lucknow get in November?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lucknow in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
