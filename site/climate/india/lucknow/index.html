Lucknow at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Lucknow is 368th warmest (25.1 °C year average), 315th sunniest (about 2,611 hours of sunshine a year) and 345th wettest (897 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Unnāo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (54 km away), Lucknow is 0.4 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 36 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.6 °C), averages 18.0 °C more than its coolest, January (14.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.8 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 869 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lucknow, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lucknow has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 869 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
