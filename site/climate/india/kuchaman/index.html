Kuchāman at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Kuchāman is 2225th warmest (25.7 °C year average), 287th sunniest (about 2,899 hours of sunshine a year) and 3700th wettest (377 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Makrāna, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Kuchāman is 0.2 °C warmer in May and about as warm in January, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.8 °C), averages 18.9 °C more than its coolest, January (14.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.9 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 607 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Kuchāman has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 607 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kuchāman itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (27.147°N, 74.857°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-568-2548), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
