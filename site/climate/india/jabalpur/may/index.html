How May compares in Jabalpur

May is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Jabalpur. Compared with April: 2.9 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. June is 2.6 °C cooler than May on average.

Jabalpur in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jabalpur in May?
In May, Jabalpur averages a day high of 41.5 °C (107 °F) and a night low of 26.3 °C (79 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 286 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Jabalpur?
May is not one of Jabalpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jabalpur in May?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (432 mm), the driest April (4 mm).
Does it snow in Jabalpur in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jabalpur get in May?
About 286 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jabalpur in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
