How October compares in Jabalpur

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Jabalpur. Compared with September: 1.3 °C cooler, 229 mm less precipitation and 77 hours more sunshine. November is 3.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Jabalpur in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jabalpur in October?
In October, Jabalpur averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 19.6 °C (67 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Jabalpur?
October is not one of Jabalpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jabalpur in October?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (426 mm), the driest April (4 mm).
Does it snow in Jabalpur in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jabalpur get in October?
About 254 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jabalpur in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
