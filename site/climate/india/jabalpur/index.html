Jabalpur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Jabalpur is 319th warmest (25.4 °C year average), 162nd sunniest (about 2,716 hours of sunshine a year) and 155th wettest (1,351 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Murwāra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (87 km away), Jabalpur is 0.3 °C warmer in May and 1.1 °C warmer in January, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.0 °C), averages 16.5 °C more than its coolest, January (17.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.3 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 675 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jabalpur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

For mild weather, the best months to visit Jabalpur are January, February, November and December.

Why Jabalpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 675 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
