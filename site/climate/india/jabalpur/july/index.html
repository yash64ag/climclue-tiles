How July compares in Jabalpur

July is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Jabalpur. Compared with June: 4.3 °C cooler, 242 mm more precipitation and 69 hours less sunshine. August is 0.8 °C cooler than July on average.

Jabalpur in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jabalpur in July?
In July, Jabalpur averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 398 mm (15.7 in) of precipitation and about 120 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Jabalpur?
July is not one of Jabalpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jabalpur in July?
398 mm (15.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (432 mm), the driest April (4 mm).
Does it snow in Jabalpur in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jabalpur get in July?
About 120 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jabalpur in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
