Jabalpur weather in March

Average March weather in Jabalpur, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Jabalpur averages 34.5 °C (94 °F) days and 17.1 °C (63 °F) nights, with 13 mm (0.5 in) of rain over the month and about 270 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Jabalpur

March is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Jabalpur. Compared with February: 5.3 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. April is 5.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Jabalpur in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jabalpur in March?
In March, Jabalpur averages a day high of 34.5 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 17.1 °C (63 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 270 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Jabalpur?
March is not one of Jabalpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jabalpur in March?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (432 mm), the driest April (4 mm).
Does it snow in Jabalpur in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jabalpur get in March?
About 270 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jabalpur in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
