Jabalpur weather in April

Average April weather in Jabalpur, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Jabalpur averages 39.5 °C (103 °F) days and 22.5 °C (73 °F) nights, with 4 mm (0.2 in) of rain over the month and about 279 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Jabalpur

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Jabalpur. Compared with March: 5.2 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. May is 2.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Jabalpur in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jabalpur in April?
In April, Jabalpur averages a day high of 39.5 °C (103 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 279 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Jabalpur?
April is not one of Jabalpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jabalpur in April?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (432 mm), the driest April (4 mm).
Does it snow in Jabalpur in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jabalpur get in April?
About 279 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jabalpur in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
