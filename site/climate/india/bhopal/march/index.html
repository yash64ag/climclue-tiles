How March compares in Bhopal

March is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Bhopal. Compared with February: 5.4 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. April is 5.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Bhopal in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhopal in March?
In March, Bhopal averages a day high of 33.1 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 18.5 °C (65 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 277 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bhopal?
March is not one of Bhopal’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhopal in March?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (413 mm), the driest April (3 mm).
Does it snow in Bhopal in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhopal get in March?
About 277 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bhopal in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
