How April compares in Bhopal

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Bhopal. Compared with March: 5.0 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. May is 2.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Bhopal in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhopal in April?
In April, Bhopal averages a day high of 37.7 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 290 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Bhopal?
April is not one of Bhopal’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhopal in April?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (425 mm), the driest April (3 mm).
Does it snow in Bhopal in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhopal get in April?
About 290 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bhopal in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
