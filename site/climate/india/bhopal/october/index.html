How October compares in Bhopal

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Bhopal. Compared with September: 0.9 °C cooler, 157 mm less precipitation and 80 hours more sunshine. November is 3.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Bhopal in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhopal in October?
In October, Bhopal averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 19.7 °C (67 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Bhopal?
October is not one of Bhopal’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhopal in October?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (413 mm), the driest April (3 mm).
Does it snow in Bhopal in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhopal get in October?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bhopal in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
