How July compares in Bhopal

July is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Bhopal. Compared with June: 4.4 °C cooler, 277 mm more precipitation and 83 hours less sunshine. August is 1.2 °C cooler than July on average.

Bhopal in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhopal in July?
In July, Bhopal averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 425 mm (16.7 in) of precipitation and about 116 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Bhopal?
July is not one of Bhopal’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhopal in July?
425 mm (16.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (425 mm), the driest April (3 mm).
Does it snow in Bhopal in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhopal get in July?
About 116 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bhopal in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
