Bhopal weather in August

Average August weather in Bhopal, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Bhopal averages 28.6 °C (83 °F) days and 22.3 °C (72 °F) nights, with 335 mm (13.2 in) of rain over the month and about 109 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Bhopal

August is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Bhopal. Compared with July: 1.2 °C cooler, 90 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. September is 0.7 °C warmer than August on average.

Bhopal in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhopal in August?
In August, Bhopal averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 335 mm (13.2 in) of precipitation and about 109 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Bhopal?
August is not one of Bhopal’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhopal in August?
335 mm (13.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (425 mm), the driest April (3 mm).
Does it snow in Bhopal in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhopal get in August?
About 109 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bhopal in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
