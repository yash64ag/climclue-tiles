How September compares in Gurugram

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Gurugram. Compared with August: 0.7 °C cooler, 55 mm less precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. October is 3.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Gurugram in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gurugram in September?
In September, Gurugram averages a day high of 32.9 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Gurugram?
September is not one of Gurugram’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gurugram in September?
111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (177 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Gurugram in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gurugram get in September?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gurugram in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
