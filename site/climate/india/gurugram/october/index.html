How October compares in Gurugram

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Gurugram. Compared with September: 3.4 °C cooler, 101 mm less precipitation and 48 hours more sunshine. November is 5.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Gurugram in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gurugram in October?
In October, Gurugram averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 19.1 °C (66 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 265 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Gurugram?
October is not one of Gurugram’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gurugram in October?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (177 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Gurugram in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gurugram get in October?
About 265 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gurugram in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
