Why Ramagundam has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 343 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Ramagundam climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Ramagundam?
May, with an average of 34.9 °C (95 °F), day highs around 40.3 °C (105 °F) and night lows around 29.0 °C (84 °F).
What is the coldest month in Ramagundam?
December, with an average of 22.4 °C (72 °F), day highs around 28.4 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 16.3 °C (61 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Ramagundam?
July, with 306 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,116 mm (43.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Ramagundam?
December, with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Ramagundam?
May, with about 284 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Ramagundam?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Ramagundam?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Ramagundam’s GeoNames location (18.755°N, 79.474°E; ClimaClue area sq-652-2594), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 242,979 (GeoNames).
