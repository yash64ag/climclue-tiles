Simdega at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Simdega is 2874th warmest (24.9 °C year average), 3222nd sunniest (about 2,349 hours of sunshine a year) and 879th wettest (1,564 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Birmitrapur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Simdega is 0.7 °C cooler in May and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 33 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.1 °C), averages 13.1 °C more than its coolest, January (18.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.8 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 292 km from the sea.

Why Simdega has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 292 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Simdega itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.615°N, 84.502°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-613-2645), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
