Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Vāniyamkulam is 938th warmest (27.0 °C year average), 2541st sunniest (about 2,481 hours of sunshine a year) and 471st wettest (2,538 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Vāniyamkulam has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 49 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
