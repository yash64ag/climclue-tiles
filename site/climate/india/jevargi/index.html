Jevargi at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Jevargi is 461st warmest (27.7 °C year average), 1110th sunniest (about 2,728 hours of sunshine a year) and 3115th wettest (721 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shāhābād, Karnataka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Jevargi is 0.2 °C cooler in May and about as warm in December, with 39 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.6 °C), averages 10.3 °C more than its coolest, December (23.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.6 °C (62 °F), in December. The area lies about 366 km from the sea.

Why Jevargi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 366 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jevargi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (17.014°N, 76.773°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-669-2567), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
