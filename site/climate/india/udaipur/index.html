Udaipur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Udaipur is 424th warmest (24.7 °C year average), 24th sunniest (about 2,976 hours of sunshine a year) and 470th wettest (633 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chittorgarh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (98 km away), Udaipur is 1.8 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 53 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.1 °C), averages 15.6 °C more than its coolest, January (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.1 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 298 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Udaipur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Udaipur

Why Udaipur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 298 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
