Navsari at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Navsari is 104th warmest (27.0 °C year average), 61st sunniest (about 2,847 hours of sunshine a year) and 100th wettest (1,627 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bilimora, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Navsari is 0.7 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 243 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.4 °C), averages 8.0 °C more than its coolest, January (22.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.9 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Navsari, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Navsari has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
