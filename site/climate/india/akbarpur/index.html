Why Akbarpur, Uttar Pradesh (26.4°N 82.5°E) has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 726 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
