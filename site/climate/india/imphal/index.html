Imphal at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Imphal is 518th warmest (20.8 °C year average), 440th sunniest (about 2,249 hours of sunshine a year) and 52nd wettest (1,868 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Silchar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (116 km away), Imphal is 3.3 °C cooler in June and 3.8 °C cooler in January, with 1,934 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (24.9 °C), averages 10.8 °C more than its coolest, January (14.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.5 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 353 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Imphal, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Imphal has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 353 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
