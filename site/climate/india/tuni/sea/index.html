Tuni sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Andhra Pradesh · India

The sea off Tuni averages 29.5 °C (85 °F) in October, its warmest month, and 25.8 °C (78 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Tuni sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Tuni?
In October, at about 29.5 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Tuni?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 25.8 °C (78 °F).
How warm is the sea in Tuni in January?
About 25.8 °C (78 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Tuni?
About 28.3 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
