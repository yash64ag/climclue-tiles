Kandamkunnu at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Kandamkunnu is 1737th warmest (26.3 °C year average), 2243rd sunniest (about 2,550 hours of sunshine a year) and 99th wettest (3,715 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kuttuparamba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Kandamkunnu is 0.1 °C cooler in April and about as warm in August, with 94 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.1 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, August (25.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.8 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Kandamkunnu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-721-2555), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
