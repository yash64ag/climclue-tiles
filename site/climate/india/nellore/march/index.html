How March compares in Nellore

March is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Nellore. Compared with February: 2.4 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. April is 2.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Nellore in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nellore in March?
In March, Nellore averages a day high of 34.5 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 280 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Nellore?
March is not one of Nellore’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nellore in March?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is October (302 mm), the driest March (5 mm).
Does it snow in Nellore in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nellore get in March?
About 280 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nellore in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
