How December compares in Nellore

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Nellore. Compared with November: 1.5 °C cooler, 143 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Nellore in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nellore in December?
In December, Nellore averages a day high of 27.8 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 20.5 °C (69 °F), with 99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Nellore?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Nellore’s mild months (January and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nellore in December?
99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (302 mm), the driest March (5 mm).
Does it snow in Nellore in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nellore get in December?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nellore in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
