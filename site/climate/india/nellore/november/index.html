How November compares in Nellore

November is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Nellore. Compared with October: 2.1 °C cooler, 62 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. December is 1.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Nellore in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nellore in November?
In November, Nellore averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 244 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Nellore?
November is not one of Nellore’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nellore in November?
244 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (306 mm), the driest March (5 mm).
Does it snow in Nellore in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nellore get in November?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nellore in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
