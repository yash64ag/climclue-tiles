Nellore at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Nellore is 9th warmest (28.7 °C year average), 270th sunniest (about 2,662 hours of sunshine a year) and 256th wettest (1,070 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tirupati, the nearest covered town with different numbers (109 km away), Nellore is 1.3 °C warmer in May and 1.8 °C warmer in January, with 30 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.9 °C), averages 8.6 °C more than its coolest, January (24.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.5 °C (67 °F), in January. The area lies about 32 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nellore, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nellore has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 32 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
