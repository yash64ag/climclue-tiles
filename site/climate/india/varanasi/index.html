Varanasi at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Varanasi is 260th warmest (25.8 °C year average), 370th sunniest (about 2,482 hours of sunshine a year) and 264th wettest (1,060 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pandit Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Nagar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Varanasi is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 11 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.3 °C), averages 17.5 °C more than its coolest, January (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.0 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 602 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Varanasi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Varanasi

For mild weather, the best months to visit Varanasi are January, February, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, November and December.

Why Varanasi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 602 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
