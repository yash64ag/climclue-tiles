Why Varanasi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 602 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Varanasi climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Varanasi?
May, with an average of 33.3 °C (92 °F), day highs around 39.5 °C (103 °F) and night lows around 26.5 °C (80 °F).
What is the coldest month in Varanasi?
January, with an average of 15.8 °C (60 °F), day highs around 22.4 °C (72 °F) and night lows around 10.0 °C (50 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Varanasi?
August, with 311 mm (12.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,060 mm (41.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Varanasi?
April, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Varanasi?
April, with about 270 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Varanasi?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Varanasi?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, November and December.
What type of climate does Varanasi have?
Mediterranean, hot summer (Köppen Csa). Hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Varanasi’s GeoNames location (25.317°N, 83.010°E; ClimaClue area sq-586-2630), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,164,404 (GeoNames).
