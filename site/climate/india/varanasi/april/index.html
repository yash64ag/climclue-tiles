Varanasi weather in April

Average April weather in Varanasi, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Varanasi averages 39.3 °C (103 °F) days and 22.3 °C (72 °F) nights, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of rain over the month and about 270 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Varanasi

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Varanasi. Compared with March: 5.7 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. May is 2.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Varanasi in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Varanasi in April?
In April, Varanasi averages a day high of 39.3 °C (103 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 270 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Varanasi?
April is not one of Varanasi’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Varanasi in April?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (310 mm), the driest April (5 mm).
Does it snow in Varanasi in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Varanasi get in April?
About 270 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Varanasi in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
