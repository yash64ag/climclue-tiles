How July compares in Varanasi

July is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Varanasi. Compared with June: 3.1 °C cooler, 201 mm more precipitation and 59 hours less sunshine. August is 0.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Varanasi in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Varanasi in July?
In July, Varanasi averages a day high of 33.8 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 25.7 °C (78 °F), with 304 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation and about 121 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Varanasi?
July is not one of Varanasi’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Varanasi in July?
304 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (310 mm), the driest April (5 mm).
Does it snow in Varanasi in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Varanasi get in July?
About 121 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Varanasi in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
