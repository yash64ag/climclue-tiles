How June compares in Varanasi

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Varanasi. Compared with May: 0.4 °C cooler, 88 mm more precipitation and 75 hours less sunshine. July is 3.0 °C cooler than June on average.

Varanasi in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Varanasi in June?
In June, Varanasi averages a day high of 37.3 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 27.9 °C (82 °F), with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Varanasi?
June is not one of Varanasi’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Varanasi in June?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (311 mm), the driest April (5 mm).
Does it snow in Varanasi in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Varanasi get in June?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Varanasi in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
