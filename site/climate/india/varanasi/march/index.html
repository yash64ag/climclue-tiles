How March compares in Varanasi

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Varanasi. Compared with February: 5.8 °C warmer, 11 mm less precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. April is 5.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Varanasi in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Varanasi in March?
In March, Varanasi averages a day high of 32.4 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Varanasi?
March is not one of Varanasi’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Varanasi in March?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (311 mm), the driest April (5 mm).
Does it snow in Varanasi in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Varanasi get in March?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Varanasi in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
