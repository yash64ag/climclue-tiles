Varanasi weather in February

Average February weather in Varanasi, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Varanasi averages 27.2 °C (81 °F) days and 11.4 °C (53 °F) nights, with 19 mm (0.8 in) of rain over the month and about 217 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Varanasi

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Varanasi. Compared with January: 3.5 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. March is 5.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Varanasi in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Varanasi in February?
In February, Varanasi averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 11.4 °C (53 °F), with 19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Varanasi?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Varanasi’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Varanasi in February?
19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (310 mm), the driest April (5 mm).
Does it snow in Varanasi in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Varanasi get in February?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Varanasi in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
