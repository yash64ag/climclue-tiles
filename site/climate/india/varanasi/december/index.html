How December compares in Varanasi

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Varanasi. Compared with November: 4.4 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. January is 1.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Varanasi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Varanasi in December?
In December, Varanasi averages a day high of 23.9 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 11.3 °C (52 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Varanasi?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Varanasi’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Varanasi in December?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (311 mm), the driest April (5 mm).
Does it snow in Varanasi in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Varanasi get in December?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Varanasi in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
