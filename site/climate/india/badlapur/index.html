Badlapur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Badlapur is 178th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 169th sunniest (about 2,711 hours of sunshine a year) and 35th wettest (2,423 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ambarnath, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Badlapur is 0.3 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 158 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.9 °C), averages 6.8 °C more than its coolest, January (23.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.2 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 54 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Badlapur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Badlapur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 54 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
