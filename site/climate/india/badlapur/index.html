Badlapur weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Badlapur, India (Maharashtra), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Badlapur at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Badlapur is 1284th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 1244th sunniest (about 2,711 hours of sunshine a year) and 543rd wettest (2,228 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ambarnath, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Badlapur is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 120 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.1 °C), averages 6.6 °C more than its coolest, January (23.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.1 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 54 km from the sea.

Why Badlapur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 54 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Badlapur itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (19.155°N, 73.266°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-648-2532), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
