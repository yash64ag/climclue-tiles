Kota weather in September

Average September weather in Kota, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Kota averages 33.7 °C (93 °F) days and 24.1 °C (75 °F) nights, with 113 mm (4.5 in) of rain over the month and about 207 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Kota

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Kota. Compared with August: 0.6 °C warmer, 123 mm less precipitation and 71 hours more sunshine. October is 1.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Kota in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota in September?
In September, Kota averages a day high of 33.7 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 113 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Kota?
September is not one of Kota’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota in September?
113 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (276 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Kota in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kota get in September?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kota in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
