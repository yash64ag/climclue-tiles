Kota at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Kota is 141st warmest (26.7 °C year average), 53rd sunniest (about 2,867 hours of sunshine a year) and 416th wettest (760 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Būndi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Kota is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 23 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (35.5 °C), averages 18.5 °C more than its coolest, January (17.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.4 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 481 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kota, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Kota

For mild weather, the best months to visit Kota are January, February, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): May (35.5 °C), June (34.3 °C) and April (31.9 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): December (4 mm), March (4 mm) and January (6 mm).

Why Kota has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 481 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
