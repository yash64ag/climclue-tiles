How January compares in Kota

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Kota. Compared with December: 1.2 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. February is 3.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Kota in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota in January?
In January, Kota averages a day high of 24.1 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 10.4 °C (51 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 260 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kota?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Kota’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota in January?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (278 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Kota in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kota get in January?
About 260 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kota in January?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
