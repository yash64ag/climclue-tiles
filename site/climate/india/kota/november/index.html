How November compares in Kota

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kota. Compared with October: 5.0 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. December is 4.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Kota in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota in November?
In November, Kota averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 16.1 °C (61 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 265 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kota?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Kota’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota in November?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (276 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Kota in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kota get in November?
About 265 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kota in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
