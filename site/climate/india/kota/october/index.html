How October compares in Kota

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Kota. Compared with September: 1.2 °C cooler, 98 mm less precipitation and 67 hours more sunshine. November is 4.9 °C cooler than October on average.

Kota in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota in October?
In October, Kota averages a day high of 33.7 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 21.0 °C (70 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 274 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kota?
October is not one of Kota’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota in October?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (278 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Kota in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kota get in October?
About 274 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kota in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
