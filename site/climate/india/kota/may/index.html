How May compares in Kota

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Kota. Compared with April: 3.6 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. June is 1.3 °C cooler than May on average.

Kota in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota in May?
In May, Kota averages a day high of 41.8 °C (107 °F) and a night low of 28.3 °C (83 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 303 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kota?
May is not one of Kota’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota in May?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (278 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Kota in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kota get in May?
About 303 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kota in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
