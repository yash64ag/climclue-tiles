Kota weather in February

Average February weather in Kota, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Kota averages 28.0 °C (82 °F) days and 12.7 °C (55 °F) nights, with 8 mm (0.3 in) of rain over the month and about 252 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Kota

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kota. Compared with January: 3.3 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. March is 6.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Kota in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kota in February?
In February, Kota averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 252 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Kota?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Kota’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kota in February?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (276 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Kota in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kota get in February?
About 252 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kota in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
