Marakkanam sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Tamil Nadu · India

The sea off Marakkanam averages 29.7 °C (85 °F) in April, its warmest month, and 26.5 °C (80 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Marakkanam sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Marakkanam?
In April, at about 29.7 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Marakkanam?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 26.5 °C (80 °F).
How warm is the sea in Marakkanam in January?
About 26.5 °C (80 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Marakkanam?
About 28.5 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
