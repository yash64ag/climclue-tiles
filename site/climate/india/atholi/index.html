Atholi at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Atholi is 1570th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 2338th sunniest (about 2,530 hours of sunshine a year) and 149th wettest (3,513 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Icchannūr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Atholi is 0.1 °C cooler in April and 0.1 °C cooler in August, with 81 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.4 °C), averages 3.1 °C more than its coolest, August (25.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.4 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Atholi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Atholi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (11.389°N, 75.760°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-726-2557), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
