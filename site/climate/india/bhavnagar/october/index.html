Bhavnagar in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhavnagar in October?
In October, Bhavnagar averages a day high of 32.3 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 25.5 °C (78 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Bhavnagar?
October is not one of Bhavnagar’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhavnagar in October?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (273 mm), the driest December (0 mm).
Does it snow in Bhavnagar in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhavnagar get in October?
About 278 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bhavnagar in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
