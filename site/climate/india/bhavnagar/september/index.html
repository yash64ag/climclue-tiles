Bhavnagar in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhavnagar in September?
In September, Bhavnagar averages a day high of 33.1 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 136 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Bhavnagar?
September is not one of Bhavnagar’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhavnagar in September?
136 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (275 mm), the driest December (0 mm).
Does it snow in Bhavnagar in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhavnagar get in September?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Bhavnagar in September?
About 28.5 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (29.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Bhavnagar in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
