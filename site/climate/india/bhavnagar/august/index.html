How August compares in Bhavnagar

August is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Bhavnagar. Compared with July: 0.9 °C cooler, 68 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. September is 0.5 °C warmer than August on average.

Bhavnagar in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bhavnagar in August?
In August, Bhavnagar averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 25.8 °C (78 °F), with 205 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation and about 110 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Bhavnagar?
August is not one of Bhavnagar’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bhavnagar in August?
205 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (273 mm), the driest December (0 mm).
Does it snow in Bhavnagar in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bhavnagar get in August?
About 110 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bhavnagar in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
