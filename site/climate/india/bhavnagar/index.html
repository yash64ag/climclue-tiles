Bhavnagar at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Bhavnagar is 79th warmest (27.4 °C year average), 29th sunniest (about 2,943 hours of sunshine a year) and 403rd wettest (794 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Botad, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Bhavnagar is 2.5 °C cooler in May and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.6 °C), averages 9.8 °C more than its coolest, January (21.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.8 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bhavnagar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bhavnagar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
