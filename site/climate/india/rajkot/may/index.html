Rājkot weather in May

Average May weather in Rājkot, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

How May compares in Rājkot

May is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Rājkot. Compared with April: 1.9 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. June is 0.6 °C cooler than May on average.
