Rājkot at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Rājkot is 174th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 10th sunniest (about 3,075 hours of sunshine a year) and 423rd wettest (741 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gondal, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Rājkot is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 89 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.6 °C), averages 12.0 °C more than its coolest, January (19.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.4 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 76 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rājkot, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Rājkot has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 76 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
