Rājkot weather in August

Average August weather in Rājkot, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Rājkot averages 31.3 °C (88 °F) days and 23.9 °C (75 °F) nights, with 165 mm (6.5 in) of rain over the month and about 136 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Rājkot

August is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Rājkot. Compared with July: 1.0 °C cooler, 128 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. September is 0.4 °C warmer than August on average.
