Bathinda at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Bathinda is 422nd warmest (24.7 °C year average), 63rd sunniest (about 2,843 hours of sunshine a year) and 511th wettest (453 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Muktsar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (50 km away), Bathinda is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 31 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.8 °C), averages 21.2 °C more than its coolest, January (12.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.6 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 934 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bathinda, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bathinda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 934 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
