Why Munger has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 428 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Munger climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Munger?
May, with an average of 31.1 °C (88 °F), day highs around 36.6 °C (98 °F) and night lows around 25.3 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Munger?
January, with an average of 16.3 °C (61 °F), day highs around 22.3 °C (72 °F) and night lows around 11.0 °C (52 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Munger?
July, with 332 mm (13.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,248 mm (49.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Munger?
December, with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Munger?
April, with about 252 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Munger?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Munger?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Munger’s GeoNames location (25.375°N, 86.475°E; ClimaClue area sq-586-2664), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 213,303 (GeoNames).
