Fīrozābād at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Fīrozābād is 351st warmest (25.2 °C year average), 193rd sunniest (about 2,697 hours of sunshine a year) and 411th wettest (774 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Agra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Fīrozābād is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 52 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.8 °C), averages 19.6 °C more than its coolest, January (14.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.2 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 815 km from the sea (deep inland).
