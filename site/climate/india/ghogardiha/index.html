Ghogardīha weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Ghogardīha, India (Bihar), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Ghogardīha is warmest in May, with 36.4 °C (98 °F) days and 24.1 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 23.4 °C (74 °F) days and 9.4 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (421 mm); November is driest (6 mm).

Ghogardīha at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Ghogardīha is 2531st warmest (25.3 °C year average), 3608th sunniest (about 2,162 hours of sunshine a year) and 859th wettest (1,589 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nirmāli, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Ghogardīha is 0.2 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 90 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.3 °C), averages 13.9 °C more than its coolest, January (16.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.4 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 523 km from the sea.

Why Ghogardīha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 523 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ghogardīha itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.280°N, 86.470°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-577-2664), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
