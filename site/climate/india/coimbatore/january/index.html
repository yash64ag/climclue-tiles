How January compares in Coimbatore

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Coimbatore. Compared with December: 0.6 °C warmer, 30 mm less precipitation and 47 hours more sunshine. February is 1.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Coimbatore in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Coimbatore in January?
In January, Coimbatore averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 18.3 °C (65 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Coimbatore?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Coimbatore’s mild months (January, July, August and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Coimbatore in January?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (183 mm), the driest February (10 mm).
Does it snow in Coimbatore in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Coimbatore get in January?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Coimbatore in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
