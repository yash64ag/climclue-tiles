How July compares in Coimbatore

July is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Coimbatore. Compared with June: 0.7 °C cooler, 15 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. August is about as warm as July on average.

Coimbatore in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Coimbatore in July?
In July, Coimbatore averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 20.7 °C (69 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Coimbatore?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Coimbatore’s mild months (January, July, August and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Coimbatore in July?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (176 mm), the driest February (9 mm).
Does it snow in Coimbatore in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Coimbatore get in July?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Coimbatore in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
