Coimbatore weather in March

Average March weather in Coimbatore, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Coimbatore averages 35.0 °C (95 °F) days and 21.8 °C (71 °F) nights, with 27 mm (1.0 in) of rain over the month and about 266 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Coimbatore

March is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Coimbatore. Compared with February: 2.1 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. April is 0.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Coimbatore in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Coimbatore in March?
In March, Coimbatore averages a day high of 35.0 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 27 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Coimbatore?
March is not one of Coimbatore’s mild months; those are January, July, August and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Coimbatore in March?
27 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (176 mm), the driest February (9 mm).
Does it snow in Coimbatore in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Coimbatore get in March?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
