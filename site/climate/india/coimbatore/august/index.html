How August compares in Coimbatore

August is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Coimbatore. Compared with July: about as warm, 11 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. September is 0.4 °C warmer than August on average.

Coimbatore in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Coimbatore in August?
In August, Coimbatore averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Coimbatore?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Coimbatore’s mild months (January, July, August and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Coimbatore in August?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (183 mm), the driest February (10 mm).
Does it snow in Coimbatore in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Coimbatore get in August?
About 145 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Coimbatore in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
