How June compares in Coimbatore

June is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Coimbatore. Compared with May: 2.3 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 87 hours less sunshine. July is 0.7 °C cooler than June on average.

Coimbatore in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Coimbatore in June?
In June, Coimbatore averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Coimbatore?
June is not one of Coimbatore’s mild months; those are January, July, August and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Coimbatore in June?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (183 mm), the driest February (10 mm).
Does it snow in Coimbatore in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Coimbatore get in June?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Coimbatore in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
