How May compares in Coimbatore

May is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Coimbatore. Compared with April: 1.0 °C cooler, 25 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. June is 2.3 °C cooler than May on average.

Coimbatore in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Coimbatore in May?
In May, Coimbatore averages a day high of 33.4 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Coimbatore?
May is not one of Coimbatore’s mild months; those are January, July, August and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Coimbatore in May?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (183 mm), the driest February (10 mm).
Does it snow in Coimbatore in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Coimbatore get in May?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Coimbatore in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
