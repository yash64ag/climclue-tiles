Coimbatore weather in April

Average April weather in Coimbatore, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Coimbatore averages 35.4 °C (96 °F) days and 22.8 °C (73 °F) nights, with 45 mm (1.8 in) of rain over the month and about 246 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Coimbatore

April is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Coimbatore. Compared with March: 0.7 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. May is 1.1 °C cooler than April on average.

Coimbatore in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Coimbatore in April?
In April, Coimbatore averages a day high of 35.4 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Coimbatore?
April is not one of Coimbatore’s mild months; those are January, July, August and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Coimbatore in April?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (176 mm), the driest February (9 mm).
Does it snow in Coimbatore in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Coimbatore get in April?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
