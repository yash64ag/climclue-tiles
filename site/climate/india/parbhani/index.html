Parbhani at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Parbhani is 122nd warmest (26.9 °C year average), 104th sunniest (about 2,780 hours of sunshine a year) and 283rd wettest (1,022 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nanded, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Parbhani is 0.7 °C cooler in May and 0.7 °C cooler in December, with 8 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.0 °C), averages 12.6 °C more than its coolest, December (21.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.1 °C (59 °F), in December. The area lies about 412 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Parbhani, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Parbhani has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 412 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
