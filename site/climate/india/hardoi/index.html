Hardoī at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Hardoī is 401st warmest (24.9 °C year average), 307th sunniest (about 2,622 hours of sunshine a year) and 322nd wettest (946 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Farrukhābād, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Hardoī is 0.3 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 41 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (32.7 °C), averages 18.4 °C more than its coolest, January (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.5 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 965 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Hardoī has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 965 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
