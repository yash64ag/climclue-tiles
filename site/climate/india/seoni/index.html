Seoni at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Seoni is 397th warmest (24.9 °C year average), 168th sunniest (about 2,711 hours of sunshine a year) and 126th wettest (1,492 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chhindwāra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (63 km away), Seoni is 0.8 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 163 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.3 °C), averages 15.1 °C more than its coolest, December (18.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.2 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 625 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Seoni, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Seoni has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 625 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
