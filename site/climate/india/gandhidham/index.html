Gāndhīdhām at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Gāndhīdhām is the 2nd sunniest (about 3,218 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Bhuj, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), Gāndhīdhām is 0.2 °C warmer in May and 1.2 °C warmer in January, with 96 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.1 °C), averages 10.4 °C more than its coolest, January (20.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.3 °C (60 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Gāndhīdhām has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
