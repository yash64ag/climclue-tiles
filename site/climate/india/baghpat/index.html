Bāghpat at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Bāghpat is 3189th warmest (24.5 °C year average), 1516th sunniest (about 2,680 hours of sunshine a year) and 3197th wettest (690 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Bāghpat has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 896 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-550-2572), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
