Sivakasi at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Sivakasi is the 5th warmest (29.0 °C year average). Compared with Rajapalayam, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Sivakasi is 1.3 °C warmer in May and 0.5 °C warmer in December, with 8 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.8 °C), averages 6.0 °C more than its coolest, December (25.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.2 °C (68 °F), in January. The area lies about 86 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sivakasi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sivakasi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 86 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
