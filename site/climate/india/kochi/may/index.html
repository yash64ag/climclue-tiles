How May compares in Kochi

May is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Kochi. Compared with April: 0.5 °C cooler, 144 mm more precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. June is 1.5 °C cooler than May on average.

Kochi in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kochi in May?
In May, Kochi averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 274 mm (10.8 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kochi?
No month in Kochi is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kochi in May?
274 mm (10.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (725 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Kochi in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kochi get in May?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kochi in May?
About 29.4 °C (85 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is April (29.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kochi in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
