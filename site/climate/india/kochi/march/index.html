Kochi weather in March

Average March weather in Kochi, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Kochi averages 31.7 °C (89 °F) days and 23.9 °C (75 °F) nights, with 47 mm (1.8 in) of rain over the month and about 266 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Kochi

March is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Kochi. Compared with February: 0.8 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. April is 0.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Kochi in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kochi in March?
In March, Kochi averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kochi?
No month in Kochi is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kochi in March?
47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (725 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Kochi in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kochi get in March?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Kochi in March?
About 29.1 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is April (29.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Kochi in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
