Kochi at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Kochi is 172nd warmest (26.5 °C year average), 379th sunniest (about 2,467 hours of sunshine a year) and 16th wettest (2,994 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Alappuzha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), Kochi is 0.4 °C cooler in April and 0.4 °C cooler in August, with 55 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.1 °C), averages 2.7 °C more than its coolest, August (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.3 °C (76 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kochi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kochi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
