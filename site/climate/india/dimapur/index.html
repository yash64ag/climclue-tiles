Dimāpur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Dimāpur is 492nd warmest (23.6 °C year average), 427th sunniest (about 2,323 hours of sunshine a year) and 48th wettest (1,940 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jorhat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (106 km away), Dimāpur is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 699 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 12.5 °C more than its coolest, January (15.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.5 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 435 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dimāpur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dimāpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 435 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
