Hanumāngarh at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Hanumāngarh is the 2nd driest (312 mm a year). Compared with Sri Ganganagar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Hanumāngarh is 0.2 °C warmer in June and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.3 °C), averages 21.1 °C more than its coolest, January (13.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.7 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 844 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Hanumāngarh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 844 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
