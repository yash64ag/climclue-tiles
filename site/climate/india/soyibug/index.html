Soyībug at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Soyībug is the 3rd coolest (12.7 °C year average). Compared with Srinagar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Soyībug is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 163 mm less precipitation a year. The area lies about 1,255 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Soyībug has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 1,255 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
