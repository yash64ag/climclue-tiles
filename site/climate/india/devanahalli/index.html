Devanahalli weather by month and climate

Devanahalli at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Devanahalli is 3545th warmest (23.4 °C year average), 2999th sunniest (about 2,406 hours of sunshine a year) and 2875th wettest (801 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vijayapura, Karnataka (13.3°N 77.8°E), the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Devanahalli is 0.1 °C cooler in April and about as warm in December, with 27 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (27.2 °C), averages 7.2 °C more than its coolest, December (20.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.4 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 278 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Devanahalli, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Devanahalli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 278 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Devanahalli itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (13.247°N, 77.712°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-707-2577), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
