Bhind at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Bhind is 324th warmest (25.4 °C year average), 177th sunniest (about 2,706 hours of sunshine a year) and 412th wettest (772 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Etāwah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Bhind is 0.4 °C warmer in June and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 27 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.0 °C), averages 19.5 °C more than its coolest, January (14.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.6 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 804 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bhind, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bhind has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 804 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
