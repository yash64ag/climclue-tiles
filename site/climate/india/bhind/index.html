Bhind at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Bhind is 2484th warmest (25.4 °C year average), 1277th sunniest (about 2,706 hours of sunshine a year) and 2953rd wettest (777 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mahgawān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Bhind is 0.2 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 28 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.9 °C), averages 19.4 °C more than its coolest, January (14.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.7 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 804 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bhind, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bhind has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 804 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bhind itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.567°N, 78.787°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-574-2587), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
