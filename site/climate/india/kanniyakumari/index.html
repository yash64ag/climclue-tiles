Kanniyākumāri at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Kanniyākumāri is 963rd warmest (27.0 °C year average), 2979th sunniest (about 2,410 hours of sunshine a year) and 2605th wettest (867 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Kanniyākumāri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
