Chorwād at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Chorwād is 1771st warmest (26.2 °C year average), 138th sunniest (about 2,995 hours of sunshine a year) and 1997th wettest (1,040 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Chorwād has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
