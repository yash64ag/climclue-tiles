Chorwād sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Gujarat · India

The sea off Chorwād averages 29.1 °C (84 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 24.2 °C (76 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Chorwād sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Chorwād?
In June, at about 29.1 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Chorwād?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 24.2 °C (76 °F).
How warm is the sea in Chorwād in February?
About 24.2 °C (76 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Chorwād?
About 26.9 °C (80 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
