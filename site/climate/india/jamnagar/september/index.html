How September compares in Jamnagar

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Jamnagar. Compared with August: 0.2 °C warmer, 78 mm less precipitation and 84 hours more sunshine. October is 0.4 °C warmer than September on average.

Jamnagar in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jamnagar in September?
In September, Jamnagar averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 25.4 °C (78 °F), with 94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Jamnagar?
September is not one of Jamnagar’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jamnagar in September?
94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (288 mm), the driest December (0 mm).
Does it snow in Jamnagar in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jamnagar get in September?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jamnagar in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
