How August compares in Jamnagar

August is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Jamnagar. Compared with July: 1.0 °C cooler, 117 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. September is 0.2 °C warmer than August on average.

Jamnagar in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jamnagar in August?
In August, Jamnagar averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 26.1 °C (79 °F), with 171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Jamnagar?
August is not one of Jamnagar’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jamnagar in August?
171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (288 mm), the driest December (0 mm).
Does it snow in Jamnagar in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jamnagar get in August?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jamnagar in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
