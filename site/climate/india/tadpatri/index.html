Tadpatri at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Tadpatri is 23rd warmest (28.4 °C year average), 302nd sunniest (about 2,626 hours of sunshine a year) and 425th wettest (739 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Anantapur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (50 km away), Tadpatri is 0.6 °C warmer in April and 0.3 °C warmer in December, with 135 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (33.1 °C), averages 9.3 °C more than its coolest, December (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.0 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 226 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tadpatri, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tadpatri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 226 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
