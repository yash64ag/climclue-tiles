Singrauli at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Singrauli is 393rd warmest (24.9 °C year average), 353rd sunniest (about 2,530 hours of sunshine a year) and 157th wettest (1,340 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mirzāpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (106 km away), Singrauli is 1.2 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 338 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.6 °C), averages 16.6 °C more than its coolest, January (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.4 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 553 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Singrauli, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Singrauli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 553 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
