Lochapada sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Odisha · India

The sea off Lochapada averages 29.5 °C (85 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 25.0 °C (77 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Lochapada sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Lochapada?
In June, at about 29.5 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Lochapada?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 25.0 °C (77 °F).
How warm is the sea in Lochapada in January?
About 25.0 °C (77 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Lochapada?
About 28.0 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
