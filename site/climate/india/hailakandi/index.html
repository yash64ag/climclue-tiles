Hailākāndi at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Hailākāndi is 3179th warmest (24.5 °C year average), 3416th sunniest (about 2,234 hours of sunshine a year) and 66th wettest (3,839 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Hailākāndi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 249 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hailākāndi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (24.684°N, 92.561°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-593-2725), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
