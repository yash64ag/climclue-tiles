Chittorgarh at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Chittorgarh is 295th warmest (25.6 °C year average), 32nd sunniest (about 2,931 hours of sunshine a year) and 448th wettest (686 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bhilwara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), Chittorgarh is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 58 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.0 °C), averages 17.2 °C more than its coolest, January (16.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.9 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 375 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chittorgarh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chittorgarh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 375 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
