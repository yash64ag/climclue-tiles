How November compares in Gwalior

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Gwalior. Compared with October: 5.3 °C cooler, 18 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. December is 4.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Gwalior in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gwalior in November?
In November, Gwalior averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Gwalior?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Gwalior’s mild months (February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gwalior in November?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (251 mm), the driest April (6 mm).
Does it snow in Gwalior in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gwalior get in November?
About 254 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gwalior in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
