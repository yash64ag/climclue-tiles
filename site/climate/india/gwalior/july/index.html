How July compares in Gwalior

July is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Gwalior. Compared with June: 3.9 °C cooler, 170 mm more precipitation and 60 hours less sunshine. August is 1.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Gwalior in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gwalior in July?
In July, Gwalior averages a day high of 33.3 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 26.7 °C (80 °F), with 245 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Gwalior?
July is not one of Gwalior’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gwalior in July?
245 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (245 mm), the driest April (6 mm).
Does it snow in Gwalior in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gwalior get in July?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gwalior in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
