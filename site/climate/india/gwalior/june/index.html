How June compares in Gwalior

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Gwalior. Compared with May: 0.2 °C cooler, 64 mm more precipitation and 82 hours less sunshine. July is 3.9 °C cooler than June on average.

Gwalior in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gwalior in June?
In June, Gwalior averages a day high of 39.1 °C (102 °F) and a night low of 28.9 °C (84 °F), with 75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Gwalior?
June is not one of Gwalior’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gwalior in June?
75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (245 mm), the driest April (6 mm).
Does it snow in Gwalior in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gwalior get in June?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gwalior in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
