How April compares in Gwalior

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Gwalior. Compared with March: 6.0 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. May is 3.7 °C warmer than April on average.

Gwalior in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gwalior in April?
In April, Gwalior averages a day high of 38.8 °C (102 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 277 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Gwalior?
April is not one of Gwalior’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gwalior in April?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (251 mm), the driest April (6 mm).
Does it snow in Gwalior in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gwalior get in April?
About 277 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gwalior in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
