Chamba at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Chamba is 3684th warmest (17.6 °C year average), 1100th sunniest (about 2,729 hours of sunshine a year) and 522nd wettest (2,336 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dharamsala, the nearest covered town with different numbers (41 km away), Chamba is 1.5 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 659 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.3 °C), averages 18.4 °C more than its coolest, January (6.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 1,213 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chamba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chamba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,213 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.4 °C. The 10 km area around Chamba, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 14.9 °C a year, 2.7 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Chamba itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (32.555°N, 76.126°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-514-2561), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
