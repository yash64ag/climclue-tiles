How January compares in Kallakurichi

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Kallakurichi. Compared with December: about as warm, 77 mm less precipitation and 55 hours more sunshine. February is 1.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Kallakurichi in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kallakurichi in January?
In January, Kallakurichi averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 18.9 °C (66 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 257 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kallakurichi?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Kallakurichi’s mild months (January and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kallakurichi in January?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (180 mm), the driest February (10 mm).
Does it snow in Kallakurichi in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kallakurichi get in January?
About 257 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kallakurichi in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
