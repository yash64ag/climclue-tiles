How May compares in Kallakurichi

Kallakurichi in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kallakurichi in May?
In May, Kallakurichi averages a day high of 37.5 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 26.5 °C (80 °F), with 80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kallakurichi?
May is not one of Kallakurichi’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kallakurichi in May?
80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (181 mm), the driest February (10 mm).
Does it snow in Kallakurichi in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kallakurichi get in May?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kallakurichi in May?
