Kallakurichi weather in June

Average June weather in Kallakurichi, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Kallakurichi averages 36.0 °C (97 °F) days and 25.6 °C (78 °F) nights, with 55 mm (2.2 in) of rain over the month and about 169 hours of sunshine.
