Chaumu weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Chaumu, India (Rajasthan), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Chaumu at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Chaumu is 3161st warmest (24.5 °C year average), 517th sunniest (about 2,823 hours of sunshine a year) and 3502nd wettest (591 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Reengus, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Chaumu is 0.1 °C warmer in May and about as warm in January, with 53 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.6 °C), averages 18.7 °C more than its coolest, January (13.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.5 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 648 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Chaumu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 648 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Chaumu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (27.170°N, 75.722°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-568-2557), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts. Sources: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010 (temperature, precipitation, wind); ERA5-Land, 1981–2010 (dew point and snow; snowfall matched to the area’s CHELSA climate; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information); relative humidity calculated from the dew point and the night lows and day highs; CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990 (sunshine and the day–night range; a different period); GeoNames (name and population 64,417, CC BY 4.0); climate type by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
