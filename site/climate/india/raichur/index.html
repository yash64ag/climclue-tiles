Rāichūr at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Rāichūr is 59th warmest (27.7 °C year average), 223rd sunniest (about 2,685 hours of sunshine a year) and 415th wettest (763 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Adoni, the nearest covered town with different numbers (65 km away), Rāichūr is 0.9 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 55 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.0 °C), averages 9.5 °C more than its coolest, December (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.4 °C (63 °F), in December. The area lies about 320 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rāichūr, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Rāichūr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 320 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
