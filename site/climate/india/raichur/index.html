Rāichūr at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Rāichūr is 523rd warmest (27.6 °C year average), 1471st sunniest (about 2,685 hours of sunshine a year) and 2883rd wettest (798 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shaktinagar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Rāichūr is 0.4 °C cooler in May and 0.3 °C cooler in December, with 63 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.9 °C), averages 9.5 °C more than its coolest, December (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.5 °C (64 °F), in December. The area lies about 320 km from the sea.

Why Rāichūr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 320 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Rāichūr itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (16.205°N, 77.356°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-677-2573), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
