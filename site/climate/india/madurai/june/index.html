How June compares in Madurai

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Madurai. Compared with May: 1.0 °C cooler, 36 mm less precipitation and 64 hours less sunshine. July is 0.7 °C cooler than June on average.

Madurai in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Madurai in June?
In June, Madurai averages a day high of 36.5 °C (98 °F) and a night low of 25.7 °C (78 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Madurai?
No month in Madurai is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Madurai in June?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (194 mm), the driest February (14 mm).
Does it snow in Madurai in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Madurai get in June?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Madurai in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
