How December compares in Madurai

December is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Madurai. Compared with November: 0.9 °C cooler, 109 mm less precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. January is 0.3 °C warmer than December on average.

Madurai in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Madurai in December?
In December, Madurai averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 20.7 °C (69 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Madurai?
No month in Madurai is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Madurai in December?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (194 mm), the driest February (14 mm).
Does it snow in Madurai in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Madurai get in December?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Madurai in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
