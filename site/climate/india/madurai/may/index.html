How May compares in Madurai

May is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Madurai. Compared with April: 0.8 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. June is 1.0 °C cooler than May on average.

Madurai in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Madurai in May?
In May, Madurai averages a day high of 38.1 °C (101 °F) and a night low of 26.4 °C (80 °F), with 70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Madurai?
No month in Madurai is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Madurai in May?
70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (194 mm), the driest February (14 mm).
Does it snow in Madurai in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Madurai get in May?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Madurai in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
