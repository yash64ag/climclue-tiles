Madurai in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Madurai in August?
In August, Madurai averages a day high of 35.6 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 25.0 °C (77 °F), with 80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Madurai?
No month in Madurai is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Madurai in August?
80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (194 mm), the driest February (14 mm).
Does it snow in Madurai in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Madurai get in August?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Madurai in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
