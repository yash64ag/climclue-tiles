Madurai at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Madurai is the 2nd warmest (29.1 °C year average). Compared with Dindigul, the nearest covered town with different numbers (52 km away), Madurai is 1.7 °C warmer in May and 1.5 °C warmer in December, with 66 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.1 °C), averages 6.7 °C more than its coolest, December (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.8 °C (68 °F), in January. The area lies about 104 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Madurai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Madurai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 104 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
