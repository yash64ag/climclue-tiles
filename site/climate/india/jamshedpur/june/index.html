How June compares in Jamshedpur

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Jamshedpur. Compared with May: 1.4 °C cooler, 181 mm more precipitation and 89 hours less sunshine. July is 1.8 °C cooler than June on average.

Jamshedpur in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jamshedpur in June?
In June, Jamshedpur averages a day high of 34.3 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 26.2 °C (79 °F), with 263 mm (10.4 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Jamshedpur?
June is not one of Jamshedpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jamshedpur in June?
263 mm (10.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (372 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Jamshedpur in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jamshedpur get in June?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jamshedpur in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
