Jamshedpur weather in August

Average August weather in Jamshedpur, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Jamshedpur averages 31.1 °C (88 °F) days and 24.9 °C (77 °F) nights, with 330 mm (13.0 in) of rain over the month and about 113 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Jamshedpur

August is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Jamshedpur. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, 18 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. September is 0.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Jamshedpur in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jamshedpur in August?
In August, Jamshedpur averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 330 mm (13.0 in) of precipitation and about 113 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Jamshedpur?
August is not one of Jamshedpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jamshedpur in August?
330 mm (13.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (349 mm), the driest December (9 mm).
Does it snow in Jamshedpur in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jamshedpur get in August?
About 113 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jamshedpur in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
