How May compares in Jamshedpur

May is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Jamshedpur. Compared with April: 1.1 °C warmer, 54 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. June is 1.4 °C cooler than May on average.

Jamshedpur in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jamshedpur in May?
In May, Jamshedpur averages a day high of 37.8 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 25.7 °C (78 °F), with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Jamshedpur?
May is not one of Jamshedpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jamshedpur in May?
83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (372 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Jamshedpur in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jamshedpur get in May?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jamshedpur in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
