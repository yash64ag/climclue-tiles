How October compares in Jamshedpur

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Jamshedpur. Compared with September: 1.6 °C cooler, 210 mm less precipitation and 71 hours more sunshine. November is 3.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Jamshedpur in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jamshedpur in October?
In October, Jamshedpur averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 21.0 °C (70 °F), with 75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Jamshedpur?
October is not one of Jamshedpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jamshedpur in October?
75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (372 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Jamshedpur in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jamshedpur get in October?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jamshedpur in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
