How April compares in Jamshedpur

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Jamshedpur. Compared with March: 4.0 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. May is 1.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Jamshedpur in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jamshedpur in April?
In April, Jamshedpur averages a day high of 37.5 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Jamshedpur?
April is not one of Jamshedpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jamshedpur in April?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (372 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Jamshedpur in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jamshedpur get in April?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jamshedpur in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
