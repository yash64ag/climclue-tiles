How September compares in Jamshedpur

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Jamshedpur. Compared with August: 0.2 °C cooler, 65 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. October is 1.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Jamshedpur in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jamshedpur in September?
In September, Jamshedpur averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 284 mm (11.2 in) of precipitation and about 129 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Jamshedpur?
September is not one of Jamshedpur’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jamshedpur in September?
284 mm (11.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (372 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Jamshedpur in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jamshedpur get in September?
About 129 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jamshedpur in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
