Kishangarh at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Kishangarh is 337th warmest (25.3 °C year average), 42nd sunniest (about 2,907 hours of sunshine a year) and 515th wettest (443 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ajmer, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Kishangarh is 0.5 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 37 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.5 °C), averages 18.4 °C more than its coolest, January (15.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.4 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 546 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kishangarh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kishangarh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 546 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
