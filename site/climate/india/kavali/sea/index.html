Kāvali sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Andhra Pradesh · India

The sea off Kāvali averages 29.7 °C (85 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 26.1 °C (79 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Kāvali sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kāvali?
In May, at about 29.7 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kāvali?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 26.1 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kāvali in January?
About 26.1 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Kāvali?
About 28.4 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
