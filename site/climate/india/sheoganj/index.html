Sheoganj at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Sheoganj is 1952nd warmest (26.0 °C year average), 67th sunniest (about 3,074 hours of sunshine a year) and 3590th wettest (515 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sumerpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Sheoganj is about as warm in May and about as warm in January, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.1 °C), averages 15.9 °C more than its coolest, January (17.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.5 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 339 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sheoganj, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sheoganj has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 339 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sheoganj itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.139°N, 73.068°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-588-2530), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
