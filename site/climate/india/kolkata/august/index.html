How August compares in Kolkata

August is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kolkata. Compared with July: 0.1 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. September is 0.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Kolkata in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kolkata in August?
In August, Kolkata averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 25.7 °C (78 °F), with 363 mm (14.3 in) of precipitation and about 100 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Kolkata?
August is not one of Kolkata’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kolkata in August?
363 mm (14.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (370 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Kolkata in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kolkata get in August?
About 100 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kolkata in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
