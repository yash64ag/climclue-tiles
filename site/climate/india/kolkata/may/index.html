How May compares in Kolkata

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Kolkata. Compared with April: 0.6 °C warmer, 96 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. June is 0.6 °C cooler than May on average.

Kolkata in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kolkata in May?
In May, Kolkata averages a day high of 34.7 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 26.6 °C (80 °F), with 153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kolkata?
May is not one of Kolkata’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kolkata in May?
153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (369 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Kolkata in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kolkata get in May?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kolkata in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
