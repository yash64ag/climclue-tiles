How January compares in Kolkata

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kolkata. Compared with December: 0.9 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. February is 3.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Kolkata in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kolkata in January?
In January, Kolkata averages a day high of 25.2 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 13.1 °C (56 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kolkata?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Kolkata’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kolkata in January?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (369 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Kolkata in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kolkata get in January?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kolkata in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
