Why Rishra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 119 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Rishra climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Rishra?
May, with an average of 30.1 °C (86 °F), day highs around 34.9 °C (95 °F) and night lows around 26.4 °C (80 °F).
What is the coldest month in Rishra?
January, with an average of 18.4 °C (65 °F), day highs around 25.0 °C (77 °F) and night lows around 12.7 °C (55 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Rishra?
July, with 344 mm (13.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,724 mm (67.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Rishra?
December, with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Rishra?
April, with about 229 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Rishra?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Rishra?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, November and December.
What type of climate does Rishra have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Rishra’s GeoNames location (22.724°N, 88.346°E; ClimaClue area sq-612-2683), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 117,014 (GeoNames).
