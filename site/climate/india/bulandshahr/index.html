Bulandshahr at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Bulandshahr is 435th warmest (24.6 °C year average), 266th sunniest (about 2,665 hours of sunshine a year) and 389th wettest (819 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Khurja, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Bulandshahr is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 30 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.0 °C), averages 19.3 °C more than its coolest, January (13.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.8 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 886 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bulandshahr, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bulandshahr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 886 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
