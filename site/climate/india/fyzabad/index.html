Fyzābād at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Fyzābād is 365th warmest (25.2 °C year average), 359th sunniest (about 2,521 hours of sunshine a year) and 225th wettest (1,141 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gondā City, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Fyzābād is 0.3 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 66 mm less precipitation a year. The area lies about 778 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Fyzābād has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 778 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
