Malda at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Malda is 331st warmest (25.4 °C year average), 488th sunniest (about 2,147 hours of sunshine a year) and 87th wettest (1,679 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rāiganj, the nearest covered town with different numbers (68 km away), Malda is 0.9 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 261 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.4 °C), averages 12.4 °C more than its coolest, January (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.8 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 368 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Malda, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Malda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 368 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
