Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Najībābād is 3485th warmest (23.6 °C year average), 1909th sunniest (about 2,632 hours of sunshine a year) and 1271st wettest (1,317 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Najībābād has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,021 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
