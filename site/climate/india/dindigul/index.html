Dindigul at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Dindigul is 73rd warmest (27.5 °C year average), 400th sunniest (about 2,416 hours of sunshine a year) and 329th wettest (939 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Madurai, the nearest covered town with different numbers (52 km away), Dindigul is 0.8 °C cooler in April and 1.5 °C cooler in December, with 66 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.6 °C), averages 6.6 °C more than its coolest, December (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.4 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 143 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dindigul, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dindigul has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 143 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
