How March compares in Warangal

March is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Warangal. Compared with February: 3.4 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. April is 2.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Warangal in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Warangal in March?
In March, Warangal averages a day high of 35.2 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 268 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Warangal?
March is not one of Warangal’s mild months; those are January, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Warangal in March?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (288 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Warangal in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Warangal get in March?
About 268 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Warangal in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
