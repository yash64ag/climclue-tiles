How August compares in Warangal

August is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Warangal. Compared with July: 0.7 °C cooler, 39 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. September is 0.2 °C warmer than August on average.

Warangal in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Warangal in August?
In August, Warangal averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 24.4 °C (76 °F), with 249 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Warangal?
August is not one of Warangal’s mild months; those are January, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Warangal in August?
249 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (288 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Warangal in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Warangal get in August?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Warangal in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
