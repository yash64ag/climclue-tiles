How February compares in Warangal

February is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Warangal. Compared with January: 2.8 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. March is 3.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Warangal in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Warangal in February?
In February, Warangal averages a day high of 32.5 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 19.1 °C (66 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 262 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Warangal?
February is not one of Warangal’s mild months; those are January, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Warangal in February?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (284 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Warangal in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Warangal get in February?
About 262 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Warangal in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
