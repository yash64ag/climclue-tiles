Solsumba at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Solsumba is 1533rd warmest (26.5 °C year average), 752nd sunniest (about 2,778 hours of sunshine a year) and 538th wettest (2,255 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sanjan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Solsumba is 0.1 °C cooler in May and about as warm in January, with 93 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.4 °C), averages 6.7 °C more than its coolest, January (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.7 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Solsumba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
