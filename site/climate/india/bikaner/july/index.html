How July compares in Bikaner

July is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Bikaner. Compared with June: 2.5 °C cooler, 56 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. August is 1.0 °C cooler than July on average.

Bikaner in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bikaner in July?
In July, Bikaner averages a day high of 36.8 °C (98 °F) and a night low of 28.1 °C (83 °F), with 88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Bikaner?
July is not one of Bikaner’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bikaner in July?
88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (88 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Bikaner in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bikaner get in July?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bikaner in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
