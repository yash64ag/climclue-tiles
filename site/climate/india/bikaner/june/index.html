How June compares in Bikaner

June is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Bikaner. Compared with May: 0.4 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 56 hours less sunshine. July is 2.5 °C cooler than June on average.

Bikaner in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bikaner in June?
In June, Bikaner averages a day high of 40.5 °C (105 °F) and a night low of 29.3 °C (85 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Bikaner?
June is not one of Bikaner’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bikaner in June?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (88 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Bikaner in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bikaner get in June?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bikaner in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
