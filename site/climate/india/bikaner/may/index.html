How May compares in Bikaner

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Bikaner. Compared with April: 4.0 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. June is 0.4 °C warmer than May on average.

Bikaner in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bikaner in May?
In May, Bikaner averages a day high of 41.1 °C (106 °F) and a night low of 26.9 °C (80 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 293 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Bikaner?
May is not one of Bikaner’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bikaner in May?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (88 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Bikaner in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bikaner get in May?
About 293 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bikaner in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
