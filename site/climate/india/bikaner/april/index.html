How April compares in Bikaner

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Bikaner. Compared with March: 6.0 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. May is 4.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Bikaner in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bikaner in April?
In April, Bikaner averages a day high of 37.3 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 282 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Bikaner?
April is not one of Bikaner’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bikaner in April?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (88 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Bikaner in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bikaner get in April?
About 282 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bikaner in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
