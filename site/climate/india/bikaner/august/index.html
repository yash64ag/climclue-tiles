How August compares in Bikaner

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Bikaner. Compared with July: 1.0 °C cooler, 35 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. September is 0.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Bikaner in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bikaner in August?
In August, Bikaner averages a day high of 35.8 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 27.1 °C (81 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Bikaner?
August is not one of Bikaner’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bikaner in August?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (88 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Bikaner in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bikaner get in August?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bikaner in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
