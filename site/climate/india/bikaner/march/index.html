How March compares in Bikaner

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Bikaner. Compared with February: 6.3 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. April is 6.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Bikaner in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bikaner in March?
In March, Bikaner averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 260 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bikaner?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Bikaner’s mild months (February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bikaner in March?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (88 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Bikaner in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bikaner get in March?
About 260 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bikaner in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
