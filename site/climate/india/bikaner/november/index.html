How November compares in Bikaner

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Bikaner. Compared with October: 6.4 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. December is 5.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Bikaner in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bikaner in November?
In November, Bikaner averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 265 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Bikaner?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Bikaner’s mild months (February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bikaner in November?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (88 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Bikaner in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bikaner get in November?
About 265 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bikaner in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
