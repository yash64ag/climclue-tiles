How September compares in Bikaner

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Bikaner. Compared with August: 0.2 °C cooler, 23 mm less precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. October is 3.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Bikaner in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bikaner in September?
In September, Bikaner averages a day high of 36.5 °C (98 °F) and a night low of 26.3 °C (79 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 257 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Bikaner?
September is not one of Bikaner’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bikaner in September?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (88 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Bikaner in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bikaner get in September?
About 257 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bikaner in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
