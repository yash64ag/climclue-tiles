How October compares in Bikaner

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Bikaner. Compared with September: 3.3 °C cooler, 20 mm less precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. November is 6.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Bikaner in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bikaner in October?
In October, Bikaner averages a day high of 34.8 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 284 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Bikaner?
October is not one of Bikaner’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bikaner in October?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (88 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Bikaner in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bikaner get in October?
About 284 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bikaner in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
