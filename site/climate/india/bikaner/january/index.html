How January compares in Bikaner

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Bikaner. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. February is 3.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Bikaner in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bikaner in January?
In January, Bikaner averages a day high of 21.4 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 7.9 °C (46 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Bikaner?
January is not one of Bikaner’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bikaner in January?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (88 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Bikaner in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bikaner get in January?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bikaner in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
