How February compares in Bikaner

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Bikaner. Compared with January: 3.7 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. March is 6.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Bikaner in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bikaner in February?
In February, Bikaner averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 10.8 °C (51 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Bikaner?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Bikaner’s mild months (February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bikaner in February?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (87 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Bikaner in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bikaner get in February?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bikaner in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
