Tumkūr at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Tumkūr is 486th warmest (23.7 °C year average), 392nd sunniest (about 2,433 hours of sunshine a year) and 420th wettest (751 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yelahanka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (60 km away), Tumkūr is 0.4 °C warmer in April and 0.4 °C warmer in December, with 97 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (27.4 °C), averages 6.8 °C more than its coolest, December (20.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.8 °C (59 °F), in January. The area lies about 261 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tumkūr, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tumkūr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 261 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
