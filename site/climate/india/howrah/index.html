Howrah at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Howrah is the 4th least sunny (about 2,078 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Bāli, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Howrah is about as warm in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 48 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.1 °C), averages 11.4 °C more than its coolest, January (18.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.1 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 98 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Howrah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Howrah

For mild weather, the best months to visit Howrah are January, February, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, November and December.

Why Howrah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 98 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
