How February compares in Howrah

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Howrah. Compared with January: 3.6 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. March is 4.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Howrah in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Howrah in February?
In February, Howrah averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 15.9 °C (61 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Howrah?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Howrah’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Howrah in February?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (365 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Howrah in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Howrah get in February?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Howrah in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
