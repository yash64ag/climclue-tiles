How March compares in Howrah

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Howrah. Compared with February: 4.6 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. April is 2.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Howrah in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Howrah in March?
In March, Howrah averages a day high of 32.9 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Howrah?
March is not one of Howrah’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Howrah in March?
37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (369 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Howrah in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Howrah get in March?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Howrah in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
