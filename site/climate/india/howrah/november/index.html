How November compares in Howrah

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Howrah. Compared with October: 3.5 °C cooler, 127 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. December is 3.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Howrah in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Howrah in November?
In November, Howrah averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 18.4 °C (65 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Howrah?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Howrah’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Howrah in November?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (365 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Howrah in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Howrah get in November?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Howrah in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
