Howrah weather in October

Average October weather in Howrah, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Howrah averages 31.2 °C (88 °F) days and 22.9 °C (73 °F) nights, with 162 mm (6.4 in) of rain over the month and about 184 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Howrah

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Howrah. Compared with September: 1.2 °C cooler, 140 mm less precipitation and 70 hours more sunshine. November is 3.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Howrah in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Howrah in October?
In October, Howrah averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 162 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Howrah?
October is not one of Howrah’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Howrah in October?
162 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (365 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Howrah in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Howrah get in October?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Howrah in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
