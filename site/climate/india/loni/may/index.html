How May compares in Loni

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Loni. Compared with April: 3.7 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. June is 0.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Loni in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Loni in May?
In May, Loni averages a day high of 39.3 °C (103 °F) and a night low of 25.0 °C (77 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 268 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Loni?
May is not one of Loni’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Loni in May?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (170 mm), the driest November (6 mm).
Does it snow in Loni in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Loni get in May?
About 268 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Loni in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
