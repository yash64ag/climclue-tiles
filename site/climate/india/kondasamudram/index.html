Kondasamudram at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Kondasamudram is 670th warmest (27.3 °C year average), 2303rd sunniest (about 2,539 hours of sunshine a year) and 2458th wettest (904 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Kondasamudram has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 158 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
