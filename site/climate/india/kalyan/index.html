Kalyān at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Kalyān is 130th warmest (26.8 °C year average), 178th sunniest (about 2,706 hours of sunshine a year) and 39th wettest (2,265 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dombivali, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Kalyān is 0.1 °C warmer in May and about as warm in January, with 127 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.3 °C), averages 7.0 °C more than its coolest, January (23.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.7 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 48 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kalyān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kalyān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 48 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
