How April compares in Kalyān

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Kalyān. Compared with March: 1.9 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. May is 1.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Kalyān in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kalyān in April?
In April, Kalyān averages a day high of 35.8 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 292 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kalyān?
April is not one of Kalyān’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kalyān in April?
1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (812 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Kalyān in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kalyān get in April?
About 292 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kalyān in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
