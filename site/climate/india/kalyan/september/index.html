Kalyān weather in September

Average September weather in Kalyān, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Kalyān averages 30.4 °C (87 °F) days and 23.6 °C (74 °F) nights, with 355 mm (14.0 in) of rain over the month and about 175 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Kalyān

September is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Kalyān. Compared with August: 0.4 °C warmer, 194 mm less precipitation and 85 hours more sunshine. October is 0.6 °C warmer than September on average.

Kalyān in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kalyān in September?
In September, Kalyān averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 355 mm (14.0 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Kalyān?
September is not one of Kalyān’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kalyān in September?
355 mm (14.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (801 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Kalyān in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kalyān get in September?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kalyān in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
