Palwal Rural at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Palwal Rural is 2812th warmest (25.0 °C year average), 1370th sunniest (about 2,693 hours of sunshine a year) and 3235th wettest (675 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Palwal Rural has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 829 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
