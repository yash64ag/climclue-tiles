Kansad weather by month and climate

Kansad at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Kansad is 902nd warmest (27.1 °C year average), 377th sunniest (about 2,860 hours of sunshine a year) and 962nd wettest (1,498 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sachīn, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Kansad is about as warm in May and about as warm in January, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Kansad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kansad itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.075°N, 72.879°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-629-2528), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
