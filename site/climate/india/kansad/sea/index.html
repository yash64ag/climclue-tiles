Kansad sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Gujarat · India

The sea off Kansad averages 29.6 °C (85 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 22.4 °C (72 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December.

Kansad sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kansad?
In June, at about 29.6 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kansad?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from March to December. The coolest month is January, at 22.4 °C (72 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kansad in January?
About 22.4 °C (72 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Kansad?
About 26.8 °C (80 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
