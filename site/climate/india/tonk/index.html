Why Tonk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 557 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Tonk climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Tonk?
May, with an average of 34.9 °C (95 °F), day highs around 41.3 °C (106 °F) and night lows around 27.3 °C (81 °F).
What is the coldest month in Tonk?
January, with an average of 15.4 °C (60 °F), day highs around 22.7 °C (73 °F) and night lows around 8.5 °C (47 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Tonk?
July, with 229 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 616 mm (24.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Tonk?
March, with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Tonk?
May, with about 296 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Tonk?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Tonk?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, November and December.
What type of climate does Tonk have?
Hot semi-arid (Köppen BSh). Dry but not desert: hot, with a short and unreliable rainy season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10.0 × 11 km, that contains Tonk’s GeoNames location (26.166°N, 75.788°E; ClimaClue area sq-578-2557), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 165,294 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).
