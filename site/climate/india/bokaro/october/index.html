Bokāro in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bokāro in October?
In October, Bokāro averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Bokāro?
October is not one of Bokāro’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bokāro in October?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (382 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Bokāro in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bokāro get in October?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bokāro in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
