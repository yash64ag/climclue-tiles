Bokāro in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bokāro in November?
In November, Bokāro averages a day high of 27.3 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Bokāro?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Bokāro’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bokāro in November?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (381 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Bokāro in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bokāro get in November?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bokāro in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
