Bokāro in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bokāro in August?
In August, Bokāro averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 24.7 °C (76 °F), with 339 mm (13.3 in) of precipitation and about 111 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Bokāro?
August is not one of Bokāro’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

339 mm (13.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (382 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Bokāro in August?
