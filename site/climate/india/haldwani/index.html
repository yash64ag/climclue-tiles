Haldwani at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Haldwani is 3572nd warmest (23.3 °C year average), 2225th sunniest (about 2,556 hours of sunshine a year) and 855th wettest (1,591 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Naini Tāl, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Haldwani is 11.8 °C warmer in June and 10.3 °C warmer in January, with 343 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (30.0 °C), averages 16.2 °C more than its coolest, January (13.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.5 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,062 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Haldwani has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,062 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.2 °C. The 10 km area around Haldwani, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 21.8 °C a year, 1.5 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Haldwani itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (29.223°N, 79.529°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-547-2595), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
