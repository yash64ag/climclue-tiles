How October compares in Sholapur

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Sholapur. Compared with September: 0.1 °C cooler, 127 mm less precipitation and 67 hours more sunshine. November is 1.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Sholapur in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sholapur in October?
In October, Sholapur averages a day high of 32.5 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Sholapur?
October is not one of Sholapur’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sholapur in October?
86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (213 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Sholapur in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sholapur get in October?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sholapur in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
