Sholapur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Sholapur is 66th warmest (27.6 °C year average), 124th sunniest (about 2,756 hours of sunshine a year) and 385th wettest (824 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dharashiv, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Sholapur is 1.2 °C warmer in May and 1.6 °C warmer in December, with 222 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.5 °C), averages 10.1 °C more than its coolest, December (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.4 °C (63 °F), in December. The area lies about 300 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sholapur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sholapur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 300 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
