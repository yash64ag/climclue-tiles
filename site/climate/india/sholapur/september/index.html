Sholapur in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sholapur in September?
In September, Sholapur averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Sholapur?
September is not one of Sholapur’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sholapur in September?
213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (213 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Sholapur in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sholapur get in September?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sholapur in September?
