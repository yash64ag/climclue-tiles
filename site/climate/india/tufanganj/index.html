Tufānganj weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Tufānganj, India (West Bengal), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Tufānganj is warmest in August, with 31.5 °C (89 °F) days and 25.5 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 23.6 °C (74 °F) days and 11.0 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (802 mm); December is driest (7 mm).

Tufānganj at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Tufānganj is 3174th warmest (24.5 °C year average), 3372nd sunniest (about 2,248 hours of sunshine a year) and 144th wettest (3,543 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Koch Bihār, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Tufānganj is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 45 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.5 °C), averages 11.2 °C more than its coolest, January (17.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.0 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 440 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tufānganj, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tufānganj has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 440 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tufānganj itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.317°N, 89.665°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-576-2696), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
