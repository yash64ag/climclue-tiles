Dondaicha at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Dondaicha is 828th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 402nd sunniest (about 2,854 hours of sunshine a year) and 2938th wettest (783 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shāhāda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Dondaicha is 0.3 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 108 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.5 °C), averages 11.0 °C more than its coolest, January (21.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.4 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 189 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dondaicha, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dondaicha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 189 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dondaicha itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.324°N, 74.568°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-626-2545), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
