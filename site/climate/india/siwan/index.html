Siwān at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Siwān is 305th warmest (25.5 °C year average), 433rd sunniest (about 2,269 hours of sunshine a year) and 234th wettest (1,112 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chāpra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (63 km away), Siwān is 0.3 °C cooler in May and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 46 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.9 °C), averages 16.4 °C more than its coolest, January (15.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.9 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 605 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Siwān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Siwān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 605 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
