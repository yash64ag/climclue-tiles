Sikka at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Sikka is 1531st warmest (26.5 °C year average), 29th sunniest (about 3,150 hours of sunshine a year) and 3425th wettest (619 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bedi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Sikka is 0.2 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 43 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (30.3 °C), averages 9.4 °C more than its coolest, January (20.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.4 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sikka, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sikka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sikka itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.432°N, 69.842°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-615-2498), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
