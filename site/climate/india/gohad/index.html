Gohad at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Gohad is 2259th warmest (25.6 °C year average), 1143rd sunniest (about 2,725 hours of sunshine a year) and 3017th wettest (757 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mahgawān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Gohad is 0.2 °C warmer in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 8 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (34.3 °C), averages 19.6 °C more than its coolest, January (14.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.0 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 773 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gohad, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gohad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 773 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Gohad itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.433°N, 78.442°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-575-2584), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
