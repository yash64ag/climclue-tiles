Deoraniān weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Deoraniān, India (Uttar Pradesh), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Deoraniān at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Deoraniān is 3237th warmest (24.3 °C year average), 2031st sunniest (about 2,607 hours of sunshine a year) and 1250th wettest (1,325 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Richha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Deoraniān is 0.2 °C warmer in June and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 69 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (31.6 °C), averages 17.6 °C more than its coolest, January (14.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.1 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,008 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Deoraniān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 1,008 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Deoraniān itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (28.630°N, 79.476°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-553-2594), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
