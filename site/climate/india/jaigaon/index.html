Jaigaon at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Jaigaon is the 5th wettest (4,090 mm a year). Compared with Jalpāiguri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (74 km away), Jaigaon is 1.0 °C cooler in August and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 398 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.1 °C), averages 10.1 °C more than its coolest, January (17.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.8 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 502 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jaigaon, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jaigaon has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 502 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
