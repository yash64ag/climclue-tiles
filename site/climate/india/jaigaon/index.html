Jaigaon at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Jaigaon is 467th warmest (24.1 °C year average), 434th sunniest (about 2,269 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (4,031 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jalpāiguri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (74 km away), Jaigaon is 0.5 °C cooler in August and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 410 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.7 °C), averages 10.0 °C more than its coolest, January (17.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.4 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 502 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jaigaon, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jaigaon has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 502 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.0 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jaigaon itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.848°N, 89.376°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-571-2693), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
