How March compares in Vadodara

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Vadodara. Compared with February: 4.6 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. April is 3.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Vadodara in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vadodara in March?
In March, Vadodara averages a day high of 36.3 °C (97 °F) and a night low of 19.3 °C (67 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 280 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Vadodara?
March is not one of Vadodara’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vadodara in March?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (384 mm), the driest March (1 mm).
Does it snow in Vadodara in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vadodara get in March?
About 280 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vadodara in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
