How August compares in Vadodara

August is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Vadodara. Compared with July: 0.9 °C cooler, 83 mm less precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. September is 0.8 °C warmer than August on average.

Vadodara in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vadodara in August?
In August, Vadodara averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 299 mm (11.8 in) of precipitation and about 102 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Vadodara?
August is not one of Vadodara’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vadodara in August?
299 mm (11.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (382 mm), the driest March (1 mm).
Does it snow in Vadodara in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vadodara get in August?
About 102 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vadodara in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
