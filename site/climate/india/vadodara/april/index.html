How April compares in Vadodara

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Vadodara. Compared with March: 3.4 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. May is 1.7 °C warmer than April on average.

Vadodara in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vadodara in April?
In April, Vadodara averages a day high of 38.3 °C (101 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 297 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Vadodara?
April is not one of Vadodara’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vadodara in April?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (382 mm), the driest March (1 mm).
Does it snow in Vadodara in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vadodara get in April?
About 297 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vadodara in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
