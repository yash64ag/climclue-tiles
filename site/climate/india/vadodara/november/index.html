How November compares in Vadodara

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Vadodara. Compared with October: 3.1 °C cooler, 21 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. December is 2.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Vadodara in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Vadodara in November?
In November, Vadodara averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 18.6 °C (65 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 268 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Vadodara?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Vadodara’s mild months (January, February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Vadodara in November?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (382 mm), the driest March (1 mm).
Does it snow in Vadodara in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Vadodara get in November?
About 268 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Vadodara in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
