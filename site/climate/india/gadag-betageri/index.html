Gadag-Betageri at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Gadag-Betageri is 310th warmest (25.5 °C year average), 279th sunniest (about 2,653 hours of sunshine a year) and 478th wettest (625 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hubballi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (52 km away), Gadag-Betageri is 1.3 °C warmer in April and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 211 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.6 °C), averages 7.0 °C more than its coolest, December (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.6 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 181 km from the sea.

Why Gadag-Betageri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
