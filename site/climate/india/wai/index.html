Wai at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Wai is 3457th warmest (23.7 °C year average), 1005th sunniest (about 2,743 hours of sunshine a year) and 904th wettest (1,538 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bhor, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Wai is 0.4 °C cooler in April and 0.4 °C cooler in December, with 67 mm more precipitation a year. Why Wai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 88 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.5 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Wai itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (17.953°N, 73.891°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-660-2538), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
