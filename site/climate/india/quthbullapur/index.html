Quthbullapur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Quthbullapur is 250th warmest (25.9 °C year average), 202nd sunniest (about 2,692 hours of sunshine a year) and 279th wettest (1,029 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kukatpally, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Quthbullapur is 0.3 °C cooler in May and 0.3 °C cooler in December, with 110 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.9 °C), averages 10.8 °C more than its coolest, December (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.1 °C (59 °F), in December. The area lies about 292 km from the sea.

Why Quthbullapur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 292 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
