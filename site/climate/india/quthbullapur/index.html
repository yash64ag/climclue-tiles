Quthbullapur at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Quthbullapur is 1847th warmest (26.1 °C year average), 1373rd sunniest (about 2,692 hours of sunshine a year) and 2290th wettest (952 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kukatpally, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Quthbullapur is about as warm in May and about as warm in December, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.1 °C), averages 10.8 °C more than its coolest, December (21.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.7 °C (58 °F), in December. The area lies about 292 km from the sea.

Why Quthbullapur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 292 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-664-2584), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
