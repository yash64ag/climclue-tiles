Ābu at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Ābu is 3673rd warmest (19.6 °C year average), 83rd sunniest (about 3,057 hours of sunshine a year) and 2236th wettest (968 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ābu Road, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Ābu is 5.8 °C cooler in May and 6.9 °C cooler in January, with 168 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (26.7 °C), averages 15.0 °C more than its coolest, January (11.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 269 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ābu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ābu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 269 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.0 °C. The 10 km area around Ābu, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 23.4 °C a year, 3.8 °C warmer than the town itself, because it takes in lower ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ābu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (24.594°N, 72.718°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-594-2527), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
