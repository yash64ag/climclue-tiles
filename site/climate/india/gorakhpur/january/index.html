January · Uttar Pradesh · India

How January compares in Gorakhpur

January is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Gorakhpur. Compared with December: 1.6 °C cooler, 9 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. February is 3.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Gorakhpur in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gorakhpur in January?
In January, Gorakhpur averages a day high of 22.3 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 7.9 °C (46 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Gorakhpur?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Gorakhpur’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gorakhpur in January?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (375 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Gorakhpur in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gorakhpur get in January?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gorakhpur in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
