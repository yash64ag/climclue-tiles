Gorakhpur weather in July

Average July weather in Gorakhpur, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Gorakhpur averages 33.4 °C (92 °F) days and 25.4 °C (78 °F) nights, with 375 mm (14.7 in) of rain over the month and about 121 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Gorakhpur

July is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Gorakhpur. Compared with June: 2.2 °C cooler, 209 mm more precipitation and 59 hours less sunshine. August is 0.2 °C cooler than July on average.

Gorakhpur in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gorakhpur in July?
In July, Gorakhpur averages a day high of 33.4 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 25.4 °C (78 °F), with 375 mm (14.7 in) of precipitation and about 121 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Gorakhpur?
July is not one of Gorakhpur’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gorakhpur in July?
375 mm (14.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (375 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Gorakhpur in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gorakhpur get in July?
About 121 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gorakhpur in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
