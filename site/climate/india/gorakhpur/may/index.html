How May compares in Gorakhpur

May is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Gorakhpur. Compared with April: 1.7 °C warmer, 39 mm more precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. June is 0.2 °C cooler than May on average.

Gorakhpur in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gorakhpur in May?
In May, Gorakhpur averages a day high of 37.4 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 25.6 °C (78 °F), with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Gorakhpur?
May is not one of Gorakhpur’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gorakhpur in May?
47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (371 mm), the driest November (4 mm).
Does it snow in Gorakhpur in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gorakhpur get in May?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gorakhpur in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
