Haldia at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Haldia is 193rd warmest (26.3 °C year average), 491st sunniest (about 2,120 hours of sunshine a year) and 59th wettest (1,801 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Uluberiya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Haldia is 0.5 °C cooler in May and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 68 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.9 °C), averages 10.3 °C more than its coolest, January (19.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.7 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 39 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Haldia, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Haldia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 39 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
