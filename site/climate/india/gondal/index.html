Gondal at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Gondal is 171st warmest (26.5 °C year average), 15th sunniest (about 3,032 hours of sunshine a year) and 381st wettest (830 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jetpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Gondal is 0.3 °C warmer in May and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 29 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.6 °C), averages 11.4 °C more than its coolest, January (20.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.8 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 106 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gondal, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gondal has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 106 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
