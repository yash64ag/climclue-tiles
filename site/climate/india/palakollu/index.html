Palakollu at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Palakollu is 358th warmest (27.9 °C year average), 1919th sunniest (about 2,630 hours of sunshine a year) and 1468th wettest (1,222 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Narasapur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Palakollu is 0.3 °C warmer in May and about as warm in January, with 17 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.8 °C), averages 7.5 °C more than its coolest, January (24.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.6 °C (67 °F), in January. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Why Palakollu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Palakollu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (16.517°N, 81.730°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-674-2617), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
