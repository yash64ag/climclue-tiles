Why Yamuna Nagar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,010 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

What is the coldest month in Yamuna Nagar?
January, with an average of 12.8 °C (55 °F), day highs around 19.0 °C (66 °F) and night lows around 6.9 °C (44 °F).

What is the driest month in Yamuna Nagar?

What is the sunniest month in Yamuna Nagar?

Does it snow in Yamuna Nagar?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Yamuna Nagar?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March, October and November.
