Sawai Madhopur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Sawai Madhopur is 195th warmest (26.2 °C year average), 78th sunniest (about 2,822 hours of sunshine a year) and 438th wettest (713 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tonk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Sawai Madhopur is 0.2 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 97 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (35.0 °C), averages 19.4 °C more than its coolest, January (15.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.8 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 587 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sawai Madhopur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sawai Madhopur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 587 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
