Bhadrāvati at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Bhadrāvati is 443rd warmest (24.5 °C year average), 343rd sunniest (about 2,554 hours of sunshine a year) and 321st wettest (956 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shivamogga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Bhadrāvati is 0.2 °C warmer in April and 0.2 °C cooler in December, with 168 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (27.8 °C), averages 5.1 °C more than its coolest, December (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.0 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 130 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bhadrāvati, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bhadrāvati has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 130 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
