How March compares in Amritsar

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Amritsar. Compared with February: 5.1 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. April is 6.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Amritsar in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amritsar in March?
In March, Amritsar averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Amritsar?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Amritsar’s mild months (March, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amritsar in March?
42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (233 mm), the driest November (9 mm).
Does it snow in Amritsar in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Amritsar get in March?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amritsar in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
