Why Amritsar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,078 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Amritsar climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Amritsar?
June, with an average of 32.8 °C (91 °F), day highs around 38.6 °C (101 °F) and night lows around 26.1 °C (79 °F).
What is the coldest month in Amritsar?
January, with an average of 11.7 °C (53 °F), day highs around 17.7 °C (64 °F) and night lows around 5.9 °C (43 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Amritsar?
July, with 233 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 758 mm (29.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Amritsar?
November, with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Amritsar?
May, with about 296 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Amritsar?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Amritsar?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: March, October and November.
What type of climate does Amritsar have?
Humid subtropical, dry winter (Köppen Cwa). Hot, wet summers and mild, dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.5 × 11 km, that contains Amritsar’s GeoNames location (31.622°N, 74.875°E; ClimaClue area sq-523-2548), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,159,227 (GeoNames).
