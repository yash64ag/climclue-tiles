How August compares in Amritsar

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Amritsar. Compared with July: 0.9 °C cooler, 50 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. September is 1.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Amritsar in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amritsar in August?
In August, Amritsar averages a day high of 32.6 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 25.4 °C (78 °F), with 180 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Amritsar?
August is not one of Amritsar’s mild months; those are March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amritsar in August?
180 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (230 mm), the driest November (9 mm).
Does it snow in Amritsar in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Amritsar get in August?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amritsar in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
