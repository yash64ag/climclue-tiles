How April compares in Amritsar

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Amritsar. Compared with March: 6.3 °C warmer, 19 mm less precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. May is 5.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Amritsar in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amritsar in April?
In April, Amritsar averages a day high of 33.1 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 17.6 °C (64 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Amritsar?
April is not one of Amritsar’s mild months; those are March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amritsar in April?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (233 mm), the driest November (9 mm).
Does it snow in Amritsar in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Amritsar get in April?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amritsar in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
