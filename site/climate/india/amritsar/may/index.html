How May compares in Amritsar

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Amritsar. Compared with April: 5.0 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. June is 1.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Amritsar in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amritsar in May?
In May, Amritsar averages a day high of 38.2 °C (101 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 296 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Amritsar?
May is not one of Amritsar’s mild months; those are March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amritsar in May?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (230 mm), the driest November (9 mm).
Does it snow in Amritsar in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Amritsar get in May?
About 296 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amritsar in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
