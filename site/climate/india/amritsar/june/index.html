How June compares in Amritsar

June is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Amritsar. Compared with May: 1.6 °C warmer, 32 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. July is 2.3 °C cooler than June on average.

Amritsar in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amritsar in June?
In June, Amritsar averages a day high of 38.6 °C (101 °F) and a night low of 26.1 °C (79 °F), with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Amritsar?
June is not one of Amritsar’s mild months; those are March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amritsar in June?
59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (233 mm), the driest November (9 mm).
Does it snow in Amritsar in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Amritsar get in June?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amritsar in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
