How July compares in Amritsar

July is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Amritsar. Compared with June: 2.2 °C cooler, 170 mm more precipitation and 45 hours less sunshine. August is 0.9 °C cooler than July on average.

Amritsar in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amritsar in July?
In July, Amritsar averages a day high of 35.3 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 25.7 °C (78 °F), with 230 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Amritsar?
July is not one of Amritsar’s mild months; those are March, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amritsar in July?
230 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (230 mm), the driest November (9 mm).
Does it snow in Amritsar in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Amritsar get in July?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amritsar in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
