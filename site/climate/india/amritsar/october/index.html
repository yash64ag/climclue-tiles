How October compares in Amritsar

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Amritsar. Compared with September: 4.3 °C cooler, 66 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. November is 5.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Amritsar in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amritsar in October?
In October, Amritsar averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 267 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Amritsar?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Amritsar’s mild months (March, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amritsar in October?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (233 mm), the driest November (9 mm).
Does it snow in Amritsar in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Amritsar get in October?
About 267 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amritsar in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
