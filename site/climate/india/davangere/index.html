Davangere at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Davangere is 332nd warmest (25.4 °C year average), 323rd sunniest (about 2,590 hours of sunshine a year) and 466th wettest (641 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ranebennur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Davangere is 0.2 °C warmer in April and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 80 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.1 °C), averages 5.9 °C more than its coolest, December (23.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.2 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 170 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Davangere, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Davangere has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 170 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
