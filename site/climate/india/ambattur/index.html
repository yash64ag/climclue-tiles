Ambattur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Ambattur is 37th warmest (28.0 °C year average), 250th sunniest (about 2,672 hours of sunshine a year) and 163rd wettest (1,322 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Avadi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Ambattur is 0.1 °C cooler in May and about as warm in January, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.4 °C), averages 6.9 °C more than its coolest, January (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.8 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ambattur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ambattur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
