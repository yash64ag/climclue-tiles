Yelahanka at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Yelahanka is 501st warmest (23.3 °C year average), 411th sunniest (about 2,391 hours of sunshine a year) and 371st wettest (847 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bengaluru, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Yelahanka is 0.1 °C warmer in April and 0.3 °C cooler in December, with 48 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (27.1 °C), averages 6.8 °C more than its coolest, December (20.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.6 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 283 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yelahanka, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yelahanka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 283 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
