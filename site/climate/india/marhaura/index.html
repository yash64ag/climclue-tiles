Marhaura at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Marhaura is 2284th warmest (25.6 °C year average), 3399th sunniest (about 2,238 hours of sunshine a year) and 1792nd wettest (1,105 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chāpra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Marhaura is 0.2 °C cooler in May and about as warm in January, with 28 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.9 °C), averages 16.1 °C more than its coolest, January (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.8 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 552 km from the sea.

Why Marhaura has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 552 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Marhaura itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.973°N, 84.868°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-580-2648), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
