Sultan Pur Majra at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Sultan Pur Majra is 399th warmest (24.9 °C year average), 207th sunniest (about 2,689 hours of sunshine a year) and 492nd wettest (595 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bhālswa Jahangirpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Sultan Pur Majra is 0.1 °C warmer in June and about as warm in January, with 14 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (33.5 °C), averages 20.0 °C more than its coolest, January (13.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.5 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 858 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Sultan Pur Majra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
