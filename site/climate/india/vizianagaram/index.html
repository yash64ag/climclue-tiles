Vizianagaram at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Vizianagaram is 90th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 348th sunniest (about 2,545 hours of sunshine a year) and 195th wettest (1,210 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rasapūdipalem, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Vizianagaram is 1.1 °C warmer in May and 1.0 °C cooler in December, with 20 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.3 °C), averages 8.9 °C more than its coolest, December (22.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.6 °C (64 °F), in December. The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Vizianagaram, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Vizianagaram has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
