Vizianagaram at a glance

Among the 3743 towns ClimaClue covers in India, Vizianagaram is 759th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 2282nd sunniest (about 2,545 hours of sunshine a year) and 1504th wettest (1,207 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nellimarla, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Vizianagaram is 0.1 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 48 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.4 °C), averages 8.9 °C more than its coolest, December (22.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.0 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Why Vizianagaram has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
