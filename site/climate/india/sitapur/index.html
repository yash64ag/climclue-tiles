Sītāpur at a glance

Among the 526 towns of 100,000+ people covered in India, Sītāpur is 413th warmest (24.8 °C year average), 328th sunniest (about 2,586 hours of sunshine a year) and 260th wettest (1,064 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lakhīmpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Sītāpur is 0.4 °C warmer in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 224 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (32.3 °C), averages 17.9 °C more than its coolest, January (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.6 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 944 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sītāpur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sītāpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 944 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
