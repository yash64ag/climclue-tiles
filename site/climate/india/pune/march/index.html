How March compares in Pune

March is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Pune. Compared with February: 3.2 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. April is 2.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Pune in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pune in March?
In March, Pune averages a day high of 36.2 °C (97 °F) and a night low of 16.6 °C (62 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 291 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Pune?
March is not one of Pune’s mild months; those are January, February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pune in March?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (312 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Pune in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pune get in March?
About 291 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pune in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
