How September compares in Pune

September is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Pune. Compared with August: 0.3 °C warmer, 55 mm less precipitation and 69 hours more sunshine. October is 0.5 °C warmer than September on average.

Pune in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pune in September?
In September, Pune averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Pune?
September is not one of Pune’s mild months; those are January, February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pune in September?
149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (312 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Pune in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pune get in September?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pune in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
