Pune weather in October

Average October weather in Pune, India: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Pune averages 31.5 °C (89 °F) days and 18.1 °C (65 °F) nights, with 87 mm (3.4 in) of rain over the month and about 243 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Pune

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Pune. Compared with September: 0.5 °C warmer, 62 mm less precipitation and 71 hours more sunshine. November is 1.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Pune in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pune in October?
In October, Pune averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 18.1 °C (65 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Pune?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Pune’s mild months (January, February, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pune in October?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (312 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Pune in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pune get in October?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pune in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
