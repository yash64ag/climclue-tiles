How May compares in Pune

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Pune. Compared with April: 0.1 °C cooler, 19 mm more precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. June is 2.2 °C cooler than May on average.

Pune in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pune in May?
In May, Pune averages a day high of 35.1 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 27 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 306 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Pune?
May is not one of Pune’s mild months; those are January, February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pune in May?
27 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (317 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Pune in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pune get in May?
About 306 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pune in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
