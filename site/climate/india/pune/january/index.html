How January compares in Pune

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Pune. Compared with December: 0.2 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. February is 1.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Pune in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pune in January?
In January, Pune averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 287 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Pune?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Pune’s mild months (January, February, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pune in January?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (312 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Pune in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pune get in January?
About 287 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pune in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
