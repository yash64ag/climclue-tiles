How June compares in Pristina

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Pristina. Compared with May: 3.7 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Pristina in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pristina in June?
In June, Pristina averages a day high of 24.7 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Pristina?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Pristina’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pristina in June?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (70 mm), the driest January (42 mm).
Does it snow in Pristina in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pristina get in June?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pristina in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
