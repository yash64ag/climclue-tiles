How April compares in Pristina

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Pristina. Compared with March: 4.7 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. May is 5.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Pristina in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pristina in April?
In April, Pristina averages a day high of 15.3 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 4.7 °C (40 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Pristina?
April is not one of Pristina’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pristina in April?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (70 mm), the driest January (42 mm).
Does it snow in Pristina in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.3 cm (2.5 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Pristina get in April?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pristina in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
