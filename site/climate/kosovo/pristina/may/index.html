Pristina weather in May

Average May weather in Pristina, Kosovo: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Pristina averages 21.8 °C (71 °F) days and 10.3 °C (51 °F) nights, with 57 mm (2.3 in) of rain over the month and about 210 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Pristina

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Pristina. Compared with April: 5.1 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 51 hours more sunshine. June is 3.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Pristina in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pristina in May?
In May, Pristina averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 10.3 °C (51 °F), with 57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Pristina?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Pristina’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pristina in May?
57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (71 mm), the driest January (43 mm).
Does it snow in Pristina in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pristina get in May?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pristina in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
