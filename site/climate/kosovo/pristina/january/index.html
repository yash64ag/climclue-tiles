How January compares in Pristina

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Pristina. Compared with December: 1.0 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. February is 1.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Pristina in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pristina in January?
In January, Pristina averages a day high of 4.4 °C (40 °F) and a night low of -3.5 °C (26 °F), with 42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 66 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Pristina?
January is not one of Pristina’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pristina in January?
42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is November (70 mm), the driest January (42 mm).
Does it snow in Pristina in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 27 cm (10.6 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 8.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Pristina get in January?
About 66 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pristina in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
