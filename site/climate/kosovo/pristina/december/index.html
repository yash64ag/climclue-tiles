How December compares in Pristina

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Pristina. Compared with November: 4.3 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. January is 1.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Pristina in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pristina in December?
In December, Pristina averages a day high of 4.8 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -2.2 °C (28 °F), with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 63 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Pristina?
December is not one of Pristina’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pristina in December?
59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (70 mm), the driest January (42 mm).
Does it snow in Pristina in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 33 cm (12.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 6.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Pristina get in December?
About 63 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pristina in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
