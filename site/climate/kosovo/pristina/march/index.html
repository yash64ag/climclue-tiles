How March compares in Pristina

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Pristina. Compared with February: 4.2 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. April is 4.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Pristina in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pristina in March?
In March, Pristina averages a day high of 11.3 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 1.4 °C (35 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 128 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Pristina?
March is not one of Pristina’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pristina in March?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (71 mm), the driest January (43 mm).
Does it snow in Pristina in March?
Yes, on average: about 4.0 cm (1.6 in) of fresh snow in March (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Pristina get in March?
About 128 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pristina in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
