How September compares in Pristina

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Pristina. Compared with August: 4.8 °C cooler, 10 mm more precipitation and 65 hours less sunshine. October is 5.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Pristina in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pristina in September?
In September, Pristina averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 10.8 °C (51 °F), with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Pristina?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Pristina’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pristina in September?
59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (71 mm), the driest January (43 mm).
Does it snow in Pristina in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pristina get in September?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pristina in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
