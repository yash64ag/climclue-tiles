How October compares in Pristina

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Pristina. Compared with September: 5.3 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 48 hours less sunshine. November is 6.2 °C cooler than October on average.

Pristina in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pristina in October?
In October, Pristina averages a day high of 16.7 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 7.0 °C (45 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Pristina?
October is not one of Pristina’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pristina in October?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (70 mm), the driest January (42 mm).
Does it snow in Pristina in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.3 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Pristina get in October?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pristina in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
