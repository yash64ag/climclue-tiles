How June compares in Asunción

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Asunción. Compared with May: 1.7 °C cooler, 43 mm less precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. July is 0.5 °C cooler than June on average.

Asunción in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Asunción in June?
In June, Asunción averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 14.5 °C (58 °F), with 65 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Asunción?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Asunción’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Asunción in June?
65 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (182 mm), the driest August (48 mm).
Does it snow in Asunción in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Asunción get in June?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Asunción in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
