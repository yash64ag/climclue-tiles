Asunción in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Asunción in April?
In April, Asunción averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.7 °C (67 °F), with 182 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Asunción?
April is not one of Asunción’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Asunción in April?
182 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is April (182 mm), the driest August (48 mm).
Does it snow in Asunción in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Asunción get in April?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Asunción in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
