Asunción at a glance

Among the 6 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Paraguay, Asunción is the warmest (22.8 °C year average), the sunniest (about 2,764 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (1,426 mm a year). Compared with Lambaré, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Asunción is 0.1 °C warmer in January and 0.2 °C warmer in July, with 17 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (27.8 °C), averages 10.5 °C more than its coolest, July (17.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.5 °C (56 °F), in July. The area lies about 861 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Asunción, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Asunción has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 861 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
