Asunción in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Asunción in October?
In October, Asunción averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 19.2 °C (67 °F), with 128 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Asunción?
October is not one of Asunción’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Asunción in October?
128 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (182 mm), the driest August (48 mm).
Does it snow in Asunción in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Asunción get in October?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Asunción in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
