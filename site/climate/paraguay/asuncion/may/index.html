How May compares in Asunción

May is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Asunción. Compared with April: 3.7 °C cooler, 76 mm less precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. June is 1.7 °C cooler than May on average.

Asunción in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Asunción in May?
In May, Asunción averages a day high of 23.7 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Asunción?
May is not one of Asunción’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Asunción in May?
108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (184 mm), the driest August (49 mm).
Does it snow in Asunción in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Asunción get in May?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Asunción in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
