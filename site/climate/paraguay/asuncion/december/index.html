How December compares in Asunción

December is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Asunción. Compared with November: 1.7 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. January is 0.9 °C warmer than December on average.

Asunción in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Asunción in December?
In December, Asunción averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 21.2 °C (70 °F), with 181 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation and about 288 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Asunción?
December is not one of Asunción’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Asunción in December?
181 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (184 mm), the driest August (49 mm).
Does it snow in Asunción in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Asunción get in December?
About 288 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Asunción in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
