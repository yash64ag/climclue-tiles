How July compares in Asunción

Asunción in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Asunción in July?
In July, Asunción averages a day high of 21.9 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 196 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Asunción?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Asunción’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Asunción in July?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (182 mm), the driest August (48 mm).
Does it snow in Asunción in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Asunción get in July?
About 196 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Asunción in July?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
