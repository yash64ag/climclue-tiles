Fernando de la Mora at a glance

Among the 6 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Paraguay, Fernando de la Mora is the 2nd coolest (22.4 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 2,739 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (1,548 mm a year). Compared with Capiatá, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Fernando de la Mora is 0.1 °C cooler in January and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (27.3 °C), averages 10.3 °C more than its coolest, July (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.3 °C (56 °F), in July. The area lies about 847 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Fernando de la Mora, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Fernando de la Mora has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 847 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
