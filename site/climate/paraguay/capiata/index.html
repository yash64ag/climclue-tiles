Capiatá at a glance

Among the 6 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Paraguay, Capiatá is the 3rd warmest (22.5 °C year average), the 2nd least sunny (about 2,709 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (1,560 mm a year). Compared with San Lorenzo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Capiatá is 0.1 °C warmer in January and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (27.4 °C), averages 10.3 °C more than its coolest, July (17.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.4 °C (56 °F), in July. The area lies about 839 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Capiatá, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Capiatá

For mild weather, the best months to visit Capiatá are June, July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July, August and September.

Why Capiatá has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 839 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
