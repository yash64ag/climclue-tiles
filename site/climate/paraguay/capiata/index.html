Why Capiatá has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 839 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Capiatá climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Capiatá?
January, with an average of 27.4 °C (81 °F), day highs around 31.9 °C (89 °F) and night lows around 23.4 °C (74 °F).
What is the coldest month in Capiatá?
July, with an average of 17.1 °C (63 °F), day highs around 21.6 °C (71 °F) and night lows around 13.4 °C (56 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Capiatá?
April, with 195 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,560 mm (61.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Capiatá?
August, with 55 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Capiatá?
December, with about 282 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Capiatá?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Capiatá?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Capiatá have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Capiatá’s GeoNames location (25.355°S, 57.445°W; ClimaClue area sq-1093-1225), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 198,553 (GeoNames).
