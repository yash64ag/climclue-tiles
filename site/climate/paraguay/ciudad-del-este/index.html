Ciudad del Este at a glance

Among the 6 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Paraguay, Ciudad del Este is the coolest (22.0 °C year average), the least sunny (about 1,932 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (1,890 mm a year). Compared with Capiatá, the nearest covered town with different numbers (281 km away), Ciudad del Este is 0.9 °C cooler in January and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 330 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (26.4 °C), averages 9.7 °C more than its coolest, July (16.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.0 °C (55 °F), in July. The area lies about 613 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ciudad del Este, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ciudad del Este has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 613 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
