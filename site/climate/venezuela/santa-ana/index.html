Santa Ana, Táchira at a glance

Among the 213 towns ClimaClue covers in Venezuela, Santa Ana, Táchira is the 2nd least sunny (about 1,874 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with San Josecito, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Santa Ana, Táchira is 0.8 °C cooler in April and 0.6 °C cooler in July, with 49 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (24.3 °C), averages 1.0 °C more than its coolest, July (23.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.0 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 179 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Santa Ana, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Santa Ana, Táchira has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 179 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.0 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Santa Ana itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (7.643°N, 72.276°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-763-1077), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
