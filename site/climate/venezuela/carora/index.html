Why Carora has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 125 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Carora climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Carora?
August, with an average of 28.8 °C (84 °F), day highs around 34.9 °C (95 °F) and night lows around 22.8 °C (73 °F).
What is the coldest month in Carora?
January, with an average of 26.1 °C (79 °F), day highs around 32.6 °C (91 °F) and night lows around 20.5 °C (69 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Carora?
October, with 125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 619 mm (24.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Carora?
January, with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Carora?
January, with about 257 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Carora?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Carora?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Carora’s GeoNames location (10.173°N, 70.081°W; ClimaClue area sq-738-1099), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 121,741 (GeoNames).
