Carora at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Carora is the 5th driest (619 mm a year). Compared with Barquisimeto, the nearest covered town with different numbers (80 km away), Carora is 4.6 °C warmer in August and 2.2 °C warmer in January, with 146 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.8 °C), averages 2.6 °C more than its coolest, January (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.5 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 125 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Carora, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Carora has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 125 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
