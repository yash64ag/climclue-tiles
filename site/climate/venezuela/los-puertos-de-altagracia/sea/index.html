Los Puertos de Altagracia sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Zulia · Venezuela

The sea off Los Puertos de Altagracia averages 29.5 °C (85 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 26.3 °C (79 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Los Puertos de Altagracia sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Los Puertos de Altagracia?
In September, at about 29.5 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Los Puertos de Altagracia?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 26.3 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in Los Puertos de Altagracia in February?
About 26.3 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Los Puertos de Altagracia?
About 28.0 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Los Puertos de Altagracia nearby?
In September: Santa Rita (30.4 °C, 20 km), Cabimas (30.4 °C, 36 km) and Concepción (30.7 °C, 38 km).
