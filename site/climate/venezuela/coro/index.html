Coro at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Coro is the sunniest (about 3,081 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th driest (605 mm a year). Compared with Punta Cardón, the nearest covered town with different numbers (65 km away), Coro is 0.4 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 317 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.8 °C), averages 2.7 °C more than its coolest, January (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.4 °C (74 °F), in January. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Coro, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Coro has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
