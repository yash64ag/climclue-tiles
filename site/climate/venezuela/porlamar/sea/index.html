Porlamar sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Nueva Esparta · Venezuela

The sea off Porlamar averages 27.4 °C (81 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 24.4 °C (76 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Porlamar sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Porlamar?
In September, at about 27.4 °C (81 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Porlamar?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 24.4 °C (76 °F).
How warm is the sea in Porlamar in February?
About 24.4 °C (76 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Porlamar?
About 25.8 °C (79 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
