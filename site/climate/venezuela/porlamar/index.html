Porlamar at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Porlamar is the 3rd driest (542 mm a year). Compared with Cumaná, the nearest covered town with different numbers (64 km away), Porlamar is 0.5 °C cooler in September and 1.3 °C cooler in February, with 353 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (26.6 °C), averages 2.4 °C more than its coolest, February (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.7 °C (75 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Porlamar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Porlamar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
