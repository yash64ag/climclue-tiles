Judibana sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Falcón · Venezuela

The sea off Judibana averages 28.1 °C (83 °F) in October, its warmest month, and 24.5 °C (76 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Judibana sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Judibana?
In October, at about 28.1 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Judibana?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 24.5 °C (76 °F).
How warm is the sea in Judibana in February?
About 24.5 °C (76 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Judibana?
About 26.2 °C (79 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
