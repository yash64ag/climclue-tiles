La Concepción at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, La Concepción is 7th warmest (28.0 °C year average), 43rd sunniest (about 2,534 hours of sunshine a year) and 54th wettest (871 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Maracaibo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), La Concepción is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C cooler in December, with 198 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 1.4 °C more than its coolest, December (27.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.6 °C (74 °F), in January. The area lies about 40 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of La Concepción, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why La Concepción has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 40 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
