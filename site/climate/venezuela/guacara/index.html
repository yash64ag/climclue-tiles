Guacara at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Guacara is 51st warmest (24.5 °C year average), 13th sunniest (about 2,713 hours of sunshine a year) and 39th wettest (1,074 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Valencia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Guacara is 0.3 °C cooler in May and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 193 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (25.6 °C), averages 2.8 °C more than its coolest, January (22.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.3 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 25 km from Guacara measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 3.2 °C (5.8 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Guacara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
