Acarigua at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Acarigua is 21st warmest (26.6 °C year average), 49th sunniest (about 2,458 hours of sunshine a year) and 10th wettest (1,781 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Araure, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Acarigua is 0.6 °C warmer in March and 0.4 °C warmer in July, with 188 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (28.8 °C), averages 3.6 °C more than its coolest, July (25.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.8 °C (71 °F), in July. The area lies about 164 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Acarigua, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Acarigua has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 164 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
