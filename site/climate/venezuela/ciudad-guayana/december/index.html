How December compares in Ciudad Guayana

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Ciudad Guayana. Compared with November: 0.8 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. January is 0.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Ciudad Guayana in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ciudad Guayana in December?
In December, Ciudad Guayana averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ciudad Guayana?
No month in Ciudad Guayana is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ciudad Guayana in December?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (220 mm), the driest March (20 mm).
Does it snow in Ciudad Guayana in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ciudad Guayana get in December?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ciudad Guayana in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
