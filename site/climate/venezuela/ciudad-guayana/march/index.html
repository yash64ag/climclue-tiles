How March compares in Ciudad Guayana

March is the 5th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Ciudad Guayana. Compared with February: 0.9 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. April is 0.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Ciudad Guayana in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ciudad Guayana in March?
In March, Ciudad Guayana averages a day high of 32.8 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Ciudad Guayana?
No month in Ciudad Guayana is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ciudad Guayana in March?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (220 mm), the driest March (20 mm).
Does it snow in Ciudad Guayana in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ciudad Guayana get in March?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ciudad Guayana in March?
