How November compares in Ciudad Guayana

Ciudad Guayana in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ciudad Guayana in November?
In November, Ciudad Guayana averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Ciudad Guayana?
No month in Ciudad Guayana is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ciudad Guayana in November?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (220 mm), the driest March (20 mm).
Does it snow in Ciudad Guayana in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ciudad Guayana get in November?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ciudad Guayana in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
