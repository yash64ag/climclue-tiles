Ciudad Guayana at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Ciudad Guayana is 18th warmest (26.9 °C year average), 48th sunniest (about 2,506 hours of sunshine a year) and 33rd wettest (1,209 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Upata, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Ciudad Guayana is 1.9 °C warmer in April and 1.6 °C warmer in January, with 141 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.1 °C), averages 2.2 °C more than its coolest, January (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.4 °C (72 °F), in January. The area lies about 192 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ciudad Guayana, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ciudad Guayana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 192 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
