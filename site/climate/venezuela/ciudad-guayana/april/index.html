How April compares in Ciudad Guayana

April is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Ciudad Guayana. Compared with March: 0.8 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. May is 0.2 °C cooler than April on average.

Ciudad Guayana in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ciudad Guayana in April?
In April, Ciudad Guayana averages a day high of 33.5 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 23.8 °C (75 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ciudad Guayana?
No month in Ciudad Guayana is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ciudad Guayana in April?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (220 mm), the driest March (20 mm).
Does it snow in Ciudad Guayana in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ciudad Guayana get in April?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ciudad Guayana in April?
