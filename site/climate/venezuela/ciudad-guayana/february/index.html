How February compares in Ciudad Guayana

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Ciudad Guayana. Compared with January: 0.5 °C warmer, 16 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. March is 0.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Ciudad Guayana in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ciudad Guayana in February?
In February, Ciudad Guayana averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Ciudad Guayana?
No month in Ciudad Guayana is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ciudad Guayana in February?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (220 mm), the driest March (20 mm).
Does it snow in Ciudad Guayana in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ciudad Guayana get in February?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ciudad Guayana in February?
