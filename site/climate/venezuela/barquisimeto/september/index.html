How September compares in Barquisimeto

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Barquisimeto. Compared with August: 0.5 °C warmer, 14 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. October is 0.1 °C warmer than September on average.

Barquisimeto in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barquisimeto in September?
In September, Barquisimeto averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 20.5 °C (69 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Barquisimeto?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Barquisimeto’s mild months (January, February, March, May, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barquisimeto in September?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (108 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Barquisimeto in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barquisimeto get in September?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Barquisimeto in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
