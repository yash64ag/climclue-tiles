How May compares in Barquisimeto

May is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Barquisimeto. Compared with April: 0.3 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. June is 0.7 °C cooler than May on average.

Barquisimeto in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barquisimeto in May?
In May, Barquisimeto averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Barquisimeto?
May is not one of Barquisimeto’s mild months; those are January, February, June, July, August, September, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barquisimeto in May?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (96 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Barquisimeto in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barquisimeto get in May?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Barquisimeto in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
