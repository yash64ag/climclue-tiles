How November compares in Barquisimeto

November is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Barquisimeto. Compared with October: about as warm, 22 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. December is 0.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Barquisimeto in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barquisimeto in November?
In November, Barquisimeto averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Barquisimeto?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Barquisimeto’s mild months (January, February, March, May, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barquisimeto in November?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (108 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Barquisimeto in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barquisimeto get in November?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Barquisimeto in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
