How August compares in Barquisimeto

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Barquisimeto. Compared with July: 0.3 °C warmer, 14 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. September is 0.5 °C warmer than August on average.

Barquisimeto in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barquisimeto in August?
In August, Barquisimeto averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Barquisimeto?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Barquisimeto’s mild months (January, February, March, May, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barquisimeto in August?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (108 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Barquisimeto in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barquisimeto get in August?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Barquisimeto in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
