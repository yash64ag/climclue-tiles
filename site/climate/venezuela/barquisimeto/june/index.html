How June compares in Barquisimeto

June is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Barquisimeto. Compared with May: 0.7 °C cooler, 18 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. July is 0.3 °C cooler than June on average.

Barquisimeto in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barquisimeto in June?
In June, Barquisimeto averages a day high of 27.3 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Barquisimeto?
June is not one of Barquisimeto’s mild months; those are January, February, March, May, July, August, September, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barquisimeto in June?
108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (108 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Barquisimeto in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barquisimeto get in June?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Barquisimeto in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
