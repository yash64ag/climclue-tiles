Barquisimeto at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Barquisimeto is 50th warmest (24.5 °C year average), 30th sunniest (about 2,600 hours of sunshine a year) and 59th wettest (765 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cabudare, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Barquisimeto is 0.8 °C cooler in April and 0.5 °C cooler in July, with 61 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (25.1 °C), averages 1.3 °C more than its coolest, July (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.3 °C (67 °F), in January. The area lies about 143 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Barquisimeto, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Barquisimeto has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 143 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
