Anaco at a glance

Among the 213 towns ClimaClue covers in Venezuela, Anaco is 34th warmest (27.4 °C year average), 47th sunniest (about 2,706 hours of sunshine a year) and 192nd wettest (670 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cantaura, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Anaco is 0.4 °C warmer in April and 0.4 °C warmer in July, with 37 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.7 °C), averages 2.0 °C more than its coolest, July (26.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.2 °C (70 °F), in January. The area lies about 89 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Anaco, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Anaco has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 89 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Anaco itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (9.430°N, 64.464°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-745-1155), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
