Anaco at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Anaco is 12th warmest (27.5 °C year average), 15th sunniest (about 2,706 hours of sunshine a year) and 61st wettest (662 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with El Tigre, the nearest covered town with different numbers (64 km away), Anaco is 0.7 °C warmer in April and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 308 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (28.8 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, January (26.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.1 °C (72 °F), in January. The area lies about 89 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Anaco, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Anaco has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 89 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
