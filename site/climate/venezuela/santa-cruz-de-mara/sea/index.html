Santa Cruz de Mara sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Zulia · Venezuela

The sea off Santa Cruz de Mara averages 29.3 °C (85 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 26.2 °C (79 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Santa Cruz de Mara sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Santa Cruz de Mara?
In September, at about 29.3 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Santa Cruz de Mara?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 26.2 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in Santa Cruz de Mara in February?
About 26.2 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Santa Cruz de Mara?
About 27.9 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Santa Cruz de Mara nearby?
In September: Santa Rita (30.4 °C, 34 km), Concepción (30.7 °C, 42 km) and Cabimas (30.4 °C, 51 km).
