San Juan de los Morros at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, San Juan de los Morros is 43rd warmest (25.0 °C year average), 46th sunniest (about 2,522 hours of sunshine a year) and 30th wettest (1,245 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cagua, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), San Juan de los Morros is 0.9 °C warmer in April and 0.5 °C cooler in July, with 182 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (26.6 °C), averages 2.7 °C more than its coolest, July (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.8 °C (68 °F), in January. The area lies about 78 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of San Juan de los Morros, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why San Juan de los Morros has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 78 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
