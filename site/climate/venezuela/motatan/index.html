Motatán at a glance

Among the 213 towns ClimaClue covers in Venezuela, Motatán is 132nd warmest (25.4 °C year average), 144th sunniest (about 2,423 hours of sunshine a year) and 113th wettest (1,228 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Valera, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Motatán is 0.8 °C warmer in August and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 11 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.0 °C), averages 1.6 °C more than its coolest, January (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.1 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 66 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Motatán, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Motatán has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 66 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C. The 10 km area around Motatán, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 24.2 °C a year, 1.2 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Motatán itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (9.392°N, 70.591°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-746-1094), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
