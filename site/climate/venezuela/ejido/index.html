Ejido at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Ejido is the 2nd coolest (18.6 °C year average) and the 4th least sunny (about 2,228 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Mérida, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Ejido is 3.7 °C warmer in May and 3.6 °C warmer in January, with 179 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (19.4 °C), averages 1.6 °C more than its coolest, January (17.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.6 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 70 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 9 km from Ejido measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 2.4 °C (4.4 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Ejido has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 70 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 9.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
