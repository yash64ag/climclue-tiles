Altagracia de Orituco at a glance

Among the 213 towns ClimaClue covers in Venezuela, Altagracia de Orituco is 136th warmest (25.3 °C year average), 91st sunniest (about 2,597 hours of sunshine a year) and 69th wettest (1,505 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Caucagua, the nearest covered town with different numbers (47 km away), Altagracia de Orituco is 0.6 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 153 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (26.1 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, January (24.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.5 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 78 km from the sea.

Why Altagracia de Orituco has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 78 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
