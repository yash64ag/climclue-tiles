Pueblo Nuevo sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Falcón · Venezuela

The sea off Pueblo Nuevo averages 28.1 °C (83 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 25.5 °C (78 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Pueblo Nuevo sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Pueblo Nuevo?
In September, at about 28.1 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Pueblo Nuevo?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 25.5 °C (78 °F).
How warm is the sea in Pueblo Nuevo in February?
About 25.5 °C (78 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Pueblo Nuevo?
About 26.8 °C (80 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
