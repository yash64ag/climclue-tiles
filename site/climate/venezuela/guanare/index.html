Guanare at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Guanare is the 2nd wettest (2,455 mm a year). Compared with Barinas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (72 km away), Guanare is 1.8 °C warmer in March and 0.4 °C warmer in July, with 380 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (28.6 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, July (25.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.5 °C (71 °F), in July. The area lies about 157 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Guanare, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Guanare has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 157 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
