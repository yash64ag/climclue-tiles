Puerto La Cruz sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Anzoátegui · Venezuela

The sea off Puerto La Cruz averages 27.9 °C (82 °F) in October, its warmest month, and 24.2 °C (76 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Puerto La Cruz sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Puerto La Cruz?
In October, at about 27.9 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Puerto La Cruz?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 24.2 °C (76 °F).
How warm is the sea in Puerto La Cruz in February?
About 24.2 °C (76 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Puerto La Cruz?
About 26.0 °C (79 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
