How April compares in Valencia

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Valencia. Compared with March: 1.0 °C warmer, 79 mm more precipitation and 62 hours less sunshine. May is 0.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Valencia in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Valencia in April?
In April, Valencia averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 19.4 °C (67 °F), with 101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Valencia?
April is not one of Valencia’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Valencia in April?
101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (203 mm), the driest January (15 mm).
Does it snow in Valencia in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Valencia get in April?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Valencia in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
