How November compares in Valencia

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Valencia. Compared with October: 0.4 °C cooler, 55 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. December is 0.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Valencia in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Valencia in November?
In November, Valencia averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Valencia?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Valencia’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Valencia in November?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (193 mm), the driest January (12 mm).
Does it snow in Valencia in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Valencia get in November?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Valencia in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
