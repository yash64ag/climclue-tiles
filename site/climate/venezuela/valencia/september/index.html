How September compares in Valencia

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Valencia. Compared with August: 0.3 °C warmer, 23 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. October is about as warm as September on average.

Valencia in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Valencia in September?
In September, Valencia averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 170 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Valencia?
September is not one of Valencia’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Valencia in September?
170 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (193 mm), the driest January (12 mm).
Does it snow in Valencia in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Valencia get in September?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Valencia in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
