How February compares in Valencia

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Valencia. Compared with January: 0.5 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. March is 0.6 °C warmer than February on average.

Valencia in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Valencia in February?
In February, Valencia averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 16.6 °C (62 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Valencia?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Valencia’s mild months (January, February, March and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Valencia in February?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (203 mm), the driest January (15 mm).
Does it snow in Valencia in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Valencia get in February?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Valencia in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
