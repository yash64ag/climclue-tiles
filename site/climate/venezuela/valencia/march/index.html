How March compares in Valencia

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Valencia. Compared with February: 0.6 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. April is 0.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Valencia in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Valencia in March?
In March, Valencia averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 267 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Valencia?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Valencia’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Valencia in March?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (193 mm), the driest January (12 mm).
Does it snow in Valencia in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Valencia get in March?
About 267 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Valencia in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
