San Carlos del Zulia at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, San Carlos del Zulia is 19th warmest (26.9 °C year average), 59th sunniest (about 2,342 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (1,882 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with El Vigía, the nearest covered town with different numbers (52 km away), San Carlos del Zulia is 0.6 °C warmer in May and 1.1 °C warmer in January, with 223 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.4 °C), averages 1.3 °C more than its coolest, January (26.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.3 °C (74 °F), in January. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of San Carlos del Zulia, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why San Carlos del Zulia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
