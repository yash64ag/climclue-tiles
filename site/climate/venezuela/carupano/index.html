Carúpano at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Carúpano is 42nd warmest (25.1 °C year average), 19th sunniest (about 2,680 hours of sunshine a year) and 29th wettest (1,260 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Porlamar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (75 km away), Carúpano is 0.4 °C cooler in September and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 717 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (26.1 °C), averages 2.3 °C more than its coolest, January (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.0 °C (72 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Carúpano, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Carúpano has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
