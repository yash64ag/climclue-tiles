San Carlos at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, San Carlos is 16th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 29th sunniest (about 2,611 hours of sunshine a year) and 12th wettest (1,610 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tinaquillo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), San Carlos is 3.7 °C warmer in March and 2.0 °C warmer in July, with 84 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (29.6 °C), averages 4.0 °C more than its coolest, July (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.3 °C (72 °F), in July. The area lies about 114 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of San Carlos, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why San Carlos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 114 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
