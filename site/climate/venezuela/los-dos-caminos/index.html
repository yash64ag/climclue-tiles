Los Dos Caminos at a glance

Among the 213 towns ClimaClue covers in Venezuela, Los Dos Caminos is 194th warmest (22.3 °C year average), 158th sunniest (about 2,385 hours of sunshine a year) and 123rd wettest (1,166 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chacao, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Los Dos Caminos is 0.1 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 39 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (23.2 °C), averages 2.3 °C more than its coolest, January (20.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.6 °C (60 °F), in January. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Why Los Dos Caminos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
