How December compares in Maracaibo

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Maracaibo. Compared with November: 0.1 °C warmer, 59 mm less precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. January is 0.1 °C warmer than December on average.

Maracaibo in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maracaibo in December?
In December, Maracaibo averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Maracaibo?
No month in Maracaibo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maracaibo in December?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (124 mm), the driest February (4 mm).
Does it snow in Maracaibo in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maracaibo get in December?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maracaibo in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
