How February compares in Maracaibo

February is the 5th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Maracaibo. Compared with January: 0.4 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. March is 0.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Maracaibo in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maracaibo in February?
In February, Maracaibo averages a day high of 33.3 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Maracaibo?
No month in Maracaibo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maracaibo in February?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (124 mm), the driest February (4 mm).
Does it snow in Maracaibo in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maracaibo get in February?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maracaibo in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
