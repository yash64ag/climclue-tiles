Maracaibo at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Maracaibo is the 2nd warmest (28.5 °C year average). Compared with Los Puertos de Altagracia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Maracaibo is 0.3 °C cooler in July and about as warm in November, with 53 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.1 °C), averages 1.4 °C more than its coolest, November (27.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.9 °C (75 °F), in January. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Maracaibo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Maracaibo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
