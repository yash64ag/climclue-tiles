Why Maracaibo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Maracaibo climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Maracaibo?
July, with an average of 29.1 °C (84 °F), day highs around 32.4 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 25.5 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Maracaibo?
November, with an average of 27.8 °C (82 °F), day highs around 30.1 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 24.4 °C (76 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Maracaibo?
September, with 124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 673 mm (26.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Maracaibo?
February, with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Maracaibo?
January, with about 258 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Maracaibo?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Maracaibo?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Maracaibo have?
Hot semi-arid (Köppen BSh). Dry but not desert: hot, with a short and unreliable rainy season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Maracaibo’s GeoNames location (10.642°N, 71.611°W; ClimaClue area sq-733-1083), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,752,602 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
