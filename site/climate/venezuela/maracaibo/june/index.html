How June compares in Maracaibo

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Maracaibo. Compared with May: 0.1 °C warmer, 30 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. July is 0.2 °C warmer than June on average.

Maracaibo in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maracaibo in June?
In June, Maracaibo averages a day high of 33.4 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Maracaibo?
No month in Maracaibo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maracaibo in June?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (121 mm), the driest February (4 mm).
Does it snow in Maracaibo in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maracaibo get in June?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maracaibo in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
