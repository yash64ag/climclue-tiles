How April compares in Maracaibo

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Maracaibo. Compared with March: 0.1 °C warmer, 42 mm more precipitation and 57 hours less sunshine. May is 0.1 °C cooler than April on average.

Maracaibo in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maracaibo in April?
In April, Maracaibo averages a day high of 32.8 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Maracaibo?
No month in Maracaibo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maracaibo in April?
52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (124 mm), the driest February (4 mm).
Does it snow in Maracaibo in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maracaibo get in April?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maracaibo in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
