Why Turmero has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Turmero climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Turmero?
May, with an average of 24.8 °C (77 °F), day highs around 27.9 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 20.9 °C (70 °F).
What is the coldest month in Turmero?
January, with an average of 22.4 °C (72 °F), day highs around 26.7 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 18.5 °C (65 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Turmero?
August, with 163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,118 mm (44.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Turmero?
January, with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Turmero?
March, with about 250 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Turmero?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Turmero?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, April, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Turmero’s GeoNames location (10.229°N, 67.474°W; ClimaClue area sq-737-1125), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 344,700 (GeoNames).
