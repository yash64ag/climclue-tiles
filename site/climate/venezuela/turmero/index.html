Turmero at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Turmero is 55th warmest (23.8 °C year average), 41st sunniest (about 2,543 hours of sunshine a year) and 37th wettest (1,118 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cagua, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Turmero is 1.0 °C cooler in May and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 55 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (24.8 °C), averages 2.3 °C more than its coolest, January (22.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.5 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 19 km from Turmero measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 3.3 °C (5.9 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Turmero has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
