How December compares in Maturín

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Maturín. Compared with November: 0.7 °C cooler, 36 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. January is 0.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Maturín in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maturín in December?
In December, Maturín averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Maturín?
No month in Maturín is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maturín in December?
130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (245 mm), the driest March (28 mm).
Does it snow in Maturín in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maturín get in December?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maturín in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
