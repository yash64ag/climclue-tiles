How June compares in Maturín

June is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Maturín. Compared with May: 1.0 °C cooler, 109 mm more precipitation and 44 hours less sunshine. July is 0.2 °C cooler than June on average.

Maturín in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maturín in June?
In June, Maturín averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 245 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Maturín?
June is not one of Maturín’s mild months; those are January, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maturín in June?
245 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (249 mm), the driest March (29 mm).
Does it snow in Maturín in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maturín get in June?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maturín in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
