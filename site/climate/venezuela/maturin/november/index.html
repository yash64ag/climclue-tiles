How November compares in Maturín

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Maturín. Compared with October: 0.6 °C cooler, 10 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. December is 0.8 °C cooler than November on average.

Maturín in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maturín in November?
In November, Maturín averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 166 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Maturín?
November is not one of Maturín’s mild months; those are January, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maturín in November?
166 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (245 mm), the driest March (28 mm).
Does it snow in Maturín in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maturín get in November?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maturín in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
