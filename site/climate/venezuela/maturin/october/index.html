How October compares in Maturín

October is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Maturín. Compared with September: 0.2 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. November is 0.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Maturín in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maturín in October?
In October, Maturín averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 158 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Maturín?
October is not one of Maturín’s mild months; those are January, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maturín in October?
158 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (249 mm), the driest March (29 mm).
Does it snow in Maturín in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maturín get in October?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maturín in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
