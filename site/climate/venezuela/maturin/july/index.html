How July compares in Maturín

July is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Maturín. Compared with June: 0.2 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. August is 0.4 °C warmer than July on average.

Maturín in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maturín in July?
In July, Maturín averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 249 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Maturín?
July is not one of Maturín’s mild months; those are January, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maturín in July?
249 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (249 mm), the driest March (29 mm).
Does it snow in Maturín in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maturín get in July?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maturín in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
