Maturín weather in May

Average May weather in Maturín, Venezuela: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Maturín averages 31.8 °C (89 °F) days and 22.3 °C (72 °F) nights, with 134 mm (5.3 in) of rain over the month and about 215 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Maturín

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Maturín. Compared with April: about as warm, 73 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. June is 0.9 °C cooler than May on average.

Maturín in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maturín in May?
In May, Maturín averages a day high of 31.8 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Maturín?
May is not one of Maturín’s mild months; those are January, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maturín in May?
134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (245 mm), the driest March (28 mm).
Does it snow in Maturín in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maturín get in May?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maturín in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
