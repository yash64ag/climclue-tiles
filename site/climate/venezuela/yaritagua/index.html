Yaritagua at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Yaritagua is 35th warmest (25.4 °C year average), 24th sunniest (about 2,625 hours of sunshine a year) and 47th wettest (977 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Los Rastrojos, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Yaritagua is 0.4 °C warmer in April and 0.1 °C warmer in July, with 151 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (26.4 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, July (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.8 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 125 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yaritagua, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yaritagua has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 125 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
