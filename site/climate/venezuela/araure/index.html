Araure at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Araure is the 5th wettest (1,969 mm a year). Compared with Acarigua, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Araure is 0.6 °C cooler in March and 0.4 °C cooler in July, with 188 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (28.1 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, July (24.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.4 °C (71 °F), in July. The area lies about 172 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Araure, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Araure has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 172 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
