Valera at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Valera is 58th warmest (22.9 °C year average), 52nd sunniest (about 2,425 hours of sunshine a year) and 28th wettest (1,267 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Barinas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (88 km away), Valera is 2.4 °C cooler in May and 3.8 °C cooler in January, with 808 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (23.6 °C), averages 1.7 °C more than its coolest, January (21.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.6 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 55 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Valera, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Valera has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 55 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
