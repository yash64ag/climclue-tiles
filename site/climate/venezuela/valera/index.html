Why Valera has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 55 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Valera climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Valera?
May, with an average of 23.6 °C (74 °F), day highs around 26.3 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 19.6 °C (67 °F).
What is the coldest month in Valera?
January, with an average of 21.9 °C (71 °F), day highs around 25.7 °C (78 °F) and night lows around 17.6 °C (64 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Valera?
October, with 186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,267 mm (49.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Valera?
January, with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Valera?
January, with about 253 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Valera?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Valera?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, June, July and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Valera’s GeoNames location (9.318°N, 70.604°W; ClimaClue area sq-746-1093), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 244,708 (GeoNames).
