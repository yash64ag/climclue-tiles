Machiques at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Machiques is 6th warmest (28.0 °C year average), 50th sunniest (about 2,454 hours of sunshine a year) and 31st wettest (1,244 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with La Concepción, the nearest covered town with different numbers (100 km away), Machiques is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C cooler in November, with 373 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.8 °C), averages 1.6 °C more than its coolest, November (27.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.9 °C (73 °F), in January. The area lies about 67 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Machiques, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Machiques has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 67 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
