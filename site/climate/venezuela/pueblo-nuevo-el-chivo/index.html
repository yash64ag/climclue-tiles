Pueblo Nuevo El Chivo at a glance

Among the 213 towns ClimaClue covers in Venezuela, Pueblo Nuevo El Chivo is 72nd warmest (26.6 °C year average), 178th sunniest (about 2,330 hours of sunshine a year) and 43rd wettest (1,760 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with La Azulita, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Pueblo Nuevo El Chivo is 6.1 °C warmer in September and 6.3 °C warmer in January, with 949 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (27.3 °C), averages 1.5 °C more than its coolest, January (25.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.7 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pueblo Nuevo El Chivo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pueblo Nuevo El Chivo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
