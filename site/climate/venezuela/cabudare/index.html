Cabudare at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Cabudare is 38th warmest (25.2 °C year average), 25th sunniest (about 2,622 hours of sunshine a year) and 56th wettest (826 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Barquisimeto, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Cabudare is 0.8 °C warmer in April and 0.5 °C warmer in July, with 61 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (25.9 °C), averages 1.6 °C more than its coolest, July (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.4 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 134 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cabudare, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cabudare has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 134 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
