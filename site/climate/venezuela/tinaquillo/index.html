Tinaquillo at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Tinaquillo is 48th warmest (24.6 °C year average), 23rd sunniest (about 2,634 hours of sunshine a year) and 15th wettest (1,526 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Carlos, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Tinaquillo is 3.0 °C cooler in April and 2.0 °C cooler in July, with 84 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (25.9 °C), averages 2.4 °C more than its coolest, July (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.2 °C (68 °F), in July. The area lies about 74 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tinaquillo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tinaquillo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 74 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
