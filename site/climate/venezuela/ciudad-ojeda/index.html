Ciudad Ojeda at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Ciudad Ojeda is the 3rd warmest (28.2 °C year average). Compared with Cabimas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Ciudad Ojeda is about as warm in March and 0.2 °C warmer in November, with 38 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (28.6 °C), averages 1.1 °C more than its coolest, November (27.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 25.0 °C (77 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ciudad Ojeda, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ciudad Ojeda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
