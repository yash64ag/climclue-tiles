Lander at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Lander is 52nd warmest (24.3 °C year average), 37th sunniest (about 2,568 hours of sunshine a year) and 26th wettest (1,270 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Santa Teresa del Tuy, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Lander is 1.3 °C cooler in May and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 251 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (25.3 °C), averages 2.2 °C more than its coolest, January (23.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.4 °C (67 °F), in January. The area lies about 67 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lander, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lander has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 67 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
