How August compares in Barcelona

August is the 6th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Barcelona. Compared with July: 0.4 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. September is 0.5 °C warmer than August on average.

Barcelona in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barcelona in August?
In August, Barcelona averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 151 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Barcelona?
No month in Barcelona is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barcelona in August?
151 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (151 mm), the driest February (8 mm).
Does it snow in Barcelona in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barcelona get in August?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Barcelona in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
