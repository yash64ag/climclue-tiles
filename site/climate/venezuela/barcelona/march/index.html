How March compares in Barcelona

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Barcelona. Compared with February: 0.5 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. April is 0.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Barcelona in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barcelona in March?
In March, Barcelona averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 271 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Barcelona?
No month in Barcelona is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barcelona in March?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (151 mm), the driest February (8 mm).
Does it snow in Barcelona in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barcelona get in March?
About 271 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Barcelona in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
