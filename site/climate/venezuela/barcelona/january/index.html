Barcelona weather in January

Average January weather in Barcelona, Venezuela: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Barcelona averages 30.8 °C (87 °F) days and 19.9 °C (68 °F) nights, with 10 mm (0.4 in) of rain over the month and about 264 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Barcelona

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Barcelona. Compared with December: 0.5 °C cooler, 18 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. February is 0.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Barcelona in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barcelona in January?
In January, Barcelona averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 19.9 °C (68 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Barcelona?
No month in Barcelona is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barcelona in January?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (132 mm), the driest February (7 mm).
Does it snow in Barcelona in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barcelona get in January?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Barcelona in January?
About 24.7 °C (76 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is October (27.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Barcelona in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
