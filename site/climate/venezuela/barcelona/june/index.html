How June compares in Barcelona

June is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Barcelona. Compared with May: 0.5 °C cooler, 41 mm more precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. July is 0.6 °C cooler than June on average.

Barcelona in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barcelona in June?
In June, Barcelona averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Barcelona?
No month in Barcelona is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barcelona in June?
94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (132 mm), the driest February (7 mm).
Does it snow in Barcelona in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barcelona get in June?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Barcelona in June?
About 26.1 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is October (27.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Barcelona in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
