How October compares in Barcelona

October is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Barcelona. Compared with September: about as warm, 27 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. November is 0.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Barcelona in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barcelona in October?
In October, Barcelona averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 247 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Barcelona?
No month in Barcelona is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barcelona in October?
84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (151 mm), the driest February (8 mm).
Does it snow in Barcelona in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barcelona get in October?
About 247 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Barcelona in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
