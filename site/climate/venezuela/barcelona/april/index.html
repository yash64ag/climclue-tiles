How April compares in Barcelona

April is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Barcelona. Compared with March: 0.9 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 36 hours less sunshine. May is 0.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Barcelona in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barcelona in April?
In April, Barcelona averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Barcelona?
No month in Barcelona is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barcelona in April?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (132 mm), the driest February (7 mm).
Does it snow in Barcelona in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barcelona get in April?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Barcelona in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
