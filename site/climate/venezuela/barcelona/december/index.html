How December compares in Barcelona

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Barcelona. Compared with November: 0.9 °C cooler, 30 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. January is 0.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Barcelona in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barcelona in December?
In December, Barcelona averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 20.7 °C (69 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Barcelona?
No month in Barcelona is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barcelona in December?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (132 mm), the driest February (7 mm).
Does it snow in Barcelona in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barcelona get in December?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Barcelona in December?
About 25.9 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is October (27.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Barcelona in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
