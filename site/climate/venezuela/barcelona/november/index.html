How November compares in Barcelona

November is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Barcelona. Compared with October: 0.5 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. December is 0.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Barcelona in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Barcelona in November?
In November, Barcelona averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Barcelona?
No month in Barcelona is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Barcelona in November?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (151 mm), the driest February (8 mm).
Does it snow in Barcelona in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Barcelona get in November?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Barcelona in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
