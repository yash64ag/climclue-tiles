San Cristóbal at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, San Cristóbal is the 2nd least sunny (about 1,918 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Táriba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), San Cristóbal is 0.7 °C warmer in October and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 119 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (24.2 °C), averages 0.9 °C more than its coolest, January (23.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.7 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 169 km from the sea.

Why San Cristóbal has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 169 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.9 °C. The 10 km area around San Cristóbal, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 22.8 °C a year, 1.0 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers
