Puerto Cabello at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Puerto Cabello is 41st warmest (25.1 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 2,827 hours of sunshine a year) and 51st wettest (934 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Guacara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Puerto Cabello is 0.9 °C warmer in September and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 140 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (26.4 °C), averages 2.7 °C more than its coolest, January (23.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.3 °C (72 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Puerto Cabello, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Puerto Cabello has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
