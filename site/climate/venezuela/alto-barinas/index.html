Alto Barinas at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Alto Barinas is 26th warmest (26.2 °C year average), 58th sunniest (about 2,348 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (1,932 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Barinas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Alto Barinas is 0.4 °C warmer in March and 0.4 °C warmer in July, with 143 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (27.3 °C), averages 2.0 °C more than its coolest, July (25.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.1 °C (70 °F), in January. The area lies about 133 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Alto Barinas, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Alto Barinas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 133 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
