Cumaná at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Cumaná is 23rd warmest (26.4 °C year average), 9th sunniest (about 2,803 hours of sunshine a year) and 53rd wettest (895 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Puerto La Cruz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Cumaná is 0.6 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 43 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.1 °C), averages 1.9 °C more than its coolest, January (25.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.0 °C (73 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cumaná, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cumaná has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
