Why Cumaná has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Cumaná climate: questions and answers

May, with an average of 27.1 °C (81 °F), day highs around 29.5 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 24.9 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Cumaná?
January, with an average of 25.3 °C (77 °F), day highs around 28.0 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 23.0 °C (73 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Cumaná?

What is the driest month in Cumaná?
March, with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Cumaná?
March, with about 256 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Cumaná?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Cumaná?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Cumaná’s GeoNames location (10.464°N, 64.179°W; ClimaClue area sq-735-1158), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 405,626 (GeoNames).
