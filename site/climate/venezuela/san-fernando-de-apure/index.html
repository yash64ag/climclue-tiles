San Fernando de Apure at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, San Fernando de Apure is 8th warmest (28.0 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 2,716 hours of sunshine a year) and 12th wettest (1,599 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Calabozo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (115 km away), San Fernando de Apure is 0.2 °C warmer in March and about as warm in July, with 288 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (30.5 °C), averages 4.3 °C more than its coolest, July (26.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.0 °C (73 °F), in July. The area lies about 302 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 24 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 119% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why San Fernando de Apure has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 302 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
