How April compares in Caracas

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Caracas. Compared with March: 0.8 °C warmer, 48 mm more precipitation and 46 hours less sunshine. May is 0.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Caracas in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Caracas in April?
In April, Caracas averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Caracas?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Caracas’s mild months (January, February, March, April and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Caracas in April?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (175 mm), the driest March (22 mm).
Does it snow in Caracas in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Caracas get in April?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Caracas in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
