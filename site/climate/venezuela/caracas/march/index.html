How March compares in Caracas

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Caracas. Compared with February: 0.8 °C warmer, 18 mm less precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. April is 0.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Caracas in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Caracas in March?
In March, Caracas averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 16.1 °C (61 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Caracas?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Caracas’s mild months (January, February, March, April and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Caracas in March?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (141 mm), the driest March (19 mm).
Does it snow in Caracas in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Caracas get in March?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Caracas in March?
About 24.7 °C (76 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is October (27.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Caracas in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
