How February compares in Caracas

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Caracas. Compared with January: 0.4 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. March is 0.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Caracas in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Caracas in February?
In February, Caracas averages a day high of 25.4 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Caracas?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Caracas’s mild months (January, February, March, April and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Caracas in February?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (175 mm), the driest March (22 mm).
Does it snow in Caracas in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Caracas get in February?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Caracas in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
