How October compares in Caracas

October is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Caracas. Compared with September: 0.1 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. November is 0.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Caracas in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Caracas in October?
In October, Caracas averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 18.5 °C (65 °F), with 150 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Caracas?
October is not one of Caracas’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Caracas in October?
150 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (175 mm), the driest March (22 mm).
Does it snow in Caracas in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Caracas get in October?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Caracas in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
