How December compares in Caracas

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Caracas. Compared with November: 0.8 °C cooler, 69 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. January is 0.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Caracas in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Caracas in December?
In December, Caracas averages a day high of 24.5 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 199 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Caracas?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Caracas’s mild months (January, February, March, April and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Caracas in December?
84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (175 mm), the driest March (22 mm).
Does it snow in Caracas in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Caracas get in December?
About 199 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Caracas in December?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
