How June compares in Caracas

June is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Caracas. Compared with May: 0.5 °C cooler, 27 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. July is 0.4 °C cooler than June on average.

Caracas in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Caracas in June?
In June, Caracas averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 19.6 °C (67 °F), with 130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Caracas?
June is not one of Caracas’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Caracas in June?
130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (141 mm), the driest March (19 mm).
Does it snow in Caracas in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Caracas get in June?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Caracas in June?
About 26.2 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is October (27.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Caracas in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
