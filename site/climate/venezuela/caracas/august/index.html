How August compares in Caracas

August is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Caracas. Compared with July: 0.1 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. September is 0.3 °C warmer than August on average.

Caracas in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Caracas in August?
In August, Caracas averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 18.3 °C (65 °F), with 166 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Caracas?
August is not one of Caracas’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Caracas in August?
166 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (175 mm), the driest March (22 mm).
Does it snow in Caracas in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Caracas get in August?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Caracas in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
