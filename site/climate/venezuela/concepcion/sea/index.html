Concepción sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Zulia · Venezuela

The sea off Concepción averages 31.0 °C (88 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 27.9 °C (82 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Concepción sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Concepción?
In August, at about 31.0 °C (88 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Concepción?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 27.9 °C (82 °F).
How warm is the sea in Concepción in January?
About 27.9 °C (82 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Concepción?
About 29.7 °C (85 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
