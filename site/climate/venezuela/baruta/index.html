Baruta at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Baruta is 60th warmest (21.6 °C year average), 54th sunniest (about 2,385 hours of sunshine a year) and 21st wettest (1,347 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Los Teques, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Baruta is 1.2 °C warmer in May and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 96 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (22.4 °C), averages 2.2 °C more than its coolest, January (20.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.8 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Baruta, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Baruta has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
