Barinas at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Barinas is the 4th wettest (2,075 mm a year). Compared with Alto Barinas, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Barinas is 0.4 °C cooler in March and 0.4 °C cooler in July, with 143 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (26.9 °C), averages 2.0 °C more than its coolest, July (24.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.9 °C (70 °F), in January. The area lies about 126 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Barinas, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Barinas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 126 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
