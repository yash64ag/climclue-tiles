Punto Fijo at a glance

Among the 67 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Venezuela, Punto Fijo is the 2nd sunniest (about 2,945 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (319 mm a year). Compared with Punta Cardón, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Punto Fijo is 0.4 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 32 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.8 °C), averages 2.5 °C more than its coolest, January (26.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.0 °C (75 °F), in January. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Punto Fijo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Punto Fijo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
