Saint Helier sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · St Helier · Jersey

The sea off Saint Helier averages 17.2 °C (63 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 8.7 °C (48 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Saint Helier sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Saint Helier?
In August, at about 17.2 °C (63 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Saint Helier?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 8.7 °C (48 °F).
How warm is the sea in Saint Helier in February?
About 8.7 °C (48 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Saint Helier?
About 12.7 °C (55 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Saint Helier nearby?
In August: Saint-Malo (18.0 °C, 61 km), Hérouville-Saint-Clair (18.0 °C, 131 km) and Le Havre (18.2 °C, 164 km).
