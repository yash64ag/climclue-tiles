Debrecen at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Hungary, Debrecen is the 2nd warmest (11.1 °C year average). Compared with Nyíregyháza, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Debrecen is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.3 °C), averages 23.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 602 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Debrecen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 602 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
