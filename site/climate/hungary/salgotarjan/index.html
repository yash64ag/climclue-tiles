Salgótarján at a glance

Among the 89 towns ClimaClue covers in Hungary, Salgótarján is the coolest (9.5 °C year average) and the 3rd least sunny (about 1,699 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Gyöngyös, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Salgótarján is 1.5 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 60 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.2 °C), averages 21.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 516 km from the sea.

Why Salgótarján has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Salgótarján itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.099°N, 19.803°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-359-1998), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
