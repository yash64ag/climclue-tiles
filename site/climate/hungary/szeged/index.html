Szeged at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Hungary, Szeged is the warmest (11.6 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 1,986 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (549 mm a year). Compared with Kecskemét, the nearest covered town with different numbers (81 km away), Szeged is 0.8 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 19 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.6 °C), averages 22.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 418 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 107% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Szeged has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 418 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
