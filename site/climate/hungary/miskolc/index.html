Miskolc at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Hungary, Miskolc is the coolest (10.3 °C year average), the 2nd least sunny (about 1,781 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (625 mm a year). Compared with Nyíregyháza, the nearest covered town with different numbers (72 km away), Miskolc is 1.0 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 57 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.4 °C), averages 23.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 578 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Miskolc, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Miskolc has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 578 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
