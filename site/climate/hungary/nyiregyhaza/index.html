Nyíregyháza at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Hungary, Nyíregyháza is the sunniest (about 1,991 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (568 mm a year). Compared with Debrecen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Nyíregyháza is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.4 °C), averages 23.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 633 km from the sea (deep inland).
