Székesfehérvár at a glance

Among the 89 towns ClimaClue covers in Hungary, Székesfehérvár is 59th warmest (10.9 °C year average), 61st sunniest (about 1,845 hours of sunshine a year) and 82nd wettest (540 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Várpalota, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Székesfehérvár is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 74 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.7 °C), averages 22.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 373 km from the sea.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Székesfehérvár itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (47.190°N, 18.410°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-368-1984), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
