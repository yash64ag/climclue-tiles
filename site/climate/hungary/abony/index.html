Abony at a glance

Among the 89 towns ClimaClue covers in Hungary, Abony is 27th warmest (11.2 °C year average), 50th sunniest (about 1,893 hours of sunshine a year) and 74th wettest (548 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Szolnok, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Abony is 0.2 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.3 °C), averages 22.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 480 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Abony, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Abony has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 480 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Abony itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (47.190°N, 20.005°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-368-2000), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
