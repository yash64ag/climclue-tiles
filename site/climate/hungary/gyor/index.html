Győr at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Hungary, Győr is the 3rd warmest (11.1 °C year average) and the least sunny (about 1,567 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Székesfehérvár, the nearest covered town with different numbers (80 km away), Győr is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 33 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.8 °C), averages 21.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 362 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Győr, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Győr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 362 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
