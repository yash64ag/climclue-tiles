Kecskemét at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Hungary, Kecskemét is 6th warmest (11.0 °C year average), 5th sunniest (about 1,916 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (568 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Szeged, the nearest covered town with different numbers (81 km away), Kecskemét is 0.8 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 19 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.9 °C), averages 22.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 443 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kecskemét, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kecskemét has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 443 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
