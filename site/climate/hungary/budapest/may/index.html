How May compares in Budapest

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Budapest. Compared with April: 5.1 °C warmer, 24 mm more precipitation and 50 hours more sunshine. June is 2.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Budapest in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Budapest in May?
In May, Budapest averages a day high of 21.9 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 11.3 °C (52 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Budapest?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Budapest’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Budapest in May?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Budapest in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Budapest get in May?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Budapest in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
