How June compares in Budapest

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Budapest. Compared with May: 2.9 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. July is 2.2 °C warmer than June on average.

Budapest in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Budapest in June?
In June, Budapest averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 247 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Budapest?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Budapest’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Budapest in June?
77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Budapest in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Budapest get in June?
About 247 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Budapest in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
