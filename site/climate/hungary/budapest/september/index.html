How September compares in Budapest

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Budapest. Compared with August: 4.8 °C cooler, 15 mm less precipitation and 58 hours less sunshine. October is 5.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Budapest in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Budapest in September?
In September, Budapest averages a day high of 22.3 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Budapest?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Budapest’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Budapest in September?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Budapest in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Budapest get in September?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Budapest in September?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
