How November compares in Budapest

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Budapest. Compared with October: 6.0 °C cooler, 15 mm more precipitation and 84 hours less sunshine. December is 4.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Budapest in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Budapest in November?
In November, Budapest averages a day high of 8.5 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 2.1 °C (36 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 65 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Budapest?
November is not one of Budapest’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Budapest in November?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (73 mm), the driest February (32 mm).
Does it snow in Budapest in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.3 cm (3.3 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Budapest get in November?
About 65 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Budapest in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
