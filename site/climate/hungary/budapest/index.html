Budapest at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Hungary, Budapest is the 2nd coolest (10.9 °C year average) and the 3rd wettest (588 mm a year). Compared with Székesfehérvár, the nearest covered town with different numbers (59 km away), Budapest is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 37 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.8 °C), averages 22.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 429 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 113% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Best time to visit Budapest

For mild weather, the best months to visit Budapest are May, June, July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May, June, July, August and September.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (21.8 °C), August (21.1 °C) and June (19.6 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): February (32 mm), March (36 mm) and January (37 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (273 h), August (250 h) and June (247 h).

Why Budapest has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 429 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
