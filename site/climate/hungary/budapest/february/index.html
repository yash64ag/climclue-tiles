How February compares in Budapest

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Budapest. Compared with January: 1.7 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. March is 4.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Budapest in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Budapest in February?
In February, Budapest averages a day high of 5.5 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -0.6 °C (31 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 82 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Budapest?
February is not one of Budapest’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Budapest in February?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (77 mm), the driest February (34 mm).
Does it snow in Budapest in February?
Yes, on average: about 14 cm (5.4 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Budapest get in February?
About 82 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Budapest in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
