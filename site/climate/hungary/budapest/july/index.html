How July compares in Budapest

July is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Budapest. Compared with June: 2.1 °C warmer, 19 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. August is 0.6 °C cooler than July on average.

Budapest in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Budapest in July?
In July, Budapest averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 273 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Budapest?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Budapest’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Budapest in July?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (73 mm), the driest February (32 mm).
Does it snow in Budapest in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Budapest get in July?
About 273 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Budapest in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
