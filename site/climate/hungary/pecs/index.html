Pécs at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Hungary, Pécs is the 3rd sunniest (about 1,982 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (702 mm a year). Compared with Székesfehérvár, the nearest covered town with different numbers (125 km away), Pécs is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 151 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.6 °C), averages 21.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 308 km from the sea.

Why Pécs has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 308 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
