Oroszlány weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Oroszlány, Hungary (Komárom-Esztergom), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Oroszlány is warmest in July, with 26.9 °C (80 °F) days and 15.2 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 2.1 °C (36 °F) days and -3.5 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 29 mm in February to 66 mm in August.

Oroszlány at a glance

Among the 89 towns ClimaClue covers in Hungary, Oroszlány is 82nd warmest (10.4 °C year average), 74th sunniest (about 1,793 hours of sunshine a year) and 68th wettest (556 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tatabánya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Oroszlány is 0.3 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 18 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.1 °C), averages 21.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 386 km from the sea.

Why Oroszlány has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Oroszlány itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (47.487°N, 18.312°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-365-1983), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
