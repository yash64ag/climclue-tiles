Plymouth at a glance

Its warmest month, August (26.4 °C), averages 2.6 °C more than its coolest, February (23.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.3 °C (69 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Plymouth, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Plymouth

For mild weather, the best months to visit Plymouth are January, February, March and April.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, March and April.
  • Warmest (average temperature): August (26.4 °C), September (26.4 °C) and July (26.1 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): March (48 mm), February (55 mm) and April (68 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): August (247 h), July (247 h) and March (246 h).

Climate only: these rules use 1981–2010 monthly averages and do not consider crowds, prices, events or holidays; any one year can differ.

Why Plymouth has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Plymouth climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Plymouth?
August, with an average of 26.4 °C (79 °F), day highs around 29.6 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 23.2 °C (74 °F).
What is the coldest month in Plymouth?
February, with an average of 23.8 °C (75 °F), day highs around 27.3 °C (81 °F) and night lows around 20.3 °C (69 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Plymouth?
October, with 174 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,240 mm (48.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Plymouth?
March, with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Plymouth?
August, with about 247 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Plymouth?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time to visit Plymouth?
For mild weather (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, March and April. Climate only, from 1981–2010 averages: crowds and prices are not considered.
How warm is the sea in Plymouth?
The nearby open sea averages 28.7 °C (84 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 26.1 °C (79 °F) in February. It is 20 °C (68 °F) or warmer in January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December. 1981–2010 averages (ERA5); beaches and shallow bays are often a little warmer in summer.
