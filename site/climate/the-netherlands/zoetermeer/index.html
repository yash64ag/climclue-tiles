Zoetermeer at a glance

Among the 25 towns of 100,000+ people covered in The Netherlands, Zoetermeer is 9th warmest (10.4 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 1,493 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (916 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Leiden, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Zoetermeer is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 27 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.6 °C), averages 14.0 °C more than its coolest, January (3.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 1.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 23 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 11 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 107% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Zoetermeer has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
