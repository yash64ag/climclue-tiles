Rotterdam at a glance

Among the 25 towns of 100,000+ people covered in The Netherlands, Rotterdam is 10th warmest (10.3 °C year average), 9th sunniest (about 1,463 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (914 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zoetermeer, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Rotterdam is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.8 °C), averages 14.2 °C more than its coolest, January (3.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 1.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 30 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 107% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

For mild weather, the best months to visit Rotterdam are June, July and August.

Why Rotterdam has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 30 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
