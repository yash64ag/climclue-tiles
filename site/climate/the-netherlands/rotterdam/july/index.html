Rotterdam weather in July

Average July weather in Rotterdam, The Netherlands: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Rotterdam averages 22.0 °C (72 °F) days and 13.6 °C (56 °F) nights, with 84 mm (3.3 in) of rain over the month and about 196 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Rotterdam

July is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Rotterdam. Compared with June: 2.3 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. August is 0.2 °C cooler than July on average.

Rotterdam in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rotterdam in July?
In July, Rotterdam averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 13.6 °C (56 °F), with 84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 196 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Rotterdam?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Rotterdam’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rotterdam in July?
84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (92 mm), the driest April (46 mm).
Does it snow in Rotterdam in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rotterdam get in July?
About 196 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rotterdam in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
