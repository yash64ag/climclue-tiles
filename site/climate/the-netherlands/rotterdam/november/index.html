How November compares in Rotterdam

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Rotterdam. Compared with October: 4.1 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. December is 3.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Rotterdam in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rotterdam in November?
In November, Rotterdam averages a day high of 9.7 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 4.1 °C (39 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 55 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Rotterdam?
November is not one of Rotterdam’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rotterdam in November?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (92 mm), the driest April (46 mm).
Does it snow in Rotterdam in November?
Yes, on average: about 0.9 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in November (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Rotterdam get in November?
About 55 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rotterdam in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
