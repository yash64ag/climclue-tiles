How June compares in Rotterdam

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Rotterdam. Compared with May: 2.5 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Rotterdam in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rotterdam in June?
In June, Rotterdam averages a day high of 19.8 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Rotterdam?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Rotterdam’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rotterdam in June?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (92 mm), the driest April (46 mm).
Does it snow in Rotterdam in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rotterdam get in June?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rotterdam in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
