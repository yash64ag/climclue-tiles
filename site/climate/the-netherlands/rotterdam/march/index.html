Rotterdam weather in March

Average March weather in Rotterdam, The Netherlands: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Rotterdam averages 9.8 °C (50 °F) days and 2.7 °C (37 °F) nights, with 69 mm (2.7 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 101 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Rotterdam

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Rotterdam. Compared with February: 2.7 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. April is 2.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Rotterdam in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rotterdam in March?
In March, Rotterdam averages a day high of 9.8 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 2.7 °C (37 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 101 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Rotterdam?
March is not one of Rotterdam’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rotterdam in March?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (92 mm), the driest April (46 mm).
Does it snow in Rotterdam in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.2 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Rotterdam get in March?
About 101 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rotterdam in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
