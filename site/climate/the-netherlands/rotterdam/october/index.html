How October compares in Rotterdam

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Rotterdam. Compared with September: 3.6 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. November is 4.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Rotterdam in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rotterdam in October?
In October, Rotterdam averages a day high of 14.2 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 8.7 °C (48 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 94 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Rotterdam?
October is not one of Rotterdam’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rotterdam in October?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is October (92 mm), the driest April (45 mm).
Does it snow in Rotterdam in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rotterdam get in October?
About 94 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rotterdam in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
