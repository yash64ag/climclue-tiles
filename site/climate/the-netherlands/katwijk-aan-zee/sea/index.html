Katwijk aan Zee sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · South Holland · The Netherlands

The sea off Katwijk aan Zee averages 18.5 °C (65 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 5.1 °C (41 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Katwijk aan Zee sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Katwijk aan Zee?
In August, at about 18.5 °C (65 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Katwijk aan Zee?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 5.1 °C (41 °F).
How warm is the sea in Katwijk aan Zee in February?
About 5.1 °C (41 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Katwijk aan Zee?
About 11.5 °C (53 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
