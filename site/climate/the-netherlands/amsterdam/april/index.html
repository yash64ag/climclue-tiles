How April compares in Amsterdam

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Amsterdam. Compared with March: 2.8 °C warmer, 25 mm less precipitation and 49 hours more sunshine. May is 3.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Amsterdam in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amsterdam in April?
In April, Amsterdam averages a day high of 11.9 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 5.3 °C (42 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Amsterdam?
April is not one of Amsterdam’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amsterdam in April?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is October (104 mm), the driest April (45 mm).
Does it snow in Amsterdam in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Amsterdam get in April?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amsterdam in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
