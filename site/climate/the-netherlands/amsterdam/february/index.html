How February compares in Amsterdam

February is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Amsterdam. Compared with January: about as warm, 21 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. March is 2.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Amsterdam in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amsterdam in February?
In February, Amsterdam averages a day high of 5.9 °C (43 °F) and a night low of 1.3 °C (34 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 71 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Amsterdam?
February is not one of Amsterdam’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amsterdam in February?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (104 mm), the driest April (45 mm).
Does it snow in Amsterdam in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.9 cm (1.9 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Amsterdam get in February?
About 71 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amsterdam in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
