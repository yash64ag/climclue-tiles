How September compares in Amsterdam

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Amsterdam. Compared with August: 2.8 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 57 hours less sunshine. October is 3.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Amsterdam in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amsterdam in September?
In September, Amsterdam averages a day high of 18.8 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 10.3 °C (51 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 131 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Amsterdam?
September is not one of Amsterdam’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amsterdam in September?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (103 mm), the driest April (45 mm).
Does it snow in Amsterdam in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Amsterdam get in September?
About 131 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amsterdam in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
