Amsterdam weather in June

Average June weather in Amsterdam, The Netherlands: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Amsterdam averages 19.3 °C (67 °F) days and 10.8 °C (51 °F) nights, with 71 mm (2.8 in) of rain over the month and about 200 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Amsterdam

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Amsterdam. Compared with May: 2.5 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Amsterdam in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amsterdam in June?
In June, Amsterdam averages a day high of 19.3 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 10.8 °C (51 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Amsterdam?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Amsterdam’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amsterdam in June?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (103 mm), the driest April (45 mm).
Does it snow in Amsterdam in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Amsterdam get in June?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amsterdam in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
