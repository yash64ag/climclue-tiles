How January compares in Amsterdam

January is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Amsterdam. Compared with December: 0.7 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. February is about as warm as January on average.

Amsterdam in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amsterdam in January?
In January, Amsterdam averages a day high of 5.5 °C (42 °F) and a night low of 1.5 °C (35 °F), with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 44 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Amsterdam?
January is not one of Amsterdam’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amsterdam in January?
81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (104 mm), the driest April (45 mm).
Does it snow in Amsterdam in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.7 cm (2.6 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Amsterdam get in January?
About 44 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amsterdam in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
