How March compares in Amsterdam

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Amsterdam. Compared with February: 2.4 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. April is 2.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Amsterdam in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amsterdam in March?
In March, Amsterdam averages a day high of 8.7 °C (48 °F) and a night low of 3.3 °C (38 °F), with 70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 108 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Amsterdam?
March is not one of Amsterdam’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amsterdam in March?
70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (104 mm), the driest April (45 mm).
Does it snow in Amsterdam in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.8 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Amsterdam get in March?
About 108 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amsterdam in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
