How July compares in Amsterdam

July is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Amsterdam. Compared with June: 2.3 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. August is 0.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Amsterdam in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amsterdam in July?
In July, Amsterdam averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 12.9 °C (55 °F), with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 196 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Amsterdam?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Amsterdam’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amsterdam in July?
86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (103 mm), the driest April (45 mm).
Does it snow in Amsterdam in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Amsterdam get in July?
About 196 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amsterdam in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
