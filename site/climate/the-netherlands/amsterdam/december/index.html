How December compares in Amsterdam

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Amsterdam. Compared with November: 3.1 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. January is 0.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Amsterdam in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Amsterdam in December?
In December, Amsterdam averages a day high of 6.1 °C (43 °F) and a night low of 2.4 °C (36 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 38 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Amsterdam?
December is not one of Amsterdam’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Amsterdam in December?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (104 mm), the driest April (45 mm).
Does it snow in Amsterdam in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.4 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Amsterdam get in December?
About 38 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Amsterdam in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
