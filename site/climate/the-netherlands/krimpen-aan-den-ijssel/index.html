Krimpen aan den IJssel at a glance

Among the 234 towns ClimaClue covers in The Netherlands, Krimpen aan den IJssel is 114th warmest (10.3 °C year average), 94th sunniest (about 1,458 hours of sunshine a year) and 67th wettest (912 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Capelle aan den IJssel, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Krimpen aan den IJssel is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.9 °C), averages 14.5 °C more than its coolest, January (3.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 0.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 41 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 12 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 107% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Krimpen aan den IJssel has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 41 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Krimpen aan den IJssel itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (51.917°N, 4.603°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-320-1846), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
