Why Zaanstad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Zaanstad climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Zaanstad?
July, with an average of 17.4 °C (63 °F), day highs around 20.1 °C (68 °F) and night lows around 14.4 °C (58 °F).
What is the coldest month in Zaanstad?
January, with an average of 3.5 °C (38 °F), day highs around 5.5 °C (42 °F) and night lows around 1.6 °C (35 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Zaanstad?
October, with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 939 mm (37.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Zaanstad?
April, with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Zaanstad?
May, with about 209 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Zaanstad?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 20 cm (7.8 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Zaanstad?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July and August.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 6.8 × 11 km, that contains Zaanstad’s GeoNames location (52.453°N, 4.814°E; ClimaClue area sq-315-1848), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 140,085 (GeoNames).
