Zwolle at a glance

Among the 25 towns of 100,000+ people covered in The Netherlands, Zwolle is the 4th coolest (9.9 °C year average). Compared with Apeldoorn, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Zwolle is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 29 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.6 °C), averages 14.9 °C more than its coolest, January (2.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 0.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 86 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 14 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 112% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Zwolle has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 86 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
