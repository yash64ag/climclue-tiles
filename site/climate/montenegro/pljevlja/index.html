Pljevlja at a glance

Among the 8 towns ClimaClue covers in Montenegro, Pljevlja is the coolest (9.6 °C year average), the least sunny (about 1,893 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (847 mm a year). Compared with Bijelo Polje, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Pljevlja is 1.0 °C cooler in July and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 254 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.3 °C), averages 20.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 132 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pljevlja, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pljevlja has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 132 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.0 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pljevlja itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (43.357°N, 19.358°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-406-1993), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
