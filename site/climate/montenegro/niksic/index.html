Nikšić at a glance

Among the 8 towns ClimaClue covers in Montenegro, Nikšić is the 3rd coolest (11.5 °C year average), the 3rd least sunny (about 2,109 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (2,276 mm a year). Compared with Cetinje, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Nikšić is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 1.8 °C cooler in January, with 993 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.5 °C), averages 19.8 °C more than its coolest, January (1.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 60 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nikšić, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nikšić has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 60 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nikšić itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (42.773°N, 18.944°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-412-1989), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
