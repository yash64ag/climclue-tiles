Rajin sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Rason · North Korea

The sea off Rajin averages 21.3 °C (70 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 2.1 °C (36 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August.

Rajin sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Rajin?
In August, at about 21.3 °C (70 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Rajin?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 2.1 °C (36 °F).
How warm is the sea in Rajin in February?
About 2.1 °C (36 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Rajin?
About 10.4 °C (51 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Rajin nearby?
In August: Chongjin (22.0 °C, 66 km), Ranam (22.0 °C, 78 km) and Kyŏngsŏng (22.0 °C, 93 km).
