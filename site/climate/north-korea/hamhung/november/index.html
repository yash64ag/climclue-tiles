Hamhŭng in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamhŭng in November?
In November, Hamhŭng averages a day high of 8.9 °C (48 °F) and a night low of 0.7 °C (33 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Hamhŭng?
November is not one of Hamhŭng’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamhŭng in November?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (209 mm), the driest January (13 mm).
Does it snow in Hamhŭng in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.8 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Hamhŭng get in November?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
