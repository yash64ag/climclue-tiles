How January compares in Hamhŭng

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Hamhŭng. Compared with December: 3.2 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. February is 2.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Hamhŭng in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamhŭng in January?
In January, Hamhŭng averages a day high of 1.0 °C (34 °F) and a night low of -9.4 °C (15 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Hamhŭng?
January is not one of Hamhŭng’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hamhŭng in January?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (211 mm), the driest January (13 mm).
Does it snow in Hamhŭng in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 12 cm (4.9 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Hamhŭng get in January?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Hamhŭng in January?
About 4.4 °C (40 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (23.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Hamhŭng in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
