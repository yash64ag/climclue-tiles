Hamhŭng sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · South Hamgyong · North Korea

The sea off Hamhŭng averages 23.8 °C (75 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 3.3 °C (38 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Hamhŭng sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Hamhŭng?
In August, at about 23.8 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Hamhŭng?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 3.3 °C (38 °F).
How warm is the sea in Hamhŭng in February?
About 3.3 °C (38 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Hamhŭng?
About 12.8 °C (55 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Hamhŭng nearby?
In August: Sil-li (24.7 °C, 183 km).
