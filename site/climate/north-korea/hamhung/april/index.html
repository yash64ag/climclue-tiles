Hamhŭng in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hamhŭng in April?
In April, Hamhŭng averages a day high of 14.1 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 4.7 °C (40 °F), with 50 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Hamhŭng?
April is not one of Hamhŭng’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
