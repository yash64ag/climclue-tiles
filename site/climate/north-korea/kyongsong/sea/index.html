Kyŏngsŏng sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · North Hamgyong · North Korea

The sea off Kyŏngsŏng averages 22.0 °C (72 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 3.4 °C (38 °F) in March. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September.

Kyŏngsŏng sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kyŏngsŏng?
In August, at about 22.0 °C (72 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kyŏngsŏng?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is March, at 3.4 °C (38 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kyŏngsŏng in March?
About 3.4 °C (38 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Kyŏngsŏng?
About 11.5 °C (53 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
