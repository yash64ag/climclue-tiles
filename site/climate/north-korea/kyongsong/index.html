Among the 97 towns ClimaClue covers in North Korea, Kyŏngsŏng is 66th warmest (9.0 °C year average), 87th sunniest (about 2,230 hours of sunshine a year) and 73rd wettest (714 mm a year), near the middle on each. The area lies about 8 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Kyŏngsŏng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
