Hyesan at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in North Korea, Hyesan is the coolest (3.9 °C year average) and the 3rd driest (719 mm a year). Compared with Changam-ch’on, the nearest covered town with different numbers (102 km away), Hyesan is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 5.8 °C cooler in January, with 112 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.9 °C), averages 35.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-15.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 134 km from the sea.

Why Hyesan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 134 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
