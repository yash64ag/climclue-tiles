Hyesan at a glance

Among the 97 towns ClimaClue covers in North Korea, Hyesan is the 4th coolest (5.3 °C year average). Compared with Hyesan-dong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Hyesan is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 5 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.3 °C), averages 35.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-13.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 134 km from the sea.

Why Hyesan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 134 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.2 °C. The 10 km area around Hyesan, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 3.9 °C a year, 1.5 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hyesan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.402°N, 128.178°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-425-3081), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
