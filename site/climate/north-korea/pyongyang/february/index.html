How February compares in Pyongyang

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Pyongyang. Compared with January: 3.7 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. March is 5.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Pyongyang in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pyongyang in February?
In February, Pyongyang averages a day high of 2.3 °C (36 °F) and a night low of -7.5 °C (19 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Pyongyang?
February is not one of Pyongyang’s mild months; those are May and June, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pyongyang in February?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (275 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Pyongyang in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.6 cm (3.8 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Pyongyang get in February?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pyongyang in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
