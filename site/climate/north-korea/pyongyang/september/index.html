How September compares in Pyongyang

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Pyongyang. Compared with August: 5.0 °C cooler, 101 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. October is 6.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Pyongyang in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pyongyang in September?
In September, Pyongyang averages a day high of 25.2 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 14.5 °C (58 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Pyongyang?
September is not one of Pyongyang’s mild months; those are May and June, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pyongyang in September?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (274 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Pyongyang in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pyongyang get in September?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pyongyang in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
