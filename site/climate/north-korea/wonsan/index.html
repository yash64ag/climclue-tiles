Wŏnsan at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in North Korea, Wŏnsan is the 4th least sunny (about 2,314 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (1,393 mm a year). Compared with Hŭngnam, the nearest covered town with different numbers (78 km away), Wŏnsan is 0.6 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 521 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.9 °C), averages 27.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Wŏnsan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
