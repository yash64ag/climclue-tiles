Why Hŭngnam has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Hŭngnam climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Hŭngnam?

What is the coldest month in Hŭngnam?
January, with an average of -3.8 °C (25 °F), day highs around -0.1 °C (32 °F) and night lows around -8.5 °C (17 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Hŭngnam?

What is the driest month in Hŭngnam?

What is the sunniest month in Hŭngnam?

Does it snow in Hŭngnam?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 119 cm (46.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 4 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Hŭngnam?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June.
