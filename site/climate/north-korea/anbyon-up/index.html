Among the 97 towns ClimaClue covers in North Korea, Anbyŏn-ŭp is 34th warmest (10.9 °C year average), 68th sunniest (about 2,304 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (1,296 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Anbyŏn-ŭp has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.1 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
