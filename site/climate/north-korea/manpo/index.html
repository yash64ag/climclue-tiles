Manp’o at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in North Korea, Manp’o is the 4th coolest (7.4 °C year average). Compared with Kanggye, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Manp’o is 0.8 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 35 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.4 °C), averages 36.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-12.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 196 km from the sea.

Why Manp’o has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 196 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 36.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
