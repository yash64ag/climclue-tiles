Kapsan-ŭp at a glance

Among the 97 towns ClimaClue covers in North Korea, Kapsan-ŭp is the 3rd coolest (5.0 °C year average). Why Kapsan-ŭp has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 98 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 34.0 °C. The 10 km area around Kapsan-ŭp, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 3.7 °C a year, 1.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
