Why Sinp’o has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Sinp’o climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Sinp’o?
August, with an average of 22.4 °C (72 °F), day highs around 23.8 °C (75 °F) and night lows around 20.8 °C (69 °F).
What is the coldest month in Sinp’o?
January, with an average of -3.4 °C (26 °F), day highs around -0.8 °C (31 °F) and night lows around -6.9 °C (20 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Sinp’o?
July, with 161 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 729 mm (28.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Sinp’o?
February, with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Sinp’o?
May, with about 244 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Sinp’o?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 124 cm (49.0 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 4 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Sinp’o?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June and September.
What type of climate does Sinp’o have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.5 × 11 km, that contains Sinp’o’s GeoNames location (40.040°N, 128.194°E; ClimaClue area sq-439-3081), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 152,759 (GeoNames).
