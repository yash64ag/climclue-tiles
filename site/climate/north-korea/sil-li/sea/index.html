Sil-li sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · South Pyongan · North Korea

The sea off Sil-li averages 24.7 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -0.5 °C (31 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Sil-li sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Sil-li?
In August, at about 24.7 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Sil-li?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at -0.5 °C (31 °F).
How warm is the sea in Sil-li in February?
About -0.5 °C (31 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Sil-li?
About 12.1 °C (54 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
