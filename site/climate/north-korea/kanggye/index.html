Why Kanggye has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 163 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Kanggye climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Kanggye?
July, with an average of 22.6 °C (73 °F), day highs around 25.9 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 18.3 °C (65 °F).
What is the coldest month in Kanggye?
January, with an average of -12.7 °C (9 °F), day highs around -6.5 °C (20 °F) and night lows around -22.2 °C (-8 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Kanggye?
July, with 234 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 885 mm (34.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Kanggye?
January, with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Kanggye?
May, with about 253 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Kanggye?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 171 cm (67.4 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in March; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Kanggye?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May and September.
What type of climate does Kanggye have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.4 × 11 km, that contains Kanggye’s GeoNames location (40.969°N, 126.585°E; ClimaClue area sq-430-3065), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 209,530 (GeoNames).
