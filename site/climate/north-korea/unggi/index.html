Unggi weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Unggi, North Korea (Rason), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Unggi is warmest in August, with 25.6 °C (78 °F) days and 17.2 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -4.4 °C (24 °F) days and -14.6 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (155 mm); January is driest (8 mm).

Unggi at a glance

Why Unggi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Unggi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (42.342°N, 130.411°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-416-3104), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
