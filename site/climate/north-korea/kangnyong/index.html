Kangnyŏng at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in North Korea, Kangnyŏng is the 5th warmest (11.4 °C year average). Compared with Haeju, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Kangnyŏng is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 1.4 °C warmer in January, with 45 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.6 °C), averages 26.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Kangnyŏng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
