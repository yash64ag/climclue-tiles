Kangnyŏng sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · South Hwanghae · North Korea

The sea off Kangnyŏng averages 24.6 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 3.4 °C (38 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Kangnyŏng sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kangnyŏng?
In August, at about 24.6 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kangnyŏng?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 3.4 °C (38 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kangnyŏng in February?
About 3.4 °C (38 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Kangnyŏng?
About 13.4 °C (56 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
