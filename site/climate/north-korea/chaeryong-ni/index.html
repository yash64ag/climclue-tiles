Chaeryŏng-ni at a glance

Among the 97 towns ClimaClue covers in North Korea, Chaeryŏng-ni is 16th warmest (11.4 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 2,457 hours of sunshine a year) and 65th wettest (812 mm a year), near the middle on each. The area lies about 17 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Chaeryŏng-ni has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
