Paech’ŏn-ŭp at a glance

Among the 97 towns ClimaClue covers in North Korea, Paech’ŏn-ŭp is 14th warmest (11.4 °C year average), 53rd sunniest (about 2,401 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (1,291 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yŏnan-ŭp, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Paech’ŏn-ŭp is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.7 °C), averages 28.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 34 km from the sea.

Why Paech’ŏn-ŭp has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 34 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
