Hwadae sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · North Hamgyong · North Korea

The sea off Hwadae averages 21.4 °C (71 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 4.0 °C (39 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August.

Hwadae sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Hwadae?
In August, at about 21.4 °C (71 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Hwadae?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 4.0 °C (39 °F).
How warm is the sea in Hwadae in February?
About 4.0 °C (39 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Hwadae?
About 11.6 °C (53 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Hwadae nearby?
In August: Ŏrang (22.0 °C, 69 km), Kyŏngsŏng (22.0 °C, 84 km) and Ranam (22.0 °C, 99 km).
