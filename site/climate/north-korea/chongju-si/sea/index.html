Chŏngju-si sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · P'yŏngan-bukto · North Korea

The sea off Chŏngju-si averages 24.7 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -0.5 °C (31 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Chŏngju-si sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Chŏngju-si?
In August, at about 24.7 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Chŏngju-si?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at -0.5 °C (31 °F).
How warm is the sea in Chŏngju-si in February?
About -0.5 °C (31 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Chŏngju-si?
About 12.1 °C (54 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
