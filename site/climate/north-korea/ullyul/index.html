Among the 97 towns ClimaClue covers in North Korea, Ŭllyul is 8th warmest (11.5 °C year average), 7th sunniest (about 2,463 hours of sunshine a year) and 68th wettest (786 mm a year), near the middle on each. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Ŭllyul has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
