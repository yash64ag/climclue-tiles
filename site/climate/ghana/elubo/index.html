Elubo at a glance

Among the 98 towns ClimaClue covers in Ghana, Elubo is the 3rd wettest (1,727 mm a year). Compared with Prestea, the nearest covered town with different numbers (71 km away), Elubo is 0.3 °C warmer in March and about as warm in August, with 53 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (27.1 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, August (24.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.5 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Elubo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Elubo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Elubo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (5.285°N, 2.767°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-787-1772), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
