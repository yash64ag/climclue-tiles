How August compares in Kumasi

August is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Kumasi. Compared with July: 0.1 °C cooler, 49 mm less precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. September is 0.4 °C warmer than August on average.

Kumasi in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kumasi in August?
In August, Kumasi averages a day high of 27.8 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 86 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Kumasi?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Kumasi’s mild months (August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kumasi in August?
86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (206 mm), the driest January (17 mm).
Does it snow in Kumasi in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kumasi get in August?
About 86 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kumasi in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
