How November compares in Kumasi

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kumasi. Compared with October: 0.5 °C warmer, 107 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. December is 0.2 °C warmer than November on average.

Kumasi in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kumasi in November?
In November, Kumasi averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kumasi?
November is not one of Kumasi’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kumasi in November?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (206 mm), the driest January (17 mm).
Does it snow in Kumasi in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kumasi get in November?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kumasi in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
