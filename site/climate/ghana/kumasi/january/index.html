How January compares in Kumasi

January is the 4th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kumasi. Compared with December: 0.9 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. February is 1.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Kumasi in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kumasi in January?
In January, Kumasi averages a day high of 32.7 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 21.0 °C (70 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kumasi?
January is not one of Kumasi’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kumasi in January?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (209 mm), the driest January (17 mm).
Does it snow in Kumasi in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kumasi get in January?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kumasi in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
