How October compares in Kumasi

October is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kumasi. Compared with September: 0.5 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 60 hours more sunshine. November is 0.5 °C warmer than October on average.

Kumasi in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kumasi in October?
In October, Kumasi averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kumasi?
October is not one of Kumasi’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kumasi in October?
160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (206 mm), the driest January (17 mm).
Does it snow in Kumasi in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kumasi get in October?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kumasi in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
