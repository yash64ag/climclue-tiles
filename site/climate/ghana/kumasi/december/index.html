How December compares in Kumasi

December is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Kumasi. Compared with November: 0.2 °C warmer, 26 mm less precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. January is 0.9 °C warmer than December on average.

Kumasi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kumasi in December?
In December, Kumasi averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kumasi?
December is not one of Kumasi’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kumasi in December?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (209 mm), the driest January (17 mm).
Does it snow in Kumasi in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kumasi get in December?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kumasi in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
