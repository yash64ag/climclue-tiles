How June compares in Kumasi

June is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Kumasi. Compared with May: 1.2 °C cooler, 37 mm more precipitation and 54 hours less sunshine. July is 0.8 °C cooler than June on average.

Kumasi in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kumasi in June?
In June, Kumasi averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 206 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Kumasi?
June is not one of Kumasi’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kumasi in June?
206 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (206 mm), the driest January (17 mm).
Does it snow in Kumasi in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kumasi get in June?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kumasi in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
