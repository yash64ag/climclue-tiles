How February compares in Kumasi

February is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Kumasi. Compared with January: 1.3 °C warmer, 35 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. March is 0.4 °C cooler than February on average.

Kumasi in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kumasi in February?
In February, Kumasi averages a day high of 33.9 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Kumasi?
February is not one of Kumasi’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kumasi in February?
52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (209 mm), the driest January (17 mm).
Does it snow in Kumasi in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kumasi get in February?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kumasi in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
