How September compares in Kumasi

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kumasi. Compared with August: 0.4 °C warmer, 79 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. October is 0.5 °C warmer than September on average.

Kumasi in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kumasi in September?
In September, Kumasi averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 165 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation and about 103 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Kumasi?
September is not one of Kumasi’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kumasi in September?
165 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (206 mm), the driest January (17 mm).
Does it snow in Kumasi in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kumasi get in September?
About 103 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kumasi in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
