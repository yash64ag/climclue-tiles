Atsiaman at a glance

Among the 16 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ghana, Atsiaman is 4th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 8th sunniest (about 2,164 hours of sunshine a year) and 11th wettest (961 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Medina Estates, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Atsiaman is about as warm in March and 0.3 °C warmer in August, with 90 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (28.1 °C), averages 3.5 °C more than its coolest, August (24.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.7 °C (73 °F), in August. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Atsiaman, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Atsiaman has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
