How May compares in Accra

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Accra. Compared with April: 0.6 °C cooler, 61 mm more precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. June is 1.3 °C cooler than May on average.

Accra in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Accra in May?
In May, Accra averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 25.5 °C (78 °F), with 151 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Accra?
May is not one of Accra’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Accra in May?
151 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (184 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Accra in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Accra get in May?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Accra in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
