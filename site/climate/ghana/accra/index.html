Accra at a glance

Among the 16 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ghana, Accra is the 3rd driest (847 mm a year). Compared with Medina Estates, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Accra is 0.3 °C cooler in March and 0.4 °C cooler in August, with 24 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (27.8 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, August (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.9 °C (73 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Accra, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Accra

For mild weather, the best months to visit Accra are July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): July, August and September.
  • Warmest (average temperature): March (27.8 °C), April (27.8 °C) and February (27.4 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): January (11 mm), February (22 mm) and December (27 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): November (229 h), October (221 h) and December (209 h).

Why Accra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
