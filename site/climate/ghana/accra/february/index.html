Accra weather in February

Average February weather in Accra, Ghana: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Accra averages 31.5 °C (89 °F) days and 23.0 °C (73 °F) nights, with 21 mm (0.8 in) of rain over the month and about 190 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Accra

February is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Accra. Compared with January: 0.6 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. March is 0.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Accra in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Accra in February?
In February, Accra averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Accra?
February is not one of Accra’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Accra in February?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (185 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Accra in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Accra get in February?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Accra in February?
About 27.4 °C (81 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (28.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Accra in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
