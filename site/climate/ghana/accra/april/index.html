How April compares in Accra

April is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Accra. Compared with March: about as warm, 27 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. May is 0.6 °C cooler than April on average.

Accra in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Accra in April?
In April, Accra averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 26.1 °C (79 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Accra?
April is not one of Accra’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Accra in April?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (185 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Accra in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Accra get in April?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Accra in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
