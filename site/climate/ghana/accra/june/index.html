How June compares in Accra

June is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Accra. Compared with May: 1.3 °C cooler, 33 mm more precipitation and 61 hours less sunshine. July is 1.4 °C cooler than June on average.

Accra in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Accra in June?
In June, Accra averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 184 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Accra?
June is not one of Accra’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Accra in June?
184 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (184 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Accra in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Accra get in June?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Accra in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
