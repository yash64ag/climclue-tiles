How January compares in Accra

January is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Accra. Compared with December: 0.3 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. February is 0.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Accra in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Accra in January?
In January, Accra averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 25.0 °C (77 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Accra?
January is not one of Accra’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Accra in January?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (184 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Accra in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Accra get in January?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Accra in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
