How September compares in Accra

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Accra. Compared with August: 0.7 °C warmer, 29 mm more precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. October is 1.2 °C warmer than September on average.

Accra in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Accra in September?
In September, Accra averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Accra?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Accra’s mild months (July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Accra in September?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (185 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Accra in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Accra get in September?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Accra in September?
About 24.2 °C (76 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (28.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Accra in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
