How October compares in Accra

October is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Accra. Compared with September: 1.2 °C warmer, 21 mm more precipitation and 56 hours more sunshine. November is 1.1 °C warmer than October on average.

Accra in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Accra in October?
In October, Accra averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Accra?
October is not one of Accra’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Accra in October?
83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (184 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Accra in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Accra get in October?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Accra in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
