Medina Estates sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Greater Accra · Ghana

The sea off Medina Estates averages 28.3 °C (83 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 23.0 °C (73 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from September to July.

Medina Estates sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Medina Estates?
In May, at about 28.3 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Medina Estates?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from September to July. The coolest month is August, at 23.0 °C (73 °F).
How warm is the sea in Medina Estates in August?
About 23.0 °C (73 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Medina Estates?
About 26.6 °C (80 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
