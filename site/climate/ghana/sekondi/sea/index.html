Sekondi sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Western · Ghana

The sea off Sekondi averages 28.5 °C (83 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 23.4 °C (74 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from September to July.

Sekondi sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Sekondi?
In May, at about 28.5 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Sekondi?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from September to July. The coolest month is August, at 23.4 °C (74 °F).
How warm is the sea in Sekondi in August?
About 23.4 °C (74 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Sekondi?
About 26.6 °C (80 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
