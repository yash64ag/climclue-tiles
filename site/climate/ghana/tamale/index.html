Tamale at a glance

Among the 16 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ghana, Tamale is the warmest (28.3 °C year average) and the sunniest (about 2,651 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Kumasi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (314 km away), Tamale is 4.0 °C warmer in March and 1.2 °C warmer in August, with 137 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (31.8 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, August (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.9 °C (70 °F), in January. The area lies about 440 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tamale, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tamale has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 440 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
