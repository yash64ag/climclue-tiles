Cape Coast at a glance

Among the 98 towns ClimaClue covers in Ghana, Cape Coast is 61st warmest (26.2 °C year average), 71st sunniest (about 2,002 hours of sunshine a year) and 69th wettest (1,048 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Elmina, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Cape Coast is 0.1 °C warmer in March and 0.1 °C warmer in August, with 64 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (27.5 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, August (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.7 °C (71 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cape Coast, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Cape Coast

For mild weather, the best months to visit Cape Coast are July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): July, August and September.

Why Cape Coast has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Cape Coast itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (5.105°N, 1.247°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-788-1787), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
