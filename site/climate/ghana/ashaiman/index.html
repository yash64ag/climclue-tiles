Ashaiman at a glance

Among the 16 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ghana, Ashaiman is the 2nd warmest (27.0 °C year average) and the 2nd driest (840 mm a year). Compared with Tema, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Ashaiman is 0.4 °C warmer in March and 0.4 °C warmer in August, with 37 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (28.6 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, August (24.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.8 °C (73 °F), in August. The area lies about 25 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ashaiman, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ashaiman has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 25 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
