Why Tema has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Tema climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Tema?
March, with an average of 28.3 °C (83 °F), day highs around 31.1 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 26.0 °C (79 °F).
What is the coldest month in Tema?
August, with an average of 24.4 °C (76 °F), day highs around 26.4 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 23.0 °C (73 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Tema?
June, with 169 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 804 mm (31.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Tema?
January, with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Tema?
November, with about 230 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Tema?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Tema?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July and August.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Tema’s GeoNames location (5.670°N, 0.017°W; ClimaClue area sq-783-1799), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 155,782 (GeoNames).
