Tema at a glance

Among the 16 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ghana, Tema is the 3rd warmest (26.8 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 2,276 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (804 mm a year). Compared with Ashaiman, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Tema is 0.4 °C cooler in March and 0.4 °C cooler in August, with 37 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (28.3 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, August (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.0 °C (73 °F), in August. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tema, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tema has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
