Anloga sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Volta · Ghana

The sea off Anloga averages 28.7 °C (84 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 23.8 °C (75 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from September to July.

Anloga sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Anloga?
In May, at about 28.7 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Anloga?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from September to July. The coolest month is August, at 23.8 °C (75 °F).
How warm is the sea in Anloga in August?
About 23.8 °C (75 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Anloga?
About 27.1 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
