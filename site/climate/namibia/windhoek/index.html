Windhoek at a glance

Its warmest month, December (23.9 °C), averages 11.2 °C more than its coolest, July (12.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.9 °C (43 °F), in July. The area lies about 267 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 95% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Windhoek has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 267 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
