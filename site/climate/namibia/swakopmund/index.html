Swakopmund at a glance

Among the 18 towns ClimaClue covers in Namibia, Swakopmund is the 3rd coolest (17.9 °C year average), the 2nd least sunny (about 2,438 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (20 mm a year). Compared with Walvis Bay, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Swakopmund is 1.3 °C warmer in March and 0.8 °C warmer in September, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (19.9 °C), averages 4.5 °C more than its coolest, September (15.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.6 °C (53 °F), in August. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Swakopmund, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Swakopmund has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Swakopmund itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.678°S, 14.527°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1066-1945), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
