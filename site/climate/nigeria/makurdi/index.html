Makurdi at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Makurdi is 8th warmest (28.2 °C year average), 30th sunniest (about 2,355 hours of sunshine a year) and 82nd wettest (1,207 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gboko, the nearest covered town with different numbers (70 km away), Makurdi is 1.3 °C warmer in March and 0.7 °C warmer in August, with 246 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (31.4 °C), averages 5.8 °C more than its coolest, August (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.0 °C (73 °F), in August. The area lies about 345 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Makurdi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Makurdi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 345 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
