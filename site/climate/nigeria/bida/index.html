Bida at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Bida is 6th warmest (28.3 °C year average), 28th sunniest (about 2,421 hours of sunshine a year) and 87th wettest (1,184 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lafiagi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (70 km away), Bida is 0.1 °C warmer in March and 0.2 °C cooler in August, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (31.8 °C), averages 6.0 °C more than its coolest, August (25.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.2 °C (68 °F), in January. The area lies about 358 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bida, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bida has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 358 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
