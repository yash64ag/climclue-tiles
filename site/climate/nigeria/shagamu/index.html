Shagamu at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Shagamu is 53rd warmest (26.2 °C year average), 77th sunniest (about 1,875 hours of sunshine a year) and 28th wettest (1,780 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ijebu Ode, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Shagamu is 0.3 °C warmer in February and 0.2 °C warmer in August, with 214 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.8 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, August (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.2 °C (72 °F), in August. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shagamu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shagamu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
