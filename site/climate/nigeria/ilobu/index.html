Ilobu at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Ilobu is 88th warmest (25.6 °C year average), 47th sunniest (about 2,011 hours of sunshine a year) and 71st wettest (1,401 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Osogbo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Ilobu is 0.1 °C warmer in February and 0.1 °C warmer in August, with 68 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.6 °C), averages 4.0 °C more than its coolest, August (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.2 °C (70 °F), in January. The area lies about 170 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ilobu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ilobu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 170 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
