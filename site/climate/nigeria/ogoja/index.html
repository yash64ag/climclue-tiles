Ogoja at a glance

Among the 269 towns ClimaClue covers in Nigeria, Ogoja is 61st warmest (27.4 °C year average), 144th sunniest (about 2,033 hours of sunshine a year) and 61st wettest (1,828 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Obudu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (40 km away), Ogoja is 1.0 °C warmer in February and 0.8 °C warmer in August, with 257 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (30.0 °C), averages 4.7 °C more than its coolest, August (25.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.2 °C (70 °F), in December. The area lies about 225 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ogoja, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ogoja has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 225 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ogoja itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (6.658°N, 8.799°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-773-1887), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
