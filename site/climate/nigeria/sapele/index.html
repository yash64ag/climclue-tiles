Sapele at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Sapele is the 5th wettest (2,675 mm a year). Compared with Warri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Sapele is 0.1 °C warmer in February and 0.1 °C cooler in August, with 141 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.4 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, August (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.3 °C (72 °F), in July. The area lies about 70 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sapele, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sapele has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 70 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
