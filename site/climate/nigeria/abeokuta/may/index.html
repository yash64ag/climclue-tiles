How May compares in Abeokuta

May is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Abeokuta. Compared with April: 0.9 °C cooler, 42 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. June is 1.1 °C cooler than May on average.

Abeokuta in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abeokuta in May?
In May, Abeokuta averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Abeokuta?
No month in Abeokuta is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abeokuta in May?
160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (215 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Abeokuta in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abeokuta get in May?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Abeokuta in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
