Abeokuta weather in March

Average March weather in Abeokuta, Nigeria: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Abeokuta averages 33.3 °C (92 °F) days and 23.3 °C (74 °F) nights, with 66 mm (2.6 in) of rain over the month and about 189 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Abeokuta

March is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Abeokuta. Compared with February: 0.3 °C cooler, 41 mm more precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. April is 0.7 °C cooler than March on average.

Abeokuta in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abeokuta in March?
In March, Abeokuta averages a day high of 33.3 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Abeokuta?
No month in Abeokuta is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abeokuta in March?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (215 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Abeokuta in March?

How many hours of sunshine does Abeokuta get in March?
