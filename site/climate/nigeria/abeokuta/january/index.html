How January compares in Abeokuta

January is the 4th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Abeokuta. Compared with December: 0.3 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. February is 1.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Abeokuta in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abeokuta in January?
In January, Abeokuta averages a day high of 33.7 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Abeokuta?
No month in Abeokuta is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abeokuta in January?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (221 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Abeokuta in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abeokuta get in January?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Abeokuta in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
