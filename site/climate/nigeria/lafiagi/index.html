Lafiagi at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Lafiagi is the 5th warmest (28.4 °C year average). Compared with Bida, the nearest covered town with different numbers (70 km away), Lafiagi is 0.1 °C cooler in March and 0.2 °C warmer in August, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (31.6 °C), averages 5.7 °C more than its coolest, August (25.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.7 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 309 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lafiagi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lafiagi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 309 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
