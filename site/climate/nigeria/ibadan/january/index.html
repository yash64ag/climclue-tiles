How January compares in Ibadan

January is the 4th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Ibadan. Compared with December: 0.3 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. February is 1.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Ibadan in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ibadan in January?
In January, Ibadan averages a day high of 33.5 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Ibadan?
No month in Ibadan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ibadan in January?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (217 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Ibadan in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ibadan get in January?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ibadan in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
