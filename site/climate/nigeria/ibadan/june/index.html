How June compares in Ibadan

June is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Ibadan. Compared with May: 1.0 °C cooler, 50 mm more precipitation and 44 hours less sunshine. July is 0.9 °C cooler than June on average.

Ibadan in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ibadan in June?
In June, Ibadan averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 226 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Ibadan?
No month in Ibadan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ibadan in June?
226 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (228 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Ibadan in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ibadan get in June?
About 145 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ibadan in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
