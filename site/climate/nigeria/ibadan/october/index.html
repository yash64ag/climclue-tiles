How October compares in Ibadan

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Ibadan. Compared with September: 0.8 °C warmer, 33 mm less precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. November is 1.1 °C warmer than October on average.

Ibadan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ibadan in October?
In October, Ibadan averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 195 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Ibadan?
No month in Ibadan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ibadan in October?
195 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (228 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Ibadan in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ibadan get in October?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ibadan in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
