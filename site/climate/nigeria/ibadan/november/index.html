How November compares in Ibadan

November is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Ibadan. Compared with October: 1.1 °C warmer, 160 mm less precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. December is 0.3 °C warmer than November on average.

Ibadan in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ibadan in November?
In November, Ibadan averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Ibadan?
No month in Ibadan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ibadan in November?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (217 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Ibadan in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ibadan get in November?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ibadan in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
