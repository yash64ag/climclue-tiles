Ibadan weather in December

Average December weather in Ibadan, Nigeria: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Ibadan averages 31.9 °C (89 °F) days and 21.1 °C (70 °F) nights, with 9 mm (0.4 in) of rain over the month and about 199 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Ibadan

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Ibadan. Compared with November: 0.3 °C warmer, 17 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. January is 0.3 °C warmer than December on average.

Ibadan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ibadan in December?
In December, Ibadan averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 199 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ibadan?
No month in Ibadan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ibadan in December?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (217 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Ibadan in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ibadan get in December?
About 199 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ibadan in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
