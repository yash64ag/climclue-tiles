Ibadan at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Ibadan is 78th warmest (25.8 °C year average), 60th sunniest (about 1,931 hours of sunshine a year) and 66th wettest (1,459 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ikire, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Ibadan is about as warm in February and 0.1 °C cooler in August, with 39 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.8 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, August (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.5 °C (71 °F), in August. The area lies about 111 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ibadan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ibadan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 111 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
