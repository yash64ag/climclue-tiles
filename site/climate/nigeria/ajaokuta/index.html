Ajaokuta at a glance

Among the 269 towns ClimaClue covers in Nigeria, Ajaokuta is 36th warmest (28.0 °C year average), 125th sunniest (about 2,124 hours of sunshine a year) and 207th wettest (1,030 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lokoja, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Ajaokuta is 0.4 °C cooler in March and 0.2 °C cooler in August, with 39 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (30.5 °C), averages 4.8 °C more than its coolest, August (25.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.1 °C (72 °F), in December. The area lies about 267 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ajaokuta, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ajaokuta has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 267 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ajaokuta itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (7.562°N, 6.655°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-764-1866), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
