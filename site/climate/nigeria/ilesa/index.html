Why Ilesa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 159 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Ilesa climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Ilesa?
February, with an average of 27.1 °C (81 °F), day highs around 34.1 °C (93 °F) and night lows around 21.8 °C (71 °F).
What is the coldest month in Ilesa?
August, with an average of 23.1 °C (74 °F), day highs around 26.9 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 20.8 °C (69 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Ilesa?
September, with 262 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,591 mm (62.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Ilesa?
January, with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Ilesa?
November, with about 211 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Ilesa?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Ilesa?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Ilesa have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Ilesa’s GeoNames location (7.628°N, 4.742°E; ClimaClue area sq-763-1847), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 325,000 (GeoNames).
