Gashua at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Gashua is the sunniest (about 3,058 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (387 mm a year). Compared with Nguru, the nearest covered town with different numbers (64 km away), Gashua is 0.2 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 26 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.5 °C), averages 11.6 °C more than its coolest, January (21.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.6 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 955 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Gashua has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 955 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
