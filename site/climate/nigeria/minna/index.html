Minna at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Minna is 16th warmest (27.4 °C year average), 24th sunniest (about 2,539 hours of sunshine a year) and 78th wettest (1,243 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bida, the nearest covered town with different numbers (84 km away), Minna is 0.7 °C cooler in March and 1.2 °C cooler in August, with 60 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (31.1 °C), averages 6.5 °C more than its coolest, August (24.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.5 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 443 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Minna, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Minna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 443 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
