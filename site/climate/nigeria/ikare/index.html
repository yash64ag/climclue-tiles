Ikare at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Ikare is 105th warmest (25.0 °C year average), 48th sunniest (about 2,003 hours of sunshine a year) and 45th wettest (1,644 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ise-Ekiti, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Ikare is 0.3 °C cooler in February and 0.2 °C cooler in August, with 153 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.9 °C), averages 3.7 °C more than its coolest, August (23.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.2 °C (68 °F), in January. The area lies about 204 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ikare, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ikare has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 204 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
