Iseyin at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Iseyin is 57th warmest (26.1 °C year average), 39th sunniest (about 2,084 hours of sunshine a year) and 80th wettest (1,222 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Oyo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Iseyin is 0.1 °C cooler in February and 0.1 °C warmer in August, with 28 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (28.1 °C), averages 4.1 °C more than its coolest, August (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.1 °C (70 °F), in January. The area lies about 178 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Iseyin, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Iseyin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 178 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
