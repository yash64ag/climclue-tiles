Hadejia at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Hadejia is the 5th sunniest (about 2,998 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (523 mm a year). Compared with Nguru, the nearest covered town with different numbers (65 km away), Hadejia is 0.9 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 110 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (32.5 °C), averages 10.8 °C more than its coolest, January (21.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.4 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 885 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hadejia, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hadejia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 885 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
