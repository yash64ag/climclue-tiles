Kisi at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Kisi is 49th warmest (26.3 °C year average), 29th sunniest (about 2,387 hours of sunshine a year) and 89th wettest (1,165 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Igboho, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Kisi is 0.4 °C warmer in March and 0.2 °C warmer in August, with 71 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (29.1 °C), averages 5.0 °C more than its coolest, August (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.0 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 300 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kisi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kisi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 300 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
