Sofo-Birnin-Gwari at a glance

Among the 269 towns ClimaClue covers in Nigeria, Sofo-Birnin-Gwari is 198th warmest (25.8 °C year average), 71st sunniest (about 2,706 hours of sunshine a year) and 185th wettest (1,163 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Funtua, the nearest covered town with different numbers (81 km away), Sofo-Birnin-Gwari is 0.7 °C warmer in April and 0.2 °C warmer in August, with 42 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.2 °C), averages 6.7 °C more than its coolest, August (23.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.7 °C (60 °F), in December. The area lies about 591 km from the sea.

Why Sofo-Birnin-Gwari has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 591 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sofo-Birnin-Gwari itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (11.015°N, 6.780°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-729-1867), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
