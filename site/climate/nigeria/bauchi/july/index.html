How July compares in Bauchi

July is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Bauchi. Compared with June: 1.9 °C cooler, 79 mm more precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Bauchi in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bauchi in July?
In July, Bauchi averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.7 °C (69 °F), with 261 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Bauchi?
July is not one of Bauchi’s mild months; those are January, February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bauchi in July?
261 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (299 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Bauchi in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bauchi get in July?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bauchi in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
