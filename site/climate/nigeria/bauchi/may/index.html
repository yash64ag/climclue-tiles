How May compares in Bauchi

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Bauchi. Compared with April: 1.1 °C cooler, 64 mm more precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. June is 2.6 °C cooler than May on average.

Bauchi in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bauchi in May?
In May, Bauchi averages a day high of 34.6 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Bauchi?
May is not one of Bauchi’s mild months; those are January, February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bauchi in May?
95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (299 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Bauchi in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bauchi get in May?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bauchi in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
