How October compares in Bauchi

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Bauchi. Compared with September: 0.7 °C warmer, 149 mm less precipitation and 50 hours more sunshine. November is 1.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Bauchi in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bauchi in October?
In October, Bauchi averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Bauchi?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Bauchi’s mild months (January, February, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bauchi in October?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (299 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Bauchi in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bauchi get in October?
About 254 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bauchi in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
