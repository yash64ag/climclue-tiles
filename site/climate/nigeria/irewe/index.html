Irewe at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Irewe is 32nd warmest (26.6 °C year average), 56th sunniest (about 1,945 hours of sunshine a year) and 62nd wettest (1,469 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ajegunle, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Irewe is about as warm in March and about as warm in August, with 137 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (27.9 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, August (24.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.5 °C (74 °F), in August. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Irewe, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Irewe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
