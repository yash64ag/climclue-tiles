How November compares in Abuja

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Abuja. Compared with October: 1.5 °C warmer, 135 mm less precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. December is 0.4 °C warmer than November on average.

Abuja in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abuja in November?
In November, Abuja averages a day high of 33.4 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 258 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Abuja?
No month in Abuja is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abuja in November?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (335 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Abuja in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abuja get in November?
About 258 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Abuja in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
