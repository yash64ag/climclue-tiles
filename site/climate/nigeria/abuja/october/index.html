How October compares in Abuja

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Abuja. Compared with September: 1.0 °C warmer, 105 mm less precipitation and 60 hours more sunshine. November is 1.5 °C warmer than October on average.

Abuja in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abuja in October?
In October, Abuja averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 144 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Abuja?
No month in Abuja is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abuja in October?
144 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (335 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Abuja in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abuja get in October?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Abuja in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
