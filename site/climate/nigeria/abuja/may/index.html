How May compares in Abuja

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Abuja. Compared with April: 2.1 °C cooler, 75 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. June is 1.8 °C cooler than May on average.

Abuja in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abuja in May?
In May, Abuja averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 150 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Abuja?
No month in Abuja is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abuja in May?
150 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (335 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Abuja in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abuja get in May?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Abuja in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
