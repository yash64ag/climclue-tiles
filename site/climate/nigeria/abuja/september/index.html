Abuja weather in September

Average September weather in Abuja, Nigeria: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Abuja averages 28.1 °C (83 °F) days and 19.6 °C (67 °F) nights, with 249 mm (9.8 in) of rain over the month and about 168 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Abuja

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Abuja. Compared with August: 0.5 °C warmer, 86 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. October is 1.0 °C warmer than September on average.

Abuja in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abuja in September?
In September, Abuja averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 19.6 °C (67 °F), with 249 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Abuja?
No month in Abuja is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abuja in September?
249 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (335 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Abuja in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abuja get in September?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Abuja in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
