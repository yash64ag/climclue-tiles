Abuja weather in December

Average December weather in Abuja, Nigeria: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Abuja averages 34.5 °C (94 °F) days and 19.3 °C (67 °F) nights, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of rain over the month and about 263 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Abuja

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Abuja. Compared with November: 0.4 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. January is 0.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Abuja in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abuja in December?
In December, Abuja averages a day high of 34.5 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 19.3 °C (67 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Abuja?
No month in Abuja is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abuja in December?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (335 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Abuja in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abuja get in December?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Abuja in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
