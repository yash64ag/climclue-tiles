Abuja weather in July

Average July weather in Abuja, Nigeria: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Abuja averages 27.2 °C (81 °F) days and 19.9 °C (68 °F) nights, with 272 mm (10.7 in) of rain over the month and about 150 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Abuja

July is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Abuja. Compared with June: 1.2 °C cooler, 69 mm more precipitation and 36 hours less sunshine. August is 0.2 °C cooler than July on average.

Abuja in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abuja in July?
In July, Abuja averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 19.9 °C (68 °F), with 272 mm (10.7 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Abuja?
No month in Abuja is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abuja in July?
272 mm (10.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (335 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Abuja in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abuja get in July?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Abuja in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
