Abuja weather in August

Average August weather in Abuja, Nigeria: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Abuja averages 27.0 °C (81 °F) days and 19.7 °C (67 °F) nights, with 335 mm (13.2 in) of rain over the month and about 140 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Abuja

August is the coolest month, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Abuja. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 63 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. September is 0.5 °C warmer than August on average.

Abuja in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abuja in August?
In August, Abuja averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 19.7 °C (67 °F), with 335 mm (13.2 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Abuja?
No month in Abuja is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abuja in August?
335 mm (13.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (335 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Abuja in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abuja get in August?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
