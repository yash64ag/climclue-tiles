Itu at a glance

Among the 269 towns ClimaClue covers in Nigeria, Itu is 168th warmest (26.0 °C year average), 251st sunniest (about 1,596 hours of sunshine a year) and 34th wettest (2,207 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Uyo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Itu is 0.3 °C warmer in February and 0.2 °C warmer in August, with 252 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.9 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, August (24.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.1 °C (70 °F), in September. The area lies about 87 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Itu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Itu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 87 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Itu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (5.201°N, 7.984°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-787-1879), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
