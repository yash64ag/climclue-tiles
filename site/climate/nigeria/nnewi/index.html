Nnewi at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Nnewi is 70th warmest (25.9 °C year average), 93rd sunniest (about 1,767 hours of sunshine a year) and 15th wettest (2,282 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nkpor, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Nnewi is 0.7 °C cooler in February and 0.5 °C cooler in July, with 288 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.9 °C), averages 3.7 °C more than its coolest, July (24.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.8 °C (71 °F), in July. The area lies about 194 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nnewi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nnewi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 194 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
