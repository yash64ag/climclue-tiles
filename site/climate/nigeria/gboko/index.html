Gboko at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Gboko is 18th warmest (27.2 °C year average), 34th sunniest (about 2,268 hours of sunshine a year) and 67th wettest (1,453 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Makurdi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (70 km away), Gboko is 1.3 °C cooler in March and 0.7 °C cooler in August, with 246 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (30.1 °C), averages 5.2 °C more than its coolest, August (24.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.1 °C (72 °F), in December. The area lies about 307 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gboko, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gboko has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 307 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
