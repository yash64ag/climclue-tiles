Why Gboko has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 307 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Gboko climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Gboko?
March, with an average of 30.1 °C (86 °F), day highs around 35.9 °C (97 °F) and night lows around 24.9 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Gboko?
August, with an average of 24.9 °C (77 °F), day highs around 28.3 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 22.2 °C (72 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Gboko?
September, with 279 mm (11.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,453 mm (57.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Gboko?
February, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Gboko?
November, with about 230 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Gboko?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Gboko?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Gboko have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Gboko’s GeoNames location (7.323°N, 9.001°E; ClimaClue area sq-766-1890), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 365,000 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).
