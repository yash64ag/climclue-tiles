Why Zaria has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 654 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Zaria climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Zaria?
April, with an average of 29.9 °C (86 °F), day highs around 36.6 °C (98 °F) and night lows around 22.7 °C (73 °F).
What is the coldest month in Zaria?
January, with an average of 21.9 °C (71 °F), day highs around 29.3 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 15.1 °C (59 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Zaria?
August, with 285 mm (11.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 992 mm (39.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Zaria?
January, with 0 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Zaria?
December, with about 264 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Zaria?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Zaria?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, October, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Zaria’s GeoNames location (11.111°N, 7.723°E; ClimaClue area sq-728-1877), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 980,000 (GeoNames).
