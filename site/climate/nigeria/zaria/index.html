Zaria at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Zaria is 100th warmest (25.1 °C year average), 20th sunniest (about 2,765 hours of sunshine a year) and 95th wettest (992 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Funtua, the nearest covered town with different numbers (64 km away), Zaria is 0.3 °C warmer in April and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 128 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.9 °C), averages 8.0 °C more than its coolest, January (21.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.1 °C (59 °F), in January. The area lies about 654 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Zaria, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Zaria has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 654 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
