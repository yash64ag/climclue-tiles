How June compares in Zaria

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Zaria. Compared with May: 2.6 °C cooler, 30 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. July is 1.7 °C cooler than June on average.

Zaria in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zaria in June?
In June, Zaria averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Zaria?
June is not one of Zaria’s mild months; those are January, February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zaria in June?
133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (285 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Zaria in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zaria get in June?
About 232 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zaria in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
