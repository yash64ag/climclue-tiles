Ila Orangun at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Ila Orangun is the 5th coolest (24.8 °C year average). Compared with Offa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Ila Orangun is 0.3 °C cooler in February and 0.5 °C cooler in August, with 168 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.9 °C), averages 4.1 °C more than its coolest, August (22.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.9 °C (68 °F), in January. The area lies about 208 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ila Orangun, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ila Orangun has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 208 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
