Gwoza at a glance

Among the 269 towns ClimaClue covers in Nigeria, Gwoza is 84th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 15th sunniest (about 2,975 hours of sunshine a year) and 214th wettest (961 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bama, the nearest covered town with different numbers (49 km away), Gwoza is 1.3 °C cooler in April and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 310 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (31.6 °C), averages 9.3 °C more than its coolest, January (22.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.0 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 904 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gwoza, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gwoza has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 904 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Gwoza itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (11.083°N, 13.696°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-729-1936), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
