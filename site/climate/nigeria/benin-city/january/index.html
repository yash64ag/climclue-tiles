How January compares in Benin City

January is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Benin City. Compared with December: 0.3 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. February is 0.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Benin City in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benin City in January?
In January, Benin City averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Benin City?
No month in Benin City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benin City in January?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (369 mm), the driest January (19 mm).
Does it snow in Benin City in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benin City get in January?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benin City in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
