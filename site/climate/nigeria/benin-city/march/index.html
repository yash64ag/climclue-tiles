How March compares in Benin City

March is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Benin City. Compared with February: 0.4 °C cooler, 65 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. April is 0.5 °C cooler than March on average.

Benin City in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benin City in March?
In March, Benin City averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 23.8 °C (75 °F), with 112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Benin City?
No month in Benin City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benin City in March?
112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (365 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Benin City in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benin City get in March?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benin City in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
