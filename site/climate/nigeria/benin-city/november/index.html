How November compares in Benin City

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Benin City. Compared with October: 0.8 °C warmer, 197 mm less precipitation and 39 hours more sunshine. December is 0.5 °C warmer than November on average.

Benin City in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benin City in November?
In November, Benin City averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Benin City?
No month in Benin City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benin City in November?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (365 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Benin City in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benin City get in November?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benin City in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
