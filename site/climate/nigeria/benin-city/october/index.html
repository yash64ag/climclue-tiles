How October compares in Benin City

October is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Benin City. Compared with September: 0.5 °C warmer, 93 mm less precipitation and 49 hours more sunshine. November is 0.9 °C warmer than October on average.

Benin City in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benin City in October?
In October, Benin City averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.0 °C (70 °F), with 276 mm (10.9 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Benin City?
No month in Benin City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benin City in October?
276 mm (10.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (369 mm), the driest January (19 mm).
Does it snow in Benin City in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benin City get in October?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benin City in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
