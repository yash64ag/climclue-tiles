How December compares in Benin City

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Benin City. Compared with November: 0.5 °C warmer, 53 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. January is 0.3 °C warmer than December on average.

Benin City in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benin City in December?
In December, Benin City averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Benin City?
No month in Benin City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benin City in December?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (365 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Benin City in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benin City get in December?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benin City in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
