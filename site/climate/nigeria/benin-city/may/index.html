How May compares in Benin City

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Benin City. Compared with April: 0.6 °C cooler, 60 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. June is 0.9 °C cooler than May on average.

Benin City in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benin City in May?
In May, Benin City averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 240 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Benin City?
No month in Benin City is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benin City in May?
240 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (365 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Benin City in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benin City get in May?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benin City in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
