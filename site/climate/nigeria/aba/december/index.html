How December compares in Aba

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Aba. Compared with November: 0.7 °C warmer, 68 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. January is 0.4 °C warmer than December on average.

Aba in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aba in December?
In December, Aba averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Aba?
No month in Aba is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aba in December?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (384 mm), the driest December (13 mm).
Does it snow in Aba in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aba get in December?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aba in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
