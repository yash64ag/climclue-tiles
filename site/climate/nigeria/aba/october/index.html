How October compares in Aba

October is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Aba. Compared with September: 0.4 °C warmer, 121 mm less precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. November is 0.8 °C warmer than October on average.

Aba in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aba in October?
In October, Aba averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 263 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation and about 116 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Aba?
No month in Aba is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aba in October?
263 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (384 mm), the driest December (13 mm).
Does it snow in Aba in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aba get in October?
About 116 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aba in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
