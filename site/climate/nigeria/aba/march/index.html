How March compares in Aba

March is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Aba. Compared with February: 0.5 °C cooler, 69 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. April is 0.6 °C cooler than March on average.

Aba in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aba in March?
In March, Aba averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Aba?
No month in Aba is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aba in March?
108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (384 mm), the driest December (13 mm).
Does it snow in Aba in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aba get in March?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aba in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
