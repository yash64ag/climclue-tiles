How May compares in Aba

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Aba. Compared with April: 0.5 °C cooler, 102 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. June is 1.0 °C cooler than May on average.

Aba in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aba in May?
In May, Aba averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 291 mm (11.4 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Aba?
No month in Aba is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aba in May?
291 mm (11.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (389 mm), the driest December (14 mm).
Does it snow in Aba in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aba get in May?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aba in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
