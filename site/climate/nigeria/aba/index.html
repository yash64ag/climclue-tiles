Aba at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Aba is 86th warmest (25.6 °C year average), 106th sunniest (about 1,498 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (2,429 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ikot Ekpene, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Aba is about as warm in February and about as warm in July, with 40 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.4 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, July (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.7 °C (71 °F), in July. The area lies about 85 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Aba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Aba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 85 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
