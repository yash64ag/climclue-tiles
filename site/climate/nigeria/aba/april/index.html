Aba weather in April

Average April weather in Aba, Nigeria: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Aba averages 30.9 °C (88 °F) days and 21.8 °C (71 °F) nights, with 189 mm (7.4 in) of rain over the month and about 142 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Aba

April is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Aba. Compared with March: 0.6 °C cooler, 76 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. May is 0.5 °C cooler than April on average.

Is April a good time to visit Aba?
