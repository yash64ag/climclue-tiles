How December compares in Oyo

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Oyo. Compared with November: 0.3 °C warmer, 12 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. January is 0.4 °C warmer than December on average.

Oyo in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oyo in December?
In December, Oyo averages a day high of 32.9 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Oyo?
No month in Oyo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oyo in December?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (195 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Oyo in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Oyo get in December?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oyo in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
