How September compares in Oyo

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Oyo. Compared with August: 0.4 °C warmer, 59 mm more precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. October is 0.8 °C warmer than September on average.

Oyo in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oyo in September?
In September, Oyo averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 197 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation and about 122 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Oyo?
No month in Oyo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oyo in September?
197 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (197 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Oyo in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Oyo get in September?
About 122 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oyo in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
