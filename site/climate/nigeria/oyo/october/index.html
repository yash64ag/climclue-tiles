How October compares in Oyo

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Oyo. Compared with September: 0.8 °C warmer, 44 mm less precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. November is 1.2 °C warmer than October on average.

Oyo in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oyo in October?
In October, Oyo averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 151 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Oyo?
No month in Oyo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oyo in October?
151 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (195 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Oyo in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Oyo get in October?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oyo in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
