How May compares in Oyo

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Oyo. Compared with April: 0.9 °C cooler, 41 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. June is 1.2 °C cooler than May on average.

Oyo in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oyo in May?
In May, Oyo averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 146 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Oyo?
No month in Oyo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oyo in May?
146 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (197 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Oyo in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Oyo get in May?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oyo in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
