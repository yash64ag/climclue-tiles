How November compares in Gombe

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Gombe. Compared with October: 0.7 °C cooler, 39 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. December is 2.0 °C cooler than November on average.
