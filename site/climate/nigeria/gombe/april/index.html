How April compares in Gombe

April is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Gombe. Compared with March: 1.5 °C warmer, 21 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. May is 1.1 °C cooler than April on average.

Gombe in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gombe in April?
In April, Gombe averages a day high of 37.4 °C (99 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Gombe?
April is not one of Gombe’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gombe in April?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (226 mm), the driest February (0 mm).
Does it snow in Gombe in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gombe get in April?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gombe in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
