Gombe at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Gombe is 42nd warmest (26.5 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 2,829 hours of sunshine a year) and 102nd wettest (742 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pindiga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (41 km away), Gombe is 0.2 °C cooler in April and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 27 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.8 °C), averages 7.4 °C more than its coolest, January (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.8 °C (60 °F), in January. The area lies about 690 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gombe, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gombe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 690 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
