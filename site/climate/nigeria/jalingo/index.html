Jalingo at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Jalingo is 11th warmest (27.9 °C year average), 22nd sunniest (about 2,684 hours of sunshine a year) and 96th wettest (955 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jimeta, the nearest covered town with different numbers (128 km away), Jalingo is 1.5 °C cooler in April and 1.3 °C cooler in August, with 146 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (31.6 °C), averages 6.4 °C more than its coolest, August (25.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.1 °C (68 °F), in January. The area lies about 562 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jalingo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jalingo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 562 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
