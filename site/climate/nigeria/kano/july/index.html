How July compares in Kano

July is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kano. Compared with June: 2.5 °C cooler, 130 mm more precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. August is 0.7 °C cooler than July on average.

Kano in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kano in July?
In July, Kano averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 283 mm (11.1 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Kano?
July is not one of Kano’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kano in July?
283 mm (11.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (377 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kano in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kano get in July?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kano in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
