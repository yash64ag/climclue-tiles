How March compares in Kano

March is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Kano. Compared with February: 4.1 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. April is 3.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Kano in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kano in March?
In March, Kano averages a day high of 35.6 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kano?
March is not one of Kano’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kano in March?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (360 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kano in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kano get in March?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kano in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
