How September compares in Kano

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kano. Compared with August: 0.8 °C warmer, 184 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. October is 0.6 °C warmer than September on average.

Kano in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kano in September?
In September, Kano averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 176 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Kano?
September is not one of Kano’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kano in September?
176 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (360 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kano in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kano get in September?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kano in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
