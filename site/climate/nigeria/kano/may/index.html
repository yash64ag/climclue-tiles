How May compares in Kano

May is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Kano. Compared with April: 0.1 °C warmer, 46 mm more precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. June is 2.1 °C cooler than May on average.

Kano in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kano in May?
In May, Kano averages a day high of 37.6 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 260 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kano?
May is not one of Kano’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kano in May?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (360 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kano in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kano get in May?
About 260 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kano in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
