Ugep at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Ugep is 20th warmest (26.8 °C year average), 91st sunniest (about 1,781 hours of sunshine a year) and 23rd wettest (1,943 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Amaigbo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Ugep is 0.6 °C warmer in February and 0.4 °C warmer in July, with 95 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (29.3 °C), averages 4.2 °C more than its coolest, July (25.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.5 °C (73 °F), in July. The area lies about 141 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ugep, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ugep has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 141 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
