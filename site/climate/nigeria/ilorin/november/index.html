How November compares in Ilorin

November is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Ilorin. Compared with October: 1.4 °C warmer, 106 mm less precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. December is about as warm as November on average.

Ilorin in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ilorin in November?
In November, Ilorin averages a day high of 33.4 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Ilorin?
No month in Ilorin is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ilorin in November?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (209 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Ilorin in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ilorin get in November?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ilorin in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
