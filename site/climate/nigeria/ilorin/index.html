Ilorin at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Ilorin is 34th warmest (26.6 °C year average), 35th sunniest (about 2,219 hours of sunshine a year) and 93rd wettest (1,085 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Offa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Ilorin is 1.8 °C warmer in March and 0.9 °C warmer in August, with 454 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (29.1 °C), averages 4.9 °C more than its coolest, August (24.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.4 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 238 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ilorin, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ilorin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 238 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
