How September compares in Ilorin

September is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Ilorin. Compared with August: 0.3 °C warmer, 60 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. October is 0.9 °C warmer than September on average.

Ilorin in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ilorin in September?
In September, Ilorin averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 209 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation and about 128 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Ilorin?
No month in Ilorin is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ilorin in September?
209 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (209 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Ilorin in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ilorin get in September?
About 128 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ilorin in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
