How May compares in Ilorin

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Ilorin. Compared with April: 1.3 °C cooler, 51 mm more precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. June is 1.5 °C cooler than May on average.

Ilorin in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ilorin in May?
In May, Ilorin averages a day high of 32.8 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Ilorin?
No month in Ilorin is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ilorin in May?
134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (209 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Ilorin in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ilorin get in May?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ilorin in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
