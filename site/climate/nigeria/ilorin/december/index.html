How December compares in Ilorin

December is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Ilorin. Compared with November: about as warm, 3 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. January is 0.1 °C warmer than December on average.

Ilorin in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ilorin in December?
In December, Ilorin averages a day high of 34.4 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 20.7 °C (69 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ilorin?
No month in Ilorin is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ilorin in December?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (209 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Ilorin in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ilorin get in December?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ilorin in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
