How January compares in Ilorin

January is the 5th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ilorin. Compared with December: 0.1 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. February is 1.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Ilorin in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ilorin in January?
In January, Ilorin averages a day high of 34.8 °C (95 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Ilorin?
No month in Ilorin is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ilorin in January?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (209 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Ilorin in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ilorin get in January?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ilorin in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
