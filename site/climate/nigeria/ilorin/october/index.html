How October compares in Ilorin

October is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Ilorin. Compared with September: 0.9 °C warmer, 89 mm less precipitation and 61 hours more sunshine. November is 1.4 °C warmer than October on average.

Ilorin in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ilorin in October?
In October, Ilorin averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Ilorin?
No month in Ilorin is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ilorin in October?
120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (209 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Ilorin in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ilorin get in October?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ilorin in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
