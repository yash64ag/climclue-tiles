How February compares in Ilorin

February is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Ilorin. Compared with January: 1.6 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. March is 0.6 °C warmer than February on average.

Ilorin in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ilorin in February?
In February, Ilorin averages a day high of 36.1 °C (97 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Ilorin?
No month in Ilorin is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ilorin in February?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (209 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Ilorin in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ilorin get in February?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ilorin in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
