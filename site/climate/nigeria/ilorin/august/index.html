How August compares in Ilorin

August is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Ilorin. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. September is 0.3 °C warmer than August on average.

Ilorin in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ilorin in August?
In August, Ilorin averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.7 °C (69 °F), with 151 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 113 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Ilorin?
No month in Ilorin is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ilorin in August?
151 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (209 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Ilorin in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ilorin get in August?
About 113 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ilorin in August?
