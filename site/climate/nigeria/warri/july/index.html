How July compares in Warri

July is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Warri. Compared with June: 0.8 °C cooler, 133 mm more precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. August is about as warm as July on average.

Warri in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Warri in July?
In July, Warri averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 462 mm (18.2 in) of precipitation and about 93 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Warri?
No month in Warri is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Warri in July?
462 mm (18.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (462 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Warri in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Warri get in July?
About 93 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
