How December compares in Warri

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Warri. Compared with November: 0.4 °C warmer, 74 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. January is 0.1 °C warmer than December on average.

Warri in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Warri in December?
In December, Warri averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Warri?
No month in Warri is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Warri in December?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (464 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Warri in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Warri get in December?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Warri in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
