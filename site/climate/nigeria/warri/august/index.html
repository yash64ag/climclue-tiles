How August compares in Warri

August is the coolest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Warri. Compared with July: about as warm, 83 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. September is 0.2 °C warmer than August on average.

Warri in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Warri in August?
In August, Warri averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 379 mm (14.9 in) of precipitation and about 85 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Warri?
No month in Warri is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Warri in August?
379 mm (14.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (462 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Warri in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Warri get in August?
About 85 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Warri in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
