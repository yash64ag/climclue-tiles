How February compares in Warri

February is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Warri. Compared with January: 0.7 °C warmer, 28 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. March is 0.2 °C cooler than February on average.

Warri in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Warri in February?
In February, Warri averages a day high of 32.3 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Warri?
No month in Warri is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Warri in February?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (464 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Warri in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Warri get in February?
About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Warri in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
