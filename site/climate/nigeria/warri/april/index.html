How April compares in Warri

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Warri. Compared with March: 0.3 °C cooler, 80 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. May is 0.5 °C cooler than April on average.

Warri in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Warri in April?
In April, Warri averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 206 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation and about 152 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Warri?
No month in Warri is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Warri in April?
206 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (464 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Warri in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Warri get in April?
About 152 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Warri in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
