Warri weather in April

Average April weather in Warri, Nigeria: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Warri averages 31.0 °C (88 °F) days and 22.5 °C (73 °F) nights, with 204 mm (8.0 in) of rain over the month and about 152 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Warri

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Warri. Compared with March: 0.3 °C cooler, 79 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. May is 0.5 °C cooler than April on average.
