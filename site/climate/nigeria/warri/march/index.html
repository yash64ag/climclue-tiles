Warri in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Warri in March?
In March, Warri averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 126 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Warri?
No month in Warri is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Warri in March?
126 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (464 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Warri in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Warri get in March?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Warri in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
