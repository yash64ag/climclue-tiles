Buguma at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Buguma is the least sunny (about 1,369 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Port Harcourt, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Buguma is about as warm in February and about as warm in July, with 69 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.4 °C), averages 2.7 °C more than its coolest, July (24.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.8 °C (73 °F), in July. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Buguma, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Buguma has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
