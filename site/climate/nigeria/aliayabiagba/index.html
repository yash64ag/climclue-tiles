Aliayabiagba at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Aliayabiagba is 29th warmest (26.6 °C year average), 70th sunniest (about 1,879 hours of sunshine a year) and 49th wettest (1,605 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Surulere, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Aliayabiagba is 0.1 °C warmer in March and about as warm in August, with 154 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (27.9 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, August (24.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.7 °C (75 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Aliayabiagba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Aliayabiagba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
