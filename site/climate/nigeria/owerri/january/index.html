How January compares in Owerri

January is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Owerri. Compared with December: 0.4 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. February is 0.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Owerri in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Owerri in January?
In January, Owerri averages a day high of 32.8 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 21.2 °C (70 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Owerri?
No month in Owerri is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Owerri in January?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (412 mm), the driest December (9 mm).
Does it snow in Owerri in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Owerri get in January?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
