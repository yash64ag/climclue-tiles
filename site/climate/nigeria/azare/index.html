Azare at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Azare is 21st warmest (26.8 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 2,929 hours of sunshine a year) and 103rd wettest (730 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chakwama, the nearest covered town with different numbers (59 km away), Azare is about as warm in April and about as warm in January, with 63 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (31.6 °C), averages 9.9 °C more than its coolest, January (21.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.9 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 801 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Azare, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Azare has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 801 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
