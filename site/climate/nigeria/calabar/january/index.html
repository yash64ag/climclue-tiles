How January compares in Calabar

January is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Calabar. Compared with December: 0.2 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. February is 0.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Calabar in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Calabar in January?
In January, Calabar averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Calabar?
No month in Calabar is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Calabar in January?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (444 mm), the driest December (26 mm).
Does it snow in Calabar in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Calabar get in January?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Calabar in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
