Calabar weather in February

Average February weather in Calabar, Nigeria: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Calabar averages 32.7 °C (91 °F) days and 22.0 °C (72 °F) nights, with 33 mm (1.3 in) of rain over the month and about 152 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Calabar

February is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Calabar. Compared with January: 0.7 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. March is 0.2 °C cooler than February on average.

Calabar in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Calabar in February?
In February, Calabar averages a day high of 32.7 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 152 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Calabar?
No month in Calabar is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Calabar in February?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (444 mm), the driest December (26 mm).
Does it snow in Calabar in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Calabar get in February?
About 152 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Calabar in February?
About 28.5 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is April (28.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Calabar in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
