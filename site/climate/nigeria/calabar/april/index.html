How April compares in Calabar

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Calabar. Compared with March: 0.4 °C cooler, 68 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. May is 0.5 °C cooler than April on average.

Calabar in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Calabar in April?
In April, Calabar averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 231 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Calabar?
No month in Calabar is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Calabar in April?
231 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (452 mm), the driest December (27 mm).
Does it snow in Calabar in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Calabar get in April?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Calabar in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
