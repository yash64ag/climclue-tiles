How May compares in Akure

May is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Akure. Compared with April: 0.7 °C cooler, 21 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. June is 1.0 °C cooler than May on average.

Akure in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Akure in May?
In May, Akure averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.0 °C (70 °F), with 186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Akure?
No month in Akure is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Akure in May?
186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (263 mm), the driest December (9 mm).
Does it snow in Akure in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Akure get in May?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Akure in May?
