Akure weather in June

Average June weather in Akure, Nigeria: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Akure averages 27.9 °C (82 °F) days and 20.5 °C (69 °F) nights, with 219 mm (8.6 in) of rain over the month and about 148 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Akure

June is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Akure. Compared with May: 1.0 °C cooler, 33 mm more precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. July is 0.8 °C cooler than June on average.
