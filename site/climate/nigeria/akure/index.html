Akure at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Akure is 106th warmest (24.9 °C year average), 76th sunniest (about 1,878 hours of sunshine a year) and 52nd wettest (1,602 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ikere-Ekiti, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Akure is 0.2 °C cooler in February and about as warm in August, with 119 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.8 °C), averages 3.5 °C more than its coolest, August (23.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.1 °C (70 °F), in August. The area lies about 135 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Akure, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Akure has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 135 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
