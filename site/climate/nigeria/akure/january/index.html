How January compares in Akure

January is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Akure. Compared with December: 0.4 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. February is 1.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Akure in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Akure in January?
In January, Akure averages a day high of 31.8 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Akure?
No month in Akure is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Akure in January?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (263 mm), the driest December (9 mm).
Does it snow in Akure in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Akure get in January?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Akure in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
