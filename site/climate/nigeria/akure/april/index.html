How April compares in Akure

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Akure. Compared with March: 0.6 °C cooler, 68 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. May is 0.7 °C cooler than April on average.

Akure in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Akure in April?
In April, Akure averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 165 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Akure?
No month in Akure is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Akure in April?
165 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (263 mm), the driest December (9 mm).
Does it snow in Akure in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Akure get in April?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Akure in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
