Uyo at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Uyo is 75th warmest (25.8 °C year average), 104th sunniest (about 1,551 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (2,478 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ikot Ekpene, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Uyo is 0.1 °C warmer in February and 0.2 °C warmer in August, with 89 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.6 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, August (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.1 °C (72 °F), in July. The area lies about 67 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Uyo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Uyo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 67 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
