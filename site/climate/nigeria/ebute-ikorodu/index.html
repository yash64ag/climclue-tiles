Ebute Ikorodu at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Ebute Ikorodu is 35th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 83rd sunniest (about 1,866 hours of sunshine a year) and 41st wettest (1,691 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shomolu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Ebute Ikorodu is about as warm in March and about as warm in August, with 58 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (27.9 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, August (24.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.1 °C (74 °F), in August. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ebute Ikorodu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ebute Ikorodu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
