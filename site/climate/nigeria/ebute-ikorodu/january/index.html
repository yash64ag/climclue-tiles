Ebute Ikorodu weather in January

Average January weather in Ebute Ikorodu, Nigeria: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Ebute Ikorodu averages 31.8 °C (89 °F) days and 22.0 °C (72 °F) nights, with 14 mm (0.5 in) of rain over the month and about 168 hours of sunshine.
