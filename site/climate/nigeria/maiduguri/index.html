Maiduguri at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Maiduguri is the 4th sunniest (about 3,002 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th driest (574 mm a year). Compared with Bama, the nearest covered town with different numbers (68 km away), Maiduguri is 0.7 °C cooler in April and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 68 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (32.3 °C), averages 10.0 °C more than its coolest, January (22.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.2 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 949 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Maiduguri, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Maiduguri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 949 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
