How May compares in Maiduguri

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Maiduguri. Compared with April: 0.1 °C cooler, 25 mm more precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. June is 1.6 °C cooler than May on average.

Maiduguri in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maiduguri in May?
In May, Maiduguri averages a day high of 38.8 °C (102 °F) and a night low of 25.6 °C (78 °F), with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Maiduguri?
May is not one of Maiduguri’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maiduguri in May?
32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (184 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Maiduguri in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maiduguri get in May?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maiduguri in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
