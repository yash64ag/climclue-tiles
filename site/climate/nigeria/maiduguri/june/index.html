How June compares in Maiduguri

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Maiduguri. Compared with May: 1.6 °C cooler, 36 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. July is 2.8 °C cooler than June on average.

Maiduguri in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maiduguri in June?
In June, Maiduguri averages a day high of 36.5 °C (98 °F) and a night low of 25.7 °C (78 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Maiduguri?
June is not one of Maiduguri’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maiduguri in June?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (184 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Maiduguri in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maiduguri get in June?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maiduguri in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
