How July compares in Lagos

July is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Lagos. Compared with June: 1.0 °C cooler, 154 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Lagos in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lagos in July?
In July, Lagos averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 230 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation and about 104 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Lagos?
July is not one of Lagos’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lagos in July?
230 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (383 mm), the driest January (14 mm).
Does it snow in Lagos in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lagos get in July?
About 104 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Lagos in July?
About 26.3 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is May (29.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Lagos in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
