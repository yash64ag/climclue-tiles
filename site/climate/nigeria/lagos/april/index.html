How April compares in Lagos

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Lagos. Compared with March: 0.1 °C cooler, 73 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. May is 0.6 °C cooler than April on average.

Lagos in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lagos in April?
In April, Lagos averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 26.3 °C (79 °F), with 137 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Lagos?
April is not one of Lagos’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lagos in April?
137 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (379 mm), the driest January (14 mm).
Does it snow in Lagos in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lagos get in April?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lagos in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
