How December compares in Lagos

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Lagos. Compared with November: about as warm, 40 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. January is 0.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Lagos in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lagos in December?
In December, Lagos averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Lagos?
December is not one of Lagos’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lagos in December?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (383 mm), the driest January (14 mm).
Does it snow in Lagos in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lagos get in December?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lagos in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
