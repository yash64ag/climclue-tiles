How July compares in Port Harcourt

July is the coolest month, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Port Harcourt. Compared with June: 0.8 °C cooler, 71 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. August is about as warm as July on average.

Port Harcourt in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Port Harcourt in July?
In July, Port Harcourt averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 365 mm (14.4 in) of precipitation and about 76 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Port Harcourt?
No month in Port Harcourt is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Port Harcourt in July?
365 mm (14.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (365 mm), the driest December (19 mm).
Does it snow in Port Harcourt in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Port Harcourt get in July?
About 76 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
