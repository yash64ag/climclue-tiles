Port Harcourt weather in December

Average December weather in Port Harcourt, Nigeria: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Port Harcourt averages 31.5 °C (89 °F) days and 21.7 °C (71 °F) nights, with 19 mm (0.7 in) of rain over the month and about 155 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Port Harcourt

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Port Harcourt. Compared with November: 0.4 °C warmer, 57 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. January is 0.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Port Harcourt in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Port Harcourt in December?
In December, Port Harcourt averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Port Harcourt?
No month in Port Harcourt is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Port Harcourt in December?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (371 mm), the driest December (19 mm).
Does it snow in Port Harcourt in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Port Harcourt get in December?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Port Harcourt in December?
About 28.4 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is April (29.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Port Harcourt in December?
