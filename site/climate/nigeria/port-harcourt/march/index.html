How March compares in Port Harcourt

March is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Port Harcourt. Compared with February: 0.2 °C cooler, 57 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. April is 0.3 °C cooler than March on average.

Port Harcourt in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Port Harcourt in March?
In March, Port Harcourt averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 120 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Port Harcourt?
No month in Port Harcourt is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Port Harcourt in March?
109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (371 mm), the driest December (19 mm).
Does it snow in Port Harcourt in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Port Harcourt get in March?
About 120 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Port Harcourt in March?
About 29.0 °C (84 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is April (29.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Port Harcourt in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
