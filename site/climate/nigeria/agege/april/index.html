Agege in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Agege in April?
In April, Agege averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 155 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Agege?
No month in Agege is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
