Agege in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Agege in February?
In February, Agege averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 24.4 °C (76 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Agege?
No month in Agege is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
