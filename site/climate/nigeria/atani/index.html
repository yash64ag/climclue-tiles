Atani at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Atani is 23rd warmest (26.7 °C year average), 94th sunniest (about 1,760 hours of sunshine a year) and 27th wettest (1,782 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Onitsha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Atani is 0.2 °C cooler in February and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 30 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (28.9 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, July (25.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.7 °C (73 °F), in July. The area lies about 183 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Atani, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Atani has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 183 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
