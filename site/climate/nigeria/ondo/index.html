Ondo at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Ondo is 99th warmest (25.1 °C year average), 89th sunniest (about 1,830 hours of sunshine a year) and 36th wettest (1,725 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Akure, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Ondo is about as warm in February and 0.3 °C warmer in August, with 123 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.8 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, August (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.3 °C (70 °F), in January. The area lies about 99 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ondo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ondo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 99 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
