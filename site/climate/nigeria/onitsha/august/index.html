How August compares in Onitsha

August is the coolest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Onitsha. Compared with July: 0.1 °C cooler, 75 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. September is 0.3 °C warmer than August on average.

Onitsha in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Onitsha in August?
In August, Onitsha averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 216 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation and about 96 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Onitsha?
No month in Onitsha is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Onitsha in August?
216 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (315 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Onitsha in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Onitsha get in August?
About 96 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Onitsha in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
