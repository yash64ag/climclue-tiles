How December compares in Onitsha

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Onitsha. Compared with November: 0.7 °C warmer, 29 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. January is 0.5 °C warmer than December on average.

Onitsha in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Onitsha in December?
In December, Onitsha averages a day high of 32.6 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Onitsha?
No month in Onitsha is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Onitsha in December?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (315 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Onitsha in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Onitsha get in December?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Onitsha in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
