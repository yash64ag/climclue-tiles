Onitsha at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Onitsha is 19th warmest (26.9 °C year average), 92nd sunniest (about 1,776 hours of sunshine a year) and 34th wettest (1,753 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nkpor, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Onitsha is 0.4 °C warmer in February and 0.4 °C warmer in July, with 241 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (29.1 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, July (25.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.8 °C (73 °F), in July. The area lies about 188 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Onitsha, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Onitsha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 188 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
