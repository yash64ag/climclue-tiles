How November compares in Onitsha

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Onitsha. Compared with October: 1.0 °C warmer, 196 mm less precipitation and 39 hours more sunshine. December is 0.7 °C warmer than November on average.

Onitsha in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Onitsha in November?
In November, Onitsha averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Onitsha?
No month in Onitsha is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Onitsha in November?
34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (315 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Onitsha in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Onitsha get in November?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Onitsha in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
