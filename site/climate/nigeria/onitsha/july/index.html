How July compares in Onitsha

July is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Onitsha. Compared with June: 0.8 °C cooler, 44 mm more precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. August is 0.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Onitsha in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Onitsha in July?
In July, Onitsha averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 291 mm (11.5 in) of precipitation and about 102 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Onitsha?
No month in Onitsha is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Onitsha in July?
291 mm (11.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (315 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Onitsha in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Onitsha get in July?
About 102 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Onitsha in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
