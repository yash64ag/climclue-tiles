How October compares in Onitsha

October is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Onitsha. Compared with September: 0.6 °C warmer, 85 mm less precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. November is 0.9 °C warmer than October on average.

Onitsha in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Onitsha in October?
In October, Onitsha averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 222 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Onitsha?
No month in Onitsha is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Onitsha in October?
222 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (307 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Onitsha in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Onitsha get in October?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Onitsha in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
