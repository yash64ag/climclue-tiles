How April compares in Onitsha

April is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Onitsha. Compared with March: 0.8 °C cooler, 82 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. May is 0.8 °C cooler than April on average.

Onitsha in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Onitsha in April?
In April, Onitsha averages a day high of 32.4 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 141 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Onitsha?
No month in Onitsha is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Onitsha in April?
141 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (307 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Onitsha in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Onitsha get in April?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Onitsha in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
