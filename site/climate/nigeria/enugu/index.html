Enugu at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Enugu is 83rd warmest (25.7 °C year average), 62nd sunniest (about 1,917 hours of sunshine a year) and 11th wettest (2,373 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nsukka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Enugu is 0.1 °C warmer in February and 0.4 °C warmer in July, with 84 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.9 °C), averages 4.0 °C more than its coolest, July (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.3 °C (70 °F), in July. The area lies about 223 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Enugu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Enugu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 223 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
