How September compares in Enugu

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Enugu. Compared with August: 0.3 °C warmer, 90 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. October is 0.5 °C warmer than September on average.

Enugu in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Enugu in September?
In September, Enugu averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 391 mm (15.4 in) of precipitation and about 113 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Enugu?
No month in Enugu is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Enugu in September?
391 mm (15.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (391 mm), the driest December (9 mm).
Does it snow in Enugu in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Enugu get in September?
About 113 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Enugu in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
