How November compares in Enugu

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Enugu. Compared with October: 1.1 °C warmer, 247 mm less precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. December is 0.6 °C warmer than November on average.

Enugu in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Enugu in November?
In November, Enugu averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 27 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Enugu?
No month in Enugu is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Enugu in November?
27 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (334 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Enugu in November?
