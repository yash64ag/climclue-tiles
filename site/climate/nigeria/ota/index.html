Ota at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Ota is 51st warmest (26.3 °C year average), 66th sunniest (about 1,904 hours of sunshine a year) and 35th wettest (1,742 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Alimosho, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Ota is 0.1 °C cooler in March and 0.1 °C cooler in August, with 24 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (27.8 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, August (24.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.5 °C (73 °F), in August. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ota, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ota has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
