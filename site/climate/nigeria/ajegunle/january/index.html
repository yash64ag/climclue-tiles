How January compares in Ajegunle

January is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Ajegunle. Compared with December: 0.3 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. February is 0.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Ajegunle in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ajegunle in January?
In January, Ajegunle averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Ajegunle?
January is not one of Ajegunle’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ajegunle in January?
14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (379 mm), the driest January (14 mm).
Does it snow in Ajegunle in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ajegunle get in January?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ajegunle in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
