How November compares in Ajegunle

November is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Ajegunle. Compared with October: 1.1 °C warmer, 106 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. December is about as warm as November on average.

Ajegunle in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ajegunle in November?
In November, Ajegunle averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Ajegunle?
November is not one of Ajegunle’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ajegunle in November?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (379 mm), the driest January (14 mm).
Does it snow in Ajegunle in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ajegunle get in November?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ajegunle in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
