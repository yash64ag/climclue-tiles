How July compares in Ajegunle

July is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Ajegunle. Compared with June: 1.0 °C cooler, 155 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Ajegunle in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ajegunle in July?
In July, Ajegunle averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 224 mm (8.8 in) of precipitation and about 104 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Ajegunle?
July is not one of Ajegunle’s mild months; those are August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ajegunle in July?
224 mm (8.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (379 mm), the driest January (14 mm).
Does it snow in Ajegunle in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ajegunle get in July?
About 104 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ajegunle in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
