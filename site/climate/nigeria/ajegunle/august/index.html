How August compares in Ajegunle

August is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Ajegunle. Compared with July: 0.4 °C cooler, 129 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. September is 0.5 °C warmer than August on average.

Ajegunle in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ajegunle in August?
In August, Ajegunle averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 23.7 °C (75 °F), with 95 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 111 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Ajegunle?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Ajegunle’s mild months (August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ajegunle in August?
95 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (379 mm), the driest January (14 mm).
Does it snow in Ajegunle in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ajegunle get in August?
About 111 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ajegunle in August?
