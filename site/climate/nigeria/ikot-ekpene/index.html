Ikot Ekpene at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Ikot Ekpene is 86th warmest (25.6 °C year average), 105th sunniest (about 1,546 hours of sunshine a year) and 10th wettest (2,389 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Uyo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Ikot Ekpene is 0.1 °C cooler in February and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 89 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.4 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, July (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.7 °C (71 °F), in July. The area lies about 78 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ikot Ekpene, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ikot Ekpene has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 78 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
