Mubi at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Mubi is 90th warmest (25.5 °C year average), 10th sunniest (about 2,895 hours of sunshine a year) and 73rd wettest (1,341 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jimeta, the nearest covered town with different numbers (141 km away), Mubi is 2.8 °C cooler in April and 3.5 °C cooler in January, with 531 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (30.4 °C), averages 7.6 °C more than its coolest, January (22.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.4 °C (60 °F), in January. The area lies about 807 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mubi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mubi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 807 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
