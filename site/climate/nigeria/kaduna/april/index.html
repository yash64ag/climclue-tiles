How April compares in Kaduna

April is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Kaduna. Compared with March: 0.8 °C warmer, 36 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. May is 1.9 °C cooler than April on average.

Kaduna in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kaduna in April?
In April, Kaduna averages a day high of 35.5 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kaduna?
April is not one of Kaduna’s mild months; those are January, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kaduna in April?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (312 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kaduna in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kaduna get in April?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kaduna in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
