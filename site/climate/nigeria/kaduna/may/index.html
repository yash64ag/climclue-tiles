How May compares in Kaduna

May is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kaduna. Compared with April: 2.0 °C cooler, 72 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. June is 2.3 °C cooler than May on average.

Kaduna in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kaduna in May?
In May, Kaduna averages a day high of 32.6 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kaduna?
May is not one of Kaduna’s mild months; those are January, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kaduna in May?
116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (312 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kaduna in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kaduna get in May?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kaduna in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
