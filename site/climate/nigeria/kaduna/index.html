Kaduna at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Kaduna is 101st warmest (25.0 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 2,685 hours of sunshine a year) and 83rd wettest (1,203 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zaria, the nearest covered town with different numbers (72 km away), Kaduna is 0.6 °C cooler in April and 1.3 °C warmer in January, with 211 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.3 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, January (23.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.5 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 581 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kaduna, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kaduna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 581 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
