How July compares in Kaduna

July is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kaduna. Compared with June: 1.4 °C cooler, 58 mm more precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Kaduna in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kaduna in July?
In July, Kaduna averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 233 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Kaduna?
July is not one of Kaduna’s mild months; those are January, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kaduna in July?
233 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (312 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kaduna in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kaduna get in July?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kaduna in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
