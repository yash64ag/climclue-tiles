Kaduna weather in October

Average October weather in Kaduna, Nigeria: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Kaduna averages 30.4 °C (87 °F) days and 18.0 °C (64 °F) nights, with 72 mm (2.8 in) of rain over the month and about 243 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Kaduna

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Kaduna. Compared with September: 0.6 °C warmer, 171 mm less precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. November is 0.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Kaduna in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kaduna in October?
In October, Kaduna averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 18.0 °C (64 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kaduna?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Kaduna’s mild months (January, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kaduna in October?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (312 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kaduna in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kaduna get in October?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kaduna in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
