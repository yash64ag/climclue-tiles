How September compares in Osogbo

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Osogbo. Compared with August: 0.4 °C warmer, 92 mm more precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. October is 0.6 °C warmer than September on average.

Osogbo in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Osogbo in September?
In September, Osogbo averages a day high of 27.8 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 243 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation and about 107 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Osogbo?
No month in Osogbo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Osogbo in September?
243 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (243 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Osogbo in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Osogbo get in September?
About 107 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Osogbo in September?
