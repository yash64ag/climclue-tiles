How August compares in Osogbo

August is the coolest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Osogbo. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 36 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. September is 0.4 °C warmer than August on average.

Osogbo in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Osogbo in August?
In August, Osogbo averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 21.2 °C (70 °F), with 152 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 88 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Osogbo?
No month in Osogbo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Osogbo in August?
152 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (245 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Osogbo in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Osogbo get in August?
About 88 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Osogbo in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
