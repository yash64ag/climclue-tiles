How April compares in Osogbo

April is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Osogbo. Compared with March: 0.7 °C cooler, 52 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. May is 0.8 °C cooler than April on average.

Osogbo in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Osogbo in April?
In April, Osogbo averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Osogbo?
No month in Osogbo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Osogbo in April?
132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (243 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Osogbo in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Osogbo get in April?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Osogbo in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
