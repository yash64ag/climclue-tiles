How July compares in Osogbo

July is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Osogbo. Compared with June: 0.9 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 52 hours less sunshine. August is 0.2 °C cooler than July on average.

Osogbo in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Osogbo in July?
In July, Osogbo averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 187 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation and about 110 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Osogbo?
No month in Osogbo is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Osogbo in July?
187 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (245 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Osogbo in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Osogbo get in July?
About 110 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Osogbo in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
