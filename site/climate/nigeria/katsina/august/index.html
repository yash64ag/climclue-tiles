How August compares in Katsina

August is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Katsina. Compared with July: 1.1 °C cooler, 38 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. September is 1.0 °C warmer than August on average.

Katsina in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Katsina in August?
In August, Katsina averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 196 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Katsina?
August is not one of Katsina’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Katsina in August?
196 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (196 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Katsina in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Katsina get in August?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Katsina in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
