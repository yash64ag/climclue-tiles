How May compares in Katsina

May is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Katsina. Compared with April: 0.4 °C warmer, 27 mm more precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. June is 1.8 °C cooler than May on average.

Katsina in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Katsina in May?
In May, Katsina averages a day high of 37.9 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 260 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Katsina?
May is not one of Katsina’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Katsina in May?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (196 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Katsina in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Katsina get in May?
About 260 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Katsina in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
