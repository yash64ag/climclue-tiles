Why Katsina has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 823 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Katsina climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Katsina?
May, with an average of 31.4 °C (88 °F), day highs around 37.9 °C (100 °F) and night lows around 24.3 °C (76 °F).
What is the coldest month in Katsina?
January, with an average of 20.8 °C (69 °F), day highs around 28.4 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 13.6 °C (56 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Katsina?
August, with 196 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 578 mm (22.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Katsina?
January, with 0 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Katsina?
October, with about 265 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Katsina?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Katsina?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Katsina’s GeoNames location (12.991°N, 7.602°E; ClimaClue area sq-710-1876), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 670,000 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
