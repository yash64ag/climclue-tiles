Katsina at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Katsina is 44th warmest (26.4 °C year average), 9th sunniest (about 2,913 hours of sunshine a year) and 106th wettest (578 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gusau, the nearest covered town with different numbers (137 km away), Katsina is 0.4 °C warmer in May and 1.8 °C cooler in January, with 320 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.4 °C), averages 10.6 °C more than its coolest, January (20.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.6 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 823 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Katsina, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Katsina has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 823 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
