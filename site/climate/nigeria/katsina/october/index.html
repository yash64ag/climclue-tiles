How October compares in Katsina

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Katsina. Compared with September: 0.7 °C warmer, 85 mm less precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. November is 2.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Katsina in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Katsina in October?
In October, Katsina averages a day high of 34.2 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 20.7 °C (69 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 265 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Katsina?
October is not one of Katsina’s mild months; those are January, February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Katsina in October?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (196 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Katsina in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Katsina get in October?
About 265 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Katsina in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
