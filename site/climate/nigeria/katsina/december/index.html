How December compares in Katsina

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Katsina. Compared with November: 3.1 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. January is 0.8 °C cooler than December on average.
