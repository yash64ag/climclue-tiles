How June compares in Sokoto

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Sokoto. Compared with May: 2.3 °C cooler, 37 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. July is 2.8 °C cooler than June on average.

Sokoto in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sokoto in June?
In June, Sokoto averages a day high of 36.9 °C (98 °F) and a night low of 26.4 °C (80 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 245 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Sokoto?
June is not one of Sokoto’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sokoto in June?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (186 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Sokoto in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sokoto get in June?
About 245 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sokoto in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
