How August compares in Sokoto

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Sokoto. Compared with July: 1.3 °C cooler, 12 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. September is 1.0 °C warmer than August on average.

Sokoto in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sokoto in August?
In August, Sokoto averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Sokoto?
August is not one of Sokoto’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sokoto in August?
186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (186 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Sokoto in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sokoto get in August?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sokoto in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
