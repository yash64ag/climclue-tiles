Ogbia weather in October

Average October weather in Ogbia, Nigeria: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Ogbia averages 29.1 °C (84 °F) days and 21.8 °C (71 °F) nights, with 328 mm (12.9 in) of rain over the month and about 105 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Ogbia

October is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Ogbia. Compared with September: 0.5 °C warmer, 93 mm less precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. November is 0.7 °C warmer than October on average.
