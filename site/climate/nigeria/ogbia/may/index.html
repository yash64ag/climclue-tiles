How May compares in Ogbia

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Ogbia. Compared with April: 0.5 °C cooler, 85 mm more precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. June is 1.0 °C cooler than May on average.
