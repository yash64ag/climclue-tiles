How February compares in Ogbia

February is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Ogbia. Compared with January: 0.7 °C warmer, 26 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. March is 0.2 °C cooler than February on average.

Ogbia in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ogbia in February?
In February, Ogbia averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Ogbia?
No month in Ogbia is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ogbia in February?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (414 mm), the driest December (27 mm).
Does it snow in Ogbia in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ogbia get in February?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ogbia in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
