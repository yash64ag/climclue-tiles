How August compares in Jos

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Jos. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 23 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. September is 0.6 °C warmer than August on average.

Jos in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jos in August?
In August, Jos averages a day high of 24.0 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 17.2 °C (63 °F), with 284 mm (11.2 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Jos?
August is not one of Jos’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jos in August?
284 mm (11.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (284 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Jos in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jos get in August?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jos in August?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
