Jos at a glance

Among the 111 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nigeria, Jos is the coolest (21.4 °C year average). Compared with Bauchi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (113 km away), Jos is 4.8 °C cooler in April and 2.0 °C cooler in January, with 203 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (24.9 °C), averages 5.5 °C more than its coolest, January (19.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.9 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 591 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jos, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jos has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 591 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
