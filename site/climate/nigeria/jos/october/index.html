How October compares in Jos

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Jos. Compared with September: 0.7 °C warmer, 129 mm less precipitation and 59 hours more sunshine. November is 0.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Jos in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jos in October?
In October, Jos averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 15.8 °C (60 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Jos?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Jos’s mild months (January, February, March, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jos in October?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (266 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Jos in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jos get in October?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jos in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
