How April compares in Jos

April is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Jos. Compared with March: 0.8 °C warmer, 78 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. May is 1.8 °C cooler than April on average.

Jos in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jos in April?
In April, Jos averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 19.1 °C (66 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Jos?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Jos’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jos in April?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (284 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Jos in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jos get in April?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jos in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
