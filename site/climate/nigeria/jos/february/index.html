How February compares in Jos

February is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Jos. Compared with January: 2.1 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. March is 2.6 °C warmer than February on average.

Jos in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jos in February?
In February, Jos averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 14.5 °C (58 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Jos?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Jos’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jos in February?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (284 mm), the driest January (0 mm).
Does it snow in Jos in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jos get in February?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
