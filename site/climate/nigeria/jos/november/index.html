How November compares in Jos

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Jos. Compared with October: 0.3 °C cooler, 56 mm less precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. December is 1.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Jos in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jos in November?
In November, Jos averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 279 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Jos?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Jos’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jos in November?

Does it snow in Jos in November?

How many hours of sunshine does Jos get in November?
