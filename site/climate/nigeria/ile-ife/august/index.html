How August compares in Ile-Ife

August is the coolest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Ile-Ife. Compared with July: 0.1 °C cooler, 48 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. September is 0.4 °C warmer than August on average.

Ile-Ife in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ile-Ife in August?
In August, Ile-Ife averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 156 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 83 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Ile-Ife?
No month in Ile-Ife is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ile-Ife in August?
156 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (258 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Ile-Ife in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ile-Ife get in August?
About 83 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ile-Ife in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
