How December compares in Ile-Ife

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Ile-Ife. Compared with November: 0.3 °C warmer, 31 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. January is 0.4 °C warmer than December on average.

Ile-Ife in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ile-Ife in December?
In December, Ile-Ife averages a day high of 32.3 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ile-Ife?
No month in Ile-Ife is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ile-Ife in December?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (258 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Ile-Ife in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ile-Ife get in December?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ile-Ife in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
