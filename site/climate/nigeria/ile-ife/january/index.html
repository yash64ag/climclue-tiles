How January compares in Ile-Ife

January is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Ile-Ife. Compared with December: 0.4 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. February is 1.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Ile-Ife in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ile-Ife in January?
In January, Ile-Ife averages a day high of 33.6 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Ile-Ife?
No month in Ile-Ife is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ile-Ife in January?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (258 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Ile-Ife in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ile-Ife get in January?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ile-Ife in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
