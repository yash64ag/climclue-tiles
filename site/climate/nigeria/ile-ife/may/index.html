How May compares in Ile-Ife

May is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Ile-Ife. Compared with April: 0.8 °C cooler, 30 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. June is 0.9 °C cooler than May on average.

Ile-Ife in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ile-Ife in May?
In May, Ile-Ife averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 183 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Ile-Ife?
No month in Ile-Ife is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ile-Ife in May?
183 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (258 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Ile-Ife in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ile-Ife get in May?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ile-Ife in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
