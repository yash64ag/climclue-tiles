How April compares in Ile-Ife

April is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ile-Ife. Compared with March: 0.7 °C cooler, 64 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. May is 0.8 °C cooler than April on average.

Ile-Ife in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ile-Ife in April?
In April, Ile-Ife averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ile-Ife?
No month in Ile-Ife is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ile-Ife in April?
153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (258 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Ile-Ife in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ile-Ife get in April?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ile-Ife in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
