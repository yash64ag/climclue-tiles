How March compares in Ile-Ife

March is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Ile-Ife. Compared with February: 0.4 °C cooler, 55 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. April is 0.7 °C cooler than March on average.

Ile-Ife in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ile-Ife in March?
In March, Ile-Ife averages a day high of 33.0 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Ile-Ife?
No month in Ile-Ife is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ile-Ife in March?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (258 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Ile-Ife in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ile-Ife get in March?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ile-Ife in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
