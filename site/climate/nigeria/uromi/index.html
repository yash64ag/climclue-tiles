Uromi at a glance

Among the 269 towns ClimaClue covers in Nigeria, Uromi is 229th warmest (25.2 °C year average), 197th sunniest (about 1,884 hours of sunshine a year) and 36th wettest (2,205 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ubiaja, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Uromi is 0.6 °C cooler in February and 0.5 °C cooler in August, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.2 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, August (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.9 °C (68 °F), in December. The area lies about 188 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Uromi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Uromi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 188 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Uromi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (6.700°N, 6.333°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-772-1863), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
