Soshanguve in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Soshanguve in December?
In December, Soshanguve averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Soshanguve?
December is not one of Soshanguve’s mild months; those are February, March, April, May, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Soshanguve in December?
112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (119 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Soshanguve in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Soshanguve get in December?
About 278 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Soshanguve in December?
