In June, Soshanguve averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 6.0 °C (43 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 271 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Soshanguve?
June is not one of Soshanguve’s mild months; those are February, March, April, May, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
