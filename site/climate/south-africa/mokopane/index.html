Mokopane at a glance

Among the 71 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Africa, Mokopane is the 4th driest (491 mm a year). Compared with Polokwane, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Mokopane is 1.1 °C warmer in January and 0.5 °C warmer in July, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (22.9 °C), averages 10.2 °C more than its coolest, July (12.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.2 °C (43 °F), in July. The area lies about 422 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mokopane, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mokopane has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 422 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
