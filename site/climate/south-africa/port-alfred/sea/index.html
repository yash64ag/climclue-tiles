Port Alfred sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Eastern Cape · South Africa

The sea off Port Alfred averages 21.6 °C (71 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 18.2 °C (65 °F) in September. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from December to May.

Port Alfred sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Port Alfred?
In February, at about 21.6 °C (71 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Port Alfred?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from December to May. The coolest month is September, at 18.2 °C (65 °F).
How warm is the sea in Port Alfred in September?
About 18.2 °C (65 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Port Alfred?
About 19.8 °C (68 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Port Alfred nearby?
In February: Mdantsane (24.1 °C, 110 km), East London (24.1 °C, 115 km) and Gonubie (24.1 °C, 127 km).
