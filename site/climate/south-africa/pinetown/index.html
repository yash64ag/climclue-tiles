Pinetown at a glance

Among the 71 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Africa, Pinetown is the least sunny (about 2,460 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Ntuzuma, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Pinetown is 0.6 °C cooler in February and 0.8 °C cooler in July, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.4 °C), averages 7.7 °C more than its coolest, July (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.8 °C (51 °F), in July. The area lies about 29 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pinetown, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pinetown has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 29 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
