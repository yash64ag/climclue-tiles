Klerksdorp at a glance

Among the 71 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Africa, Klerksdorp is the 3rd sunniest (about 3,349 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Orkney, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Klerksdorp is 0.1 °C cooler in January and about as warm in July, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (22.9 °C), averages 12.9 °C more than its coolest, July (10.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 3.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 546 km from the sea.

Why Klerksdorp has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 546 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
