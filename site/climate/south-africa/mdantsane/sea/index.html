Mdantsane sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Eastern Cape · South Africa

The sea off Mdantsane averages 24.1 °C (75 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 19.8 °C (68 °F) in September. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from February to March.

Mdantsane sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Mdantsane?
In February, at about 24.1 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Mdantsane?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from February to March. The coolest month is September, at 19.8 °C (68 °F).
How warm is the sea in Mdantsane in September?
About 19.8 °C (68 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Mdantsane?
About 21.9 °C (71 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Mdantsane nearby?
In February: Margate (25.5 °C, 336 km), Port Shepstone (25.7 °C, 351 km) and eMuziwezinto (25.6 °C, 400 km).
