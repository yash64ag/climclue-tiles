Mdantsane at a glance

Among the 71 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Africa, Mdantsane is 17th warmest (18.5 °C year average), 64th sunniest (about 2,591 hours of sunshine a year) and 13th wettest (877 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with East London, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Mdantsane is 0.6 °C cooler in February and 1.5 °C cooler in July, with 71 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.1 °C), averages 7.0 °C more than its coolest, July (15.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.7 °C (51 °F), in July. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: a NOAA weather station 12 km from Mdantsane measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 1.9 °C (3.3 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Mdantsane has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
