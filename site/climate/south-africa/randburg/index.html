Why Randburg has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 469 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Randburg climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Randburg?
January, with an average of 20.9 °C (70 °F), day highs around 25.8 °C (78 °F) and night lows around 15.6 °C (60 °F).
What is the coldest month in Randburg?
June, with an average of 10.2 °C (50 °F), day highs around 17.2 °C (63 °F) and night lows around 4.2 °C (40 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Randburg?
January, with 118 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 663 mm (26.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Randburg?
July, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Randburg?
August, with about 297 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Randburg?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Randburg?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: March, April, September, October and November.
What type of climate does Randburg have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Randburg’s GeoNames location (26.094°S, 28.001°E; ClimaClue area sq-1100-2080), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 337,053 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
