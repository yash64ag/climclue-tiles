Moorreesburg at a glance

Among the 298 towns ClimaClue covers in South Africa, Moorreesburg is 151st warmest (17.1 °C year average), 96th sunniest (about 3,184 hours of sunshine a year) and 270th wettest (478 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Malmesbury, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Moorreesburg is 0.2 °C warmer in February and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 141 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (23.2 °C), averages 12.3 °C more than its coolest, July (10.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.7 °C (42 °F), in July. The area lies about 57 km from the sea.

Why Moorreesburg has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 57 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Moorreesburg itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.154°S, 18.660°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1171-1986), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
