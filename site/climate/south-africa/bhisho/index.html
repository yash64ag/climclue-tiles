Why Bhisho has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 50 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Bhisho climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Bhisho?
February, with an average of 21.1 °C (70 °F), day highs around 26.4 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 16.6 °C (62 °F).
What is the coldest month in Bhisho?
June, with an average of 13.4 °C (56 °F), day highs around 20.1 °C (68 °F) and night lows around 7.9 °C (46 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Bhisho?
November, with 108 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 757 mm (29.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Bhisho?
July, with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Bhisho?
December, with about 250 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Bhisho?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Bhisho?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March, April, May, September, October and December.
What type of climate does Bhisho have?
Oceanic (Köppen Cfb). Mild all year, with rain in every season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.4 × 11 km, that contains Bhisho’s GeoNames location (32.847°S, 27.442°E; ClimaClue area sq-1168-2074), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 137,287 (GeoNames).
