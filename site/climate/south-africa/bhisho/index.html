Bhisho at a glance

Among the 71 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Africa, Bhisho is 31st warmest (17.2 °C year average), 63rd sunniest (about 2,611 hours of sunshine a year) and 20th wettest (757 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mdantsane, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Bhisho is 1.0 °C cooler in February and 2.0 °C cooler in June, with 120 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (21.1 °C), averages 7.7 °C more than its coolest, June (13.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.5 °C (46 °F), in July. The area lies about 50 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bhisho, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bhisho has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 50 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
