Why Kroonstad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 446 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Kroonstad climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Kroonstad?
January, with an average of 22.4 °C (72 °F), day highs around 28.5 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 16.4 °C (62 °F).
What is the coldest month in Kroonstad?
June, with an average of 9.1 °C (48 °F), day highs around 17.0 °C (63 °F) and night lows around 2.8 °C (37 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Kroonstad?
January, with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 566 mm (22.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Kroonstad?
July, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Kroonstad?
August, with about 306 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Kroonstad?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Kroonstad?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, April, September, October, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.9 × 11 km, that contains Kroonstad’s GeoNames location (27.650°S, 27.235°E; ClimaClue area sq-1116-2072), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 117,152 (GeoNames).
