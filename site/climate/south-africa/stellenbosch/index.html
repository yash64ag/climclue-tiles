Stellenbosch at a glance

Among the 298 towns ClimaClue covers in South Africa, Stellenbosch is the wettest (2,275 mm a year). Compared with Kuils River, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Stellenbosch is 0.6 °C warmer in February and 0.7 °C cooler in July, with 1,106 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (21.4 °C), averages 9.6 °C more than its coolest, July (11.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.5 °C (44 °F), in July. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Stellenbosch, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

For mild weather, the best months to visit Stellenbosch are January, February, March and December.

Why Stellenbosch has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Stellenbosch itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.935°S, 18.867°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1179-1988), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
