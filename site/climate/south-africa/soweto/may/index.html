How May compares in Soweto

May is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Soweto. Compared with April: 3.3 °C cooler, 24 mm less precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. June is 3.0 °C cooler than May on average.

Soweto in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Soweto in May?
In May, Soweto averages a day high of 18.7 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 6.4 °C (44 °F), with 14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 272 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Soweto?
May is not one of Soweto’s mild months; those are March, April, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Soweto in May?
14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (128 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Soweto in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Soweto get in May?
About 272 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Soweto in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
