How March compares in Soweto

March is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Soweto. Compared with February: 1.4 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. April is 2.8 °C cooler than March on average.

Soweto in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Soweto in March?
In March, Soweto averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 12.9 °C (55 °F), with 93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Soweto?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Soweto’s mild months (March, April, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Soweto in March?
93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (128 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Soweto in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Soweto get in March?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Soweto in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
