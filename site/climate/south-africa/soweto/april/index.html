How April compares in Soweto

April is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Soweto. Compared with March: 2.8 °C cooler, 53 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. May is 3.3 °C cooler than April on average.

Soweto in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Soweto in April?
In April, Soweto averages a day high of 21.9 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 9.1 °C (48 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Soweto?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Soweto’s mild months (March, April, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Soweto in April?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (123 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Soweto in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Soweto get in April?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Soweto in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
