How December compares in Soweto

December is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Soweto. Compared with November: 0.8 °C warmer, 29 mm more precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. January is 0.5 °C warmer than December on average.

Soweto in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Soweto in December?
In December, Soweto averages a day high of 24.7 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 275 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Soweto?
December is not one of Soweto’s mild months; those are March, April, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Soweto in December?
123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (128 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Soweto in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Soweto get in December?
About 275 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Soweto in December?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
