Why Kimberley has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 561 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Kimberley climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Kimberley?
January, with an average of 25.3 °C (77 °F), day highs around 32.2 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 18.2 °C (65 °F).
What is the coldest month in Kimberley?
June, with an average of 9.8 °C (50 °F), day highs around 17.9 °C (64 °F) and night lows around 3.8 °C (39 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Kimberley?
February, with 75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 464 mm (18.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Kimberley?
July, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Kimberley?
December, with about 330 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Kimberley?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Kimberley?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March, April, September, October, November and December.
What type of climate does Kimberley have?
Hot semi-arid (Köppen BSh). Dry but not desert: hot, with a short and unreliable rainy season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.8 × 11 km, that contains Kimberley’s GeoNames location (28.732°S, 24.762°E; ClimaClue area sq-1127-2047), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 142,089 (GeoNames).
