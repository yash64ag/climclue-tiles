Mabopane at a glance

Among the 71 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Africa, Mabopane is 15th warmest (18.7 °C year average), 10th sunniest (about 3,264 hours of sunshine a year) and 49th wettest (638 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Soshanguve, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Mabopane is 0.6 °C cooler in January and 0.4 °C cooler in June, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (23.4 °C), averages 11.5 °C more than its coolest, June (11.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.2 °C (41 °F), in July. The area lies about 473 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mabopane, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mabopane has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 473 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
