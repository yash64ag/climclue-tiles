Hermanus sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Western Cape · South Africa

The sea off Hermanus averages 18.9 °C (66 °F) in January, its warmest month, and 14.9 °C (59 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Hermanus sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Hermanus?
In January, at about 18.9 °C (66 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Hermanus?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 14.9 °C (59 °F).
How warm is the sea in Hermanus in August?
About 14.9 °C (59 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Hermanus?
About 16.6 °C (62 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Hermanus nearby?
In January: Gansbaai (19.6 °C, 21 km), Mossel Bay (21.0 °C, 269 km) and Groot-Brakrivier (21.0 °C, 278 km).
