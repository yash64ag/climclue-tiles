Kraaifontein at a glance

Among the 298 towns ClimaClue covers in South Africa, Kraaifontein is 189th warmest (16.6 °C year average), 139th sunniest (about 3,095 hours of sunshine a year) and 19th wettest (1,107 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Durbanville, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Kraaifontein is 0.4 °C warmer in February and 0.3 °C warmer in July, with 245 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (21.5 °C), averages 9.7 °C more than its coolest, July (11.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.6 °C (44 °F), in July. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kraaifontein, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kraaifontein has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kraaifontein itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.848°S, 18.717°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1178-1987), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
