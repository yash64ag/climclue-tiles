Gqeberha at a glance

Among the 71 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Africa, Gqeberha is 19th warmest (18.3 °C year average), 60th sunniest (about 2,739 hours of sunshine a year) and 63rd wettest (558 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Motherwell, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Gqeberha is 0.9 °C cooler in February and 1.0 °C warmer in July, with 52 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (21.4 °C), averages 5.9 °C more than its coolest, July (15.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.9 °C (55 °F), in July. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gqeberha, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gqeberha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
