How December compares in Gqeberha

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Gqeberha. Compared with November: 1.5 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. January is 1.1 °C warmer than December on average.

Gqeberha in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gqeberha in December?
In December, Gqeberha averages a day high of 24.6 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 277 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Gqeberha?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Gqeberha’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gqeberha in December?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (63 mm), the driest September (36 mm).
Does it snow in Gqeberha in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gqeberha get in December?
About 277 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Gqeberha in December?
About 19.7 °C (67 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is February (20.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Gqeberha in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
