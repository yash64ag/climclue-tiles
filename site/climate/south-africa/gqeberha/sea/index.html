Gqeberha sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Eastern Cape · South Africa

The sea off Gqeberha averages 20.8 °C (69 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 17.3 °C (63 °F) in September. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from January to March.

Gqeberha sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Gqeberha?
In February, at about 20.8 °C (69 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Gqeberha?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from January to March. The coolest month is September, at 17.3 °C (63 °F).
How warm is the sea in Gqeberha in September?
About 17.3 °C (63 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Gqeberha?
About 18.8 °C (66 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Gqeberha nearby?
In February: Port Alfred (21.6 °C, 124 km), Mdantsane (24.1 °C, 231 km) and East London (24.1 °C, 238 km).
