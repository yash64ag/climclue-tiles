In June, Gqeberha averages a day high of 19.2 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 13.3 °C (56 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Gqeberha?
