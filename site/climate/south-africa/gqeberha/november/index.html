Gqeberha in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gqeberha in November?
In November, Gqeberha averages a day high of 20.5 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 16.6 °C (62 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Gqeberha?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Gqeberha’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gqeberha in November?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (63 mm), the driest September (36 mm).
Does it snow in Gqeberha in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gqeberha get in November?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
