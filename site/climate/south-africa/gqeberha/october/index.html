Gqeberha in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gqeberha in October?
In October, Gqeberha averages a day high of 19.4 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Gqeberha?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Gqeberha’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. How much does it rain in Gqeberha in October?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (63 mm), the driest September (36 mm).
Does it snow in Gqeberha in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gqeberha get in October?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
