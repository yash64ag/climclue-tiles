How October compares in Gqeberha

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Gqeberha. Compared with September: 1.1 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. November is 1.3 °C warmer than October on average.

Gqeberha in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gqeberha in October?
In October, Gqeberha averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 12.8 °C (55 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Gqeberha?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Gqeberha’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gqeberha in October?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (63 mm), the driest September (36 mm).
Does it snow in Gqeberha in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gqeberha get in October?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Gqeberha in October?
About 17.6 °C (64 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is February (20.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Gqeberha in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
