Gqeberha in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gqeberha in September?
In September, Gqeberha averages a day high of 18.8 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 13.9 °C (57 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Gqeberha?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Gqeberha’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation.
