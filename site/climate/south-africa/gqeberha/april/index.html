In April, Gqeberha averages a day high of 21.4 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 17.0 °C (63 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Gqeberha?
