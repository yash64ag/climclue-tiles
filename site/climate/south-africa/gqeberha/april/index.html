How April compares in Gqeberha

April is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Gqeberha. Compared with March: 1.5 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. May is 1.4 °C cooler than April on average.

Gqeberha in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gqeberha in April?
In April, Gqeberha averages a day high of 24.0 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 13.9 °C (57 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Gqeberha?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Gqeberha’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. How much does it rain in Gqeberha in April?

Does it snow in Gqeberha in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
