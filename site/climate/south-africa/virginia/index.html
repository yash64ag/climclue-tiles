Why Virginia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 453 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Virginia climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Virginia?
January, with an average of 23.3 °C (74 °F), day highs around 29.6 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 17.0 °C (63 °F).
What is the coldest month in Virginia?
July, with an average of 9.2 °C (48 °F), day highs around 17.4 °C (63 °F) and night lows around 2.3 °C (36 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Virginia?
January, with 90 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 568 mm (22.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Virginia?
July, with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Virginia?
December, with about 299 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Virginia?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Virginia?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, April, September, October, November and December.
What type of climate does Virginia have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.8 × 11 km, that contains Virginia’s GeoNames location (28.104°S, 26.866°E; ClimaClue area sq-1121-2068), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 122,502 (GeoNames).
