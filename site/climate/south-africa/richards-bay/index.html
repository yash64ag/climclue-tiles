Richards Bay at a glance

Among the 71 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Africa, Richards Bay is the 2nd warmest (22.1 °C year average) and the 4th wettest (1,150 mm a year). Compared with KwaDukuza, the nearest covered town with different numbers (95 km away), Richards Bay is 1.0 °C warmer in February and 1.6 °C warmer in July, with 160 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (25.4 °C), averages 6.8 °C more than its coolest, July (18.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.3 °C (60 °F), in July. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Richards Bay, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Richards Bay has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
