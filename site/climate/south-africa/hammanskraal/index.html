Hammanskraal at a glance

Among the 71 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Africa, Hammanskraal is 9th warmest (19.4 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 3,304 hours of sunshine a year) and 47th wettest (658 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Soshanguve, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Hammanskraal is about as warm in January and 0.2 °C warmer in June, with 27 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (23.9 °C), averages 11.5 °C more than its coolest, June (12.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.8 °C (42 °F), in July. The area lies about 454 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hammanskraal, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hammanskraal has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 454 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
