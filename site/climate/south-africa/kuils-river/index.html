Kuils River at a glance

Among the 298 towns ClimaClue covers in South Africa, Kuils River is 197th warmest (16.5 °C year average), 148th sunniest (about 3,076 hours of sunshine a year) and 13th wettest (1,169 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kraaifontein, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Kuils River is 0.7 °C cooler in February and 0.6 °C warmer in July, with 62 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (20.8 °C), averages 8.4 °C more than its coolest, July (12.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.1 °C (45 °F), in July. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Kuils River has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kuils River itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.942°S, 18.707°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1179-1987), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
