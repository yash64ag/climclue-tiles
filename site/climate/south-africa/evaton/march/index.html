Evaton in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Evaton in March?
In March, Evaton averages a day high of 24.2 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 13.4 °C (56 °F), with 80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 253 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Evaton?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Evaton’s mild months (February, March, April, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Evaton in March?
80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (122 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Evaton in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Evaton get in March?
About 253 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Evaton in March?
