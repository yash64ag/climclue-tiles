Evaton in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Evaton in November?
In November, Evaton averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 13.1 °C (56 °F), with 93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Evaton?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Evaton’s mild months (February, March, April, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Evaton in November?
93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (121 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Evaton in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Evaton get in November?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Evaton in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
