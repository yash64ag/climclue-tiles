Evaton in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Evaton in December?
In December, Evaton averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 121 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 280 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Evaton?
December is not one of Evaton’s mild months; those are February, March, April, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
