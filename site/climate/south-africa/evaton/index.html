Evaton at a glance

Among the 71 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Africa, Evaton is 48th warmest (16.3 °C year average), 18th sunniest (about 3,227 hours of sunshine a year) and 46th wettest (660 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vereeniging, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Evaton is 0.5 °C cooler in January and 0.4 °C cooler in June, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (20.8 °C), averages 11.2 °C more than its coolest, June (9.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 2.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 471 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Evaton, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Evaton has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 471 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
