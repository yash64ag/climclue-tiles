Evaton in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Evaton in April?
In April, Evaton averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 10.3 °C (51 °F), with 34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Evaton?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Evaton’s mild months (February, March, April, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Evaton in April?
34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (122 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Evaton in April?

How many hours of sunshine does Evaton get in April?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
