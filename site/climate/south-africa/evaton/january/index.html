How January compares in Evaton

January is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Evaton. Compared with December: 0.3 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. February is 0.4 °C cooler than January on average.

Evaton in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Evaton in January?
In January, Evaton averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 119 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 262 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Evaton?
January is not one of Evaton’s mild months; those are February, March, April, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Evaton in January?
119 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (122 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Evaton in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Evaton get in January?
About 262 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Evaton in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
