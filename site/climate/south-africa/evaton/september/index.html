Evaton in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Evaton in September?
In September, Evaton averages a day high of 24.2 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 9.4 °C (49 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 294 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Evaton?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Evaton’s mild months (February, March, April, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Evaton in September?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (122 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Evaton in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Evaton get in September?
About 294 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Evaton in September?
