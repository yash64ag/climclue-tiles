Evaton in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Evaton in July?
In July, Evaton averages a day high of 17.4 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 2.8 °C (37 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 286 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Evaton?
July is not one of Evaton’s mild months; those are February, March, April, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Evaton in July?

Does it snow in Evaton in July?
