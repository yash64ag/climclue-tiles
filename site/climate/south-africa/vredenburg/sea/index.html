Vredenburg sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Western Cape · South Africa

The sea off Vredenburg averages 16.4 °C (62 °F) in January, its warmest month, and 14.5 °C (58 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Vredenburg sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Vredenburg?
In January, at about 16.4 °C (62 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Vredenburg?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 14.5 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in Vredenburg in August?
About 14.5 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Vredenburg?
About 15.3 °C (60 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Vredenburg nearby?
In January: Cape Town (17.6 °C, 120 km), Kuils River (17.6 °C, 133 km) and Muizenberg (17.6 °C, 140 km).
