Why Polokwane has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 397 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Polokwane climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Polokwane?
January, with an average of 21.9 °C (71 °F), day highs around 26.7 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 16.3 °C (61 °F).
What is the coldest month in Polokwane?
July, with an average of 12.3 °C (54 °F), day highs around 18.9 °C (66 °F) and night lows around 6.4 °C (44 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Polokwane?
November, with 93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 493 mm (19.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Polokwane?
July, with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Polokwane?
August, with about 285 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Polokwane?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Polokwane?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, April, May, September, October, November and December.
What type of climate does Polokwane have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Polokwane’s GeoNames location (23.904°S, 29.469°E; ClimaClue area sq-1079-2094), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 272,461 (GeoNames).
