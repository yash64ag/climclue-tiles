Polokwane at a glance

Among the 71 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Africa, Polokwane is the 5th driest (493 mm a year). Compared with Mokopane, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Polokwane is 1.1 °C cooler in January and 0.5 °C cooler in July, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (21.9 °C), averages 9.6 °C more than its coolest, July (12.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.4 °C (44 °F), in July. The area lies about 397 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 98% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Polokwane has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 397 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
