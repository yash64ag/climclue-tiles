Vryheid at a glance

Among the 71 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Africa, Vryheid is 49th warmest (16.2 °C year average), 57th sunniest (about 2,787 hours of sunshine a year) and 10th wettest (908 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Newcastle, the nearest covered town with different numbers (85 km away), Vryheid is 1.3 °C cooler in January and 0.1 °C cooler in June, with 216 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (20.1 °C), averages 9.2 °C more than its coolest, June (10.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 4.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 180 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Vryheid, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Vryheid has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 180 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
