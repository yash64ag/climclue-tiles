Why Mbombela has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 189 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Mbombela climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Mbombela?
January, with an average of 22.9 °C (73 °F), day highs around 27.6 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 18.1 °C (65 °F).
What is the coldest month in Mbombela?
July, with an average of 13.6 °C (56 °F), day highs around 20.8 °C (69 °F) and night lows around 7.0 °C (45 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Mbombela?
January, with 149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 893 mm (35.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Mbombela?
July, with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Mbombela?
August, with about 282 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Mbombela?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Mbombela?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, August, September and October.
What type of climate does Mbombela have?
Humid subtropical, dry winter (Köppen Cwa). Hot, wet summers and mild, dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Mbombela’s GeoNames location (25.475°S, 30.969°E; ClimaClue area sq-1094-2109), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 110,159 (GeoNames).
