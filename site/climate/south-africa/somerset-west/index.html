Somerset West at a glance

Among the 71 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Africa, Somerset West is the wettest (2,088 mm a year). Compared with Cape Town, the nearest covered town with different numbers (41 km away), Somerset West is 1.1 °C warmer in February and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 1,140 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (20.6 °C), averages 7.6 °C more than its coolest, July (13.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.1 °C (50 °F), in July. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Somerset West, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Somerset West has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
