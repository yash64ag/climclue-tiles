Why Orkney has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 539 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Orkney climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Orkney?
January, with an average of 23.1 °C (73 °F), day highs around 28.9 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 17.2 °C (63 °F).
What is the coldest month in Orkney?
June, with an average of 10.1 °C (50 °F), day highs around 18.2 °C (65 °F) and night lows around 3.7 °C (39 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Orkney?
January, with 101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 574 mm (22.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Orkney?
July, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Orkney?
August, with about 315 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Orkney?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Orkney?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March, April, September, October, November and December.
What type of climate does Orkney have?
Cold semi-arid (Köppen BSk). Dry grassland climate with cold winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.9 × 11 km, that contains Orkney’s GeoNames location (26.980°S, 26.673°E; ClimaClue area sq-1109-2066), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 110,052 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
