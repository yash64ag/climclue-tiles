eMuziwezinto sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · KwaZulu-Natal · South Africa

The sea off eMuziwezinto averages 25.6 °C (78 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 21.1 °C (70 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to April.

eMuziwezinto sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in eMuziwezinto?
In February, at about 25.6 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in eMuziwezinto?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to April. The coolest month is August, at 21.1 °C (70 °F).
How warm is the sea in eMuziwezinto in August?
About 21.1 °C (70 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in eMuziwezinto?
About 23.2 °C (74 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
