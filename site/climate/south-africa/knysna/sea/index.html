Knysna sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Western Cape · South Africa

The sea off Knysna averages 20.9 °C (70 °F) in January, its warmest month, and 16.4 °C (62 °F) in August. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from January to February.

Knysna sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Knysna?
In January, at about 20.9 °C (70 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Knysna?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from January to February. The coolest month is August, at 16.4 °C (62 °F).
How warm is the sea in Knysna in August?
About 16.4 °C (62 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Knysna?
About 18.2 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
