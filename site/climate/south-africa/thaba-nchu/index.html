Thaba Nchu at a glance

Among the 298 towns ClimaClue covers in South Africa, Thaba Nchu is 244th warmest (15.7 °C year average), 88th sunniest (about 3,203 hours of sunshine a year) and 189th wettest (624 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Botshabelo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Thaba Nchu is 0.7 °C cooler in January and 0.6 °C cooler in June, with 58 mm more precipitation a year. Why Thaba Nchu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 373 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Thaba Nchu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (29.209°S, 26.839°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1132-2068), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
