How May compares in Pretoria

May is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Pretoria. Compared with April: 3.3 °C cooler, 21 mm less precipitation and 35 hours more sunshine. June is 3.0 °C cooler than May on average.

Pretoria in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pretoria in May?
In May, Pretoria averages a day high of 20.9 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 8.3 °C (47 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Pretoria?
May is not one of Pretoria’s mild months; those are March, April, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pretoria in May?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (128 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Pretoria in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pretoria get in May?
About 278 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pretoria in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
