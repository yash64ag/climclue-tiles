How July compares in Pretoria

July is the coolest month, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Pretoria. Compared with June: about as warm, 6 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. August is 3.0 °C warmer than July on average.

Pretoria in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pretoria in July?
In July, Pretoria averages a day high of 18.6 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 4.8 °C (41 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 290 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Pretoria?
July is not one of Pretoria’s mild months; those are March, April, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pretoria in July?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (128 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Pretoria in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pretoria get in July?
About 290 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pretoria in July?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
