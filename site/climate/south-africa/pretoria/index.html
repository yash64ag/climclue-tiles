Pretoria at a glance

Among the 71 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Africa, Pretoria is 24th warmest (17.8 °C year average), 26th sunniest (about 3,202 hours of sunshine a year) and 31st wettest (707 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Centurion, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Pretoria is 0.6 °C warmer in January and 0.5 °C warmer in June, with 5 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (22.1 °C), averages 10.9 °C more than its coolest, June (11.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 4.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 461 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pretoria, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pretoria has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 461 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
