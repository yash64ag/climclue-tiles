How February compares in Pretoria

February is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Pretoria. Compared with January: 0.1 °C cooler, 22 mm less precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. March is 1.7 °C cooler than February on average.

Pretoria in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pretoria in February?
In February, Pretoria averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Pretoria?
February is not one of Pretoria’s mild months; those are March, April, May, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pretoria in February?
104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (126 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Pretoria in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pretoria get in February?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pretoria in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
