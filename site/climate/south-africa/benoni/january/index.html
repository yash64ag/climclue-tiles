How January compares in Benoni

January is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Benoni. Compared with December: 0.4 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. February is 0.4 °C cooler than January on average.

Benoni in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benoni in January?
In January, Benoni averages a day high of 24.7 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 14.5 °C (58 °F), with 132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 250 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Benoni?
January is not one of Benoni’s mild months; those are March, April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benoni in January?
132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (132 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Benoni in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benoni get in January?
About 250 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benoni in January?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
