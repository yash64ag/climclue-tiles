How July compares in Benoni

July is the coolest month, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Benoni. Compared with June: about as warm, 7 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. August is 3.0 °C warmer than July on average.

Benoni in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benoni in July?
In July, Benoni averages a day high of 16.9 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 2.8 °C (37 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 283 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Benoni?
July is not one of Benoni’s mild months; those are March, April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benoni in July?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (132 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Benoni in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benoni get in July?
About 283 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benoni in July?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
