How June compares in Benoni

June is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Benoni. Compared with May: 2.9 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. July is about as warm as June on average.

Benoni in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benoni in June?
In June, Benoni averages a day high of 16.4 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 3.3 °C (38 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Benoni?
June is not one of Benoni’s mild months; those are March, April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benoni in June?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (132 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Benoni in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benoni get in June?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benoni in June?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
