How December compares in Benoni

December is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Benoni. Compared with November: 0.8 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. January is 0.4 °C warmer than December on average.

Benoni in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benoni in December?
In December, Benoni averages a day high of 24.6 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 13.9 °C (57 °F), with 127 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Benoni?
December is not one of Benoni’s mild months; those are March, April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benoni in December?
127 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (132 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Benoni in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benoni get in December?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benoni in December?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
