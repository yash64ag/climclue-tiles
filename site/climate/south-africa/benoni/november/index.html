How November compares in Benoni

November is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Benoni. Compared with October: 0.8 °C warmer, 26 mm more precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. December is 0.8 °C warmer than November on average.

Benoni in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benoni in November?
In November, Benoni averages a day high of 24.7 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 108 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Benoni?
November is not one of Benoni’s mild months; those are March, April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benoni in November?
108 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (134 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Benoni in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Benoni get in November?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benoni in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
