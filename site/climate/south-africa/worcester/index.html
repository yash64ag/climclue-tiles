Worcester at a glance

Among the 71 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Africa, Worcester is the driest (454 mm a year). Compared with Paarl, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Worcester is 1.4 °C warmer in January and 0.8 °C warmer in July, with 1,434 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (23.3 °C), averages 11.5 °C more than its coolest, July (11.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.9 °C (44 °F), in July. The area lies about 85 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Worcester, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Worcester has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 85 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
