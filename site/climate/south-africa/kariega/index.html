Kariega at a glance

Among the 298 towns ClimaClue covers in South Africa, Kariega is 83rd warmest (18.3 °C year average), 242nd sunniest (about 2,742 hours of sunshine a year) and 280th wettest (443 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Despatch, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Kariega is 0.1 °C warmer in February and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 8 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.4 °C), averages 8.0 °C more than its coolest, July (14.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.1 °C (45 °F), in July. The area lies about 30 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kariega, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kariega has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 30 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kariega itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.756°S, 25.401°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1177-2054), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
