Botshabelo at a glance

Among the 71 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Africa, Botshabelo is 50th warmest (16.2 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 3,219 hours of sunshine a year) and 57th wettest (590 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bloemfontein, the nearest covered town with different numbers (52 km away), Botshabelo is 0.8 °C cooler in January and 0.1 °C cooler in June, with 27 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (22.6 °C), averages 14.4 °C more than its coolest, June (8.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 1.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 380 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Botshabelo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Botshabelo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 380 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
