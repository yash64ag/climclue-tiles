Montagu at a glance

Among the 298 towns ClimaClue covers in South Africa, Montagu is 117th warmest (17.6 °C year average), 183rd sunniest (about 2,962 hours of sunshine a year) and 276th wettest (462 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ashton, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Montagu is about as warm in January and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 108 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (22.8 °C), averages 11.0 °C more than its coolest, July (11.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.1 °C (41 °F), in July. The area lies about 96 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Montagu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Montagu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 96 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.0 °C. The 10 km area around Montagu, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 16.6 °C a year, 1.0 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Montagu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.787°S, 20.121°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1177-2001), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
