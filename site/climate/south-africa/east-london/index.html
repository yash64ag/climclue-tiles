East London at a glance

Among the 71 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Africa, East London is 8th warmest (19.4 °C year average), 66th sunniest (about 2,588 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (948 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mdantsane, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), East London is 0.6 °C warmer in February and 1.5 °C warmer in July, with 71 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.8 °C), averages 6.1 °C more than its coolest, July (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.5 °C (56 °F), in July. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: a NOAA weather station 8 km from East London measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 2.9 °C (5.2 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why East London has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
