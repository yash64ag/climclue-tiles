Potchefstroom at a glance

Among the 71 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Africa, Potchefstroom is 30th warmest (17.4 °C year average), 7th sunniest (about 3,282 hours of sunshine a year) and 54th wettest (606 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Klerksdorp, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Potchefstroom is 0.5 °C cooler in January and 0.1 °C cooler in June, with 32 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (22.4 °C), averages 12.4 °C more than its coolest, June (10.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 3.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 511 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Potchefstroom, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Potchefstroom has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 511 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
