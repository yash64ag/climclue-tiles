Vredendal at a glance

Among the 298 towns ClimaClue covers in South Africa, Vredendal is the driest (173 mm a year). Compared with Vredenburg, the nearest covered town with different numbers (146 km away), Vredendal is 3.9 °C warmer in February and 0.6 °C warmer in July, with 159 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.3 °C), averages 8.9 °C more than its coolest, July (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.6 °C (44 °F), in July. The area lies about 39 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Vredendal, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Vredendal has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 39 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Vredendal itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (31.668°S, 18.501°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1156-1985), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
