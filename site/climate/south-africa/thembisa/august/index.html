Thembisa weather in August

Average August weather in Thembisa, South Africa: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Thembisa averages 20.8 °C (69 °F) days and 4.8 °C (41 °F) nights, with 6 mm (0.3 in) of rain over the month and about 296 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Thembisa

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Thembisa. Compared with July: 2.9 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. September is 4.0 °C warmer than August on average.

Thembisa in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Thembisa in August?
In August, Thembisa averages a day high of 20.8 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 4.8 °C (41 °F), with 6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 296 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Thembisa?
August is not one of Thembisa’s mild months; those are March, April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
