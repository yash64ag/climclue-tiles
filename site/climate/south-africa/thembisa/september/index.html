Thembisa in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Thembisa in September?
In September, Thembisa averages a day high of 24.4 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 9.2 °C (49 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 284 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Thembisa?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Thembisa’s mild months (March, April, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Thembisa in September?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (126 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Thembisa in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Thembisa get in September?
About 284 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Thembisa in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
