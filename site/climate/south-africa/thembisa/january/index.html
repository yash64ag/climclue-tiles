How January compares in Thembisa

January is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Thembisa. Compared with December: 0.5 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. February is 0.3 °C cooler than January on average.

Thembisa in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Thembisa in January?
In January, Thembisa averages a day high of 26.9 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 126 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation and about 253 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Thembisa?
January is not one of Thembisa’s mild months; those are March, April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Thembisa in January?
126 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (126 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Thembisa in January?
