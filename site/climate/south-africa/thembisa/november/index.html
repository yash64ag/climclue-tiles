Thembisa in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Thembisa in November?
In November, Thembisa averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Thembisa?
November is not one of Thembisa’s mild months; those are March, April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Thembisa in November?
100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (124 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Thembisa in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Thembisa get in November?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Thembisa in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
