How February compares in Thembisa

February is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Thembisa. Compared with January: 0.3 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. March is 1.4 °C cooler than February on average.

Thembisa in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Thembisa in February?
In February, Thembisa averages a day high of 25.4 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Thembisa?
February is not one of Thembisa’s mild months; those are March, April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Thembisa in February?
107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (124 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Thembisa in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Thembisa get in February?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Thembisa in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
