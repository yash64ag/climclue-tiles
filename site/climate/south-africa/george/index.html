George at a glance

Among the 71 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Africa, George is 54th warmest (16.0 °C year average), 59th sunniest (about 2,739 hours of sunshine a year) and 42nd wettest (664 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kariega, the nearest covered town with different numbers (272 km away), George is 2.4 °C cooler in February and 2.1 °C cooler in July, with 181 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (19.9 °C), averages 7.8 °C more than its coolest, July (12.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.2 °C (47 °F), in July. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of George, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why George has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
