Matsulu at a glance

Among the 298 towns ClimaClue covers in South Africa, Matsulu is 12th warmest (21.7 °C year average), 203rd sunniest (about 2,908 hours of sunshine a year) and 198th wettest (615 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tekwane, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Matsulu is 1.2 °C warmer in January and 1.5 °C warmer in July, with 105 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (25.6 °C), averages 9.1 °C more than its coolest, July (16.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.2 °C (47 °F), in July. The area lies about 147 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Matsulu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Matsulu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 147 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.1 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Matsulu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.523°S, 31.357°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1095-2113), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
