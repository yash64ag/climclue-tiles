Pietermaritzburg weather in April

April · KwaZulu-Natal · South Africa

How April compares in Pietermaritzburg

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Pietermaritzburg. Compared with March: 2.3 °C cooler, 43 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. May is 2.9 °C cooler than April on average.

Pietermaritzburg in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pietermaritzburg in April?
In April, Pietermaritzburg averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Pietermaritzburg?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Pietermaritzburg’s mild months (February, March, April, May, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pietermaritzburg in April?
37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (105 mm), the driest June (15 mm).
Does it snow in Pietermaritzburg in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pietermaritzburg get in April?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pietermaritzburg in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
