Why Pietermaritzburg has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 78 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Pietermaritzburg climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Pietermaritzburg?
February, with an average of 21.4 °C (71 °F), day highs around 26.2 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 17.1 °C (63 °F).
What is the coldest month in Pietermaritzburg?
June, with an average of 12.7 °C (55 °F), day highs around 19.8 °C (68 °F) and night lows around 6.7 °C (44 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Pietermaritzburg?
December, with 123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 797 mm (31.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Pietermaritzburg?
July, with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Pietermaritzburg?
July, with about 241 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Pietermaritzburg?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Pietermaritzburg?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: March, April, May, September and October.
What type of climate does Pietermaritzburg have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.7 × 11 km, that contains Pietermaritzburg’s GeoNames location (29.617°S, 30.393°E; ClimaClue area sq-1136-2103), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 839,327 (GeoNames).
