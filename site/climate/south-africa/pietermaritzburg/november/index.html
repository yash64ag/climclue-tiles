Pietermaritzburg weather in November

Average November weather in Pietermaritzburg, South Africa: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Pietermaritzburg averages 25.2 °C (77 °F) days and 14.4 °C (58 °F) nights, with 91 mm (3.6 in) of rain over the month and about 199 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Pietermaritzburg

November is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Pietermaritzburg. Compared with October: 1.5 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. December is 1.3 °C warmer than November on average.

Pietermaritzburg in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pietermaritzburg in November?
In November, Pietermaritzburg averages a day high of 25.2 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 14.4 °C (58 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 199 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Pietermaritzburg?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Pietermaritzburg’s mild months (February, March, April, May, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pietermaritzburg in November?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (105 mm), the driest June (15 mm).
Does it snow in Pietermaritzburg in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pietermaritzburg get in November?
About 199 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pietermaritzburg in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
