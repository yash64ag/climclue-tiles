How July compares in Pietermaritzburg

July is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Pietermaritzburg. Compared with June: 0.2 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. August is 1.9 °C warmer than July on average.

Pietermaritzburg in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pietermaritzburg in July?
In July, Pietermaritzburg averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 5.0 °C (41 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Pietermaritzburg?
July is not one of Pietermaritzburg’s mild months; those are February, March, April, May, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pietermaritzburg in July?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (105 mm), the driest June (15 mm).
Does it snow in Pietermaritzburg in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pietermaritzburg get in July?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pietermaritzburg in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
