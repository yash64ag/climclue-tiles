How December compares in Pietermaritzburg

December is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Pietermaritzburg. Compared with November: 1.4 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. January is 0.8 °C warmer than December on average.

Pietermaritzburg in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pietermaritzburg in December?
In December, Pietermaritzburg averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Pietermaritzburg?
December is not one of Pietermaritzburg’s mild months; those are March, April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pietermaritzburg in December?
123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (123 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Pietermaritzburg in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pietermaritzburg get in December?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pietermaritzburg in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
