How June compares in Pietermaritzburg

June is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Pietermaritzburg. Compared with May: 2.7 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. July is 0.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Pietermaritzburg in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pietermaritzburg in June?
In June, Pietermaritzburg averages a day high of 19.8 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 6.7 °C (44 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Pietermaritzburg?
June is not one of Pietermaritzburg’s mild months; those are March, April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pietermaritzburg in June?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (123 mm), the driest July (16 mm).
Does it snow in Pietermaritzburg in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pietermaritzburg get in June?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pietermaritzburg in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
