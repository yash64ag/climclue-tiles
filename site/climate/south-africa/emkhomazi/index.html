eMkhomazi at a glance

Among the 298 towns ClimaClue covers in South Africa, eMkhomazi is the least sunny (about 2,421 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Scottburgh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), eMkhomazi is about as warm in February and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 29 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (24.3 °C), averages 6.3 °C more than its coolest, July (18.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.4 °C (54 °F), in July. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Why eMkhomazi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
