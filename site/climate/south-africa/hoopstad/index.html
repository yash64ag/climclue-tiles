Hoopstad at a glance

Among the 298 towns ClimaClue covers in South Africa, Hoopstad is 103rd warmest (17.8 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 3,400 hours of sunshine a year) and 266th wettest (491 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bloemhof, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Hoopstad is 0.3 °C cooler in January and 0.5 °C cooler in July, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (24.2 °C), averages 14.5 °C more than its coolest, July (9.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in July. The area lies about 546 km from the sea.

Why Hoopstad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 546 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1118-2059), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
