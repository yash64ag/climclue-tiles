Groot-Brakrivier at a glance

Among the 298 towns ClimaClue covers in South Africa, Groot-Brakrivier is 133rd warmest (17.3 °C year average), 276th sunniest (about 2,596 hours of sunshine a year) and 184th wettest (638 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mossel Bay, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Groot-Brakrivier is 0.1 °C warmer in February and 0.4 °C cooler in July, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (21.0 °C), averages 7.1 °C more than its coolest, July (13.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.2 °C (49 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Groot-Brakrivier has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
