Groot-Brakrivier sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Western Cape · South Africa

The sea off Groot-Brakrivier averages 21.0 °C (70 °F) in January, its warmest month, and 15.8 °C (60 °F) in August. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from January to February.

Groot-Brakrivier sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Groot-Brakrivier?
In January, at about 21.0 °C (70 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Groot-Brakrivier?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from January to February. The coolest month is August, at 15.8 °C (60 °F).
How warm is the sea in Groot-Brakrivier in August?
About 15.8 °C (60 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Groot-Brakrivier?
