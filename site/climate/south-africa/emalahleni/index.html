Emalahleni at a glance

Among the 298 towns ClimaClue covers in South Africa, Emalahleni is 238th warmest (15.8 °C year average), 125th sunniest (about 3,129 hours of sunshine a year) and 74th wettest (816 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Middelburg, Mpumalanga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Emalahleni is 0.8 °C cooler in January and 1.0 °C cooler in June, with 132 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (19.9 °C), averages 10.4 °C more than its coolest, June (9.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 1.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 350 km from the sea.

Why Emalahleni has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 350 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1098-2092), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
