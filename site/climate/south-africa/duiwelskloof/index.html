Duiwelskloof at a glance

Among the 298 towns ClimaClue covers in South Africa, Duiwelskloof is 75th warmest (18.6 °C year average), 178th sunniest (about 2,980 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (1,204 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tzaneen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Duiwelskloof is 1.5 °C cooler in January and 1.4 °C cooler in July, with 63 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (22.3 °C), averages 8.8 °C more than its coolest, July (13.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.9 °C (43 °F), in July. The area lies about 359 km from the sea.

Why Duiwelskloof has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 359 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
