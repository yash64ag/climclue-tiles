Ntuzuma at a glance

Among the 71 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Africa, Ntuzuma is the 5th warmest (20.4 °C year average) and the 2nd least sunny (about 2,499 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Pinetown, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Ntuzuma is 0.6 °C warmer in February and 0.8 °C warmer in July, with 6 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (24.1 °C), averages 7.5 °C more than its coolest, July (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.9 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 19 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ntuzuma, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ntuzuma has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
