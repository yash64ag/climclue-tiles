Westonaria at a glance

Among the 71 towns of 100,000+ people covered in South Africa, Westonaria is 65th warmest (15.6 °C year average), 15th sunniest (about 3,231 hours of sunshine a year) and 36th wettest (702 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Randfontein, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Westonaria is 0.6 °C warmer in January and 0.3 °C warmer in June, with 53 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (20.1 °C), averages 11.1 °C more than its coolest, June (8.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 2.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 501 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Westonaria, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Westonaria has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 501 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
