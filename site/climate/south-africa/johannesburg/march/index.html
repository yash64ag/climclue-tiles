How March compares in Johannesburg

March is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Johannesburg. Compared with February: 1.4 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. April is 2.7 °C cooler than March on average.

Johannesburg in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Johannesburg in March?
In March, Johannesburg averages a day high of 23.1 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 11.7 °C (53 °F), with 109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Johannesburg?
March is not one of Johannesburg’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Johannesburg in March?
109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (146 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Johannesburg in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Johannesburg get in March?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Johannesburg in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
