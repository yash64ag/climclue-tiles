How October compares in Johannesburg

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Johannesburg. Compared with September: 1.7 °C warmer, 62 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. November is 0.9 °C warmer than October on average.

Johannesburg in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Johannesburg in October?
In October, Johannesburg averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 10.6 °C (51 °F), with 85 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 276 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Johannesburg?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Johannesburg’s mild months (September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Johannesburg in October?
85 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (146 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Johannesburg in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Johannesburg get in October?
About 276 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Johannesburg in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
