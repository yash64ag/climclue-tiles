How September compares in Durban

September is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Durban. Compared with August: 1.0 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. October is 0.9 °C warmer than September on average.

Durban in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Durban in September?
In September, Durban averages a day high of 23.8 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Durban?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Durban’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Durban in September?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (125 mm), the driest June (24 mm).
Does it snow in Durban in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Durban get in September?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Durban in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
