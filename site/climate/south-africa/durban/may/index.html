How May compares in Durban

May is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Durban. Compared with April: 2.1 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. June is 2.1 °C cooler than May on average.

Durban in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Durban in May?
In May, Durban averages a day high of 23.4 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Durban?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Durban’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Durban in May?
42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (124 mm), the driest June (24 mm).
Does it snow in Durban in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Durban get in May?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Durban in May?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
