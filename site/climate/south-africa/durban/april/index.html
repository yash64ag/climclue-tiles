How April compares in Durban

April is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Durban. Compared with March: 1.7 °C cooler, 35 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. May is 2.1 °C cooler than April on average.

Durban in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Durban in April?
In April, Durban averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 209 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Durban?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Durban’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Durban in April?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (125 mm), the driest June (24 mm).
Does it snow in Durban in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Durban get in April?
About 209 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Durban in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
