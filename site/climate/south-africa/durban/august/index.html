How August compares in Durban

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Durban. Compared with July: 0.9 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. September is 1.0 °C warmer than August on average.

Durban in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Durban in August?
In August, Durban averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 14.7 °C (58 °F), with 42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Durban?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Durban’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Durban in August?
42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (124 mm), the driest June (24 mm).
Does it snow in Durban in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Durban get in August?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Durban in August?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
