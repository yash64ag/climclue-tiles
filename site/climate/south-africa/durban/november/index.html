How November compares in Durban

November is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Durban. Compared with October: 1.4 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. December is 1.4 °C warmer than November on average.

Durban in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Durban in November?
In November, Durban averages a day high of 24.0 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 18.9 °C (66 °F), with 108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Durban?
November is not one of Durban’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Durban in November?
108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (124 mm), the driest June (24 mm).
Does it snow in Durban in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Durban get in November?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Durban in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
