How February compares in Durban

February is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Durban. Compared with January: 0.4 °C warmer, 12 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. March is 0.8 °C cooler than February on average.

Durban in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Durban in February?
In February, Durban averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 21.2 °C (70 °F), with 113 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 198 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Durban?
February is not one of Durban’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Durban in February?
113 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (125 mm), the driest June (24 mm).
Does it snow in Durban in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Durban get in February?
About 198 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Durban in February?
About 25.7 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is February (25.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Durban in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
