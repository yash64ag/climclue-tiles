How October compares in Durban

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Durban. Compared with September: 0.9 °C warmer, 30 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. November is 1.4 °C warmer than October on average.

Durban in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Durban in October?
In October, Durban averages a day high of 24.2 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 16.7 °C (62 °F), with 95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Durban?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Durban’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Durban in October?
95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (125 mm), the driest June (24 mm).
Does it snow in Durban in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Durban get in October?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Durban in October?
About 21.7 °C (71 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is February (25.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Durban in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
