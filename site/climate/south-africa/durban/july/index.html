How July compares in Durban

July is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Durban. Compared with June: 0.4 °C cooler, 11 mm more precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. August is 0.9 °C warmer than July on average.

Durban in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Durban in July?
In July, Durban averages a day high of 21.4 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Durban?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Durban’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Durban in July?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (124 mm), the driest June (24 mm).
Does it snow in Durban in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Durban get in July?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Durban in July?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
