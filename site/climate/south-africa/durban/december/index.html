How December compares in Durban

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Durban. Compared with November: 1.4 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. January is 1.1 °C warmer than December on average.

Durban in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Durban in December?
In December, Durban averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 20.5 °C (69 °F), with 116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Durban?
December is not one of Durban’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Durban in December?
116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (124 mm), the driest June (24 mm).
Does it snow in Durban in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Durban get in December?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Durban in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
