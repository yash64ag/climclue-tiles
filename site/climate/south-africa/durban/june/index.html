How June compares in Durban

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Durban. Compared with May: 2.1 °C cooler, 18 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. July is 0.4 °C cooler than June on average.

Durban in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Durban in June?
In June, Durban averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 14.4 °C (58 °F), with 24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Durban?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Durban’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Durban in June?
24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (124 mm), the driest June (24 mm).
Does it snow in Durban in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Durban get in June?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Durban in June?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
