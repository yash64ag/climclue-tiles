Why Mthatha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 70 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Mthatha climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Mthatha?
February, with an average of 20.6 °C (69 °F), day highs around 25.3 °C (78 °F) and night lows around 16.3 °C (61 °F).
What is the coldest month in Mthatha?
June, with an average of 12.3 °C (54 °F), day highs around 19.2 °C (67 °F) and night lows around 6.5 °C (44 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Mthatha?
January, with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 731 mm (28.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Mthatha?
July, with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Mthatha?
August, with about 237 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Mthatha?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Mthatha?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March, April, May, September, October, November and December.
What type of climate does Mthatha have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.5 × 11 km, that contains Mthatha’s GeoNames location (31.589°S, 28.784°E; ClimaClue area sq-1155-2087), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 164,848 (GeoNames).
