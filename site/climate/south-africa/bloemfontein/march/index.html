How March compares in Bloemfontein

March is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Bloemfontein. Compared with February: 2.4 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. April is 4.0 °C cooler than March on average.

Bloemfontein in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bloemfontein in March?
In March, Bloemfontein averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bloemfontein?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Bloemfontein’s mild months (January, March, April, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bloemfontein in March?
84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (110 mm), the driest July (7 mm).
Does it snow in Bloemfontein in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bloemfontein get in March?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bloemfontein in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
