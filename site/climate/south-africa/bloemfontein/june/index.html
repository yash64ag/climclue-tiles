How June compares in Bloemfontein

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Bloemfontein. Compared with May: 3.6 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. July is about as warm as June on average.

Bloemfontein in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bloemfontein in June?
In June, Bloemfontein averages a day high of 17.3 °C (63 °F) and a night low of -0.7 °C (31 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Bloemfontein?
June is not one of Bloemfontein’s mild months; those are January, March, April, September, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bloemfontein in June?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (111 mm), the driest July (7 mm).
Does it snow in Bloemfontein in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bloemfontein get in June?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bloemfontein in June?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
