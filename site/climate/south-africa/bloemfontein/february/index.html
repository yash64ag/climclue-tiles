How February compares in Bloemfontein

February is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Bloemfontein. Compared with January: 1.0 °C cooler, 15 mm more precipitation and 50 hours less sunshine. March is 2.4 °C cooler than February on average.

Bloemfontein in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bloemfontein in February?
In February, Bloemfontein averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 16.2 °C (61 °F), with 110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Bloemfontein?
February is not one of Bloemfontein’s mild months; those are January, March, April, September, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bloemfontein in February?
110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is February (110 mm), the driest July (7 mm).
Does it snow in Bloemfontein in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bloemfontein get in February?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bloemfontein in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
