How April compares in Bloemfontein

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Bloemfontein. Compared with March: 4.0 °C cooler, 37 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. May is 4.2 °C cooler than April on average.

Bloemfontein in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bloemfontein in April?
In April, Bloemfontein averages a day high of 23.0 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 10.0 °C (50 °F), with 47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 245 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Bloemfontein?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Bloemfontein’s mild months (January, March, April, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bloemfontein in April?
47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (110 mm), the driest July (7 mm).
Does it snow in Bloemfontein in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bloemfontein get in April?
About 245 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bloemfontein in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
