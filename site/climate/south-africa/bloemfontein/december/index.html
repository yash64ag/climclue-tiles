How December compares in Bloemfontein

December is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Bloemfontein. Compared with November: 2.0 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. January is 0.9 °C warmer than December on average.

Bloemfontein in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bloemfontein in December?
In December, Bloemfontein averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 15.1 °C (59 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 317 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Bloemfontein?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Bloemfontein’s mild months (January, March, April, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bloemfontein in December?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (110 mm), the driest July (7 mm).
Does it snow in Bloemfontein in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bloemfontein get in December?
About 317 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bloemfontein in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
