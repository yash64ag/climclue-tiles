How July compares in Bloemfontein

July is the coolest month, the driest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Bloemfontein. Compared with June: about as warm, 5 mm less precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. August is 3.0 °C warmer than July on average.

Bloemfontein in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bloemfontein in July?
In July, Bloemfontein averages a day high of 16.6 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 1.5 °C (35 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 269 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Bloemfontein?
July is not one of Bloemfontein’s mild months; those are January, March, April, September, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bloemfontein in July?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is February (110 mm), the driest July (7 mm).
Does it snow in Bloemfontein in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bloemfontein get in July?
About 269 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bloemfontein in July?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
