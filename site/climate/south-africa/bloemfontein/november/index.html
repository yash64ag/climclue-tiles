How November compares in Bloemfontein

November is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Bloemfontein. Compared with October: 1.9 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. December is 2.0 °C warmer than November on average.

Bloemfontein in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bloemfontein in November?
In November, Bloemfontein averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 12.3 °C (54 °F), with 75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 295 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Bloemfontein?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Bloemfontein’s mild months (January, March, April, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bloemfontein in November?
75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (111 mm), the driest July (7 mm).
Does it snow in Bloemfontein in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bloemfontein get in November?
About 295 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bloemfontein in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
