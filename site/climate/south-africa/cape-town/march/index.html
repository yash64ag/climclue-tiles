How March compares in Cape Town

March is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Cape Town. Compared with February: 1.2 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. April is 2.0 °C cooler than March on average.

Cape Town in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cape Town in March?
In March, Cape Town averages a day high of 20.7 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 16.0 °C (61 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 283 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Cape Town?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Cape Town’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cape Town in March?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (168 mm), the driest February (24 mm).
Does it snow in Cape Town in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cape Town get in March?
About 283 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cape Town in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
