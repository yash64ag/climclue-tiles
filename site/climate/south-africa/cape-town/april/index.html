How April compares in Cape Town

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Cape Town. Compared with March: 2.0 °C cooler, 48 mm more precipitation and 64 hours less sunshine. May is 1.5 °C cooler than April on average.

Cape Town in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cape Town in April?
In April, Cape Town averages a day high of 18.7 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 14.2 °C (58 °F), with 80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Cape Town?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Cape Town’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cape Town in April?
80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (168 mm), the driest February (24 mm).
Does it snow in Cape Town in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cape Town get in April?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cape Town in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
