How May compares in Cape Town

May is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Cape Town. Compared with April: 1.4 °C cooler, 41 mm more precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. June is 1.4 °C cooler than May on average.

Cape Town in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cape Town in May?
In May, Cape Town averages a day high of 17.4 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Cape Town?
May is not one of Cape Town’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cape Town in May?
120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (169 mm), the driest February (24 mm).
Does it snow in Cape Town in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cape Town get in May?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cape Town in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
