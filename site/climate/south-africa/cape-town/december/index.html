How December compares in Cape Town

December is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Cape Town. Compared with November: 1.6 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. January is 0.8 °C warmer than December on average.

Cape Town in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cape Town in December?
In December, Cape Town averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 13.7 °C (57 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 325 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Cape Town?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Cape Town’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cape Town in December?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (169 mm), the driest February (24 mm).
Does it snow in Cape Town in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cape Town get in December?
About 325 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Cape Town in December?
About 17.0 °C (63 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is January (17.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Cape Town in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
