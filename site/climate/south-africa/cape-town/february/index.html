How February compares in Cape Town

February is the warmest month, the driest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Cape Town. Compared with January: 0.2 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 48 hours less sunshine. March is 1.2 °C cooler than February on average.

Cape Town in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cape Town in February?
In February, Cape Town averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 17.2 °C (63 °F), with 24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 287 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Cape Town?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Cape Town’s mild months (January, February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cape Town in February?
24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (168 mm), the driest February (24 mm).
Does it snow in Cape Town in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cape Town get in February?
About 287 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cape Town in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
