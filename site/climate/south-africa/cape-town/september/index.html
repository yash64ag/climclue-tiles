How September compares in Cape Town

September is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Cape Town. Compared with August: 0.9 °C warmer, 41 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. October is 1.5 °C warmer than September on average.

Cape Town in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cape Town in September?
In September, Cape Town averages a day high of 15.7 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 11.4 °C (53 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Cape Town?
September is not one of Cape Town’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cape Town in September?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (168 mm), the driest February (24 mm).
Does it snow in Cape Town in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cape Town get in September?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cape Town in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
