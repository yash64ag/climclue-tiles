How July compares in Cape Town

July is the coolest month, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Cape Town. Compared with June: 0.6 °C cooler, 16 mm more precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. August is 0.1 °C warmer than July on average.

Cape Town in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cape Town in July?
In July, Cape Town averages a day high of 14.8 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 10.3 °C (51 °F), with 168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Cape Town?
July is not one of Cape Town’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cape Town in July?
168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (168 mm), the driest February (24 mm).
Does it snow in Cape Town in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cape Town get in July?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cape Town in July?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
