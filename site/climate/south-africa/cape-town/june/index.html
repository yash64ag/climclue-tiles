How June compares in Cape Town

June is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Cape Town. Compared with May: 1.4 °C cooler, 33 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. July is 0.6 °C cooler than June on average.

Cape Town in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cape Town in June?
In June, Cape Town averages a day high of 18.1 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 9.5 °C (49 °F), with 153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 160 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Cape Town?
June is not one of Cape Town’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cape Town in June?
153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (169 mm), the driest February (24 mm).
Does it snow in Cape Town in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cape Town get in June?
About 160 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Cape Town in June?
About 15.1 °C (59 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is January (17.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Cape Town in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
