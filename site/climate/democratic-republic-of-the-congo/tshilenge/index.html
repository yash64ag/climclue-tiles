Tshilenge at a glance

Among the 38 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Tshilenge is the 3rd sunniest (about 2,716 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Mwene, the nearest covered town with different numbers (128 km away), Tshilenge is 1.0 °C cooler in September and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 351 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (24.4 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, January (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.2 °C (59 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,004 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tshilenge, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tshilenge has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,004 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
