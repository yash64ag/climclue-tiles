How December compares in Lubumbashi

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Lubumbashi. Compared with November: 1.5 °C cooler, 96 mm more precipitation and 64 hours less sunshine. January is 0.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Lubumbashi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lubumbashi in December?
In December, Lubumbashi averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 240 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation and about 123 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Lubumbashi?
December is not one of Lubumbashi’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lubumbashi in December?
240 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (256 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Lubumbashi in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lubumbashi get in December?
About 123 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lubumbashi in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
