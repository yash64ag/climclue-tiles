How November compares in Lubumbashi

November is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Lubumbashi. Compared with October: 1.8 °C cooler, 108 mm more precipitation and 81 hours less sunshine. December is 1.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Lubumbashi in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lubumbashi in November?
In November, Lubumbashi averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 18.1 °C (65 °F), with 144 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Lubumbashi?
November is not one of Lubumbashi’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lubumbashi in November?
144 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (256 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Lubumbashi in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lubumbashi get in November?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lubumbashi in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
