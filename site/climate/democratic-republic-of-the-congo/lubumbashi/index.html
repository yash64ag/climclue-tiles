Why Lubumbashi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,215 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Lubumbashi climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Lubumbashi?
October, with an average of 24.6 °C (76 °F), day highs around 30.9 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 18.7 °C (66 °F).
What is the coldest month in Lubumbashi?
July, with an average of 18.1 °C (64 °F), day highs around 25.2 °C (77 °F) and night lows around 11.8 °C (53 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Lubumbashi?
January, with 256 mm (10.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,135 mm (44.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Lubumbashi?
July, with 0 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Lubumbashi?
July, with about 308 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Lubumbashi?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Lubumbashi?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, July, August, September and October.
What type of climate does Lubumbashi have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Lubumbashi’s GeoNames location (11.661°S, 27.479°E; ClimaClue area sq-956-2074), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 2,221,925 (GeoNames).
