How September compares in Lubumbashi

September is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Lubumbashi. Compared with August: 3.0 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. October is 1.1 °C warmer than September on average.

Lubumbashi in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lubumbashi in September?
In September, Lubumbashi averages a day high of 32.4 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 14.6 °C (58 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 293 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Lubumbashi?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Lubumbashi’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lubumbashi in September?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (254 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Lubumbashi in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lubumbashi get in September?
About 293 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lubumbashi in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
