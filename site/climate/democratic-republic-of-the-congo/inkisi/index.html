Inkisi at a glance

Among the 38 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Inkisi is the 5th least sunny (about 1,679 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Mbanza-Ngungu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Inkisi is 0.8 °C warmer in March and 1.0 °C warmer in July, with 149 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (24.9 °C), averages 2.8 °C more than its coolest, July (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.8 °C (64 °F), in July. The area lies about 322 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Inkisi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Inkisi

For mild weather, the best months to visit Inkisi are June, July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July, August and September.
  • Warmest (average temperature): March (24.9 °C), February (24.6 °C) and April (24.6 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): July (2 mm), June (5 mm) and August (6 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): June (165 h), May (155 h) and March (153 h).

Why Inkisi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 322 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
