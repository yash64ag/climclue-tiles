Kipushi at a glance

Among the 115 towns ClimaClue covers in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kipushi is the 4th sunniest (about 2,723 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Lubumbashi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Kipushi is 0.7 °C cooler in October and 0.4 °C cooler in July, with 231 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (23.9 °C), averages 6.3 °C more than its coolest, July (17.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.8 °C (46 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,221 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kipushi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kipushi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,221 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kipushi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (11.761°S, 27.251°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-957-2072), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
