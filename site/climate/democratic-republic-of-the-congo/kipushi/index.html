Kipushi at a glance

Among the 38 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kipushi is the 5th coolest (20.6 °C year average) and the 2nd sunniest (about 2,723 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Lubumbashi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Kipushi is 0.7 °C cooler in October and 0.4 °C cooler in July, with 185 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (23.9 °C), averages 6.3 °C more than its coolest, July (17.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.6 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,221 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kipushi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kipushi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,221 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
