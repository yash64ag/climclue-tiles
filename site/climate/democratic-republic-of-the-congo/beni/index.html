Beni at a glance

Among the 38 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Beni is the 2nd coolest (19.9 °C year average) and the wettest (2,694 mm a year). Compared with Butembo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Beni is 3.0 °C warmer in February and 2.4 °C warmer in November, with 443 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (21.1 °C), averages 2.0 °C more than its coolest, November (19.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.1 °C (59 °F), in December. The area lies about 1,242 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Beni, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Beni has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 1,242 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
