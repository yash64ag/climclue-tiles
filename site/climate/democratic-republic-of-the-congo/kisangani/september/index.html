How September compares in Kisangani

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Kisangani. Compared with August: 0.1 °C warmer, 24 mm more precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. October is about as warm as September on average.

Kisangani in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kisangani in September?
In September, Kisangani averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 187 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Kisangani?
September is not one of Kisangani’s mild months; those are December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kisangani in September?
187 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (205 mm), the driest January (69 mm).
Does it snow in Kisangani in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kisangani get in September?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kisangani in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
