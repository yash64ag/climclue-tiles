How December compares in Kisangani

December is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Kisangani. Compared with November: 0.4 °C warmer, 65 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. January is 0.6 °C warmer than December on average.

Kisangani in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kisangani in December?
In December, Kisangani averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 98 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kisangani?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Kisangani’s mild months (December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kisangani in December?
98 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (205 mm), the driest January (69 mm).
Does it snow in Kisangani in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kisangani get in December?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kisangani in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
