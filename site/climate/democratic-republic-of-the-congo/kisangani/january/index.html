How January compares in Kisangani

January is the 4th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Kisangani. Compared with December: 0.6 °C warmer, 29 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. February is 0.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Kisangani in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kisangani in January?
In January, Kisangani averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kisangani?
January is not one of Kisangani’s mild months; those are December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kisangani in January?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is October (205 mm), the driest January (69 mm).
Does it snow in Kisangani in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kisangani get in January?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kisangani in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
