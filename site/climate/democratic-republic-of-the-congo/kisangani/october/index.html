How October compares in Kisangani

October is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kisangani. Compared with September: about as warm, 18 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. November is about as warm as October on average.

Kisangani in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kisangani in October?
In October, Kisangani averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 205 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kisangani?
October is not one of Kisangani’s mild months; those are December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kisangani in October?
205 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is October (205 mm), the driest January (69 mm).
Does it snow in Kisangani in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kisangani get in October?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kisangani in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
