How July compares in Kisangani

July is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kisangani. Compared with June: 0.4 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. August is about as warm as July on average.

Kisangani in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kisangani in July?
In July, Kisangani averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 141 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Kisangani?
July is not one of Kisangani’s mild months; those are December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kisangani in July?
107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (205 mm), the driest January (69 mm).
Does it snow in Kisangani in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kisangani get in July?
About 141 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kisangani in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
