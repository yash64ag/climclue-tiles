How August compares in Kisangani

August is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Kisangani. Compared with July: about as warm, 55 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. September is 0.1 °C warmer than August on average.

Kisangani in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kisangani in August?
In August, Kisangani averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 19.3 °C (67 °F), with 161 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Kisangani?
August is not one of Kisangani’s mild months; those are December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kisangani in August?
161 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (203 mm), the driest January (69 mm).
Does it snow in Kisangani in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kisangani get in August?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kisangani in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
