Gemena at a glance

Among the 38 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gemena is 10th warmest (25.0 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 2,104 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (1,740 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lisala, the nearest covered town with different numbers (230 km away), Gemena is about as warm in March and 0.5 °C cooler in August, with 44 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (26.4 °C), averages 2.4 °C more than its coolest, August (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.2 °C (70 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,099 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gemena, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gemena has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 1,099 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
