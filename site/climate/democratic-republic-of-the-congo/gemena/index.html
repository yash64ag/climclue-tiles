Why Gemena has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 1,099 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Gemena climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Gemena?
March, with an average of 26.4 °C (80 °F), day highs around 31.4 °C (89 °F) and night lows around 22.8 °C (73 °F).
What is the coldest month in Gemena?
August, with an average of 24.1 °C (75 °F), day highs around 28.1 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 21.2 °C (70 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Gemena?
August, with 218 mm (8.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,740 mm (68.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Gemena?
January, with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Gemena?
February, with about 205 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Gemena?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Gemena?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: December.
What type of climate does Gemena have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Gemena’s GeoNames location (3.257°N, 19.772°E; ClimaClue area sq-807-1997), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 197,159 (GeoNames).
