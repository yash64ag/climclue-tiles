How December compares in Mbuji-Mayi

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Mbuji-Mayi. Compared with November: about as warm, 19 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. January is 0.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Mbuji-Mayi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mbuji-Mayi in December?
In December, Mbuji-Mayi averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 194 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Mbuji-Mayi?
December is not one of Mbuji-Mayi’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mbuji-Mayi in December?
194 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (213 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Mbuji-Mayi in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mbuji-Mayi get in December?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mbuji-Mayi in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
