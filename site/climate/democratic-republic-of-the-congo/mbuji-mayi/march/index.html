Mbuji-Mayi in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mbuji-Mayi in March?
In March, Mbuji-Mayi averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 18.9 °C (66 °F), with 192 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Mbuji-Mayi?
March is not one of Mbuji-Mayi’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mbuji-Mayi in March?
192 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (213 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Mbuji-Mayi in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mbuji-Mayi get in March?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mbuji-Mayi in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
