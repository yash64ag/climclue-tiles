Why Mbuji-Mayi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,160 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Mbuji-Mayi climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Mbuji-Mayi?

What is the coldest month in Mbuji-Mayi?
November, with an average of 23.6 °C (75 °F), day highs around 29.1 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 20.2 °C (68 °F).

What is the driest month in Mbuji-Mayi?

What is the sunniest month in Mbuji-Mayi?
June, with about 251 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Mbuji-Mayi?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Mbuji-Mayi?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July and August.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Mbuji-Mayi’s GeoNames location (6.136°S, 23.590°E; ClimaClue area sq-901-2035), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 2,101,332 (GeoNames).
