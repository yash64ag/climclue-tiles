Mbuji-Mayi in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mbuji-Mayi in August?
In August, Mbuji-Mayi averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 19.7 °C (67 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 196 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Mbuji-Mayi?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Mbuji-Mayi’s mild months (May, June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mbuji-Mayi in August?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (214 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Mbuji-Mayi in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mbuji-Mayi get in August?
About 196 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mbuji-Mayi in August?
