How January compares in Mbuji-Mayi

January is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Mbuji-Mayi. Compared with December: 0.2 °C warmer, 41 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. February is 0.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Mbuji-Mayi in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mbuji-Mayi in January?
In January, Mbuji-Mayi averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 155 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Mbuji-Mayi?
January is not one of Mbuji-Mayi’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mbuji-Mayi in January?
155 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (214 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Mbuji-Mayi in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mbuji-Mayi get in January?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mbuji-Mayi in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
