Mbuji-Mayi weather in July

Average July weather in Mbuji-Mayi, Democratic Republic of the Congo: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Mbuji-Mayi averages 32.1 °C (90 °F) days and 17.4 °C (63 °F) nights, with 4 mm (0.2 in) of rain over the month and about 232 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Mbuji-Mayi

July is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Mbuji-Mayi. Compared with June: 0.1 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. August is 0.2 °C warmer than July on average.

Mbuji-Mayi in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mbuji-Mayi in July?
In July, Mbuji-Mayi averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Mbuji-Mayi?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Mbuji-Mayi’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mbuji-Mayi in July?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is November (213 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Mbuji-Mayi in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mbuji-Mayi get in July?
About 232 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mbuji-Mayi in July?
