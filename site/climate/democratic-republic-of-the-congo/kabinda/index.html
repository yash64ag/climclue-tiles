Kabinda at a glance

Among the 38 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kabinda is the 4th wettest (1,925 mm a year). Compared with Gandajika, the nearest covered town with different numbers (89 km away), Kabinda is 0.8 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C cooler in November, with 428 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.4 °C), averages 1.8 °C more than its coolest, November (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.4 °C (65 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,257 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kabinda, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kabinda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,257 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
