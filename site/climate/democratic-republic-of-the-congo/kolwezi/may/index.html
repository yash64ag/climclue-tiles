How May compares in Kolwezi

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Kolwezi. Compared with April: about as warm, 57 mm less precipitation and 48 hours more sunshine. June is 1.3 °C cooler than May on average.

Kolwezi in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kolwezi in May?
In May, Kolwezi averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 15.6 °C (60 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 275 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kolwezi?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Kolwezi’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kolwezi in May?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (249 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kolwezi in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kolwezi get in May?
About 275 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kolwezi in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
