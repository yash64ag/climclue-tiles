How April compares in Kolwezi

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Kolwezi. Compared with March: 0.3 °C warmer, 145 mm less precipitation and 79 hours more sunshine. May is about as warm as April on average.

Kolwezi in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kolwezi in April?
In April, Kolwezi averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 14.4 °C (58 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kolwezi?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Kolwezi’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kolwezi in April?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (249 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kolwezi in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kolwezi get in April?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kolwezi in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
