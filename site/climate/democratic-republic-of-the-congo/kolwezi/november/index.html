How November compares in Kolwezi

November is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kolwezi. Compared with October: 1.6 °C cooler, 114 mm more precipitation and 55 hours less sunshine. December is 0.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Kolwezi in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kolwezi in November?
In November, Kolwezi averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 194 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kolwezi?
November is not one of Kolwezi’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kolwezi in November?
194 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (249 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kolwezi in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kolwezi get in November?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kolwezi in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
