Bunia at a glance

Among the 115 towns ClimaClue covers in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Bunia is 96th warmest (21.1 °C year average), 55th sunniest (about 2,135 hours of sunshine a year) and 91st wettest (1,261 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Djugu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (49 km away), Bunia is 3.1 °C warmer in February and 2.9 °C warmer in July, with 1,223 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.1 °C), averages 1.7 °C more than its coolest, July (20.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.8 °C (59 °F), in November. The area lies about 1,215 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bunia, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bunia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 1,215 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bunia itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (1.559°N, 30.252°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-824-2102), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
