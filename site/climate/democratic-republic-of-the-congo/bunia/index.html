Why Bunia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 1,215 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Bunia climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Bunia?
February, with an average of 22.3 °C (72 °F), day highs around 27.8 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 17.4 °C (63 °F).
What is the coldest month in Bunia?
July, with an average of 20.4 °C (69 °F), day highs around 25.2 °C (77 °F) and night lows around 16.4 °C (62 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Bunia?
October, with 161 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,238 mm (48.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Bunia?
January, with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Bunia?
December, with about 200 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Bunia?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Bunia?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, May, June, July and December.
What type of climate does Bunia have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Bunia’s GeoNames location (1.559°N, 30.252°E; ClimaClue area sq-824-2102), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 399,282 (GeoNames).
