Bunia at a glance

Among the 38 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Bunia is 32nd warmest (21.1 °C year average), 19th sunniest (about 2,135 hours of sunshine a year) and 32nd wettest (1,238 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Beni, the nearest covered town with different numbers (147 km away), Bunia is 1.2 °C warmer in February and 0.9 °C warmer in July, with 1,457 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.3 °C), averages 1.8 °C more than its coolest, July (20.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.4 °C (62 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,215 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bunia, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bunia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 1,215 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
