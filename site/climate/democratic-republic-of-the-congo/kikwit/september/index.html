How September compares in Kikwit

September is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kikwit. Compared with August: 0.5 °C cooler, 80 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. October is 0.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Kikwit in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kikwit in September?
In September, Kikwit averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 138 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Kikwit?
No month in Kikwit is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kikwit in September?
138 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (193 mm), the driest July (9 mm).
Does it snow in Kikwit in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kikwit get in September?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kikwit in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
