Kikwit weather in October

Average October weather in Kikwit, Democratic Republic of the Congo: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Kikwit averages 29.7 °C (85 °F) days and 19.6 °C (67 °F) nights, with 175 mm (6.9 in) of rain over the month and about 175 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Kikwit

October is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Kikwit. Compared with September: 0.5 °C cooler, 37 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. November is 0.1 °C cooler than October on average.

What to pack for Kikwit in October

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella.

Kikwit in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kikwit in October?
In October, Kikwit averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 19.6 °C (67 °F), with 175 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kikwit?
No month in Kikwit is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kikwit in October?
175 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (198 mm), the driest July (8 mm).
Does it snow in Kikwit in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kikwit get in October?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kikwit in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
