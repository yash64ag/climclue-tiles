How January compares in Kikwit

January is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Kikwit. Compared with December: 0.4 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. February is 0.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Kikwit in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kikwit in January?
In January, Kikwit averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kikwit?
No month in Kikwit is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kikwit in January?
143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (193 mm), the driest July (9 mm).
Does it snow in Kikwit in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kikwit get in January?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kikwit in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
