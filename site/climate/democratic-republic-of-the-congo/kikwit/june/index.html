How June compares in Kikwit

June is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Kikwit. Compared with May: 0.1 °C cooler, 69 mm less precipitation and 55 hours more sunshine. July is 0.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Kikwit in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kikwit in June?
In June, Kikwit averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Kikwit?
No month in Kikwit is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kikwit in June?
14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (193 mm), the driest July (9 mm).
Does it snow in Kikwit in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kikwit get in June?
About 254 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kikwit in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
