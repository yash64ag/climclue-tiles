Butembo at a glance

Among the 38 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Butembo is the coolest (17.2 °C year average) and the 2nd wettest (2,251 mm a year). Compared with Beni, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Butembo is 3.0 °C cooler in February and 2.4 °C cooler in November, with 443 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (18.1 °C), averages 1.4 °C more than its coolest, November (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.9 °C (55 °F), in December. The area lies about 1,247 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Butembo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Butembo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 1,247 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
