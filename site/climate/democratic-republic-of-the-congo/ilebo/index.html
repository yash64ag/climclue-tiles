Ilebo at a glance

Among the 38 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ilebo is 11th warmest (25.0 °C year average), 27th sunniest (about 1,996 hours of sunshine a year) and 22nd wettest (1,436 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kikwit, the nearest covered town with different numbers (211 km away), Ilebo is 0.1 °C cooler in February and 0.1 °C cooler in November, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (25.4 °C), averages 1.0 °C more than its coolest, November (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.3 °C (70 °F), in July. The area lies about 914 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ilebo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ilebo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 914 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
