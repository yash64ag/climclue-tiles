Why Isiro has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 1,533 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Isiro climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Isiro?
February, with an average of 24.6 °C (76 °F), day highs around 31.0 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 20.0 °C (68 °F).
What is the coldest month in Isiro?
July, with an average of 21.9 °C (71 °F), day highs around 26.4 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 19.2 °C (67 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Isiro?
October, with 261 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,182 mm (85.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Isiro?
January, with 64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Isiro?
January, with about 213 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Isiro?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Isiro?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February and December.
What type of climate does Isiro have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Isiro’s GeoNames location (2.774°N, 27.616°E; ClimaClue area sq-812-2076), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 255,409 (GeoNames).
