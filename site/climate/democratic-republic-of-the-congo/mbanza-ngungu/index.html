Mbanza-Ngungu at a glance

Among the 38 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Mbanza-Ngungu is the 3rd least sunny (about 1,627 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Inkisi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Mbanza-Ngungu is 0.8 °C cooler in March and 1.0 °C cooler in July, with 149 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (24.1 °C), averages 3.0 °C more than its coolest, July (21.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.8 °C (62 °F), in July. The area lies about 298 km from the sea.

Why Mbanza-Ngungu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 298 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
