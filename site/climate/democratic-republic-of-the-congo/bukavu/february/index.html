How February compares in Bukavu

February is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Bukavu. Compared with January: 0.3 °C warmer, 11 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. March is 0.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Bukavu in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bukavu in February?
In February, Bukavu averages a day high of 24.6 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Bukavu?
February is not one of Bukavu’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bukavu in February?
134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (194 mm), the driest July (13 mm).
Does it snow in Bukavu in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bukavu get in February?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bukavu in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
