Bukavu at a glance

Among the 38 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Bukavu is the 4th coolest (20.5 °C year average). Compared with Uvira, the nearest covered town with different numbers (106 km away), Bukavu is 3.1 °C cooler in September and 3.6 °C cooler in January, with 465 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (21.1 °C), averages 1.0 °C more than its coolest, January (20.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.3 °C (61 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,179 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bukavu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Bukavu

For mild weather, the best months to visit Bukavu are June, July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July and August.
  • Warmest (average temperature): September (21.1 °C), August (20.9 °C) and March (20.6 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): July (13 mm), June (27 mm) and August (35 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (224 h), June (202 h) and August (184 h).

Why Bukavu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,179 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
