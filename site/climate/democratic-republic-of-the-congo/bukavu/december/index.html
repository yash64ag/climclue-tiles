How December compares in Bukavu

December is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Bukavu. Compared with November: about as warm, 61 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. January is about as warm as December on average.

Bukavu in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bukavu in December?
In December, Bukavu averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 141 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Bukavu?
December is not one of Bukavu’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bukavu in December?
141 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (202 mm), the driest July (12 mm).
Does it snow in Bukavu in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bukavu get in December?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bukavu in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
