How August compares in Bukavu

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Bukavu. Compared with July: 0.6 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. September is 0.2 °C warmer than August on average.

Bukavu in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bukavu in August?
In August, Bukavu averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Bukavu?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Bukavu’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bukavu in August?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (194 mm), the driest July (13 mm).
Does it snow in Bukavu in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bukavu get in August?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bukavu in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
