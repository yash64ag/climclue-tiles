How May compares in Bukavu

May is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Bukavu. Compared with April: 0.1 °C warmer, 56 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. June is 0.1 °C cooler than May on average.

Bukavu in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bukavu in May?
In May, Bukavu averages a day high of 24.4 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Bukavu?
May is not one of Bukavu’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bukavu in May?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (202 mm), the driest July (12 mm).
Does it snow in Bukavu in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bukavu get in May?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bukavu in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
