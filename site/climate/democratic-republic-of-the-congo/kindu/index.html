Kindu at a glance

Among the 38 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kindu is 15th warmest (24.6 °C year average), 31st sunniest (about 1,963 hours of sunshine a year) and 13th wettest (1,627 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bukavu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (328 km away), Kindu is 4.5 °C warmer in February and 3.8 °C warmer in November, with 250 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (24.9 °C), averages 1.0 °C more than its coolest, November (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.3 °C (70 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,471 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kindu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kindu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 1,471 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
