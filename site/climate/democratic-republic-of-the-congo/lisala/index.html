Why Lisala has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 1,303 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Lisala climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Lisala?
February, with an average of 26.6 °C (80 °F), day highs around 32.0 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 23.1 °C (74 °F).
What is the coldest month in Lisala?
August, with an average of 24.6 °C (76 °F), day highs around 28.5 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 21.9 °C (71 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Lisala?
September, with 196 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,696 mm (66.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Lisala?
January, with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Lisala?
February, with about 210 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Lisala?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Lisala?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Lisala’s GeoNames location (2.151°N, 21.517°E; ClimaClue area sq-818-2015), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 117,464 (GeoNames).
