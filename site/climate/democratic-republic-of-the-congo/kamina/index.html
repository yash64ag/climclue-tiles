Kamina at a glance

Among the 38 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kamina is 29th warmest (22.6 °C year average), 9th sunniest (about 2,499 hours of sunshine a year) and 25th wettest (1,366 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bukama, the nearest covered town with different numbers (108 km away), Kamina is 3.1 °C cooler in August and 2.6 °C cooler in January, with 113 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.4 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, January (21.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.9 °C (62 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,251 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kamina, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kamina has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,251 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
