How April compares in Kinshasa

April is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Kinshasa. Compared with March: 0.1 °C cooler, 38 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. May is 0.4 °C cooler than April on average.

Kinshasa in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kinshasa in April?
In April, Kinshasa averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kinshasa?
April is not one of Kinshasa’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kinshasa in April?
213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (258 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Kinshasa in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kinshasa get in April?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kinshasa in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
