How July compares in Kinshasa

July is the coolest month, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kinshasa. Compared with June: 0.7 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. August is 0.9 °C warmer than July on average.

Kinshasa in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kinshasa in July?
In July, Kinshasa averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 19.6 °C (67 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Kinshasa?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Kinshasa’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kinshasa in July?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is November (258 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Kinshasa in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kinshasa get in July?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kinshasa in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
