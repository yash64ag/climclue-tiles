How August compares in Kinshasa

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Kinshasa. Compared with July: 0.9 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. September is 1.3 °C warmer than August on average.

Kinshasa in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kinshasa in August?
In August, Kinshasa averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Kinshasa?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Kinshasa’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kinshasa in August?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (258 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Kinshasa in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kinshasa get in August?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kinshasa in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
