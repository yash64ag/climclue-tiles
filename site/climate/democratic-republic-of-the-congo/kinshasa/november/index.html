How November compares in Kinshasa

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Kinshasa. Compared with October: 0.6 °C cooler, 107 mm more precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. December is about as warm as November on average.

Kinshasa in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kinshasa in November?
In November, Kinshasa averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 260 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kinshasa?
November is not one of Kinshasa’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kinshasa in November?
260 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is November (260 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Kinshasa in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kinshasa get in November?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kinshasa in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
