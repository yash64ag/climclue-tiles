How December compares in Kinshasa

December is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Kinshasa. Compared with November: about as warm, 75 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. January is 0.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Kinshasa in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kinshasa in December?
In December, Kinshasa averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 182 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kinshasa?
December is not one of Kinshasa’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kinshasa in December?
182 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (258 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Kinshasa in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kinshasa get in December?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kinshasa in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
