How March compares in Kinshasa

March is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Kinshasa. Compared with February: 0.3 °C warmer, 25 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. April is 0.1 °C cooler than March on average.

Kinshasa in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kinshasa in March?
In March, Kinshasa averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 175 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kinshasa?
March is not one of Kinshasa’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kinshasa in March?
175 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (258 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Kinshasa in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kinshasa get in March?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kinshasa in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
