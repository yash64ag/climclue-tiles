How December compares in Likasi

December is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Likasi. Compared with November: 1.1 °C cooler, 89 mm more precipitation and 55 hours less sunshine. January is 0.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Likasi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Likasi in December?
In December, Likasi averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 257 mm (10.1 in) of precipitation and about 122 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Likasi?
December is not one of Likasi’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Likasi in December?
257 mm (10.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (257 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Likasi in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Likasi get in December?
About 122 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Likasi in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
