How November compares in Likasi

November is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Likasi. Compared with October: 1.9 °C cooler, 117 mm more precipitation and 61 hours less sunshine. December is 1.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Likasi in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Likasi in November?
In November, Likasi averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 17.3 °C (63 °F), with 169 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Likasi?
November is not one of Likasi’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Likasi in November?
169 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (257 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Likasi in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Likasi get in November?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Likasi in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
