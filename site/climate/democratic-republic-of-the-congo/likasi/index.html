Likasi at a glance

Among the 38 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Likasi is the 5th sunniest (about 2,656 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Kipushi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (103 km away), Likasi is 0.2 °C cooler in October and 0.6 °C warmer in July, with 68 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (23.8 °C), averages 5.5 °C more than its coolest, July (18.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.3 °C (54 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,324 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Likasi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Likasi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,324 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
