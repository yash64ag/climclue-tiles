How May compares in Likasi

May is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Likasi. Compared with April: 0.4 °C cooler, 56 mm less precipitation and 55 hours more sunshine. June is 1.8 °C cooler than May on average.

Likasi in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Likasi in May?
In May, Likasi averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 288 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Likasi?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Likasi’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Likasi in May?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (259 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Likasi in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Likasi get in May?
About 288 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Likasi in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
