How September compares in Kananga

September is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kananga. Compared with August: 0.6 °C cooler, 67 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. October is 0.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Kananga in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kananga in September?
In September, Kananga averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Kananga?
September is not one of Kananga’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kananga in September?
143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (193 mm), the driest July (7 mm).
Does it snow in Kananga in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kananga get in September?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kananga in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
