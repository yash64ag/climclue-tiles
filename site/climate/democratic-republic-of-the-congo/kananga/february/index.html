How February compares in Kananga

February is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Kananga. Compared with January: 0.5 °C warmer, 17 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. March is 0.1 °C cooler than February on average.

Kananga in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kananga in February?
In February, Kananga averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.0 °C (70 °F), with 132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Kananga?
February is not one of Kananga’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kananga in February?
132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (193 mm), the driest July (7 mm).
Does it snow in Kananga in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kananga get in February?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kananga in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
