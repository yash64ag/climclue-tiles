How November compares in Kananga

November is the coolest month, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kananga. Compared with October: 0.2 °C cooler, 28 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. December is 0.2 °C warmer than November on average.

Kananga in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kananga in November?
In November, Kananga averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 193 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kananga?
November is not one of Kananga’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kananga in November?
193 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is November (193 mm), the driest July (7 mm).
Does it snow in Kananga in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kananga get in November?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kananga in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
