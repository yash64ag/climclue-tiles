Kananga at a glance

Among the 38 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kananga is 19th warmest (24.3 °C year average), 20th sunniest (about 2,105 hours of sunshine a year) and 18th wettest (1,484 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mbuji-Mayi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (132 km away), Kananga is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in November, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.9 °C), averages 1.3 °C more than its coolest, November (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.4 °C (67 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,050 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kananga, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Kananga

For mild weather, the best months to visit Kananga are May, June, July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May, June, July and August.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (24.9 °C), May (24.8 °C) and August (24.6 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): July (7 mm), June (11 mm) and May (70 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (223 h), June (216 h) and May (208 h).

Why Kananga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,050 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
