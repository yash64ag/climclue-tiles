How December compares in Kananga

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kananga. Compared with November: 0.2 °C warmer, 16 mm less precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. January is 0.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Kananga in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kananga in December?
In December, Kananga averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.7 °C (69 °F), with 177 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kananga?
December is not one of Kananga’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kananga in December?
177 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (193 mm), the driest July (7 mm).
Does it snow in Kananga in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kananga get in December?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kananga in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
