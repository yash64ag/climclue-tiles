How August compares in Kananga

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Kananga. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 69 mm more precipitation and 44 hours less sunshine. September is 0.6 °C cooler than August on average.

Kananga in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kananga in August?
In August, Kananga averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 20.5 °C (69 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Kananga?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Kananga’s mild months (May, June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kananga in August?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (193 mm), the driest July (7 mm).
Does it snow in Kananga in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kananga get in August?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kananga in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
