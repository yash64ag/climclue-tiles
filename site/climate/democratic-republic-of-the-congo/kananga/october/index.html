How October compares in Kananga

October is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kananga. Compared with September: 0.3 °C cooler, 22 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. November is 0.2 °C cooler than October on average.

Kananga in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kananga in October?
In October, Kananga averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 20.7 °C (69 °F), with 165 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kananga?
October is not one of Kananga’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kananga in October?
165 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (193 mm), the driest July (7 mm).
Does it snow in Kananga in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kananga get in October?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kananga in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
