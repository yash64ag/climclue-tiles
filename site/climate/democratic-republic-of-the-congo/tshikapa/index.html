Why Tshikapa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 862 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Tshikapa climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Tshikapa?
August, with an average of 26.1 °C (79 °F), day highs around 32.3 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 21.4 °C (71 °F).
What is the coldest month in Tshikapa?
November, with an average of 24.4 °C (76 °F), day highs around 29.1 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 21.2 °C (70 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Tshikapa?
November, with 187 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,286 mm (50.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Tshikapa?
July, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Tshikapa?
July, with about 259 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Tshikapa?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Tshikapa?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Tshikapa’s GeoNames location (6.416°S, 20.800°E; ClimaClue area sq-904-2008), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 634,529 (GeoNames).
