How August compares in Tshikapa

August is the warmest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Tshikapa. Compared with July: 0.3 °C warmer, 40 mm more precipitation and 57 hours less sunshine. September is 0.8 °C cooler than August on average.

Tshikapa in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tshikapa in August?
In August, Tshikapa averages a day high of 33.1 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 19.6 °C (67 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Tshikapa?
No month in Tshikapa is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tshikapa in August?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (181 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tshikapa in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tshikapa get in August?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tshikapa in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
