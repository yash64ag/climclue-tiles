Tshikapa weather in January

Average January weather in Tshikapa, Democratic Republic of the Congo: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Tshikapa averages 29.9 °C (86 °F) days and 20.2 °C (68 °F) nights, with 135 mm (5.3 in) of rain over the month and about 148 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Tshikapa

January is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Tshikapa. Compared with December: 0.3 °C warmer, 18 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. February is 0.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Tshikapa in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tshikapa in January?
In January, Tshikapa averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Tshikapa?
No month in Tshikapa is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tshikapa in January?
135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (181 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tshikapa in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tshikapa get in January?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
