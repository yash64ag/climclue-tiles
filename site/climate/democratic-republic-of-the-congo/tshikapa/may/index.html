Tshikapa in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tshikapa in May?
In May, Tshikapa averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Tshikapa?
No month in Tshikapa is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tshikapa in May?
42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (187 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tshikapa in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tshikapa get in May?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tshikapa in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
