How March compares in Tshikapa

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Tshikapa. Compared with February: about as warm, 7 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. April is about as warm as March on average.

Tshikapa in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tshikapa in March?
In March, Tshikapa averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Tshikapa?
No month in Tshikapa is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tshikapa in March?
153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (187 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tshikapa in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tshikapa get in March?
About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tshikapa in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
