Tshikapa in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tshikapa in July?
In July, Tshikapa averages a day high of 33.1 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 19.7 °C (67 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Tshikapa?
No month in Tshikapa is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tshikapa in July?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is November (187 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tshikapa in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tshikapa get in July?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tshikapa in July?
