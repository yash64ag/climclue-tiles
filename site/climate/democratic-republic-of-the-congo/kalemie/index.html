Why Kalemie has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,073 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Kalemie climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Kalemie?
September, with an average of 25.4 °C (78 °F), day highs around 29.1 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 22.2 °C (72 °F).
What is the coldest month in Kalemie?
December, with an average of 23.4 °C (74 °F), day highs around 26.2 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 21.2 °C (70 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Kalemie?
November, with 102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 644 mm (25.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Kalemie?
July, with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Kalemie?
July, with about 298 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Kalemie?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Kalemie?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, October and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Kalemie’s GeoNames location (5.947°S, 29.195°E; ClimaClue area sq-899-2091), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 160,961 (GeoNames).
