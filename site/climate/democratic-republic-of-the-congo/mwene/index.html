Mwene at a glance

Among the 38 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Mwene is 25th warmest (23.4 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 2,645 hours of sunshine a year) and 31st wettest (1,247 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tshilenge, the nearest covered town with different numbers (128 km away), Mwene is 1.0 °C warmer in September and 1.2 °C warmer in January, with 351 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (25.4 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, January (22.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.2 °C (61 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,003 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mwene, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mwene has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,003 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
