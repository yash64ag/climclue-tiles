Bukama at a glance

Among the 38 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Bukama is the 2nd warmest (25.7 °C year average) and the 4th sunniest (about 2,695 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Kamina, the nearest covered town with different numbers (108 km away), Bukama is 3.7 °C warmer in September and 2.6 °C warmer in January, with 113 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (28.1 °C), averages 4.0 °C more than its coolest, January (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.9 °C (68 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,338 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bukama, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bukama has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,338 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
