Moanda at a glance

Among the 38 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Moanda is the 4th least sunny (about 1,628 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (895 mm a year). Compared with Boma, the nearest covered town with different numbers (76 km away), Moanda is 0.2 °C warmer in March and 0.4 °C cooler in July, with 261 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (26.9 °C), averages 5.6 °C more than its coolest, July (21.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.0 °C (68 °F), in July. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Moanda, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Moanda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
