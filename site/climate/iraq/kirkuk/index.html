Why Kirkuk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 497 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Kirkuk climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Kirkuk?
July, with an average of 36.0 °C (97 °F), day highs around 42.6 °C (109 °F) and night lows around 27.9 °C (82 °F).
What is the coldest month in Kirkuk?
January, with an average of 8.3 °C (47 °F), day highs around 12.6 °C (55 °F) and night lows around 3.6 °C (38 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Kirkuk?
January, with 84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 457 mm (18.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Kirkuk?
August, with 0 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Kirkuk?
July, with about 418 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Kirkuk?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 1.7 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Kirkuk?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, October and November.
What type of climate does Kirkuk have?
Mediterranean, hot summer (Köppen Csa). Hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.1 × 11 km, that contains Kirkuk’s GeoNames location (35.468°N, 44.392°E; ClimaClue area sq-485-2243), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,031,000 (GeoNames).
