How December compares in Kirkuk

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Kirkuk. Compared with November: 5.5 °C cooler, 11 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Kirkuk in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kirkuk in December?
In December, Kirkuk averages a day high of 14.5 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 5.5 °C (42 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kirkuk?
December is not one of Kirkuk’s mild months; those are April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kirkuk in December?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (84 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kirkuk in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kirkuk get in December?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kirkuk in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
