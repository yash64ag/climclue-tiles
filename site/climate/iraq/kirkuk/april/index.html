How April compares in Kirkuk

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Kirkuk. Compared with March: 6.1 °C warmer, 18 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. May is 6.7 °C warmer than April on average.

Kirkuk in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kirkuk in April?
In April, Kirkuk averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 13.4 °C (56 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kirkuk?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Kirkuk’s mild months (April, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kirkuk in April?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (84 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kirkuk in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kirkuk get in April?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kirkuk in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
