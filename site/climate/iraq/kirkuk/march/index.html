How March compares in Kirkuk

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kirkuk. Compared with February: 4.4 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. April is 6.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Kirkuk in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kirkuk in March?
In March, Kirkuk averages a day high of 19.0 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 7.9 °C (46 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kirkuk?
March is not one of Kirkuk’s mild months; those are April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kirkuk in March?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (84 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kirkuk in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kirkuk get in March?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kirkuk in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
