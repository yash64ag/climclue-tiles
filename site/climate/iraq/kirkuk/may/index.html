How May compares in Kirkuk

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kirkuk. Compared with April: 6.7 °C warmer, 35 mm less precipitation and 84 hours more sunshine. June is 5.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Kirkuk in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kirkuk in May?
In May, Kirkuk averages a day high of 33.2 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 19.3 °C (67 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 324 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kirkuk?
May is not one of Kirkuk’s mild months; those are April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kirkuk in May?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (84 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kirkuk in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kirkuk get in May?
About 324 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kirkuk in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
