Kufa at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iraq, Kufa is the 3rd sunniest (about 3,616 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (106 mm a year). Compared with Najaf, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Kufa is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (37.6 °C), averages 26.4 °C more than its coolest, January (11.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.5 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 442 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kufa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kufa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 442 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
