How March compares in Karbala

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Karbala. Compared with February: 4.8 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. April is 6.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Karbala in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Karbala in March?
In March, Karbala averages a day high of 23.6 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 11.6 °C (53 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Karbala?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Karbala’s mild months (March, April and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Karbala in March?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (21 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Karbala in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Karbala get in March?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Karbala in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
