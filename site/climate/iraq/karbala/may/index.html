How May compares in Karbala

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Karbala. Compared with April: 6.1 °C warmer, 11 mm less precipitation and 68 hours more sunshine. June is 4.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Karbala in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Karbala in May?
In May, Karbala averages a day high of 38.4 °C (101 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 341 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Karbala?
May is not one of Karbala’s mild months; those are March, April and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Karbala in May?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (21 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Karbala in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Karbala get in May?
About 341 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Karbala in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
