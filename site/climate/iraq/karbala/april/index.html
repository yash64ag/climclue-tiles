How April compares in Karbala

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Karbala. Compared with March: 6.5 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. May is 6.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Karbala in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Karbala in April?
In April, Karbala averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 273 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Karbala?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Karbala’s mild months (March, April and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Karbala in April?
14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (21 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Karbala in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Karbala get in April?
About 273 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Karbala in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
