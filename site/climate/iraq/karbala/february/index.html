How February compares in Karbala

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Karbala. Compared with January: 2.4 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. March is 4.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Karbala in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Karbala in February?
In February, Karbala averages a day high of 19.5 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 7.2 °C (45 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Karbala?
February is not one of Karbala’s mild months; those are March, April and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Karbala in February?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (21 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Karbala in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Karbala get in February?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Karbala in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
