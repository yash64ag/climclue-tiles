How November compares in Karbala

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Karbala. Compared with October: 8.6 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 71 hours less sunshine. December is 5.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Karbala in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Karbala in November?
In November, Karbala averages a day high of 25.0 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 11.5 °C (53 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Karbala?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Karbala’s mild months (March, April and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Karbala in November?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (21 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Karbala in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Karbala get in November?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Karbala in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
