Abū Ghurayb weather in October

Average October weather in Abū Ghurayb, Iraq: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Abū Ghurayb averages 35.8 °C (96 °F) days and 18.6 °C (65 °F) nights, with 8 mm (0.3 in) of rain over the month and about 281 hours of sunshine.
