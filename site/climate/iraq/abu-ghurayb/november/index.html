Abū Ghurayb in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abū Ghurayb in November?
In November, Abū Ghurayb averages a day high of 23.6 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 12.4 °C (54 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Abū Ghurayb?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Abū Ghurayb’s mild months (March, April and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abū Ghurayb in November?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (26 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Abū Ghurayb in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abū Ghurayb get in November?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
