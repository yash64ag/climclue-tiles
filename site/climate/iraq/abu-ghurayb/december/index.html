Abū Ghurayb weather in December

Average December weather in Abū Ghurayb, Iraq: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

How December compares in Abū Ghurayb

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Abū Ghurayb. Compared with November: 5.7 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. January is 1.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Abū Ghurayb in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Abū Ghurayb in December?
In December, Abū Ghurayb averages a day high of 18.6 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 6.6 °C (44 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Abū Ghurayb?
December is not one of Abū Ghurayb’s mild months; those are March, April and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Abū Ghurayb in December?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (26 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Abū Ghurayb in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Abū Ghurayb get in December?
