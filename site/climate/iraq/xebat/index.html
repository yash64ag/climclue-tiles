Xebat at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iraq, Xebat is 40th warmest (20.3 °C year average), 30th sunniest (about 3,196 hours of sunshine a year) and 14th wettest (495 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Erbil, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Xebat is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 134 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (34.6 °C), averages 28.2 °C more than its coolest, January (6.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 511 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Xebat, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Xebat has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 511 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
