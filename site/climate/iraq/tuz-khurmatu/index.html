Ţūz Khūrmātū at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iraq, Ţūz Khūrmātū is 34th warmest (23.1 °C year average), 28th sunniest (about 3,305 hours of sunshine a year) and 18th wettest (391 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kirkuk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (68 km away), Ţūz Khūrmātū is 0.8 °C warmer in July and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 65 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (36.9 °C), averages 27.9 °C more than its coolest, January (8.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 516 km from the sea.
