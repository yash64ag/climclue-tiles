As Samawah at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iraq, As Samawah is the sunniest (about 3,627 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (107 mm a year). Compared with Al Diwaniyah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (81 km away), As Samawah is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 6 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (38.5 °C), averages 26.7 °C more than its coolest, January (11.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.8 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 334 km from the sea.

Why As Samawah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 334 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
