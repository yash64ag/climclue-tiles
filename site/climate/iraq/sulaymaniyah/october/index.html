How October compares in Sulaymaniyah

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Sulaymaniyah. Compared with September: 6.4 °C cooler, 49 mm more precipitation and 74 hours less sunshine. November is 8.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Sulaymaniyah in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sulaymaniyah in October?
In October, Sulaymaniyah averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 11.6 °C (53 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Sulaymaniyah?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Sulaymaniyah’s mild months (May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sulaymaniyah in October?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (157 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Sulaymaniyah in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sulaymaniyah get in October?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sulaymaniyah in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
