How March compares in Sulaymaniyah

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Sulaymaniyah. Compared with February: 4.6 °C warmer, 15 mm less precipitation and 38 hours more sunshine. April is 5.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Sulaymaniyah in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sulaymaniyah in March?
In March, Sulaymaniyah averages a day high of 14.3 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 2.1 °C (36 °F), with 142 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Sulaymaniyah?
March is not one of Sulaymaniyah’s mild months; those are May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sulaymaniyah in March?
142 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (157 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Sulaymaniyah in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.7 cm (2.6 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Sulaymaniyah get in March?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sulaymaniyah in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
