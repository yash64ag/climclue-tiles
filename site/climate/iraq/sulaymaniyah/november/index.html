How November compares in Sulaymaniyah

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Sulaymaniyah. Compared with October: 8.1 °C cooler, 69 mm more precipitation and 62 hours less sunshine. December is 5.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Sulaymaniyah in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sulaymaniyah in November?
In November, Sulaymaniyah averages a day high of 17.2 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 5.9 °C (43 °F), with 119 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Sulaymaniyah?
November is not one of Sulaymaniyah’s mild months; those are May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sulaymaniyah in November?
119 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (150 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Sulaymaniyah in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sulaymaniyah get in November?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sulaymaniyah in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
