Sulaymaniyah at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iraq, Sulaymaniyah is the 3rd coolest (17.0 °C year average) and the 2nd wettest (937 mm a year). Compared with Qeładizê, the nearest covered town with different numbers (74 km away), Sulaymaniyah is 1.4 °C cooler in July and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 54 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.9 °C), averages 27.7 °C more than its coolest, January (3.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 410 km from the sea.

Why Sulaymaniyah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 410 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
