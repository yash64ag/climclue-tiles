How September compares in Erbil

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Erbil. Compared with August: 4.8 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 54 hours less sunshine. October is 6.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Erbil in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Erbil in September?
In September, Erbil averages a day high of 36.4 °C (98 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 333 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Erbil?
September is not one of Erbil’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Erbil in September?

Does it snow in Erbil in September?
