How November compares in Erbil

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Erbil. Compared with October: 8.4 °C cooler, 55 mm more precipitation and 67 hours less sunshine. December is 5.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Erbil in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Erbil in November?
In November, Erbil averages a day high of 19.5 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 8.3 °C (47 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Erbil?
November is not one of Erbil’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Erbil in November?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (102 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Erbil in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Erbil get in November?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Erbil in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
