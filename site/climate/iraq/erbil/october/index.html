How October compares in Erbil

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Erbil. Compared with September: 6.5 °C cooler, 36 mm more precipitation and 81 hours less sunshine. November is 8.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Erbil in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Erbil in October?
In October, Erbil averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 15.6 °C (60 °F), with 37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 252 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Erbil?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Erbil’s mild months (April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Erbil in October?
37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (103 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Erbil in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Erbil get in October?
About 252 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Erbil in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
