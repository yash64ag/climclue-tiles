How December compares in Erbil

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Erbil. Compared with November: 5.6 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 33 hours less sunshine. January is 1.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Erbil in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Erbil in December?
In December, Erbil averages a day high of 12.5 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 4.0 °C (39 °F), with 93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Erbil?
December is not one of Erbil’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Erbil in December?
93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (102 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Erbil in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Erbil get in December?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Erbil in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
