Al Qurnah at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iraq, Al Qurnah is the 2nd warmest (26.9 °C year average). Compared with Al Madīnah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Al Qurnah is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (39.6 °C), averages 27.1 °C more than its coolest, January (12.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.3 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 164 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Al Qurnah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Al Qurnah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 164 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
