How May compares in Baghdad

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Baghdad. Compared with April: 6.3 °C warmer, 13 mm less precipitation and 72 hours more sunshine. June is 4.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Baghdad in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baghdad in May?
In May, Baghdad averages a day high of 37.6 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 333 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Baghdad?
May is not one of Baghdad’s mild months; those are March, April and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baghdad in May?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (30 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Baghdad in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baghdad get in May?
About 333 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baghdad in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
