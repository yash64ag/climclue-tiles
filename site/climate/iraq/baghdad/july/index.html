How July compares in Baghdad

July is the warmest month, the driest month and the sunniest month in Baghdad. Compared with June: 2.4 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Baghdad in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baghdad in July?
In July, Baghdad averages a day high of 47.1 °C (117 °F) and a night low of 29.0 °C (84 °F), with 0 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 417 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Baghdad?
July is not one of Baghdad’s mild months; those are March, April and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
