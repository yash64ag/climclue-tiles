Sīnah at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iraq, Sīnah is the coolest (17.1 °C year average). Compared with Dihok, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Sīnah is 0.8 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 9 mm less precipitation a year. Why Sīnah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 521 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.4 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
