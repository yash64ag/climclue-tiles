How April compares in Nasiriyah

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Nasiriyah. Compared with March: 6.8 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. May is 6.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Nasiriyah in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nasiriyah in April?
In April, Nasiriyah averages a day high of 33.9 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 19.9 °C (68 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Nasiriyah?
April is not one of Nasiriyah’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nasiriyah in April?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (28 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Nasiriyah in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nasiriyah get in April?
About 278 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nasiriyah in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
