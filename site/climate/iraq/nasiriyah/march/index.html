How March compares in Nasiriyah

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Nasiriyah. Compared with February: 5.0 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. April is 6.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Nasiriyah in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nasiriyah in March?
In March, Nasiriyah averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Nasiriyah?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Nasiriyah’s mild months (February, March and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nasiriyah in March?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (28 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Nasiriyah in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nasiriyah get in March?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nasiriyah in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
