How November compares in Nasiriyah

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Nasiriyah. Compared with October: 8.8 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 68 hours less sunshine. December is 5.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Nasiriyah in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nasiriyah in November?
In November, Nasiriyah averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Nasiriyah?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Nasiriyah’s mild months (February, March and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nasiriyah in November?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (28 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Nasiriyah in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nasiriyah get in November?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nasiriyah in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
