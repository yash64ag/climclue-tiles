How November compares in Basrah

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Basrah. Compared with October: 8.4 °C cooler, 13 mm more precipitation and 62 hours less sunshine. December is 5.8 °C cooler than November on average.

Basrah in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Basrah in November?
In November, Basrah averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 14.5 °C (58 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Basrah?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Basrah’s mild months (February, March and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Basrah in November?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (35 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Basrah in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Basrah get in November?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Basrah in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
