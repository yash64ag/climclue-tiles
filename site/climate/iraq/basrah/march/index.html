How March compares in Basrah

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Basrah. Compared with February: 4.8 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. April is 6.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Basrah in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Basrah in March?
In March, Basrah averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 12.9 °C (55 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Basrah?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Basrah’s mild months (February, March and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Basrah in March?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (35 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Basrah in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Basrah get in March?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Basrah in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
