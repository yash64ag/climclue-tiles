How October compares in Basrah

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Basrah. Compared with September: 6.3 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 36 hours less sunshine. November is 8.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Basrah in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Basrah in October?
In October, Basrah averages a day high of 35.9 °C (97 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 265 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Basrah?
October is not one of Basrah’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Basrah in October?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (35 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Basrah in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Basrah get in October?
About 265 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Basrah in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
