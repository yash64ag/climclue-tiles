Az Zubayr at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iraq, Az Zubayr is the 5th warmest (26.8 °C year average). Compared with Basrah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Az Zubayr is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (39.4 °C), averages 26.8 °C more than its coolest, January (12.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.2 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 87 km from the sea.

Why Az Zubayr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 87 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
