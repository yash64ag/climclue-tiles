How March compares in Mosul

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Mosul. Compared with February: 4.3 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. April is 5.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Mosul in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mosul in March?
In March, Mosul averages a day high of 18.2 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 6.7 °C (44 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Mosul?
March is not one of Mosul’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mosul in March?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (73 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Mosul in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mosul get in March?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mosul in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
