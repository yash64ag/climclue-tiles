How January compares in Mosul

January is the coolest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Mosul. Compared with December: 1.8 °C cooler, 6 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. February is 1.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Mosul in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mosul in January?
In January, Mosul averages a day high of 12.0 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 2.3 °C (36 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Mosul?
January is not one of Mosul’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mosul in January?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (74 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Mosul in January?
Yes, on average: about 0.8 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Mosul get in January?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mosul in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
