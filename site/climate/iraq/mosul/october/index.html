Mosul weather in October

Average October weather in Mosul, Iraq: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Mosul averages 30.7 °C (87 °F) days and 15.2 °C (59 °F) nights, with 23 mm (0.9 in) of rain over the month and about 251 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Mosul

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Mosul. Compared with September: 6.7 °C cooler, 22 mm more precipitation and 84 hours less sunshine. November is 8.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Mosul in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mosul in October?
In October, Mosul averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Mosul?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Mosul’s mild months (April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mosul in October?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (74 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Mosul in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mosul get in October?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mosul in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
