How December compares in Mosul

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Mosul. Compared with November: 5.6 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 33 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Mosul in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mosul in December?
In December, Mosul averages a day high of 14.0 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 4.1 °C (39 °F), with 67 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Mosul?
December is not one of Mosul’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mosul in December?
67 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (74 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Mosul in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mosul get in December?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mosul in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
