Nāḩiyat al Fuhūd at a glance

Among the 105 towns ClimaClue covers in Iraq, Nāḩiyat al Fuhūd is the 4th warmest (26.9 °C year average). Compared with Nasiriyah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Nāḩiyat al Fuhūd is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (39.5 °C), averages 27.1 °C more than its coolest, January (12.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.7 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 203 km from the sea.

Why Nāḩiyat al Fuhūd has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 203 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
