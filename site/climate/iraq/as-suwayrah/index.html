Aş Şuwayrah at a glance

Among the 105 towns ClimaClue covers in Iraq, Aş Şuwayrah is 24th warmest (25.7 °C year average), 27th sunniest (about 3,504 hours of sunshine a year) and 65th wettest (158 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Al ‘Azīzīyah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Aş Şuwayrah is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 17 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (38.5 °C), averages 27.1 °C more than its coolest, January (11.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.8 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 492 km from the sea.

Why Aş Şuwayrah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 492 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Aş Şuwayrah itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (32.926°N, 44.776°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
