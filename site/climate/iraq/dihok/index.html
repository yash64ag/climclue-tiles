Dihok at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iraq, Dihok is the 5th coolest (18.0 °C year average). Compared with Sīnah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Dihok is 1.3 °C warmer in July and 1.4 °C warmer in January, with 57 mm less precipitation a year. Why Dihok has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 518 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
