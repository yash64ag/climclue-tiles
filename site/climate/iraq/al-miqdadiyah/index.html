Al Miqdādīyah at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iraq, Al Miqdādīyah is 29th warmest (24.8 °C year average), 25th sunniest (about 3,349 hours of sunshine a year) and 19th wettest (290 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Baqubah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (40 km away), Al Miqdādīyah is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 74 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (38.3 °C), averages 27.8 °C more than its coolest, January (10.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.3 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 555 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Al Miqdādīyah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Al Miqdādīyah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 555 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
