Al Maḩmūdīyah at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iraq, Al Maḩmūdīyah is 19th warmest (25.3 °C year average), 15th sunniest (about 3,517 hours of sunshine a year) and 38th wettest (135 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nāḩiyat al Iskandarīyah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Al Maḩmūdīyah is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 8 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (38.0 °C), averages 26.9 °C more than its coolest, January (11.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.9 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 527 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Al Maḩmūdīyah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Al Maḩmūdīyah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 527 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
