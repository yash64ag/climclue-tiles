Al Hindīyah at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iraq, Al Hindīyah is the 5th driest (113 mm a year). Compared with Abu Gharaq, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Al Hindīyah is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (37.5 °C), averages 26.4 °C more than its coolest, January (11.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.7 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 493 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Al Hindīyah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Al Hindīyah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 493 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
