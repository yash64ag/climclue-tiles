Qeładizê at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iraq, Qeładizê is the 3rd least sunny (about 3,020 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (991 mm a year). Compared with Rānyah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Qeładizê is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 178 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (32.4 °C), averages 28.2 °C more than its coolest, January (4.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 395 km from the sea.

Why Qeładizê has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 395 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
