Khasnahzān at a glance

Among the 52 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Iraq, Khasnahzān is 45th warmest (18.3 °C year average), 35th sunniest (about 3,161 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (813 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Erbil, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Khasnahzān is 1.9 °C cooler in July and 1.6 °C cooler in January, with 184 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (32.5 °C), averages 27.8 °C more than its coolest, January (4.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 470 km from the sea.

Why Khasnahzān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 470 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
