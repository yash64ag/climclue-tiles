Nacka at a glance

Among the 99 towns ClimaClue covers in Sweden, Nacka is 56th warmest (6.6 °C year average), 61st sunniest (about 1,488 hours of sunshine a year) and 81st wettest (596 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Stockholm, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Nacka is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in February, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.0 °C), averages 19.1 °C more than its coolest, February (-2.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 32 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nacka, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nacka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 32 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nacka itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (59.311°N, 18.164°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 5.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-246-1981), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
