Kalmar sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Sweden

The sea off Kalmar averages 17.3 °C (63 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 1.4 °C (35 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Kalmar sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kalmar?
In August, at about 17.3 °C (63 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kalmar?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 1.4 °C (35 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kalmar in February?
About 1.4 °C (35 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Kalmar?
About 8.2 °C (47 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Kalmar nearby?
In August: Halmstad (18.2 °C, 214 km), Ängelholm (18.3 °C, 220 km) and Ustka (18.4 °C, 234 km).
