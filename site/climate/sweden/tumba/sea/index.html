Tumba sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Stockholm · Sweden

The sea off Tumba averages 17.0 °C (63 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 1.1 °C (34 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Tumba sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Tumba?
In August, at about 17.0 °C (63 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Tumba?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 1.1 °C (34 °F).
How warm is the sea in Tumba in March?
About 1.1 °C (34 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Tumba?
About 7.8 °C (46 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Tumba nearby?
In August: Västervik (17.6 °C, 175 km) and Visby (17.7 °C, 175 km).
