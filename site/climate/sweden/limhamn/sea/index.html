Limhamn sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Skåne · Sweden

The sea off Limhamn averages 17.5 °C (64 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 2.0 °C (36 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Limhamn sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Limhamn?
In August, at about 17.5 °C (64 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Limhamn?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 2.0 °C (36 °F).
How warm is the sea in Limhamn in February?
About 2.0 °C (36 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Limhamn?
About 9.2 °C (49 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Limhamn nearby?
In August: Landskrona (18.3 °C, 33 km).
