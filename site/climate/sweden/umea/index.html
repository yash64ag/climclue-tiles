Umeå at a glance

Among the 11 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sweden, Umeå is the coolest (3.6 °C year average), the least sunny (about 1,436 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (779 mm a year). Compared with Uppsala, the nearest covered town with different numbers (462 km away), Umeå is 1.5 °C cooler in July and 3.7 °C cooler in February, with 152 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (15.8 °C), averages 22.6 °C more than its coolest, February (-6.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 116% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Umeå has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
