Västerås at a glance

Among the 11 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sweden, Västerås is the 3rd coolest (6.2 °C year average). Compared with Uppsala, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Västerås is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 22 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.3 °C), averages 20.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 115 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Västerås, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Västerås has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 115 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
