Örnsköldsvik sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Västernorrland · Sweden

The sea off Örnsköldsvik averages 15.6 °C (60 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -0.8 °C (31 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Örnsköldsvik sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Örnsköldsvik?
In August, at about 15.6 °C (60 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Örnsköldsvik?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at -0.8 °C (31 °F).
How warm is the sea in Örnsköldsvik in March?
About -0.8 °C (31 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Örnsköldsvik?
About 6.2 °C (43 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Örnsköldsvik nearby?
In August: Pori (16.3 °C, 256 km) and Rauma (16.3 °C, 281 km).
