Örnsköldsvik weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Örnsköldsvik, Sweden (Västernorrland), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Why Örnsköldsvik has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
