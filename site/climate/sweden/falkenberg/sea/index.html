Falkenberg sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Halland · Sweden

The sea off Falkenberg averages 17.9 °C (64 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 2.1 °C (36 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Falkenberg sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Falkenberg?
In August, at about 17.9 °C (64 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Falkenberg?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 2.1 °C (36 °F).
How warm is the sea in Falkenberg in February?
About 2.1 °C (36 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Falkenberg?
About 9.5 °C (49 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
