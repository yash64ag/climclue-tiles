Gustavsberg sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Stockholm · Sweden

The sea off Gustavsberg averages 16.8 °C (62 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 0.9 °C (34 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Gustavsberg sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Gustavsberg?
In August, at about 16.8 °C (62 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Gustavsberg?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 0.9 °C (34 °F).
How warm is the sea in Gustavsberg in March?
About 0.9 °C (34 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Gustavsberg?
About 7.5 °C (46 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Gustavsberg nearby?
In August: Visby (17.7 °C, 188 km) and Västervik (17.6 °C, 202 km).
