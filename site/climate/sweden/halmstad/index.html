Halmstad weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Halmstad, Sweden (Halland), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Halmstad at a glance

Among the 99 towns ClimaClue covers in Sweden, Halmstad is the 5th wettest (1,010 mm a year). Compared with Falkenberg, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Halmstad is about as warm in July and 0.3 °C cooler in February, with 103 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.2 °C), averages 17.5 °C more than its coolest, February (-0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 117% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Halmstad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Halmstad itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (56.674°N, 12.857°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-273-1928), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
