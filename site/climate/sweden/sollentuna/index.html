Why Sollentuna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 45 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Sollentuna climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Sollentuna?

What is the coldest month in Sollentuna?
February, with an average of -2.6 °C (27 °F), day highs around -0.2 °C (32 °F) and night lows around -5.3 °C (22 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Sollentuna?

What is the driest month in Sollentuna?

What is the sunniest month in Sollentuna?

Does it snow in Sollentuna?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 125 cm (49.1 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in December; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Sollentuna?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July and August.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 5.7 × 11 km, that contains Sollentuna’s GeoNames location (59.428°N, 17.951°E; ClimaClue area sq-245-1979), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 139,606 (GeoNames).
