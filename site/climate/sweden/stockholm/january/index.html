How January compares in Stockholm

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Stockholm. Compared with December: 1.0 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. February is 0.2 °C cooler than January on average.

Of the 11 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sweden, Stockholm ranks 6th warmest (average temperature), 9th sunniest and 7th wettest in January.

Stockholm in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Stockholm in January?
In January, Stockholm averages a day high of -0.2 °C (32 °F) and a night low of -4.2 °C (24 °F), with 42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 18 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Stockholm?
January is not one of Stockholm’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Stockholm in January?
42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (69 mm), the driest February (30 mm).
Does it snow in Stockholm in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 28 cm (10.9 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 8.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Stockholm get in January?
About 18 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Stockholm in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
