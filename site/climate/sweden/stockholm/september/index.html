How September compares in Stockholm

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Stockholm. Compared with August: 4.6 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 70 hours less sunshine. October is 4.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Stockholm in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Stockholm in September?
In September, Stockholm averages a day high of 14.9 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 9.6 °C (49 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 128 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Stockholm?
September is not one of Stockholm’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Stockholm in September?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (69 mm), the driest February (30 mm).
Does it snow in Stockholm in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Stockholm get in September?
About 128 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Stockholm in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
