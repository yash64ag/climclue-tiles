How February compares in Stockholm

February is the coolest month, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Stockholm. Compared with January: 0.2 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. March is 2.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Of the 11 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sweden, Stockholm ranks 6th warmest (average temperature), 10th sunniest and 10th wettest in February.

Stockholm in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Stockholm in February?
In February, Stockholm averages a day high of -0.1 °C (32 °F) and a night low of -4.8 °C (23 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 40 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Stockholm?
February is not one of Stockholm’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Stockholm in February?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (69 mm), the driest February (30 mm).
Does it snow in Stockholm in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 21 cm (8.1 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 6.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Stockholm get in February?
About 40 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Stockholm in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
