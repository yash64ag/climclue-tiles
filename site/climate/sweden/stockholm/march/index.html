How March compares in Stockholm

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Stockholm. Compared with February: 2.7 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 57 hours more sunshine. April is 4.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Of the 11 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sweden, Stockholm ranks 7th warmest (average temperature), 9th sunniest and 9th wettest in March.

Stockholm in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Stockholm in March?
In March, Stockholm averages a day high of 3.0 °C (37 °F) and a night low of -2.5 °C (28 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 97 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Stockholm?
March is not one of Stockholm’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Stockholm in March?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (69 mm), the driest February (30 mm).
Does it snow in Stockholm in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 21 cm (8.2 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 10 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Stockholm get in March?
About 97 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Stockholm in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
