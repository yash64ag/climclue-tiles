How October compares in Stockholm

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Stockholm. Compared with September: 5.2 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 49 hours less sunshine. November is 4.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Stockholm in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Stockholm in October?
In October, Stockholm averages a day high of 9.9 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 4.7 °C (40 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 79 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Stockholm?
October is not one of Stockholm’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Stockholm in October?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (69 mm), the driest February (30 mm).
Does it snow in Stockholm in October?
Yes, on average: about 0.9 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in October (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Stockholm get in October?
About 79 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Stockholm in October?
About 9.4 °C (49 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (16.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Stockholm in October?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
