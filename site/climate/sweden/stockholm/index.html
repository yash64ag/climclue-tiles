Stockholm at a glance

Among the 11 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sweden, Stockholm is the 2nd driest (586 mm a year). Compared with Sollentuna, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Stockholm is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C warmer in February, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.3 °C), averages 19.6 °C more than its coolest, February (-2.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Stockholm, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Stockholm

For mild weather, the best months to visit Stockholm are July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): July and August.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (17.3 °C), August (16.3 °C) and June (14.2 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): February (30 mm), April (32 mm) and March (34 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): June (256 h), May (235 h) and July (234 h).

Why Stockholm has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Stockholm climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Stockholm?
July, with an average of 17.3 °C (63 °F), day highs around 20.8 °C (69 °F) and night lows around 14.2 °C (58 °F).
What is the coldest month in Stockholm?
February, with an average of -2.4 °C (28 °F), day highs around -0.1 °C (32 °F) and night lows around -4.8 °C (23 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Stockholm?
July, with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 586 mm (23.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Stockholm?
February, with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Stockholm?
June, with about 256 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Stockholm?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 116 cm (45.6 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in December; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 4 months.
