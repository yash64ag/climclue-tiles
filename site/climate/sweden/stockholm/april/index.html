How April compares in Stockholm

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Stockholm. Compared with March: 4.5 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 54 hours more sunshine. May is 5.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Of the 11 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sweden, Stockholm ranks 9th warmest (average temperature), 10th sunniest and 11th wettest in April.

Stockholm in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Stockholm in April?
In April, Stockholm averages a day high of 8.3 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 1.5 °C (35 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Stockholm?
April is not one of Stockholm’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Stockholm in April?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (69 mm), the driest February (30 mm).
Does it snow in Stockholm in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.1 cm (2.8 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Stockholm get in April?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Stockholm in April?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
