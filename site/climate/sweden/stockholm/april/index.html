How April compares in Stockholm

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Stockholm. Compared with March: 4.9 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 54 hours more sunshine. May is 5.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Stockholm in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Stockholm in April?
In April, Stockholm averages a day high of 10.2 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 1.6 °C (35 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Stockholm?
April is not one of Stockholm’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Stockholm in April?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (69 mm), the driest February (30 mm).
Does it snow in Stockholm in April?
Yes, on average: about 7.1 cm (2.8 in) of fresh snow in April (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Stockholm get in April?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Stockholm in April?
About 2.6 °C (37 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (16.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Stockholm in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
