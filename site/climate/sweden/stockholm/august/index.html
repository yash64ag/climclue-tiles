How August compares in Stockholm

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Stockholm. Compared with July: 1.0 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 36 hours less sunshine. September is 4.6 °C cooler than August on average.

Stockholm in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Stockholm in August?
In August, Stockholm averages a day high of 19.7 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 13.7 °C (57 °F), with 67 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 198 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Stockholm?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Stockholm’s mild months (July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Stockholm in August?
67 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (69 mm), the driest February (30 mm).
Does it snow in Stockholm in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Stockholm get in August?
About 198 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Stockholm in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
