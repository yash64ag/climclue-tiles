Örebro at a glance

Among the 11 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sweden, Örebro is 7th warmest (6.5 °C year average), 5th sunniest (about 1,496 hours of sunshine a year) and 5th wettest (689 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Västerås, the nearest covered town with different numbers (85 km away), Örebro is about as warm in July and 0.3 °C warmer in February, with 40 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.3 °C), averages 20.0 °C more than its coolest, February (-2.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 129 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 101% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Örebro has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 129 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
