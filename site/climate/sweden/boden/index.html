Boden at a glance

Among the 99 towns ClimaClue covers in Sweden, Boden is the 3rd coolest (2.5 °C year average). Compared with Luleå, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Boden is 1.0 °C warmer in July and 1.3 °C cooler in January, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (16.5 °C), averages 26.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-9.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 43 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 23 km from Boden measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from these (temperature by up to 2.7 °C (4.8 °F) in one month). One station can differ from modelled values (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Boden has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 43 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Boden itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (65.825°N, 21.689°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 4.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-181-2016), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
