Visby sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Gotland · Sweden

The sea off Visby averages 17.7 °C (64 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 1.8 °C (35 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Visby sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Visby?
In August, at about 17.7 °C (64 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Visby?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 1.8 °C (35 °F).
How warm is the sea in Visby in March?
About 1.8 °C (35 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Visby?
About 8.4 °C (47 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
