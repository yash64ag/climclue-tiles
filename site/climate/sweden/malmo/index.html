Malmö at a glance

Among the 11 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Sweden, Malmö is the warmest (8.3 °C year average) and the 2nd sunniest (about 1,586 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Helsingborg, the nearest covered town with different numbers (53 km away), Malmö is 0.5 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 38 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.4 °C), averages 17.2 °C more than its coolest, January (0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 13 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.0 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 104% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Malmö has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
