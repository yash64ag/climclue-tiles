How March compares in Gothenburg

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Gothenburg. Compared with February: 2.3 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 54 hours more sunshine. April is 4.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Gothenburg in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gothenburg in March?
In March, Gothenburg averages a day high of 5.1 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -1.2 °C (30 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 100 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Gothenburg?
March is not one of Gothenburg’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gothenburg in March?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (105 mm), the driest April (51 mm).
Does it snow in Gothenburg in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 15 cm (5.9 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.8 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Gothenburg get in March?
About 100 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gothenburg in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
