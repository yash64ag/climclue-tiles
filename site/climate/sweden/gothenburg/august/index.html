Gothenburg weather in August

Average August weather in Gothenburg, Sweden: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Gothenburg averages 20.2 °C (68 °F) days and 12.4 °C (54 °F) nights, with 94 mm (3.7 in) of rain over the month and about 207 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Gothenburg

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Gothenburg. Compared with July: 0.6 °C cooler, 6 mm more precipitation and 33 hours less sunshine. September is 3.9 °C cooler than August on average.

Gothenburg in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gothenburg in August?
In August, Gothenburg averages a day high of 20.2 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 12.4 °C (54 °F), with 94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Gothenburg?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Gothenburg’s mild months (July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gothenburg in August?
94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (105 mm), the driest April (51 mm).
Does it snow in Gothenburg in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gothenburg get in August?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Gothenburg in August?
About 17.8 °C (64 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (17.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Gothenburg in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
