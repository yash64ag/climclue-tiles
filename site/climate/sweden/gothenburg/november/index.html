How November compares in Gothenburg

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Gothenburg. Compared with October: 4.4 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. December is 3.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Gothenburg in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gothenburg in November?
In November, Gothenburg averages a day high of 5.7 °C (42 °F) and a night low of 1.8 °C (35 °F), with 88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 44 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Gothenburg?
November is not one of Gothenburg’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gothenburg in November?
88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (105 mm), the driest April (51 mm).
Does it snow in Gothenburg in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.9 cm (2.7 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Gothenburg get in November?
About 44 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gothenburg in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
