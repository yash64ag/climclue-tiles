How April compares in Gothenburg

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Gothenburg. Compared with March: 4.4 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 59 hours more sunshine. May is 5.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Gothenburg in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gothenburg in April?
In April, Gothenburg averages a day high of 10.2 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 2.4 °C (36 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Gothenburg?
April is not one of Gothenburg’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gothenburg in April?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is October (105 mm), the driest April (51 mm).
Does it snow in Gothenburg in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.1 cm (1.6 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Gothenburg get in April?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gothenburg in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
