How February compares in Gothenburg

February is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Gothenburg. Compared with January: 0.2 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. March is 2.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Gothenburg in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gothenburg in February?
In February, Gothenburg averages a day high of 1.9 °C (35 °F) and a night low of -3.1 °C (26 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 47 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Gothenburg?
February is not one of Gothenburg’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gothenburg in February?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (105 mm), the driest April (51 mm).
Does it snow in Gothenburg in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 20 cm (7.8 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Gothenburg get in February?
About 47 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Gothenburg in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
