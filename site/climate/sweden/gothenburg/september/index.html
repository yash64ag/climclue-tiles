How September compares in Gothenburg

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Gothenburg. Compared with August: 4.0 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 73 hours less sunshine. October is 4.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Gothenburg in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Gothenburg in September?
In September, Gothenburg averages a day high of 16.0 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 9.3 °C (49 °F), with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 134 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Gothenburg?
September is not one of Gothenburg’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Gothenburg in September?
83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (105 mm), the driest April (51 mm).
Does it snow in Gothenburg in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Gothenburg get in September?
About 134 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Gothenburg in September?
About 15.1 °C (59 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (17.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Gothenburg in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
