Kungsbacka sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Halland · Sweden

The sea off Kungsbacka averages 17.7 °C (64 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 2.1 °C (36 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Kungsbacka sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kungsbacka?
In August, at about 17.7 °C (64 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kungsbacka?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 2.1 °C (36 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kungsbacka in February?
About 2.1 °C (36 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Kungsbacka?
About 9.4 °C (49 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Kungsbacka nearby?
In August: Ängelholm (18.3 °C, 146 km).
