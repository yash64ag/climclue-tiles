Why Asmara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 72 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Asmara climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Asmara?
June, with an average of 19.3 °C (67 °F), day highs around 25.4 °C (78 °F) and night lows around 13.3 °C (56 °F).
What is the coldest month in Asmara?
January, with an average of 13.2 °C (56 °F), day highs around 18.3 °C (65 °F) and night lows around 8.5 °C (47 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Asmara?
August, with 164 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 693 mm (27.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Asmara?
November, with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Asmara?
January, with about 281 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Asmara?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Asmara?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: March, April, May, June, September and October.
What type of climate does Asmara have?
Oceanic (Köppen Cfb). Mild all year, with rain in every season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Asmara’s GeoNames location (15.338°N, 38.932°E; ClimaClue area sq-686-2189), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 563,930 (GeoNames).
