How March compares in Asmara

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Asmara. Compared with February: 1.4 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. April is 1.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Asmara in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Asmara in March?
In March, Asmara averages a day high of 21.1 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 10.2 °C (50 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 271 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Asmara?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Asmara’s mild months (March, April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Asmara in March?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (164 mm), the driest November (23 mm).
Does it snow in Asmara in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Asmara get in March?
About 271 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Asmara in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
