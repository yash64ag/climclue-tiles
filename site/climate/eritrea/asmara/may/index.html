How May compares in Asmara

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Asmara. Compared with April: 1.3 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. June is 0.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Asmara in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Asmara in May?
In May, Asmara averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 10.7 °C (51 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 258 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Asmara?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Asmara’s mild months (March, April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Asmara in May?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (172 mm), the driest November (22 mm).
Does it snow in Asmara in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Asmara get in May?
About 258 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Asmara in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
