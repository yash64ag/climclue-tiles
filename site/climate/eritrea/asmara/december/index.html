How December compares in Asmara

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Asmara. Compared with November: 1.0 °C cooler, 16 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. January is 0.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Asmara in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Asmara in December?
In December, Asmara averages a day high of 18.4 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 8.4 °C (47 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 275 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Asmara?
December is not one of Asmara’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Asmara in December?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (172 mm), the driest November (22 mm).
Does it snow in Asmara in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Asmara get in December?
About 275 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Asmara in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
