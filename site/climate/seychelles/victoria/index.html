Victoria at a glance

Its warmest month, April (28.0 °C), averages 2.2 °C more than its coolest, July (25.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.6 °C (74 °F), in August. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: before adjustment, the modelled temperatures differed from a NOAA weather station 9 km from Victoria by up to 1.5 °C (2.7 °F) in one month. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Best time to visit Victoria

No month in Victoria is mild by the rule below; the closest in temperature are July (25.8 °C, 113 mm), August (25.8 °C, 150 mm) and September (26.3 °C, 208 mm).

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): no month.
  • Warmest (average temperature): April (28.0 °C), March (27.7 °C) and May (27.6 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): July (113 mm), June (148 mm) and August (150 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): May (247 h), August (230 h) and September (229 h).

Climate only: these rules use 1981–2010 monthly averages and do not consider crowds, prices, events or holidays; any one year can differ.

Why Victoria has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.2 °C. The 10 km area around Victoria, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 25.7 °C a year, 1.2 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Victoria climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Victoria?
April, with an average of 28.0 °C (82 °F), day highs around 31.1 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 24.9 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Victoria?
July, with an average of 25.8 °C (78 °F), day highs around 27.9 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 23.7 °C (75 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Victoria?
January, with 468 mm (18.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,824 mm (111.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Victoria?
July, with 113 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Victoria?
May, with about 247 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Victoria itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (4.620°S, 55.455°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-886-2354), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
