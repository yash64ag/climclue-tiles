Machala at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ecuador, Machala is 8th warmest (23.6 °C year average), 8th sunniest (about 1,550 hours of sunshine a year) and 13th wettest (881 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cuenca, the nearest covered town with different numbers (114 km away), Machala is 12.1 °C warmer in March and 9.7 °C warmer in August, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (25.8 °C), averages 4.0 °C more than its coolest, August (21.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.7 °C (67 °F), in August. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Machala, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Machala has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
