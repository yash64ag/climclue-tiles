Why Quevedo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 130 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Quevedo climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Quevedo?
April, with an average of 24.8 °C (77 °F), day highs around 26.9 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 22.4 °C (72 °F).
What is the coldest month in Quevedo?
August, with an average of 22.3 °C (72 °F), day highs around 25.0 °C (77 °F) and night lows around 20.0 °C (68 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Quevedo?
February, with 503 mm (19.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,608 mm (102.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Quevedo?
August, with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Quevedo?
March, with about 99 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Quevedo?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Quevedo?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July, August, September, October and November.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Quevedo’s GeoNames location (1.029°S, 79.463°W; ClimaClue area sq-850-1005), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 213,842 (GeoNames).
