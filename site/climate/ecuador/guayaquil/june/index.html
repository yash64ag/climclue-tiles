How June compares in Guayaquil

June is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Guayaquil. Compared with May: 1.2 °C cooler, 43 mm less precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. July is 0.7 °C cooler than June on average.

Guayaquil in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guayaquil in June?
In June, Guayaquil averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 72 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Guayaquil?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Guayaquil’s mild months (June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guayaquil in June?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (353 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Guayaquil in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guayaquil get in June?
About 72 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guayaquil in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
