How November compares in Guayaquil

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Guayaquil. Compared with October: 0.5 °C warmer, 23 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. December is 0.7 °C warmer than November on average.

Guayaquil in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guayaquil in November?
In November, Guayaquil averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 21.2 °C (70 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 78 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Guayaquil?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Guayaquil’s mild months (June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guayaquil in November?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (355 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Guayaquil in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guayaquil get in November?
About 78 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guayaquil in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
