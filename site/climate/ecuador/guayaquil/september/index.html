How September compares in Guayaquil

September is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Guayaquil. Compared with August: 0.1 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. October is 0.4 °C warmer than September on average.

Guayaquil in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guayaquil in September?
In September, Guayaquil averages a day high of 27.3 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 20.5 °C (69 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 78 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Guayaquil?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Guayaquil’s mild months (June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guayaquil in September?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (355 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Guayaquil in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guayaquil get in September?
About 78 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guayaquil in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
