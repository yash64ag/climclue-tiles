How July compares in Guayaquil

July is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Guayaquil. Compared with June: 0.7 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. August is 0.2 °C cooler than July on average.

Guayaquil in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guayaquil in July?
In July, Guayaquil averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 70 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Guayaquil?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Guayaquil’s mild months (June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guayaquil in July?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is February (355 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Guayaquil in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guayaquil get in July?
About 70 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guayaquil in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
