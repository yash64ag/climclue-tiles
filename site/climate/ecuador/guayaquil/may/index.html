How May compares in Guayaquil

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Guayaquil. Compared with April: 0.7 °C cooler, 137 mm less precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. June is 1.1 °C cooler than May on average.

Guayaquil in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guayaquil in May?
In May, Guayaquil averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 106 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Guayaquil?
May is not one of Guayaquil’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guayaquil in May?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (355 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Guayaquil in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guayaquil get in May?
About 106 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guayaquil in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
