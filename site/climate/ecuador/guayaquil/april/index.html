How April compares in Guayaquil

April is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Guayaquil. Compared with March: about as warm, 122 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. May is 0.7 °C cooler than April on average.

Guayaquil in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guayaquil in April?
In April, Guayaquil averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 209 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Guayaquil?
April is not one of Guayaquil’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guayaquil in April?
209 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (355 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Guayaquil in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guayaquil get in April?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guayaquil in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
