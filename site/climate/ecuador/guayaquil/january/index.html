How January compares in Guayaquil

January is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Guayaquil. Compared with December: 0.5 °C warmer, 115 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. February is 0.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Guayaquil in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guayaquil in January?
In January, Guayaquil averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 190 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation and about 91 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Guayaquil?
January is not one of Guayaquil’s mild months; those are June, July, August, September, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guayaquil in January?
190 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is February (355 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Guayaquil in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guayaquil get in January?
About 91 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guayaquil in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
