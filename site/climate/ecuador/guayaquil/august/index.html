How August compares in Guayaquil

August is the coolest month, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Guayaquil. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. September is 0.1 °C warmer than August on average.

Guayaquil in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guayaquil in August?
In August, Guayaquil averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 78 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Guayaquil?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Guayaquil’s mild months (June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guayaquil in August?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is February (353 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Guayaquil in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guayaquil get in August?
About 78 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guayaquil in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
