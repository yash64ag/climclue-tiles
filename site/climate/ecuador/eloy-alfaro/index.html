Eloy Alfaro at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ecuador, Eloy Alfaro is 4th warmest (24.6 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 1,084 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (1,319 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Milagro, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Eloy Alfaro is 0.5 °C warmer in March and 0.3 °C warmer in August, with 165 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (25.9 °C), averages 2.7 °C more than its coolest, August (23.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.5 °C (69 °F), in August. The area lies about 79 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Eloy Alfaro, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Eloy Alfaro has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 79 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
