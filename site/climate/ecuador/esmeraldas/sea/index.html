Esmeraldas sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Ecuador

The sea off Esmeraldas averages 27.0 °C (81 °F) in May, its warmest month, and 26.0 °C (79 °F) in September. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Esmeraldas sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Esmeraldas?
In May, at about 27.0 °C (81 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Esmeraldas?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is September, at 26.0 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in Esmeraldas in September?
About 26.0 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Esmeraldas?
About 26.4 °C (79 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Esmeraldas nearby?
In May: Timbiquí (27.9 °C, 299 km).
