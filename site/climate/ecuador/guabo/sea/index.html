Guabo sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · El Oro · Ecuador

The sea off Guabo averages 26.8 °C (80 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 22.3 °C (72 °F) in September. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to June.

Guabo sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Guabo?
In February, at about 26.8 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Guabo?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to June. The coolest month is September, at 22.3 °C (72 °F).
How warm is the sea in Guabo in September?
About 22.3 °C (72 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Guabo?
About 24.7 °C (76 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
