La Libertad sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Guayas · Ecuador

The sea off La Libertad averages 26.0 °C (79 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 22.3 °C (72 °F) in September. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to May.

La Libertad sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in La Libertad?
In February, at about 26.0 °C (79 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in La Libertad?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from December to May. The coolest month is September, at 22.3 °C (72 °F).
How warm is the sea in La Libertad in September?
About 22.3 °C (72 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in La Libertad?
About 24.0 °C (75 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in La Libertad nearby?
In February: Machala (26.8 °C, 155 km), Huaquillas (26.6 °C, 157 km) and Guabo (26.8 °C, 164 km).
