Loja at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ecuador, Loja is 11th warmest (15.8 °C year average), 5th sunniest (about 1,712 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (1,076 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Machala, the nearest covered town with different numbers (117 km away), Loja is 5.5 °C cooler in September and 7.2 °C cooler in July, with 194 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (16.4 °C), averages 1.4 °C more than its coolest, July (14.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.1 °C (52 °F), in July. The area lies about 126 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Loja, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Loja has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 126 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
