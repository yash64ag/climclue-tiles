How November compares in Quito

November is the coolest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Quito. Compared with October: 0.3 °C cooler, 6 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. December is about as warm as November on average.

Quito in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Quito in November?
In November, Quito averages a day high of 14.4 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 7.8 °C (46 °F), with 196 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Quito?
No month in Quito is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Quito in November?
196 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (322 mm), the driest July (48 mm).
Does it snow in Quito in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Quito get in November?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Quito in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
