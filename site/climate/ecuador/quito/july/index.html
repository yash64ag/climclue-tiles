How July compares in Quito

July is the 4th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Quito. Compared with June: 0.1 °C cooler, 56 mm less precipitation and 35 hours more sunshine. August is 0.2 °C warmer than July on average.

Quito in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Quito in July?
In July, Quito averages a day high of 15.6 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 7.8 °C (46 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Quito?
No month in Quito is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Quito in July?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is March (322 mm), the driest July (48 mm).
Does it snow in Quito in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Quito get in July?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Quito in July?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
