How April compares in Quito

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Quito. Compared with March: 0.2 °C warmer, 11 mm less precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. May is 0.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Quito in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Quito in April?
In April, Quito averages a day high of 14.6 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 8.3 °C (47 °F), with 312 mm (12.3 in) of precipitation and about 123 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Quito?
No month in Quito is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Quito in April?
312 mm (12.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (322 mm), the driest July (48 mm).
Does it snow in Quito in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Quito get in April?
About 123 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Quito in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
