How January compares in Quito

January is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Quito. Compared with December: about as warm, 5 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. February is 0.1 °C cooler than January on average.

What to pack for Quito in January

Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella.

Quito in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Quito in January?
In January, Quito averages a day high of 21.0 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 3.2 °C (38 °F), with 192 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Quito?
No month in Quito is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Quito in January?
192 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (290 mm), the driest July (41 mm).
Does it snow in Quito in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Quito get in January?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Quito in January?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
