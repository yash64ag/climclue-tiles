How June compares in Quito

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Quito. Compared with May: about as warm, 106 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. July is 0.1 °C cooler than June on average.

Quito in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Quito in June?
In June, Quito averages a day high of 15.4 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 8.1 °C (47 °F), with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Quito?
No month in Quito is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Quito in June?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (322 mm), the driest July (48 mm).
Does it snow in Quito in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Quito get in June?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Quito in June?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
