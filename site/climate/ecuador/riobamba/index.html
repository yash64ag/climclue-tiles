Riobamba at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ecuador, Riobamba is the 3rd coolest (12.0 °C year average) and the 3rd sunniest (about 1,761 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Ambato, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Riobamba is 0.4 °C warmer in January and 0.6 °C warmer in July, with 149 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (12.4 °C), averages 1.5 °C more than its coolest, July (10.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.3 °C (43 °F), in August. The area lies about 197 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Riobamba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Riobamba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 197 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
