Why Riobamba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 197 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Riobamba climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Riobamba?
January, with an average of 12.4 °C (54 °F), day highs around 16.9 °C (62 °F) and night lows around 8.1 °C (47 °F).
What is the coldest month in Riobamba?
July, with an average of 10.9 °C (52 °F), day highs around 15.8 °C (60 °F) and night lows around 6.4 °C (44 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Riobamba?
April, with 131 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,014 mm (39.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Riobamba?
August, with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Riobamba?
December, with about 164 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Riobamba?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Riobamba?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Riobamba have?
Oceanic (Köppen Cfb). Mild all year, with rain in every season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Riobamba’s GeoNames location (1.665°S, 78.659°W; ClimaClue area sq-856-1013), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 264,048 (GeoNames).
