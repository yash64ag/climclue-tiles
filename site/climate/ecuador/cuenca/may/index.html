How May compares in Cuenca

May is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Cuenca. Compared with April: 0.4 °C cooler, 43 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. June is 0.8 °C cooler than May on average.

Cuenca in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuenca in May?
In May, Cuenca averages a day high of 16.3 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 9.8 °C (50 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Cuenca?
No month in Cuenca is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuenca in May?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (127 mm), the driest August (28 mm).
Does it snow in Cuenca in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuenca get in May?
About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cuenca in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
