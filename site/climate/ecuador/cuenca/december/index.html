How December compares in Cuenca

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Cuenca. Compared with November: 0.1 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. January is 0.1 °C warmer than December on average.

Cuenca in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuenca in December?
In December, Cuenca averages a day high of 17.1 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 10.0 °C (50 °F), with 88 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Cuenca?
No month in Cuenca is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuenca in December?
88 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (127 mm), the driest August (28 mm).
Does it snow in Cuenca in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuenca get in December?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cuenca in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
