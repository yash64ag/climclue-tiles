Cuenca weather in June

Average June weather in Cuenca, Ecuador: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Cuenca averages 19.0 °C (66 °F) days and 6.6 °C (44 °F) nights, with 42 mm (1.6 in) of rain over the month and about 149 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Cuenca

June is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Cuenca. Compared with May: 0.8 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. July is 0.6 °C cooler than June on average.

Cuenca in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuenca in June?
In June, Cuenca averages a day high of 19.0 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 6.6 °C (44 °F), with 42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Cuenca?
No month in Cuenca is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuenca in June?
42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (121 mm), the driest August (25 mm).
Does it snow in Cuenca in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuenca get in June?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
