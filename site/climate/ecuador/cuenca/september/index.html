How September compares in Cuenca

September is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Cuenca. Compared with August: 0.8 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. October is 0.5 °C warmer than September on average.

Cuenca in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuenca in September?
In September, Cuenca averages a day high of 16.8 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Cuenca?
No month in Cuenca is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuenca in September?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (127 mm), the driest August (28 mm).
Does it snow in Cuenca in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuenca get in September?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cuenca in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
