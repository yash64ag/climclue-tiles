How July compares in Cuenca

July is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Cuenca. Compared with June: 0.6 °C cooler, 15 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. August is 0.1 °C warmer than July on average.

Cuenca in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuenca in July?
In July, Cuenca averages a day high of 15.3 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 8.5 °C (47 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Cuenca?
No month in Cuenca is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuenca in July?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (127 mm), the driest August (28 mm).
Does it snow in Cuenca in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuenca get in July?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cuenca in July?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
