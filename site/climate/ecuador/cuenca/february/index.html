How February compares in Cuenca

February is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Cuenca. Compared with January: about as warm, 29 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. March is 0.1 °C cooler than February on average.

Cuenca in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuenca in February?
In February, Cuenca averages a day high of 16.9 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 10.2 °C (50 °F), with 94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 108 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Cuenca?
No month in Cuenca is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuenca in February?
94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (127 mm), the driest August (28 mm).
Does it snow in Cuenca in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuenca get in February?
About 108 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cuenca in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
