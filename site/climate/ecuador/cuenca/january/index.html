How January compares in Cuenca

January is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Cuenca. Compared with December: 0.1 °C warmer, 19 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. February is about as warm as January on average.

Cuenca in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cuenca in January?
In January, Cuenca averages a day high of 20.4 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 7.5 °C (46 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Cuenca?
No month in Cuenca is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cuenca in January?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (121 mm), the driest August (25 mm).
Does it snow in Cuenca in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cuenca get in January?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cuenca in January?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
