Ambato at a glance

Among the 17 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Ecuador, Ambato is the 2nd coolest (11.6 °C year average) and the 3rd driest (865 mm a year). Compared with Latacunga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Ambato is 0.1 °C cooler in April and 1.0 °C cooler in July, with 35 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (12.2 °C), averages 1.8 °C more than its coolest, July (10.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.1 °C (43 °F), in July. The area lies about 221 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ambato, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ambato has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 221 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
