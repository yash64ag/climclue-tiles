Saint Peter Port at a glance

Its warmest month, August (16.5 °C), averages 8.7 °C more than its coolest, February (7.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.7 °C (42 °F), in February. The area lies about 7 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 106% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Best time to visit Saint Peter Port

For mild weather, the best months to visit Saint Peter Port are July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): July, August and September.
  • Warmest (average temperature): August (16.5 °C), September (15.9 °C) and July (15.6 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): July (41 mm), June (46 mm) and August (48 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (252 h), June (239 h) and May (235 h).

Why Saint Peter Port has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Saint Peter Port itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (49.460°N, 2.535°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-345-1774), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
