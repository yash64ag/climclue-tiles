Badr Ḩunayn at a glance

Among the 96 towns ClimaClue covers in Saudi Arabia, Badr Ḩunayn is 12th warmest (28.6 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 3,654 hours of sunshine a year) and 47th wettest (101 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yanbu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (81 km away), Badr Ḩunayn is 1.8 °C warmer in August and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 69 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (33.8 °C), averages 12.6 °C more than its coolest, January (21.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.3 °C (60 °F), in January. The area lies about 38 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Badr Ḩunayn, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Badr Ḩunayn has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
