Jizan at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Saudi Arabia, Jizan is the 2nd warmest (29.5 °C year average). Compared with Şabyā, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Jizan is 1.9 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 34 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (32.5 °C), averages 6.9 °C more than its coolest, January (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.8 °C (75 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jizan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jizan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
