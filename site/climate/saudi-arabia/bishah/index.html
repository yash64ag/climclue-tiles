Bīshah at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Saudi Arabia, Bīshah is 15th warmest (26.0 °C year average), 23rd sunniest (about 3,079 hours of sunshine a year) and 21st wettest (69 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sabt Alalayah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (82 km away), Bīshah is 9.7 °C warmer in July and 5.0 °C warmer in January, with 485 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (32.9 °C), averages 15.1 °C more than its coolest, January (17.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.4 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 199 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bīshah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bīshah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 199 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
