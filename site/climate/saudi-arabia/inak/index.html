‘Inak at a glance

Among the 96 towns ClimaClue covers in Saudi Arabia, ‘Inak is 42nd warmest (26.1 °C year average), 60th sunniest (about 3,288 hours of sunshine a year) and 57th wettest (91 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sayhāt, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), ‘Inak is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Why ‘Inak has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
