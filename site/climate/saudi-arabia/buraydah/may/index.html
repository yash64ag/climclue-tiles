How May compares in Buraydah

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Buraydah. Compared with April: 5.8 °C warmer, 18 mm less precipitation and 50 hours more sunshine. June is 3.0 °C warmer than May on average.

Buraydah in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Buraydah in May?
In May, Buraydah averages a day high of 37.7 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 332 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Buraydah?
May is not one of Buraydah’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Buraydah in May?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (31 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Buraydah in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Buraydah get in May?
About 332 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Buraydah in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
