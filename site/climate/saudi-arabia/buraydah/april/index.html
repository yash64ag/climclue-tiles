How April compares in Buraydah

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Buraydah. Compared with March: 5.8 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. May is 5.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Buraydah in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Buraydah in April?
In April, Buraydah averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 17.1 °C (63 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 283 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Buraydah?
April is not one of Buraydah’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Buraydah in April?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (31 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Buraydah in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Buraydah get in April?
About 283 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Buraydah in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
