How November compares in Buraydah

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Buraydah. Compared with October: 7.3 °C cooler, 19 mm more precipitation and 56 hours less sunshine. December is 5.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Buraydah in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Buraydah in November?
In November, Buraydah averages a day high of 25.4 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 12.4 °C (54 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Buraydah?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Buraydah’s mild months (February, March and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Buraydah in November?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (31 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Buraydah in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Buraydah get in November?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Buraydah in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
