How February compares in Buraydah

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Buraydah. Compared with January: 2.6 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. March is 4.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Buraydah in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Buraydah in February?
In February, Buraydah averages a day high of 21.4 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 7.5 °C (46 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Buraydah?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Buraydah’s mild months (February, March and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Buraydah in February?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (31 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Buraydah in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Buraydah get in February?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Buraydah in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
