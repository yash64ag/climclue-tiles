How October compares in Buraydah

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Buraydah. Compared with September: 5.6 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. November is 7.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Buraydah in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Buraydah in October?
In October, Buraydah averages a day high of 33.7 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 18.1 °C (65 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 306 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Buraydah?
October is not one of Buraydah’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Buraydah in October?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (31 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Buraydah in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Buraydah get in October?
About 306 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Buraydah in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
