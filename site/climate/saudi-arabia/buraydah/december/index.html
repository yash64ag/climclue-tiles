How December compares in Buraydah

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Buraydah. Compared with November: 5.2 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Buraydah in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Buraydah in December?
In December, Buraydah averages a day high of 19.9 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 7.5 °C (46 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Buraydah?
December is not one of Buraydah’s mild months; those are February, March and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Buraydah in December?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (31 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Buraydah in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Buraydah get in December?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Buraydah in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
