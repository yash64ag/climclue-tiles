Thuwal sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Mecca Region · Saudi Arabia

The sea off Thuwal averages 31.2 °C (88 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 25.0 °C (77 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Thuwal sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Thuwal?
In August, at about 31.2 °C (88 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Thuwal?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 25.0 °C (77 °F).
How warm is the sea in Thuwal in February?
About 25.0 °C (77 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Thuwal?
About 28.2 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
