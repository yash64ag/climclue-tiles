Sayhāt sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Eastern Province · Saudi Arabia

The sea off Sayhāt averages 32.8 °C (91 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 18.3 °C (65 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November.

Sayhāt sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Sayhāt?
In August, at about 32.8 °C (91 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Sayhāt?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November. The coolest month is February, at 18.3 °C (65 °F).
How warm is the sea in Sayhāt in February?
About 18.3 °C (65 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Sayhāt?
About 25.9 °C (79 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Sayhāt nearby?
In August: Khobar (33.4 °C, 28 km), Manama (33.4 °C, 61 km) and Madīnat Ḩamad (33.4 °C, 61 km).
