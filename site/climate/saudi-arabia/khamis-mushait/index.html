Khamis Mushait at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Saudi Arabia, Khamis Mushait is the 3rd coolest (20.0 °C year average), the 2nd least sunny (about 2,941 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (243 mm a year). Compared with Abha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Khamis Mushait is 2.0 °C warmer in June and 1.2 °C warmer in January, with 66 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (24.6 °C), averages 9.9 °C more than its coolest, January (14.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.1 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 115 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Khamis Mushait, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Khamis Mushait has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 115 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
