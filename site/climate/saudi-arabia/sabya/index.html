Şabyā at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Saudi Arabia, Şabyā is the warmest (30.4 °C year average) and the 5th least sunny (about 3,021 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Jizan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Şabyā is 1.9 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 34 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (34.4 °C), averages 8.8 °C more than its coolest, January (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.7 °C (71 °F), in January. The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Şabyā, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Şabyā has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
