Dammam sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Eastern Province · Saudi Arabia

The sea off Dammam averages 33.1 °C (92 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 18.3 °C (65 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November.

Dammam sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Dammam?
In August, at about 33.1 °C (92 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Dammam?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November. The coolest month is February, at 18.3 °C (65 °F).
How warm is the sea in Dammam in February?
About 18.3 °C (65 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Dammam?
About 26.0 °C (79 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
