Dammam weather in February

Average February weather in Dammam, Saudi Arabia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Dammam averages 22.2 °C (72 °F) days and 12.2 °C (54 °F) nights, with 12 mm (0.5 in) of rain over the month and about 217 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Dammam

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Dammam. Compared with January: 1.1 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. March is 3.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Dammam in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dammam in February?
In February, Dammam averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 12.2 °C (54 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Dammam?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Dammam’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dammam in February?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (23 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Dammam in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dammam get in February?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Dammam in February?
About 18.3 °C (65 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (33.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Dammam in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
