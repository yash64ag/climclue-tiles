How December compares in Dammam

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Dammam. Compared with November: 5.1 °C cooler, 6 mm more precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. January is 2.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Dammam in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dammam in December?
In December, Dammam averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Dammam?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Dammam’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dammam in December?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (23 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Dammam in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dammam get in December?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Dammam in December?
About 22.1 °C (72 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (33.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Dammam in December?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
