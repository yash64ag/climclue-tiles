How April compares in Dammam

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Dammam. Compared with March: 4.4 °C warmer, 17 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. May is 5.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Dammam in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dammam in April?
In April, Dammam averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 252 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Dammam?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Dammam’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dammam in April?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (23 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Dammam in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dammam get in April?
About 252 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Dammam in April?
About 22.7 °C (73 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (33.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Dammam in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
