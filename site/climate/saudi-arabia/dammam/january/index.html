How January compares in Dammam

January is the coolest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Dammam. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. February is 1.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Dammam in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dammam in January?
In January, Dammam averages a day high of 20.7 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 11.5 °C (53 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Dammam?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Dammam’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dammam in January?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (23 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Dammam in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dammam get in January?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Dammam in January?
About 19.3 °C (67 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (33.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Dammam in January?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
