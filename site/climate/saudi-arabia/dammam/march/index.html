How March compares in Dammam

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Dammam. Compared with February: 2.9 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. April is 4.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Dammam in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dammam in March?
In March, Dammam averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 16.7 °C (62 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Dammam?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Dammam’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dammam in March?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (23 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Dammam in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dammam get in March?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dammam in March?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
