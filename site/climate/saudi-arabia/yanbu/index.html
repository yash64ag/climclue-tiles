Yanbu at a glance

Among the 96 towns ClimaClue covers in Saudi Arabia, Yanbu is the 4th sunniest (about 3,735 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (32 mm a year). Compared with Badr Ḩunayn, the nearest covered town with different numbers (81 km away), Yanbu is 1.8 °C cooler in August and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 69 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (31.9 °C), averages 10.1 °C more than its coolest, January (21.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.6 °C (60 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yanbu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yanbu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Yanbu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (24.090°N, 38.062°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-599-2180), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
