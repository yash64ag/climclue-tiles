Yanbu at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Saudi Arabia, Yanbu is the sunniest (about 3,735 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (32 mm a year). Compared with Sulţānah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (161 km away), Yanbu is 3.0 °C cooler in August and 4.4 °C warmer in January, with 36 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (31.9 °C), averages 10.1 °C more than its coolest, January (21.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.3 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yanbu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yanbu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
