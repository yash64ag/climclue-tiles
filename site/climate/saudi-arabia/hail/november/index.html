How November compares in Ha'il

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Ha'il. Compared with October: 7.8 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 60 hours less sunshine. December is 4.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Ha'il in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ha'il in November?
In November, Ha'il averages a day high of 22.1 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 9.8 °C (50 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Ha'il?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Ha'il’s mild months (March, April, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ha'il in November?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (30 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Ha'il in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ha'il get in November?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ha'il in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
