Ha'il in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ha'il in February?
In February, Ha'il averages a day high of 17.8 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 4.8 °C (41 °F), with 14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Ha'il?
February is not one of Ha'il’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ha'il in February?
14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (30 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Ha'il in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ha'il get in February?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ha'il in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
