Ha'il weather in May

Average May weather in Ha'il, Saudi Arabia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Ha'il averages 34.7 °C (94 °F) days and 20.5 °C (69 °F) nights, with 8 mm (0.3 in) of rain over the month and about 340 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Ha'il

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ha'il. Compared with April: 5.2 °C warmer, 11 mm less precipitation and 53 hours more sunshine. June is 3.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Ha'il in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ha'il in May?
In May, Ha'il averages a day high of 34.7 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 20.5 °C (69 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 340 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Ha'il?
May is not one of Ha'il’s mild months; those are March, April and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
