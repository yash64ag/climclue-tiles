Ha'il at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Saudi Arabia, Ha'il is the 5th sunniest (about 3,638 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Buraydah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (263 km away), Ha'il is 3.1 °C cooler in August and 2.6 °C cooler in January, with 36 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (32.5 °C), averages 22.4 °C more than its coolest, January (10.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 522 km from the sea.

Why Ha'il has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 522 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
