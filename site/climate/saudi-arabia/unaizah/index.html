Unaizah at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Saudi Arabia, Unaizah is the 5th wettest (159 mm a year). Compared with Buraydah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Unaizah is 0.4 °C cooler in August and about as warm in January, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (35.2 °C), averages 22.5 °C more than its coolest, January (12.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.7 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 515 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Unaizah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Unaizah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 515 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
