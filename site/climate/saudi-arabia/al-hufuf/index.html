Al Hufūf at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Saudi Arabia, Al Hufūf is 10th warmest (26.5 °C year average), 15th sunniest (about 3,340 hours of sunshine a year) and 17th wettest (89 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Al Mubarraz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Al Hufūf is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (37.1 °C), averages 23.1 °C more than its coolest, January (13.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.9 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 87 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Al Hufūf, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Al Hufūf has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 87 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
