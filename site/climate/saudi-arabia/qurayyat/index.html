Qurayyat at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Saudi Arabia, Qurayyat is the 4th coolest (20.4 °C year average) and the 3rd driest (47 mm a year). Compared with Sakakah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (313 km away), Qurayyat is 3.5 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.9 °C), averages 20.8 °C more than its coolest, January (9.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 265 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Qurayyat, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Qurayyat has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 265 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
