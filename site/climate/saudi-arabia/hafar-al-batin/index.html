Hafar Al-Batin at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Saudi Arabia, Hafar Al-Batin is 19th warmest (24.8 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 3,469 hours of sunshine a year) and 11th wettest (117 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Az Zulfī, the nearest covered town with different numbers (263 km away), Hafar Al-Batin is 1.0 °C warmer in July and 1.4 °C cooler in January, with 44 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (36.0 °C), averages 24.9 °C more than its coolest, January (11.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 224 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hafar Al-Batin, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hafar Al-Batin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 224 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
