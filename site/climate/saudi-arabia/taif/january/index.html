How January compares in Ta’if

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Ta’if. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. February is 1.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Ta’if in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ta’if in January?
In January, Ta’if averages a day high of 19.0 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 8.9 °C (48 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Ta’if?
January is not one of Ta’if’s mild months; those are February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ta’if in January?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (36 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Ta’if in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ta’if get in January?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ta’if in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
