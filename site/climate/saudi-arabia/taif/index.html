Ta’if at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Saudi Arabia, Ta’if is the 4th wettest (228 mm a year). Compared with Makkah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (64 km away), Ta’if is 6.0 °C cooler in June and 7.3 °C cooler in January, with 97 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.1 °C), averages 13.4 °C more than its coolest, January (14.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.9 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 123 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ta’if, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ta’if has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 123 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
