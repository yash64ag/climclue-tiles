How April compares in Ta’if

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ta’if. Compared with March: 2.9 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. May is 3.7 °C warmer than April on average.

Ta’if in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ta’if in April?
In April, Ta’if averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 15.1 °C (59 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ta’if?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Ta’if’s mild months (February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ta’if in April?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (36 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Ta’if in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ta’if get in April?
About 278 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ta’if in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
