Ta’if weather in September

Average September weather in Ta’if, Saudi Arabia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Ta’if averages 33.1 °C (92 °F) days and 19.7 °C (67 °F) nights, with 22 mm (0.9 in) of rain over the month and about 288 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Ta’if

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Ta’if. Compared with August: 1.1 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. October is 3.8 °C cooler than September on average.

Ta’if in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ta’if in September?
In September, Ta’if averages a day high of 33.1 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 19.7 °C (67 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 288 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Ta’if?
September is not one of Ta’if’s mild months; those are February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ta’if in September?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (37 mm), the driest June (4 mm).
Does it snow in Ta’if in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ta’if get in September?
About 288 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ta’if in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
