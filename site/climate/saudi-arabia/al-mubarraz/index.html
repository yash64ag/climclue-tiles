Al Mubarraz at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Saudi Arabia, Al Mubarraz is 9th warmest (26.7 °C year average), 16th sunniest (about 3,339 hours of sunshine a year) and 16th wettest (90 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Al Hufūf, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Al Mubarraz is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (37.3 °C), averages 23.2 °C more than its coolest, January (14.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.1 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 83 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Al Mubarraz, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Al Mubarraz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 83 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
