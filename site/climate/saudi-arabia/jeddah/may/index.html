How May compares in Jeddah

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Jeddah. Compared with April: 2.0 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 35 hours more sunshine. June is 0.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Jeddah in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jeddah in May?
In May, Jeddah averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 26.6 °C (80 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 340 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Jeddah?
May is not one of Jeddah’s mild months; those are January, February and March, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jeddah in May?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (27 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Jeddah in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jeddah get in May?
About 340 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jeddah in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
