Jeddah at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Saudi Arabia, Jeddah is the 4th warmest (28.0 °C year average). Compared with Makkah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Jeddah is 2.2 °C cooler in August and 1.7 °C warmer in January, with 62 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (31.9 °C), averages 8.2 °C more than its coolest, January (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.9 °C (70 °F), in February. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 23 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 99% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Jeddah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
