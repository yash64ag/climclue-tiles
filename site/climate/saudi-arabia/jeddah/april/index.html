How April compares in Jeddah

April is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Jeddah. Compared with March: 2.4 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. May is 2.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Jeddah in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jeddah in April?
In April, Jeddah averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 306 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Jeddah?
April is not one of Jeddah’s mild months; those are January, February and March, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jeddah in April?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (27 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Jeddah in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jeddah get in April?
About 306 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jeddah in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
