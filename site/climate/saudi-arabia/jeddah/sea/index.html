Jeddah sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Mecca Region · Saudi Arabia

The sea off Jeddah averages 31.0 °C (88 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 25.4 °C (78 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Jeddah sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Jeddah?
In August, at about 31.0 °C (88 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Jeddah?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 25.4 °C (78 °F).
How warm is the sea in Jeddah in February?
About 25.4 °C (78 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Jeddah?
About 28.4 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Jeddah nearby?
In August: Port Sudan (31.6 °C, 292 km).
