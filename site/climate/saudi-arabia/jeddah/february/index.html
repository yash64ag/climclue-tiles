How February compares in Jeddah

February is the coolest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Jeddah. Compared with January: 0.1 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. March is 1.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Jeddah in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jeddah in February?
In February, Jeddah averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Jeddah?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Jeddah’s mild months (January, February and March), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jeddah in February?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (27 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Jeddah in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jeddah get in February?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Jeddah in February?
About 25.4 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (31.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Jeddah in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
