Sakakah at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Saudi Arabia, Sakakah is the 3rd sunniest (about 3,698 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th driest (49 mm a year). Compared with Arar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (137 km away), Sakakah is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (33.6 °C), averages 24.5 °C more than its coolest, January (9.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 526 km from the sea.

Why Sakakah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 526 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
