Al Jubayl at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Saudi Arabia, Al Jubayl is 13th warmest (26.2 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 3,258 hours of sunshine a year) and 13th wettest (103 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dammam, the nearest covered town with different numbers (80 km away), Al Jubayl is 0.8 °C warmer in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 14 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (35.3 °C), averages 19.8 °C more than its coolest, January (15.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.9 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Al Jubayl, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Al Jubayl has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
