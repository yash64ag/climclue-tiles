How February compares in Tabuk

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Tabuk. Compared with January: 2.1 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. March is 4.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Tabuk in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tabuk in February?
In February, Tabuk averages a day high of 19.4 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Tabuk?
February is not one of Tabuk’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tabuk in February?
1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (6 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Tabuk in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tabuk get in February?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tabuk in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
