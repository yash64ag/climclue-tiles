How March compares in Tabuk

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Tabuk. Compared with February: 4.0 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. April is 5.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Tabuk in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tabuk in March?
In March, Tabuk averages a day high of 23.9 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 9.4 °C (49 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 283 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Tabuk?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Tabuk’s mild months (March, April, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tabuk in March?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (6 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Tabuk in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tabuk get in March?
About 283 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tabuk in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
