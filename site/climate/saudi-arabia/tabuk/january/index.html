How January compares in Tabuk

January is the coolest month, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Tabuk. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. February is 2.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Tabuk in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tabuk in January?
In January, Tabuk averages a day high of 17.5 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 3.6 °C (38 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Tabuk?
January is not one of Tabuk’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tabuk in January?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (6 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Tabuk in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tabuk get in January?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tabuk in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
