Tabuk weather in December

Average December weather in Tabuk, Saudi Arabia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Tabuk averages 18.7 °C (66 °F) days and 5.2 °C (41 °F) nights, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of rain over the month and about 237 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Tabuk

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Tabuk. Compared with November: 4.8 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Tabuk in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tabuk in December?
In December, Tabuk averages a day high of 18.7 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 5.2 °C (41 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Tabuk?
December is not one of Tabuk’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tabuk in December?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (6 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Tabuk in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tabuk get in December?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tabuk in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
