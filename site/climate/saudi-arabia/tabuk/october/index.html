How October compares in Tabuk

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Tabuk. Compared with September: 4.9 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 33 hours less sunshine. November is 6.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Tabuk in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tabuk in October?
In October, Tabuk averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 306 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Tabuk?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Tabuk’s mild months (March, April, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tabuk in October?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (6 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Tabuk in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tabuk get in October?
About 306 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tabuk in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
