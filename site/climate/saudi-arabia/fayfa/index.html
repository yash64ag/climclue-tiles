Fayfā’ at a glance

Among the 96 towns ClimaClue covers in Saudi Arabia, Fayfā’ is the wettest (869 mm a year). Compared with Ḑamad, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Fayfā’ is 5.7 °C cooler in June and 5.3 °C cooler in January, with 672 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.7 °C), averages 8.4 °C more than its coolest, January (20.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.6 °C (58 °F), in January. The area lies about 82 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Fayfā’, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Fayfā’ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 82 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.4 °C. The 10 km area around Fayfā’, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 25.9 °C a year, 1.0 °C warmer than the town itself, because it takes in lower ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Fayfā’ itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (17.281°N, 43.137°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-667-2231), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
