How December compares in Makkah

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Makkah. Compared with November: 3.1 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. January is 1.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Makkah in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Makkah in December?
In December, Makkah averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 18.0 °C (64 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Makkah?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Makkah’s mild months (January, February and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Makkah in December?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (27 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Makkah in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Makkah get in December?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Makkah in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
