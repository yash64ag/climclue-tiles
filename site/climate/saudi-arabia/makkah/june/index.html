How June compares in Makkah

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Makkah. Compared with May: 1.4 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. July is 0.2 °C warmer than June on average.

Makkah in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Makkah in June?
In June, Makkah averages a day high of 40.6 °C (105 °F) and a night low of 25.7 °C (78 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 308 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Makkah?
June is not one of Makkah’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Makkah in June?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the driest month of the year. The wettest month is November (27 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Makkah in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Makkah get in June?
About 308 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Makkah in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
