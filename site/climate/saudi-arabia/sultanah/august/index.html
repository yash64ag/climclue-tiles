Sulţānah in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sulţānah in August?
In August, Sulţānah averages a day high of 42.2 °C (108 °F) and a night low of 28.8 °C (84 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 347 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Sulţānah?
August is not one of Sulţānah’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sulţānah in August?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (14 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Sulţānah in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sulţānah get in August?
About 347 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sulţānah in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
