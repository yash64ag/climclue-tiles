Sulţānah in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sulţānah in May?
In May, Sulţānah averages a day high of 38.5 °C (101 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 349 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Sulţānah?
May is not one of Sulţānah’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sulţānah in May?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (15 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Sulţānah in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sulţānah get in May?
About 349 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sulţānah in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
