Sulţānah at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Saudi Arabia, Sulţānah is the 5th warmest (27.8 °C year average). Compared with Madinah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Sulţānah is about as warm in August and about as warm in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (35.5 °C), averages 17.7 °C more than its coolest, January (17.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.3 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 145 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 13 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.2 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 78% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Sulţānah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 145 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sulţānah itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (24.493°N, 39.586°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-595-2195), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
