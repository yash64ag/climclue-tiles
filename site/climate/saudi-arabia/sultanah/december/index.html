How December compares in Sulţānah

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Sulţānah. Compared with November: 4.3 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. January is 1.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Sulţānah in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sulţānah in December?
In December, Sulţānah averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 13.4 °C (56 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Sulţānah?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Sulţānah’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sulţānah in December?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (14 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Sulţānah in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sulţānah get in December?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sulţānah in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
