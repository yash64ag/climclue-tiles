Sulţānah in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sulţānah in April?
In April, Sulţānah averages a day high of 34.5 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 306 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Sulţānah?
April is not one of Sulţānah’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sulţānah in April?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (14 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Sulţānah in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sulţānah get in April?
About 306 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sulţānah in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
