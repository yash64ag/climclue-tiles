How November compares in Riyadh

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Riyadh. Compared with October: 6.8 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 45 hours less sunshine. December is 5.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Riyadh in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Riyadh in November?
In November, Riyadh averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 13.4 °C (56 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Riyadh?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Riyadh’s mild months (February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Riyadh in November?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (30 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Riyadh in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Riyadh get in November?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Riyadh in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
