How October compares in Riyadh

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Riyadh. Compared with September: 5.3 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. November is 6.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Riyadh in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Riyadh in October?
In October, Riyadh averages a day high of 33.6 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 19.2 °C (67 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 303 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Riyadh?
October is not one of Riyadh’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Riyadh in October?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (30 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Riyadh in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Riyadh get in October?
About 303 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Riyadh in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
