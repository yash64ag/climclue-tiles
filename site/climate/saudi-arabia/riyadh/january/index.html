How January compares in Riyadh

January is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Riyadh. Compared with December: 1.7 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. February is 2.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Riyadh in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Riyadh in January?
In January, Riyadh averages a day high of 19.3 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 6.7 °C (44 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Riyadh?
January is not one of Riyadh’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Riyadh in January?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (30 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Riyadh in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Riyadh get in January?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Riyadh in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
