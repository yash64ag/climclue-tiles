How December compares in Riyadh

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Riyadh. Compared with November: 5.2 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. January is 1.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Riyadh in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Riyadh in December?
In December, Riyadh averages a day high of 20.9 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 8.5 °C (47 °F), with 14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Riyadh?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Riyadh’s mild months (February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Riyadh in December?
14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (30 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Riyadh in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Riyadh get in December?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Riyadh in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
