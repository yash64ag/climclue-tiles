Madinah at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Saudi Arabia, Madinah is the 5th warmest (27.7 °C year average). Compared with Sulţānah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Madinah is 0.4 °C warmer in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (35.4 °C), averages 17.7 °C more than its coolest, January (17.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.3 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 153 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 13 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 83% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Madinah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 153 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
