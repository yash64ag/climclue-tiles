Madinah weather in November

Average November weather in Madinah, Saudi Arabia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Madinah averages 30.1 °C (86 °F) days and 17.6 °C (64 °F) nights, with 14 mm (0.5 in) of rain over the month and about 250 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Madinah

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Madinah. Compared with October: 5.8 °C cooler, 11 mm more precipitation and 60 hours less sunshine. December is 4.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Madinah in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Madinah in November?
In November, Madinah averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 17.6 °C (64 °F), with 14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 250 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Madinah?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Madinah’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Madinah in November?
14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is November (14 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Madinah in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Madinah get in November?
About 250 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Madinah in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
