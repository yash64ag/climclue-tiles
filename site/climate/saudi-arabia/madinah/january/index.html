How January compares in Madinah

January is the coolest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Madinah. Compared with December: 1.7 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. February is 1.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Madinah in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Madinah in January?
In January, Madinah averages a day high of 23.6 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 9.3 °C (49 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Madinah?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Madinah’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Madinah in January?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (14 mm), the driest June (0 mm).
Does it snow in Madinah in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Madinah get in January?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Madinah in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
