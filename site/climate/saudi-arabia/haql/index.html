Ḩaql at a glance

Among the 96 towns ClimaClue covers in Saudi Arabia, Ḩaql is the 5th sunniest (about 3,733 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (22 mm a year). Compared with Tabuk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (186 km away), Ḩaql is 1.1 °C warmer in July and 4.9 °C warmer in January, with 5 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (31.7 °C), averages 16.3 °C more than its coolest, January (15.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.7 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ḩaql, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ḩaql has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ḩaql itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (29.286°N, 34.947°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-547-2149), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
