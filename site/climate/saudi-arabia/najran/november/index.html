Najrān weather in November

Average November weather in Najrān, Saudi Arabia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Najrān averages 28.5 °C (83 °F) days and 12.7 °C (55 °F) nights, with 1 mm (0.1 in) of rain over the month and about 261 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Najrān

November is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Najrān. Compared with October: 3.5 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. December is 2.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Najrān in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Najrān in November?
In November, Najrān averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Najrān?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Najrān’s mild months (January, February, March, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Najrān in November?
1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (25 mm), the driest September (0 mm).
Does it snow in Najrān in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Najrān get in November?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Najrān in November?
