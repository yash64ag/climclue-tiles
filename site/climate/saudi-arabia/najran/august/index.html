Najrān in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Najrān in August?
In August, Najrān averages a day high of 35.8 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Najrān?
August is not one of Najrān’s mild months; those are January, February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Najrān in August?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (26 mm), the driest September (0 mm).
Does it snow in Najrān in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Najrān get in August?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Najrān in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
