How December compares in Najrān

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Najrān. Compared with November: 2.4 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Najrān in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Najrān in December?
In December, Najrān averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 10.5 °C (51 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 245 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Najrān?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Najrān’s mild months (January, February, March, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Najrān in December?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (25 mm), the driest September (0 mm).
Does it snow in Najrān in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Najrān get in December?
About 245 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Najrān in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
