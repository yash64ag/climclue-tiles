How October compares in Najrān

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Najrān. Compared with September: 4.6 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. November is 3.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Najrān in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Najrān in October?
In October, Najrān averages a day high of 29.2 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 15.6 °C (60 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 290 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Najrān?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Najrān’s mild months (January, February, March, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Najrān in October?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (25 mm), the driest September (0 mm).
Does it snow in Najrān in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Najrān get in October?
About 290 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Najrān in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
