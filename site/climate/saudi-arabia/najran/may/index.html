How May compares in Najrān

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Najrān. Compared with April: 3.3 °C warmer, 17 mm less precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. June is 1.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Najrān in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Najrān in May?
In May, Najrān averages a day high of 36.1 °C (97 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 294 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Najrān?
May is not one of Najrān’s mild months; those are January, February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Najrān in May?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (25 mm), the driest September (0 mm).
Does it snow in Najrān in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Najrān get in May?
About 294 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Najrān in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
