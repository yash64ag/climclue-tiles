Najrān at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Saudi Arabia, Najrān is the 3rd least sunny (about 2,976 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Şabyā, the nearest covered town with different numbers (164 km away), Najrān is 4.3 °C cooler in July and 8.3 °C cooler in January, with 71 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.1 °C), averages 12.8 °C more than its coolest, January (17.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.6 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 183 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Najrān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Najrān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 183 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
