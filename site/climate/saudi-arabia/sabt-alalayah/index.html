Sabt Alalayah at a glance

Among the 29 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Saudi Arabia, Sabt Alalayah is the 2nd coolest (18.8 °C year average), the 4th least sunny (about 2,988 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (554 mm a year). Compared with Bīshah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (82 km away), Sabt Alalayah is 8.5 °C cooler in June and 5.0 °C cooler in January, with 485 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (23.9 °C), averages 11.1 °C more than its coolest, January (12.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.6 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 110 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sabt Alalayah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sabt Alalayah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 110 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
