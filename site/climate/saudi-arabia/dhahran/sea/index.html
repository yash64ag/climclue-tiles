Dhahran sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Eastern Province · Saudi Arabia

The sea off Dhahran averages 33.1 °C (92 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 17.8 °C (64 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November.

Dhahran sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Dhahran?
In August, at about 33.1 °C (92 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Dhahran?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November. The coolest month is February, at 17.8 °C (64 °F).
How warm is the sea in Dhahran in February?
About 17.8 °C (64 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Dhahran?
About 25.9 °C (79 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
