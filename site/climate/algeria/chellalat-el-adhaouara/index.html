Chellalat el Adhaouara at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Chellalat el Adhaouara is 272nd warmest (15.1 °C year average), 77th sunniest (about 2,933 hours of sunshine a year) and 155th wettest (402 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sidi Aïssa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Chellalat el Adhaouara is 2.4 °C cooler in July and 1.6 °C cooler in January, with 111 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.5 °C), averages 21.2 °C more than its coolest, January (5.4 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 100 km from the sea.

Why Chellalat el Adhaouara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid.
