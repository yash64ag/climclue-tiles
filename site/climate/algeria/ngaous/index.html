N’Gaous weather by month and climate

N’Gaous at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, N’Gaous is 189th warmest (17.1 °C year average), 67th sunniest (about 2,954 hours of sunshine a year) and 209th wettest (334 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Râs el Aïoun, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), N’Gaous is 1.1 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 32 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 22.0 °C more than its coolest, January (6.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 135 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of N’Gaous, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why N’Gaous has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 135 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.0 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for N’Gaous itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.555°N, 5.611°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-484-1856), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
