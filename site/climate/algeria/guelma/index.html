Guelma at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Guelma is 140th warmest (18.0 °C year average), 280th sunniest (about 2,802 hours of sunshine a year) and 109th wettest (581 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Héliopolis, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Guelma is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 23 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.5 °C), averages 17.8 °C more than its coolest, January (9.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.3 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 66 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Guelma, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Guelma has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 66 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Guelma itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.462°N, 7.426°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-475-1874), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
