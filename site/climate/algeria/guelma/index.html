Guelma at a glance

Among the 42 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Algeria, Guelma is the 3rd least sunny (about 2,802 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Souk Ahras, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), Guelma is 1.5 °C warmer in August and 2.1 °C warmer in January, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.4 °C), averages 17.8 °C more than its coolest, January (9.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 66 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Guelma, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Guelma has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 66 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
