Why Médéa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 48 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Médéa climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Médéa?
July, with an average of 25.6 °C (78 °F), day highs around 32.5 °C (91 °F) and night lows around 17.8 °C (64 °F).
What is the coldest month in Médéa?
January, with an average of 6.5 °C (44 °F), day highs around 11.0 °C (52 °F) and night lows around 1.7 °C (35 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Médéa?
December, with 113 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 719 mm (28.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Médéa?
July, with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Médéa?
July, with about 339 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Médéa?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 11 cm (4.4 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Médéa?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, September and October.
What type of climate does Médéa have?
Mediterranean, hot summer (Köppen Csa). Hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.0 × 11 km, that contains Médéa’s GeoNames location (36.264°N, 2.754°E; ClimaClue area sq-477-1827), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 145,441 (GeoNames).
