Amizour sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Béjaïa · Algeria

The sea off Amizour averages 24.2 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.6 °C (56 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Amizour sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Amizour?
In August, at about 24.2 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Amizour?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 13.6 °C (56 °F).
How warm is the sea in Amizour in February?
About 13.6 °C (56 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Amizour?
About 18.2 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Amizour nearby?
In August: Tizi Ouzou (24.8 °C, 77 km), Jijel (25.5 °C, 79 km) and Draa Ben Khedda (24.8 °C, 84 km).
