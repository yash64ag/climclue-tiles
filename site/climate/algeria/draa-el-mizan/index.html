Draa el Mizan at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Draa el Mizan is 198th warmest (17.0 °C year average), 154th sunniest (about 2,891 hours of sunshine a year) and 35th wettest (729 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tizi Gheniff, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Draa el Mizan is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.7 °C), averages 18.1 °C more than its coolest, January (8.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 45 km from the sea.

Why Draa el Mizan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 45 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
