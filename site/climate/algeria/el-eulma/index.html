El Eulma at a glance

Among the 42 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Algeria, El Eulma is the 4th coolest (15.0 °C year average). Compared with Sétif, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), El Eulma is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 51 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.9 °C), averages 20.0 °C more than its coolest, January (5.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 69 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of El Eulma, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why El Eulma has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 69 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
