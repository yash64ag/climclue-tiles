District of Taher sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Jijel · Algeria

The sea off District of Taher averages 25.5 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.8 °C (59 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

District of Taher sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in District of Taher?
In August, at about 25.5 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in District of Taher?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 14.8 °C (59 °F).
How warm is the sea in District of Taher in February?
About 14.8 °C (59 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in District of Taher?
About 19.5 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
