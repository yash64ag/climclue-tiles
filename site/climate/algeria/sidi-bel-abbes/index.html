Sidi Bel Abbes at a glance

Among the 42 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Algeria, Sidi Bel Abbes is 26th warmest (17.7 °C year average), 17th sunniest (about 2,916 hours of sunshine a year) and 30th wettest (331 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Oran, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Sidi Bel Abbes is 0.9 °C warmer in August and 2.5 °C cooler in January, with 19 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.1 °C), averages 17.3 °C more than its coolest, January (9.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 72 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sidi Bel Abbes, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sidi Bel Abbes has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 72 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
