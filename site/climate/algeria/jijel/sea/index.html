Jijel sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Algeria

The sea off Jijel averages 25.5 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.8 °C (59 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Jijel sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Jijel?
In August, at about 25.5 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Jijel?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 14.8 °C (59 °F).
How warm is the sea in Jijel in February?
About 14.8 °C (59 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Jijel?
About 19.5 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
