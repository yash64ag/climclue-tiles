In Salah at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, In Salah is the 3rd warmest (26.2 °C year average), the 4th sunniest (about 3,633 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (15 mm a year). Compared with Aoulef, the nearest covered town with different numbers (141 km away), In Salah is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (37.9 °C), averages 24.6 °C more than its coolest, January (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.0 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 983 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of In Salah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why In Salah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 983 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for In Salah itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (27.197°N, 2.479°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-568-1824), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
