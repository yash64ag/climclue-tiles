Béjaïa at a glance

Among the 42 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Algeria, Béjaïa is the 4th wettest (771 mm a year). Compared with Jijel, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Béjaïa is 1.0 °C cooler in August and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 124 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.4 °C), averages 13.7 °C more than its coolest, January (11.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.5 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Béjaïa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Béjaïa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
