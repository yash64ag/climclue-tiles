Laghouat at a glance

Among the 42 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Algeria, Laghouat is 13th warmest (18.6 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 3,202 hours of sunshine a year) and 36th wettest (170 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Djelfa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (104 km away), Laghouat is 3.4 °C warmer in July and 2.5 °C warmer in January, with 105 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (31.1 °C), averages 23.6 °C more than its coolest, January (7.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 313 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Laghouat, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Laghouat has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 313 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
