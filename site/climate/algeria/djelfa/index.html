Djelfa at a glance

Among the 42 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Algeria, Djelfa is 34th warmest (15.3 °C year average), 8th sunniest (about 3,078 hours of sunshine a year) and 33rd wettest (275 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bou Saâda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (102 km away), Djelfa is 2.7 °C cooler in July and 2.6 °C cooler in January, with 70 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.8 °C), averages 22.7 °C more than its coolest, January (5.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 231 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Djelfa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Djelfa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 231 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
