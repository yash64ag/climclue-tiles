Râs el Oued at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Râs el Oued is 284th warmest (14.5 °C year average), 110th sunniest (about 2,903 hours of sunshine a year) and 147th wettest (420 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bordj Ghdir, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Râs el Oued is 0.4 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 11 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.8 °C), averages 20.8 °C more than its coolest, January (5.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 89 km from the sea.

Why Râs el Oued has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 89 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
