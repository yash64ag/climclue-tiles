Feidh el Botma at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Feidh el Botma is 232nd warmest (16.2 °C year average), 46th sunniest (about 3,090 hours of sunshine a year) and 249th wettest (239 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Messaad, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Feidh el Botma is 2.6 °C cooler in July and 1.8 °C cooler in January, with 66 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 22.7 °C more than its coolest, January (5.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 257 km from the sea.

Why Feidh el Botma has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 257 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Feidh el Botma itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.500°N, 3.767°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-494-1837), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
