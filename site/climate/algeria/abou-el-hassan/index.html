Abou el Hassan at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Abou el Hassan is 162nd warmest (17.9 °C year average), 255th sunniest (about 2,840 hours of sunshine a year) and 157th wettest (400 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sidi Akkacha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Abou el Hassan is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.0 °C), averages 14.8 °C more than its coolest, January (11.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.4 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Abou el Hassan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
