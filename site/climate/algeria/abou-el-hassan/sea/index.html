Abou el Hassan sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Chlef · Algeria

The sea off Abou el Hassan averages 24.7 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.7 °C (58 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Abou el Hassan sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Abou el Hassan?
In August, at about 24.7 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Abou el Hassan?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 14.7 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in Abou el Hassan in February?
About 14.7 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Abou el Hassan?
About 18.9 °C (66 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
