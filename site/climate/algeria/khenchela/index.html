Khenchela at a glance

Among the 42 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Algeria, Khenchela is 37th warmest (15.2 °C year average), 13th sunniest (about 2,922 hours of sunshine a year) and 21st wettest (396 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Aïn Beïda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Khenchela is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 17 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.4 °C), averages 20.8 °C more than its coolest, January (5.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 168 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Khenchela, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Khenchela has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 168 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
