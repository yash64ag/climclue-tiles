Tizi Rached at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Tizi Rached is 236th warmest (16.1 °C year average), 198th sunniest (about 2,879 hours of sunshine a year) and 15th wettest (822 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with L’Arbaa Naït Irathen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Tizi Rached is 0.8 °C warmer in August and 1.4 °C warmer in January, with 27 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.9 °C), averages 17.9 °C more than its coolest, January (8.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Why Tizi Rached has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.9 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
