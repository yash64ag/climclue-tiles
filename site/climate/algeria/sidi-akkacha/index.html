Sidi Akkacha at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Sidi Akkacha is 131st warmest (18.3 °C year average), 244th sunniest (about 2,849 hours of sunshine a year) and 163rd wettest (390 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ténès, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Sidi Akkacha is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 18 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.2 °C), averages 14.4 °C more than its coolest, January (11.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.1 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Sidi Akkacha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.4 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-475-1813), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
