Souk Ahras at a glance

Among the 42 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Algeria, Souk Ahras is the 2nd least sunny (about 2,784 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Guelma, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), Souk Ahras is 1.4 °C cooler in July and 2.1 °C cooler in January, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.9 °C), averages 18.4 °C more than its coolest, January (7.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 78 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Souk Ahras, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Souk Ahras has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 78 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
