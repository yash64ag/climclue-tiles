Aïn Temouchent at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Aïn Temouchent is 117th warmest (18.4 °C year average), 215th sunniest (about 2,871 hours of sunshine a year) and 193rd wettest (353 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with El Malah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Aïn Temouchent is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 24 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.6 °C), averages 15.4 °C more than its coolest, January (11.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.5 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 29 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Aïn Temouchent, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Aïn Temouchent has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 29 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-487-1788), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
