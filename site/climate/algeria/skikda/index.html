Skikda at a glance

Among the 42 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Algeria, Skikda is the 4th least sunny (about 2,803 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Constantine, the nearest covered town with different numbers (63 km away), Skikda is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 4.6 °C warmer in January, with 134 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.6 °C), averages 13.7 °C more than its coolest, January (11.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.0 °C (48 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Skikda, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Skikda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
