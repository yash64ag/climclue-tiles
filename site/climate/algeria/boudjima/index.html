Boudjima at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Boudjima is the wettest (1,085 mm a year). Compared with Makouda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Boudjima is 0.4 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 115 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.7 °C), averages 15.7 °C more than its coolest, January (9.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.4 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Boudjima has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.7 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
