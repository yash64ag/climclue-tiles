Aïn Touta weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Aïn Touta, Algeria (Batna), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Aïn Touta is warmest in July, with 35.8 °C (96 °F) days and 20.2 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 10.8 °C (51 °F) days and 1.9 °C nights. Most rain falls around September (41 mm); July is driest (9 mm).

Aïn Touta at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Aïn Touta is 227th warmest (16.3 °C year average), 65th sunniest (about 2,964 hours of sunshine a year) and 185th wettest (360 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Merouana, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Aïn Touta is 1.5 °C warmer in July and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 71 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 21.6 °C more than its coolest, January (6.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 162 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Aïn Touta, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Aïn Touta has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 162 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Aïn Touta itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.377°N, 5.900°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-486-1859), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
