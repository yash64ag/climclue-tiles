Didouche Mourad at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Didouche Mourad is 226th warmest (16.2 °C year average), 261st sunniest (about 2,832 hours of sunshine a year) and 53rd wettest (684 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hamma Bouziane, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Didouche Mourad is 0.6 °C cooler in August and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 132 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.1 °C), averages 18.0 °C more than its coolest, January (8.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 62 km from the sea.

Why Didouche Mourad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 62 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.0 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Didouche Mourad itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.452°N, 6.636°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-475-1866), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
