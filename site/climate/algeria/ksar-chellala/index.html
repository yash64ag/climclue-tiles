Ksar Chellala at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Ksar Chellala is 213th warmest (16.6 °C year average), 56th sunniest (about 3,009 hours of sunshine a year) and 239th wettest (287 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mehdia daira de meghila, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Ksar Chellala is 1.0 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 38 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.7 °C), averages 22.2 °C more than its coolest, January (6.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 156 km from the sea.

Why Ksar Chellala has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 156 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
