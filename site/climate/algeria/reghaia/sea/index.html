Reghaïa sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Algiers · Algeria

The sea off Reghaïa averages 25.1 °C (77 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 15.0 °C (59 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September.

Reghaïa sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Reghaïa?
In August, at about 25.1 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Reghaïa?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is February, at 15.0 °C (59 °F).
How warm is the sea in Reghaïa in February?
About 15.0 °C (59 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Reghaïa?
About 19.3 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
