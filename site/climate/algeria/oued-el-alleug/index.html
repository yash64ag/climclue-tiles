Oued el Alleug at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Oued el Alleug is 96th warmest (18.5 °C year average), 124th sunniest (about 2,899 hours of sunshine a year) and 90th wettest (618 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Beni Tamou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Oued el Alleug is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 8 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.2 °C), averages 14.3 °C more than its coolest, January (11.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.2 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Oued el Alleug has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
