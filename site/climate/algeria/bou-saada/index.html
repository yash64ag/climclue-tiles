Why Bou Saâda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 189 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Bou Saâda climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Bou Saâda?
July, with an average of 30.4 °C (87 °F), day highs around 36.9 °C (98 °F) and night lows around 21.6 °C (71 °F).
What is the coldest month in Bou Saâda?
January, with an average of 7.7 °C (46 °F), day highs around 11.9 °C (53 °F) and night lows around 2.8 °C (37 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Bou Saâda?
September, with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 206 mm (8.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Bou Saâda?
July, with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Bou Saâda?
July, with about 349 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Bou Saâda?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 2.8 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Bou Saâda?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, September and October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.1 × 11 km, that contains Bou Saâda’s GeoNames location (35.209°N, 4.174°E; ClimaClue area sq-487-1841), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 111,787 (GeoNames).
