Boû Arfa at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Boû Arfa is 178th warmest (17.5 °C year average), 189th sunniest (about 2,882 hours of sunshine a year) and 48th wettest (694 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Blida, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Boû Arfa is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 17 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.2 °C), averages 16.1 °C more than its coolest, January (10.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.0 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Why Boû Arfa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.1 °C. The 10 km area around Boû Arfa, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 15.2 °C a year, 2.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
