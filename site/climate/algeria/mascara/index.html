Mascara at a glance

Among the 42 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Algeria, Mascara is 23rd warmest (17.9 °C year average), 16th sunniest (about 2,918 hours of sunshine a year) and 27th wettest (353 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Relizane, the nearest covered town with different numbers (53 km away), Mascara is 0.9 °C cooler in July and 1.6 °C cooler in January, with 46 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 18.1 °C more than its coolest, January (9.4 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 62 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mascara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mascara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 62 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
