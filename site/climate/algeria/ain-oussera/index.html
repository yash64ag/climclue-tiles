Aïn Oussera at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Aïn Oussera is 177th warmest (17.5 °C year average), 57th sunniest (about 3,002 hours of sunshine a year) and 248th wettest (239 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Birine, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Aïn Oussera is 0.8 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 31 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.8 °C), averages 22.9 °C more than its coolest, January (6.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 138 km from the sea.

Why Aïn Oussera has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 138 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Aïn Oussera itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.451°N, 2.906°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-485-1829), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
