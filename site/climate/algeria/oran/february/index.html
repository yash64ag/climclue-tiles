How February compares in Oran

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Oran. Compared with January: 0.7 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. March is 1.6 °C warmer than February on average.

Oran in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oran in February?
In February, Oran averages a day high of 16.0 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 9.5 °C (49 °F), with 47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Oran?
February is not one of Oran’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oran in February?
47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (59 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Oran in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Oran get in February?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oran in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
