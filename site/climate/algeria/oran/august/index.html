How August compares in Oran

August is the warmest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Oran. Compared with July: 0.7 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. September is 2.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Oran in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oran in August?
In August, Oran averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 296 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Oran?
August is not one of Oran’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oran in August?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (59 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Oran in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Oran get in August?
About 296 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oran in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
