How January compares in Oran

January is the coolest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Oran. Compared with December: 1.3 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. February is 0.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Oran in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oran in January?
In January, Oran averages a day high of 15.0 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 9.7 °C (49 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Oran?
January is not one of Oran’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oran in January?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (56 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Oran in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Oran get in January?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oran in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
