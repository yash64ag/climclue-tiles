How April compares in Oran

April is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Oran. Compared with March: 1.7 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. May is 2.7 °C warmer than April on average.

Oran in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oran in April?
In April, Oran averages a day high of 19.5 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Oran?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Oran’s mild months (April, May, June, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oran in April?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (59 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Oran in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Oran get in April?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oran in April?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
