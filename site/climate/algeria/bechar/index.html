Béchar at a glance

Among the 42 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Algeria, Béchar is the 5th warmest (21.9 °C year average), the sunniest (about 3,467 hours of sunshine a year) and the 5th driest (105 mm a year). Compared with Tlemcen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (372 km away), Béchar is 10.4 °C warmer in July and 3.5 °C warmer in January, with 385 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (34.5 °C), averages 24.4 °C more than its coolest, January (10.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 389 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Béchar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Béchar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 389 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
