Oued el Abtal at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Oued el Abtal is 45th warmest (19.2 °C year average), 76th sunniest (about 2,937 hours of sunshine a year) and 229th wettest (308 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zemoura, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Oued el Abtal is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 79 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.2 °C), averages 18.8 °C more than its coolest, January (10.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.3 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 84 km from the sea.

Why Oued el Abtal has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 84 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Oued el Abtal itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.456°N, 0.688°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-485-1806), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
