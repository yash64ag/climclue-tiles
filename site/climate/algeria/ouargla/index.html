Ouargla at a glance

Among the 42 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Algeria, Ouargla is the warmest (23.3 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 3,455 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (38 mm a year). Compared with Touggourt, the nearest covered town with different numbers (147 km away), Ouargla is 1.2 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 26 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (35.3 °C), averages 24.1 °C more than its coolest, January (11.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.2 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 500 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ouargla, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ouargla has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 500 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
