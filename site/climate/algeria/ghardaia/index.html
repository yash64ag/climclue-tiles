Ghardaïa at a glance

Among the 42 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Algeria, Ghardaïa is the 3rd sunniest (about 3,414 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th driest (88 mm a year). Compared with Laghouat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (164 km away), Ghardaïa is 2.2 °C warmer in July and 2.3 °C warmer in January, with 82 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (33.4 °C), averages 23.5 °C more than its coolest, January (9.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 478 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ghardaïa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ghardaïa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 478 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
