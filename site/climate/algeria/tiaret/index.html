Tiaret at a glance

Among the 42 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Algeria, Tiaret is 33rd warmest (15.4 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 2,959 hours of sunshine a year) and 18th wettest (453 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Relizane, the nearest covered town with different numbers (80 km away), Tiaret is 2.0 °C cooler in July and 4.8 °C cooler in January, with 145 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.4 °C), averages 20.2 °C more than its coolest, January (6.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 130 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tiaret, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tiaret has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 130 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
