Aflou at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Aflou is 286th warmest (14.4 °C year average), 39th sunniest (about 3,126 hours of sunshine a year) and 210th wettest (330 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with El Idrissia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (54 km away), Aflou is 1.9 °C cooler in July and 1.7 °C cooler in January, with 69 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.8 °C), averages 22.7 °C more than its coolest, January (4.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 278 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Aflou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Aflou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 278 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Aflou itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.113°N, 2.102°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-498-1821), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
