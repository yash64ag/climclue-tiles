How January compares in Algiers

January is the coolest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Algiers. Compared with December: 1.2 °C cooler, 32 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 0.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Algiers in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Algiers in January?
In January, Algiers averages a day high of 14.5 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Algiers?
January is not one of Algiers’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Algiers in January?
109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (141 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Algiers in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Algiers get in January?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Algiers in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
