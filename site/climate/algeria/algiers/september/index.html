How September compares in Algiers

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Algiers. Compared with August: 2.6 °C cooler, 27 mm more precipitation and 63 hours less sunshine. October is 3.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Algiers in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Algiers in September?
In September, Algiers averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.3 °C (67 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 252 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Algiers?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Algiers’s mild months (April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Algiers in September?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (141 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Algiers in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Algiers get in September?
About 252 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Algiers in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
