How April compares in Algiers

April is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Algiers. Compared with March: 1.9 °C warmer, 15 mm less precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. May is 3.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Algiers in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Algiers in April?
In April, Algiers averages a day high of 18.8 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 10.9 °C (52 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Algiers?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Algiers’s mild months (April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Algiers in April?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (141 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Algiers in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Algiers get in April?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Algiers in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
