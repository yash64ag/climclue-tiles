How December compares in Algiers

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Algiers. Compared with November: 3.0 °C cooler, 20 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. January is 1.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Algiers in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Algiers in December?
In December, Algiers averages a day high of 15.5 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 9.4 °C (49 °F), with 141 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Algiers?
December is not one of Algiers’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Algiers in December?
141 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (141 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Algiers in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Algiers get in December?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Algiers in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
