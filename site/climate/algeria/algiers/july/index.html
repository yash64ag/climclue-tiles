How July compares in Algiers

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Algiers. Compared with June: 3.1 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. August is 0.5 °C warmer than July on average.

Algiers in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Algiers in July?
In July, Algiers averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 343 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Algiers?
July is not one of Algiers’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Algiers in July?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (141 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Algiers in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Algiers get in July?
About 343 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Algiers in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
