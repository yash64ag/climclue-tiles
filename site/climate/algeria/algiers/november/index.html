How November compares in Algiers

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Algiers. Compared with October: 4.4 °C cooler, 50 mm more precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. December is 3.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Algiers in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Algiers in November?
In November, Algiers averages a day high of 18.8 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 12.3 °C (54 °F), with 120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Algiers?
November is not one of Algiers’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Algiers in November?
120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (141 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Algiers in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Algiers get in November?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Algiers in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
