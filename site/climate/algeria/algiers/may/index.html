How May compares in Algiers

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Algiers. Compared with April: 3.2 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 54 hours more sunshine. June is 4.1 °C warmer than May on average.

Algiers in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Algiers in May?
In May, Algiers averages a day high of 22.1 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 13.9 °C (57 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 291 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Algiers?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Algiers’s mild months (April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Algiers in May?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (141 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Algiers in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Algiers get in May?
About 291 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Algiers in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
