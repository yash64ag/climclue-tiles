Boudouaou at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Boudouaou is 102nd warmest (18.5 °C year average), 149th sunniest (about 2,892 hours of sunshine a year) and 62nd wettest (671 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ouled Moussa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Boudouaou is about as warm in August and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.8 °C), averages 15.2 °C more than its coolest, January (11.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.6 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 14 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 109% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Boudouaou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Boudouaou itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.727°N, 3.410°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-472-1834), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
