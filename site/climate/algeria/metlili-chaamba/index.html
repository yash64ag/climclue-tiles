Metlili Chaamba weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Metlili Chaamba, Algeria (Ghardaia), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Metlili Chaamba at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Metlili Chaamba is 33rd warmest (21.6 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 3,437 hours of sunshine a year) and 271st wettest (79 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ghardaïa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Metlili Chaamba is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (33.6 °C), averages 23.6 °C more than its coolest, January (10.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 500 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Metlili Chaamba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Metlili Chaamba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 500 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Metlili Chaamba itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (32.267°N, 3.633°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-517-1836), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
