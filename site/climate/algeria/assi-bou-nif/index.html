Assi Bou Nif at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Assi Bou Nif is 65th warmest (18.9 °C year average), 235th sunniest (about 2,856 hours of sunshine a year) and 206th wettest (338 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sidi ech Chahmi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Assi Bou Nif is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.4 °C), averages 13.8 °C more than its coolest, January (12.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.4 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Assi Bou Nif, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Assi Bou Nif has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Assi Bou Nif itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.692°N, 0.499°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-483-1795), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
