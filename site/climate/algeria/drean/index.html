Drean at a glance

Among the 294 towns ClimaClue covers in Algeria, Drean is 50th warmest (19.2 °C year average), 285th sunniest (about 2,777 hours of sunshine a year) and 80th wettest (643 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Besbes, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Drean is 0.5 °C warmer in August and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 40 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (28.1 °C), averages 16.9 °C more than its coolest, January (11.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.7 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Drean, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Drean has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Drean itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.685°N, 7.751°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-473-1877), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
