Blida at a glance

Among the 42 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Algeria, Blida is the 3rd wettest (787 mm a year). Compared with Médéa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Blida is 0.8 °C cooler in August and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 68 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.6 °C), averages 17.2 °C more than its coolest, January (7.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Blida, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Blida has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 8.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
