Annaba at a glance

Among the 42 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Algeria, Annaba is the least sunny (about 2,744 hours of sunshine a year) and the 5th wettest (750 mm a year). Compared with Guelma, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Annaba is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 1.6 °C warmer in January, with 160 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (27.1 °C), averages 16.0 °C more than its coolest, January (11.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.2 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Annaba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Annaba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
