How September compares in Bujumbura

September is the warmest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Bujumbura. Compared with August: 0.7 °C warmer, 45 mm more precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. October is 0.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Bujumbura in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bujumbura in September?
In September, Bujumbura averages a day high of 32.6 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 18.8 °C (66 °F), with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Bujumbura?
September is not one of Bujumbura’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bujumbura in September?
83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (205 mm), the driest July (23 mm).
Does it snow in Bujumbura in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bujumbura get in September?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bujumbura in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
