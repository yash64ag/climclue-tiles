How November compares in Bujumbura

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Bujumbura. Compared with October: 0.5 °C cooler, 47 mm more precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. December is about as warm as November on average.

What to pack for Bujumbura in November

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella.

Bujumbura in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bujumbura in November?
In November, Bujumbura averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Bujumbura?
November is not one of Bujumbura’s mild months; those are June and July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bujumbura in November?
186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is November (186 mm), the driest July (21 mm).
Does it snow in Bujumbura in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bujumbura get in November?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bujumbura in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
