How January compares in Bujumbura

January is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Bujumbura. Compared with December: 0.2 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. February is 0.3 °C warmer than January on average.

What to pack for Bujumbura in January

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella.

Bujumbura in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bujumbura in January?
In January, Bujumbura averages a day high of 29.2 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.0 °C (70 °F), with 163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Bujumbura?
January is not one of Bujumbura’s mild months; those are June and July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bujumbura in January?
163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (186 mm), the driest July (21 mm).
Does it snow in Bujumbura in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bujumbura get in January?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bujumbura in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
