How March compares in Bujumbura

March is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Bujumbura. Compared with February: about as warm, 40 mm more precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. April is 0.3 °C cooler than March on average.

Bujumbura in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bujumbura in March?
In March, Bujumbura averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 176 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bujumbura?
March is not one of Bujumbura’s mild months; those are June and July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bujumbura in March?
176 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (186 mm), the driest July (21 mm).
Does it snow in Bujumbura in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bujumbura get in March?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bujumbura in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
