How July compares in Bujumbura

July is the coolest month, the driest month and the sunniest month in Bujumbura. Compared with June: 0.2 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. August is 0.7 °C warmer than July on average.

Bujumbura in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bujumbura in July?
In July, Bujumbura averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Bujumbura?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Bujumbura’s mild months (June and July), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bujumbura in July?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is November (186 mm), the driest July (21 mm).
Does it snow in Bujumbura in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bujumbura get in July?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bujumbura in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
