How August compares in Bujumbura

August is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Bujumbura. Compared with July: 0.6 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. September is 0.7 °C warmer than August on average.

Bujumbura in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bujumbura in August?
In August, Bujumbura averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 17.8 °C (64 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Bujumbura?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Bujumbura’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bujumbura in August?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (205 mm), the driest July (23 mm).
Does it snow in Bujumbura in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bujumbura get in August?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bujumbura in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
