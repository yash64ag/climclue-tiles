Asunción Mita at a glance

Among the 102 towns ClimaClue covers in Guatemala, Asunción Mita is the driest (815 mm a year). Compared with Jutiapa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Asunción Mita is 3.1 °C warmer in May and 2.3 °C warmer in January, with 275 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.3 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, January (23.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.6 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 89 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Asunción Mita, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Asunción Mita has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 89 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C. The 10 km area around Asunción Mita, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 23.3 °C a year, 2.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Asunción Mita itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.334°N, 89.711°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-696-902), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
