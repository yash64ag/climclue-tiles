Cobán at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Guatemala, Cobán is the 2nd least sunny (about 2,374 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Chinautla, the nearest covered town with different numbers (86 km away), Cobán is 2.7 °C cooler in May and 3.0 °C cooler in January, with 1,954 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (19.9 °C), averages 4.2 °C more than its coolest, January (15.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.5 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 170 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Cobán, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Cobán has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 170 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
