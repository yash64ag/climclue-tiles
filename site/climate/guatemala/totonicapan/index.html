Totonicapán at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Guatemala, Totonicapán is the coolest (12.8 °C year average). Compared with Quetzaltenango, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Totonicapán is about as warm in May and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 1,011 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (14.2 °C), averages 3.1 °C more than its coolest, January (11.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.6 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 104 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Totonicapán, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Totonicapán has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 104 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
