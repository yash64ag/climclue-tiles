Totonicapán at a glance

Among the 102 towns ClimaClue covers in Guatemala, Totonicapán is 97th warmest (13.5 °C year average), 48th sunniest (about 2,569 hours of sunshine a year) and 49th wettest (2,372 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nahualá, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Totonicapán is 0.4 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 330 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (14.7 °C), averages 2.8 °C more than its coolest, January (11.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 104 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Totonicapán, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Totonicapán has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 104 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Totonicapán itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.912°N, 91.361°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-690-886), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
