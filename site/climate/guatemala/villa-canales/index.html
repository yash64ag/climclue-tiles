Villa Canales at a glance

Among the 102 towns ClimaClue covers in Guatemala, Villa Canales is 50th warmest (20.3 °C year average), 85th sunniest (about 2,435 hours of sunshine a year) and 72nd wettest (1,675 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Petapa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Villa Canales is about as warm in April and about as warm in January, with 26 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (21.4 °C), averages 2.4 °C more than its coolest, January (19.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.1 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 67 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Villa Canales, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Villa Canales has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 67 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.4 °C. The 10 km area around Villa Canales, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 19.1 °C a year, 1.2 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Villa Canales itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.483°N, 90.534°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-695-894), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
