Why Jalapa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 109 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Jalapa climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Jalapa?
May, with an average of 20.9 °C (70 °F), day highs around 26.0 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 16.2 °C (61 °F).
What is the coldest month in Jalapa?
January, with an average of 17.1 °C (63 °F), day highs around 22.8 °C (73 °F) and night lows around 12.3 °C (54 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Jalapa?
June, with 256 mm (10.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,228 mm (48.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Jalapa?
January, with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Jalapa?
March, with about 231 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Jalapa?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Jalapa?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, April, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Jalapa’s GeoNames location (14.635°N, 89.991°W; ClimaClue area sq-693-900), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 159,840 (GeoNames).
