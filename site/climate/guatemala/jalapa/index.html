Jalapa at a glance

Among the 102 towns ClimaClue covers in Guatemala, Jalapa is 55th warmest (19.4 °C year average), 94th sunniest (about 2,358 hours of sunshine a year) and 88th wettest (1,290 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Guastatoya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Jalapa is 5.6 °C cooler in May and 5.3 °C cooler in January, with 215 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (21.1 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, January (17.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.9 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 109 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jalapa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jalapa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 109 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jalapa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.635°N, 89.991°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-693-900), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
