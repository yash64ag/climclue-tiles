Jalapa at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Guatemala, Jalapa is the least sunny (about 2,358 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Jutiapa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (40 km away), Jalapa is 2.9 °C cooler in May and 3.7 °C cooler in January, with 64 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (20.9 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, January (17.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.3 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 109 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jalapa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jalapa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 109 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
