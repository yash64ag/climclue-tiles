Petapa at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Guatemala, Petapa is 8th warmest (19.7 °C year average), 10th sunniest (about 2,535 hours of sunshine a year) and 10th wettest (1,456 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Villa Canales, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Petapa is 0.7 °C warmer in April and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 617 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (20.9 °C), averages 2.6 °C more than its coolest, January (18.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.9 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 78 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Petapa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Petapa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 78 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
