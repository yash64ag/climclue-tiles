Santa Lucía Cotzumalguapa at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Guatemala, Santa Lucía Cotzumalguapa is the 2nd warmest (24.8 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 2,676 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (3,599 mm a year). Compared with Escuintla, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Santa Lucía Cotzumalguapa is 1.7 °C warmer in April and 1.4 °C warmer in December, with 1,010 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (26.3 °C), averages 2.3 °C more than its coolest, December (23.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.1 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Why Santa Lucía Cotzumalguapa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
