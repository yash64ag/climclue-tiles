How February compares in Guatemala City

February is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Guatemala City. Compared with January: 1.0 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. March is 1.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Guatemala City in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guatemala City in February?
In February, Guatemala City averages a day high of 25.4 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 12.4 °C (54 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Guatemala City?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Guatemala City’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guatemala City in February?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (253 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Guatemala City in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guatemala City get in February?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guatemala City in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
