How June compares in Guatemala City

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Guatemala City. Compared with May: 0.8 °C cooler, 125 mm more precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. July is 0.3 °C cooler than June on average.

Guatemala City in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guatemala City in June?
In June, Guatemala City averages a day high of 24.3 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 253 mm (10.0 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Guatemala City?
June is not one of Guatemala City’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guatemala City in June?
253 mm (10.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (253 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Guatemala City in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guatemala City get in June?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guatemala City in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
