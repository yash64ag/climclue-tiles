How January compares in Guatemala City

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Guatemala City. Compared with December: 0.2 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. February is 1.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Guatemala City in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guatemala City in January?
In January, Guatemala City averages a day high of 23.8 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 11.9 °C (53 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Guatemala City?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Guatemala City’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guatemala City in January?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (253 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Guatemala City in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guatemala City get in January?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guatemala City in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
