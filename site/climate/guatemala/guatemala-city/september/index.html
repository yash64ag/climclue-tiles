How September compares in Guatemala City

September is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Guatemala City. Compared with August: 0.4 °C cooler, 54 mm more precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. October is 0.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Guatemala City in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guatemala City in September?
In September, Guatemala City averages a day high of 24.1 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 15.1 °C (59 °F), with 234 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Guatemala City?
September is not one of Guatemala City’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guatemala City in September?
234 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (253 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Guatemala City in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guatemala City get in September?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guatemala City in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
