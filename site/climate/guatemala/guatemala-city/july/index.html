How July compares in Guatemala City

July is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Guatemala City. Compared with June: 0.3 °C cooler, 48 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C warmer than July on average.

Guatemala City in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guatemala City in July?
In July, Guatemala City averages a day high of 24.3 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 14.9 °C (59 °F), with 206 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Guatemala City?
July is not one of Guatemala City’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guatemala City in July?
206 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (253 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Guatemala City in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guatemala City get in July?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guatemala City in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
