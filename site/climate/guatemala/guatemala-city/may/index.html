How May compares in Guatemala City

May is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Guatemala City. Compared with April: about as warm, 95 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. June is 0.8 °C cooler than May on average.

Guatemala City in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guatemala City in May?
In May, Guatemala City averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 127 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Guatemala City?
May is not one of Guatemala City’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guatemala City in May?
127 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (252 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Guatemala City in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guatemala City get in May?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guatemala City in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
