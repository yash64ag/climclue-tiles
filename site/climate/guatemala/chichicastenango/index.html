Chichicastenango at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Guatemala, Chichicastenango is the 3rd coolest (15.3 °C year average). Compared with Totonicapán, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Chichicastenango is 2.5 °C warmer in May and 2.4 °C warmer in January, with 556 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (16.6 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, January (13.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.9 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 116 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chichicastenango, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chichicastenango has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 116 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
