Jalpatagua at a glance

Among the 102 towns ClimaClue covers in Guatemala, Jalpatagua is 29th warmest (24.6 °C year average), 10th sunniest (about 2,709 hours of sunshine a year) and 83rd wettest (1,463 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jutiapa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Jalpatagua is 1.7 °C warmer in April and 2.4 °C warmer in January, with 373 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (26.1 °C), averages 2.5 °C more than its coolest, January (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.4 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 55 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jalpatagua, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jalpatagua has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 55 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.5 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jalpatagua itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.137°N, 90.009°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-698-899), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
