San Felipe at a glance

Among the 102 towns ClimaClue covers in Guatemala, San Felipe is 32nd warmest (24.0 °C year average), 50th sunniest (about 2,557 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (3,986 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with El Palmar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), San Felipe is 1.0 °C warmer in April and 0.8 °C warmer in December, with 218 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (24.8 °C), averages 1.5 °C more than its coolest, December (23.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.8 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 64 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of San Felipe, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why San Felipe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 64 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.5 °C. The 10 km area around San Felipe, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 22.2 °C a year, 1.8 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for San Felipe itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.623°N, 91.595°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-693-884), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
