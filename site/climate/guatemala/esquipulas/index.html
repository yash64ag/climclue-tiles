Esquipulas at a glance

Among the 102 towns ClimaClue covers in Guatemala, Esquipulas is 44th warmest (21.2 °C year average), 70th sunniest (about 2,498 hours of sunshine a year) and 76th wettest (1,612 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chiquimula, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Esquipulas is 4.1 °C cooler in May and 2.9 °C cooler in January, with 730 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (22.9 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, January (19.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.6 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 123 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Esquipulas, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Esquipulas has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 123 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C. The 10 km area around Esquipulas, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 19.7 °C a year, 1.5 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Esquipulas itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.566°N, 89.352°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-694-906), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
