Escuintla at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Guatemala, Escuintla is 4th warmest (23.5 °C year average), 4th sunniest (about 2,620 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (2,590 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Santa Lucía Cotzumalguapa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Escuintla is 1.7 °C cooler in April and 1.4 °C cooler in December, with 1,010 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (24.6 °C), averages 2.0 °C more than its coolest, December (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.2 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Escuintla, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Escuintla has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
