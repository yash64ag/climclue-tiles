Mixco at a glance

Among the 102 towns ClimaClue covers in Guatemala, Mixco is 66th warmest (17.9 °C year average), 43rd sunniest (about 2,575 hours of sunshine a year) and 85th wettest (1,411 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Lucas Sacatepéquez, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Mixco is 2.8 °C warmer in April and 2.6 °C warmer in January, with 146 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (19.3 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, January (16.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.4 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 89 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mixco, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mixco has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 89 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C. The 10 km area around Mixco, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 15.9 °C a year, 2.0 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Mixco itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.631°N, 90.607°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-693-893), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
