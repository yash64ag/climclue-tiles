Mixco at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Guatemala, Mixco is 15th warmest (15.9 °C year average), 7th sunniest (about 2,575 hours of sunshine a year) and 12th wettest (1,413 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Guatemala City, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Mixco is 3.3 °C cooler in April and 3.1 °C cooler in January, with 192 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (17.3 °C), averages 3.0 °C more than its coolest, January (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.0 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 89 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mixco, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mixco has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 89 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
