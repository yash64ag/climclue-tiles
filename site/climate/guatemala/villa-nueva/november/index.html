How November compares in Villa Nueva

November is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Villa Nueva. Compared with October: 0.7 °C cooler, 124 mm less precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. December is 0.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Villa Nueva in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Villa Nueva in November?
In November, Villa Nueva averages a day high of 23.8 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 14.3 °C (58 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 209 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Villa Nueva?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Villa Nueva’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Villa Nueva in November?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (301 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Villa Nueva in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Villa Nueva get in November?
About 209 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Villa Nueva in November?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
