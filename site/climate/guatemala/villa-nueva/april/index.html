How April compares in Villa Nueva

April is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Villa Nueva. Compared with March: 0.8 °C warmer, 31 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. May is 0.1 °C cooler than April on average.

Villa Nueva in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Villa Nueva in April?
In April, Villa Nueva averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Villa Nueva?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Villa Nueva’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Villa Nueva in April?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (301 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Villa Nueva in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Villa Nueva get in April?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Villa Nueva in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
