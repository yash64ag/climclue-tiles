How October compares in Villa Nueva

Villa Nueva in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Villa Nueva in October?
In October, Villa Nueva averages a day high of 23.8 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 159 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Villa Nueva?
October is not one of Villa Nueva’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Villa Nueva in October?
159 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (301 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Villa Nueva in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Villa Nueva get in October?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Villa Nueva in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
