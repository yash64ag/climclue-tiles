How December compares in Villa Nueva

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Villa Nueva. Compared with November: 0.4 °C cooler, 30 mm less precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. January is 0.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Villa Nueva in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Villa Nueva in December?
In December, Villa Nueva averages a day high of 24.5 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Villa Nueva?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Villa Nueva’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Villa Nueva in December?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (347 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Villa Nueva in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Villa Nueva get in December?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Villa Nueva in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
