San Andrés Itzapa at a glance

Among the 102 towns ClimaClue covers in Guatemala, San Andrés Itzapa is 75th warmest (16.7 °C year average), 19th sunniest (about 2,670 hours of sunshine a year) and 55th wettest (2,187 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chimaltenango, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), San Andrés Itzapa is 0.8 °C cooler in April and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 510 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (17.7 °C), averages 2.3 °C more than its coolest, January (15.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.0 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 89 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of San Andrés Itzapa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why San Andrés Itzapa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 89 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for San Andrés Itzapa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.622°N, 90.843°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-693-891), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
