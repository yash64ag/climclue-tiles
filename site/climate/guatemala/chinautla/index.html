Chinautla at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Guatemala, Chinautla is the driest (913 mm a year). Compared with Guatemala City, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Chinautla is 2.0 °C warmer in May and 1.3 °C warmer in January, with 308 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (22.6 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, January (18.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.1 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 101 km from the sea.

Why Chinautla has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 101 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
