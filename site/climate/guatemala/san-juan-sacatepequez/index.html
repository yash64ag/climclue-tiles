San Juan Sacatepéquez at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Guatemala, San Juan Sacatepéquez is the 3rd driest (1,077 mm a year). Compared with Mixco, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), San Juan Sacatepéquez is 2.7 °C warmer in May and 2.2 °C warmer in January, with 336 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (19.9 °C), averages 3.5 °C more than its coolest, January (16.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.2 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 100 km from the sea.

Why San Juan Sacatepéquez has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 100 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
