Prague weather in April

Average April weather in Prague, Czechia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Prague averages 14.9 °C (59 °F) days and 4.2 °C (40 °F) nights, with 28 mm (1.1 in) of rain over the month and about 165 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Prague

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Prague. Compared with March: 4.7 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. May is 5.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Prague in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Prague in April?
In April, Prague averages a day high of 14.9 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 4.2 °C (40 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Prague?
April is not one of Prague’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Prague in April?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (82 mm), the driest February (23 mm).
Does it snow in Prague in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Prague get in April?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Prague in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
