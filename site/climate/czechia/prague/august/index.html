How August compares in Prague

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Prague. Compared with July: 0.1 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. September is 4.6 °C cooler than August on average.

Prague in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Prague in August?
In August, Prague averages a day high of 25.2 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Prague?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Prague’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Prague in August?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (82 mm), the driest February (23 mm).
Does it snow in Prague in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Prague get in August?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Prague in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
