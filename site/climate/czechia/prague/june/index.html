How June compares in Prague

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Prague. Compared with May: 2.7 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. July is 2.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Prague in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Prague in June?
In June, Prague averages a day high of 23.1 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 13.4 °C (56 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Prague?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Prague’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Prague in June?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (82 mm), the driest February (23 mm).
Does it snow in Prague in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Prague get in June?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Prague in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
