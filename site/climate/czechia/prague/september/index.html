How September compares in Prague

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Prague. Compared with August: 4.6 °C cooler, 30 mm less precipitation and 54 hours less sunshine. October is 5.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Prague in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Prague in September?
In September, Prague averages a day high of 19.3 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 10.7 °C (51 °F), with 42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Prague?
September is not one of Prague’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Prague in September?
42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (82 mm), the driest February (23 mm).
Does it snow in Prague in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Prague get in September?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Prague in September?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
