How October compares in Prague

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Prague. Compared with September: 5.0 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. November is 5.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Prague in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Prague in October?
In October, Prague averages a day high of 13.7 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 6.3 °C (43 °F), with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 117 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Prague?
October is not one of Prague’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Prague in October?
32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (84 mm), the driest February (24 mm).
Does it snow in Prague in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.5 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Prague get in October?
About 117 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Prague in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
