How May compares in Prague

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Prague. Compared with April: 5.0 °C warmer, 35 mm more precipitation and 48 hours more sunshine. June is 2.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Prague in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Prague in May?
In May, Prague averages a day high of 18.9 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 9.0 °C (48 °F), with 64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Prague?
May is not one of Prague’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Prague in May?
64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (84 mm), the driest February (24 mm).
Does it snow in Prague in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Prague get in May?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Prague in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
