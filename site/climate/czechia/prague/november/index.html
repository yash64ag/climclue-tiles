How November compares in Prague

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Prague. Compared with October: 5.5 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 65 hours less sunshine. December is 3.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Prague in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Prague in November?
In November, Prague averages a day high of 7.1 °C (45 °F) and a night low of 1.5 °C (35 °F), with 34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 52 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Prague?
November is not one of Prague’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Prague in November?
34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (84 mm), the driest February (24 mm).
Does it snow in Prague in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.2 cm (3.6 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Prague get in November?
About 52 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Prague in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
