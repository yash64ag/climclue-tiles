How December compares in Prague

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Prague. Compared with November: 3.6 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. January is 1.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Prague in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Prague in December?
In December, Prague averages a day high of 3.3 °C (38 °F) and a night low of -1.9 °C (29 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 44 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Prague?
December is not one of Prague’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Prague in December?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (82 mm), the driest February (23 mm).
Does it snow in Prague in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 10 cm (4.0 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Prague get in December?
About 44 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Prague in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
