Pilsen at a glance

Among the 5 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Czechia, Pilsen is the 2nd coolest (8.7 °C year average), the 2nd least sunny (about 1,574 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (531 mm a year). Compared with Prague, the nearest covered town with different numbers (84 km away), Pilsen is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 10 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.6 °C), averages 19.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 451 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pilsen, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pilsen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 451 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
