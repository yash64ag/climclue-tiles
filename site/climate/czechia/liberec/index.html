Liberec at a glance

Among the 5 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Czechia, Liberec is the coolest (7.4 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 1,575 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (1,006 mm a year). Compared with Prague, the nearest covered town with different numbers (88 km away), Liberec is 2.3 °C cooler in July and 1.7 °C cooler in January, with 465 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.1 °C), averages 19.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 336 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Liberec, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Liberec has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 336 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
