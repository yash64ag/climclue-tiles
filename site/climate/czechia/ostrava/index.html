Ostrava at a glance

Among the 5 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Czechia, Ostrava is the 3rd warmest (9.2 °C year average), the least sunny (about 1,541 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (723 mm a year). Compared with Brno, the nearest covered town with different numbers (140 km away), Ostrava is 1.4 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 211 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 20.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 506 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ostrava, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ostrava has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 506 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
