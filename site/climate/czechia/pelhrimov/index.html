Pelhřimov at a glance

Among the 117 towns ClimaClue covers in Czechia, Pelhřimov is 107th warmest (7.9 °C year average), 77th sunniest (about 1,596 hours of sunshine a year) and 30th wettest (674 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jihlava, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Pelhřimov is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 18 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.6 °C), averages 19.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 439 km from the sea.

Why Pelhřimov has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pelhřimov itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (49.431°N, 15.223°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-345-1952), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
