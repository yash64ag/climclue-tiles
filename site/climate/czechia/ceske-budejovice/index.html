České Budějovice at a glance

Among the 117 towns ClimaClue covers in Czechia, České Budějovice is 64th warmest (9.1 °C year average), 50th sunniest (about 1,629 hours of sunshine a year) and 54th wettest (606 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jindřichův Hradec, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), České Budějovice is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 100 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.9 °C), averages 19.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 372 km from the sea.

Why České Budějovice has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for České Budějovice itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.974°N, 14.474°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-350-1944), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
