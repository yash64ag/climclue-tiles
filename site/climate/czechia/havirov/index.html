Havířov at a glance

Among the 117 towns ClimaClue covers in Czechia, Havířov is 70th warmest (8.9 °C year average), 103rd sunniest (about 1,543 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (830 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Orlová, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Havířov is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 24 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.8 °C), averages 20.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 523 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Havířov, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Havířov has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Havířov itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (49.780°N, 18.437°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-342-1984), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
