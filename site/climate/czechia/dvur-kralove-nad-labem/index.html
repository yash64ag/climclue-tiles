Among the 117 towns ClimaClue covers in Czechia, Dvůr Králové nad Labem is 94th warmest (8.4 °C year average), 108th sunniest (about 1,536 hours of sunshine a year) and 23rd wettest (710 mm a year), near the middle on each.
