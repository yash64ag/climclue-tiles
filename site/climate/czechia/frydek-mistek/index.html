Frýdek-Místek at a glance

Among the 117 towns ClimaClue covers in Czechia, Frýdek-Místek is 83rd warmest (8.7 °C year average), 107th sunniest (about 1,537 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (846 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Havířov, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Frýdek-Místek is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 15 mm more precipitation a year. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Frýdek-Místek itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (49.683°N, 18.350°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-343-1983), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
