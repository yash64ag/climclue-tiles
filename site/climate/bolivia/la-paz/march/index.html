How March compares in La Paz

March is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in La Paz. Compared with February: 0.1 °C cooler, 52 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. April is 0.4 °C cooler than March on average.

La Paz in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in La Paz in March?
In March, La Paz averages a day high of 11.6 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 2.5 °C (37 °F), with 158 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 152 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit La Paz?
No month in La Paz is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in La Paz in March?
158 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (280 mm), the driest July (23 mm).
Does it snow in La Paz in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does La Paz get in March?
About 152 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for La Paz in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
