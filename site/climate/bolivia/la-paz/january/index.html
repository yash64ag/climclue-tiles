How January compares in La Paz

January is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in La Paz. Compared with December: 0.4 °C cooler, 76 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. February is 0.2 °C cooler than January on average.

La Paz in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in La Paz in January?
In January, La Paz averages a day high of 15.0 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 3.9 °C (39 °F), with 229 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit La Paz?
No month in La Paz is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in La Paz in January?
229 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (229 mm), the driest July (15 mm).
Does it snow in La Paz in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does La Paz get in January?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for La Paz in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
