How June compares in La Paz

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in La Paz. Compared with May: 1.4 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. July is 0.4 °C cooler than June on average.

La Paz in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in La Paz in June?
In June, La Paz averages a day high of 10.3 °C (51 °F) and a night low of -2.4 °C (28 °F), with 27 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit La Paz?
No month in La Paz is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in La Paz in June?
27 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (280 mm), the driest July (23 mm).
Does it snow in La Paz in June?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.7 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in June (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does La Paz get in June?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for La Paz in June?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
