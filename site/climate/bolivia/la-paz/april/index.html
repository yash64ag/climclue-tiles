La Paz weather in April

Average April weather in La Paz, Bolivia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, La Paz averages 15.0 °C (59 °F) days and 2.2 °C (36 °F) nights, with 67 mm (2.6 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 175 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in La Paz

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in La Paz. Compared with March: 0.5 °C cooler, 61 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. May is 1.1 °C cooler than April on average.

La Paz in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in La Paz in April?
In April, La Paz averages a day high of 15.0 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 2.2 °C (36 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit La Paz?
No month in La Paz is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in La Paz in April?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (229 mm), the driest July (15 mm).
Does it snow in La Paz in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.5 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does La Paz get in April?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for La Paz in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
