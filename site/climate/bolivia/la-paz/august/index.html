How August compares in La Paz

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in La Paz. Compared with July: 1.0 °C warmer, 29 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. September is 1.1 °C warmer than August on average.

La Paz in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in La Paz in August?
In August, La Paz averages a day high of 10.7 °C (51 °F) and a night low of -1.9 °C (29 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit La Paz?
No month in La Paz is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in La Paz in August?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (280 mm), the driest July (23 mm).
Does it snow in La Paz in August?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.1 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in August (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does La Paz get in August?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for La Paz in August?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
