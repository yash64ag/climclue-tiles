La Paz weather in October

Average October weather in La Paz, Bolivia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, La Paz averages 16.2 °C (61 °F) days and 2.4 °C (36 °F) nights, with 80 mm (3.2 in) of rain over the month and about 195 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in La Paz

October is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in La Paz. Compared with September: 1.3 °C warmer, 24 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. November is 0.7 °C warmer than October on average.

La Paz in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in La Paz in October?
In October, La Paz averages a day high of 16.2 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 2.4 °C (36 °F), with 80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit La Paz?
No month in La Paz is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in La Paz in October?
80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (229 mm), the driest July (15 mm).
Does it snow in La Paz in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does La Paz get in October?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for La Paz in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
