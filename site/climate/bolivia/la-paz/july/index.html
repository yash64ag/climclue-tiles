How July compares in La Paz

July is the coolest month, the driest month and the sunniest month in La Paz. Compared with June: 0.4 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. August is 1.0 °C warmer than July on average.

La Paz in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in La Paz in July?
In July, La Paz averages a day high of 10.0 °C (50 °F) and a night low of -2.9 °C (27 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit La Paz?
No month in La Paz is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in La Paz in July?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (280 mm), the driest July (23 mm).
Does it snow in La Paz in July?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.3 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in July (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does La Paz get in July?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for La Paz in July?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
