How September compares in La Paz

September is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in La Paz. Compared with August: 1.1 °C warmer, 23 mm more precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. October is 1.4 °C warmer than September on average.

La Paz in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in La Paz in September?
In September, La Paz averages a day high of 11.3 °C (52 °F) and a night low of -0.6 °C (31 °F), with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit La Paz?
No month in La Paz is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in La Paz in September?
74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (280 mm), the driest July (23 mm).
Does it snow in La Paz in September?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in September (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does La Paz get in September?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for La Paz in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
