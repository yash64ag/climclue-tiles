La Paz weather in November

Average November weather in La Paz, Bolivia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, La Paz averages 16.6 °C (62 °F) days and 3.3 °C (38 °F) nights, with 91 mm (3.6 in) of rain over the month and about 177 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in La Paz

November is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in La Paz. Compared with October: 0.7 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. December is 0.2 °C cooler than November on average.

La Paz in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in La Paz in November?
In November, La Paz averages a day high of 16.6 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 3.3 °C (38 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit La Paz?
No month in La Paz is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in La Paz in November?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (229 mm), the driest July (15 mm).
Does it snow in La Paz in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does La Paz get in November?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for La Paz in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
