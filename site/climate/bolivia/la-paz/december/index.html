How December compares in La Paz

December is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in La Paz. Compared with November: 0.1 °C cooler, 76 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. January is 0.4 °C cooler than December on average.

La Paz in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in La Paz in December?
In December, La Paz averages a day high of 12.2 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 2.9 °C (37 °F), with 192 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit La Paz?
No month in La Paz is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in La Paz in December?
192 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (280 mm), the driest July (23 mm).
Does it snow in La Paz in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does La Paz get in December?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for La Paz in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
