Quillacollo at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bolivia, Quillacollo is the 3rd warmest (14.1 °C year average). Compared with Cochabamba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Quillacollo is 1.8 °C warmer in November and 1.9 °C warmer in July, with 555 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (15.7 °C), averages 3.9 °C more than its coolest, July (11.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, July, averages 4.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 461 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Quillacollo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Quillacollo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 461 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
