How June compares in Cochabamba

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Cochabamba. Compared with May: 1.3 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. July is 0.2 °C cooler than June on average.

Cochabamba in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cochabamba in June?
In June, Cochabamba averages a day high of 16.3 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 3.8 °C (39 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Cochabamba?
No month in Cochabamba is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cochabamba in June?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (288 mm), the driest July (25 mm).
Does it snow in Cochabamba in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cochabamba get in June?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cochabamba in June?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
