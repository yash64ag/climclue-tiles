How May compares in Cochabamba

May is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Cochabamba. Compared with April: 1.3 °C cooler, 42 mm less precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. June is 1.3 °C cooler than May on average.

Cochabamba in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cochabamba in May?
In May, Cochabamba averages a day high of 16.9 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 5.3 °C (42 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Cochabamba?
No month in Cochabamba is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cochabamba in May?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (288 mm), the driest July (25 mm).
Does it snow in Cochabamba in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cochabamba get in May?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cochabamba in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
