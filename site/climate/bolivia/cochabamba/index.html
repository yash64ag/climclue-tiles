Why Cochabamba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 471 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 8.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Cochabamba climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Cochabamba?
November, with an average of 13.8 °C (57 °F), day highs around 18.2 °C (65 °F) and night lows around 8.4 °C (47 °F).
What is the coldest month in Cochabamba?
July, with an average of 9.8 °C (50 °F), day highs around 16.0 °C (61 °F) and night lows around 3.4 °C (38 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Cochabamba?
January, with 288 mm (11.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,507 mm (59.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Cochabamba?
July, with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Cochabamba?
August, with about 265 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Cochabamba?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Cochabamba?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Cochabamba have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Cochabamba’s GeoNames location (17.382°S, 66.160°W; ClimaClue area sq-1013-1138), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 841,276 (GeoNames).
