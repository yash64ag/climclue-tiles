How October compares in Cochabamba

October is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Cochabamba. Compared with September: 1.2 °C warmer, 32 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. November is 0.5 °C warmer than October on average.

Cochabamba in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cochabamba in October?
In October, Cochabamba averages a day high of 24.5 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 7.0 °C (45 °F), with 85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Cochabamba?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Cochabamba’s mild months (October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cochabamba in October?
85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (237 mm), the driest July (17 mm).
Does it snow in Cochabamba in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cochabamba get in October?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cochabamba in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
