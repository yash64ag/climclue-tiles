How March compares in Cochabamba

March is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Cochabamba. Compared with February: 0.2 °C cooler, 25 mm less precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. April is 0.4 °C cooler than March on average.

Cochabamba in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cochabamba in March?
In March, Cochabamba averages a day high of 16.8 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 8.3 °C (47 °F), with 209 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Cochabamba?
No month in Cochabamba is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cochabamba in March?
209 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (288 mm), the driest July (25 mm).
Does it snow in Cochabamba in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cochabamba get in March?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cochabamba in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
