How December compares in Cochabamba

December is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Cochabamba. Compared with November: 0.1 °C cooler, 46 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. January is 0.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Cochabamba in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cochabamba in December?
In December, Cochabamba averages a day high of 17.6 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 9.0 °C (48 °F), with 208 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Cochabamba?
No month in Cochabamba is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cochabamba in December?
208 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (288 mm), the driest July (25 mm).
Does it snow in Cochabamba in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cochabamba get in December?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cochabamba in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
