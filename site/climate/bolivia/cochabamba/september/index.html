Cochabamba weather in September

Average September weather in Cochabamba, Bolivia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Cochabamba averages 23.8 °C (75 °F) days and 5.2 °C (41 °F) nights, with 53 mm (2.1 in) of rain over the month and about 226 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Cochabamba

September is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Cochabamba. Compared with August: 1.1 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 39 hours less sunshine. October is 1.2 °C warmer than September on average.

Cochabamba in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cochabamba in September?
In September, Cochabamba averages a day high of 23.8 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 5.2 °C (41 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Cochabamba?
September is not one of Cochabamba’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cochabamba in September?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (237 mm), the driest July (17 mm).
Does it snow in Cochabamba in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cochabamba get in September?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cochabamba in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
