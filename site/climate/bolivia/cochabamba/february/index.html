How February compares in Cochabamba

February is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Cochabamba. Compared with January: 0.1 °C cooler, 44 mm less precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. March is 0.2 °C cooler than February on average.

Cochabamba in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cochabamba in February?
In February, Cochabamba averages a day high of 21.9 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 9.0 °C (48 °F), with 193 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Cochabamba?
February is not one of Cochabamba’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cochabamba in February?
193 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (237 mm), the driest July (17 mm).
Does it snow in Cochabamba in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cochabamba get in February?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cochabamba in February?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
