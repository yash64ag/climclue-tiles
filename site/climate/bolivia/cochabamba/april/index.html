How April compares in Cochabamba

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Cochabamba. Compared with March: 0.4 °C cooler, 122 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. May is 1.3 °C cooler than April on average.

Cochabamba in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cochabamba in April?
In April, Cochabamba averages a day high of 16.8 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 7.3 °C (45 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Cochabamba?
No month in Cochabamba is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cochabamba in April?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (288 mm), the driest July (25 mm).
Does it snow in Cochabamba in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cochabamba get in April?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cochabamba in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
