Santa Cruz de la Sierra at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bolivia, Santa Cruz de la Sierra is the warmest (23.5 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 2,665 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (1,501 mm a year). Compared with Sucre, the nearest covered town with different numbers (260 km away), Santa Cruz de la Sierra is 10.6 °C warmer in January and 9.1 °C warmer in July, with 846 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, January (25.6 °C), averages 5.9 °C more than its coolest, July (19.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.3 °C (61 °F), in July. The area lies about 775 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Santa Cruz de la Sierra, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Santa Cruz de la Sierra

For mild weather, the best months to visit Santa Cruz de la Sierra are June, July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July, August and September.

Why Santa Cruz de la Sierra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 775 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
