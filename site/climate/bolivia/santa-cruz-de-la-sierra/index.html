Why Santa Cruz de la Sierra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 775 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Santa Cruz de la Sierra climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Santa Cruz de la Sierra?
January, with an average of 25.6 °C (78 °F), day highs around 29.4 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 22.0 °C (72 °F).
What is the coldest month in Santa Cruz de la Sierra?
July, with an average of 19.8 °C (68 °F), day highs around 24.1 °C (75 °F) and night lows around 16.3 °C (61 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Santa Cruz de la Sierra?
December, with 219 mm (8.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,501 mm (59.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Santa Cruz de la Sierra?
August, with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Santa Cruz de la Sierra?
August, with about 262 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Santa Cruz de la Sierra?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Santa Cruz de la Sierra?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Santa Cruz de la Sierra have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Santa Cruz de la Sierra’s GeoNames location (17.786°S, 63.181°W; ClimaClue area sq-1017-1168), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,831,434 (GeoNames).
