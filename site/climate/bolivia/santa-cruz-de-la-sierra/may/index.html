How May compares in Santa Cruz de la Sierra

May is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Santa Cruz de la Sierra. Compared with April: 2.5 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. June is 1.4 °C cooler than May on average.

Santa Cruz de la Sierra in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in May?
In May, Santa Cruz de la Sierra averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 16.2 °C (61 °F), with 105 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Santa Cruz de la Sierra?
May is not one of Santa Cruz de la Sierra’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in May?
105 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (216 mm), the driest August (42 mm).
Does it snow in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santa Cruz de la Sierra get in May?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santa Cruz de la Sierra in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
