Santa Cruz de la Sierra weather in November

Average November weather in Santa Cruz de la Sierra, Bolivia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Santa Cruz de la Sierra averages 31.1 °C (88 °F) days and 20.0 °C (68 °F) nights, with 145 mm (5.7 in) of rain over the month and about 229 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Santa Cruz de la Sierra

November is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Santa Cruz de la Sierra. Compared with October: 0.4 °C warmer, 25 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. December is 0.1 °C warmer than November on average.

Santa Cruz de la Sierra in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in November?
In November, Santa Cruz de la Sierra averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Santa Cruz de la Sierra?
November is not one of Santa Cruz de la Sierra’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in November?
145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (216 mm), the driest August (42 mm).
Does it snow in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santa Cruz de la Sierra get in November?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santa Cruz de la Sierra in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
