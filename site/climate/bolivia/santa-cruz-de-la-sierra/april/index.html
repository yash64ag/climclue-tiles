How April compares in Santa Cruz de la Sierra

Santa Cruz de la Sierra in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in April?
In April, Santa Cruz de la Sierra averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Santa Cruz de la Sierra?
April is not one of Santa Cruz de la Sierra’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in April?
117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (219 mm), the driest August (43 mm).
Does it snow in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santa Cruz de la Sierra get in April?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santa Cruz de la Sierra in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
