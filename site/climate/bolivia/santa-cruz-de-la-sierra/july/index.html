How July compares in Santa Cruz de la Sierra

July is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Santa Cruz de la Sierra. Compared with June: 0.1 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. August is 2.1 °C warmer than July on average.

Santa Cruz de la Sierra in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in July?
In July, Santa Cruz de la Sierra averages a day high of 24.1 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 16.3 °C (61 °F), with 62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Santa Cruz de la Sierra?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Santa Cruz de la Sierra’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in July?
62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (219 mm), the driest August (43 mm).
Does it snow in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santa Cruz de la Sierra get in July?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santa Cruz de la Sierra in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
