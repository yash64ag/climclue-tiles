How February compares in Santa Cruz de la Sierra

February is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Santa Cruz de la Sierra. Compared with January: 0.4 °C cooler, 20 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. March is 0.1 °C cooler than February on average.

Santa Cruz de la Sierra in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in February?
In February, Santa Cruz de la Sierra averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 182 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Santa Cruz de la Sierra?
February is not one of Santa Cruz de la Sierra’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in February?
182 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (219 mm), the driest August (43 mm).
Does it snow in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santa Cruz de la Sierra get in February?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santa Cruz de la Sierra in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
