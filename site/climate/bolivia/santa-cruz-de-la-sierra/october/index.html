How October compares in Santa Cruz de la Sierra

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Santa Cruz de la Sierra. Compared with September: 1.8 °C warmer, 41 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. November is 0.4 °C warmer than October on average.

Santa Cruz de la Sierra in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in October?
In October, Santa Cruz de la Sierra averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Santa Cruz de la Sierra?
October is not one of Santa Cruz de la Sierra’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in October?
123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (219 mm), the driest August (43 mm).
Does it snow in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santa Cruz de la Sierra get in October?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santa Cruz de la Sierra in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
