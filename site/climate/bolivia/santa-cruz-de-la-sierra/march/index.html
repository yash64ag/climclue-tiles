How March compares in Santa Cruz de la Sierra

March is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Santa Cruz de la Sierra. Compared with February: 0.1 °C cooler, 32 mm less precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. April is 1.4 °C cooler than March on average.

Santa Cruz de la Sierra in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in March?
In March, Santa Cruz de la Sierra averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Santa Cruz de la Sierra?
March is not one of Santa Cruz de la Sierra’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in March?
149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (219 mm), the driest August (43 mm).
Does it snow in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santa Cruz de la Sierra get in March?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santa Cruz de la Sierra in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
