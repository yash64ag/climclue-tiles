How June compares in Santa Cruz de la Sierra

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Santa Cruz de la Sierra. Compared with May: 1.3 °C cooler, 40 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. July is 0.1 °C cooler than June on average.

Santa Cruz de la Sierra in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in June?
In June, Santa Cruz de la Sierra averages a day high of 23.7 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 17.0 °C (63 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Santa Cruz de la Sierra?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Santa Cruz de la Sierra’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in June?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (219 mm), the driest August (43 mm).
Does it snow in Santa Cruz de la Sierra in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Santa Cruz de la Sierra get in June?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Santa Cruz de la Sierra in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
