Why Potosí has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Low sun and long winters keep it cold; even the warmest month stays below 10 °C, too cool for trees. Cold air holds little moisture, so precipitation is usually light. About 472 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Potosí climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Potosí?
November, with an average of 9.7 °C (49 °F), day highs around 15.0 °C (59 °F) and night lows around 3.5 °C (38 °F).
What is the coldest month in Potosí?
June, with an average of 4.8 °C (41 °F), day highs around 12.8 °C (55 °F) and night lows around -3.6 °C (26 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Potosí?
January, with 149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 595 mm (23.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Potosí?
July, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Potosí?
August, with about 285 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Potosí?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Potosí?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Potosí have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Potosí’s GeoNames location (19.584°S, 65.753°W; ClimaClue area sq-1035-1142), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 141,251 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
