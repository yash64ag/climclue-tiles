Potosí at a glance

Among the 9 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bolivia, Potosí is the 2nd coolest (7.8 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 2,783 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (595 mm a year). Compared with Sucre, the nearest covered town with different numbers (80 km away), Potosí is 6.0 °C cooler in November and 6.0 °C cooler in June, with 60 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (9.7 °C), averages 4.8 °C more than its coolest, June (4.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in May, June, July, August and September. The area lies about 472 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Potosí, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Potosí has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Low sun and long winters keep it cold; even the warmest month stays below 10 °C, too cool for trees. Cold air holds little moisture, so precipitation is usually light. About 472 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
