Niš at a glance

Among the 8 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Serbia, Niš is the 3rd coolest (11.8 °C year average) and the 2nd wettest (775 mm a year). Compared with Kragujevac, the nearest covered town with different numbers (110 km away), Niš is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 132 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.9 °C), averages 23.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 271 km from the sea.

Why Niš has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 271 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
