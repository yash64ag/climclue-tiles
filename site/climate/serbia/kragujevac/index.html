Kragujevac at a glance

Among the 8 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Serbia, Kragujevac is the 3rd driest (643 mm a year). Compared with Čačak, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Kragujevac is 1.6 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 164 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.4 °C), averages 22.1 °C more than its coolest, January (0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 271 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kragujevac, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kragujevac has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 271 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
