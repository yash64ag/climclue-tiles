Novi Beograd at a glance

Among the 8 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Serbia, Novi Beograd is the warmest (12.6 °C year average), the sunniest (about 1,991 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (684 mm a year). Compared with Novi Sad, the nearest covered town with different numbers (68 km away), Novi Beograd is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 52 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.3 °C), averages 22.4 °C more than its coolest, January (0.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 316 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 99% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Novi Beograd has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 316 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
