How April compares in Belgrade

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Belgrade. Compared with March: 5.4 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 35 hours more sunshine. May is 5.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Belgrade in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Belgrade in April?
In April, Belgrade averages a day high of 17.8 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 7.6 °C (46 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Belgrade?
April is not one of Belgrade’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Belgrade in April?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (99 mm), the driest February (38 mm).
Does it snow in Belgrade in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Belgrade get in April?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Belgrade in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
