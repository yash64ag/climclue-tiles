How December compares in Belgrade

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Belgrade. Compared with November: 4.8 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. January is 1.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Belgrade in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Belgrade in December?
In December, Belgrade averages a day high of 5.4 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -0.9 °C (30 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 60 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Belgrade?
December is not one of Belgrade’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Belgrade in December?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (97 mm), the driest February (36 mm).
Does it snow in Belgrade in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 16 cm (6.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Belgrade get in December?
About 60 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Belgrade in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
