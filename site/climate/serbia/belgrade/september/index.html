How September compares in Belgrade

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Belgrade. Compared with August: 4.5 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 63 hours less sunshine. October is 5.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Belgrade in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Belgrade in September?
In September, Belgrade averages a day high of 24.0 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Belgrade?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Belgrade’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Belgrade in September?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (99 mm), the driest February (38 mm).
Does it snow in Belgrade in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Belgrade get in September?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Belgrade in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
