Gornji Milanovac at a glance

Among the 51 towns ClimaClue covers in Serbia, Gornji Milanovac is the 3rd coolest (11.1 °C year average) and the 5th wettest (739 mm a year). Compared with Čačak, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Gornji Milanovac is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 69 mm more precipitation a year. Why Gornji Milanovac has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Gornji Milanovac itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (44.026°N, 20.462°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-399-2004), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
