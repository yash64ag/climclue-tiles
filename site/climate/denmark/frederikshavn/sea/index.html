Frederikshavn sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · North Denmark · Denmark

The sea off Frederikshavn averages 17.1 °C (63 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 3.0 °C (37 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Frederikshavn sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Frederikshavn?
In August, at about 17.1 °C (63 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Frederikshavn?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 3.0 °C (37 °F).
How warm is the sea in Frederikshavn in February?
About 3.0 °C (37 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Frederikshavn?
About 9.5 °C (49 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Frederikshavn nearby?
In August: Björlanda (17.8 °C, 85 km), Västra Frölunda (17.8 °C, 86 km) and Gothenburg (17.8 °C, 90 km).
