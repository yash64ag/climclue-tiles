Århus at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Denmark, Århus is the coolest (8.3 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 1,633 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (711 mm a year). Compared with Odense, the nearest covered town with different numbers (85 km away), Århus is 0.5 °C cooler in July and about as warm in February, with 35 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (16.6 °C), averages 15.6 °C more than its coolest, February (1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 13 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Århus, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Århus has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
