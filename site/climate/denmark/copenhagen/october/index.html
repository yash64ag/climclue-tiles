How October compares in Copenhagen

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Copenhagen. Compared with September: 4.0 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 52 hours less sunshine. November is 4.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Copenhagen in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Copenhagen in October?
In October, Copenhagen averages a day high of 11.7 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 7.6 °C (46 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 95 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Copenhagen?
October is not one of Copenhagen’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Copenhagen in October?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (77 mm), the driest April (34 mm).
Does it snow in Copenhagen in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Copenhagen get in October?
About 95 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Copenhagen in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
