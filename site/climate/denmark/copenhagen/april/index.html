How April compares in Copenhagen

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Copenhagen. Compared with March: 4.0 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 67 hours more sunshine. May is 4.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Copenhagen in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Copenhagen in April?
In April, Copenhagen averages a day high of 11.2 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 3.5 °C (38 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Copenhagen?
April is not one of Copenhagen’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Copenhagen in April?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (78 mm), the driest April (35 mm).
Does it snow in Copenhagen in April?
Yes, on average: about 1.0 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in April (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Copenhagen get in April?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Copenhagen in April?
About 5.1 °C (41 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (17.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Copenhagen in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
