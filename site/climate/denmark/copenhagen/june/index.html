Copenhagen weather in June

Average June weather in Copenhagen, Denmark: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Copenhagen averages 19.1 °C (66 °F) days and 10.3 °C (51 °F) nights, with 68 mm (2.7 in) of rain over the month and about 241 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Copenhagen

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Copenhagen. Compared with May: 3.4 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. July is 2.6 °C warmer than June on average.

Copenhagen in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Copenhagen in June?
In June, Copenhagen averages a day high of 19.1 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 10.3 °C (51 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Copenhagen?
June is not one of Copenhagen’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Copenhagen in June?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (78 mm), the driest April (35 mm).
Does it snow in Copenhagen in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Copenhagen get in June?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Copenhagen in June?
About 13.9 °C (57 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (17.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Copenhagen in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
