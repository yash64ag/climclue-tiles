How September compares in Copenhagen

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Copenhagen. Compared with August: 3.6 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 77 hours less sunshine. October is 4.0 °C cooler than September on average.

Copenhagen in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Copenhagen in September?
In September, Copenhagen averages a day high of 16.9 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 9.8 °C (50 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Copenhagen?
September is not one of Copenhagen’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Copenhagen in September?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (78 mm), the driest April (35 mm).
Does it snow in Copenhagen in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Copenhagen get in September?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Copenhagen in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
