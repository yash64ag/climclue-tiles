How August compares in Copenhagen

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Copenhagen. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 14 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. September is 3.5 °C cooler than August on average.

Copenhagen in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Copenhagen in August?
In August, Copenhagen averages a day high of 20.0 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 14.3 °C (58 °F), with 77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Copenhagen?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Copenhagen’s mild months (July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Copenhagen in August?
77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (77 mm), the driest April (34 mm).
Does it snow in Copenhagen in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Copenhagen get in August?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Copenhagen in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
