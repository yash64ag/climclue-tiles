How November compares in Copenhagen

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Copenhagen. Compared with October: 4.3 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. December is 3.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Copenhagen in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Copenhagen in November?
In November, Copenhagen averages a day high of 7.0 °C (45 °F) and a night low of 3.6 °C (38 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 53 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Copenhagen?
November is not one of Copenhagen’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Copenhagen in November?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (77 mm), the driest April (34 mm).
Does it snow in Copenhagen in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.6 cm (1.8 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Copenhagen get in November?
About 53 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Copenhagen in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
