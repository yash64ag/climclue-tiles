How March compares in Copenhagen

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Copenhagen. Compared with February: 1.9 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 52 hours more sunshine. April is 3.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Copenhagen in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Copenhagen in March?
In March, Copenhagen averages a day high of 5.3 °C (42 °F) and a night low of 0.4 °C (33 °F), with 42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 109 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Copenhagen?
March is not one of Copenhagen’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Copenhagen in March?
42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (77 mm), the driest April (34 mm).
Does it snow in Copenhagen in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 12 cm (4.5 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Copenhagen get in March?
About 109 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Copenhagen in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
