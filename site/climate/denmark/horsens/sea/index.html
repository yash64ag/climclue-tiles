Horsens sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Central Jutland · Denmark

The sea off Horsens averages 17.2 °C (63 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 2.1 °C (36 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Horsens sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Horsens?
In August, at about 17.2 °C (63 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Horsens?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 2.1 °C (36 °F).
How warm is the sea in Horsens in February?
About 2.1 °C (36 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Horsens?
About 9.2 °C (49 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Horsens nearby?
In August: Kolding (18.0 °C, 48 km), Haderslev (18.0 °C, 72 km) and Kalundborg (17.8 °C, 80 km).
