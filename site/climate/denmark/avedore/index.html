Avedøre at a glance

Among the 63 towns ClimaClue covers in Denmark, Avedøre is 17th warmest (8.6 °C year average), 16th sunniest (about 1,654 hours of sunshine a year) and 46th wettest (664 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hvidovre, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Avedøre is about as warm in July and about as warm in February, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.3 °C), averages 16.3 °C more than its coolest, February (0.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 8 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 103% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Avedøre has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Avedøre itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (55.627°N, 12.456°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-283-1924), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
