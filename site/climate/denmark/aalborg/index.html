Aalborg at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Denmark, Aalborg is the 3rd warmest (8.4 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 1,648 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (750 mm a year). Compared with Århus, the nearest covered town with different numbers (101 km away), Aalborg is 0.2 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 39 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (16.9 °C), averages 15.7 °C more than its coolest, January (1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 27 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Aalborg, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Aalborg has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 27 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
