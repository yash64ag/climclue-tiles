Hjørring sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · North Denmark · Denmark

The sea off Hjørring averages 16.9 °C (62 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 4.1 °C (39 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Hjørring sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Hjørring?
In August, at about 16.9 °C (62 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Hjørring?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 4.1 °C (39 °F).
How warm is the sea in Hjørring in March?
About 4.1 °C (39 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Hjørring?
About 9.9 °C (50 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Hjørring nearby?
In August: Björlanda (17.8 °C, 115 km), Västra Frölunda (17.8 °C, 118 km) and Gothenburg (17.8 °C, 121 km).
