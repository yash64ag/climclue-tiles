Holstebro sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Central Jutland · Denmark

The sea off Holstebro averages 17.4 °C (63 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 3.1 °C (38 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Holstebro sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Holstebro?
In August, at about 17.4 °C (63 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Holstebro?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 3.1 °C (38 °F).
How warm is the sea in Holstebro in February?
About 3.1 °C (38 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Holstebro?
About 9.8 °C (50 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Holstebro nearby?
In August: Kolding (18.0 °C, 110 km) and Haderslev (18.0 °C, 135 km).
