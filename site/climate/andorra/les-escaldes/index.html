les Escaldes weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in les Escaldes, Andorra (Escaldes-Engordany), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

les Escaldes is warmest in July, with 23.9 °C (75 °F) days and 12.1 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 3.5 °C (38 °F) days and -3.4 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 45 mm in February to 98 mm in May.

les Escaldes at a glance

Compared with Andorra la Vella, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), les Escaldes is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 21 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.0 °C), averages 17.9 °C more than its coolest, January (0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 131 km from the sea.

Why les Escaldes has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 131 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.9 °C. The 10 km area around les Escaldes, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 5.5 °C a year, 2.7 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for les Escaldes itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (42.507°N, 1.534°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-414-1815), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
