Kakamega weather in January

Average January weather in Kakamega, Kenya: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Kakamega averages 29.1 °C (84 °F) days and 12.6 °C (55 °F) nights, with 80 mm (3.2 in) of rain over the month and about 266 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Kakamega

January is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Kakamega. Compared with December: 0.3 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 0.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Kakamega in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kakamega in January?
In January, Kakamega averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kakamega?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Kakamega’s mild months (January, February and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kakamega in January?
80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is April (232 mm), the driest January (80 mm).
Does it snow in Kakamega in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kakamega get in January?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kakamega in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
