How June compares in Kakamega

June is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Kakamega. Compared with May: 0.5 °C cooler, 78 mm less precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. July is 0.4 °C cooler than June on average.

Kakamega in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kakamega in June?
In June, Kakamega averages a day high of 24.1 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 14.9 °C (59 °F), with 127 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Kakamega?
June is not one of Kakamega’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kakamega in June?
127 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (238 mm), the driest January (86 mm).
Does it snow in Kakamega in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kakamega get in June?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kakamega in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
