How May compares in Kakamega

May is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Kakamega. Compared with April: 0.5 °C cooler, 29 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. June is 0.5 °C cooler than May on average.

Kakamega in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kakamega in May?
In May, Kakamega averages a day high of 24.4 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 203 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kakamega?
May is not one of Kakamega’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kakamega in May?
203 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (232 mm), the driest January (80 mm).
Does it snow in Kakamega in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kakamega get in May?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kakamega in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
