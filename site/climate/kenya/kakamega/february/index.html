How February compares in Kakamega

February is the warmest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kakamega. Compared with January: 0.6 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. March is 0.2 °C cooler than February on average.

Kakamega in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kakamega in February?
In February, Kakamega averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Kakamega?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Kakamega’s mild months (January, February and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kakamega in February?
84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (232 mm), the driest January (80 mm).
Does it snow in Kakamega in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kakamega get in February?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kakamega in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
