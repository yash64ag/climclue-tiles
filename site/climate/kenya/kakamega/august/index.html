How August compares in Kakamega

August is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kakamega. Compared with July: 0.3 °C warmer, 29 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. September is 0.5 °C warmer than August on average.

Kakamega in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kakamega in August?
In August, Kakamega averages a day high of 24.2 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 151 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Kakamega?
August is not one of Kakamega’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kakamega in August?
151 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (238 mm), the driest January (86 mm).
Does it snow in Kakamega in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kakamega get in August?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kakamega in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
