Naivasha at a glance

Among the 28 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kenya, Naivasha is the 3rd coolest (17.0 °C year average) and the least sunny (about 1,975 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Limuru, the nearest covered town with different numbers (50 km away), Naivasha is 1.0 °C warmer in March and 1.5 °C warmer in July, with 77 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (18.4 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, July (15.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.9 °C (52 °F), in July. The area lies about 482 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Naivasha, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Naivasha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 482 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
