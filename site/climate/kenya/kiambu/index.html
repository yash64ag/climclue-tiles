Kiambu at a glance

Among the 28 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kenya, Kiambu is the 5th least sunny (about 2,383 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Karuri, Kiambu County, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Kiambu is 1.4 °C warmer in March and 1.5 °C warmer in July, with 74 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (20.1 °C), averages 3.6 °C more than its coolest, July (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.9 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 422 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kiambu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kiambu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 422 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
