Eldoret at a glance

Among the 28 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kenya, Eldoret is the 5th coolest (17.2 °C year average) and the 5th wettest (1,362 mm a year). Compared with Kitale, the nearest covered town with different numbers (62 km away), Eldoret is 0.7 °C cooler in March and 1.0 °C cooler in July, with 333 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (18.9 °C), averages 3.6 °C more than its coolest, July (15.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.0 °C (52 °F), in July. The area lies about 669 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Eldoret, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Eldoret has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 669 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
