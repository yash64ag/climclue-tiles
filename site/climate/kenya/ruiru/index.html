Ruiru at a glance

Among the 28 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kenya, Ruiru is 15th warmest (19.2 °C year average), 23rd sunniest (about 2,396 hours of sunshine a year) and 18th wettest (909 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Juja, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Ruiru is 0.2 °C cooler in March and 0.4 °C cooler in July, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (20.9 °C), averages 3.6 °C more than its coolest, July (17.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.8 °C (55 °F), in July. The area lies about 413 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ruiru, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ruiru has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 413 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
