Jomvu at a glance

Among the 28 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kenya, Jomvu is the 4th warmest (26.1 °C year average) and the 4th sunniest (about 2,945 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Mombasa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Jomvu is about as warm in March and 0.1 °C cooler in July, with 24 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (28.1 °C), averages 4.0 °C more than its coolest, July (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.8 °C (71 °F), in August. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jomvu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jomvu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
