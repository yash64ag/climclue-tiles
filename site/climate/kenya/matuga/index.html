Matuga at a glance

Among the 28 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kenya, Matuga is the 5th sunniest (about 2,943 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Mombasa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Matuga is 0.1 °C cooler in March and 0.3 °C cooler in August, with 82 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (27.9 °C), averages 4.2 °C more than its coolest, August (23.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.4 °C (71 °F), in August. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Matuga, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Matuga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
