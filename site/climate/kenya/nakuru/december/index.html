How December compares in Nakuru

December is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Nakuru. Compared with November: 0.2 °C warmer, 21 mm less precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. January is 0.7 °C warmer than December on average.

Nakuru in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nakuru in December?
In December, Nakuru averages a day high of 23.7 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 209 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Nakuru?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Nakuru’s mild months (January, February, March, June, July, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nakuru in December?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (123 mm), the driest January (29 mm).
Does it snow in Nakuru in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nakuru get in December?
About 209 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nakuru in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
