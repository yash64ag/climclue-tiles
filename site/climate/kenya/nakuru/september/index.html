How September compares in Nakuru

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Nakuru. Compared with August: 0.8 °C warmer, 26 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. October is 0.5 °C warmer than September on average.

Nakuru in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nakuru in September?
In September, Nakuru averages a day high of 24.1 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Nakuru?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Nakuru’s mild months (January, February, March, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nakuru in September?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (113 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Nakuru in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nakuru get in September?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nakuru in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
