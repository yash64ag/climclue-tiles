Juja at a glance

Among the 28 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kenya, Juja is 13th warmest (19.5 °C year average), 20th sunniest (about 2,443 hours of sunshine a year) and 20th wettest (905 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ruiru, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Juja is 0.3 °C warmer in February and 0.4 °C warmer in July, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (21.1 °C), averages 3.4 °C more than its coolest, July (17.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.1 °C (56 °F), in July. The area lies about 403 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Juja, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Juja has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 403 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
