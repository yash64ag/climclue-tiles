Kitengela at a glance

Among the 120 towns ClimaClue covers in Kenya, Kitengela is 66th warmest (19.5 °C year average), 77th sunniest (about 2,574 hours of sunshine a year) and 98th wettest (775 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Athi River, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Kitengela is 0.3 °C cooler in March and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 21 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (21.3 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, July (17.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.8 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 398 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kitengela, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kitengela has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 398 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kitengela itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (1.476°S, 36.961°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-854-2169), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
