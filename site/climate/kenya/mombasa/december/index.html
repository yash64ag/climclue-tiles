How December compares in Mombasa

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Mombasa. Compared with November: 0.7 °C warmer, 23 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. January is 0.1 °C warmer than December on average.

Mombasa in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mombasa in December?
In December, Mombasa averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 262 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Mombasa?
December is not one of Mombasa’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mombasa in December?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (253 mm), the driest February (12 mm).
Does it snow in Mombasa in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mombasa get in December?
About 262 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mombasa in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
