Why Mombasa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Mombasa climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Mombasa?
March, with an average of 28.1 °C (82 °F), day highs around 31.4 °C (89 °F) and night lows around 25.1 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Mombasa?
August, with an average of 24.1 °C (75 °F), day highs around 26.4 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 21.9 °C (71 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Mombasa?
May, with 245 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,092 mm (43.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Mombasa?
February, with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Mombasa?
November, with about 274 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Mombasa?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Mombasa?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July, August and September.
What type of climate does Mombasa have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Mombasa’s GeoNames location (4.055°S, 39.664°E; ClimaClue area sq-880-2196), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,208,333 (GeoNames).
