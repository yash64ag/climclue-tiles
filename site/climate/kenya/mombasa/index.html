Mombasa at a glance

Among the 28 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kenya, Mombasa is the 3rd warmest (26.2 °C year average). Compared with Jomvu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Mombasa is about as warm in March and 0.1 °C cooler in August, with 24 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (28.1 °C), averages 4.0 °C more than its coolest, August (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.9 °C (71 °F), in August. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mombasa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Mombasa

For mild weather, the best months to visit Mombasa are July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): July, August and September.
  • Warmest (average temperature): March (28.1 °C), February (27.8 °C) and January (27.4 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): February (12 mm), January (26 mm) and March (52 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): November (274 h), March (270 h) and October (266 h).

Why Mombasa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
