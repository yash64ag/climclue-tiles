How November compares in Mombasa

November is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Mombasa. Compared with October: 0.9 °C warmer, 15 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. December is 0.7 °C warmer than November on average.

Mombasa in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mombasa in November?
In November, Mombasa averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.7 °C (75 °F), with 95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 274 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Mombasa?
November is not one of Mombasa’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mombasa in November?
95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (245 mm), the driest February (12 mm).
Does it snow in Mombasa in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mombasa get in November?
About 274 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mombasa in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
