How March compares in Mombasa

March is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Mombasa. Compared with February: 0.3 °C warmer, 40 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. April is 0.9 °C cooler than March on average.

Mombasa in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mombasa in March?
In March, Mombasa averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 270 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Mombasa?
March is not one of Mombasa’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mombasa in March?
52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (245 mm), the driest February (12 mm).
Does it snow in Mombasa in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mombasa get in March?
About 270 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mombasa in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
