How August compares in Mombasa

August is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Mombasa. Compared with July: 0.1 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. September is 0.7 °C warmer than August on average.

Mombasa in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mombasa in August?
In August, Mombasa averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Mombasa?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Mombasa’s mild months (July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mombasa in August?
70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (253 mm), the driest February (12 mm).
Does it snow in Mombasa in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mombasa get in August?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mombasa in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
