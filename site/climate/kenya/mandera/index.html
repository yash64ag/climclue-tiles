Mandera at a glance

Among the 28 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kenya, Mandera is the warmest (29.4 °C year average), the sunniest (about 3,163 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (296 mm a year). Compared with Garissa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (546 km away), Mandera is 1.2 °C warmer in March and about as warm in November, with 53 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (31.6 °C), averages 3.5 °C more than its coolest, November (28.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.5 °C (74 °F), in November. The area lies about 407 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mandera, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mandera has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 407 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
