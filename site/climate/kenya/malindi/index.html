Malindi at a glance

Among the 28 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kenya, Malindi is the 5th warmest (26.1 °C year average) and the 2nd sunniest (about 3,105 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Jomvu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (102 km away), Malindi is 0.4 °C cooler in March and 0.3 °C warmer in August, with 31 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (27.6 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, August (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.1 °C (74 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Malindi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Malindi

For mild weather, the best months to visit Malindi are July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): July, August and September.
  • Warmest (average temperature): March (27.6 °C), April (27.3 °C) and December (27.1 °C).

Why Malindi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
