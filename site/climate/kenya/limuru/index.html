Limuru at a glance

Among the 28 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kenya, Limuru is the coolest (15.7 °C year average) and the 4th least sunny (about 2,378 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Karuri, Kiambu County, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Limuru is 1.3 °C cooler in March and 1.4 °C cooler in July, with 54 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (17.4 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, July (13.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.9 °C (48 °F), in July. The area lies about 442 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Limuru, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Limuru has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 442 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
