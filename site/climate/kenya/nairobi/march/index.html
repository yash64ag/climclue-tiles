How March compares in Nairobi

March is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Nairobi. Compared with February: 0.1 °C warmer, 46 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. April is 0.9 °C cooler than March on average.

Nairobi in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nairobi in March?
In March, Nairobi averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Nairobi?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Nairobi’s mild months (January, February, March, June, July, August, September, October and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nairobi in March?
85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (193 mm), the driest August (18 mm).
Does it snow in Nairobi in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nairobi get in March?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nairobi in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
