How October compares in Nairobi

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Nairobi. Compared with September: 0.9 °C warmer, 41 mm more precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. November is 0.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Nairobi in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nairobi in October?
In October, Nairobi averages a day high of 24.4 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 14.0 °C (57 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Nairobi?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Nairobi’s mild months (January, February, March, June, July, August, September, October and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nairobi in October?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (193 mm), the driest August (18 mm).
Does it snow in Nairobi in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nairobi get in October?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nairobi in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
