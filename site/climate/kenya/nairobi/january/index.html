How January compares in Nairobi

January is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Nairobi. Compared with December: 0.7 °C warmer, 31 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. February is 1.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Nairobi in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nairobi in January?
In January, Nairobi averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 13.0 °C (55 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 282 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Nairobi?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Nairobi’s mild months (January, February, March, June, July, August, September, October and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nairobi in January?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (191 mm), the driest August (18 mm).
Does it snow in Nairobi in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nairobi get in January?
About 282 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nairobi in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
