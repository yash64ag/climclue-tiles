Karuri, Kiambu County at a glance

Among the 120 towns ClimaClue covers in Kenya, Karuri, Kiambu County is 101st warmest (17.3 °C year average), 107th sunniest (about 2,357 hours of sunshine a year) and 71st wettest (973 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kiambu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Karuri, Kiambu County is 1.1 °C cooler in March and 1.2 °C cooler in July, with 34 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (19.1 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, July (15.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.1 °C (50 °F), in July. The area lies about 432 km from the sea.

Why Karuri, Kiambu County has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 432 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-851-2167), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
