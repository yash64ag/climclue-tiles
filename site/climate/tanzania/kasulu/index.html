Kasulu at a glance

Among the 34 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tanzania, Kasulu is the 4th least sunny (about 2,487 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Kigoma, the nearest covered town with different numbers (62 km away), Kasulu is 1.7 °C cooler in September and 2.6 °C cooler in December, with 188 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (23.8 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, December (20.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.5 °C (60 °F), in July. The area lies about 978 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kasulu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Kasulu

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May, June, July, August, September and October.

Why Kasulu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 978 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
