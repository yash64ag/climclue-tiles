Why Kasulu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 978 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Kasulu climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Kasulu?
September, with an average of 23.8 °C (75 °F), day highs around 30.2 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 17.9 °C (64 °F).
What is the coldest month in Kasulu?
December, with an average of 20.9 °C (70 °F), day highs around 25.4 °C (78 °F) and night lows around 16.9 °C (62 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Kasulu?
January, with 153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 943 mm (37.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Kasulu?
July, with 1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Kasulu?
July, with about 290 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Kasulu?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Kasulu?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August, September and October.
What type of climate does Kasulu have?
Tropical savanna (Köppen Aw). Hot all year, with a clear wet season and a clear dry season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Kasulu’s GeoNames location (4.577°S, 30.102°E; ClimaClue area sq-885-2101), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 238,321 (GeoNames).
