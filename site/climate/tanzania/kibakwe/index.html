Kibakwe at a glance

Among the 227 towns ClimaClue covers in Tanzania, Kibakwe is 155th warmest (21.1 °C year average), 162nd sunniest (about 2,660 hours of sunshine a year) and 162nd wettest (872 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mpwapwa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Kibakwe is 0.9 °C cooler in November and 1.3 °C cooler in July, with 273 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (22.9 °C), averages 4.9 °C more than its coolest, July (18.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.1 °C (54 °F), in July. The area lies about 287 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kibakwe, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kibakwe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 287 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.9 °C. The 10 km area around Kibakwe, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 18.8 °C a year, 2.2 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kibakwe itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (6.717°S, 36.367°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-907-2163), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
