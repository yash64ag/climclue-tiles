Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Tingi, Tanzania (Ruvuma), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Tingi at a glance

Among the 227 towns ClimaClue covers in Tanzania, Tingi is 198th warmest (19.2 °C year average), 202nd sunniest (about 2,523 hours of sunshine a year) and 14th wettest (1,592 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mbinga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (41 km away), Tingi is 0.9 °C warmer in November and 0.7 °C warmer in July, with 172 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (22.2 °C), averages 6.7 °C more than its coolest, July (15.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.8 °C (50 °F), in July. The area lies about 547 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tingi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tingi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 547 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.7 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tingi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (11.300°S, 35.033°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-952-2150), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
