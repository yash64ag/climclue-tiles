Kamachumu at a glance

Among the 227 towns ClimaClue covers in Tanzania, Kamachumu is 193rd warmest (19.5 °C year average), 219th sunniest (about 2,275 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (1,689 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nshamba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Kamachumu is 0.5 °C warmer in February and 0.5 °C warmer in November, with 246 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (20.1 °C), averages 1.0 °C more than its coolest, November (19.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.3 °C (56 °F), in July. The area lies about 920 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kamachumu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kamachumu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 920 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.0 °C. The 10 km area around Kamachumu, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 21.0 °C a year, 1.5 °C warmer than the town itself, because it takes in lower ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kamachumu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (1.619°S, 31.620°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-856-2116), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
