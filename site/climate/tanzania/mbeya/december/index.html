How December compares in Mbeya

December is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Mbeya. Compared with November: 0.9 °C cooler, 139 mm more precipitation and 62 hours less sunshine. January is 0.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Mbeya in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mbeya in December?
In December, Mbeya averages a day high of 24.1 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 15.3 °C (60 °F), with 218 mm (8.6 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Mbeya?
December is not one of Mbeya’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mbeya in December?
218 mm (8.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (256 mm), the driest September (4 mm).
Does it snow in Mbeya in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mbeya get in December?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mbeya in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
