Why Mbeya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 650 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Mbeya climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Mbeya?
October, with an average of 18.4 °C (65 °F), day highs around 24.4 °C (76 °F) and night lows around 12.6 °C (55 °F).
What is the coldest month in Mbeya?
July, with an average of 13.8 °C (57 °F), day highs around 20.0 °C (68 °F) and night lows around 8.5 °C (47 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Mbeya?
January, with 261 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,228 mm (48.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Mbeya?
August, with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Mbeya?
July, with about 308 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Mbeya?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Mbeya?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, August, September, October and November.
What type of climate does Mbeya have?
Mediterranean, warm summer (Köppen Csb). Warm, dry summers and mild, wet winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Mbeya’s GeoNames location (8.900°S, 33.450°E; ClimaClue area sq-928-2134), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 541,603 (GeoNames).
