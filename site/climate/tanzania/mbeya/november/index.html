How November compares in Mbeya

November is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Mbeya. Compared with October: 0.1 °C cooler, 59 mm more precipitation and 52 hours less sunshine. December is 0.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Mbeya in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mbeya in November?
In November, Mbeya averages a day high of 26.9 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 14.0 °C (57 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Mbeya?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Mbeya’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mbeya in November?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (256 mm), the driest September (4 mm).
Does it snow in Mbeya in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mbeya get in November?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mbeya in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
