How April compares in Mbeya

April is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Mbeya. Compared with March: 0.3 °C cooler, 87 mm less precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. May is 0.6 °C cooler than April on average.

Mbeya in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mbeya in April?
In April, Mbeya averages a day high of 20.7 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 12.3 °C (54 °F), with 139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Mbeya?
April is not one of Mbeya’s mild months; those are May, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mbeya in April?
139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (261 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Mbeya in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mbeya get in April?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mbeya in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
