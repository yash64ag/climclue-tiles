How July compares in Mbeya

July is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Mbeya. Compared with June: 0.5 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. August is 1.4 °C warmer than July on average.

Mbeya in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mbeya in July?
In July, Mbeya averages a day high of 23.7 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 7.9 °C (46 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 308 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Mbeya?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Mbeya’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mbeya in July?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (256 mm), the driest September (4 mm).
Does it snow in Mbeya in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mbeya get in July?
About 308 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mbeya in July?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
