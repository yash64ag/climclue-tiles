How May compares in Mbeya

May is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Mbeya. Compared with April: 0.6 °C cooler, 100 mm less precipitation and 64 hours more sunshine. June is 1.4 °C cooler than May on average.

Mbeya in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mbeya in May?
In May, Mbeya averages a day high of 20.7 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Mbeya?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Mbeya’s mild months (May, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mbeya in May?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (261 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Mbeya in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mbeya get in May?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mbeya in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
