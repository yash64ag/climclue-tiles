How August compares in Mbeya

August is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Mbeya. Compared with July: 1.3 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. September is 2.0 °C warmer than August on average.

Mbeya in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mbeya in August?
In August, Mbeya averages a day high of 21.7 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 9.5 °C (49 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 307 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Mbeya?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Mbeya’s mild months (May, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mbeya in August?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (261 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Mbeya in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mbeya get in August?
About 307 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mbeya in August?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
