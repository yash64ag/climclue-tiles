How January compares in Mbeya

January is the 6th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Mbeya. Compared with December: 0.8 °C cooler, 34 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. February is 0.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Mbeya in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mbeya in January?
In January, Mbeya averages a day high of 20.9 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 261 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Mbeya?
January is not one of Mbeya’s mild months; those are May, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mbeya in January?
261 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (261 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Mbeya in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mbeya get in January?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mbeya in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
