How February compares in Mbeya

February is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Mbeya. Compared with January: 0.1 °C warmer, 48 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. March is about as warm as February on average.

Mbeya in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mbeya in February?
In February, Mbeya averages a day high of 21.1 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Mbeya?
February is not one of Mbeya’s mild months; those are May, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mbeya in February?
213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (261 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Mbeya in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mbeya get in February?
About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mbeya in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
