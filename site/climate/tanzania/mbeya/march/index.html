How March compares in Mbeya

March is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Mbeya. Compared with February: about as warm, 12 mm more precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. April is 0.3 °C cooler than March on average.

Mbeya in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mbeya in March?
In March, Mbeya averages a day high of 21.1 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 226 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Mbeya?
March is not one of Mbeya’s mild months; those are May, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mbeya in March?
226 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (261 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Mbeya in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mbeya get in March?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mbeya in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
