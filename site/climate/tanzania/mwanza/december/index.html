How December compares in Mwanza

December is the coolest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Mwanza. Compared with November: about as warm, 1 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. January is 0.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Mwanza in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mwanza in December?
In December, Mwanza averages a day high of 24.1 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 142 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Mwanza?
December is not one of Mwanza’s mild months; those are February, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mwanza in December?
142 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (161 mm), the driest July (6 mm).
Does it snow in Mwanza in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mwanza get in December?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mwanza in December?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
