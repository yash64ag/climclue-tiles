Mwanza at a glance

Among the 34 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tanzania, Mwanza is 18th warmest (22.7 °C year average), 16th sunniest (about 2,789 hours of sunshine a year) and 17th wettest (1,049 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sengerema, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Mwanza is 0.3 °C warmer in September and 0.5 °C warmer in November, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (23.1 °C), averages 0.9 °C more than its coolest, November (22.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.8 °C (66 °F), in July. The area lies about 748 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mwanza, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mwanza has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 748 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 0.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
