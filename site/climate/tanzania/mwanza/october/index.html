How October compares in Mwanza

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Mwanza. Compared with September: 0.4 °C cooler, 58 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. November is 0.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Mwanza in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mwanza in October?
In October, Mwanza averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Mwanza?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Mwanza’s mild months (February, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mwanza in October?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (161 mm), the driest July (6 mm).
Does it snow in Mwanza in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mwanza get in October?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mwanza in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
