How August compares in Mwanza

August is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Mwanza. Compared with July: 0.3 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. September is 0.3 °C warmer than August on average.

Mwanza in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mwanza in August?
In August, Mwanza averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 19.3 °C (67 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 265 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Mwanza?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Mwanza’s mild months (February, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mwanza in August?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (161 mm), the driest July (6 mm).
Does it snow in Mwanza in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mwanza get in August?
About 265 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mwanza in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
