How March compares in Mwanza

March is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Mwanza. Compared with February: about as warm, 54 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. April is 0.3 °C cooler than March on average.

Mwanza in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mwanza in March?
In March, Mwanza averages a day high of 25.4 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Mwanza?
March is not one of Mwanza’s mild months; those are February, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mwanza in March?
149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (161 mm), the driest July (6 mm).
Does it snow in Mwanza in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mwanza get in March?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mwanza in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
