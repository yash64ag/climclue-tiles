How November compares in Mwanza

November is the coolest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Mwanza. Compared with October: 0.4 °C cooler, 47 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. December is about as warm as November on average.

Mwanza in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mwanza in November?
In November, Mwanza averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 18.1 °C (65 °F), with 133 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Mwanza?
November is not one of Mwanza’s mild months; those are February, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mwanza in November?
133 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (152 mm), the driest July (7 mm).
Does it snow in Mwanza in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mwanza get in November?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mwanza in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
