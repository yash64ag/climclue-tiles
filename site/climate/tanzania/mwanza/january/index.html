How January compares in Mwanza

January is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Mwanza. Compared with December: 0.2 °C warmer, 27 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. February is 0.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Mwanza in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mwanza in January?
In January, Mwanza averages a day high of 24.5 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 19.6 °C (67 °F), with 115 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Mwanza?
January is not one of Mwanza’s mild months; those are February, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mwanza in January?
115 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (161 mm), the driest July (6 mm).
Does it snow in Mwanza in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mwanza get in January?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mwanza in January?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
