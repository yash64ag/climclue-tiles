How September compares in Mwanza

September is the warmest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Mwanza. Compared with August: 0.4 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. October is 0.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Mwanza in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mwanza in September?
In September, Mwanza averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Mwanza?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Mwanza’s mild months (February, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mwanza in September?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (152 mm), the driest July (7 mm).
Does it snow in Mwanza in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mwanza get in September?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mwanza in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
