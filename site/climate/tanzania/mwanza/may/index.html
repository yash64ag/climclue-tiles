How May compares in Mwanza

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Mwanza. Compared with April: 0.2 °C warmer, 80 mm less precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. June is 0.1 °C cooler than May on average.

Mwanza in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mwanza in May?
In May, Mwanza averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 19.9 °C (68 °F), with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Mwanza?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Mwanza’s mild months (February, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mwanza in May?
81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (161 mm), the driest July (6 mm).
Does it snow in Mwanza in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mwanza get in May?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mwanza in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
