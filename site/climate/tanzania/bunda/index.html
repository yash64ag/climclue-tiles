Why Bunda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 680 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Bunda climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Bunda?
September, with an average of 23.9 °C (75 °F), day highs around 28.5 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 19.5 °C (67 °F).
What is the coldest month in Bunda?
December, with an average of 22.6 °C (73 °F), day highs around 26.1 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 18.6 °C (65 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Bunda?
April, with 147 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 873 mm (34.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Bunda?
July, with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Bunda?
June, with about 251 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Bunda?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Bunda?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, May, June, July, August, September, October and December.
What type of climate does Bunda have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Bunda’s GeoNames location (2.022°S, 33.871°E; ClimaClue area sq-860-2138), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 182,970 (GeoNames).
