Bunda at a glance

Among the 34 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tanzania, Bunda is 11th warmest (23.2 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 2,832 hours of sunshine a year) and 28th wettest (873 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Musoma, the nearest covered town with different numbers (59 km away), Bunda is 0.3 °C warmer in September and 0.2 °C cooler in December, with 41 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (23.9 °C), averages 1.3 °C more than its coolest, December (22.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.4 °C (65 °F), in July. The area lies about 680 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bunda, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bunda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 680 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
