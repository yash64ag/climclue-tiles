Singida at a glance

Among the 34 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tanzania, Singida is the driest (651 mm a year). Compared with Nzega, the nearest covered town with different numbers (185 km away), Singida is 1.5 °C cooler in November and 3.8 °C cooler in July, with 270 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (22.3 °C), averages 3.8 °C more than its coolest, July (18.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.8 °C (55 °F), in July. The area lies about 473 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Singida, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Singida has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 473 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
