Geita at a glance

Among the 34 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tanzania, Geita is 25th warmest (21.3 °C year average), 24th sunniest (about 2,706 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (1,170 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sengerema, the nearest covered town with different numbers (52 km away), Geita is 0.6 °C cooler in August and 1.2 °C cooler in December, with 120 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (22.1 °C), averages 1.7 °C more than its coolest, December (20.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.2 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 808 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Geita, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Geita has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 808 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
