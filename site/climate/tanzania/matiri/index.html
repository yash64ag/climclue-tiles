Matiri at a glance

Among the 227 towns ClimaClue covers in Tanzania, Matiri is 197th warmest (19.3 °C year average), 196th sunniest (about 2,541 hours of sunshine a year) and 12th wettest (1,657 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kigonsera, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Matiri is 0.5 °C cooler in November and 0.4 °C cooler in July, with 422 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (22.1 °C), averages 6.3 °C more than its coolest, July (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.9 °C (50 °F), in July. The area lies about 545 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Matiri, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Matiri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 545 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Matiri itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (10.750°S, 34.900°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-947-2149), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
