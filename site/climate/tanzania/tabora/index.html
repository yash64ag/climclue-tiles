Tabora at a glance

Among the 34 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tanzania, Tabora is the sunniest (about 2,968 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Nzega, the nearest covered town with different numbers (97 km away), Tabora is 0.5 °C warmer in October and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 26 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (25.8 °C), averages 3.5 °C more than its coolest, January (22.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.5 °C (62 °F), in July. The area lies about 673 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tabora, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tabora has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 673 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
