Tarime at a glance

Among the 34 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tanzania, Tarime is the 5th sunniest (about 2,909 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Musoma, the nearest covered town with different numbers (65 km away), Tarime is 1.3 °C cooler in September and 1.9 °C cooler in December, with 180 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (22.4 °C), averages 1.4 °C more than its coolest, December (20.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.8 °C (60 °F), in July. The area lies about 672 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tarime, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tarime has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 672 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
