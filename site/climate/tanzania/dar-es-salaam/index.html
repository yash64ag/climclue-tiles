Dar es Salaam at a glance

Among the 34 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tanzania, Dar es Salaam is the 3rd warmest (26.0 °C year average). Compared with Kibaha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Dar es Salaam is 0.4 °C warmer in February and 0.8 °C warmer in July, with 5 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.8 °C), averages 3.7 °C more than its coolest, July (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.7 °C (71 °F), in August. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dar es Salaam, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Dar es Salaam

For mild weather, the best months to visit Dar es Salaam are June, July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July, August and September.
  • Warmest (average temperature): February (27.8 °C), January (27.4 °C) and March (27.4 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): September (19 mm), July (20 mm) and August (27 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): October (261 h), November (255 h) and September (246 h).

Why Dar es Salaam has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
