How April compares in Dar es Salaam

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Dar es Salaam. Compared with March: 1.0 °C cooler, 99 mm more precipitation and 43 hours less sunshine. May is 0.8 °C cooler than April on average.

Dar es Salaam in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dar es Salaam in April?
In April, Dar es Salaam averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 263 mm (10.4 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Dar es Salaam?
April is not one of Dar es Salaam’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dar es Salaam in April?
263 mm (10.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is April (263 mm), the driest September (19 mm).
Does it snow in Dar es Salaam in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dar es Salaam get in April?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dar es Salaam in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
