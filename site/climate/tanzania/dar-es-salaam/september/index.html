How September compares in Dar es Salaam

September is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Dar es Salaam. Compared with August: 0.7 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. October is 0.9 °C warmer than September on average.

Dar es Salaam in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dar es Salaam in September?
In September, Dar es Salaam averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Dar es Salaam?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Dar es Salaam’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dar es Salaam in September?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the driest month of the year. The wettest month is April (263 mm), the driest September (19 mm).
Does it snow in Dar es Salaam in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dar es Salaam get in September?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dar es Salaam in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
