Nzega at a glance

Among the 34 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tanzania, Nzega is the 3rd sunniest (about 2,925 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Shinyanga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Nzega is 0.8 °C cooler in October and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 181 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (25.3 °C), averages 3.1 °C more than its coolest, January (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.5 °C (62 °F), in July. The area lies about 662 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nzega, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nzega has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 662 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
