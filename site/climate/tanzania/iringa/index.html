Iringa at a glance

Among the 34 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tanzania, Iringa is the 4th coolest (18.3 °C year average) and the 2nd driest (657 mm a year). Compared with Ifakara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (116 km away), Iringa is 7.9 °C cooler in November and 6.0 °C cooler in July, with 616 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (20.1 °C), averages 4.3 °C more than its coolest, July (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.2 °C (52 °F), in July. The area lies about 386 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Iringa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Iringa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 386 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
