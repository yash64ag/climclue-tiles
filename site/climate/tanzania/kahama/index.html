Kahama at a glance

Among the 34 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tanzania, Kahama is 13th warmest (23.1 °C year average), 7th sunniest (about 2,865 hours of sunshine a year) and 27th wettest (897 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nzega, the nearest covered town with different numbers (77 km away), Kahama is 0.1 °C warmer in September and about as warm in January, with 25 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (24.9 °C), averages 2.7 °C more than its coolest, January (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.1 °C (63 °F), in July. The area lies about 729 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kahama, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kahama has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 729 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
