Mtwara at a glance

Among the 34 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tanzania, Mtwara is the 4th warmest (25.8 °C year average) and the 4th sunniest (about 2,912 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Dar es Salaam, the nearest covered town with different numbers (396 km away), Mtwara is 0.7 °C cooler in February and about as warm in August, with 165 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.1 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, August (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.8 °C (71 °F), in July. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mtwara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mtwara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
