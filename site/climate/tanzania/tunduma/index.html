Tunduma at a glance

Among the 34 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tanzania, Tunduma is the 4th wettest (1,446 mm a year). Compared with Mbeya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (87 km away), Tunduma is 3.8 °C warmer in October and 3.0 °C warmer in July, with 217 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (22.3 °C), averages 5.4 °C more than its coolest, July (16.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.7 °C (53 °F), in July. The area lies about 731 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tunduma, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tunduma has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 731 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
