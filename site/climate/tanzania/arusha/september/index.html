How September compares in Arusha

September is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Arusha. Compared with August: 1.8 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. October is 1.5 °C warmer than September on average.

Arusha in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Arusha in September?
In September, Arusha averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 12.2 °C (54 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Arusha?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Arusha’s mild months (January, February, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Arusha in September?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the driest month of the year. The wettest month is April (229 mm), the driest September (8 mm).
Does it snow in Arusha in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Arusha get in September?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Arusha in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
