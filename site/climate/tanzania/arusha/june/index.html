How June compares in Arusha

June is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Arusha. Compared with May: 1.8 °C cooler, 98 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. July is 0.8 °C cooler than June on average.

Arusha in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Arusha in June?
In June, Arusha averages a day high of 20.9 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 11.9 °C (53 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Arusha?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Arusha’s mild months (January, February, June, July, August, September, October and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Arusha in June?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (241 mm), the driest September (9 mm).
Does it snow in Arusha in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Arusha get in June?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Arusha in June?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
