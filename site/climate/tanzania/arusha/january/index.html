How January compares in Arusha

January is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Arusha. Compared with December: 0.4 °C warmer, 34 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. February is 0.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Arusha in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Arusha in January?
In January, Arusha averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 14.7 °C (58 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 258 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Arusha?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Arusha’s mild months (January, February, June, July, August, September, October and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Arusha in January?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (241 mm), the driest September (9 mm).
Does it snow in Arusha in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Arusha get in January?
About 258 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Arusha in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
