How December compares in Arusha

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Arusha. Compared with November: 0.1 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. January is 0.4 °C warmer than December on average.

Arusha in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Arusha in December?
In December, Arusha averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Arusha?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Arusha’s mild months (January, February, June, July, August, September, October and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Arusha in December?
100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (241 mm), the driest September (9 mm).
Does it snow in Arusha in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Arusha get in December?
About 232 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Arusha in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
