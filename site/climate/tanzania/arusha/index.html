Arusha at a glance

Among the 34 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tanzania, Arusha is 29th warmest (19.0 °C year average), 27th sunniest (about 2,639 hours of sunshine a year) and 26th wettest (910 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Moshi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (72 km away), Arusha is 3.9 °C cooler in February and 4.3 °C cooler in July, with 209 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (21.4 °C), averages 5.7 °C more than its coolest, July (15.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.8 °C (51 °F), in July. The area lies about 338 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Arusha, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Arusha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 338 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
