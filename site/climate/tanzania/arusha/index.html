Why Arusha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 338 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Arusha climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Arusha?
February, with an average of 21.4 °C (70 °F), day highs around 27.3 °C (81 °F) and night lows around 15.0 °C (59 °F).
What is the coldest month in Arusha?
July, with an average of 15.7 °C (60 °F), day highs around 20.4 °C (69 °F) and night lows around 10.8 °C (51 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Arusha?
April, with 241 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 910 mm (35.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Arusha?
September, with 9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Arusha?
October, with about 262 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Arusha?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Arusha?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, June, July, August, September, October and December.
What type of climate does Arusha have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 11 × 11 km, that contains Arusha’s GeoNames location (3.367°S, 36.683°E; ClimaClue area sq-873-2166), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 617,631 (GeoNames).
