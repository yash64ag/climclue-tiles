How January compares in Dodoma

January is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Dodoma. Compared with December: 0.7 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. February is 0.1 °C cooler than January on average.

Dodoma in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dodoma in January?
In January, Dodoma averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 18.7 °C (66 °F), with 141 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Dodoma?
January is not one of Dodoma’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dodoma in January?
141 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (159 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Dodoma in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dodoma get in January?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dodoma in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
