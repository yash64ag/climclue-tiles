Dodoma at a glance

Among the 34 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tanzania, Dodoma is the 3rd driest (663 mm a year). Compared with Iringa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (177 km away), Dodoma is 4.5 °C warmer in November and 4.4 °C warmer in July, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (24.6 °C), averages 4.4 °C more than its coolest, July (20.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.4 °C (58 °F), in July. The area lies about 343 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dodoma, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dodoma has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 343 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
