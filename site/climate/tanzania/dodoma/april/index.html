How April compares in Dodoma

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Dodoma. Compared with March: 0.4 °C cooler, 60 mm less precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. May is 0.5 °C cooler than April on average.

Dodoma in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dodoma in April?
In April, Dodoma averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 18.5 °C (65 °F), with 62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Dodoma?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Dodoma’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dodoma in April?
62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (159 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Dodoma in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dodoma get in April?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dodoma in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
