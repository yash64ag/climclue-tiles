How December compares in Dodoma

December is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Dodoma. Compared with November: 0.4 °C cooler, 120 mm more precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. January is 0.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Dodoma in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dodoma in December?
In December, Dodoma averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 18.9 °C (66 °F), with 159 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Dodoma?
December is not one of Dodoma’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dodoma in December?
159 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (159 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Dodoma in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dodoma get in December?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dodoma in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
