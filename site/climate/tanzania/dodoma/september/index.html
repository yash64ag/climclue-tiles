Dodoma in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dodoma in September?
In September, Dodoma averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 16.1 °C (61 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Dodoma?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Dodoma’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dodoma in September?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (159 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Dodoma in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dodoma get in September?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dodoma in September?
