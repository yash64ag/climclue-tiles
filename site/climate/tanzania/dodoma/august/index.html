Dodoma in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dodoma in August?
In August, Dodoma averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Dodoma?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Dodoma’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
