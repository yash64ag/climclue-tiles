How November compares in Dodoma

November is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Dodoma. Compared with October: 0.8 °C warmer, 37 mm more precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. December is 0.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Dodoma in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dodoma in November?
In November, Dodoma averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 250 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Dodoma?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Dodoma’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dodoma in November?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (164 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Dodoma in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dodoma get in November?
About 250 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dodoma in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
