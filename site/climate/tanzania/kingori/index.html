Kingori at a glance

Among the 227 towns ClimaClue covers in Tanzania, Kingori is 177th warmest (20.3 °C year average), 181st sunniest (about 2,600 hours of sunshine a year) and 59th wettest (1,148 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Usa River, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Kingori is 0.7 °C cooler in February and about as warm in July, with 300 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (22.5 °C), averages 5.3 °C more than its coolest, July (17.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.1 °C (54 °F), in July. The area lies about 314 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kingori, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kingori has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 314 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.3 °C. The 10 km area around Kingori, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 19.1 °C a year, 1.2 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kingori itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (3.283°S, 36.983°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-872-2169), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
