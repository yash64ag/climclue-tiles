Mwandiga at a glance

Among the 227 towns ClimaClue covers in Tanzania, Mwandiga is 57th warmest (24.1 °C year average), 192nd sunniest (about 2,573 hours of sunshine a year) and 55th wettest (1,177 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kigoma, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Mwandiga is 0.3 °C cooler in September and 0.3 °C cooler in December, with 85 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (25.4 °C), averages 2.0 °C more than its coolest, December (23.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.7 °C (64 °F), in July. The area lies about 1,027 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mwandiga, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mwandiga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 1,027 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Mwandiga itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (4.830°S, 29.658°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-888-2096), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
