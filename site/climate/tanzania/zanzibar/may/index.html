How May compares in Zanzibar

May is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Zanzibar. Compared with April: 0.7 °C cooler, 109 mm less precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. June is 0.8 °C cooler than May on average.

Zanzibar in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zanzibar in May?
In May, Zanzibar averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 24.4 °C (76 °F), with 249 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Zanzibar?
May is not one of Zanzibar’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zanzibar in May?
249 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (358 mm), the driest September (40 mm).
Does it snow in Zanzibar in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zanzibar get in May?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zanzibar in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
