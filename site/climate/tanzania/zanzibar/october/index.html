How October compares in Zanzibar

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Zanzibar. Compared with September: 0.8 °C warmer, 61 mm more precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. November is 0.8 °C warmer than October on average.

Zanzibar in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zanzibar in October?
In October, Zanzibar averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Zanzibar?
October is not one of Zanzibar’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zanzibar in October?
100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (357 mm), the driest September (40 mm).
Does it snow in Zanzibar in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zanzibar get in October?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zanzibar in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
