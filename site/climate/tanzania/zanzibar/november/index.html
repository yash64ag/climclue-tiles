How November compares in Zanzibar

November is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Zanzibar. Compared with October: 0.8 °C warmer, 65 mm more precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. December is 0.5 °C warmer than November on average.

Zanzibar in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zanzibar in November?
In November, Zanzibar averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 165 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Zanzibar?
November is not one of Zanzibar’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zanzibar in November?
165 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (357 mm), the driest September (40 mm).
Does it snow in Zanzibar in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zanzibar get in November?
About 232 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zanzibar in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
