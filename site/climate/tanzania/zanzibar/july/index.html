Zanzibar weather in July

Average July weather in Zanzibar, Tanzania: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Zanzibar averages 28.9 °C (84 °F) days and 20.3 °C (69 °F) nights, with 45 mm (1.8 in) of rain over the month and about 233 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Zanzibar

July is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Zanzibar. Compared with June: 0.6 °C cooler, 24 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. August is 0.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Zanzibar in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zanzibar in July?
In July, Zanzibar averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Zanzibar?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Zanzibar’s mild months (July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zanzibar in July?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (358 mm), the driest September (40 mm).
Does it snow in Zanzibar in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zanzibar get in July?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Zanzibar in July?
About 25.8 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (29.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Zanzibar in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
