Zanzibar at a glance

Among the 34 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tanzania, Zanzibar is the warmest (26.2 °C year average) and the 3rd wettest (1,557 mm a year). Compared with Dar es Salaam, the nearest covered town with different numbers (74 km away), Zanzibar is about as warm in February and 0.5 °C warmer in July, with 415 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (27.8 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, July (24.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.8 °C (73 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Zanzibar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Zanzibar

For mild weather, the best months to visit Zanzibar are July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): July and August.
  • Warmest (average temperature): February (27.8 °C), March (27.6 °C) and January (27.4 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): September (40 mm), July (44 mm) and August (48 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): October (255 h), January (254 h) and December (242 h).

Why Zanzibar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
