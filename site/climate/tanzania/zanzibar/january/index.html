How January compares in Zanzibar

January is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Zanzibar. Compared with December: 0.2 °C warmer, 92 mm less precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. February is 0.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Zanzibar in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zanzibar in January?
In January, Zanzibar averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 26.2 °C (79 °F), with 83 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Zanzibar?
January is not one of Zanzibar’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zanzibar in January?
83 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (357 mm), the driest September (40 mm).
Does it snow in Zanzibar in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zanzibar get in January?
About 254 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zanzibar in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
