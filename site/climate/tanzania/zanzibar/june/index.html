How June compares in Zanzibar

June is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Zanzibar. Compared with May: 0.8 °C cooler, 181 mm less precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. July is 0.6 °C cooler than June on average.

Zanzibar in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zanzibar in June?
In June, Zanzibar averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Zanzibar?
June is not one of Zanzibar’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zanzibar in June?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (358 mm), the driest September (40 mm).
Does it snow in Zanzibar in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zanzibar get in June?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Zanzibar in June?
About 26.8 °C (80 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (29.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Zanzibar in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
