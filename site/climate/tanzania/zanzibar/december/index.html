How December compares in Zanzibar

December is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Zanzibar. Compared with November: 0.6 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. January is 0.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Zanzibar in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zanzibar in December?
In December, Zanzibar averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 175 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 242 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Zanzibar?
December is not one of Zanzibar’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zanzibar in December?
175 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (358 mm), the driest September (40 mm).
Does it snow in Zanzibar in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zanzibar get in December?
About 242 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Zanzibar in December?
About 28.4 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is March (29.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Zanzibar in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
