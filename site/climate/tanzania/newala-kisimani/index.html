Newala Kisimani at a glance

Among the 227 towns ClimaClue covers in Tanzania, Newala Kisimani is 125th warmest (22.5 °C year average), 131st sunniest (about 2,725 hours of sunshine a year) and 117th wettest (988 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Newala, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Newala Kisimani is 0.2 °C cooler in December and 0.3 °C cooler in July, with 101 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, December (24.1 °C), averages 4.2 °C more than its coolest, July (19.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 14.6 °C (58 °F), in July. The area lies about 133 km from the sea.

Why Newala Kisimani has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 133 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
