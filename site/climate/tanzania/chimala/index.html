Chimala at a glance

Among the 227 towns ClimaClue covers in Tanzania, Chimala is the 2nd wettest (2,108 mm a year). Compared with Igurusi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Chimala is 0.7 °C warmer in November and 0.9 °C warmer in July, with 577 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, November (24.1 °C), averages 4.2 °C more than its coolest, July (19.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.9 °C (55 °F), in July. The area lies about 584 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chimala, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chimala has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 584 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Chimala itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (8.856°S, 34.024°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-928-2140), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
