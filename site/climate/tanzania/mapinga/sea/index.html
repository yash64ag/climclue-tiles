Mapinga sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Pwani · Tanzania

The sea off Mapinga averages 29.0 °C (84 °F) in March, its warmest month, and 25.4 °C (78 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Mapinga sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Mapinga?
In March, at about 29.0 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Mapinga?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is August, at 25.4 °C (78 °F).
How warm is the sea in Mapinga in August?
About 25.4 °C (78 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Mapinga?
About 27.4 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
