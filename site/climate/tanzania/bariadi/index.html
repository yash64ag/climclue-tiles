Bariadi at a glance

Among the 34 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Tanzania, Bariadi is 16th warmest (23.0 °C year average), 9th sunniest (about 2,849 hours of sunshine a year) and 29th wettest (842 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bunda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (87 km away), Bariadi is 0.5 °C warmer in September and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 32 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, September (24.4 °C), averages 2.1 °C more than its coolest, January (22.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.8 °C (62 °F), in July. The area lies about 637 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bariadi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bariadi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 637 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
