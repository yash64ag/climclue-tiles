Why Manzini has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 147 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Manzini climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Manzini?
January, with an average of 22.4 °C (72 °F), day highs around 26.5 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 17.8 °C (64 °F).
What is the coldest month in Manzini?
July, with an average of 13.8 °C (57 °F), day highs around 20.4 °C (69 °F) and night lows around 7.8 °C (46 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Manzini?
February, with 157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 945 mm (37.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Manzini?
July, with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Manzini?
July, with about 265 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Manzini?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Manzini?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, August, September and October.
What type of climate does Manzini have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10.0 × 11 km, that contains Manzini’s GeoNames location (26.499°S, 31.380°E; ClimaClue area sq-1104-2113), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 110,537 (GeoNames).
