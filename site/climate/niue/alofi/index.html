Alofi at a glance

Its warmest month, February (26.4 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, August (23.3 °C). The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Best time to visit Alofi

No month in Alofi is mild by the rule below; the closest in temperature are June (24.3 °C, 131 mm), July (23.4 °C, 174 mm) and August (23.3 °C, 137 mm).

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): no month.
  • Warmest (average temperature): February (26.4 °C), March (26.4 °C) and January (26.1 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): June (131 mm), August (137 mm) and July (174 mm).

Climate only: these rules use 1981–2010 monthly averages and do not consider crowds, prices, events or holidays; any one year can differ.

Why Alofi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
