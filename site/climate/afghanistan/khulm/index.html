Khulm at a glance

Among the 57 towns ClimaClue covers in Afghanistan, Khulm is 15th warmest (18.2 °C year average), 40th sunniest (about 2,818 hours of sunshine a year) and 40th wettest (300 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mazār-e Sharīf, the nearest covered town with different numbers (52 km away), Khulm is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 80 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (31.5 °C), averages 26.6 °C more than its coolest, January (4.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 1,222 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Khulm, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Khulm has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,222 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.6 °C. The 10 km area around Khulm, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 14.7 °C a year, 3.5 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Khulm itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.697°N, 67.698°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-473-2476), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
