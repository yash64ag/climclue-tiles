Khān Neshīn at a glance

Among the 57 towns ClimaClue covers in Afghanistan, Khān Neshīn is the warmest (23.5 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 3,293 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (64 mm a year). Compared with Lashkar Gāh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (129 km away), Khān Neshīn is 0.8 °C warmer in July and 1.9 °C warmer in January, with 89 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (36.0 °C), averages 26.3 °C more than its coolest, January (9.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 589 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Khān Neshīn, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Khān Neshīn has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 589 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Khān Neshīn itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (30.549°N, 63.789°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-534-2437), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
