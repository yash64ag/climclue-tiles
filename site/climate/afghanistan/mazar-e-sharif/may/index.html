Mazār-e Sharīf in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mazār-e Sharīf in May?
In May, Mazār-e Sharīf averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 305 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Mazār-e Sharīf?
May is not one of Mazār-e Sharīf’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mazār-e Sharīf in May?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (50 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Mazār-e Sharīf in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mazār-e Sharīf get in May?
About 305 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mazār-e Sharīf in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
