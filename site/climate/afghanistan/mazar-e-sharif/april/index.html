Mazār-e Sharīf in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mazār-e Sharīf in April?
In April, Mazār-e Sharīf averages a day high of 24.1 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 12.9 °C (55 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Mazār-e Sharīf?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Mazār-e Sharīf’s mild months (April, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mazār-e Sharīf in April?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (50 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Mazār-e Sharīf in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mazār-e Sharīf get in April?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mazār-e Sharīf in April?
