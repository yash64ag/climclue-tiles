How March compares in Mazār-e Sharīf

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Mazār-e Sharīf. Compared with February: 5.3 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. April is 6.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Mazār-e Sharīf in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mazār-e Sharīf in March?
In March, Mazār-e Sharīf averages a day high of 16.8 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 6.8 °C (44 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Mazār-e Sharīf?
March is not one of Mazār-e Sharīf’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mazār-e Sharīf in March?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (50 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Mazār-e Sharīf in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.6 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Mazār-e Sharīf get in March?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mazār-e Sharīf in March?
