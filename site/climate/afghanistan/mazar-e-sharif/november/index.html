How November compares in Mazār-e Sharīf

Mazār-e Sharīf in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mazār-e Sharīf in November?
In November, Mazār-e Sharīf averages a day high of 16.8 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 7.2 °C (45 °F), with 17 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Mazār-e Sharīf?
November is not one of Mazār-e Sharīf’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mazār-e Sharīf in November?
17 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (50 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Mazār-e Sharīf in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mazār-e Sharīf get in November?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mazār-e Sharīf in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
