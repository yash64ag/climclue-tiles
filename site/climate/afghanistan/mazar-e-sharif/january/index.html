How January compares in Mazār-e Sharīf

January is the coolest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Mazār-e Sharīf. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, 6 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. February is 2.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Mazār-e Sharīf in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mazār-e Sharīf in January?
In January, Mazār-e Sharīf averages a day high of 9.2 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 0.8 °C (33 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 126 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Mazār-e Sharīf?
January is not one of Mazār-e Sharīf’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mazār-e Sharīf in January?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (50 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Mazār-e Sharīf in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.9 cm (3.1 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Mazār-e Sharīf get in January?
About 126 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mazār-e Sharīf in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
