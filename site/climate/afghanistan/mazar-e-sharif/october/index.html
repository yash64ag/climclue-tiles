Mazār-e Sharīf in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mazār-e Sharīf in October?
In October, Mazār-e Sharīf averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 12.3 °C (54 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Mazār-e Sharīf?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Mazār-e Sharīf’s mild months (April, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mazār-e Sharīf in October?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (50 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Mazār-e Sharīf in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mazār-e Sharīf get in October?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
