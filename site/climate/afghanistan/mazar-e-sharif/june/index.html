Mazār-e Sharīf in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mazār-e Sharīf in June?
In June, Mazār-e Sharīf averages a day high of 36.0 °C (97 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 356 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Mazār-e Sharīf?
June is not one of Mazār-e Sharīf’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mazār-e Sharīf in June?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (50 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Mazār-e Sharīf in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mazār-e Sharīf get in June?
About 356 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
