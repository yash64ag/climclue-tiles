How December compares in Mazār-e Sharīf

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Mazār-e Sharīf. Compared with November: 5.0 °C cooler, 10 mm more precipitation and 48 hours less sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Mazār-e Sharīf in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mazār-e Sharīf in December?
In December, Mazār-e Sharīf averages a day high of 11.8 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 1.0 °C (34 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 129 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Mazār-e Sharīf?
December is not one of Mazār-e Sharīf’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mazār-e Sharīf in December?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (51 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Mazār-e Sharīf in December?
Yes, on average: about 1.8 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Mazār-e Sharīf get in December?
About 129 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mazār-e Sharīf in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
