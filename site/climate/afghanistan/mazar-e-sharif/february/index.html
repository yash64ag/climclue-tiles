How February compares in Mazār-e Sharīf

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Mazār-e Sharīf. Compared with January: 2.0 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. March is 5.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Mazār-e Sharīf in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mazār-e Sharīf in February?
In February, Mazār-e Sharīf averages a day high of 11.2 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 2.1 °C (36 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Mazār-e Sharīf?
February is not one of Mazār-e Sharīf’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mazār-e Sharīf in February?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (50 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Mazār-e Sharīf in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.5 cm (1.8 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Mazār-e Sharīf get in February?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mazār-e Sharīf in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
