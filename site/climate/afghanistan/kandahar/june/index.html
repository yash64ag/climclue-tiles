How June compares in Kandahār

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Kandahār. Compared with May: 4.3 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. July is 1.6 °C warmer than June on average.

Kandahār in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kandahār in June?
In June, Kandahār averages a day high of 38.2 °C (101 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 356 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Kandahār?
June is not one of Kandahār’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kandahār in June?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (69 mm), the driest September (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kandahār in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kandahār get in June?
About 356 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kandahār in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
