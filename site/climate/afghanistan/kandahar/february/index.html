How February compares in Kandahār

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Kandahār. Compared with January: 2.2 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. March is 5.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Kandahār in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kandahār in February?
In February, Kandahār averages a day high of 13.8 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 1.9 °C (35 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Kandahār?
February is not one of Kandahār’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kandahār in February?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (69 mm), the driest September (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kandahār in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.5 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kandahār get in February?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kandahār in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
