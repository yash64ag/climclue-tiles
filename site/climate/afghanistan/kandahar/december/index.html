How December compares in Kandahār

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Kandahār. Compared with November: 5.2 °C cooler, 24 mm more precipitation and 46 hours less sunshine. January is 1.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Kandahār in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kandahār in December?
In December, Kandahār averages a day high of 15.8 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 1.2 °C (34 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kandahār?
December is not one of Kandahār’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kandahār in December?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (64 mm), the driest September (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kandahār in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kandahār get in December?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kandahār in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
