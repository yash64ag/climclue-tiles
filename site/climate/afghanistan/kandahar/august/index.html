How August compares in Kandahār

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kandahār. Compared with July: 1.8 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. September is 5.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Kandahār in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kandahār in August?
In August, Kandahār averages a day high of 37.8 °C (100 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 330 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Kandahār?
August is not one of Kandahār’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
