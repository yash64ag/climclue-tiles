How January compares in Kandahār

January is the coolest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kandahār. Compared with December: 1.9 °C cooler, 17 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. February is 2.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Kandahār in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kandahār in January?
In January, Kandahār averages a day high of 11.4 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 0.4 °C (33 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kandahār?
January is not one of Kandahār’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kandahār in January?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (69 mm), the driest September (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kandahār in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.2 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kandahār get in January?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kandahār in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
