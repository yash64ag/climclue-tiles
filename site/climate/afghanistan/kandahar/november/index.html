How November compares in Kandahār

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Kandahār. Compared with October: 6.1 °C cooler, 6 mm more precipitation and 36 hours less sunshine. December is 5.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Kandahār in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kandahār in November?
In November, Kandahār averages a day high of 20.2 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 6.1 °C (43 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 257 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kandahār?
November is not one of Kandahār’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kandahār in November?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (69 mm), the driest September (0 mm).
Does it snow in Kandahār in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kandahār get in November?
About 257 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kandahār in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
