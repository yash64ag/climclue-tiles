Shīnḏanḏ at a glance

Among the 57 towns ClimaClue covers in Afghanistan, Shīnḏanḏ is 13th warmest (18.5 °C year average), 10th sunniest (about 3,144 hours of sunshine a year) and 42nd wettest (242 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kushk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Shīnḏanḏ is 0.7 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (31.4 °C), averages 26.3 °C more than its coolest, January (5.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and December. The area lies about 854 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Shīnḏanḏ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Shīnḏanḏ itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.303°N, 62.147°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-506-2421), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
