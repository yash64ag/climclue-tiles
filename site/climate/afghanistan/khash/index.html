Khāsh at a glance

Among the 57 towns ClimaClue covers in Afghanistan, Khāsh is the 3rd warmest (23.1 °C year average), the 5th sunniest (about 3,246 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (85 mm a year). Compared with Zaranj, the nearest covered town with different numbers (109 km away), Khāsh is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 36 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (36.4 °C), averages 27.5 °C more than its coolest, January (8.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 706 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Khāsh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Khāsh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 706 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Khāsh itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (31.529°N, 62.791°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-524-2427), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
