How August compares in Kabul

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Kabul. Compared with July: 0.6 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. September is 3.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Kabul in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kabul in August?
In August, Kabul averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 324 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Kabul?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Kabul’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kabul in August?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is March (77 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Kabul in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kabul get in August?
About 324 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kabul in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
