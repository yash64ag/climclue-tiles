How September compares in Kabul

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kabul. Compared with August: 3.8 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. October is 5.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Kabul in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kabul in September?
In September, Kabul averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 296 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Kabul?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Kabul’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kabul in September?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (85 mm), the driest August (6 mm).
Does it snow in Kabul in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kabul get in September?
About 296 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kabul in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
