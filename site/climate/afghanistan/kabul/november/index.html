How November compares in Kabul

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Kabul. Compared with October: 6.0 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. December is 7.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Kabul in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kabul in November?
In November, Kabul averages a day high of 13.1 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 0.4 °C (33 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Kabul?
November is not one of Kabul’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kabul in November?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (85 mm), the driest August (6 mm).
Does it snow in Kabul in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.8 cm (3.9 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kabul get in November?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kabul in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
