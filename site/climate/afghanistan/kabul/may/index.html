Kabul weather in May

Average May weather in Kabul, Afghanistan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Kabul averages 24.9 °C (77 °F) days and 10.5 °C (51 °F) nights, with 34 mm (1.3 in) of rain over the month and about 297 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Kabul

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kabul. Compared with April: 5.3 °C warmer, 19 mm less precipitation and 75 hours more sunshine. June is 3.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Kabul in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kabul in May?
In May, Kabul averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 10.5 °C (51 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 297 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kabul?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Kabul’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kabul in May?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (77 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Kabul in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kabul get in May?
About 297 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kabul in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
