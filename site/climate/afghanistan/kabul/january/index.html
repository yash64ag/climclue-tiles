How January compares in Kabul

January is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kabul. Compared with December: 3.6 °C cooler, 15 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. February is 2.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Kabul in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kabul in January?
In January, Kabul averages a day high of 2.6 °C (37 °F) and a night low of -8.4 °C (17 °F), with 42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kabul?
January is not one of Kabul’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kabul in January?
42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (77 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Kabul in January?
Yes, on average: about 42 cm (16.4 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kabul get in January?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kabul in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
