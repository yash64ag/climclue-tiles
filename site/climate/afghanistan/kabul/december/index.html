How December compares in Kabul

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Kabul. Compared with November: 7.2 °C cooler, 14 mm more precipitation and 61 hours less sunshine. January is 3.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Of the 11 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Afghanistan, Kabul ranks 9th warmest (average temperature), 5th sunniest and 8th wettest in December.

Kabul in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kabul in December?
In December, Kabul averages a day high of 6.1 °C (43 °F) and a night low of -6.6 °C (20 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kabul?
December is not one of Kabul’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kabul in December?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (85 mm), the driest August (6 mm).
Does it snow in Kabul in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 35 cm (13.7 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 7.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kabul get in December?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kabul in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
