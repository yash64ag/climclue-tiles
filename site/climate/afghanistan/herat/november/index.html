How November compares in Herāt

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Herāt. Compared with October: 5.0 °C cooler, 16 mm more precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. December is 4.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Herāt in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Herāt in November?
In November, Herāt averages a day high of 18.2 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 4.7 °C (40 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Herāt?
November is not one of Herāt’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Herāt in November?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (88 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Herāt in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Herāt get in November?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Herāt in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
