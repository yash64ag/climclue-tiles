How May compares in Herāt

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Herāt. Compared with April: 5.3 °C warmer, 31 mm less precipitation and 95 hours more sunshine. June is 4.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Herāt in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Herāt in May?
In May, Herāt averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 13.9 °C (57 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 315 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Herāt?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Herāt’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Herāt in May?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (88 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Herāt in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Herāt get in May?
About 315 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Herāt in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
