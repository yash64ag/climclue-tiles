How March compares in Herāt

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Herāt. Compared with February: 4.8 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. April is 6.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Herāt in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Herāt in March?
In March, Herāt averages a day high of 16.6 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 4.1 °C (39 °F), with 88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Herāt?
March is not one of Herāt’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Herāt in March?
88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (88 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Herāt in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Herāt get in March?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Herāt in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
