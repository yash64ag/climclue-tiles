How February compares in Herāt

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Herāt. Compared with January: 1.8 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. March is 4.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Herāt in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Herāt in February?
In February, Herāt averages a day high of 11.5 °C (53 °F) and a night low of -0.1 °C (32 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Herāt?
February is not one of Herāt’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Herāt in February?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (88 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Herāt in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.2 cm (2.8 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Herāt get in February?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Herāt in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
