How October compares in Herāt

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Herāt. Compared with September: 5.7 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 53 hours less sunshine. November is 5.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Herāt in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Herāt in October?
In October, Herāt averages a day high of 24.1 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 8.6 °C (47 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Herāt?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Herāt’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Herāt in October?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (88 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Herāt in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Herāt get in October?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Herāt in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
