How December compares in Herāt

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Herāt. Compared with November: 4.8 °C cooler, 30 mm more precipitation and 75 hours less sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Herāt in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Herāt in December?
In December, Herāt averages a day high of 13.2 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 0.1 °C (32 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Herāt?
December is not one of Herāt’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Herāt in December?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (86 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Herāt in December?
Yes, on average: about 3.5 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Herāt get in December?
About 145 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Herāt in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
