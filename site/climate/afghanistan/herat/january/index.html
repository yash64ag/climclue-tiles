How January compares in Herāt

January is the coolest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Herāt. Compared with December: 2.1 °C cooler, 21 mm more precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. February is 1.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Herāt in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Herāt in January?
In January, Herāt averages a day high of 9.6 °C (49 °F) and a night low of -1.4 °C (29 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Herāt?
January is not one of Herāt’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Herāt in January?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (88 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Herāt in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 10 cm (4.1 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Herāt get in January?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Herāt in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
