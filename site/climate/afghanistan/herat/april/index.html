How April compares in Herāt

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Herāt. Compared with March: 6.2 °C warmer, 47 mm less precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. May is 5.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Herāt in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Herāt in April?
In April, Herāt averages a day high of 23.6 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 9.4 °C (49 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Herāt?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Herāt’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Herāt in April?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (88 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Herāt in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Herāt get in April?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Herāt in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
