Why Jalālābād has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,083 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Jalālābād climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Jalālābād?

What is the coldest month in Jalālābād?

What is the driest month in Jalālābād?

What is the sunniest month in Jalālābād?

Does it snow in Jalālābād?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 2.1 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Jalālābād?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: March, April, October and November.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.2 × 11 km, that contains Jalālābād’s GeoNames location (34.426°N, 70.452°E; ClimaClue area sq-495-2504), averaged over the area’s land. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 271,900 (GeoNames).
