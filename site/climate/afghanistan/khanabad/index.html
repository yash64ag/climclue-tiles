Khanabad at a glance

Among the 57 towns ClimaClue covers in Afghanistan, Khanabad is 20th warmest (17.8 °C year average), 48th sunniest (about 2,674 hours of sunshine a year) and 27th wettest (351 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kunduz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Khanabad is 0.3 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 61 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (31.7 °C), averages 28.0 °C more than its coolest, January (3.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 1,282 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Khanabad, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Khanabad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,282 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.0 °C. The 10 km area around Khanabad, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 16.7 °C a year, 1.1 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Khanabad itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.683°N, 69.113°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-473-2491), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
