Balkh at a glance

Among the 11 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Afghanistan, Balkh is the driest (213 mm a year). Compared with Mazār-e Sharīf, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Balkh is 2.5 °C cooler in July and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.3 °C), averages 25.1 °C more than its coolest, January (4.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 1,154 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Balkh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Balkh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,154 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
