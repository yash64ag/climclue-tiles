Bāmyān at a glance

Among the 57 towns ClimaClue covers in Afghanistan, Bāmyān is the 2nd coolest (5.2 °C year average). Compared with Paghmān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (106 km away), Bāmyān is 4.9 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 385 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (15.8 °C), averages 23.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-8.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 1,065 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bāmyān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bāmyān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 1,065 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.9 °C. The 10 km area around Bāmyān, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 3.5 °C a year, 1.7 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bāmyān itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.822°N, 67.827°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-491-2478), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
