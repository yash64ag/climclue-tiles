Āsmār at a glance

Among the 57 towns ClimaClue covers in Afghanistan, Āsmār is the wettest (885 mm a year). Compared with Asadābād, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Āsmār is 0.9 °C cooler in July and 3.1 °C cooler in January, with 108 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 24.2 °C more than its coolest, January (3.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 1,176 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Āsmār has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 1,176 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.2 °C. The 10 km area around Āsmār, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 14.2 °C a year, 2.6 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Āsmār itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.033°N, 71.358°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-489-2513), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
