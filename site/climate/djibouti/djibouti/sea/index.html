Djibouti sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Djibouti

The sea off Djibouti averages 31.2 °C (88 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 26.3 °C (79 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Djibouti sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Djibouti?
In June, at about 31.2 °C (88 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Djibouti?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 26.3 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in Djibouti in January?
About 26.3 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Djibouti?
About 29.0 °C (84 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
