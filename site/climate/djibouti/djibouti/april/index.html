How April compares in Djibouti

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Djibouti. Compared with March: 1.7 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. May is 2.7 °C warmer than April on average.

What to pack for Djibouti in April

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses.

Djibouti in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Djibouti in April?
In April, Djibouti averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 26.0 °C (79 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 272 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Djibouti?
April is not one of Djibouti’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Djibouti in April?
52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is April (52 mm), the driest June (2 mm).
Does it snow in Djibouti in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Djibouti get in April?
About 272 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Djibouti in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
