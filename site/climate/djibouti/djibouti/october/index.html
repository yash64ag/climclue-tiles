How October compares in Djibouti

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Djibouti. Compared with September: 3.1 °C cooler, 18 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. November is 2.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Djibouti in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Djibouti in October?
In October, Djibouti averages a day high of 32.7 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 24.7 °C (76 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 292 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Djibouti?
October is not one of Djibouti’s mild months; those are January and February, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Djibouti in October?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (48 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Djibouti in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Djibouti get in October?
About 292 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Djibouti in October?
About 30.5 °C (87 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (31.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Djibouti in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
