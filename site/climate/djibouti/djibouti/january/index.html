How January compares in Djibouti

January is the coolest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Djibouti. Compared with December: 0.5 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. February is 0.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Djibouti in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Djibouti in January?
In January, Djibouti averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Djibouti?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Djibouti’s mild months (January and February), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Djibouti in January?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (48 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Djibouti in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Djibouti get in January?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Djibouti in January?
About 26.3 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (31.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Djibouti in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
