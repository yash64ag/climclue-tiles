How March compares in Djibouti

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Djibouti. Compared with February: 1.3 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. April is 1.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Djibouti in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Djibouti in March?
In March, Djibouti averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Djibouti?
March is not one of Djibouti’s mild months; those are January and February, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Djibouti in March?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (48 mm), the driest June (1 mm).
Does it snow in Djibouti in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Djibouti get in March?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Djibouti in March?
About 27.2 °C (81 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (31.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Djibouti in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
