How January compares in Balbala

January is the coolest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Balbala. Compared with December: 0.5 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. February is 0.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Balbala in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Balbala in January?
In January, Balbala averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Balbala?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Balbala’s mild months (January, February and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Balbala in January?
24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (52 mm), the driest June (2 mm).
Does it snow in Balbala in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Balbala get in January?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Balbala in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
