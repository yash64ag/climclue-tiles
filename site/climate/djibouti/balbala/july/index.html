How July compares in Balbala

July is the warmest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Balbala. Compared with June: 0.7 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. August is 0.6 °C cooler than July on average.

Balbala in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Balbala in July?
In July, Balbala averages a day high of 38.3 °C (101 °F) and a night low of 31.0 °C (88 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 252 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Balbala?
July is not one of Balbala’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Balbala in July?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (52 mm), the driest June (2 mm).
Does it snow in Balbala in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Balbala get in July?
About 252 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Balbala in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
