How August compares in Balbala

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Balbala. Compared with July: 0.6 °C cooler, 9 mm more precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. September is 1.5 °C cooler than August on average.

Balbala in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Balbala in August?
In August, Balbala averages a day high of 38.5 °C (101 °F) and a night low of 28.0 °C (82 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 269 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Balbala?
August is not one of Balbala’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Balbala in August?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (51 mm), the driest June (2 mm).
Does it snow in Balbala in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Balbala get in August?
About 269 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Balbala in August?
About 30.0 °C (86 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (31.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Balbala in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
