How May compares in Balbala

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Balbala. Compared with April: 2.7 °C warmer, 39 mm less precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. June is 2.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Balbala in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Balbala in May?
In May, Balbala averages a day high of 33.2 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 28.2 °C (83 °F), with 14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 310 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Balbala?
May is not one of Balbala’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Balbala in May?
14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (52 mm), the driest June (2 mm).
Does it snow in Balbala in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Balbala get in May?
About 310 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Balbala in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
