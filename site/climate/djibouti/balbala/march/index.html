How March compares in Balbala

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Balbala. Compared with February: 1.3 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. April is 1.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Balbala in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Balbala in March?
In March, Balbala averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Balbala?
March is not one of Balbala’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Balbala in March?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (52 mm), the driest June (2 mm).
Does it snow in Balbala in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Balbala get in March?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Balbala in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
