How June compares in Balbala

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Balbala. Compared with May: 2.8 °C warmer, 12 mm less precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. July is 0.6 °C warmer than June on average.

Balbala in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Balbala in June?
In June, Balbala averages a day high of 38.1 °C (101 °F) and a night low of 28.3 °C (83 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Balbala?
June is not one of Balbala’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Balbala in June?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the driest month of the year. The wettest month is April (51 mm), the driest June (2 mm).
Does it snow in Balbala in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Balbala get in June?
About 278 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Balbala in June?
About 31.2 °C (88 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is June (31.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Balbala in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
