How October compares in Balbala

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Balbala. Compared with September: 3.3 °C cooler, 18 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. November is 2.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Balbala in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Balbala in October?
In October, Balbala averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 26.6 °C (80 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 292 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Balbala?
October is not one of Balbala’s mild months; those are January, February and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Balbala in October?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (52 mm), the driest June (2 mm).
Does it snow in Balbala in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Balbala get in October?
About 292 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Balbala in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
