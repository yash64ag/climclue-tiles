Sühbaatar at a glance

Among the 24 towns ClimaClue covers in Mongolia, Sühbaatar is the least sunny (about 2,483 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th wettest (302 mm a year). Compared with Darhan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (85 km away), Sühbaatar is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 1.4 °C cooler in January, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.5 °C), averages 44.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-23.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,538 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: a NOAA weather station 23 km from Sühbaatar measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from these (temperature by up to 3.1 °C (5.6 °F) in one month). One station can differ from modelled values (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Sühbaatar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,538 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 44.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sühbaatar itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (50.231°N, 106.208°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-337-2862), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
