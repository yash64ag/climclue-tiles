Dalandzadgad at a glance

Among the 24 towns ClimaClue covers in Mongolia, Dalandzadgad is the warmest (5.3 °C year average), the sunniest (about 3,094 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (128 mm a year). Compared with Bogd, the nearest covered town with different numbers (217 km away), Dalandzadgad is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 1.4 °C warmer in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.4 °C), averages 35.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-13.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,225 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Dalandzadgad has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,225 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
