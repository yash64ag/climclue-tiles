How July compares in Ulan Bator

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Ulan Bator. Compared with June: 2.7 °C warmer, 23 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. August is 2.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses.

Ulan Bator in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulan Bator in July?
In July, Ulan Bator averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 10.7 °C (51 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 260 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Ulan Bator?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Ulan Bator’s mild months (July), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulan Bator in July?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (69 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Ulan Bator in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ulan Bator get in July?
About 260 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulan Bator in July?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
