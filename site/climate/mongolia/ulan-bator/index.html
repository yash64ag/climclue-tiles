Ulan Bator at a glance

Its warmest month, July (18.1 °C), averages 39.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-21.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,323 km from the sea (deep inland).

Best time to visit Ulan Bator

For mild weather, the best months to visit Ulan Bator are June, July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July and August.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (18.1 °C), August (15.9 °C) and June (15.5 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): January (2 mm), February (2 mm) and December (3 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): May (295 h), June (277 h) and July (260 h).

Why Ulan Bator has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,323 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 39.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ulan Bator itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (47.908°N, 106.883°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-360-2868), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
