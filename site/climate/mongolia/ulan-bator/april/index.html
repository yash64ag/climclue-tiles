How April compares in Ulan Bator

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ulan Bator. Compared with March: 9.9 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. May is 7.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Ulan Bator in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulan Bator in April?
In April, Ulan Bator averages a day high of 9.7 °C (49 °F) and a night low of -5.0 °C (23 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 258 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ulan Bator?
April is not one of Ulan Bator’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulan Bator in April?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (60 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ulan Bator in April?
Yes, on average: about 4.6 cm (1.8 in) of fresh snow in April (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Ulan Bator get in April?
About 258 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulan Bator in April?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
