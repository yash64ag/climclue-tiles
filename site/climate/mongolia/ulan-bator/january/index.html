How January compares in Ulan Bator

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Ulan Bator. Compared with December: 2.8 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. February is 4.5 °C warmer than January on average.

What to pack for Ulan Bator in January

Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots.

Ulan Bator in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulan Bator in January?
In January, Ulan Bator averages a day high of -13.7 °C (7 °F) and a night low of -27.4 °C (-17 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Ulan Bator?
January is not one of Ulan Bator’s mild months; those are July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulan Bator in January?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (69 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Ulan Bator in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.2 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 11 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Ulan Bator get in January?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulan Bator in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
