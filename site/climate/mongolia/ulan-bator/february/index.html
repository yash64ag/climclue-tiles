How February compares in Ulan Bator

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Ulan Bator. Compared with January: 4.6 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. March is 9.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Ulan Bator in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulan Bator in February?
In February, Ulan Bator averages a day high of -9.8 °C (14 °F) and a night low of -22.7 °C (-9 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Ulan Bator?
February is not one of Ulan Bator’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulan Bator in February?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (60 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ulan Bator in February?
Yes, on average: about 2.6 cm (1.0 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Ulan Bator get in February?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulan Bator in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
