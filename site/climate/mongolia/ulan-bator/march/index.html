How March compares in Ulan Bator

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Ulan Bator. Compared with February: 9.1 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 59 hours more sunshine. April is 9.9 °C warmer than March on average.

What to pack for Ulan Bator in March

Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings, waterproof boots and sunglasses.

Ulan Bator in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulan Bator in March?
In March, Ulan Bator averages a day high of -1.4 °C (29 °F) and a night low of -16.0 °C (3 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Ulan Bator?
March is not one of Ulan Bator’s mild months; those are July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulan Bator in March?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (69 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Ulan Bator in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 11 cm (4.4 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 7.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Ulan Bator get in March?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulan Bator in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings, waterproof boots and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
