How December compares in Ulan Bator

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Ulan Bator. Compared with November: 7.4 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. January is 2.8 °C cooler than December on average.

What to pack for Ulan Bator in December

Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots.

Ulan Bator in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulan Bator in December?
In December, Ulan Bator averages a day high of -11.9 °C (11 °F) and a night low of -23.3 °C (-10 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ulan Bator?
December is not one of Ulan Bator’s mild months; those are July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulan Bator in December?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (69 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Ulan Bator in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.5 cm (2.2 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 8.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Ulan Bator get in December?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulan Bator in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
