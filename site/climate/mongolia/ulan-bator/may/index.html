How May compares in Ulan Bator

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Ulan Bator. Compared with April: 7.8 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. June is 5.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Ulan Bator in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulan Bator in May?
In May, Ulan Bator averages a day high of 14.6 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 1.1 °C (34 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 295 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Ulan Bator?
May is not one of Ulan Bator’s mild months; those are July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulan Bator in May?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (69 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Ulan Bator in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ulan Bator get in May?
About 295 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulan Bator in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
