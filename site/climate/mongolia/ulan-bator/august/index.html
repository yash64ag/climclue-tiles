How August compares in Ulan Bator

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Ulan Bator. Compared with July: 2.3 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. September is 6.8 °C cooler than August on average.

Ulan Bator in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulan Bator in August?
In August, Ulan Bator averages a day high of 20.1 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 8.4 °C (47 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 256 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Ulan Bator?
August is not one of Ulan Bator’s mild months; those are July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulan Bator in August?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (69 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Ulan Bator in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ulan Bator get in August?
About 256 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulan Bator in August?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
