Ulan Bator weather in June

Average June weather in Ulan Bator, Mongolia: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Ulan Bator averages 22.5 °C (73 °F) days and 8.6 °C (47 °F) nights, with 39 mm (1.5 in) of rain over the month and about 277 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Ulan Bator

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Ulan Bator. Compared with May: 5.4 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. July is 2.6 °C warmer than June on average.

Ulan Bator in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulan Bator in June?
In June, Ulan Bator averages a day high of 22.5 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 8.6 °C (47 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 277 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Ulan Bator?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Ulan Bator’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulan Bator in June?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (60 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ulan Bator in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ulan Bator get in June?
About 277 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulan Bator in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
