How October compares in Ulan Bator

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Ulan Bator. Compared with September: 8.6 °C cooler, 20 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. November is 11.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots.

Ulan Bator in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulan Bator in October?
In October, Ulan Bator averages a day high of 5.2 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -6.0 °C (21 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Ulan Bator?
October is not one of Ulan Bator’s mild months; those are July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulan Bator in October?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (69 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Ulan Bator in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 13 cm (5.1 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Ulan Bator get in October?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulan Bator in October?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
