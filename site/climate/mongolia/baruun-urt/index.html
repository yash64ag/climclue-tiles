Baruun-Urt at a glance

Among the 24 towns ClimaClue covers in Mongolia, Baruun-Urt is 11th warmest (1.3 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 2,997 hours of sunshine a year) and 13th wettest (239 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Choibalsan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (181 km away), Baruun-Urt is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.4 °C), averages 41.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-21.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 907 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Baruun-Urt has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-373-2932), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
