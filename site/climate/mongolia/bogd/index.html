Bogd at a glance

Among the 24 towns ClimaClue covers in Mongolia, Bogd is the 2nd warmest (4.5 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 3,072 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (121 mm a year). Compared with Arvayheer, the nearest covered town with different numbers (183 km away), Bogd is 5.2 °C warmer in July and 2.6 °C warmer in January, with 184 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.8 °C), averages 36.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-14.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,440 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bogd, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bogd has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,440 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 36.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bogd itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (44.670°N, 102.175°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-393-2821), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
