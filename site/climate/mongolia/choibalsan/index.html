Choibalsan at a glance

Among the 24 towns ClimaClue covers in Mongolia, Choibalsan is 6th warmest (1.9 °C year average), 9th sunniest (about 2,910 hours of sunshine a year) and 14th wettest (236 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Baruun-Urt, the nearest covered town with different numbers (181 km away), Choibalsan is 0.7 °C warmer in July and 1.1 °C warmer in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.1 °C), averages 41.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-20.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 960 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 100% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Choibalsan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 960 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 41.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Choibalsan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.073°N, 114.533°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-359-2945), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
