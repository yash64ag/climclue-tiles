Chisinau weather in March

Average March weather in Chisinau, Moldova: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Chisinau averages 7.9 °C (46 °F) days and 0.3 °C (33 °F) nights, with 32 mm (1.3 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 125 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Chisinau

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Chisinau. Compared with February: 4.8 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 47 hours more sunshine. April is 6.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Chisinau in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chisinau in March?
In March, Chisinau averages a day high of 7.9 °C (46 °F) and a night low of 0.3 °C (33 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Chisinau?
March is not one of Chisinau’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chisinau in March?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (64 mm), the driest February (30 mm).
Does it snow in Chisinau in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 10 cm (4.0 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Chisinau get in March?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chisinau in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
