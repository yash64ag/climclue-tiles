How September compares in Chisinau

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Chisinau. Compared with August: 5.2 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 67 hours less sunshine. October is 6.0 °C cooler than September on average.

Chisinau in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chisinau in September?
In September, Chisinau averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 11.8 °C (53 °F), with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Chisinau?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Chisinau’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chisinau in September?
47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (64 mm), the driest February (30 mm).
Does it snow in Chisinau in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chisinau get in September?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chisinau in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
