How December compares in Chisinau

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Chisinau. Compared with November: 4.9 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. January is 1.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Chisinau in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chisinau in December?
In December, Chisinau averages a day high of 2.7 °C (37 °F) and a night low of -2.7 °C (27 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 59 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Chisinau?
December is not one of Chisinau’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chisinau in December?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (64 mm), the driest February (30 mm).
Does it snow in Chisinau in December?
Yes, on average: about 17 cm (6.6 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Chisinau get in December?
About 59 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chisinau in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
