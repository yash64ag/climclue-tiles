Chisinau at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Moldova, Chisinau is the 3rd warmest (10.1 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 2,088 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (531 mm a year). Compared with Bender, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), Chisinau is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 29 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.9 °C), averages 24.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 166 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 99% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Best time to visit Chisinau

For mild weather, the best months to visit Chisinau are May, June, July, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May, June, July, August and September.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (21.9 °C), August (21.4 °C) and June (19.9 °C).

Why Chisinau has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 166 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
