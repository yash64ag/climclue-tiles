How July compares in Chisinau

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Chisinau. Compared with June: 2.0 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. August is 0.6 °C cooler than July on average.

Chisinau in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chisinau in July?
In July, Chisinau averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 17.1 °C (63 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 294 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Chisinau?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Chisinau’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chisinau in July?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (67 mm), the driest February (30 mm).
Does it snow in Chisinau in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chisinau get in July?
About 294 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chisinau in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
