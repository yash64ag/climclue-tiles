How October compares in Chisinau

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Chisinau. Compared with September: 5.8 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 56 hours less sunshine. November is 6.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Chisinau in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chisinau in October?
In October, Chisinau averages a day high of 14.6 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 6.6 °C (44 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Chisinau?
October is not one of Chisinau’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chisinau in October?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (67 mm), the driest February (30 mm).
Does it snow in Chisinau in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chisinau get in October?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chisinau in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
