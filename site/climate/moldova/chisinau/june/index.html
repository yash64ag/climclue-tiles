How June compares in Chisinau

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Chisinau. Compared with May: 3.3 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Chisinau in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chisinau in June?
In June, Chisinau averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Chisinau?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Chisinau’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chisinau in June?
64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (64 mm), the driest February (30 mm).
Does it snow in Chisinau in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chisinau get in June?
About 278 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chisinau in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
