How January compares in Chisinau

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Chisinau. Compared with December: 1.3 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. February is 1.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Chisinau in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chisinau in January?
In January, Chisinau averages a day high of 1.1 °C (34 °F) and a night low of -5.0 °C (23 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 74 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Chisinau?
January is not one of Chisinau’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chisinau in January?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (67 mm), the driest February (30 mm).
Does it snow in Chisinau in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 25 cm (9.8 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 9.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Chisinau get in January?
About 74 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chisinau in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
