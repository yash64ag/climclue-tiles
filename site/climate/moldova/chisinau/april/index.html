How April compares in Chisinau

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Chisinau. Compared with March: 6.7 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 59 hours more sunshine. May is 6.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Chisinau in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chisinau in April?
In April, Chisinau averages a day high of 15.8 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 6.0 °C (43 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Chisinau?
April is not one of Chisinau’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chisinau in April?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (64 mm), the driest February (30 mm).
Does it snow in Chisinau in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chisinau get in April?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chisinau in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
