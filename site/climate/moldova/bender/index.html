Bender at a glance

Among the 4 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Moldova, Bender is the 2nd warmest (10.3 °C year average), the sunniest (about 2,100 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (560 mm a year). Compared with Tiraspol, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Bender is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 41 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.3 °C), averages 24.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 116 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bender, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bender has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 116 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
