Bharatpur at a glance

Among the 15 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nepal, Bharatpur is the 3rd wettest (2,287 mm a year). Compared with Hetauda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (65 km away), Bharatpur is 2.8 °C warmer in June and 2.4 °C warmer in January, with 121 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (29.3 °C), averages 13.6 °C more than its coolest, January (15.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.3 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 741 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bharatpur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Bharatpur

Why Bharatpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 741 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
