Madhyapur Thimi at a glance

Among the 15 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nepal, Madhyapur Thimi is the 2nd coolest (17.9 °C year average) and the 2nd least sunny (about 1,994 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Kathmandu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Madhyapur Thimi is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 292 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (22.8 °C), averages 12.6 °C more than its coolest, January (10.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.4 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 707 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Madhyapur Thimi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Madhyapur Thimi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 707 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
