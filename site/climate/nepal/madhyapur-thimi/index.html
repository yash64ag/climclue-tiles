Why Madhyapur Thimi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 707 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Madhyapur Thimi climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Madhyapur Thimi?
August, with an average of 22.8 °C (73 °F), day highs around 24.8 °C (77 °F) and night lows around 19.3 °C (67 °F).
What is the coldest month in Madhyapur Thimi?
January, with an average of 10.2 °C (50 °F), day highs around 15.2 °C (59 °F) and night lows around 5.4 °C (42 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Madhyapur Thimi?
July, with 453 mm (17.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,693 mm (66.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Madhyapur Thimi?
November, with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Madhyapur Thimi?
April, with about 269 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Madhyapur Thimi?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Madhyapur Thimi?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: March, April and October.
What type of climate does Madhyapur Thimi have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.9 × 11 km, that contains Madhyapur Thimi’s GeoNames location (27.681°N, 85.388°E; ClimaClue area sq-563-2653), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 119,955 (GeoNames).
