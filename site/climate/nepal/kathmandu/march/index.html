How March compares in Kathmandu

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Kathmandu. Compared with February: 3.8 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 73 hours more sunshine. April is 3.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Kathmandu in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kathmandu in March?
In March, Kathmandu averages a day high of 20.8 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kathmandu?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Kathmandu’s mild months (March, April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kathmandu in March?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (528 mm), the driest November (9 mm).
Does it snow in Kathmandu in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kathmandu get in March?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kathmandu in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
