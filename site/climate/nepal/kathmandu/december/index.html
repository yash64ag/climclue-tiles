How December compares in Kathmandu

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Kathmandu. Compared with November: 3.3 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. January is 1.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Kathmandu in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kathmandu in December?
In December, Kathmandu averages a day high of 16.8 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 7.8 °C (46 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kathmandu?
December is not one of Kathmandu’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kathmandu in December?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (437 mm), the driest November (8 mm).
Does it snow in Kathmandu in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kathmandu get in December?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kathmandu in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
