How April compares in Kathmandu

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Kathmandu. Compared with March: 3.4 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. May is 1.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Kathmandu in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kathmandu in April?
In April, Kathmandu averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 270 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kathmandu?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Kathmandu’s mild months (March, April, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kathmandu in April?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (437 mm), the driest November (8 mm).
Does it snow in Kathmandu in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kathmandu get in April?
About 270 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kathmandu in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
