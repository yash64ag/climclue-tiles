Lahān at a glance

Among the 15 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nepal, Lahān is the 3rd warmest (25.0 °C year average) and the 2nd driest (1,501 mm a year). Compared with Janakpur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Lahān is 0.8 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 25 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.1 °C), averages 13.8 °C more than its coolest, January (16.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.6 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 576 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lahān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Lahān

For mild weather, the best months to visit Lahān are January, February, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): May (30.1 °C), June (29.6 °C) and April (29.3 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): November (6 mm), December (8 mm) and February (10 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): April (258 h), March (234 h) and May (209 h).

Why Lahān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 576 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
