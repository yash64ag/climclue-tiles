Lahān weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Lahān, Nepal (Madhesh), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Lahān is warmest in May, with 36.3 °C (97 °F) days and 24.1 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 23.4 °C (74 °F) days and 9.4 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (431 mm); November is driest (6 mm).

Lahān at a glance

Among the 46 towns ClimaClue covers in Nepal, Lahān is the 5th driest (1,479 mm a year). Compared with Triyuga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Lahān is 0.6 °C warmer in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.1 °C), averages 13.8 °C more than its coolest, January (16.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.4 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 576 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lahān, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Lahān

For mild weather, the best months to visit Lahān are January, February, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): May (30.1 °C), June (29.7 °C) and April (29.3 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): November (6 mm), December (8 mm) and February (9 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): April (258 h), March (234 h) and May (209 h).

Why Lahān has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 576 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Lahān itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.720°N, 86.483°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-572-2664), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts. Sources: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010 (temperature, precipitation, wind); ERA5-Land, 1981–2010 (dew point and snow; snowfall matched to the area’s CHELSA climate; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information); relative humidity calculated from the dew point and the night lows and day highs; CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990 (sunshine and the day–night range; a different period); GeoNames (name and population 102,955, CC BY 4.0); climate type by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
