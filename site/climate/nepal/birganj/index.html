Birgañj at a glance

Among the 15 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nepal, Birgañj is the 2nd warmest (25.0 °C year average). Compared with Hetauda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Birgañj is 4.7 °C warmer in May and 2.4 °C warmer in January, with 660 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.6 °C), averages 15.0 °C more than its coolest, January (15.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.0 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 665 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Birgañj, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Birgañj has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 665 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
