Why Birgañj has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 665 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Birgañj climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Birgañj?
May, with an average of 30.6 °C (87 °F), day highs around 35.9 °C (97 °F) and night lows around 24.8 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Birgañj?
January, with an average of 15.7 °C (60 °F), day highs around 22.0 °C (72 °F) and night lows around 10.0 °C (50 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Birgañj?
July, with 517 mm (20.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,748 mm (68.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Birgañj?
November, with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Birgañj?
April, with about 269 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Birgañj?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Birgañj?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, November and December.
What type of climate does Birgañj have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.9 × 11 km, that contains Birgañj’s GeoNames location (27.017°N, 84.880°E; ClimaClue area sq-569-2648), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 268,273 (GeoNames).
