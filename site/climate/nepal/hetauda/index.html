Hetauda at a glance

Among the 15 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nepal, Hetauda is the 2nd wettest (2,408 mm a year). Compared with Pātan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Hetauda is 3.8 °C warmer in June and 3.1 °C warmer in January, with 716 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (26.4 °C), averages 13.2 °C more than its coolest, January (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.5 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 698 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hetauda, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hetauda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 698 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 9.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
