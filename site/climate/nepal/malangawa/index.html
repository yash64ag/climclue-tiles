Malaṅgawā at a glance

Among the 46 towns ClimaClue covers in Nepal, Malaṅgawā is the 5th warmest (25.2 °C year average). Compared with Gaur, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Malaṅgawā is 0.1 °C cooler in May and about as warm in January, with 17 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.9 °C), averages 15.0 °C more than its coolest, January (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.8 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 617 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Malaṅgawā has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 617 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-571-2655), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
