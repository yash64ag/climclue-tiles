Why Butwāl has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 784 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Butwāl climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Butwāl?
June, with an average of 30.1 °C (86 °F), day highs around 33.7 °C (93 °F) and night lows around 25.7 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Butwāl?
January, with an average of 15.1 °C (59 °F), day highs around 20.9 °C (70 °F) and night lows around 9.9 °C (50 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Butwāl?
July, with 649 mm (25.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,153 mm (84.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Butwāl?
November, with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Butwāl?
April, with about 271 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Butwāl?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Butwāl?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, March, November and December.
What type of climate does Butwāl have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.9 × 11 km, that contains Butwāl’s GeoNames location (27.691°N, 83.453°E; ClimaClue area sq-563-2634), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 195,054 (GeoNames).
