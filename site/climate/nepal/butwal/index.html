Butwāl at a glance

Among the 15 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nepal, Butwāl is 5th warmest (24.5 °C year average), 4th sunniest (about 2,309 hours of sunshine a year) and 4th wettest (2,153 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pokhara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (82 km away), Butwāl is 6.0 °C warmer in June and 4.0 °C warmer in January, with 1,011 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (30.1 °C), averages 15.0 °C more than its coolest, January (15.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.9 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 784 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Butwāl, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Butwāl has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 784 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
