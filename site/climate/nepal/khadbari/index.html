Khā̃dbāri̇̄ at a glance

Among the 46 towns ClimaClue covers in Nepal, Khā̃dbāri̇̄ is 30th warmest (19.7 °C year average), 27th sunniest (about 2,161 hours of sunshine a year) and 13th wettest (2,044 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dhankutā, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Khā̃dbāri̇̄ is 1.6 °C warmer in August and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 408 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.0 °C), averages 14.6 °C more than its coolest, January (11.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.0 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 627 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Khā̃dbāri̇̄, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Khā̃dbāri̇̄ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 627 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.6 °C. The 10 km area around Khā̃dbāri̇̄, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 21.1 °C a year, 1.4 °C warmer than the town itself, because it takes in lower ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
