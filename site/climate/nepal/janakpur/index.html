Janakpur at a glance

Among the 15 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nepal, Janakpur is the warmest (25.3 °C year average) and the driest (1,476 mm a year). Compared with Lahān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (55 km away), Janakpur is 0.8 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 25 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.9 °C), averages 14.8 °C more than its coolest, January (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.4 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 592 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Janakpur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Janakpur

For mild weather, the best months to visit Janakpur are January, February, November and December.

Why Janakpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 592 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
