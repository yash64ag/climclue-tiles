Why Janakpur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 592 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Janakpur climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Janakpur?
May, with an average of 30.9 °C (88 °F), day highs around 35.7 °C (96 °F) and night lows around 25.0 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Janakpur?
January, with an average of 16.1 °C (61 °F), day highs around 22.1 °C (72 °F) and night lows around 10.4 °C (51 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Janakpur?
July, with 443 mm (17.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,476 mm (58.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Janakpur?
November, with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Janakpur?
April, with about 263 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Janakpur?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Janakpur?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February, November and December.
What type of climate does Janakpur have?
Humid subtropical, dry winter (Köppen Cwa). Hot, wet summers and mild, dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.9 × 11 km, that contains Janakpur’s GeoNames location (26.729°N, 85.926°E; ClimaClue area sq-572-2659), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 195,438 (GeoNames).
