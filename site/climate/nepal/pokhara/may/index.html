How May compares in Pokhara

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Pokhara. Compared with April: 2.6 °C warmer, 144 mm more precipitation and 56 hours less sunshine. June is 2.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Pokhara in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pokhara in May?
In May, Pokhara averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 243 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Pokhara?
May is not one of Pokhara’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pokhara in May?
243 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (816 mm), the driest November (17 mm).
Does it snow in Pokhara in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pokhara get in May?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pokhara in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
