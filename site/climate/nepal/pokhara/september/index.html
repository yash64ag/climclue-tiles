How September compares in Pokhara

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Pokhara. Compared with August: 1.1 °C cooler, 257 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. October is 3.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Pokhara in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pokhara in September?
In September, Pokhara averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 19.0 °C (66 °F), with 458 mm (18.0 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Pokhara?
September is not one of Pokhara’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pokhara in September?
458 mm (18.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (779 mm), the driest November (17 mm).
Does it snow in Pokhara in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pokhara get in September?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pokhara in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
