How February compares in Pokhara

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Pokhara. Compared with January: 1.4 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. March is 3.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Pokhara in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pokhara in February?
In February, Pokhara averages a day high of 20.1 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 4.7 °C (40 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Pokhara?
February is not one of Pokhara’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pokhara in February?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (779 mm), the driest November (17 mm).
Does it snow in Pokhara in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pokhara get in February?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pokhara in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
