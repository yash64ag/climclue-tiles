How October compares in Pokhara

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Pokhara. Compared with September: 3.6 °C cooler, 374 mm less precipitation and 57 hours more sunshine. November is 4.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Pokhara in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pokhara in October?
In October, Pokhara averages a day high of 23.0 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 13.7 °C (57 °F), with 97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Pokhara?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Pokhara’s mild months (March, April, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pokhara in October?
97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (816 mm), the driest November (17 mm).
Does it snow in Pokhara in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pokhara get in October?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pokhara in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
