Bhadrapur at a glance

Among the 46 towns ClimaClue covers in Nepal, Bhadrapur is the 3rd wettest (2,728 mm a year). Compared with Ilām, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Bhadrapur is 5.3 °C warmer in June and 5.4 °C warmer in January, with 161 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.3 °C), averages 11.7 °C more than its coolest, January (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.9 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 527 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bhadrapur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bhadrapur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 527 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bhadrapur itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.544°N, 88.094°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-574-2680), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
