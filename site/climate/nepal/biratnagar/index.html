Biratnagar at a glance

Among the 15 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nepal, Biratnagar is 4th warmest (25.0 °C year average), 8th sunniest (about 2,176 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (1,839 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dharān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (40 km away), Biratnagar is 4.1 °C warmer in May and 3.0 °C warmer in January, with 215 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (29.4 °C), averages 12.8 °C more than its coolest, January (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.8 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 527 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Biratnagar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Biratnagar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 527 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
