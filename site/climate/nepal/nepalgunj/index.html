Nepalgunj at a glance

Among the 46 towns ClimaClue covers in Nepal, Nepalgunj is the 5th sunniest (about 2,496 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Gulariyā, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Nepalgunj is 0.1 °C warmer in June and about as warm in January, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (31.2 °C), averages 17.0 °C more than its coolest, January (14.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.2 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 920 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nepalgunj, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nepalgunj has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 920 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nepalgunj itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (28.050°N, 81.617°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-559-2616), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
