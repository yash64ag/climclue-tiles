Dhangaḍhi̇̄ at a glance

Among the 15 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nepal, Dhangaḍhi̇̄ is the sunniest (about 2,546 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Birendranagar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (101 km away), Dhangaḍhi̇̄ is 2.7 °C warmer in June and 1.1 °C warmer in January, with 32 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (30.6 °C), averages 16.8 °C more than its coolest, January (13.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.0 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 1,049 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Dhangaḍhi̇̄ has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 1,049 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
