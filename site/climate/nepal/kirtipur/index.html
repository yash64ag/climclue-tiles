Ki̇̄rtipur at a glance

Among the 46 towns ClimaClue covers in Nepal, Ki̇̄rtipur is the least sunny (about 1,985 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Pātan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Ki̇̄rtipur is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 165 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.7 °C), averages 13.3 °C more than its coolest, January (10.4 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 710 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ki̇̄rtipur, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station. Best time to visit Ki̇̄rtipur

Why Ki̇̄rtipur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 710 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.3 °C. The 10 km area around Ki̇̄rtipur, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 15.9 °C a year, 2.5 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ki̇̄rtipur itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (27.679°N, 85.276°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-563-2652), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
