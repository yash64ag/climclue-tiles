Fort-de-France sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Martinique · Martinique

The sea off Fort-de-France averages 28.6 °C (83 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 26.2 °C (79 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Fort-de-France sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Fort-de-France?
In September, at about 28.6 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Fort-de-France?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 26.2 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in Fort-de-France in February?
About 26.2 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Fort-de-France?
About 27.4 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
