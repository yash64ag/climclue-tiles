Douglas sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Isle of Man

The sea off Douglas averages 14.8 °C (59 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.0 °C (45 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Douglas sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Douglas?
In August, at about 14.8 °C (59 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Douglas?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 7.0 °C (45 °F).
How warm is the sea in Douglas in March?
About 7.0 °C (45 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Douglas?
About 10.8 °C (51 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Douglas nearby?
In August: Whitehaven (16.0 °C, 73 km), Workington (16.0 °C, 82 km) and Barrow in Furness (16.7 °C, 82 km).
