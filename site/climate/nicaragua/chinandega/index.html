Chinandega at a glance

Among the 5 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nicaragua, Chinandega is the warmest (27.9 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 2,688 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (1,889 mm a year). Compared with León, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Chinandega is 0.3 °C warmer in April and 0.2 °C warmer in October, with 74 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (29.4 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, October (26.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 23.7 °C (75 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chinandega, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chinandega has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
