Matagalpa at a glance

Among the 5 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Nicaragua, Matagalpa is the coolest (22.2 °C year average), the least sunny (about 2,318 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (1,382 mm a year). Compared with Managua, the nearest covered town with different numbers (95 km away), Matagalpa is 6.1 °C cooler in April and 6.4 °C cooler in January, with 15 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (23.9 °C), averages 3.1 °C more than its coolest, January (20.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.8 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 132 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Matagalpa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Matagalpa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 132 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
