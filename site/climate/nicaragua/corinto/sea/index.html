Corinto sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Chinandega Department · Nicaragua

The sea off Corinto averages 29.2 °C (85 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 26.8 °C (80 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Corinto sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Corinto?
In August, at about 29.2 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Corinto?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 26.8 °C (80 °F).
How warm is the sea in Corinto in February?
About 26.8 °C (80 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Corinto?
About 28.2 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Corinto nearby?
In August: Marcovia (30.1 °C, 91 km), La Unión (29.9 °C, 120 km) and Puerto El Triunfo (29.8 °C, 174 km).
