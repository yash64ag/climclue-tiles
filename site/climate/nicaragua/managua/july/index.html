How July compares in Managua

July is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Managua. Compared with June: 0.4 °C cooler, 79 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. August is 0.2 °C warmer than July on average.

Managua in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Managua in July?
In July, Managua averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 150 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Managua?
No month in Managua is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Managua in July?
150 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (273 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Managua in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Managua get in July?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Managua in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
