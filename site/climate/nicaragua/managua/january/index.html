How January compares in Managua

January is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Managua. Compared with December: 0.2 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. February is 0.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Managua in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Managua in January?
In January, Managua averages a day high of 32.4 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Managua?
No month in Managua is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Managua in January?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (261 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Managua in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Managua get in January?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Managua in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
