How March compares in Managua

March is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Managua. Compared with February: 1.0 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. April is 1.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Managua in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Managua in March?
In March, Managua averages a day high of 34.3 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 267 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Managua?
No month in Managua is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Managua in March?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (273 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Managua in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Managua get in March?
About 267 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Managua in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
