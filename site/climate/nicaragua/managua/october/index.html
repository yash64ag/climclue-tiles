Managua weather in October

Average October weather in Managua, Nicaragua: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Managua averages 31.0 °C (88 °F) days and 22.1 °C (72 °F) nights, with 258 mm (10.2 in) of rain over the month and about 180 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Managua

October is the coolest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Managua. Compared with September: 0.3 °C cooler, 15 mm less precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. November is 0.4 °C warmer than October on average.

Managua in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Managua in October?
In October, Managua averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 258 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Managua?
No month in Managua is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Managua in October?
258 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (273 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Managua in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Managua get in October?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Managua in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
