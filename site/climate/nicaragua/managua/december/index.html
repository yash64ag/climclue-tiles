How December compares in Managua

December is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Managua. Compared with November: 0.1 °C warmer, 59 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. January is 0.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Managua in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Managua in December?
In December, Managua averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Managua?
No month in Managua is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Managua in December?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (273 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Managua in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Managua get in December?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Managua in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
