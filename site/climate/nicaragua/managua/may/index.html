How May compares in Managua

May is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Managua. Compared with April: 0.9 °C cooler, 189 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. June is 1.5 °C cooler than May on average.

Managua in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Managua in May?
In May, Managua averages a day high of 33.6 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 210 mm (8.3 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Managua?
No month in Managua is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Managua in May?
210 mm (8.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (261 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Managua in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Managua get in May?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Managua in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
