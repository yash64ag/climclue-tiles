San Rafael del Sur sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Managua Department · Nicaragua

The sea off San Rafael del Sur averages 29.1 °C (84 °F) in June, its warmest month, and 26.9 °C (80 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

San Rafael del Sur sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in San Rafael del Sur?
In June, at about 29.1 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in San Rafael del Sur?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 26.9 °C (80 °F).
How warm is the sea in San Rafael del Sur in February?
About 26.9 °C (80 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in San Rafael del Sur?
About 28.2 °C (83 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in San Rafael del Sur nearby?
In June: Marcovia (29.8 °C, 186 km).
