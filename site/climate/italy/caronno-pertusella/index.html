Caronno Pertusella at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Caronno Pertusella is 420th warmest (14.2 °C year average), 538th sunniest (about 1,933 hours of sunshine a year) and 44th wettest (1,188 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Saronno, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Caronno Pertusella is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 32 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.7 °C), averages 20.8 °C more than its coolest, January (4.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 134 km from the sea.

Station check: before adjustment, the modelled temperatures differed from a NOAA weather station 18 km from Caronno Pertusella by up to 1.9 °C (3.4 °F) in one month. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Caronno Pertusella has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 134 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Caronno Pertusella itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (45.598°N, 9.046°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-384-1890), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
