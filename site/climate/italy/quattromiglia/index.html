Quattromiglia at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Quattromiglia is 182nd warmest (16.3 °C year average), 156th sunniest (about 2,421 hours of sunshine a year) and 202nd wettest (950 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cosenza, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Quattromiglia is 0.5 °C warmer in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 50 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.5 °C), averages 15.5 °C more than its coolest, January (9.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.3 °C (43 °F), in February. The area lies about 26 km from the sea.

Why Quattromiglia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 26 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-446-1962), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
