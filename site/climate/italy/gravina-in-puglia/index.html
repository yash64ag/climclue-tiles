Gravina in Puglia at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Gravina in Puglia is 321st warmest (14.8 °C year average), 170th sunniest (about 2,403 hours of sunshine a year) and 543rd wettest (571 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Altamura, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Gravina in Puglia is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 41 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.3 °C), averages 17.8 °C more than its coolest, January (6.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 48 km from the sea.

Why Gravina in Puglia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 48 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
