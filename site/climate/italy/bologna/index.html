Bologna at a glance

Among the 44 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Italy, Bologna is 28th warmest (14.0 °C year average), 34th sunniest (about 2,025 hours of sunshine a year) and 33rd wettest (736 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Modena, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Bologna is 0.6 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 98 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.9 °C), averages 21.3 °C more than its coolest, January (3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 79 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bologna, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bologna has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 79 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
