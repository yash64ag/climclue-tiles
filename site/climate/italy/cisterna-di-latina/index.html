Cisterna di Latina at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Cisterna di Latina is 236th warmest (15.9 °C year average), 181st sunniest (about 2,385 hours of sunshine a year) and 151st wettest (1,002 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Velletri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Cisterna di Latina is 1.3 °C warmer in August and 1.8 °C warmer in January, with 47 mm less precipitation a year. Why Cisterna di Latina has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
