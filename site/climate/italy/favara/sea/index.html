Favara sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Sicily · Italy

The sea off Favara averages 24.8 °C (77 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.4 °C (58 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Favara sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Favara?
In August, at about 24.8 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Favara?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 14.4 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in Favara in February?
About 14.4 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Favara?
About 18.9 °C (66 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Favara nearby?
In August: Licata (25.4 °C, 34 km), Termini Imerese (26.0 °C, 74 km) and Misilmeri (26.0 °C, 82 km).
