Follonica sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Tuscany · Italy

The sea off Follonica averages 24.5 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.0 °C (55 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Follonica sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Follonica?
In August, at about 24.5 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Follonica?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 13.0 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in Follonica in February?
About 13.0 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Follonica?
About 18.0 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Follonica nearby?
In August: Pisa (25.2 °C, 92 km), Massarosa (25.2 °C, 110 km) and Viareggio (25.2 °C, 112 km).
