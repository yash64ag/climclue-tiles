Udine at a glance

Among the 44 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Italy, Udine is the 5th coolest (13.0 °C year average) and the 2nd wettest (1,510 mm a year). Compared with Trieste, the nearest covered town with different numbers (63 km away), Udine is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 251 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.1 °C), averages 20.3 °C more than its coolest, January (2.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Udine, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Udine has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
