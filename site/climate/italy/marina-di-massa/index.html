Marina di Massa weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Marina di Massa, Italy (Tuscany), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Marina di Massa is warmest in August, with 28.3 °C (83 °F) days and 19.4 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 12.0 °C (54 °F) days and 6.2 °C nights. Most rain falls around October (164 mm); July is driest (36 mm).

Marina di Massa at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Marina di Massa is 242nd warmest (15.9 °C year average), 395th sunniest (about 2,105 hours of sunshine a year) and 78th wettest (1,113 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Marina di Massa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.7 °C. The 10 km area around Marina di Massa, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 13.6 °C a year, 2.2 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Marina di Massa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (44.004°N, 10.107°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-399-1901), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
