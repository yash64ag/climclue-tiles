Castel Volturno sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Campania · Italy

The sea off Castel Volturno averages 25.7 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.8 °C (57 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August.

Castel Volturno sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Castel Volturno?
In August, at about 25.7 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Castel Volturno?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August. The coolest month is February, at 13.8 °C (57 °F).
How warm is the sea in Castel Volturno in February?
About 13.8 °C (57 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Castel Volturno?
About 19.1 °C (66 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
