How June compares in Rome

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Rome. Compared with May: 3.8 °C warmer, 22 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. July is 3.0 °C warmer than June on average.

Rome in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rome in June?
In June, Rome averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 281 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Rome?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Rome’s mild months (May, June, July and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rome in June?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (119 mm), the driest July (25 mm).
Does it snow in Rome in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rome get in June?
About 281 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rome in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
