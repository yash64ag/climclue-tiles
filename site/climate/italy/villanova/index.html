Villanova at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Villanova is 255th warmest (15.8 °C year average), 178th sunniest (about 2,385 hours of sunshine a year) and 121st wettest (1,052 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tivoli, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Villanova is 1.4 °C warmer in July and 1.1 °C warmer in January, with 89 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.4 °C), averages 18.5 °C more than its coolest, January (7.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 51 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 25 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 133% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Villanova has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 51 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Villanova itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.964°N, 12.756°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-420-1927), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
