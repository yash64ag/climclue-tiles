How March compares in Genoa

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Genoa. Compared with February: 3.0 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. April is 2.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Genoa in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Genoa in March?
In March, Genoa averages a day high of 12.4 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 6.0 °C (43 °F), with 77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Genoa?
March is not one of Genoa’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Genoa in March?
77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (188 mm), the driest July (44 mm).
Does it snow in Genoa in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.0 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Genoa get in March?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Genoa in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
