How August compares in Genoa

August is the warmest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Genoa. Compared with July: 0.2 °C warmer, 41 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. September is 3.1 °C cooler than August on average.

Genoa in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Genoa in August?
In August, Genoa averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 257 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Genoa?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Genoa’s mild months (May, June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Genoa in August?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (175 mm), the driest July (38 mm).
Does it snow in Genoa in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Genoa get in August?
About 257 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Genoa in August?
About 24.4 °C (76 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (24.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Genoa in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
