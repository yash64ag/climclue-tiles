How April compares in Genoa

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Genoa. Compared with March: 2.7 °C warmer, 44 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. May is 3.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Genoa in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Genoa in April?
In April, Genoa averages a day high of 14.9 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 121 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Genoa?
April is not one of Genoa’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Genoa in April?
121 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (188 mm), the driest July (44 mm).
Does it snow in Genoa in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Genoa get in April?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Genoa in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
