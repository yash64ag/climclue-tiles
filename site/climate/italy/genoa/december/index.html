How December compares in Genoa

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Genoa. Compared with November: 3.3 °C cooler, 67 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. January is 0.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Genoa in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Genoa in December?
In December, Genoa averages a day high of 9.0 °C (48 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 114 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 108 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Genoa?
December is not one of Genoa’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Genoa in December?
114 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (188 mm), the driest July (44 mm).
Does it snow in Genoa in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.3 cm (3.7 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Genoa get in December?
About 108 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Genoa in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
