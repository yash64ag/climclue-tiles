How December compares in Genoa

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Genoa. Compared with November: 3.2 °C cooler, 65 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. January is 1.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Genoa in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Genoa in December?
In December, Genoa averages a day high of 11.6 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 5.5 °C (42 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 108 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Genoa?
December is not one of Genoa’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Genoa in December?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (175 mm), the driest July (38 mm).
Does it snow in Genoa in December?
Yes, on average: about 4.2 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Genoa get in December?
About 108 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Genoa in December?
About 14.9 °C (59 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (24.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Genoa in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
