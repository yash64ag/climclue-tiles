How November compares in Genoa

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Genoa. Compared with October: 4.9 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 55 hours less sunshine. December is 3.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Genoa in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Genoa in November?
In November, Genoa averages a day high of 12.3 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 7.5 °C (46 °F), with 181 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation and about 107 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Genoa?
November is not one of Genoa’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Genoa in November?
181 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (188 mm), the driest July (44 mm).
Does it snow in Genoa in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Genoa get in November?
About 107 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Genoa in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
