How February compares in Genoa

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Genoa. Compared with January: 0.8 °C warmer, 24 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. March is 3.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Genoa in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Genoa in February?
In February, Genoa averages a day high of 9.4 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 3.6 °C (38 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 123 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Genoa?
February is not one of Genoa’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Genoa in February?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (188 mm), the driest July (44 mm).
Does it snow in Genoa in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.3 cm (2.5 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Genoa get in February?
About 123 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Genoa in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
