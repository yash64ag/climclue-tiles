Why Pescara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Pescara climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Pescara?
July, with an average of 24.9 °C (77 °F), day highs around 27.9 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 20.6 °C (69 °F).
What is the coldest month in Pescara?
January, with an average of 7.3 °C (45 °F), day highs around 10.1 °C (50 °F) and night lows around 4.3 °C (40 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Pescara?
November, with 99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 744 mm (29.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Pescara?
May, with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Pescara?
July, with about 300 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Pescara?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 11 cm (4.4 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Pescara?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August, September and October.
What type of climate does Pescara have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.2 × 11 km, that contains Pescara’s GeoNames location (42.458°N, 14.203°E; ClimaClue area sq-415-1942), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 119,554 (GeoNames).
