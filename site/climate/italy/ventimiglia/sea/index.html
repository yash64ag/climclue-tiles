Ventimiglia sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Liguria · Italy

The sea off Ventimiglia averages 24.0 °C (75 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.8 °C (55 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Ventimiglia sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ventimiglia?
In August, at about 24.0 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ventimiglia?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 12.8 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ventimiglia in February?
About 12.8 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Ventimiglia?
About 17.5 °C (63 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
