Martina Franca at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Martina Franca is 312th warmest (15.1 °C year average), 158th sunniest (about 2,419 hours of sunshine a year) and 482nd wettest (649 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fasano, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Martina Franca is 1.4 °C cooler in July and 2.1 °C cooler in February, with 68 mm more precipitation a year. Why Martina Franca has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
