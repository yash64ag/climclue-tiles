Paternò sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Sicily · Italy

The sea off Paternò averages 26.2 °C (79 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.7 °C (58 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Paternò sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Paternò?
In August, at about 26.2 °C (79 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Paternò?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 14.7 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in Paternò in February?
About 14.7 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Paternò?
About 19.6 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
