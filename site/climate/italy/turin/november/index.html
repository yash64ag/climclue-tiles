Turin weather in November

Average November weather in Turin, Italy: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Turin averages 11.2 °C (52 °F) days and 3.6 °C (38 °F) nights, with 69 mm (2.7 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 106 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Turin

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Turin. Compared with October: 6.0 °C cooler, 15 mm less precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. December is 4.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Turin in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Turin in November?
In November, Turin averages a day high of 11.2 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 3.6 °C (38 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 106 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Turin?
November is not one of Turin’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Turin in November?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (125 mm), the driest January (31 mm).
Does it snow in Turin in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Turin get in November?
About 106 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Turin in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
