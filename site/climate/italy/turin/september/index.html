How September compares in Turin

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Turin. Compared with August: 4.4 °C cooler, 21 mm more precipitation and 61 hours less sunshine. October is 5.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Turin in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Turin in September?
In September, Turin averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Turin?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Turin’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Turin in September?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (126 mm), the driest January (31 mm).
Does it snow in Turin in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Turin get in September?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Turin in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
