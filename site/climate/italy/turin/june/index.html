How June compares in Turin

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Turin. Compared with May: 3.7 °C warmer, 44 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. July is 2.9 °C warmer than June on average.

Turin in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Turin in June?
In June, Turin averages a day high of 24.3 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Turin?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Turin’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Turin in June?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (126 mm), the driest January (31 mm).
Does it snow in Turin in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Turin get in June?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Turin in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
