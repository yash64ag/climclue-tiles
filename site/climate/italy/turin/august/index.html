How August compares in Turin

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Turin. Compared with July: 0.7 °C cooler, 12 mm more precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. September is 4.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Turin in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Turin in August?
In August, Turin averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Turin?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Turin’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Turin in August?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (126 mm), the driest January (31 mm).
Does it snow in Turin in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Turin get in August?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Turin in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
