How December compares in Turin

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Turin. Compared with November: 4.1 °C cooler, 22 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. January is 0.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Turin in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Turin in December?
In December, Turin averages a day high of 6.8 °C (44 °F) and a night low of -0.7 °C (31 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 113 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Turin?
December is not one of Turin’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Turin in December?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (126 mm), the driest January (31 mm).
Does it snow in Turin in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 21 cm (8.1 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Turin get in December?
About 113 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Turin in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
