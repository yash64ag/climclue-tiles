How April compares in Turin

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Turin. Compared with March: 3.4 °C warmer, 58 mm more precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. May is 4.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Turin in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Turin in April?
In April, Turin averages a day high of 16.1 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 6.5 °C (44 °F), with 107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Turin?
April is not one of Turin’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Turin in April?
107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (125 mm), the driest January (31 mm).
Does it snow in Turin in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Turin get in April?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Turin in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
