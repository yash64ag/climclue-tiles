Turin at a glance

Among the 44 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Italy, Turin is 39th warmest (13.0 °C year average), 36th sunniest (about 1,977 hours of sunshine a year) and 26th wettest (835 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Novara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (84 km away), Turin is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 181 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.6 °C), averages 21.1 °C more than its coolest, January (2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 114 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Turin, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Turin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 114 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
