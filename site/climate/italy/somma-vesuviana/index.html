Somma Vesuviana at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Somma Vesuviana is 269th warmest (15.7 °C year average), 238th sunniest (about 2,330 hours of sunshine a year) and 38th wettest (1,198 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sant'Anastasia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Somma Vesuviana is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 85 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.2 °C), averages 15.6 °C more than its coolest, January (8.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 4.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 20 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Somma Vesuviana has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
