Tradate at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Tradate is 558th warmest (12.9 °C year average), 529th sunniest (about 1,935 hours of sunshine a year) and 16th wettest (1,331 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cassano Magnago, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Tradate is 0.2 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 85 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.3 °C), averages 20.4 °C more than its coolest, January (2.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 156 km from the sea.

Why Tradate has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 156 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tradate itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (45.708°N, 8.908°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-382-1889), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
