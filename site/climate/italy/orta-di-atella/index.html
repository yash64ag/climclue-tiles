Orta di Atella weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Orta di Atella, Italy (Campania), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Orta di Atella is warmest in August, with 30.5 °C (87 °F) days and 19.0 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 13.4 °C (56 °F) days and 4.9 °C nights. Most rain falls around November (138 mm); July is driest (21 mm).

Why Orta di Atella has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Orta di Atella itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.964°N, 14.269°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-430-1942), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
