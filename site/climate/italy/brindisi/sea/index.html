Brindisi sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Apulia · Italy

The sea off Brindisi averages 25.6 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.6 °C (56 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August.

Brindisi sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Brindisi?
In August, at about 25.6 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Brindisi?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August. The coolest month is February, at 13.6 °C (56 °F).
How warm is the sea in Brindisi in February?
About 13.6 °C (56 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Brindisi?
About 18.6 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
