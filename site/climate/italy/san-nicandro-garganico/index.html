San Nicandro Garganico at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, San Nicandro Garganico is 248th warmest (15.8 °C year average), 188th sunniest (about 2,362 hours of sunshine a year) and 485th wettest (646 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with San Giovanni Rotondo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), San Nicandro Garganico is 2.1 °C warmer in July and 3.5 °C warmer in February, with 56 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.0 °C), averages 17.0 °C more than its coolest, February (8.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 4.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Why San Nicandro Garganico has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.0 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
