Giulianova sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Abruzzo · Italy

The sea off Giulianova averages 25.5 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.4 °C (54 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August.

Giulianova sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Giulianova?
In August, at about 25.5 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Giulianova?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August. The coolest month is March, at 12.4 °C (54 °F).
How warm is the sea in Giulianova in March?
About 12.4 °C (54 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Giulianova?
About 18.0 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
