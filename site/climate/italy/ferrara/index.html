Ferrara at a glance

Among the 44 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Italy, Ferrara is 18th warmest (14.6 °C year average), 33rd sunniest (about 2,026 hours of sunshine a year) and 37th wettest (657 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bologna, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Ferrara is 0.9 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 79 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.8 °C), averages 22.1 °C more than its coolest, January (3.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ferrara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ferrara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Does it snow in Ferrara?
