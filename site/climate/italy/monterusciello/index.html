Monterusciello weather by month and climate

Monterusciello at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Monterusciello is 140th warmest (16.7 °C year average), 197th sunniest (about 2,341 hours of sunshine a year) and 307th wettest (860 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pozzuoli, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Monterusciello is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C warmer in February, with 15 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.5 °C), averages 14.5 °C more than its coolest, February (10.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.7 °C (42 °F), in February. The area lies about 8 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Monterusciello has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-431-1940), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
