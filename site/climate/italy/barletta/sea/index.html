Barletta sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Apulia · Italy

The sea off Barletta averages 25.7 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.2 °C (54 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August.

Barletta sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Barletta?
In August, at about 25.7 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Barletta?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August. The coolest month is February, at 12.2 °C (54 °F).
How warm is the sea in Barletta in February?
About 12.2 °C (54 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Barletta?
About 17.9 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
