Giarre sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Sicily · Italy

The sea off Giarre averages 24.3 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.8 °C (55 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Giarre sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Giarre?
In August, at about 24.3 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Giarre?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 12.8 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in Giarre in February?
About 12.8 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Giarre?
About 17.7 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Giarre nearby?
In August: Aci Castello (26.2 °C, 19 km), Misterbianco (26.2 °C, 28 km) and Catania (26.2 °C, 28 km).
