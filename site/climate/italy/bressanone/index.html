Bressanone at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Bressanone is the coolest (10.2 °C year average) and the 3rd least sunny (about 1,834 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Bolzano, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Bressanone is 3.1 °C cooler in July and 2.3 °C cooler in January, with 159 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.3 °C), averages 20.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 164 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bressanone, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bressanone has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 164 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.7 °C. The 10 km area around Bressanone, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 7.8 °C a year, 2.5 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bressanone itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (46.715°N, 11.656°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-372-1916), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
