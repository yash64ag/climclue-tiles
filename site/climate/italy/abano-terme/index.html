Abano Terme at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Abano Terme is 426th warmest (13.9 °C year average), 478th sunniest (about 1,979 hours of sunshine a year) and 296th wettest (868 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Abano Terme has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 47 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
