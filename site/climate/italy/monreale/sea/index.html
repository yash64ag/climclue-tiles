Monreale sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Sicily · Italy

The sea off Monreale averages 26.0 °C (79 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.6 °C (56 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Monreale sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Monreale?
In August, at about 26.0 °C (79 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Monreale?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 13.6 °C (56 °F).
How warm is the sea in Monreale in February?
About 13.6 °C (56 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Monreale?
About 19.0 °C (66 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Monreale nearby?
In August: Carini (26.7 °C, 11 km), Partinico (26.7 °C, 15 km) and Alcamo (26.7 °C, 31 km).
