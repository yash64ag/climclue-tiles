Eboli at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Eboli is 272nd warmest (15.7 °C year average), 191st sunniest (about 2,359 hours of sunshine a year) and 66th wettest (1,140 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Battipaglia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Eboli is 0.5 °C cooler in August and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 17 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.4 °C), averages 16.4 °C more than its coolest, January (8.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 28 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Eboli, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Eboli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 28 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.4 °C. The 10 km area around Eboli, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 14.1 °C a year, 1.6 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Eboli itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.617°N, 15.057°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-433-1950), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
