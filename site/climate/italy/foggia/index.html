Foggia at a glance

Among the 44 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Italy, Foggia is the driest (439 mm a year). Compared with Salerno, the nearest covered town with different numbers (108 km away), Foggia is 3.3 °C warmer in July and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 687 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.9 °C), averages 18.9 °C more than its coolest, January (8.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 3.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 43 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Foggia, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Foggia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 43 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
