San Giovanni la Punta at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, San Giovanni la Punta is 144th warmest (16.6 °C year average), 62nd sunniest (about 2,544 hours of sunshine a year) and 315th wettest (849 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why San Giovanni la Punta has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.7 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-464-1950), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
