Porto Sant'Elpidio sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · The Marches · Italy

The sea off Porto Sant'Elpidio averages 25.5 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 11.3 °C (52 °F) in March. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August.

Porto Sant'Elpidio sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Porto Sant'Elpidio?
In August, at about 25.5 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Porto Sant'Elpidio?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August. The coolest month is March, at 11.3 °C (52 °F).
How warm is the sea in Porto Sant'Elpidio in March?
About 11.3 °C (52 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Porto Sant'Elpidio?
About 17.5 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
