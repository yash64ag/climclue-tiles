Porto Sant'Elpidio at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Porto Sant'Elpidio is 225th warmest (16.0 °C year average), 312th sunniest (about 2,190 hours of sunshine a year) and 475th wettest (657 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Civitanova Marche, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Porto Sant'Elpidio is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C warmer in February, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.9 °C), averages 16.5 °C more than its coolest, February (8.4 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 4.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 8 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Porto Sant'Elpidio has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
