Monopoli at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Monopoli is 63rd warmest (17.3 °C year average), 128th sunniest (about 2,439 hours of sunshine a year) and 556th wettest (549 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fasano, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Monopoli is 0.4 °C warmer in August and 0.7 °C warmer in February, with 31 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.8 °C), averages 15.6 °C more than its coolest, February (10.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.3 °C (43 °F), in February. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Monopoli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-430-1972), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
