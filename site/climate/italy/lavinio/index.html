Lavinio at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Lavinio is 114th warmest (16.8 °C year average), 129th sunniest (about 2,439 hours of sunshine a year) and 347th wettest (811 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Anzio, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Lavinio is about as warm in August and 0.2 °C cooler in February, with 5 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.6 °C), averages 14.2 °C more than its coolest, February (10.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.9 °C (43 °F), in February. The area lies about 8 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Lavinio has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Lavinio itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.504°N, 12.590°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-424-1925), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
