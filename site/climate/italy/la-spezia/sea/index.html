La Spezia sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Liguria · Italy

The sea off La Spezia averages 24.5 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.8 °C (55 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

La Spezia sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in La Spezia?
In August, at about 24.5 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in La Spezia?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 12.8 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in La Spezia in February?
About 12.8 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in La Spezia?
About 17.9 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in La Spezia nearby?
In August: Viareggio (25.2 °C, 43 km), Massarosa (25.2 °C, 49 km) and Pisa (25.2 °C, 64 km).
