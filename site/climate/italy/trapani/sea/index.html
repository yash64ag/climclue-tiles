Trapani sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Sicily · Italy

The sea off Trapani averages 25.8 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.2 °C (58 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Trapani sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Trapani?
In August, at about 25.8 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Trapani?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 14.2 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in Trapani in February?
About 14.2 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Trapani?
About 19.1 °C (66 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Trapani nearby?
In August: Alcamo (26.7 °C, 38 km), Partinico (26.7 °C, 51 km) and Carini (26.7 °C, 58 km).
