How October compares in Milan

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Milan. Compared with September: 5.3 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 49 hours less sunshine. November is 6.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Milan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Milan in October?
In October, Milan averages a day high of 17.6 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 9.9 °C (50 °F), with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 134 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Milan?
October is not one of Milan’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Milan in October?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (113 mm), the driest February (42 mm).
Does it snow in Milan in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Milan get in October?
About 134 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Milan in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
