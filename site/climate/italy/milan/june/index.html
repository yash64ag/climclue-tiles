How June compares in Milan

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Milan. Compared with May: 3.8 °C warmer, 36 mm less precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. July is 2.8 °C warmer than June on average.

Milan in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Milan in June?
In June, Milan averages a day high of 26.2 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Milan?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Milan’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Milan in June?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (113 mm), the driest February (42 mm).
Does it snow in Milan in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Milan get in June?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Milan in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
