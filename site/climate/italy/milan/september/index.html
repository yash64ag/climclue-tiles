Milan weather in September

Average September weather in Milan, Italy: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Milan averages 24.4 °C (76 °F) days and 13.7 °C (57 °F) nights, with 113 mm (4.4 in) of rain over the month and about 183 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Milan

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Milan. Compared with August: 4.7 °C cooler, 18 mm more precipitation and 61 hours less sunshine. October is 5.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Milan in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Milan in September?
In September, Milan averages a day high of 24.4 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 13.7 °C (57 °F), with 113 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Milan?
September is not one of Milan’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Milan in September?
113 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is September (113 mm), the driest February (42 mm).
Does it snow in Milan in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Milan get in September?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Milan in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
