How February compares in Milan

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Milan. Compared with January: 1.8 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. March is 4.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Milan in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Milan in February?
In February, Milan averages a day high of 8.8 °C (48 °F) and a night low of 0.5 °C (33 °F), with 42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 101 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Milan?
February is not one of Milan’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Milan in February?
42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (113 mm), the driest February (42 mm).
Does it snow in Milan in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.0 cm (2.4 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Milan get in February?
About 101 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Milan in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
