How May compares in Milan

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Milan. Compared with April: 4.9 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. June is 3.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Milan in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Milan in May?
In May, Milan averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Milan?
May is not one of Milan’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Milan in May?
109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (113 mm), the driest February (42 mm).
Does it snow in Milan in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Milan get in May?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Milan in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
