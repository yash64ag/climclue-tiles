How January compares in Milan

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Milan. Compared with December: 0.7 °C cooler, 20 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. February is 1.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Milan in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Milan in January?
In January, Milan averages a day high of 7.0 °C (45 °F) and a night low of 1.3 °C (34 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 75 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Milan?
January is not one of Milan’s mild months; those are June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Milan in January?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (113 mm), the driest February (42 mm).
Does it snow in Milan in January?
Yes, on average: about 11 cm (4.3 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Milan get in January?
About 75 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Milan in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
