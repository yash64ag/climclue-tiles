How April compares in Milan

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Milan. Compared with March: 3.7 °C warmer, 36 mm more precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. May is 4.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Milan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Milan in April?
In April, Milan averages a day high of 17.1 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 7.2 °C (45 °F), with 95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Milan?
April is not one of Milan’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Milan in April?
95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (113 mm), the driest February (42 mm).
Does it snow in Milan in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Milan get in April?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Milan in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
