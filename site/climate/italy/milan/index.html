Milan at a glance

Among the 44 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Italy, Milan is the 4th least sunny (about 1,933 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Monza, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Milan is 0.4 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 55 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.6 °C), averages 21.8 °C more than its coolest, January (2.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 125 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station under 1 km from Milan measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 1.8 °C (3.2 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Best time to visit Milan

For mild weather, the best months to visit Milan are June, July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July and August.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (24.6 °C), August (23.9 °C) and June (21.9 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): February (42 mm), January (49 mm) and March (59 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (276 h), August (244 h) and June (235 h).

Why Milan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 125 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

What is the coldest month in Milan?
January, with an average of 2.9 °C (37 °F), day highs around 6.6 °C (44 °F) and night lows around -0.9 °C (30 °F).

Does it snow in Milan?
