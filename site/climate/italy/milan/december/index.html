How December compares in Milan

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Milan. Compared with November: 4.2 °C cooler, 33 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. January is 0.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Milan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Milan in December?
In December, Milan averages a day high of 7.0 °C (45 °F) and a night low of 0.1 °C (32 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 74 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Milan?
December is not one of Milan’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Milan in December?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (113 mm), the driest February (42 mm).
Does it snow in Milan in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 11 cm (4.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Milan get in December?
About 74 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Milan in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
