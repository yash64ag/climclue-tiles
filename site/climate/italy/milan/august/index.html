How August compares in Milan

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Milan. Compared with July: 0.8 °C cooler, 22 mm more precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. September is 4.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Milan in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Milan in August?
In August, Milan averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 18.5 °C (65 °F), with 96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Milan?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Milan’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Milan in August?
96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (113 mm), the driest February (42 mm).
Does it snow in Milan in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Milan get in August?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Milan in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
