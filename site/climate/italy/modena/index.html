Modena at a glance

Among the 44 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Italy, Modena is 20th warmest (14.4 °C year average), 28th sunniest (about 2,096 hours of sunshine a year) and 38th wettest (637 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Reggio nell'Emilia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Modena is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 31 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.4 °C), averages 21.9 °C more than its coolest, January (3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 106 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Modena, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Modena has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 106 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
