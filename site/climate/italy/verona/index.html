Verona at a glance

Among the 44 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Italy, Verona is 29th warmest (14.0 °C year average), 20th sunniest (about 2,155 hours of sunshine a year) and 22nd wettest (883 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vicenza, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Verona is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 162 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.6 °C), averages 21.3 °C more than its coolest, January (3.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 110 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 12 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 112% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Verona has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 110 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
