Piacenza at a glance

Among the 44 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Italy, Piacenza is 25th warmest (14.1 °C year average), 29th sunniest (about 2,080 hours of sunshine a year) and 28th wettest (763 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Parma, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Piacenza is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 55 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.1 °C), averages 21.8 °C more than its coolest, January (3.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 94 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Piacenza, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Piacenza has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 94 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
