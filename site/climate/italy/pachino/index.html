Pachino weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Pachino, Italy (Sicily), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Pachino is warmest in August, with 30.2 °C (86 °F) days and 21.6 °C nights, and coolest in February, with 16.1 °C (61 °F) days and 9.0 °C nights. Most rain falls around December (124 mm); July is driest (4 mm).

Pachino at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Pachino is the sunniest (about 2,704 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Rosolini, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Pachino is about as warm in August and 0.9 °C warmer in February, with 15 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.9 °C), averages 13.4 °C more than its coolest, February (12.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.0 °C (48 °F), in February. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Pachino has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pachino itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.715°N, 15.090°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-472-1950), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
