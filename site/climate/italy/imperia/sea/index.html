Imperia sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Liguria · Italy

The sea off Imperia averages 24.2 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.0 °C (55 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Imperia sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Imperia?
In August, at about 24.2 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Imperia?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 13.0 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in Imperia in February?
About 13.0 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Imperia?
About 17.7 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
