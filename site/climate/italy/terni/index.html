Terni at a glance

Among the 44 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Italy, Terni is 23rd warmest (14.4 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 2,261 hours of sunshine a year) and 12th wettest (985 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Perugia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (64 km away), Terni is 1.6 °C warmer in July and 1.3 °C warmer in January, with 88 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.4 °C), averages 19.1 °C more than its coolest, January (5.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 88 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Terni, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Terni has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 88 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
