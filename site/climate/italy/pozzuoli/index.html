Pozzuoli at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Pozzuoli is 150th warmest (16.6 °C year average), 197th sunniest (about 2,341 hours of sunshine a year) and 290th wettest (874 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Monterusciello, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Pozzuoli is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C cooler in February, with 15 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.4 °C), averages 14.5 °C more than its coolest, February (10.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.6 °C (42 °F), in February. The area lies about 8 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Pozzuoli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pozzuoli itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.844°N, 14.095°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-431-1940), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
