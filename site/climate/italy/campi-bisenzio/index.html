Campi Bisenzio at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Campi Bisenzio is 343rd warmest (14.5 °C year average), 411th sunniest (about 2,094 hours of sunshine a year) and 193rd wettest (956 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sesto Fiorentino, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Campi Bisenzio is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 46 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.4 °C), averages 19.3 °C more than its coolest, January (5.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 80 km from the sea.

Why Campi Bisenzio has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 80 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
