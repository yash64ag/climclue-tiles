Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Quartu Sant'Elena is 41st warmest (17.6 °C year average), 60th sunniest (about 2,547 hours of sunshine a year) and 581st wettest (508 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Quartu Sant'Elena has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
