Why Palermo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Palermo climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Palermo?
August, with an average of 26.1 °C (79 °F), day highs around 28.9 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 23.2 °C (74 °F).
What is the coldest month in Palermo?
February, with an average of 11.4 °C (53 °F), day highs around 13.4 °C (56 °F) and night lows around 8.9 °C (48 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Palermo?
November, with 110 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 754 mm (29.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Palermo?
July, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Palermo?
July, with about 333 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Palermo?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Palermo?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, September and October.
What type of climate does Palermo have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.8 × 11 km, that contains Palermo’s GeoNames location (38.117°N, 13.364°E; ClimaClue area sq-458-1933), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 648,260 (GeoNames).
