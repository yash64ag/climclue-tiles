How May compares in Palermo

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Palermo. Compared with April: 3.7 °C warmer, 29 mm less precipitation and 57 hours more sunshine. June is 3.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Palermo in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Palermo in May?
In May, Palermo averages a day high of 22.6 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 267 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Palermo?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Palermo’s mild months (April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Palermo in May?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (101 mm), the driest July (5 mm).
Does it snow in Palermo in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Palermo get in May?
About 267 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Palermo in May?
About 18.1 °C (65 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (26.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Palermo in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
