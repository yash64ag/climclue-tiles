Palermo weather in December

Average December weather in Palermo, Italy: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Palermo averages 15.9 °C (61 °F) days and 11.1 °C (52 °F) nights, with 100 mm (3.9 in) of rain over the month and about 126 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Palermo

December is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Palermo. Compared with November: 3.1 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. January is 1.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Palermo in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Palermo in December?
In December, Palermo averages a day high of 15.9 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 11.1 °C (52 °F), with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 126 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Palermo?
December is not one of Palermo’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Palermo in December?
100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (101 mm), the driest July (5 mm).
Does it snow in Palermo in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Palermo get in December?
About 126 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Palermo in December?
About 16.3 °C (61 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (26.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Palermo in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
