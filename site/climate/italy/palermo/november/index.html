Palermo weather in November

Average November weather in Palermo, Italy: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Palermo averages 19.3 °C (67 °F) days and 13.8 °C (57 °F) nights, with 101 mm (4.0 in) of rain over the month and about 155 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Palermo

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Palermo. Compared with October: 4.1 °C cooler, 12 mm more precipitation and 45 hours less sunshine. December is 3.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Palermo in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Palermo in November?
In November, Palermo averages a day high of 19.3 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Palermo?
November is not one of Palermo’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Palermo in November?
101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is November (101 mm), the driest July (5 mm).
Does it snow in Palermo in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Palermo get in November?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Palermo in November?
About 19.2 °C (67 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (26.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Palermo in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
