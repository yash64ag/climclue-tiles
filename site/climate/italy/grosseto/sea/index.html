Grosseto sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Tuscany · Italy

The sea off Grosseto averages 24.9 °C (77 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.8 °C (57 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Grosseto sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Grosseto?
In August, at about 24.9 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Grosseto?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 13.8 °C (57 °F).
How warm is the sea in Grosseto in February?
About 13.8 °C (57 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Grosseto?
About 18.6 °C (66 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
