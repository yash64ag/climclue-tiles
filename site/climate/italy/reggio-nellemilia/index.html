Reggio nell'Emilia at a glance

Among the 44 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Italy, Reggio nell'Emilia is 26th warmest (14.0 °C year average), 26th sunniest (about 2,113 hours of sunshine a year) and 35th wettest (668 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Modena, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Reggio nell'Emilia is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 31 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.9 °C), averages 21.5 °C more than its coolest, January (3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 91 km from the sea.

Why Reggio nell'Emilia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 91 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
