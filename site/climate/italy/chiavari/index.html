Chiavari at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Chiavari is 280th warmest (15.5 °C year average), 405th sunniest (about 2,098 hours of sunshine a year) and 17th wettest (1,296 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sestri Levante, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Chiavari is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 11 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.5 °C), averages 14.8 °C more than its coolest, January (8.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.7 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Chiavari has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.8 °C. The 10 km area around Chiavari, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 14.2 °C a year, 1.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
