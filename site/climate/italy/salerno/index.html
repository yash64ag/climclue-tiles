Why Salerno has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Salerno climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Salerno?
August, with an average of 23.9 °C (75 °F), day highs around 26.3 °C (79 °F) and night lows around 21.1 °C (70 °F).
What is the coldest month in Salerno?
February, with an average of 8.8 °C (48 °F), day highs around 10.9 °C (52 °F) and night lows around 6.3 °C (43 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Salerno?
November, with 170 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,126 mm (44.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Salerno?
July, with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Salerno?
July, with about 306 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Salerno?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 5.3 cm (2.1 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Salerno?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July and August.
What type of climate does Salerno have?
Mediterranean, hot summer (Köppen Csa). Hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.4 × 11 km, that contains Salerno’s GeoNames location (40.675°N, 14.793°E; ClimaClue area sq-433-1947), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 125,797 (GeoNames).
