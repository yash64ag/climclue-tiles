Salerno at a glance

Among the 44 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Italy, Salerno is the 4th wettest (1,126 mm a year). Compared with Naples, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Salerno is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 0.5 °C cooler in February, with 145 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.9 °C), averages 15.1 °C more than its coolest, February (8.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.3 °C (43 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Salerno, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Salerno has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
