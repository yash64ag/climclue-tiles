Livorno at a glance

Among the 44 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Italy, Livorno is 15th warmest (15.6 °C year average), 15th sunniest (about 2,219 hours of sunshine a year) and 24th wettest (861 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pisa, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Livorno is 0.7 °C warmer in August and 2.1 °C warmer in January, with 17 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.1 °C), averages 15.9 °C more than its coolest, January (8.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.5 °C (42 °F), in February. The area lies about 8 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Livorno, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Livorno has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
