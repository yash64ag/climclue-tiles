Gallipoli sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Apulia · Italy

The sea off Gallipoli averages 25.7 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.1 °C (56 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Gallipoli sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Gallipoli?
In August, at about 25.7 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Gallipoli?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 13.1 °C (56 °F).
How warm is the sea in Gallipoli in February?
About 13.1 °C (56 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Gallipoli?
About 18.5 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
