Malnate weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Malnate, Italy (Lombardy), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Malnate is warmest in July, with 28.4 °C (83 °F) days and 17.4 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 6.7 °C (44 °F) days and -0.9 °C nights. Most rain falls around May (186 mm); February is driest (59 mm).

Malnate at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Malnate is 570th warmest (12.6 °C year average), 548th sunniest (about 1,932 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (1,481 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Varese, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Malnate is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 123 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.9 °C), averages 19.9 °C more than its coolest, January (2.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 156 km from the sea.

Why Malnate has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 156 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Malnate itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (45.794°N, 8.881°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-382-1888), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
