Rossano Stazione at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Rossano Stazione is 46th warmest (17.4 °C year average), 157th sunniest (about 2,419 hours of sunshine a year) and 423rd wettest (713 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Corigliano Scalo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Rossano Stazione is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C cooler in February, with 35 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.3 °C), averages 16.3 °C more than its coolest, February (10.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.2 °C (45 °F), in February. The area lies about 20 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Rossano Stazione has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.3 °C. The 10 km area around Rossano Stazione, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 15.4 °C a year, 2.0 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-444-1966), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
