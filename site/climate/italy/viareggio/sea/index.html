Viareggio sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Tuscany · Italy

The sea off Viareggio averages 25.2 °C (77 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.2 °C (56 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August.

Viareggio sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Viareggio?
In August, at about 25.2 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Viareggio?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August. The coolest month is February, at 13.2 °C (56 °F).
How warm is the sea in Viareggio in February?
About 13.2 °C (56 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Viareggio?
About 18.4 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
