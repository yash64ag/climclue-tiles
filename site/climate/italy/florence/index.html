Florence at a glance

Among the 44 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Italy, Florence is 24th warmest (14.3 °C year average), 25th sunniest (about 2,116 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (1,045 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Prato, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Florence is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 128 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.1 °C), averages 19.1 °C more than its coolest, January (5.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 83 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Florence, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Florence

For mild weather, the best months to visit Florence are May, June, July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May, June, July and August.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (24.1 °C), August (23.9 °C) and June (21.1 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): July (38 mm), August (66 mm) and June (67 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (298 h), August (268 h) and June (254 h).

Why Florence has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 83 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Florence climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Florence?
July, with an average of 24.1 °C (75 °F), day highs around 29.4 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 17.7 °C (64 °F).
What is the coldest month in Florence?
January, with an average of 5.0 °C (41 °F), day highs around 8.8 °C (48 °F) and night lows around 1.4 °C (35 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Florence?
November, with 135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,045 mm (41.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Florence?
July, with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Florence?
July, with about 298 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Florence?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 8.7 cm (3.4 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
