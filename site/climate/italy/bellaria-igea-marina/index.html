Bellaria-Igea Marina at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Bellaria-Igea Marina is 283rd warmest (15.4 °C year average), 390th sunniest (about 2,111 hours of sunshine a year) and 461st wettest (671 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cesenatico, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Bellaria-Igea Marina is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 23 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.6 °C), averages 19.6 °C more than its coolest, January (6.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Bellaria-Igea Marina has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
