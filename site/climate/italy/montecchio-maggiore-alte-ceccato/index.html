Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Montecchio Maggiore-Alte Ceccato is 505th warmest (13.6 °C year average), 432nd sunniest (about 2,053 hours of sunshine a year) and 134th wettest (1,036 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Arzignano, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Montecchio Maggiore-Alte Ceccato is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 178 mm less precipitation a year. Why Montecchio Maggiore-Alte Ceccato has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.
