Sava at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Sava is 80th warmest (17.1 °C year average), 121st sunniest (about 2,444 hours of sunshine a year) and 515th wettest (610 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Manduria, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Sava is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C cooler in February, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.8 °C), averages 16.1 °C more than its coolest, February (9.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.9 °C (43 °F), in February. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sava, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Sava has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sava itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.402°N, 17.553°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-435-1975), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
