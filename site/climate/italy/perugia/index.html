Why Perugia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 101 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Perugia climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Perugia?
July, with an average of 22.9 °C (73 °F), day highs around 28.4 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 16.2 °C (61 °F).
What is the coldest month in Perugia?
January, with an average of 4.0 °C (39 °F), day highs around 7.7 °C (46 °F) and night lows around 0.2 °C (32 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Perugia?
November, with 112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 897 mm (35.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Perugia?
July, with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Perugia?
July, with about 306 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Perugia?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 22 cm (8.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Perugia?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July and August.
What type of climate does Perugia have?
Mediterranean, hot summer (Köppen Csa). Hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.1 × 11 km, that contains Perugia’s GeoNames location (43.112°N, 12.389°E; ClimaClue area sq-408-1923), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 120,137 (GeoNames).
