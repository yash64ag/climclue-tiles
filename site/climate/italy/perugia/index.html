Perugia at a glance

Among the 44 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Italy, Perugia is the 4th coolest (12.9 °C year average). Compared with Arezzo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Perugia is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 49 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.9 °C), averages 18.8 °C more than its coolest, January (4.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 0.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 101 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Perugia, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Perugia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 101 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
