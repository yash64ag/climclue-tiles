Albenga sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Liguria · Italy

The sea off Albenga averages 23.8 °C (75 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.5 °C (55 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to September.

Albenga sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Albenga?
In August, at about 23.8 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Albenga?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to September. The coolest month is February, at 12.5 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in Albenga in February?
About 12.5 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Albenga?
About 17.3 °C (63 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Albenga nearby?
In August: Genoa (24.4 °C, 70 km).
