Albenga at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Albenga is 270th warmest (15.7 °C year average), 298th sunniest (about 2,248 hours of sunshine a year) and 417th wettest (720 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Imperia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Albenga is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.6 °C), averages 14.8 °C more than its coolest, January (8.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.2 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 8 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Albenga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-399-1882), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
