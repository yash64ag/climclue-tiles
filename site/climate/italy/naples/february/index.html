How February compares in Naples

February is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Naples. Compared with January: 0.2 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. March is 1.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Naples in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Naples in February?
In February, Naples averages a day high of 11.5 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 6.9 °C (44 °F), with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 119 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Naples?
February is not one of Naples’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Naples in February?
83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (159 mm), the driest July (21 mm).
Does it snow in Naples in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Naples get in February?
About 119 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Naples in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
