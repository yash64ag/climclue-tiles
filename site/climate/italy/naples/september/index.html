How September compares in Naples

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Naples. Compared with August: 2.9 °C cooler, 67 mm more precipitation and 60 hours less sunshine. October is 3.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Naples in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Naples in September?
In September, Naples averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 16.6 °C (62 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Naples?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Naples’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Naples in September?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (152 mm), the driest July (20 mm).
Does it snow in Naples in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Naples get in September?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Naples in September?
About 23.8 °C (75 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Naples in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
