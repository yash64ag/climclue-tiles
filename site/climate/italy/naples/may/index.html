How May compares in Naples

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Naples. Compared with April: 4.0 °C warmer, 44 mm less precipitation and 55 hours more sunshine. June is 3.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Naples in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Naples in May?
In May, Naples averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 14.6 °C (58 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 242 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Naples?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Naples’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Naples in May?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (159 mm), the driest July (21 mm).
Does it snow in Naples in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Naples get in May?
About 242 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Naples in May?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
