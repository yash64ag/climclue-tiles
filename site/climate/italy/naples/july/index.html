How July compares in Naples

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Naples. Compared with June: 2.8 °C warmer, 13 mm less precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. August is 0.5 °C warmer than July on average.

Naples in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Naples in July?
In July, Naples averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 18.7 °C (66 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 309 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Naples?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Naples’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Naples in July?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is November (152 mm), the driest July (20 mm).
Does it snow in Naples in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Naples get in July?
About 309 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Naples in July?
About 24.7 °C (76 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (25.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Naples in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
