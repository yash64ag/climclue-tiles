How December compares in Naples

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Naples. Compared with November: 3.1 °C cooler, 33 mm less precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. January is 1.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Naples in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Naples in December?
In December, Naples averages a day high of 15.5 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 7.3 °C (45 °F), with 119 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 104 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Naples?
December is not one of Naples’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Naples in December?
119 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (152 mm), the driest July (20 mm).
Does it snow in Naples in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Naples get in December?
About 104 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Naples in December?
About 16.3 °C (61 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Naples in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
