How October compares in Naples

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Naples. Compared with September: 3.3 °C cooler, 21 mm more precipitation and 46 hours less sunshine. November is 4.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Naples in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Naples in October?
In October, Naples averages a day high of 20.7 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 16.3 °C (61 °F), with 113 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Naples?
October is not one of Naples’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Naples in October?
113 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (152 mm), the driest July (20 mm).
Does it snow in Naples in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Naples get in October?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Naples in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
