Bergamo at a glance

Among the 44 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Italy, Bergamo is the 3rd coolest (12.8 °C year average). Compared with Monza, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Bergamo is 1.0 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 36 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.3 °C), averages 21.1 °C more than its coolest, January (2.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 158 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bergamo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Bergamo

For mild weather, the best months to visit Bergamo are June, July and August.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): June, July and August.

Why Bergamo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 158 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
