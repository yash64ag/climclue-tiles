Novara at a glance

Among the 44 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Italy, Novara is the 5th least sunny (about 1,953 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Milan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Novara is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 31 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.3 °C), averages 21.6 °C more than its coolest, January (2.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 123 km from the sea.

Why Novara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 123 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
