Comiso at a glance

Among the 598 towns ClimaClue covers in Italy, Comiso is 77th warmest (17.2 °C year average), 9th sunniest (about 2,650 hours of sunshine a year) and 439th wettest (699 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Vittoria, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Comiso is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.5 °C cooler in February, with 6 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.2 °C), averages 14.6 °C more than its coolest, February (10.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.2 °C (45 °F), in February. The area lies about 28 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Comiso, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Comiso has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 28 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.6 °C. The 10 km area around Comiso, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 15.9 °C a year, 1.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Comiso itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.949°N, 14.607°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-470-1946), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
