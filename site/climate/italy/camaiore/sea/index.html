Camaiore sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Tuscany · Italy

The sea off Camaiore averages 23.9 °C (75 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 11.7 °C (53 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to September.

Camaiore sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Camaiore?
In August, at about 23.9 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Camaiore?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to September. The coolest month is February, at 11.7 °C (53 °F).
How warm is the sea in Camaiore in February?
About 11.7 °C (53 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Camaiore?
About 17.0 °C (63 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Camaiore nearby?
In August: Viareggio (25.2 °C, 9 km), Massarosa (25.2 °C, 9 km) and Pisa (25.2 °C, 27 km).
