Reggio Calabria sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Calabria · Italy

The sea off Reggio Calabria averages 24.7 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.6 °C (55 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Reggio Calabria sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Reggio Calabria?
In August, at about 24.7 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Reggio Calabria?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 12.6 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in Reggio Calabria in February?
About 12.6 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Reggio Calabria?
About 17.9 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Reggio Calabria nearby?
In August: Messina (26.6 °C, 13 km), Milazzo (26.6 °C, 39 km) and Barcellona Pozzo di Gotto (26.6 °C, 39 km).
