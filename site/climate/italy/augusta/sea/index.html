Augusta sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Sicily · Italy

The sea off Augusta averages 26.0 °C (79 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.5 °C (58 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Augusta sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Augusta?
In August, at about 26.0 °C (79 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Augusta?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 14.5 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in Augusta in February?
About 14.5 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Augusta?
About 19.4 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
