Jericho at a glance

Among the 45 towns ClimaClue covers in the Palestinian Territory, Jericho is the warmest (22.4 °C year average). Compared with Ma‘ale Adummim, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Jericho is 3.2 °C warmer in July and 4.2 °C warmer in January, with 94 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.7 °C), averages 15.6 °C more than its coolest, January (14.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.0 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 79 km from the sea.

Station check: a NOAA weather station 25 km from Jericho measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from these (temperature by up to 5.7 °C (10.3 °F) in one month). One station can differ from modelled values (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Jericho has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 79 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jericho itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (31.867°N, 35.450°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-521-2154), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
