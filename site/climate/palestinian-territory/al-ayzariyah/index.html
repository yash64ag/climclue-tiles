Al ‘Ayzarīyah at a glance

Among the 45 towns ClimaClue covers in the Palestinian Territory, Al ‘Ayzarīyah is 25th warmest (18.1 °C year average), 26th sunniest (about 3,334 hours of sunshine a year) and 24th wettest (578 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ma‘ale Adummim, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Al ‘Ayzarīyah is 0.6 °C cooler in August and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 50 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.1 °C), averages 16.9 °C more than its coolest, January (9.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.0 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 66 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 109% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Al ‘Ayzarīyah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 66 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
