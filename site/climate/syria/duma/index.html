Dūmā at a glance

Among the 13 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Syria, Dūmā is the 3rd sunniest (about 3,106 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (200 mm a year). Compared with Damascus, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Dūmā is 2.0 °C warmer in July and 1.3 °C warmer in January, with 127 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.3 °C), averages 21.0 °C more than its coolest, January (7.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 100 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dūmā, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dūmā has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 100 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
