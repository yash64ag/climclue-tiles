Ar Raqqah at a glance

Among the 13 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Syria, Ar Raqqah is the 2nd warmest (20.0 °C year average), the sunniest (about 3,157 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (186 mm a year). Compared with Deir ez-Zor, the nearest covered town with different numbers (123 km away), Ar Raqqah is 1.3 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 41 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (32.3 °C), averages 25.0 °C more than its coolest, January (7.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 271 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ar Raqqah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ar Raqqah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 271 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
