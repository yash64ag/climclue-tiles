How December compares in Ar Raqqah

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Ar Raqqah. Compared with November: 4.9 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 42 hours less sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Ar Raqqah in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ar Raqqah in December?
In December, Ar Raqqah averages a day high of 13.2 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 4.1 °C (39 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ar Raqqah?
December is not one of Ar Raqqah’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ar Raqqah in December?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (36 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Ar Raqqah in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ar Raqqah get in December?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ar Raqqah in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
