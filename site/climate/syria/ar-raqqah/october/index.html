Ar Raqqah in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ar Raqqah in October?
In October, Ar Raqqah averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Ar Raqqah?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Ar Raqqah’s mild months (April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ar Raqqah in October?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (36 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Ar Raqqah in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ar Raqqah get in October?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ar Raqqah in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
