How May compares in Ar Raqqah

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Ar Raqqah. Compared with April: 5.7 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 69 hours more sunshine. June is 4.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Ar Raqqah in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ar Raqqah in May?
In May, Ar Raqqah averages a day high of 32.6 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 17.2 °C (63 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 316 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Ar Raqqah?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Ar Raqqah’s mild months (April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ar Raqqah in May?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (36 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Ar Raqqah in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ar Raqqah get in May?
About 316 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ar Raqqah in May?
