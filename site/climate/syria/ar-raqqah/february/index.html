How February compares in Ar Raqqah

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Ar Raqqah. Compared with January: 1.9 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. March is 4.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Ar Raqqah in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ar Raqqah in February?
In February, Ar Raqqah averages a day high of 13.9 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 3.6 °C (38 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Ar Raqqah?
February is not one of Ar Raqqah’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ar Raqqah in February?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (36 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Ar Raqqah in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.6 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ar Raqqah get in February?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ar Raqqah in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
