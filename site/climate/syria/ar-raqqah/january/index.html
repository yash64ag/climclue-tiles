How January compares in Ar Raqqah

January is the coolest month, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Ar Raqqah. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, 11 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. February is 1.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Ar Raqqah in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ar Raqqah in January?
In January, Ar Raqqah averages a day high of 11.7 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 2.6 °C (37 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Ar Raqqah?
January is not one of Ar Raqqah’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ar Raqqah in January?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (36 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Ar Raqqah in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.7 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ar Raqqah get in January?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ar Raqqah in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
