How January compares in Latakia

January is the coolest month, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Latakia. Compared with December: 1.5 °C cooler, 21 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. February is 0.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Latakia in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Latakia in January?
In January, Latakia averages a day high of 14.3 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 10.1 °C (50 °F), with 209 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Latakia?
January is not one of Latakia’s mild months; those are April, May, June and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Latakia in January?
209 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (209 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Latakia in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Latakia get in January?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Latakia in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
