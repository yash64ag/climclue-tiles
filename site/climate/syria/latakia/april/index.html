How April compares in Latakia

April is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Latakia. Compared with March: 2.6 °C warmer, 47 mm less precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. May is 3.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Latakia in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Latakia in April?
In April, Latakia averages a day high of 19.4 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Latakia?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Latakia’s mild months (April, May, June and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Latakia in April?
52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (209 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Latakia in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Latakia get in April?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Latakia in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
