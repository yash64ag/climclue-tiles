How November compares in Latakia

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Latakia. Compared with October: 5.0 °C cooler, 49 mm more precipitation and 55 hours less sunshine. December is 3.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Latakia in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Latakia in November?
In November, Latakia averages a day high of 20.1 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 127 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Latakia?
November is not one of Latakia’s mild months; those are April, May, June and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Latakia in November?
127 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (209 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Latakia in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Latakia get in November?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Latakia in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
