How February compares in Latakia

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Latakia. Compared with January: 0.5 °C warmer, 67 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. March is 1.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Latakia in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Latakia in February?
In February, Latakia averages a day high of 14.8 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 10.4 °C (51 °F), with 142 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Latakia?
February is not one of Latakia’s mild months; those are April, May, June and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Latakia in February?
142 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (209 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Latakia in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Latakia get in February?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Latakia in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
