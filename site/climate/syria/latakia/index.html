Latakia at a glance

Among the 13 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Syria, Latakia is the least sunny (about 2,848 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (956 mm a year). Compared with Ţarţūs, the nearest covered town with different numbers (72 km away), Latakia is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 326 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.4 °C), averages 14.1 °C more than its coolest, January (12.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.1 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 131% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Latakia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
