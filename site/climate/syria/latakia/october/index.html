How October compares in Latakia

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Latakia. Compared with September: 2.7 °C cooler, 60 mm more precipitation and 42 hours less sunshine. November is 5.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Latakia in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Latakia in October?
In October, Latakia averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 242 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Latakia?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Latakia’s mild months (April, May, June and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Latakia in October?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (209 mm), the driest August (4 mm).
Does it snow in Latakia in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Latakia get in October?
About 242 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Latakia in October?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
