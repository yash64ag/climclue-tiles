How August compares in Latakia

August is the warmest month, the driest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Latakia. Compared with July: 0.8 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. September is 1.5 °C cooler than August on average.

Latakia in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Latakia in August?
In August, Latakia averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 316 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Latakia?
August is not one of Latakia’s mild months; those are April, May, June and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Latakia in August?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (208 mm), the driest August (3 mm).
Does it snow in Latakia in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Latakia get in August?
About 316 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Latakia in August?
About 27.8 °C (82 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (27.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Latakia in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
