How January compares in Homs

January is the coolest month, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Homs. Compared with December: 1.5 °C cooler, 21 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. February is 1.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Homs in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Homs in January?
In January, Homs averages a day high of 12.3 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 2.7 °C (37 °F), with 88 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Homs?
January is not one of Homs’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Homs in January?
88 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (88 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Homs in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.6 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Homs get in January?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Homs in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
