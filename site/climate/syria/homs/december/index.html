How December compares in Homs

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Homs. Compared with November: 4.5 °C cooler, 18 mm more precipitation and 52 hours less sunshine. January is 1.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Homs in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Homs in December?
In December, Homs averages a day high of 14.1 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Homs?
December is not one of Homs’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Homs in December?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (89 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Homs in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Homs get in December?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Homs in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
