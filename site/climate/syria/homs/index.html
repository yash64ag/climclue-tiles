Homs at a glance

Among the 13 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Syria, Homs is the 3rd coolest (17.5 °C year average). Compared with Ḩamāh, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Homs is 1.4 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 61 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.6 °C), averages 19.2 °C more than its coolest, January (7.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 82 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Homs, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Homs has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 82 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
