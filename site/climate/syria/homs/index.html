Why Homs has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 82 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Homs climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Homs?
July, with an average of 26.6 °C (80 °F), day highs around 33.4 °C (92 °F) and night lows around 19.8 °C (68 °F).
What is the coldest month in Homs?
January, with an average of 7.5 °C (45 °F), day highs around 12.3 °C (54 °F) and night lows around 2.7 °C (37 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Homs?
January, with 88 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 405 mm (15.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Homs?
August, with 0 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Homs?
July, with about 377 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Homs?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 5.0 cm (2.0 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Homs?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, September and October.
What type of climate does Homs have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.1 × 11 km, that contains Homs’s GeoNames location (34.724°N, 36.726°E; ClimaClue area sq-492-2167), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 775,404 (GeoNames).
