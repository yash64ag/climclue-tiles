How May compares in Homs

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Homs. Compared with April: 4.3 °C warmer, 12 mm less precipitation and 73 hours more sunshine. June is 3.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Homs in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Homs in May?
In May, Homs averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 13.4 °C (56 °F), with 14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 317 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Homs?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Homs’s mild months (April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Homs in May?
14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (89 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Homs in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Homs get in May?
About 317 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Homs in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
