How November compares in Homs

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Homs. Compared with October: 6.5 °C cooler, 24 mm more precipitation and 61 hours less sunshine. December is 4.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Homs in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Homs in November?
In November, Homs averages a day high of 19.3 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 7.5 °C (46 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Homs?
November is not one of Homs’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Homs in November?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (89 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Homs in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Homs get in November?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Homs in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
