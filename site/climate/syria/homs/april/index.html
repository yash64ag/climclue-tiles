How April compares in Homs

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Homs. Compared with March: 4.5 °C warmer, 29 mm less precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. May is 4.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Homs in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Homs in April?
In April, Homs averages a day high of 22.9 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 9.6 °C (49 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Homs?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Homs’s mild months (April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Homs in April?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (88 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Homs in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Homs get in April?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Homs in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
