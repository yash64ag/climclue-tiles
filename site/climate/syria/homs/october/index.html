How October compares in Homs

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Homs. Compared with September: 4.2 °C cooler, 23 mm more precipitation and 52 hours less sunshine. November is 6.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Homs in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Homs in October?
In October, Homs averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 250 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Homs?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Homs’s mild months (April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Homs in October?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (88 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Homs in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Homs get in October?
About 250 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Homs in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
