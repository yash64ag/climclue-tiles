Al Bāb at a glance

Among the 89 towns ClimaClue covers in Syria, Al Bāb is 35th warmest (18.1 °C year average), 41st sunniest (about 3,082 hours of sunshine a year) and 42nd wettest (397 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tādif, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Al Bāb is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 19 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.6 °C), averages 23.4 °C more than its coolest, January (6.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 130 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Al Bāb, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Al Bāb has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 130 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Al Bāb itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.371°N, 37.516°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-476-2175), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
