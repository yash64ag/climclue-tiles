An Nabk weather by month and climate

An Nabk at a glance

Among the 89 towns ClimaClue covers in Syria, An Nabk is the 4th coolest (14.4 °C year average). Compared with Yabrūd, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), An Nabk is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.0 °C), averages 21.5 °C more than its coolest, January (3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 100 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of An Nabk, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why An Nabk has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 100 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for An Nabk itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.024°N, 36.728°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-499-2167), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
