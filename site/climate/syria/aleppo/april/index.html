How April compares in Aleppo

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Aleppo. Compared with March: 4.8 °C warmer, 17 mm less precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. May is 5.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Aleppo in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aleppo in April?
In April, Aleppo averages a day high of 22.1 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 9.8 °C (50 °F), with 37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Aleppo?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Aleppo’s mild months (April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aleppo in April?
37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (67 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Aleppo in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aleppo get in April?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aleppo in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
