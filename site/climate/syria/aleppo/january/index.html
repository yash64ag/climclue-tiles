How January compares in Aleppo

January is the coolest month, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Aleppo. Compared with December: 1.3 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. February is 1.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Of the 13 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Syria, Aleppo ranks 9th warmest (average temperature), 13th sunniest and 8th wettest in January.

Aleppo in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aleppo in January?
In January, Aleppo averages a day high of 10.4 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 2.1 °C (36 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 124 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Aleppo?
January is not one of Aleppo’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aleppo in January?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (67 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Aleppo in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.7 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Aleppo get in January?
About 124 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aleppo in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
