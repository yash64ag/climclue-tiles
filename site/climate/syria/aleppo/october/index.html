How October compares in Aleppo

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Aleppo. Compared with September: 5.2 °C cooler, 22 mm more precipitation and 63 hours less sunshine. November is 7.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Aleppo in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aleppo in October?
In October, Aleppo averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 14.2 °C (58 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Aleppo?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Aleppo’s mild months (April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aleppo in October?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (67 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Aleppo in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aleppo get in October?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aleppo in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
