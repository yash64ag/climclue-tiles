How February compares in Aleppo

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Aleppo. Compared with January: 1.3 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. March is 3.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Aleppo in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aleppo in February?
In February, Aleppo averages a day high of 12.0 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 2.6 °C (37 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Aleppo?
February is not one of Aleppo’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aleppo in February?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (67 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Aleppo in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Aleppo get in February?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aleppo in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
