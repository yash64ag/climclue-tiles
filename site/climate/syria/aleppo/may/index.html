How May compares in Aleppo

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Aleppo. Compared with April: 5.0 °C warmer, 14 mm less precipitation and 75 hours more sunshine. June is 4.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Aleppo in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aleppo in May?
In May, Aleppo averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 316 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Aleppo?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Aleppo’s mild months (April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aleppo in May?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (67 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Aleppo in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aleppo get in May?
About 316 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aleppo in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
