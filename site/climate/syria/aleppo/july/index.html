How July compares in Aleppo

July is the warmest month, the driest month and the sunniest month in Aleppo. Compared with June: 2.5 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. August is 0.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Aleppo in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aleppo in July?
In July, Aleppo averages a day high of 35.8 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 21.0 °C (70 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 390 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Aleppo?
July is not one of Aleppo’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aleppo in July?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is January (67 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Aleppo in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aleppo get in July?
About 390 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aleppo in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
