How March compares in Aleppo

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Aleppo. Compared with February: 3.8 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 61 hours more sunshine. April is 4.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Aleppo in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aleppo in March?
In March, Aleppo averages a day high of 16.4 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 5.7 °C (42 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Aleppo?
March is not one of Aleppo’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aleppo in March?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (67 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Aleppo in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.5 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Aleppo get in March?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aleppo in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
