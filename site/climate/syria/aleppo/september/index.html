How September compares in Aleppo

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Aleppo. Compared with August: 3.1 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 63 hours less sunshine. October is 5.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Aleppo in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aleppo in September?
In September, Aleppo averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 18.6 °C (65 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 303 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Aleppo?
September is not one of Aleppo’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aleppo in September?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (67 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Aleppo in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aleppo get in September?
About 303 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aleppo in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
