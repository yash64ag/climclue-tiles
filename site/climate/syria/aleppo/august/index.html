How August compares in Aleppo

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Aleppo. Compared with July: 0.1 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. September is 3.0 °C cooler than August on average.

Aleppo in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aleppo in August?
In August, Aleppo averages a day high of 35.8 °C (96 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 366 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Aleppo?
August is not one of Aleppo’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aleppo in August?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (62 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Aleppo in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aleppo get in August?
About 366 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aleppo in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
