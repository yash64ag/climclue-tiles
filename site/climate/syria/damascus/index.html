Why Damascus has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 85 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Damascus climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Damascus?
July, with an average of 26.3 °C (79 °F), day highs around 33.7 °C (93 °F) and night lows around 17.3 °C (63 °F).
What is the coldest month in Damascus?
January, with an average of 6.0 °C (43 °F), day highs around 10.2 °C (50 °F) and night lows around 1.3 °C (34 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Damascus?
January, with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 327 mm (12.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Damascus?
July, with 0 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Damascus?
July, with about 385 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Damascus?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 20 cm (7.9 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Damascus?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June, September and October.
What type of climate does Damascus have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.3 × 11 km, that contains Damascus’s GeoNames location (33.510°N, 36.291°E; ClimaClue area sq-504-2162), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,569,394 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
