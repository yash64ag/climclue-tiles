How April compares in Damascus

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Damascus. Compared with March: 4.9 °C warmer, 23 mm less precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. May is 4.7 °C warmer than April on average.

Damascus in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Damascus in April?
In April, Damascus averages a day high of 23.2 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 9.6 °C (49 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Damascus?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Damascus’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Damascus in April?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (63 mm), the driest August (0 mm).
Does it snow in Damascus in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Damascus get in April?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Damascus in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
