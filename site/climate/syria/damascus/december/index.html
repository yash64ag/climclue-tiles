How December compares in Damascus

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Damascus. Compared with November: 4.5 °C cooler, 12 mm more precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. January is 1.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Damascus in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Damascus in December?
In December, Damascus averages a day high of 12.0 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 3.1 °C (38 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Damascus?
December is not one of Damascus’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Damascus in December?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (74 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Damascus in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.6 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Damascus get in December?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Damascus in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
