How October compares in Damascus

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Damascus. Compared with September: 4.9 °C cooler, 15 mm more precipitation and 55 hours less sunshine. November is 6.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Damascus in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Damascus in October?
In October, Damascus averages a day high of 24.7 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Damascus?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Damascus’s mild months (April, May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Damascus in October?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (74 mm), the driest July (0 mm).
Does it snow in Damascus in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Damascus get in October?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Damascus in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
