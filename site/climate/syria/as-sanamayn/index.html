Aş Şanamayn at a glance

Among the 89 towns ClimaClue covers in Syria, Aş Şanamayn is 67th warmest (17.2 °C year average), 19th sunniest (about 3,129 hours of sunshine a year) and 58th wettest (340 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Inkhil, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Aş Şanamayn is 0.3 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 24 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.5 °C), averages 18.0 °C more than its coolest, January (7.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 96 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Aş Şanamayn, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Aş Şanamayn has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 96 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Aş Şanamayn itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.072°N, 36.183°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-509-2161), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
