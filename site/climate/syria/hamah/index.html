Ḩamāh at a glance

Among the 13 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Syria, Ḩamāh is 6th warmest (18.6 °C year average), 7th sunniest (about 3,021 hours of sunshine a year) and 4th wettest (466 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Homs, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Ḩamāh is 1.4 °C warmer in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 61 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 20.0 °C more than its coolest, January (8.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 83 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ḩamāh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ḩamāh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 83 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
