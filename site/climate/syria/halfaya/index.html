Among the 89 towns ClimaClue covers in Syria, Ḩalfāyā is 28th warmest (18.2 °C year average), 65th sunniest (about 2,998 hours of sunshine a year) and 15th wettest (637 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Ḩalfāyā has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 73 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
