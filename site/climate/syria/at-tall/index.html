At Tall at a glance

Among the 89 towns ClimaClue covers in Syria, At Tall is 83rd warmest (15.8 °C year average), 38th sunniest (about 3,086 hours of sunshine a year) and 53rd wettest (360 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ḩarastā, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), At Tall is 1.8 °C cooler in July and 1.8 °C cooler in January, with 96 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.8 °C), averages 20.7 °C more than its coolest, January (5.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 86 km from the sea.

Why At Tall has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 86 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.7 °C. The 10 km area around At Tall, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 14.7 °C a year, 1.1 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
