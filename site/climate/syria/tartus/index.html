Ţarţūs at a glance

Among the 13 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Syria, Ţarţūs is the 2nd least sunny (about 2,937 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (1,282 mm a year). Compared with Latakia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (72 km away), Ţarţūs is 0.3 °C warmer in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 326 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.8 °C), averages 14.2 °C more than its coolest, January (12.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.9 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ţarţūs, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ţarţūs has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
