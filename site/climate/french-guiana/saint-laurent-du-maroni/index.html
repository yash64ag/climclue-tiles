Saint-Laurent-du-Maroni at a glance

Among the 5 towns ClimaClue covers in French Guiana, Saint-Laurent-du-Maroni is the 2nd coolest (26.0 °C year average), the sunniest (about 2,253 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (2,466 mm a year). Compared with Kourou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (158 km away), Saint-Laurent-du-Maroni is about as warm in October and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 513 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (26.8 °C), averages 1.5 °C more than its coolest, January (25.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 21.4 °C (71 °F), in July. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Saint-Laurent-du-Maroni, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Saint-Laurent-du-Maroni has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Near the equator the midday sun is high all year, so temperatures barely change between months. Warm, moist air rises almost every day near the equatorial rain belt, building showers and thunderstorms, so no month is truly dry. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-784-1259), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
