Matoury sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Guyane · French Guiana

The sea off Matoury averages 28.1 °C (83 °F) in July, its warmest month, and 27.0 °C (81 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Matoury sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Matoury?
In July, at about 28.1 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Matoury?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is January, at 27.0 °C (81 °F).
How warm is the sea in Matoury in January?
About 27.0 °C (81 °F): bath-warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Matoury?
About 27.7 °C (82 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
