Villeurbanne at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in France, Villeurbanne is 10th warmest (12.4 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 1,956 hours of sunshine a year) and 15th wettest (840 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Saint-Étienne, the nearest covered town with different numbers (53 km away), Villeurbanne is 2.1 °C warmer in July and 1.1 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.9 °C), averages 18.6 °C more than its coolest, January (3.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 263 km from the sea.

Why Villeurbanne has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 263 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
