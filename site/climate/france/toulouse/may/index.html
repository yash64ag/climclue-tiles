How May compares in Toulouse

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Toulouse. Compared with April: 3.9 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. June is 3.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Toulouse in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Toulouse in May?
In May, Toulouse averages a day high of 20.4 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 10.9 °C (52 °F), with 75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 209 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Toulouse?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Toulouse’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Toulouse in May?
75 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is May (75 mm), the driest July (41 mm).
Does it snow in Toulouse in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Toulouse get in May?
About 209 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Toulouse in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
