How February compares in Toulouse

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Toulouse. Compared with January: 0.9 °C warmer, 11 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. March is 2.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Toulouse in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Toulouse in February?
In February, Toulouse averages a day high of 11.0 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 2.9 °C (37 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 109 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Toulouse?
February is not one of Toulouse’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Toulouse in February?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (74 mm), the driest July (40 mm).
Does it snow in Toulouse in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.3 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Toulouse get in February?
About 109 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Toulouse in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
