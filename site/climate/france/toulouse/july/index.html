How July compares in Toulouse

July is the warmest month, the driest month and the sunniest month in Toulouse. Compared with June: 2.5 °C warmer, 20 mm less precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Toulouse in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Toulouse in July?
In July, Toulouse averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 274 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Toulouse?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Toulouse’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Toulouse in July?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is May (75 mm), the driest July (41 mm).
Does it snow in Toulouse in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Toulouse get in July?
About 274 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Toulouse in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
