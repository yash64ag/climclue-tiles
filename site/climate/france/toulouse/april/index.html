How April compares in Toulouse

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Toulouse. Compared with March: 2.4 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. May is 3.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Toulouse in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Toulouse in April?
In April, Toulouse averages a day high of 16.5 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 7.0 °C (45 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Toulouse?
April is not one of Toulouse’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Toulouse in April?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (75 mm), the driest July (41 mm).
Does it snow in Toulouse in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Toulouse get in April?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Toulouse in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
