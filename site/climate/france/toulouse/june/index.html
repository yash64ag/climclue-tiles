How June compares in Toulouse

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Toulouse. Compared with May: 3.8 °C warmer, 15 mm less precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. July is 2.5 °C warmer than June on average.

Toulouse in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Toulouse in June?
In June, Toulouse averages a day high of 25.2 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 14.4 °C (58 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Toulouse?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Toulouse’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Toulouse in June?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (74 mm), the driest July (40 mm).
Does it snow in Toulouse in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Toulouse get in June?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Toulouse in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
