How November compares in Toulouse

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Toulouse. Compared with October: 5.4 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 57 hours less sunshine. December is 3.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Toulouse in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Toulouse in November?
In November, Toulouse averages a day high of 13.4 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 5.7 °C (42 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 101 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Toulouse?
November is not one of Toulouse’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Toulouse in November?
52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (74 mm), the driest July (40 mm).
Does it snow in Toulouse in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Toulouse get in November?
About 101 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Toulouse in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
