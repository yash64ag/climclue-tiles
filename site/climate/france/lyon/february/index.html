Lyon weather in February

Average February weather in Lyon, France: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Lyon averages 8.4 °C (47 °F) days and 1.3 °C (34 °F) nights, with 43 mm (1.7 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 89 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Lyon

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Lyon. Compared with January: 1.4 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. March is 3.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Lyon in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lyon in February?
In February, Lyon averages a day high of 8.4 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 1.3 °C (34 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 89 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Lyon?
February is not one of Lyon’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lyon in February?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is October (92 mm), the driest February (43 mm).
Does it snow in Lyon in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.5 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Lyon get in February?
About 89 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lyon in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
