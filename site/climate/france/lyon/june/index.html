How June compares in Lyon

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Lyon. Compared with May: 3.5 °C warmer, 17 mm less precipitation and 35 hours more sunshine. July is 2.5 °C warmer than June on average.

Lyon in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lyon in June?
In June, Lyon averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 13.7 °C (57 °F), with 75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Lyon?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Lyon’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lyon in June?
75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (93 mm), the driest February (44 mm).
Does it snow in Lyon in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lyon get in June?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lyon in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
