How May compares in Lyon

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Lyon. Compared with April: 4.3 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. June is 3.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Lyon in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lyon in May?
In May, Lyon averages a day high of 19.9 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 10.2 °C (50 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Lyon?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Lyon’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lyon in May?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (93 mm), the driest February (44 mm).
Does it snow in Lyon in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lyon get in May?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lyon in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
