How November compares in Lyon

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Lyon. Compared with October: 5.5 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 56 hours less sunshine. December is 3.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Lyon in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lyon in November?
In November, Lyon averages a day high of 10.4 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 3.9 °C (39 °F), with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 75 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Lyon?
November is not one of Lyon’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lyon in November?
83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (93 mm), the driest February (44 mm).
Does it snow in Lyon in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.1 cm (1.6 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Lyon get in November?
About 75 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lyon in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
