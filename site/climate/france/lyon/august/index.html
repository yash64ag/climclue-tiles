Lyon weather in August

Average August weather in Lyon, France: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Lyon averages 27.1 °C (81 °F) days and 15.8 °C (60 °F) nights, with 65 mm (2.6 in) of rain over the month and about 255 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Lyon

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Lyon. Compared with July: 0.7 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. September is 4.1 °C cooler than August on average.

Lyon in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lyon in August?
In August, Lyon averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 15.8 °C (60 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Lyon?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Lyon’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lyon in August?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (92 mm), the driest February (43 mm).
Does it snow in Lyon in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lyon get in August?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lyon in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
