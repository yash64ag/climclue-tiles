How July compares in Lyon

July is the warmest month, the 6th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Lyon. Compared with June: 2.5 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. August is 0.6 °C cooler than July on average.

Lyon in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lyon in July?
In July, Lyon averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 16.2 °C (61 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 290 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Lyon?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Lyon’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lyon in July?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (93 mm), the driest February (44 mm).
Does it snow in Lyon in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lyon get in July?
About 290 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lyon in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
