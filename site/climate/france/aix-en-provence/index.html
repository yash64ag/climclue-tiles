Aix-en-Provence at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in France, Aix-en-Provence is the 3rd sunniest (about 2,663 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Marseille, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Aix-en-Provence is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 3.1 °C cooler in January, with 87 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.6 °C), averages 18.4 °C more than its coolest, January (5.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 37 km from the sea.

Why Aix-en-Provence has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 37 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
