Why Rillieux-la-Pape has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 276 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
