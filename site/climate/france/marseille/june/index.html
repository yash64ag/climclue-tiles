How June compares in Marseille

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Marseille. Compared with May: 3.6 °C warmer, 20 mm less precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. July is 2.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Marseille in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Marseille in June?
In June, Marseille averages a day high of 22.9 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 17.2 °C (63 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 312 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Marseille?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Marseille’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Marseille in June?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (80 mm), the driest July (10 mm).
Does it snow in Marseille in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Marseille get in June?
About 312 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Marseille in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
