How July compares in Marseille

July is the warmest month, the driest month and the sunniest month in Marseille. Compared with June: 2.7 °C warmer, 15 mm less precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. August is about as warm as July on average.

Marseille in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Marseille in July?
In July, Marseille averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 357 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Marseille?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Marseille’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Marseille in July?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is September (80 mm), the driest July (10 mm).
Does it snow in Marseille in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Marseille get in July?
About 357 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Marseille in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
