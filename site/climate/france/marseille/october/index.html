Marseille weather in October

Average October weather in Marseille, France: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Marseille averages 20.9 °C (70 °F) days and 12.7 °C (55 °F) nights, with 78 mm (3.1 in) of rain over the month and about 195 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Marseille

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Marseille. Compared with September: 3.5 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. November is 4.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Marseille in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Marseille in October?
In October, Marseille averages a day high of 20.9 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Marseille?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Marseille’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Marseille in October?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (81 mm), the driest July (10 mm).
Does it snow in Marseille in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Marseille get in October?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Marseille in October?
About 19.2 °C (67 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (22.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Marseille in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
