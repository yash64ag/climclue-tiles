How April compares in Marseille

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Marseille. Compared with March: 2.4 °C warmer, 23 mm more precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. May is 3.7 °C warmer than April on average.

Marseille in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Marseille in April?
In April, Marseille averages a day high of 17.7 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 9.1 °C (48 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Marseille?
April is not one of Marseille’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Marseille in April?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (81 mm), the driest July (10 mm).
Does it snow in Marseille in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Marseille get in April?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Marseille in April?
About 13.8 °C (57 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (22.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Marseille in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
