Marseille at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in France, Marseille is the 4th warmest (15.4 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 2,734 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (577 mm a year). Compared with Aix-en-Provence, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Marseille is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 2.7 °C warmer in January, with 58 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.5 °C), averages 15.3 °C more than its coolest, January (8.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.3 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 8 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.0 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 108% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Marseille has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Marseille itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (43.297°N, 5.381°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-407-1853), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
