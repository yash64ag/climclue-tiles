How March compares in Marseille

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Marseille. Compared with February: 2.2 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 57 hours more sunshine. April is 2.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Marseille in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Marseille in March?
In March, Marseille averages a day high of 13.4 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 7.8 °C (46 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Marseille?
March is not one of Marseille’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Marseille in March?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (80 mm), the driest July (10 mm).
Does it snow in Marseille in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Marseille get in March?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Marseille in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
