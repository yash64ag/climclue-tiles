How February compares in Marseille

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Marseille. Compared with January: 0.3 °C warmer, 19 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. March is 2.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Marseille in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Marseille in February?
In February, Marseille averages a day high of 11.1 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 5.8 °C (42 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Marseille?
February is not one of Marseille’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Marseille in February?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (80 mm), the driest July (10 mm).
Does it snow in Marseille in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.8 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Marseille get in February?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Marseille in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
