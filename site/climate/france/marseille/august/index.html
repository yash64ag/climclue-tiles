How August compares in Marseille

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Marseille. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 17 mm more precipitation and 39 hours less sunshine. September is 3.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Marseille in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Marseille in August?
In August, Marseille averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 18.8 °C (66 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 318 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Marseille?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Marseille’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Marseille in August?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (81 mm), the driest July (10 mm).
Does it snow in Marseille in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Marseille get in August?
About 318 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Marseille in August?
About 22.2 °C (72 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (22.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Marseille in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
