How November compares in Marseille

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Marseille. Compared with October: 4.7 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 46 hours less sunshine. December is 2.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Marseille in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Marseille in November?
In November, Marseille averages a day high of 14.2 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 9.8 °C (50 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Marseille?
November is not one of Marseille’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Marseille in November?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (80 mm), the driest July (10 mm).
Does it snow in Marseille in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Marseille get in November?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Marseille in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
