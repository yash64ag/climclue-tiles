How May compares in Marseille

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Marseille. Compared with April: 3.9 °C warmer, 11 mm less precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. June is 3.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Marseille in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Marseille in May?
In May, Marseille averages a day high of 22.3 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 12.8 °C (55 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 281 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Marseille?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Marseille’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Marseille in May?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (81 mm), the driest July (10 mm).
Does it snow in Marseille in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Marseille get in May?
About 281 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Marseille in May?
About 16.2 °C (61 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (22.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Marseille in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
