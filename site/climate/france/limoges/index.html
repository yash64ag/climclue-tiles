Limoges at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in France, Limoges is the 3rd wettest (1,157 mm a year). Compared with Clermont-Ferrand, the nearest covered town with different numbers (143 km away), Limoges is 1.4 °C warmer in July and 2.1 °C warmer in January, with 165 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.6 °C), averages 15.5 °C more than its coolest, January (4.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 187 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 113% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Limoges has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 187 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
