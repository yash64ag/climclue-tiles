Mulhouse at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in France, Mulhouse is the 4th least sunny (about 1,630 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Strasbourg, the nearest covered town with different numbers (98 km away), Mulhouse is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 41 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.1 °C), averages 18.5 °C more than its coolest, January (1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 393 km from the sea.

Why Mulhouse has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 393 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
