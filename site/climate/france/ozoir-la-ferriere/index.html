Ozoir-la-Ferrière at a glance

Among the 657 towns ClimaClue covers in France, Ozoir-la-Ferrière is 497th warmest (11.0 °C year average), 498th sunniest (about 1,736 hours of sunshine a year) and 305th wettest (751 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Roissy-en-Brie, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Ozoir-la-Ferrière is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Why Ozoir-la-Ferrière has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 181 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
