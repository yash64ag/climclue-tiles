Amiens at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in France, Amiens is the 4th coolest (10.5 °C year average). Compared with Cergy-Pontoise, the nearest covered town with different numbers (97 km away), Amiens is 0.9 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 70 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.9 °C), averages 14.2 °C more than its coolest, January (3.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 1.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 72 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Amiens, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Amiens has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 72 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
