Bayeux sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Normandy · France

The sea off Bayeux averages 17.6 °C (64 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 8.0 °C (46 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Bayeux sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Bayeux?
In August, at about 17.6 °C (64 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Bayeux?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 8.0 °C (46 °F).
How warm is the sea in Bayeux in February?
About 8.0 °C (46 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Bayeux?
About 12.6 °C (55 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Bayeux nearby?
In August: Le Havre (18.2 °C, 63 km) and Montivilliers (18.2 °C, 71 km).
