Rodez at a glance

Among the 657 towns ClimaClue covers in France, Rodez is 548th warmest (10.9 °C year average), 113th sunniest (about 2,055 hours of sunshine a year) and 45th wettest (1,066 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Millau, the nearest covered town with different numbers (49 km away), Rodez is 1.9 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 164 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.5 °C), averages 16.2 °C more than its coolest, January (3.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 147 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rodez, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Rodez has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 147 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Rodez itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (44.353°N, 2.573°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-396-1825), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
