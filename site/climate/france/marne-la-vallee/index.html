Marne La Vallée at a glance

Among the 657 towns ClimaClue covers in France, Marne La Vallée is 408th warmest (11.6 °C year average), 530th sunniest (about 1,730 hours of sunshine a year) and 346th wettest (724 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lognes, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Marne La Vallée is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 7 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.8 °C), averages 15.7 °C more than its coolest, January (4.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 172 km from the sea.

Why Marne La Vallée has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 172 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Marne La Vallée itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.836°N, 2.642°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-351-1826), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
