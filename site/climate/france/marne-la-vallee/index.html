Why Marne La Vallée has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 172 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Marne La Vallée climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Marne La Vallée?
July, with an average of 19.3 °C (67 °F), day highs around 23.5 °C (74 °F) and night lows around 14.4 °C (58 °F).
What is the coldest month in Marne La Vallée?
January, with an average of 3.8 °C (39 °F), day highs around 6.5 °C (44 °F) and night lows around 1.1 °C (34 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Marne La Vallée?
May, with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 718 mm (28.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Marne La Vallée?
February, with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Marne La Vallée?
July, with about 237 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Marne La Vallée?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 19 cm (7.4 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in December; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Marne La Vallée?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Marne La Vallée have?
Oceanic (Köppen Cfb). Mild all year, with rain in every season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.3 × 11 km, that contains Marne La Vallée’s GeoNames location (48.836°N, 2.642°E; ClimaClue area sq-351-1826), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 318,325 (GeoNames).
