Douarnenez sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Brittany · France

The sea off Douarnenez averages 16.8 °C (62 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 9.9 °C (50 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Douarnenez sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Douarnenez?
In August, at about 16.8 °C (62 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Douarnenez?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 9.9 °C (50 °F).
How warm is the sea in Douarnenez in February?
About 9.9 °C (50 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Douarnenez?
About 13.2 °C (56 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Douarnenez nearby?
In August: Quimper (17.9 °C, 20 km), Concarneau (17.9 °C, 39 km) and Ploemeur (17.9 °C, 78 km).
