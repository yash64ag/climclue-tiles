Pontarlier at a glance

Among the 657 towns ClimaClue covers in France, Pontarlier is the coolest (8.3 °C year average). Compared with Besançon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Pontarlier is 3.4 °C cooler in July and 2.9 °C cooler in January, with 157 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.1 °C), averages 17.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 344 km from the sea.

Why Pontarlier has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 344 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pontarlier itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (46.903°N, 6.355°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-370-1863), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
