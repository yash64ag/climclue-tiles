Nancy at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in France, Nancy is the 2nd coolest (10.0 °C year average) and the 5th least sunny (about 1,635 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Metz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Nancy is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 89 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.9 °C), averages 17.4 °C more than its coolest, January (1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 366 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 20 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 107% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Nancy has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 366 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
