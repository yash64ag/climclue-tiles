Paris weather in June

Average June weather in Paris, France: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Paris averages 21.6 °C (71 °F) days and 12.7 °C (55 °F) nights, with 54 mm (2.1 in) of rain over the month and about 218 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Paris

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Paris. Compared with May: 3.1 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. July is 2.2 °C warmer than June on average.

Paris in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Paris in June?
In June, Paris averages a day high of 21.6 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Paris?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Paris’s mild months (June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Paris in June?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (67 mm), the driest February (45 mm).
Does it snow in Paris in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Paris get in June?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Paris in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
