How February compares in Paris

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Paris. Compared with January: 0.4 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. March is 3.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Paris in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Paris in February?
In February, Paris averages a day high of 7.9 °C (46 °F) and a night low of 1.2 °C (34 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 85 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Paris?
February is not one of Paris’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Paris in February?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is October (67 mm), the driest February (45 mm).
Does it snow in Paris in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.0 cm (2.0 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Paris get in February?
About 85 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Paris in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
