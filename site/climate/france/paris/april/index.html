How April compares in Paris

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Paris. Compared with March: 2.7 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. May is 3.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Paris in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Paris in April?
In April, Paris averages a day high of 14.3 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 5.4 °C (42 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Paris?
April is not one of Paris’s mild months; those are June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Paris in April?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (67 mm), the driest February (45 mm).
Does it snow in Paris in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Paris get in April?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Paris in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
