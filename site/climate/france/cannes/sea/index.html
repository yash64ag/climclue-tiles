Cannes sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur · France

The sea off Cannes averages 24.1 °C (75 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.0 °C (55 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Cannes sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Cannes?
In August, at about 24.1 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Cannes?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 13.0 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in Cannes in February?
About 13.0 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Cannes?
About 17.7 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
