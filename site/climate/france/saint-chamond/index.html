Saint-Chamond at a glance

Among the 657 towns ClimaClue covers in France, Saint-Chamond is 237th warmest (11.5 °C year average), 147th sunniest (about 2,029 hours of sunshine a year) and 202nd wettest (829 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Saint-Chamond has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 226 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.2 °C. The 10 km area around Saint-Chamond, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 10.4 °C a year, 1.1 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
