Cherbourg sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Normandy · France

The sea off Cherbourg averages 16.7 °C (62 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 9.0 °C (48 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Cherbourg sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Cherbourg?
In September, at about 16.7 °C (62 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Cherbourg?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 9.0 °C (48 °F).
How warm is the sea in Cherbourg in March?
About 9.0 °C (48 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Cherbourg?
About 12.6 °C (55 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Cherbourg nearby?
In September: Bayeux (17.5 °C, 77 km), Hérouville-Saint-Clair (17.5 °C, 106 km) and Saint-Malo (17.6 °C, 114 km).
