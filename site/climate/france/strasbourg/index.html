Strasbourg at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in France, Strasbourg is the 3rd least sunny (about 1,613 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Mulhouse, the nearest covered town with different numbers (98 km away), Strasbourg is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 41 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.3 °C), averages 18.3 °C more than its coolest, January (1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 445 km from the sea.

Why Strasbourg has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 445 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
