Quimper sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Brittany · France

The sea off Quimper averages 17.9 °C (64 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 10.0 °C (50 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Quimper sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Quimper?
In August, at about 17.9 °C (64 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Quimper?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 10.0 °C (50 °F).
How warm is the sea in Quimper in February?
About 10.0 °C (50 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Quimper?
About 13.8 °C (57 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Quimper nearby?
In August: La Baule-Escoublac (18.6 °C, 152 km).
