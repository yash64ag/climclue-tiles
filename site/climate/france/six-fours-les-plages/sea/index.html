Six-Fours-les-Plages sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur · France

The sea off Six-Fours-les-Plages averages 22.6 °C (73 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.7 °C (55 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Six-Fours-les-Plages sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Six-Fours-les-Plages?
In August, at about 22.6 °C (73 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Six-Fours-les-Plages?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 12.7 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in Six-Fours-les-Plages in February?
About 12.7 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Six-Fours-les-Plages?
About 16.9 °C (62 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
