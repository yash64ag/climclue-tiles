Why Cergy-Pontoise has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 129 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Cergy-Pontoise climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Cergy-Pontoise?
July, with an average of 18.9 °C (66 °F), day highs around 23.1 °C (74 °F) and night lows around 14.1 °C (57 °F).
What is the coldest month in Cergy-Pontoise?
January, with an average of 4.0 °C (39 °F), day highs around 6.6 °C (44 °F) and night lows around 1.3 °C (34 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Cergy-Pontoise?

What is the driest month in Cergy-Pontoise?

What is the sunniest month in Cergy-Pontoise?
July, with about 233 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Cergy-Pontoise?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 17 cm (6.9 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Cergy-Pontoise?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July, August and September.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.3 × 11 km, that contains Cergy-Pontoise’s GeoNames location (49.039°N, 2.078°E; ClimaClue area sq-349-1820), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 183,430 (GeoNames).
