Aix-les-Bains weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Aix-les-Bains, France (Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Aix-les-Bains is warmest in July, with 28.0 °C (82 °F) days and 16.3 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 5.1 °C (41 °F) days and -0.9 °C nights. Rain is spread through the year, from 90 mm in February to 133 mm in October.

Why Aix-les-Bains has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 252 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.0 °C. The 10 km area around Aix-les-Bains, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 9.3 °C a year, 3.0 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Aix-les-Bains itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (45.692°N, 5.909°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-383-1859), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
