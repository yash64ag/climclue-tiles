Les Sables-d'Olonne sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Pays de la Loire · France

The sea off Les Sables-d'Olonne averages 19.3 °C (67 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 9.3 °C (49 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Les Sables-d'Olonne sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Les Sables-d'Olonne?
In August, at about 19.3 °C (67 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Les Sables-d'Olonne?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 9.3 °C (49 °F).
How warm is the sea in Les Sables-d'Olonne in February?
About 9.3 °C (49 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Les Sables-d'Olonne?
About 14.1 °C (57 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
