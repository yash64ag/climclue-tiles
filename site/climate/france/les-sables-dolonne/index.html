Les Sables-d'Olonne at a glance

Among the 657 towns ClimaClue covers in France, Les Sables-d'Olonne is 130th warmest (13.1 °C year average), 133rd sunniest (about 2,047 hours of sunshine a year) and 200th wettest (831 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with La Roche-sur-Yon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Les Sables-d'Olonne is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 1.4 °C warmer in February, with 43 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (19.3 °C), averages 11.6 °C more than its coolest, February (7.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 4.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 8 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Les Sables-d'Olonne has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
