Nice at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in France, Nice is the 5th sunniest (about 2,525 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Toulon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (126 km away), Nice is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 140 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.8 °C), averages 15.9 °C more than its coolest, January (6.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 20 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: a NOAA weather station 8 km from Nice measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from this area’s (temperature by up to 2.4 °C (4.3 °F) in one month). One station can differ from a 10 km area’s average (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Nice has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
