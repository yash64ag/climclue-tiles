Clermont-Ferrand at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in France, Clermont-Ferrand is the coolest (9.6 °C year average) and the 5th wettest (992 mm a year). Compared with Saint-Étienne, the nearest covered town with different numbers (108 km away), Clermont-Ferrand is 1.7 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 151 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.1 °C), averages 16.2 °C more than its coolest, January (1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 265 km from the sea.

Why Clermont-Ferrand has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 265 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
