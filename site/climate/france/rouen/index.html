Rouen at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in France, Rouen is 43rd warmest (10.8 °C year average), 40th sunniest (about 1,743 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (878 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Le Havre, the nearest covered town with different numbers (72 km away), Rouen is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 1.3 °C cooler in January, with 23 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.1 °C), averages 13.8 °C more than its coolest, January (4.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 1.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 57 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 9 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 103% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Rouen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 57 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
