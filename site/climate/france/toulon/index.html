Why Toulon has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Toulon climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Toulon?
July, with an average of 22.9 °C (73 °F), day highs around 26.7 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 18.6 °C (65 °F).
What is the coldest month in Toulon?
January, with an average of 7.7 °C (46 °F), day highs around 9.9 °C (50 °F) and night lows around 4.5 °C (40 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Toulon?
October, with 111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 734 mm (28.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Toulon?
July, with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Toulon?
July, with about 357 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Toulon?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 4.8 cm (1.9 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Toulon?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Toulon have?
Mediterranean, hot summer (Köppen Csa). Hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.1 × 11 km, that contains Toulon’s GeoNames location (43.124°N, 5.928°E; ClimaClue area sq-408-1859), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 168,701 (GeoNames).
