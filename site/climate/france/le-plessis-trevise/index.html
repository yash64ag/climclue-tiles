Le Plessis-Trévise at a glance

Among the 657 towns ClimaClue covers in France, Le Plessis-Trévise is 490th warmest (11.0 °C year average), 506th sunniest (about 1,732 hours of sunshine a year) and 301st wettest (755 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pontault-Combault, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Le Plessis-Trévise is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 9 mm less precipitation a year. Why Le Plessis-Trévise has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 167 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
