Montivilliers sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Normandy · France

The sea off Montivilliers averages 18.2 °C (65 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.2 °C (45 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Montivilliers sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Montivilliers?
In August, at about 18.2 °C (65 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Montivilliers?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 7.2 °C (45 °F).
How warm is the sea in Montivilliers in February?
About 7.2 °C (45 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Montivilliers?
About 12.4 °C (54 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
