Narbonne sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Occitanie · France

The sea off Narbonne averages 21.9 °C (71 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 11.8 °C (53 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Narbonne sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Narbonne?
In August, at about 21.9 °C (71 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Narbonne?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 11.8 °C (53 °F).
How warm is the sea in Narbonne in February?
About 11.8 °C (53 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Narbonne?
About 16.4 °C (62 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Narbonne nearby?
In August: Roses (22.5 °C, 104 km).
