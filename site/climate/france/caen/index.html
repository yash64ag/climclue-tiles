Caen at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in France, Caen is 19th warmest (11.4 °C year average), 25th sunniest (about 1,754 hours of sunshine a year) and 24th wettest (766 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Le Havre, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Caen is 0.8 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 134 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.4 °C), averages 13.1 °C more than its coolest, January (5.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 2.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Caen, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Caen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
