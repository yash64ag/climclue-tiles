Vence sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur · France

The sea off Vence averages 23.2 °C (74 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.1 °C (54 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Vence sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Vence?
In August, at about 23.2 °C (74 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Vence?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 12.1 °C (54 °F).
How warm is the sea in Vence in February?
About 12.1 °C (54 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Vence?
About 16.7 °C (62 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Vence nearby?
In August: Cagnes-sur-Mer (24.1 °C, 7 km), Antibes (24.1 °C, 16 km) and Grasse (24.1 °C, 17 km).
