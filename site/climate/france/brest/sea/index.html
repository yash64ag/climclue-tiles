Brest sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Brittany · France

The sea off Brest averages 16.2 °C (61 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 9.8 °C (50 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Brest sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Brest?
In August, at about 16.2 °C (61 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Brest?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 9.8 °C (50 °F).
How warm is the sea in Brest in February?
About 9.8 °C (50 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Brest?
About 12.9 °C (55 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Brest nearby?
In August: Douarnenez (16.8 °C, 35 km), Quimper (17.9 °C, 52 km) and Concarneau (17.9 °C, 71 km).
