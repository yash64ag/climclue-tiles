Bastia at a glance

Among the 657 towns ClimaClue covers in France, Bastia is the 2nd warmest (16.5 °C year average). Compared with Ajaccio, the nearest covered town with different numbers (105 km away), Bastia is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C warmer in February, with 298 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.1 °C), averages 13.6 °C more than its coolest, February (10.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.0 °C (46 °F), in February. The area lies about 8 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 18 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 89% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Bastia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.6 °C. The 10 km area around Bastia, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 13.4 °C a year, 3.0 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Bastia itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (42.702°N, 9.451°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-412-1894), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
