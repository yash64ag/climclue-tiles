Bastia sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Corsica · France

The sea off Bastia averages 24.2 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.6 °C (55 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Bastia sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Bastia?
In August, at about 24.2 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Bastia?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 12.6 °C (55 °F).
How warm is the sea in Bastia in February?
About 12.6 °C (55 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Bastia?
About 17.5 °C (63 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Bastia nearby?
In August: Rosignano Marittimo (25.0 °C, 114 km), Livorno (25.0 °C, 118 km) and Grosseto (24.9 °C, 136 km).
