Saint-Malo sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Brittany · France

The sea off Saint-Malo averages 18.0 °C (64 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 8.2 °C (47 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Saint-Malo sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Saint-Malo?
In August, at about 18.0 °C (64 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Saint-Malo?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 8.2 °C (47 °F).
How warm is the sea in Saint-Malo in February?
About 8.2 °C (47 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Saint-Malo?
About 12.8 °C (55 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
