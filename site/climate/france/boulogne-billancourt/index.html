Boulogne-Billancourt at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in France, Boulogne-Billancourt is 34th warmest (11.2 °C year average), 26th sunniest (about 1,748 hours of sunshine a year) and 37th wettest (664 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Paris 15 Vaugirard, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Boulogne-Billancourt is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.3 °C), averages 15.3 °C more than its coolest, January (4.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 1.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 155 km from the sea.

Why Boulogne-Billancourt has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 155 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
