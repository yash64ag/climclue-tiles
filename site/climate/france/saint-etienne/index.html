Saint-Étienne at a glance

Among the 51 towns of 100,000+ people covered in France, Saint-Étienne is 45th warmest (10.7 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 2,031 hours of sunshine a year) and 14th wettest (842 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lyon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (50 km away), Saint-Étienne is 2.1 °C cooler in July and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.9 °C), averages 17.6 °C more than its coolest, January (2.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 224 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Saint-Étienne, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Saint-Étienne has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 224 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
