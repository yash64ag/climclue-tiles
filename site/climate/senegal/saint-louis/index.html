Saint-Louis at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Senegal, Saint-Louis is the driest (264 mm a year). Compared with Louga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (53 km away), Saint-Louis is 1.0 °C cooler in October and 1.8 °C cooler in January, with 84 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (28.1 °C), averages 6.2 °C more than its coolest, January (21.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.3 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Saint-Louis, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Saint-Louis has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
