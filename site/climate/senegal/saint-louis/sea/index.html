Saint-Louis sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Senegal

The sea off Saint-Louis averages 28.2 °C (83 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 18.1 °C (65 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to November.

Saint-Louis sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Saint-Louis?
In September, at about 28.2 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Saint-Louis?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to November. The coolest month is February, at 18.1 °C (65 °F).
How warm is the sea in Saint-Louis in February?
About 18.1 °C (65 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Saint-Louis?
About 22.8 °C (73 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
