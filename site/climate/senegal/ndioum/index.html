Ndioum at a glance

Among the 67 towns ClimaClue covers in Senegal, Ndioum is the 5th warmest (30.0 °C year average) and the driest (232 mm a year). Compared with Dagana, the nearest covered town with different numbers (102 km away), Ndioum is 2.9 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 8 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (33.7 °C), averages 9.6 °C more than its coolest, January (24.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.2 °C (59 °F), in January. The area lies about 187 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ndioum, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ndioum has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 187 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ndioum itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (16.513°N, 14.647°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-674-1653), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
