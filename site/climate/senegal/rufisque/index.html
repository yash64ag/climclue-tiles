Rufisque at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Senegal, Rufisque is 14th warmest (25.0 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 2,987 hours of sunshine a year) and 13th wettest (418 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pikine, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Rufisque is 0.2 °C warmer in October and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 26 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.9 °C), averages 5.6 °C more than its coolest, January (22.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.2 °C (67 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rufisque, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Rufisque has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
