Diamniadio at a glance

Among the 67 towns ClimaClue covers in Senegal, Diamniadio is 52nd warmest (25.3 °C year average), 36th sunniest (about 2,994 hours of sunshine a year) and 42nd wettest (438 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sébikhotane, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Diamniadio is about as warm in October and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (28.1 °C), averages 5.4 °C more than its coolest, January (22.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.4 °C (63 °F), in February. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Diamniadio has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
