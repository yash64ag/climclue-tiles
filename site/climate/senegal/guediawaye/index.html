Guédiawaye at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Senegal, Guédiawaye is the coolest (24.1 °C year average) and the 3rd driest (384 mm a year). Compared with Pikine, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Guédiawaye is 0.3 °C cooler in October and 0.8 °C cooler in February, with 7 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.4 °C), averages 6.7 °C more than its coolest, February (20.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.9 °C (68 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Guédiawaye, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Guédiawaye has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
