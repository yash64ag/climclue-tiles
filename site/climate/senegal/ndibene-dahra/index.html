Ndibène Dahra at a glance

Among the 67 towns ClimaClue covers in Senegal, Ndibène Dahra is 19th warmest (28.2 °C year average), 40th sunniest (about 2,991 hours of sunshine a year) and 49th wettest (387 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dara, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Ndibène Dahra is about as warm in May and about as warm in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (30.8 °C), averages 7.0 °C more than its coolest, January (23.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.3 °C (60 °F), in January. The area lies about 136 km from the sea.

Why Ndibène Dahra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 136 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
