How July compares in Touba

July is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Touba. Compared with June: 0.3 °C cooler, 78 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. August is 0.7 °C cooler than July on average.

Touba in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Touba in July?
In July, Touba averages a day high of 33.5 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 103 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 242 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Touba?
July is not one of Touba’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Touba in July?
103 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (167 mm), the driest April (0 mm).
Does it snow in Touba in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Touba get in July?
About 242 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Touba in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
