How August compares in Touba

August is the 6th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Touba. Compared with July: 0.7 °C cooler, 64 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. September is about as warm as August on average.

Touba in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Touba in August?
In August, Touba averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.7 °C (75 °F), with 167 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Touba?
August is not one of Touba’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Touba in August?
167 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (167 mm), the driest April (0 mm).
Does it snow in Touba in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Touba get in August?
About 232 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Touba in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
