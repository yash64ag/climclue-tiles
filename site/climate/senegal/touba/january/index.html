How January compares in Touba

January is the coolest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Touba. Compared with December: 1.0 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. February is 2.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Touba in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Touba in January?
In January, Touba averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Touba?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Touba’s mild months (January and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Touba in January?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (167 mm), the driest April (0 mm).
Does it snow in Touba in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Touba get in January?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Touba in January?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
