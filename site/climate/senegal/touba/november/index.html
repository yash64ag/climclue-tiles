Touba in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Touba in November?

Is November a good time to visit Touba?
November is not one of Touba’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
