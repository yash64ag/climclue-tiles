How December compares in Touba

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Touba. Compared with November: 2.6 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. January is 1.0 °C cooler than December on average.
