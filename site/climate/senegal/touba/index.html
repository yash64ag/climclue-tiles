Touba at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Senegal, Touba is 6th warmest (27.3 °C year average), 5th sunniest (about 3,032 hours of sunshine a year) and 10th wettest (469 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mbaké, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Touba is 0.2 °C warmer in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.9 °C), averages 5.3 °C more than its coolest, January (23.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.8 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 126 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Touba, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Touba has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 126 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
