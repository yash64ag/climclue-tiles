How September compares in Touba

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Touba. Compared with August: about as warm, 28 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. October is 0.8 °C warmer than September on average.

Touba in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Touba in September?
In September, Touba averages a day high of 31.8 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 138 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Touba?
September is not one of Touba’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Touba in September?
138 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (167 mm), the driest April (0 mm).
Does it snow in Touba in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Touba get in September?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Touba in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
