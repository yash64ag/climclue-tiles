Touba in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Touba in February?
In February, Touba averages a day high of 33.4 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Touba?
February is not one of Touba’s mild months; those are January and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
