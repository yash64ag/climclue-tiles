Kaolack at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Senegal, Kaolack is the sunniest (about 3,063 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Tiébo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (56 km away), Kaolack is 0.5 °C warmer in June and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 90 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.9 °C), averages 4.7 °C more than its coolest, January (24.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.7 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 87 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kaolack, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kaolack has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 87 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
