How January compares in Dakar

January is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Dakar. Compared with December: 2.4 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. February is 0.6 °C cooler than January on average.

Dakar in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dakar in January?
In January, Dakar averages a day high of 22.7 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Dakar?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Dakar’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dakar in January?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (169 mm), the driest April (0 mm).
Does it snow in Dakar in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dakar get in January?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dakar in January?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
