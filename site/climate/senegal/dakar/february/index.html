How February compares in Dakar

February is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Dakar. Compared with January: 0.6 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. March is 0.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Dakar in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dakar in February?
In February, Dakar averages a day high of 22.1 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 19.9 °C (68 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Dakar?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Dakar’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dakar in February?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (169 mm), the driest April (0 mm).
Does it snow in Dakar in February?

How many hours of sunshine does Dakar get in February?
