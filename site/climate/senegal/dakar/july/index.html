How July compares in Dakar

July is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Dakar. Compared with June: 1.6 °C warmer, 53 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. August is 0.7 °C warmer than July on average.

Dakar in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dakar in July?
In July, Dakar averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 25.7 °C (78 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Dakar?
July is not one of Dakar’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, May, June and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dakar in July?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (169 mm), the driest April (0 mm).
Does it snow in Dakar in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dakar get in July?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dakar in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
