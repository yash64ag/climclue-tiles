Thiès at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Senegal, Thiès is 10th warmest (25.8 °C year average), 7th sunniest (about 3,007 hours of sunshine a year) and 5th wettest (501 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tivaouane, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Thiès is 0.1 °C warmer in October and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 53 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.9 °C), averages 4.9 °C more than its coolest, January (23.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.1 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 39 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Thiès, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Thiès has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 39 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
