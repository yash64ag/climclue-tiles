Ouro Sogui at a glance

Among the 67 towns ClimaClue covers in Senegal, Ouro Sogui is the 3rd warmest (30.7 °C year average) and the 2nd sunniest (about 3,123 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Matam, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Ouro Sogui is 0.2 °C cooler in May and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 14 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (35.6 °C), averages 10.4 °C more than its coolest, January (25.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.6 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 353 km from the sea.

Why Ouro Sogui has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 353 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ouro Sogui itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (15.606°N, 13.322°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-683-1666), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
