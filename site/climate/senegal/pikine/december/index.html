How December compares in Pikine

December is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Pikine. Compared with November: 2.5 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. January is 2.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Pikine in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pikine in December?
In December, Pikine averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 19.6 °C (67 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Pikine?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Pikine’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pikine in December?
