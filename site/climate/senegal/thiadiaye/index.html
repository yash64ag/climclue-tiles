Thiadiaye at a glance

Among the 67 towns ClimaClue covers in Senegal, Thiadiaye is 40th warmest (26.6 °C year average), 22nd sunniest (about 3,022 hours of sunshine a year) and 23rd wettest (533 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mbour, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Thiadiaye is 0.3 °C warmer in October and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 36 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (28.3 °C), averages 4.5 °C more than its coolest, January (23.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.6 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 34 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Thiadiaye, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Thiadiaye has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 34 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Thiadiaye itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.420°N, 16.703°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-695-1632), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
