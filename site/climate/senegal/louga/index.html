Louga at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Senegal, Louga is the 2nd driest (349 mm a year). Compared with Saint-Louis, the nearest covered town with different numbers (53 km away), Louga is 1.0 °C warmer in October and 1.8 °C warmer in January, with 84 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (29.1 °C), averages 5.4 °C more than its coolest, January (23.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.2 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Louga, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Louga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
