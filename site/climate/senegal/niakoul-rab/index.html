Niakoul Rab at a glance

Among the 67 towns ClimaClue covers in Senegal, Niakoul Rab is 58th warmest (24.9 °C year average), 44th sunniest (about 2,987 hours of sunshine a year) and 46th wettest (406 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rufisque, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Niakoul Rab is 0.1 °C cooler in October and 0.2 °C cooler in February, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, October (27.9 °C), averages 5.8 °C more than its coolest, February (22.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.6 °C (62 °F), in February. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Niakoul Rab has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
