Diourbel at a glance

Among the 67 towns ClimaClue covers in Senegal, Diourbel is 36th warmest (27.1 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 3,047 hours of sunshine a year) and 29th wettest (486 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tiébo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Diourbel is about as warm in June and about as warm in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.5 °C), averages 4.7 °C more than its coolest, January (23.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.3 °C (60 °F), in January. The area lies about 92 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Diourbel, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Diourbel has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 92 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Diourbel itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (14.648°N, 16.244°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-693-1637), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
