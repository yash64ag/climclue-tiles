Ziguinchor at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Senegal, Ziguinchor is the 3rd warmest (27.4 °C year average), the 2nd least sunny (about 2,865 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (1,305 mm a year). Compared with Kolda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (149 km away), Ziguinchor is 2.6 °C cooler in May and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 279 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (28.8 °C), averages 3.3 °C more than its coolest, January (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.0 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 55 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ziguinchor, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ziguinchor has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 55 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
