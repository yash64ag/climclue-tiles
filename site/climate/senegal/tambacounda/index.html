Tambacounda at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Senegal, Tambacounda is the warmest (29.7 °C year average), the 3rd least sunny (about 2,956 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (751 mm a year). Compared with Kolda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (169 km away), Tambacounda is 3.0 °C warmer in May and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 275 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (34.4 °C), averages 7.7 °C more than its coolest, January (26.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.6 °C (67 °F), in January. The area lies about 311 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tambacounda, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tambacounda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 311 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
