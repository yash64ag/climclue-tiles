Kolda at a glance

Among the 18 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Senegal, Kolda is the 2nd warmest (28.0 °C year average), the least sunny (about 2,846 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (1,026 mm a year). Compared with Ziguinchor, the nearest covered town with different numbers (149 km away), Kolda is 2.6 °C warmer in May and 0.6 °C cooler in December, with 279 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (31.4 °C), averages 6.0 °C more than its coolest, December (25.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.7 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 144 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kolda, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kolda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 144 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
