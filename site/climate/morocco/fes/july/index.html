How July compares in Fes

July is the warmest month, the driest month and the sunniest month in Fes. Compared with June: 3.4 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. August is 0.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Fes in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fes in July?
In July, Fes averages a day high of 34.7 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 324 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Fes?
July is not one of Fes’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fes in July?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (95 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Fes in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fes get in July?
About 324 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fes in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
