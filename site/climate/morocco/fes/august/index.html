How August compares in Fes

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Fes. Compared with July: 0.1 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. September is 2.8 °C cooler than August on average.

Fes in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fes in August?
In August, Fes averages a day high of 34.2 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 305 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Fes?
August is not one of Fes’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fes in August?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (97 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Fes in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fes get in August?
About 305 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fes in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
