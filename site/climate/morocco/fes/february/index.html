How February compares in Fes

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Fes. Compared with January: 1.7 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. March is 2.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Fes in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fes in February?
In February, Fes averages a day high of 17.6 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 6.8 °C (44 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Fes?
February is not one of Fes’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fes in February?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (97 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Fes in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fes get in February?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fes in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
