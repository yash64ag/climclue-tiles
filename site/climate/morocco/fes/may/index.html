How May compares in Fes

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Fes. Compared with April: 3.2 °C warmer, 30 mm less precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. June is 4.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Fes in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fes in May?
In May, Fes averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 280 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Fes?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Fes’s mild months (March, April, May, June, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fes in May?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (97 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Fes in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fes get in May?
About 280 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fes in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
