How March compares in Fes

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Fes. Compared with February: 2.4 °C warmer, 9 mm less precipitation and 58 hours more sunshine. April is 1.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Fes in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fes in March?
In March, Fes averages a day high of 20.6 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 9.1 °C (48 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Fes?
March is not one of Fes’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fes in March?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (95 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Fes in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fes get in March?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fes in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
