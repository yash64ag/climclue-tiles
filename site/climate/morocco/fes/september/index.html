How September compares in Fes

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Fes. Compared with August: 2.8 °C cooler, 14 mm more precipitation and 51 hours less sunshine. October is 4.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Fes in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fes in September?
In September, Fes averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 17.3 °C (63 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Fes?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Fes’s mild months (April, May, June, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fes in September?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (95 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Fes in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fes get in September?
About 254 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fes in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
