How December compares in Fes

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Fes. Compared with November: 3.2 °C cooler, 15 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. January is 1.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Fes in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fes in December?
In December, Fes averages a day high of 16.8 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 7.1 °C (45 °F), with 97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Fes?
December is not one of Fes’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fes in December?
97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (97 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Fes in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fes get in December?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fes in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
