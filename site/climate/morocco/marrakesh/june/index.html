Marrakesh weather in June

Average June weather in Marrakesh, Morocco: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Marrakesh averages 30.9 °C (88 °F) days and 16.1 °C (61 °F) nights, with 6 mm (0.3 in) of rain over the month and about 312 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Marrakesh

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Marrakesh. Compared with May: 3.6 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. July is 3.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Marrakesh in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Marrakesh in June?
In June, Marrakesh averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 16.1 °C (61 °F), with 6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 312 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Marrakesh?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Marrakesh’s mild months (March, April, May, June, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Marrakesh in June?
6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (39 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Marrakesh in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Marrakesh get in June?
About 312 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Marrakesh in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
