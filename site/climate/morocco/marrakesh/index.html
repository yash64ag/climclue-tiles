Marrakesh at a glance

Among the 39 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Morocco, Marrakesh is the 3rd sunniest (about 3,102 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (242 mm a year). Compared with El Kelaa des Srarhna, the nearest covered town with different numbers (72 km away), Marrakesh is 1.5 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 5 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.9 °C), averages 16.0 °C more than its coolest, January (10.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 148 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Marrakesh, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Marrakesh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 148 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
