How February compares in Marrakesh

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Marrakesh. Compared with January: 2.1 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. March is 2.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Marrakesh in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Marrakesh in February?
In February, Marrakesh averages a day high of 19.4 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 6.0 °C (43 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Marrakesh?
February is not one of Marrakesh’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Marrakesh in February?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (38 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Marrakesh in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Marrakesh get in February?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Marrakesh in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
