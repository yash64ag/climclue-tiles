How January compares in Marrakesh

January is the coolest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Marrakesh. Compared with December: 1.3 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. February is 2.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Marrakesh in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Marrakesh in January?
In January, Marrakesh averages a day high of 17.4 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 4.2 °C (40 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Marrakesh?
January is not one of Marrakesh’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Marrakesh in January?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (38 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Marrakesh in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Marrakesh get in January?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Marrakesh in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
