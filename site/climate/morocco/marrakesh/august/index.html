How August compares in Marrakesh

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Marrakesh. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. September is 2.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Marrakesh in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Marrakesh in August?
In August, Marrakesh averages a day high of 34.5 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 18.5 °C (65 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 309 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Marrakesh?
August is not one of Marrakesh’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Marrakesh in August?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (39 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Marrakesh in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Marrakesh get in August?
About 309 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Marrakesh in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
