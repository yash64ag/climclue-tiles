How April compares in Marrakesh

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Marrakesh. Compared with March: 1.6 °C warmer, 12 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. May is 2.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Marrakesh in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Marrakesh in April?
In April, Marrakesh averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 10.6 °C (51 °F), with 27 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 258 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Marrakesh?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Marrakesh’s mild months (March, April, May, June, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Marrakesh in April?
27 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (39 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Marrakesh in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Marrakesh get in April?
About 258 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Marrakesh in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
