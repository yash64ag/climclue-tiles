How December compares in Marrakesh

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Marrakesh. Compared with November: 3.4 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. January is 1.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Marrakesh in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Marrakesh in December?
In December, Marrakesh averages a day high of 18.5 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 5.8 °C (42 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Marrakesh?
December is not one of Marrakesh’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Marrakesh in December?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (38 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Marrakesh in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Marrakesh get in December?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Marrakesh in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
