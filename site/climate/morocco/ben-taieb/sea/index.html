Ben Taieb sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Oriental · Morocco

The sea off Ben Taieb averages 22.4 °C (72 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.9 °C (59 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Ben Taieb sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ben Taieb?
In August, at about 22.4 °C (72 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ben Taieb?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 14.9 °C (59 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ben Taieb in February?
About 14.9 °C (59 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Ben Taieb?
About 18.1 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Ben Taieb nearby?
In August: Nador (24.1 °C, 49 km), Melilla (24.1 °C, 55 km) and Ahfir (24.3 °C, 124 km).
