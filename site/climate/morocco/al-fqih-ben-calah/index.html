Al Fqih Ben Çalah at a glance

Among the 39 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Morocco, Al Fqih Ben Çalah is the warmest (20.1 °C year average) and the 5th sunniest (about 3,063 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Beni Mellal, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Al Fqih Ben Çalah is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 149 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.8 °C), averages 18.6 °C more than its coolest, January (11.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 158 km from the sea.

Why Al Fqih Ben Çalah has this climate
