How December compares in Tangier

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Tangier. Compared with November: 2.1 °C cooler, 13 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. January is 1.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Tangier in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tangier in December?
In December, Tangier averages a day high of 17.2 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 10.9 °C (52 °F), with 125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Tangier?
December is not one of Tangier’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tangier in December?
125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (125 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Tangier in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tangier get in December?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Tangier in December?
About 17.5 °C (64 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (22.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Tangier in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
