How May compares in Tangier

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Tangier. Compared with April: 2.3 °C warmer, 22 mm less precipitation and 48 hours more sunshine. June is 2.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Tangier in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tangier in May?
In May, Tangier averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 14.9 °C (59 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 288 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Tangier?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Tangier’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tangier in May?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (125 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Tangier in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tangier get in May?
About 288 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Tangier in May?
About 17.7 °C (64 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (22.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Tangier in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
