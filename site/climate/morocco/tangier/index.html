Tangier at a glance

Among the 39 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Morocco, Tangier is the 2nd wettest (693 mm a year). Compared with Tétouan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Tangier is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 7 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.1 °C), averages 11.7 °C more than its coolest, January (12.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.2 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tangier, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tangier has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
