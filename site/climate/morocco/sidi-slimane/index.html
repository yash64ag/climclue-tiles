Sidi Slimane at a glance

Among the 39 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Morocco, Sidi Slimane is 9th warmest (19.0 °C year average), 29th sunniest (about 2,850 hours of sunshine a year) and 16th wettest (438 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Khemisset, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), Sidi Slimane is 0.9 °C warmer in July and 1.6 °C warmer in January, with 55 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.8 °C), averages 13.9 °C more than its coolest, January (11.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.6 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 64 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sidi Slimane, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sidi Slimane has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 64 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
