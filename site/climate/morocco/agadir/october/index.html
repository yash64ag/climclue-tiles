How October compares in Agadir

October is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Agadir. Compared with September: 1.7 °C cooler, 23 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. November is 2.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Agadir in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Agadir in October?
In October, Agadir averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 18.3 °C (65 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Agadir?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Agadir’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Agadir in October?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (65 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Agadir in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Agadir get in October?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Agadir in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
