How December compares in Agadir

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Agadir. Compared with November: 2.6 °C cooler, 20 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. January is 1.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Agadir in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Agadir in December?
In December, Agadir averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 11.0 °C (52 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Agadir?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Agadir’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Agadir in December?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (65 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Agadir in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Agadir get in December?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Agadir in December?
About 18.1 °C (65 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (19.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Agadir in December?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
