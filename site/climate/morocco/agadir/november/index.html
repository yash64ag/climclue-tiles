How November compares in Agadir

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Agadir. Compared with October: 3.2 °C cooler, 21 mm more precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. December is 2.8 °C cooler than November on average.

Agadir in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Agadir in November?
In November, Agadir averages a day high of 22.4 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 14.2 °C (58 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Agadir?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Agadir’s mild months (February, March, April, May, June, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Agadir in November?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (69 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Agadir in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Agadir get in November?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Agadir in November?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
