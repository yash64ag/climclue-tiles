How August compares in Agadir

August is the warmest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Agadir. Compared with July: 0.3 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. September is 1.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Agadir in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Agadir in August?
In August, Agadir averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.0 °C (70 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 271 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Agadir?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Agadir’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Agadir in August?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (65 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Agadir in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Agadir get in August?
About 271 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Agadir in August?
About 19.9 °C (68 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (19.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Agadir in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
