How June compares in Agadir

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Agadir. Compared with May: 2.3 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. July is 3.2 °C warmer than June on average.

Agadir in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Agadir in June?
In June, Agadir averages a day high of 27.8 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 17.8 °C (64 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 273 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Agadir?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Agadir’s mild months (February, March, April, May, June, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Agadir in June?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (69 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Agadir in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Agadir get in June?
About 273 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Agadir in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
