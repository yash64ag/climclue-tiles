How September compares in Agadir

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Agadir. Compared with August: 1.9 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. October is 2.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Agadir in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Agadir in September?
In September, Agadir averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 19.6 °C (67 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Agadir?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Agadir’s mild months (February, March, April, May, June, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Agadir in September?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (69 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Agadir in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Agadir get in September?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Agadir in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
