How January compares in Agadir

January is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Agadir. Compared with December: 1.2 °C cooler, 23 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. February is 1.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Agadir in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Agadir in January?
In January, Agadir averages a day high of 21.2 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 9.7 °C (49 °F), with 42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Agadir?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Agadir’s mild months (January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Agadir in January?
42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (65 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Agadir in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Agadir get in January?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Agadir in January?
About 17.3 °C (63 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (19.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Agadir in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
