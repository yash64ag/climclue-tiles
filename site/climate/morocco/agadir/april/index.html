How April compares in Agadir

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Agadir. Compared with March: 0.7 °C warmer, 14 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. May is 1.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Agadir in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Agadir in April?
In April, Agadir averages a day high of 23.4 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 13.7 °C (57 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Agadir?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Agadir’s mild months (February, March, April, May, June, September, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Agadir in April?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (69 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Agadir in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Agadir get in April?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Agadir in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
