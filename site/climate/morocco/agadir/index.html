Why Agadir has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Agadir climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Agadir?
August, with an average of 25.6 °C (78 °F), day highs around 31.9 °C (89 °F) and night lows around 20.9 °C (70 °F).
What is the coldest month in Agadir?
January, with an average of 14.3 °C (58 °F), day highs around 18.6 °C (65 °F) and night lows around 10.0 °C (50 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Agadir?
December, with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 317 mm (12.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Agadir?
July, with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Agadir?
May, with about 282 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Agadir?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Agadir?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March, April, May, June, September, October, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.6 × 11 km, that contains Agadir’s GeoNames location (30.420°N, 9.598°W; ClimaClue area sq-535-1704), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 698,310 (GeoNames).
