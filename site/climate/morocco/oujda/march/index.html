How March compares in Oujda

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Oujda. Compared with February: 2.3 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. April is 1.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Oujda in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oujda in March?
In March, Oujda averages a day high of 18.9 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 6.3 °C (43 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Oujda?
March is not one of Oujda’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oujda in March?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (38 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Oujda in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Oujda get in March?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oujda in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
