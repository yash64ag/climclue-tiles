How May compares in Oujda

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Oujda. Compared with April: 3.1 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. June is 4.4 °C warmer than May on average.

Oujda in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oujda in May?
In May, Oujda averages a day high of 24.7 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 11.5 °C (53 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Oujda?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Oujda’s mild months (May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oujda in May?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (38 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Oujda in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Oujda get in May?
About 278 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oujda in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
