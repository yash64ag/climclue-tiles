How January compares in Oujda

January is the coolest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Oujda. Compared with December: 1.3 °C cooler, 9 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. February is 1.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Oujda in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oujda in January?
In January, Oujda averages a day high of 14.1 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 3.4 °C (38 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Oujda?
January is not one of Oujda’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oujda in January?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (38 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Oujda in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.9 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Oujda get in January?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oujda in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
