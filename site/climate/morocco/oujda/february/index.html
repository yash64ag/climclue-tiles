How February compares in Oujda

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Oujda. Compared with January: 1.6 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. March is 2.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Oujda in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oujda in February?
In February, Oujda averages a day high of 16.0 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 4.5 °C (40 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Oujda?
February is not one of Oujda’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oujda in February?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (38 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Oujda in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Oujda get in February?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oujda in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
