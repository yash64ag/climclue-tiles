How July compares in Oujda

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Oujda. Compared with June: 3.5 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. August is 0.1 °C warmer than July on average.

Oujda in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Oujda in July?
In July, Oujda averages a day high of 33.7 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 331 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Oujda?
July is not one of Oujda’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Oujda in July?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is March (38 mm), the driest July (4 mm).
Does it snow in Oujda in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Oujda get in July?
About 331 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Oujda in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
