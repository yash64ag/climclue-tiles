Beni Mellal at a glance

Among the 39 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Morocco, Beni Mellal is the 2nd sunniest (about 3,107 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Al Fqih Ben Çalah, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Beni Mellal is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 149 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.4 °C), averages 19.1 °C more than its coolest, January (10.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 189 km from the sea.

Why Beni Mellal has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 189 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
