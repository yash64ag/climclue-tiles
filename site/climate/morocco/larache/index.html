Larache at a glance

Among the 39 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Morocco, Larache is the 4th wettest (662 mm a year). Compared with Ksar El Kebir, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Larache is 1.0 °C cooler in August and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 72 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.4 °C), averages 10.8 °C more than its coolest, January (12.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.7 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Larache, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Larache has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
