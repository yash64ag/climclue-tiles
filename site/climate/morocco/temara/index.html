Temara at a glance

Among the 39 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Morocco, Temara is 19th warmest (18.5 °C year average), 22nd sunniest (about 2,882 hours of sunshine a year) and 15th wettest (465 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rabat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Temara is about as warm in August and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 20 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.6 °C), averages 10.7 °C more than its coolest, January (12.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.4 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Temara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Temara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
