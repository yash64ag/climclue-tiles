Gueznaia sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Tanger-Tetouan-Al Hoceima · Morocco

The sea off Gueznaia averages 22.0 °C (72 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 15.9 °C (61 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to October.

Gueznaia sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Gueznaia?
In August, at about 22.0 °C (72 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Gueznaia?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to October. The coolest month is February, at 15.9 °C (61 °F).
How warm is the sea in Gueznaia in February?
About 15.9 °C (61 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Gueznaia?
About 18.6 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
