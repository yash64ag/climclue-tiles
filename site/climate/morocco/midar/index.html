Midar at a glance

Among the 176 towns ClimaClue covers in Morocco, Midar is 148th warmest (17.4 °C year average), 160th sunniest (about 2,734 hours of sunshine a year) and 83rd wettest (386 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ben Taieb, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Midar is 1.1 °C warmer in August and about as warm in January, with 35 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.3 °C), averages 14.8 °C more than its coolest, January (10.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.2 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 38 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Midar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Midar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Midar itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.940°N, 3.533°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-490-1764), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
