How August compares in Casablanca

August is the warmest month, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Casablanca. Compared with July: 0.3 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. September is 0.8 °C cooler than August on average.

Casablanca in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Casablanca in August?
In August, Casablanca averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.4 °C (67 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 294 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Casablanca?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Casablanca’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Casablanca in August?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (65 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Casablanca in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Casablanca get in August?
About 294 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Casablanca in August?
About 22.6 °C (73 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (22.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Casablanca in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
