How May compares in Casablanca

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Casablanca. Compared with April: 2.1 °C warmer, 17 mm less precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. June is 2.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Casablanca in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Casablanca in May?
In May, Casablanca averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 14.7 °C (58 °F), with 14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 291 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Casablanca?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Casablanca’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Casablanca in May?
14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (65 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Casablanca in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Casablanca get in May?
About 291 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Casablanca in May?
About 18.7 °C (66 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (22.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Casablanca in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
