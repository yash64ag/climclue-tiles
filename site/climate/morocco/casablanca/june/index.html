How June compares in Casablanca

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Casablanca. Compared with May: 2.6 °C warmer, 11 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. July is 1.8 °C warmer than June on average.

Casablanca in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Casablanca in June?
In June, Casablanca averages a day high of 26.2 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 17.2 °C (63 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 285 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Casablanca?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Casablanca’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Casablanca in June?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (66 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Casablanca in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Casablanca get in June?
About 285 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Casablanca in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
