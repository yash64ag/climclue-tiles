How December compares in Casablanca

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Casablanca. Compared with November: 2.5 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. January is 1.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Casablanca in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Casablanca in December?
In December, Casablanca averages a day high of 18.2 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 10.6 °C (51 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Casablanca?
December is not one of Casablanca’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Casablanca in December?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (66 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Casablanca in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Casablanca get in December?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Casablanca in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
