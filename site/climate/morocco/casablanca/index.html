Casablanca at a glance

Among the 39 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Morocco, Casablanca is 20th warmest (18.4 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 2,895 hours of sunshine a year) and 24th wettest (365 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bouskoura, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Casablanca is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.6 °C), averages 10.8 °C more than its coolest, January (12.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.8 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Casablanca, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Casablanca has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
