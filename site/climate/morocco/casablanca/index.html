Why Casablanca has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Casablanca climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Casablanca?
August, with an average of 23.6 °C (75 °F), day highs around 28.5 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 19.5 °C (67 °F).
What is the coldest month in Casablanca?
January, with an average of 12.8 °C (55 °F), day highs around 16.9 °C (62 °F) and night lows around 8.8 °C (48 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Casablanca?
December, with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 365 mm (14.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Casablanca?
July, with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Casablanca?
July, with about 304 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Casablanca?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Casablanca?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.3 × 11 km, that contains Casablanca’s GeoNames location (33.588°N, 7.611°W; ClimaClue area sq-504-1723), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 3,665,954 (GeoNames).
