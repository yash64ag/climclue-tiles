How February compares in Casablanca

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Casablanca. Compared with January: 1.2 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. March is 1.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Casablanca in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Casablanca in February?
In February, Casablanca averages a day high of 18.1 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 9.8 °C (50 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Casablanca?
February is not one of Casablanca’s mild months; those are March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Casablanca in February?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (66 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Casablanca in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Casablanca get in February?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Casablanca in February?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
