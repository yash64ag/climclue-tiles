How March compares in Casablanca

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Casablanca. Compared with February: 1.7 °C warmer, 12 mm less precipitation and 54 hours more sunshine. April is 1.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Casablanca in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Casablanca in March?
In March, Casablanca averages a day high of 20.1 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 11.6 °C (53 °F), with 37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Casablanca?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Casablanca’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Casablanca in March?
37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (65 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Casablanca in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Casablanca get in March?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Casablanca in March?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
