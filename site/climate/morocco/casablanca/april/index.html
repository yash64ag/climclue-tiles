Casablanca weather in April

Average April weather in Casablanca, Morocco: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Casablanca averages 21.6 °C (71 °F) days and 12.3 °C (54 °F) nights, with 31 mm (1.2 in) of rain over the month and about 258 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Casablanca

April is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Casablanca. Compared with March: 1.0 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. May is 2.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Casablanca in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Casablanca in April?
In April, Casablanca averages a day high of 21.6 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 12.3 °C (54 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 258 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Casablanca?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Casablanca’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Casablanca in April?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (65 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Casablanca in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Casablanca get in April?
About 258 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Casablanca in April?
About 17.4 °C (63 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (22.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Casablanca in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
