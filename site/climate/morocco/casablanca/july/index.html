How July compares in Casablanca

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Casablanca. Compared with June: 1.8 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C warmer than July on average.

Casablanca in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Casablanca in July?
In July, Casablanca averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 19.0 °C (66 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 304 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Casablanca?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Casablanca’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation.
