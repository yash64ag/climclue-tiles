How November compares in Casablanca

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Casablanca. Compared with October: 3.3 °C cooler, 31 mm more precipitation and 43 hours less sunshine. December is 2.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Casablanca in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Casablanca in November?
In November, Casablanca averages a day high of 20.9 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 12.9 °C (55 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Casablanca?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Casablanca’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Casablanca in November?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (66 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Casablanca in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Casablanca get in November?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Casablanca in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
