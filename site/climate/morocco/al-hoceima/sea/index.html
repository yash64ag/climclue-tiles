Al Hoceïma sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Tanger-Tetouan-Al Hoceima · Morocco

The sea off Al Hoceïma averages 22.6 °C (73 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 15.0 °C (59 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Al Hoceïma sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Al Hoceïma?
In August, at about 22.6 °C (73 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Al Hoceïma?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 15.0 °C (59 °F).
How warm is the sea in Al Hoceïma in February?
About 15.0 °C (59 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Al Hoceïma?
About 18.2 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Al Hoceïma nearby?
In August: Melilla (24.1 °C, 91 km) and Nador (24.1 °C, 92 km).
