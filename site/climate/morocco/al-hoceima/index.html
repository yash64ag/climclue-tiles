Al Hoceïma at a glance

Among the 39 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Morocco, Al Hoceïma is the 5th coolest (17.4 °C year average) and the 3rd least sunny (about 2,735 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Nador, the nearest covered town with different numbers (92 km away), Al Hoceïma is 3.3 °C cooler in August and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 27 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.1 °C), averages 10.4 °C more than its coolest, January (12.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.3 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Al Hoceïma, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Al Hoceïma has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
