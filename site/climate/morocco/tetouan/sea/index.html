Tétouan sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Tanger-Tetouan-Al Hoceima · Morocco

The sea off Tétouan averages 21.2 °C (70 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 15.1 °C (59 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Tétouan sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Tétouan?
In August, at about 21.2 °C (70 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Tétouan?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 15.1 °C (59 °F).
How warm is the sea in Tétouan in February?
About 15.1 °C (59 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Tétouan?
About 17.8 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Tétouan nearby?
In August: Tangier (22.0 °C, 44 km), Gueznaia (22.0 °C, 49 km) and Asilah (22.4 °C, 62 km).
