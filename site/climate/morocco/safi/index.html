Safi at a glance

Among the 39 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Morocco, Safi is 12th warmest (18.9 °C year average), 6th sunniest (about 3,043 hours of sunshine a year) and 27th wettest (338 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with El Jadida, the nearest covered town with different numbers (126 km away), Safi is 0.6 °C warmer in August and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 78 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.4 °C), averages 9.7 °C more than its coolest, January (13.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.4 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Safi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Safi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
