Berrechid at a glance

Among the 39 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Morocco, Berrechid is 30th warmest (18.0 °C year average), 15th sunniest (about 2,932 hours of sunshine a year) and 26th wettest (356 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bouskoura, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Berrechid is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 7 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.6 °C), averages 11.9 °C more than its coolest, January (11.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.8 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 48 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Berrechid, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Berrechid has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 48 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
