Salé Al Jadida at a glance

Among the 39 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Morocco, Salé Al Jadida is 23rd warmest (18.3 °C year average), 23rd sunniest (about 2,879 hours of sunshine a year) and 14th wettest (468 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Salé, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Salé Al Jadida is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 47 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.9 °C), averages 11.3 °C more than its coolest, January (12.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.6 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Salé Al Jadida has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
