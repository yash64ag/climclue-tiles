Aourir sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Souss-Massa · Morocco

The sea off Aourir averages 19.9 °C (68 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 16.8 °C (62 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Aourir sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Aourir?
In August, at about 19.9 °C (68 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Aourir?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 16.8 °C (62 °F).
How warm is the sea in Aourir in February?
About 16.8 °C (62 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Aourir?
About 18.4 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Aourir nearby?
In August: Safi (20.9 °C, 204 km), El Jadida (22.6 °C, 325 km) and Azemmour (22.6 °C, 334 km).
