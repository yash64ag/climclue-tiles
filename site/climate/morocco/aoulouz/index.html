Aoulouz at a glance

Among the 176 towns ClimaClue covers in Morocco, Aoulouz is 12th warmest (20.9 °C year average), 18th sunniest (about 3,128 hours of sunshine a year) and 150th wettest (218 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Oulad Barhil, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Aoulouz is 0.9 °C cooler in July and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 7 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.4 °C), averages 17.5 °C more than its coolest, January (12.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.6 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 155 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Aoulouz, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Aoulouz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 155 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.5 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Aoulouz itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (30.673°N, 8.181°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-533-1718), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
