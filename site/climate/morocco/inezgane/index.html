Inezgane at a glance

Among the 176 towns ClimaClue covers in Morocco, Inezgane is 21st warmest (20.1 °C year average), 71st sunniest (about 2,937 hours of sunshine a year) and 140th wettest (241 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ait Melloul, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Inezgane is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 8 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.5 °C), averages 9.2 °C more than its coolest, January (15.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.6 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 19 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Inezgane, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Inezgane has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Inezgane itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (30.355°N, 9.536°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-536-1704), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
