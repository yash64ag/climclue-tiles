Inezgane at a glance

Among the 39 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Morocco, Inezgane is the 5th warmest (20.1 °C year average) and the 5th driest (241 mm a year). Compared with Ait Melloul, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Inezgane is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 8 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.5 °C), averages 9.2 °C more than its coolest, January (15.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.0 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 19 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Inezgane, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Inezgane has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
