Tit Mellil sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Casablanca-Settat · Morocco

The sea off Tit Mellil averages 22.6 °C (73 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 16.4 °C (62 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to October.

Tit Mellil sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Tit Mellil?
In August, at about 22.6 °C (73 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Tit Mellil?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to October. The coolest month is February, at 16.4 °C (62 °F).
How warm is the sea in Tit Mellil in February?
About 16.4 °C (62 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Tit Mellil?
About 19.3 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
