Sidi Yahia El Gharb at a glance

Among the 176 towns ClimaClue covers in Morocco, Sidi Yahia El Gharb is 75th warmest (18.5 °C year average), 133rd sunniest (about 2,848 hours of sunshine a year) and 61st wettest (464 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kenitra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Sidi Yahia El Gharb is 1.2 °C warmer in August and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 84 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.4 °C), averages 12.1 °C more than its coolest, January (12.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.2 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 29 km from the sea.

Why Sidi Yahia El Gharb has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 29 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sidi Yahia El Gharb itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.305°N, 6.304°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-496-1736), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
