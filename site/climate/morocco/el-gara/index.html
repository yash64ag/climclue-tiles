El Gara at a glance

Among the 176 towns ClimaClue covers in Morocco, El Gara is 134th warmest (17.7 °C year average), 73rd sunniest (about 2,936 hours of sunshine a year) and 104th wettest (360 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ben Ahmed, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), El Gara is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 11 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.9 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, January (11.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.7 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 62 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of El Gara, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why El Gara has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 62 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for El Gara itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.277°N, 7.232°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-507-1727), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
