Essaouira sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Marrakesh-Safi · Morocco

The sea off Essaouira averages 20.2 °C (68 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 16.7 °C (62 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September.

Essaouira sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Essaouira?
In September, at about 20.2 °C (68 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Essaouira?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is February, at 16.7 °C (62 °F).
How warm is the sea in Essaouira in February?
About 16.7 °C (62 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Essaouira?
About 18.3 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Essaouira nearby?
In September: Safi (21.0 °C, 101 km), El Jadida (22.4 °C, 227 km) and Azemmour (22.4 °C, 239 km).
