Essaouira at a glance

Among the 176 towns ClimaClue covers in Morocco, Essaouira is 50th warmest (18.9 °C year average), 68th sunniest (about 2,944 hours of sunshine a year) and 131st wettest (279 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sebt Gzoula, the nearest covered town with different numbers (93 km away), Essaouira is 1.7 °C cooler in August and 2.4 °C warmer in January, with 18 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (22.7 °C), averages 7.6 °C more than its coolest, January (15.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.2 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Essaouira, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Essaouira has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Essaouira itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (31.512°N, 9.770°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-524-1702), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
