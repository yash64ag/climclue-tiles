Khenifra at a glance

Among the 39 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Morocco, Khenifra is the 4th coolest (17.3 °C year average) and the 3rd wettest (672 mm a year). Compared with Oued Zem, the nearest covered town with different numbers (86 km away), Khenifra is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 288 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 19.0 °C more than its coolest, January (8.4 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 171 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Khenifra, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Khenifra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 171 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
