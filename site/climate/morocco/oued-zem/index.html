Oued Zem at a glance

Among the 39 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Morocco, Oued Zem is 31st warmest (18.0 °C year average), 7th sunniest (about 3,003 hours of sunshine a year) and 20th wettest (391 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Khouribga, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Oued Zem is 1.3 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.2 °C), averages 17.5 °C more than its coolest, January (9.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 131 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Oued Zem, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Oued Zem has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 131 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
