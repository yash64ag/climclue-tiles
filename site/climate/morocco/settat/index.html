Settat at a glance

Among the 176 towns ClimaClue covers in Morocco, Settat is 130th warmest (17.9 °C year average), 65th sunniest (about 2,954 hours of sunshine a year) and 114th wettest (337 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Berrechid, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Settat is 0.4 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 28 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.1 °C), averages 12.6 °C more than its coolest, January (11.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.8 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 62 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Settat, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Settat has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 62 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Settat itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.001°N, 7.617°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-509-1723), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
