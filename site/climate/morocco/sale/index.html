Salé at a glance

Among the 39 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Morocco, Salé is 21st warmest (18.4 °C year average), 25th sunniest (about 2,869 hours of sunshine a year) and 9th wettest (515 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rabat, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Salé is about as warm in August and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 31 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.6 °C), averages 10.6 °C more than its coolest, January (13.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.7 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Salé, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Salé has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
