How August compares in Salé

August is the warmest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Salé. Compared with July: 0.3 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. September is 0.9 °C cooler than August on average.

Salé in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Salé in August?
In August, Salé averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 20.1 °C (68 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 305 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Salé?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Salé’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Salé in August?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (96 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Salé in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Salé get in August?
About 305 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Salé in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
