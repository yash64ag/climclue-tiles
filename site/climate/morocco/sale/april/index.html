How April compares in Salé

April is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Salé. Compared with March: 1.0 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. May is 2.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Salé in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Salé in April?
In April, Salé averages a day high of 21.6 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 11.9 °C (53 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 252 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Salé?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Salé’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Salé in April?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (94 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Salé in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Salé get in April?
About 252 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Salé in April?
About 17.6 °C (64 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (22.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Salé in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
