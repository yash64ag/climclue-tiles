Salé weather in June

Average June weather in Salé, Morocco: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Salé averages 25.7 °C (78 °F) days and 16.9 °C (62 °F) nights, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of rain over the month and about 289 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Salé

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Salé. Compared with May: 2.5 °C warmer, 16 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. July is 2.0 °C warmer than June on average.

Salé in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Salé in June?
In June, Salé averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 289 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Salé?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Salé’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Salé in June?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (94 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Salé in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Salé get in June?
About 289 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Salé in June?
About 20.8 °C (69 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (22.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Salé in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
