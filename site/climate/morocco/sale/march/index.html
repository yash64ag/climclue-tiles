How March compares in Salé

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Salé. Compared with February: 1.6 °C warmer, 16 mm less precipitation and 54 hours more sunshine. April is 1.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Salé in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Salé in March?
In March, Salé averages a day high of 19.1 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 11.7 °C (53 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Salé?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Salé’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Salé in March?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (96 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Salé in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Salé get in March?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Salé in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
