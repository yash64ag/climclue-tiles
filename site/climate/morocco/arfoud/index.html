Arfoud at a glance

Among the 176 towns ClimaClue covers in Morocco, Arfoud is the 4th sunniest (about 3,349 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Reçani, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Arfoud is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (34.5 °C), averages 24.2 °C more than its coolest, January (10.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 382 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Arfoud, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Arfoud has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 382 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Arfoud itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (31.435°N, 4.233°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-525-1757), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
