Souk El Arbaa at a glance

Among the 176 towns ClimaClue covers in Morocco, Souk El Arbaa is 18th warmest (20.4 °C year average), 138th sunniest (about 2,833 hours of sunshine a year) and 41st wettest (530 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Souq Larb’a al Gharb, the nearest covered town with different numbers (0 km away), Souk El Arbaa is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.3 °C), averages 15.9 °C more than its coolest, January (12.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.8 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 38 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Souk El Arbaa, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Souk El Arbaa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Souk El Arbaa itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.685°N, 6.003°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-493-1739), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
