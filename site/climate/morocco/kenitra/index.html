Kenitra at a glance

Among the 39 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Morocco, Kenitra is 24th warmest (18.3 °C year average), 28th sunniest (about 2,853 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (532 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Salé, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Kenitra is 0.4 °C cooler in August and about as warm in January, with 17 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.3 °C), averages 10.2 °C more than its coolest, January (13.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.9 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 18 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kenitra, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kenitra has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
