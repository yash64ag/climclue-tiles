How February compares in Meknes

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Meknes. Compared with January: 1.8 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. March is 2.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Meknes in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Meknes in February?
In February, Meknes averages a day high of 16.8 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 5.2 °C (41 °F), with 64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Meknes?
February is not one of Meknes’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Meknes in February?
64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (86 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Meknes in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Meknes get in February?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Meknes in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
