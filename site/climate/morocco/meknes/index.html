Meknes at a glance

Among the 39 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Morocco, Meknes is the 2nd coolest (17.2 °C year average). Compared with Khemisset, the nearest covered town with different numbers (49 km away), Meknes is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 28 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.4 °C), averages 15.8 °C more than its coolest, January (9.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 119 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Meknes, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Meknes has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 119 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
