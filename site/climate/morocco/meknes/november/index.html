How November compares in Meknes

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Meknes. Compared with October: 4.5 °C cooler, 28 mm more precipitation and 48 hours less sunshine. December is 3.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Meknes in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Meknes in November?
In November, Meknes averages a day high of 19.4 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 8.4 °C (47 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Meknes?
November is not one of Meknes’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Meknes in November?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (86 mm), the driest July (3 mm).
Does it snow in Meknes in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Meknes get in November?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Meknes in November?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
