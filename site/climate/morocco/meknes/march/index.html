How March compares in Meknes

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Meknes. Compared with February: 2.4 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 53 hours more sunshine. April is 1.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Meknes in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Meknes in March?
In March, Meknes averages a day high of 19.7 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 8.3 °C (47 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Meknes?
March is not one of Meknes’s mild months; those are April, May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Meknes in March?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (82 mm), the driest July (2 mm).
Does it snow in Meknes in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Meknes get in March?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Meknes in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
