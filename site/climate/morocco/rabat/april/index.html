How April compares in Rabat

April is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Rabat. Compared with March: 1.0 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. May is 2.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Rabat in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rabat in April?
In April, Rabat averages a day high of 20.1 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 13.2 °C (56 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 252 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Rabat?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Rabat’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rabat in April?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (91 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Rabat in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rabat get in April?
About 252 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rabat in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
