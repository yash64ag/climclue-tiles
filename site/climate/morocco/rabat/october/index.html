How October compares in Rabat

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Rabat. Compared with September: 2.3 °C cooler, 30 mm more precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. November is 3.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Rabat in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rabat in October?
In October, Rabat averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Rabat?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Rabat’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rabat in October?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (91 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Rabat in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rabat get in October?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Rabat in October?
About 21.1 °C (70 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (22.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Rabat in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
