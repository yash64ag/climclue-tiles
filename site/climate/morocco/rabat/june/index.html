How June compares in Rabat

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Rabat. Compared with May: 2.5 °C warmer, 15 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. July is 2.0 °C warmer than June on average.

Rabat in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rabat in June?
In June, Rabat averages a day high of 25.0 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 17.8 °C (64 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 289 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Rabat?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Rabat’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rabat in June?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (91 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Rabat in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rabat get in June?
About 289 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rabat in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
