How November compares in Rabat

November is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Rabat. Compared with October: 3.3 °C cooler, 42 mm more precipitation and 46 hours less sunshine. December is 2.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Rabat in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rabat in November?
In November, Rabat averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 12.3 °C (54 °F), with 85 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Rabat?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Rabat’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rabat in November?
85 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (91 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Rabat in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rabat get in November?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rabat in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
