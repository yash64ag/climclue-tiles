How July compares in Rabat

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Rabat. Compared with June: 2.0 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C warmer than July on average.

Rabat in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rabat in July?
In July, Rabat averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 19.6 °C (67 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 314 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Rabat?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Rabat’s mild months (March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rabat in July?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (91 mm), the driest July (1 mm).
Does it snow in Rabat in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rabat get in July?
About 314 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rabat in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
