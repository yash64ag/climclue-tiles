Mers El Kheir at a glance

Among the 176 towns ClimaClue covers in Morocco, Mers El Kheir is 94th warmest (18.3 °C year average), 101st sunniest (about 2,885 hours of sunshine a year) and 63rd wettest (461 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Aïn Attig, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Mers El Kheir is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 5 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.8 °C), averages 11.3 °C more than its coolest, January (12.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.7 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Mers El Kheir has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
