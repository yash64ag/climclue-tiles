Levin sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Manawatu-Wanganui · New Zealand

The sea off Levin averages 17.1 °C (63 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 11.8 °C (53 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Levin sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Levin?
In February, at about 17.1 °C (63 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Levin?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 11.8 °C (53 °F).
How warm is the sea in Levin in August?
About 11.8 °C (53 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Levin?
About 14.2 °C (58 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Levin nearby?
In February: Whanganui (18.2 °C, 80 km), Hastings (18.9 °C, 174 km) and Nelson (18.7 °C, 182 km).
