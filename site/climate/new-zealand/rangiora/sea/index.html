Rangiora sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Canterbury · New Zealand

The sea off Rangiora averages 16.3 °C (61 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 8.5 °C (47 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Rangiora sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Rangiora?
In February, at about 16.3 °C (61 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Rangiora?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 8.5 °C (47 °F).
How warm is the sea in Rangiora in August?
About 8.5 °C (47 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Rangiora?
About 12.4 °C (54 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Rangiora nearby?
In February: Richmond (18.7 °C, 225 km), Stoke (18.7 °C, 227 km) and Nelson (18.7 °C, 233 km).
