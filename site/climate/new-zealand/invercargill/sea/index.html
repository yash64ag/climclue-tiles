Invercargill sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Southland · New Zealand

The sea off Invercargill averages 14.5 °C (58 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 10.0 °C (50 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Invercargill sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Invercargill?
In February, at about 14.5 °C (58 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Invercargill?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 10.0 °C (50 °F).
How warm is the sea in Invercargill in August?
About 10.0 °C (50 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Invercargill?
About 12.1 °C (54 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Invercargill nearby?
In February: Timaru (16.2 °C, 318 km), Christchurch (16.4 °C, 464 km) and Rangiora (16.3 °C, 479 km).
