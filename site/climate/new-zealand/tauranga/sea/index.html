Tauranga sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Bay of Plenty · New Zealand

The sea off Tauranga averages 20.6 °C (69 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 14.5 °C (58 °F) in August. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from February to March.

Tauranga sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Tauranga?
In February, at about 20.6 °C (69 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Tauranga?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from February to March. The coolest month is August, at 14.5 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in Tauranga in August?
About 14.5 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Tauranga?
About 17.2 °C (63 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
