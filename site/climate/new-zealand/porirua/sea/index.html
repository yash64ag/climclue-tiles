Porirua sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Wellington Region · New Zealand

The sea off Porirua averages 16.1 °C (61 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 11.3 °C (52 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Porirua sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Porirua?
In February, at about 16.1 °C (61 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Porirua?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 11.3 °C (52 °F).
How warm is the sea in Porirua in August?
About 11.3 °C (52 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Porirua?
About 13.5 °C (56 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Porirua nearby?
In February: Levin (17.1 °C, 66 km), Nelson (18.7 °C, 132 km) and Whanganui (18.2 °C, 135 km).
