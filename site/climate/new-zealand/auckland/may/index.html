How May compares in Auckland

May is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Auckland. Compared with April: 2.1 °C cooler, 20 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. June is 1.9 °C cooler than May on average.

Auckland in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Auckland in May?
In May, Auckland averages a day high of 18.4 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 11.0 °C (52 °F), with 114 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 137 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Auckland?
May is not one of Auckland’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Auckland in May?
114 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (142 mm), the driest January (73 mm).
Does it snow in Auckland in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Auckland get in May?
About 137 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Auckland in May?
About 16.9 °C (62 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is February (20.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Auckland in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
