How October compares in Auckland

October is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Auckland. Compared with September: 1.3 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. November is 1.6 °C warmer than October on average.

Auckland in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Auckland in October?
In October, Auckland averages a day high of 16.0 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Auckland?
October is not one of Auckland’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Auckland in October?
100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (146 mm), the driest January (75 mm).
Does it snow in Auckland in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Auckland get in October?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Auckland in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
