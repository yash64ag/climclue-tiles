How November compares in Auckland

November is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Auckland. Compared with October: 1.5 °C warmer, 15 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. December is 2.1 °C warmer than November on average.

Auckland in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Auckland in November?
In November, Auckland averages a day high of 17.7 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Auckland?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Auckland’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Auckland in November?
81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (142 mm), the driest January (73 mm).
Does it snow in Auckland in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Auckland get in November?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Auckland in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
