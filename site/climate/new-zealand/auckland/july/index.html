How July compares in Auckland

July is the coolest month, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Auckland. Compared with June: 1.0 °C cooler, 16 mm more precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C warmer than July on average.

Auckland in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Auckland in July?
In July, Auckland averages a day high of 13.3 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 9.6 °C (49 °F), with 146 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Auckland?
July is not one of Auckland’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Auckland in July?
146 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (146 mm), the driest January (75 mm).
Does it snow in Auckland in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Auckland get in July?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Auckland in July?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
