How March compares in Auckland

March is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Auckland. Compared with February: 1.2 °C cooler, 9 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. April is 2.1 °C cooler than March on average.

Auckland in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Auckland in March?
In March, Auckland averages a day high of 20.4 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 16.3 °C (61 °F), with 85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Auckland?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Auckland’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Auckland in March?
85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (146 mm), the driest January (75 mm).
Does it snow in Auckland in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Auckland get in March?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Auckland in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
