How August compares in Auckland

August is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Auckland. Compared with July: 0.3 °C warmer, 21 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. September is 1.1 °C warmer than August on average.

Auckland in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Auckland in August?
In August, Auckland averages a day high of 15.7 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 8.6 °C (47 °F), with 121 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Auckland?
August is not one of Auckland’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Auckland in August?
121 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (142 mm), the driest January (73 mm).
Does it snow in Auckland in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Auckland get in August?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Auckland in August?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
