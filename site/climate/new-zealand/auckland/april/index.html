How April compares in Auckland

April is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Auckland. Compared with March: 2.1 °C cooler, 11 mm more precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. May is 2.1 °C cooler than April on average.

Auckland in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Auckland in April?
In April, Auckland averages a day high of 20.5 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 13.0 °C (55 °F), with 93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Auckland?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Auckland’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Auckland in April?
93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (142 mm), the driest January (73 mm).
Does it snow in Auckland in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Auckland get in April?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Auckland in April?
About 18.4 °C (65 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is February (20.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Auckland in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
