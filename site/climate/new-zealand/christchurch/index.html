Why Christchurch has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.3 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Christchurch climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Christchurch?
January, with an average of 16.8 °C (62 °F), day highs around 21.1 °C (70 °F) and night lows around 13.0 °C (55 °F).
What is the coldest month in Christchurch?
July, with an average of 6.5 °C (44 °F), day highs around 9.5 °C (49 °F) and night lows around 3.1 °C (38 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Christchurch?
July, with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 700 mm (27.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Christchurch?
January, with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Christchurch?
January, with about 216 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Christchurch?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 3.5 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in August; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Christchurch?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: January, February and December.
What type of climate does Christchurch have?
Oceanic (Köppen Cfb). Mild all year, with rain in every season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.1 × 11 km, that contains Christchurch’s GeoNames location (43.533°S, 172.633°E; ClimaClue area sq-1275-3526), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 419,200 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007).
