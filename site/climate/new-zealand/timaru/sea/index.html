Timaru sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Canterbury · New Zealand

The sea off Timaru averages 16.2 °C (61 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 9.0 °C (48 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Timaru sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Timaru?
In February, at about 16.2 °C (61 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Timaru?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 9.0 °C (48 °F).
How warm is the sea in Timaru in August?
About 9.0 °C (48 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Timaru?
About 12.4 °C (54 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Timaru nearby?
In February: Richmond (18.7 °C, 375 km), Stoke (18.7 °C, 379 km) and Nelson (18.7 °C, 385 km).
