Dunedin sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Otago · New Zealand

The sea off Dunedin averages 13.8 °C (57 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 9.5 °C (49 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Dunedin sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Dunedin?
In February, at about 13.8 °C (57 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Dunedin?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 9.5 °C (49 °F).
How warm is the sea in Dunedin in August?
About 9.5 °C (49 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Dunedin?
About 11.4 °C (53 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Dunedin nearby?
In February: Timaru (16.2 °C, 174 km), Invercargill (14.5 °C, 176 km) and Christchurch (16.4 °C, 310 km).
