Whanganui sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Manawatu-Wanganui · New Zealand

The sea off Whanganui averages 18.2 °C (65 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 12.2 °C (54 °F) in August. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Whanganui sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Whanganui?
In February, at about 18.2 °C (65 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Whanganui?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is August, at 12.2 °C (54 °F).
How warm is the sea in Whanganui in August?
About 12.2 °C (54 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Whanganui?
About 15.0 °C (59 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Whanganui nearby?
In February: New Plymouth (19.5 °C, 127 km), Hastings (18.9 °C, 157 km) and Napier (19.3 °C, 167 km).
