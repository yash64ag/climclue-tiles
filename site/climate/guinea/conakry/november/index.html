How November compares in Conakry

November is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Conakry. Compared with October: 0.7 °C warmer, 231 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. December is about as warm as November on average.

Conakry in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Conakry in November?
In November, Conakry averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Conakry?
No month in Conakry is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Conakry in November?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (1,041 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Conakry in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Conakry get in November?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Conakry in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
