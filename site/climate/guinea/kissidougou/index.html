Kissidougou at a glance

Among the 12 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Guinea, Kissidougou is the 3rd coolest (24.9 °C year average). Compared with Kankan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (159 km away), Kissidougou is 2.2 °C cooler in March and 1.3 °C cooler in August, with 568 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (27.6 °C), averages 4.4 °C more than its coolest, August (23.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.8 °C (66 °F), in January. The area lies about 304 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kissidougou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Kissidougou

For mild weather, the best months to visit Kissidougou are November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): March (27.6 °C), February (27.3 °C) and April (26.6 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): December (8 mm), January (9 mm) and February (15 mm).

Why Kissidougou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 304 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
