Nzérékoré at a glance

Among the 12 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Guinea, Nzérékoré is the 2nd coolest (24.5 °C year average) and the least sunny (about 2,128 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Kissidougou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (212 km away), Nzérékoré is 1.4 °C cooler in March and 0.2 °C cooler in August, with 221 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (26.3 °C), averages 3.2 °C more than its coolest, August (23.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 19.7 °C (67 °F), in January. The area lies about 253 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nzérékoré, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nzérékoré has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 253 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 3.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
