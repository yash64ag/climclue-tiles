Yende Millimou at a glance

Among the 47 towns ClimaClue covers in Guinea, Yende Millimou is the 4th least sunny (about 2,181 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Kissidougou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Yende Millimou is 0.6 °C cooler in March and 0.2 °C cooler in August, with 244 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (27.4 °C), averages 4.2 °C more than its coolest, August (23.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.2 °C (63 °F), in December. The area lies about 271 km from the sea.

Why Yende Millimou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 271 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 4.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Yende Millimou itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (8.887°N, 10.172°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-751-1698), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
