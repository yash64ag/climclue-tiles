Donghol-Sigon at a glance

Among the 47 towns ClimaClue covers in Guinea, Donghol-Sigon is the 3rd coolest (23.2 °C year average). Compared with Labé, the nearest covered town with different numbers (54 km away), Donghol-Sigon is 0.1 °C warmer in April and 0.5 °C warmer in August, with 400 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (26.6 °C), averages 5.1 °C more than its coolest, August (21.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.9 °C (57 °F), in December. The area lies about 289 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Donghol-Sigon, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Donghol-Sigon has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 289 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.1 °C. The 10 km area around Donghol-Sigon, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 24.3 °C a year, 1.1 °C warmer than the town itself, because it takes in lower ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Donghol-Sigon itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (11.800°N, 12.217°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 11 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-721-1677), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
