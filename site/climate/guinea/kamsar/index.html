Kamsar at a glance

Among the 12 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Guinea, Kamsar is the 2nd warmest (26.4 °C year average). Compared with Dubréka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (153 km away), Kamsar is 0.4 °C cooler in May and 0.5 °C warmer in August, with 759 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (27.1 °C), averages 1.5 °C more than its coolest, August (25.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 22.7 °C (73 °F), in January. The area lies about 16 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kamsar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kamsar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
