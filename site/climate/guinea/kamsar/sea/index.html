Kamsar sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Boké Region · Guinea

The sea off Kamsar averages 28.6 °C (83 °F) in November, its warmest month, and 26.2 °C (79 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year.

Kamsar sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Kamsar?
In November, at about 28.6 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Kamsar?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) all year. The coolest month is February, at 26.2 °C (79 °F).
How warm is the sea in Kamsar in February?
About 26.2 °C (79 °F): warm.
What is the average sea temperature in Kamsar?
About 27.4 °C (81 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Kamsar nearby?
In November: Bissau (29.2 °C, 172 km), Contumo (29.2 °C, 172 km) and Reino de Antula (29.2 °C, 174 km).
