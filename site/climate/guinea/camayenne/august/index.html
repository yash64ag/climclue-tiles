How August compares in Camayenne

August is the coolest month, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Camayenne. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 49 mm more precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. September is 0.4 °C warmer than August on average.

Camayenne in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Camayenne in August?
In August, Camayenne averages a day high of 26.2 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 1,057 mm (41.6 in) of precipitation and about 85 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Camayenne?
No month in Camayenne is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Camayenne in August?
1,057 mm (41.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (1,057 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Camayenne in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Camayenne get in August?
About 85 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Camayenne in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
