How September compares in Camayenne

September is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Camayenne. Compared with August: 0.3 °C warmer, 511 mm less precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. October is 0.6 °C warmer than September on average.

Camayenne in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Camayenne in September?
In September, Camayenne averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 22.4 °C (72 °F), with 530 mm (20.9 in) of precipitation and about 131 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Camayenne?
No month in Camayenne is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Camayenne in September?
530 mm (20.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (1,041 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Camayenne in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Camayenne get in September?
About 131 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Camayenne in September?
About 27.6 °C (82 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is November (28.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Camayenne in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
