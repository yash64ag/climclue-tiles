Camayenne in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Camayenne in November?
In November, Camayenne averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Camayenne?
No month in Camayenne is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Camayenne in November?
62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (1,057 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Camayenne in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Camayenne get in November?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Camayenne in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
