Camayenne in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Camayenne in February?
In February, Camayenne averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 24.4 °C (76 °F), with 1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Camayenne?
No month in Camayenne is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Camayenne in February?
1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (1,057 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Camayenne in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Camayenne get in February?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
