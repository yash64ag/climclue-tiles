Camayenne in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Camayenne in December?
In December, Camayenne averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Camayenne?
No month in Camayenne is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Camayenne in December?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (1,057 mm), the driest February (1 mm).
Does it snow in Camayenne in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Camayenne get in December?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Camayenne in December?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
