Dixinn at a glance

Among the 12 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Guinea, Dixinn is the 2nd least sunny (about 2,185 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (3,562 mm a year). Compared with Dubréka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Dixinn is 0.6 °C cooler in May and about as warm in August, with 469 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, May (26.9 °C), averages 1.8 °C more than its coolest, August (25.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 24.2 °C (76 °F), in August. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dixinn, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dixinn has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 1.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
