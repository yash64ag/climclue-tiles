Kankan at a glance

Among the 12 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Guinea, Kankan is the 2nd sunniest (about 2,607 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (1,499 mm a year). Compared with Siguiri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (116 km away), Kankan is 0.8 °C cooler in March and 0.5 °C cooler in August, with 324 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, March (29.9 °C), averages 5.3 °C more than its coolest, August (24.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.5 °C (64 °F), in January. The area lies about 458 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kankan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kankan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 458 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
