Labé at a glance

Among the 12 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Guinea, Labé is the coolest (23.2 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 2,529 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd driest (1,596 mm a year). Compared with Kindia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (154 km away), Labé is 1.2 °C cooler in April and 2.0 °C cooler in August, with 586 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (26.9 °C), averages 5.6 °C more than its coolest, August (21.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.9 °C (61 °F), in January. The area lies about 249 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Labé, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Labé has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. About 249 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
