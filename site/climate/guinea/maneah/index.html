Manéah at a glance

Among the 12 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Guinea, Manéah is 8th warmest (25.7 °C year average), 8th sunniest (about 2,204 hours of sunshine a year) and 4th wettest (3,338 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dubréka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Manéah is 0.3 °C cooler in April and 0.8 °C cooler in August, with 245 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, April (27.3 °C), averages 2.9 °C more than its coolest, August (24.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 20.3 °C (69 °F), in January. The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Manéah, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Manéah has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 2.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
