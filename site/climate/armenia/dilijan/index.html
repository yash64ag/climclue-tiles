Dilijan at a glance

Among the 19 towns ClimaClue covers in Armenia, Dilijan is the 2nd least sunny (about 2,404 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Sevan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Dilijan is 3.2 °C warmer in August and 2.9 °C warmer in January, with 28 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (19.3 °C), averages 22.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 290 km from the sea.

Why Dilijan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 290 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.1 °C. The 10 km area around Dilijan, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 6.8 °C a year, 2.0 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dilijan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.740°N, 44.863°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-432-2248), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
