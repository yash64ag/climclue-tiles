Gavar at a glance

Among the 19 towns ClimaClue covers in Armenia, Gavar is the 2nd coolest (5.6 °C year average). Compared with Sevan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Gavar is about as warm in August and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (16.1 °C), averages 21.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-5.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 332 km from the sea.

Why Gavar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 332 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Gavar itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.354°N, 45.124°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-436-2251), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
