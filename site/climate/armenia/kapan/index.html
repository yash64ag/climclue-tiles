Kapan weather by month and climate

Kapan at a glance

Among the 19 towns ClimaClue covers in Armenia, Kapan is 8th warmest (11.8 °C year average), 7th sunniest (about 2,499 hours of sunshine a year) and 12th wettest (535 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Goris, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Kapan is 4.0 °C warmer in July and 4.5 °C warmer in January, with 200 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.9 °C), averages 22.9 °C more than its coolest, January (0.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 220 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kapan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kapan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 220 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.9 °C. The 10 km area around Kapan, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 10.5 °C a year, 1.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kapan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (39.208°N, 46.407°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-447-2264), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
