Masis at a glance

Among the 19 towns ClimaClue covers in Armenia, Masis is the warmest (13.8 °C year average). Compared with Yerevan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Masis is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 28.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 302 km from the sea.

Why Masis has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 302 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Masis itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.066°N, 44.437°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-439-2244), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
