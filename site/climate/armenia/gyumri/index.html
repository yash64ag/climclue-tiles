Gyumri at a glance

Compared with Yerevan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (89 km away), Gyumri is 5.5 °C cooler in July and 2.7 °C cooler in January, with 177 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.6 °C), averages 25.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-5.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 215 km from the sea.

Why Gyumri has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 215 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
