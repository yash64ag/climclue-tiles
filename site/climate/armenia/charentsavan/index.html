Charentsavan at a glance

Among the 19 towns ClimaClue covers in Armenia, Charentsavan is the 3rd wettest (784 mm a year). Compared with Abovyan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Charentsavan is 3.1 °C cooler in July and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 162 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.5 °C), averages 24.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-5.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 290 km from the sea.

Why Charentsavan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 290 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.9 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
