Yerevan at a glance

Compared with Gyumri, the nearest covered town with different numbers (89 km away), Yerevan is 5.5 °C warmer in July and 2.7 °C warmer in January, with 177 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.1 °C), averages 27.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 302 km from the sea.

Best time to visit Yerevan

For mild weather, the best months to visit Yerevan are May, June, August and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May, June, August and September.

Why Yerevan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 302 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
