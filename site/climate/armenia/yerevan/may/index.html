How May compares in Yerevan

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Yerevan. Compared with April: 4.8 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 56 hours more sunshine. June is 5.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella.

Yerevan in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yerevan in May?
In May, Yerevan averages a day high of 21.1 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Yerevan?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Yerevan’s mild months (May, June, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yerevan in May?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is April (72 mm), the driest August (15 mm).
Does it snow in Yerevan in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yerevan get in May?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yerevan in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
