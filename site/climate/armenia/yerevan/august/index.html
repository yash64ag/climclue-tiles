How August compares in Yerevan

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Yerevan. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. September is 4.9 °C cooler than August on average.

Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses.

Yerevan in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yerevan in August?
In August, Yerevan averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 19.1 °C (66 °F), with 14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 325 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Yerevan?
August is not one of Yerevan’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yerevan in August?
14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is April (67 mm), the driest August (14 mm).
Does it snow in Yerevan in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yerevan get in August?
About 325 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yerevan in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
