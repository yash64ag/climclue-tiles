Dunfermline sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Scotland · United Kingdom

The sea off Dunfermline averages 13.3 °C (56 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 5.2 °C (41 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Dunfermline sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Dunfermline?
In August, at about 13.3 °C (56 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Dunfermline?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 5.2 °C (41 °F).
How warm is the sea in Dunfermline in February?
About 5.2 °C (41 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Dunfermline?
About 9.1 °C (48 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Dunfermline nearby?
In August: Irvine (14.4 °C, 90 km) and Kilwinning (14.4 °C, 91 km).
