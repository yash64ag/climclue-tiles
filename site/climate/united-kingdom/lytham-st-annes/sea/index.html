Lytham St Annes sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · England · United Kingdom

The sea off Lytham St Annes averages 16.8 °C (62 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 5.8 °C (42 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Lytham St Annes sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Lytham St Annes?
In August, at about 16.8 °C (62 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Lytham St Annes?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 5.8 °C (42 °F).
How warm is the sea in Lytham St Annes in February?
About 5.8 °C (42 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Lytham St Annes?
About 11.0 °C (52 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
