West Bromwich at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United Kingdom, West Bromwich is 81st warmest (9.7 °C year average), 71st sunniest (about 1,324 hours of sunshine a year) and 51st wettest (753 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dudley, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), West Bromwich is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in February, with 36 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (16.4 °C), averages 12.2 °C more than its coolest, February (4.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 1.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 133 km from the sea.

Why West Bromwich has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 133 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
