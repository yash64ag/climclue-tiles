How May compares in Glasgow

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Glasgow. Compared with April: 2.9 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. June is 2.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Glasgow in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Glasgow in May?
In May, Glasgow averages a day high of 15.7 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 7.0 °C (45 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Glasgow?
May is not one of Glasgow’s mild months; those are July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Glasgow in May?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (157 mm), the driest April (74 mm).
Does it snow in Glasgow in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Glasgow get in May?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Glasgow in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
