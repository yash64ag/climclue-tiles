How July compares in Glasgow

July is the warmest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Glasgow. Compared with June: 1.8 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. August is 0.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Glasgow in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Glasgow in July?
In July, Glasgow averages a day high of 19.7 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 11.7 °C (53 °F), with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Glasgow?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Glasgow’s mild months (July), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Glasgow in July?
90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is January (157 mm), the driest April (74 mm).
Does it snow in Glasgow in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Glasgow get in July?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Glasgow in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
