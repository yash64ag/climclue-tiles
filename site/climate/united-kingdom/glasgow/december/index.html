How December compares in Glasgow

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Glasgow. Compared with November: 2.2 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. January is about as warm as December on average.

Glasgow in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Glasgow in December?
In December, Glasgow averages a day high of 7.1 °C (45 °F) and a night low of 1.3 °C (34 °F), with 139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 34 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Glasgow?
December is not one of Glasgow’s mild months; those are July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Glasgow in December?
139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (157 mm), the driest April (74 mm).
Does it snow in Glasgow in December?
Yes, on average: about 3.8 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Glasgow get in December?
About 34 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Glasgow in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
