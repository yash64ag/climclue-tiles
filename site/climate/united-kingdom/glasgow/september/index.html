How September compares in Glasgow

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Glasgow. Compared with August: 2.2 °C cooler, 20 mm more precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. October is 2.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Glasgow in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Glasgow in September?
In September, Glasgow averages a day high of 16.2 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 9.4 °C (49 °F), with 129 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 105 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Glasgow?
September is not one of Glasgow’s mild months; those are July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Glasgow in September?
129 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (157 mm), the driest April (74 mm).
Does it snow in Glasgow in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Glasgow get in September?
About 105 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Glasgow in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
