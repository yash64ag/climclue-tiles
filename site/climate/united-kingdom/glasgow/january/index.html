How January compares in Glasgow

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Glasgow. Compared with December: 0.1 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. February is 0.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Glasgow in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Glasgow in January?
In January, Glasgow averages a day high of 5.8 °C (42 °F) and a night low of 1.4 °C (35 °F), with 155 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 38 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Glasgow?
January is not one of Glasgow’s mild months; those are July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Glasgow in January?
155 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is January (155 mm), the driest April (74 mm).
Does it snow in Glasgow in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 17 cm (6.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Glasgow get in January?
About 38 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Glasgow in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
