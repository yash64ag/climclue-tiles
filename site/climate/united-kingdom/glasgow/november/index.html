How November compares in Glasgow

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Glasgow. Compared with October: 3.0 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. December is 2.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Glasgow in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Glasgow in November?
In November, Glasgow averages a day high of 8.3 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 3.6 °C (38 °F), with 141 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 52 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Glasgow?
November is not one of Glasgow’s mild months; those are July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Glasgow in November?
141 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is January (155 mm), the driest April (74 mm).
Does it snow in Glasgow in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.1 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Glasgow get in November?
About 52 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Glasgow in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
