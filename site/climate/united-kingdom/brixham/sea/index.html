Brixham sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · England · United Kingdom

The sea off Brixham averages 16.3 °C (61 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 9.0 °C (48 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Brixham sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Brixham?
In August, at about 16.3 °C (61 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Brixham?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 9.0 °C (48 °F).
How warm is the sea in Brixham in March?
About 9.0 °C (48 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Brixham?
About 12.6 °C (55 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Brixham nearby?
In August: Taunton (17.7 °C, 75 km).
