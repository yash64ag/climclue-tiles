Worthing at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United Kingdom, Worthing is the 5th sunniest (about 1,644 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Brighton, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Worthing is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.2 °C warmer in February, with 56 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (16.8 °C), averages 11.2 °C more than its coolest, February (5.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 3.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Worthing, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Worthing has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
