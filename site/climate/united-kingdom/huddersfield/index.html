Why Huddersfield has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 92 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Huddersfield climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Huddersfield?
July, with an average of 15.6 °C (60 °F), day highs around 18.7 °C (66 °F) and night lows around 11.7 °C (53 °F).
What is the coldest month in Huddersfield?
February, with an average of 3.8 °C (39 °F), day highs around 6.1 °C (43 °F) and night lows around 0.8 °C (33 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Huddersfield?
December, with 119 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,141 mm (44.9 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Huddersfield?
May, with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Huddersfield?
June, with about 169 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Huddersfield?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 58 cm (22.8 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 2 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Huddersfield?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July and August.
What type of climate does Huddersfield have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 6.6 × 11 km, that contains Huddersfield’s GeoNames location (53.649°N, 1.784°W; ClimaClue area sq-303-1782), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 149,017 (GeoNames).
