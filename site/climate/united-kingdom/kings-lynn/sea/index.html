King's Lynn sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · England · United Kingdom

The sea off King's Lynn averages 16.8 °C (62 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 4.8 °C (41 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

King's Lynn sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in King's Lynn?
In August, at about 16.8 °C (62 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in King's Lynn?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 4.8 °C (41 °F).
How warm is the sea in King's Lynn in February?
About 4.8 °C (41 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in King's Lynn?
About 10.3 °C (51 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in King's Lynn nearby?
In August: Lowestoft (17.5 °C, 97 km), Colchester (18.2 °C, 102 km) and Harwich (18.2 °C, 108 km).
