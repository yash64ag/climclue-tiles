Bolton at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United Kingdom, Bolton is the 5th least sunny (about 1,244 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Salford, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Bolton is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in February, with 91 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (15.7 °C), averages 11.4 °C more than its coolest, February (4.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 1.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Bolton, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Bolton has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
