Walton-on-the-Naze sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · England · United Kingdom

The sea off Walton-on-the-Naze averages 18.2 °C (65 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 5.8 °C (42 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Walton-on-the-Naze sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Walton-on-the-Naze?
In August, at about 18.2 °C (65 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Walton-on-the-Naze?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 5.8 °C (42 °F).
How warm is the sea in Walton-on-the-Naze in February?
About 5.8 °C (42 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Walton-on-the-Naze?
