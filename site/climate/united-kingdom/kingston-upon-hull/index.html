Why Kingston upon Hull has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 29 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Kingston upon Hull climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Kingston upon Hull?
July, with an average of 16.4 °C (61 °F), day highs around 19.6 °C (67 °F) and night lows around 12.7 °C (55 °F).
What is the coldest month in Kingston upon Hull?
January, with an average of 4.5 °C (40 °F), day highs around 6.7 °C (44 °F) and night lows around 2.1 °C (36 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Kingston upon Hull?
October, with 62 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 665 mm (26.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Kingston upon Hull?
February, with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Kingston upon Hull?
June, with about 194 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Kingston upon Hull?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 21 cm (8.3 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Kingston upon Hull?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July and August.
What type of climate does Kingston upon Hull have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 6.6 × 11 km, that contains Kingston upon Hull’s GeoNames location (53.745°N, 0.335°W; ClimaClue area sq-302-1796), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 314,018 (GeoNames).
