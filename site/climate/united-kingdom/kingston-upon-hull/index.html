Kingston upon Hull at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United Kingdom, Kingston upon Hull is 65th warmest (9.9 °C year average), 55th sunniest (about 1,396 hours of sunshine a year) and 82nd wettest (665 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with York, the nearest covered town with different numbers (54 km away), Kingston upon Hull is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 15 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (16.4 °C), averages 11.8 °C more than its coolest, January (4.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 1.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 29 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kingston upon Hull, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kingston upon Hull has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 29 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
