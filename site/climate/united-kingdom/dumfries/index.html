Dumfries at a glance

Among the 804 towns ClimaClue covers in the United Kingdom, Dumfries is 720th warmest (9.2 °C year average), 660th sunniest (about 1,287 hours of sunshine a year) and 91st wettest (1,305 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Carlisle, the nearest covered town with different numbers (47 km away), Dumfries is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in December, with 196 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (15.7 °C), averages 11.8 °C more than its coolest, December (3.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, December, averages 0.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dumfries, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Dumfries has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Dumfries climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Dumfries?
July, with an average of 15.7 °C (60 °F), day highs around 19.7 °C (67 °F) and night lows around 11.6 °C (53 °F).
What is the coldest month in Dumfries?
December, with an average of 3.9 °C (39 °F), day highs around 6.9 °C (44 °F) and night lows around 0.9 °C (34 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Dumfries?
October, with 157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,305 mm (51.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Dumfries?
April, with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Dumfries?
May, with about 177 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Dumfries?
Yes. About 14 cm (5.7 in) of fresh snow falls in a typical year (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), most in January; it usually melts within days.

How warm is the sea in Dumfries?

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dumfries itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (55.070°N, 3.611°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-289-1763), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts. Sources: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010 (temperature, precipitation, wind); ERA5-Land, 1981–2010 (dew point and snow; snowfall matched to the area’s CHELSA climate; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information); relative humidity calculated from the dew point and the night lows and day highs; CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990 (sunshine and the day–night range; a different period); GeoNames (name and population 46,500, CC BY 4.0); climate type by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). How ClimaClue makes these numbers.
