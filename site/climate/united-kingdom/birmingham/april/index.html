How April compares in Birmingham

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Birmingham. Compared with March: 1.9 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. May is 3.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Birmingham in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Birmingham in April?
In April, Birmingham averages a day high of 13.0 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 4.1 °C (39 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Birmingham?
April is not one of Birmingham’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Birmingham in April?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (74 mm), the driest February (46 mm).
Does it snow in Birmingham in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Birmingham get in April?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Birmingham in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
