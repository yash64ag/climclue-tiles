How June compares in Birmingham

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Birmingham. Compared with May: 2.8 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Birmingham in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Birmingham in June?
In June, Birmingham averages a day high of 19.2 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 9.9 °C (50 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Birmingham?
June is not one of Birmingham’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Birmingham in June?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (74 mm), the driest February (46 mm).
Does it snow in Birmingham in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Birmingham get in June?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Birmingham in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
