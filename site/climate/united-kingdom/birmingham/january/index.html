How January compares in Birmingham

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Birmingham. Compared with December: 0.2 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. February is 0.1 °C cooler than January on average.

Birmingham in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Birmingham in January?
In January, Birmingham averages a day high of 6.5 °C (44 °F) and a night low of 1.5 °C (35 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 48 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Birmingham?
January is not one of Birmingham’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Birmingham in January?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (75 mm), the driest February (46 mm).
Does it snow in Birmingham in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.0 cm (2.8 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Birmingham get in January?
About 48 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Birmingham in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
