How March compares in Birmingham

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Birmingham. Compared with February: 2.0 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. April is 2.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Birmingham in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Birmingham in March?
In March, Birmingham averages a day high of 9.4 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 2.5 °C (37 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 100 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Birmingham?
March is not one of Birmingham’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Birmingham in March?
52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (75 mm), the driest February (46 mm).
Does it snow in Birmingham in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.5 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Birmingham get in March?
About 100 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Birmingham in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
