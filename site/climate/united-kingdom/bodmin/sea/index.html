Bodmin sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · England · United Kingdom

The sea off Bodmin averages 16.8 °C (62 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 9.1 °C (48 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Bodmin sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Bodmin?
In August, at about 16.8 °C (62 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Bodmin?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 9.1 °C (48 °F).
How warm is the sea in Bodmin in March?
About 9.1 °C (48 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Bodmin?
About 12.6 °C (55 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
