Among the 804 towns ClimaClue covers in the United Kingdom, Liversedge is 718th warmest (9.2 °C year average), 711th sunniest (about 1,254 hours of sunshine a year) and 159th wettest (1,079 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Heckmondwike, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Liversedge is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in February, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Why Liversedge has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 98 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-302-1783), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
