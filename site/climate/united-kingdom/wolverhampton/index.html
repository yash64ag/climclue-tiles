Why Wolverhampton has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 124 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Wolverhampton climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Wolverhampton?
July, with an average of 16.3 °C (61 °F), day highs around 19.9 °C (68 °F) and night lows around 11.9 °C (53 °F).
What is the coldest month in Wolverhampton?
February, with an average of 4.2 °C (39 °F), day highs around 6.8 °C (44 °F) and night lows around 1.1 °C (34 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Wolverhampton?
October, with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 770 mm (30.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Wolverhampton?
February, with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Wolverhampton?
June, with about 176 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Wolverhampton?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 27 cm (10.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Wolverhampton?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July and August.
What type of climate does Wolverhampton have?
Oceanic (Köppen Cfb). Mild all year, with rain in every season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 6.8 × 11 km, that contains Wolverhampton’s GeoNames location (52.585°N, 2.123°W; ClimaClue area sq-314-1778), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 263,700 (GeoNames).
