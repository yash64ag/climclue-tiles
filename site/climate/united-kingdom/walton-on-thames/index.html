Walton-on-Thames at a glance

Among the 804 towns ClimaClue covers in the United Kingdom, Walton-on-Thames is 108th warmest (10.7 °C year average), 134th sunniest (about 1,495 hours of sunshine a year) and 553rd wettest (684 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sunbury-on-Thames, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Walton-on-Thames is about as warm in July and about as warm in February, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.6 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, February (4.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 1.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 67 km from the sea.

Why Walton-on-Thames has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 67 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
