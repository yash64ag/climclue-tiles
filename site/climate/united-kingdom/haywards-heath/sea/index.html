Haywards Heath sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · England · United Kingdom

The sea off Haywards Heath averages 17.0 °C (63 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.8 °C (46 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Haywards Heath sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Haywards Heath?
In August, at about 17.0 °C (63 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Haywards Heath?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 7.8 °C (46 °F).
How warm is the sea in Haywards Heath in March?
About 7.8 °C (46 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Haywards Heath?
About 12.3 °C (54 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
