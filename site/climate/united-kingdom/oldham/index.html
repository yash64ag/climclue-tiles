Why Oldham has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 66 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Oldham climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Oldham?
July, with an average of 15.4 °C (60 °F), day highs around 18.6 °C (65 °F) and night lows around 11.4 °C (53 °F).
What is the coldest month in Oldham?
January, with an average of 3.9 °C (39 °F), day highs around 5.8 °C (42 °F) and night lows around 1.0 °C (34 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Oldham?
October, with 152 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,403 mm (55.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Oldham?
May, with 85 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Oldham?
June, with about 170 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Oldham?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 44 cm (17.3 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Oldham?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July.
What type of climate does Oldham have?
Oceanic (Köppen Cfb). Mild all year, with rain in every season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 6.6 × 11 km, that contains Oldham’s GeoNames location (53.541°N, 2.118°W; ClimaClue area sq-304-1778), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 237,110 (GeoNames).
