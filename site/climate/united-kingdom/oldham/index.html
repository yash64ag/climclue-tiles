Oldham at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United Kingdom, Oldham is the 5th coolest (9.1 °C year average), the 2nd least sunny (about 1,225 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th wettest (1,403 mm a year). Compared with Manchester, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Oldham is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 191 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (15.4 °C), averages 11.6 °C more than its coolest, January (3.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 0.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 66 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Oldham, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Oldham has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 66 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
