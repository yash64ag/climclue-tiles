How March compares in Leeds

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Leeds. Compared with February: 1.9 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. April is 1.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Leeds in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Leeds in March?
In March, Leeds averages a day high of 9.0 °C (48 °F) and a night low of 2.4 °C (36 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 97 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Leeds?
March is not one of Leeds’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Leeds in March?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (90 mm), the driest May (61 mm).
Does it snow in Leeds in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.0 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Leeds get in March?
About 97 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Leeds in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
