How October compares in London

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in London. Compared with September: 3.4 °C cooler, 16 mm more precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. November is 3.6 °C cooler than October on average.

London in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in London in October?
In October, London averages a day high of 14.7 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 7.8 °C (46 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 104 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit London?
October is not one of London’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in London in October?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is October (73 mm), the driest February (44 mm).
Does it snow in London in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does London get in October?
About 104 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for London in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
