How December compares in London

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in London. Compared with November: 2.5 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. January is 0.3 °C cooler than December on average.

London in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in London in December?
In December, London averages a day high of 7.2 °C (45 °F) and a night low of 2.5 °C (37 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 43 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit London?
December is not one of London’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in London in December?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (74 mm), the driest February (45 mm).
Does it snow in London in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.7 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does London get in December?
About 43 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for London in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
