How November compares in London

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in London. Compared with October: 3.6 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. December is 2.5 °C cooler than November on average.

London in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in London in November?
In November, London averages a day high of 10.0 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 4.7 °C (40 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 63 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit London?
November is not one of London’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in London in November?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (74 mm), the driest February (45 mm).
Does it snow in London in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.6 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does London get in November?
About 63 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for London in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
