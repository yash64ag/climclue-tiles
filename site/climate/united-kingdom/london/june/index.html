How June compares in London

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in London. Compared with May: 3.0 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. July is 2.1 °C warmer than June on average.

London in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in London in June?
In June, London averages a day high of 19.0 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 10.9 °C (52 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit London?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of London’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in London in June?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (74 mm), the driest February (45 mm).
Does it snow in London in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does London get in June?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for London in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
