How March compares in London

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in London. Compared with February: 2.1 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. April is 2.1 °C warmer than March on average.

London in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in London in March?
In March, London averages a day high of 10.0 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 3.2 °C (38 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 107 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit London?
March is not one of London’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in London in March?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (74 mm), the driest February (45 mm).
Does it snow in London in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.5 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does London get in March?
About 107 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for London in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
