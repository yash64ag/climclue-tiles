Blackburn at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United Kingdom, Blackburn is the 4th coolest (8.9 °C year average) and the 3rd wettest (1,427 mm a year). Compared with Preston, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Blackburn is 0.8 °C cooler in July and 1.2 °C cooler in February, with 312 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (15.2 °C), averages 11.4 °C more than its coolest, February (3.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 0.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Why Blackburn has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
