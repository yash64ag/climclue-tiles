Ayr sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Scotland · United Kingdom

The sea off Ayr averages 14.4 °C (58 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 6.4 °C (44 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Ayr sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ayr?
In August, at about 14.4 °C (58 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ayr?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 6.4 °C (44 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ayr in February?
About 6.4 °C (44 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Ayr?
About 10.1 °C (50 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
