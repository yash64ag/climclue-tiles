Chichester sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · England · United Kingdom

The sea off Chichester averages 17.4 °C (63 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 7.6 °C (46 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Chichester sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Chichester?
In August, at about 17.4 °C (63 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Chichester?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 7.6 °C (46 °F).
How warm is the sea in Chichester in March?
About 7.6 °C (46 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Chichester?
About 12.2 °C (54 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
