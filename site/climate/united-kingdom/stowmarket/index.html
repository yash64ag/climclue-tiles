Stowmarket at a glance

Among the 804 towns ClimaClue covers in the United Kingdom, Stowmarket is 345th warmest (10.2 °C year average), 91st sunniest (about 1,532 hours of sunshine a year) and 758th wettest (614 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ipswich, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Stowmarket is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in February, with 22 mm more precipitation a year. Why Stowmarket has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 43 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-318-1809), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
