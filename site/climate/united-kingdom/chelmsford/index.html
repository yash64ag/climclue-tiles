Chelmsford at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United Kingdom, Chelmsford is the 2nd driest (579 mm a year). Compared with Basildon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Chelmsford is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in February, with 39 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.4 °C), averages 12.8 °C more than its coolest, February (4.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 1.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 43 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 98% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Chelmsford has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 43 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
