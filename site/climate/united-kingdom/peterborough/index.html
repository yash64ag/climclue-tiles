Peterborough at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United Kingdom, Peterborough is 38th warmest (10.4 °C year average), 43rd sunniest (about 1,430 hours of sunshine a year) and 96th wettest (617 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cambridge, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Peterborough is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in February, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.4 °C), averages 12.8 °C more than its coolest, February (4.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 1.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 52 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Peterborough, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Peterborough has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 52 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
