Why Eastbourne has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Eastbourne climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Eastbourne?
August, with an average of 16.4 °C (62 °F), day highs around 17.8 °C (64 °F) and night lows around 15.1 °C (59 °F).
What is the coldest month in Eastbourne?
February, with an average of 5.8 °C (43 °F), day highs around 7.1 °C (45 °F) and night lows around 4.5 °C (40 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Eastbourne?
October, with 124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 945 mm (37.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Eastbourne?
June, with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Eastbourne?
June, with about 236 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Eastbourne?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 9.9 cm (3.9 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Eastbourne?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July, August and September.
What type of climate does Eastbourne have?
Oceanic (Köppen Cfb). Mild all year, with rain in every season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.0 × 11 km, that contains Eastbourne’s GeoNames location (50.769°N, 0.285°E; ClimaClue area sq-332-1802), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 101,689 (GeoNames).
