Manchester weather in June

Average June weather in Manchester, United Kingdom: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Manchester averages 18.1 °C (65 °F) days and 10.0 °C (50 °F) nights, with 89 mm (3.5 in) of rain over the month and about 176 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Manchester

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Manchester. Compared with May: 2.6 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. July is 1.9 °C warmer than June on average.

Manchester in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Manchester in June?
In June, Manchester averages a day high of 18.1 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 10.0 °C (50 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Manchester?
June is not one of Manchester’s mild months; those are July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Manchester in June?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (138 mm), the driest May (79 mm).
Does it snow in Manchester in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Manchester get in June?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Manchester in June?
