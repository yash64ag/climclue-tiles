How September compares in Manchester

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Manchester. Compared with August: 2.4 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. October is 3.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Manchester in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Manchester in September?
In September, Manchester averages a day high of 16.9 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 9.9 °C (50 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 115 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Manchester?
September is not one of Manchester’s mild months; those are July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Manchester in September?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (138 mm), the driest May (79 mm).
Does it snow in Manchester in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Manchester get in September?
About 115 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Manchester in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
