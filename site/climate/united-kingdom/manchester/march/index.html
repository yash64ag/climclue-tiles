How March compares in Manchester

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Manchester. Compared with February: 1.9 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. April is 2.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Manchester in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Manchester in March?
In March, Manchester averages a day high of 9.1 °C (48 °F) and a night low of 2.5 °C (37 °F), with 103 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 94 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Manchester?
March is not one of Manchester’s mild months; those are July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Manchester in March?
103 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (138 mm), the driest May (79 mm).
Does it snow in Manchester in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.2 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Manchester get in March?
About 94 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Manchester in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
