How January compares in Manchester

January is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Manchester. Compared with December: 0.2 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. February is about as warm as January on average.

Manchester in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Manchester in January?
In January, Manchester averages a day high of 6.4 °C (44 °F) and a night low of 1.5 °C (35 °F), with 116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 42 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Manchester?
January is not one of Manchester’s mild months; those are July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Manchester in January?
116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (134 mm), the driest May (78 mm).
Does it snow in Manchester in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.8 cm (3.9 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Manchester get in January?
About 42 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Manchester in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
