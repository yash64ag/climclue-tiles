How October compares in Manchester

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Manchester. Compared with September: 3.2 °C cooler, 31 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. November is 3.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Manchester in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Manchester in October?
In October, Manchester averages a day high of 12.9 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 7.2 °C (45 °F), with 134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 87 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Manchester?
October is not one of Manchester’s mild months; those are July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Manchester in October?
134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is October (134 mm), the driest May (78 mm).
Does it snow in Manchester in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Manchester get in October?
About 87 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Manchester in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
