Manchester weather in May

Average May weather in Manchester, United Kingdom: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Manchester averages 15.5 °C (60 °F) days and 7.4 °C (45 °F) nights, with 79 mm (3.1 in) of rain over the month and about 177 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Manchester

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Manchester. Compared with April: 3.2 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. June is 2.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Manchester in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Manchester in May?
In May, Manchester averages a day high of 15.5 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 7.4 °C (45 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Manchester?
May is not one of Manchester’s mild months; those are July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Manchester in May?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the driest month of the year. The wettest month is October (138 mm), the driest May (79 mm).
Does it snow in Manchester in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Manchester get in May?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Manchester in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
