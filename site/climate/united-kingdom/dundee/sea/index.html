Dundee sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Scotland · United Kingdom

The sea off Dundee averages 13.4 °C (56 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 5.6 °C (42 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Dundee sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Dundee?
In August, at about 13.4 °C (56 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Dundee?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 5.6 °C (42 °F).
How warm is the sea in Dundee in March?
About 5.6 °C (42 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Dundee?
About 9.3 °C (49 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
