Bradford at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United Kingdom, Bradford is the 3rd coolest (8.9 °C year average) and the 3rd least sunny (about 1,233 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Leeds, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Bradford is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 240 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (15.3 °C), averages 11.7 °C more than its coolest, January (3.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 0.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 91 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 132% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Bradford has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 91 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
