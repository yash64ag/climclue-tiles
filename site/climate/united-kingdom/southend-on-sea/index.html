Why Southend-on-Sea has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Southend-on-Sea climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Southend-on-Sea?
August, with an average of 17.4 °C (63 °F), day highs around 20.0 °C (68 °F) and night lows around 14.6 °C (58 °F).
What is the coldest month in Southend-on-Sea?
February, with an average of 5.2 °C (41 °F), day highs around 7.1 °C (45 °F) and night lows around 3.3 °C (38 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Southend-on-Sea?
October, with 65 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 602 mm (23.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Southend-on-Sea?
February, with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Southend-on-Sea?
June, with about 211 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Southend-on-Sea?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 14 cm (5.6 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Southend-on-Sea?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July, August and September.
What type of climate does Southend-on-Sea have?
Oceanic (Köppen Cfb). Mild all year, with rain in every season. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 295,310 (GeoNames).
