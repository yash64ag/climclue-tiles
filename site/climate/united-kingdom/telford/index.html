Telford at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United Kingdom, Telford is 86th warmest (9.6 °C year average), 85th sunniest (about 1,304 hours of sunshine a year) and 49th wettest (762 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Wolverhampton, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Telford is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 8 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (16.1 °C), averages 11.9 °C more than its coolest, January (4.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 1.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 104 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 20 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 113% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Telford has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 104 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
