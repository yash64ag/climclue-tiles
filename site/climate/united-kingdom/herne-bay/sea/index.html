Herne Bay sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · England · United Kingdom

The sea off Herne Bay averages 18.3 °C (65 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 6.0 °C (43 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Herne Bay sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Herne Bay?
In August, at about 18.3 °C (65 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Herne Bay?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 6.0 °C (43 °F).
How warm is the sea in Herne Bay in February?
About 6.0 °C (43 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Herne Bay?
About 11.7 °C (53 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
