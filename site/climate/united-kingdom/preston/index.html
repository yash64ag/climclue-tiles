Preston at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United Kingdom, Preston is 57th warmest (10.0 °C year average), 58th sunniest (about 1,386 hours of sunshine a year) and 13th wettest (1,115 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Blackburn, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Preston is 0.8 °C warmer in July and 1.2 °C warmer in February, with 312 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (15.9 °C), averages 11.0 °C more than its coolest, February (5.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 2.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 26 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 1 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 109% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Preston has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 26 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
