Sunderland sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · England · United Kingdom

The sea off Sunderland averages 14.9 °C (59 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 6.3 °C (43 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Sunderland sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Sunderland?
In August, at about 14.9 °C (59 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Sunderland?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 6.3 °C (43 °F).
How warm is the sea in Sunderland in March?
About 6.3 °C (43 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Sunderland?
About 10.1 °C (50 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
