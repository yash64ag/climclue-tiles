Whitehaven sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · England · United Kingdom

The sea off Whitehaven averages 16.0 °C (61 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 5.5 °C (42 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Whitehaven sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Whitehaven?
In August, at about 16.0 °C (61 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Whitehaven?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 5.5 °C (42 °F).
How warm is the sea in Whitehaven in February?
About 5.5 °C (42 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Whitehaven?
About 10.5 °C (51 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Whitehaven nearby?
In August: Barrow in Furness (16.7 °C, 54 km), Morecambe (16.8 °C, 71 km) and Lancaster (16.8 °C, 76 km).
