Newtownards sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Northern Ireland · United Kingdom

The sea off Newtownards averages 13.4 °C (56 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 7.1 °C (45 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Newtownards sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Newtownards?
In September, at about 13.4 °C (56 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Newtownards?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 7.1 °C (45 °F).
How warm is the sea in Newtownards in March?
About 7.1 °C (45 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Newtownards?
About 10.3 °C (51 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Newtownards nearby?
In September: Douglas (14.6 °C, 92 km), Drogheda (14.1 °C, 106 km) and Balbriggan (14.1 °C, 114 km).
