Elgin sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Scotland · United Kingdom

The sea off Elgin averages 13.8 °C (57 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 6.0 °C (43 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Elgin sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Elgin?
In August, at about 13.8 °C (57 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Elgin?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 6.0 °C (43 °F).
How warm is the sea in Elgin in March?
About 6.0 °C (43 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Elgin?
About 9.6 °C (49 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
