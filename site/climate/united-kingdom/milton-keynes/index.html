Milton Keynes at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United Kingdom, Milton Keynes is 53rd warmest (10.1 °C year average), 63rd sunniest (about 1,358 hours of sunshine a year) and 86th wettest (655 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bedford, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Milton Keynes is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in February, with 33 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.1 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, February (4.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 1.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 116 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Milton Keynes, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Milton Keynes has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 116 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
