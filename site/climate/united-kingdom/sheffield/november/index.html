Sheffield weather in November

Average November weather in Sheffield, United Kingdom: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Sheffield averages 9.5 °C (49 °F) days and 4.1 °C (39 °F) nights, with 78 mm (3.1 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 55 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Sheffield

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Sheffield. Compared with October: 3.4 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. December is 2.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Sheffield in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sheffield in November?
In November, Sheffield averages a day high of 9.5 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 4.1 °C (39 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 55 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Sheffield?
November is not one of Sheffield’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sheffield in November?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (84 mm), the driest February (58 mm).
Does it snow in Sheffield in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.9 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Sheffield get in November?
About 55 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sheffield in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
