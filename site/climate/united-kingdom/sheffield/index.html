Sheffield at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United Kingdom, Sheffield is 95th warmest (9.3 °C year average), 89th sunniest (about 1,297 hours of sunshine a year) and 29th wettest (883 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rotherham, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Sheffield is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in February, with 119 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (15.9 °C), averages 12.2 °C more than its coolest, February (3.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 0.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 114 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.0 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 105% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

For mild weather, the best months to visit Sheffield are July and August.

Why Sheffield has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 114 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
