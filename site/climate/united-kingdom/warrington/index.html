Warrington at a glance

Among the 104 towns of 100,000+ people covered in the United Kingdom, Warrington is 62nd warmest (9.9 °C year average), 76th sunniest (about 1,313 hours of sunshine a year) and 21st wettest (946 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with St Helens, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Warrington is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (16.3 °C), averages 11.6 °C more than its coolest, January (4.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 1.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 46 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Warrington, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Warrington has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 46 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
