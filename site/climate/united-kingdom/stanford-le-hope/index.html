Stanford-le-Hope at a glance

Among the 804 towns ClimaClue covers in the United Kingdom, Stanford-le-Hope is 49th warmest (10.9 °C year average), 305th sunniest (about 1,444 hours of sunshine a year) and 775th wettest (608 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Basildon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Stanford-le-Hope is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in February, with 26 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (18.1 °C), averages 13.2 °C more than its coolest, February (4.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 1.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 41 km from the sea.

Why Stanford-le-Hope has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 41 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
