How May compares in Edinburgh

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Edinburgh. Compared with April: 2.6 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. June is 2.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Edinburgh in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Edinburgh in May?
In May, Edinburgh averages a day high of 14.7 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 6.3 °C (43 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Edinburgh?
May is not one of Edinburgh’s mild months; those are July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Edinburgh in May?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (92 mm), the driest April (50 mm).
Does it snow in Edinburgh in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Edinburgh get in May?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Edinburgh in May?
About 8.5 °C (47 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (13.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Edinburgh in May?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
