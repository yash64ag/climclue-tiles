How April compares in Edinburgh

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Edinburgh. Compared with March: 1.8 °C warmer, 15 mm less precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. May is 2.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Edinburgh in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Edinburgh in April?
In April, Edinburgh averages a day high of 10.1 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 4.0 °C (39 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 134 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Edinburgh?
April is not one of Edinburgh’s mild months; those are July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Edinburgh in April?
52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is October (96 mm), the driest April (52 mm).
Does it snow in Edinburgh in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.0 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Edinburgh get in April?
About 134 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Edinburgh in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
