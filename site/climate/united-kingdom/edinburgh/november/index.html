How November compares in Edinburgh

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Edinburgh. Compared with October: 3.2 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. December is 2.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Edinburgh in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Edinburgh in November?
In November, Edinburgh averages a day high of 9.3 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 3.1 °C (38 °F), with 80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 59 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Edinburgh?
November is not one of Edinburgh’s mild months; those are July, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Edinburgh in November?
80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (92 mm), the driest April (50 mm).
Does it snow in Edinburgh in November?
Yes, on average: about 0.7 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in November (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Edinburgh get in November?
About 59 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Edinburgh in November?
About 9.3 °C (49 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (13.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Edinburgh in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
