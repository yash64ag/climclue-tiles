How August compares in Edinburgh

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Edinburgh. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. September is 2.1 °C cooler than August on average.

Edinburgh in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Edinburgh in August?
In August, Edinburgh averages a day high of 19.0 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 11.0 °C (52 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Edinburgh?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Edinburgh’s mild months (July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Edinburgh in August?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (92 mm), the driest April (50 mm).
Does it snow in Edinburgh in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Edinburgh get in August?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Edinburgh in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
