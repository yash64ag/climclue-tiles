Newton-le-Willows at a glance

Among the 804 towns ClimaClue covers in the United Kingdom, Newton-le-Willows is 433rd warmest (10.0 °C year average), 561st sunniest (about 1,312 hours of sunshine a year) and 198th wettest (1,010 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Golborne, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Newton-le-Willows is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in February, with 25 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (16.2 °C), averages 11.4 °C more than its coolest, February (4.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, February, averages 2.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 41 km from the sea.

Why Newton-le-Willows has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 41 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
