Linda-a-Velha at a glance

Among the 173 towns ClimaClue covers in Portugal, Linda-a-Velha is 43rd warmest (17.0 °C year average), 47th sunniest (about 2,731 hours of sunshine a year) and 131st wettest (704 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Carnaxide, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Linda-a-Velha is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 24 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (22.3 °C), averages 10.3 °C more than its coolest, January (11.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.8 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Linda-a-Velha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
