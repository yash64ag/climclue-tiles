Senhora da Hora at a glance

Among the 173 towns ClimaClue covers in Portugal, Senhora da Hora is 127th warmest (15.4 °C year average), 127th sunniest (about 2,422 hours of sunshine a year) and 43rd wettest (1,442 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with São Tiago de Custoias, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Senhora da Hora is 0.2 °C cooler in August and about as warm in January, with 28 mm less precipitation a year. The area lies about 8 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Senhora da Hora has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
