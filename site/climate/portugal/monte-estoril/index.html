Among the 173 towns ClimaClue covers in Portugal, Monte Estoril is 72nd warmest (16.0 °C year average), 74th sunniest (about 2,716 hours of sunshine a year) and 139th wettest (690 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Monte Estoril has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 6.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
