Lagos sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Faro · Portugal

The sea off Lagos averages 19.8 °C (68 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 15.3 °C (60 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Lagos sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Lagos?
In September, at about 19.8 °C (68 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Lagos?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 15.3 °C (60 °F).
How warm is the sea in Lagos in February?
About 15.3 °C (60 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Lagos?
About 17.5 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Lagos nearby?
In September: Loulé (20.5 °C, 58 km), Faro (20.5 °C, 67 km) and Olhão (21.0 °C, 74 km).
