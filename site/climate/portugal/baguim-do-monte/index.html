Baguim do Monte weather by month and climate

Baguim do Monte at a glance

Among the 173 towns ClimaClue covers in Portugal, Baguim do Monte is 149th warmest (15.1 °C year average), 118th sunniest (about 2,426 hours of sunshine a year) and 19th wettest (1,528 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rio Tinto, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Baguim do Monte is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 34 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.0 °C), averages 11.6 °C more than its coolest, January (9.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.3 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 17 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Baguim do Monte has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
