Ermesinde sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Porto · Portugal

The sea off Ermesinde averages 17.4 °C (63 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 13.4 °C (56 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Ermesinde sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ermesinde?
In September, at about 17.4 °C (63 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ermesinde?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 13.4 °C (56 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ermesinde in February?
About 13.4 °C (56 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Ermesinde?
About 15.5 °C (60 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Ermesinde nearby?
In September: Ovar (18.0 °C, 40 km).
