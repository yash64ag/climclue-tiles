São João da Talha at a glance

Among the 173 towns ClimaClue covers in Portugal, São João da Talha is 43rd warmest (16.3 °C year average), 76th sunniest (about 2,716 hours of sunshine a year) and 166th wettest (626 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Santa Iria da Azóia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), São João da Talha is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.9 °C), averages 11.0 °C more than its coolest, January (10.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.6 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 38 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 13 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 82% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why São João da Talha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
