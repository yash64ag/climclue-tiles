Quinta Do Conde sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Setúbal · Portugal

The sea off Quinta Do Conde averages 18.8 °C (66 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 14.7 °C (58 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Quinta Do Conde sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Quinta Do Conde?
In September, at about 18.8 °C (66 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Quinta Do Conde?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 14.7 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in Quinta Do Conde in February?
About 14.7 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Quinta Do Conde?
About 16.8 °C (62 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
