Leiria at a glance

Among the 10 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Portugal, Leiria is the 3rd coolest (15.3 °C year average). Compared with Coimbra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Leiria is 1.7 °C cooler in August and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 145 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (20.1 °C), averages 9.8 °C more than its coolest, January (10.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.1 °C (45 °F), in January. The area lies about 26 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Leiria, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Leiria has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 26 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
