Moscavide e Portela at a glance

Among the 173 towns ClimaClue covers in Portugal, Moscavide e Portela is 25th warmest (17.2 °C year average), 33rd sunniest (about 2,740 hours of sunshine a year) and 143rd wettest (685 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Olivais, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Moscavide e Portela is about as warm in August and about as warm in January, with 8 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.3 °C), averages 12.1 °C more than its coolest, January (11.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.0 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Moscavide e Portela has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
