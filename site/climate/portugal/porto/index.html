Why Porto has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Porto climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Porto?
August, with an average of 20.9 °C (70 °F), day highs around 24.5 °C (76 °F) and night lows around 17.8 °C (64 °F).
What is the coldest month in Porto?
January, with an average of 10.2 °C (50 °F), day highs around 12.5 °C (55 °F) and night lows around 7.1 °C (45 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Porto?
December, with 223 mm (8.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,432 mm (56.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Porto?
July, with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Porto?
July, with about 305 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Porto?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Porto?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Porto have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.4 × 11 km, that contains Porto’s GeoNames location (41.148°N, 8.611°W; ClimaClue area sq-428-1713), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 252,687 (GeoNames).
