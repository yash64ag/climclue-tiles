Caniço sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Madeira · Portugal

The sea off Caniço averages 22.8 °C (73 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 17.2 °C (63 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to November.

Caniço sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Caniço?
In September, at about 22.8 °C (73 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Caniço?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to November. The coolest month is February, at 17.2 °C (63 °F).
How warm is the sea in Caniço in February?
About 17.2 °C (63 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Caniço?
About 19.7 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
