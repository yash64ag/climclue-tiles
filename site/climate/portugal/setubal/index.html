Setúbal at a glance

Among the 10 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Portugal, Setúbal is the warmest (16.5 °C year average), the sunniest (about 2,752 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (686 mm a year). Compared with Lisbon, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Setúbal is 0.5 °C warmer in August and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 39 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.9 °C), averages 10.6 °C more than its coolest, January (11.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.5 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Setúbal, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Setúbal has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
