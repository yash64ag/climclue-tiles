Olhão sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Faro · Portugal

The sea off Olhão averages 21.3 °C (70 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 15.4 °C (60 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Olhão sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Olhão?
In August, at about 21.3 °C (70 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Olhão?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 15.4 °C (60 °F).
How warm is the sea in Olhão in February?
About 15.4 °C (60 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Olhão?
About 18.2 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Olhão nearby?
In August: Isla Cristina (22.0 °C, 50 km), Lepe (22.0 °C, 62 km) and Cartaya (22.0 °C, 67 km).
