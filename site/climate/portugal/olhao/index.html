Olhão at a glance

Among the 173 towns ClimaClue covers in Portugal, Olhão is the 4th warmest (17.7 °C year average), the sunniest (about 2,943 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd driest (598 mm a year). Compared with Quelfes, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Olhão is 0.3 °C cooler in August and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 15 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.0 °C), averages 10.0 °C more than its coolest, January (13.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.0 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 20 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 114% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Olhão has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Olhão itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.029°N, 7.841°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-469-1721), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
