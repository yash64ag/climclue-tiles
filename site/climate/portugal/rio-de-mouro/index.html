Rio de Mouro weather by month and climate

Rio de Mouro at a glance

Among the 173 towns ClimaClue covers in Portugal, Rio de Mouro is 93rd warmest (15.7 °C year average), 67th sunniest (about 2,721 hours of sunshine a year) and 104th wettest (738 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Cacém, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Rio de Mouro is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 11 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (20.0 °C), averages 8.3 °C more than its coolest, January (11.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.6 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: a NOAA weather station 16 km from Rio de Mouro measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from these (temperature by up to 3.4 °C (6.2 °F) in one month). One station can differ from modelled values (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Rio de Mouro has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Rio de Mouro itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.766°N, 9.328°W). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-452-1706), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
