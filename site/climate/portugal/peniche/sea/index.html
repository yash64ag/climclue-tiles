Peniche sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Leiria · Portugal

The sea off Peniche averages 18.6 °C (65 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.3 °C (58 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Peniche sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Peniche?
In August, at about 18.6 °C (65 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Peniche?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 14.3 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in Peniche in February?
About 14.3 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Peniche?
About 16.4 °C (62 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
