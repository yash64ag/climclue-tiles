How September compares in Lisbon

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Lisbon. Compared with August: 1.0 °C cooler, 27 mm more precipitation and 82 hours less sunshine. October is 2.8 °C cooler than September on average.

Lisbon in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lisbon in September?
In September, Lisbon averages a day high of 24.4 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 17.3 °C (63 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 253 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Lisbon?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Lisbon’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lisbon in September?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (118 mm), the driest July (5 mm).
Does it snow in Lisbon in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lisbon get in September?
About 253 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lisbon in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
