How June compares in Lisbon

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Lisbon. Compared with May: 2.9 °C warmer, 32 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. July is 1.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Lisbon in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lisbon in June?
In June, Lisbon averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 16.0 °C (61 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 297 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Lisbon?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Lisbon’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lisbon in June?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (118 mm), the driest July (5 mm).
Does it snow in Lisbon in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lisbon get in June?
About 297 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lisbon in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
