How January compares in Lisbon

January is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Lisbon. Compared with December: 1.0 °C cooler, 29 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. February is 0.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Lisbon in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lisbon in January?
In January, Lisbon averages a day high of 13.8 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 8.5 °C (47 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Lisbon?
January is not one of Lisbon’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lisbon in January?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (118 mm), the driest July (5 mm).
Does it snow in Lisbon in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lisbon get in January?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lisbon in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
