How October compares in Lisbon

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Lisbon. Compared with September: 2.8 °C cooler, 65 mm more precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. November is 3.2 °C cooler than October on average.

Lisbon in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lisbon in October?
In October, Lisbon averages a day high of 20.6 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 98 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Lisbon?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Lisbon’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lisbon in October?
98 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (118 mm), the driest July (5 mm).
Does it snow in Lisbon in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lisbon get in October?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lisbon in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
