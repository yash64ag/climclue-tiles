How July compares in Lisbon

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the sunniest month in Lisbon. Compared with June: 1.7 °C warmer, 11 mm less precipitation and 49 hours more sunshine. August is 0.4 °C warmer than July on average.

Lisbon in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lisbon in July?
In July, Lisbon averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 17.6 °C (64 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 346 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Lisbon?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Lisbon’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lisbon in July?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the driest month of the year. The wettest month is December (118 mm), the driest July (5 mm).
Does it snow in Lisbon in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lisbon get in July?
About 346 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lisbon in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
