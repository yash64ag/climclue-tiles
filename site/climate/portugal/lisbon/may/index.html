How May compares in Lisbon

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Lisbon. Compared with April: 2.1 °C warmer, 21 mm less precipitation and 56 hours more sunshine. June is 3.1 °C warmer than May on average.

Lisbon in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lisbon in May?
In May, Lisbon averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 286 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Lisbon?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Lisbon’s mild months (April, May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lisbon in May?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (121 mm), the driest July (5 mm).
Does it snow in Lisbon in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lisbon get in May?
About 286 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Lisbon in May?
About 16.2 °C (61 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is September (18.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Lisbon in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
