How December compares in Lisbon

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the least sunny month in Lisbon. Compared with November: 2.5 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. January is 0.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Lisbon in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lisbon in December?
In December, Lisbon averages a day high of 15.2 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 9.4 °C (49 °F), with 121 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Lisbon?
December is not one of Lisbon’s mild months; those are April, May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lisbon in December?
121 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is December (121 mm), the driest July (5 mm).
Does it snow in Lisbon in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lisbon get in December?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Lisbon in December?
About 16.0 °C (61 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is September (18.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Lisbon in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
