How November compares in Lisbon

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Lisbon. Compared with October: 3.2 °C cooler, 18 mm more precipitation and 53 hours less sunshine. December is 2.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Lisbon in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lisbon in November?
In November, Lisbon averages a day high of 17.0 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Lisbon?
November is not one of Lisbon’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lisbon in November?
116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is December (118 mm), the driest July (5 mm).
Does it snow in Lisbon in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lisbon get in November?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lisbon in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
