How August compares in Lisbon

August is the warmest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Lisbon. Compared with July: 0.4 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. September is 1.0 °C cooler than August on average.

Lisbon in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lisbon in August?
In August, Lisbon averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 18.0 °C (64 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 335 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Lisbon?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Lisbon’s mild months (May, June, July, August, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lisbon in August?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is December (118 mm), the driest July (5 mm).
Does it snow in Lisbon in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lisbon get in August?
About 335 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lisbon in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
