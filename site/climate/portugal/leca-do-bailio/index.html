Leça do Bailio at a glance

Among the 173 towns ClimaClue covers in Portugal, Leça do Bailio is 114th warmest (15.5 °C year average), 140th sunniest (about 2,402 hours of sunshine a year) and 40th wettest (1,460 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Leça do Bailio has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
