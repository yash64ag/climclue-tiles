Viana do Castelo sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Portugal

The sea off Viana do Castelo averages 17.1 °C (63 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.2 °C (56 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Viana do Castelo sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Viana do Castelo?
In August, at about 17.1 °C (63 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Viana do Castelo?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 13.2 °C (56 °F).
How warm is the sea in Viana do Castelo in February?
About 13.2 °C (56 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Viana do Castelo?
About 15.2 °C (59 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
