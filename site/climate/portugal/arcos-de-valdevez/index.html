Arcos de Valdevez at a glance

Among the 173 towns ClimaClue covers in Portugal, Arcos de Valdevez is the 2nd wettest (1,884 mm a year). Compared with Ponte de Lima, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Arcos de Valdevez is 0.2 °C warmer in August and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 357 mm more precipitation a year. Why Arcos de Valdevez has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 41 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
