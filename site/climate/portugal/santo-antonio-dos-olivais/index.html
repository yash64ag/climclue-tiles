Santo António dos Olivais at a glance

Among the 173 towns ClimaClue covers in Portugal, Santo António dos Olivais is 102nd warmest (15.7 °C year average), 98th sunniest (about 2,551 hours of sunshine a year) and 70th wettest (1,008 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Coimbra, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Santo António dos Olivais is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (22.0 °C), averages 12.4 °C more than its coolest, January (9.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 42 km from the sea.

Why Santo António dos Olivais has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 42 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
