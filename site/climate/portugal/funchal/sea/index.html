Funchal sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Madeira · Portugal

The sea off Funchal averages 23.1 °C (74 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 17.5 °C (64 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to November.

Funchal sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Funchal?
In September, at about 23.1 °C (74 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Funchal?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from June to November. The coolest month is February, at 17.5 °C (64 °F).
How warm is the sea in Funchal in February?
About 17.5 °C (64 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Funchal?
About 20.0 °C (68 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
