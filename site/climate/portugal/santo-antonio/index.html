Santo António at a glance

Among the 173 towns ClimaClue covers in Portugal, Santo António is the 2nd least sunny (about 2,143 hours of sunshine a year) and the wettest (1,929 mm a year). Compared with Funchal, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Santo António is 0.1 °C cooler in August and 0.3 °C cooler in February, with 46 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.2 °C), averages 7.1 °C more than its coolest, February (14.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.4 °C (53 °F), in February. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Santo António has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.1 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
