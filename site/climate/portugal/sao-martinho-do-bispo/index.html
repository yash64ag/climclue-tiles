São Martinho do Bispo at a glance

Among the 173 towns ClimaClue covers in Portugal, São Martinho do Bispo is 100th warmest (15.6 °C year average), 98th sunniest (about 2,551 hours of sunshine a year) and 74th wettest (974 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ribeira de Frades, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), São Martinho do Bispo is about as warm in August and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 10 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.8 °C), averages 12.4 °C more than its coolest, January (9.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.2 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 42 km from the sea.

Why São Martinho do Bispo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 42 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
