Sintra sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Lisbon · Portugal

The sea off Sintra averages 18.5 °C (65 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.4 °C (58 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Sintra sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Sintra?
In August, at about 18.5 °C (65 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Sintra?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 14.4 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in Sintra in February?
About 14.4 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Sintra?
About 16.5 °C (62 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
