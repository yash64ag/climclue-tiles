Braga at a glance

Among the 10 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Portugal, Braga is the 2nd coolest (14.8 °C year average), the 2nd least sunny (about 2,308 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (1,572 mm a year). Compared with Porto, the nearest covered town with different numbers (47 km away), Braga is 0.3 °C warmer in August and 1.6 °C cooler in January, with 140 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.3 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, January (8.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Braga, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Braga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
