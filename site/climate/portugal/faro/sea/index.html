Faro sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Portugal

The sea off Faro averages 20.6 °C (69 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 15.3 °C (60 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Faro sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Faro?
In August, at about 20.6 °C (69 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Faro?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 15.3 °C (60 °F).
How warm is the sea in Faro in February?
About 15.3 °C (60 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Faro?
About 17.9 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Faro nearby?
In August: Olhão (21.3 °C, 8 km), Tavira (21.3 °C, 27 km) and Ayamonte (21.6 °C, 51 km).
