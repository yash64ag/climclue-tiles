Portimão sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Faro · Portugal

The sea off Portimão averages 20.1 °C (68 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 15.4 °C (60 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September.

Portimão sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Portimão?
In September, at about 20.1 °C (68 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Portimão?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is February, at 15.4 °C (60 °F).
How warm is the sea in Portimão in February?
About 15.4 °C (60 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Portimão?
About 17.7 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Portimão nearby?
In September: Olhão (21.0 °C, 63 km), Tavira (21.0 °C, 79 km) and Ayamonte (21.3 °C, 100 km).
