Ponta Delgada sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Azores · Portugal

The sea off Ponta Delgada averages 22.4 °C (72 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 15.4 °C (60 °F) in March. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to October.

Ponta Delgada sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ponta Delgada?
In August, at about 22.4 °C (72 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ponta Delgada?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to October. The coolest month is March, at 15.4 °C (60 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ponta Delgada in March?
About 15.4 °C (60 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Ponta Delgada?
About 18.4 °C (65 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
