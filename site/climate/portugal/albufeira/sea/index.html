Albufeira sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Faro · Portugal

The sea off Albufeira averages 20.3 °C (69 °F) in September, its warmest month, and 15.4 °C (60 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September.

Albufeira sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Albufeira?
In September, at about 20.3 °C (69 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Albufeira?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is February, at 15.4 °C (60 °F).
How warm is the sea in Albufeira in February?
About 15.4 °C (60 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Albufeira?
About 17.8 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Albufeira nearby?
In September: Olhão (21.0 °C, 37 km), Tavira (21.0 °C, 53 km) and Ayamonte (21.3 °C, 76 km).
