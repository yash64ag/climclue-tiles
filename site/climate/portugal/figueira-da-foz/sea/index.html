Figueira da Foz sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Coimbra · Portugal

The sea off Figueira da Foz averages 18.2 °C (65 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 13.8 °C (57 °F) in February. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Figueira da Foz sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Figueira da Foz?
In August, at about 18.2 °C (65 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Figueira da Foz?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is February, at 13.8 °C (57 °F).
How warm is the sea in Figueira da Foz in February?
About 13.8 °C (57 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Figueira da Foz?
About 16.1 °C (61 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Figueira da Foz nearby?
In August: Caldas da Rainha (19.0 °C, 86 km).
