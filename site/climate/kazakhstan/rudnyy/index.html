About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Rudnyy itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (52.972°N, 63.111°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-310-2431), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
