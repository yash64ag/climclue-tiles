Taraz at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kazakhstan, Taraz is the 4th sunniest (about 2,709 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Shymkent, the nearest covered town with different numbers (159 km away), Taraz is 1.0 °C cooler in July and 2.3 °C cooler in January, with 257 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.9 °C), averages 29.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,396 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Taraz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 1,396 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
