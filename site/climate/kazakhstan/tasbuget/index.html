Tasbuget at a glance

Among the 83 towns ClimaClue covers in Kazakhstan, Tasbuget is the 5th sunniest (about 2,899 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Kyzylorda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Tasbuget is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 34.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-6.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 980 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Tasbuget has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 980 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 34.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km).
