Pavlodar at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kazakhstan, Pavlodar is 17th warmest (3.7 °C year average), 17th sunniest (about 2,394 hours of sunshine a year) and 15th wettest (292 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ekibastuz, the nearest covered town with different numbers (128 km away), Pavlodar is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 19 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.8 °C), averages 37.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-15.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 1,589 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pavlodar, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pavlodar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,589 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 37.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
