How July compares in Astana

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Astana. Compared with June: 1.3 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. August is 2.0 °C cooler than July on average.

Astana in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Astana in July?
In July, Astana averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 14.9 °C (59 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 329 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Astana?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Astana’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Astana in July?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (49 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Astana in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Astana get in July?
About 329 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Astana in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
