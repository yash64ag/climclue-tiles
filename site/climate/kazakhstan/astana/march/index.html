How March compares in Astana

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Astana. Compared with February: 7.0 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 48 hours more sunshine. April is 12.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Astana in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Astana in March?
In March, Astana averages a day high of -1.7 °C (29 °F) and a night low of -11.0 °C (12 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Astana?
March is not one of Astana’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Astana in March?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (49 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Astana in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 19 cm (7.3 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 34 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Astana get in March?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Astana in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
