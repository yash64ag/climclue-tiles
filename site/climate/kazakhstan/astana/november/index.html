Astana weather in November

Average November weather in Astana, Kazakhstan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Astana averages -2.1 °C (28 °F) days and -9.6 °C (15 °F) nights, with 29 mm (1.1 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 96 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Astana

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Astana. Compared with October: 10.0 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 36 hours less sunshine. December is 6.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Astana in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Astana in November?
In November, Astana averages a day high of -2.1 °C (28 °F) and a night low of -9.6 °C (15 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 96 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Astana?
November is not one of Astana’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Astana in November?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (50 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Astana in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 19 cm (7.5 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Astana get in November?
About 96 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Astana in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
