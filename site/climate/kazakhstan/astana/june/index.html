Astana weather in June

Average June weather in Astana, Kazakhstan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Astana averages 25.9 °C (79 °F) days and 12.7 °C (55 °F) nights, with 39 mm (1.5 in) of rain over the month and about 330 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Astana

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Astana. Compared with May: 5.7 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. July is 1.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Astana in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Astana in June?
In June, Astana averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 330 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Astana?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Astana’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Astana in June?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (50 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Astana in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Astana get in June?
About 330 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Astana in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
