How February compares in Astana

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Astana. Compared with January: 0.3 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. March is 7.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Astana in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Astana in February?
In February, Astana averages a day high of -9.0 °C (16 °F) and a night low of -17.3 °C (1 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 141 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Astana?
February is not one of Astana’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Astana in February?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (49 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Astana in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 22 cm (8.5 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 37 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Astana get in February?
About 141 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Astana in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
