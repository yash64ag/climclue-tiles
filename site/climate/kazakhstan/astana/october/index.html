How October compares in Astana

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Astana. Compared with September: 7.9 °C cooler, 5 mm more precipitation and 92 hours less sunshine. November is 10.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Astana in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Astana in October?
In October, Astana averages a day high of 8.6 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 0.1 °C (32 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Astana?
October is not one of Astana’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Astana in October?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (50 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Astana in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.4 cm (2.9 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Astana get in October?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Astana in October?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
