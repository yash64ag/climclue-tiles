How May compares in Astana

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Astana. Compared with April: 8.5 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 61 hours more sunshine. June is 5.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Astana in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Astana in May?
In May, Astana averages a day high of 19.3 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 6.3 °C (43 °F), with 37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 295 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Astana?
May is not one of Astana’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Astana in May?
37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (49 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Astana in May?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.1 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in May (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Astana get in May?
About 295 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Astana in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
