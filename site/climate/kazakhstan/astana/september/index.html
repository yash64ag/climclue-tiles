How September compares in Astana

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Astana. Compared with August: 6.5 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 64 hours less sunshine. October is 7.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Astana in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Astana in September?
In September, Astana averages a day high of 17.7 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 7.0 °C (45 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Astana?
September is not one of Astana’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Astana in September?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (49 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Astana in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Astana get in September?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Astana in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
