How April compares in Astana

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Astana. Compared with March: 12.0 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. May is 8.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Astana in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Astana in April?
In April, Astana averages a day high of 10.7 °C (51 °F) and a night low of -1.6 °C (29 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Astana?
April is not one of Astana’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Astana in April?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (49 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Astana in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.7 cm (3.0 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 5.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Astana get in April?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Astana in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
