How January compares in Astana

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Astana. Compared with December: 1.5 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. February is 0.2 °C cooler than January on average.

Astana in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Astana in January?
In January, Astana averages a day high of -9.6 °C (15 °F) and a night low of -18.0 °C (0 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 101 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Astana?
January is not one of Astana’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Astana in January?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (50 mm), the driest February (16 mm).
Does it snow in Astana in January?
Yes, on average: about 17 cm (6.6 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 14 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Astana get in January?
About 101 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Astana in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
