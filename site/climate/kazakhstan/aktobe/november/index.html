How November compares in Aktobe

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Aktobe. Compared with October: 9.3 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 59 hours less sunshine. December is 6.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Aktobe in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aktobe in November?
In November, Aktobe averages a day high of -0.1 °C (32 °F) and a night low of -6.1 °C (21 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 76 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Aktobe?
November is not one of Aktobe’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aktobe in November?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (37 mm), the driest September (22 mm).
Does it snow in Aktobe in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 21 cm (8.3 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Aktobe get in November?
About 76 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aktobe in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
