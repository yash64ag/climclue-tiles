How December compares in Aktobe

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Aktobe. Compared with November: 6.5 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. January is 2.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Aktobe in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aktobe in December?
In December, Aktobe averages a day high of -5.9 °C (21 °F) and a night low of -13.7 °C (7 °F), with 29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 67 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Aktobe?
December is not one of Aktobe’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aktobe in December?
29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (37 mm), the driest September (22 mm).
Does it snow in Aktobe in December?
Yes, on average: about 27 cm (10.7 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 6.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Aktobe get in December?
About 67 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aktobe in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
