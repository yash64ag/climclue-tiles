How February compares in Aktobe

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Aktobe. Compared with January: 0.3 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. March is 6.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Aktobe in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aktobe in February?
In February, Aktobe averages a day high of -8.2 °C (17 °F) and a night low of -15.0 °C (5 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Aktobe?
February is not one of Aktobe’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aktobe in February?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (37 mm), the driest September (22 mm).
Does it snow in Aktobe in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 29 cm (11.5 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 45 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Aktobe get in February?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aktobe in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
