Aktobe weather in March

Average March weather in Aktobe, Kazakhstan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Aktobe averages -1.0 °C (30 °F) days and -10.1 °C (14 °F) nights, with 26 mm (1.0 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 173 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Aktobe

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Aktobe. Compared with February: 6.5 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. April is 12.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Aktobe in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aktobe in March?
In March, Aktobe averages a day high of -1.0 °C (30 °F) and a night low of -10.1 °C (14 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Aktobe?
March is not one of Aktobe’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aktobe in March?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (37 mm), the driest September (22 mm).
Does it snow in Aktobe in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 18 cm (7.2 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 16 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Aktobe get in March?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aktobe in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
