How October compares in Aktobe

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Aktobe. Compared with September: 8.3 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 89 hours less sunshine. November is 9.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Aktobe in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aktobe in October?
In October, Aktobe averages a day high of 11.7 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 0.7 °C (33 °F), with 29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Aktobe?
October is not one of Aktobe’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aktobe in October?
29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (37 mm), the driest September (22 mm).
Does it snow in Aktobe in October?
Yes, on average: about 3.8 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in October (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Aktobe get in October?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aktobe in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
