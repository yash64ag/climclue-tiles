Aktobe in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aktobe in August?
In August, Aktobe averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 15.3 °C (60 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 297 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Aktobe?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Aktobe’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aktobe in August?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (37 mm), the driest September (22 mm).
Does it snow in Aktobe in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aktobe get in August?
About 297 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aktobe in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
