How May compares in Aktobe

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Aktobe. Compared with April: 8.2 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 74 hours more sunshine. June is 6.1 °C warmer than May on average.

Aktobe in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aktobe in May?
In May, Aktobe averages a day high of 20.1 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 308 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Aktobe?
May is not one of Aktobe’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aktobe in May?
37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is May (37 mm), the driest September (22 mm).
Does it snow in Aktobe in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aktobe get in May?
About 308 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aktobe in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
