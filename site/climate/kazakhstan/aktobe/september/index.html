How September compares in Aktobe

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Aktobe. Compared with August: 6.6 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 73 hours less sunshine. October is 8.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Aktobe in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aktobe in September?
In September, Aktobe averages a day high of 20.0 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 9.4 °C (49 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Aktobe?
September is not one of Aktobe’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aktobe in September?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the driest month of the year. The wettest month is May (37 mm), the driest September (22 mm).
Does it snow in Aktobe in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aktobe get in September?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aktobe in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
