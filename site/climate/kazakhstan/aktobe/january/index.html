How January compares in Aktobe

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Aktobe. Compared with December: 2.5 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. February is 0.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Aktobe in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aktobe in January?
In January, Aktobe averages a day high of -8.9 °C (16 °F) and a night low of -15.2 °C (5 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 86 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Aktobe?
January is not one of Aktobe’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aktobe in January?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (37 mm), the driest September (22 mm).
Does it snow in Aktobe in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 38 cm (14.9 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 34 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Aktobe get in January?
About 86 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aktobe in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
