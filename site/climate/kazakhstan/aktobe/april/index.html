How April compares in Aktobe

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Aktobe. Compared with March: 12.3 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 61 hours more sunshine. May is 8.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Aktobe in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aktobe in April?
In April, Aktobe averages a day high of 11.6 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 0.5 °C (33 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Aktobe?
April is not one of Aktobe’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aktobe in April?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (37 mm), the driest September (22 mm).
Does it snow in Aktobe in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.6 cm (3.0 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 7.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Aktobe get in April?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aktobe in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
