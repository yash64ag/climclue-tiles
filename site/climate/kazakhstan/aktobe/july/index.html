How July compares in Aktobe

July is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Aktobe. Compared with June: 1.9 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. August is 1.9 °C cooler than July on average.

Aktobe in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aktobe in July?
In July, Aktobe averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 330 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Aktobe?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Aktobe’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aktobe in July?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (37 mm), the driest September (22 mm).
Does it snow in Aktobe in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aktobe get in July?
About 330 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aktobe in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
