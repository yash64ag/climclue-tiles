Saran at a glance

Among the 83 towns ClimaClue covers in Kazakhstan, Saran is 63rd warmest (4.0 °C year average), 49th sunniest (about 2,529 hours of sunshine a year) and 37th wettest (326 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Abay, Karaganda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Saran is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.0 °C), averages 34.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-13.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,500 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 23 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 93% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern. Why Saran has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,500 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 34.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Saran itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (49.802°N, 72.832°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-341-2528), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
