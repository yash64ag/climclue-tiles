Taldykorgan at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kazakhstan, Taldykorgan is the 4th wettest (439 mm a year). Compared with Almaty, the nearest covered town with different numbers (228 km away), Taldykorgan is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 2.9 °C cooler in January, with 567 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.3 °C), averages 32.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-9.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,958 km from the sea (deep inland).
