Karagandy at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kazakhstan, Karagandy is the 5th coolest (3.6 °C year average). Compared with Temirtau, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Karagandy is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 32 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.4 °C), averages 34.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-13.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,521 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Karagandy has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,521 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 34.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
