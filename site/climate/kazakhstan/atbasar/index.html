Atbasar at a glance

Among the 83 towns ClimaClue covers in Kazakhstan, Atbasar is 76th warmest (3.0 °C year average), 69th sunniest (about 2,389 hours of sunshine a year) and 46th wettest (319 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Makinsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (168 km away), Atbasar is 1.6 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 15 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.7 °C), averages 36.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-15.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,243 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Atbasar has this climate

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Atbasar itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (51.809°N, 68.358°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 6.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-321-2483), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
