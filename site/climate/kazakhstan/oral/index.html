Oral at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kazakhstan, Oral is the 3rd least sunny (about 2,274 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Aktobe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (420 km away), Oral is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 2.0 °C warmer in January, with 8 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.1 °C), averages 33.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-10.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 467 km from the sea.

Why Oral has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 467 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 33.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
