Kostanay at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kazakhstan, Kostanay is the 4th least sunny (about 2,350 hours of sunshine a year). The area lies about 1,042 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Kostanay has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
