Almaty weather in August

August · Kazakhstan

Average August weather in Almaty: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

How August compares in Almaty

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Almaty. Compared with July: 0.9 °C cooler, 34 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. September is 5.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Almaty in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Almaty in August?
In August, Almaty averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 17.0 °C (63 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 302 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Almaty?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Almaty’s mild months (July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Almaty in August?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (162 mm), the driest January (36 mm).
Does it snow in Almaty in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Almaty get in August?
About 302 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Almaty in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
