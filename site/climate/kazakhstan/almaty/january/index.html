How January compares in Almaty

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Almaty. Compared with December: 2.2 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. February is 2.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Almaty in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Almaty in January?
In January, Almaty averages a day high of -1.2 °C (30 °F) and a night low of -9.7 °C (15 °F), with 39 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 122 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Almaty?
January is not one of Almaty’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Almaty in January?
39 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is May (176 mm), the driest January (39 mm).
Does it snow in Almaty in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 28 cm (10.8 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 39 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Almaty get in January?
About 122 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Almaty in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
