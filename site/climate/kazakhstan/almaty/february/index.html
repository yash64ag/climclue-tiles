How February compares in Almaty

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Almaty. Compared with January: 2.5 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. March is 6.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Almaty in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Almaty in February?
In February, Almaty averages a day high of 1.1 °C (34 °F) and a night low of -8.7 °C (16 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Almaty?
February is not one of Almaty’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Almaty in February?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (162 mm), the driest January (36 mm).
Does it snow in Almaty in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 39 cm (15.4 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 19 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Almaty get in February?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Almaty in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
