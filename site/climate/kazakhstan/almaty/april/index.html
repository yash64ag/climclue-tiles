How April compares in Almaty

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Almaty. Compared with March: 7.6 °C warmer, 56 mm more precipitation and 47 hours more sunshine. May is 5.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Almaty in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Almaty in April?
In April, Almaty averages a day high of 14.2 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 4.1 °C (39 °F), with 152 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Almaty?
April is not one of Almaty’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Almaty in April?
152 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (176 mm), the driest January (39 mm).
Does it snow in Almaty in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 44 cm (17.4 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 21 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Almaty get in April?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Almaty in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
