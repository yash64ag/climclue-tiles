How June compares in Almaty

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Almaty. Compared with May: 5.0 °C warmer, 61 mm less precipitation and 39 hours more sunshine. July is 2.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Almaty in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Almaty in June?
In June, Almaty averages a day high of 27.8 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 15.9 °C (61 °F), with 101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 294 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Almaty?
June is not one of Almaty’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Almaty in June?
101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (162 mm), the driest January (36 mm).
Does it snow in Almaty in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Almaty get in June?
About 294 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Almaty in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
