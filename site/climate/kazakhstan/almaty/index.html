Almaty at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kazakhstan, Almaty is the wettest (1,006 mm a year). Compared with Taldykorgan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (228 km away), Almaty is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 2.9 °C warmer in January, with 567 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.6 °C), averages 28.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-6.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,852 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Almaty has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,852 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
