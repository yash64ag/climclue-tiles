September · Kazakhstan

How September compares in Almaty

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Almaty. Compared with August: 5.2 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 49 hours less sunshine. October is 7.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Almaty in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Almaty in September?
In September, Almaty averages a day high of 24.7 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 11.7 °C (53 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 253 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Almaty?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Almaty’s mild months (July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Almaty in September?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (162 mm), the driest January (36 mm).
Does it snow in Almaty in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Almaty get in September?
About 253 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Almaty in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
