How November compares in Almaty

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Almaty. Compared with October: 7.7 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 59 hours less sunshine. December is 6.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Almaty in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Almaty in November?
In November, Almaty averages a day high of 7.1 °C (45 °F) and a night low of -2.7 °C (27 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 131 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Almaty?
November is not one of Almaty’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Almaty in November?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (162 mm), the driest January (36 mm).
Does it snow in Almaty in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 16 cm (6.3 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Almaty get in November?
About 131 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Almaty in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
