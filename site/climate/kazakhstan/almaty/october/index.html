How October compares in Almaty

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Almaty. Compared with September: 7.4 °C cooler, 36 mm more precipitation and 63 hours less sunshine. November is 7.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Almaty in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Almaty in October?
In October, Almaty averages a day high of 14.5 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Almaty?
October is not one of Almaty’s mild months; those are July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Almaty in October?
80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (176 mm), the driest January (39 mm).
Does it snow in Almaty in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 30 cm (11.7 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Almaty get in October?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Almaty in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
