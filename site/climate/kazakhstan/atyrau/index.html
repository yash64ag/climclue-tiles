Why Atyrau has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 34 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 33.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Atyrau climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Atyrau?
July, with an average of 27.3 °C (81 °F), day highs around 32.1 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 22.1 °C (72 °F).
What is the coldest month in Atyrau?
February, with an average of -6.5 °C (20 °F), day highs around -2.6 °C (27 °F) and night lows around -9.3 °C (15 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Atyrau?
May, with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 174 mm (6.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Atyrau?
September, with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Atyrau?
July, with about 339 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Atyrau?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 57 cm (22.5 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 4 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Atyrau?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June and September.
What type of climate does Atyrau have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.6 × 11 km, that contains Atyrau’s GeoNames location (47.105°N, 51.884°E; ClimaClue area sq-368-2318), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 290,700 (GeoNames).
