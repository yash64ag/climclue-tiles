Atyrau at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kazakhstan, Atyrau is the 2nd driest (174 mm a year). Compared with Aktau, the nearest covered town with different numbers (387 km away), Atyrau is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 7.4 °C cooler in February, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.3 °C), averages 33.7 °C more than its coolest, February (-6.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 34 km from the sea.

Why Atyrau has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 34 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 33.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
