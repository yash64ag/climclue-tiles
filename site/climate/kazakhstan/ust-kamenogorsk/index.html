Ust-Kamenogorsk at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kazakhstan, Ust-Kamenogorsk is the 3rd wettest (496 mm a year). Compared with Semey, the nearest covered town with different numbers (175 km away), Ust-Kamenogorsk is 1.8 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 207 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.9 °C), averages 34.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-13.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 1,917 km from the sea (deep inland).
