Merke at a glance

Among the 83 towns ClimaClue covers in Kazakhstan, Merke is 26th warmest (10.5 °C year average), 36th sunniest (about 2,621 hours of sunshine a year) and 16th wettest (422 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (94 km away), Merke is 3.8 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 98 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.3 °C), averages 28.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,542 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Merke, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Merke has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Merke itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (42.872°N, 73.196°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-411-2531), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
