Why Aktau has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Aktau climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Aktau?
July, with an average of 26.6 °C (80 °F), day highs around 30.1 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 23.2 °C (74 °F).
What is the coldest month in Aktau?
January, with an average of 0.8 °C (33 °F), day highs around 3.4 °C (38 °F) and night lows around -1.3 °C (30 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Aktau?
November, with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 177 mm (7.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Aktau?
August, with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Aktau?
July, with about 328 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Aktau?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 31 cm (12.0 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 2 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Aktau?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June and September.
What type of climate does Aktau have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.1 × 11 km, that contains Aktau’s GeoNames location (43.661°N, 51.174°E; ClimaClue area sq-403-2311), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 147,443 (GeoNames).
