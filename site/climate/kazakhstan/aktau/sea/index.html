Aktau sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Mangystau · Kazakhstan

The sea off Aktau averages 23.4 °C (74 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 6.6 °C (44 °F) in March. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Aktau sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Aktau?
In August, at about 23.4 °C (74 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Aktau?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is March, at 6.6 °C (44 °F).
How warm is the sea in Aktau in March?
About 6.6 °C (44 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Aktau?
About 13.9 °C (57 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Aktau nearby?
In August: Izberbash (25.0 °C, 294 km), Derbent (24.9 °C, 295 km) and Dagestanskiye Ogni (24.9 °C, 298 km).
