Why Semey has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,825 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 37.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Semey climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Semey?
July, with an average of 22.8 °C (73 °F), day highs around 28.1 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 16.5 °C (62 °F).
What is the coldest month in Semey?
January, with an average of -14.7 °C (6 °F), day highs around -10.0 °C (14 °F) and night lows around -18.6 °C (-1 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Semey?
July, with 52 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 289 mm (11.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Semey?
January, with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Semey?
July, with about 335 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Semey?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 110 cm (43.3 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in December; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Semey?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July and August.
What type of climate does Semey have?
Humid continental, hot summer (Köppen Dfa). Hot summers and cold winters, with rain or snow all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.1 × 11 km, that contains Semey’s GeoNames location (50.421°N, 80.250°E; ClimaClue area sq-335-2602), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 292,780 (GeoNames).
