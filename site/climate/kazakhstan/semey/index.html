Semey at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kazakhstan, Semey is 13th warmest (4.7 °C year average), 11th sunniest (about 2,504 hours of sunshine a year) and 16th wettest (289 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ust-Kamenogorsk, the nearest covered town with different numbers (175 km away), Semey is 1.8 °C warmer in July and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 207 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.8 °C), averages 37.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-14.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 1,825 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 104% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Semey has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,825 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 37.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
