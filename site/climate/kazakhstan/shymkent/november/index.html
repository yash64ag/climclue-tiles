Shymkent weather in November

Average November weather in Shymkent, Kazakhstan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Shymkent averages 11.2 °C (52 °F) days and 0.2 °C (32 °F) nights, with 68 mm (2.7 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 149 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Shymkent

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Shymkent. Compared with October: 6.3 °C cooler, 29 mm more precipitation and 70 hours less sunshine. December is 5.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Shymkent in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shymkent in November?
In November, Shymkent averages a day high of 11.2 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 0.2 °C (32 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Shymkent?
November is not one of Shymkent’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shymkent in November?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (85 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Shymkent in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 13 cm (5.0 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Shymkent get in November?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shymkent in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
