How February compares in Shymkent

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Shymkent. Compared with January: 2.0 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. March is 6.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Shymkent in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shymkent in February?
In February, Shymkent averages a day high of 5.3 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -5.3 °C (22 °F), with 84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 133 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Shymkent?
February is not one of Shymkent’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shymkent in February?
84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (87 mm), the driest August (6 mm).
Does it snow in Shymkent in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 36 cm (14.3 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 15 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Shymkent get in February?
About 133 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shymkent in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
