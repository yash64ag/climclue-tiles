How April compares in Shymkent

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Shymkent. Compared with March: 6.9 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 49 hours more sunshine. May is 5.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Shymkent in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shymkent in April?
In April, Shymkent averages a day high of 18.6 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 6.2 °C (43 °F), with 77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Shymkent?
April is not one of Shymkent’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shymkent in April?
77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (87 mm), the driest August (6 mm).
Does it snow in Shymkent in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.3 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Shymkent get in April?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shymkent in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
