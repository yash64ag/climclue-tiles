How January compares in Shymkent

January is the coolest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Shymkent. Compared with December: 1.7 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. February is 2.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Shymkent in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shymkent in January?
In January, Shymkent averages a day high of 3.2 °C (38 °F) and a night low of -6.7 °C (20 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 121 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Shymkent?
January is not one of Shymkent’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shymkent in January?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is March (87 mm), the driest August (6 mm).
Does it snow in Shymkent in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 44 cm (17.2 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 16 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Shymkent get in January?
About 121 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shymkent in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
