Shymkent weather in October

Average October weather in Shymkent, Kazakhstan: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Shymkent averages 19.0 °C (66 °F) days and 4.9 °C (41 °F) nights, with 40 mm (1.6 in) of rain over the month and about 218 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Shymkent

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Shymkent. Compared with September: 7.2 °C cooler, 28 mm more precipitation and 77 hours less sunshine. November is 6.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Shymkent in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shymkent in October?
In October, Shymkent averages a day high of 19.0 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 4.9 °C (41 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Shymkent?
October is not one of Shymkent’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shymkent in October?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (85 mm), the driest August (5 mm).
Does it snow in Shymkent in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shymkent get in October?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shymkent in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
