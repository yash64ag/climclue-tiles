Shymkent at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kazakhstan, Shymkent is the 3rd warmest (12.3 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 2,812 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (631 mm a year). Compared with Turkistan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (155 km away), Shymkent is 3.4 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 381 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.9 °C), averages 27.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 1,254 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shymkent, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shymkent has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Far from the moderating sea, winters turn cold below freezing while summers are warm. Summer high pressure brings a dry season; most precipitation arrives in winter, often as snow. About 1,254 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
