How August compares in Shymkent

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Shymkent. Compared with July: 1.2 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. September is 5.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Shymkent in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shymkent in August?
In August, Shymkent averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 360 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Shymkent?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Shymkent’s mild months (May, June, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shymkent in August?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the driest month of the year. The wettest month is March (87 mm), the driest August (6 mm).
Does it snow in Shymkent in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shymkent get in August?
About 360 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shymkent in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
