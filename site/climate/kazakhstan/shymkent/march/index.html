How March compares in Shymkent

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Shymkent. Compared with February: 6.3 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. April is 6.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Shymkent in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shymkent in March?
In March, Shymkent averages a day high of 11.6 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 0.0 °C (32 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Shymkent?
March is not one of Shymkent’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shymkent in March?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is March (87 mm), the driest August (6 mm).
Does it snow in Shymkent in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 15 cm (5.9 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Shymkent get in March?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shymkent in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
