How June compares in Shymkent

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Shymkent. Compared with May: 5.1 °C warmer, 43 mm less precipitation and 54 hours more sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Shymkent in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shymkent in June?
In June, Shymkent averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 16.0 °C (61 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 357 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Shymkent?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Shymkent’s mild months (May, June, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shymkent in June?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is March (87 mm), the driest August (6 mm).
Does it snow in Shymkent in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shymkent get in June?
About 357 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shymkent in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
