Zaysan at a glance

Among the 83 towns ClimaClue covers in Kazakhstan, Zaysan is 54th warmest (5.2 °C year average), 34th sunniest (about 2,651 hours of sunshine a year) and 49th wettest (311 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Maqanshy, the nearest covered town with different numbers (229 km away), Zaysan is 1.2 °C cooler in July and 4.9 °C cooler in January, with 39 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.0 °C), averages 38.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-15.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 2,227 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: before adjustment, the modelled temperatures differed from a NOAA weather station 3 km from Zaysan by up to 1.8 °C (3.2 °F) in one month. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Why Zaysan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 2,227 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 38.4 °C.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Zaysan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (47.467°N, 84.871°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-365-2648), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
