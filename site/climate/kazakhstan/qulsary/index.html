Qulsary at a glance

Among the 83 towns ClimaClue covers in Kazakhstan, Qulsary is 33rd warmest (9.9 °C year average), 39th sunniest (about 2,566 hours of sunshine a year) and 70th wettest (184 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Atyrau, the nearest covered town with different numbers (163 km away), Qulsary is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 1.3 °C cooler in January, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 35.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-7.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 79 km from the sea.

Why Qulsary has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 79 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Qulsary itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (46.953°N, 54.020°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-370-2340), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
