Balykshi sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Atyrau Oblisi · Kazakhstan

The sea off Balykshi averages 26.0 °C (79 °F) in July, its warmest month, and -1.6 °C (29 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August.

Balykshi sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Balykshi?
In July, at about 26.0 °C (79 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Balykshi?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to August. The coolest month is February, at -1.6 °C (29 °F).
How warm is the sea in Balykshi in February?
About -1.6 °C (29 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Balykshi?
About 11.0 °C (52 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
