Zhezqazghan weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Zhezqazghan, Kazakhstan (Ulytau Region), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Zhezqazghan is warmest in July, with 31.3 °C (88 °F) days and 16.6 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -8.4 °C (17 °F) days and -17.3 °C nights. Most rain falls around May (21 mm); September is driest (6 mm).

Zhezqazghan at a glance

Among the 83 towns ClimaClue covers in Kazakhstan, Zhezqazghan is 50th warmest (6.0 °C year average), 29th sunniest (about 2,676 hours of sunshine a year) and 67th wettest (201 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Satpayev, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Zhezqazghan is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 27 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.9 °C), averages 36.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-12.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,113 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Zhezqazghan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Zhezqazghan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (47.794°N, 67.706°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-362-2477), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
