Ekibastuz at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kazakhstan, Ekibastuz is 15th warmest (4.1 °C year average), 16th sunniest (about 2,428 hours of sunshine a year) and 17th wettest (273 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pavlodar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (128 km away), Ekibastuz is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 1.2 °C warmer in January, with 19 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.6 °C), averages 35.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-14.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 1,633 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ekibastuz, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ekibastuz has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter.
