Kokshetau at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kazakhstan, Kokshetau is the 2nd coolest (2.6 °C year average) and the 2nd least sunny (about 2,263 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Petropavl, the nearest covered town with different numbers (177 km away), Kokshetau is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 50 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.4 °C), averages 34.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-14.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 1,365 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kokshetau, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kokshetau has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,365 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 34.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
