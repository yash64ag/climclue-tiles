Temirtau at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kazakhstan, Temirtau is the 4th coolest (3.5 °C year average). Compared with Karagandy, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Temirtau is about as warm in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 29 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.4 °C), averages 34.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-14.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,509 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Temirtau has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 1,509 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 34.5 °C.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Temirtau itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (50.052°N, 72.955°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-339-2529), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
