Kyzylorda at a glance

Among the 23 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Kazakhstan, Kyzylorda is the 5th warmest (10.7 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 2,894 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th driest (182 mm a year). Compared with Turkistan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (280 km away), Kyzylorda is 1.6 °C cooler in July and 4.3 °C cooler in January, with 68 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 34.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-7.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 984 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kyzylorda, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kyzylorda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 984 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 34.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
