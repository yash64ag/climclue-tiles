Sarajevo in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sarajevo in July?
In July, Sarajevo averages a day high of 25.0 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 257 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Sarajevo?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Sarajevo’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sarajevo in July?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (103 mm), the driest January (72 mm).
Does it snow in Sarajevo in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sarajevo get in July?
About 257 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sarajevo in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
