Sarajevo weather in March

Average March weather in Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Sarajevo averages 10.7 °C (51 °F) days and 1.3 °C (34 °F) nights, with 75 mm (3.0 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 128 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Sarajevo

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Sarajevo. Compared with February: 4.1 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. April is 4.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Sarajevo in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sarajevo in March?
In March, Sarajevo averages a day high of 10.7 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 1.3 °C (34 °F), with 75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 128 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Sarajevo?
March is not one of Sarajevo’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sarajevo in March?
75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (99 mm), the driest January (70 mm).
Does it snow in Sarajevo in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.3 cm (2.9 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Sarajevo get in March?
About 128 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sarajevo in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
