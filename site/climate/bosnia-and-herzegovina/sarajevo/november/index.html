How November compares in Sarajevo

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Sarajevo. Compared with October: 5.9 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 65 hours less sunshine. December is 4.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Sarajevo in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sarajevo in November?
In November, Sarajevo averages a day high of 9.7 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 1.6 °C (35 °F), with 103 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 85 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Sarajevo?
November is not one of Sarajevo’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sarajevo in November?
103 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is November (103 mm), the driest January (72 mm).
Does it snow in Sarajevo in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 39 cm (15.2 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 5.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Sarajevo get in November?
About 85 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sarajevo in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a rain jacket or umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
