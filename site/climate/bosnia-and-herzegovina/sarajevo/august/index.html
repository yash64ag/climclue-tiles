Sarajevo in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sarajevo in August?
In August, Sarajevo averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 14.9 °C (59 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 239 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Sarajevo?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Sarajevo’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sarajevo in August?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is November (103 mm), the driest January (72 mm).
Does it snow in Sarajevo in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sarajevo get in August?
About 239 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sarajevo in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
