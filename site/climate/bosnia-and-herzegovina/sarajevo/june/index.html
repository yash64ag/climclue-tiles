How June compares in Sarajevo

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Sarajevo. Compared with May: 3.1 °C warmer, 21 mm more precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. July is 2.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Sarajevo in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sarajevo in June?
In June, Sarajevo averages a day high of 22.6 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Sarajevo?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Sarajevo’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sarajevo in June?
95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (103 mm), the driest January (72 mm).
Does it snow in Sarajevo in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sarajevo get in June?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sarajevo in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
