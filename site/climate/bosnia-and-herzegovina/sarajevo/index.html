Sarajevo at a glance

Among the 5 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bosnia and Herzegovina, Sarajevo is the coolest (11.0 °C year average), the least sunny (about 1,798 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (976 mm a year). Compared with Zenica, the nearest covered town with different numbers (53 km away), Sarajevo is 1.1 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 76 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.8 °C), averages 20.1 °C more than its coolest, January (0.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 131 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 104% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Sarajevo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 131 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sarajevo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (43.849°N, 18.356°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-401-1983), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
