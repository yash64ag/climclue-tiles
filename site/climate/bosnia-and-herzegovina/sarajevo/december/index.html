How December compares in Sarajevo

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Sarajevo. Compared with November: 4.3 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. January is 1.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Sarajevo in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sarajevo in December?
In December, Sarajevo averages a day high of 4.9 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -1.3 °C (30 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 51 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Sarajevo?
December is not one of Sarajevo’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sarajevo in December?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (99 mm), the driest January (70 mm).
Does it snow in Sarajevo in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 24 cm (9.6 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.8 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Sarajevo get in December?
About 51 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sarajevo in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
