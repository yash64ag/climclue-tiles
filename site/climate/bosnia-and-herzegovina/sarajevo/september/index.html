Sarajevo in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sarajevo in September?
In September, Sarajevo averages a day high of 20.9 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Sarajevo?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Sarajevo’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sarajevo in September?
90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (103 mm), the driest January (72 mm).
Does it snow in Sarajevo in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sarajevo get in September?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sarajevo in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
