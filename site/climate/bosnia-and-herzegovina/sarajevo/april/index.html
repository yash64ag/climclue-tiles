Sarajevo in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sarajevo in April?
In April, Sarajevo averages a day high of 14.3 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 4.5 °C (40 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 152 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Sarajevo?
April is not one of Sarajevo’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sarajevo in April?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is November (103 mm), the driest January (72 mm).
Does it snow in Sarajevo in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 25 cm (9.7 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Sarajevo get in April?
About 152 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sarajevo in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
