Banja Luka at a glance

Among the 5 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bosnia and Herzegovina, Banja Luka is the 2nd coolest (10.8 °C year average), the 2nd sunniest (about 1,998 hours of sunshine a year) and the 3rd wettest (1,072 mm a year). Compared with Zenica, the nearest covered town with different numbers (85 km away), Banja Luka is 1.5 °C warmer in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 2 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.9 °C), averages 20.7 °C more than its coolest, January (0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 158 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Banja Luka, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Banja Luka has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 158 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
