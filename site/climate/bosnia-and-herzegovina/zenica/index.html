Why Zenica has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 137 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Zenica climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Zenica?
July, with an average of 19.4 °C (67 °F), day highs around 23.9 °C (75 °F) and night lows around 13.5 °C (56 °F).
What is the coldest month in Zenica?
January, with an average of -0.5 °C (31 °F), day highs around 3.3 °C (38 °F) and night lows around -4.9 °C (23 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Zenica?
June, with 125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,074 mm (42.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Zenica?
February, with 62 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Zenica?
July, with about 268 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Zenica?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 274 cm (107.8 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in December; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Zenica?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: July, August and September.
What type of climate does Zenica have?
Humid continental, warm summer (Köppen Dfb). Warm summers and cold, snowy winters, with rain or snow all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.0 × 11 km, that contains Zenica’s GeoNames location (44.202°N, 17.904°E; ClimaClue area sq-397-1979), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 164,423 (GeoNames).
