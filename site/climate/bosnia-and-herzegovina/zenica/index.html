Zenica at a glance

Among the 5 towns of 100,000+ people covered in Bosnia and Herzegovina, Zenica is the coolest (9.7 °C year average), the 3rd sunniest (about 1,877 hours of sunshine a year) and the 2nd wettest (1,074 mm a year). Compared with Sarajevo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (53 km away), Zenica is 1.3 °C cooler in July and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 58 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.4 °C), averages 19.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 137 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Zenica, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Zenica has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 137 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
