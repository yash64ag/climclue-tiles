Saint-Louis sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Reunion · Reunion

The sea off Saint-Louis averages 27.5 °C (82 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 23.0 °C (73 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from November to June.

Saint-Louis sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Saint-Louis?
In February, at about 27.5 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Saint-Louis?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from November to June. The coolest month is August, at 23.0 °C (73 °F).
How warm is the sea in Saint-Louis in August?
About 23.0 °C (73 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Saint-Louis?
About 25.2 °C (77 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
