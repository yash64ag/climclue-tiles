Saint-Joseph sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Reunion · Reunion

The sea off Saint-Joseph averages 26.9 °C (80 °F) in February, its warmest month, and 22.4 °C (72 °F) in August. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from November to June.

Saint-Joseph sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Saint-Joseph?
In February, at about 26.9 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Saint-Joseph?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from November to June. The coolest month is August, at 22.4 °C (72 °F).
How warm is the sea in Saint-Joseph in August?
About 22.4 °C (72 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Saint-Joseph?
About 24.7 °C (76 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Saint-Joseph nearby?
In February: Saint-Louis (27.5 °C, 24 km), Piton Saint-Leu (27.5 °C, 36 km) and Saint-Leu (27.5 °C, 41 km).
