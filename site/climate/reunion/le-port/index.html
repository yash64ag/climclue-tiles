Le Port at a glance

Among the 14 towns ClimaClue covers in Reunion, Le Port is the 2nd warmest (24.1 °C year average) and the 2nd driest (1,457 mm a year). Compared with La Possession, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Le Port is 0.1 °C warmer in February and 0.1 °C warmer in August, with 183 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, February (26.6 °C), averages 5.0 °C more than its coolest, August (21.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.0 °C (64 °F), in August. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Le Port, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Le Port has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 5.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Le Port itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.938°S, 55.294°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-1049-2352), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
