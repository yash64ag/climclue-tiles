Jūrmala sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Latvia

The sea off Jūrmala averages 18.8 °C (66 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 0.4 °C (33 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Jūrmala sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Jūrmala?
In August, at about 18.8 °C (66 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Jūrmala?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 0.4 °C (33 °F).
How warm is the sea in Jūrmala in March?
About 0.4 °C (33 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Jūrmala?
About 8.3 °C (47 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
