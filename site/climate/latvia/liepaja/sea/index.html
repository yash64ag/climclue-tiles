Liepāja sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Liepaja · Latvia

The sea off Liepāja averages 18.0 °C (64 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 1.8 °C (35 °F) in March. It is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F).

Liepāja sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Liepāja?
In August, at about 18.0 °C (64 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Liepāja?
On average it is cold for most swimmers all year (under 20 °C / 68 °F). The coolest month is March, at 1.8 °C (35 °F).
How warm is the sea in Liepāja in March?
About 1.8 °C (35 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Liepāja?
About 9.0 °C (48 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Liepāja nearby?
In August: Jūrmala (18.8 °C, 176 km), Mārupe (18.9 °C, 191 km) and Riga (18.9 °C, 195 km).
