How August compares in Riga

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Riga. Compared with July: 0.8 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. September is 4.9 °C cooler than August on average.

Riga in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Riga in August?
In August, Riga averages a day high of 21.0 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Riga?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Riga’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Riga in August?
81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (87 mm), the driest April (34 mm).
Does it snow in Riga in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Riga get in August?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Riga in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
