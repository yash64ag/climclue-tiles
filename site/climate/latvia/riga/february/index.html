How February compares in Riga

February is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Riga. Compared with January: 0.5 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. March is 3.5 °C warmer than February on average.

What to pack for Riga in February

Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots.

Riga in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Riga in February?
In February, Riga averages a day high of -0.9 °C (30 °F) and a night low of -5.8 °C (22 °F), with 37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 62 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Riga?
February is not one of Riga’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Riga in February?
37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (87 mm), the driest April (34 mm).
Does it snow in Riga in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 26 cm (10.3 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 13 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Riga get in February?
About 62 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Riga in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
