How January compares in Riga

January is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Riga. Compared with December: 1.3 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 0.4 °C cooler than January on average.

Riga in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Riga in January?
In January, Riga averages a day high of -0.4 °C (31 °F) and a night low of -5.4 °C (22 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 31 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Riga?
January is not one of Riga’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Riga in January?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (87 mm), the driest April (34 mm).
Does it snow in Riga in January?
Yes, on average: about 29 cm (11.4 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 5.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Riga get in January?
About 31 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Riga in January?
About 1.4 °C (35 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (18.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Riga in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
