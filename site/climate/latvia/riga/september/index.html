How September compares in Riga

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Riga. Compared with August: 4.9 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 77 hours less sunshine. October is 5.0 °C cooler than September on average.

Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella.

Riga in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Riga in September?
In September, Riga averages a day high of 15.8 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 9.9 °C (50 °F), with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Riga?
September is not one of Riga’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Riga in September?
74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (87 mm), the driest April (34 mm).
Does it snow in Riga in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Riga get in September?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Riga in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
