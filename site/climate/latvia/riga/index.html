Riga at a glance

Its warmest month, July (18.1 °C), averages 21.4 °C more than its coolest, February (-3.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Riga has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
