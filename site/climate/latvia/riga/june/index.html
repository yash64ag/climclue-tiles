How June compares in Riga

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Riga. Compared with May: 3.5 °C warmer, 23 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. July is 2.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses.

Riga in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Riga in June?
In June, Riga averages a day high of 19.2 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 11.7 °C (53 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 282 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Riga?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Riga’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Riga in June?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (87 mm), the driest April (34 mm).
Does it snow in Riga in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Riga get in June?
About 282 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Riga in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
