How April compares in Riga

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Riga. Compared with March: 5.4 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 56 hours more sunshine. May is 6.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Riga in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Riga in April?
In April, Riga averages a day high of 10.5 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 1.8 °C (35 °F), with 34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Riga?
April is not one of Riga’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Riga in April?
34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (87 mm), the driest April (34 mm).
Does it snow in Riga in April?
Yes, on average: about 3.8 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in April (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Riga get in April?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Riga in April?
About 2.0 °C (36 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (18.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Riga in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
