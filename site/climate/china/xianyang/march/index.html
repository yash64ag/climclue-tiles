How March compares in Xianyang

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Xianyang. Compared with February: 5.1 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. April is 6.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Xianyang in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xianyang in March?
In March, Xianyang averages a day high of 15.2 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 4.7 °C (40 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Xianyang?
March is not one of Xianyang’s mild months; those are April and May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xianyang in March?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (105 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Xianyang in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xianyang get in March?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xianyang in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
