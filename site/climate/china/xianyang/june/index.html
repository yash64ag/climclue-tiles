How June compares in Xianyang

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Xianyang. Compared with May: 4.1 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. July is 1.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Xianyang in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xianyang in June?
In June, Xianyang averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 65 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Xianyang?
June is not one of Xianyang’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xianyang in June?
65 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (105 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Xianyang in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xianyang get in June?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xianyang in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
