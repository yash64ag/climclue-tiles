How December compares in Xianyang

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Xianyang. Compared with November: 5.6 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. January is 1.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Xianyang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xianyang in December?
In December, Xianyang averages a day high of 8.3 °C (47 °F) and a night low of -2.3 °C (28 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 124 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Xianyang?
December is not one of Xianyang’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xianyang in December?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (105 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Xianyang in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.5 cm (2.2 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Xianyang get in December?
About 124 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xianyang in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
