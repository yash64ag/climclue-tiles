Hailin at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Hailin is 648th warmest (4.7 °C year average), 230th sunniest (about 2,416 hours of sunshine a year) and 565th wettest (544 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mudanjiang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Hailin is 0.3 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 15 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.9 °C), averages 38.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-16.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 247 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 17 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 97% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Hailin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 247 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 38.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
