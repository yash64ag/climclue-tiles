Jianchang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Jianchang is 118th warmest (19.2 °C year average), 538th sunniest (about 1,805 hours of sunshine a year) and 98th wettest (1,747 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fuzhou, Jiangxi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (54 km away), Jianchang is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 259 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.4 °C), averages 23.4 °C more than its coolest, January (7.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 345 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 101% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Jianchang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 345 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
