Taizhou, Jiangsu at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Taizhou, Jiangsu is 358th warmest (15.6 °C year average), 335th sunniest (about 2,078 hours of sunshine a year) and 326th wettest (1,116 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yangzhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Taizhou, Jiangsu is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 32 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 25.1 °C more than its coolest, January (2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 99 km from the sea.

Why Taizhou, Jiangsu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 99 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
