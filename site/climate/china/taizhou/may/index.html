Taizhou, Jiangsu in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taizhou, Jiangsu in May?
In May, Taizhou, Jiangsu averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 88 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 196 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Taizhou, Jiangsu?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Taizhou, Jiangsu’s mild months (May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taizhou, Jiangsu in May?
88 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (220 mm), the driest December (31 mm).
Does it snow in Taizhou, Jiangsu in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taizhou, Jiangsu get in May?
About 196 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taizhou, Jiangsu in May?
