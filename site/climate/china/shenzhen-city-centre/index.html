Shenzhen City Centre at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Shenzhen City Centre is 70th warmest (23.1 °C year average), 1194th sunniest (about 1,940 hours of sunshine a year) and 65th wettest (2,125 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shenzhen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (1 km away), Shenzhen City Centre is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 51 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 13.5 °C more than its coolest, January (15.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.2 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 30 km from the sea.

Why Shenzhen City Centre has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 30 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Shenzhen City Centre itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.536°N, 114.077°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-614-2940), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
