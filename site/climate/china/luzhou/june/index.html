How June compares in Luzhou

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Luzhou. Compared with May: 2.4 °C warmer, 48 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. July is 2.6 °C warmer than June on average.

Luzhou in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Luzhou in June?
In June, Luzhou averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 195 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation and about 127 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Luzhou?
June is not one of Luzhou’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Luzhou in June?
195 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (195 mm), the driest January (29 mm).
Does it snow in Luzhou in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Luzhou get in June?
About 127 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Luzhou in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
