How January compares in Luzhou

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Luzhou. Compared with December: 1.3 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. February is 2.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Luzhou in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Luzhou in January?
In January, Luzhou averages a day high of 10.9 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 5.2 °C (41 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 65 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Luzhou?
January is not one of Luzhou’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Luzhou in January?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (198 mm), the driest February (31 mm).
Does it snow in Luzhou in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.5 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Luzhou get in January?
About 65 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Luzhou in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
