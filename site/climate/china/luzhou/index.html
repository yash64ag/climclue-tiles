Luzhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Luzhou is 145th warmest (18.6 °C year average), 653rd sunniest (about 1,330 hours of sunshine a year) and 286th wettest (1,214 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fuji, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Luzhou is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 39 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 19.6 °C more than its coolest, January (8.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.2 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 863 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Luzhou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Luzhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 863 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
