How March compares in Luzhou

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Luzhou. Compared with February: 3.8 °C warmer, 21 mm more precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. April is 4.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Luzhou in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Luzhou in March?
In March, Luzhou averages a day high of 17.3 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 9.8 °C (50 °F), with 52 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 108 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Luzhou?
March is not one of Luzhou’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Luzhou in March?
52 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (198 mm), the driest February (31 mm).
Does it snow in Luzhou in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Luzhou get in March?
About 108 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Luzhou in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
