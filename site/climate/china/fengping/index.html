Fengping, Yunnan at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Fengping, Yunnan is 164th warmest (21.9 °C year average), 893rd sunniest (about 2,155 hours of sunshine a year) and 385th wettest (1,473 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Menghuan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Fengping, Yunnan is 0.2 °C warmer in June and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 253 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (25.6 °C), averages 9.8 °C more than its coolest, January (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.8 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 725 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Fengping, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Fengping, Yunnan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 725 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.8 °C. The 10 km area around Fengping, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 20.7 °C a year, 1.2 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Fengping itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (24.397°N, 98.518°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-596-2785), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
