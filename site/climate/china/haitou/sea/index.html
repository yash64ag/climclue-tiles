Haitou sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Hainan · China

The sea off Haitou averages 29.5 °C (85 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 21.3 °C (70 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to November.

Haitou sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Haitou?
In August, at about 29.5 °C (85 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Haitou?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to November. The coolest month is February, at 21.3 °C (70 °F).
How warm is the sea in Haitou in February?
About 21.3 °C (70 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Haitou?
About 25.9 °C (79 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
