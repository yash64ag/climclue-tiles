Lianyungang in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lianyungang in August?
In August, Lianyungang averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 184 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Lianyungang?
August is not one of Lianyungang’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lianyungang in August?
184 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (253 mm), the driest December (15 mm).
Does it snow in Lianyungang in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lianyungang get in August?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lianyungang in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
