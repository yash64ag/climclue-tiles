How September compares in Lianyungang

Lianyungang in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lianyungang in September?
In September, Lianyungang averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 18.3 °C (65 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Lianyungang?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Lianyungang’s mild months (May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lianyungang in September?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (253 mm), the driest December (15 mm).
Does it snow in Lianyungang in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lianyungang get in September?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lianyungang in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
