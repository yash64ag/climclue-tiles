How December compares in Lianyungang

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Lianyungang. Compared with November: 6.3 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. January is 2.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Lianyungang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lianyungang in December?
In December, Lianyungang averages a day high of 7.3 °C (45 °F) and a night low of -1.7 °C (29 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Lianyungang?
December is not one of Lianyungang’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lianyungang in December?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (247 mm), the driest December (15 mm).
Does it snow in Lianyungang in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.3 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Lianyungang get in December?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Lianyungang in December?
About 9.2 °C (49 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (26.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Lianyungang in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
