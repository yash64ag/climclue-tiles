Lianyungang weather in January

Average January weather in Lianyungang, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Lianyungang averages 4.9 °C (41 °F) days and -3.7 °C (25 °F) nights, with 17 mm (0.7 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 166 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Lianyungang

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Lianyungang. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. February is 2.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Lianyungang in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lianyungang in January?
In January, Lianyungang averages a day high of 4.9 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -3.7 °C (25 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Lianyungang?
January is not one of Lianyungang’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lianyungang in January?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (247 mm), the driest December (15 mm).
Does it snow in Lianyungang in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.7 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Lianyungang get in January?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Lianyungang in January?
About 5.3 °C (42 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (26.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Lianyungang in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
