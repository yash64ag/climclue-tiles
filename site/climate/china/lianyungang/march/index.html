Lianyungang in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lianyungang in March?
In March, Lianyungang averages a day high of 12.3 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 3.0 °C (37 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Lianyungang?
March is not one of Lianyungang’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lianyungang in March?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (247 mm), the driest December (15 mm).
Does it snow in Lianyungang in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lianyungang get in March?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Lianyungang in March?
About 5.7 °C (42 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (26.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Lianyungang in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
