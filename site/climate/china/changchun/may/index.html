How May compares in Changchun

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Changchun. Compared with April: 7.3 °C warmer, 29 mm more precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. June is 5.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Changchun in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changchun in May?
In May, Changchun averages a day high of 20.9 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 9.5 °C (49 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 262 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Changchun?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Changchun’s mild months (May and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changchun in May?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (176 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Changchun in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Changchun get in May?
About 262 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Changchun in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
