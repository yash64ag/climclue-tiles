How August compares in Changchun

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Changchun. Compared with July: 1.3 °C cooler, 38 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. September is 6.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Changchun in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changchun in August?
In August, Changchun averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 18.1 °C (65 °F), with 138 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Changchun?
August is not one of Changchun’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changchun in August?
138 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (176 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Changchun in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Changchun get in August?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Changchun in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
