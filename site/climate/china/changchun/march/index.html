How March compares in Changchun

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Changchun. Compared with February: 8.5 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 48 hours more sunshine. April is 9.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Changchun in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changchun in March?
In March, Changchun averages a day high of 4.2 °C (40 °F) and a night low of -7.0 °C (19 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Changchun?
March is not one of Changchun’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changchun in March?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (176 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Changchun in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 15 cm (6.0 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Changchun get in March?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Changchun in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
