How September compares in Changchun

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Changchun. Compared with August: 6.1 °C cooler, 96 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. October is 8.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Changchun in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changchun in September?
In September, Changchun averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 10.6 °C (51 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Changchun?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Changchun’s mild months (May and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changchun in September?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (181 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Changchun in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Changchun get in September?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Changchun in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
