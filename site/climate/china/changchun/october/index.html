How October compares in Changchun

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Changchun. Compared with September: 8.3 °C cooler, 20 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. November is 10.9 °C cooler than October on average.

Changchun in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changchun in October?
In October, Changchun averages a day high of 13.5 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 2.4 °C (36 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Changchun?
October is not one of Changchun’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changchun in October?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (181 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Changchun in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Changchun get in October?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Changchun in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
