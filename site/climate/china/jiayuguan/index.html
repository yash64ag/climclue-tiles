Why Jiayuguan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,671 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Jiayuguan climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Jiayuguan?
July, with an average of 22.1 °C (72 °F), day highs around 28.2 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 16.2 °C (61 °F).
What is the coldest month in Jiayuguan?
January, with an average of -8.7 °C (16 °F), day highs around -3.4 °C (26 °F) and night lows around -13.0 °C (9 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Jiayuguan?
July, with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 109 mm (4.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Jiayuguan?
January, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Jiayuguan?
May, with about 293 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Jiayuguan?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 35 cm (13.9 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in March; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 2 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Jiayuguan?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July and August.
What type of climate does Jiayuguan have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.5 × 11 km, that contains Jiayuguan’s GeoNames location (39.811°N, 98.286°E; ClimaClue area sq-441-2782), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 231,853 (GeoNames).
