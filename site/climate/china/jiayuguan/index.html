Jiayuguan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Jiayuguan is the 3rd sunniest (about 3,055 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Jiuquan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Jiayuguan is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.1 °C), averages 30.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-8.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,671 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Jiayuguan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,671 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
