Hejiang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Hejiang is 133rd warmest (18.8 °C year average), 666th sunniest (about 1,279 hours of sunshine a year) and 293rd wettest (1,199 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Baisha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Hejiang is 0.2 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 59 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 19.9 °C more than its coolest, January (8.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.3 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 849 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hejiang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hejiang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 849 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
