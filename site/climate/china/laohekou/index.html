Laohekou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Laohekou is 309th warmest (16.5 °C year average), 515th sunniest (about 1,847 hours of sunshine a year) and 388th wettest (884 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dengzhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), Laohekou is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 24.5 °C more than its coolest, January (3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 757 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Laohekou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 757 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
