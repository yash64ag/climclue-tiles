Mentougou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Mentougou is 520th warmest (11.4 °C year average), 61st sunniest (about 2,713 hours of sunshine a year) and 486th wettest (629 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Beijing, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Mentougou is 1.9 °C cooler in July and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 61 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.4 °C), averages 30.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-5.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 186 km from the sea.

Why Mentougou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 186 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
