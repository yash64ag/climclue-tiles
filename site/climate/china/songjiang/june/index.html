How June compares in Songjiang

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Songjiang. Compared with May: 3.8 °C warmer, 104 mm more precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. July is 4.2 °C warmer than June on average.

Songjiang in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Songjiang in June?
In June, Songjiang averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.0 °C (70 °F), with 221 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Songjiang?
June is not one of Songjiang’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Songjiang in June?
221 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (221 mm), the driest December (45 mm).
Does it snow in Songjiang in June?

How many hours of sunshine does Songjiang get in June?
