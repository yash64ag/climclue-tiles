Songjiang weather in December

Average December weather in Songjiang, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Songjiang averages 10.9 °C (52 °F) days and 2.7 °C (37 °F) nights, with 45 mm (1.8 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 147 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Songjiang

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Songjiang. Compared with November: 6.1 °C cooler, 22 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. January is 2.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Songjiang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Songjiang in December?
In December, Songjiang averages a day high of 10.9 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 2.7 °C (37 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Songjiang?
December is not one of Songjiang’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Songjiang in December?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (221 mm), the driest December (45 mm).
Does it snow in Songjiang in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.0 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Songjiang get in December?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Songjiang in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
