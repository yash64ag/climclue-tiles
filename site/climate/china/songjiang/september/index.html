Songjiang weather in September

Average September weather in Songjiang, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Songjiang averages 27.4 °C (81 °F) days and 20.6 °C (69 °F) nights, with 134 mm (5.3 in) of rain over the month and about 164 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Songjiang

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Songjiang. Compared with August: 4.1 °C cooler, 87 mm less precipitation and 73 hours less sunshine. October is 5.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Songjiang in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Songjiang in September?
In September, Songjiang averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Songjiang?
September is not one of Songjiang’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Songjiang in September?
134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (221 mm), the driest December (45 mm).
Does it snow in Songjiang in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Songjiang get in September?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Songjiang in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
