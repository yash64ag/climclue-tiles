How May compares in Songjiang

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Songjiang. Compared with April: 5.3 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. June is 3.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Songjiang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Songjiang in May?
In May, Songjiang averages a day high of 24.6 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 16.3 °C (61 °F), with 118 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Songjiang?
May is not one of Songjiang’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Songjiang in May?
118 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (223 mm), the driest December (46 mm).
Does it snow in Songjiang in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Songjiang get in May?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Songjiang in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
