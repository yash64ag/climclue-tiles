How April compares in Songjiang

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Songjiang. Compared with March: 5.5 °C warmer, 11 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. May is 5.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Songjiang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Songjiang in April?
In April, Songjiang averages a day high of 19.7 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 11.0 °C (52 °F), with 102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Songjiang?
April is not one of Songjiang’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Songjiang in April?
102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (221 mm), the driest December (45 mm).
Does it snow in Songjiang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Songjiang get in April?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Songjiang in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
