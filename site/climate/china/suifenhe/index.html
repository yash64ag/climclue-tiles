Suifenhe at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Suifenhe is 674th warmest (3.6 °C year average), 252nd sunniest (about 2,358 hours of sunshine a year) and 544th wettest (554 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hengshan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (92 km away), Suifenhe is 2.1 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.7 °C), averages 35.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-16.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 145 km from the sea.

Why Suifenhe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 145 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Suifenhe itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (44.400°N, 131.148°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-395-3111), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
