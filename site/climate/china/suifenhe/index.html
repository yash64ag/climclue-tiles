Suifenhe at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Suifenhe is 675th warmest (3.0 °C year average), 252nd sunniest (about 2,358 hours of sunshine a year) and 488th wettest (626 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hengshan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (92 km away), Suifenhe is 2.3 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 66 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.1 °C), averages 35.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-16.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 145 km from the sea.

Why Suifenhe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 145 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
