Why Fangchenggang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

What is the coldest month in Fangchenggang?
January, with an average of 14.4 °C (58 °F), day highs around 17.4 °C (63 °F) and night lows around 11.4 °C (53 °F).

What is the driest month in Fangchenggang?

What is the sunniest month in Fangchenggang?

Does it snow in Fangchenggang?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Fangchenggang?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Fangchenggang’s GeoNames location (21.769°N, 108.357°E; ClimaClue area sq-622-2883), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 276,315 (GeoNames).
