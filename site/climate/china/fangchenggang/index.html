Fangchenggang at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Fangchenggang is the 4th wettest (2,726 mm a year). Compared with Qisha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Fangchenggang is about as warm in July and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 164 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.7 °C), averages 14.4 °C more than its coolest, January (14.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.8 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Fangchenggang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station. Temperatures on this page are then adjusted toward nearby NOAA stations, so they follow the measurements more closely.

Best time to visit Fangchenggang

For mild weather, the best months to visit Fangchenggang are February, March, November and December.

Why Fangchenggang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Fangchenggang itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.769°N, 108.357°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-622-2883), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
