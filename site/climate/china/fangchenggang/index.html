Fangchenggang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Fangchenggang is the 2nd wettest (2,715 mm a year). Compared with Qinzhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Fangchenggang is 0.3 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 295 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 14.2 °C more than its coolest, January (14.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.4 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Fangchenggang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Fangchenggang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
