How October compares in Wuzhong

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Wuzhong. Compared with September: 7.1 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. November is 8.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Wuzhong in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuzhong in October?
In October, Wuzhong averages a day high of 17.3 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 4.6 °C (40 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Wuzhong?
October is not one of Wuzhong’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuzhong in October?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (42 mm), the driest December (1 mm).
Does it snow in Wuzhong in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuzhong get in October?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuzhong in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
