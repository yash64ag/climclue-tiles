How March compares in Wuzhong

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Wuzhong. Compared with February: 6.8 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. April is 7.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Wuzhong in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuzhong in March?
In March, Wuzhong averages a day high of 12.2 °C (54 °F) and a night low of -2.0 °C (28 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Wuzhong?
March is not one of Wuzhong’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuzhong in March?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (42 mm), the driest December (1 mm).
Does it snow in Wuzhong in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.7 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuzhong get in March?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuzhong in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
