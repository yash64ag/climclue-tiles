How February compares in Wuzhong

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Wuzhong. Compared with January: 4.6 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. March is 6.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Wuzhong in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuzhong in February?
In February, Wuzhong averages a day high of 5.5 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -7.6 °C (18 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Wuzhong?
February is not one of Wuzhong’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuzhong in February?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (42 mm), the driest December (1 mm).
Does it snow in Wuzhong in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuzhong get in February?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuzhong in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
