Wuzhong at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Wuzhong is 526th warmest (11.3 °C year average), 44th sunniest (about 2,752 hours of sunshine a year) and 669th wettest (187 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yinchuan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (54 km away), Wuzhong is 1.6 °C warmer in July and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.6 °C), averages 31.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-5.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 997 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Wuzhong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 997 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 31.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
