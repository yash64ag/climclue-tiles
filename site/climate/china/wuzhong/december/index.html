How December compares in Wuzhong

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Wuzhong. Compared with November: 6.7 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Wuzhong in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuzhong in December?
In December, Wuzhong averages a day high of 2.4 °C (36 °F) and a night low of -9.6 °C (15 °F), with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Wuzhong?
December is not one of Wuzhong’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuzhong in December?
1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (42 mm), the driest December (1 mm).
Does it snow in Wuzhong in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.0 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuzhong get in December?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuzhong in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
