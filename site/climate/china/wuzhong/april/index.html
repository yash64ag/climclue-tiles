How April compares in Wuzhong

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Wuzhong. Compared with March: 7.4 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. May is 5.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Wuzhong in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuzhong in April?
In April, Wuzhong averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 4.1 °C (39 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Wuzhong?
April is not one of Wuzhong’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuzhong in April?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (42 mm), the driest December (1 mm).
Does it snow in Wuzhong in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.2 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuzhong get in April?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuzhong in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
