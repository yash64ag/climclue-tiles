Naliang at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Naliang is 136th warmest (22.4 °C year average), 1461st sunniest (about 1,727 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (2,617 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dongxing, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Naliang is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 491 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.3 °C), averages 14.3 °C more than its coolest, January (14.0 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.3 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 38 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Naliang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Naliang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Naliang itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.667°N, 107.867°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-623-2878), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
