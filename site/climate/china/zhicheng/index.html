Zhicheng at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Zhicheng is 231st warmest (17.4 °C year average), 590th sunniest (about 1,631 hours of sunshine a year) and 233rd wettest (1,352 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zhijiang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Zhicheng is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 72 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 23.6 °C more than its coolest, January (5.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 881 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Zhicheng, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Zhicheng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 881 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
