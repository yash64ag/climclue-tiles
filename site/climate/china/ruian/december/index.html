How December compares in Rui’an

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Rui’an. Compared with November: 5.4 °C cooler, 28 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. January is 2.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Rui’an in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rui’an in December?
In December, Rui’an averages a day high of 13.7 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 6.7 °C (44 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 141 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Rui’an?
December is not one of Rui’an’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rui’an in December?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (223 mm), the driest December (45 mm).
Does it snow in Rui’an in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rui’an get in December?
About 141 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rui’an in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
