Rui’an in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rui’an in August?
In August, Rui’an averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 25.4 °C (78 °F), with 214 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Rui’an?
August is not one of Rui’an’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rui’an in August?
214 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (227 mm), the driest December (47 mm).
Does it snow in Rui’an in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rui’an get in August?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Rui’an in August?
About 28.0 °C (82 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Rui’an in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
