How January compares in Rui’an

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Rui’an. Compared with December: 2.6 °C cooler, 17 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. February is 1.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Rui’an in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rui’an in January?
In January, Rui’an averages a day high of 12.5 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 6.0 °C (43 °F), with 64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 122 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Rui’an?
January is not one of Rui’an’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rui’an in January?
64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (227 mm), the driest December (47 mm).
Does it snow in Rui’an in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rui’an get in January?
About 122 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Rui’an in January?
About 12.4 °C (54 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (28.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Rui’an in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
