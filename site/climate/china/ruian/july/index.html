Rui’an in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rui’an in July?
In July, Rui’an averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 25.8 °C (78 °F), with 157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Rui’an?
July is not one of Rui’an’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rui’an in July?
157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (223 mm), the driest December (45 mm).
Does it snow in Rui’an in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rui’an get in July?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rui’an in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
