Gejiu at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Gejiu is 402nd warmest (14.8 °C year average), 552nd sunniest (about 1,775 hours of sunshine a year) and 257th wettest (1,299 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mengzi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Gejiu is 4.3 °C cooler in June and 4.1 °C cooler in January, with 326 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (19.3 °C), averages 10.7 °C more than its coolest, January (8.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, December, averages 3.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 484 km from the sea.

Why Gejiu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 484 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
