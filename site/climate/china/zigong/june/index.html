How June compares in Zigong

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Zigong. Compared with May: 2.2 °C warmer, 71 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. July is 2.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Zigong in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zigong in June?
In June, Zigong averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 190 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Zigong?
June is not one of Zigong’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zigong in June?
190 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (230 mm), the driest January (17 mm).
Does it snow in Zigong in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zigong get in June?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zigong in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
