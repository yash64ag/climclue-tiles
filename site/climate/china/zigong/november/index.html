How November compares in Zigong

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Zigong. Compared with October: 4.3 °C cooler, 30 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. December is 4.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Zigong in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zigong in November?
In November, Zigong averages a day high of 17.0 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 73 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Zigong?
November is not one of Zigong’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zigong in November?
32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (224 mm), the driest January (17 mm).
Does it snow in Zigong in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zigong get in November?
About 73 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zigong in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
