How September compares in Zigong

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Zigong. Compared with August: 3.7 °C cooler, 67 mm less precipitation and 84 hours less sunshine. October is 4.8 °C cooler than September on average.

Zigong in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zigong in September?
In September, Zigong averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 20.5 °C (69 °F), with 123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 104 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Zigong?
September is not one of Zigong’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zigong in September?
123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (224 mm), the driest January (17 mm).
Does it snow in Zigong in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zigong get in September?
About 104 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zigong in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
