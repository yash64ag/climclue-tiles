How May compares in Zigong

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Zigong. Compared with April: 4.1 °C warmer, 49 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. June is 2.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Zigong in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zigong in May?
In May, Zigong averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 119 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Zigong?
May is not one of Zigong’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zigong in May?
119 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (230 mm), the driest January (17 mm).
Does it snow in Zigong in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zigong get in May?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zigong in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
