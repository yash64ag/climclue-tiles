How August compares in Zigong

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Zigong. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, 35 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. September is 3.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Zigong in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zigong in August?
In August, Zigong averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 195 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Zigong?
August is not one of Zigong’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zigong in August?
195 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (230 mm), the driest January (17 mm).
Does it snow in Zigong in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zigong get in August?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zigong in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
