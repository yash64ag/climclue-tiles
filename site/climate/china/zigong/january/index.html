How January compares in Zigong

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Zigong. Compared with December: 1.3 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 2.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Zigong in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zigong in January?
In January, Zigong averages a day high of 10.9 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 4.5 °C (40 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 76 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Zigong?
January is not one of Zigong’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zigong in January?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (230 mm), the driest January (17 mm).
Does it snow in Zigong in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zigong get in January?
About 76 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zigong in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
