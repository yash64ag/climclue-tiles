How December compares in Zigong

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the least sunny month in Zigong. Compared with November: 4.9 °C cooler, 15 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. January is 1.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Zigong in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zigong in December?
In December, Zigong averages a day high of 11.9 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 6.1 °C (43 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 69 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Zigong?
December is not one of Zigong’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zigong in December?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (230 mm), the driest January (17 mm).
Does it snow in Zigong in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.5 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Zigong get in December?
About 69 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zigong in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
