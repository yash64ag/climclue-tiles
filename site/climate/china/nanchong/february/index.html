Nanchong in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanchong in February?
In February, Nanchong averages a day high of 12.4 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 5.1 °C (41 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 57 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Nanchong?
February is not one of Nanchong’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanchong in February?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (217 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Nanchong in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanchong get in February?
About 57 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanchong in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
