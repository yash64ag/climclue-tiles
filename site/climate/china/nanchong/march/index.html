Nanchong in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanchong in March?
In March, Nanchong averages a day high of 16.4 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 8.2 °C (47 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 94 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Nanchong?
March is not one of Nanchong’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanchong in March?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (217 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Nanchong in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanchong get in March?
About 94 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanchong in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
