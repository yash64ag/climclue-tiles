How December compares in Nanchong

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Nanchong. Compared with November: 5.0 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. January is 1.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Nanchong in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanchong in December?
In December, Nanchong averages a day high of 11.3 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 6.5 °C (44 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 53 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Nanchong?
December is not one of Nanchong’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanchong in December?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (212 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Nanchong in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanchong get in December?
About 53 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanchong in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
