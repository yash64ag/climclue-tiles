Nanchong in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanchong in August?
In August, Nanchong averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 175 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Nanchong?
August is not one of Nanchong’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanchong in August?
175 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (217 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Nanchong in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanchong get in August?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanchong in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
