Nanchong in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanchong in July?
In July, Nanchong averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 212 mm (8.3 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Nanchong?
July is not one of Nanchong’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanchong in July?
212 mm (8.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (212 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Nanchong in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanchong get in July?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanchong in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
