Nanchong at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Nanchong is 184th warmest (18.1 °C year average), 662nd sunniest (about 1,292 hours of sunshine a year) and 320th wettest (1,139 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gaoping, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Nanchong is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 55 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 20.4 °C more than its coolest, January (7.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 1,044 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Nanchong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,044 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
