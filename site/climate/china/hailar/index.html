Hailar at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Hailar is the 5th coolest (-0.2 °C year average). Compared with Yakeshi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (76 km away), Hailar is 0.9 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 68 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.3 °C), averages 45.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-24.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 941 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Hailar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 941 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 45.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
