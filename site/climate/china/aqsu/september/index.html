How September compares in Aqsu

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Aqsu. Compared with August: 4.4 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. October is 7.0 °C cooler than September on average.

Aqsu in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aqsu in September?
In September, Aqsu averages a day high of 24.2 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Aqsu?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Aqsu’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aqsu in September?
9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (16 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Aqsu in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aqsu get in September?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aqsu in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
