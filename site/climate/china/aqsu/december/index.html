How December compares in Aqsu

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Aqsu. Compared with November: 7.0 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. January is 2.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Aqsu in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aqsu in December?
In December, Aqsu averages a day high of -0.2 °C (32 °F) and a night low of -8.5 °C (17 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Aqsu?
December is not one of Aqsu’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aqsu in December?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (16 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Aqsu in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.4 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Aqsu get in December?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aqsu in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
