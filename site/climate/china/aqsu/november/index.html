How November compares in Aqsu

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Aqsu. Compared with October: 8.7 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 51 hours less sunshine. December is 7.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Aqsu in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aqsu in November?
In November, Aqsu averages a day high of 7.5 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -2.4 °C (28 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Aqsu?
November is not one of Aqsu’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aqsu in November?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (16 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Aqsu in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.7 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Aqsu get in November?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aqsu in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
