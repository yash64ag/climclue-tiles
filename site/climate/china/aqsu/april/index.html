How April compares in Aqsu

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Aqsu. Compared with March: 8.2 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. May is 5.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Aqsu in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aqsu in April?
In April, Aqsu averages a day high of 20.2 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 4.4 °C (40 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Aqsu?
April is not one of Aqsu’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aqsu in April?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (16 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Aqsu in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aqsu get in April?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aqsu in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
