How February compares in Aqsu

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Aqsu. Compared with January: 4.9 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. March is 7.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Aqsu in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aqsu in February?
In February, Aqsu averages a day high of 3.9 °C (39 °F) and a night low of -7.6 °C (18 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Aqsu?
February is not one of Aqsu’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aqsu in February?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (16 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Aqsu in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.6 cm (2.2 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Aqsu get in February?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aqsu in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
