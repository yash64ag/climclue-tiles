How October compares in Aqsu

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Aqsu. Compared with September: 7.0 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. November is 8.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Aqsu in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aqsu in October?
In October, Aqsu averages a day high of 17.0 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 4.6 °C (40 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Aqsu?
October is not one of Aqsu’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aqsu in October?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (16 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Aqsu in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.7 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Aqsu get in October?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aqsu in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
