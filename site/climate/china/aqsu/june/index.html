Aqsu in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aqsu in June?
In June, Aqsu averages a day high of 29.2 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 14.7 °C (58 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 283 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Aqsu?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Aqsu’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aqsu in June?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (17 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Aqsu in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aqsu get in June?
About 283 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aqsu in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
