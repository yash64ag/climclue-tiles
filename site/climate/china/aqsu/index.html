Why Aqsu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 2,139 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Aqsu climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Aqsu?
July, with an average of 22.9 °C (73 °F), day highs around 29.7 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 16.2 °C (61 °F).
What is the coldest month in Aqsu?
January, with an average of -7.5 °C (18 °F), day highs around -1.3 °C (30 °F) and night lows around -11.5 °C (11 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Aqsu?
July, with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 83 mm (3.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Aqsu?
November, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Aqsu?
July, with about 296 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Aqsu?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 18 cm (7.0 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 2 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Aqsu?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Aqsu have?
Cold desert (Köppen BWk). Very dry, with cold winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.4 × 11 km, that contains Aqsu’s GeoNames location (41.184°N, 80.279°E; ClimaClue area sq-428-2602), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 535,657 (GeoNames).
