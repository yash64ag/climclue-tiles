How May compares in Aqsu

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Aqsu. Compared with April: 5.6 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. June is 3.1 °C warmer than May on average.

Aqsu in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aqsu in May?
In May, Aqsu averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 10.4 °C (51 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Aqsu?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Aqsu’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aqsu in May?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (16 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Aqsu in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Aqsu get in May?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aqsu in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
