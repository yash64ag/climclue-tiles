Aqsu weather in January

Average January weather in Aqsu, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Aqsu averages -0.7 °C (31 °F) days and -14.4 °C (6 °F) nights, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 158 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Aqsu

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Aqsu. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 4.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Aqsu in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Aqsu in January?
In January, Aqsu averages a day high of -0.7 °C (31 °F) and a night low of -14.4 °C (6 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Aqsu?
January is not one of Aqsu’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Aqsu in January?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (17 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Aqsu in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Aqsu get in January?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Aqsu in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
