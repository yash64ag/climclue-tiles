How August compares in Qingdao

August is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Qingdao. Compared with July: 1.1 °C warmer, 4 mm less precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. September is 3.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Qingdao in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qingdao in August?
In August, Qingdao averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 166 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Qingdao?
August is not one of Qingdao’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qingdao in August?
166 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (170 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Qingdao in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qingdao get in August?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Qingdao in August?
About 25.6 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (25.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Qingdao in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
