Qingdao weather in June

Average June weather in Qingdao, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Qingdao averages 23.4 °C (74 °F) days and 16.7 °C (62 °F) nights, with 78 mm (3.1 in) of rain over the month and about 228 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Qingdao

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Qingdao. Compared with May: 4.7 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. July is 4.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Qingdao in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qingdao in June?
In June, Qingdao averages a day high of 23.4 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 16.7 °C (62 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Qingdao?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Qingdao’s mild months (May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qingdao in June?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (170 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Qingdao in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qingdao get in June?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Qingdao in June?
About 18.4 °C (65 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (25.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Qingdao in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
