Why Qingdao has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Qingdao climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Qingdao?
August, with an average of 25.4 °C (78 °F), day highs around 26.8 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 23.8 °C (75 °F).
What is the coldest month in Qingdao?
January, with an average of 0.8 °C (33 °F), day highs around 3.1 °C (38 °F) and night lows around -1.8 °C (29 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Qingdao?
July, with 171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 726 mm (28.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Qingdao?
January, with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Qingdao?
May, with about 249 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Qingdao?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 15 cm (5.8 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Qingdao?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, September and October.
What type of climate does Qingdao have?
Humid subtropical, dry winter (Köppen Cwa). Hot, wet summers and mild, dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.0 × 11 km, that contains Qingdao’s GeoNames location (36.065°N, 120.380°E; ClimaClue area sq-479-3003), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 7,172,451 (GeoNames).
