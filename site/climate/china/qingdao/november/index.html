How November compares in Qingdao

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Qingdao. Compared with October: 7.3 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. December is 6.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Qingdao in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qingdao in November?
In November, Qingdao averages a day high of 13.1 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 6.4 °C (44 °F), with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Qingdao?
November is not one of Qingdao’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qingdao in November?
32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (170 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Qingdao in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qingdao get in November?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Qingdao in November?
About 15.3 °C (60 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Qingdao in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
