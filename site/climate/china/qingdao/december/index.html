How December compares in Qingdao

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Qingdao. Compared with November: 6.4 °C cooler, 18 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. January is 2.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Qingdao in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qingdao in December?
In December, Qingdao averages a day high of 5.8 °C (42 °F) and a night low of 1.1 °C (34 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Qingdao?
December is not one of Qingdao’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qingdao in December?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (171 mm), the driest January (11 mm).
Does it snow in Qingdao in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.2 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Qingdao get in December?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qingdao in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
