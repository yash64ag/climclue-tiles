Why Suicheng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 163 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Suicheng climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Suicheng?
July, with an average of 27.4 °C (81 °F), day highs around 31.0 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 24.0 °C (75 °F).
What is the coldest month in Suicheng?
January, with an average of 0.8 °C (34 °F), day highs around 6.0 °C (43 °F) and night lows around -3.8 °C (25 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Suicheng?
July, with 263 mm (10.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 953 mm (37.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Suicheng?
December, with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Suicheng?
May, with about 223 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Suicheng?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 22 cm (8.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Suicheng?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, September and October.
What type of climate does Suicheng have?
Humid subtropical, dry winter (Köppen Cwa). Hot, wet summers and mild, dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.2 × 11 km, that contains Suicheng’s GeoNames location (33.896°N, 117.933°E; ClimaClue area sq-501-2979), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 256,665 (GeoNames).
