How August compares in Chengde

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Chengde. Compared with July: 1.3 °C cooler, 28 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. September is 5.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Chengde in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chengde in August?
In August, Chengde averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 18.1 °C (65 °F), with 102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Chengde?
August is not one of Chengde’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chengde in August?
102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (131 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Chengde in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chengde get in August?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chengde in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
