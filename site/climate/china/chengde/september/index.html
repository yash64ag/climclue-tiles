How September compares in Chengde

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Chengde. Compared with August: 5.5 °C cooler, 52 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. October is 7.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Chengde in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chengde in September?
In September, Chengde averages a day high of 25.2 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 11.7 °C (53 °F), with 47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 242 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Chengde?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Chengde’s mild months (May, June, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chengde in September?
47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (127 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Chengde in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chengde get in September?
About 242 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chengde in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
