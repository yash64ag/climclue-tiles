How February compares in Chengde

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Chengde. Compared with January: 4.5 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. March is 6.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Chengde in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chengde in February?
In February, Chengde averages a day high of 2.3 °C (36 °F) and a night low of -9.9 °C (14 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Chengde?
February is not one of Chengde’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chengde in February?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (131 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Chengde in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.4 cm (2.1 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Chengde get in February?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chengde in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
