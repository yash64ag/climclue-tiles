How November compares in Chengde

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Chengde. Compared with October: 9.4 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. December is 7.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Chengde in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chengde in November?
In November, Chengde averages a day high of 7.2 °C (45 °F) and a night low of -5.2 °C (23 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Chengde?
November is not one of Chengde’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chengde in November?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (127 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Chengde in November?
Yes, on average: about 3.3 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in November (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Chengde get in November?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chengde in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
