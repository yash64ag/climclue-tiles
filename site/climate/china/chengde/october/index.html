Chengde weather in October

Average October weather in Chengde, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Chengde averages 17.0 °C (63 °F) days and 3.8 °C (39 °F) nights, with 24 mm (0.9 in) of rain over the month and about 225 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Chengde

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Chengde. Compared with September: 7.7 °C cooler, 23 mm less precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. November is 9.2 °C cooler than October on average.

Chengde in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chengde in October?
In October, Chengde averages a day high of 17.0 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 3.8 °C (39 °F), with 24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Chengde?
October is not one of Chengde’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chengde in October?
24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (127 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Chengde in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chengde get in October?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chengde in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
