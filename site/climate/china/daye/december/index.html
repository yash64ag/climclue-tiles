How December compares in Daye

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Daye. Compared with November: 5.7 °C cooler, 32 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. January is 2.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Daye in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daye in December?
In December, Daye averages a day high of 11.6 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 2.2 °C (36 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Daye?
December is not one of Daye’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daye in December?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (257 mm), the driest December (39 mm).
Does it snow in Daye in December?
Yes, on average: about 2.5 cm (1.0 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Daye get in December?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daye in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
