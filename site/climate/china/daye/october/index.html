How October compares in Daye

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Daye. Compared with September: 5.7 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. November is 6.2 °C cooler than October on average.

Daye in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daye in October?
In October, Daye averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Daye?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Daye’s mild months (September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daye in October?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (257 mm), the driest December (39 mm).
Does it snow in Daye in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Daye get in October?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daye in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
