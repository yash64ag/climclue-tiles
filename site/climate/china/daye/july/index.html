Daye in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daye in July?
In July, Daye averages a day high of 33.7 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 233 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Daye?
July is not one of Daye’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daye in July?
233 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (257 mm), the driest December (39 mm).
Does it snow in Daye in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Daye get in July?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daye in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
