How September compares in Daye

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Daye. Compared with August: 4.2 °C cooler, 53 mm less precipitation and 72 hours less sunshine. October is 5.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Daye in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daye in September?
In September, Daye averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Daye?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Daye’s mild months (September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daye in September?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (257 mm), the driest December (39 mm).
Does it snow in Daye in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Daye get in September?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daye in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
