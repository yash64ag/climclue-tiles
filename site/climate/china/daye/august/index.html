How August compares in Daye

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Daye. Compared with July: 0.8 °C cooler, 94 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. September is 4.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Daye in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daye in August?
In August, Daye averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 156 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Daye?
August is not one of Daye’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daye in August?
156 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (277 mm), the driest December (42 mm).
Does it snow in Daye in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Daye get in August?
About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daye in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
