How November compares in Daye

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Daye. Compared with October: 6.2 °C cooler, 21 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. December is 5.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Daye in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daye in November?
In November, Daye averages a day high of 16.4 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Daye?
November is not one of Daye’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daye in November?
77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (277 mm), the driest December (42 mm).
Does it snow in Daye in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Daye get in November?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daye in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
