Guli in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guli in October?
In October, Guli averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 14.9 °C (59 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 160 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Guli?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Guli’s mild months (October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guli in October?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (263 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Guli in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guli get in October?
About 160 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guli in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
