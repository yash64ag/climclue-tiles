Guli at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Guli is 186th warmest (18.0 °C year average), 513th sunniest (about 1,849 hours of sunshine a year) and 137th wettest (1,585 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jinhua, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Guli is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 35 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.6 °C), averages 23.6 °C more than its coolest, January (6.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 132 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Guli, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Guli has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 132 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
