Guli in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guli in June?
In June, Guli averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 249 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Guli?
June is not one of Guli’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guli in June?
249 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (249 mm), the driest December (46 mm).
Does it snow in Guli in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guli get in June?
About 145 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guli in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
