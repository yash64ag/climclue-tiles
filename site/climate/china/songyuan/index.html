Why Songyuan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 547 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 39.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Songyuan climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Songyuan?
July, with an average of 23.8 °C (75 °F), day highs around 27.7 °C (82 °F) and night lows around 19.5 °C (67 °F).
What is the coldest month in Songyuan?
January, with an average of -15.8 °C (4 °F), day highs around -11.0 °C (12 °F) and night lows around -20.8 °C (-5 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Songyuan?
July, with 133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 442 mm (17.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Songyuan?
January, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Songyuan?
May, with about 267 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Songyuan?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 43 cm (16.9 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in March; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 5 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Songyuan?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, August and September.

About these numbers
