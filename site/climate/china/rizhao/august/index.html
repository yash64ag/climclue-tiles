How August compares in Rizhao

August is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Rizhao. Compared with July: 0.5 °C warmer, 35 mm less precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. September is 3.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Rizhao in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rizhao in August?
In August, Rizhao averages a day high of 27.3 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 23.7 °C (75 °F), with 182 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Rizhao?
August is not one of Rizhao’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rizhao in August?
182 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (217 mm), the driest December (13 mm).
Does it snow in Rizhao in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rizhao get in August?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rizhao in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
