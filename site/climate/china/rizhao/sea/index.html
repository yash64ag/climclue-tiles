Rizhao sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Shandong · China

The sea off Rizhao averages 26.0 °C (79 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 4.3 °C (40 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September.

Rizhao sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Rizhao?
In August, at about 26.0 °C (79 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Rizhao?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August to September. The coolest month is February, at 4.3 °C (40 °F).
How warm is the sea in Rizhao in February?
About 4.3 °C (40 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Rizhao?
About 14.8 °C (59 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Rizhao nearby?
In August: Xiaying (27.0 °C, 181 km) and Laizhou (27.0 °C, 200 km).
