Rizhao at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Rizhao is 459th warmest (13.6 °C year average), 244th sunniest (about 2,399 hours of sunshine a year) and 399th wettest (811 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zhu Cheng City, the nearest covered town with different numbers (66 km away), Rizhao is 0.3 °C warmer in August and 2.3 °C warmer in January, with 109 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.6 °C), averages 24.7 °C more than its coolest, January (0.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Rizhao, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Rizhao has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Rizhao itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.414°N, 119.529°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-485-2995), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
