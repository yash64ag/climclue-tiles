How September compares in Rizhao

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Rizhao. Compared with August: 3.3 °C cooler, 103 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. October is 5.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Rizhao in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rizhao in September?
In September, Rizhao averages a day high of 24.3 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 19.9 °C (68 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Rizhao?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Rizhao’s mild months (May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rizhao in September?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (217 mm), the driest December (13 mm).
Does it snow in Rizhao in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rizhao get in September?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rizhao in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
