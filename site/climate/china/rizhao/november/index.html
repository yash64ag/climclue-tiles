How November compares in Rizhao

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Rizhao. Compared with October: 7.1 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. December is 6.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Rizhao in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rizhao in November?
In November, Rizhao averages a day high of 12.4 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 6.9 °C (44 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Rizhao?
November is not one of Rizhao’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rizhao in November?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (217 mm), the driest December (13 mm).
Does it snow in Rizhao in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.5 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Rizhao get in November?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Rizhao in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
