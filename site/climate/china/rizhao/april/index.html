How April compares in Rizhao

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Rizhao. Compared with March: 5.4 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. May is 5.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Rizhao in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Rizhao in April?
In April, Rizhao averages a day high of 15.3 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 7.5 °C (46 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 213 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Rizhao?
April is not one of Rizhao’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Rizhao in April?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (211 mm), the driest December (12 mm).
Does it snow in Rizhao in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Rizhao get in April?
About 213 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Rizhao in April?
About 8.6 °C (47 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (26.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Rizhao in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
