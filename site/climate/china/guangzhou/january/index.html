Guangzhou weather in January

Average January weather in Guangzhou, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Guangzhou averages 18.8 °C (66 °F) days and 10.3 °C (51 °F) nights, with 51 mm (2.0 in) of rain over the month and about 131 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Guangzhou

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Guangzhou. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, 18 mm more precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. February is 1.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Guangzhou in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guangzhou in January?
In January, Guangzhou averages a day high of 18.8 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 10.3 °C (51 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 131 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Guangzhou?
January is not one of Guangzhou’s mild months; those are February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guangzhou in January?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (362 mm), the driest December (33 mm).
Does it snow in Guangzhou in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guangzhou get in January?
About 131 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guangzhou in January?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
