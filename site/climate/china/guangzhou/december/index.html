How December compares in Guangzhou

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Guangzhou. Compared with November: 4.5 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. January is 1.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Guangzhou in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guangzhou in December?
In December, Guangzhou averages a day high of 20.7 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 11.8 °C (53 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Guangzhou?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Guangzhou’s mild months (February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guangzhou in December?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (362 mm), the driest December (33 mm).
Does it snow in Guangzhou in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guangzhou get in December?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guangzhou in December?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
