How March compares in Guangzhou

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Guangzhou. Compared with February: 3.1 °C warmer, 32 mm more precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. April is 4.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Guangzhou in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guangzhou in March?
In March, Guangzhou averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 15.3 °C (60 °F), with 116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 77 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Guangzhou?
March is not one of Guangzhou’s mild months; those are February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guangzhou in March?
116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (373 mm), the driest December (34 mm).
Does it snow in Guangzhou in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guangzhou get in March?
About 77 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guangzhou in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
