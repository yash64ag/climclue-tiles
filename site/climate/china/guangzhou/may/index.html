How May compares in Guangzhou

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Guangzhou. Compared with April: 3.2 °C warmer, 100 mm more precipitation and 49 hours more sunshine. June is 2.0 °C warmer than May on average.

Guangzhou in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guangzhou in May?
In May, Guangzhou averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 314 mm (12.3 in) of precipitation and about 137 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Guangzhou?
May is not one of Guangzhou’s mild months; those are February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guangzhou in May?
314 mm (12.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (362 mm), the driest December (33 mm).
Does it snow in Guangzhou in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guangzhou get in May?
About 137 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guangzhou in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
