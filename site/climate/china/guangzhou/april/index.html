How April compares in Guangzhou

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Guangzhou. Compared with March: 4.3 °C warmer, 101 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. May is 3.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Guangzhou in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guangzhou in April?
In April, Guangzhou averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation and about 88 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Guangzhou?
April is not one of Guangzhou’s mild months; those are February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guangzhou in April?
213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (362 mm), the driest December (33 mm).
Does it snow in Guangzhou in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guangzhou get in April?
About 88 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guangzhou in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
