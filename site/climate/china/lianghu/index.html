Lianghu at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Lianghu is 230th warmest (17.4 °C year average), 500th sunniest (about 1,866 hours of sunshine a year) and 164th wettest (1,519 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shangyu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Lianghu is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 69 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.6 °C), averages 24.6 °C more than its coolest, January (5.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 48 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lianghu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lianghu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 48 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
