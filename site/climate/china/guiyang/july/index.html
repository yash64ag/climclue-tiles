How July compares in Guiyang

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Guiyang. Compared with June: 1.7 °C warmer, 14 mm less precipitation and 59 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Guiyang in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guiyang in July?
In July, Guiyang averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation and about 183 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Guiyang?
July is not one of Guiyang’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guiyang in July?
171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (185 mm), the driest December (19 mm).
Does it snow in Guiyang in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guiyang get in July?
About 183 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guiyang in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
