Guiyang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Guiyang is 353rd warmest (15.6 °C year average), 656th sunniest (about 1,307 hours of sunshine a year) and 342nd wettest (1,065 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Anshun, the nearest covered town with different numbers (86 km away), Guiyang is 1.7 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 374 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.1 °C), averages 18.7 °C more than its coolest, January (5.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 578 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 99% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Guiyang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 578 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
