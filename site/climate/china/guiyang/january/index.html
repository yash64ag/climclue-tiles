How January compares in Guiyang

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Guiyang. Compared with December: 2.1 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. February is 2.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Guiyang in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guiyang in January?
In January, Guiyang averages a day high of 8.6 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 2.5 °C (37 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 52 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Guiyang?
January is not one of Guiyang’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guiyang in January?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (185 mm), the driest December (19 mm).
Does it snow in Guiyang in January?
Yes, on average: about 2.9 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Guiyang get in January?
About 52 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guiyang in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
