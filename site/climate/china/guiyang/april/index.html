How April compares in Guiyang

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Guiyang. Compared with March: 4.8 °C warmer, 48 mm more precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. May is 3.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Guiyang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guiyang in April?
In April, Guiyang averages a day high of 19.2 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 121 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Guiyang?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Guiyang’s mild months (April, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guiyang in April?
86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (195 mm), the driest December (20 mm).
Does it snow in Guiyang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guiyang get in April?
About 121 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guiyang in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
