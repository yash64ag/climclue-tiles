Liuzhi at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Liuzhi is 405th warmest (14.7 °C year average), 611th sunniest (about 1,534 hours of sunshine a year) and 201st wettest (1,443 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Anshun, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), Liuzhi is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.1 °C), averages 16.7 °C more than its coolest, January (5.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 599 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Liuzhi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Liuzhi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 599 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
