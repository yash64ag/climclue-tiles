Sanhe, Hebei in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sanhe, Hebei in June?
In June, Sanhe, Hebei averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 95 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 272 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Sanhe, Hebei?
June is not one of Sanhe, Hebei’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
