Sanhe, Hebei in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sanhe, Hebei in March?
In March, Sanhe, Hebei averages a day high of 12.4 °C (54 °F) and a night low of -1.1 °C (30 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Sanhe, Hebei?
March is not one of Sanhe, Hebei’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (187 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Sanhe, Hebei in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sanhe, Hebei get in March?
About 232 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
