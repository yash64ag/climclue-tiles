How June compares in Shangrao

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Shangrao. Compared with May: 3.1 °C warmer, 112 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. July is 3.5 °C warmer than June on average.

Shangrao in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shangrao in June?
In June, Shangrao averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 384 mm (15.1 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Shangrao?
June is not one of Shangrao’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shangrao in June?
384 mm (15.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (384 mm), the driest December (63 mm).
Does it snow in Shangrao in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shangrao get in June?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shangrao in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
