How September compares in Shangrao

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Shangrao. Compared with August: 3.7 °C cooler, 39 mm less precipitation and 67 hours less sunshine. October is 5.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Shangrao in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shangrao in September?
In September, Shangrao averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 98 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Shangrao?
September is not one of Shangrao’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shangrao in September?
98 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (384 mm), the driest December (63 mm).
Does it snow in Shangrao in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shangrao get in September?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shangrao in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
