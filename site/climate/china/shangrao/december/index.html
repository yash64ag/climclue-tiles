How December compares in Shangrao

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Shangrao. Compared with November: 5.8 °C cooler, 34 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. January is 1.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Shangrao in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shangrao in December?
In December, Shangrao averages a day high of 13.3 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 3.3 °C (38 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Shangrao?
December is not one of Shangrao’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shangrao in December?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (384 mm), the driest December (63 mm).
Does it snow in Shangrao in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.6 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Shangrao get in December?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shangrao in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
