Shangrao weather in February

Average February weather in Shangrao, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Shangrao averages 13.1 °C (56 °F) days and 5.0 °C (41 °F) nights, with 133 mm (5.2 in) of rain over the month and about 90 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Shangrao

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Shangrao. Compared with January: 2.2 °C warmer, 28 mm more precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. March is 3.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Shangrao in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shangrao in February?
In February, Shangrao averages a day high of 13.1 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 5.0 °C (41 °F), with 133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 90 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Shangrao?
February is not one of Shangrao’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shangrao in February?
133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (380 mm), the driest December (63 mm).
Does it snow in Shangrao in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shangrao get in February?
About 90 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shangrao in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
