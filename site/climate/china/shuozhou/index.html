Shuozhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Shuozhou is 595th warmest (8.0 °C year average), 41st sunniest (about 2,761 hours of sunshine a year) and 641st wettest (353 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yuanping, the nearest covered town with different numbers (73 km away), Shuozhou is 1.4 °C cooler in July and 2.0 °C cooler in January, with 34 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.4 °C), averages 31.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-8.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 454 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shuozhou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shuozhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 454 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 31.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
