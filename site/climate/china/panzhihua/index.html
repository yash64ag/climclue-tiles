Panzhihua at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Panzhihua is 103rd warmest (20.4 °C year average), 268th sunniest (about 2,287 hours of sunshine a year) and 411th wettest (784 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lijiang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (152 km away), Panzhihua is 6.9 °C warmer in June and 6.2 °C warmer in December, with 241 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (25.3 °C), averages 12.4 °C more than its coolest, December (12.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.2 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 837 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Panzhihua, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Panzhihua has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 837 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
