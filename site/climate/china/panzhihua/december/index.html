How December compares in Panzhihua

December is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Panzhihua. Compared with November: 3.1 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. January is 0.5 °C warmer than December on average.

Panzhihua in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Panzhihua in December?
In December, Panzhihua averages a day high of 17.4 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 6.6 °C (44 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Panzhihua?
December is not one of Panzhihua’s mild months; those are February, March, April, May, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Panzhihua in December?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (198 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Panzhihua in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Panzhihua get in December?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Panzhihua in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
