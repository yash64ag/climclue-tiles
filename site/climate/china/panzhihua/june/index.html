How June compares in Panzhihua

June is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Panzhihua. Compared with May: 0.3 °C warmer, 90 mm more precipitation and 75 hours less sunshine. July is 0.2 °C cooler than June on average.

Panzhihua in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Panzhihua in June?
In June, Panzhihua averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 20.1 °C (68 °F), with 143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Panzhihua?
June is not one of Panzhihua’s mild months; those are February, March, April, May, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Panzhihua in June?
143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (198 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Panzhihua in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Panzhihua get in June?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Panzhihua in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
