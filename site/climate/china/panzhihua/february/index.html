How February compares in Panzhihua

February is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Panzhihua. Compared with January: 2.9 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. March is 3.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Panzhihua in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Panzhihua in February?
In February, Panzhihua averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 9.8 °C (50 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Panzhihua?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Panzhihua’s mild months (February, March, April, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Panzhihua in February?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (197 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Panzhihua in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Panzhihua get in February?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Panzhihua in February?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
