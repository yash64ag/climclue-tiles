Panzhihua in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Panzhihua in October?
In October, Panzhihua averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 15.3 °C (60 °F), with 55 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Panzhihua?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Panzhihua’s mild months (February, March, April, May, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Panzhihua in October?
55 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (198 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Panzhihua in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Panzhihua get in October?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Panzhihua in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
