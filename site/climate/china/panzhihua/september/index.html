How September compares in Panzhihua

September is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Panzhihua. Compared with August: 1.9 °C cooler, 33 mm less precipitation and 36 hours less sunshine. October is 2.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Panzhihua in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Panzhihua in September?
In September, Panzhihua averages a day high of 25.2 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Panzhihua?
September is not one of Panzhihua’s mild months; those are February, March, April, May, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Panzhihua in September?
130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (198 mm), the driest February (3 mm).
Does it snow in Panzhihua in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Panzhihua get in September?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Panzhihua in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
