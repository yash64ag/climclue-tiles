How October compares in Sishui

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Sishui. Compared with September: 6.1 °C cooler, 33 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. November is 7.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Sishui in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sishui in October?
In October, Sishui averages a day high of 20.5 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 9.3 °C (49 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Sishui?
October is not one of Sishui’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sishui in October?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (211 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Sishui in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sishui get in October?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sishui in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
