How June compares in Sishui

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Sishui. Compared with May: 4.5 °C warmer, 41 mm more precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. July is 1.8 °C warmer than June on average.

Sishui in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sishui in June?
In June, Sishui averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 19.6 °C (67 °F), with 103 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Sishui?
June is not one of Sishui’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sishui in June?
103 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (213 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Sishui in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sishui get in June?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sishui in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
