How November compares in Sishui

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Sishui. Compared with October: 7.8 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. December is 6.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Sishui in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sishui in November?
In November, Sishui averages a day high of 12.7 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 2.1 °C (36 °F), with 19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Sishui?
November is not one of Sishui’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sishui in November?
19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (211 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Sishui in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sishui get in November?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sishui in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
