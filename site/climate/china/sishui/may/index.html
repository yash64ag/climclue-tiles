How May compares in Sishui

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Sishui. Compared with April: 5.5 °C warmer, 31 mm more precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. June is 4.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Sishui in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sishui in May?
In May, Sishui averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 14.7 °C (58 °F), with 62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 253 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Sishui?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Sishui’s mild months (May and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sishui in May?
62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (213 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Sishui in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sishui get in May?
About 253 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sishui in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
