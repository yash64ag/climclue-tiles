Runan at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Runan is 1089th warmest (15.6 °C year average), 959th sunniest (about 2,116 hours of sunshine a year) and 1066th wettest (1,011 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zhumadian, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Runan is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 9 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.7 °C), averages 25.7 °C more than its coolest, January (1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and December. The area lies about 494 km from the sea.

Why Runan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 494 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Runan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.000°N, 114.346°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-509-2943), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
