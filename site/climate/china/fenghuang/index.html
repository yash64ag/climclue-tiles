Fenghuang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Fenghuang is 280th warmest (16.7 °C year average), 632nd sunniest (about 1,433 hours of sunshine a year) and 178th wettest (1,478 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jishou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Fenghuang is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 33 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 22.9 °C more than its coolest, January (4.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 708 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Fenghuang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Fenghuang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 708 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
