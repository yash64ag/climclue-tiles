How October compares in Hegang

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Hegang. Compared with September: 9.0 °C cooler, 36 mm less precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. November is 11.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Hegang in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hegang in October?
In October, Hegang averages a day high of 9.2 °C (49 °F) and a night low of -0.6 °C (31 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Hegang?
No month in Hegang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hegang in October?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (163 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Hegang in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.4 cm (2.9 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Hegang get in October?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hegang in October?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
