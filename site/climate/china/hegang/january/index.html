How January compares in Hegang

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Hegang. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. February is 5.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Hegang in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hegang in January?
In January, Hegang averages a day high of -13.5 °C (8 °F) and a night low of -25.4 °C (-14 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Hegang?
No month in Hegang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hegang in January?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (163 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Hegang in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.5 cm (2.2 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 6.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Hegang get in January?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hegang in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
