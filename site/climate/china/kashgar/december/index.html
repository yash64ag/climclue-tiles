How December compares in Kashgar

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kashgar. Compared with November: 7.1 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Kashgar in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kashgar in December?
In December, Kashgar averages a day high of 2.2 °C (36 °F) and a night low of -7.6 °C (18 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kashgar?
December is not one of Kashgar’s mild months; those are April, May, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kashgar in December?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (12 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Kashgar in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kashgar get in December?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kashgar in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
