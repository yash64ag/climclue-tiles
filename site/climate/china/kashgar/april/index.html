How April compares in Kashgar

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Kashgar. Compared with March: 8.2 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. May is 5.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Kashgar in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kashgar in April?
In April, Kashgar averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 7.5 °C (46 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Kashgar?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Kashgar’s mild months (April, May, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kashgar in April?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (12 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Kashgar in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.7 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kashgar get in April?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kashgar in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
