How March compares in Kashgar

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Kashgar. Compared with February: 8.2 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. April is 8.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Kashgar in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kashgar in March?
In March, Kashgar averages a day high of 13.4 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 0.4 °C (33 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kashgar?
March is not one of Kashgar’s mild months; those are April, May, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kashgar in March?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (12 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Kashgar in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.5 cm (2.2 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kashgar get in March?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kashgar in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
