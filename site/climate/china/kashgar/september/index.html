How September compares in Kashgar

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Kashgar. Compared with August: 5.1 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. October is 7.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Kashgar in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kashgar in September?
In September, Kashgar averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 13.6 °C (56 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Kashgar?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Kashgar’s mild months (April, May, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kashgar in September?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (12 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Kashgar in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kashgar get in September?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kashgar in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
