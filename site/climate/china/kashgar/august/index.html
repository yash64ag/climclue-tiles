How August compares in Kashgar

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Kashgar. Compared with July: 1.8 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. September is 5.1 °C cooler than August on average.

Kashgar in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kashgar in August?
In August, Kashgar averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 18.5 °C (65 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 281 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Kashgar?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Kashgar’s mild months (April, May, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kashgar in August?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (12 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Kashgar in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kashgar get in August?
About 281 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kashgar in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
