How May compares in Kashgar

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Kashgar. Compared with April: 5.2 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 50 hours more sunshine. June is 4.0 °C warmer than May on average.

Kashgar in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kashgar in May?
In May, Kashgar averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 13.3 °C (56 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kashgar?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Kashgar’s mild months (April, May, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kashgar in May?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (12 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Kashgar in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kashgar get in May?
About 254 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kashgar in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
