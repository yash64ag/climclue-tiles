Dachang, Chongqing at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Dachang, Chongqing is 236th warmest (19.7 °C year average), 1546th sunniest (about 1,556 hours of sunshine a year) and 962nd wettest (1,103 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Futian, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Dachang, Chongqing is 1.8 °C warmer in July and 1.5 °C warmer in January, with 319 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.8 °C), averages 21.4 °C more than its coolest, January (8.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.1 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 963 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Dachang, Chongqing has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 963 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.4 °C. The 10 km area around Dachang, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 18.1 °C a year, 1.6 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dachang itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (31.269°N, 109.802°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-527-2898), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
