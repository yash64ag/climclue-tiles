Chuzhou weather in April

Average April weather in Chuzhou, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Chuzhou averages 20.4 °C (69 °F) days and 10.8 °C (51 °F) nights, with 76 mm (3.0 in) of rain over the month and about 169 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Chuzhou

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Chuzhou. Compared with March: 6.3 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. May is 5.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Chuzhou in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chuzhou in April?
In April, Chuzhou averages a day high of 20.4 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 10.8 °C (51 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Chuzhou?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Chuzhou’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chuzhou in April?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (234 mm), the driest December (31 mm).
Does it snow in Chuzhou in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chuzhou get in April?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chuzhou in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
