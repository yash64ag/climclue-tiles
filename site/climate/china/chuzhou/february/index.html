How February compares in Chuzhou

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Chuzhou. Compared with January: 2.5 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. March is 4.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Chuzhou in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chuzhou in February?
In February, Chuzhou averages a day high of 9.1 °C (48 °F) and a night low of 0.7 °C (33 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 128 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Chuzhou?
February is not one of Chuzhou’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chuzhou in February?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (234 mm), the driest December (31 mm).
Does it snow in Chuzhou in February?
Yes, on average: about 7.9 cm (3.1 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Chuzhou get in February?
About 128 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chuzhou in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
