How October compares in Chuzhou

Chuzhou in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chuzhou in October?
In October, Chuzhou averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 12.8 °C (55 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Chuzhou?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Chuzhou’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chuzhou in October?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (234 mm), the driest December (30 mm).
Does it snow in Chuzhou in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chuzhou get in October?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chuzhou in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
