How December compares in Chuzhou

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Chuzhou. Compared with November: 6.1 °C cooler, 32 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. January is 2.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Chuzhou in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chuzhou in December?
In December, Chuzhou averages a day high of 9.2 °C (49 °F) and a night low of -0.1 °C (32 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Chuzhou?
December is not one of Chuzhou’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chuzhou in December?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (234 mm), the driest December (31 mm).
Does it snow in Chuzhou in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.3 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Chuzhou get in December?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chuzhou in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
