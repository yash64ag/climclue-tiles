Chuzhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Chuzhou is 356th warmest (15.6 °C year average), 336th sunniest (about 2,078 hours of sunshine a year) and 314th wettest (1,150 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nanjing, the nearest covered town with different numbers (54 km away), Chuzhou is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 17 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.8 °C), averages 25.5 °C more than its coolest, January (2.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 257 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chuzhou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chuzhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 257 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
