How June compares in Chuzhou

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Chuzhou. Compared with May: 3.7 °C warmer, 72 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. July is 3.2 °C warmer than June on average.

Chuzhou in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chuzhou in June?
In June, Chuzhou averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Chuzhou?
June is not one of Chuzhou’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chuzhou in June?
160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (234 mm), the driest December (30 mm).
Does it snow in Chuzhou in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chuzhou get in June?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chuzhou in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
