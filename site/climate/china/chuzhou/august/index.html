How August compares in Chuzhou

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Chuzhou. Compared with July: 0.7 °C cooler, 64 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. September is 4.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Chuzhou in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chuzhou in August?
In August, Chuzhou averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.7 °C (75 °F), with 170 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Chuzhou?
August is not one of Chuzhou’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chuzhou in August?
170 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (234 mm), the driest December (30 mm).
Does it snow in Chuzhou in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chuzhou get in August?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chuzhou in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
