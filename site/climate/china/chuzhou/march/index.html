Chuzhou weather in March

Average March weather in Chuzhou, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Chuzhou averages 13.9 °C (57 °F) days and 4.8 °C (41 °F) nights, with 84 mm (3.3 in) of rain over the month and about 151 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Chuzhou

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Chuzhou. Compared with February: 4.5 °C warmer, 29 mm more precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. April is 6.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Chuzhou in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chuzhou in March?
In March, Chuzhou averages a day high of 13.9 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 4.8 °C (41 °F), with 84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Chuzhou?
March is not one of Chuzhou’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chuzhou in March?
84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (234 mm), the driest December (31 mm).
Does it snow in Chuzhou in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chuzhou get in March?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chuzhou in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
