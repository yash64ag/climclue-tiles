Chuzhou weather in November

Average November weather in Chuzhou, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Chuzhou averages 15.3 °C (60 °F) days and 5.9 °C (43 °F) nights, with 63 mm (2.5 in) of rain over the month and about 154 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Chuzhou

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Chuzhou. Compared with October: 6.6 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. December is 6.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Chuzhou in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chuzhou in November?
In November, Chuzhou averages a day high of 15.3 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 5.9 °C (43 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Chuzhou?
November is not one of Chuzhou’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chuzhou in November?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (234 mm), the driest December (31 mm).
Does it snow in Chuzhou in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chuzhou get in November?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chuzhou in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
