Xinyi, Guangdong at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Xinyi, Guangdong is 62nd warmest (22.4 °C year average), 518th sunniest (about 1,840 hours of sunshine a year) and 58th wettest (1,970 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gaozhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), Xinyi, Guangdong is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 1.7 °C cooler in January, with 240 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.3 °C), averages 13.9 °C more than its coolest, January (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.4 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 111 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Xinyi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Xinyi, Guangdong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 111 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
