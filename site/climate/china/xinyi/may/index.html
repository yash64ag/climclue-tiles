Xinyi, Guangdong in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xinyi, Guangdong in May?
In May, Xinyi, Guangdong averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 308 mm (12.1 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Xinyi, Guangdong?
May is not one of Xinyi, Guangdong’s mild months; those are February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xinyi, Guangdong in May?
308 mm (12.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (345 mm), the driest December (27 mm).
Does it snow in Xinyi, Guangdong in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xinyi, Guangdong get in May?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xinyi, Guangdong in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
