How January compares in Xinyi, Guangdong

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Xinyi, Guangdong. Compared with December: 1.5 °C cooler, 17 mm more precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. February is 1.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Xinyi, Guangdong in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xinyi, Guangdong in January?
In January, Xinyi, Guangdong averages a day high of 18.8 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 10.4 °C (51 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 117 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Xinyi, Guangdong?
January is not one of Xinyi, Guangdong’s mild months; those are February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xinyi, Guangdong in January?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (354 mm), the driest December (27 mm).
Does it snow in Xinyi, Guangdong in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xinyi, Guangdong get in January?
About 117 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
