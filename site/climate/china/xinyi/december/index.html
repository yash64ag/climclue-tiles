How December compares in Xinyi, Guangdong

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Xinyi, Guangdong. Compared with November: 4.1 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. January is 1.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Xinyi, Guangdong in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xinyi, Guangdong in December?
In December, Xinyi, Guangdong averages a day high of 20.4 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 11.1 °C (52 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Xinyi, Guangdong?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Xinyi, Guangdong’s mild months (February, March, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xinyi, Guangdong in December?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (354 mm), the driest December (27 mm).
Does it snow in Xinyi, Guangdong in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xinyi, Guangdong get in December?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
