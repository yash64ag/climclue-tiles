Xinyi, Guangdong in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xinyi, Guangdong in March?
In March, Xinyi, Guangdong averages a day high of 23.1 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 75 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Xinyi, Guangdong?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Xinyi, Guangdong’s mild months (February, March, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xinyi, Guangdong in March?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (354 mm), the driest December (27 mm).
Does it snow in Xinyi, Guangdong in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xinyi, Guangdong get in March?
About 75 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xinyi, Guangdong in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
