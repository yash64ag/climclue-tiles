How August compares in Xinzhou

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Xinzhou. Compared with July: 1.7 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. September is 4.9 °C cooler than August on average.

Xinzhou in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xinzhou in August?
In August, Xinzhou averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Xinzhou?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Xinzhou’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xinzhou in August?
88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (89 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Xinzhou in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xinzhou get in August?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xinzhou in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
