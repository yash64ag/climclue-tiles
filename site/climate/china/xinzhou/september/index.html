How September compares in Xinzhou, Shanxi

Xinzhou, Shanxi in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xinzhou, Shanxi in September?
In September, Xinzhou, Shanxi averages a day high of 23.9 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 11.0 °C (52 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Xinzhou, Shanxi?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Xinzhou, Shanxi’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xinzhou, Shanxi in September?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (93 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Xinzhou, Shanxi in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xinzhou, Shanxi get in September?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
