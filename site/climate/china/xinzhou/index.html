Xinzhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Xinzhou is 546th warmest (10.3 °C year average), 109th sunniest (about 2,628 hours of sunshine a year) and 627th wettest (398 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yuanping, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Xinzhou is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 25 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.1 °C), averages 30.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-5.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 427 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Xinzhou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Xinzhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 427 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Xinzhou itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.409°N, 112.733°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-455-2927), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
