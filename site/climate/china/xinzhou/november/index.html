Xinzhou, Shanxi in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xinzhou, Shanxi in November?
In November, Xinzhou, Shanxi averages a day high of 8.7 °C (48 °F) and a night low of -3.8 °C (25 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Xinzhou, Shanxi?
November is not one of Xinzhou, Shanxi’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xinzhou, Shanxi in November?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (93 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Xinzhou, Shanxi in November?
Yes, on average: about 2.1 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in November (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Xinzhou, Shanxi get in November?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xinzhou, Shanxi in November?
