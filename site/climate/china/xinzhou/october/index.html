Xinzhou, Shanxi in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xinzhou, Shanxi in October?
In October, Xinzhou, Shanxi averages a day high of 17.5 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 3.9 °C (39 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Xinzhou, Shanxi?
October is not one of Xinzhou, Shanxi’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xinzhou, Shanxi in October?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (93 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Xinzhou, Shanxi in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xinzhou, Shanxi get in October?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
