Xinzhou, Shanxi in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xinzhou, Shanxi in March?
In March, Xinzhou, Shanxi averages a day high of 11.5 °C (53 °F) and a night low of -2.0 °C (28 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Xinzhou, Shanxi?
March is not one of Xinzhou, Shanxi’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xinzhou, Shanxi in March?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (93 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Xinzhou, Shanxi in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.4 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Xinzhou, Shanxi get in March?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xinzhou, Shanxi in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
