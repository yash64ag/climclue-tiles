How July compares in Xinzhou, Shanxi

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Xinzhou, Shanxi. Compared with June: 1.9 °C warmer, 34 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. August is 1.7 °C cooler than July on average.
