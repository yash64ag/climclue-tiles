How July compares in Xinzhou

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Xinzhou. Compared with June: 1.9 °C warmer, 32 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. August is 1.7 °C cooler than July on average.

Xinzhou in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xinzhou in July?
In July, Xinzhou averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 18.4 °C (65 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 242 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Xinzhou?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Xinzhou’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xinzhou in July?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (89 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Xinzhou in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xinzhou get in July?
About 242 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xinzhou in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
