Why Laixi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 63 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Laixi climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Laixi?
July, with an average of 25.4 °C (78 °F), day highs around 29.7 °C (85 °F) and night lows around 21.9 °C (71 °F).
What is the coldest month in Laixi?
January, with an average of -2.1 °C (28 °F), day highs around 2.9 °C (37 °F) and night lows around -6.5 °C (20 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Laixi?
July, with 185 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 681 mm (26.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Laixi?
January, with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Laixi?
May, with about 261 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Laixi?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 22 cm (8.5 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 1 month.
When is the best time for mild weather in Laixi?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June and September.
What type of climate does Laixi have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.9 × 11 km, that contains Laixi’s GeoNames location (36.859°N, 120.527°E; ClimaClue area sq-471-3005), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 341,470 (GeoNames).
