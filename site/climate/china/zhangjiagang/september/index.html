How September compares in Zhangjiagang

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Zhangjiagang. Compared with August: 4.2 °C cooler, 64 mm less precipitation and 65 hours less sunshine. October is 5.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Zhangjiagang in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhangjiagang in September?
In September, Zhangjiagang averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 19.7 °C (67 °F), with 98 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Zhangjiagang?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Zhangjiagang’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhangjiagang in September?
98 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (212 mm), the driest December (33 mm).
Does it snow in Zhangjiagang in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhangjiagang get in September?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhangjiagang in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
