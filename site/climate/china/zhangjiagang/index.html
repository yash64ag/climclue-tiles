Zhangjiagang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Zhangjiagang is 334th warmest (16.1 °C year average), 356th sunniest (about 2,026 hours of sunshine a year) and 302nd wettest (1,183 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jiangyin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Zhangjiagang is about as warm in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 36 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 24.7 °C more than its coolest, January (3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 96 km from the sea.

Why Zhangjiagang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 96 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
