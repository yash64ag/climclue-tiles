Ālā'ĕr at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Ālā'ĕr is the 5th driest (64 mm a year). Compared with Aqsu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (109 km away), Ālā'ĕr is 3.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 20 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.1 °C), averages 33.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-7.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, November and December. The area lies about 2,176 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Ālā'ĕr has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 2,176 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 33.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
