Changning, Shanghai in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changning, Shanghai in April?
In April, Changning, Shanghai averages a day high of 18.9 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 10.9 °C (52 °F), with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Changning, Shanghai?
April is not one of Changning, Shanghai’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changning, Shanghai in April?
104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (230 mm), the driest December (49 mm).
Does it snow in Changning, Shanghai in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Changning, Shanghai get in April?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
