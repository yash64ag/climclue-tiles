Changning at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Changning is 290th warmest (16.6 °C year average), 402nd sunniest (about 1,969 hours of sunshine a year) and 182nd wettest (1,459 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xuhui, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Changning is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 23.5 °C more than its coolest, January (4.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 120% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Changning has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
