Changning, Shanghai in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changning, Shanghai in March?
In March, Changning, Shanghai averages a day high of 13.3 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 5.8 °C (42 °F), with 116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Changning, Shanghai?
March is not one of Changning, Shanghai’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changning, Shanghai in March?
116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (230 mm), the driest December (49 mm).
Does it snow in Changning, Shanghai in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Changning, Shanghai get in March?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Changning, Shanghai in March?
