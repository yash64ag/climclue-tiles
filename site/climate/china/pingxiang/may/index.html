Pingxiang, Jiangxi in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pingxiang, Jiangxi in May?
In May, Pingxiang, Jiangxi averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 227 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Pingxiang, Jiangxi?
May is not one of Pingxiang, Jiangxi’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
