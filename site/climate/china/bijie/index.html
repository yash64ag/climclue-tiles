Bijie at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Bijie is 508th warmest (12.2 °C year average), 616th sunniest (about 1,514 hours of sunshine a year) and 338th wettest (1,072 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Liupanshui, the nearest covered town with different numbers (91 km away), Bijie is 1.1 °C warmer in July and 1.5 °C cooler in January, with 255 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.9 °C), averages 18.9 °C more than its coolest, January (2.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 719 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Bijie has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 719 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
