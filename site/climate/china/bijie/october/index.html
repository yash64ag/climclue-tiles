Bijie in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bijie in October?
In October, Bijie averages a day high of 15.2 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 9.1 °C (48 °F), with 77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 97 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Bijie?
No month in Bijie is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bijie in October?
77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (201 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Bijie in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bijie get in October?
About 97 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bijie in October?
