Bijie in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bijie in June?
In June, Bijie averages a day high of 21.6 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 14.6 °C (58 °F), with 189 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Bijie?
No month in Bijie is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bijie in June?
189 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (201 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Bijie in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bijie get in June?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bijie in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
