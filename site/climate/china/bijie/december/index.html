Bijie weather in December

Average December weather in Bijie, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Bijie averages 8.5 °C (47 °F) days and 1.7 °C (35 °F) nights, with 15 mm (0.6 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 94 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Bijie

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the least sunny month in Bijie. Compared with November: 4.8 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. January is 1.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Bijie in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bijie in December?
In December, Bijie averages a day high of 8.5 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 1.7 °C (35 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 94 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Bijie?
No month in Bijie is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bijie in December?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (182 mm), the driest December (15 mm).
Does it snow in Bijie in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.3 cm (2.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Bijie get in December?
About 94 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bijie in December?
