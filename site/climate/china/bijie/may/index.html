Bijie in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bijie in May?
In May, Bijie averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 14.6 °C (58 °F), with 112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Bijie?
May is not one of Bijie’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bijie in May?
112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (182 mm), the driest December (15 mm).
Does it snow in Bijie in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bijie get in May?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bijie in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
