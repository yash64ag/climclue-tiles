How April compares in Pingdu

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Pingdu. Compared with March: 7.2 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. May is 5.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Pingdu in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pingdu in April?
In April, Pingdu averages a day high of 19.7 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 7.2 °C (45 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Pingdu?
April is not one of Pingdu’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pingdu in April?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (182 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Pingdu in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pingdu get in April?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pingdu in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
