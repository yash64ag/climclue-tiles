Zhalantun at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Zhalantun is 676th warmest (2.7 °C year average), 65th sunniest (about 2,708 hours of sunshine a year) and 601st wettest (483 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Longjiang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (82 km away), Zhalantun is 1.9 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 15 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.9 °C), averages 39.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-18.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 808 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Zhalantun has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 808 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 39.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
