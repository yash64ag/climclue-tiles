How March compares in Bozhou

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Bozhou. Compared with February: 5.1 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 33 hours more sunshine. April is 6.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Bozhou in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bozhou in March?
In March, Bozhou averages a day high of 14.7 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 3.7 °C (39 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bozhou?
March is not one of Bozhou’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bozhou in March?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (214 mm), the driest December (16 mm).
Does it snow in Bozhou in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bozhou get in March?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bozhou in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
