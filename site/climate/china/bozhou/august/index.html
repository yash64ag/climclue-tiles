How August compares in Bozhou

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Bozhou. Compared with July: 1.0 °C cooler, 75 mm less precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. September is 4.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Bozhou in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bozhou in August?
In August, Bozhou averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Bozhou?
August is not one of Bozhou’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bozhou in August?
139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (214 mm), the driest December (16 mm).
Does it snow in Bozhou in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bozhou get in August?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bozhou in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
