Bozhou weather in December

Average December weather in Bozhou, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Bozhou averages 8.1 °C (47 °F) days and -1.8 °C (29 °F) nights, with 16 mm (0.6 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 157 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Bozhou

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Bozhou. Compared with November: 6.0 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Bozhou in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bozhou in December?
In December, Bozhou averages a day high of 8.1 °C (47 °F) and a night low of -1.8 °C (29 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Bozhou?
December is not one of Bozhou’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bozhou in December?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (214 mm), the driest December (16 mm).
Does it snow in Bozhou in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.1 cm (1.6 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Bozhou get in December?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bozhou in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
