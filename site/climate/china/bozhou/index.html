Bozhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Bozhou is 375th warmest (15.2 °C year average), 287th sunniest (about 2,220 hours of sunshine a year) and 405th wettest (816 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shangqiu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Bozhou is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 97 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 26.2 °C more than its coolest, January (1.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 340 km from the sea.

Why Bozhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 340 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
