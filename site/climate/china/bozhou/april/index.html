Bozhou weather in April

Average April weather in Bozhou, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Bozhou averages 21.7 °C (71 °F) days and 10.1 °C (50 °F) nights, with 40 mm (1.6 in) of rain over the month and about 190 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Bozhou

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Bozhou. Compared with March: 6.7 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. May is 5.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Bozhou in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bozhou in April?
In April, Bozhou averages a day high of 21.7 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 10.1 °C (50 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Bozhou?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Bozhou’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bozhou in April?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (214 mm), the driest December (16 mm).
Does it snow in Bozhou in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bozhou get in April?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bozhou in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
