How September compares in Bozhou

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Bozhou. Compared with August: 4.5 °C cooler, 59 mm less precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. October is 5.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Bozhou in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bozhou in September?
In September, Bozhou averages a day high of 26.5 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 17.6 °C (64 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Bozhou?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Bozhou’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bozhou in September?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (214 mm), the driest December (16 mm).
Does it snow in Bozhou in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bozhou get in September?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bozhou in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
