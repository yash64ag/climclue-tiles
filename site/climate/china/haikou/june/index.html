Haikou, Hainan in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Haikou, Hainan in June?
In June, Haikou, Hainan averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 26.8 °C (80 °F), with 225 mm (8.8 in) of precipitation and about 209 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Haikou, Hainan?
June is not one of Haikou, Hainan’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

225 mm (8.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (294 mm), the driest January (21 mm).
Does it snow in Haikou, Hainan in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).

About 209 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
