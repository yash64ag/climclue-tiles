Haikou, Hainan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Haikou, Hainan in April?
In April, Haikou, Hainan averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Haikou, Hainan?
April is not one of Haikou, Hainan’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Haikou, Hainan in April?
102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (291 mm), the driest January (20 mm).
Does it snow in Haikou, Hainan in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Haikou, Hainan get in April?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Haikou, Hainan in April?
About 22.6 °C (73 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (29.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
