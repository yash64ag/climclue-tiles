Haikou, Hainan in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Haikou, Hainan in May?
In May, Haikou, Hainan averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 183 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Haikou, Hainan?
May is not one of Haikou, Hainan’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Haikou, Hainan in May?
183 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (291 mm), the driest January (20 mm).
Does it snow in Haikou, Hainan in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Haikou, Hainan get in May?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
