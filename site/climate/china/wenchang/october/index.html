How October compares in Wenchang

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Wenchang. Compared with September: 1.6 °C cooler, 21 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. November is 2.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Wenchang in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wenchang in October?
In October, Wenchang averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 334 mm (13.2 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Wenchang?
October is not one of Wenchang’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wenchang in October?
334 mm (13.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is September (355 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Wenchang in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wenchang get in October?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wenchang in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
