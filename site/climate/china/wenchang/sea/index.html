Wenchang sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Hainan · China

The sea off Wenchang averages 28.6 °C (83 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 21.7 °C (71 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to November.

Wenchang sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Wenchang?
In August, at about 28.6 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Wenchang?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from April to November. The coolest month is January, at 21.7 °C (71 °F).
How warm is the sea in Wenchang in January?
About 21.7 °C (71 °F): refreshing.
What is the average sea temperature in Wenchang?
About 25.6 °C (78 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Wenchang nearby?
In August: Lingao (29.7 °C, 123 km), Dongcheng (29.8 °C, 142 km) and Mutang (29.8 °C, 155 km).
