Wenchang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wenchang in April?
In April, Wenchang averages a day high of 29.2 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Wenchang?
April is not one of Wenchang’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wenchang in April?
117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (368 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Wenchang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wenchang get in April?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Wenchang in April?
About 24.6 °C (76 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (28.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Wenchang in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
