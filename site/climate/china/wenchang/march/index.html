Wenchang weather in March

Average March weather in Wenchang, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Wenchang averages 26.1 °C (79 °F) days and 19.4 °C (67 °F) nights, with 51 mm (2.0 in) of rain over the month and about 142 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Wenchang

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Wenchang. Compared with February: 2.1 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. April is 2.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Wenchang in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wenchang in March?
In March, Wenchang averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 19.4 °C (67 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Wenchang?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Wenchang’s mild months (January, February, March and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wenchang in March?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (368 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Wenchang in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wenchang get in March?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Wenchang in March?
About 22.5 °C (73 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (28.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Wenchang in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
