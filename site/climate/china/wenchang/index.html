Wenchang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Wenchang is the 4th warmest (25.0 °C year average). Compared with Qionghai, the nearest covered town with different numbers (50 km away), Wenchang is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 47 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 9.4 °C more than its coolest, January (19.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 17.4 °C (63 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Wenchang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Wenchang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
