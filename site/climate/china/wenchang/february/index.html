How February compares in Wenchang

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Wenchang. Compared with January: 1.1 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. March is 2.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Wenchang in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wenchang in February?
In February, Wenchang averages a day high of 23.4 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 19.1 °C (66 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 112 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Wenchang?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Wenchang’s mild months (January, February, March and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wenchang in February?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is September (355 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Wenchang in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wenchang get in February?
About 112 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wenchang in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
