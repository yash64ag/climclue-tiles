Zhongduo at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Zhongduo is 1138th warmest (15.1 °C year average), 1778th sunniest (about 1,329 hours of sunshine a year) and 329th wettest (1,520 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tonggu, Chongqing (28.8°N 108.7°E), the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Zhongduo is 0.9 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 325 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.3 °C), averages 21.8 °C more than its coolest, January (3.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 801 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 116% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Zhongduo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 801 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.8 °C. The 10 km area around Zhongduo, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 14.1 °C a year, 1.0 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Zhongduo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (28.847°N, 108.764°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-551-2887), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
