How July compares in Tai’an, Shandong

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Tai’an, Shandong. Compared with June: 1.9 °C warmer, 126 mm more precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. August is 0.9 °C cooler than July on average.

Tai’an, Shandong in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tai’an, Shandong in July?
In July, Tai’an, Shandong averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 233 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Tai’an, Shandong?
July is not one of Tai’an, Shandong’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tai’an, Shandong in July?
233 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (233 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Tai’an, Shandong in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tai’an, Shandong get in July?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tai’an, Shandong in July?
