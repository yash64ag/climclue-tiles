Tai’an, Shandong weather in August

Average August weather in Tai’an, Shandong, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Tai’an, Shandong averages 29.3 °C (85 °F) days and 21.3 °C (70 °F) nights, with 184 mm (7.2 in) of rain over the month and about 222 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Tai’an, Shandong

Tai’an, Shandong in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tai’an, Shandong in August?
In August, Tai’an, Shandong averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 184 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Tai’an, Shandong?
August is not one of Tai’an, Shandong’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
