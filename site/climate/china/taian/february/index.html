How February compares in Tai’an

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Tai’an. Compared with January: 3.2 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. March is 5.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Tai’an in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tai’an in February?
In February, Tai’an averages a day high of 6.9 °C (44 °F) and a night low of -3.8 °C (25 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Tai’an?
February is not one of Tai’an’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tai’an in February?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (233 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Tai’an in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.7 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Tai’an get in February?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tai’an in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
