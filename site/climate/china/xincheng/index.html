Xincheng at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Xincheng is 515th warmest (11.6 °C year average), 47th sunniest (about 2,742 hours of sunshine a year) and 683rd wettest (96 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ālā'ĕr, the nearest covered town with different numbers (191 km away), Xincheng is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 33 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.3 °C), averages 33.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-7.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 2,258 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Xincheng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 2,258 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 33.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
