How March compares in Nanyang

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Nanyang. Compared with February: 4.6 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. April is 6.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Nanyang in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanyang in March?
In March, Nanyang averages a day high of 15.0 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 3.7 °C (39 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Nanyang?
March is not one of Nanyang’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanyang in March?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (200 mm), the driest January (14 mm).
Does it snow in Nanyang in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.1 cm (1.6 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanyang get in March?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanyang in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
