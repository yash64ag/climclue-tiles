How April compares in Nanyang

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Nanyang. Compared with March: 6.6 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. May is 5.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Nanyang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanyang in April?
In April, Nanyang averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 11.0 °C (52 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Nanyang?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Nanyang’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanyang in April?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (199 mm), the driest January (14 mm).
Does it snow in Nanyang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanyang get in April?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanyang in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
