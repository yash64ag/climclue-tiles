How November compares in Nanyang

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Nanyang. Compared with October: 6.6 °C cooler, 23 mm less precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. December is 5.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Nanyang in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanyang in November?
In November, Nanyang averages a day high of 15.1 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 4.8 °C (41 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Nanyang?
November is not one of Nanyang’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanyang in November?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (199 mm), the driest January (14 mm).
Does it snow in Nanyang in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanyang get in November?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanyang in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
