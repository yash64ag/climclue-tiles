How July compares in Nanyang

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Nanyang. Compared with June: 1.5 °C warmer, 73 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. August is 1.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Nanyang in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanyang in July?
In July, Nanyang averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 199 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Nanyang?
July is not one of Nanyang’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanyang in July?
199 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (199 mm), the driest January (14 mm).
Does it snow in Nanyang in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanyang get in July?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanyang in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
