Nanyang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Nanyang is 360th warmest (15.6 °C year average), 370th sunniest (about 2,008 hours of sunshine a year) and 397th wettest (843 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zhenping, the nearest covered town with different numbers (30 km away), Nanyang is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 56 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.3 °C), averages 25.1 °C more than its coolest, January (2.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 649 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nanyang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nanyang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 649 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
