How January compares in Nanyang

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Nanyang. Compared with December: 1.8 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. February is 2.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Nanyang in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanyang in January?
In January, Nanyang averages a day high of 7.7 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -2.8 °C (27 °F), with 14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Nanyang?
January is not one of Nanyang’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanyang in January?
14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (199 mm), the driest January (14 mm).
Does it snow in Nanyang in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.1 cm (2.8 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanyang get in January?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanyang in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
