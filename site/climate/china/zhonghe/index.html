Zhonghe at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Zhonghe is 265th warmest (16.9 °C year average), 647th sunniest (about 1,350 hours of sunshine a year) and 301st wettest (1,184 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jishou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (74 km away), Zhonghe is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 261 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 22.2 °C more than its coolest, January (5.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 757 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Zhonghe, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Zhonghe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 757 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
