Dunhuang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Dunhuang is the 2nd sunniest (about 3,123 hours of sunshine a year) and the driest (41 mm a year). Compared with Jiayuguan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (309 km away), Dunhuang is 5.5 °C warmer in July and 2.2 °C warmer in January, with 67 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 34.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-6.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,969 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Dunhuang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,969 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 34.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
