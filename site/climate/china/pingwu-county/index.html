Pingwu County at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Pingwu County is 516th warmest (11.5 °C year average), 585th sunniest (about 1,649 hours of sunshine a year) and 374th wettest (932 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jiangyou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (73 km away), Pingwu County is 4.9 °C cooler in July and 6.2 °C cooler in January, with 53 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.6 °C), averages 21.5 °C more than its coolest, January (0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 1,267 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pingwu County, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pingwu County has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,267 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 10.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
