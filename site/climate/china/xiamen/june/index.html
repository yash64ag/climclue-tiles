How June compares in Xiamen

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Xiamen. Compared with May: 2.9 °C warmer, 40 mm more precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. July is 2.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Xiamen in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xiamen in June?
In June, Xiamen averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 214 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Xiamen?
June is not one of Xiamen’s mild months; those are October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xiamen in June?
214 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (214 mm), the driest November (30 mm).
Does it snow in Xiamen in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xiamen get in June?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Xiamen in June?
About 25.9 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (27.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Xiamen in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
