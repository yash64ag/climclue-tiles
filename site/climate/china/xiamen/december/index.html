How December compares in Xiamen

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Xiamen. Compared with November: 4.5 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. January is 1.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Xiamen in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xiamen in December?
In December, Xiamen averages a day high of 19.2 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 11.9 °C (53 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Xiamen?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Xiamen’s mild months (October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xiamen in December?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (214 mm), the driest November (30 mm).
Does it snow in Xiamen in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xiamen get in December?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Xiamen in December?
About 18.0 °C (64 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (27.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Xiamen in December?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
