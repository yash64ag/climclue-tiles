How October compares in Xiamen

October is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Xiamen. Compared with September: 3.0 °C cooler, 108 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. November is 4.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Xiamen in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xiamen in October?
In October, Xiamen averages a day high of 26.9 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Xiamen?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Xiamen’s mild months (October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xiamen in October?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (224 mm), the driest November (31 mm).
Does it snow in Xiamen in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xiamen get in October?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xiamen in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
