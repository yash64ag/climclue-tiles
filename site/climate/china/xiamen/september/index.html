How September compares in Xiamen

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Xiamen. Compared with August: 1.3 °C cooler, 61 mm less precipitation and 39 hours less sunshine. October is 3.0 °C cooler than September on average.

Xiamen in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xiamen in September?
In September, Xiamen averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 140 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Xiamen?
September is not one of Xiamen’s mild months; those are October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xiamen in September?
140 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (214 mm), the driest November (30 mm).
Does it snow in Xiamen in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xiamen get in September?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Xiamen in September?
About 27.3 °C (81 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (27.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Xiamen in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
