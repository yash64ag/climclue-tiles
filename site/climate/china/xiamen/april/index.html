Xiamen weather in April

Average April weather in Xiamen, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Xiamen averages 23.4 °C (74 °F) days and 16.7 °C (62 °F) nights, with 143 mm (5.6 in) of rain over the month and about 131 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Xiamen

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Xiamen. Compared with March: 4.0 °C warmer, 31 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. May is 3.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Xiamen in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xiamen in April?
In April, Xiamen averages a day high of 23.4 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 16.7 °C (62 °F), with 143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 131 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Xiamen?
April is not one of Xiamen’s mild months; those are October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xiamen in April?
143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (214 mm), the driest November (30 mm).
Does it snow in Xiamen in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xiamen get in April?
About 131 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Xiamen in April?
About 19.1 °C (66 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (27.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Xiamen in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
