Xiamen sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Fujian · China

The sea off Xiamen averages 27.5 °C (82 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 14.6 °C (58 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October.

Xiamen sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Xiamen?
In August, at about 27.5 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Xiamen?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October. The coolest month is February, at 14.6 °C (58 °F).
How warm is the sea in Xiamen in February?
About 14.6 °C (58 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Xiamen?
About 21.7 °C (71 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Xiamen nearby?
In August: Magong (28.1 °C, 184 km) and Erlin (28.2 °C, 241 km).
