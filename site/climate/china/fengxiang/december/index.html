How December compares in Fengxiang

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Fengxiang. Compared with November: 6.0 °C cooler, 21 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. January is 2.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Fengxiang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fengxiang in December?
In December, Fengxiang averages a day high of 10.7 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Fengxiang?
December is not one of Fengxiang’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fengxiang in December?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (204 mm), the driest December (51 mm).
Does it snow in Fengxiang in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.7 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Fengxiang get in December?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fengxiang in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
