How April compares in Fengxiang

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Fengxiang. Compared with March: 4.9 °C warmer, 16 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. May is 5.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Fengxiang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fengxiang in April?
In April, Fengxiang averages a day high of 18.3 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 10.4 °C (51 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Fengxiang?
April is not one of Fengxiang’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fengxiang in April?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (204 mm), the driest December (51 mm).
Does it snow in Fengxiang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fengxiang get in April?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fengxiang in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
