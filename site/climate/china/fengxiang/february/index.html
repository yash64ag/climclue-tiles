How February compares in Fengxiang

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Fengxiang. Compared with January: 1.0 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. March is 3.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Fengxiang in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fengxiang in February?
In February, Fengxiang averages a day high of 9.7 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 2.8 °C (37 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 111 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Fengxiang?
February is not one of Fengxiang’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fengxiang in February?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (204 mm), the driest December (51 mm).
Does it snow in Fengxiang in February?
Yes, on average: about 1.9 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Fengxiang get in February?
About 111 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Fengxiang in February?
About 6.6 °C (44 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (28.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Fengxiang in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
