How October compares in Fengxiang

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Fengxiang. Compared with September: 4.9 °C cooler, 61 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. November is 5.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Fengxiang in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fengxiang in October?
In October, Fengxiang averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Fengxiang?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Fengxiang’s mild months (October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fengxiang in October?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (204 mm), the driest December (51 mm).
Does it snow in Fengxiang in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fengxiang get in October?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fengxiang in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
