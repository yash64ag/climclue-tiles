How July compares in Pingliang

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Pingliang. Compared with June: 2.0 °C warmer, 31 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. August is 1.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Pingliang in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pingliang in July?
In July, Pingliang averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 16.4 °C (62 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Pingliang?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Pingliang’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pingliang in July?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (94 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Pingliang in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pingliang get in July?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pingliang in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
