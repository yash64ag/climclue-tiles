How July compares in Pingliang

Pingliang in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pingliang in July?
In July, Pingliang averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.8 °C (60 °F), with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Pingliang?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Pingliang’s mild months (May, June, July and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pingliang in July?
100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (102 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Pingliang in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pingliang get in July?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pingliang in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
