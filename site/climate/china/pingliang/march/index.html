Pingliang in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pingliang in March?
In March, Pingliang averages a day high of 10.1 °C (50 °F) and a night low of -1.9 °C (29 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Pingliang?
March is not one of Pingliang’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pingliang in March?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (94 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Pingliang in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.4 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Pingliang get in March?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pingliang in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
