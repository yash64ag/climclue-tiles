How November compares in Pingliang

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Pingliang. Compared with October: 6.6 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. December is 5.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Pingliang in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pingliang in November?
In November, Pingliang averages a day high of 8.3 °C (47 °F) and a night low of -2.7 °C (27 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Pingliang?
November is not one of Pingliang’s mild months; those are May, June, July and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pingliang in November?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (102 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Pingliang in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 14 cm (5.4 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Pingliang get in November?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pingliang in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
