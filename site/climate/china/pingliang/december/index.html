How December compares in Pingliang

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Pingliang. Compared with November: 5.5 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. January is 1.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Pingliang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pingliang in December?
In December, Pingliang averages a day high of 3.9 °C (39 °F) and a night low of -7.0 °C (19 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Pingliang?
December is not one of Pingliang’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pingliang in December?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (94 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Pingliang in December?
Yes, on average: about 2.9 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Pingliang get in December?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pingliang in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
