Pingliang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pingliang in April?
In April, Pingliang averages a day high of 16.0 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 2.9 °C (37 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Pingliang?
April is not one of Pingliang’s mild months; those are May, June, July and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pingliang in April?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (102 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Pingliang in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.0 cm (2.0 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Pingliang get in April?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pingliang in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
