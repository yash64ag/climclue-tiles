How September compares in Pingliang

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Pingliang. Compared with August: 4.7 °C cooler, 30 mm less precipitation and 63 hours less sunshine. October is 6.0 °C cooler than September on average.

Pingliang in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pingliang in September?
In September, Pingliang averages a day high of 21.0 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 11.0 °C (52 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 160 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Pingliang?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Pingliang’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pingliang in September?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (94 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Pingliang in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pingliang get in September?
About 160 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pingliang in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
