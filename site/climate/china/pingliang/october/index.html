Pingliang in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pingliang in October?
In October, Pingliang averages a day high of 14.2 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 3.8 °C (39 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Pingliang?
October is not one of Pingliang’s mild months; those are May, June, July and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pingliang in October?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (102 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Pingliang in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.0 cm (2.8 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Pingliang get in October?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pingliang in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
