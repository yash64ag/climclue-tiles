How March compares in Lianjiang

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Lianjiang. Compared with February: 2.9 °C warmer, 30 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. April is 3.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Lianjiang in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lianjiang in March?
In March, Lianjiang averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 77 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Lianjiang?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Lianjiang’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lianjiang in March?
88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (343 mm), the driest December (31 mm).
Does it snow in Lianjiang in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lianjiang get in March?
About 77 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lianjiang in March?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
