Lianjiang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Lianjiang is 12th warmest (23.5 °C year average), 488th sunniest (about 1,881 hours of sunshine a year) and 44th wettest (2,073 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zhanjiang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (47 km away), Lianjiang is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 85 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 12.8 °C more than its coolest, January (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.3 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 66 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lianjiang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lianjiang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 66 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
