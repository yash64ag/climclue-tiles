How April compares in Lianjiang

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Lianjiang. Compared with March: 3.8 °C warmer, 109 mm more precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. May is 2.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Lianjiang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lianjiang in April?
In April, Lianjiang averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 198 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation and about 108 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Lianjiang?
April is not one of Lianjiang’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lianjiang in April?
198 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (347 mm), the driest December (30 mm).
Does it snow in Lianjiang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lianjiang get in April?
About 108 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lianjiang in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
