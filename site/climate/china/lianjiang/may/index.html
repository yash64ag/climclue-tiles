How May compares in Lianjiang

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Lianjiang. Compared with April: 2.6 °C warmer, 90 mm more precipitation and 74 hours more sunshine. June is 1.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Lianjiang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lianjiang in May?
In May, Lianjiang averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 287 mm (11.3 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Lianjiang?
May is not one of Lianjiang’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lianjiang in May?
287 mm (11.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (347 mm), the driest December (30 mm).
Does it snow in Lianjiang in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lianjiang get in May?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lianjiang in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
