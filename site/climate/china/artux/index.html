Why Artux has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,822 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 31.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Artux climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Artux?
July, with an average of 26.4 °C (80 °F), day highs around 31.9 °C (89 °F) and night lows around 18.9 °C (66 °F).
What is the coldest month in Artux?
January, with an average of -5.2 °C (23 °F), day highs around -0.8 °C (31 °F) and night lows around -9.3 °C (15 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Artux?
July, with 14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 101 mm (4.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Artux?
December, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Artux?
July, with about 302 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Artux?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 36 cm (14.1 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 3 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Artux?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, June and September.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.6 × 11 km, that contains Artux’s GeoNames location (39.708°N, 76.180°E; ClimaClue area sq-442-2561), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 285,000 (GeoNames).
