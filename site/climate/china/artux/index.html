Artux at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Artux is 507th warmest (12.2 °C year average), 110th sunniest (about 2,621 hours of sunshine a year) and 682nd wettest (101 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kashgar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (31 km away), Artux is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.4 °C), averages 31.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-5.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,822 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Artux has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,822 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 31.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
