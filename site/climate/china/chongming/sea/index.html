Chongming sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · China

The sea off Chongming averages 26.8 °C (80 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 6.2 °C (43 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Chongming sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Chongming?
In August, at about 26.8 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Chongming?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 6.2 °C (43 °F).
How warm is the sea in Chongming in February?
About 6.2 °C (43 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Chongming?
About 16.6 °C (62 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Chongming nearby?
In August: Baoshan, Shanghai (27.5 °C, 31 km), Luodian (27.5 °C, 41 km) and Gucun (27.5 °C, 42 km).
