How January compares in Chongming

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Chongming. Compared with December: 2.8 °C cooler, 24 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. February is 0.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Chongming in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chongming in January?
In January, Chongming averages a day high of 8.2 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 1.6 °C (35 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 137 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Chongming?
January is not one of Chongming’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chongming in January?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (202 mm), the driest December (43 mm).
Does it snow in Chongming in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.0 cm (1.2 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Chongming get in January?
About 137 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Chongming in January?
About 7.2 °C (45 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (26.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Chongming in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
