Chongming at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Chongming is 321st warmest (16.4 °C year average), 368th sunniest (about 2,009 hours of sunshine a year) and 243rd wettest (1,332 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chengqiao, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Chongming is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 52 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 22.7 °C more than its coolest, January (4.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chongming, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chongming has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
