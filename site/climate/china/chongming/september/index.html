How September compares in Chongming

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Chongming. Compared with August: 3.2 °C cooler, 76 mm less precipitation and 68 hours less sunshine. October is 4.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Chongming in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chongming in September?
In September, Chongming averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Chongming?
September is not one of Chongming’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chongming in September?
124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (202 mm), the driest December (43 mm).
Does it snow in Chongming in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chongming get in September?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Chongming in September?
About 25.1 °C (77 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (26.8 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Chongming in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
