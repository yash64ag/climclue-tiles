How December compares in Chongming

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Chongming. Compared with November: 6.1 °C cooler, 25 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. January is 2.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Chongming in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chongming in December?
In December, Chongming averages a day high of 9.7 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 5.1 °C (41 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Chongming?
December is not one of Chongming’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chongming in December?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (206 mm), the driest December (43 mm).
Does it snow in Chongming in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chongming get in December?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chongming in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
