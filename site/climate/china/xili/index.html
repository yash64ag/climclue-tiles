Xili at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Xili is 93rd warmest (22.9 °C year average), 1220th sunniest (about 1,927 hours of sunshine a year) and 22nd wettest (2,237 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bao'an Centre, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Xili is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 36 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.5 °C), averages 13.1 °C more than its coolest, January (15.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.3 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Xili has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Xili itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.588°N, 113.948°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-614-2939), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
