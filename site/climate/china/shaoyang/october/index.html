How October compares in Shaoyang

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Shaoyang. Compared with September: 5.5 °C cooler, 14 mm more precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. November is 5.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Shaoyang in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shaoyang in October?
In October, Shaoyang averages a day high of 22.4 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 14.7 °C (58 °F), with 85 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Shaoyang?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Shaoyang’s mild months (September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shaoyang in October?
85 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (214 mm), the driest December (42 mm).
Does it snow in Shaoyang in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shaoyang get in October?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shaoyang in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
