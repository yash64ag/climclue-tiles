How September compares in Shaoyang

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Shaoyang. Compared with August: 3.9 °C cooler, 56 mm less precipitation and 58 hours less sunshine. October is 5.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Shaoyang in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shaoyang in September?
In September, Shaoyang averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Shaoyang?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Shaoyang’s mild months (September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shaoyang in September?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (215 mm), the driest December (42 mm).
Does it snow in Shaoyang in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shaoyang get in September?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shaoyang in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
