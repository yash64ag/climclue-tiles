How May compares in Shaoyang

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Shaoyang. Compared with April: 4.8 °C warmer, 32 mm more precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. June is 3.4 °C warmer than May on average.

Shaoyang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shaoyang in May?
In May, Shaoyang averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 183 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation and about 126 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Shaoyang?
May is not one of Shaoyang’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shaoyang in May?
183 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (214 mm), the driest December (42 mm).
Does it snow in Shaoyang in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shaoyang get in May?
About 126 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shaoyang in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
