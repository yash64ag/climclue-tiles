How June compares in Shaoyang

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Shaoyang. Compared with May: 3.4 °C warmer, 31 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. July is 3.0 °C warmer than June on average.

Shaoyang in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shaoyang in June?
In June, Shaoyang averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 215 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Shaoyang?
June is not one of Shaoyang’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shaoyang in June?
215 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (215 mm), the driest December (42 mm).
Does it snow in Shaoyang in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shaoyang get in June?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shaoyang in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
