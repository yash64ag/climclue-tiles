Qionghai at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Qionghai is the 5th warmest (24.8 °C year average). Compared with Wenchang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (50 km away), Qionghai is 0.1 °C warmer in June and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 189 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.8 °C), averages 9.6 °C more than its coolest, January (19.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.6 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 1 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 109% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Qionghai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

The tropical sun keeps it warm all year; the hottest weeks often come just before the rains. Seasonal monsoon winds bring a very wet season, and a short dry season follows when they reverse. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
