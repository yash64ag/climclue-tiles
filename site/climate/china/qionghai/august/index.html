Qionghai in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qionghai in August?
In August, Qionghai averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 25.9 °C (79 °F), with 321 mm (12.6 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Qionghai?
August is not one of Qionghai’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qionghai in August?
321 mm (12.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (402 mm), the driest January (25 mm).
Does it snow in Qionghai in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qionghai get in August?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qionghai in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
