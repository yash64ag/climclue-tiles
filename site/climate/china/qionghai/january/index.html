How January compares in Qionghai

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Qionghai. Compared with December: 0.9 °C cooler, 34 mm less precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. February is 1.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Qionghai in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qionghai in January?
In January, Qionghai averages a day high of 22.4 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 16.6 °C (62 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 141 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Qionghai?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Qionghai’s mild months (January, February, March and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qionghai in January?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is October (402 mm), the driest January (25 mm).
Does it snow in Qionghai in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qionghai get in January?
About 141 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qionghai in January?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
