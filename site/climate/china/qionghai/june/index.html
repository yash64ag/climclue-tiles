How June compares in Qionghai

June is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Qionghai. Compared with May: 1.1 °C warmer, 48 mm less precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. July is about as warm as June on average.

Qionghai in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qionghai in June?
In June, Qionghai averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 26.4 °C (80 °F), with 213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Qionghai?
June is not one of Qionghai’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qionghai in June?
213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (402 mm), the driest January (25 mm).
Does it snow in Qionghai in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qionghai get in June?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qionghai in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
