Why Chenghua has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Chenghua climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Chenghua?
July, with an average of 28.6 °C (84 °F), day highs around 30.2 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 25.9 °C (79 °F).
What is the coldest month in Chenghua?
January, with an average of 14.7 °C (58 °F), day highs around 17.7 °C (64 °F) and night lows around 11.1 °C (52 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Chenghua?

What is the driest month in Chenghua?

What is the sunniest month in Chenghua?
July, with about 246 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Chenghua?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Chenghua?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, October, November and December.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Chenghua’s GeoNames location (23.461°N, 116.770°E; ClimaClue area sq-605-2967), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 152,453 (GeoNames).
