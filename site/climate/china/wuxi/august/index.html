How August compares in Wuxi

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Wuxi. Compared with July: 0.6 °C cooler, 30 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. September is 4.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Wuxi in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuxi in August?
In August, Wuxi averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Wuxi?
August is not one of Wuxi’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuxi in August?
157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (203 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Wuxi in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuxi get in August?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuxi in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
