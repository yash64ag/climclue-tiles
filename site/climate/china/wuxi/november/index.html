Wuxi, Jiangsu in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuxi, Jiangsu in November?
In November, Wuxi, Jiangsu averages a day high of 16.7 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 7.8 °C (46 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Wuxi, Jiangsu?
November is not one of Wuxi, Jiangsu’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuxi, Jiangsu in November?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (203 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Wuxi, Jiangsu in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuxi, Jiangsu get in November?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuxi, Jiangsu in November?
