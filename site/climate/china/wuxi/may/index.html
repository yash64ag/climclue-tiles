Wuxi, Jiangsu weather in May

Average May weather in Wuxi, Jiangsu, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Wuxi, Jiangsu averages 25.6 °C (78 °F) days and 16.5 °C (62 °F) nights, with 106 mm (4.2 in) of rain over the month and about 182 hours of sunshine.

What is the weather like in Wuxi, Jiangsu in May?

Is May a good time to visit Wuxi, Jiangsu?
May is not one of Wuxi, Jiangsu’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
