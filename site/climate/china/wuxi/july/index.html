Is July a good time to visit Wuxi, Jiangsu?
July is not one of Wuxi, Jiangsu’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
