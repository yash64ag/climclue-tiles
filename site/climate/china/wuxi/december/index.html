Wuxi, Jiangsu in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuxi, Jiangsu in December?
In December, Wuxi, Jiangsu averages a day high of 10.5 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 1.7 °C (35 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 152 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Wuxi, Jiangsu?
December is not one of Wuxi, Jiangsu’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuxi, Jiangsu in December?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (203 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Wuxi, Jiangsu in December?
Yes, on average: about 1.0 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
