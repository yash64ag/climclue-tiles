Taoluo weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Taoluo, China (Shandong), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Taoluo is warmest in August, with 29.0 °C (84 °F) days and 22.8 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 4.4 °C (40 °F) days and -3.6 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (219 mm); December is driest (12 mm).

Taoluo at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Taoluo is 1366th warmest (13.8 °C year average), 632nd sunniest (about 2,380 hours of sunshine a year) and 1246th wettest (830 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rizhao, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Taoluo is 0.3 °C warmer in August and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 19 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (25.9 °C), averages 25.5 °C more than its coolest, January (0.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 14 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Taoluo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Taoluo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.274°N, 119.370°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-487-2993), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
