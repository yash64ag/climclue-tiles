How October compares in Yangpu

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Yangpu. Compared with September: 5.1 °C cooler, 61 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. November is 5.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Yangpu in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yangpu in October?
In October, Yangpu averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 16.4 °C (62 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Yangpu?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Yangpu’s mild months (October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yangpu in October?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (222 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Yangpu in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yangpu get in October?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Yangpu in October?
About 21.1 °C (70 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (27.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Yangpu in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
