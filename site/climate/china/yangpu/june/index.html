How June compares in Yangpu

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Yangpu. Compared with May: 3.7 °C warmer, 103 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. July is 4.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Yangpu in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yangpu in June?
In June, Yangpu averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 217 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Yangpu?
June is not one of Yangpu’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yangpu in June?
217 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (222 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Yangpu in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yangpu get in June?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Yangpu in June?
About 22.4 °C (72 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (27.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Yangpu in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
