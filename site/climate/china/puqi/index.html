Puqi at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Puqi is 762nd warmest (17.5 °C year average), 1375th sunniest (about 1,827 hours of sunshine a year) and 249th wettest (1,618 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xindi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Puqi is about as warm in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 112 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.3 °C), averages 24.7 °C more than its coolest, January (4.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 696 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Puqi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Puqi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 696 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Puqi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (29.717°N, 113.883°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-542-2938), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
