Chengqiao at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Chengqiao is 318th warmest (16.4 °C year average), 364th sunniest (about 2,015 hours of sunshine a year) and 261st wettest (1,281 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yuepu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Chengqiao is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 77 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 23.8 °C more than its coolest, January (4.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 29 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chengqiao, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chengqiao has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 29 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
