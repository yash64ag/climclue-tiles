How November compares in Ma’anshan

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Ma’anshan. Compared with October: 6.4 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. December is 6.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Ma’anshan in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ma’anshan in November?
In November, Ma’anshan averages a day high of 16.1 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 7.1 °C (45 °F), with 64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Ma’anshan?
November is not one of Ma’anshan’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ma’anshan in November?
64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (209 mm), the driest December (35 mm).
Does it snow in Ma’anshan in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.9 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ma’anshan get in November?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ma’anshan in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
