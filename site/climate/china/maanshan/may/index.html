How May compares in Ma’anshan

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Ma’anshan. Compared with April: 5.3 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. June is 3.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Ma’anshan in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ma’anshan in May?
In May, Ma’anshan averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Ma’anshan?
May is not one of Ma’anshan’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ma’anshan in May?
112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (209 mm), the driest December (35 mm).
Does it snow in Ma’anshan in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ma’anshan get in May?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ma’anshan in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
