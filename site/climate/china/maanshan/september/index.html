Ma’anshan weather in September

Average September weather in Ma’anshan, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Ma’anshan averages 27.5 °C (82 °F) days and 19.5 °C (67 °F) nights, with 79 mm (3.1 in) of rain over the month and about 170 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Ma’anshan

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ma’anshan. Compared with August: 4.3 °C cooler, 75 mm less precipitation and 66 hours less sunshine. October is 5.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Ma’anshan in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ma’anshan in September?
In September, Ma’anshan averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Ma’anshan?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Ma’anshan’s mild months (April, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ma’anshan in September?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (213 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Ma’anshan in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ma’anshan get in September?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ma’anshan in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
