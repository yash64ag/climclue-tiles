How July compares in Ma’anshan

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Ma’anshan. Compared with June: 3.4 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. August is 0.7 °C cooler than July on average.

Ma’anshan in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ma’anshan in July?
In July, Ma’anshan averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Ma’anshan?
July is not one of Ma’anshan’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ma’anshan in July?
213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (213 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Ma’anshan in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ma’anshan get in July?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ma’anshan in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
