How October compares in Kaifeng

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Kaifeng. Compared with September: 5.6 °C cooler, 35 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. November is 7.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Kaifeng in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kaifeng in October?
In October, Kaifeng averages a day high of 21.6 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 10.9 °C (52 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kaifeng?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Kaifeng’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kaifeng in October?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (172 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Kaifeng in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kaifeng get in October?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kaifeng in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
