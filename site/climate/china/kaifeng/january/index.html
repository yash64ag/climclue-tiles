How January compares in Kaifeng

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Kaifeng. Compared with December: 2.0 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. February is 3.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Kaifeng in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kaifeng in January?
In January, Kaifeng averages a day high of 6.2 °C (43 °F) and a night low of -4.2 °C (24 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Kaifeng?
January is not one of Kaifeng’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kaifeng in January?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (172 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Kaifeng in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.6 cm (3.8 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Kaifeng get in January?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kaifeng in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
