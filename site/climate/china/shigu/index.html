Shigu at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Shigu is 1117th warmest (15.4 °C year average), 803rd sunniest (about 2,229 hours of sunshine a year) and 1272nd wettest (805 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jiuhe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Shigu is 1.9 °C warmer in June and 4.0 °C warmer in January, with 216 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (20.1 °C), averages 10.8 °C more than its coolest, January (9.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 971 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shigu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shigu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 971 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.8 °C. The 10 km area around Shigu, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 13.2 °C a year, 2.2 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Shigu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.869°N, 99.956°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-571-2799), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
