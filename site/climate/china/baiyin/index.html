Why Baiyin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,211 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Baiyin climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Baiyin?
July, with an average of 22.1 °C (72 °F), day highs around 27.3 °C (81 °F) and night lows around 15.5 °C (60 °F).
What is the coldest month in Baiyin?
January, with an average of -5.5 °C (22 °F), day highs around -0.4 °C (31 °F) and night lows around -11.9 °C (11 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Baiyin?
August, with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 198 mm (7.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Baiyin?
December, with 1 mm (0.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Baiyin?
May, with about 250 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Baiyin?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 39 cm (15.3 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in March; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Baiyin?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August and September.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.9 × 11 km, that contains Baiyin’s GeoNames location (36.547°N, 104.170°E; ClimaClue area sq-474-2841), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 294,400 (GeoNames).
