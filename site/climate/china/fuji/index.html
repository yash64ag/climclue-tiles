Why Fuji has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 897 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Fuji climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Fuji?
July, with an average of 27.8 °C (82 °F), day highs around 30.9 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 23.7 °C (75 °F).
What is the coldest month in Fuji?
January, with an average of 8.3 °C (47 °F), day highs around 10.9 °C (52 °F) and night lows around 5.0 °C (41 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Fuji?
June, with 217 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,253 mm (49.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Fuji?
January, with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Fuji?
August, with about 195 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Fuji?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 1.0 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in December; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Fuji?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April and October.
What type of climate does Fuji have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.7 × 11 km, that contains Fuji’s GeoNames location (29.148°N, 105.374°E; ClimaClue area sq-548-2853), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 131,927 (GeoNames).
