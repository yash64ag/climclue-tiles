Datong, Shanxi in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Datong, Shanxi in November?
In November, Datong, Shanxi averages a day high of 5.8 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -6.6 °C (20 °F), with 6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Datong, Shanxi?
November is not one of Datong, Shanxi’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

6 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (101 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
