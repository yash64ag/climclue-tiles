How March compares in Datong

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Datong. Compared with February: 6.9 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 49 hours more sunshine. April is 7.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Datong in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Datong in March?
In March, Datong averages a day high of 8.1 °C (47 °F) and a night low of -5.5 °C (22 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Datong?
March is not one of Datong’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Datong in March?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (103 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Datong in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.7 cm (1.9 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Datong get in March?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Datong in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
