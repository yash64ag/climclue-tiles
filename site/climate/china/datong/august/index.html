August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Datong. Compared with July: 2.0 °C cooler, 21 mm less precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. September is 5.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Datong in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Datong in August?
In August, Datong averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 15.3 °C (60 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Datong?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Datong’s mild months (May, June, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Datong in August?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (103 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Datong in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Datong get in August?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Datong in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
