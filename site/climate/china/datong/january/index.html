How January compares in Datong

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Datong. Compared with December: 2.4 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. February is 4.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Datong in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Datong in January?
In January, Datong averages a day high of -3.0 °C (27 °F) and a night low of -16.8 °C (2 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Datong?
January is not one of Datong’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Datong in January?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (103 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Datong in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.8 cm (1.9 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Datong get in January?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Datong in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
