Datong, Shanxi in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Datong, Shanxi in January?
In January, Datong, Shanxi averages a day high of -3.6 °C (26 °F) and a night low of -16.7 °C (2 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Datong, Shanxi?
January is not one of Datong, Shanxi’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Datong, Shanxi in January?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (101 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Datong, Shanxi in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.5 cm (1.0 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Datong, Shanxi get in January?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Datong, Shanxi in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
