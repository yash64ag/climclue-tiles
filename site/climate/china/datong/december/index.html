How December compares in Datong

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the least sunny month in Datong. Compared with November: 7.2 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. January is 2.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Datong in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Datong in December?
In December, Datong averages a day high of -1.4 °C (29 °F) and a night low of -13.4 °C (8 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Datong?
December is not one of Datong’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Datong in December?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (103 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Datong in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.6 cm (1.8 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Datong get in December?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Datong in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
