How May compares in Datong

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Datong. Compared with April: 7.0 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 38 hours more sunshine. June is 4.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Datong in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Datong in May?
In May, Datong averages a day high of 22.7 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 8.2 °C (47 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 289 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Datong?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Datong’s mild months (May, June, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Datong in May?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (103 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Datong in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Datong get in May?
About 289 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Datong in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
