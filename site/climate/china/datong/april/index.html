April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Datong. Compared with March: 7.8 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. May is 7.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Datong in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Datong in April?
In April, Datong averages a day high of 16.1 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 0.6 °C (33 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 252 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Datong?
April is not one of Datong’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Datong in April?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (103 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Datong in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.4 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Datong get in April?
About 252 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Datong in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
