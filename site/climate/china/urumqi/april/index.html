Ürümqi in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ürümqi in April?
In April, Ürümqi averages a day high of 16.5 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 5.1 °C (41 °F), with 37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 255 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ürümqi?
April is not one of Ürümqi’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ürümqi in April?
37 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (42 mm), the driest January (12 mm).
Does it snow in Ürümqi in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ürümqi get in April?
About 255 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ürümqi in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
