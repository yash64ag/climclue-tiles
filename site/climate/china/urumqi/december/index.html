How December compares in Ürümqi

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Ürümqi. Compared with November: 7.6 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. January is 2.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Ürümqi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ürümqi in December?
In December, Ürümqi averages a day high of -2.7 °C (27 °F) and a night low of -11.9 °C (11 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 123 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ürümqi?
December is not one of Ürümqi’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ürümqi in December?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (41 mm), the driest January (13 mm).
Does it snow in Ürümqi in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 14 cm (5.4 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 9.8 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Ürümqi get in December?
About 123 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ürümqi in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
