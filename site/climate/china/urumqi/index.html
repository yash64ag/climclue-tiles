Ürümqi at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Ürümqi is 603rd warmest (7.8 °C year average), 37th sunniest (about 2,777 hours of sunshine a year) and 653rd wettest (317 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Changji, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Ürümqi is 2.5 °C cooler in July and 2.9 °C warmer in January, with 103 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.6 °C), averages 34.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-11.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 2,382 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Ürümqi has this climate
