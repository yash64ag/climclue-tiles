What is the weather like in Ürümqi in August?
In August, Ürümqi averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 16.1 °C (61 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 308 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Ürümqi?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Ürümqi’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
