Ürümqi weather in September

Average September weather in Ürümqi, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Ürümqi averages 22.8 °C (73 °F) days and 10.3 °C (51 °F) nights, with 25 mm (1.0 in) of rain over the month and about 267 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Ürümqi

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ürümqi. Compared with August: 5.5 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. October is 8.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Ürümqi in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ürümqi in September?
In September, Ürümqi averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 10.3 °C (51 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 267 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Ürümqi?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Ürümqi’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ürümqi in September?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (42 mm), the driest January (12 mm).
Does it snow in Ürümqi in September?

How many hours of sunshine does Ürümqi get in September?
