Ürümqi in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ürümqi in September?
In September, Ürümqi averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 10.9 °C (52 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 267 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Ürümqi?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Ürümqi’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ürümqi in September?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (41 mm), the driest January (13 mm).
Does it snow in Ürümqi in September?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.0 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in September (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ürümqi get in September?
About 267 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ürümqi in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
