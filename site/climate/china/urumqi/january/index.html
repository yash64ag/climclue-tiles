How January compares in Ürümqi

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Ürümqi. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. February is 3.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Ürümqi in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ürümqi in January?
In January, Ürümqi averages a day high of -4.8 °C (23 °F) and a night low of -14.1 °C (7 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Ürümqi?
January is not one of Ürümqi’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ürümqi in January?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (42 mm), the driest January (12 mm).
Does it snow in Ürümqi in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 13 cm (5.0 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 5.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Ürümqi get in January?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ürümqi in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
