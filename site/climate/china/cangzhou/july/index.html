Cangzhou weather in July

Average July weather in Cangzhou, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Cangzhou averages 31.8 °C (89 °F) days and 22.9 °C (73 °F) nights, with 181 mm (7.1 in) of rain over the month and about 223 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Cangzhou

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Cangzhou. Compared with June: 1.6 °C warmer, 102 mm more precipitation and 43 hours less sunshine. August is 1.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Cangzhou in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cangzhou in July?
In July, Cangzhou averages a day high of 31.8 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 181 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Cangzhou?
July is not one of Cangzhou’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cangzhou in July?
181 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (181 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Cangzhou in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cangzhou get in July?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
