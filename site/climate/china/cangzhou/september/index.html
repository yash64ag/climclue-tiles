How September compares in Cangzhou

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Cangzhou. Compared with August: 4.5 °C cooler, 89 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. October is 6.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Cangzhou in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cangzhou in September?
In September, Cangzhou averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.4 °C (62 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Cangzhou?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Cangzhou’s mild months (April, May and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cangzhou in September?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (180 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Cangzhou in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cangzhou get in September?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cangzhou in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
