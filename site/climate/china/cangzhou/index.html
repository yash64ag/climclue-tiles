Why Cangzhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 77 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Cangzhou climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Cangzhou?
July, with an average of 27.4 °C (81 °F), day highs around 31.6 °C (89 °F) and night lows around 22.9 °C (73 °F).
What is the coldest month in Cangzhou?
January, with an average of -2.5 °C (27 °F), day highs around 2.8 °C (37 °F) and night lows around -7.3 °C (19 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Cangzhou?
July, with 180 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 558 mm (22.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Cangzhou?
January, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Cangzhou?
May, with about 277 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Cangzhou?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 18 cm (7.0 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 1 month.
When is the best time for mild weather in Cangzhou?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May and September.
What type of climate does Cangzhou have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.7 × 11 km, that contains Cangzhou’s GeoNames location (38.311°N, 116.853°E; ClimaClue area sq-456-2968), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 527,681 (GeoNames).
