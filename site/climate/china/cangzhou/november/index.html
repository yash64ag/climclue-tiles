How November compares in Cangzhou

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Cangzhou. Compared with October: 8.8 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. December is 6.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Cangzhou in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cangzhou in November?
In November, Cangzhou averages a day high of 11.2 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 1.4 °C (35 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Cangzhou?
November is not one of Cangzhou’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cangzhou in November?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (181 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Cangzhou in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cangzhou get in November?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cangzhou in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
