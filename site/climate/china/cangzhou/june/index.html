Cangzhou in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cangzhou in June?
In June, Cangzhou averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Cangzhou?
June is not one of Cangzhou’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cangzhou in June?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (181 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Cangzhou in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cangzhou get in June?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cangzhou in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
