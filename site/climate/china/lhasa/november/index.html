How November compares in Lhasa

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Lhasa. Compared with October: 8.0 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. December is 2.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Lhasa in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lhasa in November?
In November, Lhasa averages a day high of 7.6 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -8.8 °C (16 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Lhasa?
No month in Lhasa is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lhasa in November?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (143 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Lhasa in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.7 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Lhasa get in November?
About 254 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lhasa in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
