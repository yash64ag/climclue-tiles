How September compares in Lhasa

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Lhasa. Compared with August: 1.8 °C cooler, 69 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. October is 5.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Lhasa in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lhasa in September?
In September, Lhasa averages a day high of 16.5 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 5.6 °C (42 °F), with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Lhasa?
No month in Lhasa is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lhasa in September?
83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (152 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Lhasa in September?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.5 cm (1.0 in) of fresh snow in September (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Lhasa get in September?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lhasa in September?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
