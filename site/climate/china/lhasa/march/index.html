How March compares in Lhasa

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Lhasa. Compared with February: 3.9 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. April is 3.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Lhasa in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lhasa in March?
In March, Lhasa averages a day high of 8.3 °C (47 °F) and a night low of -5.0 °C (23 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Lhasa?
No month in Lhasa is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lhasa in March?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (143 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Lhasa in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.2 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Lhasa get in March?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lhasa in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
