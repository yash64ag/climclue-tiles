How May compares in Lhasa

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Lhasa. Compared with April: 4.1 °C warmer, 25 mm more precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. June is 3.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Lhasa in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lhasa in May?
In May, Lhasa averages a day high of 19.8 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 5.7 °C (42 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 253 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Lhasa?
May is not one of Lhasa’s mild months; those are June, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lhasa in May?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (143 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Lhasa in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lhasa get in May?
About 253 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lhasa in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
