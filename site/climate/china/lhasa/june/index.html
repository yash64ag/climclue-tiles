How June compares in Lhasa

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Lhasa. Compared with May: 3.7 °C warmer, 50 mm more precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. July is 0.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Lhasa in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lhasa in June?
In June, Lhasa averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 7.0 °C (45 °F), with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Lhasa?
No month in Lhasa is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lhasa in June?
86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (152 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Lhasa in June?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.9 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in June (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Lhasa get in June?
About 232 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lhasa in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
