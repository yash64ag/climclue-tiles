Why Lhasa has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 779 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Lhasa climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Lhasa?
July, with an average of 14.8 °C (59 °F), day highs around 19.1 °C (66 °F) and night lows around 8.8 °C (48 °F).
What is the coldest month in Lhasa?
January, with an average of -4.5 °C (24 °F), day highs around 1.5 °C (35 °F) and night lows around -13.5 °C (8 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Lhasa?
August, with 152 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 544 mm (21.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Lhasa?
January, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Lhasa?
October, with about 261 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Lhasa?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 99 cm (39.1 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in April; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 5 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Lhasa?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Lhasa have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.7 × 11 km, that contains Lhasa’s GeoNames location (29.650°N, 91.100°E; ClimaClue area sq-543-2711), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 867,900 (GeoNames).
