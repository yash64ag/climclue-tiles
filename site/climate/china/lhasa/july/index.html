How July compares in Lhasa

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Lhasa. Compared with June: 0.3 °C warmer, 65 mm more precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. August is 0.8 °C cooler than July on average.

Lhasa in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lhasa in July?
In July, Lhasa averages a day high of 19.1 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 151 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Lhasa?
No month in Lhasa is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lhasa in July?
151 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (152 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Lhasa in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lhasa get in July?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lhasa in July?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
