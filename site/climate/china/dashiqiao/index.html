Dashiqiao at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Dashiqiao is 1753rd warmest (10.0 °C year average), 273rd sunniest (about 2,602 hours of sunshine a year) and 1471st wettest (657 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yingkou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Dashiqiao is about as warm in July and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.3 °C), averages 34.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-8.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 40 km from the sea.

Why Dashiqiao has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 40 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 34.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-433-3025), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
