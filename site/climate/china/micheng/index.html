Micheng at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Micheng is 676th warmest (17.9 °C year average), 844th sunniest (about 2,199 hours of sunshine a year) and 1518th wettest (631 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xiangcheng, Yunnan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Micheng is 1.7 °C warmer in June and 1.8 °C warmer in January, with 182 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (23.1 °C), averages 12.1 °C more than its coolest, January (11.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 837 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Micheng, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Micheng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 837 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.1 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Micheng itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.343°N, 100.487°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-586-2804), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
