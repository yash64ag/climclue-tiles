Jingdezhen at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Jingdezhen is 194th warmest (17.9 °C year average), 481st sunniest (about 1,892 hours of sunshine a year) and 6th wettest (2,424 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pengze, the nearest covered town with different numbers (93 km away), Jingdezhen is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 740 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 23.2 °C more than its coolest, January (5.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 386 km from the sea.

Why Jingdezhen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 386 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
