Haimen sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Guangdong · China

The sea off Haimen averages 27.1 °C (81 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 16.6 °C (62 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

Haimen sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Haimen?
In August, at about 27.1 °C (81 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Haimen?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is February, at 16.6 °C (62 °F).
How warm is the sea in Haimen in February?
About 16.6 °C (62 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Haimen?
About 22.5 °C (73 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Haimen nearby?
In August: Huicheng, Guangdong (27.9 °C, 37 km), Jiazi (27.9 °C, 65 km) and Jieshi, Guangdong (28.5 °C, 91 km).
