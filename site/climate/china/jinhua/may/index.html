Jinhua, Zhejiang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinhua, Zhejiang in May?
In May, Jinhua, Zhejiang averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 18.5 °C (65 °F), with 185 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Jinhua, Zhejiang?
May is not one of Jinhua, Zhejiang’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinhua, Zhejiang in May?
185 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (270 mm), the driest December (50 mm).
Does it snow in Jinhua, Zhejiang in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jinhua, Zhejiang get in May?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jinhua, Zhejiang in May?
