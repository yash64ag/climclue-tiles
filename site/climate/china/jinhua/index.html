Jinhua at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Jinhua is 187th warmest (18.0 °C year average), 485th sunniest (about 1,885 hours of sunshine a year) and 127th wettest (1,620 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Guli, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Jinhua is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 35 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.4 °C), averages 23.6 °C more than its coolest, January (5.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 183 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jinhua, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jinhua has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 183 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
