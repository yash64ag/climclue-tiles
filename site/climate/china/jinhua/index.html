Why Jinhua has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 183 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Jinhua climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Jinhua?
July, with an average of 29.4 °C (85 °F), day highs around 33.1 °C (92 °F) and night lows around 25.0 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Jinhua?
January, with an average of 5.8 °C (43 °F), day highs around 9.3 °C (49 °F) and night lows around 1.1 °C (34 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Jinhua?
June, with 286 mm (11.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,620 mm (63.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Jinhua?
December, with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Jinhua?
August, with about 248 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Jinhua?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 24 cm (9.3 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Jinhua?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: October.
What type of climate does Jinhua have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.7 × 11 km, that contains Jinhua’s GeoNames location (29.107°N, 119.644°E; ClimaClue area sq-548-2996), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,463,990 (GeoNames).
