How July compares in Jinhua

July is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Jinhua. Compared with June: 3.9 °C warmer, 138 mm less precipitation and 92 hours more sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Jinhua in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinhua in July?
In July, Jinhua averages a day high of 33.1 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 25.0 °C (77 °F), with 148 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Jinhua?
July is not one of Jinhua’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinhua in July?
148 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (286 mm), the driest December (53 mm).
Does it snow in Jinhua in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jinhua get in July?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jinhua in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
