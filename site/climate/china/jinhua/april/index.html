Jinhua, Zhejiang weather in April

Average April weather in Jinhua, Zhejiang, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Jinhua, Zhejiang averages 22.2 °C (72 °F) days and 13.2 °C (56 °F) nights, with 174 mm (6.9 in) of rain over the month and about 133 hours of sunshine.
