How April compares in Jinhua

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Jinhua. Compared with March: 5.9 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. May is 5.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Jinhua in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinhua in April?
In April, Jinhua averages a day high of 21.6 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 174 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 133 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Jinhua?
April is not one of Jinhua’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinhua in April?
174 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (270 mm), the driest December (50 mm).
Does it snow in Jinhua in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jinhua get in April?
About 133 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jinhua in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
