How December compares in Zhuzhou

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Zhuzhou. Compared with November: 5.6 °C cooler, 34 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. January is 2.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Zhuzhou in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhuzhou in December?
In December, Zhuzhou averages a day high of 12.5 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 3.9 °C (39 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 106 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Zhuzhou?
December is not one of Zhuzhou’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhuzhou in December?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (238 mm), the driest December (56 mm).
Does it snow in Zhuzhou in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhuzhou get in December?
About 106 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhuzhou in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
