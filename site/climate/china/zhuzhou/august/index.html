How August compares in Zhuzhou

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Zhuzhou. Compared with July: 0.9 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. September is 4.1 °C cooler than August on average.

Zhuzhou in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhuzhou in August?
In August, Zhuzhou averages a day high of 33.6 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Zhuzhou?
August is not one of Zhuzhou’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhuzhou in August?
135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (234 mm), the driest December (55 mm).
Does it snow in Zhuzhou in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhuzhou get in August?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhuzhou in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
