Why Changsha, Guangdong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 70 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Changsha, Guangdong climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Changsha, Guangdong?
July, with an average of 29.1 °C (84 °F), day highs around 31.4 °C (89 °F) and night lows around 25.6 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Changsha, Guangdong?
January, with an average of 14.8 °C (59 °F), day highs around 19.1 °C (66 °F) and night lows around 10.5 °C (51 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Changsha, Guangdong?
June, with 374 mm (14.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 2,143 mm (84.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Changsha, Guangdong?
December, with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Changsha, Guangdong?
July, with about 229 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Changsha, Guangdong?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Changsha, Guangdong?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, March, October, November and December.
What type of climate does Changsha, Guangdong have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Changsha’s GeoNames location (22.381°N, 112.685°E; ClimaClue area sq-616-2926), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 688,242 (GeoNames).
