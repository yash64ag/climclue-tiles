How December compares in Changsha, Guangdong

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Changsha, Guangdong. Compared with November: 4.2 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Changsha, Guangdong in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changsha, Guangdong in December?
In December, Changsha, Guangdong averages a day high of 20.5 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 11.3 °C (52 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Changsha, Guangdong?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Changsha, Guangdong’s mild months (February, March, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changsha, Guangdong in December?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (374 mm), the driest December (30 mm).
Does it snow in Changsha, Guangdong in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Changsha, Guangdong get in December?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
