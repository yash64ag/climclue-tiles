How October compares in Changsha, Guangdong

Changsha, Guangdong in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changsha, Guangdong in October?
In October, Changsha, Guangdong averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 20.5 °C (69 °F), with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Changsha, Guangdong?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Changsha, Guangdong’s mild months (February, March, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changsha, Guangdong in October?
90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (374 mm), the driest December (30 mm).
Does it snow in Changsha, Guangdong in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Changsha, Guangdong get in October?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Changsha, Guangdong in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
