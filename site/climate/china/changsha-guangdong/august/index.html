Changsha, Guangdong in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changsha, Guangdong in August?
In August, Changsha, Guangdong averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 324 mm (12.8 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Changsha, Guangdong?
August is not one of Changsha, Guangdong’s mild months; those are February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changsha, Guangdong in August?
324 mm (12.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (374 mm), the driest December (30 mm).
Does it snow in Changsha, Guangdong in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Changsha, Guangdong get in August?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Changsha, Guangdong in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
