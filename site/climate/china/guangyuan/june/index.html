How June compares in Guangyuan

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Guangyuan. Compared with May: 3.0 °C warmer, 29 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. July is 1.9 °C warmer than June on average.

Guangyuan in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guangyuan in June?
In June, Guangyuan averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 18.9 °C (66 °F), with 126 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Guangyuan?
June is not one of Guangyuan’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guangyuan in June?
126 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (280 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Guangyuan in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guangyuan get in June?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guangyuan in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
