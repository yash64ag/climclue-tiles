How October compares in Guangyuan

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Guangyuan. Compared with September: 4.7 °C cooler, 123 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. November is 5.2 °C cooler than October on average.

Guangyuan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guangyuan in October?
In October, Guangyuan averages a day high of 19.1 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 11.7 °C (53 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 96 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Guangyuan?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Guangyuan’s mild months (April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guangyuan in October?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (280 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Guangyuan in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guangyuan get in October?
About 96 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guangyuan in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
