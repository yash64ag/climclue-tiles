How September compares in Guangyuan

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Guangyuan. Compared with August: 4.1 °C cooler, 27 mm less precipitation and 90 hours less sunshine. October is 4.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Guangyuan in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guangyuan in September?
In September, Guangyuan averages a day high of 23.9 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 173 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 110 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Guangyuan?
September is not one of Guangyuan’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guangyuan in September?
173 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (280 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Guangyuan in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guangyuan get in September?
About 110 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guangyuan in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
