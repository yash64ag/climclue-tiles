How March compares in Guangyuan

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Guangyuan. Compared with February: 4.0 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. April is 4.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Guangyuan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guangyuan in March?
In March, Guangyuan averages a day high of 15.6 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 6.0 °C (43 °F), with 19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 117 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Guangyuan?
March is not one of Guangyuan’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guangyuan in March?
19 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (268 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Guangyuan in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guangyuan get in March?
About 117 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guangyuan in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
