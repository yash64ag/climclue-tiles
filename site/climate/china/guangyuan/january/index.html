How January compares in Guangyuan

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Guangyuan. Compared with December: 1.1 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 2.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Guangyuan in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guangyuan in January?
In January, Guangyuan averages a day high of 8.4 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 0.3 °C (33 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 96 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Guangyuan?
January is not one of Guangyuan’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guangyuan in January?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (280 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Guangyuan in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.3 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Guangyuan get in January?
About 96 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guangyuan in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
