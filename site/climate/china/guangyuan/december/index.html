How December compares in Guangyuan

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Guangyuan. Compared with November: 4.9 °C cooler, 15 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. January is 1.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Guangyuan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guangyuan in December?
In December, Guangyuan averages a day high of 9.5 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 1.5 °C (35 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 90 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Guangyuan?
December is not one of Guangyuan’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guangyuan in December?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (280 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Guangyuan in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.7 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Guangyuan get in December?
About 90 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guangyuan in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
