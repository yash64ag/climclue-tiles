Jiading at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Jiading is 307th warmest (16.5 °C year average), 395th sunniest (about 1,979 hours of sunshine a year) and 212th wettest (1,411 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Luodian, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Jiading is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 52 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.3 °C), averages 23.9 °C more than its coolest, January (4.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 31 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 21 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.2 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 121% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Jiading has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 31 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
