Jiading weather in April

Average April weather in Jiading, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Jiading averages 19.1 °C (66 °F) days and 10.9 °C (52 °F) nights, with 98 mm (3.9 in) of rain over the month and about 157 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Jiading

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Jiading. Compared with March: 5.4 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. May is 5.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Jiading in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiading in April?
In April, Jiading averages a day high of 19.1 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 10.9 °C (52 °F), with 98 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Jiading?
April is not one of Jiading’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiading in April?
98 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (224 mm), the driest December (45 mm).
Does it snow in Jiading in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiading get in April?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiading in April?
