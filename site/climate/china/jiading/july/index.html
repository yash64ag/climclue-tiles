How July compares in Jiading

July is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Jiading. Compared with June: 4.2 °C warmer, 28 mm less precipitation and 58 hours more sunshine. August is 0.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Jiading in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiading in July?
In July, Jiading averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 25.4 °C (78 °F), with 195 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Jiading?
July is not one of Jiading’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiading in July?
195 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (224 mm), the driest December (45 mm).
Does it snow in Jiading in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiading get in July?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiading in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
