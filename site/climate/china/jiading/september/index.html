Jiading weather in September

Average September weather in Jiading, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Jiading averages 27.3 °C (81 °F) days and 20.6 °C (69 °F) nights, with 127 mm (5.0 in) of rain over the month and about 166 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Jiading

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Jiading. Compared with August: 3.9 °C cooler, 97 mm less precipitation and 71 hours less sunshine. October is 5.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Jiading in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiading in September?
In September, Jiading averages a day high of 27.3 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 127 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Jiading?
September is not one of Jiading’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiading in September?
127 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (224 mm), the driest December (45 mm).
Does it snow in Jiading in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiading get in September?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiading in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
