Why Changyuan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 921 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Changyuan climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Changyuan?
July, with an average of 27.6 °C (82 °F), day highs around 30.6 °C (87 °F) and night lows around 23.5 °C (74 °F).
What is the coldest month in Changyuan?
January, with an average of 8.0 °C (46 °F), day highs around 10.6 °C (51 °F) and night lows around 4.6 °C (40 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Changyuan?
July, with 235 mm (9.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,276 mm (50.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Changyuan?
January, with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Changyuan?
August, with about 197 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Changyuan?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 1.7 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in December; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Changyuan?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April and October.
What type of climate does Changyuan have?
Humid subtropical, dry winter (Köppen Cwa). Hot, wet summers and mild, dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.7 × 11 km, that contains Changyuan’s GeoNames location (29.405°N, 105.588°E; ClimaClue area sq-545-2855), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 186,653 (GeoNames).
