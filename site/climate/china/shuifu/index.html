Shuifu at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Shuifu is 215th warmest (17.6 °C year average), 613th sunniest (about 1,525 hours of sunshine a year) and 321st wettest (1,139 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yibin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Shuifu is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 27 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.6 °C), averages 19.3 °C more than its coolest, January (7.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 883 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shuifu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shuifu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 883 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
