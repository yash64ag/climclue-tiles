Jiuquan at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Jiuquan is 1865th warmest (8.3 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 3,027 hours of sunshine a year) and 2029th wettest (116 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jiayuguan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Jiuquan is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. The area lies about 1,645 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Jiuquan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,645 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jiuquan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (39.743°N, 98.517°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-442-2785), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
