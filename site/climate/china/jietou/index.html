Jietou at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Jietou is 898th warmest (16.8 °C year average), 948th sunniest (about 2,120 hours of sunshine a year) and 738th wettest (1,232 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mingguang, Yunnan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Jietou is 2.2 °C warmer in August and 2.5 °C warmer in January, with 729 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (21.7 °C), averages 11.6 °C more than its coolest, January (10.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 785 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jietou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jietou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 785 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jietou itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.427°N, 98.653°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-585-2786), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
