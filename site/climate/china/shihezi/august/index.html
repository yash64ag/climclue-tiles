How August compares in Shihezi

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Shihezi. Compared with July: 1.5 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. September is 5.9 °C cooler than August on average.

Shihezi in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shihezi in August?
In August, Shihezi averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 17.8 °C (64 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 309 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Shihezi?
August is not one of Shihezi’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shihezi in August?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (32 mm), the driest February (9 mm).
Does it snow in Shihezi in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shihezi get in August?
About 309 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shihezi in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
