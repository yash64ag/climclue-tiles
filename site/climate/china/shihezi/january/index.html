Shihezi weather in January

Average January weather in Shihezi, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Shihezi averages -11.1 °C (12 °F) days and -20.8 °C (-5 °F) nights, with 9 mm (0.4 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 147 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Shihezi

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Shihezi. Compared with December: 4.1 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. February is 5.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Shihezi in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shihezi in January?
In January, Shihezi averages a day high of -11.1 °C (12 °F) and a night low of -20.8 °C (-5 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Shihezi?
January is not one of Shihezi’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shihezi in January?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (34 mm), the driest February (9 mm).
Does it snow in Shihezi in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.7 cm (3.4 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Shihezi get in January?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shihezi in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
