How December compares in Shihezi

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Shihezi. Compared with November: 11.1 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. January is 4.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Shihezi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shihezi in December?
In December, Shihezi averages a day high of -7.2 °C (19 °F) and a night low of -14.9 °C (5 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 124 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Shihezi?
December is not one of Shihezi’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shihezi in December?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (32 mm), the driest February (9 mm).
Does it snow in Shihezi in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 10 cm (4.0 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 6.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Shihezi get in December?
About 124 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shihezi in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
