How September compares in Shihezi

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Shihezi. Compared with August: 5.9 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 45 hours less sunshine. October is 8.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Shihezi in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shihezi in September?
In September, Shihezi averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 12.4 °C (54 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Shihezi?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Shihezi’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shihezi in September?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (32 mm), the driest February (9 mm).
Does it snow in Shihezi in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shihezi get in September?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shihezi in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
