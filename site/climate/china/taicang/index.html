Taicang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Taicang is 314th warmest (16.5 °C year average), 383rd sunniest (about 1,984 hours of sunshine a year) and 249th wettest (1,319 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kunshan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Taicang is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 21 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.3 °C), averages 24.1 °C more than its coolest, January (4.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 47 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Taicang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Taicang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 47 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
