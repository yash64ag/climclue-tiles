How August compares in Taicang

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Taicang. Compared with July: 0.4 °C cooler, 15 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. September is 4.0 °C cooler than August on average.

Taicang in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taicang in August?
In August, Taicang averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 212 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Taicang?
August is not one of Taicang’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taicang in August?
212 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (221 mm), the driest December (42 mm).
Does it snow in Taicang in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taicang get in August?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taicang in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
