Taicang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taicang in April?
In April, Taicang averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 10.8 °C (51 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Taicang?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Taicang’s mild months (April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taicang in April?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (214 mm), the driest December (40 mm).
Does it snow in Taicang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taicang get in April?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taicang in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
