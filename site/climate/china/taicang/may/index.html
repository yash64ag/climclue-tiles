How May compares in Taicang

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Taicang. Compared with April: 5.4 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. June is 3.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Taicang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taicang in May?
In May, Taicang averages a day high of 24.7 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Taicang?
May is not one of Taicang’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taicang in May?
112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (221 mm), the driest December (42 mm).
Does it snow in Taicang in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taicang get in May?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taicang in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
