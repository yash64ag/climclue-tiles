How December compares in Taicang

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Taicang. Compared with November: 6.1 °C cooler, 22 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. January is 2.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Taicang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taicang in December?
In December, Taicang averages a day high of 10.8 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 2.1 °C (36 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Taicang?
December is not one of Taicang’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taicang in December?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (214 mm), the driest December (40 mm).
Does it snow in Taicang in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.8 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Taicang get in December?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taicang in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
