How September compares in Taicang

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Taicang. Compared with August: 4.1 °C cooler, 90 mm less precipitation and 71 hours less sunshine. October is 5.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Taicang in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taicang in September?
In September, Taicang averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 113 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Taicang?
September is not one of Taicang’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taicang in September?
113 mm (4.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (214 mm), the driest December (40 mm).
Does it snow in Taicang in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taicang get in September?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taicang in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
