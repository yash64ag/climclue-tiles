How June compares in Taicang

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Taicang. Compared with May: 3.8 °C warmer, 109 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. July is 4.0 °C warmer than June on average.

Taicang in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taicang in June?
In June, Taicang averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 221 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Taicang?
June is not one of Taicang’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taicang in June?
221 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (221 mm), the driest December (42 mm).
Does it snow in Taicang in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taicang get in June?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taicang in June?
