Laibin weather in March

Average March weather in Laibin, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Laibin averages 20.2 °C (68 °F) days and 13.8 °C (57 °F) nights, with 81 mm (3.2 in) of rain over the month and about 63 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Laibin

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Laibin. Compared with February: 3.3 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. April is 5.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Laibin in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Laibin in March?
In March, Laibin averages a day high of 20.2 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 63 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Laibin?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Laibin’s mild months (March, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Laibin in March?
81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (251 mm), the driest December (28 mm).
Does it snow in Laibin in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Laibin get in March?
About 63 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Laibin in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
