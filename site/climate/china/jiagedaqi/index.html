Jiagedaqi at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Jiagedaqi is the 4th coolest (-0.5 °C year average). Compared with Daxing’anling, the nearest covered town with different numbers (217 km away), Jiagedaqi is about as warm in July and 1.5 °C warmer in January, with 68 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.4 °C), averages 42.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-22.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 899 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Jiagedaqi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 899 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 42.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
