Shanwei at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Shanwei is 64th warmest (22.4 °C year average), 372nd sunniest (about 2,004 hours of sunshine a year) and 8th wettest (2,409 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Donghai, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Shanwei is 0.8 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 53 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 12.4 °C more than its coolest, January (15.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.4 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 123% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Shanwei has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
