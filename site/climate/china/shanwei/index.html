Shanwei at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Shanwei is 120th warmest (22.6 °C year average), 1085th sunniest (about 2,004 hours of sunshine a year) and 12th wettest (2,387 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Donghai, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Shanwei is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 62 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.2 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, January (15.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.8 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 121% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Shanwei has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Shanwei itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.782°N, 115.348°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-612-2953), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
