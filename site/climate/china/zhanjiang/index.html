Zhanjiang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Zhanjiang is 9th warmest (23.7 °C year average), 455th sunniest (about 1,920 hours of sunshine a year) and 56th wettest (1,988 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Wuchuan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Zhanjiang is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 109 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 12.4 °C more than its coolest, January (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.3 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 38 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Zhanjiang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Zhanjiang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
