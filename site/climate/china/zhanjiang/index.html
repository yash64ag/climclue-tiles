Zhanjiang at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Zhanjiang is 31st warmest (23.8 °C year average), 1240th sunniest (about 1,920 hours of sunshine a year) and 101st wettest (1,982 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Wuchuan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Zhanjiang is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 101 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.0 °C), averages 12.4 °C more than its coolest, January (16.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.3 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 38 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Zhanjiang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Zhanjiang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Zhanjiang itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.234°N, 110.387°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-627-2903), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
