How February compares in Zhanjiang

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Zhanjiang. Compared with January: 1.2 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 49 hours less sunshine. March is 2.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Zhanjiang in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhanjiang in February?
In February, Zhanjiang averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 15.3 °C (60 °F), with 50 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 70 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Zhanjiang?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Zhanjiang’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhanjiang in February?
50 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (353 mm), the driest January (28 mm).
Does it snow in Zhanjiang in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhanjiang get in February?
About 70 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhanjiang in February?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
