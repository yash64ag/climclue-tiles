How November compares in Zhanjiang

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Zhanjiang. Compared with October: 3.7 °C cooler, 75 mm less precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. December is 3.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Zhanjiang in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhanjiang in November?
In November, Zhanjiang averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Zhanjiang?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Zhanjiang’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhanjiang in November?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (353 mm), the driest January (28 mm).
Does it snow in Zhanjiang in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhanjiang get in November?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhanjiang in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
