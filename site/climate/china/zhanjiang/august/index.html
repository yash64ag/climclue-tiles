Zhanjiang weather in August

Average August weather in Zhanjiang, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Zhanjiang averages 31.6 °C (89 °F) days and 25.5 °C (78 °F) nights, with 348 mm (13.7 in) of rain over the month and about 206 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Zhanjiang

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Zhanjiang. Compared with July: 0.4 °C cooler, 72 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. September is 1.1 °C cooler than August on average.

Zhanjiang in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhanjiang in August?
In August, Zhanjiang averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 25.5 °C (78 °F), with 348 mm (13.7 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Zhanjiang?
August is not one of Zhanjiang’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhanjiang in August?
348 mm (13.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (348 mm), the driest January (28 mm).
Does it snow in Zhanjiang in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhanjiang get in August?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Zhanjiang in August?
About 29.3 °C (85 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (29.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Zhanjiang in August?
