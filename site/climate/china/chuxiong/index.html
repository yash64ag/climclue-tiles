Chuxiong at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Chuxiong is 243rd warmest (17.2 °C year average), 284th sunniest (about 2,227 hours of sunshine a year) and 408th wettest (805 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jianshui, the nearest covered town with different numbers (90 km away), Chuxiong is 2.7 °C warmer in June and 1.6 °C warmer in December, with 780 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (21.9 °C), averages 11.6 °C more than its coolest, December (10.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 731 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.0 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 90% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Chuxiong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 731 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
