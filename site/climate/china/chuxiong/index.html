Chuxiong at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Chuxiong is 767th warmest (17.5 °C year average), 808th sunniest (about 2,227 hours of sunshine a year) and 1294th wettest (779 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Guangtong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Chuxiong is 0.1 °C warmer in June and 0.1 °C warmer in December, with 25 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (22.2 °C), averages 11.6 °C more than its coolest, December (10.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 731 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Chuxiong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 731 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-589-2815), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
