Chuxiong in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chuxiong in April?
In April, Chuxiong averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 12.3 °C (54 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Chuxiong?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Chuxiong’s mild months (March, April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
