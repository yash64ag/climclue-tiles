Chuxiong in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chuxiong in December?
In December, Chuxiong averages a day high of 15.4 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Chuxiong?
December is not one of Chuxiong’s mild months; those are March, April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chuxiong in December?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (180 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Chuxiong in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chuxiong get in December?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
