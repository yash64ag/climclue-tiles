Yichang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Yichang is 252nd warmest (17.1 °C year average), 591st sunniest (about 1,628 hours of sunshine a year) and 246th wettest (1,326 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zhicheng, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), Yichang is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 26 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 23.1 °C more than its coolest, January (4.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 875 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Yichang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 875 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
