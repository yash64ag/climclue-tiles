Yichang in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yichang in October?
In October, Yichang averages a day high of 21.4 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Yichang?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Yichang’s mild months (April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yichang in October?
99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (253 mm), the driest December (22 mm).
Does it snow in Yichang in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yichang get in October?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yichang in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
