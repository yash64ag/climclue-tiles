How September compares in Yichang

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Yichang. Compared with August: 3.8 °C cooler, 80 mm less precipitation and 72 hours less sunshine. October is 5.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Yichang in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yichang in September?
In September, Yichang averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Yichang?
September is not one of Yichang’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yichang in September?
117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (233 mm), the driest December (20 mm).
Does it snow in Yichang in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yichang get in September?
About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yichang in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
