Fengcheng, Liaoning at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Fengcheng, Liaoning is 584th warmest (8.7 °C year average), 181st sunniest (about 2,524 hours of sunshine a year) and 354th wettest (1,014 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dandong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Fengcheng, Liaoning is 0.6 °C cooler in August and 1.5 °C cooler in January, with 36 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (23.4 °C), averages 32.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-9.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. Why Fengcheng, Liaoning has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 78 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 32.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
