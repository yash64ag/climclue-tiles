Donghai at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Donghai is 49th warmest (22.7 °C year average), 375th sunniest (about 2,000 hours of sunshine a year) and 11th wettest (2,356 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jieshi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Donghai is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 223 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 13.5 °C more than its coolest, January (15.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.8 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 22 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Donghai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Donghai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
