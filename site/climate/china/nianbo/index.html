Nianbo at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Nianbo is 613th warmest (6.9 °C year average), 94th sunniest (about 2,649 hours of sunshine a year) and 646th wettest (345 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xining, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Nianbo is 1.0 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 89 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.9 °C), averages 24.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-6.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 1,360 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nianbo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nianbo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,360 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.4 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
