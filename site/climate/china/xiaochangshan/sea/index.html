Xiaochangshan sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Liaoning · China

The sea off Xiaochangshan averages 24.2 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 1.5 °C (35 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Xiaochangshan sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Xiaochangshan?
In August, at about 24.2 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Xiaochangshan?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 1.5 °C (35 °F).
How warm is the sea in Xiaochangshan in February?
About 1.5 °C (35 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Xiaochangshan?
About 12.3 °C (54 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Xiaochangshan nearby?
In August: Yingkou (25.5 °C, 164 km).
