Liuzhou weather in January

Average January weather in Liuzhou, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Liuzhou averages 15.1 °C (59 °F) days and 7.3 °C (45 °F) nights, with 58 mm (2.3 in) of rain over the month and about 79 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Liuzhou

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Liuzhou. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, 22 mm more precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. February is 1.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Liuzhou in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Liuzhou in January?
In January, Liuzhou averages a day high of 15.1 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 7.3 °C (45 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 79 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Liuzhou?
January is not one of Liuzhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Liuzhou in January?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (293 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Liuzhou in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Liuzhou get in January?
About 79 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Liuzhou in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
