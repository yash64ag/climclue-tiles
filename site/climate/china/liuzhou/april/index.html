How April compares in Liuzhou

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Liuzhou. Compared with March: 5.5 °C warmer, 41 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. May is 3.7 °C warmer than April on average.

Liuzhou in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Liuzhou in April?
In April, Liuzhou averages a day high of 24.8 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 17.6 °C (64 °F), with 143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 82 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Liuzhou?
April is not one of Liuzhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Liuzhou in April?
143 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (293 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Liuzhou in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Liuzhou get in April?
About 82 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Liuzhou in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
