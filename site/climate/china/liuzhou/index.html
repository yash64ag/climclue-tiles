Liuzhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Liuzhou is 93rd warmest (21.3 °C year average), 605th sunniest (about 1,568 hours of sunshine a year) and 130th wettest (1,608 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Laibin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Liuzhou is about as warm in July and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 264 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.1 °C), averages 17.9 °C more than its coolest, January (11.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.5 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 311 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Liuzhou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Liuzhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 311 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
