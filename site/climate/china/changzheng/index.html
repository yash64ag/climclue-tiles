Changzheng at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Changzheng is 297th warmest (16.6 °C year average), 407th sunniest (about 1,969 hours of sunshine a year) and 188th wettest (1,454 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Changning, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Changzheng is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 5 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.3 °C), averages 23.8 °C more than its coolest, January (4.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 29 km from the sea.

Why Changzheng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 29 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
