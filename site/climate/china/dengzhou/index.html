Dengzhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Dengzhou is 330th warmest (16.2 °C year average), 459th sunniest (about 1,918 hours of sunshine a year) and 379th wettest (900 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zhenping, the nearest covered town with different numbers (41 km away), Dengzhou is 0.8 °C warmer in July and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.8 °C), averages 24.9 °C more than its coolest, January (2.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 709 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dengzhou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dengzhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 709 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
