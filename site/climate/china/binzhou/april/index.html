Binzhou weather in April

Average April weather in Binzhou, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

How April compares in Binzhou

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Binzhou. Compared with March: 7.8 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. May is 5.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Binzhou in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Binzhou in April?
In April, Binzhou averages a day high of 21.3 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 8.6 °C (47 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Binzhou?
April is not one of Binzhou’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Binzhou in April?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (168 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Binzhou in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Binzhou get in April?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Binzhou in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
