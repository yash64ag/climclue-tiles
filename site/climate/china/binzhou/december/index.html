Binzhou weather in December

Average December weather in Binzhou, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Binzhou averages 5.1 °C (41 °F) days and -4.8 °C (23 °F) nights, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 177 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Binzhou

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the least sunny month in Binzhou. Compared with November: 6.6 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. January is 2.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Binzhou in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Binzhou in December?
In December, Binzhou averages a day high of 5.1 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -4.8 °C (23 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Binzhou?
December is not one of Binzhou’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Binzhou in December?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (168 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Binzhou in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.9 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Binzhou get in December?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Binzhou in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
