How November compares in Binzhou

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Binzhou. Compared with October: 8.3 °C cooler, 15 mm less precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. December is 6.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Binzhou in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Binzhou in November?
In November, Binzhou averages a day high of 12.6 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 1.6 °C (35 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Binzhou?
November is not one of Binzhou’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Binzhou in November?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (167 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Binzhou in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Binzhou get in November?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Binzhou in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
