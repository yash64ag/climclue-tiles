Baoshan, Shanghai in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baoshan, Shanghai in October?
In October, Baoshan, Shanghai averages a day high of 22.9 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 16.4 °C (62 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Baoshan, Shanghai?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Baoshan, Shanghai’s mild months (April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baoshan, Shanghai in October?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (221 mm), the driest December (46 mm).
Does it snow in Baoshan, Shanghai in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baoshan, Shanghai get in October?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Baoshan, Shanghai in October?
About 21.1 °C (70 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (27.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
