Baoshan, Shanghai in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baoshan, Shanghai in November?
In November, Baoshan, Shanghai averages a day high of 16.3 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 10.1 °C (50 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Baoshan, Shanghai?
November is not one of Baoshan, Shanghai’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
