Why Baoshan, Shanghai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Baoshan, Shanghai climate: questions and answers

What is the coldest month in Baoshan, Shanghai?
January, with an average of 4.5 °C (40 °F), day highs around 7.6 °C (46 °F) and night lows around 1.3 °C (34 °F).

What is the sunniest month in Baoshan, Shanghai?

Does it snow in Baoshan, Shanghai?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 7.6 cm (3.0 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Baoshan, Shanghai?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.5 × 11 km, that contains Baoshan’s GeoNames location (31.408°N, 121.490°E; ClimaClue area sq-525-3014), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 2,265,900 (GeoNames).
