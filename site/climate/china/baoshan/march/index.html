Baoshan, Shanghai in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baoshan, Shanghai in March?
In March, Baoshan, Shanghai averages a day high of 13.5 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 6.6 °C (44 °F), with 108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Baoshan, Shanghai?
March is not one of Baoshan, Shanghai’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baoshan, Shanghai in March?
108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (221 mm), the driest December (46 mm).
Does it snow in Baoshan, Shanghai in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baoshan, Shanghai get in March?
About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Baoshan, Shanghai in March?
About 8.5 °C (47 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (27.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Baoshan, Shanghai in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
