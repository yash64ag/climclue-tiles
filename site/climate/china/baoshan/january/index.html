How January compares in Baoshan, Shanghai

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Baoshan, Shanghai. Compared with December: 2.5 °C cooler, 27 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. February is 1.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Baoshan, Shanghai in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baoshan, Shanghai in January?
In January, Baoshan, Shanghai averages a day high of 7.6 °C (46 °F) and a night low of 1.3 °C (34 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Baoshan, Shanghai?
January is not one of Baoshan, Shanghai’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baoshan, Shanghai in January?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (215 mm), the driest December (45 mm).
Does it snow in Baoshan, Shanghai in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.9 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Baoshan, Shanghai get in January?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baoshan, Shanghai in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
