How January compares in Tonghua

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Tonghua. Compared with December: 3.1 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. February is 4.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Tonghua in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tonghua in January?
In January, Tonghua averages a day high of -8.7 °C (16 °F) and a night low of -23.6 °C (-10 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Tonghua?
January is not one of Tonghua’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tonghua in January?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (210 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Tonghua in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.9 cm (3.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 5.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Tonghua get in January?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tonghua in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
