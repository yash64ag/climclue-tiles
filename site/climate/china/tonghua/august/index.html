Tonghua weather in August

Average August weather in Tonghua, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Tonghua averages 27.1 °C (81 °F) days and 17.0 °C (63 °F) nights, with 183 mm (7.2 in) of rain over the month and about 204 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Tonghua

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Tonghua. Compared with July: 0.7 °C cooler, 32 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. September is 6.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Tonghua in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tonghua in August?
In August, Tonghua averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 17.0 °C (63 °F), with 183 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Tonghua?
August is not one of Tonghua’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tonghua in August?
183 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (214 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Tonghua in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tonghua get in August?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tonghua in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
