How December compares in Tonghua

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Tonghua. Compared with November: 9.4 °C cooler, 15 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. January is 3.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Tonghua in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tonghua in December?
In December, Tonghua averages a day high of -5.8 °C (22 °F) and a night low of -19.2 °C (-3 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Tonghua?
December is not one of Tonghua’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tonghua in December?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (214 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Tonghua in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 12 cm (4.6 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Tonghua get in December?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tonghua in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
