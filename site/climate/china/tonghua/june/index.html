How June compares in Tonghua

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Tonghua. Compared with May: 5.0 °C warmer, 31 mm more precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. July is 2.8 °C warmer than June on average.

Tonghua in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tonghua in June?
In June, Tonghua averages a day high of 23.7 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Tonghua?
June is not one of Tonghua’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tonghua in June?
104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (210 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Tonghua in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tonghua get in June?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tonghua in June?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
