How July compares in Tonghua

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Tonghua. Compared with June: 2.8 °C warmer, 109 mm more precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. August is 0.7 °C cooler than July on average.

Tonghua in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tonghua in July?
In July, Tonghua averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 214 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Tonghua?
July is not one of Tonghua’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tonghua in July?
214 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (214 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Tonghua in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tonghua get in July?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tonghua in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
