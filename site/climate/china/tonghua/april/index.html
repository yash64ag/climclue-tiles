How April compares in Tonghua

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Tonghua. Compared with March: 9.5 °C warmer, 25 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. May is 6.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Tonghua in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tonghua in April?
In April, Tonghua averages a day high of 13.0 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 1.8 °C (35 °F), with 47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Tonghua?
April is not one of Tonghua’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tonghua in April?
47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (210 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Tonghua in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 14 cm (5.5 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.8 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Tonghua get in April?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tonghua in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
