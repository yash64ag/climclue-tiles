Shilong at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Shilong is 37th warmest (22.9 °C year average), 506th sunniest (about 1,854 hours of sunshine a year) and 50th wettest (2,041 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shijie, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Shilong is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 80 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.3 °C), averages 14.6 °C more than its coolest, January (14.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.7 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 67 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shilong, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Shilong

Why Shilong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 67 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
