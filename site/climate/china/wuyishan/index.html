Why Wuyishan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 230 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Wuyishan climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Wuyishan?
July, with an average of 28.6 °C (83 °F), day highs around 32.5 °C (91 °F) and night lows around 23.9 °C (75 °F).
What is the coldest month in Wuyishan?
January, with an average of 7.5 °C (46 °F), day highs around 11.6 °C (53 °F) and night lows around 2.9 °C (37 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Wuyishan?
June, with 380 mm (14.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,934 mm (76.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Wuyishan?
December, with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Wuyishan?
July, with about 251 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Wuyishan?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 13 cm (5.2 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Wuyishan?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: October.
What type of climate does Wuyishan have?

About these numbers

Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest).
