How April compares in Huaihua

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Huaihua. Compared with March: 5.9 °C warmer, 64 mm more precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. May is 4.7 °C warmer than April on average.

Huaihua in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huaihua in April?
In April, Huaihua averages a day high of 20.3 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation and about 95 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Huaihua?
April is not one of Huaihua’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huaihua in April?
171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (226 mm), the driest December (44 mm).
Does it snow in Huaihua in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huaihua get in April?
About 95 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huaihua in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
