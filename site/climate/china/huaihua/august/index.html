Huaihua weather in August

Average August weather in Huaihua, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Huaihua averages 32.9 °C (91 °F) days and 23.6 °C (74 °F) nights, with 112 mm (4.4 in) of rain over the month and about 228 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Huaihua

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Huaihua. Compared with July: 0.4 °C cooler, 38 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. September is 3.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Huaihua in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huaihua in August?
In August, Huaihua averages a day high of 32.9 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Huaihua?
August is not one of Huaihua’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huaihua in August?
112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (210 mm), the driest December (40 mm).
Does it snow in Huaihua in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huaihua get in August?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huaihua in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
