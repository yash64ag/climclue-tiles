How July compares in Baotou

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Baotou. Compared with June: 1.9 °C warmer, 49 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. August is 2.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Baotou in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baotou in July?
In July, Baotou averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 279 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Baotou?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Baotou’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baotou in July?
83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (87 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Baotou in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baotou get in July?
About 279 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baotou in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
