How August compares in Baotou

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Baotou. Compared with July: 2.5 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. September is 5.5 °C cooler than August on average.

Baotou in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baotou in August?
In August, Baotou averages a day high of 26.9 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 15.3 °C (60 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 265 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Baotou?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Baotou’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baotou in August?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (87 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Baotou in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baotou get in August?
About 265 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baotou in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
