How November compares in Baotou

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Baotou. Compared with October: 9.5 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. December is 7.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Baotou in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baotou in November?
In November, Baotou averages a day high of 4.4 °C (40 °F) and a night low of -7.4 °C (19 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Baotou?
November is not one of Baotou’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baotou in November?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (85 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Baotou in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.1 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Baotou get in November?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baotou in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
