Baotou in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baotou in April?
In April, Baotou averages a day high of 16.2 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 0.6 °C (33 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Baotou?
April is not one of Baotou’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baotou in April?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (87 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Baotou in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.6 cm (0.6 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Baotou get in April?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baotou in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
