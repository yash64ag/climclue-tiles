How March compares in Baotou

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Baotou. Compared with February: 7.1 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. April is 8.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Baotou in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baotou in March?
In March, Baotou averages a day high of 7.6 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -6.3 °C (21 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Baotou?
March is not one of Baotou’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baotou in March?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (87 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Baotou in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.2 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Baotou get in March?
About 254 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baotou in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
