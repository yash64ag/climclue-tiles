How September compares in Baotou

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Baotou. Compared with August: 5.5 °C cooler, 40 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. October is 7.8 °C cooler than September on average.

Baotou in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baotou in September?
In September, Baotou averages a day high of 21.9 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 9.2 °C (49 °F), with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 247 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Baotou?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Baotou’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baotou in September?
47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (87 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Baotou in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baotou get in September?
About 247 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baotou in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
