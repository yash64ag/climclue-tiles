Gaozhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Gaozhou is 15th warmest (23.3 °C year average), 484th sunniest (about 1,887 hours of sunshine a year) and 20th wettest (2,209 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Maoming, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Gaozhou is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 128 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 12.5 °C more than its coolest, January (16.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.0 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 68 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gaozhou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gaozhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 68 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
