Shajing at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Shajing is 41st warmest (22.9 °C year average), 474th sunniest (about 1,900 hours of sunshine a year) and 18th wettest (2,210 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gongheyong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Shajing is 0.1 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 70 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 13.3 °C more than its coolest, January (15.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.7 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shajing, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shajing has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
