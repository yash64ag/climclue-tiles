Fuyang, Anhui weather in November

Average November weather in Fuyang, Anhui, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

Fuyang, Anhui in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fuyang, Anhui in November?
In November, Fuyang, Anhui averages a day high of 15.0 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 5.1 °C (41 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Fuyang, Anhui?
November is not one of Fuyang, Anhui’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
