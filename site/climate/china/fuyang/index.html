Why Fuyang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 378 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Fuyang climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Fuyang?
July, with an average of 27.8 °C (82 °F), day highs around 31.3 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 24.2 °C (76 °F).
What is the coldest month in Fuyang?
January, with an average of 1.9 °C (36 °F), day highs around 7.2 °C (45 °F) and night lows around -2.7 °C (27 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Fuyang?
July, with 232 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 997 mm (39.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Fuyang?
December, with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Fuyang?
August, with about 228 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Fuyang?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 30 cm (11.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 1 month.
When is the best time for mild weather in Fuyang?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, September and October.
What type of climate does Fuyang have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.3 × 11 km, that contains Fuyang’s GeoNames location (32.900°N, 115.817°E; ClimaClue area sq-510-2958), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,768,947 (GeoNames).
