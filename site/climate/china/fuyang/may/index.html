How May compares in Fuyang

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Fuyang. Compared with April: 5.5 °C warmer, 32 mm more precipitation and 35 hours more sunshine. June is 4.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Fuyang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fuyang in May?
In May, Fuyang averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.4 °C (62 °F), with 88 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Fuyang?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Fuyang’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fuyang in May?
88 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (234 mm), the driest December (22 mm).
Does it snow in Fuyang in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fuyang get in May?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fuyang in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
