Fuyang, Anhui weather in February

Average February weather in Fuyang, Anhui, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

How February compares in Fuyang, Anhui

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Fuyang, Anhui. Compared with January: 2.7 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. March is 4.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Fuyang, Anhui in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fuyang, Anhui in February?
In February, Fuyang, Anhui averages a day high of 9.4 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 0.1 °C (32 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Fuyang, Anhui?
February is not one of Fuyang, Anhui’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fuyang, Anhui in February?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (234 mm), the driest December (22 mm).
Does it snow in Fuyang, Anhui in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.3 cm (3.3 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).

About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
