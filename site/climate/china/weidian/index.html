Weidian at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Weidian is 1806th warmest (9.2 °C year average), 862nd sunniest (about 2,182 hours of sunshine a year) and 1805th wettest (491 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Wangpu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Weidian is 1.2 °C warmer in July and 1.2 °C warmer in January, with 91 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.1 °C), averages 25.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,143 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Weidian, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Weidian has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 1,143 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Weidian itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.102°N, 105.566°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-488-2855), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
