Why Wushan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 973 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 8.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Wushan climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Wushan?
July, with an average of 26.3 °C (79 °F), day highs around 29.1 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 21.9 °C (71 °F).
What is the coldest month in Wushan?
January, with an average of 4.8 °C (41 °F), day highs around 7.6 °C (46 °F) and night lows around 0.2 °C (32 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Wushan?
July, with 255 mm (10.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,501 mm (59.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Wushan?
January, with 24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Wushan?
August, with about 210 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Wushan?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 15 cm (6.0 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Wushan?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Wushan have?
Humid subtropical, dry winter (Köppen Cwa). Hot, wet summers and mild, dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.5 × 11 km, that contains Wushan’s GeoNames location (31.077°N, 109.876°E; ClimaClue area sq-529-2898), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 117,873 (GeoNames).
