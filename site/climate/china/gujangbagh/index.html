Gujangbagh at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Gujangbagh is the 3rd driest (50 mm a year). Compared with Shache, the nearest covered town with different numbers (278 km away), Gujangbagh is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 20 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.9 °C), averages 30.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, November and December. The area lies about 1,817 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Gujangbagh has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,817 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-468-2599), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
