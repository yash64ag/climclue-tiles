Wujiaqu at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Wujiaqu is 585th warmest (8.6 °C year average), 38th sunniest (about 2,771 hours of sunshine a year) and 665th wettest (195 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Changji, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Wujiaqu is 0.7 °C warmer in July and 1.3 °C cooler in January, with 19 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.9 °C), averages 42.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-15.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 2,416 km from the sea (deep inland).
