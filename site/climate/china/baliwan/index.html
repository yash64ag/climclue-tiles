Baliwan at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Baliwan is 1852nd warmest (8.5 °C year average), 943rd sunniest (about 2,126 hours of sunshine a year) and 1770th wettest (516 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jinshan, Gansu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Baliwan is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 36 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.4 °C), averages 25.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,173 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Baliwan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 1,173 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.2 °C. The 10 km area around Baliwan, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 9.5 °C a year, 1.0 °C warmer than the town itself, because it takes in lower ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Baliwan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.848°N, 105.370°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-491-2853), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
