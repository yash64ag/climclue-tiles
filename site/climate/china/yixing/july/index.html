How July compares in Yixing

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Yixing. Compared with June: 3.9 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 51 hours more sunshine. August is 0.7 °C cooler than July on average.

Yixing in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yixing in July?
In July, Yixing averages a day high of 32.5 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 176 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Yixing?
July is not one of Yixing’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yixing in July?
176 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (187 mm), the driest December (40 mm).
Does it snow in Yixing in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yixing get in July?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yixing in July?
