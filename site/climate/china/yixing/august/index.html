How August compares in Yixing

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Yixing. Compared with July: 0.7 °C cooler, 38 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. September is 4.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Yixing in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yixing in August?
In August, Yixing averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 25.5 °C (78 °F), with 139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Yixing?
August is not one of Yixing’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yixing in August?
139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (187 mm), the driest December (40 mm).
Does it snow in Yixing in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yixing get in August?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yixing in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
