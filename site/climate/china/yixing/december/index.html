How December compares in Yixing

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Yixing. Compared with November: 6.2 °C cooler, 24 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. January is 2.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Yixing in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yixing in December?
In December, Yixing averages a day high of 10.5 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 1.6 °C (35 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Yixing?
December is not one of Yixing’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yixing in December?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (187 mm), the driest December (40 mm).
Does it snow in Yixing in December?
Yes, on average: about 1.3 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Yixing get in December?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yixing in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
