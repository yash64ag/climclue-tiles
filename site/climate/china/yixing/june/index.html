How June compares in Yixing

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Yixing. Compared with May: 3.5 °C warmer, 80 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. July is 4.0 °C warmer than June on average.

Yixing in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yixing in June?
In June, Yixing averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 190 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Yixing?
June is not one of Yixing’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yixing in June?
190 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (190 mm), the driest December (41 mm).
Does it snow in Yixing in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yixing get in June?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yixing in June?
