Yixing weather in May

Average May weather in Yixing, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Yixing averages 26.0 °C (79 °F) days and 16.8 °C (62 °F) nights, with 108 mm (4.3 in) of rain over the month and about 180 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Yixing

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Yixing. Compared with April: 5.4 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. June is 3.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Yixing in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yixing in May?
In May, Yixing averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Yixing?
May is not one of Yixing’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yixing in May?
108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (187 mm), the driest December (40 mm).
Does it snow in Yixing in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yixing get in May?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
