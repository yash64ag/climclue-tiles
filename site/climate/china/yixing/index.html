Yixing at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Yixing is 272nd warmest (16.8 °C year average), 386th sunniest (about 1,982 hours of sunshine a year) and 281st wettest (1,234 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Changzhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Yixing is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 9 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 25.1 °C more than its coolest, January (3.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 152 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yixing, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yixing has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 152 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
