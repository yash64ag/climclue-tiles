Zhutang weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Zhutang, China (Yunnan), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Zhutang is warmest in June, with 26.0 °C (79 °F) days and 18.0 °C nights, and coolest in December, with 20.4 °C (69 °F) days and 5.9 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (367 mm); January is driest (13 mm).

Zhutang at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Zhutang is 396th warmest (18.8 °C year average), 909th sunniest (about 2,149 hours of sunshine a year) and 163rd wettest (1,783 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nanling, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Zhutang is 1.3 °C cooler in June and 0.7 °C cooler in December, with 401 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (22.0 °C), averages 8.8 °C more than its coolest, December (13.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.0 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 688 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: a NOAA weather station 21 km from Zhutang measured 1981–2010 averages that differ from these (temperature by up to 3.1 °C (5.6 °F) in one month). One station can differ from modelled values (hills, coasts, city heat), so treat these numbers with some care.

Why Zhutang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 688 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Zhutang itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (22.715°N, 99.807°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-612-2798), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
