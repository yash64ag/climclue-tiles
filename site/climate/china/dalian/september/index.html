How September compares in Dalian

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Dalian. Compared with August: 3.5 °C cooler, 79 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. October is 6.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Dalian in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dalian in September?
In September, Dalian averages a day high of 24.5 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 18.0 °C (64 °F), with 64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Dalian?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Dalian’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dalian in September?
64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (144 mm), the driest February (7 mm).
Does it snow in Dalian in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dalian get in September?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Dalian in September?
About 22.2 °C (72 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (24.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Dalian in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
