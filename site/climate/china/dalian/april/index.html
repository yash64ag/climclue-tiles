Dalian weather in April

Average April weather in Dalian, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Dalian averages 12.1 °C (54 °F) days and 3.8 °C (39 °F) nights, with 29 mm (1.2 in) of rain over the month and about 242 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Dalian

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Dalian. Compared with March: 5.1 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. May is 5.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Dalian in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dalian in April?
In April, Dalian averages a day high of 12.1 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 3.8 °C (39 °F), with 29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 242 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Dalian?
April is not one of Dalian’s mild months; those are June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dalian in April?
29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (144 mm), the driest February (7 mm).
Does it snow in Dalian in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dalian get in April?
About 242 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Dalian in April?
About 5.6 °C (42 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (24.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Dalian in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
