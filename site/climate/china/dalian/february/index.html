How February compares in Dalian

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Dalian. Compared with January: 1.4 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. March is 3.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Dalian in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dalian in February?
In February, Dalian averages a day high of 0.9 °C (34 °F) and a night low of -2.8 °C (27 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Dalian?
February is not one of Dalian’s mild months; those are June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dalian in February?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (142 mm), the driest February (7 mm).
Does it snow in Dalian in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.8 cm (2.3 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Dalian get in February?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dalian in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
