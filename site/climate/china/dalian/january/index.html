How January compares in Dalian

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Dalian. Compared with December: 3.3 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. February is 1.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Dalian in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dalian in January?
In January, Dalian averages a day high of -0.3 °C (31 °F) and a night low of -4.4 °C (24 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Dalian?
January is not one of Dalian’s mild months; those are June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dalian in January?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (142 mm), the driest February (7 mm).
Does it snow in Dalian in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.6 cm (3.4 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Dalian get in January?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dalian in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
