How July compares in Dalian

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Dalian. Compared with June: 3.9 °C warmer, 65 mm more precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. August is 1.3 °C warmer than July on average.

Dalian in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dalian in July?
In July, Dalian averages a day high of 24.1 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Dalian?
July is not one of Dalian’s mild months; those are June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dalian in July?
139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (142 mm), the driest February (7 mm).
Does it snow in Dalian in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dalian get in July?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dalian in July?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
