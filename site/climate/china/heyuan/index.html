Heyuan at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Heyuan is 155th warmest (22.0 °C year average), 1328th sunniest (about 1,856 hours of sunshine a year) and 128th wettest (1,889 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Huizhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (74 km away), Heyuan is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 1.4 °C cooler in January, with 73 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.0 °C), averages 16.2 °C more than its coolest, January (12.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.1 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 122 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 9 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.0 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 96% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Heyuan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 122 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Heyuan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (23.733°N, 114.683°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-602-2946), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
