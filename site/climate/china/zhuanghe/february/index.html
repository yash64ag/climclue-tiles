How February compares in Zhuanghe

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Zhuanghe. Compared with January: 3.2 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. March is 5.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Zhuanghe in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhuanghe in February?
In February, Zhuanghe averages a day high of 1.7 °C (35 °F) and a night low of -7.0 °C (19 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Zhuanghe?
February is not one of Zhuanghe’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhuanghe in February?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (198 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Zhuanghe in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.3 cm (2.1 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhuanghe get in February?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhuanghe in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
