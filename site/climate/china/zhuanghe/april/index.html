How April compares in Zhuanghe

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Zhuanghe. Compared with March: 7.2 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. May is 5.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Zhuanghe in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhuanghe in April?
In April, Zhuanghe averages a day high of 14.2 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 4.7 °C (40 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Zhuanghe?
April is not one of Zhuanghe’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhuanghe in April?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (195 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Zhuanghe in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.5 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhuanghe get in April?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhuanghe in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
