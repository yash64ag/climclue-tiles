Zhuanghe at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Zhuanghe is 545th warmest (10.1 °C year average), 120th sunniest (about 2,588 hours of sunshine a year) and 438th wettest (715 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Wafangdian, the nearest covered town with different numbers (85 km away), Zhuanghe is 0.1 °C warmer in August and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 74 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.4 °C), averages 30.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-6.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 28 km from the sea.

Why Zhuanghe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 28 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
