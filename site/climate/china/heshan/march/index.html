How March compares in Heshan

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Heshan. Compared with February: 3.9 °C warmer, 46 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. April is 6.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Heshan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Heshan in March?
In March, Heshan averages a day high of 15.3 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 7.3 °C (45 °F), with 142 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 80 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Heshan?
March is not one of Heshan’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Heshan in March?
142 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (222 mm), the driest December (51 mm).
Does it snow in Heshan in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.3 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Heshan get in March?
About 80 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Heshan in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
