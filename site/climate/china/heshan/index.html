Heshan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Heshan is 196th warmest (17.8 °C year average), 600th sunniest (about 1,588 hours of sunshine a year) and 143rd wettest (1,570 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Changsha, Hunan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (74 km away), Heshan is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 111 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.4 °C), averages 23.9 °C more than its coolest, January (5.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 680 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Heshan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Heshan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 680 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
