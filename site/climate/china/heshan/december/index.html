How December compares in Heshan

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Heshan. Compared with November: 5.5 °C cooler, 40 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. January is 2.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Heshan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Heshan in December?
In December, Heshan averages a day high of 11.7 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 3.7 °C (39 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 99 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Heshan?
December is not one of Heshan’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Heshan in December?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (222 mm), the driest December (51 mm).
Does it snow in Heshan in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.3 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Heshan get in December?
About 99 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Heshan in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
