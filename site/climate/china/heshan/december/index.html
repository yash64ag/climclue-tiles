How December compares in Heshan, Hunan

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Heshan, Hunan. Compared with November: 5.5 °C cooler, 40 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. January is 2.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Heshan, Hunan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Heshan, Hunan in December?
In December, Heshan, Hunan averages a day high of 11.6 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 4.0 °C (39 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 99 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Heshan, Hunan?
December is not one of Heshan, Hunan’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Heshan, Hunan in December?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (224 mm), the driest December (51 mm).
Does it snow in Heshan, Hunan in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Heshan, Hunan get in December?
About 99 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Heshan, Hunan in December?
