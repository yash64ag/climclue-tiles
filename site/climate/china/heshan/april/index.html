How April compares in Heshan

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Heshan. Compared with March: 6.2 °C warmer, 57 mm more precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. May is 4.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Heshan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Heshan in April?
In April, Heshan averages a day high of 21.6 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 199 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation and about 106 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Heshan?
April is not one of Heshan’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Heshan in April?
199 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (222 mm), the driest December (51 mm).
Does it snow in Heshan in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Heshan get in April?
About 106 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Heshan in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
