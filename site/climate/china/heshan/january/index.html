Heshan, Hunan weather in January

Average January weather in Heshan, Hunan, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Heshan, Hunan averages 8.5 °C (47 °F) days and 2.5 °C (37 °F) nights, with 86 mm (3.4 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 81 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Heshan, Hunan

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Heshan, Hunan. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, 34 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. February is 2.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Heshan, Hunan in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Heshan, Hunan in January?
In January, Heshan, Hunan averages a day high of 8.5 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 2.5 °C (37 °F), with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 81 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Heshan, Hunan?
January is not one of Heshan, Hunan’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Heshan, Hunan in January?
86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (224 mm), the driest December (51 mm).
Does it snow in Heshan, Hunan in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 12 cm (4.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).

About 81 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
