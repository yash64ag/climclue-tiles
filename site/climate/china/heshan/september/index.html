How September compares in Heshan

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Heshan. Compared with August: 4.2 °C cooler, 44 mm less precipitation and 69 hours less sunshine. October is 5.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Heshan in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Heshan in September?
In September, Heshan averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Heshan?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Heshan’s mild months (September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Heshan in September?
84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (222 mm), the driest December (51 mm).
Does it snow in Heshan in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Heshan get in September?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Heshan in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
