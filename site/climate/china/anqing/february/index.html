How February compares in Anqing

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Anqing. Compared with January: 2.4 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. March is 4.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Anqing in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Anqing in February?
In February, Anqing averages a day high of 10.3 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 2.4 °C (36 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 108 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Anqing?
February is not one of Anqing’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Anqing in February?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (289 mm), the driest December (35 mm).
Does it snow in Anqing in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.0 cm (2.8 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Anqing get in February?
About 108 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Anqing in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
