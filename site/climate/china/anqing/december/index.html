How December compares in Anqing

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Anqing. Compared with November: 5.8 °C cooler, 36 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. January is 2.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Anqing in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Anqing in December?
In December, Anqing averages a day high of 10.5 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 2.2 °C (36 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Anqing?
December is not one of Anqing’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Anqing in December?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (289 mm), the driest December (35 mm).
Does it snow in Anqing in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.9 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Anqing get in December?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Anqing in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
