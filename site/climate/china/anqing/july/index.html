How July compares in Anqing

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Anqing. Compared with June: 3.4 °C warmer, 62 mm less precipitation and 57 hours more sunshine. August is 0.8 °C cooler than July on average.

Anqing in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Anqing in July?
In July, Anqing averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 25.9 °C (79 °F), with 235 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Anqing?
July is not one of Anqing’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Anqing in July?
235 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (297 mm), the driest December (37 mm).
Does it snow in Anqing in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Anqing get in July?
About 232 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Anqing in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
