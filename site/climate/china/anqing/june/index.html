Anqing in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Anqing in June?
In June, Anqing averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 289 mm (11.4 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Anqing?
June is not one of Anqing’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Anqing in June?
289 mm (11.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (289 mm), the driest December (35 mm).
Does it snow in Anqing in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Anqing get in June?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Anqing in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
