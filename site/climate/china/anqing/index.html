Anqing at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Anqing is 245th warmest (17.2 °C year average), 430th sunniest (about 1,944 hours of sunshine a year) and 146th wettest (1,568 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chizhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Anqing is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.1 °C), averages 25.0 °C more than its coolest, January (4.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 383 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 109% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Anqing has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 383 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
