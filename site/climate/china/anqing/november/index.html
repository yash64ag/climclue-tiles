How November compares in Anqing

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Anqing. Compared with October: 6.3 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. December is 5.8 °C cooler than November on average.

Anqing in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Anqing in November?
In November, Anqing averages a day high of 16.3 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 8.1 °C (47 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Anqing?
November is not one of Anqing’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Anqing in November?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (289 mm), the driest December (35 mm).
Does it snow in Anqing in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.9 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Anqing get in November?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Anqing in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
