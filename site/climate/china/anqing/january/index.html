How January compares in Anqing

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Anqing. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, 26 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. February is 2.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Anqing in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Anqing in January?
In January, Anqing averages a day high of 7.7 °C (46 °F) and a night low of 1.5 °C (35 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 124 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Anqing?
January is not one of Anqing’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Anqing in January?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (297 mm), the driest December (37 mm).
Does it snow in Anqing in January?
Yes, on average: about 9.6 cm (3.8 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Anqing get in January?
About 124 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Anqing in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
