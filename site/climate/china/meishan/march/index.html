How March compares in Meishan

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Meishan. Compared with February: 3.9 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 40 hours more sunshine. April is 5.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Meishan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Meishan in March?
In March, Meishan averages a day high of 16.7 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 8.3 °C (47 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 123 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Meishan?
March is not one of Meishan’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Meishan in March?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (294 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Meishan in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Meishan get in March?
About 123 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Meishan in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
