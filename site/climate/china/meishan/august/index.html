How August compares in Meishan

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the sunniest month in Meishan. Compared with July: 0.6 °C cooler, 10 mm more precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. September is 3.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Meishan in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Meishan in August?
In August, Meishan averages a day high of 29.2 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 294 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Meishan?
August is not one of Meishan’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Meishan in August?
294 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (294 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Meishan in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Meishan get in August?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Meishan in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
