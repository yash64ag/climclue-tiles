Meishan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Meishan in October?
In October, Meishan averages a day high of 20.8 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 14.9 °C (59 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 84 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Meishan?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Meishan’s mild months (April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Meishan in October?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (294 mm), the driest December (10 mm).
Does it snow in Meishan in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Meishan get in October?
About 84 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Meishan in October?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
