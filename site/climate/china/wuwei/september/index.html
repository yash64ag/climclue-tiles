How September compares in Wuwei

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Wuwei. Compared with August: 5.0 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 42 hours less sunshine. October is 6.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Wuwei in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuwei in September?
In September, Wuwei averages a day high of 22.3 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 9.4 °C (49 °F), with 24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Wuwei?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Wuwei’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuwei in September?
24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (35 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Wuwei in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuwei get in September?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuwei in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
