How August compares in Wuwei

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Wuwei. Compared with July: 1.4 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. September is 5.0 °C cooler than August on average.

Wuwei in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuwei in August?
In August, Wuwei averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 14.0 °C (57 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Wuwei?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Wuwei’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuwei in August?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (35 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Wuwei in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuwei get in August?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuwei in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
