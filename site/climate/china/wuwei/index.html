Wuwei at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Wuwei is 579th warmest (9.0 °C year average), 29th sunniest (about 2,814 hours of sunshine a year) and 672nd wettest (169 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jinchang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (74 km away), Wuwei is 1.3 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 36 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.2 °C), averages 28.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-6.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,310 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Wuwei has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,310 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Wuwei itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.927°N, 102.632°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-460-2826), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
