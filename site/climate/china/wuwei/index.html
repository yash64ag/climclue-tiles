Wuwei at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Wuwei is 577th warmest (9.1 °C year average), 29th sunniest (about 2,814 hours of sunshine a year) and 674th wettest (161 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jinchang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (74 km away), Wuwei is 0.9 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 24 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.4 °C), averages 28.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-6.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,310 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Wuwei, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Wuwei has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,310 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
