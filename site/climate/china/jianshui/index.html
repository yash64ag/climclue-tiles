Why Jianshui has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 696 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 10.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Jianshui climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Jianshui?
June, with an average of 19.3 °C (67 °F), day highs around 22.0 °C (72 °F) and night lows around 14.3 °C (58 °F).
What is the coldest month in Jianshui?
January, with an average of 8.8 °C (48 °F), day highs around 14.2 °C (58 °F) and night lows around 2.5 °C (37 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Jianshui?
July, with 301 mm (11.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,586 mm (62.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Jianshui?
December, with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Jianshui?
March, with about 237 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Jianshui?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Jianshui?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Jianshui’s GeoNames location (24.277°N, 101.224°E; ClimaClue area sq-597-2812), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 490,000 (GeoNames).
