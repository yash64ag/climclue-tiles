Nantou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Nantou is 17th warmest (23.2 °C year average), 504th sunniest (about 1,857 hours of sunshine a year) and 51st wettest (2,033 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Daliang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Nantou is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 17 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.6 °C), averages 14.6 °C more than its coolest, January (14.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.6 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 47 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nantou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nantou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 47 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
