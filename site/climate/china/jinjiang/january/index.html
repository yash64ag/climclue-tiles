How January compares in Jinjiang, Fujian

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Jinjiang, Fujian. Compared with December: 2.0 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. February is 0.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Jinjiang, Fujian in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinjiang, Fujian in January?
In January, Jinjiang, Fujian averages a day high of 16.7 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 9.7 °C (49 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Jinjiang, Fujian?
January is not one of Jinjiang, Fujian’s mild months; those are October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinjiang, Fujian in January?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (194 mm), the driest December (28 mm).
Does it snow in Jinjiang, Fujian in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jinjiang, Fujian get in January?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Jinjiang, Fujian in January?
About 15.4 °C (60 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (27.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Jinjiang, Fujian in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
