How December compares in Jinjiang

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Jinjiang. Compared with November: 4.6 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. January is 1.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Jinjiang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinjiang in December?
In December, Jinjiang averages a day high of 18.6 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 11.0 °C (52 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jinjiang?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Jinjiang’s mild months (October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinjiang in December?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (201 mm), the driest December (28 mm).
Does it snow in Jinjiang in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jinjiang get in December?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jinjiang in December?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
