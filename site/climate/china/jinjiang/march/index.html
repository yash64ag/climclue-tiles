How March compares in Jinjiang

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Jinjiang. Compared with February: 2.3 °C warmer, 35 mm more precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. April is 4.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Jinjiang in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinjiang in March?
In March, Jinjiang averages a day high of 19.5 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 12.4 °C (54 °F), with 112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 110 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Jinjiang?
March is not one of Jinjiang’s mild months; those are October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinjiang in March?
112 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (201 mm), the driest December (28 mm).
Does it snow in Jinjiang in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jinjiang get in March?
About 110 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jinjiang in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
