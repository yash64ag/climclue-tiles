How May compares in Jinjiang

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Jinjiang. Compared with April: 3.8 °C warmer, 36 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. June is 2.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Jinjiang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinjiang in May?
In May, Jinjiang averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 20.6 °C (69 °F), with 174 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 141 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Jinjiang?
May is not one of Jinjiang’s mild months; those are October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinjiang in May?
174 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (201 mm), the driest December (28 mm).
Does it snow in Jinjiang in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jinjiang get in May?
About 141 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jinjiang in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
