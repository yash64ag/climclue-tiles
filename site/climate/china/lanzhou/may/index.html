How May compares in Lanzhou

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Lanzhou. Compared with April: 4.9 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. June is 3.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Lanzhou in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lanzhou in May?
In May, Lanzhou averages a day high of 23.0 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 9.7 °C (49 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Lanzhou?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Lanzhou’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lanzhou in May?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (66 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Lanzhou in May?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.5 cm (0.6 in) of fresh snow in May (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Lanzhou get in May?
About 244 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lanzhou in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
