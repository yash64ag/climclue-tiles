How February compares in Lanzhou

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Lanzhou. Compared with January: 3.9 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. March is 5.6 °C warmer than February on average.

Lanzhou in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lanzhou in February?
In February, Lanzhou averages a day high of 6.3 °C (43 °F) and a night low of -5.0 °C (23 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Lanzhou?
February is not one of Lanzhou’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lanzhou in February?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (61 mm), the driest December (1 mm).
Does it snow in Lanzhou in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.1 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Lanzhou get in February?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lanzhou in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
