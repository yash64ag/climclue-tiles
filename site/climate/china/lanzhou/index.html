Lanzhou weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Lanzhou, China (Gansu), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Lanzhou is warmest in July, with 29.8 °C (86 °F) days and 17.5 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 3.8 °C (39 °F) days and -8.8 °C nights. Most rain falls around August (61 mm); December is driest (1 mm).

Lanzhou at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Lanzhou is 1660th warmest (11.5 °C year average), 522nd sunniest (about 2,476 hours of sunshine a year) and 1964th wettest (285 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Baiyin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (62 km away), Lanzhou is 1.3 °C warmer in July and 2.8 °C warmer in January, with 99 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.7 °C), averages 26.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, November and December. The area lies about 1,252 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 96% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Lanzhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,252 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Lanzhou itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.057°N, 103.840°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-479-2838), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
