How November compares in Lanzhou

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Lanzhou. Compared with October: 7.0 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. December is 5.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Lanzhou in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lanzhou in November?
In November, Lanzhou averages a day high of 10.1 °C (50 °F) and a night low of -1.2 °C (30 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Lanzhou?
November is not one of Lanzhou’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lanzhou in November?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (61 mm), the driest December (1 mm).
Does it snow in Lanzhou in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.7 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Lanzhou get in November?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lanzhou in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
