How January compares in Yunfu

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Yunfu. Compared with December: 1.5 °C cooler, 22 mm more precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. February is 1.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Yunfu in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yunfu in January?
In January, Yunfu averages a day high of 17.6 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 9.5 °C (49 °F), with 57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 124 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Yunfu?
January is not one of Yunfu’s mild months; those are October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yunfu in January?
57 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (301 mm), the driest December (35 mm).
Does it snow in Yunfu in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yunfu get in January?
About 124 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yunfu in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
