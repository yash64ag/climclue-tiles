How March compares in Benxi

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Benxi. Compared with February: 7.9 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 49 hours more sunshine. April is 8.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Benxi in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benxi in March?
In March, Benxi averages a day high of 5.9 °C (43 °F) and a night low of -5.5 °C (22 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Benxi?
March is not one of Benxi’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benxi in March?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (213 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Benxi in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 20 cm (7.7 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 5.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Benxi get in March?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benxi in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
