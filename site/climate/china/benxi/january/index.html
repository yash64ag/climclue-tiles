How January compares in Benxi

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Benxi. Compared with December: 3.2 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. February is 4.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Benxi in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benxi in January?
In January, Benxi averages a day high of -6.5 °C (20 °F) and a night low of -18.6 °C (-1 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Benxi?
January is not one of Benxi’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benxi in January?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (213 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Benxi in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 14 cm (5.6 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 12 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Benxi get in January?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benxi in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
