Benxi weather in February

Average February weather in Benxi, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Benxi averages -0.6 °C (31 °F) days and -12.8 °C (9 °F) nights, with 11 mm (0.4 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 184 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Benxi

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Benxi. Compared with January: 4.9 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. March is 8.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Benxi in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benxi in February?
In February, Benxi averages a day high of -0.6 °C (31 °F) and a night low of -12.8 °C (9 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Benxi?
February is not one of Benxi’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benxi in February?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (203 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Benxi in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.4 cm (3.7 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Benxi get in February?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benxi in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
