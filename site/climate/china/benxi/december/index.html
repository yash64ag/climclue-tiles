Benxi weather in December

Average December weather in Benxi, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Benxi averages -2.8 °C (27 °F) days and -14.0 °C (7 °F) nights, with 11 mm (0.4 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 158 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Benxi

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Benxi. Compared with November: 8.3 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. January is 3.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Benxi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Benxi in December?
In December, Benxi averages a day high of -2.8 °C (27 °F) and a night low of -14.0 °C (7 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Benxi?
December is not one of Benxi’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Benxi in December?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (203 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Benxi in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.8 cm (3.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Benxi get in December?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Benxi in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
