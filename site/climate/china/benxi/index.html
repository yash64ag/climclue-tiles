Benxi at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Benxi is 605th warmest (7.6 °C year average), 171st sunniest (about 2,536 hours of sunshine a year) and 409th wettest (805 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Liaoyang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (50 km away), Benxi is 1.9 °C cooler in July and 1.8 °C cooler in January, with 63 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.4 °C), averages 35.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-11.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 158 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.2 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 102% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Benxi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 158 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
