How March compares in Suizhou

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Suizhou. Compared with February: 4.5 °C warmer, 21 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. April is 6.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Suizhou in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suizhou in March?
In March, Suizhou averages a day high of 15.2 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 4.8 °C (41 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 131 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Suizhou?
March is not one of Suizhou’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suizhou in March?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (217 mm), the driest December (20 mm).
Does it snow in Suizhou in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.9 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Suizhou get in March?
About 131 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suizhou in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
