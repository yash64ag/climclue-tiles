How June compares in Suizhou

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Suizhou. Compared with May: 3.8 °C warmer, 23 mm more precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Suizhou in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suizhou in June?
In June, Suizhou averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 21.0 °C (70 °F), with 146 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Suizhou?
June is not one of Suizhou’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suizhou in June?
146 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (221 mm), the driest December (21 mm).
Does it snow in Suizhou in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suizhou get in June?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suizhou in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
