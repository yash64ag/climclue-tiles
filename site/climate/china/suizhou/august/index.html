How August compares in Suizhou

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Suizhou. Compared with July: 0.8 °C cooler, 66 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. September is 4.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Suizhou in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suizhou in August?
In August, Suizhou averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 151 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Suizhou?
August is not one of Suizhou’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suizhou in August?
151 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (217 mm), the driest December (20 mm).
Does it snow in Suizhou in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suizhou get in August?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suizhou in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
