Suizhou weather in May

Average May weather in Suizhou, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Suizhou averages 26.5 °C (80 °F) days and 17.1 °C (63 °F) nights, with 123 mm (4.8 in) of rain over the month and about 185 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Suizhou

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Suizhou. Compared with April: 5.0 °C warmer, 41 mm more precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. June is 3.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Suizhou in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suizhou in May?
In May, Suizhou averages a day high of 26.5 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 17.1 °C (63 °F), with 123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Suizhou?
May is not one of Suizhou’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suizhou in May?
123 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (221 mm), the driest December (21 mm).
Does it snow in Suizhou in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suizhou get in May?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suizhou in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
