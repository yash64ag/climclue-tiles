How November compares in Suizhou

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Suizhou. Compared with October: 6.4 °C cooler, 29 mm less precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. December is 5.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Suizhou in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suizhou in November?
In November, Suizhou averages a day high of 16.0 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 6.1 °C (43 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Suizhou?
November is not one of Suizhou’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suizhou in November?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (217 mm), the driest December (20 mm).
Does it snow in Suizhou in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suizhou get in November?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suizhou in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
