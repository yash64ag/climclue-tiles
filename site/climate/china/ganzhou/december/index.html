How December compares in Ganzhou

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Ganzhou. Compared with November: 5.6 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Ganzhou in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ganzhou in December?
In December, Ganzhou averages a day high of 14.5 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 5.1 °C (41 °F), with 42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ganzhou?
December is not one of Ganzhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ganzhou in December?
42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is May (247 mm), the driest December (42 mm).
Does it snow in Ganzhou in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.1 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ganzhou get in December?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ganzhou in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
