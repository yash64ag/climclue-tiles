How November compares in Ganzhou

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Ganzhou. Compared with October: 5.9 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. December is 5.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Ganzhou in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ganzhou in November?
In November, Ganzhou averages a day high of 19.7 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 10.8 °C (51 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Ganzhou?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Ganzhou’s mild months (October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ganzhou in November?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (247 mm), the driest December (42 mm).
Does it snow in Ganzhou in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ganzhou get in November?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ganzhou in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
