How July compares in Ganzhou

July is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Ganzhou. Compared with June: 2.5 °C warmer, 69 mm less precipitation and 100 hours more sunshine. August is 0.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Ganzhou in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ganzhou in July?
In July, Ganzhou averages a day high of 34.6 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 25.9 °C (79 °F), with 146 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Ganzhou?
July is not one of Ganzhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ganzhou in July?
146 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (247 mm), the driest December (42 mm).
Does it snow in Ganzhou in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ganzhou get in July?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ganzhou in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
