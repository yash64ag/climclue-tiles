How February compares in Ganzhou

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Ganzhou. Compared with January: 2.3 °C warmer, 52 mm more precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. March is 3.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Ganzhou in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ganzhou in February?
In February, Ganzhou averages a day high of 13.8 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 6.0 °C (43 °F), with 125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 73 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Ganzhou?
February is not one of Ganzhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ganzhou in February?
125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (247 mm), the driest December (42 mm).
Does it snow in Ganzhou in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.6 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ganzhou get in February?
About 73 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ganzhou in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
