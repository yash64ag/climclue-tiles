How June compares in Ganzhou

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ganzhou. Compared with May: 3.1 °C warmer, 32 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. July is 2.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Ganzhou in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ganzhou in June?
In June, Ganzhou averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.8 °C (75 °F), with 215 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Ganzhou?
June is not one of Ganzhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ganzhou in June?
215 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (247 mm), the driest December (42 mm).
Does it snow in Ganzhou in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ganzhou get in June?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ganzhou in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
