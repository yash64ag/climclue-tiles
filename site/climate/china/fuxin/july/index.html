How July compares in Fuxin

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Fuxin. Compared with June: 2.3 °C warmer, 55 mm more precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. August is 1.0 °C cooler than July on average.

Fuxin in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fuxin in July?
In July, Fuxin averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Fuxin?
July is not one of Fuxin’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fuxin in July?
134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (134 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Fuxin in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fuxin get in July?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fuxin in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
