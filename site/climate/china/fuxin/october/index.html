How October compares in Fuxin

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Fuxin. Compared with September: 8.2 °C cooler, 22 mm less precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. November is 10.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Fuxin in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fuxin in October?
In October, Fuxin averages a day high of 15.0 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 4.1 °C (39 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Fuxin?
October is not one of Fuxin’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fuxin in October?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (134 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Fuxin in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Fuxin get in October?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fuxin in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
