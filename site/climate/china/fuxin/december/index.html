How December compares in Fuxin

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Fuxin. Compared with November: 7.2 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. January is 2.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Fuxin in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fuxin in December?
In December, Fuxin averages a day high of -1.9 °C (29 °F) and a night low of -13.0 °C (9 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Fuxin?
December is not one of Fuxin’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fuxin in December?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (132 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Fuxin in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.7 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Fuxin get in December?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fuxin in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
