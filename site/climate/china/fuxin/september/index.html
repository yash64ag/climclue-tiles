How September compares in Fuxin

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Fuxin. Compared with August: 5.6 °C cooler, 65 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. October is 8.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Fuxin in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fuxin in September?
In September, Fuxin averages a day high of 23.1 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 239 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Fuxin?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Fuxin’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fuxin in September?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (134 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Fuxin in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fuxin get in September?
About 239 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fuxin in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
