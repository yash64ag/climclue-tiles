How May compares in Fuxin

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Fuxin. Compared with April: 7.0 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. June is 4.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Fuxin in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fuxin in May?
In May, Fuxin averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 10.9 °C (52 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 278 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Fuxin?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Fuxin’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fuxin in May?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (134 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Fuxin in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fuxin get in May?
About 278 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fuxin in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
