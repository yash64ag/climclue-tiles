Renqiu at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Renqiu is 1365th warmest (13.8 °C year average), 271st sunniest (about 2,608 hours of sunshine a year) and 1773rd wettest (515 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Baoding, Hebei, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Renqiu is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 30.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 130 km from the sea.

Why Renqiu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 130 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Renqiu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.709°N, 116.101°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-452-2961), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
