How February compares in Foshan

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Foshan. Compared with January: 1.3 °C warmer, 30 mm more precipitation and 56 hours less sunshine. March is 3.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Foshan in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Foshan in February?
In February, Foshan averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 12.3 °C (54 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 74 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Foshan?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Foshan’s mild months (February, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Foshan in February?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (334 mm), the driest December (32 mm).
Does it snow in Foshan in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Foshan get in February?
About 74 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Foshan in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
