How September compares in Foshan

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Foshan. Compared with August: 1.3 °C cooler, 45 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. October is 2.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Foshan in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Foshan in September?
In September, Foshan averages a day high of 31.8 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 24.4 °C (76 °F), with 221 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Foshan?
September is not one of Foshan’s mild months; those are February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Foshan in September?
221 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (334 mm), the driest December (32 mm).
Does it snow in Foshan in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Foshan get in September?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Foshan in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
