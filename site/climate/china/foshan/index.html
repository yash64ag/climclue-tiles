Why Foshan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 76 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Foshan climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Foshan?
July, with an average of 29.6 °C (85 °F), day highs around 31.9 °C (89 °F) and night lows around 25.8 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Foshan?
January, with an average of 14.7 °C (58 °F), day highs around 18.5 °C (65 °F) and night lows around 10.4 °C (51 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Foshan?
June, with 337 mm (13.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,983 mm (78.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Foshan?
December, with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Foshan?
July, with about 225 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Foshan?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Foshan?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, November and December.
What type of climate does Foshan have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Foshan’s GeoNames location (23.027°N, 113.131°E; ClimaClue area sq-609-2931), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 9,042,509 (GeoNames).
