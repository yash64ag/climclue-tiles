Renhe at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Renhe is 813th warmest (17.3 °C year average), 726th sunniest (about 2,281 hours of sunshine a year) and 918th wettest (1,131 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rongjiang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Renhe is 3.8 °C cooler in June and 3.6 °C cooler in December, with 199 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (22.1 °C), averages 11.6 °C more than its coolest, December (10.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 875 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Renhe, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Renhe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 875 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 9.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Renhe itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.417°N, 101.017°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-575-2810), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
