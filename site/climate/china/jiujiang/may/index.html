How May compares in Jiujiang

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Jiujiang. Compared with April: 5.5 °C warmer, 23 mm more precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. June is 3.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Jiujiang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiujiang in May?
In May, Jiujiang averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 19.2 °C (67 °F), with 227 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Jiujiang?
No month in Jiujiang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiujiang in May?
227 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (283 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Jiujiang in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiujiang get in May?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiujiang in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
