How December compares in Jiujiang

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Jiujiang. Compared with November: 5.8 °C cooler, 40 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. January is 2.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Jiujiang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiujiang in December?
In December, Jiujiang averages a day high of 10.5 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 3.8 °C (39 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jiujiang?
No month in Jiujiang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiujiang in December?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (283 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Jiujiang in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.3 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiujiang get in December?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiujiang in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
