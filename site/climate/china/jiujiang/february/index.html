Jiujiang weather in February

Average February weather in Jiujiang, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Jiujiang averages 10.6 °C (51 °F) days and 3.0 °C (37 °F) nights, with 105 mm (4.1 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 97 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Jiujiang

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Jiujiang. Compared with January: 2.2 °C warmer, 21 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. March is 4.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Jiujiang in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiujiang in February?

Is February a good time to visit Jiujiang?

How much does it rain in Jiujiang in February?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (290 mm), the driest December (49 mm).
