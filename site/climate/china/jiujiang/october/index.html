How October compares in Jiujiang

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Jiujiang. Compared with September: 5.5 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. November is 6.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Jiujiang in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiujiang in October?
In October, Jiujiang averages a day high of 22.4 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 160 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Jiujiang?
No month in Jiujiang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiujiang in October?
111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (290 mm), the driest December (49 mm).
Does it snow in Jiujiang in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiujiang get in October?
About 160 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiujiang in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
