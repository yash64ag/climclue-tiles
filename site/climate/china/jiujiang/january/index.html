How January compares in Jiujiang

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Jiujiang. Compared with December: 2.4 °C cooler, 34 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. February is 2.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Jiujiang in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiujiang in January?
In January, Jiujiang averages a day high of 8.0 °C (46 °F) and a night low of 1.7 °C (35 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 114 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Jiujiang?
No month in Jiujiang is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiujiang in January?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (283 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Jiujiang in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.6 cm (3.4 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiujiang get in January?
About 114 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiujiang in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
