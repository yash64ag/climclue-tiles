How May compares in Putuo

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Putuo. Compared with April: 5.4 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. June is 3.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Putuo in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Putuo in May?
In May, Putuo averages a day high of 24.8 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Putuo?
May is not one of Putuo’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Putuo in May?
117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (230 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Putuo in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Putuo get in May?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Putuo in May?
