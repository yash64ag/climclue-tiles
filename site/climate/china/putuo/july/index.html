How July compares in Putuo

July is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Putuo. Compared with June: 4.1 °C warmer, 35 mm less precipitation and 61 hours more sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Putuo in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Putuo in July?
In July, Putuo averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 189 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Putuo?
July is not one of Putuo’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Putuo in July?
189 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (228 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Putuo in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Putuo get in July?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Putuo in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
