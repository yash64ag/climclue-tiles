How August compares in Putuo

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the sunniest month in Putuo. Compared with July: 0.4 °C cooler, 39 mm more precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. September is 3.9 °C cooler than August on average.

Putuo in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Putuo in August?
In August, Putuo averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 228 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Putuo?
August is not one of Putuo’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Putuo in August?
228 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (228 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Putuo in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Putuo get in August?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
