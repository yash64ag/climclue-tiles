How December compares in Putuo

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Putuo. Compared with November: 6.1 °C cooler, 23 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. January is 2.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Putuo in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Putuo in December?
In December, Putuo averages a day high of 11.0 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 2.9 °C (37 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Putuo?
December is not one of Putuo’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Putuo in December?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (230 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Putuo in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.6 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Putuo get in December?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Putuo in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
