How January compares in Putuo

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Putuo. Compared with December: 2.4 °C cooler, 29 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. February is 1.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Putuo in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Putuo in January?
In January, Putuo averages a day high of 8.2 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 0.6 °C (33 °F), with 77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 134 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Putuo?
January is not one of Putuo’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Putuo in January?
77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (230 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Putuo in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.4 cm (2.1 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Putuo get in January?
About 134 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Putuo in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
