Putuo in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Putuo in April?
In April, Putuo averages a day high of 19.3 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 11.0 °C (52 °F), with 102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Putuo?
April is not one of Putuo’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Putuo in April?
102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (228 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Putuo in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Putuo get in April?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Putuo in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
