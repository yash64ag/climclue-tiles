How September compares in Putuo

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Putuo. Compared with August: 3.9 °C cooler, 93 mm less precipitation and 72 hours less sunshine. October is 5.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Putuo in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Putuo in September?
In September, Putuo averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.2 °C (70 °F), with 137 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Putuo?
September is not one of Putuo’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Putuo in September?
137 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (230 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Putuo in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Putuo get in September?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Putuo in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
