Changzhou weather in May

Average May weather in Changzhou, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Changzhou averages 25.7 °C (78 °F) days and 16.3 °C (61 °F) nights, with 108 mm (4.2 in) of rain over the month and about 186 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Changzhou

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Changzhou. Compared with April: 5.4 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. June is 3.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Changzhou in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changzhou in May?
In May, Changzhou averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 16.3 °C (61 °F), with 108 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Changzhou?
May is not one of Changzhou’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changzhou in May?
108 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (213 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Changzhou in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Changzhou get in May?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Changzhou in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
