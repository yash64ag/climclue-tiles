How March compares in Changzhou

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Changzhou. Compared with February: 4.1 °C warmer, 34 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. April is 6.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Changzhou in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changzhou in March?
In March, Changzhou averages a day high of 13.9 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 5.2 °C (41 °F), with 97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Changzhou?
March is not one of Changzhou’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changzhou in March?
97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (213 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Changzhou in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Changzhou get in March?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Changzhou in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
