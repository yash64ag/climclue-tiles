How July compares in Changzhou

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Changzhou. Compared with June: 3.8 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. August is 0.7 °C cooler than July on average.

Changzhou in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changzhou in July?
In July, Changzhou averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Changzhou?
July is not one of Changzhou’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changzhou in July?
213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (213 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Changzhou in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Changzhou get in July?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Changzhou in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
