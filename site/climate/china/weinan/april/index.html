Weinan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Weinan in April?
In April, Weinan averages a day high of 23.8 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 10.5 °C (51 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Weinan?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Weinan’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Weinan in April?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (86 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Weinan in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.6 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Weinan get in April?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Weinan in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
