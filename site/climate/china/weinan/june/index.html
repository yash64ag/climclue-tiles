Weinan weather in June

Average June weather in Weinan, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Weinan averages 33.7 °C (93 °F) days and 20.9 °C (70 °F) nights, with 51 mm (2.0 in) of rain over the month and about 220 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Weinan

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Weinan. Compared with May: 4.4 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. July is 1.5 °C warmer than June on average.

Weinan in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Weinan in June?
In June, Weinan averages a day high of 33.7 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Weinan?
June is not one of Weinan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Weinan in June?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (88 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Weinan in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Weinan get in June?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Weinan in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
