Why Weinan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 851 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Weinan climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Weinan?
July, with an average of 28.9 °C (84 °F), day highs around 33.4 °C (92 °F) and night lows around 23.6 °C (74 °F).
What is the coldest month in Weinan?
January, with an average of 1.9 °C (35 °F), day highs around 7.2 °C (45 °F) and night lows around -2.9 °C (27 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Weinan?
July, with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 475 mm (18.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Weinan?
December, with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Weinan?
August, with about 223 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Weinan?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 30 cm (11.8 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Weinan?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, September and October.
What type of climate does Weinan have?
Cold semi-arid (Köppen BSk). Dry grassland climate with cold winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.2 × 11 km, that contains Weinan’s GeoNames location (34.504°N, 109.509°E; ClimaClue area sq-494-2895), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,199,290 (GeoNames).
