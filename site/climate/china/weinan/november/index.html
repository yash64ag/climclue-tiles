How November compares in Weinan

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Weinan. Compared with October: 7.0 °C cooler, 31 mm less precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. December is 6.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Weinan in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Weinan in November?
In November, Weinan averages a day high of 14.3 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 3.7 °C (39 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Weinan?
November is not one of Weinan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Weinan in November?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (86 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Weinan in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Weinan get in November?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Weinan in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
