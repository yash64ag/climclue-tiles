How December compares in Guang’an

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Guang’an. Compared with November: 5.0 °C cooler, 27 mm less precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. January is 1.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Guang’an in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guang’an in December?
In December, Guang’an averages a day high of 11.6 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 5.7 °C (42 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 48 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Guang’an?
No month in Guang’an is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guang’an in December?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (221 mm), the driest January (23 mm).
Does it snow in Guang’an in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.0 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Guang’an get in December?
About 48 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guang’an in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
