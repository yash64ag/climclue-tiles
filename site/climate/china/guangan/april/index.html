How April compares in Guang’an

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Guang’an. Compared with March: 4.8 °C warmer, 61 mm more precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. May is 4.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Guang’an in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guang’an in April?
In April, Guang’an averages a day high of 21.7 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 121 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Guang’an?
No month in Guang’an is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guang’an in April?
117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (221 mm), the driest January (23 mm).
Does it snow in Guang’an in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guang’an get in April?
About 121 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guang’an in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
