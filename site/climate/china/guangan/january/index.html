How January compares in Guang’an

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Guang’an. Compared with December: 1.2 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. February is 2.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Guang’an in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guang’an in January?
In January, Guang’an averages a day high of 10.4 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 52 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Guang’an?
No month in Guang’an is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guang’an in January?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (221 mm), the driest January (23 mm).
Does it snow in Guang’an in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.4 cm (0.6 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Guang’an get in January?
About 52 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guang’an in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
