How September compares in Guang’an

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Guang’an. Compared with August: 4.1 °C cooler, 37 mm less precipitation and 100 hours less sunshine. October is 5.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Guang’an in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guang’an in September?
In September, Guang’an averages a day high of 26.9 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 144 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 106 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Guang’an?
No month in Guang’an is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guang’an in September?
144 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (221 mm), the driest January (23 mm).
Does it snow in Guang’an in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guang’an get in September?
About 106 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guang’an in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
