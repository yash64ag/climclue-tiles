How June compares in Qingpu

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Qingpu. Compared with May: 3.8 °C warmer, 107 mm more precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. July is 4.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Qingpu in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qingpu in June?
In June, Qingpu averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 222 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Qingpu?
June is not one of Qingpu’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qingpu in June?
222 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (222 mm), the driest December (44 mm).
Does it snow in Qingpu in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qingpu get in June?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qingpu in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
