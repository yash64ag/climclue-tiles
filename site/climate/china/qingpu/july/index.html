Qingpu weather in July

Average July weather in Qingpu, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Qingpu averages 32.1 °C (90 °F) days and 24.9 °C (77 °F) nights, with 189 mm (7.5 in) of rain over the month and about 225 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Qingpu

July is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Qingpu. Compared with June: 4.1 °C warmer, 35 mm less precipitation and 62 hours more sunshine. August is 0.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Qingpu in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qingpu in July?
In July, Qingpu averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 189 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Qingpu?
July is not one of Qingpu’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qingpu in July?
189 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (224 mm), the driest December (43 mm).
Does it snow in Qingpu in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qingpu get in July?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qingpu in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
