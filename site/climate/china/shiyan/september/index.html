Shiyan, Hubei in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shiyan, Hubei in September?
In September, Shiyan, Hubei averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Shiyan, Hubei?
September is not one of Shiyan, Hubei’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
