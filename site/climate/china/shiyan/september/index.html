How September compares in Shiyan

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Shiyan. Compared with August: 4.5 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and 73 hours less sunshine. October is 5.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Shiyan in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shiyan in September?
In September, Shiyan averages a day high of 25.0 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Shiyan?
September is not one of Shiyan’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shiyan in September?
117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (172 mm), the driest December (16 mm).
Does it snow in Shiyan in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shiyan get in September?
About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shiyan in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
