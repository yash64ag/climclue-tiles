Shiyan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Shiyan is 362nd warmest (15.5 °C year average), 541st sunniest (about 1,798 hours of sunshine a year) and 370th wettest (950 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Laohekou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (88 km away), Shiyan is 1.0 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 66 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.9 °C), averages 24.3 °C more than its coolest, January (2.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and December. The area lies about 823 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shiyan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shiyan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 823 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
