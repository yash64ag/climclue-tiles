How December compares in Shiyan

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Shiyan. Compared with November: 5.7 °C cooler, 20 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Shiyan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shiyan in December?
In December, Shiyan averages a day high of 9.1 °C (48 °F) and a night low of 0.1 °C (32 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 112 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Shiyan?
December is not one of Shiyan’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shiyan in December?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (161 mm), the driest December (16 mm).
Does it snow in Shiyan in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.7 cm (1.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Shiyan get in December?
About 112 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shiyan in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
