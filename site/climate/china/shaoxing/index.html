Shaoxing at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Shaoxing is 214th warmest (17.6 °C year average), 493rd sunniest (about 1,876 hours of sunshine a year) and 210th wettest (1,422 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shangyu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Shaoxing is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 27 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.8 °C), averages 24.8 °C more than its coolest, January (5.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 58 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shaoxing, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shaoxing has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 58 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
