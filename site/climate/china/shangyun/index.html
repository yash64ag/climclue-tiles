Shangyun at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Shangyun is 130th warmest (22.5 °C year average), 913th sunniest (about 2,147 hours of sunshine a year) and 703rd wettest (1,255 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fubang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Shangyun is 6.2 °C warmer in June and 5.8 °C warmer in January, with 606 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (26.2 °C), averages 9.5 °C more than its coolest, January (16.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.4 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 718 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shangyun, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shangyun has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 718 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.5 °C. The 10 km area around Shangyun, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 21.2 °C a year, 1.3 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Shangyun itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (23.052°N, 99.828°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-609-2798), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
