Tianjin weather in February

Average February weather in Tianjin, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Tianjin averages 5.3 °C (42 °F) days and -4.7 °C (24 °F) nights, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 179 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Tianjin

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Tianjin. Compared with January: 3.6 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. March is 6.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Tianjin in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tianjin in February?
In February, Tianjin averages a day high of 5.3 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -4.7 °C (24 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Tianjin?
February is not one of Tianjin’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tianjin in February?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (159 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tianjin in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.4 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Tianjin get in February?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tianjin in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
