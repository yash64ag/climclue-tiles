Tianjin weather in March

Average March weather in Tianjin, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Tianjin averages 12.2 °C (54 °F) days and 1.4 °C (35 °F) nights, with 9 mm (0.4 in) of rain over the month and about 223 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Tianjin

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Tianjin. Compared with February: 6.5 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. April is 8.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Tianjin in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tianjin in March?
In March, Tianjin averages a day high of 12.2 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 1.4 °C (35 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Tianjin?
March is not one of Tianjin’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tianjin in March?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (159 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tianjin in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tianjin get in March?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tianjin in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
