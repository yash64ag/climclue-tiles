How April compares in Tangshan

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Tangshan. Compared with March: 8.7 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. May is 5.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Tangshan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tangshan in April?
In April, Tangshan averages a day high of 20.1 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 7.0 °C (45 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Tangshan?
April is not one of Tangshan’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tangshan in April?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (185 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tangshan in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tangshan get in April?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tangshan in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
