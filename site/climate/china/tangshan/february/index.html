How February compares in Tangshan

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Tangshan. Compared with January: 3.7 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. March is 6.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Tangshan in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tangshan in February?
In February, Tangshan averages a day high of 4.8 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -6.5 °C (20 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Tangshan?
February is not one of Tangshan’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tangshan in February?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (186 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tangshan in February?
Yes, on average: about 2.7 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Tangshan get in February?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tangshan in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
