How June compares in Tangshan

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Tangshan. Compared with May: 4.1 °C warmer, 39 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. July is 2.0 °C warmer than June on average.

Tangshan in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tangshan in June?
In June, Tangshan averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 18.6 °C (65 °F), with 88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 269 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Tangshan?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Tangshan’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tangshan in June?
88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (185 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tangshan in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tangshan get in June?
About 269 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tangshan in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
