How October compares in Tangshan

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Tangshan. Compared with September: 7.2 °C cooler, 23 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. November is 9.2 °C cooler than October on average.

Tangshan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tangshan in October?
In October, Tangshan averages a day high of 18.8 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 7.6 °C (46 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Tangshan?
October is not one of Tangshan’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tangshan in October?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (186 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tangshan in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tangshan get in October?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tangshan in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
