How March compares in Tangshan

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Tangshan. Compared with February: 6.4 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. April is 8.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Tangshan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tangshan in March?
In March, Tangshan averages a day high of 11.3 °C (52 °F) and a night low of -0.3 °C (31 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Tangshan?
March is not one of Tangshan’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tangshan in March?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (186 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Tangshan in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Tangshan get in March?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tangshan in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
