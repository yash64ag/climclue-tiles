How November compares in Changzhi, Shanxi

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Changzhi, Shanxi. Compared with October: 7.4 °C cooler, 21 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. December is 6.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Changzhi, Shanxi in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changzhi, Shanxi in November?
In November, Changzhi, Shanxi averages a day high of 9.0 °C (48 °F) and a night low of -2.2 °C (28 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Changzhi, Shanxi?
November is not one of Changzhi, Shanxi’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changzhi, Shanxi in November?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (157 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Changzhi, Shanxi in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.6 cm (3.4 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Changzhi, Shanxi get in November?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Changzhi, Shanxi in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
