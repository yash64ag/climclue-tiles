Changzhi, Shanxi in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changzhi, Shanxi in August?
In August, Changzhi, Shanxi averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Changzhi, Shanxi?
August is not one of Changzhi, Shanxi’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changzhi, Shanxi in August?
145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (157 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Changzhi, Shanxi in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Changzhi, Shanxi get in August?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Changzhi, Shanxi in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
