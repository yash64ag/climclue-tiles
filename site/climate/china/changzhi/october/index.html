Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) in October?
In October, Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E) averages a day high of 16.2 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 5.0 °C (41 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E)?
October is not one of Changzhi, Shanxi (36.2°N 113.1°E)’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
