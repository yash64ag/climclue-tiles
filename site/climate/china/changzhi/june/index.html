Changzhi, Shanxi in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changzhi, Shanxi in June?
In June, Changzhi, Shanxi averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 15.1 °C (59 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 250 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Changzhi, Shanxi?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Changzhi, Shanxi’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changzhi, Shanxi in June?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (157 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Changzhi, Shanxi in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Changzhi, Shanxi get in June?
About 250 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Changzhi, Shanxi in June?
