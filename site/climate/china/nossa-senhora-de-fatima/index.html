Nossa Senhora de Fátima at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Nossa Senhora de Fátima is 24th warmest (23.1 °C year average), 427th sunniest (about 1,945 hours of sunshine a year) and 39th wettest (2,096 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tanzhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Nossa Senhora de Fátima is 0.2 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 25 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 12.9 °C more than its coolest, January (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.9 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Nossa Senhora de Fátima has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
