Jinshanlu at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Jinshanlu is 673rd warmest (3.2 °C year average), 25th sunniest (about 2,827 hours of sunshine a year) and 621st wettest (435 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Karamay, the nearest covered town with different numbers (353 km away), Jinshanlu is 8.9 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 313 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.9 °C), averages 35.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-15.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 2,255 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Jinshanlu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 2,255 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.
