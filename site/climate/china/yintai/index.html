Yintai at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Yintai is 517th warmest (11.5 °C year average), 314th sunniest (about 2,133 hours of sunshine a year) and 474th wettest (639 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tongchuanshi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Yintai is 1.3 °C cooler in July and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 100 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.9 °C), averages 26.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 857 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Yintai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 857 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
