How December compares in Jiaozhou

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Jiaozhou. Compared with November: 6.7 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. January is 2.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Jiaozhou in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiaozhou in December?
In December, Jiaozhou averages a day high of 5.9 °C (43 °F) and a night low of -3.4 °C (26 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jiaozhou?
December is not one of Jiaozhou’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiaozhou in December?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (191 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Jiaozhou in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.3 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiaozhou get in December?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiaozhou in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
