How August compares in Jiaozhou

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Jiaozhou. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. September is 4.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Jiaozhou in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiaozhou in August?
In August, Jiaozhou averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 175 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Jiaozhou?
August is not one of Jiaozhou’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiaozhou in August?
175 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (193 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Jiaozhou in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiaozhou get in August?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiaozhou in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
