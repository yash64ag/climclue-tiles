How February compares in Jiaozhou

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Jiaozhou. Compared with January: 2.3 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. March is 5.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Jiaozhou in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiaozhou in February?
In February, Jiaozhou averages a day high of 6.3 °C (43 °F) and a night low of -4.0 °C (25 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Jiaozhou?
February is not one of Jiaozhou’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiaozhou in February?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (193 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Jiaozhou in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.8 cm (1.9 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiaozhou get in February?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiaozhou in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
