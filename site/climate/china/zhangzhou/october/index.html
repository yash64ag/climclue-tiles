How October compares in Zhangzhou

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Zhangzhou. Compared with September: 3.1 °C cooler, 132 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. November is 4.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Zhangzhou in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhangzhou in October?
In October, Zhangzhou averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 18.8 °C (66 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Zhangzhou?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Zhangzhou’s mild months (October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhangzhou in October?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (272 mm), the driest December (32 mm).
Does it snow in Zhangzhou in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhangzhou get in October?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhangzhou in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
