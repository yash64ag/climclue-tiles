How March compares in Zhangzhou

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Zhangzhou. Compared with February: 2.5 °C warmer, 37 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. April is 4.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Zhangzhou in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhangzhou in March?
In March, Zhangzhou averages a day high of 20.3 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 13.6 °C (56 °F), with 118 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 108 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Zhangzhou?
March is not one of Zhangzhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhangzhou in March?
118 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (269 mm), the driest December (31 mm).
Does it snow in Zhangzhou in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhangzhou get in March?
About 108 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhangzhou in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
