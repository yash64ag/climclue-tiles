Zhangzhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Zhangzhou is 81st warmest (21.9 °C year average), 365th sunniest (about 2,013 hours of sunshine a year) and 150th wettest (1,558 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xiamen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Zhangzhou is 0.9 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 163 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.1 °C), averages 15.7 °C more than its coolest, January (13.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.0 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 60 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Zhangzhou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Zhangzhou

For mild weather, the best months to visit Zhangzhou are October and November.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): October and November.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (29.1 °C), August (28.9 °C) and June (27.4 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): December (32 mm), November (34 mm) and January (37 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (260 h), August (241 h) and September (200 h).

Why Zhangzhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 60 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
