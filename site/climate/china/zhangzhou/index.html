Why Zhangzhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 60 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Zhangzhou climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Zhangzhou?
July, with an average of 29.1 °C (84 °F), day highs around 32.3 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 24.8 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Zhangzhou?
January, with an average of 13.4 °C (56 °F), day highs around 18.4 °C (65 °F) and night lows around 8.0 °C (46 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Zhangzhou?
June, with 272 mm (10.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,558 mm (61.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Zhangzhou?
December, with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Zhangzhou?
July, with about 260 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Zhangzhou?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Zhangzhou?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: October and November.
What type of climate does Zhangzhou have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Zhangzhou’s GeoNames location (24.513°N, 117.656°E; ClimaClue area sq-594-2976), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 589,831 (GeoNames).

Sources: CHELSA V2.1 (1981–2010; temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity), ERA5-Land (Copernicus, 1981–2010; dew point, snow; contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information), CRU CL 2.0 (New et al. 2002, about 1961–1990; sunshine), GeoNames (town names and populations, CC BY 4.0), climate types by the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al.
