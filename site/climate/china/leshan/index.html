Why Leshan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,002 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Leshan climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Leshan?
July, with an average of 26.9 °C (80 °F), day highs around 29.9 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 23.1 °C (74 °F).
What is the coldest month in Leshan?
January, with an average of 7.8 °C (46 °F), day highs around 10.4 °C (51 °F) and night lows around 4.1 °C (39 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Leshan?
August, with 309 mm (12.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,320 mm (52.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Leshan?
December, with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Leshan?
August, with about 184 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Leshan?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 2.1 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Leshan?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April and October.
What type of climate does Leshan have?
Humid subtropical, dry winter (Köppen Cwa). Hot, wet summers and mild, dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.7 × 11 km, that contains Leshan’s GeoNames location (29.562°N, 103.764°E; ClimaClue area sq-544-2837), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 662,814 (GeoNames).
