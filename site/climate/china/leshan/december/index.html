How December compares in Leshan

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Leshan. Compared with November: 4.9 °C cooler, 20 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Leshan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Leshan in December?
In December, Leshan averages a day high of 11.5 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 5.8 °C (42 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 92 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Leshan?
December is not one of Leshan’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Leshan in December?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (309 mm), the driest December (15 mm).
Does it snow in Leshan in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Leshan get in December?
About 92 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Leshan in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
