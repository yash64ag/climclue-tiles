How November compares in Leshan

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Leshan. Compared with October: 4.2 °C cooler, 29 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. December is 4.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Leshan in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Leshan in November?
In November, Leshan averages a day high of 16.8 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 11.5 °C (53 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 91 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Leshan?
November is not one of Leshan’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Leshan in November?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (307 mm), the driest December (15 mm).
Does it snow in Leshan in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Leshan get in November?
About 91 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Leshan in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
