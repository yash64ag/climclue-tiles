How April compares in Ordos

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ordos. Compared with March: 8.1 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. May is 6.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Ordos in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ordos in April?
In April, Ordos averages a day high of 14.8 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 1.2 °C (34 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 253 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ordos?
April is not one of Ordos’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ordos in April?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (85 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ordos in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.6 cm (1.0 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ordos get in April?
About 253 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ordos in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
