How March compares in Ordos

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Ordos. Compared with February: 6.6 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. April is 8.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Ordos in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ordos in March?
In March, Ordos averages a day high of 6.8 °C (44 °F) and a night low of -5.4 °C (22 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Ordos?
March is not one of Ordos’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ordos in March?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (85 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ordos in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 11 cm (4.3 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ordos get in March?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ordos in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
