How June compares in Ordos

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Ordos. Compared with May: 4.5 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. July is 1.9 °C warmer than June on average.

Ordos in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ordos in June?
In June, Ordos averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 14.2 °C (58 °F), with 42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 289 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Ordos?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Ordos’s mild months (May, June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ordos in June?
42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (85 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ordos in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ordos get in June?
About 289 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ordos in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
