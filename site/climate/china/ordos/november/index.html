How November compares in Ordos

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Ordos. Compared with October: 8.6 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. December is 6.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Ordos in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ordos in November?
In November, Ordos averages a day high of 4.6 °C (40 °F) and a night low of -5.9 °C (21 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Ordos?
November is not one of Ordos’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ordos in November?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (85 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ordos in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.2 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ordos get in November?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ordos in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
