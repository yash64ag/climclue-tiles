Pengcheng weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Pengcheng, China (Hebei), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Pengcheng is warmest in July, with 31.9 °C (89 °F) days and 22.4 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 4.3 °C (40 °F) days and -6.5 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (154 mm); December is driest (5 mm).

Pengcheng at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Pengcheng is 1328th warmest (14.3 °C year average), 574th sunniest (about 2,430 hours of sunshine a year) and 1705th wettest (549 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Linshui, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Pengcheng is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.1 °C), averages 28.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 388 km from the sea.

Why Pengcheng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 388 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pengcheng itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.431°N, 114.170°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-475-2941), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
