How December compares in Xingtai

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Xingtai. Compared with November: 6.4 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. January is 1.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Xingtai in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xingtai in December?
In December, Xingtai averages a day high of 6.3 °C (43 °F) and a night low of -4.5 °C (24 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Xingtai?
December is not one of Xingtai’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xingtai in December?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (150 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Xingtai in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.1 cm (1.6 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Xingtai get in December?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xingtai in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
