How May compares in Xingtai

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Xingtai. Compared with April: 5.4 °C warmer, 24 mm more precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. June is 4.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Xingtai in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xingtai in May?
In May, Xingtai averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Xingtai?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Xingtai’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xingtai in May?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (150 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Xingtai in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xingtai get in May?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xingtai in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
