How January compares in Xingtai

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Xingtai. Compared with December: 1.6 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 3.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Xingtai in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xingtai in January?
In January, Xingtai averages a day high of 4.6 °C (40 °F) and a night low of -6.2 °C (21 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Xingtai?
January is not one of Xingtai’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xingtai in January?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (150 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Xingtai in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.5 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Xingtai get in January?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xingtai in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
