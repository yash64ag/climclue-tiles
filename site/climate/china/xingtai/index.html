Xingtai weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Xingtai, China (Hebei), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Xingtai at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Xingtai is 1321st warmest (14.3 °C year average), 505th sunniest (about 2,490 hours of sunshine a year) and 1761st wettest (521 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shahecheng, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Xingtai is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 29.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 329 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Xingtai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Xingtai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 329 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Xingtai itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.062°N, 114.493°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-469-2944), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
