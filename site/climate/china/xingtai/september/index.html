How September compares in Xingtai

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Xingtai. Compared with August: 4.5 °C cooler, 84 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. October is 6.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Xingtai in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xingtai in September?
In September, Xingtai averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 15.8 °C (60 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Xingtai?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Xingtai’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xingtai in September?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (149 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Xingtai in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xingtai get in September?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xingtai in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
