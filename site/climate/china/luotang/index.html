Luotang at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Luotang is 1629th warmest (12.0 °C year average), 1455th sunniest (about 1,756 hours of sunshine a year) and 1362nd wettest (726 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Waina, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Luotang is 2.4 °C cooler in July and 1.8 °C cooler in January, with 195 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.4 °C), averages 22.4 °C more than its coolest, January (0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 1,276 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Luotang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,276 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.4 °C. The 10 km area around Luotang, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 10.8 °C a year, 1.2 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Luotang itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.068°N, 105.265°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-509-2852), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
