Shuizhai at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Shuizhai is 78th warmest (21.9 °C year average), 457th sunniest (about 1,918 hours of sunshine a year) and 151st wettest (1,557 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Huacheng, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Shuizhai is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.1 °C), averages 16.1 °C more than its coolest, January (12.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.6 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 133 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shuizhai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shuizhai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 133 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
