How March compares in Ya'an

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Ya'an. Compared with February: 4.0 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. April is 4.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Ya'an in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ya'an in March?
In March, Ya'an averages a day high of 15.5 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 7.9 °C (46 °F), with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Ya'an?
March is not one of Ya'an’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ya'an in March?
47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (362 mm), the driest December (16 mm).
Does it snow in Ya'an in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ya'an get in March?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ya'an in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
