How December compares in Ya'an

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Ya'an. Compared with November: 5.3 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. January is 1.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Ya'an in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ya'an in December?
In December, Ya'an averages a day high of 11.3 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 4.9 °C (41 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 122 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ya'an?
December is not one of Ya'an’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ya'an in December?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (362 mm), the driest December (16 mm).
Does it snow in Ya'an in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.7 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ya'an get in December?
About 122 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ya'an in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
