Changbai weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Changbai, China (Jilin), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Changbai is warmest in July, with 25.7 °C (78 °F) days and 16.2 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -8.3 °C (17 °F) days and -20.0 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (157 mm); January is driest (9 mm).

Changbai at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Changbai is 1985th warmest (5.0 °C year average), 662nd sunniest (about 2,329 hours of sunshine a year) and 1504th wettest (640 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Songjianghe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (104 km away), Changbai is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 239 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.0 °C), averages 35.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-14.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 134 km from the sea.

Why Changbai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 134 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.2 °C. The 10 km area around Changbai, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 3.9 °C a year, 1.1 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Changbai itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.418°N, 128.196°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-425-3081), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
