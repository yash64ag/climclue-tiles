Xiangcheng at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Xiangcheng is 343rd warmest (16.1 °C year average), 289th sunniest (about 2,213 hours of sunshine a year) and 398th wettest (813 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dali, the nearest covered town with different numbers (37 km away), Xiangcheng is 0.6 °C warmer in June and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 290 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (21.5 °C), averages 12.3 °C more than its coolest, January (9.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 836 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Xiangcheng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 836 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
