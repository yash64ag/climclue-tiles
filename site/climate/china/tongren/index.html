Tongren at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Tongren is 668th warmest (3.8 °C year average), 142nd sunniest (about 2,561 hours of sunshine a year) and 596th wettest (486 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Linxia Chengguanzhen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (108 km away), Tongren is 6.3 °C cooler in July and 3.1 °C cooler in January, with 66 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (14.4 °C), averages 22.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-8.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 1,424 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tongren, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tongren has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 1,424 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
