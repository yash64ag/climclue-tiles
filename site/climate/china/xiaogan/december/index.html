Xiaogan weather in December

Average December weather in Xiaogan, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Xiaogan averages 10.4 °C (51 °F) days and 1.5 °C (35 °F) nights, with 29 mm (1.1 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 135 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Xiaogan

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Xiaogan. Compared with November: 5.7 °C cooler, 33 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. January is 2.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Xiaogan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xiaogan in December?
In December, Xiaogan averages a day high of 10.4 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 1.5 °C (35 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Xiaogan?
December is not one of Xiaogan’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xiaogan in December?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (231 mm), the driest December (29 mm).
Does it snow in Xiaogan in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.3 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Xiaogan get in December?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xiaogan in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
