Xiaogan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Xiaogan is 266th warmest (16.8 °C year average), 417th sunniest (about 1,958 hours of sunshine a year) and 227th wettest (1,363 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chengzhong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Xiaogan is 0.2 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 93 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 24.6 °C more than its coolest, January (3.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 658 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Xiaogan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Xiaogan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 658 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
