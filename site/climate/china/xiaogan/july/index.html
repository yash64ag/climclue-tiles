How July compares in Xiaogan

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Xiaogan. Compared with June: 2.7 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. August is 0.7 °C cooler than July on average.

Xiaogan in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xiaogan in July?
In July, Xiaogan averages a day high of 32.3 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 231 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Xiaogan?
July is not one of Xiaogan’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xiaogan in July?
231 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (231 mm), the driest December (29 mm).
Does it snow in Xiaogan in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xiaogan get in July?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xiaogan in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
