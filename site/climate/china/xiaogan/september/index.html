How September compares in Xiaogan

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Xiaogan. Compared with August: 4.1 °C cooler, 62 mm less precipitation and 73 hours less sunshine. October is 5.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Xiaogan in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xiaogan in September?
In September, Xiaogan averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.6 °C (67 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Xiaogan?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Xiaogan’s mild months (September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xiaogan in September?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (231 mm), the driest December (28 mm).
Does it snow in Xiaogan in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xiaogan get in September?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xiaogan in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
