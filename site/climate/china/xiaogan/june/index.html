How June compares in Xiaogan

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Xiaogan. Compared with May: 3.6 °C warmer, 52 mm more precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. July is 2.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Xiaogan in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xiaogan in June?
In June, Xiaogan averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 227 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Xiaogan?
June is not one of Xiaogan’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xiaogan in June?
227 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (231 mm), the driest December (29 mm).
Does it snow in Xiaogan in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xiaogan get in June?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xiaogan in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
