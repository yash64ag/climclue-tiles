Kaqun at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Kaqun is 1578th warmest (12.6 °C year average), 247th sunniest (about 2,616 hours of sunshine a year) and 2031st wettest (101 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Huoshilafu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (19 km away), Kaqun is 2.1 °C warmer in July and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 44 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.0 °C), averages 30.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, November and December. The area lies about 1,710 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Kaqun has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,710 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kaqun itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.978°N, 76.867°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-460-2568), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
