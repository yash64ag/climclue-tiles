How May compares in Ulanqab

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Ulanqab. Compared with April: 6.9 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 36 hours more sunshine. June is 4.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Ulanqab in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulanqab in May?
In May, Ulanqab averages a day high of 20.0 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 6.0 °C (43 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 296 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Ulanqab?
May is not one of Ulanqab’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulanqab in May?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (94 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ulanqab in May?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.9 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in May (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ulanqab get in May?
About 296 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulanqab in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
