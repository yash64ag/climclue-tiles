Ulanqab weather in February

Average February weather in Ulanqab, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Ulanqab averages -2.0 °C (28 °F) days and -15.4 °C (4 °F) nights, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 203 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Ulanqab

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Ulanqab. Compared with January: 4.0 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. March is 7.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Ulanqab in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulanqab in February?
In February, Ulanqab averages a day high of -2.0 °C (28 °F) and a night low of -15.4 °C (4 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Ulanqab?
February is not one of Ulanqab’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulanqab in February?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (98 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ulanqab in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.0 cm (1.2 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ulanqab get in February?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulanqab in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
