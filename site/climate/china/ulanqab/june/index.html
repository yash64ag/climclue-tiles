How June compares in Ulanqab

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Ulanqab. Compared with May: 4.8 °C warmer, 26 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. July is 2.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Ulanqab in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulanqab in June?
In June, Ulanqab averages a day high of 24.8 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 292 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Ulanqab?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Ulanqab’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulanqab in June?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (94 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ulanqab in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ulanqab get in June?
About 292 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulanqab in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
