Ulanqab at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Ulanqab is 634th warmest (5.5 °C year average), 21st sunniest (about 2,898 hours of sunshine a year) and 642nd wettest (351 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jining, Inner Mongolia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Ulanqab is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 8 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.1 °C), averages 33.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-12.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 455 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ulanqab, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ulanqab has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 455 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 33.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
