How July compares in Ulanqab

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Ulanqab. Compared with June: 2.1 °C warmer, 41 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. August is 2.0 °C cooler than July on average.

Ulanqab in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ulanqab in July?
In July, Ulanqab averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Ulanqab?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Ulanqab’s mild months (June, July and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ulanqab in July?
94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (94 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Ulanqab in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ulanqab get in July?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ulanqab in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
