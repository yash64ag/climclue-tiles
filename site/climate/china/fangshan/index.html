Fangshan at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Fangshan is 1447th warmest (13.3 °C year average), 177th sunniest (about 2,684 hours of sunshine a year) and 1778th wettest (513 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Liangxiang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Fangshan is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 14 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.3 °C), averages 30.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, November and December. The area lies about 177 km from the sea.

Why Fangshan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 177 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Fangshan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (39.687°N, 115.997°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-443-2959), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
