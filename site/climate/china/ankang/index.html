Ankang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Ankang is 285th warmest (16.7 °C year average), 579th sunniest (about 1,674 hours of sunshine a year) and 395th wettest (832 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shangluo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (157 km away), Ankang is 3.0 °C warmer in July and 4.7 °C warmer in January, with 255 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 23.6 °C more than its coolest, January (4.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 974 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ankang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ankang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 974 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Ankang itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (32.680°N, 109.017°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-513-2890), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
