Ankang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Ankang is 326th warmest (16.3 °C year average), 579th sunniest (about 1,674 hours of sunshine a year) and 390th wettest (881 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shangluo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (157 km away), Ankang is 3.1 °C warmer in July and 4.8 °C warmer in January, with 245 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 23.6 °C more than its coolest, January (4.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 974 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ankang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ankang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 974 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
