How July compares in Ankang

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Ankang. Compared with June: 2.1 °C warmer, 39 mm more precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. August is 0.8 °C cooler than July on average.

Ankang in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ankang in July?
In July, Ankang averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.0 °C (73 °F), with 167 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Ankang?
July is not one of Ankang’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ankang in July?
167 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (167 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Ankang in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ankang get in July?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ankang in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
