How June compares in Ankang

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Ankang. Compared with May: 4.0 °C warmer, 35 mm more precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. July is 2.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Ankang in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ankang in June?
In June, Ankang averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Ankang?
June is not one of Ankang’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ankang in June?
120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (156 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Ankang in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ankang get in June?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ankang in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
