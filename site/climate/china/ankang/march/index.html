How March compares in Ankang

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Ankang. Compared with February: 4.4 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. April is 6.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Ankang in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ankang in March?
In March, Ankang averages a day high of 16.3 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 6.5 °C (44 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 121 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Ankang?
March is not one of Ankang’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ankang in March?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (156 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Ankang in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ankang get in March?
About 121 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ankang in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
