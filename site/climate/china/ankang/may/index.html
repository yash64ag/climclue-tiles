How May compares in Ankang

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Ankang. Compared with April: 4.6 °C warmer, 32 mm more precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. June is 4.0 °C warmer than May on average.

Ankang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ankang in May?
In May, Ankang averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Ankang?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Ankang’s mild months (April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ankang in May?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (167 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Ankang in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ankang get in May?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ankang in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
