How August compares in Ankang

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Ankang. Compared with July: 0.8 °C cooler, 25 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. September is 4.8 °C cooler than August on average.

Ankang in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ankang in August?
In August, Ankang averages a day high of 30.3 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 142 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Ankang?
August is not one of Ankang’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ankang in August?
142 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (167 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Ankang in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ankang get in August?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ankang in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
