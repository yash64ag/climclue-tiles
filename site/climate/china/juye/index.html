Juye at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Juye is 1261st warmest (14.7 °C year average), 625th sunniest (about 2,386 hours of sunshine a year) and 1449th wettest (668 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jining, Shandong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Juye is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 23 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.3 °C), averages 27.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 294 km from the sea.

Why Juye has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 294 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Juye itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.387°N, 116.072°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-486-2960), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
