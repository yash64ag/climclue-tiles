Qingyang, Gansu in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qingyang, Gansu in November?
In November, Qingyang, Gansu averages a day high of 6.9 °C (44 °F) and a night low of -2.2 °C (28 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Qingyang, Gansu?
November is not one of Qingyang, Gansu’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (131 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
