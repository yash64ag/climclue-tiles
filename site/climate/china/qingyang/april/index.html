How April compares in Qingyang

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Qingyang. Compared with March: 6.8 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. May is 5.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Qingyang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qingyang in April?
In April, Qingyang averages a day high of 17.1 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 3.2 °C (38 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Qingyang?
April is not one of Qingyang’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qingyang in April?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (127 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Qingyang in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.7 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Qingyang get in April?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qingyang in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
