Is September a good time to visit Qingyang, Gansu?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Qingyang, Gansu’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
