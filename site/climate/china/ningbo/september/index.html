How September compares in Ningbo

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Ningbo. Compared with August: 3.5 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 68 hours less sunshine. October is 4.8 °C cooler than September on average.

Ningbo in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ningbo in September?
In September, Ningbo averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 195 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Ningbo?
September is not one of Ningbo’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ningbo in September?
195 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (232 mm), the driest December (60 mm).
Does it snow in Ningbo in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ningbo get in September?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ningbo in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
