Ningbo weather in April

Average April weather in Ningbo, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Ningbo averages 19.4 °C (67 °F) days and 11.7 °C (53 °F) nights, with 125 mm (4.9 in) of rain over the month and about 141 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Ningbo

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Ningbo. Compared with March: 5.2 °C warmer, 25 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. May is 5.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Ningbo in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ningbo in April?
In April, Ningbo averages a day high of 19.4 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 11.7 °C (53 °F), with 125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 141 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Ningbo?
April is not one of Ningbo’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ningbo in April?
125 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (232 mm), the driest December (60 mm).
Does it snow in Ningbo in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ningbo get in April?
About 141 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ningbo in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
