How February compares in Ningbo

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Ningbo. Compared with January: 1.2 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. March is 3.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Ningbo in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ningbo in February?
In February, Ningbo averages a day high of 10.5 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 3.9 °C (39 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 102 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Ningbo?
February is not one of Ningbo’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ningbo in February?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (232 mm), the driest December (60 mm).
Does it snow in Ningbo in February?
Yes, on average: about 2.6 cm (1.0 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Ningbo get in February?
About 102 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ningbo in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
