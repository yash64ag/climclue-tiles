How June compares in Ningbo

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Ningbo. Compared with May: 3.8 °C warmer, 99 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. July is 4.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Ningbo in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ningbo in June?
In June, Ningbo averages a day high of 27.8 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 232 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Ningbo?
June is not one of Ningbo’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ningbo in June?
232 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (232 mm), the driest December (60 mm).
Does it snow in Ningbo in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ningbo get in June?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ningbo in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
