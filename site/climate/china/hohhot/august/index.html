Hohhot in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hohhot in August?
In August, Hohhot averages a day high of 26.5 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 15.1 °C (59 °F), with 93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 258 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Hohhot?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Hohhot’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hohhot in August?
93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (99 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Hohhot in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hohhot get in August?
About 258 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
