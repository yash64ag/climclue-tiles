Hohhot at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Hohhot is 615th warmest (6.7 °C year average), 13th sunniest (about 2,933 hours of sunshine a year) and 639th wettest (376 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Salaqi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (101 km away), Hohhot is 1.1 °C cooler in July and 1.6 °C cooler in January, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.8 °C), averages 34.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-12.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 563 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 2.0 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 95% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Hohhot has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 563 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 34.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
