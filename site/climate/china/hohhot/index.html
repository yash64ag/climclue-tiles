Hohhot at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Hohhot is 615th warmest (7.0 °C year average), 13th sunniest (about 2,933 hours of sunshine a year) and 636th wettest (374 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Salaqi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (101 km away), Hohhot is 0.9 °C cooler in July and 1.5 °C cooler in January, with 17 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.1 °C), averages 35.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-11.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 563 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 94% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Hohhot has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 563 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-431-2916), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
