How December compares in Hohhot

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Hohhot. Compared with November: 7.8 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. January is 2.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Hohhot in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hohhot in December?
In December, Hohhot averages a day high of -3.7 °C (25 °F) and a night low of -15.2 °C (5 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 191 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Hohhot?
December is not one of Hohhot’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hohhot in December?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (99 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Hohhot in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.4 cm (2.5 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Hohhot get in December?
About 191 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hohhot in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
