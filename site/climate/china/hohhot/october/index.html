Hohhot in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hohhot in October?
In October, Hohhot averages a day high of 13.3 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 1.0 °C (34 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 242 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Hohhot?
October is not one of Hohhot’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hohhot in October?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (99 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Hohhot in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.4 cm (0.6 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Hohhot get in October?
About 242 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hohhot in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
