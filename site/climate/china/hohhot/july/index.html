What is the weather like in Hohhot in July?
In July, Hohhot averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 273 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Hohhot?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Hohhot’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
