Hohhot in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hohhot in September?
In September, Hohhot averages a day high of 20.6 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 8.6 °C (47 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Hohhot?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Hohhot’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hohhot in September?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (99 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Hohhot in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hohhot get in September?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hohhot in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
