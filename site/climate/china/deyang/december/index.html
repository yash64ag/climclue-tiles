Deyang weather in December

Average December weather in Deyang, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Deyang averages 11.4 °C (53 °F) days and 4.2 °C (40 °F) nights, with 7 mm (0.3 in) of rain over the month and about 70 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Deyang

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the least sunny month in Deyang. Compared with November: 5.1 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. January is 1.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Deyang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Deyang in December?
In December, Deyang averages a day high of 11.4 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 4.2 °C (40 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 70 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Deyang?
December is not one of Deyang’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Deyang in December?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (209 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Deyang in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Deyang get in December?
About 70 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Deyang in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
