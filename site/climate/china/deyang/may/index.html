How May compares in Deyang

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Deyang. Compared with April: 4.6 °C warmer, 30 mm more precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. June is 2.4 °C warmer than May on average.

Deyang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Deyang in May?
In May, Deyang averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 17.6 °C (64 °F), with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Deyang?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Deyang’s mild months (April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Deyang in May?
74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (204 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Deyang in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Deyang get in May?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Deyang in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
