How April compares in Deyang

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Deyang. Compared with March: 5.3 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. May is 4.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Deyang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Deyang in April?
In April, Deyang averages a day high of 21.7 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 137 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Deyang?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Deyang’s mild months (April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Deyang in April?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (204 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Deyang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Deyang get in April?
About 137 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Deyang in April?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
