Tongren, Guizhou at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Tongren, Guizhou is 738th warmest (17.6 °C year average), 1702nd sunniest (about 1,396 hours of sunshine a year) and 1020th wettest (1,066 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fenghuang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Tongren, Guizhou is 0.9 °C warmer in July and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 349 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.3 °C), averages 22.8 °C more than its coolest, January (5.6 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 680 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Tongren, Guizhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 680 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tongren itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (27.717°N, 109.185°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-562-2891), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
