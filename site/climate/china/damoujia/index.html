Damoujia at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Damoujia is 1491st warmest (13.2 °C year average), 464th sunniest (about 2,510 hours of sunshine a year) and 1465th wettest (659 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jiangzhuang, Shandong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Damoujia is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 6 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.1 °C), averages 27.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 52 km from the sea.

Why Damoujia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 52 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Damoujia itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.544°N, 119.813°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-474-2998), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
