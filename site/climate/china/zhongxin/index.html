Zhongxin at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Zhongxin is 194th warmest (21.2 °C year average), 704th sunniest (about 2,296 hours of sunshine a year) and 1190th wettest (884 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Rongjiang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Zhongxin is about as warm in June and about as warm in December, with 48 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (25.9 °C), averages 11.8 °C more than its coolest, December (14.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.0 °C (45 °F), in December. The area lies about 877 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Zhongxin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 877 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C. The 10 km area around Zhongxin, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 19.4 °C a year, 1.9 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Zhongxin itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.617°N, 101.272°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-573-2812), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
