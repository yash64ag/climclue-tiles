How May compares in Longyan

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Longyan. Compared with April: 3.3 °C warmer, 27 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. June is 2.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Longyan in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Longyan in May?
In May, Longyan averages a day high of 26.0 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 18.7 °C (66 °F), with 230 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation and about 134 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Longyan?
May is not one of Longyan’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Longyan in May?
230 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (286 mm), the driest December (37 mm).
Does it snow in Longyan in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Longyan get in May?
About 134 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Longyan in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
