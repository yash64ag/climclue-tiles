How March compares in Longyan

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Longyan. Compared with February: 2.8 °C warmer, 83 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. April is 4.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Longyan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Longyan in March?
In March, Longyan averages a day high of 19.5 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 10.9 °C (52 °F), with 194 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation and about 97 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Longyan?
March is not one of Longyan’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Longyan in March?
194 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (286 mm), the driest December (37 mm).
Does it snow in Longyan in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Longyan get in March?
About 97 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Longyan in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
