How February compares in Longyan

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Longyan. Compared with January: 1.5 °C warmer, 56 mm more precipitation and 39 hours less sunshine. March is 2.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Longyan in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Longyan in February?
In February, Longyan averages a day high of 16.6 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 7.9 °C (46 °F), with 111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 87 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Longyan?
February is not one of Longyan’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Longyan in February?
111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (286 mm), the driest December (37 mm).
Does it snow in Longyan in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Longyan get in February?
About 87 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Longyan in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
