How October compares in Longyan

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Longyan. Compared with September: 3.3 °C cooler, 90 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. November is 4.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Longyan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Longyan in October?
In October, Longyan averages a day high of 25.9 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 16.3 °C (61 °F), with 42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Longyan?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Longyan’s mild months (October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Longyan in October?
42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (286 mm), the driest December (37 mm).
Does it snow in Longyan in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Longyan get in October?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Longyan in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
