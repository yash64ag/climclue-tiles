Longyan weather in January

Average January weather in Longyan, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Longyan averages 16.4 °C (62 °F) days and 7.5 °C (46 °F) nights, with 53 mm (2.1 in) of rain over the month and about 125 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Longyan

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Longyan. Compared with December: 1.1 °C cooler, 18 mm more precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. February is 1.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Longyan in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Longyan in January?
In January, Longyan averages a day high of 16.4 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 7.5 °C (46 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Longyan?
January is not one of Longyan’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Longyan in January?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (261 mm), the driest December (35 mm).
Does it snow in Longyan in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Longyan get in January?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Longyan in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
