How November compares in Longyan

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Longyan. Compared with October: 4.7 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. December is 4.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Longyan in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Longyan in November?
In November, Longyan averages a day high of 21.7 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Longyan?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Longyan’s mild months (October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Longyan in November?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (286 mm), the driest December (37 mm).
Does it snow in Longyan in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Longyan get in November?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Longyan in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
