Suining in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suining in June?
In June, Suining averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 161 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 139 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Suining?
June is not one of Suining’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suining in June?
161 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (223 mm), the driest January (16 mm).
Does it snow in Suining in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suining get in June?
About 139 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suining in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
