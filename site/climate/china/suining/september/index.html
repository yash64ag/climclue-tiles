How September compares in Suining

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Suining. Compared with August: 4.1 °C cooler, 52 mm less precipitation and 95 hours less sunshine. October is 5.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Suining in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suining in September?
In September, Suining averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 126 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 100 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Suining?
September is not one of Suining’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suining in September?
126 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (223 mm), the driest January (16 mm).
Does it snow in Suining in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suining get in September?
About 100 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suining in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
