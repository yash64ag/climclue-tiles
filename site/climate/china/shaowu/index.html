Shaowu at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Shaowu is 121st warmest (19.1 °C year average), 547th sunniest (about 1,786 hours of sunshine a year) and 77th wettest (1,837 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Wuyishan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (71 km away), Shaowu is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 97 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.1 °C), averages 21.3 °C more than its coolest, January (7.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 264 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shaowu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shaowu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 264 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
