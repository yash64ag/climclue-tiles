Jiazi sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Guangdong · China

The sea off Jiazi averages 28.0 °C (82 °F) in July, its warmest month, and 18.1 °C (65 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

Jiazi sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Jiazi?
In July, at about 28.0 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Jiazi?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is February, at 18.1 °C (65 °F).
How warm is the sea in Jiazi in February?
About 18.1 °C (65 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Jiazi?
About 23.5 °C (74 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
