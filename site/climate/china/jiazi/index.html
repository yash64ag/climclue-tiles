Jiazi at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Jiazi is 58th warmest (22.5 °C year average), 357th sunniest (about 2,024 hours of sunshine a year) and 74th wettest (1,867 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jieshi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Jiazi is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 266 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 13.4 °C more than its coolest, January (15.2 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.3 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jiazi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jiazi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
