Why Yan’an has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 759 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Yan’an climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Yan’an?
July, with an average of 23.6 °C (74 °F), day highs around 28.4 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 18.1 °C (65 °F).
What is the coldest month in Yan’an?
January, with an average of -4.8 °C (23 °F), day highs around 1.6 °C (35 °F) and night lows around -10.0 °C (14 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Yan’an?
August, with 134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 560 mm (22.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Yan’an?
December, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Yan’an?
June, with about 253 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Yan’an?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 46 cm (17.9 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in March; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 3 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Yan’an?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June and September.
What type of climate does Yan’an have?
