Zhengding at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Zhengding is 441st warmest (13.9 °C year average), 133rd sunniest (about 2,569 hours of sunshine a year) and 587th wettest (511 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shijiazhuang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Zhengding is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 7 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 29.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, November and December. The area lies about 276 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 18 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 99% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Zhengding has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 276 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
