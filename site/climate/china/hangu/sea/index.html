Hangu sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Tianjin · China

The sea off Hangu averages 24.7 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 1.4 °C (35 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Hangu sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Hangu?
In August, at about 24.7 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Hangu?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 1.4 °C (35 °F).
How warm is the sea in Hangu in February?
About 1.4 °C (35 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Hangu?
About 13.1 °C (56 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Hangu nearby?
In August: Qinhuangdao (25.6 °C, 172 km), Shanhaiguan (25.8 °C, 188 km) and Xiaying (27.0 °C, 286 km).
