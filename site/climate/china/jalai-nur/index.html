Jalai Nur at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Jalai Nur is 686th warmest (1.0 °C year average), 52nd sunniest (about 2,734 hours of sunshine a year) and 656th wettest (276 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hailar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (148 km away), Jalai Nur is 1.2 °C warmer in July and 1.9 °C warmer in January, with 71 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.4 °C), averages 44.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-22.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 995 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Jalai Nur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 995 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 44.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
