Dongguan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Dongguan is 29th warmest (23.0 °C year average), 510th sunniest (about 1,850 hours of sunshine a year) and 34th wettest (2,121 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shilong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Dongguan is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 80 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.3 °C), averages 14.4 °C more than its coolest, January (14.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.1 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dongguan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Dongguan

For mild weather, the best months to visit Dongguan are February, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): February, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (29.3 °C), August (29.1 °C) and June (28.3 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): December (31 mm), January (39 mm) and November (42 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (227 h), August (205 h) and October (190 h).

Why Dongguan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
