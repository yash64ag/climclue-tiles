How May compares in Dongguan

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Dongguan. Compared with April: 3.2 °C warmer, 103 mm more precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. June is 1.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Dongguan in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dongguan in May?
In May, Dongguan averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 22.7 °C (73 °F), with 319 mm (12.6 in) of precipitation and about 141 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Dongguan?
May is not one of Dongguan’s mild months; those are February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dongguan in May?
319 mm (12.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (416 mm), the driest December (31 mm).
Does it snow in Dongguan in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dongguan get in May?
About 141 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dongguan in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
