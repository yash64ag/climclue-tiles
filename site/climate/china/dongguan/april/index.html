How April compares in Dongguan

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Dongguan. Compared with March: 4.1 °C warmer, 112 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. May is 3.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Dongguan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dongguan in April?
In April, Dongguan averages a day high of 26.5 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 216 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation and about 96 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Dongguan?
April is not one of Dongguan’s mild months; those are February, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dongguan in April?
216 mm (8.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (420 mm), the driest December (31 mm).
Does it snow in Dongguan in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dongguan get in April?
About 96 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dongguan in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
