Why Changle has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 62 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Changle climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Changle?
July, with an average of 26.8 °C (80 °F), day highs around 31.1 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 22.9 °C (73 °F).
What is the coldest month in Changle?
January, with an average of -1.6 °C (29 °F), day highs around 3.7 °C (39 °F) and night lows around -6.1 °C (21 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Changle?
July, with 141 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 576 mm (22.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Changle?
January, with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Changle?
May, with about 262 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Changle?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 25 cm (10.0 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 1 month.
When is the best time for mild weather in Changle?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, September and October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.9 × 11 km, that contains Changle’s GeoNames location (36.706°N, 118.828°E; ClimaClue area sq-472-2988), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 259,161 (GeoNames).
