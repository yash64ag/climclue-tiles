Nanchuan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Nanchuan is 315th warmest (16.4 °C year average), 679th sunniest (about 1,232 hours of sunshine a year) and 304th wettest (1,182 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Wansheng, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Nanchuan is 0.9 °C cooler in July and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 11 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.3 °C), averages 20.7 °C more than its coolest, January (5.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 847 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nanchuan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nanchuan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 847 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
