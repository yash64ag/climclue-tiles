How March compares in Nanjing

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Nanjing. Compared with February: 4.4 °C warmer, 28 mm more precipitation and 21 hours more sunshine. April is 6.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Nanjing in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanjing in March?
In March, Nanjing averages a day high of 14.3 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 4.6 °C (40 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Nanjing?
March is not one of Nanjing’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanjing in March?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (225 mm), the driest December (32 mm).
Does it snow in Nanjing in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.6 cm (1.0 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanjing get in March?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanjing in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
