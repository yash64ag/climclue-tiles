Nanjing at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Nanjing is 337th warmest (16.1 °C year average), 344th sunniest (about 2,055 hours of sunshine a year) and 308th wettest (1,167 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zhenzhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Nanjing is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 98 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.3 °C), averages 25.4 °C more than its coolest, January (2.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 221 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 18 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 107% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Nanjing has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 221 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
