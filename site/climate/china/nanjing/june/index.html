How June compares in Nanjing

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Nanjing. Compared with May: 3.7 °C warmer, 83 mm more precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. July is 3.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Nanjing in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanjing in June?
In June, Nanjing averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 176 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Nanjing?
June is not one of Nanjing’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanjing in June?
176 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (225 mm), the driest December (32 mm).
Does it snow in Nanjing in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanjing get in June?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanjing in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
