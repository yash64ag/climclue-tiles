How May compares in Nanjing

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Nanjing. Compared with April: 5.4 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. June is 3.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Nanjing in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanjing in May?
In May, Nanjing averages a day high of 25.4 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 93 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Nanjing?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Nanjing’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanjing in May?
93 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (225 mm), the driest December (32 mm).
Does it snow in Nanjing in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanjing get in May?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanjing in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
