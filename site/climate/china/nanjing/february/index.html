How February compares in Nanjing

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Nanjing. Compared with January: 2.3 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. March is 4.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Nanjing in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanjing in February?
In February, Nanjing averages a day high of 9.4 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 0.9 °C (34 °F), with 59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 126 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Nanjing?
February is not one of Nanjing’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanjing in February?
59 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (229 mm), the driest December (32 mm).
Does it snow in Nanjing in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.5 cm (3.0 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanjing get in February?
About 126 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanjing in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
