How April compares in Nanjing

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Nanjing. Compared with March: 6.1 °C warmer, 3 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. May is 5.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Nanjing in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanjing in April?
In April, Nanjing averages a day high of 20.7 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 10.8 °C (51 °F), with 84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Nanjing?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Nanjing’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanjing in April?
84 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (229 mm), the driest December (32 mm).
Does it snow in Nanjing in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanjing get in April?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanjing in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
