How September compares in Nanjing

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Nanjing. Compared with August: 4.2 °C cooler, 82 mm less precipitation and 62 hours less sunshine. October is 5.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Nanjing in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanjing in September?
In September, Nanjing averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Nanjing?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Nanjing’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanjing in September?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (229 mm), the driest December (32 mm).
Does it snow in Nanjing in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanjing get in September?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanjing in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
