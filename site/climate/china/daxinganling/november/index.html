Daxing’anling in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daxing’anling in November?
In November, Daxing’anling averages a day high of -8.9 °C (16 °F) and a night low of -18.8 °C (-2 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 139 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Daxing’anling?
November is not one of Daxing’anling’s mild months; those are June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daxing’anling in November?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (124 mm), the driest February (4 mm).
Does it snow in Daxing’anling in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 11 cm (4.4 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Daxing’anling get in November?
About 139 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daxing’anling in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
