Daxing’anling in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daxing’anling in March?
In March, Daxing’anling averages a day high of -3.2 °C (26 °F) and a night low of -18.4 °C (-1 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Daxing’anling?
March is not one of Daxing’anling’s mild months; those are June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daxing’anling in March?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (124 mm), the driest February (4 mm).
Does it snow in Daxing’anling in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 16 cm (6.3 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 20 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Daxing’anling get in March?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daxing’anling in March?
