Daxing’anling in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daxing’anling in December?
In December, Daxing’anling averages a day high of -17.3 °C (1 °F) and a night low of -29.9 °C (-22 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 113 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Daxing’anling?
December is not one of Daxing’anling’s mild months; those are June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daxing’anling in December?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (117 mm), the driest February (4 mm).
Does it snow in Daxing’anling in December?
Yes, on average: about 7.6 cm (3.0 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 6.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Daxing’anling get in December?
About 113 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daxing’anling in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
