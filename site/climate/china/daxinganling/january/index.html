Daxing’anling in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daxing’anling in January?
In January, Daxing’anling averages a day high of -17.3 °C (1 °F) and a night low of -31.7 °C (-25 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Daxing’anling?
January is not one of Daxing’anling’s mild months; those are June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daxing’anling in January?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (117 mm), the driest February (4 mm).
Does it snow in Daxing’anling in January?
Yes, on average: about 6.2 cm (2.4 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 7.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Daxing’anling get in January?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daxing’anling in January?
