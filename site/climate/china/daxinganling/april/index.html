Daxing’anling in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daxing’anling in April?
In April, Daxing’anling averages a day high of 7.5 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -4.8 °C (23 °F), with 22 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Daxing’anling?
April is not one of Daxing’anling’s mild months; those are June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daxing’anling in April?
22 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (124 mm), the driest February (4 mm).
Does it snow in Daxing’anling in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 26 cm (10.2 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 11 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Daxing’anling get in April?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daxing’anling in April?
