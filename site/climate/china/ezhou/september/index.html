E’zhou weather in September

Average September weather in E’zhou, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, E’zhou averages 29.0 °C (84 °F) days and 20.0 °C (68 °F) nights, with 94 mm (3.7 in) of rain over the month and about 178 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in E’zhou

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in E’zhou. Compared with August: 4.1 °C cooler, 52 mm less precipitation and 72 hours less sunshine. October is 5.7 °C cooler than September on average.

E’zhou in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in E’zhou in September?
In September, E’zhou averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit E’zhou?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of E’zhou’s mild months (September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in E’zhou in September?
94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (273 mm), the driest December (37 mm).
Does it snow in E’zhou in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does E’zhou get in September?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for E’zhou in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
