How March compares in E’zhou

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in E’zhou. Compared with February: 4.1 °C warmer, 41 mm more precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. April is 6.3 °C warmer than March on average.

E’zhou in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in E’zhou in March?
In March, E’zhou averages a day high of 15.4 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 7.3 °C (45 °F), with 121 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation and about 117 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit E’zhou?
March is not one of E’zhou’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in E’zhou in March?
121 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (267 mm), the driest December (37 mm).
Does it snow in E’zhou in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.0 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does E’zhou get in March?
About 117 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for E’zhou in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
