How December compares in Jilin

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Jilin. Compared with November: 9.4 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. January is 3.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Jilin in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jilin in December?
In December, Jilin averages a day high of -7.3 °C (19 °F) and a night low of -17.9 °C (0 °F), with 9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jilin?
December is not one of Jilin’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jilin in December?
9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (176 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Jilin in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 18 cm (7.0 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 11 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Jilin get in December?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jilin in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
