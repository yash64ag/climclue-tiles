How November compares in Jilin

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Jilin. Compared with October: 10.5 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 39 hours less sunshine. December is 9.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Jilin in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jilin in November?
In November, Jilin averages a day high of 1.3 °C (34 °F) and a night low of -7.2 °C (19 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Jilin?
November is not one of Jilin’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jilin in November?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (176 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Jilin in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.8 cm (3.9 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Jilin get in November?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jilin in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
