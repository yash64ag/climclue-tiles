Jilin weather in October

Average October weather in Jilin, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Jilin averages 14.0 °C (57 °F) days and 0.9 °C (34 °F) nights, with 30 mm (1.2 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 204 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Jilin

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Jilin. Compared with September: 8.3 °C cooler, 24 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. November is 10.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Jilin in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jilin in October?
In October, Jilin averages a day high of 14.0 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 0.9 °C (34 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Jilin?
October is not one of Jilin’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jilin in October?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (176 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Jilin in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.9 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Jilin get in October?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jilin in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
