How February compares in Jilin

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Jilin. Compared with January: 5.4 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. March is 8.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Jilin in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jilin in February?
In February, Jilin averages a day high of -5.1 °C (23 °F) and a night low of -17.8 °C (0 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Jilin?
February is not one of Jilin’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jilin in February?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (176 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Jilin in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.7 cm (2.6 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Jilin get in February?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jilin in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
