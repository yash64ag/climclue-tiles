Jilin at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Jilin is 631st warmest (5.9 °C year average), 192nd sunniest (about 2,506 hours of sunshine a year) and 469th wettest (650 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jishu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (56 km away), Jilin is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 10 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.3 °C), averages 39.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-15.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 355 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jilin, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Jilin

For mild weather, the best months to visit Jilin are May and September.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): May and September.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (23.3 °C), August (22.1 °C) and June (20.8 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): January (7 mm), February (7 mm) and December (9 mm).

Why Jilin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 355 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 39.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
