How September compares in Jilin

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Jilin. Compared with August: 6.4 °C cooler, 87 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. October is 8.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Jilin in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jilin in September?
In September, Jilin averages a day high of 20.7 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 11.3 °C (52 °F), with 54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Jilin?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Jilin’s mild months (May and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jilin in September?
54 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (176 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Jilin in September?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.5 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in September (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Jilin get in September?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jilin in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
