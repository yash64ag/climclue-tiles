Huzhai at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Huzhai is 1230th warmest (14.8 °C year average), 676th sunniest (about 2,310 hours of sunshine a year) and 1267th wettest (810 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zhangzhai, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Huzhai is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 6 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.3 °C), averages 26.9 °C more than its coolest, January (0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 202 km from the sea.

Why Huzhai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 202 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Huzhai itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.669°N, 117.008°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-493-2970), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
