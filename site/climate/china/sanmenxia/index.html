Sanmenxia at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Sanmenxia is 434th warmest (14.2 °C year average), 312th sunniest (about 2,133 hours of sunshine a year) and 562nd wettest (546 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yuncheng, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Sanmenxia is 1.1 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 46 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.9 °C), averages 27.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 718 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sanmenxia, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sanmenxia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 718 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
