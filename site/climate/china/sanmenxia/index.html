Sanmenxia weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Sanmenxia, China (Henan), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Sanmenxia is warmest in July, with 31.9 °C (89 °F) days and 22.1 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 5.3 °C (42 °F) days and -5.7 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (108 mm); December is driest (5 mm).

Sanmenxia at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Sanmenxia is 1322nd warmest (14.3 °C year average), 928th sunniest (about 2,133 hours of sunshine a year) and 1742nd wettest (528 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yuncheng, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Sanmenxia is 1.0 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 21 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.9 °C), averages 27.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 718 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Sanmenxia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 718 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sanmenxia itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.781°N, 111.193°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-492-2911), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
