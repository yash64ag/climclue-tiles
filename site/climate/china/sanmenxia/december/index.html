How December compares in Sanmenxia

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Sanmenxia. Compared with November: 6.2 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Sanmenxia in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sanmenxia in December?
In December, Sanmenxia averages a day high of 6.5 °C (44 °F) and a night low of -4.3 °C (24 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Sanmenxia?
December is not one of Sanmenxia’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sanmenxia in December?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (108 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Sanmenxia in December?
Yes, on average: about 2.6 cm (1.0 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Sanmenxia get in December?
About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sanmenxia in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
