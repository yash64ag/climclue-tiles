How July compares in Sanmenxia

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Sanmenxia. Compared with June: 1.5 °C warmer, 45 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. August is 1.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Sanmenxia in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sanmenxia in July?
In July, Sanmenxia averages a day high of 31.8 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 108 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Sanmenxia?
July is not one of Sanmenxia’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sanmenxia in July?
108 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (108 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Sanmenxia in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sanmenxia get in July?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sanmenxia in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
