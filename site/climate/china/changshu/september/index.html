How September compares in Changshu

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Changshu. Compared with August: 4.2 °C cooler, 78 mm less precipitation and 68 hours less sunshine. October is 5.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Changshu in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changshu in September?
In September, Changshu averages a day high of 26.9 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 103 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Changshu?
September is not one of Changshu’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changshu in September?
103 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (215 mm), the driest December (35 mm).
Does it snow in Changshu in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Changshu get in September?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Changshu in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
