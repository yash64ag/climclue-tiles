How November compares in Korla

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Korla. Compared with October: 8.8 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 65 hours less sunshine. December is 7.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Korla in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Korla in November?
In November, Korla averages a day high of 7.7 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -1.5 °C (29 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Korla?
November is not one of Korla’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Korla in November?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (31 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Korla in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.9 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Korla get in November?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Korla in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
