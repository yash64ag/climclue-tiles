How March compares in Korla

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Korla. Compared with February: 7.7 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. April is 8.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Korla in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Korla in March?
In March, Korla averages a day high of 12.1 °C (54 °F) and a night low of -0.4 °C (31 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Korla?
March is not one of Korla’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Korla in March?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (31 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Korla in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.7 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Korla get in March?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Korla in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
