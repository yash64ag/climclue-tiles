Korla in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Korla in September?
In September, Korla averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 268 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Korla?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Korla’s mild months (May, June, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Korla in September?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (32 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Korla in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Korla get in September?
About 268 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Korla in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
