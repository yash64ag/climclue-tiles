Korla in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Korla in April?
In April, Korla averages a day high of 20.7 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 6.8 °C (44 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 245 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Korla?
April is not one of Korla’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Korla in April?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (31 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Korla in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.0 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Korla get in April?
About 245 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Korla in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
