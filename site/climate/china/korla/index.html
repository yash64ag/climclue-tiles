Korla at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Korla is 522nd warmest (11.3 °C year average), 26th sunniest (about 2,823 hours of sunshine a year) and 679th wettest (116 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ürümqi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (256 km away), Korla is 1.9 °C warmer in July and 4.2 °C warmer in January, with 201 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.6 °C), averages 32.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-7.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 2,178 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Korla, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Korla has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 2,178 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 32.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
