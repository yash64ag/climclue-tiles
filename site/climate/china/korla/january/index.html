How January compares in Korla

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Korla. Compared with December: 1.7 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. February is 5.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Korla in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Korla in January?
In January, Korla averages a day high of -0.7 °C (31 °F) and a night low of -10.5 °C (13 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Korla?
January is not one of Korla’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Korla in January?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (31 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Korla in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.7 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Korla get in January?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Korla in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
