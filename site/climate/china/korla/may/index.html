How May compares in Korla

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Korla. Compared with April: 5.8 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 39 hours more sunshine. June is 3.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Korla in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Korla in May?
In May, Korla averages a day high of 26.5 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 12.9 °C (55 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 284 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Korla?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Korla’s mild months (May, June, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Korla in May?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (31 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Korla in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Korla get in May?
About 284 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Korla in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
