How February compares in Korla

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Korla. Compared with January: 5.3 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. March is 7.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Korla in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Korla in February?
In February, Korla averages a day high of 4.4 °C (40 °F) and a night low of -6.0 °C (21 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Korla?
February is not one of Korla’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Korla in February?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (31 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Korla in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.6 cm (1.8 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Korla get in February?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Korla in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
