How June compares in Korla

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Korla. Compared with May: 3.8 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. July is 1.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Korla in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Korla in June?
In June, Korla averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 17.4 °C (63 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 291 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Korla?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Korla’s mild months (May, June, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Korla in June?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (31 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Korla in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Korla get in June?
About 291 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Korla in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
