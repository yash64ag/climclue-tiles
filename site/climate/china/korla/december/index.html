How December compares in Korla

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Korla. Compared with November: 7.7 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Korla in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Korla in December?
In December, Korla averages a day high of 0.1 °C (32 °F) and a night low of -10.5 °C (13 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Korla?
December is not one of Korla’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Korla in December?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (32 mm), the driest November (2 mm).
Does it snow in Korla in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.2 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Korla get in December?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Korla in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
