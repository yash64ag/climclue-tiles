Nyêmo at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Nyêmo is 1970th warmest (5.4 °C year average), 164th sunniest (about 2,699 hours of sunshine a year) and 1895th wettest (406 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Quxu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Nyêmo is 1.5 °C cooler in July and 1.8 °C cooler in January, with 181 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (12.7 °C), averages 15.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 766 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nyêmo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nyêmo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 766 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nyêmo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (29.437°N, 90.148°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-545-2701), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
