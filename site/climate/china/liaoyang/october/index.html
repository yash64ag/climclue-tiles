Liaoyang weather in October

Average October weather in Liaoyang, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Liaoyang averages 16.7 °C (62 °F) days and 5.0 °C (41 °F) nights, with 41 mm (1.6 in) of rain over the month and about 215 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Liaoyang

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Liaoyang. Compared with September: 7.8 °C cooler, 29 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. November is 9.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Liaoyang in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Liaoyang in October?
In October, Liaoyang averages a day high of 16.7 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 5.0 °C (41 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Liaoyang?
October is not one of Liaoyang’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Liaoyang in October?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (185 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Liaoyang in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Liaoyang get in October?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Liaoyang in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
