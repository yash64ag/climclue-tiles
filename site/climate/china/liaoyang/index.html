Liaoyang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Liaoyang is 566th warmest (9.5 °C year average), 141st sunniest (about 2,562 hours of sunshine a year) and 425th wettest (741 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Anshan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Liaoyang is about as warm in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.3 °C), averages 35.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-10.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 114 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Liaoyang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Liaoyang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 114 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
