How September compares in Liaoyang

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Liaoyang. Compared with August: 5.6 °C cooler, 100 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. October is 7.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Liaoyang in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Liaoyang in September?
In September, Liaoyang averages a day high of 23.7 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 13.7 °C (57 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Liaoyang?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Liaoyang’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Liaoyang in September?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (187 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Liaoyang in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Liaoyang get in September?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Liaoyang in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
