How June compares in Liaoyang

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Liaoyang. Compared with May: 4.8 °C warmer, 28 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Liaoyang in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Liaoyang in June?
In June, Liaoyang averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 17.7 °C (64 °F), with 90 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 252 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Liaoyang?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Liaoyang’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Liaoyang in June?
90 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (187 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Liaoyang in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Liaoyang get in June?
About 252 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Liaoyang in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
