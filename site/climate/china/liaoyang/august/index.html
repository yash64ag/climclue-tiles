How August compares in Liaoyang

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Liaoyang. Compared with July: 1.0 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. September is 5.6 °C cooler than August on average.

Liaoyang in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Liaoyang in August?
In August, Liaoyang averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.1 °C (68 °F), with 172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Liaoyang?
August is not one of Liaoyang’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Liaoyang in August?
172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (187 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Liaoyang in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Liaoyang get in August?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Liaoyang in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
