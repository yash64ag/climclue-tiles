How December compares in Liaoyang

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Liaoyang. Compared with November: 7.5 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. January is 3.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Liaoyang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Liaoyang in December?
In December, Liaoyang averages a day high of -1.6 °C (29 °F) and a night low of -11.4 °C (11 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Liaoyang?
December is not one of Liaoyang’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Liaoyang in December?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (187 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Liaoyang in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.1 cm (3.2 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Liaoyang get in December?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Liaoyang in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
