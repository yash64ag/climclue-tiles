How March compares in Liaoyang

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Liaoyang. Compared with February: 7.6 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 49 hours more sunshine. April is 9.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Liaoyang in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Liaoyang in March?
In March, Liaoyang averages a day high of 7.8 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -2.8 °C (27 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Liaoyang?
March is not one of Liaoyang’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Liaoyang in March?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (185 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Liaoyang in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.8 cm (3.5 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Liaoyang get in March?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Liaoyang in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
