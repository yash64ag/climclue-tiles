Fuzhou, Fujian in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fuzhou, Fujian in November?
In November, Fuzhou, Fujian averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 124 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Fuzhou, Fujian?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Fuzhou, Fujian’s mild months (October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fuzhou, Fujian in November?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (190 mm), the driest December (32 mm).
Does it snow in Fuzhou, Fujian in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fuzhou, Fujian get in November?
About 124 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
