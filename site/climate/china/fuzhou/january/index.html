How January compares in Fuzhou, Fujian

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Fuzhou, Fujian. Compared with December: 2.0 °C cooler, 13 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. February is 0.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Fuzhou, Fujian in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fuzhou, Fujian in January?
In January, Fuzhou, Fujian averages a day high of 13.3 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 6.2 °C (43 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 120 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Fuzhou, Fujian?
January is not one of Fuzhou, Fujian’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fuzhou, Fujian in January?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (201 mm), the driest December (34 mm).
Does it snow in Fuzhou, Fujian in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fuzhou, Fujian get in January?
About 120 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fuzhou, Fujian in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
