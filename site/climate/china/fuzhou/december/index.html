How December compares in Fuzhou, Fujian

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Fuzhou, Fujian. Compared with November: 4.9 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Fuzhou, Fujian in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fuzhou, Fujian in December?
In December, Fuzhou, Fujian averages a day high of 15.4 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Fuzhou, Fujian?
December is not one of Fuzhou, Fujian’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fuzhou, Fujian in December?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (201 mm), the driest December (34 mm).
Does it snow in Fuzhou, Fujian in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fuzhou, Fujian get in December?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fuzhou, Fujian in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
