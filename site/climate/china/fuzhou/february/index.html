How February compares in Fuzhou, Fujian

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Fuzhou, Fujian. Compared with January: 0.5 °C warmer, 34 mm more precipitation and 36 hours less sunshine. March is 2.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Fuzhou, Fujian in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fuzhou, Fujian in February?
In February, Fuzhou, Fujian averages a day high of 13.7 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 7.1 °C (45 °F), with 80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 84 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Fuzhou, Fujian?
February is not one of Fuzhou, Fujian’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fuzhou, Fujian in February?
80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (201 mm), the driest December (34 mm).
Does it snow in Fuzhou, Fujian in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fuzhou, Fujian get in February?
About 84 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fuzhou, Fujian in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
