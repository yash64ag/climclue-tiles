Fuzhou, Fujian in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fuzhou, Fujian in June?
In June, Fuzhou, Fujian averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 201 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Fuzhou, Fujian?
June is not one of Fuzhou, Fujian’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fuzhou, Fujian in June?
201 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (201 mm), the driest December (34 mm).
Does it snow in Fuzhou, Fujian in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fuzhou, Fujian get in June?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fuzhou, Fujian in June?
