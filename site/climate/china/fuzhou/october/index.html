How October compares in Fuzhou, Fujian

Fuzhou, Fujian in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fuzhou, Fujian in October?
In October, Fuzhou, Fujian averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 19.4 °C (67 °F), with 47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Fuzhou, Fujian?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Fuzhou, Fujian’s mild months (October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fuzhou, Fujian in October?
47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (190 mm), the driest December (32 mm).
Does it snow in Fuzhou, Fujian in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fuzhou, Fujian get in October?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Fuzhou, Fujian in October?
About 24.1 °C (75 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (27.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Fuzhou, Fujian in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
