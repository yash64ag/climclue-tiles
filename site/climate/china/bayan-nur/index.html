Bayan Nur at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Bayan Nur is the 5th sunniest (about 3,006 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Wuhai, the nearest covered town with different numbers (127 km away), Bayan Nur is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 1.7 °C cooler in January, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.8 °C), averages 34.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-10.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 908 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Bayan Nur has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 908 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 34.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
