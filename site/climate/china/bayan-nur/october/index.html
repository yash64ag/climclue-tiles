Bayan Nur in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bayan Nur in October?
In October, Bayan Nur averages a day high of 15.9 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 2.0 °C (36 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 244 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Bayan Nur?
October is not one of Bayan Nur’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
