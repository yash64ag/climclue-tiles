Bayan Nur in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bayan Nur in November?
In November, Bayan Nur averages a day high of 5.6 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -5.9 °C (21 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Bayan Nur?
November is not one of Bayan Nur’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (45 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Bayan Nur in November?
