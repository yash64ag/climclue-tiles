Bayan Nur in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bayan Nur in December?
In December, Bayan Nur averages a day high of -2.0 °C (28 °F) and a night low of -13.7 °C (7 °F), with 1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Bayan Nur?
December is not one of Bayan Nur’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bayan Nur in December?
1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (45 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Bayan Nur in December?
Yes, on average: about 1.4 cm (0.6 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Bayan Nur get in December?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bayan Nur in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
