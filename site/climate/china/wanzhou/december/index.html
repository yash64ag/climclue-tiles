How December compares in Wanzhou

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Wanzhou. Compared with November: 4.8 °C cooler, 31 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. January is 1.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Wanzhou in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wanzhou in December?
In December, Wanzhou averages a day high of 11.3 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 4.9 °C (41 °F), with 24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 65 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Wanzhou?
No month in Wanzhou is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wanzhou in December?
24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (221 mm), the driest January (21 mm).
Does it snow in Wanzhou in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.0 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Wanzhou get in December?
About 65 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wanzhou in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
