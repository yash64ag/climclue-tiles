How February compares in Wanzhou

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Wanzhou. Compared with January: 2.0 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. March is 3.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Wanzhou in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wanzhou in February?
In February, Wanzhou averages a day high of 11.9 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 4.9 °C (41 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 62 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Wanzhou?
No month in Wanzhou is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wanzhou in February?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (221 mm), the driest January (21 mm).
Does it snow in Wanzhou in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.8 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Wanzhou get in February?
About 62 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wanzhou in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
