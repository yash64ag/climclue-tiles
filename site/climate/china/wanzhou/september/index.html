How September compares in Wanzhou

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Wanzhou. Compared with August: 4.2 °C cooler, 32 mm less precipitation and 91 hours less sunshine. October is 5.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Wanzhou in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wanzhou in September?
In September, Wanzhou averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 120 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Wanzhou?
No month in Wanzhou is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wanzhou in September?
134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (194 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Wanzhou in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wanzhou get in September?
About 120 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wanzhou in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
