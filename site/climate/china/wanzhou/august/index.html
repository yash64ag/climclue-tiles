How August compares in Wanzhou

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Wanzhou. Compared with July: 0.1 °C cooler, 25 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. September is 4.1 °C cooler than August on average.

Wanzhou in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wanzhou in August?
In August, Wanzhou averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 189 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Wanzhou?
No month in Wanzhou is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wanzhou in August?
189 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (221 mm), the driest January (21 mm).
Does it snow in Wanzhou in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wanzhou get in August?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wanzhou in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
