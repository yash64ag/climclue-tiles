How October compares in Quanzhou

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Quanzhou. Compared with September: 3.3 °C cooler, 85 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. November is 4.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Quanzhou in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Quanzhou in October?
In October, Quanzhou averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Quanzhou?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Quanzhou’s mild months (October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Quanzhou in October?
38 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (210 mm), the driest December (27 mm).
Does it snow in Quanzhou in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Quanzhou get in October?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Quanzhou in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
