How August compares in Quanzhou

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Quanzhou. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 14 mm more precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. September is 1.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Quanzhou in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Quanzhou in August?
In August, Quanzhou averages a day high of 32.6 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 142 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Quanzhou?
August is not one of Quanzhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Quanzhou in August?
142 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (192 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Quanzhou in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Quanzhou get in August?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Quanzhou in August?
About 27.7 °C (82 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (27.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Quanzhou in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
