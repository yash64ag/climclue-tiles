Quanzhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Quanzhou is 89th warmest (21.4 °C year average), 414th sunniest (about 1,958 hours of sunshine a year) and 274th wettest (1,251 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jinjiang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Quanzhou is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 16.0 °C more than its coolest, January (12.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.3 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 38 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Quanzhou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Quanzhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
