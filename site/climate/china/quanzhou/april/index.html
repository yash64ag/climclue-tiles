How April compares in Quanzhou

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Quanzhou. Compared with March: 4.3 °C warmer, 25 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. May is 3.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Quanzhou in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Quanzhou in April?
In April, Quanzhou averages a day high of 23.9 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 16.2 °C (61 °F), with 132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 129 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Quanzhou?
April is not one of Quanzhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Quanzhou in April?
132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (210 mm), the driest December (27 mm).
Does it snow in Quanzhou in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Quanzhou get in April?
About 129 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Quanzhou in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
