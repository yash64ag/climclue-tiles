Why Sanshui has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 106 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Sanshui climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Sanshui?
July, with an average of 29.6 °C (85 °F), day highs around 31.9 °C (89 °F) and night lows around 25.6 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Sanshui?
January, with an average of 14.2 °C (57 °F), day highs around 18.0 °C (64 °F) and night lows around 10.0 °C (50 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Sanshui?
June, with 315 mm (12.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,926 mm (75.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Sanshui?
December, with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Sanshui?
July, with about 227 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Sanshui?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Sanshui?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: February, October, November and December.
What type of climate does Sanshui have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Sanshui’s GeoNames location (23.155°N, 112.892°E; ClimaClue area sq-608-2928), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 153,714 (GeoNames).
