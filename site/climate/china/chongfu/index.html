Why Chóngfú has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 59 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Chóngfú climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Chóngfú?
July, with an average of 29.1 °C (84 °F), day highs around 32.5 °C (91 °F) and night lows around 25.5 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Chóngfú?
January, with an average of 4.7 °C (40 °F), day highs around 8.5 °C (47 °F) and night lows around 0.2 °C (32 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Chóngfú?
June, with 232 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,454 mm (57.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Chóngfú?
December, with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Chóngfú?
August, with about 237 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Chóngfú?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 18 cm (6.9 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Chóngfú?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.6 × 11 km, that contains Chóngfú’s GeoNames location (30.531°N, 120.431°E; ClimaClue area sq-534-3004), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 112,060 (GeoNames).
