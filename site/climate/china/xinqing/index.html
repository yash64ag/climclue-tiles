Xinqing at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Xinqing is 2078th warmest (1.1 °C year average), 567th sunniest (about 2,438 hours of sunshine a year) and 1662nd wettest (565 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Youhao, the nearest covered town with different numbers (70 km away), Xinqing is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.6 °C), averages 42.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-22.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 584 km from the sea.

Why Xinqing has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 584 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 42.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Xinqing itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (48.287°N, 129.523°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-357-3095), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
