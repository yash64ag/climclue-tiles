How July compares in Linfen

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Linfen. Compared with June: 1.8 °C warmer, 63 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. August is 1.6 °C cooler than July on average.

Linfen in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Linfen in July?
In July, Linfen averages a day high of 31.8 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.1 °C (72 °F), with 126 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Linfen?
July is not one of Linfen’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Linfen in July?
126 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (126 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Linfen in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Linfen get in July?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Linfen in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
