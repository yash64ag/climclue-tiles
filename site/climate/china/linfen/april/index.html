Linfen in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Linfen in April?
In April, Linfen averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 8.5 °C (47 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Linfen?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Linfen’s mild months (April, May and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Linfen in April?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (126 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Linfen in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Linfen get in April?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Linfen in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
