How November compares in Linfen

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Linfen. Compared with October: 7.6 °C cooler, 23 mm less precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. December is 6.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Linfen in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Linfen in November?
In November, Linfen averages a day high of 12.5 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 1.3 °C (34 °F), with 14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Linfen?
November is not one of Linfen’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Linfen in November?
14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (124 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Linfen in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.0 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Linfen get in November?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Linfen in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
