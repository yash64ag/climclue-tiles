How October compares in Linfen

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Linfen. Compared with September: 6.4 °C cooler, 36 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. November is 7.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Linfen in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Linfen in October?
In October, Linfen averages a day high of 19.6 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 8.5 °C (47 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Linfen?
October is not one of Linfen’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Linfen in October?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (126 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Linfen in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Linfen get in October?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Linfen in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
