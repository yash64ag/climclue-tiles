How February compares in Linfen

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Linfen. Compared with January: 3.8 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. March is 5.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Linfen in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Linfen in February?
In February, Linfen averages a day high of 8.8 °C (48 °F) and a night low of -2.7 °C (27 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Linfen?
February is not one of Linfen’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Linfen in February?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (126 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Linfen in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.1 cm (2.8 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Linfen get in February?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Linfen in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
