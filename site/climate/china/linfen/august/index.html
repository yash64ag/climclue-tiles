How August compares in Linfen

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Linfen. Compared with July: 1.6 °C cooler, 24 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. September is 5.1 °C cooler than August on average.

Linfen in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Linfen in August?
In August, Linfen averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Linfen?
August is not one of Linfen’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Linfen in August?
102 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (126 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Linfen in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Linfen get in August?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Linfen in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
