How June compares in Linfen

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Linfen. Compared with May: 4.3 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. July is 1.8 °C warmer than June on average.

Linfen in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Linfen in June?
In June, Linfen averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 19.2 °C (67 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Linfen?
June is not one of Linfen’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Linfen in June?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (126 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Linfen in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Linfen get in June?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Linfen in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
