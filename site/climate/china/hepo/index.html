Hepo at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Hepo is 85th warmest (21.6 °C year average), 421st sunniest (about 1,954 hours of sunshine a year) and 66th wettest (1,920 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Anliu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Hepo is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.9 °C warmer in January, with 104 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 15.0 °C more than its coolest, January (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.1 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 78 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hepo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hepo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 78 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
