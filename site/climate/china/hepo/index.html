Why Hepo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 78 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Hepo climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Hepo?
July, with an average of 28.4 °C (83 °F), day highs around 30.9 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 24.5 °C (76 °F).
What is the coldest month in Hepo?
January, with an average of 13.3 °C (56 °F), day highs around 18.0 °C (64 °F) and night lows around 8.1 °C (47 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Hepo?
June, with 338 mm (13.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,920 mm (75.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Hepo?
January, with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Hepo?
July, with about 241 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Hepo?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Hepo?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: October and November.
What type of climate does Hepo have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Hepo’s GeoNames location (23.431°N, 115.830°E; ClimaClue area sq-605-2958), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 131,238 (GeoNames).
