Hepo at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Hepo is 90th warmest (21.8 °C year average), 421st sunniest (about 1,954 hours of sunshine a year) and 69th wettest (1,879 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Anliu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Hepo is about as warm in July and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 149 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 15.2 °C more than its coolest, January (13.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.2 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 78 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hepo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hepo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 78 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Hepo itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (23.431°N, 115.830°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-605-2958), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
