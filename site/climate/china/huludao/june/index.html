How June compares in Huludao

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Huludao. Compared with May: 4.9 °C warmer, 33 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. July is 3.5 °C warmer than June on average.

Huludao in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huludao in June?
In June, Huludao averages a day high of 26.2 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 16.1 °C (61 °F), with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 265 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Huludao?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Huludao’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huludao in June?
83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (161 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Huludao in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huludao get in June?
About 265 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Huludao in June?
About 18.3 °C (65 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (25.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Huludao in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
