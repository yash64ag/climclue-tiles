Huludao in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huludao in May?
In May, Huludao averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 10.4 °C (51 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 281 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Huludao?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Huludao’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huludao in May?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (161 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Huludao in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huludao get in May?
About 281 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huludao in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
