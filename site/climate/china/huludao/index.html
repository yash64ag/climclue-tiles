Huludao at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Huludao is 537th warmest (10.4 °C year average), 76th sunniest (about 2,682 hours of sunshine a year) and 513th wettest (600 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nanpiao, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Huludao is 0.5 °C warmer in August and 2.4 °C warmer in January, with 38 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.6 °C), averages 30.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-5.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 17 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Huludao has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
