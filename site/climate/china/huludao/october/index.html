Huludao in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huludao in October?
In October, Huludao averages a day high of 16.3 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 9.1 °C (48 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Huludao?
October is not one of Huludao’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huludao in October?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (165 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Huludao in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huludao get in October?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huludao in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
