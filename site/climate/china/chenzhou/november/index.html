How November compares in Chenzhou

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Chenzhou. Compared with October: 6.0 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. December is 5.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Chenzhou in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chenzhou in November?
In November, Chenzhou averages a day high of 18.5 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 10.1 °C (50 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Chenzhou?
November is not one of Chenzhou’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chenzhou in November?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (211 mm), the driest December (49 mm).
Does it snow in Chenzhou in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chenzhou get in November?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chenzhou in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
