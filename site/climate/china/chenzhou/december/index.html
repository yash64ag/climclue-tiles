How December compares in Chenzhou

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Chenzhou. Compared with November: 5.7 °C cooler, 27 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. January is 2.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Chenzhou in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chenzhou in December?
In December, Chenzhou averages a day high of 12.9 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 4.2 °C (40 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 127 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Chenzhou?
December is not one of Chenzhou’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chenzhou in December?
49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is April (211 mm), the driest December (49 mm).
Does it snow in Chenzhou in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chenzhou get in December?
About 127 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chenzhou in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
