How January compares in Chenzhou

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Chenzhou. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, 40 mm more precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. February is 2.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Chenzhou in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chenzhou in January?
In January, Chenzhou averages a day high of 9.9 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 2.5 °C (37 °F), with 93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 93 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Chenzhou?
January is not one of Chenzhou’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chenzhou in January?
93 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (225 mm), the driest December (54 mm).
Does it snow in Chenzhou in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.3 cm (2.9 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Chenzhou get in January?
About 93 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chenzhou in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
