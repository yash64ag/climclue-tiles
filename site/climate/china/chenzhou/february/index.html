How February compares in Chenzhou

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Chenzhou. Compared with January: 2.3 °C warmer, 38 mm more precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. March is 3.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Chenzhou in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chenzhou in February?
In February, Chenzhou averages a day high of 12.0 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 4.6 °C (40 °F), with 132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 60 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Chenzhou?
February is not one of Chenzhou’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chenzhou in February?
132 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (225 mm), the driest December (54 mm).
Does it snow in Chenzhou in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.2 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Chenzhou get in February?
About 60 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chenzhou in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
