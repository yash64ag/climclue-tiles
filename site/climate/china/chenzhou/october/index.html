How October compares in Chenzhou

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Chenzhou. Compared with September: 5.4 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. November is 5.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Chenzhou in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chenzhou in October?
In October, Chenzhou averages a day high of 23.7 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Chenzhou?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Chenzhou’s mild months (October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chenzhou in October?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is April (225 mm), the driest December (54 mm).
Does it snow in Chenzhou in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chenzhou get in October?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chenzhou in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
