Pengze at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Pengze is 204th warmest (17.7 °C year average), 464th sunniest (about 1,912 hours of sunshine a year) and 113th wettest (1,684 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jiujiang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Pengze is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 136 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.6 °C), averages 24.9 °C more than its coolest, January (4.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 436 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pengze, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pengze has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 436 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
