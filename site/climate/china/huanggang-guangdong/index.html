Huanggang, Guangdong at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Huanggang, Guangdong is 83rd warmest (21.8 °C year average), 363rd sunniest (about 2,016 hours of sunshine a year) and 155th wettest (1,547 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chenghua, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Huanggang, Guangdong is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 48 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 13.6 °C more than its coolest, January (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.7 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 23 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Huanggang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Huanggang, Guangdong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
