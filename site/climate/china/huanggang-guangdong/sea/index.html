Huanggang, Guangdong sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Guangdong · China

The sea off Huanggang, Guangdong averages 27.0 °C (81 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 16.0 °C (61 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October.

Huanggang, Guangdong sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Huanggang, Guangdong?
In August, at about 27.0 °C (81 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Huanggang, Guangdong?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October. The coolest month is February, at 16.0 °C (61 °F).
How warm is the sea in Huanggang, Guangdong in February?
About 16.0 °C (61 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Huanggang, Guangdong?
About 22.3 °C (72 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Huanggang, Guangdong nearby?
In August: Huicheng, Guangdong (27.9 °C, 101 km), Jiazi (27.9 °C, 130 km) and Jieshi, Guangdong (28.5 °C, 153 km).
