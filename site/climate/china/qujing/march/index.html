How March compares in Qujing

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Qujing. Compared with February: 3.6 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. April is 3.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Qujing in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qujing in March?
In March, Qujing averages a day high of 19.8 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 7.6 °C (46 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Qujing?
March is not one of Qujing’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qujing in March?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (203 mm), the driest December (18 mm).
Does it snow in Qujing in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qujing get in March?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qujing in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
