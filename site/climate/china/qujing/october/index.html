How October compares in Qujing

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Qujing. Compared with September: 2.7 °C cooler, 20 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. November is 3.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Qujing in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qujing in October?
In October, Qujing averages a day high of 18.1 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 11.0 °C (52 °F), with 90 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 126 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Qujing?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Qujing’s mild months (April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qujing in October?
90 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (198 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Qujing in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qujing get in October?
About 126 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qujing in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
