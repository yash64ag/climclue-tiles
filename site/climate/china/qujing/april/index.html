Qujing weather in April

Average April weather in Qujing, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Qujing averages 24.1 °C (75 °F) days and 10.4 °C (51 °F) nights, with 35 mm (1.4 in) of rain over the month and about 202 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Qujing

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Qujing. Compared with March: 3.0 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. May is 1.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Qujing in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qujing in April?
In April, Qujing averages a day high of 24.1 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 10.4 °C (51 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Qujing?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Qujing’s mild months (April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qujing in April?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (198 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Qujing in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qujing get in April?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
