How December compares in Qujing

December is the coolest month, the driest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Qujing. Compared with November: 3.3 °C cooler, 22 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. January is about as warm as December on average.

Qujing in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qujing in December?
In December, Qujing averages a day high of 12.2 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 3.2 °C (38 °F), with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Qujing?
December is not one of Qujing’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qujing in December?
18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (203 mm), the driest December (18 mm).
Does it snow in Qujing in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qujing get in December?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qujing in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
