How July compares in Qujing

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Qujing. Compared with June: 0.3 °C warmer, 16 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Qujing in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qujing in July?
In July, Qujing averages a day high of 23.1 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 15.8 °C (60 °F), with 187 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Qujing?
July is not one of Qujing’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qujing in July?
187 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (203 mm), the driest December (18 mm).
Does it snow in Qujing in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qujing get in July?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qujing in July?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
