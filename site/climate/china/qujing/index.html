Qujing at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Qujing is 1107th warmest (15.4 °C year average), 1213th sunniest (about 1,933 hours of sunshine a year) and 1071st wettest (1,007 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shilin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (87 km away), Qujing is 1.0 °C cooler in July and 1.6 °C cooler in January, with 261 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.6 °C), averages 12.1 °C more than its coolest, January (8.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 614 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Qujing has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 614 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Qujing itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.483°N, 103.783°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-585-2837), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
