Qujing in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qujing in August?
In August, Qujing averages a day high of 22.9 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 164 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Qujing?
August is not one of Qujing’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qujing in August?
164 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (203 mm), the driest December (18 mm).
Does it snow in Qujing in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qujing get in August?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qujing in August?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
