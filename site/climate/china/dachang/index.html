Dachang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Dachang is 307th warmest (16.6 °C year average), 387th sunniest (about 1,981 hours of sunshine a year) and 192nd wettest (1,431 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pengpu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (3 km away), Dachang is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 7 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.2 °C), averages 23.7 °C more than its coolest, January (4.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 11 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 123% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Dachang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dachang itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (31.309°N, 121.415°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-526-3014), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
