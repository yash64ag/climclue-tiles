How July compares in Jiaxing

July is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Jiaxing. Compared with June: 4.2 °C warmer, 33 mm less precipitation and 69 hours more sunshine. August is 0.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Jiaxing in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiaxing in July?
In July, Jiaxing averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 25.5 °C (78 °F), with 195 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Jiaxing?
July is not one of Jiaxing’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiaxing in July?
195 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (228 mm), the driest December (45 mm).
Does it snow in Jiaxing in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiaxing get in July?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiaxing in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
