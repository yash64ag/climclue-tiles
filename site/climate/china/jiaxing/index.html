Jiaxing at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Jiaxing is 279th warmest (16.8 °C year average), 454th sunniest (about 1,922 hours of sunshine a year) and 181st wettest (1,455 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chóngfú, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Jiaxing is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.8 °C), averages 24.2 °C more than its coolest, January (4.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Why Jiaxing has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jiaxing itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (30.752°N, 120.750°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-532-3007), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
