Jiaxing weather in June

Average June weather in Jiaxing, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Jiaxing averages 28.3 °C (83 °F) days and 20.9 °C (70 °F) nights, with 229 mm (9.0 in) of rain over the month and about 158 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Jiaxing

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Jiaxing. Compared with May: 3.7 °C warmer, 100 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. July is 4.2 °C warmer than June on average.
