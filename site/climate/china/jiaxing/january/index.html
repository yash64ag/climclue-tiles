How January compares in Jiaxing

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Jiaxing. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, 30 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. February is 1.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Jiaxing in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiaxing in January?
In January, Jiaxing averages a day high of 8.2 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 0.8 °C (33 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 129 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Jiaxing?
January is not one of Jiaxing’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiaxing in January?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (229 mm), the driest December (46 mm).
Does it snow in Jiaxing in January?
Yes, on average: about 7.2 cm (2.8 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiaxing get in January?
About 129 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiaxing in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
