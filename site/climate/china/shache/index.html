Shache at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Shache is 495th warmest (12.7 °C year average), 112th sunniest (about 2,615 hours of sunshine a year) and 687th wettest (71 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kashgar, the nearest covered town with different numbers (159 km away), Shache is 1.0 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 18 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.8 °C), averages 30.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, November and December. The area lies about 1,775 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 115% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Shache has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,775 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
