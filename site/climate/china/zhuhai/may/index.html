How May compares in Zhuhai

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Zhuhai. Compared with April: 3.0 °C warmer, 107 mm more precipitation and 46 hours more sunshine. June is 1.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Zhuhai in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhuhai in May?
In May, Zhuhai averages a day high of 27.3 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 307 mm (12.1 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Zhuhai?
May is not one of Zhuhai’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhuhai in May?
307 mm (12.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (362 mm), the driest January (27 mm).
Does it snow in Zhuhai in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhuhai get in May?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhuhai in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
