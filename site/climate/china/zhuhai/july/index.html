How July compares in Zhuhai, Guangdong

July is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Zhuhai, Guangdong. Compared with June: 0.8 °C warmer, 68 mm less precipitation and 62 hours more sunshine. August is 0.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Zhuhai, Guangdong in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhuhai, Guangdong in July?
In July, Zhuhai, Guangdong averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 25.8 °C (78 °F), with 295 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Zhuhai, Guangdong?
July is not one of Zhuhai, Guangdong’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhuhai, Guangdong in July?
295 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (362 mm), the driest January (27 mm).
Does it snow in Zhuhai, Guangdong in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhuhai, Guangdong get in July?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Zhuhai, Guangdong in July?
About 28.6 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (28.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Zhuhai, Guangdong in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
