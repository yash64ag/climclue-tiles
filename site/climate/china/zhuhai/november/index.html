How November compares in Zhuhai

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Zhuhai. Compared with October: 4.1 °C cooler, 33 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. December is 4.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Zhuhai in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhuhai in November?
In November, Zhuhai averages a day high of 23.4 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 18.1 °C (65 °F), with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Zhuhai?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Zhuhai’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhuhai in November?
47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (366 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Zhuhai in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhuhai get in November?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhuhai in November?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
