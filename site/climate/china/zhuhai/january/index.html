How January compares in Zhuhai, Guangdong

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Zhuhai, Guangdong. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. February is 1.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Zhuhai, Guangdong in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhuhai, Guangdong in January?
In January, Zhuhai, Guangdong averages a day high of 19.0 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 27 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Zhuhai, Guangdong?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Zhuhai, Guangdong’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhuhai, Guangdong in January?
27 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (362 mm), the driest January (27 mm).
Does it snow in Zhuhai, Guangdong in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhuhai, Guangdong get in January?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Zhuhai, Guangdong in January?
About 18.0 °C (64 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (28.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
