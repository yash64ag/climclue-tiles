Yantai, Shandong in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yantai, Shandong in October?
In October, Yantai, Shandong averages a day high of 19.0 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 11.9 °C (53 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Yantai, Shandong?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Yantai, Shandong’s mild months (May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yantai, Shandong in October?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (165 mm), the driest January (13 mm).
Does it snow in Yantai, Shandong in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yantai, Shandong get in October?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
