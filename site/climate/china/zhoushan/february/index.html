How February compares in Zhoushan

February is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Zhoushan. Compared with January: about as warm, 6 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. March is 2.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Zhoushan in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhoushan in February?
In February, Zhoushan averages a day high of 8.4 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 6.3 °C (43 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 102 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Zhoushan?
February is not one of Zhoushan’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhoushan in February?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (192 mm), the driest December (62 mm).
Does it snow in Zhoushan in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhoushan get in February?
About 102 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhoushan in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
