How March compares in Zhoushan

March is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Zhoushan. Compared with February: 2.1 °C warmer, 64 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. April is 3.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Zhoushan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhoushan in March?
In March, Zhoushan averages a day high of 10.5 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 8.5 °C (47 °F), with 145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Zhoushan?
March is not one of Zhoushan’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhoushan in March?
145 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (192 mm), the driest December (62 mm).
Does it snow in Zhoushan in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhoushan get in March?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhoushan in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
