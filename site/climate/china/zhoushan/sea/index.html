Zhoushan sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Zhejiang · China

The sea off Zhoushan averages 26.7 °C (80 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 8.4 °C (47 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Zhoushan sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Zhoushan?
In August, at about 26.7 °C (80 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Zhoushan?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 8.4 °C (47 °F).
How warm is the sea in Zhoushan in February?
About 8.4 °C (47 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Zhoushan?
About 17.6 °C (64 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Zhoushan nearby?
In August: Zhaobaoshan (28.0 °C, 50 km), Fengxiang (28.4 °C, 120 km) and Nanqiao (28.4 °C, 126 km).
