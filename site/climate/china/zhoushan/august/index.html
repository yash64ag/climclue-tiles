Zhoushan weather in August

Average August weather in Zhoushan, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Zhoushan averages 30.0 °C (86 °F) days and 24.1 °C (75 °F) nights, with 160 mm (6.3 in) of rain over the month and about 238 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Zhoushan

August is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Zhoushan. Compared with July: 0.7 °C warmer, 38 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. September is 2.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Zhoushan in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhoushan in August?
In August, Zhoushan averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Zhoushan?
August is not one of Zhoushan’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhoushan in August?
160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (185 mm), the driest December (59 mm).
Does it snow in Zhoushan in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhoushan get in August?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Zhoushan in August?
About 26.7 °C (80 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (26.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Zhoushan in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
