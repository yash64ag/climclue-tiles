Huanren at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Huanren is 612th warmest (7.0 °C year average), 207th sunniest (about 2,477 hours of sunshine a year) and 399th wettest (835 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tonghua, the nearest covered town with different numbers (69 km away), Huanren is 0.7 °C warmer in July and 1.3 °C warmer in January, with 33 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.1 °C), averages 36.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-13.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 183 km from the sea.

Why Huanren has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 183 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 36.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
