How September compares in Zhenjiang

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Zhenjiang. Compared with August: 4.2 °C cooler, 59 mm less precipitation and 60 hours less sunshine. October is 5.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Zhenjiang in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhenjiang in September?
In September, Zhenjiang averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 19.7 °C (67 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Zhenjiang?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Zhenjiang’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhenjiang in September?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (206 mm), the driest December (33 mm).
Does it snow in Zhenjiang in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhenjiang get in September?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhenjiang in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
