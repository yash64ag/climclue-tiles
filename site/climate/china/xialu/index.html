Xialu at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Xialu is 859th warmest (17.1 °C year average), 1801st sunniest (about 1,312 hours of sunshine a year) and 795th wettest (1,203 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nanbin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Xialu is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 29 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.1 °C), averages 21.0 °C more than its coolest, January (6.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 925 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Xialu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 925 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.0 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Xialu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (29.942°N, 108.084°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-540-2880), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
