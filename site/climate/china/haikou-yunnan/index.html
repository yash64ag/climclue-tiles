Haikou, Yunnan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Haikou, Yunnan is 348th warmest (15.8 °C year average), 304th sunniest (about 2,168 hours of sunshine a year) and 333rd wettest (1,100 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Anning, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Haikou, Yunnan is 0.6 °C cooler in June and 0.6 °C cooler in December, with 230 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (20.4 °C), averages 11.3 °C more than its coolest, December (9.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 634 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Haikou, Yunnan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 634 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
