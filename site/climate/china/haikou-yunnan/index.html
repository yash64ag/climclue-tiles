Haikou, Yunnan at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Haikou, Yunnan is 962nd warmest (16.5 °C year average), 881st sunniest (about 2,168 hours of sunshine a year) and 1063rd wettest (1,014 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Anning, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Haikou, Yunnan is 0.6 °C cooler in June and 0.6 °C cooler in December, with 190 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (21.1 °C), averages 11.2 °C more than its coolest, December (9.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 634 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Haikou, Yunnan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 634 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.2 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Haikou itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (24.780°N, 102.575°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-592-2825), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
