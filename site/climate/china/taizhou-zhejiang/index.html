Taizhou, Zhejiang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Taizhou, Zhejiang is 144th warmest (18.6 °C year average), 534th sunniest (about 1,811 hours of sunshine a year) and 111th wettest (1,688 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Wenzhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (105 km away), Taizhou, Zhejiang is 1.2 °C warmer in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 121 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.3 °C), averages 21.6 °C more than its coolest, January (7.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 20 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Taizhou, Zhejiang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
