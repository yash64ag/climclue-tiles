Taizhou, Zhejiang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taizhou, Zhejiang in May?
In May, Taizhou, Zhejiang averages a day high of 24.6 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 18.9 °C (66 °F), with 163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Taizhou, Zhejiang?
May is not one of Taizhou, Zhejiang’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
