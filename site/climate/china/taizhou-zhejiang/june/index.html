Taizhou, Zhejiang in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taizhou, Zhejiang in June?
In June, Taizhou, Zhejiang averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 226 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Taizhou, Zhejiang?
June is not one of Taizhou, Zhejiang’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
