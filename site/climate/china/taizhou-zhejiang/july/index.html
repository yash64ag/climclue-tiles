Taizhou, Zhejiang in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taizhou, Zhejiang in July?
In July, Taizhou, Zhejiang averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 25.8 °C (78 °F), with 172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Taizhou, Zhejiang?
July is not one of Taizhou, Zhejiang’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taizhou, Zhejiang in July?
172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (260 mm), the driest December (46 mm).
Does it snow in Taizhou, Zhejiang in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taizhou, Zhejiang get in July?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taizhou, Zhejiang in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
