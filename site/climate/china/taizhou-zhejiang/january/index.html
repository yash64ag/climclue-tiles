How January compares in Taizhou, Zhejiang

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Taizhou, Zhejiang. Compared with December: 2.2 °C cooler, 26 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. February is 1.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Taizhou, Zhejiang in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taizhou, Zhejiang in January?
In January, Taizhou, Zhejiang averages a day high of 10.7 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 3.3 °C (38 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 119 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Taizhou, Zhejiang?
January is not one of Taizhou, Zhejiang’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taizhou, Zhejiang in January?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (260 mm), the driest December (46 mm).
Does it snow in Taizhou, Zhejiang in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.4 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Taizhou, Zhejiang get in January?
About 119 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taizhou, Zhejiang in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
