Taizhou, Zhejiang in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taizhou, Zhejiang in March?
In March, Taizhou, Zhejiang averages a day high of 15.0 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 8.9 °C (48 °F), with 148 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation and about 110 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Taizhou, Zhejiang?
March is not one of Taizhou, Zhejiang’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taizhou, Zhejiang in March?
148 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (266 mm), the driest December (46 mm).
Does it snow in Taizhou, Zhejiang in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taizhou, Zhejiang get in March?
About 110 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
