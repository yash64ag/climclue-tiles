How December compares in Taizhou, Zhejiang

Taizhou, Zhejiang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Taizhou, Zhejiang in December?
In December, Taizhou, Zhejiang averages a day high of 13.2 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 5.2 °C (41 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 137 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Taizhou, Zhejiang?
December is not one of Taizhou, Zhejiang’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Taizhou, Zhejiang in December?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (260 mm), the driest December (46 mm).
Does it snow in Taizhou, Zhejiang in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Taizhou, Zhejiang get in December?
About 137 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Taizhou, Zhejiang in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
