Yingkou sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Liaoning · China

The sea off Yingkou averages 25.5 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -0.8 °C (31 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Yingkou sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Yingkou?
In August, at about 25.5 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Yingkou?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at -0.8 °C (31 °F).
How warm is the sea in Yingkou in February?
About -0.8 °C (31 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Yingkou?
About 11.7 °C (53 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
