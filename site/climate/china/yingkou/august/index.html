Yingkou in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yingkou in August?
In August, Yingkou averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 162 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Yingkou?
August is not one of Yingkou’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yingkou in August?
162 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (172 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Yingkou in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yingkou get in August?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yingkou in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
