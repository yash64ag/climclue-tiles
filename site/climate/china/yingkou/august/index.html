Yingkou weather in August

Average August weather in Yingkou, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Yingkou averages 28.5 °C (83 °F) days and 20.8 °C (69 °F) nights, with 163 mm (6.4 in) of rain over the month and about 227 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Yingkou

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Yingkou. Compared with July: 0.4 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. September is 5.0 °C cooler than August on average.

Yingkou in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yingkou in August?
In August, Yingkou averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Yingkou?
August is not one of Yingkou’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yingkou in August?
163 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (174 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Yingkou in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yingkou get in August?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Yingkou in August?
About 25.5 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (25.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Yingkou in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
