Yingkou in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yingkou in March?
In March, Yingkou averages a day high of 7.0 °C (45 °F) and a night low of -2.0 °C (28 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Yingkou?
March is not one of Yingkou’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yingkou in March?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (174 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Yingkou in March?
Yes, on average: about 6.1 cm (2.4 in) of fresh snow in March (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Yingkou get in March?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Yingkou in March?
About 1.1 °C (34 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Yingkou in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
