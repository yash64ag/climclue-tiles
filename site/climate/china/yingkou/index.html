Yingkou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Yingkou is 544th warmest (10.1 °C year average), 111th sunniest (about 2,619 hours of sunshine a year) and 468th wettest (653 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Haicheng, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Yingkou is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 1.5 °C warmer in January, with 31 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.1 °C), averages 32.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-7.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 17 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Yingkou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 32.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
