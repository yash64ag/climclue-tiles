Xuzhou in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xuzhou in September?
In September, Xuzhou averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 17.6 °C (64 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Xuzhou?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Xuzhou’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xuzhou in September?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (262 mm), the driest December (16 mm).
Does it snow in Xuzhou in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xuzhou get in September?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xuzhou in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
