Xuzhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Xuzhou is 405th warmest (14.8 °C year average), 275th sunniest (about 2,262 hours of sunshine a year) and 384th wettest (886 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yunlong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Xuzhou is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.3 °C), averages 26.6 °C more than its coolest, January (0.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 195 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 15 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 105% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Xuzhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 195 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Xuzhou itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.204°N, 117.284°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-497-2972), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
