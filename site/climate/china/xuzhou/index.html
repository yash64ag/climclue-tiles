Xuzhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Xuzhou is 397th warmest (14.8 °C year average), 275th sunniest (about 2,262 hours of sunshine a year) and 385th wettest (888 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tongshan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Xuzhou is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 24 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.3 °C), averages 26.6 °C more than its coolest, January (0.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 195 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 15 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 105% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Xuzhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 195 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
