How December compares in Xuzhou

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Xuzhou. Compared with November: 6.1 °C cooler, 15 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. January is 2.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Xuzhou in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xuzhou in December?
In December, Xuzhou averages a day high of 7.5 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -1.4 °C (29 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Xuzhou?
December is not one of Xuzhou’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xuzhou in December?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (262 mm), the driest December (16 mm).
Does it snow in Xuzhou in December?
Yes, on average: about 3.5 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Xuzhou get in December?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xuzhou in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
