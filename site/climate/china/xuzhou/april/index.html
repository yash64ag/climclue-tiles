Xuzhou in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xuzhou in April?
In April, Xuzhou averages a day high of 20.8 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 9.6 °C (49 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 198 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Xuzhou?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Xuzhou’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xuzhou in April?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (263 mm), the driest December (16 mm).
Does it snow in Xuzhou in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xuzhou get in April?
About 198 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xuzhou in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
