How October compares in Xuzhou

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Xuzhou. Compared with September: 5.8 °C cooler, 30 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. November is 7.3 °C cooler than October on average.

Xuzhou in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xuzhou in October?
In October, Xuzhou averages a day high of 21.5 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 11.3 °C (52 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Xuzhou?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Xuzhou’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xuzhou in October?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (263 mm), the driest December (16 mm).
Does it snow in Xuzhou in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xuzhou get in October?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xuzhou in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
