Linxi at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Linxi is 514th warmest (11.7 °C year average), 79th sunniest (about 2,680 hours of sunshine a year) and 464th wettest (660 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tangshan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Linxi is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 34 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.8 °C), averages 30.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 75 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Linxi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Linxi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 75 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
