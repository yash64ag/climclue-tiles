Huayin weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Huayin, China (Shaanxi), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Huayin at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Huayin is 1003rd warmest (16.2 °C year average), 1040th sunniest (about 2,046 hours of sunshine a year) and 1670th wettest (562 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yongji, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Huayin is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 1.5 °C warmer in January, with 25 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 26.3 °C more than its coolest, January (2.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and December. The area lies about 813 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Huayin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 813 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Huayin itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.565°N, 110.066°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-494-2900), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
