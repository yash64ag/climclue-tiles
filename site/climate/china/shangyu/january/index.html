How January compares in Shangyu

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Shangyu. Compared with December: 2.2 °C cooler, 29 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. February is 1.8 °C warmer than January on average.

Shangyu in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shangyu in January?
In January, Shangyu averages a day high of 8.6 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 0.6 °C (33 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 124 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Shangyu?
January is not one of Shangyu’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shangyu in January?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (224 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Shangyu in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.2 cm (3.2 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Shangyu get in January?
About 124 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shangyu in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
