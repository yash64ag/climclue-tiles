Shangyu at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Shangyu is 212th warmest (17.6 °C year average), 491st sunniest (about 1,878 hours of sunshine a year) and 194th wettest (1,450 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lianghu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Shangyu is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 69 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.9 °C), averages 24.7 °C more than its coolest, January (5.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 38 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shangyu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shangyu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
