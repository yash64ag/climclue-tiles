How December compares in Shangyu

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Shangyu. Compared with November: 6.1 °C cooler, 25 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. January is 2.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Shangyu in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shangyu in December?
In December, Shangyu averages a day high of 11.5 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 3.2 °C (38 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Shangyu?
December is not one of Shangyu’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shangyu in December?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (224 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Shangyu in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shangyu get in December?
About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Shangyu in December?
About 11.1 °C (52 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (28.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Shangyu in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
