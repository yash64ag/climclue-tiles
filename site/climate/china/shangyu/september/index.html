Shangyu weather in September

Average September weather in Shangyu, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Shangyu averages 28.3 °C (83 °F) days and 21.0 °C (70 °F) nights, with 135 mm (5.3 in) of rain over the month and about 166 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Shangyu

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Shangyu. Compared with August: 4.3 °C cooler, 43 mm less precipitation and 72 hours less sunshine. October is 5.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Shangyu in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shangyu in September?
In September, Shangyu averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 21.0 °C (70 °F), with 135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Shangyu?
September is not one of Shangyu’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shangyu in September?
135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (224 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Shangyu in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shangyu get in September?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Shangyu in September?
About 26.0 °C (79 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (28.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Shangyu in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
