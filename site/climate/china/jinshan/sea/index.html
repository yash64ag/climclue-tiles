Jinshan, Shanghai sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Shanghai · China

The sea off Jinshan, Shanghai averages 28.4 °C (83 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 6.6 °C (44 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Jinshan, Shanghai sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Jinshan, Shanghai?
In August, at about 28.4 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Jinshan, Shanghai?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 6.6 °C (44 °F).
How warm is the sea in Jinshan, Shanghai in February?
About 6.6 °C (44 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Jinshan, Shanghai?
About 17.4 °C (63 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
