How July compares in Jinshan, Shanghai

July is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Jinshan, Shanghai. Compared with June: 4.2 °C warmer, 32 mm less precipitation and 68 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Jinshan, Shanghai in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinshan, Shanghai in July?
In July, Jinshan, Shanghai averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Jinshan, Shanghai?
July is not one of Jinshan, Shanghai’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinshan, Shanghai in July?
171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (204 mm), the driest December (47 mm).
Does it snow in Jinshan, Shanghai in July?
