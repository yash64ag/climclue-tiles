How June compares in Jinshan

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Jinshan. Compared with May: 4.0 °C warmer, 90 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. July is 4.2 °C warmer than June on average.

Jinshan in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinshan in June?
In June, Jinshan averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 206 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation and about 160 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Jinshan?
June is not one of Jinshan’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinshan in June?
206 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (206 mm), the driest December (46 mm).
Does it snow in Jinshan in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jinshan get in June?
About 160 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jinshan in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
