Jinshan, Shanghai in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinshan, Shanghai in December?
In December, Jinshan, Shanghai averages a day high of 11.4 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 3.3 °C (38 °F), with 47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jinshan, Shanghai?
December is not one of Jinshan, Shanghai’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinshan, Shanghai in December?
47 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (204 mm), the driest December (47 mm).
Does it snow in Jinshan, Shanghai in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jinshan, Shanghai get in December?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Jinshan, Shanghai in December?
About 11.4 °C (53 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (28.4 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Jinshan, Shanghai in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
