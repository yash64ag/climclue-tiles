How January compares in Jinshan

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Jinshan. Compared with December: 2.4 °C cooler, 26 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. February is 1.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Jinshan in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinshan in January?
In January, Jinshan averages a day high of 8.3 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 0.7 °C (33 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Jinshan?
January is not one of Jinshan’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinshan in January?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (206 mm), the driest December (46 mm).
Does it snow in Jinshan in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.6 cm (1.8 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Jinshan get in January?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jinshan in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
