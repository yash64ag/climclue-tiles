How May compares in Yueyang

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Yueyang. Compared with April: 5.1 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. June is 3.4 °C warmer than May on average.

Yueyang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yueyang in May?
In May, Yueyang averages a day high of 26.2 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 19.0 °C (66 °F), with 199 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Yueyang?
May is not one of Yueyang’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yueyang in May?
199 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (226 mm), the driest December (45 mm).
Does it snow in Yueyang in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yueyang get in May?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yueyang in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
