How August compares in Yueyang

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Yueyang. Compared with July: 0.8 °C cooler, 74 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. September is 4.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Yueyang in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yueyang in August?
In August, Yueyang averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Yueyang?
August is not one of Yueyang’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yueyang in August?
130 mm (5.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (226 mm), the driest December (45 mm).
Does it snow in Yueyang in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yueyang get in August?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yueyang in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
