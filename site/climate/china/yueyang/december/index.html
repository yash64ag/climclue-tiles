How December compares in Yueyang

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Yueyang. Compared with November: 5.6 °C cooler, 43 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. January is 2.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Yueyang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yueyang in December?
In December, Yueyang averages a day high of 11.4 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 3.7 °C (39 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 111 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Yueyang?
December is not one of Yueyang’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yueyang in December?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (221 mm), the driest December (44 mm).
Does it snow in Yueyang in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.0 cm (1.2 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Yueyang get in December?
About 111 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yueyang in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
