How April compares in Yueyang

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Yueyang. Compared with March: 6.4 °C warmer, 64 mm more precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. May is 5.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Yueyang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yueyang in April?
In April, Yueyang averages a day high of 21.5 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 13.9 °C (57 °F), with 204 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation and about 119 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Yueyang?
April is not one of Yueyang’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yueyang in April?
204 mm (8.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (221 mm), the driest December (44 mm).
Does it snow in Yueyang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yueyang get in April?
About 119 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yueyang in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
