How January compares in Yancheng

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Yancheng. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. February is 1.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Yancheng in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yancheng in January?
In January, Yancheng averages a day high of 5.9 °C (43 °F) and a night low of -2.2 °C (28 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Yancheng?
January is not one of Yancheng’s mild months; those are May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yancheng in January?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (233 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Yancheng in January?
Yes, on average: about 3.9 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Yancheng get in January?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yancheng in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
