How September compares in Yancheng

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Yancheng. Compared with August: 4.2 °C cooler, 84 mm less precipitation and 46 hours less sunshine. October is 5.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Yancheng in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yancheng in September?
In September, Yancheng averages a day high of 26.2 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 18.6 °C (65 °F), with 101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Yancheng?
September is not one of Yancheng’s mild months; those are May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yancheng in September?
101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (233 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Yancheng in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yancheng get in September?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yancheng in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
