How December compares in Yancheng

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Yancheng. Compared with November: 6.3 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. January is 2.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Yancheng in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yancheng in December?
In December, Yancheng averages a day high of 9.2 °C (49 °F) and a night low of -0.6 °C (31 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Yancheng?
December is not one of Yancheng’s mild months; those are May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yancheng in December?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (232 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Yancheng in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.7 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Yancheng get in December?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yancheng in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
