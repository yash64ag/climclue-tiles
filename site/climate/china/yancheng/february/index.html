Yancheng weather in February

Average February weather in Yancheng, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Yancheng averages 7.8 °C (46 °F) days and -0.3 °C (31 °F) nights, with 39 mm (1.5 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 143 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Yancheng

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Yancheng. Compared with January: 1.9 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. March is 4.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Yancheng in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yancheng in February?
In February, Yancheng averages a day high of 7.8 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -0.3 °C (31 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Yancheng?
February is not one of Yancheng’s mild months; those are May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yancheng in February?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (233 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Yancheng in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.3 cm (2.1 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Yancheng get in February?
About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yancheng in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
