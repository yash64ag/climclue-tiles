Why Yancheng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 51 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Yancheng climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Yancheng?
July, with an average of 27.1 °C (81 °F), day highs around 30.8 °C (87 °F) and night lows around 23.8 °C (75 °F).
What is the coldest month in Yancheng?
January, with an average of 1.9 °C (35 °F), day highs around 6.7 °C (44 °F) and night lows around -2.7 °C (27 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Yancheng?
July, with 232 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,049 mm (41.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Yancheng?
December, with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Yancheng?
August, with about 225 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Yancheng?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 13 cm (5.1 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Yancheng?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May and October.
What type of climate does Yancheng have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.3 × 11 km, that contains Yancheng’s GeoNames location (33.358°N, 120.157°E; ClimaClue area sq-506-3001), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,615,717 (GeoNames).
