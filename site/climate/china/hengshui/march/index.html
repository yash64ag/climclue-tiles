Hengshui in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hengshui in March?
In March, Hengshui averages a day high of 14.2 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 2.7 °C (37 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Hengshui?
March is not one of Hengshui’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hengshui in March?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (151 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Hengshui in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hengshui get in March?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hengshui in March?
