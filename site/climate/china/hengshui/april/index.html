Hengshui in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hengshui in April?
In April, Hengshui averages a day high of 22.5 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 10.2 °C (50 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Hengshui?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Hengshui’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hengshui in April?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (151 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Hengshui in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hengshui get in April?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hengshui in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
