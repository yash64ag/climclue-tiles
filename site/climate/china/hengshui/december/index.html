How December compares in Hengshui

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Hengshui. Compared with November: 6.6 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Hengshui in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hengshui in December?
In December, Hengshui averages a day high of 4.9 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -4.7 °C (24 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Hengshui?
December is not one of Hengshui’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hengshui in December?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (151 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Hengshui in December?
Yes, on average: about 2.3 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Hengshui get in December?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hengshui in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
