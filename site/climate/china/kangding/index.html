Kangding at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Kangding is 671st warmest (3.6 °C year average), 351st sunniest (about 2,040 hours of sunshine a year) and 280th wettest (1,240 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ya'an, the nearest covered town with different numbers (100 km away), Kangding is 12.0 °C cooler in July and 10.2 °C cooler in January, with 559 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (11.8 °C), averages 17.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-6.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 1,133 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Kangding has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 1,133 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 11.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
