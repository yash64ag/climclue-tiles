Beihai at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Beihai is 13th warmest (23.5 °C year average), 510th sunniest (about 1,850 hours of sunshine a year) and 61st wettest (1,955 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hepu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Beihai is about as warm in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 93 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 13.0 °C more than its coolest, January (15.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.7 °C (57 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Beihai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Beihai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
