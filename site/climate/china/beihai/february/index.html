How February compares in Beihai, Guangxi

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Beihai, Guangxi. Compared with January: 1.2 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. March is 2.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Beihai, Guangxi in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Beihai, Guangxi in February?
In February, Beihai, Guangxi averages a day high of 20.2 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 14.0 °C (57 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 67 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Beihai, Guangxi?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Beihai, Guangxi’s mild months (January, February, March, April, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Beihai, Guangxi in February?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (448 mm), the driest December (28 mm).
Does it snow in Beihai, Guangxi in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Beihai, Guangxi get in February?
About 67 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Beihai, Guangxi in February?
