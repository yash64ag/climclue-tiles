How July compares in Beihai

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the sunniest month in Beihai. Compared with June: 0.4 °C warmer, 102 mm more precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Beihai in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Beihai in July?
In July, Beihai averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 27.5 °C (82 °F), with 428 mm (16.8 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Beihai?
July is not one of Beihai’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Beihai in July?
428 mm (16.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (428 mm), the driest December (27 mm).
Does it snow in Beihai in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Beihai get in July?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Beihai in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
