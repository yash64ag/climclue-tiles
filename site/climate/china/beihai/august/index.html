Beihai, Guangxi weather in August

Average August weather in Beihai, Guangxi, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

Beihai, Guangxi in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Beihai, Guangxi in August?
In August, Beihai, Guangxi averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 25.5 °C (78 °F), with 424 mm (16.7 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Beihai, Guangxi?
August is not one of Beihai, Guangxi’s mild months; those are January, February, March, April, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
