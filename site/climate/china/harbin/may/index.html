How May compares in Harbin

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Harbin. Compared with April: 7.6 °C warmer, 21 mm more precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. June is 5.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Harbin in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Harbin in May?
In May, Harbin averages a day high of 20.5 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 8.7 °C (48 °F), with 42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 260 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Harbin?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Harbin’s mild months (May and June), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Harbin in May?
42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (151 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Harbin in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Harbin get in May?
About 260 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Harbin in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
