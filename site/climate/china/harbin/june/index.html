Harbin weather in June

Average June weather in Harbin, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Harbin averages 27.0 °C (81 °F) days and 15.0 °C (59 °F) nights, with 92 mm (3.6 in) of rain over the month and about 258 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Harbin

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Harbin. Compared with May: 5.8 °C warmer, 51 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. July is 2.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Harbin in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Harbin in June?
In June, Harbin averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 258 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Harbin?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Harbin’s mild months (May and June), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Harbin in June?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (152 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Harbin in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Harbin get in June?
About 258 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Harbin in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
