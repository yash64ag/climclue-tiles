Why Harbin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 494 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 41.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Harbin climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Harbin?
July, with an average of 23.1 °C (73 °F), day highs around 27.0 °C (81 °F) and night lows around 18.7 °C (66 °F).
What is the coldest month in Harbin?
January, with an average of -18.6 °C (-1 °F), day highs around -13.2 °C (8 °F) and night lows around -24.6 °C (-12 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Harbin?
July, with 151 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 549 mm (21.6 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Harbin?
January, with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Harbin?
May, with about 260 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Harbin?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 58 cm (22.8 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in March; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 5 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Harbin?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May and June.
What type of climate does Harbin have?
Continental, hot summer, dry winter (Köppen Dwa). Hot, wet summers and cold, dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.8 × 11 km, that contains Harbin’s GeoNames location (45.750°N, 126.650°E; ClimaClue area sq-382-3066), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 10,009,854 (GeoNames).
