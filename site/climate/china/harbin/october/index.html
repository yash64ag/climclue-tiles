How October compares in Harbin

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Harbin. Compared with September: 8.7 °C cooler, 31 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. November is 11.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Harbin in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Harbin in October?
In October, Harbin averages a day high of 11.6 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 1.4 °C (35 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Harbin?
October is not one of Harbin’s mild months; those are May and June, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Harbin in October?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (151 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Harbin in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.7 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Harbin get in October?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Harbin in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
