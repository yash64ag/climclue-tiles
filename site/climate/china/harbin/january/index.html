How January compares in Harbin

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Harbin. Compared with December: 3.4 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. February is 5.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Harbin in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Harbin in January?
In January, Harbin averages a day high of -12.1 °C (10 °F) and a night low of -23.3 °C (-10 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Harbin?
January is not one of Harbin’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Harbin in January?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (152 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Harbin in January?
Yes, on average: about 4.2 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Harbin get in January?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Harbin in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
