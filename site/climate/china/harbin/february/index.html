How February compares in Harbin

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Harbin. Compared with January: 5.5 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. March is 10.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Harbin in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Harbin in February?
In February, Harbin averages a day high of -7.3 °C (19 °F) and a night low of -20.2 °C (-4 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Harbin?
February is not one of Harbin’s mild months; those are May and June, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Harbin in February?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (151 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Harbin in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.0 cm (3.1 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 12 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Harbin get in February?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Harbin in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
