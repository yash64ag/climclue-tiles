Guigang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Guigang is 87th warmest (21.6 °C year average), 571st sunniest (about 1,708 hours of sunshine a year) and 59th wettest (1,960 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yulin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (78 km away), Guigang is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 1.4 °C cooler in January, with 207 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.3 °C), averages 15.7 °C more than its coolest, January (12.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.7 °C (48 °F), in January. The area lies about 185 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Guigang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Guigang

For mild weather, the best months to visit Guigang are October and November.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): October and November.

Why Guigang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 185 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
