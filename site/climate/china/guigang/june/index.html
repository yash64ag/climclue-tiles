How June compares in Guigang

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Guigang. Compared with May: 2.0 °C warmer, 49 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. July is 0.9 °C warmer than June on average.

Guigang in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guigang in June?
In June, Guigang averages a day high of 29.8 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.7 °C (75 °F), with 354 mm (13.9 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Guigang?
June is not one of Guigang’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guigang in June?
354 mm (13.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (354 mm), the driest December (41 mm).
Does it snow in Guigang in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guigang get in June?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guigang in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
