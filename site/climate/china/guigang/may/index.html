Guigang weather in May

Average May weather in Guigang, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Guigang averages 29.7 °C (85 °F) days and 22.8 °C (73 °F) nights, with 261 mm (10.3 in) of rain over the month and about 154 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Guigang

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Guigang. Compared with April: 3.4 °C warmer, 120 mm more precipitation and 65 hours more sunshine. June is 2.0 °C warmer than May on average.

Guigang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guigang in May?
In May, Guigang averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 261 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Guigang?
May is not one of Guigang’s mild months; those are March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guigang in May?
261 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (302 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Guigang in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guigang get in May?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guigang in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
