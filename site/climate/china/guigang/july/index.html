How July compares in Guigang

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Guigang. Compared with June: 0.9 °C warmer, 11 mm less precipitation and 58 hours more sunshine. August is 0.2 °C cooler than July on average.

Guigang in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guigang in July?
In July, Guigang averages a day high of 32.8 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 25.4 °C (78 °F), with 290 mm (11.4 in) of precipitation and about 223 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Guigang?
July is not one of Guigang’s mild months; those are March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guigang in July?
290 mm (11.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (302 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Guigang in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guigang get in July?
About 223 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guigang in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
