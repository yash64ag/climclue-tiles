How January compares in Guigang

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Guigang. Compared with December: 2.0 °C cooler, 37 mm more precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. February is 1.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Guigang in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guigang in January?
In January, Guigang averages a day high of 16.3 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 8.7 °C (48 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 94 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Guigang?
January is not one of Guigang’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guigang in January?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (354 mm), the driest December (41 mm).
Does it snow in Guigang in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guigang get in January?
About 94 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guigang in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
