How March compares in Guigang

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Guigang. Compared with February: 3.1 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. April is 4.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Guigang in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guigang in March?
In March, Guigang averages a day high of 21.3 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 13.9 °C (57 °F), with 109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 65 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Guigang?
March is not one of Guigang’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guigang in March?
109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (354 mm), the driest December (41 mm).
Does it snow in Guigang in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guigang get in March?
About 65 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guigang in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
