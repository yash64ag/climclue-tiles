How October compares in Xinxiang

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Xinxiang. Compared with September: 5.8 °C cooler, 33 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. November is 7.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Xinxiang in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xinxiang in October?
In October, Xinxiang averages a day high of 21.6 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 10.0 °C (50 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Xinxiang?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Xinxiang’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xinxiang in October?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (164 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Xinxiang in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xinxiang get in October?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xinxiang in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
