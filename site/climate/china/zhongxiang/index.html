Zhongxiang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Zhongxiang is 262nd warmest (16.9 °C year average), 529th sunniest (about 1,820 hours of sunshine a year) and 329th wettest (1,108 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jingmen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Zhongxiang is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 76 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.3 °C), averages 24.1 °C more than its coolest, January (4.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 748 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Zhongxiang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Zhongxiang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 748 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
