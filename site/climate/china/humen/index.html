Humen at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Humen is 31st warmest (22.9 °C year average), 501st sunniest (about 1,866 hours of sunshine a year) and 36th wettest (2,117 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gongheyong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Humen is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 23 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 13.9 °C more than its coolest, January (15.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.1 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Humen, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Humen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
