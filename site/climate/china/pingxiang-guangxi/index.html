Pingxiang, Guangxi at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Pingxiang, Guangxi is 92nd warmest (21.3 °C year average), 594th sunniest (about 1,610 hours of sunshine a year) and 140th wettest (1,576 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chongzuo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (70 km away), Pingxiang, Guangxi is 1.6 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 47 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.9 °C), averages 13.5 °C more than its coolest, January (13.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.5 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 129 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pingxiang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pingxiang, Guangxi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 129 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
