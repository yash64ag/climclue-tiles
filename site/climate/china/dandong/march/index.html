How March compares in Dandong

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Dandong. Compared with February: 6.2 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 47 hours more sunshine. April is 7.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Dandong in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dandong in March?
In March, Dandong averages a day high of 7.4 °C (45 °F) and a night low of -2.9 °C (27 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Dandong?
March is not one of Dandong’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dandong in March?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (295 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Dandong in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.4 cm (3.3 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Dandong get in March?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dandong in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
