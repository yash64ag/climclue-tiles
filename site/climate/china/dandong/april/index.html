How April compares in Dandong

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Dandong. Compared with March: 7.1 °C warmer, 29 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. May is 5.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Dandong in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dandong in April?
In April, Dandong averages a day high of 15.6 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 4.5 °C (40 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Dandong?
April is not one of Dandong’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dandong in April?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (292 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Dandong in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dandong get in April?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dandong in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
