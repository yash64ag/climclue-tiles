How August compares in Dandong

August is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Dandong. Compared with July: 0.3 °C warmer, 52 mm less precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. September is 5.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Dandong in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dandong in August?
In August, Dandong averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 20.2 °C (68 °F), with 243 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Dandong?
August is not one of Dandong’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dandong in August?
243 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (295 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Dandong in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dandong get in August?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dandong in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
