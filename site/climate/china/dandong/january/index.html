How January compares in Dandong

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Dandong. Compared with December: 2.9 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. February is 4.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Dandong in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dandong in January?
In January, Dandong averages a day high of -2.6 °C (27 °F) and a night low of -11.9 °C (11 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Dandong?
January is not one of Dandong’s mild months; those are May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dandong in January?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (292 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Dandong in January?
Yes, on average: about 7.9 cm (3.1 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Dandong get in January?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Dandong in January?
About 1.9 °C (35 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (24.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Dandong in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
