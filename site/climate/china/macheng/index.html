Why Macheng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 568 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Macheng climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Macheng?
July, with an average of 28.3 °C (83 °F), day highs around 31.4 °C (89 °F) and night lows around 24.9 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Macheng?
January, with an average of 3.5 °C (38 °F), day highs around 8.4 °C (47 °F) and night lows around -0.8 °C (31 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Macheng?
July, with 285 mm (11.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,424 mm (56.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Macheng?
December, with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Macheng?
August, with about 244 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Macheng?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 20 cm (7.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Macheng?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: September and October.
What type of climate does Macheng have?
Humid subtropical, dry winter (Köppen Cwa). Hot, wet summers and mild, dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.5 × 11 km, that contains Macheng’s GeoNames location (31.180°N, 115.022°E; ClimaClue area sq-528-2950), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 435,076 (GeoNames).
