Macheng at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Macheng is 287th warmest (16.6 °C year average), 378th sunniest (about 1,995 hours of sunshine a year) and 209th wettest (1,424 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Huanggang, Hubei, the nearest covered town with different numbers (82 km away), Macheng is 1.0 °C cooler in July and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 105 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.3 °C), averages 24.7 °C more than its coolest, January (3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 568 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Macheng, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Macheng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 568 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
