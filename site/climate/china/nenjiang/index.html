Nenjiang weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Nenjiang, China (Heilongjiang), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Nenjiang at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Nenjiang is 2073rd warmest (1.6 °C year average), 272nd sunniest (about 2,604 hours of sunshine a year) and 1811th wettest (487 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nehe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (81 km away), Nenjiang is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 9 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.6 °C), averages 43.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-22.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 829 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Nenjiang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 829 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 43.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nenjiang itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (49.174°N, 125.220°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-348-3052), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
