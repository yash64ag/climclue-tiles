How November compares in Yulin

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Yulin. Compared with October: 4.5 °C cooler, 18 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. December is 4.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Yulin in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yulin in November?
In November, Yulin averages a day high of 24.5 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Yulin?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Yulin’s mild months (February, March, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yulin in November?
52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (291 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Yulin in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yulin get in November?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yulin in November?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
