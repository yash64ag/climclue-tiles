How April compares in Yulin

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Yulin. Compared with March: 4.4 °C warmer, 75 mm more precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. May is 3.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Yulin in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yulin in April?
In April, Yulin averages a day high of 26.2 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 94 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Yulin?
April is not one of Yulin’s mild months; those are February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yulin in April?
172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (291 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Yulin in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yulin get in April?
About 94 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yulin in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
