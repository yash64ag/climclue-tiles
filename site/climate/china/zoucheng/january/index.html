Zoucheng in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zoucheng in January?
In January, Zoucheng averages a day high of 5.1 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -4.9 °C (23 °F), with 9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Zoucheng?
January is not one of Zoucheng’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zoucheng in January?
9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (185 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Zoucheng in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.2 cm (2.4 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Zoucheng get in January?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zoucheng in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
