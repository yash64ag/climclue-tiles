How May compares in Zoucheng

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Zoucheng. Compared with April: 5.4 °C warmer, 28 mm more precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. June is 4.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Zoucheng in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zoucheng in May?
In May, Zoucheng averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 250 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Zoucheng?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Zoucheng’s mild months (May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zoucheng in May?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (186 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Zoucheng in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zoucheng get in May?
About 250 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zoucheng in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
