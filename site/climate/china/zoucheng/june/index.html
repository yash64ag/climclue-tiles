Zoucheng in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zoucheng in June?
In June, Zoucheng averages a day high of 31.2 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 19.2 °C (67 °F), with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 245 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Zoucheng?
June is not one of Zoucheng’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (186 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
