Jiyuan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Jiyuan is 404th warmest (14.7 °C year average), 280th sunniest (about 2,245 hours of sunshine a year) and 577th wettest (530 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Luoyang, Henan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Jiyuan is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 52 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.1 °C), averages 27.1 °C more than its coolest, January (0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 598 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jiyuan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jiyuan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 598 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
