How December compares in Anshun

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Anshun. Compared with November: 4.5 °C cooler, 22 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Anshun in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Anshun in December?
In December, Anshun averages a day high of 9.4 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 2.7 °C (37 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 85 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Anshun?
December is not one of Anshun’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Anshun in December?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (301 mm), the driest December (23 mm).
Does it snow in Anshun in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.2 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Anshun get in December?
About 85 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Anshun in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
