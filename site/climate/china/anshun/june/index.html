How June compares in Anshun

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Anshun. Compared with May: 2.2 °C warmer, 91 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. July is 1.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Anshun in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Anshun in June?
In June, Anshun averages a day high of 23.6 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 301 mm (11.8 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Anshun?
June is not one of Anshun’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Anshun in June?
301 mm (11.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (301 mm), the driest December (23 mm).
Does it snow in Anshun in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Anshun get in June?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Anshun in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
