Puyang, Zhejiang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Puyang, Zhejiang in December?
In December, Puyang, Zhejiang averages a day high of 11.0 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 2.1 °C (36 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Puyang, Zhejiang?
December is not one of Puyang, Zhejiang’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Puyang, Zhejiang in December?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (295 mm), the driest December (56 mm).
Does it snow in Puyang, Zhejiang in December?

About 143 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
