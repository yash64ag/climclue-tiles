Tanzhou sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Guangdong · China

The sea off Tanzhou averages 28.9 °C (84 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 18.4 °C (65 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

Tanzhou sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Tanzhou?
In August, at about 28.9 °C (84 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Tanzhou?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is January, at 18.4 °C (65 °F).
How warm is the sea in Tanzhou in January?
About 18.4 °C (65 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Tanzhou?
About 24.1 °C (75 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
