Hecun at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Hecun is 476th warmest (13.0 °C year average), 221st sunniest (about 2,446 hours of sunshine a year) and 529th wettest (580 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Linshui, the nearest covered town with different numbers (15 km away), Hecun is 1.3 °C cooler in July and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 25 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.9 °C), averages 28.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 381 km from the sea.

Why Hecun has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 381 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.0 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
