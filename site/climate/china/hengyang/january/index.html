How January compares in Hengyang

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Hengyang. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, 40 mm more precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. February is 2.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Hengyang in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hengyang in January?
In January, Hengyang averages a day high of 10.1 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 3.1 °C (38 °F), with 94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 83 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Hengyang?
January is not one of Hengyang’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hengyang in January?
94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (221 mm), the driest December (54 mm).
Does it snow in Hengyang in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.9 cm (2.3 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Hengyang get in January?
About 83 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hengyang in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
