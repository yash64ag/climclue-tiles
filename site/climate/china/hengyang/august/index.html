August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Hengyang. Compared with July: 0.8 °C cooler, 9 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. September is 4.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Hengyang in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hengyang in August?
In August, Hengyang averages a day high of 34.2 °C (94 °F) and a night low of 25.7 °C (78 °F), with 138 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Hengyang?
August is not one of Hengyang’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hengyang in August?
138 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (225 mm), the driest December (54 mm).
Does it snow in Hengyang in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hengyang get in August?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hengyang in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
