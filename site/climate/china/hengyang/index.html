Hengyang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Hengyang is 120th warmest (19.1 °C year average), 593rd sunniest (about 1,612 hours of sunshine a year) and 145th wettest (1,568 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Leiyang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (59 km away), Hengyang is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 184 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.8 °C), averages 24.1 °C more than its coolest, January (6.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 489 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hengyang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Hengyang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 489 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
