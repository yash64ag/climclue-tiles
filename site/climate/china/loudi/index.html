Loudi at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Loudi is 202nd warmest (17.7 °C year average), 608th sunniest (about 1,549 hours of sunshine a year) and 163rd wettest (1,528 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xiangxiang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (53 km away), Loudi is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 190 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.3 °C), averages 24.0 °C more than its coolest, January (5.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 603 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Loudi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Loudi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 603 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
