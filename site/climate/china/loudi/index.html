Loudi at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Loudi is 694th warmest (17.8 °C year average), 1551st sunniest (about 1,549 hours of sunshine a year) and 381st wettest (1,476 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lianyuan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Loudi is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 44 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.4 °C), averages 24.1 °C more than its coolest, January (5.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 603 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Loudi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Loudi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 603 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Loudi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (27.734°N, 111.994°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-562-2919), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
