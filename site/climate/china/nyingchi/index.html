Nyingchi at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Nyingchi is 663rd warmest (4.0 °C year average), 135th sunniest (about 2,565 hours of sunshine a year) and 398th wettest (836 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lhoka, the nearest covered town with different numbers (254 km away), Nyingchi is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 4.8 °C cooler in January, with 375 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (13.1 °C), averages 20.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-7.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 830 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Nyingchi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 830 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 11.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
