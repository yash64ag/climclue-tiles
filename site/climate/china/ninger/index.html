Ning’er at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Ning’er is 113th warmest (19.4 °C year average), 337th sunniest (about 2,077 hours of sunshine a year) and 109th wettest (1,709 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pu'er, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Ning’er is 0.5 °C cooler in June and 0.7 °C cooler in December, with 52 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (22.9 °C), averages 9.6 °C more than its coolest, December (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.9 °C (46 °F), in December. The area lies about 628 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Ning’er, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Ning’er has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 628 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
