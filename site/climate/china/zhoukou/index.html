Why Zhoukou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 443 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Zhoukou climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Zhoukou?
July, with an average of 27.6 °C (82 °F), day highs around 31.2 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 23.7 °C (75 °F).
What is the coldest month in Zhoukou?
January, with an average of 1.4 °C (35 °F), day highs around 7.0 °C (45 °F) and night lows around -3.4 °C (26 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Zhoukou?
July, with 235 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 881 mm (34.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Zhoukou?
January, with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Zhoukou?
June, with about 225 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Zhoukou?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 28 cm (10.9 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 1 month.
When is the best time for mild weather in Zhoukou?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, September and October.
What type of climate does Zhoukou have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.3 × 11 km, that contains Zhoukou’s GeoNames location (33.615°N, 114.650°E; ClimaClue area sq-503-2946), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 505,171 (GeoNames).
