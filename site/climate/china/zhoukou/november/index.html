How November compares in Zhoukou

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Zhoukou. Compared with October: 7.0 °C cooler, 23 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. December is 6.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Zhoukou in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhoukou in November?
In November, Zhoukou averages a day high of 15.1 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 4.4 °C (40 °F), with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Zhoukou?
November is not one of Zhoukou’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhoukou in November?
32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (235 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Zhoukou in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.1 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhoukou get in November?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhoukou in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
