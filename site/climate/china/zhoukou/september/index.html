How September compares in Zhoukou

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Zhoukou. Compared with August: 4.4 °C cooler, 62 mm less precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. October is 5.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Zhoukou in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhoukou in September?
In September, Zhoukou averages a day high of 26.5 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 17.6 °C (64 °F), with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Zhoukou?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Zhoukou’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhoukou in September?
86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (235 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Zhoukou in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhoukou get in September?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhoukou in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
