How December compares in Feicheng

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Feicheng. Compared with November: 6.2 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. January is 1.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Feicheng in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Feicheng in December?
In December, Feicheng averages a day high of 5.7 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -3.8 °C (25 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Feicheng?
December is not one of Feicheng’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Feicheng in December?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (209 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Feicheng in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.9 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Feicheng get in December?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Feicheng in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
