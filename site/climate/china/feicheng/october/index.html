How October compares in Feicheng

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Feicheng. Compared with September: 6.3 °C cooler, 31 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. November is 7.9 °C cooler than October on average.

Feicheng in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Feicheng in October?
In October, Feicheng averages a day high of 20.1 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 9.6 °C (49 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 205 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Feicheng?
October is not one of Feicheng’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Feicheng in October?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (214 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Feicheng in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Feicheng get in October?
About 205 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Feicheng in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
