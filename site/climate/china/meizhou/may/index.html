How May compares in Meizhou

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Meizhou. Compared with April: 3.3 °C warmer, 6 mm less precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. June is 2.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Meizhou in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Meizhou in May?
In May, Meizhou averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 208 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation and about 139 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Meizhou?
May is not one of Meizhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Meizhou in May?
208 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (249 mm), the driest October (35 mm).
Does it snow in Meizhou in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Meizhou get in May?
About 139 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Meizhou in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
