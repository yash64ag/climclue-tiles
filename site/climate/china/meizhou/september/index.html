How September compares in Meizhou

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Meizhou. Compared with August: 1.4 °C cooler, 77 mm less precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. October is 3.0 °C cooler than September on average.

Meizhou in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Meizhou in September?
In September, Meizhou averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 22.2 °C (72 °F), with 138 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Meizhou?
September is not one of Meizhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Meizhou in September?
138 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (249 mm), the driest October (35 mm).
Does it snow in Meizhou in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Meizhou get in September?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Meizhou in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
