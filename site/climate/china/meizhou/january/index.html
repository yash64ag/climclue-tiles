How January compares in Meizhou

January is the coolest month, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Meizhou. Compared with December: 1.3 °C cooler, 9 mm more precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. February is 1.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Meizhou in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Meizhou in January?
In January, Meizhou averages a day high of 17.4 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 6.9 °C (44 °F), with 47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Meizhou?
January is not one of Meizhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Meizhou in January?
47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (249 mm), the driest October (35 mm).
Does it snow in Meizhou in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Meizhou get in January?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Meizhou in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
