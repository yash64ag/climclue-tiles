Meizhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Meizhou is 91st warmest (21.3 °C year average), 468th sunniest (about 1,908 hours of sunshine a year) and 122nd wettest (1,645 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xingning, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Meizhou is 0.8 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 68 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 15.8 °C more than its coolest, January (12.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.9 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 129 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Meizhou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Meizhou

Why Meizhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 129 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
