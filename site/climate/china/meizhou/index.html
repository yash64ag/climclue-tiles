Why Meizhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 129 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Meizhou climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Meizhou?
July, with an average of 28.4 °C (83 °F), day highs around 31.3 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 24.0 °C (75 °F).
What is the coldest month in Meizhou?
January, with an average of 12.6 °C (55 °F), day highs around 17.4 °C (63 °F) and night lows around 6.9 °C (44 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Meizhou?
June, with 249 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,645 mm (64.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Meizhou?
October, with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Meizhou?
July, with about 249 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Meizhou?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Meizhou?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: October and November.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Meizhou’s GeoNames location (24.289°N, 116.118°E; ClimaClue area sq-597-2961), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 992,351 (GeoNames).
