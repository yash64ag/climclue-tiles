Why Shaoshan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 611 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Shaoshan climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Shaoshan?
July, with an average of 29.6 °C (85 °F), day highs around 32.6 °C (91 °F) and night lows around 25.6 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Shaoshan?
January, with an average of 5.5 °C (42 °F), day highs around 9.2 °C (49 °F) and night lows around 1.7 °C (35 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Shaoshan?
June, with 239 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,631 mm (64.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Shaoshan?
December, with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Shaoshan?
July, with about 241 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Shaoshan?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 17 cm (6.5 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Shaoshan?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Shaoshan have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.8 × 11 km, that contains Shaoshan’s GeoNames location (27.917°N, 112.517°E; ClimaClue area sq-560-2925), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 118,000 (GeoNames).
