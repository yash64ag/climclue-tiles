Shaoshan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Shaoshan is 209th warmest (17.8 °C year average), 603rd sunniest (about 1,575 hours of sunshine a year) and 85th wettest (1,763 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xiangxiang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Shaoshan is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 58 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.4 °C), averages 23.9 °C more than its coolest, January (5.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.7 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 611 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shaoshan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shaoshan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 611 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Shaoshan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (27.917°N, 112.517°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-560-2925), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
