Jieshi, Guangdong sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Guangdong · China

The sea off Jieshi, Guangdong averages 28.5 °C (83 °F) in July, its warmest month, and 19.3 °C (67 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

Jieshi, Guangdong sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Jieshi, Guangdong?
In July, at about 28.5 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Jieshi, Guangdong?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is February, at 19.3 °C (67 °F).
How warm is the sea in Jieshi, Guangdong in February?
About 19.3 °C (67 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Jieshi, Guangdong?
About 24.1 °C (75 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
