Jieshi at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Jieshi is 53rd warmest (22.6 °C year average), 361st sunniest (about 2,017 hours of sunshine a year) and 30th wettest (2,133 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Donghai, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Jieshi is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 223 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 13.2 °C more than its coolest, January (15.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.1 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jieshi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jieshi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
