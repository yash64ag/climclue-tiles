How August compares in Lingyuan

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Lingyuan. Compared with July: 1.4 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. September is 5.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Lingyuan in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lingyuan in August?
In August, Lingyuan averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 17.3 °C (63 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Lingyuan?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Lingyuan’s mild months (May, June, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lingyuan in August?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (109 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Lingyuan in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lingyuan get in August?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lingyuan in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
