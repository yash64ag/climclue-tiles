Lingyuan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Lingyuan is 586th warmest (8.6 °C year average), 46th sunniest (about 2,743 hours of sunshine a year) and 627th wettest (407 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chaoyang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (96 km away), Lingyuan is 1.2 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 51 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.9 °C), averages 33.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-9.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 142 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lingyuan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lingyuan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 142 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 33.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
