How December compares in Lingyuan

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Lingyuan. Compared with November: 6.8 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. January is 2.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Lingyuan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lingyuan in December?
In December, Lingyuan averages a day high of -0.2 °C (32 °F) and a night low of -12.9 °C (9 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Lingyuan?
December is not one of Lingyuan’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lingyuan in December?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (103 mm), the driest January (1 mm).
Does it snow in Lingyuan in December?
Yes, on average: about 2.5 cm (1.0 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Lingyuan get in December?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lingyuan in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
