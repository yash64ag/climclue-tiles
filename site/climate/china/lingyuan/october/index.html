How October compares in Lingyuan

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Lingyuan. Compared with September: 7.8 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. November is 9.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Lingyuan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lingyuan in October?
In October, Lingyuan averages a day high of 14.9 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 2.8 °C (37 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Lingyuan?
October is not one of Lingyuan’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lingyuan in October?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (109 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Lingyuan in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lingyuan get in October?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lingyuan in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
