Shuangcheng at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Shuangcheng is 642nd warmest (4.9 °C year average), 144th sunniest (about 2,559 hours of sunshine a year) and 559th wettest (550 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Harbin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (49 km away), Shuangcheng is about as warm in July and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.1 °C), averages 40.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-17.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 476 km from the sea.

Why Shuangcheng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 476 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 40.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
