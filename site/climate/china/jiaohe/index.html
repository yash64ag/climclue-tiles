Jiaohe at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Jiaohe is 646th warmest (4.7 °C year average), 213th sunniest (about 2,465 hours of sunshine a year) and 455th wettest (674 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jilin, the nearest covered town with different numbers (64 km away), Jiaohe is 1.1 °C cooler in July and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 24 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.1 °C), averages 39.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-17.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 297 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jiaohe, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jiaohe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 297 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 39.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
