How September compares in Zhu Cheng City

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Zhu Cheng City. Compared with August: 4.3 °C cooler, 100 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. October is 5.9 °C cooler than September on average.

Zhu Cheng City in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhu Cheng City in September?
In September, Zhu Cheng City averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Zhu Cheng City?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Zhu Cheng City’s mild months (May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhu Cheng City in September?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (188 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Zhu Cheng City in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhu Cheng City get in September?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhu Cheng City in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
