How March compares in Zhu Cheng City

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Zhu Cheng City. Compared with February: 5.1 °C warmer, 6 mm more precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. April is 6.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Zhu Cheng City in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhu Cheng City in March?
In March, Zhu Cheng City averages a day high of 11.9 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 0.9 °C (34 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Zhu Cheng City?
March is not one of Zhu Cheng City’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhu Cheng City in March?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (193 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Zhu Cheng City in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhu Cheng City get in March?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhu Cheng City in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
