How November compares in Zhu Cheng City

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Zhu Cheng City. Compared with October: 7.6 °C cooler, 15 mm less precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. December is 6.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Zhu Cheng City in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhu Cheng City in November?
In November, Zhu Cheng City averages a day high of 12.4 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 2.6 °C (37 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Zhu Cheng City?
November is not one of Zhu Cheng City’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhu Cheng City in November?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (193 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Zhu Cheng City in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.2 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhu Cheng City get in November?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhu Cheng City in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
