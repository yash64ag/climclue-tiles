Liaocheng in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Liaocheng in April?
In April, Liaocheng averages a day high of 21.7 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 9.5 °C (49 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Liaocheng?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Liaocheng’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Liaocheng in April?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (165 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Liaocheng in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Liaocheng get in April?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
