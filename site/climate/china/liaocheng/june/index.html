Liaocheng weather in June

Average June weather in Liaocheng, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

How June compares in Liaocheng

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Liaocheng. Compared with May: 4.9 °C warmer, 21 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. July is 1.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Is June a good time to visit Liaocheng?
June is not one of Liaocheng’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
