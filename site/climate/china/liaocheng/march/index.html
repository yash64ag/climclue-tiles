Liaocheng in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Liaocheng in March?
In March, Liaocheng averages a day high of 14.1 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 2.0 °C (36 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 209 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Liaocheng?
March is not one of Liaocheng’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Liaocheng in March?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (165 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Liaocheng in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.4 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Liaocheng get in March?
About 209 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Liaocheng in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
