Liaocheng weather in March

Average March weather in Liaocheng, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Liaocheng averages 14.1 °C (57 °F) days and 2.8 °C (37 °F) nights, with 15 mm (0.6 in) of rain over the month and about 209 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Liaocheng

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Liaocheng. Compared with February: 5.8 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. April is 7.3 °C warmer than March on average.

Liaocheng in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Liaocheng in March?
In March, Liaocheng averages a day high of 14.1 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 2.8 °C (37 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 209 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Liaocheng?
March is not one of Liaocheng’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (163 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
