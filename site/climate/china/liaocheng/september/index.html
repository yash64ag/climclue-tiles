Liaocheng in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Liaocheng in September?
In September, Liaocheng averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Liaocheng?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Liaocheng’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Liaocheng in September?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (165 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Liaocheng in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Liaocheng get in September?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Liaocheng in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
