Wuzhishan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Wuzhishan is 47th warmest (22.9 °C year average), 347th sunniest (about 2,051 hours of sunshine a year) and 104th wettest (1,676 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sanya, the nearest covered town with different numbers (59 km away), Wuzhishan is 1.8 °C cooler in June and 3.2 °C cooler in January, with 61 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (26.5 °C), averages 9.0 °C more than its coolest, January (17.5 °C). Even the coolest nights average 13.6 °C (56 °F), in January. The area lies about 59 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Wuzhishan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Wuzhishan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 59 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.0 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.6 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers
