How January compares in Siping

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Siping. Compared with December: 3.4 °C cooler, 2 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. February is 5.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Siping in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Siping in January?
In January, Siping averages a day high of -8.9 °C (16 °F) and a night low of -19.7 °C (-3 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Siping?
January is not one of Siping’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Siping in January?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (175 mm), the driest February (5 mm).
Does it snow in Siping in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.4 cm (2.1 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Siping get in January?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Siping in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
