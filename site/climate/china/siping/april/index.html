Siping in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Siping in April?
In April, Siping averages a day high of 14.6 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 2.9 °C (37 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 239 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Siping?
April is not one of Siping’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Siping in April?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (178 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Siping in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.1 cm (1.6 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Siping get in April?
About 239 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Siping in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
