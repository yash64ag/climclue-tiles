Siping at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Siping is 611th warmest (7.0 °C year average), 128th sunniest (about 2,576 hours of sunshine a year) and 485th wettest (630 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gongzhuling, the nearest covered town with different numbers (52 km away), Siping is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 17 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.8 °C), averages 37.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-13.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 337 km from the sea.

Why Siping has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 337 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 37.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
