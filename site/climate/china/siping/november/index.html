Siping weather in November

Average November weather in Siping, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Siping averages 3.2 °C (38 °F) days and -7.7 °C (18 °F) nights, with 15 mm (0.6 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 168 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Siping

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Siping. Compared with October: 10.5 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 43 hours less sunshine. December is 8.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Siping in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Siping in November?
In November, Siping averages a day high of 3.2 °C (38 °F) and a night low of -7.7 °C (18 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Siping?
November is not one of Siping’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Siping in November?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (175 mm), the driest February (5 mm).
Does it snow in Siping in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.6 cm (3.0 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Siping get in November?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Siping in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
