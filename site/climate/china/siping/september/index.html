Siping weather in September

Average September weather in Siping, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Siping averages 22.8 °C (73 °F) days and 10.2 °C (50 °F) nights, with 45 mm (1.8 in) of rain over the month and about 232 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Siping

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Siping. Compared with August: 6.1 °C cooler, 100 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. October is 8.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Siping in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Siping in September?
In September, Siping averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 10.2 °C (50 °F), with 45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 232 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Siping?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Siping’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Siping in September?
45 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (175 mm), the driest February (5 mm).
Does it snow in Siping in September?

How many hours of sunshine does Siping get in September?
