Siping in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Siping in October?
In October, Siping averages a day high of 13.4 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 3.1 °C (38 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Siping?
October is not one of Siping’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Siping in October?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (178 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Siping in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.3 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Siping get in October?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Siping in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
