Leiyang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Leiyang is 115th warmest (19.3 °C year average), 589th sunniest (about 1,640 hours of sunshine a year) and 97th wettest (1,752 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hengyang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (59 km away), Leiyang is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 184 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.9 °C), averages 24.1 °C more than its coolest, January (6.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 441 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Leiyang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Leiyang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 441 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
