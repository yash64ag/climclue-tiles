How March compares in Baise

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Baise. Compared with February: 3.7 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. April is 5.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Baise in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baise in March?
In March, Baise averages a day high of 23.8 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 14.6 °C (58 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 86 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Baise?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Baise’s mild months (February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baise in March?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (201 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Baise in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baise get in March?
About 86 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baise in March?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
