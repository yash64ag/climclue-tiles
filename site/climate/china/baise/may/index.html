Baise in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baise in May?
In May, Baise averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Baise?
May is not one of Baise’s mild months; those are February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baise in May?
160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (201 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Baise in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baise get in May?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baise in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
