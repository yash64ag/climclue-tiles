How July compares in Baise

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the sunniest month in Baise. Compared with June: 0.6 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. August is 0.2 °C cooler than July on average.

Baise in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baise in July?
In July, Baise averages a day high of 34.0 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 25.0 °C (77 °F), with 201 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Baise?
July is not one of Baise’s mild months; those are February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baise in July?
201 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (201 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Baise in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baise get in July?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baise in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
