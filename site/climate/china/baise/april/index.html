Baise weather in April

Average April weather in Baise, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Baise averages 29.5 °C (85 °F) days and 19.6 °C (67 °F) nights, with 65 mm (2.6 in) of rain over the month and about 117 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Baise

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Baise. Compared with March: 5.0 °C warmer, 24 mm more precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. May is 2.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Baise in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baise in April?
In April, Baise averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 19.6 °C (67 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 117 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Baise?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Baise’s mild months (February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baise in April?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (201 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Baise in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baise get in April?
About 117 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baise in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
