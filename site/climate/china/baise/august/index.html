Baise in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baise in August?
In August, Baise averages a day high of 34.0 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 164 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Baise?
August is not one of Baise’s mild months; those are February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baise in August?
164 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (201 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Baise in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baise get in August?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baise in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
