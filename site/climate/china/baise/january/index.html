How January compares in Baise

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Baise. Compared with December: 1.5 °C cooler, 6 mm more precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. February is 2.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Baise in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baise in January?
In January, Baise averages a day high of 16.8 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 9.2 °C (49 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 71 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Baise?
January is not one of Baise’s mild months; those are February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baise in January?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (203 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Baise in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baise get in January?
About 71 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baise in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
