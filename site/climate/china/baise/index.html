Baise at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Baise is 47th warmest (22.7 °C year average), 609th sunniest (about 1,544 hours of sunshine a year) and 325th wettest (1,117 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hechi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (173 km away), Baise is 1.5 °C warmer in July and 3.3 °C warmer in January, with 620 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.1 °C), averages 15.6 °C more than its coolest, January (13.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.2 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 307 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 3 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 105% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Baise has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 307 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
