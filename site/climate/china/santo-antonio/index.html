Santo António at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Santo António is 63rd warmest (23.1 °C year average), 1182nd sunniest (about 1,945 hours of sunshine a year) and 33rd wettest (2,195 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with São Lourenço, the nearest covered town with different numbers (2 km away), Santo António is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 29 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 12.7 °C more than its coolest, January (15.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.6 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Santo António has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-617-2935), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
