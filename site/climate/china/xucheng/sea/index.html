Xucheng sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Guangdong · China

The sea off Xucheng averages 28.6 °C (83 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 18.8 °C (66 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November.

Xucheng sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Xucheng?
In August, at about 28.6 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Xucheng?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to November. The coolest month is January, at 18.8 °C (66 °F).
How warm is the sea in Xucheng in January?
About 18.8 °C (66 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Xucheng?
About 24.4 °C (76 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Xucheng nearby?
In August: Lingao (29.7 °C, 69 km), Dongcheng (29.8 °C, 101 km) and Mutang (29.8 °C, 104 km).
