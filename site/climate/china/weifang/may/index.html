How May compares in Weifang

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Weifang. Compared with April: 5.7 °C warmer, 25 mm more precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. June is 4.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Weifang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Weifang in May?
In May, Weifang averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 13.1 °C (56 °F), with 47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 261 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Weifang?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Weifang’s mild months (May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Weifang in May?
47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (141 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Weifang in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Weifang get in May?
About 261 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Weifang in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
