How June compares in Weifang

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Weifang. Compared with May: 4.7 °C warmer, 28 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. July is 2.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Weifang in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Weifang in June?
In June, Weifang averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 18.3 °C (65 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Weifang?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Weifang’s mild months (May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Weifang in June?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (141 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Weifang in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Weifang get in June?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Weifang in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
