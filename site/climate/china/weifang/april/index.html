Weifang weather in April

Average April weather in Weifang, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Weifang averages 20.4 °C (69 °F) days and 8.1 °C (47 °F) nights, with 23 mm (0.9 in) of rain over the month and about 226 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Weifang

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Weifang. Compared with March: 7.5 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. May is 5.7 °C warmer than April on average.

Weifang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Weifang in April?
In April, Weifang averages a day high of 20.4 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 8.1 °C (47 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Weifang?
April is not one of Weifang’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Weifang in April?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (141 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Weifang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Weifang get in April?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Weifang in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
