Weifang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Weifang is 457th warmest (13.5 °C year average), 180th sunniest (about 2,525 hours of sunshine a year) and 543rd wettest (570 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Changle, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Weifang is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 6 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.6 °C), averages 28.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 56 km from the sea.

Why Weifang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 56 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
