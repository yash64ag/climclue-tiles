Weifang in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Weifang in October?
In October, Weifang averages a day high of 21.0 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 9.2 °C (49 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Weifang?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Weifang’s mild months (May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Weifang in October?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (141 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Weifang in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Weifang get in October?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Weifang in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
