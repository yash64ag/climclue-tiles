How December compares in Weifang

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Weifang. Compared with November: 6.7 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. January is 2.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Weifang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Weifang in December?
In December, Weifang averages a day high of 5.7 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -3.6 °C (26 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Weifang?
December is not one of Weifang’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Weifang in December?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (144 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Weifang in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.0 cm (1.2 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Weifang get in December?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Weifang in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
