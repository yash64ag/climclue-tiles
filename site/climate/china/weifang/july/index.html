Weifang in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Weifang in July?
In July, Weifang averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 144 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Weifang?
July is not one of Weifang’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Weifang in July?
144 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (144 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Weifang in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Weifang get in July?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Weifang in July?
