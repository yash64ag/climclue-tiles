Hami at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Hami is the sunniest (about 3,215 hours of sunshine a year) and the 4th driest (61 mm a year). Compared with Dunhuang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (312 km away), Hami is 0.7 °C warmer in July and 2.3 °C cooler in January, with 19 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 37.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-8.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 2,072 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Hami has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 2,072 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 37.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
