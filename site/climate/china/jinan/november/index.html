How November compares in Jinan

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Jinan. Compared with October: 8.1 °C cooler, 18 mm less precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. December is 6.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Jinan in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinan in November?
In November, Jinan averages a day high of 12.1 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 2.6 °C (37 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Jinan?
November is not one of Jinan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinan in November?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (181 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Jinan in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jinan get in November?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jinan in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
