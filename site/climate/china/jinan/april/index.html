How April compares in Jinan

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Jinan. Compared with March: 7.4 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. May is 5.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Jinan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinan in April?
In April, Jinan averages a day high of 21.2 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 9.4 °C (49 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Jinan?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Jinan’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinan in April?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (186 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Jinan in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jinan get in April?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jinan in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
