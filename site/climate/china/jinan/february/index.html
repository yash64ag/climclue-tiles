How February compares in Jinan

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Jinan. Compared with January: 3.4 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. March is 5.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Jinan in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinan in February?
In February, Jinan averages a day high of 8.6 °C (47 °F) and a night low of -0.6 °C (31 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Jinan?
February is not one of Jinan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinan in February?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (181 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Jinan in February?
Yes, on average: about 2.9 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Jinan get in February?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jinan in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
