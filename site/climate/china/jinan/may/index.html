Jinan weather in May

Average May weather in Jinan, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Jinan averages 26.8 °C (80 °F) days and 15.3 °C (60 °F) nights, with 60 mm (2.4 in) of rain over the month and about 269 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Jinan

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Jinan. Compared with April: 5.5 °C warmer, 33 mm more precipitation and 41 hours more sunshine. June is 4.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Jinan in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinan in May?
In May, Jinan averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 15.3 °C (60 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 269 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Jinan?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Jinan’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinan in May?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (181 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Jinan in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jinan get in May?
About 269 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jinan in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
