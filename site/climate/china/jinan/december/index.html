How December compares in Jinan

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Jinan. Compared with November: 6.4 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Jinan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinan in December?
In December, Jinan averages a day high of 5.3 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -3.3 °C (26 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jinan?
December is not one of Jinan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinan in December?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (181 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Jinan in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.5 cm (1.0 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Jinan get in December?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jinan in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
