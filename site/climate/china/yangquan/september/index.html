How September compares in Yangquan

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Yangquan. Compared with August: 4.6 °C cooler, 64 mm less precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. October is 6.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Yangquan in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yangquan in September?
In September, Yangquan averages a day high of 22.4 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 12.0 °C (54 °F), with 62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Yangquan?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Yangquan’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yangquan in September?
62 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (126 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Yangquan in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yangquan get in September?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yangquan in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
