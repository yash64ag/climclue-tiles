How March compares in Yangquan

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Yangquan. Compared with February: 5.5 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. April is 7.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Yangquan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yangquan in March?
In March, Yangquan averages a day high of 10.4 °C (51 °F) and a night low of -1.9 °C (29 °F), with 14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Yangquan?
March is not one of Yangquan’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yangquan in March?
14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (126 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Yangquan in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 14 cm (5.5 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Yangquan get in March?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yangquan in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
