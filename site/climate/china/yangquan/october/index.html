Yangquan weather in October

Average October weather in Yangquan, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Yangquan averages 18.1 °C (65 °F) days and 5.6 °C (42 °F) nights, with 24 mm (1.0 in) of rain over the month and about 208 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Yangquan

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Yangquan. Compared with September: 6.1 °C cooler, 36 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. November is 7.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Yangquan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yangquan in October?
In October, Yangquan averages a day high of 18.1 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 5.6 °C (42 °F), with 24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Yangquan?
October is not one of Yangquan’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yangquan in October?
24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (124 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Yangquan in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yangquan get in October?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yangquan in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
