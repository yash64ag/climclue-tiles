How April compares in Zibo

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Zibo. Compared with March: 7.6 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. May is 5.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Zibo in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zibo in April?
In April, Zibo averages a day high of 21.2 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 9.1 °C (48 °F), with 27 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Zibo?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Zibo’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zibo in April?
27 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (158 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Zibo in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zibo get in April?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zibo in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
