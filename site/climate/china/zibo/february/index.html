How February compares in Zibo

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Zibo. Compared with January: 3.4 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. March is 5.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Zibo in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zibo in February?
In February, Zibo averages a day high of 7.3 °C (45 °F) and a night low of -3.8 °C (25 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Zibo?
February is not one of Zibo’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zibo in February?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (156 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Zibo in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.2 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Zibo get in February?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zibo in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
