Zibo weather in March

Average March weather in Zibo, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Zibo averages 13.4 °C (56 °F) days and 1.7 °C (35 °F) nights, with 15 mm (0.6 in) of rain over the month and about 218 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Zibo

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Zibo. Compared with February: 5.8 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 43 hours more sunshine. April is 7.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Zibo in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zibo in March?
In March, Zibo averages a day high of 13.4 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 1.7 °C (35 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Zibo?
March is not one of Zibo’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zibo in March?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (156 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Zibo in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zibo get in March?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zibo in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
