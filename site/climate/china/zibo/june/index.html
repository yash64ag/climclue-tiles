How June compares in Zibo

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Zibo. Compared with May: 4.6 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. July is 1.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Zibo in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zibo in June?
In June, Zibo averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 253 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Zibo?
June is not one of Zibo’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zibo in June?
75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (156 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Zibo in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zibo get in June?
About 253 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zibo in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
