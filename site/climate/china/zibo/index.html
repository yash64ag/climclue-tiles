Zibo at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Zibo is 444th warmest (13.8 °C year average), 169th sunniest (about 2,536 hours of sunshine a year) and 498th wettest (615 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zhoucun, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Zibo is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.1 °C), averages 28.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 111 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Zibo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Zibo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 111 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
