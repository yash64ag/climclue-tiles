Tanggu at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Tanggu is 485th warmest (12.9 °C year average), 99th sunniest (about 2,635 hours of sunshine a year) and 549th wettest (561 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hangu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Tanggu is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 14 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.9 °C), averages 28.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 24 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tanggu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tanggu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
