Tanggu weather in March

Average March weather in Tanggu, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Tanggu averages 11.1 °C (52 °F) days and 0.1 °C (32 °F) nights, with 10 mm (0.4 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 224 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Tanggu

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Tanggu. Compared with February: 5.3 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. April is 7.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Tanggu in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tanggu in March?
In March, Tanggu averages a day high of 11.1 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 0.1 °C (32 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Tanggu?
March is not one of Tanggu’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tanggu in March?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (160 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Tanggu in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.5 cm (0.6 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Tanggu get in March?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Tanggu in March?
About 3.5 °C (38 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Tanggu in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
