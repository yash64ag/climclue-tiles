How February compares in Tanggu

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Tanggu. Compared with January: 2.7 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. March is 5.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Tanggu in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tanggu in February?
In February, Tanggu averages a day high of 5.4 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -4.9 °C (23 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Tanggu?
February is not one of Tanggu’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tanggu in February?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (160 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Tanggu in February?
Yes, on average: about 2.9 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Tanggu get in February?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Tanggu in February?
About 1.7 °C (35 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Tanggu in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
