How June compares in Tanggu

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Tanggu. Compared with May: 4.4 °C warmer, 38 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. July is 2.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Tanggu in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tanggu in June?
In June, Tanggu averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 17.7 °C (64 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 268 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Tanggu?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Tanggu’s mild months (May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tanggu in June?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (160 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Tanggu in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tanggu get in June?
About 268 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Tanggu in June?
About 19.9 °C (68 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (25.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Tanggu in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
