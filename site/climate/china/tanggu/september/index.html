Tanggu weather in September

Average September weather in Tanggu, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Tanggu averages 26.9 °C (80 °F) days and 16.5 °C (62 °F) nights, with 43 mm (1.7 in) of rain over the month and about 230 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Tanggu

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Tanggu. Compared with August: 3.8 °C cooler, 106 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. October is 6.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Tanggu in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tanggu in September?
In September, Tanggu averages a day high of 26.9 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Tanggu?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Tanggu’s mild months (May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tanggu in September?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (160 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Tanggu in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tanggu get in September?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Tanggu in September?
About 23.4 °C (74 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Tanggu in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
