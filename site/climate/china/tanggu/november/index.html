How November compares in Tanggu

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Tanggu. Compared with October: 8.5 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. December is 6.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Tanggu in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tanggu in November?
In November, Tanggu averages a day high of 11.6 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 1.7 °C (35 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Tanggu?
November is not one of Tanggu’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tanggu in November?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (160 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Tanggu in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tanggu get in November?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Tanggu in November?
About 12.2 °C (54 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Tanggu in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
