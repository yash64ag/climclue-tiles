Daliang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Daliang is 18th warmest (23.2 °C year average), 520th sunniest (about 1,840 hours of sunshine a year) and 47th wettest (2,049 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nantou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Daliang is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 17 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.6 °C), averages 14.8 °C more than its coolest, January (14.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.5 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 53 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Daliang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Daliang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 53 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
