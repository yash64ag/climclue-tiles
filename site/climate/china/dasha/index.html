Dasha at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Dasha is 35th warmest (23.0 °C year average), 537th sunniest (about 1,805 hours of sunshine a year) and 38th wettest (2,077 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Guangzhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Dasha is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 24 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.4 °C), averages 14.8 °C more than its coolest, January (14.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.1 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 70 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 13 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 0.3 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 115% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Dasha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 70 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dasha itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (23.110°N, 113.442°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-608-2934), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
