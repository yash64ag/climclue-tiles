Dasha at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Dasha is 46th warmest (22.8 °C year average), 537th sunniest (about 1,805 hours of sunshine a year) and 31st wettest (2,130 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Guangzhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Dasha is 0.2 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 21 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.3 °C), averages 14.8 °C more than its coolest, January (14.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.0 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 70 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 13 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 118% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Dasha has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 70 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
