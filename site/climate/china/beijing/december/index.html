How December compares in Beijing

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the least sunny month in Beijing. Compared with November: 6.0 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. January is 1.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Beijing in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Beijing in December?
In December, Beijing averages a day high of 3.3 °C (38 °F) and a night low of -6.6 °C (20 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Beijing?
December is not one of Beijing’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Beijing in December?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (173 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Beijing in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.9 cm (1.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.2 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Beijing get in December?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Beijing in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
