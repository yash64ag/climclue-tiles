How September compares in Beijing

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Beijing. Compared with August: 4.9 °C cooler, 96 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. October is 7.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Beijing in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Beijing in September?
In September, Beijing averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Beijing?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Beijing’s mild months (April, May and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Beijing in September?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (172 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Beijing in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Beijing get in September?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Beijing in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
