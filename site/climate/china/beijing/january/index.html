How January compares in Beijing

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Beijing. Compared with December: 1.8 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. February is 3.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Beijing in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Beijing in January?
In January, Beijing averages a day high of 1.8 °C (35 °F) and a night low of -9.1 °C (16 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Beijing?
January is not one of Beijing’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Beijing in January?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (172 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Beijing in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.1 cm (1.2 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Beijing get in January?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Beijing in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
