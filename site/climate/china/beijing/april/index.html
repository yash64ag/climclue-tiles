How April compares in Beijing

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Beijing. Compared with March: 8.3 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. May is 6.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Beijing in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Beijing in April?
In April, Beijing averages a day high of 21.1 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 8.8 °C (48 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Beijing?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Beijing’s mild months (April, May and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Beijing in April?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (172 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Beijing in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Beijing get in April?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Beijing in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
