Beijing weather in June

Average June weather in Beijing, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Beijing averages 32.5 °C (91 °F) days and 20.0 °C (68 °F) nights, with 78 mm (3.1 in) of rain over the month and about 274 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Beijing

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Beijing. Compared with May: 4.2 °C warmer, 39 mm more precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. July is 1.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Beijing in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Beijing in June?
In June, Beijing averages a day high of 32.5 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 274 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Beijing?
June is not one of Beijing’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Beijing in June?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (172 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Beijing in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Beijing get in June?
About 274 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Beijing in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
