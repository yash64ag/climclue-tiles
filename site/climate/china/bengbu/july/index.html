Bengbu in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bengbu in July?
In July, Bengbu averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 238 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Bengbu?
July is not one of Bengbu’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bengbu in July?
238 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (238 mm), the driest December (22 mm).
Does it snow in Bengbu in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bengbu get in July?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bengbu in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
