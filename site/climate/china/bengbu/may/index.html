Bengbu in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bengbu in May?
In May, Bengbu averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.2 °C (61 °F), with 80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 209 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Bengbu?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Bengbu’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bengbu in May?
80 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (239 mm), the driest December (22 mm).
Does it snow in Bengbu in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bengbu get in May?
About 209 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bengbu in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
