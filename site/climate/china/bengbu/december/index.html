How December compares in Bengbu

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Bengbu. Compared with November: 6.1 °C cooler, 23 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. January is 2.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Bengbu in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bengbu in December?
In December, Bengbu averages a day high of 9.1 °C (48 °F) and a night low of -0.3 °C (31 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Bengbu?
December is not one of Bengbu’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bengbu in December?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (238 mm), the driest December (22 mm).
Does it snow in Bengbu in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.1 cm (1.6 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Bengbu get in December?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bengbu in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
