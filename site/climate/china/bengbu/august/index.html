How August compares in Bengbu

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Bengbu. Compared with July: 1.0 °C cooler, 93 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. September is 4.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Bengbu in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bengbu in August?
In August, Bengbu averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 146 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Bengbu?
August is not one of Bengbu’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bengbu in August?
146 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (239 mm), the driest December (22 mm).
Does it snow in Bengbu in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bengbu get in August?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bengbu in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
