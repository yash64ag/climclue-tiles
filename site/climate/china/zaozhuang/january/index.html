How January compares in Zaozhuang

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Zaozhuang. Compared with December: 2.0 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. February is 2.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Zaozhuang in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zaozhuang in January?
In January, Zaozhuang averages a day high of 5.1 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -4.5 °C (24 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Zaozhuang?
January is not one of Zaozhuang’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zaozhuang in January?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (213 mm), the driest December (12 mm).
Does it snow in Zaozhuang in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.2 cm (2.4 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Zaozhuang get in January?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zaozhuang in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
