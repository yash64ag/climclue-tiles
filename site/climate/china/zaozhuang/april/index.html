Zaozhuang weather in April

Average April weather in Zaozhuang, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Zaozhuang averages 20.6 °C (69 °F) days and 9.0 °C (48 °F) nights, with 28 mm (1.1 in) of rain over the month and about 206 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Zaozhuang

Zaozhuang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zaozhuang in April?
In April, Zaozhuang averages a day high of 20.6 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 9.0 °C (48 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Zaozhuang?
April is not one of Zaozhuang’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zaozhuang in April?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (207 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Zaozhuang in April?
