Zaozhuang in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zaozhuang in August?
In August, Zaozhuang averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 154 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Zaozhuang?
August is not one of Zaozhuang’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zaozhuang in August?
154 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (207 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Zaozhuang in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zaozhuang get in August?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zaozhuang in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
