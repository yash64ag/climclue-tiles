How December compares in Zaozhuang

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Zaozhuang. Compared with November: 6.2 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Zaozhuang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zaozhuang in December?
In December, Zaozhuang averages a day high of 6.9 °C (44 °F) and a night low of -2.5 °C (28 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Zaozhuang?
December is not one of Zaozhuang’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zaozhuang in December?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (213 mm), the driest December (12 mm).
Does it snow in Zaozhuang in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.5 cm (1.0 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Zaozhuang get in December?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zaozhuang in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
