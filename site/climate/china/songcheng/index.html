Songcheng at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Songcheng is 117th warmest (19.2 °C year average), 559th sunniest (about 1,763 hours of sunshine a year) and 99th wettest (1,743 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fu’an, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Songcheng is 0.8 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 11 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 18.2 °C more than its coolest, January (9.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.4 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 10 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Songcheng, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Songcheng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
