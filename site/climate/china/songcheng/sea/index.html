Songcheng sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Fujian · China

The sea off Songcheng averages 27.1 °C (81 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.1 °C (54 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to September.

Songcheng sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Songcheng?
In August, at about 27.1 °C (81 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Songcheng?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to September. The coolest month is February, at 12.1 °C (54 °F).
How warm is the sea in Songcheng in February?
About 12.1 °C (54 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Songcheng?
About 19.8 °C (68 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Songcheng nearby?
In August: Kunyang (28.0 °C, 104 km), Rui’an (28.0 °C, 119 km) and Jiangkou, Fujian (27.7 °C, 175 km).
