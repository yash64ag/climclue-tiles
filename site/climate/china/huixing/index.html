Huixing at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Huixing is the 3rd least sunny (about 1,204 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Shuanglonghu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Huixing is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 105 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 20.3 °C more than its coolest, January (8.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 912 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Huixing has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 912 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
