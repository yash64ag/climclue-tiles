How January compares in Shijiazhuang

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Shijiazhuang. Compared with December: 1.6 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. February is 3.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Shijiazhuang in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shijiazhuang in January?
In January, Shijiazhuang averages a day high of 4.0 °C (39 °F) and a night low of -6.9 °C (20 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Shijiazhuang?
January is not one of Shijiazhuang’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shijiazhuang in January?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (142 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Shijiazhuang in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.8 cm (1.9 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Shijiazhuang get in January?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shijiazhuang in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
