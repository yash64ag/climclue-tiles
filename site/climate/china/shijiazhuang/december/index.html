How December compares in Shijiazhuang

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Shijiazhuang. Compared with November: 6.4 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. January is 1.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Shijiazhuang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shijiazhuang in December?
In December, Shijiazhuang averages a day high of 5.5 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -5.0 °C (23 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Shijiazhuang?
December is not one of Shijiazhuang’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shijiazhuang in December?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (142 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Shijiazhuang in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.2 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Shijiazhuang get in December?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shijiazhuang in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
