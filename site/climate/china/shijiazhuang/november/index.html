How November compares in Shijiazhuang

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Shijiazhuang. Compared with October: 8.6 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. December is 6.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Shijiazhuang in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shijiazhuang in November?
In November, Shijiazhuang averages a day high of 12.3 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 0.1 °C (32 °F), with 14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Shijiazhuang?
November is not one of Shijiazhuang’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shijiazhuang in November?
14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (142 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Shijiazhuang in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.7 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Shijiazhuang get in November?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shijiazhuang in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
