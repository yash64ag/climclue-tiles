Shijiazhuang weather in June

How June compares in Shijiazhuang

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Shijiazhuang. Compared with May: 4.4 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. July is 1.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Shijiazhuang in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shijiazhuang in June?
In June, Shijiazhuang averages a day high of 33.0 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 268 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Shijiazhuang?
June is not one of Shijiazhuang’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shijiazhuang in June?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (142 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Shijiazhuang in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shijiazhuang get in June?
About 268 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shijiazhuang in June?
