How September compares in Shijiazhuang

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Shijiazhuang. Compared with August: 4.6 °C cooler, 91 mm less precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. October is 6.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Shijiazhuang in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shijiazhuang in September?
In September, Shijiazhuang averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 15.3 °C (60 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Shijiazhuang?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Shijiazhuang’s mild months (April, May and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shijiazhuang in September?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (142 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Shijiazhuang in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shijiazhuang get in September?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shijiazhuang in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
