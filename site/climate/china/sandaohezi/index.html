Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Sandaohezi is 1864th warmest (8.3 °C year average), 123rd sunniest (about 2,738 hours of sunshine a year) and 1971st wettest (260 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shihezi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Sandaohezi is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 1.6 °C warmer in January, with 35 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.8 °C), averages 40.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-14.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 2,469 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Sandaohezi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 2,469 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 40.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
