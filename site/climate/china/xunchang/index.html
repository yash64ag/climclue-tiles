Why Xunchang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 850 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Xunchang climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Xunchang?
July, with an average of 26.6 °C (80 °F), day highs around 29.8 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 22.7 °C (73 °F).
What is the coldest month in Xunchang?
January, with an average of 7.3 °C (45 °F), day highs around 9.7 °C (49 °F) and night lows around 4.1 °C (39 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Xunchang?
July, with 234 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,200 mm (47.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Xunchang?
December, with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Xunchang?
August, with about 190 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Xunchang?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 4.5 cm (1.8 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Xunchang?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April and October.
What type of climate does Xunchang have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.8 × 11 km, that contains Xunchang’s GeoNames location (28.454°N, 104.715°E; ClimaClue area sq-555-2847), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 118,664 (GeoNames).
