Why Changde has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 750 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Changde climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Changde?
July, with an average of 29.1 °C (84 °F), day highs around 32.1 °C (90 °F) and night lows around 25.3 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Changde?
January, with an average of 5.5 °C (42 °F), day highs around 8.9 °C (48 °F) and night lows around 1.2 °C (34 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Changde?
June, with 219 mm (8.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,507 mm (59.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Changde?
December, with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Changde?
July, with about 230 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Changde?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 25 cm (9.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 1 month.
When is the best time for mild weather in Changde?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: September and October.
What type of climate does Changde have?
Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). Hot, humid summers and mild winters, with rain all year. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.7 × 11 km, that contains Changde’s GeoNames location (29.032°N, 111.698°E; ClimaClue area sq-549-2916), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,457,419 (GeoNames).
