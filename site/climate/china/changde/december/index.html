How December compares in Changde

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Changde. Compared with November: 5.3 °C cooler, 41 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. January is 2.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Changde in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changde in December?
In December, Changde averages a day high of 11.6 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 3.5 °C (38 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 92 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Changde?
December is not one of Changde’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changde in December?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (220 mm), the driest December (44 mm).
Does it snow in Changde in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Changde get in December?
About 92 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Changde in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
