How May compares in Changde

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Changde. Compared with April: 4.8 °C warmer, 5 mm more precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. June is 3.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Changde in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changde in May?
In May, Changde averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 18.3 °C (65 °F), with 191 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Changde?
May is not one of Changde’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changde in May?
191 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (219 mm), the driest December (43 mm).
Does it snow in Changde in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Changde get in May?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Changde in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
