How August compares in Dazhou

August is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Dazhou. Compared with July: about as warm, 71 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. September is 4.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Dazhou in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dazhou in August?
In August, Dazhou averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 205 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Dazhou?
No month in Dazhou is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dazhou in August?
205 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (276 mm), the driest January (20 mm).
Does it snow in Dazhou in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dazhou get in August?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dazhou in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
