Dazhou weather in August

Average August weather in Dazhou, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Dazhou averages 33.1 °C (92 °F) days and 23.6 °C (74 °F) nights, with 174 mm (6.8 in) of rain over the month and about 207 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Dazhou

August is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Dazhou. Compared with July: about as warm, 58 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. September is 4.3 °C cooler than August on average.
