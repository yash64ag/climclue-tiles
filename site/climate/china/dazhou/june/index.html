How June compares in Dazhou

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Dazhou. Compared with May: 3.0 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. July is 2.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Dazhou in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dazhou in June?
In June, Dazhou averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 209 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Dazhou?
No month in Dazhou is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dazhou in June?
209 mm (8.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (276 mm), the driest January (20 mm).
Does it snow in Dazhou in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dazhou get in June?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dazhou in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
