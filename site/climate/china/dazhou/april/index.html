Dazhou in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dazhou in April?
In April, Dazhou averages a day high of 21.5 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 127 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Dazhou?
No month in Dazhou is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dazhou in April?
104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (232 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Dazhou in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dazhou get in April?
About 127 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dazhou in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
