Dazhou weather in September

Average September weather in Dazhou, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Dazhou averages 27.7 °C (82 °F) days and 20.4 °C (69 °F) nights, with 160 mm (6.3 in) of rain over the month and about 112 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Dazhou

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Dazhou. Compared with August: 4.3 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 96 hours less sunshine. October is 5.5 °C cooler than September on average.

What is the weather like in Dazhou in September?
In September, Dazhou averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 160 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 112 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Dazhou?
No month in Dazhou is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dazhou in September?
