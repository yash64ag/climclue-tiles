Wuda at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Wuda is 547th warmest (10.0 °C year average), 12th sunniest (about 2,933 hours of sunshine a year) and 675th wettest (157 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Wuhai, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Wuda is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 27 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.3 °C), averages 32.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-7.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 943 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Wuda, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Wuda has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 943 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 32.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
