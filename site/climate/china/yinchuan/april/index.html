How April compares in Yinchuan

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Yinchuan. Compared with March: 7.6 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. May is 6.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Yinchuan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yinchuan in April?
In April, Yinchuan averages a day high of 18.9 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 2.7 °C (37 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Yinchuan?
April is not one of Yinchuan’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yinchuan in April?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (43 mm), the driest December (1 mm).
Does it snow in Yinchuan in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.8 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Yinchuan get in April?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yinchuan in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
