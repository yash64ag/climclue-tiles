Why Yinchuan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 991 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Yinchuan climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Yinchuan?
July, with an average of 24.1 °C (75 °F), day highs around 30.0 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 17.8 °C (64 °F).
What is the coldest month in Yinchuan?
January, with an average of -6.3 °C (21 °F), day highs around 1.1 °C (34 °F) and night lows around -12.1 °C (10 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Yinchuan?
August, with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 191 mm (7.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Yinchuan?
December, with 1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Yinchuan?
June, with about 280 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Yinchuan?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 19 cm (7.4 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in February; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Yinchuan?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Yinchuan have?
Cold desert (Köppen BWk). Very dry, with cold winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.7 × 11 km, that contains Yinchuan’s GeoNames location (38.468°N, 106.273°E; ClimaClue area sq-455-2862), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 2,859,074 (GeoNames).
