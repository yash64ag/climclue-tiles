Yinchuan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Yinchuan is 550th warmest (9.9 °C year average), 24th sunniest (about 2,845 hours of sunshine a year) and 667th wettest (191 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xigang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Yinchuan is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.1 °C), averages 30.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-6.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 991 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 104% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Yinchuan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 991 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
