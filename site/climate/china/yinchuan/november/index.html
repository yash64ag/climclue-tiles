How November compares in Yinchuan

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Yinchuan. Compared with October: 8.3 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. December is 6.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Yinchuan in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yinchuan in November?
In November, Yinchuan averages a day high of 9.0 °C (48 °F) and a night low of -3.7 °C (25 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Yinchuan?
November is not one of Yinchuan’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yinchuan in November?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (43 mm), the driest December (1 mm).
Does it snow in Yinchuan in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.6 cm (1.0 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Yinchuan get in November?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yinchuan in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
