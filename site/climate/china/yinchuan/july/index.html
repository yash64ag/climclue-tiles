How July compares in Yinchuan

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Yinchuan. Compared with June: 1.9 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. August is 1.8 °C cooler than July on average.

Yinchuan in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yinchuan in July?
In July, Yinchuan averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 17.8 °C (64 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 271 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Yinchuan?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Yinchuan’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yinchuan in July?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (43 mm), the driest December (1 mm).
Does it snow in Yinchuan in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yinchuan get in July?
About 271 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yinchuan in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
