How August compares in Yinchuan

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Yinchuan. Compared with July: 1.8 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. September is 4.9 °C cooler than August on average.

Yinchuan in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yinchuan in August?
In August, Yinchuan averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 16.3 °C (61 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Yinchuan?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Yinchuan’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yinchuan in August?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (43 mm), the driest December (1 mm).
Does it snow in Yinchuan in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yinchuan get in August?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yinchuan in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
