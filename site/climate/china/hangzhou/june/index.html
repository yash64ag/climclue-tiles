Hangzhou weather in June

Average June weather in Hangzhou, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Hangzhou averages 28.9 °C (84 °F) days and 21.5 °C (71 °F) nights, with 233 mm (9.2 in) of rain over the month and about 154 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Hangzhou

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Hangzhou. Compared with May: 3.5 °C warmer, 99 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. July is 4.2 °C warmer than June on average.

Hangzhou in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hangzhou in June?
In June, Hangzhou averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 233 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Hangzhou?
June is not one of Hangzhou’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hangzhou in June?
233 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (233 mm), the driest December (49 mm).
Does it snow in Hangzhou in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hangzhou get in June?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
