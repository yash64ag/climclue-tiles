Zizhuang weather by month and climate

Zizhuang at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Zizhuang is 1263rd warmest (14.7 °C year average), 733rd sunniest (about 2,276 hours of sunshine a year) and 1220th wettest (854 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jiawang Zhen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Zizhuang is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.1 °C), averages 26.7 °C more than its coolest, January (0.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 174 km from the sea.

Why Zizhuang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 174 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Zizhuang itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.362°N, 117.488°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-496-2974), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
