Huimin, Yunnan at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Huimin, Yunnan is 173rd warmest (21.8 °C year average), 871st sunniest (about 2,176 hours of sunshine a year) and 525th wettest (1,361 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fazhanhe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Huimin, Yunnan is 2.4 °C warmer in June and 2.8 °C warmer in December, with 378 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (25.1 °C), averages 8.8 °C more than its coolest, December (16.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.2 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 648 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Huimin, Yunnan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 648 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 8.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-617-2800), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
