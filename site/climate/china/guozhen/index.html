Guozhen at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Guozhen is 1182nd warmest (14.9 °C year average), 1202nd sunniest (about 1,937 hours of sunshine a year) and 1556th wettest (607 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Baoji, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Guozhen is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 46 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.9 °C), averages 24.9 °C more than its coolest, January (1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and December. The area lies about 1,036 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Guozhen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,036 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Guozhen itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.366°N, 107.359°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-496-2873), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
