How April compares in Yangjiang

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Yangjiang. Compared with March: 3.9 °C warmer, 144 mm more precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. May is 3.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Yangjiang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yangjiang in April?
In April, Yangjiang averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 238 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation and about 109 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Yangjiang?
April is not one of Yangjiang’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yangjiang in April?
238 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (508 mm), the driest December (30 mm).
Does it snow in Yangjiang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yangjiang get in April?
About 109 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yangjiang in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
