How February compares in Yangjiang

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Yangjiang. Compared with January: 1.1 °C warmer, 44 mm more precipitation and 52 hours less sunshine. March is 2.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Yangjiang in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yangjiang in February?
In February, Yangjiang averages a day high of 20.9 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 81 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Yangjiang?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Yangjiang’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yangjiang in February?
74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (508 mm), the driest December (30 mm).
Does it snow in Yangjiang in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yangjiang get in February?
About 81 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yangjiang in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
