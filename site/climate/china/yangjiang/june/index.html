How June compares in Yangjiang

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Yangjiang. Compared with May: 1.6 °C warmer, 28 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. July is 0.6 °C warmer than June on average.

Yangjiang in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yangjiang in June?
In June, Yangjiang averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 500 mm (19.7 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Yangjiang?
June is not one of Yangjiang’s mild months; those are January, February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yangjiang in June?
500 mm (19.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (500 mm), the driest December (30 mm).
Does it snow in Yangjiang in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yangjiang get in June?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Yangjiang in June?
About 28.5 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is July (29.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Yangjiang in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
