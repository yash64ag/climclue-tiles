How October compares in Yangjiang

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Yangjiang. Compared with September: 2.5 °C cooler, 195 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. November is 4.0 °C cooler than October on average.

Yangjiang in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yangjiang in October?
In October, Yangjiang averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 196 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Yangjiang?
October is not one of Yangjiang’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yangjiang in October?
100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (508 mm), the driest December (30 mm).
Does it snow in Yangjiang in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yangjiang get in October?
About 196 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yangjiang in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
