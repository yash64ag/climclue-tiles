How May compares in Yangjiang

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Yangjiang. Compared with April: 3.1 °C warmer, 237 mm more precipitation and 60 hours more sunshine. June is 1.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Yangjiang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yangjiang in May?
In May, Yangjiang averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 473 mm (18.6 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Yangjiang?
May is not one of Yangjiang’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yangjiang in May?
473 mm (18.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (500 mm), the driest December (30 mm).
Does it snow in Yangjiang in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yangjiang get in May?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Yangjiang in May?
About 26.4 °C (80 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is July (29.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Yangjiang in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
