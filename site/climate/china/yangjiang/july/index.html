Yangjiang weather in July

Average July weather in Yangjiang, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

How July compares in Yangjiang

July is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Yangjiang. Compared with June: 0.6 °C warmer, 107 mm less precipitation and 58 hours more sunshine. August is 0.2 °C cooler than July on average.

Yangjiang in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yangjiang in July?
In July, Yangjiang averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 25.6 °C (78 °F), with 393 mm (15.5 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Yangjiang?
July is not one of Yangjiang’s mild months; those are January, February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yangjiang in July?
393 mm (15.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (500 mm), the driest December (30 mm).
Does it snow in Yangjiang in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yangjiang get in July?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Yangjiang in July?
About 29.2 °C (85 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is July (29.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Yangjiang in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
