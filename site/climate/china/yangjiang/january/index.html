How January compares in Yangjiang

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Yangjiang. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. February is 1.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Yangjiang in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yangjiang in January?
In January, Yangjiang averages a day high of 20.0 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 11.6 °C (53 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 133 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Yangjiang?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Yangjiang’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yangjiang in January?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (508 mm), the driest December (30 mm).
Does it snow in Yangjiang in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yangjiang get in January?
About 133 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yangjiang in January?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
