Laiwu weather in May

Average May weather in Laiwu, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Laiwu averages 25.3 °C (78 °F) days and 13.3 °C (56 °F) nights, with 56 mm (2.2 in) of rain over the month and about 259 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Laiwu

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Laiwu. Compared with April: 5.6 °C warmer, 27 mm more precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. June is 4.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Laiwu in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Laiwu in May?
In May, Laiwu averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 13.3 °C (56 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Laiwu?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Laiwu’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Laiwu in May?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (181 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Laiwu in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Laiwu get in May?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Laiwu in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
