Tewulike at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Tewulike is 1752nd warmest (10.0 °C year average), 53rd sunniest (about 2,834 hours of sunshine a year) and 2014th wettest (147 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yanqi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Tewulike is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 1.3 °C warmer in January, with 24 mm more precipitation a year. The area lies about 2,219 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Tewulike has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 2,219 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 31.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tewulike itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (42.259°N, 86.857°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-417-2668), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
