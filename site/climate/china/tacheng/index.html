Tacheng at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Tacheng is 600th warmest (7.9 °C year average), 82nd sunniest (about 2,675 hours of sunshine a year) and 649th wettest (334 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Karamay, the nearest covered town with different numbers (197 km away), Tacheng is 5.2 °C cooler in July and 4.6 °C warmer in January, with 212 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.6 °C), averages 33.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-9.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 2,264 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tacheng, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tacheng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 2,264 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 33.5 °C.
