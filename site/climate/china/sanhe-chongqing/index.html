Why Sanhe, Chongqing has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 916 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Sanhe, Chongqing climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Sanhe, Chongqing?

What is the coldest month in Sanhe, Chongqing?
January, with an average of 7.7 °C (46 °F), day highs around 10.0 °C (50 °F) and night lows around 3.6 °C (38 °F).

What is the sunniest month in Sanhe, Chongqing?

Does it snow in Sanhe, Chongqing?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 3.0 cm (1.2 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Sanhe, Chongqing?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April and October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.7 × 11 km, that contains Sanhe’s GeoNames location (29.869°N, 107.730°E; ClimaClue area sq-541-2877), averaged over the area’s land. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest).
