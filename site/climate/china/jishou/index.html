Jishou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Jishou is 256th warmest (17.3 °C year average), 633rd sunniest (about 1,429 hours of sunshine a year) and 217th wettest (1,382 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fenghuang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Jishou is 0.7 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 33 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.2 °C), averages 23.1 °C more than its coolest, January (5.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 753 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jishou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jishou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 753 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jishou itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (28.319°N, 109.733°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-556-2897), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
