Mengmao at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Mengmao is 98th warmest (20.8 °C year average), 297th sunniest (about 2,185 hours of sunshine a year) and 141st wettest (1,576 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Longling County, the nearest covered town with different numbers (106 km away), Mengmao is 2.1 °C warmer in June and 2.5 °C warmer in January, with 518 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (24.1 °C), averages 9.6 °C more than its coolest, January (14.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.2 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 650 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mengmao, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mengmao has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 650 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
