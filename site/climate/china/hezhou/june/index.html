How June compares in Hezhou

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Hezhou. Compared with May: 2.5 °C warmer, 7 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. July is 1.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Hezhou in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hezhou in June?
In June, Hezhou averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.7 °C (75 °F), with 263 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Hezhou?
June is not one of Hezhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hezhou in June?
263 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (270 mm), the driest December (37 mm).
Does it snow in Hezhou in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hezhou get in June?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hezhou in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
