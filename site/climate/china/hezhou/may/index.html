How May compares in Hezhou

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Hezhou. Compared with April: 4.1 °C warmer, 84 mm more precipitation and 53 hours more sunshine. June is 2.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Hezhou in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hezhou in May?
In May, Hezhou averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 270 mm (10.6 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Hezhou?
May is not one of Hezhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hezhou in May?
270 mm (10.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is May (270 mm), the driest December (37 mm).
Does it snow in Hezhou in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hezhou get in May?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hezhou in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
