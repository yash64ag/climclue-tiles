Hezhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Hezhou is 102nd warmest (20.5 °C year average), 573rd sunniest (about 1,703 hours of sunshine a year) and 128th wettest (1,620 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Wuzhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (106 km away), Hezhou is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 3.3 °C cooler in January, with 28 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.8 °C), averages 18.9 °C more than its coolest, January (9.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.6 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 301 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Hezhou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Hezhou

For mild weather, the best months to visit Hezhou are October and November.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): October and November.

Why Hezhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 301 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
