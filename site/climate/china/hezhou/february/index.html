How February compares in Hezhou

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Hezhou. Compared with January: 1.8 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 43 hours less sunshine. March is 3.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Hezhou in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hezhou in February?
In February, Hezhou averages a day high of 15.0 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 8.0 °C (46 °F), with 99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 58 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Hezhou?
February is not one of Hezhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hezhou in February?
99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (274 mm), the driest December (39 mm).
Does it snow in Hezhou in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hezhou get in February?
About 58 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hezhou in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
