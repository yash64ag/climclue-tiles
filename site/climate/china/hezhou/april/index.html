How April compares in Hezhou

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Hezhou. Compared with March: 5.5 °C warmer, 51 mm more precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. May is 4.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Hezhou in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hezhou in April?
In April, Hezhou averages a day high of 23.7 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 191 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation and about 82 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Hezhou?
April is not one of Hezhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hezhou in April?
191 mm (7.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (274 mm), the driest December (39 mm).
Does it snow in Hezhou in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hezhou get in April?
About 82 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hezhou in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
