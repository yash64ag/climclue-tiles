How December compares in Jingmen

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Jingmen. Compared with November: 5.6 °C cooler, 28 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. January is 2.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Jingmen in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jingmen in December?
In December, Jingmen averages a day high of 10.5 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 1.8 °C (35 °F), with 24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 112 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jingmen?
December is not one of Jingmen’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jingmen in December?
24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (209 mm), the driest December (24 mm).
Does it snow in Jingmen in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.0 cm (1.6 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Jingmen get in December?
About 112 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jingmen in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
