How February compares in Jingmen

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Jingmen. Compared with January: 2.5 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. March is 4.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Jingmen in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jingmen in February?
In February, Jingmen averages a day high of 10.5 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 2.5 °C (37 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 93 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Jingmen?
February is not one of Jingmen’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jingmen in February?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (234 mm), the driest December (26 mm).
Does it snow in Jingmen in February?
Yes, on average: about 3.5 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Jingmen get in February?
About 93 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jingmen in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
