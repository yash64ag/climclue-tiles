Jingmen weather in May

Average May weather in Jingmen, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Jingmen averages 26.6 °C (80 °F) days and 17.9 °C (64 °F) nights, with 152 mm (6.0 in) of rain over the month and about 166 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Jingmen

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Jingmen. Compared with April: 5.0 °C warmer, 42 mm more precipitation and 28 hours more sunshine. June is 3.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Jingmen in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jingmen in May?
In May, Jingmen averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 152 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 166 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Jingmen?
May is not one of Jingmen’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jingmen in May?
152 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (234 mm), the driest December (26 mm).
Does it snow in Jingmen in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jingmen get in May?
About 166 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jingmen in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
