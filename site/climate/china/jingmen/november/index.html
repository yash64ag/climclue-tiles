How November compares in Jingmen

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Jingmen. Compared with October: 6.0 °C cooler, 37 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. December is 5.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Jingmen in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jingmen in November?
In November, Jingmen averages a day high of 16.1 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 7.3 °C (45 °F), with 51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 120 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Jingmen?
November is not one of Jingmen’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jingmen in November?
51 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (209 mm), the driest December (24 mm).
Does it snow in Jingmen in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.7 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Jingmen get in November?
About 120 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jingmen in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
