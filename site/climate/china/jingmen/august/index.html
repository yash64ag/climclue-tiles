How August compares in Jingmen

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Jingmen. Compared with July: 0.7 °C cooler, 33 mm less precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. September is 4.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Jingmen in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jingmen in August?
In August, Jingmen averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.9 °C (75 °F), with 176 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Jingmen?
August is not one of Jingmen’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jingmen in August?
176 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (209 mm), the driest December (24 mm).
Does it snow in Jingmen in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jingmen get in August?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jingmen in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
