Jingmen weather in January

Average January weather in Jingmen, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Jingmen averages 8.1 °C (47 °F) days and 0.0 °C (32 °F) nights, with 32 mm (1.3 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 106 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Jingmen

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Jingmen. Compared with December: 2.1 °C cooler, 6 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. February is 2.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Jingmen in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jingmen in January?
In January, Jingmen averages a day high of 8.1 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 0.0 °C (32 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 106 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Jingmen?
January is not one of Jingmen’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jingmen in January?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (234 mm), the driest December (26 mm).
Does it snow in Jingmen in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.0 cm (3.1 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Jingmen get in January?
About 106 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jingmen in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
