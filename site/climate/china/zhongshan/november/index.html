How November compares in Zhongshan

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Zhongshan. Compared with October: 4.5 °C cooler, 37 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. December is 4.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Zhongshan in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhongshan in November?
In November, Zhongshan averages a day high of 24.3 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 16.2 °C (61 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Zhongshan?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Zhongshan’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhongshan in November?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (348 mm), the driest December (28 mm).
Does it snow in Zhongshan in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhongshan get in November?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhongshan in November?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
