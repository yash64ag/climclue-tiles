How September compares in Zhongshan

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Zhongshan. Compared with August: 1.3 °C cooler, 60 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. October is 2.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Zhongshan in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhongshan in September?
In September, Zhongshan averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 252 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation and about 187 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Zhongshan?
September is not one of Zhongshan’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhongshan in September?
252 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (348 mm), the driest December (28 mm).
Does it snow in Zhongshan in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhongshan get in September?
About 187 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhongshan in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
