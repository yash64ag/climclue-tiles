Zhongshan, Guangdong weather in October

Average October weather in Zhongshan, Guangdong, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

Zhongshan, Guangdong in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhongshan, Guangdong in October?
In October, Zhongshan, Guangdong averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Zhongshan, Guangdong?
October is not one of Zhongshan, Guangdong’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
