Zhongshan, Guangdong weather in December

Average December weather in Zhongshan, Guangdong, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Zhongshan, Guangdong averages 20.5 °C (69 °F) days and 12.5 °C (55 °F) nights, with 28 mm (1.1 in) of rain over the month and about 173 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Zhongshan, Guangdong

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Zhongshan, Guangdong. Compared with November: 4.2 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. January is 1.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Zhongshan, Guangdong in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhongshan, Guangdong in December?
In December, Zhongshan, Guangdong averages a day high of 20.5 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Zhongshan, Guangdong?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Zhongshan, Guangdong’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhongshan, Guangdong in December?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (350 mm), the driest December (28 mm).
Does it snow in Zhongshan, Guangdong in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
