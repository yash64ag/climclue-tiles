How July compares in Zhongshan

July is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Zhongshan. Compared with June: 0.9 °C warmer, 76 mm less precipitation and 65 hours more sunshine. August is 0.2 °C cooler than July on average.

Zhongshan in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhongshan in July?
In July, Zhongshan averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 26.1 °C (79 °F), with 272 mm (10.7 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Zhongshan?
July is not one of Zhongshan’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhongshan in July?
272 mm (10.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (348 mm), the driest December (28 mm).
Does it snow in Zhongshan in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhongshan get in July?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhongshan in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
