Ghulja at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Ghulja is 523rd warmest (11.5 °C year average), 50th sunniest (about 2,735 hours of sunshine a year) and 654th wettest (298 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Huocheng, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Ghulja is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 1.8 °C warmer in January, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.0 °C), averages 29.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-4.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March and December. The area lies about 2,206 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Ghulja has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 2,206 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.4 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-400-2613), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs.
