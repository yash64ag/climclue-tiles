Yatou sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Shandong · China

The sea off Yatou averages 24.0 °C (75 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 3.5 °C (38 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Yatou sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Yatou?
In August, at about 24.0 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Yatou?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 3.5 °C (38 °F).
How warm is the sea in Yatou in February?
About 3.5 °C (38 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Yatou?
About 13.2 °C (56 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Yatou nearby?
In August: Dongcun (25.9 °C, 121 km) and Longgang, Shandong (25.1 °C, 194 km).
