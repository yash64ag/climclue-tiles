Yatou at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Yatou is 1600th warmest (12.4 °C year average), 412th sunniest (about 2,535 hours of sunshine a year) and 1416th wettest (686 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tianfu, Shandong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (36 km away), Yatou is 0.2 °C cooler in August and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.8 °C), averages 25.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Yatou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Yatou itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.157°N, 122.438°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-468-3024), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
