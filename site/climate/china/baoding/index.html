Baoding, Hebei at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Baoding, Hebei is 1374th warmest (13.8 °C year average), 269th sunniest (about 2,609 hours of sunshine a year) and 1772nd wettest (516 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Beiwangli, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Baoding, Hebei is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 9 mm less precipitation a year. The area lies about 191 km from the sea.

Why Baoding, Hebei has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 191 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Baoding itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.873°N, 115.462°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-451-2954), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
