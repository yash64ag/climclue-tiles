Baoding at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Baoding is 446th warmest (13.8 °C year average), 114th sunniest (about 2,609 hours of sunshine a year) and 585th wettest (516 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dingzhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (57 km away), Baoding is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 36 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 30.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, November and December. The area lies about 191 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Baoding, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Baoding has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 191 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
