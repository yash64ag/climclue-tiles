How July compares in Baoding

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Baoding. Compared with June: 1.0 °C warmer, 84 mm more precipitation and 44 hours less sunshine. August is 1.8 °C cooler than July on average.

Baoding in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baoding in July?
In July, Baoding averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.9 °C (73 °F), with 157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Baoding?
July is not one of Baoding’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baoding in July?
157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (157 mm), the driest December (3 mm).
Does it snow in Baoding in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baoding get in July?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baoding in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
