Xiantao at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Xiantao is 265th warmest (17.1 °C year average), 509th sunniest (about 1,852 hours of sunshine a year) and 157th wettest (1,505 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jingling, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Xiantao is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 215 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 24.4 °C more than its coolest, January (4.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 729 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Xiantao has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 729 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Xiantao itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (30.371°N, 113.443°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-536-2934), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
