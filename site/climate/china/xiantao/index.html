Xiantao at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Xiantao is 246th warmest (17.2 °C year average), 509th sunniest (about 1,852 hours of sunshine a year) and 175th wettest (1,485 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jingling, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Xiantao is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 181 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 24.3 °C more than its coolest, January (4.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 729 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Xiantao, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Xiantao has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 729 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
