Panshan weather in February

Average February weather in Panshan, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Panshan averages 1.0 °C (34 °F) days and -10.0 °C (14 °F) nights, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 189 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Panshan

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Panshan. Compared with January: 4.4 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. March is 7.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Panshan in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Panshan in February?
In February, Panshan averages a day high of 1.0 °C (34 °F) and a night low of -10.0 °C (14 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 189 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Panshan?
February is not one of Panshan’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Panshan in February?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (178 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Panshan in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.2 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Panshan get in February?
About 189 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Panshan in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
