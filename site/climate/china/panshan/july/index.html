Panshan weather in July

Average July weather in Panshan, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Panshan averages 29.1 °C (84 °F) days and 21.3 °C (70 °F) nights, with 178 mm (7.0 in) of rain over the month and about 220 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Panshan

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Panshan. Compared with June: 2.5 °C warmer, 96 mm more precipitation and 39 hours less sunshine. August is 0.8 °C cooler than July on average.

Panshan in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Panshan in July?
In July, Panshan averages a day high of 29.1 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 178 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Panshan?
July is not one of Panshan’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
