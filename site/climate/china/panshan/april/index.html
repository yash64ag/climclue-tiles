Panshan weather in April

Average April weather in Panshan, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Panshan averages 16.9 °C (62 °F) days and 5.7 °C (42 °F) nights, with 35 mm (1.4 in) of rain over the month and about 243 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Panshan

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Panshan. Compared with March: 8.8 °C warmer, 21 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. May is 6.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Panshan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Panshan in April?
In April, Panshan averages a day high of 16.9 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 5.7 °C (42 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Panshan?
April is not one of Panshan’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Panshan in April?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (178 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Panshan in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Panshan get in April?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Panshan in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
