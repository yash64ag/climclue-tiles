How June compares in Panshan

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Panshan. Compared with May: 4.6 °C warmer, 30 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. July is 2.5 °C warmer than June on average.

Panshan in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Panshan in June?
In June, Panshan averages a day high of 27.5 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Panshan?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Panshan’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Panshan in June?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (178 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Panshan in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Panshan get in June?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Panshan in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
