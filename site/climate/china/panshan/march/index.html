How March compares in Panshan

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Panshan. Compared with February: 7.0 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 47 hours more sunshine. April is 8.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Panshan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Panshan in March?
In March, Panshan averages a day high of 7.9 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -3.5 °C (26 °F), with 14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Panshan?
March is not one of Panshan’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Panshan in March?
14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (180 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Panshan in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.1 cm (2.4 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Panshan get in March?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Panshan in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
