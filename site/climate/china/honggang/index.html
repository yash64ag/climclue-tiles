Honggang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Honggang is 645th warmest (4.7 °C year average), 88th sunniest (about 2,662 hours of sunshine a year) and 617th wettest (447 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Daqing, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Honggang is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 10 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.4 °C), averages 41.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-18.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 637 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Honggang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 637 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 41.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
