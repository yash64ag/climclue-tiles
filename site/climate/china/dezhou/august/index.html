How August compares in Dezhou

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Dezhou. Compared with July: 1.4 °C cooler, 43 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. September is 4.6 °C cooler than August on average.

Dezhou in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dezhou in August?
In August, Dezhou averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 21.7 °C (71 °F), with 124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Dezhou?
August is not one of Dezhou’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dezhou in August?
124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (167 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Dezhou in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dezhou get in August?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dezhou in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
