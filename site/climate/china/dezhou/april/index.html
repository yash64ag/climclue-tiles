Dezhou in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dezhou in April?
In April, Dezhou averages a day high of 21.9 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 9.3 °C (49 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Dezhou?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Dezhou’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dezhou in April?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (167 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Dezhou in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dezhou get in April?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dezhou in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
