How December compares in Dezhou

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Dezhou. Compared with November: 6.7 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. January is 1.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Dezhou in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dezhou in December?
In December, Dezhou averages a day high of 5.6 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -4.5 °C (24 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Dezhou?
December is not one of Dezhou’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dezhou in December?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (167 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Dezhou in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.4 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Dezhou get in December?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dezhou in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
