How January compares in Dezhou

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Dezhou. Compared with December: 1.9 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 3.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Dezhou in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dezhou in January?
In January, Dezhou averages a day high of 3.3 °C (38 °F) and a night low of -6.8 °C (20 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Dezhou?
January is not one of Dezhou’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dezhou in January?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (168 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Dezhou in January?
Yes, on average: about 2.8 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Dezhou get in January?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dezhou in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
