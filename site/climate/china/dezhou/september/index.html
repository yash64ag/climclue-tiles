How September compares in Dezhou

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Dezhou. Compared with August: 4.6 °C cooler, 82 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. October is 6.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Dezhou in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dezhou in September?
In September, Dezhou averages a day high of 26.5 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.5 °C (62 °F), with 42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 216 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Dezhou?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Dezhou’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dezhou in September?
42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (167 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Dezhou in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dezhou get in September?
About 216 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dezhou in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
