How November compares in Dezhou

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Dezhou. Compared with October: 8.3 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. December is 6.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Dezhou in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dezhou in November?
In November, Dezhou averages a day high of 12.6 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 1.4 °C (35 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Dezhou?
November is not one of Dezhou’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dezhou in November?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (167 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Dezhou in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.2 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Dezhou get in November?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dezhou in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
