Wanxian weather in October

Average October weather in Wanxian, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Wanxian averages 23.0 °C (73 °F) days and 16.2 °C (61 °F) nights, with 107 mm (4.2 in) of rain over the month and about 94 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Wanxian

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Wanxian. Compared with September: 5.6 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. November is 4.9 °C cooler than October on average.
