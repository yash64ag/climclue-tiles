Wanxian in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wanxian in April?
In April, Wanxian averages a day high of 23.8 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 123 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Wanxian?
No month in Wanxian is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wanxian in April?
104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (191 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Wanxian in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wanxian get in April?
About 123 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
