How August compares in Wanxian

August is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Wanxian. Compared with July: about as warm, 28 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. September is 4.1 °C cooler than August on average.

Wanxian in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wanxian in August?
In August, Wanxian averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.8 °C (75 °F), with 172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Wanxian?
No month in Wanxian is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wanxian in August?
172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (202 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Wanxian in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wanxian get in August?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wanxian in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
