How November compares in Wanxian

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Wanxian. Compared with October: 4.9 °C cooler, 63 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. December is 4.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Wanxian in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wanxian in November?
In November, Wanxian averages a day high of 16.5 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 9.9 °C (50 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 74 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Wanxian?
No month in Wanxian is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wanxian in November?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (202 mm), the driest January (18 mm).
Does it snow in Wanxian in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wanxian get in November?
About 74 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wanxian in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
