How December compares in Langfang

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Langfang. Compared with November: 6.2 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Langfang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Langfang in December?
In December, Langfang averages a day high of 3.0 °C (37 °F) and a night low of -5.9 °C (21 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Langfang?
December is not one of Langfang’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Langfang in December?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (176 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Langfang in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.2 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Langfang get in December?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Langfang in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
