How July compares in Langfang

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Langfang. Compared with June: 1.4 °C warmer, 86 mm more precipitation and 42 hours less sunshine. August is 1.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Langfang in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Langfang in July?
In July, Langfang averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 176 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Langfang?
July is not one of Langfang’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Langfang in July?
176 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (176 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Langfang in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Langfang get in July?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Langfang in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
