How April compares in Langfang

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Langfang. Compared with March: 8.8 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. May is 6.2 °C warmer than April on average.

Langfang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Langfang in April?
In April, Langfang averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 9.5 °C (49 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 239 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Langfang?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Langfang’s mild months (April, May and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Langfang in April?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (173 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Langfang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Langfang get in April?
About 239 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Langfang in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
