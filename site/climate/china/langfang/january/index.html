Langfang weather in January

Average January weather in Langfang, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Langfang averages 2.1 °C (36 °F) days and -8.7 °C (16 °F) nights, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 192 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Langfang

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Langfang. Compared with December: 1.7 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. February is 3.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Langfang in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Langfang in January?
In January, Langfang averages a day high of 2.1 °C (36 °F) and a night low of -8.7 °C (16 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Langfang?
January is not one of Langfang’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Langfang in January?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (173 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Langfang in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.7 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Langfang get in January?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Langfang in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
