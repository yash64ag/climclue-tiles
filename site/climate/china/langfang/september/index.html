How September compares in Langfang

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Langfang. Compared with August: 4.9 °C cooler, 105 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. October is 7.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Langfang in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Langfang in September?
In September, Langfang averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Langfang?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Langfang’s mild months (April, May and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Langfang in September?
52 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (173 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Langfang in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Langfang get in September?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Langfang in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
