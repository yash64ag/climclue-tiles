Lichuan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Lichuan is 483rd warmest (12.9 °C year average), 641st sunniest (about 1,400 hours of sunshine a year) and 157th wettest (1,536 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Enshi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Lichuan is 3.4 °C cooler in July and 3.3 °C cooler in January, with 19 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.8 °C), averages 20.9 °C more than its coolest, January (1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 968 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lichuan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lichuan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 968 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
