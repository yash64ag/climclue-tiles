How October compares in Mengzi

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Mengzi. Compared with September: 2.2 °C cooler, 47 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. November is 3.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Mengzi in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mengzi in October?
In October, Mengzi averages a day high of 22.1 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 116 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Mengzi?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Mengzi’s mild months (March, April, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mengzi in October?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (187 mm), the driest December (15 mm).
Does it snow in Mengzi in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mengzi get in October?
About 116 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mengzi in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
