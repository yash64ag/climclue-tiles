Mengzi in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mengzi in February?
In February, Mengzi averages a day high of 19.0 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 9.6 °C (49 °F), with 20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Mengzi?
February is not one of Mengzi’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mengzi in February?
20 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (187 mm), the driest December (15 mm).
Does it snow in Mengzi in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mengzi get in February?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mengzi in February?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
