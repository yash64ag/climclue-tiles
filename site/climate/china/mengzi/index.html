Mengzi at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Mengzi is 124th warmest (19.0 °C year average), 567th sunniest (about 1,723 hours of sunshine a year) and 364th wettest (973 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gejiu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Mengzi is 4.3 °C warmer in June and 4.1 °C warmer in January, with 326 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (23.6 °C), averages 10.9 °C more than its coolest, January (12.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.1 °C (47 °F), in December. The area lies about 469 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 2 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 114% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Mengzi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 469 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
