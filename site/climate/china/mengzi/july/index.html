Mengzi in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mengzi in July?
In July, Mengzi averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 19.2 °C (67 °F), with 187 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation and about 133 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Mengzi?
July is not one of Mengzi’s mild months; those are March, April, October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mengzi in July?
187 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (187 mm), the driest December (15 mm).
Does it snow in Mengzi in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mengzi get in July?
About 133 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mengzi in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
