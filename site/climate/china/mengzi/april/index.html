Mengzi in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mengzi in April?
In April, Mengzi averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 60 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Mengzi?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Mengzi’s mild months (March, April, October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mengzi in April?
60 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (187 mm), the driest December (15 mm).
Does it snow in Mengzi in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mengzi get in April?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mengzi in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
