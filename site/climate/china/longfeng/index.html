Longfeng at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Longfeng is 652nd warmest (4.5 °C year average), 93rd sunniest (about 2,650 hours of sunshine a year) and 614th wettest (453 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Daqing, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Longfeng is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.4 °C), averages 42.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-18.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 637 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 23 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 105% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Longfeng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 637 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 42.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
