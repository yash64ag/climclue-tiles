How February compares in Luoyang, Henan

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Luoyang, Henan. Compared with January: 3.4 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. March is 5.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Luoyang, Henan in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Luoyang, Henan in February?
In February, Luoyang, Henan averages a day high of 10.1 °C (50 °F) and a night low of -1.8 °C (29 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Luoyang, Henan?
February is not one of Luoyang, Henan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Luoyang, Henan in February?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (133 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Luoyang, Henan in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.7 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Luoyang, Henan get in February?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Luoyang, Henan in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
