How December compares in Luoyang, Henan

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Luoyang, Henan. Compared with November: 6.1 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Luoyang, Henan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Luoyang, Henan in December?
In December, Luoyang, Henan averages a day high of 7.9 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -2.7 °C (27 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Luoyang, Henan?
December is not one of Luoyang, Henan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Luoyang, Henan in December?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (130 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Luoyang, Henan in December?
Yes, on average: about 4.2 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Luoyang, Henan get in December?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Luoyang, Henan in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
