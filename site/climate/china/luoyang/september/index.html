Luoyang, Henan in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Luoyang, Henan in September?
In September, Luoyang, Henan averages a day high of 26.1 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Luoyang, Henan?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Luoyang, Henan’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Luoyang, Henan in September?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (133 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Luoyang, Henan in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Luoyang, Henan get in September?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Luoyang, Henan in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
