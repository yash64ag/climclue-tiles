How July compares in Luoyang, Henan

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Luoyang, Henan. Compared with June: 1.0 °C warmer, 70 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. August is 1.6 °C cooler than July on average.

Luoyang, Henan in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Luoyang, Henan in July?
In July, Luoyang, Henan averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Luoyang, Henan?
July is not one of Luoyang, Henan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Luoyang, Henan in July?
133 mm (5.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (133 mm), the driest December (8 mm).
Does it snow in Luoyang, Henan in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Luoyang, Henan get in July?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Luoyang, Henan in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
