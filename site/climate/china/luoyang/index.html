Luoyang, Henan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Luoyang, Henan is 374th warmest (15.2 °C year average), 298th sunniest (about 2,182 hours of sunshine a year) and 528th wettest (582 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jiyuan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Luoyang, Henan is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 52 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 26.7 °C more than its coolest, January (0.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 622 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Luoyang, Henan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 622 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
