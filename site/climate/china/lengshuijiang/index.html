Lengshuijiang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Lengshuijiang is 238th warmest (17.3 °C year average), 614th sunniest (about 1,519 hours of sunshine a year) and 250th wettest (1,313 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shaoyang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (50 km away), Lengshuijiang is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 38 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 23.6 °C more than its coolest, January (5.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 609 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Lengshuijiang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 609 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
