Guyuan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Guyuan is 601st warmest (7.8 °C year average), 248th sunniest (about 2,381 hours of sunshine a year) and 625th wettest (420 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pingliang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (64 km away), Guyuan is 1.3 °C cooler in July and 2.2 °C cooler in January, with 61 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.1 °C), averages 26.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-6.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,048 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Guyuan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Guyuan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,048 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
