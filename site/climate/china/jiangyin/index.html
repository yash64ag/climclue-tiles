Jiangyin at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Jiangyin is 338th warmest (16.0 °C year average), 353rd sunniest (about 2,031 hours of sunshine a year) and 315th wettest (1,147 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zhangjiagang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Jiangyin is about as warm in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 36 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 25.0 °C more than its coolest, January (3.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 108 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jiangyin, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jiangyin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 108 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
