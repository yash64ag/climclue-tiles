How December compares in Jiangyin

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Jiangyin. Compared with November: 6.1 °C cooler, 22 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. January is 2.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Jiangyin in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiangyin in December?
In December, Jiangyin averages a day high of 9.9 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 1.0 °C (34 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jiangyin?
December is not one of Jiangyin’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiangyin in December?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (203 mm), the driest December (32 mm).
Does it snow in Jiangyin in December?
Yes, on average: about 0.9 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiangyin get in December?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiangyin in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
