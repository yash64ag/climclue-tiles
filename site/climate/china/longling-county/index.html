Longling County at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Longling County is 171st warmest (18.3 °C year average), 323rd sunniest (about 2,121 hours of sunshine a year) and 42nd wettest (2,094 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tengyue, the nearest covered town with different numbers (49 km away), Longling County is 3.2 °C warmer in August and 4.2 °C warmer in January, with 337 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (22.1 °C), averages 10.1 °C more than its coolest, January (12.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.1 °C (41 °F), in January. The area lies about 748 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Longling County, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Longling County has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 748 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
