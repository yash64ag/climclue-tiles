Why Longling County has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 748 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.9 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Longling County climate: questions and answers

August, with an average of 22.1 °C (72 °F), day highs around 24.6 °C (76 °F) and night lows around 18.2 °C (65 °F).
What is the coldest month in Longling County?
January, with an average of 12.1 °C (54 °F), day highs around 18.0 °C (64 °F) and night lows around 5.1 °C (41 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Longling County?

What is the driest month in Longling County?

What is the sunniest month in Longling County?

Does it snow in Longling County?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Longling County?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: March, April and November.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Longling County’s GeoNames location (24.587°N, 98.689°E; ClimaClue area sq-594-2786), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 270,000 (GeoNames).
