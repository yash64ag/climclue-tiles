Why Hengshan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 236 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 38.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Hengshan climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Hengshan?
July, with an average of 21.4 °C (71 °F), day highs around 25.7 °C (78 °F) and night lows around 16.9 °C (62 °F).
What is the coldest month in Hengshan?
January, with an average of -16.9 °C (1 °F), day highs around -11.9 °C (11 °F) and night lows around -23.1 °C (-10 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Hengshan?
July, with 134 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 560 mm (22.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Hengshan?
February, with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Hengshan?
May, with about 235 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Hengshan?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 98 cm (38.4 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in March; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Hengshan?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June.
What type of climate does Hengshan have?

Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 164,844 (GeoNames).
