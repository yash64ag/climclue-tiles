How January compares in Puyang, Henan

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Puyang, Henan. Compared with December: 1.9 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. February is 3.7 °C warmer than January on average.

Puyang, Henan in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Puyang, Henan in January?
In January, Puyang, Henan averages a day high of 4.7 °C (40 °F) and a night low of -5.4 °C (22 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Puyang, Henan?
January is not one of Puyang, Henan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Puyang, Henan in January?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (155 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Puyang, Henan in January?
Yes, on average: about 4.5 cm (1.8 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Puyang, Henan get in January?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Puyang, Henan in January?
