Puyang, Henan in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Puyang, Henan in June?
In June, Puyang, Henan averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 21.0 °C (70 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 251 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Puyang, Henan?
June is not one of Puyang, Henan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (155 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Puyang, Henan in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).

About 251 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
