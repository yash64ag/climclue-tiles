Puyang, Henan in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Puyang, Henan in May?
In May, Puyang, Henan averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 15.3 °C (60 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 252 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Puyang, Henan?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Puyang, Henan’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Puyang, Henan in May?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (155 mm), the driest January (6 mm).
Does it snow in Puyang, Henan in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Puyang, Henan get in May?
About 252 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Puyang, Henan in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
