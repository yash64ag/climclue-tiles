Suiling weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Suiling, China (Heilongjiang), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Suiling is warmest in July, with 27.1 °C (81 °F) days and 17.0 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -15.9 °C (3 °F) days and -26.8 °C nights. Most rain falls around July (150 mm); February is driest (4 mm).

Suiling at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Suiling is 2060th warmest (2.7 °C year average), 396th sunniest (about 2,538 hours of sunshine a year) and 1648th wettest (571 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hailun, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Suiling is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 7 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.1 °C), averages 43.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-21.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 577 km from the sea.

Why Suiling has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 577 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 43.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Suiling itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (47.235°N, 127.108°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-367-3071), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
