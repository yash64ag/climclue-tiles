Wuxue at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Wuxue is 220th warmest (17.5 °C year average), 470th sunniest (about 1,907 hours of sunshine a year) and 108th wettest (1,710 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jiujiang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Wuxue is 0.2 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 111 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.4 °C), averages 24.7 °C more than its coolest, January (4.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 532 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Wuxue, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Wuxue has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 532 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
