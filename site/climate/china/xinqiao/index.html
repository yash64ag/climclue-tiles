Xinqiao at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Xinqiao is 278th warmest (16.7 °C year average), 420th sunniest (about 1,954 hours of sunshine a year) and 195th wettest (1,449 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Minhang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Xinqiao is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 23.7 °C more than its coolest, January (4.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 38 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 16 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 120% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Xinqiao has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.7 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
