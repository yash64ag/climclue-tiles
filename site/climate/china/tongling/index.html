Tongling at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Tongling is 261st warmest (16.9 °C year average), 398th sunniest (about 1,975 hours of sunshine a year) and 187th wettest (1,458 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chizhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Tongling is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 113 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.1 °C), averages 25.2 °C more than its coolest, January (3.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 320 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tongling, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tongling has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 320 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
