How September compares in Hanzhong

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Hanzhong. Compared with August: 4.5 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 93 hours less sunshine. October is 5.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Hanzhong in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hanzhong in September?
In September, Hanzhong averages a day high of 24.5 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 17.3 °C (63 °F), with 159 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 119 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Hanzhong?
September is not one of Hanzhong’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hanzhong in September?
159 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (196 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Hanzhong in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hanzhong get in September?
About 119 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hanzhong in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
