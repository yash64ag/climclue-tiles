How May compares in Hanzhong

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Hanzhong. Compared with April: 4.4 °C warmer, 44 mm more precipitation and 24 hours more sunshine. June is 3.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Hanzhong in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hanzhong in May?
In May, Hanzhong averages a day high of 25.6 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 100 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Hanzhong?
May is not one of Hanzhong’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hanzhong in May?
100 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (196 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Hanzhong in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hanzhong get in May?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hanzhong in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
