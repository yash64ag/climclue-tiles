Hanzhong weather in October

Average October weather in Hanzhong, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Hanzhong averages 19.5 °C (67 °F) days and 12.3 °C (54 °F) nights, with 76 mm (3.0 in) of rain over the month and about 108 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Hanzhong

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Hanzhong. Compared with September: 4.9 °C cooler, 83 mm less precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. November is 5.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Hanzhong in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hanzhong in October?
In October, Hanzhong averages a day high of 19.5 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 12.3 °C (54 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 108 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Hanzhong?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Hanzhong’s mild months (April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hanzhong in October?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (196 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Hanzhong in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hanzhong get in October?
About 108 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hanzhong in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
