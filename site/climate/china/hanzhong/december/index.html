How December compares in Hanzhong

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Hanzhong. Compared with November: 5.2 °C cooler, 21 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. January is 1.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Hanzhong in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hanzhong in December?
In December, Hanzhong averages a day high of 8.1 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 1.0 °C (34 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 99 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Hanzhong?
December is not one of Hanzhong’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hanzhong in December?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (196 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Hanzhong in December?
Yes, on average: about 1.1 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Hanzhong get in December?
About 99 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hanzhong in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
