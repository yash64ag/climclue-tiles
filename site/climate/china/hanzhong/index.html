Why Hanzhong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,136 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Hanzhong climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Hanzhong?
July, with an average of 25.9 °C (79 °F), day highs around 28.9 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 21.4 °C (71 °F).
What is the coldest month in Hanzhong?
January, with an average of 4.0 °C (39 °F), day highs around 7.9 °C (46 °F) and night lows around -0.9 °C (30 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Hanzhong?
July, with 199 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,000 mm (39.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Hanzhong?
January, with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Hanzhong?
August, with about 212 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Hanzhong?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 9.4 cm (3.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Hanzhong?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April and October.
What type of climate does Hanzhong have?
Humid subtropical, dry winter (Köppen Cwa). Hot, wet summers and mild, dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.3 × 11 km, that contains Hanzhong’s GeoNames location (33.075°N, 107.022°E; ClimaClue area sq-509-2870), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,006,557 (GeoNames).
