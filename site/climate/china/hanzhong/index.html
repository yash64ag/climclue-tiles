Hanzhong at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Hanzhong is 365th warmest (15.5 °C year average), 580th sunniest (about 1,669 hours of sunshine a year) and 356th wettest (1,000 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chenggu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Hanzhong is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 65 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.9 °C), averages 21.9 °C more than its coolest, January (4.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 1,136 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Hanzhong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,136 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
