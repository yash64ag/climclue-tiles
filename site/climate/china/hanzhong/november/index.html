How November compares in Hanzhong

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Hanzhong. Compared with October: 6.0 °C cooler, 43 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. December is 5.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Hanzhong in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hanzhong in November?
In November, Hanzhong averages a day high of 13.4 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 6.1 °C (43 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 95 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Hanzhong?
November is not one of Hanzhong’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hanzhong in November?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (196 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Hanzhong in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hanzhong get in November?
About 95 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hanzhong in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
