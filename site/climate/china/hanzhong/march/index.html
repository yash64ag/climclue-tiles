How March compares in Hanzhong

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Hanzhong. Compared with February: 4.3 °C warmer, 21 mm more precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. April is 5.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Hanzhong in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hanzhong in March?
In March, Hanzhong averages a day high of 14.3 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 4.6 °C (40 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 121 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Hanzhong?
March is not one of Hanzhong’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hanzhong in March?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (199 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Hanzhong in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.6 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Hanzhong get in March?
About 121 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hanzhong in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
