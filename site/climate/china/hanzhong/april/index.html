How April compares in Hanzhong

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Hanzhong. Compared with March: 5.6 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. May is 4.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Hanzhong in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hanzhong in April?
In April, Hanzhong averages a day high of 21.2 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 11.1 °C (52 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Hanzhong?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Hanzhong’s mild months (April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hanzhong in April?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (196 mm), the driest January (10 mm).
Does it snow in Hanzhong in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hanzhong get in April?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hanzhong in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
