Xininglu at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Xininglu is 1881st warmest (7.9 °C year average), 124th sunniest (about 2,737 hours of sunshine a year) and 1969th wettest (273 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Wusu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Xininglu is 1.8 °C cooler in July and 2.1 °C warmer in January, with 48 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.8 °C), averages 35.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-11.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 2,481 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Xininglu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Xininglu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 2,481 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.3 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Xininglu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (44.340°N, 84.904°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-396-2649), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
