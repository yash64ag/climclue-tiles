How February compares in Wuchuan

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Wuchuan. Compared with January: 1.2 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 50 hours less sunshine. March is 2.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Wuchuan in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuchuan in February?
In February, Wuchuan averages a day high of 21.4 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 15.3 °C (60 °F), with 52 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 72 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Wuchuan?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Wuchuan’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuchuan in February?
52 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (309 mm), the driest December (28 mm).
Does it snow in Wuchuan in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuchuan get in February?
About 72 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuchuan in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
