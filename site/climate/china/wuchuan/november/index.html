How November compares in Wuchuan

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Wuchuan. Compared with October: 3.8 °C cooler, 61 mm less precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. December is 3.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Wuchuan in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuchuan in November?
In November, Wuchuan averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 17.6 °C (64 °F), with 34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Wuchuan?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Wuchuan’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuchuan in November?
34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (309 mm), the driest December (28 mm).
Does it snow in Wuchuan in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuchuan get in November?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuchuan in November?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
