How July compares in Wuchuan

July is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Wuchuan. Compared with June: 0.5 °C warmer, 23 mm less precipitation and 51 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Wuchuan in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuchuan in July?
In July, Wuchuan averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 26.2 °C (79 °F), with 281 mm (11.1 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Wuchuan?
July is not one of Wuchuan’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuchuan in July?
281 mm (11.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (309 mm), the driest December (28 mm).
Does it snow in Wuchuan in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuchuan get in July?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuchuan in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
