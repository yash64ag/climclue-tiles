How April compares in Wuchuan

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Wuchuan. Compared with March: 3.6 °C warmer, 83 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. May is 2.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Wuchuan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuchuan in April?
In April, Wuchuan averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 114 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Wuchuan?
April is not one of Wuchuan’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuchuan in April?
157 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (309 mm), the driest December (28 mm).
Does it snow in Wuchuan in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuchuan get in April?
About 114 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuchuan in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
