How May compares in Wuchuan

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Wuchuan. Compared with April: 2.8 °C warmer, 130 mm more precipitation and 71 hours more sunshine. June is 1.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Wuchuan in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuchuan in May?
In May, Wuchuan averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 287 mm (11.3 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Wuchuan?
May is not one of Wuchuan’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuchuan in May?
287 mm (11.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (309 mm), the driest December (28 mm).
Does it snow in Wuchuan in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuchuan get in May?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuchuan in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
