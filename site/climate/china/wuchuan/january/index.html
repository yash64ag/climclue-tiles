How January compares in Wuchuan

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Wuchuan. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. February is 1.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Wuchuan in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuchuan in January?
In January, Wuchuan averages a day high of 20.1 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 13.0 °C (55 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 123 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Wuchuan?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Wuchuan’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuchuan in January?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (309 mm), the driest December (28 mm).
Does it snow in Wuchuan in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuchuan get in January?
About 123 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Wuchuan in January?
About 18.9 °C (66 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (29.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Wuchuan in January?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
