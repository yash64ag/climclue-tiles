Lüshun sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Liaoning · China

The sea off Lüshun averages 23.6 °C (74 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 3.1 °C (38 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Lüshun sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Lüshun?
In August, at about 23.6 °C (74 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Lüshun?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 3.1 °C (38 °F).
How warm is the sea in Lüshun in February?
About 3.1 °C (38 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Lüshun?
About 12.5 °C (55 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Lüshun nearby?
In August: Dachang Shandao (24.2 °C, 127 km), Xiaochangshan (24.2 °C, 135 km) and Qingyang, Shandong (24.4 °C, 145 km).
