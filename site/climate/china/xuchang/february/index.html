How February compares in Xuchang

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Xuchang. Compared with January: 3.2 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. March is 5.1 °C warmer than February on average.

Xuchang in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xuchang in February?
In February, Xuchang averages a day high of 9.4 °C (49 °F) and a night low of -0.7 °C (31 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Xuchang?
February is not one of Xuchang’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xuchang in February?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (191 mm), the driest January (13 mm).
Does it snow in Xuchang in February?
Yes, on average: about 4.8 cm (1.9 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Xuchang get in February?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xuchang in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
