How January compares in Xuchang

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Xuchang. Compared with December: 2.0 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. February is 3.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Xuchang in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xuchang in January?
In January, Xuchang averages a day high of 6.1 °C (43 °F) and a night low of -3.8 °C (25 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Xuchang?
January is not one of Xuchang’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xuchang in January?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (191 mm), the driest January (13 mm).
Does it snow in Xuchang in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.8 cm (3.1 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Xuchang get in January?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xuchang in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
