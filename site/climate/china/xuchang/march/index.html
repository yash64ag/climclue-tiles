How March compares in Xuchang

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Xuchang. Compared with February: 5.1 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. April is 6.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Xuchang in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xuchang in March?
In March, Xuchang averages a day high of 14.9 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 3.2 °C (38 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Xuchang?
March is not one of Xuchang’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xuchang in March?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (189 mm), the driest January (12 mm).
Does it snow in Xuchang in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.9 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Xuchang get in March?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xuchang in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
