Xuchang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xuchang in April?
In April, Xuchang averages a day high of 22.3 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 10.4 °C (51 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Xuchang?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Xuchang’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xuchang in April?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (191 mm), the driest January (13 mm).
Does it snow in Xuchang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xuchang get in April?
About 188 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xuchang in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
