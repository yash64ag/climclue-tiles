Daxing at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Daxing is 467th warmest (13.3 °C year average), 73rd sunniest (about 2,691 hours of sunshine a year) and 576th wettest (531 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Beijing, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Daxing is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 38 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 31.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 153 km from the sea.

Why Daxing has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 153 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 31.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
