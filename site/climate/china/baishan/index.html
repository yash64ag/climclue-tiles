Why Baishan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 260 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 37.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Baishan climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Baishan?
July, with an average of 21.9 °C (72 °F), day highs around 25.2 °C (77 °F) and night lows around 17.6 °C (64 °F).
What is the coldest month in Baishan?
January, with an average of -15.1 °C (5 °F), day highs around -9.6 °C (15 °F) and night lows around -23.2 °C (-10 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Baishan?
July, with 213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 817 mm (32.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Baishan?
January, with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Baishan?
May, with about 255 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Baishan?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 168 cm (66.1 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in March; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 6 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Baishan?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).
What type of climate does Baishan have?
Continental, warm summer, dry winter (Köppen Dwb). Warm, wet summers and cold, dry winters. Calculated from this area’s 1981–2010 monthly averages.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.3 × 11 km, that contains Baishan’s GeoNames location (41.939°N, 126.420°E; ClimaClue area sq-420-3064), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 183,880 (GeoNames).
