Mabai at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Mabai is 864th warmest (17.1 °C year average), 1540th sunniest (about 1,565 hours of sunshine a year) and 118th wettest (1,937 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kaihua, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Mabai is 1.3 °C cooler in June and 1.3 °C cooler in January, with 1,032 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (21.9 °C), averages 11.8 °C more than its coolest, January (10.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.2 °C (43 °F), in January. The area lies about 364 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mabai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mabai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 364 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C. The 10 km area around Mabai, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 18.1 °C a year, 1.0 °C warmer than the town itself, because it takes in lower ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Mabai itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (23.013°N, 104.451°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-609-2844), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
