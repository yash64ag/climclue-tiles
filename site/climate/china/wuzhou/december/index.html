How December compares in Wuzhou

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Wuzhou. Compared with November: 4.6 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Wuzhou in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuzhou in December?
In December, Wuzhou averages a day high of 19.7 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 9.8 °C (50 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Wuzhou?
December is not one of Wuzhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuzhou in December?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is May (276 mm), the driest December (34 mm).
Does it snow in Wuzhou in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuzhou get in December?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuzhou in December?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
