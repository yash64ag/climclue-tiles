Why Wuzhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 206 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Wuzhou climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Wuzhou?
July, with an average of 29.4 °C (85 °F), day highs around 31.8 °C (89 °F) and night lows around 25.4 °C (78 °F).
What is the coldest month in Wuzhou?
January, with an average of 13.2 °C (56 °F), day highs around 17.1 °C (63 °F) and night lows around 9.0 °C (48 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Wuzhou?
May, with 278 mm (11.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,592 mm (62.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Wuzhou?
December, with 34 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Wuzhou?
July, with about 238 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Wuzhou?
Not in a typical year: ERA5-Land gives less than 1 cm of fresh snow a year for this area (1981–2010).
When is the best time for mild weather in Wuzhou?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: October and November.
What type of climate does Wuzhou have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10 × 11 km, that contains Wuzhou’s GeoNames location (23.481°N, 111.288°E; ClimaClue area sq-605-2912), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 761,948 (GeoNames).
