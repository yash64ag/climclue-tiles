How August compares in Wuzhou

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Wuzhou. Compared with July: 0.1 °C cooler, 39 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. September is 1.5 °C cooler than August on average.

Wuzhou in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuzhou in August?
In August, Wuzhou averages a day high of 31.9 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 25.4 °C (78 °F), with 171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Wuzhou?
August is not one of Wuzhou’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuzhou in August?
171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (278 mm), the driest December (34 mm).
Does it snow in Wuzhou in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuzhou get in August?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuzhou in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
