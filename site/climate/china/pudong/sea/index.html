Pudong sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Shanghai · China

The sea off Pudong averages 27.5 °C (82 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 6.2 °C (43 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Pudong sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Pudong?
In August, at about 27.5 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Pudong?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 6.2 °C (43 °F).
How warm is the sea in Pudong in February?
About 6.2 °C (43 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Pudong?
About 17.0 °C (63 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Pudong nearby?
In August: Nanqiao (28.4 °C, 36 km), Fengxiang (28.4 °C, 43 km) and Jinshan, Shanghai (28.4 °C, 49 km).
