How December compares in Pudong

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Pudong. Compared with November: 6.1 °C cooler, 23 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. January is 2.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Pudong in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Pudong in December?
In December, Pudong averages a day high of 11.2 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 3.4 °C (38 °F), with 47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Pudong?
December is not one of Pudong’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Pudong in December?
47 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (219 mm), the driest December (47 mm).
Does it snow in Pudong in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Pudong get in December?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Pudong in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
