Daqing weather in July

Average July weather in Daqing, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Daqing averages 28.4 °C (83 °F) days and 18.4 °C (65 °F) nights, with 148 mm (5.8 in) of rain over the month and about 243 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Daqing

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Daqing. Compared with June: 2.3 °C warmer, 67 mm more precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. August is 1.6 °C cooler than July on average.

Daqing in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daqing in July?
In July, Daqing averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 18.4 °C (65 °F), with 148 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Daqing?
July is not one of Daqing’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daqing in July?
148 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (148 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Daqing in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Daqing get in July?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daqing in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
