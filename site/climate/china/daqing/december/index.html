Daqing weather in December

Average December weather in Daqing, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Daqing averages -10.3 °C (13 °F) days and -21.0 °C (-6 °F) nights, with 4 mm (0.1 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 157 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Daqing

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Daqing. Compared with November: 10.1 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. January is 3.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Daqing in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daqing in December?
In December, Daqing averages a day high of -10.3 °C (13 °F) and a night low of -21.0 °C (-6 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Daqing?
December is not one of Daqing’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daqing in December?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (148 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Daqing in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.6 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Daqing get in December?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daqing in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
