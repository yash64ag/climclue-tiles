How May compares in Daqing

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Daqing. Compared with April: 8.0 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. June is 6.0 °C warmer than May on average.

Daqing in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daqing in May?
In May, Daqing averages a day high of 20.6 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 8.4 °C (47 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 268 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Daqing?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Daqing’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daqing in May?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (147 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Daqing in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Daqing get in May?
About 268 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daqing in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
