How September compares in Daqing

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Daqing. Compared with August: 6.6 °C cooler, 62 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. October is 9.0 °C cooler than September on average.

Daqing in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daqing in September?
In September, Daqing averages a day high of 20.0 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 10.8 °C (51 °F), with 39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Daqing?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Daqing’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daqing in September?
39 mm (1.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (147 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Daqing in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Daqing get in September?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daqing in September?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
