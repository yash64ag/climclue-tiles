How February compares in Daqing

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Daqing. Compared with January: 5.2 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. March is 9.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Daqing in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daqing in February?
In February, Daqing averages a day high of -6.7 °C (20 °F) and a night low of -19.9 °C (-4 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Daqing?
February is not one of Daqing’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daqing in February?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (148 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Daqing in February?
Yes, on average: about 2.1 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Daqing get in February?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daqing in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
