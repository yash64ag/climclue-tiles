Daqing weather in January

Average January weather in Daqing, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Daqing averages -12.7 °C (9 °F) days and -24.6 °C (-12 °F) nights, with 2 mm (0.1 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 179 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Daqing

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Daqing. Compared with December: 3.0 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. February is 5.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Daqing in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Daqing in January?
In January, Daqing averages a day high of -12.7 °C (9 °F) and a night low of -24.6 °C (-12 °F), with 2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Daqing?
January is not one of Daqing’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Daqing in January?
2 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (148 mm), the driest February (2 mm).
Does it snow in Daqing in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.2 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Daqing get in January?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Daqing in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
