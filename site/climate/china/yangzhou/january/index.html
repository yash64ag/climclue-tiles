How January compares in Yangzhou

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Yangzhou. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, 15 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. February is 2.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Yangzhou in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yangzhou in January?
In January, Yangzhou averages a day high of 7.2 °C (45 °F) and a night low of -1.6 °C (29 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Yangzhou?
January is not one of Yangzhou’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yangzhou in January?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (210 mm), the driest December (33 mm).
Does it snow in Yangzhou in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.6 cm (3.4 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Yangzhou get in January?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yangzhou in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
