How September compares in Yangzhou

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Yangzhou. Compared with August: 4.3 °C cooler, 67 mm less precipitation and 59 hours less sunshine. October is 5.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Yangzhou in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yangzhou in September?
In September, Yangzhou averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 19.4 °C (67 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Yangzhou?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Yangzhou’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yangzhou in September?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (214 mm), the driest December (33 mm).
Does it snow in Yangzhou in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yangzhou get in September?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yangzhou in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
