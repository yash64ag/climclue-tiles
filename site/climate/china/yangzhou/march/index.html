How March compares in Yangzhou

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Yangzhou. Compared with February: 4.3 °C warmer, 23 mm more precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. April is 6.1 °C warmer than March on average.

Yangzhou in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yangzhou in March?
In March, Yangzhou averages a day high of 13.8 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 4.2 °C (40 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 153 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Yangzhou?
March is not one of Yangzhou’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yangzhou in March?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (210 mm), the driest December (33 mm).
Does it snow in Yangzhou in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.2 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Yangzhou get in March?
About 153 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yangzhou in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
