How October compares in Yangzhou

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Yangzhou. Compared with September: 5.5 °C cooler, 23 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. November is 6.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Yangzhou in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yangzhou in October?
In October, Yangzhou averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Yangzhou?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Yangzhou’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yangzhou in October?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (210 mm), the driest December (33 mm).
Does it snow in Yangzhou in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yangzhou get in October?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yangzhou in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
