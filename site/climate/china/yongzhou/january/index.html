How January compares in Yongzhou

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Yongzhou. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, 38 mm more precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. February is 2.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Yongzhou in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yongzhou in January?
In January, Yongzhou averages a day high of 10.0 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 2.9 °C (37 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 79 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Yongzhou?
January is not one of Yongzhou’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yongzhou in January?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (214 mm), the driest December (50 mm).
Does it snow in Yongzhou in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.1 cm (2.4 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Yongzhou get in January?
About 79 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yongzhou in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
