How December compares in Yongzhou

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Yongzhou. Compared with November: 5.5 °C cooler, 27 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. January is 2.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Yongzhou in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yongzhou in December?
In December, Yongzhou averages a day high of 12.8 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 4.5 °C (40 °F), with 50 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 107 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Yongzhou?
December is not one of Yongzhou’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yongzhou in December?
50 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is May (214 mm), the driest December (50 mm).
Does it snow in Yongzhou in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.4 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Yongzhou get in December?
About 107 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yongzhou in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
