How September compares in Yongzhou

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Yongzhou. Compared with August: 3.9 °C cooler, 64 mm less precipitation and 50 hours less sunshine. October is 5.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Yongzhou in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yongzhou in September?
In September, Yongzhou averages a day high of 29.2 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Yongzhou?
September is not one of Yongzhou’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yongzhou in September?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (215 mm), the driest December (50 mm).
Does it snow in Yongzhou in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yongzhou get in September?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yongzhou in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
