Wenling sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Zhejiang · China

The sea off Wenling averages 28.0 °C (82 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 11.1 °C (52 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Wenling sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Wenling?
In August, at about 28.0 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Wenling?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 11.1 °C (52 °F).
How warm is the sea in Wenling in February?
About 11.1 °C (52 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Wenling?
About 19.2 °C (67 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
