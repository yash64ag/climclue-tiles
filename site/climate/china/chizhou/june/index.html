How June compares in Chizhou

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Chizhou. Compared with May: 3.4 °C warmer, 113 mm more precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. July is 3.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Chizhou in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chizhou in June?
In June, Chizhou averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 287 mm (11.3 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Chizhou?
June is not one of Chizhou’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chizhou in June?
287 mm (11.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (287 mm), the driest December (38 mm).
Does it snow in Chizhou in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chizhou get in June?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chizhou in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
