How October compares in Chizhou

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Chizhou. Compared with September: 5.6 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. November is 6.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Chizhou in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chizhou in October?
In October, Chizhou averages a day high of 22.4 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 14.5 °C (58 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Chizhou?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Chizhou’s mild months (September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chizhou in October?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (284 mm), the driest December (37 mm).
Does it snow in Chizhou in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chizhou get in October?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chizhou in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
