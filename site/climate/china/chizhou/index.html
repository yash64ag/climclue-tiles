Chizhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Chizhou is 250th warmest (17.1 °C year average), 419th sunniest (about 1,955 hours of sunshine a year) and 142nd wettest (1,571 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tongling, the nearest covered town with different numbers (43 km away), Chizhou is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 113 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.3 °C), averages 25.3 °C more than its coolest, January (4.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 345 km from the sea.

Why Chizhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 345 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
