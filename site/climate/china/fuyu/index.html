Fuyu at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Fuyu is 635th warmest (5.9 °C year average), 102nd sunniest (about 2,631 hours of sunshine a year) and 616th wettest (442 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Songyuan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Fuyu is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.8 °C), averages 39.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-15.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 547 km from the sea.

Why Fuyu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 547 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 39.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-388-3048), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
