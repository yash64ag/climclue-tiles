Huolu at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Huolu is 1015th warmest (16.1 °C year average), 1854th sunniest (about 1,274 hours of sunshine a year) and 550th wettest (1,348 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Wulong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Huolu is 2.7 °C cooler in July and 2.6 °C cooler in January, with 339 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.1 °C), averages 21.1 °C more than its coolest, January (5.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 860 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Huolu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Huolu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 860 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.1 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 8.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Huolu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (29.394°N, 107.873°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-546-2878), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
