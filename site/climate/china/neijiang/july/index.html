How July compares in Neijiang

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Neijiang. Compared with June: 2.3 °C warmer, 24 mm more precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. August is 0.2 °C cooler than July on average.

Neijiang in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Neijiang in July?
In July, Neijiang averages a day high of 30.7 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 219 mm (8.6 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Neijiang?
July is not one of Neijiang’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Neijiang in July?
219 mm (8.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (219 mm), the driest January (17 mm).
Does it snow in Neijiang in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Neijiang get in July?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Neijiang in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
