Neijiang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Neijiang is 167th warmest (18.3 °C year average), 657th sunniest (about 1,305 hours of sunshine a year) and 335th wettest (1,091 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zigong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Neijiang is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 27 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 19.4 °C more than its coolest, January (8.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 950 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Neijiang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Neijiang

For mild weather, the best months to visit Neijiang are April and October.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): April and October.

Why Neijiang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 950 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
