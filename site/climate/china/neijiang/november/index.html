Neijiang weather in November

Average November weather in Neijiang, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Neijiang averages 16.8 °C (62 °F) days and 11.7 °C (53 °F) nights, with 30 mm (1.2 in) of rain over the month and about 65 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Neijiang

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Neijiang. Compared with October: 4.3 °C cooler, 28 mm less precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. December is 4.9 °C cooler than November on average.

Neijiang in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Neijiang in November?
In November, Neijiang averages a day high of 16.8 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 11.7 °C (53 °F), with 30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 65 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Neijiang?
November is not one of Neijiang’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Neijiang in November?
30 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (219 mm), the driest January (16 mm).
Does it snow in Neijiang in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Neijiang get in November?
About 65 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Neijiang in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
