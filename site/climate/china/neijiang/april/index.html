Neijiang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Neijiang in April?
In April, Neijiang averages a day high of 22.6 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 13.7 °C (57 °F), with 64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Neijiang?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Neijiang’s mild months (April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Neijiang in April?
64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (219 mm), the driest January (17 mm).
Does it snow in Neijiang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Neijiang get in April?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Neijiang in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
