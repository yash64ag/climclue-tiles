Gongguan at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Gongguan is 39th warmest (23.5 °C year average), 1370th sunniest (about 1,829 hours of sunshine a year) and 100th wettest (1,995 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Baisha, Guangxi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Gongguan is about as warm in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 79 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 13.2 °C more than its coolest, January (15.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.2 °C (54 °F), in January. The area lies about 44 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Gongguan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Gongguan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 44 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Gongguan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (21.800°N, 109.600°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-621-2896), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
