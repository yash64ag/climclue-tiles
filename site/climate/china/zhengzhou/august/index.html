How August compares in Zhengzhou

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Zhengzhou. Compared with July: 1.5 °C cooler, 20 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. September is 4.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Zhengzhou in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhengzhou in August?
In August, Zhengzhou averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.0 °C (72 °F), with 135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Zhengzhou?
August is not one of Zhengzhou’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhengzhou in August?
135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (155 mm), the driest December (9 mm).
Does it snow in Zhengzhou in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhengzhou get in August?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhengzhou in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
