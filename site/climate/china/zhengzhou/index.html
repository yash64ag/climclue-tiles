Zhengzhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Zhengzhou is 373rd warmest (15.2 °C year average), 277th sunniest (about 2,257 hours of sunshine a year) and 482nd wettest (632 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xinxiang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (50 km away), Zhengzhou is about as warm in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 16 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 26.7 °C more than its coolest, January (0.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 511 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.5 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 99% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Zhengzhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 511 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
