How November compares in Zhengzhou

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Zhengzhou. Compared with October: 7.2 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 23 hours less sunshine. December is 6.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Zhengzhou in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhengzhou in November?
In November, Zhengzhou averages a day high of 14.4 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 3.8 °C (39 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Zhengzhou?
November is not one of Zhengzhou’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhengzhou in November?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (155 mm), the driest December (9 mm).
Does it snow in Zhengzhou in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.3 cm (2.1 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhengzhou get in November?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhengzhou in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
