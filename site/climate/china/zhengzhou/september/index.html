Zhengzhou weather in September

Average September weather in Zhengzhou, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Zhengzhou averages 26.7 °C (80 °F) days and 16.4 °C (62 °F) nights, with 71 mm (2.8 in) of rain over the month and about 179 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Zhengzhou

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Zhengzhou. Compared with August: 4.4 °C cooler, 63 mm less precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. October is 5.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Zhengzhou in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhengzhou in September?
In September, Zhengzhou averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.4 °C (62 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Zhengzhou?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Zhengzhou’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhengzhou in September?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (154 mm), the driest December (9 mm).
Does it snow in Zhengzhou in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhengzhou get in September?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhengzhou in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
