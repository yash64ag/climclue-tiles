How April compares in Zhengzhou

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Zhengzhou. Compared with March: 7.0 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. May is 5.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Zhengzhou in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhengzhou in April?
In April, Zhengzhou averages a day high of 22.4 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 10.3 °C (51 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 196 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Zhengzhou?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Zhengzhou’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhengzhou in April?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (154 mm), the driest December (9 mm).
Does it snow in Zhengzhou in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhengzhou get in April?
About 196 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhengzhou in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
