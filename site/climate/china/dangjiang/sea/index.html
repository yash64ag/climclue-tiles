Dangjiang sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Guangxi · China

The sea off Dangjiang averages 30.1 °C (86 °F) in July, its warmest month, and 17.6 °C (64 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

Dangjiang sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Dangjiang?
In July, at about 30.1 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Dangjiang?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is January, at 17.6 °C (64 °F).
How warm is the sea in Dangjiang in January?
About 17.6 °C (64 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Dangjiang?
About 24.6 °C (76 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
