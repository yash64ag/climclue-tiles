Xinyu weather in March

Average March weather in Xinyu, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Xinyu averages 16.1 °C (61 °F) days and 8.7 °C (48 °F) nights, with 217 mm (8.6 in) of rain over the month and about 86 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Xinyu

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Xinyu. Compared with February: 3.8 °C warmer, 86 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. April is 6.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Xinyu in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xinyu in March?
In March, Xinyu averages a day high of 16.1 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 8.7 °C (48 °F), with 217 mm (8.6 in) of precipitation and about 86 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Xinyu?
March is not one of Xinyu’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xinyu in March?
217 mm (8.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (322 mm), the driest December (59 mm).
Does it snow in Xinyu in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xinyu get in March?
About 86 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xinyu in March?
