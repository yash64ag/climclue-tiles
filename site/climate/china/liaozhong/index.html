Liaozhong at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Liaozhong is 1799th warmest (9.4 °C year average), 289th sunniest (about 2,587 hours of sunshine a year) and 1426th wettest (681 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Liaoyang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Liaozhong is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 52 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.1 °C), averages 35.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-9.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 116 km from the sea.

Why Liaozhong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 116 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Liaozhong itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.506°N, 122.724°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.3 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-424-3027), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
