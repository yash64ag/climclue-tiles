Liaozhong at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Liaozhong is 572nd warmest (9.4 °C year average), 121st sunniest (about 2,587 hours of sunshine a year) and 454th wettest (675 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Liaoyang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (46 km away), Liaozhong is 0.2 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 66 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.1 °C), averages 35.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-10.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 116 km from the sea.

Why Liaozhong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 116 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
