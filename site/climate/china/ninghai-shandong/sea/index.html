Ninghai, Shandong sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Shandong · China

The sea off Ninghai, Shandong averages 24.3 °C (76 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 2.8 °C (37 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Ninghai, Shandong sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Ninghai, Shandong?
In August, at about 24.3 °C (76 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Ninghai, Shandong?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 2.8 °C (37 °F).
How warm is the sea in Ninghai, Shandong in February?
About 2.8 °C (37 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Ninghai, Shandong?
About 13.1 °C (56 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Ninghai, Shandong nearby?
In August: Dongcun (25.9 °C, 78 km), Longgang, Shandong (25.1 °C, 117 km) and Laizhou (27.0 °C, 149 km).
