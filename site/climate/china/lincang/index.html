Lincang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Lincang is 275th warmest (16.7 °C year average), 312th sunniest (about 2,133 hours of sunshine a year) and 299th wettest (1,187 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jianshui, the nearest covered town with different numbers (123 km away), Lincang is 1.4 °C warmer in June and 2.2 °C warmer in January, with 399 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (20.6 °C), averages 9.7 °C more than its coolest, January (10.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 762 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Lincang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 762 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 9.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
