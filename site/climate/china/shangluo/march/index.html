Shangluo in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shangluo in March?
In March, Shangluo averages a day high of 13.2 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 3.0 °C (37 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Shangluo?
March is not one of Shangluo’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shangluo in March?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (110 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Shangluo in March?
Yes, on average: about 0.9 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in March (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Shangluo get in March?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shangluo in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
