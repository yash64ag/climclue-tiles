How April compares in Shangluo

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Shangluo. Compared with March: 6.3 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. May is 4.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Shangluo in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shangluo in April?
In April, Shangluo averages a day high of 18.7 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 7.2 °C (45 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Shangluo?
April is not one of Shangluo’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shangluo in April?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (121 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Shangluo in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.3 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Shangluo get in April?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shangluo in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
