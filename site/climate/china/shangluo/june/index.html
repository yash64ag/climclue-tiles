How June compares in Shangluo

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Shangluo. Compared with May: 4.0 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. July is 2.0 °C warmer than June on average.

Shangluo in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shangluo in June?
In June, Shangluo averages a day high of 26.9 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 17.1 °C (63 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Shangluo?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Shangluo’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shangluo in June?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (121 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Shangluo in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shangluo get in June?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shangluo in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
