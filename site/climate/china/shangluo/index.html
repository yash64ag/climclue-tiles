Why Shangluo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 861 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Shangluo climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Shangluo?
July, with an average of 24.6 °C (76 °F), day highs around 28.2 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 19.9 °C (68 °F).
What is the coldest month in Shangluo?
January, with an average of -0.8 °C (31 °F), day highs around 4.1 °C (39 °F) and night lows around -5.9 °C (21 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Shangluo?
July, with 121 mm (4.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 636 mm (25.1 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Shangluo?
December, with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Shangluo?
August, with about 214 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Shangluo?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 91 cm (35.8 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 5 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Shangluo?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June and September.
What type of climate does Shangluo have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.2 × 11 km, that contains Shangluo’s GeoNames location (33.867°N, 109.931°E; ClimaClue area sq-501-2899), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 531,696 (GeoNames).
