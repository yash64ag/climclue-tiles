Xiangyang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Xiangyang is 311th warmest (16.5 °C year average), 502nd sunniest (about 1,864 hours of sunshine a year) and 372nd wettest (943 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zaoyang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (58 km away), Xiangyang is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 30 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 24.5 °C more than its coolest, January (3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 728 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Xiangyang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 728 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
