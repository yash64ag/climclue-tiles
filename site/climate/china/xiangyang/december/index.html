How December compares in Xiangyang

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Xiangyang. Compared with November: 5.8 °C cooler, 23 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Xiangyang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xiangyang in December?
In December, Xiangyang averages a day high of 10.5 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 0.4 °C (33 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 120 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Xiangyang?
December is not one of Xiangyang’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xiangyang in December?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (174 mm), the driest December (19 mm).
Does it snow in Xiangyang in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.8 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Xiangyang get in December?
About 120 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xiangyang in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
