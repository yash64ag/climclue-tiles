Zhuoshui at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Zhuoshui is 902nd warmest (16.9 °C year average), 1756th sunniest (about 1,341 hours of sunshine a year) and 704th wettest (1,255 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fengjia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (10 km away), Zhuoshui is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.1 °C), averages 21.7 °C more than its coolest, January (5.4 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 856 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Zhuoshui has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 856 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 21.7 °C. The 10 km area around Zhuoshui, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 15.7 °C a year, 1.2 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Zhuoshui itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (29.303°N, 108.770°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-546-2887), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
