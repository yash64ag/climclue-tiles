Changzhi, Shanxi at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Changzhi, Shanxi is 473rd warmest (13.1 °C year average), 281st sunniest (about 2,236 hours of sunshine a year) and 595th wettest (489 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yima, the nearest covered town with different numbers (54 km away), Changzhi, Shanxi is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 113 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.6 °C), averages 26.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 641 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Changzhi, Shanxi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 641 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
