Changzhi, Shanxi (35.2°N 111.7°E) in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changzhi, Shanxi (35.2°N 111.7°E) in February?
In February, Changzhi, Shanxi (35.2°N 111.7°E) averages a day high of 8.5 °C (47 °F) and a night low of -2.4 °C (28 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Changzhi, Shanxi (35.2°N 111.7°E)?
February is not one of Changzhi, Shanxi (35.2°N 111.7°E)’s mild months; those are April, May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
