Changzhi, Shanxi (35.2°N 111.7°E) in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changzhi, Shanxi (35.2°N 111.7°E) in February?
In February, Changzhi, Shanxi (35.2°N 111.7°E) averages a day high of 7.2 °C (45 °F) and a night low of -2.9 °C (27 °F), with 9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 143 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Changzhi, Shanxi (35.2°N 111.7°E)?
February is not one of Changzhi, Shanxi (35.2°N 111.7°E)’s mild months; those are May, June, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changzhi, Shanxi (35.2°N 111.7°E) in February?
9 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (107 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Changzhi, Shanxi (35.2°N 111.7°E) in February?
