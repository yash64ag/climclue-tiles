Jining, Shandong in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jining, Shandong in June?
In June, Jining, Shandong averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 19.7 °C (67 °F), with 96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 246 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Jining, Shandong?
June is not one of Jining, Shandong’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jining, Shandong in June?
96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (167 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Jining, Shandong in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jining, Shandong get in June?
About 246 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
