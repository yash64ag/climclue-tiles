Jining, Shandong in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jining, Shandong in March?
In March, Jining, Shandong averages a day high of 14.1 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 2.3 °C (36 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 199 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Jining, Shandong?
March is not one of Jining, Shandong’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jining, Shandong in March?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (168 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Jining, Shandong in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jining, Shandong get in March?
About 199 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jining, Shandong in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
