Jining, Shandong in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jining, Shandong in September?
In September, Jining, Shandong averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 16.3 °C (61 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Jining, Shandong?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Jining, Shandong’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jining, Shandong in September?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (167 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Jining, Shandong in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
