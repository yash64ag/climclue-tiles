Chaohu at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Chaohu is 323rd warmest (16.3 °C year average), 358th sunniest (about 2,024 hours of sunshine a year) and 290th wettest (1,208 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Wuhu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (60 km away), Chaohu is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 133 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 25.3 °C more than its coolest, January (3.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 316 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chaohu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chaohu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 316 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
