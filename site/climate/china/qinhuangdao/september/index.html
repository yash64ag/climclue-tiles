How September compares in Qinhuangdao

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Qinhuangdao. Compared with August: 4.3 °C cooler, 104 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. October is 7.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Qinhuangdao in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qinhuangdao in September?
In September, Qinhuangdao averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 14.7 °C (58 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Qinhuangdao?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Qinhuangdao’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qinhuangdao in September?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (171 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Qinhuangdao in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qinhuangdao get in September?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Qinhuangdao in September?
About 23.0 °C (73 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Qinhuangdao in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
