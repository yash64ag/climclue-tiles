Qinhuangdao in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qinhuangdao in February?
In February, Qinhuangdao averages a day high of 4.0 °C (39 °F) and a night low of -8.0 °C (18 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Qinhuangdao?
February is not one of Qinhuangdao’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qinhuangdao in February?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (171 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Qinhuangdao in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.8 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Qinhuangdao get in February?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Qinhuangdao in February?
About 0.2 °C (32 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Qinhuangdao in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
