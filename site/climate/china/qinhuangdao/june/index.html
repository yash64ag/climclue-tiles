Qinhuangdao weather in June

Average June weather in Qinhuangdao, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Qinhuangdao averages 27.2 °C (81 °F) days and 16.0 °C (61 °F) nights, with 89 mm (3.5 in) of rain over the month and about 267 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Qinhuangdao

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Qinhuangdao. Compared with May: 4.7 °C warmer, 36 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. July is 3.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Qinhuangdao in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qinhuangdao in June?
In June, Qinhuangdao averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 16.0 °C (61 °F), with 89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 267 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Qinhuangdao?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Qinhuangdao’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qinhuangdao in June?
89 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (171 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Qinhuangdao in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qinhuangdao get in June?
About 267 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Qinhuangdao in June?
About 18.9 °C (66 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (25.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
