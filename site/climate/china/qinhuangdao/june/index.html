Qinhuangdao in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qinhuangdao in June?
In June, Qinhuangdao averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 267 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Qinhuangdao?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Qinhuangdao’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qinhuangdao in June?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (175 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Qinhuangdao in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qinhuangdao get in June?
About 267 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qinhuangdao in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
