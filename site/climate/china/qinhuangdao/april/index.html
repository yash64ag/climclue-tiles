Qinhuangdao in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qinhuangdao in April?
In April, Qinhuangdao averages a day high of 15.1 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 5.8 °C (42 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 243 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Qinhuangdao?
April is not one of Qinhuangdao’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qinhuangdao in April?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (175 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Qinhuangdao in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.1 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Qinhuangdao get in April?
About 243 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qinhuangdao in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
