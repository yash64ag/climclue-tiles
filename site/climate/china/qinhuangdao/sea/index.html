Qinhuangdao sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Hebei · China

The sea off Qinhuangdao averages 25.6 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 0.2 °C (32 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Qinhuangdao sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Qinhuangdao?
In August, at about 25.6 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Qinhuangdao?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 0.2 °C (32 °F).
How warm is the sea in Qinhuangdao in February?
About 0.2 °C (32 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Qinhuangdao?
About 12.4 °C (54 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
