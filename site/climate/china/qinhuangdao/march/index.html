Qinhuangdao weather in March

Average March weather in Qinhuangdao, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Qinhuangdao averages 9.1 °C (48 °F) days and -2.8 °C (27 °F) nights, with 12 mm (0.5 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 235 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Qinhuangdao

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Qinhuangdao. Compared with February: 5.1 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. April is 7.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Qinhuangdao in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qinhuangdao in March?
In March, Qinhuangdao averages a day high of 9.1 °C (48 °F) and a night low of -2.8 °C (27 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Qinhuangdao?
March is not one of Qinhuangdao’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qinhuangdao in March?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (171 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Qinhuangdao in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.6 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Qinhuangdao get in March?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Qinhuangdao in March?
About 1.9 °C (35 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.6 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Qinhuangdao in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
