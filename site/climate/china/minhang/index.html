Why Minhang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 38 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.8 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Minhang climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Minhang?
July, with an average of 28.4 °C (83 °F), day highs around 31.6 °C (89 °F) and night lows around 25.2 °C (77 °F).
What is the coldest month in Minhang?
January, with an average of 4.5 °C (40 °F), day highs around 8.4 °C (47 °F) and night lows around 0.3 °C (33 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Minhang?
August, with 227 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,461 mm (57.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Minhang?
December, with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Minhang?
August, with about 238 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Minhang?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 8.6 cm (3.4 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Minhang?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.5 × 11 km, that contains Minhang’s GeoNames location (31.109°N, 121.375°E; ClimaClue area sq-528-3013), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 2,716,600 (GeoNames).
