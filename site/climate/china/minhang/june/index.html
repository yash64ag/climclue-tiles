Minhang in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Minhang in June?
In June, Minhang averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 225 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Minhang?
June is not one of Minhang’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Minhang in June?
225 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (227 mm), the driest December (49 mm).
Does it snow in Minhang in June?
