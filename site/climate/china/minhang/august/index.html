Minhang weather in August

Average August weather in Minhang, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In August, Minhang averages 31.4 °C (89 °F) days and 24.6 °C (76 °F) nights, with 227 mm (8.9 in) of rain over the month and about 238 hours of sunshine.

How August compares in Minhang

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the sunniest month in Minhang. Compared with July: 0.4 °C cooler, 39 mm more precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. September is 3.9 °C cooler than August on average.
