How December compares in Minhang

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Minhang. Compared with November: 6.1 °C cooler, 24 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. January is 2.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Minhang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Minhang in December?
In December, Minhang averages a day high of 11.2 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 2.5 °C (37 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Minhang?
December is not one of Minhang’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Minhang in December?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (227 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Minhang in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.7 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Minhang get in December?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Minhang in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
