How February compares in Minhang

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Minhang. Compared with January: 1.6 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. March is 3.6 °C warmer than February on average.

Minhang in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Minhang in February?
In February, Minhang averages a day high of 10.1 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 1.7 °C (35 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 114 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Minhang?
February is not one of Minhang’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Minhang in February?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (227 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Minhang in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.7 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Minhang get in February?
About 114 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Minhang in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
