Why Huocheng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 2,166 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.9 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

Huocheng climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Huocheng?
July, with an average of 24.8 °C (77 °F), day highs around 31.0 °C (88 °F) and night lows around 17.3 °C (63 °F).
What is the coldest month in Huocheng?
January, with an average of -6.2 °C (21 °F), day highs around 1.9 °C (35 °F) and night lows around -10.4 °C (13 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Huocheng?
May, with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 290 mm (11.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Huocheng?
August, with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Huocheng?
July, with about 322 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Huocheng?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 70 cm (27.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in December; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 5 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Huocheng?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August and September.
What type of climate does Huocheng have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.0 × 11 km, that contains Huocheng’s GeoNames location (44.053°N, 80.872°E; ClimaClue area sq-399-2608), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 360,000 (GeoNames).
