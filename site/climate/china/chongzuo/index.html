Chongzuo at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Chongzuo is 59th warmest (22.5 °C year average), 588th sunniest (about 1,645 hours of sunshine a year) and 158th wettest (1,529 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pingxiang, Guangxi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (70 km away), Chongzuo is 1.6 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 47 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 14.6 °C more than its coolest, January (13.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.9 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 126 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chongzuo, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Chongzuo has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 126 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
