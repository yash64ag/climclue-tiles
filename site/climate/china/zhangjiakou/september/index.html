How September compares in Zhangjiakou

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Zhangjiakou. Compared with August: 5.4 °C cooler, 30 mm less precipitation and about the same sunshine. October is 7.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Zhangjiakou in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhangjiakou in September?
In September, Zhangjiakou averages a day high of 23.3 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 10.9 °C (52 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 242 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Zhangjiakou?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Zhangjiakou’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhangjiakou in September?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (91 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Zhangjiakou in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhangjiakou get in September?
About 242 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhangjiakou in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
