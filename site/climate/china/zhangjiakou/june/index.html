How June compares in Zhangjiakou

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Zhangjiakou. Compared with May: 4.6 °C warmer, 29 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. July is 2.0 °C warmer than June on average.

Zhangjiakou in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhangjiakou in June?
In June, Zhangjiakou averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 285 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Zhangjiakou?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Zhangjiakou’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhangjiakou in June?
61 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (91 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Zhangjiakou in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhangjiakou get in June?
About 285 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhangjiakou in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
