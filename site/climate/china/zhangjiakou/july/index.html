How July compares in Zhangjiakou

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Zhangjiakou. Compared with June: 2.0 °C warmer, 30 mm more precipitation and 36 hours less sunshine. August is 1.8 °C cooler than July on average.

Zhangjiakou in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhangjiakou in July?
In July, Zhangjiakou averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 18.3 °C (65 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Zhangjiakou?
For mild weather, yes: July is one of Zhangjiakou’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhangjiakou in July?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (92 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Zhangjiakou in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhangjiakou get in July?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhangjiakou in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
