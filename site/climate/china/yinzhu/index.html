Yinzhu at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Yinzhu is 1409th warmest (13.5 °C year average), 558th sunniest (about 2,446 hours of sunshine a year) and 1386th wettest (707 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Licha, the nearest covered town with different numbers (26 km away), Yinzhu is 0.8 °C warmer in August and 1.3 °C warmer in January, with 40 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (26.0 °C), averages 26.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 18 km from the sea (coastal).

Why Yinzhu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Yinzhu itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (35.879°N, 119.975°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-481-2999), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
