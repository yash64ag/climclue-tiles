Dongxing sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Guangxi · China

The sea off Dongxing averages 30.0 °C (86 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 18.4 °C (65 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

Dongxing sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Dongxing?
In August, at about 30.0 °C (86 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Dongxing?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is January, at 18.4 °C (65 °F).
How warm is the sea in Dongxing in January?
About 18.4 °C (65 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Dongxing?
About 24.6 °C (76 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
