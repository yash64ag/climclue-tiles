Dongxing at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Dongxing is the wettest (3,051 mm a year). Compared with Fangchenggang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (47 km away), Dongxing is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 336 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 13.4 °C more than its coolest, January (15.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 12.9 °C (55 °F), in January. The area lies about 23 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dongxing, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dongxing has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
