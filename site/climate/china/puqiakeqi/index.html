Puqiakeqi at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Puqiakeqi is 1585th warmest (12.5 °C year average), 300th sunniest (about 2,583 hours of sunshine a year) and 2099th wettest (42 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Kuoyiqi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (4 km away), Puqiakeqi is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm more precipitation a year. The area lies about 1,830 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Puqiakeqi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 1,830 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Puqiakeqi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.317°N, 79.705°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-466-2597), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
