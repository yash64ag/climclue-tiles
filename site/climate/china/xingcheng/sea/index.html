Xingcheng sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Liaoning · China

The sea off Xingcheng averages 25.2 °C (77 °F) in August, its warmest month, and -0.6 °C (31 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Xingcheng sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Xingcheng?
In August, at about 25.2 °C (77 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Xingcheng?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at -0.6 °C (31 °F).
How warm is the sea in Xingcheng in February?
About -0.6 °C (31 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Xingcheng?
About 11.7 °C (53 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Xingcheng nearby?
In August: Shanhaiguan (25.8 °C, 107 km).
