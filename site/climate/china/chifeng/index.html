Chifeng at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Chifeng is 594th warmest (8.1 °C year average), 34th sunniest (about 2,792 hours of sunshine a year) and 637th wettest (384 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Wūdān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (74 km away), Chifeng is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 39 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.1 °C), averages 34.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-10.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 242 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 11 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 104% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Chifeng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 242 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 34.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
