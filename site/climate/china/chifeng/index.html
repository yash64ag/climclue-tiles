Chifeng at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Chifeng is 591st warmest (8.4 °C year average), 34th sunniest (about 2,792 hours of sunshine a year) and 639th wettest (369 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Wūdān, the nearest covered town with different numbers (74 km away), Chifeng is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 30 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.4 °C), averages 34.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-10.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 242 km from the sea.

Why Chifeng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 242 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 34.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-417-2989), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
