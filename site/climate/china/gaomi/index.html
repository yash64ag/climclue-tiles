Gaomi at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Gaomi is 469th warmest (13.2 °C year average), 200th sunniest (about 2,492 hours of sunshine a year) and 446th wettest (686 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jiaozhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Gaomi is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 47 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.1 °C), averages 27.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 42 km from the sea.

Why Gaomi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 42 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
