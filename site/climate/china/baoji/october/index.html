How October compares in Baoji

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Baoji. Compared with September: 5.7 °C cooler, 57 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. November is 6.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Baoji in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baoji in October?
In October, Baoji averages a day high of 18.9 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 9.2 °C (49 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Baoji?
October is not one of Baoji’s mild months; those are April, May and June, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baoji in October?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (139 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Baoji in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.8 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Baoji get in October?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baoji in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
