How August compares in Baoji

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the sunniest month in Baoji. Compared with July: 1.3 °C cooler, 21 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. September is 4.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Baoji in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baoji in August?
In August, Baoji averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Baoji?
August is not one of Baoji’s mild months; those are April, May and June, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baoji in August?
139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (139 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Baoji in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baoji get in August?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baoji in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
