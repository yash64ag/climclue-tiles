How December compares in Baoji

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Baoji. Compared with November: 5.5 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. January is 1.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Baoji in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baoji in December?
In December, Baoji averages a day high of 7.1 °C (45 °F) and a night low of -1.8 °C (29 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 131 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Baoji?
December is not one of Baoji’s mild months; those are April, May and June, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baoji in December?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (136 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Baoji in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.3 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Baoji get in December?
About 131 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baoji in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
