How November compares in Baoji

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Baoji. Compared with October: 6.5 °C cooler, 35 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. December is 5.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Baoji in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baoji in November?
In November, Baoji averages a day high of 13.0 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 2.3 °C (36 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 124 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Baoji?
November is not one of Baoji’s mild months; those are April, May and June, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baoji in November?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (136 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Baoji in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baoji get in November?
About 124 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baoji in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
