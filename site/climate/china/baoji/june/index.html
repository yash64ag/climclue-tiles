How June compares in Baoji

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Baoji. Compared with May: 3.9 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. July is 2.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Baoji in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baoji in June?
In June, Baoji averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 17.9 °C (64 °F), with 80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Baoji?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Baoji’s mild months (April, May and June), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baoji in June?
80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (139 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Baoji in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baoji get in June?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baoji in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
