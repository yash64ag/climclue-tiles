How January compares in Tianshui

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Tianshui. Compared with December: 1.5 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. February is 3.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Tianshui in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tianshui in January?
In January, Tianshui averages a day high of 3.3 °C (38 °F) and a night low of -6.1 °C (21 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Tianshui?
January is not one of Tianshui’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tianshui in January?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (92 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Tianshui in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.7 cm (1.9 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Tianshui get in January?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tianshui in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
