How September compares in Tianshui

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Tianshui. Compared with August: 4.5 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 72 hours less sunshine. October is 5.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Tianshui in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tianshui in September?
In September, Tianshui averages a day high of 22.8 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Tianshui?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Tianshui’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tianshui in September?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (92 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Tianshui in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tianshui get in September?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tianshui in September?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
