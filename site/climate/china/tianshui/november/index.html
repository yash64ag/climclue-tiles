How November compares in Tianshui

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Tianshui. Compared with October: 6.2 °C cooler, 32 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. December is 5.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Tianshui in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tianshui in November?
In November, Tianshui averages a day high of 10.6 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 0.9 °C (34 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Tianshui?
November is not one of Tianshui’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tianshui in November?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (92 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Tianshui in November?
Yes, on average: about 1.9 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in November (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Tianshui get in November?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tianshui in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
