How August compares in Tianshui

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Tianshui. Compared with July: 1.1 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. September is 4.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Tianshui in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tianshui in August?
In August, Tianshui averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 16.2 °C (61 °F), with 107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Tianshui?
August is not one of Tianshui’s mild months; those are May, June, July and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tianshui in August?
107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (107 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Tianshui in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Tianshui get in August?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tianshui in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
