How December compares in Tianshui

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Tianshui. Compared with November: 5.0 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. January is 1.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Tianshui in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Tianshui in December?
In December, Tianshui averages a day high of 5.6 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -3.8 °C (25 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Tianshui?
December is not one of Tianshui’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Tianshui in December?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (92 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Tianshui in December?
Yes, on average: about 3.3 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Tianshui get in December?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Tianshui in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
