Tianshui at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Tianshui is 529th warmest (11.0 °C year average), 355th sunniest (about 2,026 hours of sunshine a year) and 533rd wettest (577 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Gangu Chengguanzhen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Tianshui is 0.6 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 184 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.6 °C), averages 24.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 1,155 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Tianshui has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 1,155 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
