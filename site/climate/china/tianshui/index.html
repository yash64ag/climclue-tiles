Why Tianshui has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 1,155 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Tianshui climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Tianshui?
July, with an average of 22.6 °C (73 °F), day highs around 26.6 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 17.1 °C (63 °F).
What is the coldest month in Tianshui?
January, with an average of -2.0 °C (28 °F), day highs around 2.0 °C (36 °F) and night lows around -7.4 °C (19 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Tianshui?
August, with 107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 577 mm (22.7 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Tianshui?
December, with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Tianshui?
June, with about 210 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Tianshui?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 79 cm (31.1 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 3 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Tianshui?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July and September.
