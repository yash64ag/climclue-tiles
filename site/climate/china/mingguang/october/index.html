How October compares in Mingguang

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Mingguang. Compared with September: 5.6 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and 3 hours more sunshine. November is 6.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Mingguang in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mingguang in October?
In October, Mingguang averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 12.8 °C (55 °F), with 60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation and about 177 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Mingguang?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Mingguang’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mingguang in October?
60 mm (2.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (234 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Mingguang in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mingguang get in October?
About 177 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mingguang in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
