How December compares in Mingguang

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Mingguang. Compared with November: 6.0 °C cooler, 28 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. January is 2.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Mingguang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mingguang in December?
In December, Mingguang averages a day high of 9.2 °C (49 °F) and a night low of -0.2 °C (32 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Mingguang?
December is not one of Mingguang’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mingguang in December?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (234 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Mingguang in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.6 cm (1.0 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Mingguang get in December?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mingguang in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
