How January compares in Mingguang, Anhui

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Mingguang, Anhui. Compared with December: 2.2 °C cooler, 10 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. February is 2.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Mingguang, Anhui in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mingguang, Anhui in January?
In January, Mingguang, Anhui averages a day high of 6.4 °C (44 °F) and a night low of -2.2 °C (28 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Mingguang, Anhui?
January is not one of Mingguang, Anhui’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mingguang, Anhui in January?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (234 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Mingguang, Anhui in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.4 cm (3.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Mingguang, Anhui get in January?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mingguang, Anhui in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
