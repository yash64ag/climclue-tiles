Mingguang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Mingguang is 355th warmest (15.6 °C year average), 326th sunniest (about 2,114 hours of sunshine a year) and 348th wettest (1,041 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bengbu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (59 km away), Mingguang is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 43 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 25.8 °C more than its coolest, January (2.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 263 km from the sea.

Why Mingguang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 263 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
