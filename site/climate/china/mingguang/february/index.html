How February compares in Mingguang

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Mingguang. Compared with January: 2.5 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. March is 4.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Mingguang in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mingguang in February?
In February, Mingguang averages a day high of 9.2 °C (49 °F) and a night low of -0.4 °C (31 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Mingguang?
February is not one of Mingguang’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mingguang in February?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (234 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Mingguang in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.2 cm (3.2 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Mingguang get in February?
About 132 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mingguang in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
