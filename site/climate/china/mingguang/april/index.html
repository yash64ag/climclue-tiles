How April compares in Mingguang

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Mingguang. Compared with March: 6.5 °C warmer, 8 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. May is 5.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Mingguang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mingguang in April?
In April, Mingguang averages a day high of 20.6 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 10.6 °C (51 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Mingguang?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Mingguang’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mingguang in April?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (234 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Mingguang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mingguang get in April?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mingguang in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
