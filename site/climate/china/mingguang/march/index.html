How March compares in Mingguang

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Mingguang. Compared with February: 4.7 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. April is 6.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Mingguang in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mingguang in March?
In March, Mingguang averages a day high of 14.1 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 4.1 °C (39 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Mingguang?
March is not one of Mingguang’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mingguang in March?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (234 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Mingguang in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.1 cm (1.2 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Mingguang get in March?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mingguang in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
