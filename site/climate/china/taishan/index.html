Taishan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Taishan is 36th warmest (22.9 °C year average), 466th sunniest (about 1,910 hours of sunshine a year) and 14th wettest (2,289 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Changsha, Guangdong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Taishan is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 146 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 14.0 °C more than its coolest, January (14.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.7 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 59 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Taishan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Taishan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 59 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
