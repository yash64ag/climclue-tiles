How November compares in Xiangtan

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Xiangtan. Compared with October: 5.8 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. December is 5.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Xiangtan in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xiangtan in November?
In November, Xiangtan averages a day high of 17.8 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 9.4 °C (49 °F), with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 112 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Xiangtan?
No month in Xiangtan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xiangtan in November?
100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (264 mm), the driest December (59 mm).
Does it snow in Xiangtan in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xiangtan get in November?
About 112 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xiangtan in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
