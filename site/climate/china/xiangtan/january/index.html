How January compares in Xiangtan

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Xiangtan. Compared with December: 2.2 °C cooler, 38 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. February is 2.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Xiangtan in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xiangtan in January?
In January, Xiangtan averages a day high of 9.6 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 2.3 °C (36 °F), with 97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 81 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Xiangtan?
No month in Xiangtan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xiangtan in January?
97 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (264 mm), the driest December (59 mm).
Does it snow in Xiangtan in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.9 cm (2.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Xiangtan get in January?
About 81 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xiangtan in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
