Xiangtan weather in July

Average July weather in Xiangtan, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In July, Xiangtan averages 34.3 °C (94 °F) days and 26.1 °C (79 °F) nights, with 156 mm (6.1 in) of rain over the month and about 243 hours of sunshine.

How July compares in Xiangtan

July is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Xiangtan. Compared with June: 3.5 °C warmer, 106 mm less precipitation and 93 hours more sunshine. August is 0.9 °C cooler than July on average.
