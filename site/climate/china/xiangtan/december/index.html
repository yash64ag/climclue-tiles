How December compares in Xiangtan

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Xiangtan. Compared with November: 5.5 °C cooler, 41 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. January is 2.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Xiangtan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xiangtan in December?
In December, Xiangtan averages a day high of 12.4 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 4.0 °C (39 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 104 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Xiangtan?
No month in Xiangtan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xiangtan in December?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (262 mm), the driest December (58 mm).
Does it snow in Xiangtan in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xiangtan get in December?
About 104 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xiangtan in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
