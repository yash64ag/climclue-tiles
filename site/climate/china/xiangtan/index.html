Xiangtan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Xiangtan is 160th warmest (18.4 °C year average), 598th sunniest (about 1,593 hours of sunshine a year) and 82nd wettest (1,810 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zhuzhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Xiangtan is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 148 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.1 °C), averages 24.1 °C more than its coolest, January (6.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 594 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Xiangtan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Xiangtan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 594 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.1 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
