Xiangtan weather in April

Average April weather in Xiangtan, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Xiangtan averages 22.2 °C (72 °F) days and 14.5 °C (58 °F) nights, with 226 mm (8.9 in) of rain over the month and about 100 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Xiangtan

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Xiangtan. Compared with March: 6.2 °C warmer, 53 mm more precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. May is 4.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Xiangtan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xiangtan in April?
In April, Xiangtan averages a day high of 22.2 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 14.5 °C (58 °F), with 226 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation and about 100 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Xiangtan?
No month in Xiangtan is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xiangtan in April?
226 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (262 mm), the driest December (58 mm).
Does it snow in Xiangtan in April?
