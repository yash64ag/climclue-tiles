Nangong at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Nangong is 1325th warmest (14.4 °C year average), 425th sunniest (about 2,529 hours of sunshine a year) and 1807th wettest (488 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jizhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Nangong is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 15 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 29.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 244 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nangong, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

For mild weather, the best months to visit Nangong are April, May, September and October.

Why Nangong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 244 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nangong itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.355°N, 115.389°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-466-2953), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
