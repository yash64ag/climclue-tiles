Xuanhua at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Xuanhua is 1741st warmest (10.1 °C year average), 75th sunniest (about 2,801 hours of sunshine a year) and 1949th wettest (322 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zhangjiakou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Xuanhua is 0.8 °C warmer in July and 1.5 °C warmer in January, with 51 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.3 °C), averages 32.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-7.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 298 km from the sea.

Why Xuanhua has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 298 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 32.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Xuanhua itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (40.612°N, 115.065°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-433-2950), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
