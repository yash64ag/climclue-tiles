Heihe at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Heihe is 685th warmest (1.1 °C year average), 235th sunniest (about 2,408 hours of sunshine a year) and 564th wettest (544 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Aihui, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Heihe is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 12 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.4 °C), averages 44.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-22.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 736 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 1 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 99% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Heihe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 736 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 44.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
