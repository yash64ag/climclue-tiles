Shuangyashan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shuangyashan in March?
In March, Shuangyashan averages a day high of 0.9 °C (34 °F) and a night low of -9.5 °C (15 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Shuangyashan?
March is not one of Shuangyashan’s mild months; those are June, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shuangyashan in March?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (137 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Shuangyashan in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 24 cm (9.3 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 17 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Shuangyashan get in March?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shuangyashan in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
