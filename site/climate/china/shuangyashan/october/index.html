Shuangyashan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shuangyashan in October?
In October, Shuangyashan averages a day high of 10.1 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 1.2 °C (34 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Shuangyashan?
October is not one of Shuangyashan’s mild months; those are June, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shuangyashan in October?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (137 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Shuangyashan in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 11 cm (4.2 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Shuangyashan get in October?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shuangyashan in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
