Shuangyashan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Shuangyashan is 664th warmest (3.9 °C year average), 231st sunniest (about 2,413 hours of sunshine a year) and 531st wettest (578 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Baoshan, Heilongjiang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Shuangyashan is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 16 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.3 °C), averages 40.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-18.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 382 km from the sea.

Why Shuangyashan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 382 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 40.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
