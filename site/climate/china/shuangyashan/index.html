Shuangyashan weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Shuangyashan, China (Heilongjiang), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Shuangyashan is warmest in July, with 27.6 °C (82 °F) days and 17.2 °C nights, and coolest in January, with -12.6 °C (9 °F) days and -23.7 °C nights. Most rain falls around August (138 mm); February is driest (6 mm).

Why Shuangyashan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 382 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 40.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Shuangyashan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (46.677°N, 131.133°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.6 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-373-3111), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
