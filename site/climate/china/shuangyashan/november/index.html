How November compares in Shuangyashan

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Shuangyashan. Compared with October: 11.7 °C cooler, 21 mm less precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. December is 9.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Shuangyashan in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shuangyashan in November?
In November, Shuangyashan averages a day high of -2.0 °C (28 °F) and a night low of -10.1 °C (14 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Shuangyashan?
November is not one of Shuangyashan’s mild months; those are June, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shuangyashan in November?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (137 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Shuangyashan in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 15 cm (6.0 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Shuangyashan get in November?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shuangyashan in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
