Yibin in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yibin in August?
In August, Yibin averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 188 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Yibin?
August is not one of Yibin’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yibin in August?
188 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (230 mm), the driest January (22 mm).
Does it snow in Yibin in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yibin get in August?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yibin in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
