June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Yibin. Compared with May: 2.0 °C warmer, 61 mm more precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Yibin in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yibin in June?
In June, Yibin averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation and about 130 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Yibin?
June is not one of Yibin’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yibin in June?
171 mm (6.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (224 mm), the driest January (21 mm).
Does it snow in Yibin in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yibin get in June?
About 130 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yibin in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
