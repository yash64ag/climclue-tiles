How November compares in Yibin

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Yibin. Compared with October: 4.2 °C cooler, 30 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. December is 5.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Yibin in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yibin in November?
In November, Yibin averages a day high of 17.5 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 84 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Yibin?
November is not one of Yibin’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yibin in November?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (224 mm), the driest January (21 mm).
Does it snow in Yibin in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yibin get in November?
About 84 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yibin in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
