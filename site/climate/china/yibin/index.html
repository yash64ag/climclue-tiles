Yibin at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Yibin is 174th warmest (18.2 °C year average), 626th sunniest (about 1,464 hours of sunshine a year) and 327th wettest (1,111 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shuifu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Yibin is 0.7 °C warmer in July and 0.7 °C warmer in January, with 27 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 19.3 °C more than its coolest, January (8.1 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 885 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 109% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Yibin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 885 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
