How January compares in Yibin

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Yibin. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. February is 2.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Yibin in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yibin in January?
In January, Yibin averages a day high of 10.8 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 6.2 °C (43 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 89 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Yibin?
January is not one of Yibin’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yibin in January?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (224 mm), the driest January (21 mm).
Does it snow in Yibin in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yibin get in January?
About 89 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yibin in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
