How July compares in Yibin

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Yibin. Compared with June: 2.2 °C warmer, 53 mm more precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. August is 0.4 °C cooler than July on average.

Yibin in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yibin in July?
In July, Yibin averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 230 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Yibin?
July is not one of Yibin’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yibin in July?
230 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (230 mm), the driest January (22 mm).
Does it snow in Yibin in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yibin get in July?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yibin in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
