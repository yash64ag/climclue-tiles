Yibin in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yibin in October?
In October, Yibin averages a day high of 20.6 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 15.6 °C (60 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 90 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Yibin?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Yibin’s mild months (April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yibin in October?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (230 mm), the driest January (22 mm).
Does it snow in Yibin in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yibin get in October?
About 90 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yibin in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
