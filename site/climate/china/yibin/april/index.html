Yibin in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yibin in April?
In April, Yibin averages a day high of 23.5 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Yibin?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Yibin’s mild months (April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yibin in April?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (224 mm), the driest January (21 mm).
Does it snow in Yibin in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yibin get in April?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yibin in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
