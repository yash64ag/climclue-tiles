Yibin in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yibin in February?
In February, Yibin averages a day high of 13.0 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 7.1 °C (45 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 88 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Yibin?
February is not one of Yibin’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yibin in February?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (224 mm), the driest January (21 mm).
Does it snow in Yibin in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yibin get in February?
About 88 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yibin in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
