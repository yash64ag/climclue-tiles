How June compares in Nanchang

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Nanchang. Compared with May: 3.1 °C warmer, 77 mm more precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. July is 3.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Nanchang in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanchang in June?
In June, Nanchang averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.1 °C (74 °F), with 327 mm (12.9 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Nanchang?
June is not one of Nanchang’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanchang in June?
327 mm (12.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (327 mm), the driest December (49 mm).
Does it snow in Nanchang in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanchang get in June?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanchang in June?
