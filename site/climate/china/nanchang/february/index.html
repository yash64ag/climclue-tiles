How February compares in Nanchang

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Nanchang. Compared with January: 2.4 °C warmer, 28 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. March is 3.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Nanchang in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanchang in February?
In February, Nanchang averages a day high of 11.9 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 4.1 °C (39 °F), with 119 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 83 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Nanchang?
February is not one of Nanchang’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanchang in February?
119 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (327 mm), the driest December (49 mm).
Does it snow in Nanchang in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.7 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanchang get in February?
About 83 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanchang in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
