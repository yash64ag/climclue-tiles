Nanchang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Nanchang is 154th warmest (18.5 °C year average), 521st sunniest (about 1,839 hours of sunshine a year) and 86th wettest (1,801 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jinfeng, the nearest covered town with different numbers (88 km away), Nanchang is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 358 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (30.1 °C), averages 24.2 °C more than its coolest, January (5.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 468 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 11 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 112% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Nanchang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 468 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
