How August compares in Nanchang

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Nanchang. Compared with July: 0.7 °C cooler, 20 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. September is 3.9 °C cooler than August on average.

Nanchang in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanchang in August?
In August, Nanchang averages a day high of 32.5 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 25.8 °C (78 °F), with 137 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 257 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Nanchang?
August is not one of Nanchang’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanchang in August?
137 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (327 mm), the driest December (49 mm).
Does it snow in Nanchang in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanchang get in August?
About 257 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanchang in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, a rain jacket or umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
