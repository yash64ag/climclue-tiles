How November compares in Nanchang

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Nanchang. Compared with October: 6.2 °C cooler, 19 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. December is 5.8 °C cooler than November on average.

Nanchang in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanchang in November?
In November, Nanchang averages a day high of 17.8 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 10.1 °C (50 °F), with 87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 141 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Nanchang?
November is not one of Nanchang’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanchang in November?
87 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (325 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Nanchang in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanchang get in November?
About 141 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanchang in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
