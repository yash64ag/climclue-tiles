Nanchang weather in January

Average January weather in Nanchang, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Nanchang averages 9.4 °C (49 °F) days and 2.4 °C (36 °F) nights, with 90 mm (3.6 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 102 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Nanchang

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Nanchang. Compared with December: 2.1 °C cooler, 42 mm more precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. February is 2.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Nanchang in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanchang in January?
In January, Nanchang averages a day high of 9.4 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 2.4 °C (36 °F), with 90 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 102 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Nanchang?
January is not one of Nanchang’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanchang in January?
90 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (325 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Nanchang in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.0 cm (2.4 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanchang get in January?
About 102 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanchang in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
