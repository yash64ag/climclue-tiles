How April compares in Nanchang

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Nanchang. Compared with March: 6.3 °C warmer, 49 mm more precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. May is 5.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Nanchang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanchang in April?
In April, Nanchang averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 243 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation and about 118 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Nanchang?
April is not one of Nanchang’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanchang in April?
243 mm (9.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (325 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Nanchang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanchang get in April?
About 118 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanchang in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
