Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) weather in May

Average May weather in Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E), China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).
