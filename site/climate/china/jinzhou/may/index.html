Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) in May?
In May, Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) averages a day high of 23.1 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 12.1 °C (54 °F), with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 280 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E)?
