What is the weather like in Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) in January?
In January, Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E) averages a day high of -1.6 °C (29 °F) and a night low of -12.8 °C (9 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 188 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
