Jinzhou, Liaoning in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinzhou, Liaoning in October?
In October, Jinzhou, Liaoning averages a day high of 16.4 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 6.6 °C (44 °F), with 29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Jinzhou, Liaoning?
October is not one of Jinzhou, Liaoning’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinzhou, Liaoning in October?
29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (167 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Jinzhou, Liaoning in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.7 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Jinzhou, Liaoning get in October?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jinzhou, Liaoning in October?
