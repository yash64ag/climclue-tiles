Jinzhou, Liaoning in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinzhou, Liaoning in December?
In December, Jinzhou, Liaoning averages a day high of -0.4 °C (31 °F) and a night low of -9.1 °C (16 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jinzhou, Liaoning?
December is not one of Jinzhou, Liaoning’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinzhou, Liaoning in December?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (167 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Jinzhou, Liaoning in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.4 cm (2.1 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.6 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Jinzhou, Liaoning get in December?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jinzhou, Liaoning in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
