Shizuishan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Shizuishan is 539th warmest (10.6 °C year average), 22nd sunniest (about 2,896 hours of sunshine a year) and 666th wettest (187 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dawukou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Shizuishan is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.8 °C), averages 30.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-5.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 978 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Shizuishan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 978 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
