Shizuishan in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shizuishan in August?
In August, Shizuishan averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 17.8 °C (64 °F), with 41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Shizuishan?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Shizuishan’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shizuishan in August?
41 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (46 mm), the driest December (1 mm).
Does it snow in Shizuishan in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shizuishan get in August?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shizuishan in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
