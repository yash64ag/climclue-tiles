Shizuishan in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shizuishan in June?
In June, Shizuishan averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 17.0 °C (63 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 284 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Shizuishan?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Shizuishan’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shizuishan in June?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (46 mm), the driest December (1 mm).
Does it snow in Shizuishan in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shizuishan get in June?
About 284 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shizuishan in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
