Shizuishan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shizuishan in April?
In April, Shizuishan averages a day high of 18.4 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 4.8 °C (41 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 247 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Shizuishan?
April is not one of Shizuishan’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shizuishan in April?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (46 mm), the driest December (1 mm).
Does it snow in Shizuishan in April?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.6 cm (0.6 in) of fresh snow in April (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Shizuishan get in April?
About 247 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shizuishan in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
