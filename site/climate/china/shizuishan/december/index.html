Shizuishan weather in December

Average December weather in Shizuishan, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Shizuishan averages 2.1 °C (36 °F) days and -10.1 °C (14 °F) nights, with 1 mm (0.1 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 210 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Shizuishan

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Shizuishan. Compared with November: 6.7 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. January is 1.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Shizuishan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shizuishan in December?
In December, Shizuishan averages a day high of 2.1 °C (36 °F) and a night low of -10.1 °C (14 °F), with 1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 210 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Shizuishan?
December is not one of Shizuishan’s mild months; those are May, June, July, August and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shizuishan in December?
1 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (45 mm), the driest December (1 mm).
Does it snow in Shizuishan in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.2 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Shizuishan get in December?
About 210 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shizuishan in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
