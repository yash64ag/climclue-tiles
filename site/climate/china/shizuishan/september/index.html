Shizuishan in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shizuishan in September?
In September, Shizuishan averages a day high of 23.1 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Shizuishan?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Shizuishan’s mild months (May, June, July, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shizuishan in September?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (46 mm), the driest December (1 mm).
Does it snow in Shizuishan in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shizuishan get in September?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shizuishan in September?
