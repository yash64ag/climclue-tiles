Xindi at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Xindi is 201st warmest (17.7 °C year average), 542nd sunniest (about 1,797 hours of sunshine a year) and 177th wettest (1,478 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Puqi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Xindi is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 164 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.4 °C), averages 24.4 °C more than its coolest, January (5.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.2 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 733 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Xindi, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Xindi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 733 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
