Xinyang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Xinyang is 354th warmest (15.6 °C year average), 354th sunniest (about 2,028 hours of sunshine a year) and 313th wettest (1,150 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Guangshui, the nearest covered town with different numbers (56 km away), Xinyang is 0.7 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 124 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 25.2 °C more than its coolest, January (2.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 568 km from the sea.

Why Xinyang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 568 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
