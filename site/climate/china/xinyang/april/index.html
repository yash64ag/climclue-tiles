Xinyang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xinyang in April?
In April, Xinyang averages a day high of 21.2 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Xinyang?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Xinyang’s mild months (April, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xinyang in April?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (227 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Xinyang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xinyang get in April?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xinyang in April?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
