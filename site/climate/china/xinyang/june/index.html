How June compares in Xinyang

Xinyang in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xinyang in June?
In June, Xinyang averages a day high of 29.2 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 155 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Xinyang?
June is not one of Xinyang’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xinyang in June?
155 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (227 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Xinyang in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xinyang get in June?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xinyang in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
