How July compares in Xinyang

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Xinyang. Compared with June: 2.2 °C warmer, 72 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. August is 1.0 °C cooler than July on average.

Xinyang in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xinyang in July?
In July, Xinyang averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 227 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Xinyang?
July is not one of Xinyang’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xinyang in July?
227 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (227 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Xinyang in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xinyang get in July?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xinyang in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
