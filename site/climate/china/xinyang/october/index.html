Xinyang in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xinyang in October?
In October, Xinyang averages a day high of 21.6 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 12.4 °C (54 °F), with 80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Xinyang?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Xinyang’s mild months (April, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xinyang in October?
80 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (227 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Xinyang in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xinyang get in October?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xinyang in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
