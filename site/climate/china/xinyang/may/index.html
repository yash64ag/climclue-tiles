How May compares in Xinyang

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Xinyang. Compared with April: 4.9 °C warmer, 42 mm more precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. June is 3.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Xinyang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xinyang in May?
In May, Xinyang averages a day high of 26.5 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 17.0 °C (63 °F), with 120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Xinyang?
May is not one of Xinyang’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xinyang in May?
120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (224 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Xinyang in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xinyang get in May?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xinyang in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
