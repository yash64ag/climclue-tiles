How September compares in Xinyang

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Xinyang. Compared with August: 4.4 °C cooler, 84 mm less precipitation and 63 hours less sunshine. October is 5.3 °C cooler than September on average.

Xinyang in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xinyang in September?
In September, Xinyang averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 18.0 °C (64 °F), with 92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Xinyang?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Xinyang’s mild months (April, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xinyang in September?
92 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (224 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Xinyang in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xinyang get in September?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xinyang in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
