How July compares in Anshan

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Anshan. Compared with June: 2.3 °C warmer, 95 mm more precipitation and 39 hours less sunshine. August is 0.9 °C cooler than July on average.

Anshan in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Anshan in July?
In July, Anshan averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 185 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Anshan?
July is not one of Anshan’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Anshan in July?
185 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (185 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Anshan in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Anshan get in July?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Anshan in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
