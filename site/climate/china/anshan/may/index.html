How May compares in Anshan

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Anshan. Compared with April: 6.6 °C warmer, 25 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. June is 4.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Anshan in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Anshan in May?
In May, Anshan averages a day high of 23.7 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 12.4 °C (54 °F), with 65 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 271 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Anshan?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Anshan’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Anshan in May?
65 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (187 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Anshan in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Anshan get in May?
About 271 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Anshan in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
