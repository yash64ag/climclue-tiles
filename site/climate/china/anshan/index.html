Anshan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Anshan is 568th warmest (9.6 °C year average), 127th sunniest (about 2,577 hours of sunshine a year) and 415th wettest (754 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jiupu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Anshan is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 4 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.1 °C), averages 34.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-9.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 94 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Anshan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Anshan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 94 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 34.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Anshan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.124°N, 122.990°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-428-3029), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
