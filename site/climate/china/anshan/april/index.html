How April compares in Anshan

April is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Anshan. Compared with March: 8.9 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. May is 6.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Anshan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Anshan in April?
In April, Anshan averages a day high of 17.1 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 5.8 °C (42 °F), with 39 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Anshan?
April is not one of Anshan’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Anshan in April?
39 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (187 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Anshan in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Anshan get in April?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Anshan in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
