Xianning weather in May

Average May weather in Xianning, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Xianning averages 27.0 °C (81 °F) days and 18.3 °C (65 °F) nights, with 193 mm (7.6 in) of rain over the month and about 163 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Xianning

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Xianning. Compared with April: 5.0 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. June is 3.4 °C warmer than May on average.

Xianning in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xianning in May?
In May, Xianning averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 18.3 °C (65 °F), with 193 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Xianning?
May is not one of Xianning’s mild months; those are September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xianning in May?
193 mm (7.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (250 mm), the driest December (42 mm).
Does it snow in Xianning in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xianning get in May?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xianning in May?
