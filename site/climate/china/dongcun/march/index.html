How March compares in Dongcun

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Dongcun. Compared with February: 4.1 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 47 hours more sunshine. April is 5.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Dongcun in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dongcun in March?
In March, Dongcun averages a day high of 7.9 °C (46 °F) and a night low of 2.0 °C (36 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Dongcun?
March is not one of Dongcun’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dongcun in March?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (164 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Dongcun in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.1 cm (1.6 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Dongcun get in March?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dongcun in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
