How August compares in Dongcun

August is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Dongcun. Compared with July: 0.7 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. September is 3.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Dongcun in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dongcun in August?
In August, Dongcun averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 164 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Dongcun?
August is not one of Dongcun’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dongcun in August?
164 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (164 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Dongcun in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dongcun get in August?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dongcun in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
