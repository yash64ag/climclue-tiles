How June compares in Dongcun

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Dongcun. Compared with May: 4.6 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. July is 3.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Dongcun in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dongcun in June?
In June, Dongcun averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 240 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Dongcun?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Dongcun’s mild months (May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dongcun in June?
74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (163 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Dongcun in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dongcun get in June?
About 240 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Dongcun in June?
About 19.4 °C (67 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (25.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Dongcun in June?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
