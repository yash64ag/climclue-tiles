How September compares in Dongcun

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Dongcun. Compared with August: 3.8 °C cooler, 95 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. October is 5.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Dongcun in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dongcun in September?
In September, Dongcun averages a day high of 25.8 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 17.1 °C (63 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 220 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Dongcun?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Dongcun’s mild months (May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dongcun in September?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (163 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Dongcun in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dongcun get in September?
About 220 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Dongcun in September?
About 23.8 °C (75 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (25.9 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Dongcun in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
