Dongcun in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dongcun in October?
In October, Dongcun averages a day high of 18.7 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 13.0 °C (55 °F), with 29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Dongcun?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Dongcun’s mild months (May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dongcun in October?
29 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (164 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Dongcun in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dongcun get in October?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dongcun in October?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
