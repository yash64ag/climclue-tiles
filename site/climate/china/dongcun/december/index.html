How December compares in Dongcun

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Dongcun. Compared with November: 6.5 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. January is 2.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Dongcun in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dongcun in December?
In December, Dongcun averages a day high of 4.9 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -0.5 °C (31 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Dongcun?
December is not one of Dongcun’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dongcun in December?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (164 mm), the driest January (9 mm).
Does it snow in Dongcun in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.4 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Dongcun get in December?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dongcun in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
