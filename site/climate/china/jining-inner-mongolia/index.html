Jining, Inner Mongolia at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Jining, Inner Mongolia is 1985th warmest (5.0 °C year average), 31st sunniest (about 2,914 hours of sunshine a year) and 1936th wettest (357 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ulanqab, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Jining, Inner Mongolia is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 9 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.6 °C), averages 33.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-12.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 460 km from the sea.

Why Jining, Inner Mongolia has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 460 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 33.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Jining itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.028°N, 113.106°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-429-2931), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
