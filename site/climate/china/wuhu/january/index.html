How January compares in Wuhu

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Wuhu. Compared with December: 2.1 °C cooler, 28 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. February is 2.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Wuhu in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuhu in January?
In January, Wuhu averages a day high of 7.6 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -0.4 °C (31 °F), with 67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Wuhu?
January is not one of Wuhu’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuhu in January?
67 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (234 mm), the driest December (40 mm).
Does it snow in Wuhu in January?
Yes, on average: about 13 cm (5.2 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuhu get in January?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuhu in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
