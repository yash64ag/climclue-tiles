Wuhu at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Wuhu is 279th warmest (16.7 °C year average), 373rd sunniest (about 2,004 hours of sunshine a year) and 241st wettest (1,341 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ma’anshan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Wuhu is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 95 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.8 °C), averages 25.1 °C more than its coolest, January (3.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 267 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Wuhu, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Wuhu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 267 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
