How October compares in Wuhu

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Wuhu. Compared with September: 5.5 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. November is 6.5 °C cooler than October on average.

Wuhu in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuhu in October?
In October, Wuhu averages a day high of 22.7 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Wuhu?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Wuhu’s mild months (September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuhu in October?
70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (234 mm), the driest December (40 mm).
Does it snow in Wuhu in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuhu get in October?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuhu in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
