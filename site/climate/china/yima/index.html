Yima at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Yima is 471st warmest (13.2 °C year average), 303rd sunniest (about 2,169 hours of sunshine a year) and 508th wettest (602 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Luoyang, Henan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (51 km away), Yima is 2.1 °C cooler in July and 1.8 °C cooler in January, with 20 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.4 °C), averages 26.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 668 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yima, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yima has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 668 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
