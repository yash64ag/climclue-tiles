How December compares in Liupanshui

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Liupanshui. Compared with November: 4.3 °C cooler, 21 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Liupanshui in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Liupanshui in December?
In December, Liupanshui averages a day high of 9.9 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 1.1 °C (34 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 117 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Liupanshui?
No month in Liupanshui is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Liupanshui in December?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (246 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Liupanshui in December?
Yes, on average: about 3.5 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Liupanshui get in December?
About 117 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
