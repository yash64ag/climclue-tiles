Liupanshui weather in May

Average May weather in Liupanshui, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Liupanshui averages 21.6 °C (71 °F) days and 12.3 °C (54 °F) nights, with 146 mm (5.8 in) of rain over the month and about 162 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Liupanshui

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Liupanshui. Compared with April: 3.0 °C warmer, 81 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. June is 2.2 °C warmer than May on average.
