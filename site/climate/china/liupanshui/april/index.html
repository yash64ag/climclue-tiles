Liupanshui in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Liupanshui in April?
In April, Liupanshui averages a day high of 17.7 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 7.9 °C (46 °F), with 70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Liupanshui?
No month in Liupanshui is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Liupanshui in April?
70 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (265 mm), the driest December (18 mm).
Does it snow in Liupanshui in April?

How many hours of sunshine does Liupanshui get in April?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
