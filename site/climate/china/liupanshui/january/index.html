How January compares in Liupanshui

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Liupanshui. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, 6 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. February is 2.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Liupanshui in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Liupanshui in January?
In January, Liupanshui averages a day high of 7.4 °C (45 °F) and a night low of -0.5 °C (31 °F), with 24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 123 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Liupanshui?
No month in Liupanshui is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Liupanshui in January?
24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (265 mm), the driest December (18 mm).
Does it snow in Liupanshui in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.0 cm (3.1 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Liupanshui get in January?
About 123 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Liupanshui in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
