Liupanshui in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Liupanshui in March?
In March, Liupanshui averages a day high of 14.3 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 4.0 °C (39 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Liupanshui?
No month in Liupanshui is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Liupanshui in March?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (265 mm), the driest December (18 mm).
Does it snow in Liupanshui in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.6 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Liupanshui get in March?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Liupanshui in March?
