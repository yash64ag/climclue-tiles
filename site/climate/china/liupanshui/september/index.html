Liupanshui in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Liupanshui in September?
In September, Liupanshui averages a day high of 19.7 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 12.5 °C (55 °F), with 144 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation and about 122 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Liupanshui?
No month in Liupanshui is mild by the rule used here, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Liupanshui in September?
144 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (265 mm), the driest December (18 mm).
Does it snow in Liupanshui in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Liupanshui get in September?
About 122 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
