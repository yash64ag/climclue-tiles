Liupanshui at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Liupanshui is 499th warmest (12.5 °C year average), 586th sunniest (about 1,649 hours of sunshine a year) and 245th wettest (1,327 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Liuzhi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (71 km away), Liupanshui is 2.2 °C cooler in July and 1.8 °C cooler in January, with 116 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (19.9 °C), averages 16.3 °C more than its coolest, January (3.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 654 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Liupanshui has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 654 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
