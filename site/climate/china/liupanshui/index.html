Why Liupanshui has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 654 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Liupanshui climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Liupanshui?
July, with an average of 19.9 °C (68 °F), day highs around 22.7 °C (73 °F) and night lows around 15.5 °C (60 °F).
What is the coldest month in Liupanshui?
January, with an average of 3.5 °C (38 °F), day highs around 7.4 °C (45 °F) and night lows around -0.5 °C (31 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Liupanshui?
June, with 265 mm (10.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,327 mm (52.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Liupanshui?
December, with 18 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Liupanshui?
August, with about 169 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Liupanshui?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 17 cm (6.5 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Liupanshui?
No month meets the definition used here (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation).

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10.0 × 11 km, that contains Liupanshui’s GeoNames location (26.594°N, 104.833°E; ClimaClue area sq-574-2848), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,320,825 (GeoNames).
