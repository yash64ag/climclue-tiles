Liangping at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Liangping is 224th warmest (17.5 °C year average), 650th sunniest (about 1,344 hours of sunshine a year) and 226th wettest (1,375 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Guixi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (54 km away), Liangping is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 119 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 20.5 °C more than its coolest, January (6.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.1 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 1,005 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Liangping, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Liangping has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,005 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.5 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
