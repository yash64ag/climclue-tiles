How December compares in Shenzhen

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Shenzhen. Compared with November: 4.2 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Shenzhen in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shenzhen in December?
In December, Shenzhen averages a day high of 20.2 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 12.7 °C (55 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Shenzhen?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Shenzhen’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shenzhen in December?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (408 mm), the driest January (25 mm).
Does it snow in Shenzhen in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shenzhen get in December?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shenzhen in December?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
