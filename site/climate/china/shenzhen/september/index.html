How September compares in Shenzhen

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Shenzhen. Compared with August: 1.0 °C cooler, 96 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. October is 2.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Shenzhen in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shenzhen in September?
In September, Shenzhen averages a day high of 29.4 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 24.3 °C (76 °F), with 308 mm (12.1 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Shenzhen?
September is not one of Shenzhen’s mild months; those are January, February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shenzhen in September?
308 mm (12.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (433 mm), the driest January (27 mm).
Does it snow in Shenzhen in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shenzhen get in September?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shenzhen in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
