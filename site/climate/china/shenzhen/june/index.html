Shenzhen weather in June

Average June weather in Shenzhen, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Shenzhen averages 31.0 °C (88 °F) days and 24.9 °C (77 °F) nights, with 408 mm (16.1 in) of rain over the month and about 162 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Shenzhen

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Shenzhen. Compared with May: 1.9 °C warmer, 127 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. July is 0.9 °C warmer than June on average.

Shenzhen in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shenzhen in June?
In June, Shenzhen averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 24.9 °C (77 °F), with 408 mm (16.1 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Shenzhen?
June is not one of Shenzhen’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shenzhen in June?
408 mm (16.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (408 mm), the driest January (25 mm).
Does it snow in Shenzhen in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shenzhen get in June?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Shenzhen in June?
About 27.5 °C (82 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (28.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Shenzhen in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
