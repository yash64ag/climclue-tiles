Shenzhen at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Shenzhen is 44th warmest (22.8 °C year average), 435th sunniest (about 1,940 hours of sunshine a year) and 13th wettest (2,314 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bao'an Centre, the nearest covered town with different numbers (16 km away), Shenzhen is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 99 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 13.2 °C more than its coolest, January (15.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.5 °C (53 °F), in January. The area lies about 30 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shenzhen, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Shenzhen

For mild weather, the best months to visit Shenzhen are January, February, March, October, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): January, February, March, October, November and December.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (28.6 °C), August (28.4 °C) and June (27.6 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): January (27 mm), December (29 mm) and November (43 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (228 h), August (206 h) and October (194 h).

Why Shenzhen has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 30 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
