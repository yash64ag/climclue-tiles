How March compares in Shenzhen

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Shenzhen. Compared with February: 2.6 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. April is 3.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Shenzhen in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shenzhen in March?
In March, Shenzhen averages a day high of 22.5 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 16.0 °C (61 °F), with 68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 100 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Shenzhen?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Shenzhen’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shenzhen in March?
68 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (408 mm), the driest January (25 mm).
Does it snow in Shenzhen in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shenzhen get in March?
About 100 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Shenzhen in March?
About 19.2 °C (67 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is August (28.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Shenzhen in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
