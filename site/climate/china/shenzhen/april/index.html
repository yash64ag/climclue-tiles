How April compares in Shenzhen

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Shenzhen. Compared with March: 3.7 °C warmer, 87 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. May is 3.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Shenzhen in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shenzhen in April?
In April, Shenzhen averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 19.7 °C (67 °F), with 156 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation and about 110 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Shenzhen?
April is not one of Shenzhen’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shenzhen in April?
156 mm (6.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (408 mm), the driest January (25 mm).
Does it snow in Shenzhen in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shenzhen get in April?
About 110 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Shenzhen in April?
About 22.1 °C (72 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (28.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Shenzhen in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
