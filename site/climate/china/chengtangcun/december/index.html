Chengtangcun in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chengtangcun in December?
In December, Chengtangcun averages a day high of 6.9 °C (44 °F) and a night low of -2.4 °C (28 °F), with 11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Chengtangcun?
December is not one of Chengtangcun’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chengtangcun in December?
11 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (199 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Chengtangcun in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.3 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Chengtangcun get in December?
About 168 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
