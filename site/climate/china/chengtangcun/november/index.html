Chengtangcun in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chengtangcun in November?
In November, Chengtangcun averages a day high of 13.4 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 3.5 °C (38 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Chengtangcun?
November is not one of Chengtangcun’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chengtangcun in November?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (199 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Chengtangcun in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.4 cm (0.6 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Chengtangcun get in November?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chengtangcun in November?
