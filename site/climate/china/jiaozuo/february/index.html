How February compares in Jiaozuo

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Jiaozuo. Compared with January: 3.4 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. March is 5.6 °C warmer than February on average.

Jiaozuo in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiaozuo in February?
In February, Jiaozuo averages a day high of 9.5 °C (49 °F) and a night low of -2.0 °C (28 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Jiaozuo?
February is not one of Jiaozuo’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiaozuo in February?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (148 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Jiaozuo in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.1 cm (2.4 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiaozuo get in February?
About 145 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiaozuo in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
