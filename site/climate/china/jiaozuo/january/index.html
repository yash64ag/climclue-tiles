How January compares in Jiaozuo

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Jiaozuo. Compared with December: 1.8 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. February is 3.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Jiaozuo in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiaozuo in January?
In January, Jiaozuo averages a day high of 5.8 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -4.9 °C (23 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Jiaozuo?
January is not one of Jiaozuo’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiaozuo in January?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (143 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Jiaozuo in January?
Yes, on average: about 5.1 cm (2.0 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiaozuo get in January?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiaozuo in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
