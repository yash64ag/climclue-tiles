How March compares in Jiaozuo

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Jiaozuo. Compared with February: 5.6 °C warmer, 11 mm more precipitation and 37 hours more sunshine. April is 7.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Jiaozuo in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiaozuo in March?
In March, Jiaozuo averages a day high of 15.0 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 2.6 °C (37 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Jiaozuo?
March is not one of Jiaozuo’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiaozuo in March?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (148 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Jiaozuo in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.0 cm (2.0 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiaozuo get in March?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiaozuo in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
