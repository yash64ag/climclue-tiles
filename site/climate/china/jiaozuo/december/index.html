How December compares in Jiaozuo

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Jiaozuo. Compared with November: 6.1 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. January is 1.8 °C cooler than December on average.

Jiaozuo in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiaozuo in December?
In December, Jiaozuo averages a day high of 7.6 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -3.1 °C (26 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jiaozuo?
December is not one of Jiaozuo’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiaozuo in December?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (143 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Jiaozuo in December?
Yes, on average: about 3.0 cm (1.2 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiaozuo get in December?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiaozuo in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
