Fuding at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Fuding is 164th warmest (18.4 °C year average), 554th sunniest (about 1,771 hours of sunshine a year) and 106th wettest (1,714 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Songcheng, the nearest covered town with different numbers (54 km away), Fuding is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 1.6 °C cooler in January, with 29 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.3 °C), averages 20.0 °C more than its coolest, January (8.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.6 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 35 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Fuding, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Fuding has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 35 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
