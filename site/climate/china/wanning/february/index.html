How February compares in Wanning

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Wanning. Compared with January: 1.0 °C warmer, 10 mm more precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. March is 1.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Wanning in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wanning in February?
In February, Wanning averages a day high of 22.7 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Wanning?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Wanning’s mild months (January, February, March and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wanning in February?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is October (505 mm), the driest January (34 mm).
Does it snow in Wanning in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wanning get in February?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wanning in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
