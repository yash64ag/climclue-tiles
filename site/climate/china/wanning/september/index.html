How September compares in Wanning

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Wanning. Compared with August: 0.9 °C cooler, 142 mm more precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. October is 1.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Wanning in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wanning in September?
In September, Wanning averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 25.7 °C (78 °F), with 403 mm (15.9 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Wanning?
September is not one of Wanning’s mild months; those are January, February, March and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wanning in September?
403 mm (15.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is October (505 mm), the driest January (34 mm).
Does it snow in Wanning in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wanning get in September?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wanning in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
