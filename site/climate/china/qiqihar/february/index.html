How February compares in Qiqihar

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Qiqihar. Compared with January: 5.3 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. March is 9.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Qiqihar in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qiqihar in February?
In February, Qiqihar averages a day high of -6.4 °C (20 °F) and a night low of -19.0 °C (-2 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 199 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Qiqihar?
February is not one of Qiqihar’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qiqihar in February?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (145 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Qiqihar in February?
Yes, on average: about 2.8 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Qiqihar get in February?
About 199 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qiqihar in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
