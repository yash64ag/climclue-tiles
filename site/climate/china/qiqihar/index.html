Qiqihar at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Qiqihar is 661st warmest (4.2 °C year average), 56th sunniest (about 2,728 hours of sunshine a year) and 609th wettest (466 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Hulan Ergi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Qiqihar is about as warm in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.3 °C), averages 42.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-18.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 752 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Qiqihar has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 752 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 42.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
