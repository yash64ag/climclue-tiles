Qiqihar in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qiqihar in March?
In March, Qiqihar averages a day high of 2.2 °C (36 °F) and a night low of -9.4 °C (15 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Qiqihar?
March is not one of Qiqihar’s mild months; those are May, June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qiqihar in March?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (145 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Qiqihar in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 10 cm (4.0 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.5 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Qiqihar get in March?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qiqihar in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
