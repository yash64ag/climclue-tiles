How November compares in Qiqihar

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Qiqihar. Compared with October: 12.2 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 38 hours less sunshine. December is 9.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Qiqihar in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qiqihar in November?
In November, Qiqihar averages a day high of -2.0 °C (28 °F) and a night low of -10.7 °C (13 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Qiqihar?
November is not one of Qiqihar’s mild months; those are May, June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qiqihar in November?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (145 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Qiqihar in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.2 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Qiqihar get in November?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qiqihar in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
