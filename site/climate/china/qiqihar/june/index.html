Qiqihar weather in June

Average June weather in Qiqihar, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Qiqihar averages 27.5 °C (82 °F) days and 15.1 °C (59 °F) nights, with 77 mm (3.0 in) of rain over the month and about 278 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Qiqihar

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Qiqihar. Compared with May: 6.0 °C warmer, 42 mm more precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. July is 2.0 °C warmer than June on average.
