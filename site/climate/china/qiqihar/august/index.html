Qiqihar in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qiqihar in August?
In August, Qiqihar averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 17.1 °C (63 °F), with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Qiqihar?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Qiqihar’s mild months (May, June and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qiqihar in August?
100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (145 mm), the driest January (3 mm).
Does it snow in Qiqihar in August?

How many hours of sunshine does Qiqihar get in August?
