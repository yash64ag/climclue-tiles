How August compares in Shantou

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Shantou. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, 27 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. September is 1.0 °C cooler than August on average.

Shantou in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shantou in August?
In August, Shantou averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 25.7 °C (78 °F), with 263 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Shantou?
August is not one of Shantou’s mild months; those are February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shantou in August?
263 mm (10.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (273 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Shantou in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shantou get in August?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shantou in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
