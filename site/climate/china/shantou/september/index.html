How September compares in Shantou

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Shantou. Compared with August: 1.3 °C cooler, 97 mm less precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. October is 2.8 °C cooler than September on average.

Shantou in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shantou in September?
In September, Shantou averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 159 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Shantou?
September is not one of Shantou’s mild months; those are February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shantou in September?
159 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (267 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Shantou in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shantou get in September?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Shantou in September?
About 27.1 °C (81 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (27.1 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Shantou in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
