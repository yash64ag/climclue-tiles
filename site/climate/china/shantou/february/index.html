How February compares in Shantou

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Shantou. Compared with January: 0.6 °C warmer, 39 mm more precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. March is 2.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Shantou in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shantou in February?
In February, Shantou averages a day high of 18.0 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 12.4 °C (54 °F), with 65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 100 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Shantou?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Shantou’s mild months (February, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shantou in February?
65 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (273 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Shantou in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shantou get in February?
About 100 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shantou in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
