How January compares in Shantou

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Shantou. Compared with December: 1.6 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. February is 0.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Shantou in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shantou in January?
In January, Shantou averages a day high of 17.5 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 11.3 °C (52 °F), with 26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Shantou?
January is not one of Shantou’s mild months; those are February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shantou in January?
26 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (273 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Shantou in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shantou get in January?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shantou in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
