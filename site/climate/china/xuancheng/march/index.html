How March compares in Xuancheng

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Xuancheng. Compared with February: 4.3 °C warmer, 56 mm more precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. April is 6.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Xuancheng in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xuancheng in March?
In March, Xuancheng averages a day high of 14.4 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 6.2 °C (43 °F), with 139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 129 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Xuancheng?
March is not one of Xuancheng’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xuancheng in March?
139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (255 mm), the driest December (44 mm).
Does it snow in Xuancheng in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xuancheng get in March?
About 129 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xuancheng in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
