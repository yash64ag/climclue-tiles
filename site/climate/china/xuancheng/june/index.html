How June compares in Xuancheng

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Xuancheng. Compared with May: 3.4 °C warmer, 103 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. July is 3.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Xuancheng in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xuancheng in June?
In June, Xuancheng averages a day high of 28.7 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 251 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Xuancheng?
June is not one of Xuancheng’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xuancheng in June?
251 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (251 mm), the driest December (43 mm).
Does it snow in Xuancheng in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xuancheng get in June?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xuancheng in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
