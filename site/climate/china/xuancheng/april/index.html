Xuancheng in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xuancheng in April?
In April, Xuancheng averages a day high of 21.0 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 12.1 °C (54 °F), with 124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Xuancheng?
April is not one of Xuancheng’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xuancheng in April?
124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (255 mm), the driest December (44 mm).
Does it snow in Xuancheng in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xuancheng get in April?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xuancheng in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
