How February compares in Xuancheng

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Xuancheng. Compared with January: 2.3 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. March is 4.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Xuancheng in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xuancheng in February?
In February, Xuancheng averages a day high of 10.1 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 1.1 °C (34 °F), with 82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 112 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Xuancheng?
February is not one of Xuancheng’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xuancheng in February?
82 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (251 mm), the driest December (43 mm).
Does it snow in Xuancheng in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.3 cm (3.3 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Xuancheng get in February?
About 112 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xuancheng in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
