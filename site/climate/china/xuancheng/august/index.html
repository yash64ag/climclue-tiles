How August compares in Xuancheng

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Xuancheng. Compared with July: 0.8 °C cooler, 41 mm less precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. September is 4.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Xuancheng in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xuancheng in August?
In August, Xuancheng averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 24.4 °C (76 °F), with 162 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Xuancheng?
August is not one of Xuancheng’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xuancheng in August?
162 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (251 mm), the driest December (43 mm).
Does it snow in Xuancheng in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xuancheng get in August?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xuancheng in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
