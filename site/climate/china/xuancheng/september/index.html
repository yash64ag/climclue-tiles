How September compares in Xuancheng

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Xuancheng. Compared with August: 4.3 °C cooler, 68 mm less precipitation and 70 hours less sunshine. October is 5.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Xuancheng in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xuancheng in September?
In September, Xuancheng averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 20.0 °C (68 °F), with 96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Xuancheng?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Xuancheng’s mild months (September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xuancheng in September?
96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (255 mm), the driest December (44 mm).
Does it snow in Xuancheng in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xuancheng get in September?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xuancheng in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
