Xuancheng weather in October

Average October weather in Xuancheng, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Xuancheng averages 22.6 °C (73 °F) days and 13.9 °C (57 °F) nights, with 76 mm (3.0 in) of rain over the month and about 165 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Xuancheng

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Xuancheng. Compared with September: 5.6 °C cooler, 20 mm less precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. November is 6.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Xuancheng in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xuancheng in October?
In October, Xuancheng averages a day high of 22.6 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 13.9 °C (57 °F), with 76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Xuancheng?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Xuancheng’s mild months (September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xuancheng in October?
76 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (255 mm), the driest December (44 mm).
Does it snow in Xuancheng in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xuancheng get in October?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xuancheng in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
