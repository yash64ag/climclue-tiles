Zhaoqing at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Zhaoqing is 54th warmest (22.5 °C year average), 531st sunniest (about 1,814 hours of sunshine a year) and 104th wettest (1,718 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yunfu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (45 km away), Zhaoqing is 0.7 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 187 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.1 °C), averages 15.2 °C more than its coolest, January (13.9 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.8 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 135 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Zhaoqing, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Zhaoqing has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 135 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
