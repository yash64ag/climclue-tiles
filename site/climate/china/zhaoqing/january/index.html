How January compares in Zhaoqing

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Zhaoqing. Compared with December: 1.6 °C cooler, 16 mm more precipitation and 35 hours less sunshine. February is 1.3 °C warmer than January on average.

Zhaoqing in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhaoqing in January?
In January, Zhaoqing averages a day high of 17.7 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 9.8 °C (50 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 125 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Zhaoqing?
January is not one of Zhaoqing’s mild months; those are February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhaoqing in January?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (281 mm), the driest December (29 mm).
Does it snow in Zhaoqing in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhaoqing get in January?
About 125 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhaoqing in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
