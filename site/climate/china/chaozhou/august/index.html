How August compares in Chaozhou

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Chaozhou. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 37 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. September is 1.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Chaozhou in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chaozhou in August?
In August, Chaozhou averages a day high of 30.9 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 25.1 °C (77 °F), with 316 mm (12.4 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Chaozhou?
August is not one of Chaozhou’s mild months; those are February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chaozhou in August?
316 mm (12.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (316 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Chaozhou in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chaozhou get in August?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chaozhou in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
