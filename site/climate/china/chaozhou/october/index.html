How October compares in Chaozhou

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Chaozhou. Compared with September: 3.0 °C cooler, 146 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. November is 4.1 °C cooler than October on average.

Chaozhou in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chaozhou in October?
In October, Chaozhou averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.7 °C (69 °F), with 37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Chaozhou?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Chaozhou’s mild months (February, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chaozhou in October?
37 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (314 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Chaozhou in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chaozhou get in October?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chaozhou in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
