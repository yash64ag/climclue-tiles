Chaozhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Chaozhou is 72nd warmest (22.2 °C year average), 379th sunniest (about 1,986 hours of sunshine a year) and 84th wettest (1,809 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Anbu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Chaozhou is about as warm in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 75 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.8 °C), averages 14.5 °C more than its coolest, January (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.4 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 45 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Chaozhou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Chaozhou

For mild weather, the best months to visit Chaozhou are February, October, November and December.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): February, October, November and December.

Why Chaozhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 45 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
