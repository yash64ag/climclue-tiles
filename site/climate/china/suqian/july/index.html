How July compares in Suqian

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Suqian. Compared with June: 2.5 °C warmer, 134 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. August is 0.8 °C cooler than July on average.

Suqian in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suqian in July?
In July, Suqian averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 268 mm (10.6 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Suqian?
July is not one of Suqian’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suqian in July?
268 mm (10.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (268 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Suqian in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suqian get in July?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suqian in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
