Suqian weather in April

Average April weather in Suqian, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Suqian averages 20.4 °C (69 °F) days and 9.6 °C (49 °F) nights, with 42 mm (1.6 in) of rain over the month and about 192 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Suqian

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Suqian. Compared with March: 6.6 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. May is 5.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Suqian in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suqian in April?
In April, Suqian averages a day high of 20.4 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 9.6 °C (49 °F), with 42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Suqian?
April is not one of Suqian’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suqian in April?
42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (268 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Suqian in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suqian get in April?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suqian in April?
