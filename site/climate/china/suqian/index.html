Suqian at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Suqian is 393rd warmest (14.8 °C year average), 285th sunniest (about 2,225 hours of sunshine a year) and 368th wettest (955 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Suicheng, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Suqian is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 2 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 26.6 °C more than its coolest, January (0.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 136 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Suqian, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Suqian has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 136 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
