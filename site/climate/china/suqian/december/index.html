How December compares in Suqian

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Suqian. Compared with November: 6.2 °C cooler, 18 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. January is 2.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Suqian in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suqian in December?
In December, Suqian averages a day high of 8.0 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -1.7 °C (29 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Suqian?
December is not one of Suqian’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suqian in December?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (266 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Suqian in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.2 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Suqian get in December?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suqian in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
