How November compares in Hebi

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Hebi. Compared with October: 7.9 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. December is 6.4 °C cooler than November on average.

Hebi in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hebi in November?
In November, Hebi averages a day high of 13.8 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 2.3 °C (36 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Hebi?
November is not one of Hebi’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hebi in November?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (167 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Hebi in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.1 cm (2.0 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Hebi get in November?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hebi in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
