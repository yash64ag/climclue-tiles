How June compares in Hebi

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Hebi. Compared with May: 4.7 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and about the same sunshine. July is 1.0 °C warmer than June on average.

Hebi in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hebi in June?
In June, Hebi averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 250 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Hebi?
June is not one of Hebi’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hebi in June?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (167 mm), the driest December (6 mm).
Does it snow in Hebi in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hebi get in June?
About 250 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hebi in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
