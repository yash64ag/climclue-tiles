Wuhai at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Wuhai is 571st warmest (9.4 °C year average), 8th sunniest (about 2,955 hours of sunshine a year) and 671st wettest (183 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Wuda, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Wuhai is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 27 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.9 °C), averages 33.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-8.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 935 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Wuhai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 935 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 33.2 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
