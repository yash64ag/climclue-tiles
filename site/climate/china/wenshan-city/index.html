Wenshan City at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Wenshan City is 148th warmest (18.6 °C year average), 597th sunniest (about 1,594 hours of sunshine a year) and 365th wettest (967 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mengzi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (89 km away), Wenshan City is about as warm in June and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 7 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (23.6 °C), averages 11.8 °C more than its coolest, January (11.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.1 °C (45 °F), in December. The area lies about 402 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Wenshan City, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Wenshan City has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 402 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
