Pingyin at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Pingyin is 443rd warmest (13.9 °C year average), 198th sunniest (about 2,493 hours of sunshine a year) and 443rd wettest (703 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Feicheng, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Pingyin is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 49 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.9 °C), averages 28.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 262 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Pingyin, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Pingyin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 262 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
