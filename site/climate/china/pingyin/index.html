Pingyin at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Pingyin is 1345th warmest (14.2 °C year average), 492nd sunniest (about 2,493 hours of sunshine a year) and 1410th wettest (689 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Feicheng, Shandong (36.2°N 116.8°E), the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Pingyin is 0.5 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 46 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.1 °C), averages 28.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 262 km from the sea.

Why Pingyin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 262 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Pingyin itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.283°N, 116.445°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-477-2964), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
