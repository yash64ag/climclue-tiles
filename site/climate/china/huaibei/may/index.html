How May compares in Huaibei

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Huaibei. Compared with April: 5.7 °C warmer, 31 mm more precipitation and 34 hours more sunshine. June is 4.4 °C warmer than May on average.

Huaibei in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huaibei in May?
In May, Huaibei averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 15.3 °C (60 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 226 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Huaibei?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Huaibei’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huaibei in May?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (257 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Huaibei in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huaibei get in May?
About 226 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huaibei in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
