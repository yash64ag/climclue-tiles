How November compares in Huaibei

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Huaibei. Compared with October: 7.1 °C cooler, 17 mm less precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. December is 6.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Huaibei in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huaibei in November?
In November, Huaibei averages a day high of 14.7 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 4.0 °C (39 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Huaibei?
November is not one of Huaibei’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huaibei in November?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (244 mm), the driest December (16 mm).
Does it snow in Huaibei in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.4 cm (0.6 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Huaibei get in November?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huaibei in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
