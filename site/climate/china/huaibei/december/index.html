How December compares in Huaibei

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Huaibei. Compared with November: 6.1 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Huaibei in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huaibei in December?
In December, Huaibei averages a day high of 8.2 °C (47 °F) and a night low of -1.9 °C (29 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 159 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Huaibei?
December is not one of Huaibei’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huaibei in December?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (257 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Huaibei in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.8 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Huaibei get in December?
About 159 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huaibei in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
