How April compares in Huaibei

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Huaibei. Compared with March: 6.7 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. May is 5.7 °C warmer than April on average.

Huaibei in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huaibei in April?
In April, Huaibei averages a day high of 21.0 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 9.8 °C (50 °F), with 42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Huaibei?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Huaibei’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huaibei in April?
42 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (257 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Huaibei in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huaibei get in April?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huaibei in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
