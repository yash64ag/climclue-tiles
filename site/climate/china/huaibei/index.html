Huaibei at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Huaibei is 1161st warmest (14.9 °C year average), 803rd sunniest (about 2,229 hours of sunshine a year) and 1153rd wettest (907 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Suixi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Huaibei is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 28 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.3 °C), averages 26.4 °C more than its coolest, January (0.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 250 km from the sea.

Why Huaibei has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 250 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Huaibei itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (33.974°N, 116.792°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-500-2967), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
