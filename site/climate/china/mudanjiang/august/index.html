Mudanjiang in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mudanjiang in August?
In August, Mudanjiang averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Mudanjiang?
August is not one of Mudanjiang’s mild months; those are June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mudanjiang in August?
107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (126 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Mudanjiang in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mudanjiang get in August?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mudanjiang in August?
