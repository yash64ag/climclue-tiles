Mudanjiang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mudanjiang in May?
In May, Mudanjiang averages a day high of 19.5 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 7.8 °C (46 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 241 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Mudanjiang?
May is not one of Mudanjiang’s mild months; those are June, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mudanjiang in May?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (128 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Mudanjiang in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mudanjiang get in May?
About 241 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mudanjiang in May?
