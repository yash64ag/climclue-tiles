Mudanjiang in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mudanjiang in July?
In July, Mudanjiang averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 17.5 °C (64 °F), with 128 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Mudanjiang?
July is not one of Mudanjiang’s mild months; those are June, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
