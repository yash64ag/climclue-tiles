Mudanjiang in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mudanjiang in October?
In October, Mudanjiang averages a day high of 11.7 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 1.7 °C (35 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 200 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Mudanjiang?
October is not one of Mudanjiang’s mild months; those are June, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mudanjiang in October?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (128 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Mudanjiang in October?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.4 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in October (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Mudanjiang get in October?
About 200 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mudanjiang in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
