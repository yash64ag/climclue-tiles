Mudanjiang in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mudanjiang in February?
In February, Mudanjiang averages a day high of -4.6 °C (24 °F) and a night low of -17.5 °C (1 °F), with 6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Mudanjiang?
February is not one of Mudanjiang’s mild months; those are June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mudanjiang in February?
6 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (126 mm), the driest February (6 mm).
Does it snow in Mudanjiang in February?
Yes, on average: about 5.7 cm (2.2 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 4.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Mudanjiang get in February?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mudanjiang in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
