Yuanping at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Yuanping is 559th warmest (9.8 °C year average), 86th sunniest (about 2,669 hours of sunshine a year) and 633rd wettest (387 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xinzhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Yuanping is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.8 °C cooler in January, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.8 °C), averages 30.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-6.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 425 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 94% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Yuanping has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 425 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
