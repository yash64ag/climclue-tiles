Aoxi at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Aoxi is 492nd warmest (18.5 °C year average), 1428th sunniest (about 1,785 hours of sunshine a year) and 132nd wettest (1,877 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fenggang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (40 km away), Aoxi is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 137 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.8 °C), averages 23.6 °C more than its coolest, January (6.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.4 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 413 km from the sea.

Why Aoxi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 413 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.3 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Aoxi itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (27.426°N, 115.836°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-565-2958), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
