Panjin weather in January

Average January weather in Panjin, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Panjin averages -3.6 °C (26 °F) days and -14.3 °C (6 °F) nights, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 182 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Panjin

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Panjin. Compared with December: 3.3 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. February is 4.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Panjin in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Panjin in January?
In January, Panjin averages a day high of -3.6 °C (26 °F) and a night low of -14.3 °C (6 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 182 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Panjin?
January is not one of Panjin’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Panjin in January?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (181 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Panjin in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.2 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Panjin get in January?
About 182 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Panjin in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
