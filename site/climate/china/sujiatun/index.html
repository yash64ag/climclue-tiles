Why Sujiatun has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 156 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Sujiatun climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Sujiatun?
July, with an average of 24.9 °C (77 °F), day highs around 28.6 °C (83 °F) and night lows around 20.7 °C (69 °F).
What is the coldest month in Sujiatun?
January, with an average of -10.9 °C (12 °F), day highs around -5.6 °C (22 °F) and night lows around -17.3 °C (1 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Sujiatun?
July, with 185 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 717 mm (28.2 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Sujiatun?
January, with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Sujiatun?
May, with about 269 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Sujiatun?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 55 cm (21.5 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in March; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 5 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Sujiatun?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June and September.
