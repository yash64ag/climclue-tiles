Sujiatun at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Sujiatun is 578th warmest (9.1 °C year average), 163rd sunniest (about 2,544 hours of sunshine a year) and 437th wettest (717 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shenyang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (17 km away), Sujiatun is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with about the same precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.9 °C), averages 35.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-10.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 156 km from the sea.

Why Sujiatun has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 156 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 35.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
