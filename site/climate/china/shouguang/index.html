Shouguang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Shouguang is 451st warmest (13.7 °C year average), 172nd sunniest (about 2,535 hours of sunshine a year) and 530th wettest (579 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Changle, the nearest covered town with different numbers (21 km away), Shouguang is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 3 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.9 °C), averages 28.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 57 km from the sea.

Why Shouguang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 57 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
