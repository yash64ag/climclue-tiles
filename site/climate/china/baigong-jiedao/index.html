Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Báigōng Jiēdào is 231st warmest (19.7 °C year average), 1755th sunniest (about 1,342 hours of sunshine a year) and 1024th wettest (1,061 mm a year), near the middle on each. Why Báigōng Jiēdào has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 958 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
