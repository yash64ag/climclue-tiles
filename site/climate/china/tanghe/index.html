Tanghe at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Tanghe is 1059th warmest (15.8 °C year average), 1107th sunniest (about 1,983 hours of sunshine a year) and 1155th wettest (906 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nanyang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Tanghe is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 63 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 25.1 °C more than its coolest, January (2.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and December. The area lies about 640 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Tanghe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 640 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tanghe itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (32.685°N, 112.832°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-513-2928), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
