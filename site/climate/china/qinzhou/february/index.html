How February compares in Qinzhou

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Qinzhou. Compared with January: 1.3 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. March is 3.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Qinzhou in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qinzhou in February?
In February, Qinzhou averages a day high of 18.7 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 12.8 °C (55 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 61 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Qinzhou?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Qinzhou’s mild months (February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qinzhou in February?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (518 mm), the driest December (34 mm).
Does it snow in Qinzhou in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qinzhou get in February?
About 61 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Qinzhou in February?
About 18.1 °C (65 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is July (30.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Qinzhou in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
