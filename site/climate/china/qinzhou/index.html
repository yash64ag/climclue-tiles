Qinzhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Qinzhou is 35th warmest (22.9 °C year average), 549th sunniest (about 1,777 hours of sunshine a year) and 7th wettest (2,420 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Fangchenggang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Qinzhou is 0.3 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 295 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 14.5 °C more than its coolest, January (14.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 11.0 °C (52 °F), in January. The area lies about 33 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.7 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 111% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

For mild weather, the best months to visit Qinzhou are February, March, November and December.

Why Qinzhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 33 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
