How March compares in Qinzhou

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Qinzhou. Compared with February: 3.1 °C warmer, 12 mm more precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. April is 4.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Qinzhou in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qinzhou in March?
In March, Qinzhou averages a day high of 23.0 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 16.0 °C (61 °F), with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 74 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Qinzhou?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Qinzhou’s mild months (February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qinzhou in March?
81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (523 mm), the driest December (34 mm).
Does it snow in Qinzhou in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qinzhou get in March?
About 74 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qinzhou in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
