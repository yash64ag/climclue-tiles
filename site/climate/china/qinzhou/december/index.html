How December compares in Qinzhou

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Qinzhou. Compared with November: 4.0 °C cooler, 40 mm less precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. January is 1.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Qinzhou in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qinzhou in December?
In December, Qinzhou averages a day high of 20.6 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 12.1 °C (54 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Qinzhou?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Qinzhou’s mild months (February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qinzhou in December?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (518 mm), the driest December (34 mm).
Does it snow in Qinzhou in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qinzhou get in December?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Qinzhou in December?
About 19.8 °C (68 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, fresh: many find it cool; its warmest month is July (30.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Qinzhou in December?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
