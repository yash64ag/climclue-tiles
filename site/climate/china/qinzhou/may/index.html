How May compares in Qinzhou

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Qinzhou. Compared with April: 3.4 °C warmer, 122 mm more precipitation and 72 hours more sunshine. June is 1.7 °C warmer than May on average.

Qinzhou in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qinzhou in May?
In May, Qinzhou averages a day high of 30.1 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 257 mm (10.1 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Qinzhou?
May is not one of Qinzhou’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qinzhou in May?
257 mm (10.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (518 mm), the driest December (34 mm).
Does it snow in Qinzhou in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qinzhou get in May?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Qinzhou in May?
About 26.9 °C (80 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is July (30.0 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Qinzhou in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
