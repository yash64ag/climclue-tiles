Shangqiu weather in September

Average September weather in Shangqiu, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Shangqiu averages 26.8 °C (80 °F) days and 16.8 °C (62 °F) nights, with 73 mm (2.9 in) of rain over the month and about 185 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Shangqiu

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Shangqiu. Compared with August: 4.5 °C cooler, 74 mm less precipitation and 36 hours less sunshine. October is 5.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Shangqiu in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shangqiu in September?
In September, Shangqiu averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Shangqiu?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Shangqiu’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shangqiu in September?
73 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (177 mm), the driest December (13 mm).
Does it snow in Shangqiu in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shangqiu get in September?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shangqiu in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
