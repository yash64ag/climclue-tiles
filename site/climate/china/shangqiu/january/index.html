Shangqiu weather in January

Average January weather in Shangqiu, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Shangqiu averages 5.6 °C (42 °F) days and -4.1 °C (25 °F) nights, with 15 mm (0.6 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 158 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Shangqiu

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Shangqiu. Compared with December: 2.0 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. February is 3.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Shangqiu in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shangqiu in January?
In January, Shangqiu averages a day high of 5.6 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -4.1 °C (25 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 158 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Shangqiu?
January is not one of Shangqiu’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shangqiu in January?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (177 mm), the driest December (13 mm).
Does it snow in Shangqiu in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.9 cm (2.7 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Shangqiu get in January?
About 158 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shangqiu in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
