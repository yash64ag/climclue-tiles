Why Jiangzhuang, Shandong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 49 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown.
