How February compares in Cixi

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Cixi. Compared with January: 1.5 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. March is 3.4 °C warmer than February on average.

Cixi in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cixi in February?
In February, Cixi averages a day high of 10.1 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 2.8 °C (37 °F), with 85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 105 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Cixi?
February is not one of Cixi’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cixi in February?
85 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (214 mm), the driest December (54 mm).
Does it snow in Cixi in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.4 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Cixi get in February?
About 105 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cixi in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
