Cixi weather in June

Average June weather in Cixi, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Cixi averages 28.3 °C (83 °F) days and 21.3 °C (70 °F) nights, with 211 mm (8.3 in) of rain over the month and about 152 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Cixi

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Cixi. Compared with May: 3.8 °C warmer, 86 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. July is 4.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Cixi in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cixi in June?
In June, Cixi averages a day high of 28.3 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 211 mm (8.3 in) of precipitation and about 152 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Cixi?
June is not one of Cixi’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cixi in June?
211 mm (8.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (211 mm), the driest December (54 mm).
Does it snow in Cixi in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Cixi get in June?
About 152 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Cixi in June?
About 22.7 °C (73 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, comfortable for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (28.3 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Cixi in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
