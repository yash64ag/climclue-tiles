How March compares in Cixi

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Cixi. Compared with February: 3.4 °C warmer, 53 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. April is 5.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Cixi in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Cixi in March?
In March, Cixi averages a day high of 13.9 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 6.3 °C (43 °F), with 138 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 124 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Cixi?
March is not one of Cixi’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Cixi in March?
138 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (214 mm), the driest December (54 mm).
Does it snow in Cixi in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.8 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Cixi get in March?
About 124 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Cixi in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
