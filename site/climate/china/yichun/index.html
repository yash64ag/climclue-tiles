Yichun, Jiangxi at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Yichun, Jiangxi is 173rd warmest (18.2 °C year average), 568th sunniest (about 1,721 hours of sunshine a year) and 91st wettest (1,777 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xinyu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (53 km away), Yichun, Jiangxi is 0.5 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 161 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.4 °C), averages 23.5 °C more than its coolest, January (6.0 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 543 km from the sea.

Why Yichun, Jiangxi has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 543 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.5 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
