Yichun, Jiangxi in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yichun, Jiangxi in May?
In May, Yichun, Jiangxi averages a day high of 26.6 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 19.0 °C (66 °F), with 233 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Yichun, Jiangxi?
May is not one of Yichun, Jiangxi’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yichun, Jiangxi in May?
233 mm (9.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (273 mm), the driest December (55 mm).
Does it snow in Yichun, Jiangxi in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yichun, Jiangxi get in May?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yichun, Jiangxi in May?
