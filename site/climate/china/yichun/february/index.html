Yichun, Jiangxi in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yichun, Jiangxi in February?
In February, Yichun, Jiangxi averages a day high of 11.6 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 4.3 °C (40 °F), with 116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 72 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Yichun, Jiangxi?
February is not one of Yichun, Jiangxi’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yichun, Jiangxi in February?
116 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (273 mm), the driest December (55 mm).
Does it snow in Yichun, Jiangxi in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.0 cm (1.2 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Yichun, Jiangxi get in February?
About 72 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yichun, Jiangxi in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
