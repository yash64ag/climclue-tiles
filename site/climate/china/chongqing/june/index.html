Chongqing weather in June

Average June weather in Chongqing, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Chongqing averages 29.5 °C (85 °F) days and 22.3 °C (72 °F) nights, with 184 mm (7.2 in) of rain over the month and about 128 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Chongqing

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Chongqing. Compared with May: 2.6 °C warmer, 33 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. July is 3.2 °C warmer than June on average.

Chongqing in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chongqing in June?
In June, Chongqing averages a day high of 29.5 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 184 mm (7.2 in) of precipitation and about 128 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Chongqing?
June is not one of Chongqing’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
