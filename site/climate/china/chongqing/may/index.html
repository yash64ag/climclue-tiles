How May compares in Chongqing

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Chongqing. Compared with April: 4.0 °C warmer, 48 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. June is 2.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Chongqing in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chongqing in May?
In May, Chongqing averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 152 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 124 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Chongqing?
May is not one of Chongqing’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chongqing in May?
152 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (184 mm), the driest January (20 mm).
Does it snow in Chongqing in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chongqing get in May?
About 124 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chongqing in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
