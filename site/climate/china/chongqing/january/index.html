How January compares in Chongqing

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Chongqing. Compared with December: 1.2 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. February is 2.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Chongqing in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chongqing in January?
In January, Chongqing averages a day high of 10.9 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 4.8 °C (41 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 40 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Chongqing?
January is not one of Chongqing’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chongqing in January?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (191 mm), the driest January (21 mm).
Does it snow in Chongqing in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.5 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Chongqing get in January?
About 40 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chongqing in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
