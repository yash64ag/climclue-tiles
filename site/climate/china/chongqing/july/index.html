How July compares in Chongqing

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Chongqing. Compared with June: 3.2 °C warmer, 5 mm less precipitation and 64 hours more sunshine. August is 0.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Chongqing in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chongqing in July?
In July, Chongqing averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 24.5 °C (76 °F), with 186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 192 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Chongqing?
July is not one of Chongqing’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chongqing in July?
186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (191 mm), the driest January (21 mm).
Does it snow in Chongqing in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chongqing get in July?
About 192 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chongqing in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
