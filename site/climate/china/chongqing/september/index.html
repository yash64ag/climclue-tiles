Chongqing weather in September

Average September weather in Chongqing, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In September, Chongqing averages 28.2 °C (83 °F) days and 21.6 °C (71 °F) nights, with 107 mm (4.2 in) of rain over the month and about 107 hours of sunshine.

How September compares in Chongqing

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Chongqing. Compared with August: 4.1 °C cooler, 22 mm less precipitation and 101 hours less sunshine. October is 5.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Chongqing in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chongqing in September?
In September, Chongqing averages a day high of 28.2 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 21.6 °C (71 °F), with 107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 107 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Chongqing?
September is not one of Chongqing’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chongqing in September?
107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (184 mm), the driest January (20 mm).
Does it snow in Chongqing in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chongqing get in September?
About 107 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chongqing in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
