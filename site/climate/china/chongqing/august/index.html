How August compares in Chongqing

August is the warmest month, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Chongqing. Compared with July: about as warm, 51 mm less precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. September is 4.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Chongqing in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chongqing in August?
In August, Chongqing averages a day high of 33.3 °C (92 °F) and a night low of 24.8 °C (77 °F), with 128 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Chongqing?
August is not one of Chongqing’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chongqing in August?
128 mm (5.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (184 mm), the driest January (20 mm).
Does it snow in Chongqing in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chongqing get in August?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chongqing in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
