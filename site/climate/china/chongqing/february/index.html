How February compares in Chongqing

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Chongqing. Compared with January: 2.1 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. March is 3.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Chongqing in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chongqing in February?
In February, Chongqing averages a day high of 13.3 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 6.7 °C (44 °F), with 24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 44 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Chongqing?
February is not one of Chongqing’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chongqing in February?
24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (184 mm), the driest January (20 mm).
Does it snow in Chongqing in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chongqing get in February?
About 44 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chongqing in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
