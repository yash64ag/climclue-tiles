How April compares in Chongqing

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Chongqing. Compared with March: 4.7 °C warmer, 63 mm more precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. May is 4.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Chongqing in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Chongqing in April?
In April, Chongqing averages a day high of 23.1 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 15.4 °C (60 °F), with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 115 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Chongqing?
April is not one of Chongqing’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Chongqing in April?
104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (184 mm), the driest January (20 mm).
Does it snow in Chongqing in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Chongqing get in April?
About 115 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Chongqing in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
