How July compares in Hongkou

July is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Hongkou. Compared with June: 4.1 °C warmer, 36 mm less precipitation and 61 hours more sunshine. August is 0.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Hongkou in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hongkou in July?
In July, Hongkou averages a day high of 31.1 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 25.4 °C (78 °F), with 187 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Hongkou?
July is not one of Hongkou’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hongkou in July?
187 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (228 mm), the driest December (49 mm).
Does it snow in Hongkou in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hongkou get in July?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hongkou in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
