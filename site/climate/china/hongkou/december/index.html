Hongkou weather in December

Average December weather in Hongkou, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Hongkou averages 11.3 °C (52 °F) days and 3.2 °C (38 °F) nights, with 48 mm (1.9 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 148 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Hongkou

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Hongkou. Compared with November: 6.0 °C cooler, 24 mm less precipitation and 2 hours more sunshine. January is 2.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Hongkou in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hongkou in December?
In December, Hongkou averages a day high of 11.3 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 3.2 °C (38 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Hongkou?
December is not one of Hongkou’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hongkou in December?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (223 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Hongkou in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.5 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Hongkou get in December?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Hongkou in December?
About 10.9 °C (52 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (27.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Hongkou in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
