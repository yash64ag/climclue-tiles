How January compares in Hongkou

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Hongkou. Compared with December: 2.7 °C cooler, 27 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. February is 1.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Hongkou in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hongkou in January?
In January, Hongkou averages a day high of 8.2 °C (47 °F) and a night low of 1.7 °C (35 °F), with 75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 134 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Hongkou?
January is not one of Hongkou’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hongkou in January?
75 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (223 mm), the driest December (48 mm).
Does it snow in Hongkou in January?
Yes, on average: about 4.5 cm (1.8 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Hongkou get in January?
About 134 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Hongkou in January?
About 7.2 °C (45 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (27.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Hongkou in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
