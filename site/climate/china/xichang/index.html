Xichang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Xichang is 273rd warmest (16.8 °C year average), 266th sunniest (about 2,292 hours of sunshine a year) and 324th wettest (1,118 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Panzhihua, the nearest covered town with different numbers (156 km away), Xichang is 2.6 °C cooler in July and 4.5 °C cooler in January, with 333 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.4 °C), averages 13.6 °C more than its coolest, January (8.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 915 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Xichang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 915 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.6 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.5 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
