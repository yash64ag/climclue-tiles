Anxiang at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Anxiang is 1399th warmest (13.6 °C year average), 290th sunniest (about 2,586 hours of sunshine a year) and 1713th wettest (543 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xinle, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Anxiang is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 9 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.4 °C), averages 30.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-2.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, November and December. The area lies about 272 km from the sea.

Why Anxiang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 272 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Anxiang itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (38.383°N, 114.540°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-456-2945), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
