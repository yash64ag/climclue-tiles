How November compares in Shenyang

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Shenyang. Compared with October: 10.0 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 49 hours less sunshine. December is 7.6 °C cooler than November on average.

Shenyang in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shenyang in November?
In November, Shenyang averages a day high of 5.0 °C (41 °F) and a night low of -5.0 °C (23 °F), with 21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Shenyang?
November is not one of Shenyang’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shenyang in November?
21 mm (0.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (186 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Shenyang in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 10 cm (4.1 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.4 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Shenyang get in November?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shenyang in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
