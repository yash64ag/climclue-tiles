How January compares in Shenyang

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Shenyang. Compared with December: 3.9 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. February is 5.2 °C warmer than January on average.

Shenyang in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shenyang in January?
In January, Shenyang averages a day high of -5.8 °C (22 °F) and a night low of -17.6 °C (0 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 169 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Shenyang?
January is not one of Shenyang’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shenyang in January?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (186 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Shenyang in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 9.6 cm (3.8 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 7.0 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Shenyang get in January?
About 169 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shenyang in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
