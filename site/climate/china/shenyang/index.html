Shenyang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Shenyang is 580th warmest (8.9 °C year average), 165th sunniest (about 2,540 hours of sunshine a year) and 436th wettest (717 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dongling, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Shenyang is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 16 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.9 °C), averages 36.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-11.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 169 km from the sea.

Why Shenyang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 169 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 36.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
