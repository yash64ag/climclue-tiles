How February compares in Shenyang

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Shenyang. Compared with January: 5.2 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. March is 7.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Shenyang in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shenyang in February?
In February, Shenyang averages a day high of -0.6 °C (31 °F) and a night low of -12.7 °C (9 °F), with 9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 181 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Shenyang?
February is not one of Shenyang’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shenyang in February?
9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (186 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Shenyang in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.2 cm (2.4 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.7 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Shenyang get in February?
About 181 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shenyang in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
