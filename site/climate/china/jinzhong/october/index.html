Jinzhong in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinzhong in October?
In October, Jinzhong averages a day high of 17.9 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 4.8 °C (41 °F), with 27 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 206 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Jinzhong?
October is not one of Jinzhong’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinzhong in October?
27 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (106 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Jinzhong in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jinzhong get in October?
About 206 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jinzhong in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
