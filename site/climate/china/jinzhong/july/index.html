How July compares in Jinzhong

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Jinzhong. Compared with June: 1.8 °C warmer, 43 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. August is 1.8 °C cooler than July on average.

Jinzhong in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinzhong in July?
In July, Jinzhong averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Jinzhong?
July is not one of Jinzhong’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinzhong in July?
106 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (106 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Jinzhong in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jinzhong get in July?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jinzhong in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
