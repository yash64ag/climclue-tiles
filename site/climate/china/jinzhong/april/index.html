Jinzhong in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinzhong in April?
In April, Jinzhong averages a day high of 20.1 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 5.7 °C (42 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Jinzhong?
April is not one of Jinzhong’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinzhong in April?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (106 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Jinzhong in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jinzhong get in April?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jinzhong in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
