How March compares in Jinzhong

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Jinzhong. Compared with February: 5.7 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 42 hours more sunshine. April is 7.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Jinzhong in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinzhong in March?
In March, Jinzhong averages a day high of 12.0 °C (54 °F) and a night low of -1.2 °C (30 °F), with 14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 211 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Jinzhong?
March is not one of Jinzhong’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinzhong in March?
14 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (106 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Jinzhong in March?
Yes, on average: about 2.3 cm (0.9 in) of fresh snow in March (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Jinzhong get in March?
About 211 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jinzhong in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
