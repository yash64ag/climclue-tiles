How November compares in Jinzhong

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Jinzhong. Compared with October: 7.7 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 27 hours less sunshine. December is 6.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Jinzhong in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jinzhong in November?
In November, Jinzhong averages a day high of 9.6 °C (49 °F) and a night low of -2.5 °C (28 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 179 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Jinzhong?
November is not one of Jinzhong’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jinzhong in November?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (106 mm), the driest December (4 mm).
Does it snow in Jinzhong in November?
Yes, on average: about 2.7 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in November (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Jinzhong get in November?
About 179 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jinzhong in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
