How April compares in Sanya

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Sanya. Compared with March: 2.5 °C warmer, 48 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. May is 1.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Sanya in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sanya in April?
In April, Sanya averages a day high of 27.6 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 174 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Sanya?
April is not one of Sanya’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sanya in April?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (275 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Sanya in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sanya get in April?
About 174 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sanya in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
