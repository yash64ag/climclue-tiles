Sanya at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Sanya is the 3rd warmest (24.9 °C year average). Compared with Wuzhishan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (59 km away), Sanya is 2.6 °C warmer in June and 3.8 °C warmer in January, with 273 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (28.1 °C), averages 7.6 °C more than its coolest, January (20.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 18.2 °C (65 °F), in January. The area lies about 15 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sanya, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sanya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 7.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
