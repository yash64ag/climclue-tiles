How February compares in Sanya

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Sanya. Compared with January: 0.9 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 25 hours less sunshine. March is 1.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Sanya in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sanya in February?
In February, Sanya averages a day high of 23.8 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 19.5 °C (67 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 133 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Sanya?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Sanya’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sanya in February?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (275 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Sanya in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sanya get in February?
About 133 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sanya in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
