How July compares in Sanya

July is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Sanya. Compared with June: 0.2 °C cooler, 41 mm more precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. August is 0.2 °C cooler than July on average.

Sanya in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sanya in July?
In July, Sanya averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 26.3 °C (79 °F), with 252 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Sanya?
July is not one of Sanya’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sanya in July?
252 mm (9.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (275 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Sanya in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sanya get in July?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sanya in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
