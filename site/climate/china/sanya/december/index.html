How December compares in Sanya

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Sanya. Compared with November: 2.4 °C cooler, 56 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. January is 0.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Sanya in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sanya in December?
In December, Sanya averages a day high of 23.6 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 18.8 °C (66 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Sanya?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Sanya’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sanya in December?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (275 mm), the driest January (7 mm).
Does it snow in Sanya in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sanya get in December?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sanya in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
