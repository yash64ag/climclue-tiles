How October compares in Jingzhou

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Jingzhou. Compared with September: 5.5 °C cooler, 17 mm more precipitation and 17 hours less sunshine. November is 5.9 °C cooler than October on average.

Jingzhou in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jingzhou in October?
In October, Jingzhou averages a day high of 22.6 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 95 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Jingzhou?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Jingzhou’s mild months (September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jingzhou in October?
95 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (200 mm), the driest December (29 mm).
Does it snow in Jingzhou in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jingzhou get in October?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jingzhou in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
