How December compares in Jingzhou

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Jingzhou. Compared with November: 5.6 °C cooler, 36 mm less precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. January is 2.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Jingzhou in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jingzhou in December?
In December, Jingzhou averages a day high of 11.0 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 2.8 °C (37 °F), with 29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 105 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jingzhou?
December is not one of Jingzhou’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jingzhou in December?
29 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (201 mm), the driest December (29 mm).
Does it snow in Jingzhou in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.1 cm (1.2 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Jingzhou get in December?
About 105 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jingzhou in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
