Jingzhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Jingzhou is 229th warmest (17.4 °C year average), 574th sunniest (about 1,701 hours of sunshine a year) and 271st wettest (1,260 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Buhe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Jingzhou is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 42 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.8 °C), averages 24.0 °C more than its coolest, January (4.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 832 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jingzhou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jingzhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 832 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
