Awuliya at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Awuliya is 1807th warmest (9.2 °C year average), 114th sunniest (about 2,740 hours of sunshine a year) and 1903rd wettest (396 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yuqunweng, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Awuliya is 1.1 °C cooler in July and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 20 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.2 °C), averages 28.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-6.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 2,230 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Awuliya has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Inland from the sea, land loses heat in winter and warms quickly in summer, so seasons are strongly contrasted. Storm systems bring precipitation year-round: rain in summer, snow in winter. About 2,230 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.7 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.

About these numbers

Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-400-2616), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
