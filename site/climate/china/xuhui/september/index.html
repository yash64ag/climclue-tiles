How September compares in Xuhui

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Xuhui. Compared with August: 3.8 °C cooler, 85 mm less precipitation and 73 hours less sunshine. October is 5.1 °C cooler than September on average.

Xuhui in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xuhui in September?
In September, Xuhui averages a day high of 26.9 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Xuhui?
September is not one of Xuhui’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xuhui in September?
139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (225 mm), the driest December (49 mm).
Does it snow in Xuhui in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xuhui get in September?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xuhui in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
