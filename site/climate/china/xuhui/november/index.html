How November compares in Xuhui

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Xuhui. Compared with October: 5.8 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. December is 6.0 °C cooler than November on average.

Xuhui in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xuhui in November?
In November, Xuhui averages a day high of 17.2 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 9.2 °C (49 °F), with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Xuhui?
November is not one of Xuhui’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xuhui in November?
74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (233 mm), the driest December (50 mm).
Does it snow in Xuhui in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xuhui get in November?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xuhui in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
