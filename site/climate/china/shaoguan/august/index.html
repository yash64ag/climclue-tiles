Shaoguan in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shaoguan in August?
In August, Shaoguan averages a day high of 34.1 °C (93 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 136 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 225 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Shaoguan?
August is not one of Shaoguan’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shaoguan in August?
136 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (263 mm), the driest December (44 mm).
Does it snow in Shaoguan in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shaoguan get in August?
About 225 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shaoguan in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
