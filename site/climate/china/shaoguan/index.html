Shaoguan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Shaoguan is 97th warmest (20.9 °C year average), 566th sunniest (about 1,749 hours of sunshine a year) and 89th wettest (1,787 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Maba, the nearest covered town with different numbers (13 km away), Shaoguan is about as warm in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 15 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.4 °C), averages 18.8 °C more than its coolest, January (10.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.8 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 256 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 15 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 112% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Shaoguan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 256 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
