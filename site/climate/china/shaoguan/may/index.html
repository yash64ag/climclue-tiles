Shaoguan weather in May

Average May weather in Shaoguan, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Shaoguan averages 29.0 °C (84 °F) days and 21.5 °C (71 °F) nights, with 260 mm (10.2 in) of rain over the month and about 134 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Shaoguan

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Shaoguan. Compared with April: 4.0 °C warmer, 29 mm more precipitation and 45 hours more sunshine. June is 2.6 °C warmer than May on average.

Shaoguan in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shaoguan in May?
In May, Shaoguan averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 21.5 °C (71 °F), with 260 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation and about 134 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Shaoguan?
May is not one of Shaoguan’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shaoguan in May?
260 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (263 mm), the driest December (44 mm).
Does it snow in Shaoguan in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shaoguan get in May?
About 134 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shaoguan in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
