Wafangdian at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Wafangdian is 546th warmest (10.1 °C year average), 98th sunniest (about 2,637 hours of sunshine a year) and 473rd wettest (641 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pulandian, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Wafangdian is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 1.2 °C cooler in January, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.4 °C), averages 31.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-7.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 48 km from the sea.

Why Wafangdian has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 48 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 31.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
