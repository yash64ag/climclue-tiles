Banan in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Banan in June?
In June, Banan averages a day high of 29.3 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 196 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation and about 127 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Banan?
June is not one of Banan’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
