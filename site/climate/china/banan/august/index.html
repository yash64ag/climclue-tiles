Banan in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Banan in August?
In August, Banan averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 140 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Banan?
August is not one of Banan’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Banan in August?
140 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (196 mm), the driest January (23 mm).
Does it snow in Banan in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Banan get in August?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Banan in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
