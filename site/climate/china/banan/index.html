Banan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Banan is the 2nd least sunny (about 1,201 hours of sunshine a year). Compared with Chongqing, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Banan is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 85 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 20.2 °C more than its coolest, January (8.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 882 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 24 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 111% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Banan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 882 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
