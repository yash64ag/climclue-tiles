How September compares in Banan

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Banan. Compared with August: 4.0 °C cooler, 30 mm less precipitation and 99 hours less sunshine. October is 5.5 °C cooler than September on average.

Banan in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Banan in September?
In September, Banan averages a day high of 27.4 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 20.1 °C (68 °F), with 117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 109 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Banan?
September is not one of Banan’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Banan in September?
117 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (205 mm), the driest January (24 mm).
Does it snow in Banan in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Banan get in September?
About 109 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Banan in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
