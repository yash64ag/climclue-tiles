How February compares in Banan

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Banan. Compared with January: 2.1 °C warmer, 3 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. March is 3.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Banan in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Banan in February?
In February, Banan averages a day high of 12.9 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 6.3 °C (43 °F), with 27 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 45 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Banan?
February is not one of Banan’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Banan in February?
27 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (205 mm), the driest January (24 mm).
Does it snow in Banan in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Banan get in February?
About 45 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Banan in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
