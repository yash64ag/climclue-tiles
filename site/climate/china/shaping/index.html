Shaping at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Shaping is 27th warmest (23.0 °C year average), 514th sunniest (about 1,847 hours of sunshine a year) and 70th wettest (1,885 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jiangmen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Shaping is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 288 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.4 °C), averages 14.8 °C more than its coolest, January (14.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.5 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 75 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shaping, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shaping has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 75 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
