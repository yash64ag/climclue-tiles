Shaping at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Shaping is 29th warmest (23.1 °C year average), 514th sunniest (about 1,847 hours of sunshine a year) and 72nd wettest (1,835 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jiangmen, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Shaping is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 304 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.5 °C), averages 14.7 °C more than its coolest, January (14.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.5 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 75 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Shaping, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Shaping has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 75 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-612-2929), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
