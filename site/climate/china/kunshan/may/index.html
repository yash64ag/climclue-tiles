How May compares in Kunshan

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Kunshan. Compared with April: 5.4 °C warmer, 19 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. June is 3.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Kunshan in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kunshan in May?
In May, Kunshan averages a day high of 24.8 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 16.3 °C (61 °F), with 111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Kunshan?
May is not one of Kunshan’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kunshan in May?
111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (218 mm), the driest December (39 mm).
Does it snow in Kunshan in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kunshan get in May?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kunshan in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
