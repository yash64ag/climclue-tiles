How March compares in Kunshan

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Kunshan. Compared with February: 3.8 °C warmer, 31 mm more precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. April is 5.6 °C warmer than March on average.

Kunshan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kunshan in March?
In March, Kunshan averages a day high of 14.0 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 5.2 °C (41 °F), with 96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Kunshan?
March is not one of Kunshan’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kunshan in March?
96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (214 mm), the driest December (39 mm).
Does it snow in Kunshan in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.1 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Kunshan get in March?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kunshan in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
