How December compares in Maoming

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Maoming. Compared with November: 3.8 °C cooler, 5 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. January is 1.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Maoming in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maoming in December?
In December, Maoming averages a day high of 21.7 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 154 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Maoming?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Maoming’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maoming in December?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (354 mm), the driest December (27 mm).
Does it snow in Maoming in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maoming get in December?
About 154 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maoming in December?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
