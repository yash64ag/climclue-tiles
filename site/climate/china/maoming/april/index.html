How April compares in Maoming

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Maoming. Compared with March: 3.8 °C warmer, 103 mm more precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. May is 2.8 °C warmer than April on average.

Maoming in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maoming in April?
In April, Maoming averages a day high of 27.7 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 21.3 °C (70 °F), with 187 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 110 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Maoming?
April is not one of Maoming’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maoming in April?
187 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (356 mm), the driest December (27 mm).
Does it snow in Maoming in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maoming get in April?
About 110 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maoming in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
