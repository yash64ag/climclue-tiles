How May compares in Maoming

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Maoming. Compared with April: 2.8 °C warmer, 166 mm more precipitation and 70 hours more sunshine. June is 1.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Maoming in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maoming in May?
In May, Maoming averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.6 °C (74 °F), with 353 mm (13.9 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Maoming?
May is not one of Maoming’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maoming in May?
353 mm (13.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (356 mm), the driest December (27 mm).
Does it snow in Maoming in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maoming get in May?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maoming in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
