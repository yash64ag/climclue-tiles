How March compares in Maoming

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Maoming. Compared with February: 2.7 °C warmer, 27 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. April is 3.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Maoming in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maoming in March?
In March, Maoming averages a day high of 24.8 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 17.6 °C (64 °F), with 83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation and about 83 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Maoming?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Maoming’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maoming in March?
83 mm (3.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (356 mm), the driest December (27 mm).
Does it snow in Maoming in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maoming get in March?
About 83 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maoming in March?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
