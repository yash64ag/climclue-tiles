How August compares in Maoming

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Maoming. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 26 hours less sunshine. September is 1.0 °C cooler than August on average.

Maoming in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Maoming in August?
In August, Maoming averages a day high of 31.3 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 321 mm (12.6 in) of precipitation and about 209 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Maoming?
August is not one of Maoming’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Maoming in August?
321 mm (12.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (356 mm), the driest December (27 mm).
Does it snow in Maoming in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Maoming get in August?
About 209 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Maoming in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
