Aihui at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Aihui is 684th warmest (1.4 °C year average), 229th sunniest (about 2,425 hours of sunshine a year) and 572nd wettest (533 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Heihe, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Aihui is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.3 °C warmer in January, with 12 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.6 °C), averages 43.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-22.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 761 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Aihui has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 761 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 43.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
