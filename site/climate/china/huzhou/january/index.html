How January compares in Huzhou

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Huzhou. Compared with December: 2.2 °C cooler, 28 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. February is 2.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Huzhou in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huzhou in January?
In January, Huzhou averages a day high of 8.0 °C (46 °F) and a night low of 0.5 °C (33 °F), with 69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation and about 131 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Huzhou?
January is not one of Huzhou’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huzhou in January?
69 mm (2.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (209 mm), the driest December (41 mm).
Does it snow in Huzhou in January?
Yes, on average: about 9.4 cm (3.7 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Huzhou get in January?
About 131 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huzhou in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
