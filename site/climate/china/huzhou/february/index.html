How February compares in Huzhou

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Huzhou. Compared with January: 2.0 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. March is 3.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Huzhou in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huzhou in February?
In February, Huzhou averages a day high of 9.6 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 2.1 °C (36 °F), with 74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation and about 111 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Huzhou?
February is not one of Huzhou’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huzhou in February?
74 mm (2.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (214 mm), the driest December (41 mm).
Does it snow in Huzhou in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.1 cm (2.8 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Huzhou get in February?
About 111 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huzhou in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
