How August compares in Huzhou

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Huzhou. Compared with July: 0.6 °C cooler, 20 mm less precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. September is 4.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Huzhou in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huzhou in August?
In August, Huzhou averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 161 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Huzhou?
August is not one of Huzhou’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huzhou in August?
161 mm (6.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (209 mm), the driest December (41 mm).
Does it snow in Huzhou in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huzhou get in August?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huzhou in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
