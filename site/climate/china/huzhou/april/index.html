How April compares in Huzhou

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Huzhou. Compared with March: 5.9 °C warmer, 15 mm less precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. May is 5.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Huzhou in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huzhou in April?
In April, Huzhou averages a day high of 19.9 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 11.5 °C (53 °F), with 98 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Huzhou?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Huzhou’s mild months (April and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huzhou in April?
98 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (214 mm), the driest December (41 mm).
Does it snow in Huzhou in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huzhou get in April?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huzhou in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
