Huzhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Huzhou is 269th warmest (16.8 °C year average), 442nd sunniest (about 1,934 hours of sunshine a year) and 251st wettest (1,312 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chóngfú, the nearest covered town with different numbers (50 km away), Huzhou is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.5 °C cooler in January, with 141 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 24.7 °C more than its coolest, January (4.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 0.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 105 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Huzhou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Huzhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 105 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
