Dengzhou, Shandong sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Shandong · China

The sea off Dengzhou, Shandong averages 24.0 °C (75 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 2.3 °C (36 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Dengzhou, Shandong sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Dengzhou, Shandong?
In August, at about 24.0 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Dengzhou, Shandong?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 2.3 °C (36 °F).
How warm is the sea in Dengzhou, Shandong in February?
About 2.3 °C (36 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Dengzhou, Shandong?
About 12.7 °C (55 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Dengzhou, Shandong nearby?
In August: Longgang, Shandong (25.1 °C, 41 km), Laizhou (27.0 °C, 100 km) and Dongcun (25.9 °C, 120 km).
