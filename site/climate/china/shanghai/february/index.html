How February compares in Shanghai

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Shanghai. Compared with January: 1.4 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. March is 3.5 °C warmer than February on average.

Shanghai in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shanghai in February?
In February, Shanghai averages a day high of 9.6 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 2.6 °C (37 °F), with 77 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 116 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Shanghai?
February is not one of Shanghai’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shanghai in February?
77 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (230 mm), the driest December (49 mm).
Does it snow in Shanghai in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.7 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Shanghai get in February?
About 116 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Shanghai in February?
About 6.2 °C (43 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (27.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Shanghai in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
