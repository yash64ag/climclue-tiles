How August compares in Shanghai

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the sunniest month in Shanghai. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, 41 mm more precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. September is 3.8 °C cooler than August on average.

Shanghai in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shanghai in August?
In August, Shanghai averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 228 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation and about 238 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Shanghai?
August is not one of Shanghai’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shanghai in August?
228 mm (9.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (228 mm), the driest December (49 mm).
Does it snow in Shanghai in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shanghai get in August?
About 238 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Shanghai in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
