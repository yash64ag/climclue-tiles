How April compares in Shanghai

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Shanghai. Compared with March: 5.5 °C warmer, 12 mm less precipitation and 15 hours more sunshine. May is 5.4 °C warmer than April on average.

Shanghai in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Shanghai in April?
In April, Shanghai averages a day high of 19.2 °C (67 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 155 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Shanghai?
April is not one of Shanghai’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Shanghai in April?
104 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (230 mm), the driest December (49 mm).
Does it snow in Shanghai in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Shanghai get in April?
About 155 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Shanghai in April?
About 12.9 °C (55 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (27.5 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Shanghai in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
