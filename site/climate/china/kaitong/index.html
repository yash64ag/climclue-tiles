Kaitong weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Kaitong, China (Jilin), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Kaitong at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Kaitong is 1917th warmest (6.5 °C year average), 171st sunniest (about 2,691 hours of sunshine a year) and 1905th wettest (393 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Taonan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (63 km away), Kaitong is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 5 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.1 °C), averages 38.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-14.2 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 466 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Kaitong, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Kaitong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 466 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 38.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kaitong itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (44.809°N, 123.081°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 7.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-391-3030), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
