How March compares in Guankou

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Guankou. Compared with February: 3.8 °C warmer, 68 mm more precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. April is 6.0 °C warmer than March on average.

Guankou in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guankou in March?
In March, Guankou averages a day high of 15.9 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 7.7 °C (46 °F), with 186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation and about 80 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Guankou?
March is not one of Guankou’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guankou in March?
186 mm (7.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (276 mm), the driest December (56 mm).
Does it snow in Guankou in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.8 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Guankou get in March?
About 80 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guankou in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
