How August compares in Guankou

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Guankou. Compared with July: 0.8 °C cooler, 23 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. September is 3.9 °C cooler than August on average.

Guankou in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guankou in August?
In August, Guankou averages a day high of 32.7 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 25.0 °C (77 °F), with 137 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 237 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Guankou?
August is not one of Guankou’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guankou in August?
137 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (274 mm), the driest December (55 mm).
Does it snow in Guankou in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guankou get in August?
About 237 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guankou in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
