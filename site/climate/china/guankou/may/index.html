How May compares in Guankou

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Guankou. Compared with April: 4.9 °C warmer, 10 mm less precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. June is 3.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Guankou in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guankou in May?
In May, Guankou averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 18.6 °C (65 °F), with 238 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Guankou?
May is not one of Guankou’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guankou in May?
238 mm (9.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (276 mm), the driest December (56 mm).
Does it snow in Guankou in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guankou get in May?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guankou in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
