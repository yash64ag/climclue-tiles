How April compares in Guankou

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Guankou. Compared with March: 6.0 °C warmer, 62 mm more precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. May is 4.9 °C warmer than April on average.

Guankou in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guankou in April?
In April, Guankou averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 13.7 °C (57 °F), with 248 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation and about 105 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Guankou?
April is not one of Guankou’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guankou in April?
248 mm (9.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (276 mm), the driest December (56 mm).
Does it snow in Guankou in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Guankou get in April?
About 105 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guankou in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
