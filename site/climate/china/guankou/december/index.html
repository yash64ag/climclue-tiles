How December compares in Guankou

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Guankou. Compared with November: 5.5 °C cooler, 39 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Guankou in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guankou in December?
In December, Guankou averages a day high of 12.5 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 3.1 °C (38 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 112 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Guankou?
December is not one of Guankou’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guankou in December?
56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (276 mm), the driest December (56 mm).
Does it snow in Guankou in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.6 cm (1.0 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Guankou get in December?
About 112 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guankou in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
