How February compares in Guankou

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Guankou. Compared with January: 2.3 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. March is 3.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Guankou in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Guankou in February?
In February, Guankou averages a day high of 11.9 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 4.0 °C (39 °F), with 118 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation and about 68 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Guankou?
February is not one of Guankou’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Guankou in February?
118 mm (4.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (276 mm), the driest December (56 mm).
Does it snow in Guankou in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.8 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Guankou get in February?
About 68 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Guankou in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
