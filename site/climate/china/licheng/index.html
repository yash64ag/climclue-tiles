Licheng at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Licheng is 50th warmest (22.6 °C year average), 523rd sunniest (about 1,836 hours of sunshine a year) and 26th wettest (2,150 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shilong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Licheng is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 109 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 14.6 °C more than its coolest, January (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.3 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 79 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Licheng, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Licheng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 79 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
