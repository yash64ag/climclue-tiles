Licheng, Guangdong at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Licheng, Guangdong is 117th warmest (22.7 °C year average), 1360th sunniest (about 1,836 hours of sunshine a year) and 17th wettest (2,257 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shilong, Guangdong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (20 km away), Licheng, Guangdong is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 232 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.1 °C), averages 14.7 °C more than its coolest, January (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.8 °C (50 °F), in January. The area lies about 79 km from the sea.

Why Licheng, Guangdong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 79 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Licheng itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (23.295°N, 113.825°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-607-2938), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
