Xiuying weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Xiuying, China (Hainan), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Xiuying is warmest in July, with 32.5 °C (91 °F) days and 24.6 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 21.6 °C (71 °F) days and 15.2 °C nights. Most rain falls around September (308 mm); January is driest (21 mm).

Xiuying at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Xiuying is 23rd warmest (24.3 °C year average), 1003rd sunniest (about 2,086 hours of sunshine a year) and 124th wettest (1,910 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Qiongshan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (6 km away), Xiuying is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 34 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 10.2 °C more than its coolest, January (18.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 15.2 °C (59 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 113% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Xiuying has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Xiuying itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (20.001°N, 110.294°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-639-2902), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
