Xiuying at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Xiuying is 7th warmest (24.3 °C year average), 333rd sunniest (about 2,086 hours of sunshine a year) and 75th wettest (1,862 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Haikou, Hainan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Xiuying is 0.1 °C warmer in July and about as warm in January, with 37 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 10.3 °C more than its coolest, January (18.4 °C). Even the coolest nights average 16.9 °C (62 °F), in January. The area lies about 11 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 110% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Xiuying has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

It stays warm all year; the dry season brings clearer skies and wider day–night swings. The tropical rain belt follows the sun: it brings a wet season when overhead and a long dry season when it moves away. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
