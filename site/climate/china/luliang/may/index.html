How May compares in Lüliang

Lüliang in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lüliang in May?
In May, Lüliang averages a day high of 23.4 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 10.9 °C (52 °F), with 40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation and about 264 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Lüliang?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Lüliang’s mild months (May, June and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lüliang in May?
40 mm (1.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (111 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Lüliang in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lüliang get in May?
About 264 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lüliang in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
