How December compares in Lüliang

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Lüliang. Compared with November: 6.8 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. January is 1.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Lüliang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lüliang in December?
In December, Lüliang averages a day high of 0.9 °C (34 °F) and a night low of -9.1 °C (16 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 180 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Lüliang?
December is not one of Lüliang’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lüliang in December?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (111 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Lüliang in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 7.4 cm (2.9 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Lüliang get in December?
About 180 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lüliang in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
