Lüliang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lüliang in April?
In April, Lüliang averages a day high of 17.8 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 4.7 °C (40 °F), with 24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Lüliang?
April is not one of Lüliang’s mild months; those are May, June, July and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lüliang in April?
24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (107 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Lüliang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lüliang get in April?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lüliang in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
