How August compares in Lüliang

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Lüliang. Compared with July: 1.9 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. September is 5.1 °C cooler than August on average.

Lüliang in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lüliang in August?
In August, Lüliang averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 17.0 °C (63 °F), with 107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Lüliang?
August is not one of Lüliang’s mild months; those are May, June, July and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lüliang in August?
107 mm (4.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (107 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
