How January compares in Ziyang

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Ziyang. Compared with December: 1.2 °C cooler, 2 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. February is 2.1 °C warmer than January on average.

Ziyang in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ziyang in January?
In January, Ziyang averages a day high of 10.7 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 3.6 °C (38 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 69 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Ziyang?
January is not one of Ziyang’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ziyang in January?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (210 mm), the driest December (11 mm).
Does it snow in Ziyang in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.6 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ziyang get in January?
About 69 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ziyang in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
