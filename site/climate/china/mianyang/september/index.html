How September compares in Mianyang

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Mianyang. Compared with August: 3.7 °C cooler, 69 mm less precipitation and 86 hours less sunshine. October is 4.6 °C cooler than September on average.

Mianyang in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mianyang in September?
In September, Mianyang averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 18.3 °C (65 °F), with 151 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 100 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Mianyang?
September is not one of Mianyang’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mianyang in September?
151 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (232 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Mianyang in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mianyang get in September?
About 100 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mianyang in September?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
