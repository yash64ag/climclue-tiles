Mianyang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Mianyang is 241st warmest (17.2 °C year average), 642nd sunniest (about 1,394 hours of sunshine a year) and 367th wettest (956 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Luojiang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (25 km away), Mianyang is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 32 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.4 °C), averages 20.0 °C more than its coolest, January (6.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 1,160 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Mianyang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Mianyang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 1,160 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
