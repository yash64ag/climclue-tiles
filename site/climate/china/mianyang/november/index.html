How November compares in Mianyang

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Mianyang. Compared with October: 4.7 °C cooler, 28 mm less precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. December is 5.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Mianyang in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mianyang in November?
In November, Mianyang averages a day high of 15.6 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 8.7 °C (48 °F), with 15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 74 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Mianyang?
November is not one of Mianyang’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mianyang in November?
15 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (232 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Mianyang in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mianyang get in November?
About 74 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mianyang in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
