How December compares in Mianyang, Sichuan

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the least sunny month in Mianyang, Sichuan. Compared with November: 5.1 °C cooler, 7 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. January is 1.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Mianyang, Sichuan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mianyang, Sichuan in December?
In December, Mianyang, Sichuan averages a day high of 11.5 °C (53 °F) and a night low of 4.0 °C (39 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 73 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Mianyang, Sichuan?
December is not one of Mianyang, Sichuan’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mianyang, Sichuan in December?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (224 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Mianyang, Sichuan in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mianyang, Sichuan get in December?
About 73 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mianyang, Sichuan in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
