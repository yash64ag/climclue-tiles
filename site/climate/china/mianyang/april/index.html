Mianyang, Sichuan in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mianyang, Sichuan in April?
In April, Mianyang, Sichuan averages a day high of 23.0 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 13.4 °C (56 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Mianyang, Sichuan?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Mianyang, Sichuan’s mild months (April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
