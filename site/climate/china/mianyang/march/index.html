Mianyang, Sichuan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mianyang, Sichuan in March?
In March, Mianyang, Sichuan averages a day high of 17.3 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 8.6 °C (47 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 110 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Mianyang, Sichuan?
March is not one of Mianyang, Sichuan’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mianyang, Sichuan in March?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (224 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Mianyang, Sichuan in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mianyang, Sichuan get in March?
About 110 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mianyang, Sichuan in March?
