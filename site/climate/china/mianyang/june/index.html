How June compares in Mianyang

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Mianyang. Compared with May: 2.5 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. July is 1.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Mianyang in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mianyang in June?
In June, Mianyang averages a day high of 28.6 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.4 °C (69 °F), with 108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Mianyang?
June is not one of Mianyang’s mild months; those are April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mianyang in June?
108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (232 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Mianyang in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mianyang get in June?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mianyang in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
