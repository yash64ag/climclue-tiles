How October compares in Mianyang

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Mianyang. Compared with September: 4.6 °C cooler, 108 mm less precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. November is 4.7 °C cooler than October on average.

Mianyang in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Mianyang in October?
In October, Mianyang averages a day high of 20.1 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 13.8 °C (57 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 83 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Mianyang?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Mianyang’s mild months (April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Mianyang in October?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (232 mm), the driest December (7 mm).
Does it snow in Mianyang in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Mianyang get in October?
About 83 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Mianyang in October?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
