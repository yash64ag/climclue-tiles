Tantou, Fujian sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Fujian · China

The sea off Tantou, Fujian averages 27.2 °C (81 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 12.0 °C (54 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October.

Tantou, Fujian sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Tantou, Fujian?
In August, at about 27.2 °C (81 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Tantou, Fujian?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from June to October. The coolest month is February, at 12.0 °C (54 °F).
How warm is the sea in Tantou, Fujian in February?
About 12.0 °C (54 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Tantou, Fujian?
About 20.1 °C (68 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Tantou, Fujian nearby?
In August: Hsinchu (28.5 °C, 194 km), Zhubei (28.5 °C, 194 km) and Toufen (28.4 °C, 199 km).
