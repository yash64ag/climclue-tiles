How January compares in Suzhou, Jiangsu

January is the coolest month, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Suzhou, Jiangsu. Compared with December: 2.3 °C cooler, 27 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. February is 1.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Suzhou, Jiangsu in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suzhou, Jiangsu in January?
In January, Suzhou, Jiangsu averages a day high of 7.9 °C (46 °F) and a night low of 0.3 °C (33 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Suzhou, Jiangsu?
January is not one of Suzhou, Jiangsu’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suzhou, Jiangsu in January?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (201 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Suzhou, Jiangsu in January?
Yes, on average: about 6.9 cm (2.7 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Suzhou, Jiangsu get in January?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suzhou, Jiangsu in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
