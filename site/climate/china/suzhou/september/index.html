How September compares in Suzhou, Jiangsu

Suzhou, Jiangsu in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suzhou, Jiangsu in September?
In September, Suzhou, Jiangsu averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 21.0 °C (70 °F), with 96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Suzhou, Jiangsu?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Suzhou, Jiangsu’s mild months (April, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suzhou, Jiangsu in September?
96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (200 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Suzhou, Jiangsu in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suzhou, Jiangsu get in September?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suzhou, Jiangsu in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
