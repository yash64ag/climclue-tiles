How February compares in Suzhou, Jiangsu

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Suzhou, Jiangsu. Compared with January: 1.9 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. March is 3.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Suzhou, Jiangsu in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suzhou, Jiangsu in February?
In February, Suzhou, Jiangsu averages a day high of 9.7 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 2.3 °C (36 °F), with 63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 117 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Suzhou, Jiangsu?
February is not one of Suzhou, Jiangsu’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suzhou, Jiangsu in February?
63 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (201 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Suzhou, Jiangsu in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.6 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Suzhou, Jiangsu get in February?
About 117 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suzhou, Jiangsu in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
