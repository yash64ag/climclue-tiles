Suzhou, Jiangsu in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suzhou, Jiangsu in April?
In April, Suzhou, Jiangsu averages a day high of 20.0 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 85 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Suzhou, Jiangsu?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Suzhou, Jiangsu’s mild months (April, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suzhou, Jiangsu in April?
85 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (201 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Suzhou, Jiangsu in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suzhou, Jiangsu get in April?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suzhou, Jiangsu in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
