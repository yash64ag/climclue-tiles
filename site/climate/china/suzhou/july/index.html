How July compares in Suzhou, Jiangsu

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Suzhou, Jiangsu. Compared with June: 4.0 °C warmer, 22 mm less precipitation and 55 hours more sunshine. August is 0.6 °C cooler than July on average.

Suzhou, Jiangsu in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suzhou, Jiangsu in July?
In July, Suzhou, Jiangsu averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 25.8 °C (78 °F), with 179 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Suzhou, Jiangsu?
July is not one of Suzhou, Jiangsu’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suzhou, Jiangsu in July?
179 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (200 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Suzhou, Jiangsu in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suzhou, Jiangsu get in July?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suzhou, Jiangsu in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
