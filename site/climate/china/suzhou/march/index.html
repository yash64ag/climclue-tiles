How March compares in Suzhou, Jiangsu

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Suzhou, Jiangsu. Compared with February: 3.9 °C warmer, 31 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. April is 5.7 °C warmer than March on average.

Suzhou, Jiangsu in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suzhou, Jiangsu in March?
In March, Suzhou, Jiangsu averages a day high of 13.9 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 5.8 °C (42 °F), with 94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 139 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Suzhou, Jiangsu?
March is not one of Suzhou, Jiangsu’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suzhou, Jiangsu in March?
94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (201 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Suzhou, Jiangsu in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suzhou, Jiangsu get in March?
About 139 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
