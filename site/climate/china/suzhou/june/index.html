How June compares in Suzhou, Jiangsu

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Suzhou, Jiangsu. Compared with May: 3.7 °C warmer, 94 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. July is 4.0 °C warmer than June on average.

Suzhou, Jiangsu in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suzhou, Jiangsu in June?
In June, Suzhou, Jiangsu averages a day high of 28.4 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.8 °C (69 °F), with 201 mm (7.9 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Suzhou, Jiangsu?
June is not one of Suzhou, Jiangsu’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
