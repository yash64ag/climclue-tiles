Zitong at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Zitong is 172nd warmest (18.2 °C year average), 672nd sunniest (about 1,245 hours of sunshine a year) and 311th wettest (1,154 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bachuan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (42 km away), Zitong is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 142 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 20.1 °C more than its coolest, January (7.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 986 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Zitong, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Zitong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 986 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
