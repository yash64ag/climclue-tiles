Lishui at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Lishui is 177th warmest (18.2 °C year average), 522nd sunniest (about 1,838 hours of sunshine a year) and 154th wettest (1,554 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Guli, the nearest covered town with different numbers (48 km away), Lishui is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 31 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.3 °C), averages 22.9 °C more than its coolest, January (6.3 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 117 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Lishui, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Lishui has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 117 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 22.9 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.2 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
