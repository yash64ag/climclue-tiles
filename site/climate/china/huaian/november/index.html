How November compares in Huai'an

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Huai'an. Compared with October: 6.9 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. December is 6.2 °C cooler than November on average.

Huai'an in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huai'an in November?
In November, Huai'an averages a day high of 15.0 °C (59 °F) and a night low of 5.2 °C (41 °F), with 42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Huai'an?
November is not one of Huai'an’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huai'an in November?
42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (242 mm), the driest December (20 mm).
Does it snow in Huai'an in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huai'an get in November?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huai'an in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
