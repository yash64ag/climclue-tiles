How July compares in Huai'an

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Huai'an. Compared with June: 2.9 °C warmer, 98 mm more precipitation and 9 hours less sunshine. August is 0.7 °C cooler than July on average.

Huai'an in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huai'an in July?
In July, Huai'an averages a day high of 30.8 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 24.1 °C (75 °F), with 242 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation and about 194 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Huai'an?
July is not one of Huai'an’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huai'an in July?
242 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (242 mm), the driest December (20 mm).
Does it snow in Huai'an in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huai'an get in July?
About 194 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huai'an in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
