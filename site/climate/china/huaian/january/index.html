How January compares in Huai'an

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Huai'an. Compared with December: 2.2 °C cooler, 8 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. February is 2.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Huai'an in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huai'an in January?
In January, Huai'an averages a day high of 5.6 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -3.0 °C (27 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Huai'an?
January is not one of Huai'an’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huai'an in January?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (244 mm), the driest December (19 mm).
Does it snow in Huai'an in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 5.3 cm (2.1 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Huai'an get in January?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huai'an in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
