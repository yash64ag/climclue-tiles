How May compares in Huai'an

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Huai'an. Compared with April: 5.7 °C warmer, 20 mm more precipitation and 30 hours more sunshine. June is 4.0 °C warmer than May on average.

Huai'an in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huai'an in May?
In May, Huai'an averages a day high of 25.3 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Huai'an?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Huai'an’s mild months (May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huai'an in May?
66 mm (2.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (242 mm), the driest December (20 mm).
Does it snow in Huai'an in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huai'an get in May?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huai'an in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
