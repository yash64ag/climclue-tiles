How April compares in Huai'an

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Huai'an. Compared with March: 6.4 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 14 hours more sunshine. May is 5.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Huai'an in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Huai'an in April?
In April, Huai'an averages a day high of 20.0 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 9.6 °C (49 °F), with 46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Huai'an?
April is not one of Huai'an’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Huai'an in April?
46 mm (1.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (244 mm), the driest December (19 mm).
Does it snow in Huai'an in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Huai'an get in April?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Huai'an in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
