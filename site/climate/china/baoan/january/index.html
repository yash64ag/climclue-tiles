How January compares in Bao'an

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Bao'an. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. February is 1.0 °C warmer than January on average.

Bao'an in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bao'an in January?
In January, Bao'an averages a day high of 18.4 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 12.4 °C (54 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Bao'an?
For mild weather, yes: January is one of Bao'an’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bao'an in January?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (398 mm), the driest December (31 mm).
Does it snow in Bao'an in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bao'an get in January?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bao'an in January?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
