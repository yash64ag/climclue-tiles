Bao'an weather in May

Average May weather in Bao'an, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In May, Bao'an averages 28.9 °C (84 °F) days and 22.5 °C (73 °F) nights, with 282 mm (11.1 in) of rain over the month and about 151 hours of sunshine.

How May compares in Bao'an

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Bao'an. Compared with April: 3.1 °C warmer, 110 mm more precipitation and 44 hours more sunshine. June is 1.8 °C warmer than May on average.

Bao'an in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bao'an in May?
In May, Bao'an averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 282 mm (11.1 in) of precipitation and about 151 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Bao'an?
May is not one of Bao'an’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bao'an in May?
282 mm (11.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (393 mm), the driest December (31 mm).
Does it snow in Bao'an in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bao'an get in May?
About 151 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Bao'an in May?
About 25.5 °C (78 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (28.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Bao'an in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
