How December compares in Bao'an

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Bao'an. Compared with November: 4.1 °C cooler, 16 mm less precipitation and 1 hours less sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Bao'an in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bao'an in December?
In December, Bao'an averages a day high of 19.9 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 13.6 °C (56 °F), with 31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Bao'an?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Bao'an’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bao'an in December?
31 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (398 mm), the driest December (31 mm).
Does it snow in Bao'an in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bao'an get in December?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bao'an in December?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
