How March compares in Bao'an

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Bao'an. Compared with February: 2.6 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. April is 3.5 °C warmer than March on average.

Bao'an in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bao'an in March?
In March, Bao'an averages a day high of 21.8 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 16.7 °C (62 °F), with 81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation and about 97 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Bao'an?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Bao'an’s mild months (January, February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bao'an in March?
81 mm (3.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (398 mm), the driest December (31 mm).
Does it snow in Bao'an in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bao'an get in March?
About 97 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Bao'an in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
