How July compares in Bao'an

July is the warmest month, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Bao'an. Compared with June: 0.8 °C warmer, 45 mm less precipitation and 66 hours more sunshine. August is 0.1 °C cooler than July on average.

Bao'an in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Bao'an in July?
In July, Bao'an averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 25.2 °C (77 °F), with 349 mm (13.7 in) of precipitation and about 227 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Bao'an?
July is not one of Bao'an’s mild months; those are January, February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Bao'an in July?
349 mm (13.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (393 mm), the driest December (31 mm).
Does it snow in Bao'an in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Bao'an get in July?
About 227 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Bao'an in July?
About 28.5 °C (83 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (28.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Bao'an in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
