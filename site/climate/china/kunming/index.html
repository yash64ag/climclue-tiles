Kunming at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Kunming is 312th warmest (16.6 °C year average), 292nd sunniest (about 2,203 hours of sunshine a year) and 369th wettest (929 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Majie, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Kunming is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in December, with 202 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (21.2 °C), averages 11.3 °C more than its coolest, December (9.9 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.8 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 645 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.2 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 95% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Kunming has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 645 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Kunming itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (25.039°N, 102.718°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-589-2827), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
