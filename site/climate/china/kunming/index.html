Kunming at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Kunming is 317th warmest (16.4 °C year average), 292nd sunniest (about 2,203 hours of sunshine a year) and 362nd wettest (974 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Majie, the nearest covered town with different numbers (8 km away), Kunming is 0.2 °C warmer in June and 0.2 °C warmer in December, with 94 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (20.9 °C), averages 11.3 °C more than its coolest, December (9.7 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 4.5 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 645 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 4 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.0 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 99% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Kunming has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 645 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
