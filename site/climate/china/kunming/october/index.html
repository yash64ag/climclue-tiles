How October compares in Kunming

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Kunming. Compared with September: 2.4 °C cooler, 33 mm less precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. November is 3.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Kunming in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kunming in October?
In October, Kunming averages a day high of 20.9 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 12.6 °C (55 °F), with 77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Kunming?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Kunming’s mild months (April, May and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kunming in October?
77 mm (3.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (190 mm), the driest December (15 mm).
Does it snow in Kunming in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kunming get in October?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kunming in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
