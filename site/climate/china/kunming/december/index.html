How December compares in Kunming

December is the coolest month, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Kunming. Compared with November: 3.2 °C cooler, 22 mm less precipitation and 25 hours more sunshine. January is 0.2 °C warmer than December on average.

Kunming in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kunming in December?
In December, Kunming averages a day high of 13.6 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 4.7 °C (40 °F), with 16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation and about 197 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Kunming?
December is not one of Kunming’s mild months; those are March, April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kunming in December?
16 mm (0.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (201 mm), the driest December (16 mm).
Does it snow in Kunming in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kunming get in December?
About 197 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kunming in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
