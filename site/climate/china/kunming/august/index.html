How August compares in Kunming

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Kunming. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 18 hours more sunshine. September is 1.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Kunming in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Kunming in August?
In August, Kunming averages a day high of 24.8 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Kunming?
August is not one of Kunming’s mild months; those are March, April, May and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Kunming in August?
172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (190 mm), the driest December (15 mm).
Does it snow in Kunming in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Kunming get in August?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Kunming in August?
Going by the averages: light layers, a rain jacket or umbrella and breathable fabrics (humid air). Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
