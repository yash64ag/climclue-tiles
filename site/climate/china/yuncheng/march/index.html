Yuncheng in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yuncheng in March?
In March, Yuncheng averages a day high of 15.4 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 3.6 °C (38 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Yuncheng?
March is not one of Yuncheng’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yuncheng in March?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (98 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Yuncheng in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yuncheng get in March?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yuncheng in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
