How December compares in Yuncheng

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Yuncheng. Compared with November: 6.3 °C cooler, 13 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. January is 1.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Yuncheng in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yuncheng in December?
In December, Yuncheng averages a day high of 6.6 °C (44 °F) and a night low of -3.2 °C (26 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 146 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Yuncheng?
December is not one of Yuncheng’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yuncheng in December?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (97 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Yuncheng in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.5 cm (2.6 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.1 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Yuncheng get in December?
About 146 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yuncheng in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
