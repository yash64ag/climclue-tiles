Yuncheng at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Yuncheng is 383rd warmest (15.1 °C year average), 301st sunniest (about 2,176 hours of sunshine a year) and 592nd wettest (500 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Sanmenxia, the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Yuncheng is 1.1 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 46 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 27.8 °C more than its coolest, January (0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 714 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 6 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.9 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 96% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Yuncheng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 714 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
