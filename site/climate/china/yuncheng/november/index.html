How November compares in Yuncheng

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Yuncheng. Compared with October: 7.4 °C cooler, 31 mm less precipitation and 19 hours less sunshine. December is 6.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Yuncheng in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yuncheng in November?
In November, Yuncheng averages a day high of 13.0 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 2.6 °C (37 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 147 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Yuncheng?
November is not one of Yuncheng’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yuncheng in November?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (97 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Yuncheng in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yuncheng get in November?
About 147 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yuncheng in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
