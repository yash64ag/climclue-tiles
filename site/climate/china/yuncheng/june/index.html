How June compares in Yuncheng

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Yuncheng. Compared with May: 4.5 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. July is 1.2 °C warmer than June on average.

Yuncheng in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Yuncheng in June?
In June, Yuncheng averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 20.7 °C (69 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Yuncheng?
June is not one of Yuncheng’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Yuncheng in June?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (97 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Yuncheng in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Yuncheng get in June?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Yuncheng in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
