How February compares in Xinmin

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Xinmin. Compared with January: 4.8 °C warmer, 1 mm more precipitation and 10 hours more sunshine. March is 7.6 °C warmer than February on average.

Xinmin in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xinmin in February?
In February, Xinmin averages a day high of -0.5 °C (31 °F) and a night low of -12.3 °C (10 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 184 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Xinmin?
February is not one of Xinmin’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xinmin in February?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (159 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Xinmin in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.0 cm (1.6 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Xinmin get in February?
About 184 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xinmin in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
