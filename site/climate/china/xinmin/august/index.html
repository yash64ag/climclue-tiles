How August compares in Xinmin

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Xinmin. Compared with July: 1.1 °C cooler, 26 mm less precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. September is 5.7 °C cooler than August on average.

Xinmin in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xinmin in August?
In August, Xinmin averages a day high of 27.9 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 19.6 °C (67 °F), with 135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Xinmin?
August is not one of Xinmin’s mild months; those are May, June and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xinmin in August?
135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (160 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Xinmin in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Xinmin get in August?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xinmin in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
