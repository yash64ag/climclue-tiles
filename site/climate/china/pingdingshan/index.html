Pingdingshan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Pingdingshan is 378th warmest (15.2 °C year average), 315th sunniest (about 2,132 hours of sunshine a year) and 404th wettest (816 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xuchang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (61 km away), Pingdingshan is 0.3 °C cooler in July and about as warm in January, with 61 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.3 °C), averages 26.1 °C more than its coolest, January (1.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 556 km from the sea.

Why Pingdingshan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 556 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
