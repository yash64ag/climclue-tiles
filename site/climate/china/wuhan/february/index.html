How February compares in Wuhan

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Wuhan. Compared with January: 2.6 °C warmer, 17 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. March is 4.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Wuhan in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuhan in February?
In February, Wuhan averages a day high of 11.0 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 3.0 °C (37 °F), with 79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 106 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Wuhan?
February is not one of Wuhan’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuhan in February?
79 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (262 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Wuhan in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 4.2 cm (1.7 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuhan get in February?
About 106 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuhan in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
