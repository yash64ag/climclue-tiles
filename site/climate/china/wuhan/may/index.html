How May compares in Wuhan

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Wuhan. Compared with April: 5.1 °C warmer, 28 mm more precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. June is 3.5 °C warmer than May on average.

Wuhan in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuhan in May?
In May, Wuhan averages a day high of 26.4 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 18.8 °C (66 °F), with 188 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Wuhan?
May is not one of Wuhan’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuhan in May?
188 mm (7.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (262 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Wuhan in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuhan get in May?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuhan in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
