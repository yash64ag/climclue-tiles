How November compares in Wuhan

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Wuhan. Compared with October: 6.3 °C cooler, 29 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. December is 5.7 °C cooler than November on average.

Wuhan in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuhan in November?
In November, Wuhan averages a day high of 16.6 °C (62 °F) and a night low of 8.4 °C (47 °F), with 71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 140 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Wuhan?
November is not one of Wuhan’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuhan in November?
71 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (262 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Wuhan in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.5 cm (0.2 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuhan get in November?
About 140 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuhan in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
