How December compares in Wuhan

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Wuhan. Compared with November: 5.7 °C cooler, 35 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. January is 2.2 °C cooler than December on average.

Wuhan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Wuhan in December?
In December, Wuhan averages a day high of 11.1 °C (52 °F) and a night low of 2.5 °C (37 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 137 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Wuhan?
December is not one of Wuhan’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Wuhan in December?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (263 mm), the driest December (35 mm).
Does it snow in Wuhan in December?
Yes, on average: about 2.1 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Wuhan get in December?
About 137 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Wuhan in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
