How January compares in Changsha, Hunan

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Changsha, Hunan. Compared with December: 2.2 °C cooler, 39 mm more precipitation and 22 hours less sunshine. February is 2.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Changsha, Hunan in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changsha, Hunan in January?
In January, Changsha, Hunan averages a day high of 9.5 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 2.0 °C (36 °F), with 94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 81 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Changsha, Hunan?
January is not one of Changsha, Hunan’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changsha, Hunan in January?
94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (253 mm), the driest December (55 mm).
Does it snow in Changsha, Hunan in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.6 cm (3.4 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Changsha, Hunan get in January?
About 81 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Changsha, Hunan in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a compact umbrella and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
