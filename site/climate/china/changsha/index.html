Changsha, Hunan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Changsha, Hunan is 176th warmest (18.2 °C year average), 599th sunniest (about 1,589 hours of sunshine a year) and 116th wettest (1,681 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xiangtan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (39 km away), Changsha, Hunan is 0.4 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 129 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.8 °C), averages 24.0 °C more than its coolest, January (5.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 2.0 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 627 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 11 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.2 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 114% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Changsha, Hunan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 627 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.0 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
