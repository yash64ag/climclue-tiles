How December compares in Changsha, Hunan

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Changsha, Hunan. Compared with November: 5.5 °C cooler, 42 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. January is 2.3 °C cooler than December on average.

Changsha, Hunan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changsha, Hunan in December?
In December, Changsha, Hunan averages a day high of 12.2 °C (54 °F) and a night low of 3.8 °C (39 °F), with 55 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 103 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Changsha, Hunan?
December is not one of Changsha, Hunan’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changsha, Hunan in December?
55 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (252 mm), the driest December (55 mm).
Does it snow in Changsha, Hunan in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.7 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Changsha, Hunan get in December?
About 103 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Changsha, Hunan in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
