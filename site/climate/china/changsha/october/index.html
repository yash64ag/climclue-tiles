Changsha, Hunan weather in October

Average October weather in Changsha, Hunan, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Changsha, Hunan averages 23.2 °C (74 °F) days and 15.6 °C (60 °F) nights, with 94 mm (3.7 in) of rain over the month and about 132 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Changsha, Hunan

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Changsha, Hunan. Compared with September: 5.6 °C cooler, 9 mm more precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. November is 5.8 °C cooler than October on average.

Changsha, Hunan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changsha, Hunan in October?
In October, Changsha, Hunan averages a day high of 23.2 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 15.6 °C (60 °F), with 94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation and about 132 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Changsha, Hunan?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Changsha, Hunan’s mild months (September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changsha, Hunan in October?
94 mm (3.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (253 mm), the driest December (55 mm).
Does it snow in Changsha, Hunan in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
