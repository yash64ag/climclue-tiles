How November compares in Changsha, Hunan

Changsha, Hunan in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Changsha, Hunan in November?
In November, Changsha, Hunan averages a day high of 17.6 °C (64 °F) and a night low of 9.2 °C (49 °F), with 96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation and about 110 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Changsha, Hunan?
November is not one of Changsha, Hunan’s mild months; those are September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Changsha, Hunan in November?
96 mm (3.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (252 mm), the driest December (55 mm).
Does it snow in Changsha, Hunan in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Changsha, Hunan get in November?
About 110 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Changsha, Hunan in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
