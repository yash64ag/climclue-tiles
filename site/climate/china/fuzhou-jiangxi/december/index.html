How December compares in Fuzhou, Jiangxi

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Fuzhou, Jiangxi. Compared with November: 5.9 °C cooler, 32 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. January is 1.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Fuzhou, Jiangxi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fuzhou, Jiangxi in December?
In December, Fuzhou, Jiangxi averages a day high of 12.8 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 4.6 °C (40 °F), with 56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 133 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Fuzhou, Jiangxi?
December is not one of Fuzhou, Jiangxi’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.

56 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (353 mm), the driest December (56 mm).
