Fuzhou, Jiangxi weather in July

Average July weather in Fuzhou, Jiangxi, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

How July compares in Fuzhou, Jiangxi

July is the warmest month, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Fuzhou, Jiangxi. Compared with June: 3.5 °C warmer, 193 mm less precipitation and 99 hours more sunshine. August is 0.8 °C cooler than July on average.
