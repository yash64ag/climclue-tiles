Fuzhou, Jiangxi in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fuzhou, Jiangxi in May?
In May, Fuzhou, Jiangxi averages a day high of 27.2 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 19.4 °C (67 °F), with 274 mm (10.8 in) of precipitation and about 144 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Fuzhou, Jiangxi?
May is not one of Fuzhou, Jiangxi’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fuzhou, Jiangxi in May?
274 mm (10.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (353 mm), the driest December (56 mm).
Does it snow in Fuzhou, Jiangxi in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fuzhou, Jiangxi get in May?
About 144 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fuzhou, Jiangxi in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
