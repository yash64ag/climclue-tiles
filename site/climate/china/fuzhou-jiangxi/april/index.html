Fuzhou, Jiangxi in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fuzhou, Jiangxi in April?
In April, Fuzhou, Jiangxi averages a day high of 22.9 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 15.3 °C (60 °F), with 274 mm (10.8 in) of precipitation and about 116 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Fuzhou, Jiangxi?
April is not one of Fuzhou, Jiangxi’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fuzhou, Jiangxi in April?
274 mm (10.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (353 mm), the driest December (56 mm).
Does it snow in Fuzhou, Jiangxi in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fuzhou, Jiangxi get in April?
About 116 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fuzhou, Jiangxi in April?
