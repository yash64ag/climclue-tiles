Didao at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Didao is 660th warmest (4.3 °C year average), 239th sunniest (about 2,404 hours of sunshine a year) and 574th wettest (531 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jixi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (11 km away), Didao is about as warm in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 28 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (21.8 °C), averages 38.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-17.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 247 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 11 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.6 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 98% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Didao has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 247 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 38.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
