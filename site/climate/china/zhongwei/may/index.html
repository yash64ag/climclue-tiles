How May compares in Zhongwei

May is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Zhongwei. Compared with April: 5.7 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 29 hours more sunshine. June is 4.4 °C warmer than May on average.

Zhongwei in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhongwei in May?
In May, Zhongwei averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 10.8 °C (51 °F), with 19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 263 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Zhongwei?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Zhongwei’s mild months (May, June, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhongwei in May?
19 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (43 mm), the driest December (1 mm).
Does it snow in Zhongwei in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhongwei get in May?
About 263 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhongwei in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
