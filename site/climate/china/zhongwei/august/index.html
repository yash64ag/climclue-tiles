How August compares in Zhongwei

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Zhongwei. Compared with July: 2.1 °C cooler, 7 mm more precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. September is 5.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Zhongwei in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhongwei in August?
In August, Zhongwei averages a day high of 28.9 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 16.8 °C (62 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 254 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Zhongwei?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Zhongwei’s mild months (May, June, August and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhongwei in August?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is August (43 mm), the driest December (1 mm).
Does it snow in Zhongwei in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhongwei get in August?
About 254 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhongwei in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
