How February compares in Puning

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Puning. Compared with January: 1.0 °C warmer, 36 mm more precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. March is 2.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Puning in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Puning in February?
In February, Puning averages a day high of 18.7 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 11.9 °C (53 °F), with 60 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 97 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Puning?
For mild weather, yes: February is one of Puning’s mild months (February, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Puning in February?
60 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (299 mm), the driest January (23 mm).
Does it snow in Puning in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Puning get in February?
About 97 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Puning in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
