Puning weather in March

Average March weather in Puning, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Puning averages 21.3 °C (70 °F) days and 14.7 °C (58 °F) nights, with 101 mm (4.0 in) of rain over the month and about 105 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Puning

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Puning. Compared with February: 2.7 °C warmer, 41 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. April is 3.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Puning in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Puning in March?
In March, Puning averages a day high of 21.3 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 14.7 °C (58 °F), with 101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation and about 105 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Puning?
March is not one of Puning’s mild months; those are February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Puning in March?
101 mm (4.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (299 mm), the driest January (23 mm).
Does it snow in Puning in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Puning get in March?
About 105 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Puning in March?
