Liulin at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Liulin is 2026th warmest (4.1 °C year average), 766th sunniest (about 2,260 hours of sunshine a year) and 1644th wettest (574 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xincheng, Gansu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (12 km away), Liulin is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 1.4 °C warmer in January, with 93 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (14.9 °C), averages 24.1 °C more than its coolest, January (-9.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 1,337 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Liulin has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 1,337 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.1 °C. The 10 km area around Liulin, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 2.9 °C a year, 1.2 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Liulin itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.590°N, 103.504°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.2 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-494-2835), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
