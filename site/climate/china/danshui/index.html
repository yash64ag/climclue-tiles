Danshui at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Danshui is 52nd warmest (22.6 °C year average), 431st sunniest (about 1,944 hours of sunshine a year) and 55th wettest (1,995 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Longgang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Danshui is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 166 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 13.9 °C more than its coolest, January (14.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 10.8 °C (51 °F), in January. The area lies about 23 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Danshui, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Danshui has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 13.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
