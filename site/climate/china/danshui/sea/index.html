Danshui sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Guangdong · China

The sea off Danshui averages 28.1 °C (83 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 18.5 °C (65 °F) in January. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October.

Danshui sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Danshui?
In August, at about 28.1 °C (83 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Danshui?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from May to October. The coolest month is January, at 18.5 °C (65 °F).
How warm is the sea in Danshui in January?
About 18.5 °C (65 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Danshui?
About 23.7 °C (75 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Danshui nearby?
In August: Sheung Shui (28.7 °C, 48 km), Tai Po (28.7 °C, 49 km) and Tin Shui Wai (28.7 °C, 61 km).
