Baoshan, Yunnan in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baoshan, Yunnan in May?
In May, Baoshan, Yunnan averages a day high of 22.4 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 12.4 °C (54 °F), with 111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Baoshan, Yunnan?
May is not one of Baoshan, Yunnan’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baoshan, Yunnan in May?
111 mm (4.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (210 mm), the driest December (14 mm).
Does it snow in Baoshan, Yunnan in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baoshan, Yunnan get in May?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baoshan, Yunnan in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
