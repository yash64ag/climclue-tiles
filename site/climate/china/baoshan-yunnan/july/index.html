Baoshan, Yunnan in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baoshan, Yunnan in July?
In July, Baoshan, Yunnan averages a day high of 24.7 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 18.1 °C (65 °F), with 172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation and about 91 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Baoshan, Yunnan?
July is not one of Baoshan, Yunnan’s mild months; those are March, April and May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baoshan, Yunnan in July?
172 mm (6.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (184 mm), the driest December (12 mm).
Does it snow in Baoshan, Yunnan in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baoshan, Yunnan get in July?
About 91 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baoshan, Yunnan in July?
