Baoshan, Yunnan in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baoshan, Yunnan in November?
In November, Baoshan, Yunnan averages a day high of 16.2 °C (61 °F) and a night low of 6.4 °C (44 °F), with 43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 203 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Baoshan, Yunnan?
November is not one of Baoshan, Yunnan’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baoshan, Yunnan in November?
43 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (210 mm), the driest December (14 mm).
Does it snow in Baoshan, Yunnan in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baoshan, Yunnan get in November?
About 203 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baoshan, Yunnan in November?
