Baoshan, Yunnan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Baoshan, Yunnan is 379th warmest (15.2 °C year average), 320th sunniest (about 2,128 hours of sunshine a year) and 289th wettest (1,211 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tengyue, the nearest covered town with different numbers (67 km away), Baoshan, Yunnan is 0.8 °C warmer in June and 1.0 °C warmer in January, with 546 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (19.6 °C), averages 10.7 °C more than its coolest, January (8.8 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 820 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Baoshan, Yunnan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 820 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 10.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 6.7 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
