Baoshan, Yunnan weather in October

Average October weather in Baoshan, Yunnan, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

How October compares in Baoshan, Yunnan

Baoshan, Yunnan in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baoshan, Yunnan in October?
In October, Baoshan, Yunnan averages a day high of 23.7 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 13.5 °C (56 °F), with 120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation and about 168 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Baoshan, Yunnan?
October is not one of Baoshan, Yunnan’s mild months; those are March, April and May, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baoshan, Yunnan in October?
120 mm (4.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (184 mm), the driest December (12 mm).
Does it snow in Baoshan, Yunnan in October?
