Baoshan, Yunnan in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baoshan, Yunnan in June?
In June, Baoshan, Yunnan averages a day high of 21.9 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 15.0 °C (59 °F), with 165 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation and about 108 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Baoshan, Yunnan?
June is not one of Baoshan, Yunnan’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baoshan, Yunnan in June?
165 mm (6.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (210 mm), the driest December (14 mm).
Does it snow in Baoshan, Yunnan in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baoshan, Yunnan get in June?
About 108 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baoshan, Yunnan in June?
