How December compares in Baoshan, Yunnan

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Baoshan, Yunnan. Compared with November: 3.1 °C cooler, 29 mm less precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. January is 0.5 °C cooler than December on average.

Baoshan, Yunnan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baoshan, Yunnan in December?
In December, Baoshan, Yunnan averages a day high of 14.0 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 2.9 °C (37 °F), with 14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 234 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Baoshan, Yunnan?
December is not one of Baoshan, Yunnan’s mild months; those are April, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baoshan, Yunnan in December?
14 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (210 mm), the driest December (14 mm).
Does it snow in Baoshan, Yunnan in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baoshan, Yunnan get in December?
About 234 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baoshan, Yunnan in December?
