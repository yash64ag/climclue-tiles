Baoshan, Yunnan in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Baoshan, Yunnan in March?
In March, Baoshan, Yunnan averages a day high of 21.1 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 8.7 °C (48 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 229 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Baoshan, Yunnan?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Baoshan, Yunnan’s mild months (March, April and May), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Baoshan, Yunnan in March?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (184 mm), the driest December (12 mm).
Does it snow in Baoshan, Yunnan in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Baoshan, Yunnan get in March?
About 229 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Baoshan, Yunnan in March?
