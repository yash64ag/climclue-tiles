Ji’an, Jiangxi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ji’an, Jiangxi in December?
In December, Ji’an, Jiangxi averages a day high of 13.4 °C (56 °F) and a night low of 5.1 °C (41 °F), with 50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation and about 129 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Ji’an, Jiangxi?
December is not one of Ji’an, Jiangxi’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ji’an, Jiangxi in December?
50 mm (2.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (294 mm), the driest December (50 mm).
Does it snow in Ji’an, Jiangxi in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Ji’an, Jiangxi get in December?
About 129 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ji’an, Jiangxi in December?
