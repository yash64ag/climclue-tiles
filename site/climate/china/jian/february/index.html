How February compares in Ji’an

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Ji’an. Compared with January: 2.3 °C warmer, 33 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. March is 3.7 °C warmer than February on average.

Ji’an in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Ji’an in February?
In February, Ji’an averages a day high of 13.0 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 5.3 °C (42 °F), with 124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation and about 72 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Ji’an?
February is not one of Ji’an’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Ji’an in February?
124 mm (4.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (293 mm), the driest December (51 mm).
Does it snow in Ji’an in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 0.7 cm (0.3 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Ji’an get in February?
About 72 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Ji’an in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
