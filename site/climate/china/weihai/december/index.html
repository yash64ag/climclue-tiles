How December compares in Weihai

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Weihai. Compared with November: 6.1 °C cooler, 6 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. January is 3.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Weihai in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Weihai in December?
In December, Weihai averages a day high of 4.6 °C (40 °F) and a night low of 1.7 °C (35 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Weihai?
December is not one of Weihai’s mild months; those are June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Weihai in December?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (162 mm), the driest February (14 mm).
Does it snow in Weihai in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 19 cm (7.5 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 1.9 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Weihai get in December?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Weihai in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
