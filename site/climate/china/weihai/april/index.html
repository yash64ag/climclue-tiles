How April compares in Weihai

April is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Weihai. Compared with March: 4.9 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 4 hours more sunshine. May is 5.5 °C warmer than April on average.

Weihai in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Weihai in April?
In April, Weihai averages a day high of 10.4 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 7.0 °C (45 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 235 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Weihai?
April is not one of Weihai’s mild months; those are June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Weihai in April?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (162 mm), the driest February (14 mm).
Does it snow in Weihai in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Weihai get in April?
About 235 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Weihai in April?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
