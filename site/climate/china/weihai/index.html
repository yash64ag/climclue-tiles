Weihai at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Weihai is 510th warmest (12.0 °C year average), 149th sunniest (about 2,554 hours of sunshine a year) and 444th wettest (701 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tianfu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Weihai is 0.7 °C cooler in August and 2.0 °C warmer in January, with 21 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, August (24.3 °C), averages 24.1 °C more than its coolest, January (0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January and February. The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Weihai, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Weihai has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 24.1 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
