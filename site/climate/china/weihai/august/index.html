How August compares in Weihai

August is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Weihai. Compared with July: 1.0 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. September is 2.9 °C cooler than August on average.

Weihai in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Weihai in August?
In August, Weihai averages a day high of 25.4 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 161 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Weihai?
August is not one of Weihai’s mild months; those are June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Weihai in August?
161 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (162 mm), the driest February (14 mm).
Does it snow in Weihai in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Weihai get in August?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Weihai in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
