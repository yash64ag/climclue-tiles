How May compares in Weihai

May is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Weihai. Compared with April: 5.5 °C warmer, 22 mm more precipitation and 32 hours more sunshine. June is 5.2 °C warmer than May on average.

Weihai in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Weihai in May?
In May, Weihai averages a day high of 18.1 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 10.5 °C (51 °F), with 57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 267 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Weihai?
May is not one of Weihai’s mild months; those are June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Weihai in May?
57 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (165 mm), the driest February (14 mm).
Does it snow in Weihai in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Weihai get in May?
About 267 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Weihai in May?
About 11.9 °C (53 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, cold for most swimmers; its warmest month is August (24.2 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Weihai in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
