Linyi weather in April

Average April weather in Linyi, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Linyi averages 20.0 °C (68 °F) days and 8.6 °C (47 °F) nights, with 32 mm (1.3 in) of rain over the month and about 208 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Linyi

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Linyi. Compared with March: 7.0 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 11 hours more sunshine. May is 5.6 °C warmer than April on average.

Linyi in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Linyi in April?
In April, Linyi averages a day high of 20.0 °C (68 °F) and a night low of 8.6 °C (47 °F), with 32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 208 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Linyi?
April is not one of Linyi’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Linyi in April?
32 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (232 mm), the driest December (12 mm).
Does it snow in Linyi in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Linyi get in April?
About 208 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Linyi in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
