How December compares in Linyi

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Linyi. Compared with November: 6.3 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Linyi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Linyi in December?
In December, Linyi averages a day high of 6.7 °C (44 °F) and a night low of -3.1 °C (26 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Linyi?
December is not one of Linyi’s mild months; those are May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Linyi in December?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (232 mm), the driest December (12 mm).
Does it snow in Linyi in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.0 cm (1.2 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Linyi get in December?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Linyi in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
