How January compares in Zunyi

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Zunyi. Compared with December: 2.1 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. February is 1.9 °C warmer than January on average.

Zunyi in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zunyi in January?
In January, Zunyi averages a day high of 7.2 °C (45 °F) and a night low of 1.3 °C (34 °F), with 27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 50 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Zunyi?
January is not one of Zunyi’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zunyi in January?
27 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (193 mm), the driest December (23 mm).
Does it snow in Zunyi in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 6.1 cm (2.4 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Zunyi get in January?
About 50 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zunyi in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
