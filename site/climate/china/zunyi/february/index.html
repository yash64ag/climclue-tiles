Zunyi in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zunyi in February?
In February, Zunyi averages a day high of 9.8 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 4.2 °C (40 °F), with 24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 52 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Zunyi?
February is not one of Zunyi’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zunyi in February?
24 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (196 mm), the driest December (23 mm).
Does it snow in Zunyi in February?
Yes, on average: about 1.3 cm (0.5 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Zunyi get in February?
About 52 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zunyi in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
