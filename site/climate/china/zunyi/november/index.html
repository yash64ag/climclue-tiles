Zunyi in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zunyi in November?
In November, Zunyi averages a day high of 14.0 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 7.8 °C (46 °F), with 42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 69 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Zunyi?
November is not one of Zunyi’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zunyi in November?
42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (196 mm), the driest December (23 mm).
Does it snow in Zunyi in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zunyi get in November?
About 69 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zunyi in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
