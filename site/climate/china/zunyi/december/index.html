How December compares in Zunyi

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Zunyi. Compared with November: 5.0 °C cooler, 19 mm less precipitation and 13 hours less sunshine. January is 2.0 °C cooler than December on average.

Zunyi in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zunyi in December?
In December, Zunyi averages a day high of 10.1 °C (50 °F) and a night low of 4.4 °C (40 °F), with 23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 56 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Zunyi?
December is not one of Zunyi’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zunyi in December?
23 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (196 mm), the driest December (23 mm).
Does it snow in Zunyi in December?
Yes, on average: about 1.1 cm (0.4 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Zunyi get in December?
About 56 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zunyi in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
