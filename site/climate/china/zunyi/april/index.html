Zunyi weather in April

Average April weather in Zunyi, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Zunyi averages 20.4 °C (69 °F) days and 11.9 °C (53 °F) nights, with 86 mm (3.4 in) of rain over the month and about 117 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Zunyi

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Zunyi. Compared with March: 5.2 °C warmer, 46 mm more precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. May is 4.0 °C warmer than April on average.

Zunyi in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zunyi in April?
In April, Zunyi averages a day high of 20.4 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 11.9 °C (53 °F), with 86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation and about 117 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Zunyi?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Zunyi’s mild months (April, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zunyi in April?
86 mm (3.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (196 mm), the driest December (23 mm).
Does it snow in Zunyi in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zunyi get in April?
About 117 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zunyi in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
