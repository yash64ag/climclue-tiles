How May compares in Zunyi

Zunyi in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zunyi in May?
In May, Zunyi averages a day high of 23.0 °C (73 °F) and a night low of 15.2 °C (59 °F), with 147 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation and about 126 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Zunyi?
May is not one of Zunyi’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zunyi in May?
147 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (196 mm), the driest December (23 mm).
Does it snow in Zunyi in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zunyi get in May?
About 126 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zunyi in May?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
