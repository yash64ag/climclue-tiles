How July compares in Zunyi

July is the warmest month, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Zunyi. Compared with June: 2.4 °C warmer, 28 mm less precipitation and 65 hours more sunshine. August is 0.2 °C cooler than July on average.

Zunyi in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zunyi in July?
In July, Zunyi averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 20.9 °C (70 °F), with 168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation and about 190 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Zunyi?
July is not one of Zunyi’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zunyi in July?
168 mm (6.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (196 mm), the driest December (23 mm).
Does it snow in Zunyi in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zunyi get in July?
About 190 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zunyi in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
