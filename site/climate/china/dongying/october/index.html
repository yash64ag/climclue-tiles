How October compares in Dongying

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Dongying. Compared with September: 6.4 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. November is 8.2 °C cooler than October on average.

Dongying in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dongying in October?
In October, Dongying averages a day high of 20.6 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 10.3 °C (51 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Dongying?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Dongying’s mild months (May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dongying in October?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (153 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Dongying in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dongying get in October?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dongying in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
