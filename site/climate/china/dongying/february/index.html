Dongying weather in February

Average February weather in Dongying, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In February, Dongying averages 6.1 °C (43 °F) days and -4.7 °C (24 °F) nights, with 10 mm (0.4 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 178 hours of sunshine.

How February compares in Dongying

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Dongying. Compared with January: 2.9 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. March is 6.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Dongying in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dongying in February?
In February, Dongying averages a day high of 6.1 °C (43 °F) and a night low of -4.7 °C (24 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Dongying?
February is not one of Dongying’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dongying in February?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (153 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Dongying in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.8 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Dongying get in February?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dongying in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
