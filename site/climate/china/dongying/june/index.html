How June compares in Dongying

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Dongying. Compared with May: 4.5 °C warmer, 26 mm more precipitation and 14 hours less sunshine. July is 2.1 °C warmer than June on average.

Dongying in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dongying in June?
In June, Dongying averages a day high of 30.5 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation and about 256 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Dongying?
For mild weather, yes: June is one of Dongying’s mild months (May, June, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dongying in June?
78 mm (3.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (155 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Dongying in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dongying get in June?
About 256 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dongying in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
