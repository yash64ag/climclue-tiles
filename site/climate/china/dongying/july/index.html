How July compares in Dongying

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Dongying. Compared with June: 2.2 °C warmer, 75 mm more precipitation and 41 hours less sunshine. August is 1.0 °C cooler than July on average.

Dongying in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dongying in July?
In July, Dongying averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.5 °C (73 °F), with 153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation and about 215 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Dongying?
July is not one of Dongying’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dongying in July?
153 mm (6.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (153 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Dongying in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dongying get in July?
About 215 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dongying in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
