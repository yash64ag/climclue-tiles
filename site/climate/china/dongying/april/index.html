Dongying weather in April

Average April weather in Dongying, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Dongying averages 20.9 °C (70 °F) days and 8.2 °C (47 °F) nights, with 25 mm (1.0 in) of rain over the month and about 231 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Dongying

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Dongying. Compared with March: 7.9 °C warmer, 13 mm more precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. May is 5.7 °C warmer than April on average.

Dongying in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dongying in April?
In April, Dongying averages a day high of 20.9 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 8.2 °C (47 °F), with 25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 231 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Dongying?
April is not one of Dongying’s mild months; those are May, June, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dongying in April?
25 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (153 mm), the driest January (5 mm).
Does it snow in Dongying in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dongying get in April?
About 231 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dongying in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
