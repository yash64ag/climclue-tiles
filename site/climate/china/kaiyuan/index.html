Kaiyuan, Yunnan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Kaiyuan, Yunnan is 129th warmest (18.9 °C year average), 551st sunniest (about 1,776 hours of sunshine a year) and 393rd wettest (870 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Mengzi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (38 km away), Kaiyuan, Yunnan is 0.1 °C warmer in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 104 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (23.6 °C), averages 11.1 °C more than its coolest, January (12.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.2 °C (45 °F), in December. The area lies about 491 km from the sea.

Why Kaiyuan, Yunnan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 491 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 11.1 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 7.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
