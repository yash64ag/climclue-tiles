Zhaozhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Zhaozhou is 640th warmest (5.1 °C year average), 108th sunniest (about 2,628 hours of sunshine a year) and 605th wettest (475 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zhaodong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (66 km away), Zhaozhou is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.6 °C warmer in January, with 29 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (23.4 °C), averages 40.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-17.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 569 km from the sea.

Why Zhaozhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 569 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 40.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
