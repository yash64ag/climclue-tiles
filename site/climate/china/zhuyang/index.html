Zhuyang at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Zhuyang is 330th warmest (19.1 °C year average), 1891st sunniest (about 1,255 hours of sunshine a year) and 909th wettest (1,138 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shima, Chongqing (29.0°N 105.9°E), the nearest covered town with different numbers (9 km away), Zhuyang is 0.8 °C warmer in July and 0.8 °C warmer in January, with 152 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.7 °C), averages 20.0 °C more than its coolest, January (8.8 °C). Even the coolest nights average 6.6 °C (44 °F), in January. The area lies about 866 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Zhuyang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 866 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 20.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Zhuyang itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (29.064°N, 105.945°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.7 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-549-2859), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
