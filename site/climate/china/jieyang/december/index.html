How December compares in Jieyang

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Jieyang. Compared with November: 4.5 °C cooler, 1 mm more precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Jieyang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jieyang in December?
In December, Jieyang averages a day high of 20.6 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 10.1 °C (50 °F), with 33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 172 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jieyang?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Jieyang’s mild months (February, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jieyang in December?
33 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (306 mm), the driest January (27 mm).
Does it snow in Jieyang in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jieyang get in December?
About 172 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jieyang in December?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
