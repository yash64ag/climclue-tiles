Jieyang in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jieyang in August?
In August, Jieyang averages a day high of 32.1 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 295 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation and about 228 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Jieyang?
August is not one of Jieyang’s mild months; those are February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jieyang in August?
295 mm (11.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (302 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Jieyang in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jieyang get in August?
About 228 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jieyang in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
