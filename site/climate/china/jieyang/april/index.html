Jieyang in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jieyang in April?
In April, Jieyang averages a day high of 25.7 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 18.2 °C (65 °F), with 177 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation and about 115 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Jieyang?
April is not one of Jieyang’s mild months; those are February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jieyang in April?
177 mm (7.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (306 mm), the driest January (27 mm).
Does it snow in Jieyang in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jieyang get in April?
About 115 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jieyang in April?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
