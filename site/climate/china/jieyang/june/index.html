How June compares in Jieyang

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Jieyang. Compared with May: 2.3 °C warmer, 90 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. July is 1.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Jieyang in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jieyang in June?
In June, Jieyang averages a day high of 30.0 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 24.2 °C (76 °F), with 306 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Jieyang?
June is not one of Jieyang’s mild months; those are February, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jieyang in June?
306 mm (12.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (306 mm), the driest January (27 mm).
Does it snow in Jieyang in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jieyang get in June?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jieyang in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
