Jieyang weather in November

Average November weather in Jieyang, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In November, Jieyang averages 24.5 °C (76 °F) days and 16.0 °C (61 °F) nights, with 32 mm (1.2 in) of rain over the month and about 173 hours of sunshine.

How November compares in Jieyang

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Jieyang. Compared with October: 4.2 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 28 hours less sunshine. December is 4.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Jieyang in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jieyang in November?
In November, Jieyang averages a day high of 24.5 °C (76 °F) and a night low of 16.0 °C (61 °F), with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 173 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Jieyang?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Jieyang’s mild months (February, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jieyang in November?
32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (302 mm), the driest January (26 mm).
Does it snow in Jieyang in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jieyang get in November?
About 173 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jieyang in November?
