Jieyang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Jieyang is 65th warmest (22.3 °C year average), 394th sunniest (about 1,979 hours of sunshine a year) and 96th wettest (1,753 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chaozhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (29 km away), Jieyang is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 56 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 14.5 °C more than its coolest, January (14.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.2 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 60 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Jieyang, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Jieyang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 60 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 14.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
