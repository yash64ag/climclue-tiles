How November compares in Fushun

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Fushun. Compared with October: 9.9 °C cooler, 22 mm less precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. December is 8.1 °C cooler than November on average.

Fushun in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fushun in November?
In November, Fushun averages a day high of 5.4 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -5.6 °C (22 °F), with 22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation and about 164 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Fushun?
November is not one of Fushun’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fushun in November?
22 mm (0.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (206 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Fushun in November?
Yes, on average: about 8.0 cm (3.1 in) of fresh snow in November (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Fushun get in November?
About 164 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fushun in November?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
