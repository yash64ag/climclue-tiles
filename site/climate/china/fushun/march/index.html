How March compares in Fushun

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Fushun. Compared with February: 8.2 °C warmer, 14 mm more precipitation and 50 hours more sunshine. April is 8.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Fushun in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fushun in March?
In March, Fushun averages a day high of 7.5 °C (46 °F) and a night low of -4.1 °C (25 °F), with 24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Fushun?
March is not one of Fushun’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fushun in March?
24 mm (1.0 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (206 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Fushun in March?
Yes, on average: about 9.7 cm (3.8 in) of fresh snow in March (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Fushun get in March?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fushun in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
