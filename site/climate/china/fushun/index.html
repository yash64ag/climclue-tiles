Fushun at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Fushun is 588th warmest (8.5 °C year average), 174th sunniest (about 2,534 hours of sunshine a year) and 415th wettest (765 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Dongling, the nearest covered town with different numbers (32 km away), Fushun is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.4 °C cooler in January, with 33 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.6 °C), averages 36.5 °C more than its coolest, January (-11.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 207 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Fushun, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Fushun has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 207 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 36.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
