How June compares in Fushun

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Fushun. Compared with May: 4.9 °C warmer, 47 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. July is 2.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Fushun in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fushun in June?
In June, Fushun averages a day high of 26.7 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation and about 249 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Fushun?
June is not one of Fushun’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fushun in June?
105 mm (4.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (205 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Fushun in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fushun get in June?
About 249 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fushun in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
