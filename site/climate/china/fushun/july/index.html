How July compares in Fushun

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Fushun. Compared with June: 2.4 °C warmer, 101 mm more precipitation and 37 hours less sunshine. August is 1.0 °C cooler than July on average.

Fushun in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fushun in July?
In July, Fushun averages a day high of 28.1 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 20.5 °C (69 °F), with 205 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation and about 212 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Fushun?
July is not one of Fushun’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fushun in July?
205 mm (8.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (205 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Fushun in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fushun get in July?
About 212 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fushun in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
