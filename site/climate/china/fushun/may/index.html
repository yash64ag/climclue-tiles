How May compares in Fushun

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Fushun. Compared with April: 6.8 °C warmer, 15 mm more precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. June is 4.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Fushun in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Fushun in May?
In May, Fushun averages a day high of 21.9 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 11.0 °C (52 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 266 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Fushun?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Fushun’s mild months (May and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Fushun in May?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (205 mm), the driest January (8 mm).
Does it snow in Fushun in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Fushun get in May?
About 266 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Fushun in May?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
