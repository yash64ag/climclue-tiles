Qingyuan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Qingyuan is the 5th wettest (2,431 mm a year). Compared with Sanshui, the nearest covered town with different numbers (62 km away), Qingyuan is 1.1 °C cooler in July and 1.5 °C cooler in January, with 506 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.4 °C), averages 15.8 °C more than its coolest, January (12.7 °C). Even the coolest nights average 8.4 °C (47 °F), in January. The area lies about 147 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Qingyuan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Qingyuan

For mild weather, the best months to visit Qingyuan are October and November.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): October and November.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (28.4 °C), August (28.3 °C) and June (27.3 °C).
  • Driest (least precipitation): December (42 mm), November (54 mm) and January (68 mm).
  • Sunniest (hours of sunshine, about 1961–1990): July (230 h), August (207 h) and September (186 h).

Why Qingyuan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 147 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
