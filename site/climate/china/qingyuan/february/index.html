How February compares in Qingyuan

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Qingyuan. Compared with January: 1.4 °C warmer, 40 mm more precipitation and 52 hours less sunshine. March is 3.0 °C warmer than February on average.

Qingyuan in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qingyuan in February?
In February, Qingyuan averages a day high of 17.2 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 10.4 °C (51 °F), with 108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 68 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Qingyuan?
February is not one of Qingyuan’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qingyuan in February?
108 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (440 mm), the driest December (42 mm).
Does it snow in Qingyuan in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qingyuan get in February?
About 68 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qingyuan in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
