How September compares in Qingyuan

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Qingyuan. Compared with August: 1.4 °C cooler, 86 mm less precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. October is 3.2 °C cooler than September on average.

Qingyuan in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qingyuan in September?
In September, Qingyuan averages a day high of 30.2 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 23.5 °C (74 °F), with 141 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation and about 186 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Qingyuan?
September is not one of Qingyuan’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qingyuan in September?
141 mm (5.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (376 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Qingyuan in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qingyuan get in September?
About 186 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qingyuan in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
