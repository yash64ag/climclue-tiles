How December compares in Qingyuan

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Qingyuan. Compared with November: 4.5 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 8 hours less sunshine. January is 1.7 °C cooler than December on average.

Qingyuan in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Qingyuan in December?
In December, Qingyuan averages a day high of 18.3 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 9.2 °C (49 °F), with 42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Qingyuan?
December is not one of Qingyuan’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Qingyuan in December?
42 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (440 mm), the driest December (42 mm).
Does it snow in Qingyuan in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Qingyuan get in December?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Qingyuan in December?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
