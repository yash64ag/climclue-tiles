Jinzhou, Liaoning (39.1°N 121.7°E) sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Liaoning · China

The sea off Jinzhou, Liaoning (39.1°N 121.7°E) averages 23.9 °C (75 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 0.5 °C (33 °F) in February. It is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September.

Jinzhou, Liaoning (39.1°N 121.7°E) sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Jinzhou, Liaoning (39.1°N 121.7°E)?
In August, at about 23.9 °C (75 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Jinzhou, Liaoning (39.1°N 121.7°E)?
On average it is never warm by tropical standards; refreshing (20 °C / 68 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 0.5 °C (33 °F).
How warm is the sea in Jinzhou, Liaoning (39.1°N 121.7°E) in February?
About 0.5 °C (33 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Jinzhou, Liaoning (39.1°N 121.7°E)?
About 11.8 °C (53 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Jinzhou, Liaoning (39.1°N 121.7°E) nearby?
In August: Yingkou (25.5 °C, 179 km).
