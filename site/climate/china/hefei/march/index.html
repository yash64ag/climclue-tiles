How March compares in Hefei

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Hefei. Compared with February: 4.6 °C warmer, 27 mm more precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. April is 6.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Hefei in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hefei in March?
In March, Hefei averages a day high of 14.4 °C (58 °F) and a night low of 6.0 °C (43 °F), with 88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 142 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Hefei?
March is not one of Hefei’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hefei in March?
88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (191 mm), the driest December (34 mm).
Does it snow in Hefei in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hefei get in March?
About 142 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hefei in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
