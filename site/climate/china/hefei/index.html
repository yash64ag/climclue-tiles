Hefei at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Hefei is 316th warmest (16.4 °C year average), 350th sunniest (about 2,044 hours of sunshine a year) and 318th wettest (1,140 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Chaohu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (63 km away), Hefei is 0.1 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 67 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.6 °C), averages 25.5 °C more than its coolest, January (3.0 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 362 km from the sea.

Why Hefei has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 362 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 25.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
