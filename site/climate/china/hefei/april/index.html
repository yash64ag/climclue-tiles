Hefei weather in April

Average April weather in Hefei, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In April, Hefei averages 21.0 °C (70 °F) days and 12.1 °C (54 °F) nights, with 88 mm (3.5 in) of rain over the month and about 161 hours of sunshine.

How April compares in Hefei

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Hefei. Compared with March: 6.4 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 19 hours more sunshine. May is 5.3 °C warmer than April on average.

Hefei in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hefei in April?
In April, Hefei averages a day high of 21.0 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 12.1 °C (54 °F), with 88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Hefei?
For mild weather, yes: April is one of Hefei’s mild months (April, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hefei in April?
88 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (191 mm), the driest December (34 mm).
Does it snow in Hefei in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hefei get in April?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hefei in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
