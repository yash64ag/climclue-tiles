How July compares in Hefei

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Hefei. Compared with June: 3.1 °C warmer, 28 mm more precipitation and 27 hours more sunshine. August is 0.8 °C cooler than July on average.

Hefei in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Hefei in July?
In July, Hefei averages a day high of 31.5 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 194 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation and about 218 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Hefei?
July is not one of Hefei’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Hefei in July?
194 mm (7.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (194 mm), the driest December (34 mm).
Does it snow in Hefei in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Hefei get in July?
About 218 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Hefei in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
