How June compares in Putian

June is the 4th warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Putian. Compared with May: 3.3 °C warmer, 41 mm more precipitation and 23 hours more sunshine. July is 2.3 °C warmer than June on average.

Putian in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Putian in June?
In June, Putian averages a day high of 29.7 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 231 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation and about 157 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Putian?
June is not one of Putian’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Putian in June?
231 mm (9.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (231 mm), the driest December (31 mm).
Does it snow in Putian in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Putian get in June?
About 157 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
How warm is the sea in Putian in June?
About 25.1 °C (77 °F) on average in the nearby open sea, warm for swimming; its warmest month is August (27.7 °C). 1981–2010 averages (ERA5).
What should I pack for Putian in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
