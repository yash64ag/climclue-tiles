How August compares in Putian

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Putian. Compared with July: 0.2 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 21 hours less sunshine. September is 2.0 °C cooler than August on average.

Putian in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Putian in August?
In August, Putian averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 25.3 °C (78 °F), with 158 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation and about 233 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Putian?
August is not one of Putian’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Putian in August?
158 mm (6.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (215 mm), the driest December (29 mm).
Does it snow in Putian in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Putian get in August?
About 233 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Putian in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
