How September compares in Putian

September is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Putian. Compared with August: 2.0 °C cooler, 24 mm less precipitation and 47 hours less sunshine. October is 3.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Putian in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Putian in September?
In September, Putian averages a day high of 28.5 °C (83 °F) and a night low of 23.3 °C (74 °F), with 135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation and about 185 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Putian?
September is not one of Putian’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Putian in September?
135 mm (5.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (215 mm), the driest December (29 mm).
Does it snow in Putian in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Putian get in September?
About 185 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Putian in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
