How November compares in Putian

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Putian. Compared with October: 4.4 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 30 hours less sunshine. December is 4.8 °C cooler than November on average.

Putian in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Putian in November?
In November, Putian averages a day high of 21.2 °C (70 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation and about 136 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Putian?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Putian’s mild months (October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Putian in November?
34 mm (1.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (215 mm), the driest December (29 mm).
Does it snow in Putian in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Putian get in November?
About 136 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Putian in November?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
