How April compares in Putian

April is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Putian. Compared with March: 4.5 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 22 hours more sunshine. May is 4.1 °C warmer than April on average.

Putian in April: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Putian in April?
In April, Putian averages a day high of 22.0 °C (72 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 137 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 128 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is April a good time to visit Putian?
April is not one of Putian’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Putian in April?
137 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making April the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (215 mm), the driest December (29 mm).
Does it snow in Putian in April?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for April in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Putian get in April?
About 128 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Putian in April?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
