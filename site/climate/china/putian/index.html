Putian at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Putian is 100th warmest (20.5 °C year average), 503rd sunniest (about 1,859 hours of sunshine a year) and 247th wettest (1,324 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Quanzhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (72 km away), Putian is 0.2 °C cooler in July and 1.4 °C cooler in January, with 73 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.8 °C), averages 17.2 °C more than its coolest, January (11.6 °C). Even the coolest nights average 7.6 °C (46 °F), in January. The area lies about 32 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Putian, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Best time to visit Putian

For mild weather, the best months to visit Putian are October and November.

  • Mild (average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation): October and November.
  • Warmest (average temperature): July (28.8 °C), August (28.6 °C) and September (26.6 °C).

Why Putian has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 32 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.2 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
