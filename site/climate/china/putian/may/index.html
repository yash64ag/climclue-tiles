How May compares in Putian

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Putian. Compared with April: 4.1 °C warmer, 38 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. June is 3.3 °C warmer than May on average.

Putian in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Putian in May?
In May, Putian averages a day high of 25.5 °C (78 °F) and a night low of 19.8 °C (68 °F), with 175 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation and about 135 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Putian?
May is not one of Putian’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Putian in May?
175 mm (6.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (215 mm), the driest December (29 mm).
Does it snow in Putian in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Putian get in May?
About 135 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Putian in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
