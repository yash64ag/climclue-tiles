Lijiang at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Lijiang is 1464th warmest (13.3 °C year average), 797th sunniest (about 2,235 hours of sunshine a year) and 1111th wettest (951 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Huangshan, Yunnan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (5 km away), Lijiang is 0.1 °C cooler in June and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 131 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, June (18.7 °C), averages 12.0 °C more than its coolest, January (6.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January. The area lies about 962 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Lijiang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Summers are warm under persistent high pressure; nearby sea often softens winter cold. In summer the subtropical high pressure moves over and blocks storms, so summers are dry; in winter it retreats and storms from the ocean bring rain. About 962 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 12.0 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Lijiang itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.869°N, 100.221°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-571-2802), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
