How March compares in Xining

March is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Xining. Compared with February: 4.9 °C warmer, 7 mm more precipitation and 26 hours more sunshine. April is 5.4 °C warmer than March on average.

Xining in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xining in March?
In March, Xining averages a day high of 9.9 °C (50 °F) and a night low of -4.0 °C (25 °F), with 10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Xining?
March is not one of Xining’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xining in March?
10 mm (0.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (84 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Xining in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.6 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Xining get in March?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xining in March?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
