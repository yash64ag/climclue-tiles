Xining at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Xining is 620th warmest (6.3 °C year average), 51st sunniest (about 2,734 hours of sunshine a year) and 622nd wettest (433 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Qiaotou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Xining is 2.2 °C warmer in July and 3.8 °C warmer in January, with 170 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (16.9 °C), averages 23.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-6.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 1,416 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 1 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.8 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 109% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Xining has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 1,416 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
