Xining at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Xining is 617th warmest (6.7 °C year average), 51st sunniest (about 2,734 hours of sunshine a year) and 625th wettest (410 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Qiaotou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (35 km away), Xining is 1.9 °C warmer in July and 3.3 °C warmer in January, with 133 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (17.1 °C), averages 23.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-5.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, November and December. The area lies about 1,416 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 1 km away, agrees with these values: monthly temperatures within 1.2 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 103% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Xining has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 1,416 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.0 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.1 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Xining itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.626°N, 101.757°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-473-2817), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
