How February compares in Xining

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Xining. Compared with January: 3.6 °C warmer, about the same precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. March is 5.3 °C warmer than February on average.

Xining in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Xining in February?
In February, Xining averages a day high of 5.3 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -9.8 °C (14 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Xining?
February is not one of Xining’s mild months; those are June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Xining in February?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (84 mm), the driest December (2 mm).
Does it snow in Xining in February?
Yes, on average: about 2.7 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in February (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Xining get in February?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Xining in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
