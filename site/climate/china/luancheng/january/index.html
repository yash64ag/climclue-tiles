How January compares in Luancheng

January is the coolest month, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Luancheng. Compared with December: 1.7 °C cooler, about the same precipitation and 9 hours more sunshine. February is 3.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Luancheng in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Luancheng in January?
In January, Luancheng averages a day high of 3.7 °C (39 °F) and a night low of -6.6 °C (20 °F), with 4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 178 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Luancheng?
January is not one of Luancheng’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Luancheng in January?
4 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the driest month of the year. The wettest month is August (133 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Luancheng in January?
Yes, on average: about 3.5 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in January (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Luancheng get in January?
About 178 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Luancheng in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
