How September compares in Luancheng

September is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Luancheng. Compared with August: 4.6 °C cooler, 84 mm less precipitation and 11 hours less sunshine. October is 6.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Luancheng in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Luancheng in September?
In September, Luancheng averages a day high of 27.0 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 214 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Luancheng?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Luancheng’s mild months (April, May and September), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Luancheng in September?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is August (133 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Luancheng in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Luancheng get in September?
About 214 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Luancheng in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
