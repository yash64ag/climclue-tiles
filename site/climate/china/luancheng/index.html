Luancheng at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Luancheng is 433rd warmest (14.2 °C year average), 159th sunniest (about 2,549 hours of sunshine a year) and 600th wettest (483 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shijiazhuang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Luancheng is 0.3 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C warmer in January, with 21 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 29.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 277 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Luancheng, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Luancheng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 277 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
