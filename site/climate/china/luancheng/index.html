Luancheng weather by month and climate

Average temperature, rain and snow, sunshine and more for every month in Luancheng, China (Hebei), from modelled 1981–2010 averages for the town itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre).

Luancheng is warmest in July, with 32.7 °C (91 °F) days and 23.0 °C nights, and coolest in January, with 3.8 °C (39 °F) days and -7.4 °C nights. Most rain falls around August (133 mm); January is driest (4 mm).

Luancheng at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Luancheng is 1341st warmest (14.2 °C year average), 373rd sunniest (about 2,549 hours of sunshine a year) and 1814th wettest (484 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Huaiyang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (18 km away), Luancheng is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 17 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.8 °C), averages 29.6 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 277 km from the sea.

Why Luancheng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 277 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.6 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Luancheng itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (37.885°N, 114.646°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.8 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-461-2946), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
