How December compares in Luancheng

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Luancheng. Compared with November: 6.5 °C cooler, 9 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. January is 1.6 °C cooler than December on average.

Luancheng in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Luancheng in December?
In December, Luancheng averages a day high of 5.7 °C (42 °F) and a night low of -5.1 °C (23 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Luancheng?
December is not one of Luancheng’s mild months; those are April, May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Luancheng in December?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (133 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Luancheng in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.2 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Luancheng get in December?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Luancheng in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
