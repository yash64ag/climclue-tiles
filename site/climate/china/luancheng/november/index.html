How November compares in Luancheng

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Luancheng. Compared with October: 8.7 °C cooler, 11 mm less precipitation and 31 hours less sunshine. December is 6.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Luancheng in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Luancheng in November?
In November, Luancheng averages a day high of 12.6 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 0.4 °C (33 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 176 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Luancheng?
November is not one of Luancheng’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Luancheng in November?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is August (131 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Luancheng in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.6 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Luancheng get in November?
About 176 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Luancheng in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
