How February compares in Handan

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Handan. Compared with January: 3.8 °C warmer, 4 mm more precipitation and 12 hours less sunshine. March is 6.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Handan in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Handan in February?
In February, Handan averages a day high of 8.8 °C (48 °F) and a night low of -3.3 °C (26 °F), with 7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Handan?
February is not one of Handan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Handan in February?
7 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (147 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Handan in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.6 cm (1.4 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Handan get in February?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Handan in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
