Handan weather in June

Average June weather in Handan, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In June, Handan averages 33.0 °C (91 °F) days and 20.3 °C (69 °F) nights, with 53 mm (2.1 in) of rain over the month and about 258 hours of sunshine.

How June compares in Handan

June is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Handan. Compared with May: 4.7 °C warmer, 8 mm more precipitation and 3 hours less sunshine. July is 1.0 °C warmer than June on average.

Handan in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Handan in June?
In June, Handan averages a day high of 33.0 °C (91 °F) and a night low of 20.3 °C (69 °F), with 53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation and about 258 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Handan?
June is not one of Handan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Handan in June?
53 mm (2.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (144 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Handan in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Handan get in June?
About 258 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Handan in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
