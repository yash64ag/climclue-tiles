How August compares in Handan

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Handan. Compared with July: 1.7 °C cooler, 4 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. September is 4.5 °C cooler than August on average.

Handan in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Handan in August?
In August, Handan averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 21.4 °C (71 °F), with 140 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Handan?
August is not one of Handan’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Handan in August?
140 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (144 mm), the driest January (4 mm).
Does it snow in Handan in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Handan get in August?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Handan in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
