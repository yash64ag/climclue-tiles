How May compares in Jiangmen

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Jiangmen. Compared with April: 3.1 °C warmer, 104 mm more precipitation and 50 hours more sunshine. June is 1.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Jiangmen in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiangmen in May?
In May, Jiangmen averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 23.7 °C (75 °F), with 327 mm (12.9 in) of precipitation and about 149 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Jiangmen?
May is not one of Jiangmen’s mild months; those are February, March, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiangmen in May?
327 mm (12.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (360 mm), the driest December (32 mm).
Does it snow in Jiangmen in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiangmen get in May?
About 149 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiangmen in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
