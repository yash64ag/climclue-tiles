How August compares in Jiangmen

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Jiangmen. Compared with July: 0.3 °C cooler, 39 mm more precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. September is 1.4 °C cooler than August on average.

Jiangmen in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiangmen in August?
In August, Jiangmen averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 25.4 °C (78 °F), with 327 mm (12.9 in) of precipitation and about 202 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Jiangmen?
August is not one of Jiangmen’s mild months; those are February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiangmen in August?
327 mm (12.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (371 mm), the driest December (31 mm).
Does it snow in Jiangmen in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiangmen get in August?
About 202 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiangmen in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
