How December compares in Jiangmen

December is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Jiangmen. Compared with November: 4.3 °C cooler, 14 mm less precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Jiangmen in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiangmen in December?
In December, Jiangmen averages a day high of 20.4 °C (69 °F) and a night low of 11.2 °C (52 °F), with 32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation and about 171 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Jiangmen?
For mild weather, yes: December is one of Jiangmen’s mild months (February, March, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiangmen in December?
32 mm (1.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is June (360 mm), the driest December (32 mm).
Does it snow in Jiangmen in December?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for December in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiangmen get in December?
About 171 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiangmen in December?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
