How October compares in Jiangmen

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Jiangmen. Compared with September: 2.7 °C cooler, 187 mm less precipitation and 5 hours more sunshine. November is 4.4 °C cooler than October on average.

Jiangmen in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiangmen in October?
In October, Jiangmen averages a day high of 28.0 °C (82 °F) and a night low of 20.5 °C (69 °F), with 91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation and about 193 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Jiangmen?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Jiangmen’s mild months (February, March, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiangmen in October?
91 mm (3.6 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (371 mm), the driest December (31 mm).
Does it snow in Jiangmen in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiangmen get in October?
About 193 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiangmen in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
