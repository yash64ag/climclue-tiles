How January compares in Jiangmen

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Jiangmen. Compared with December: 1.4 °C cooler, 3 mm more precipitation and 32 hours less sunshine. February is 1.4 °C warmer than January on average.

Jiangmen in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Jiangmen in January?
In January, Jiangmen averages a day high of 19.0 °C (66 °F) and a night low of 10.5 °C (51 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 139 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Jiangmen?
January is not one of Jiangmen’s mild months; those are February, March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Jiangmen in January?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (371 mm), the driest December (31 mm).
Does it snow in Jiangmen in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Jiangmen get in January?
About 139 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Jiangmen in January?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
