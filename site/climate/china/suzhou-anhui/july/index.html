How July compares in Suzhou, Anhui

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Suzhou, Anhui. Compared with June: 1.9 °C warmer, 119 mm more precipitation and 16 hours less sunshine. August is 1.0 °C cooler than July on average.

Suzhou, Anhui in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suzhou, Anhui in July?
In July, Suzhou, Anhui averages a day high of 31.6 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.4 °C (74 °F), with 246 mm (9.7 in) of precipitation and about 204 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Suzhou, Anhui?
July is not one of Suzhou, Anhui’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suzhou, Anhui in July?
246 mm (9.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (246 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Suzhou, Anhui in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suzhou, Anhui get in July?
About 204 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suzhou, Anhui in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
