Suzhou, Anhui in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Suzhou, Anhui in May?
In May, Suzhou, Anhui averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 15.6 °C (60 °F), with 72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 222 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Suzhou, Anhui?
For mild weather, yes: May is one of Suzhou, Anhui’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Suzhou, Anhui in May?
72 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (246 mm), the driest December (17 mm).
Does it snow in Suzhou, Anhui in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Suzhou, Anhui get in May?
About 222 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Suzhou, Anhui in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes, an extra layer for cooler evenings and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
