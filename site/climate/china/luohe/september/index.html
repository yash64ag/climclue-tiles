How September compares in Luohe

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Luohe. Compared with August: 4.4 °C cooler, 72 mm less precipitation and 49 hours less sunshine. October is 5.4 °C cooler than September on average.

Luohe in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Luohe in September?
In September, Luohe averages a day high of 26.8 °C (80 °F) and a night low of 16.9 °C (62 °F), with 90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Luohe?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Luohe’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Luohe in September?
90 mm (3.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (225 mm), the driest January (17 mm).
Does it snow in Luohe in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Luohe get in September?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Luohe in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
