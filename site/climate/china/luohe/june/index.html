How June compares in Luohe

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Luohe. Compared with May: 4.5 °C warmer, 26 mm more precipitation and 6 hours more sunshine. July is 1.4 °C warmer than June on average.

Luohe in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Luohe in June?
In June, Luohe averages a day high of 31.0 °C (88 °F) and a night low of 21.1 °C (70 °F), with 109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation and about 224 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Luohe?
June is not one of Luohe’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Luohe in June?
109 mm (4.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (225 mm), the driest January (17 mm).
Does it snow in Luohe in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Luohe get in June?
About 224 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Luohe in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
