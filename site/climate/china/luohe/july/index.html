How July compares in Luohe

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Luohe. Compared with June: 1.4 °C warmer, 116 mm more precipitation and 7 hours less sunshine. August is 1.3 °C cooler than July on average.

Luohe in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Luohe in July?
In July, Luohe averages a day high of 32.0 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 225 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation and about 217 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Luohe?
July is not one of Luohe’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Luohe in July?
225 mm (8.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (225 mm), the driest January (17 mm).
Does it snow in Luohe in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Luohe get in July?
About 217 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Luohe in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
