Anyang at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Anyang is 401st warmest (14.8 °C year average), 237th sunniest (about 2,406 hours of sunshine a year) and 541st wettest (571 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Linshui, the nearest covered town with different numbers (40 km away), Anyang is 0.4 °C warmer in July and 0.4 °C warmer in January, with 16 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 28.3 °C more than its coolest, January (-0.7 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 402 km from the sea.

Why Anyang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 402 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
