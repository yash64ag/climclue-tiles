Why Anyang has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 402 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.3 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Anyang climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Anyang?
July, with an average of 27.6 °C (82 °F), day highs around 31.7 °C (89 °F) and night lows around 23.2 °C (74 °F).
What is the coldest month in Anyang?
January, with an average of -0.7 °C (31 °F), day highs around 5.3 °C (42 °F) and night lows around -5.4 °C (22 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Anyang?
July, with 162 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 571 mm (22.5 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Anyang?
December, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Anyang?
May, with about 254 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Anyang?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 24 cm (9.6 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 1 month.
When is the best time for mild weather in Anyang?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: April, May, September and October.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.0 × 11 km, that contains Anyang’s GeoNames location (36.096°N, 114.383°E; ClimaClue area sq-479-2943), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 1,146,839 (GeoNames).
