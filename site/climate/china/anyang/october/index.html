Anyang in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Anyang in October?
In October, Anyang averages a day high of 21.6 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 10.0 °C (50 °F), with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation and about 195 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Anyang?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Anyang’s mild months (April, May, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Anyang in October?
28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (157 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Anyang in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Anyang get in October?
About 195 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Anyang in October?
Going by the averages: light layers and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
