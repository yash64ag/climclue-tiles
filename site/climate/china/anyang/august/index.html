How August compares in Anyang

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 3rd sunniest of the 12 in Anyang. Compared with July: 1.7 °C cooler, 25 mm less precipitation and 1 hours more sunshine. September is 4.3 °C cooler than August on average.

Anyang in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Anyang in August?
In August, Anyang averages a day high of 29.9 °C (86 °F) and a night low of 21.8 °C (71 °F), with 137 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation and about 221 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Anyang?
August is not one of Anyang’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Anyang in August?
137 mm (5.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (162 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Anyang in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Anyang get in August?
About 221 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Anyang in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
