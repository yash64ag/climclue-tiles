How December compares in Anyang

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Anyang. Compared with November: 6.4 °C cooler, 10 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. January is 1.9 °C cooler than December on average.

Anyang in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Anyang in December?
In December, Anyang averages a day high of 6.4 °C (44 °F) and a night low of -3.9 °C (25 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Anyang?
December is not one of Anyang’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Anyang in December?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (157 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Anyang in December?
Yes, on average: about 3.3 cm (1.3 in) of fresh snow in December (ERA5-Land snowfall at 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow, adjusted to nearby weather stations).
How many hours of sunshine does Anyang get in December?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Anyang in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
