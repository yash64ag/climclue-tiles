How July compares in Anyang

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Anyang. Compared with June: 1.0 °C warmer, 99 mm more precipitation and 34 hours less sunshine. August is 1.7 °C cooler than July on average.

Anyang in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Anyang in July?
In July, Anyang averages a day high of 31.7 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 23.2 °C (74 °F), with 162 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation and about 219 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Anyang?
July is not one of Anyang’s mild months; those are April, May, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Anyang in July?
162 mm (6.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (162 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Anyang in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Anyang get in July?
About 219 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Anyang in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
