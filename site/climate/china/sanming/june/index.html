How June compares in Sanming

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Sanming. Compared with May: 2.9 °C warmer, 2 mm less precipitation and 20 hours more sunshine. July is 2.7 °C warmer than June on average.

Sanming in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sanming in June?
In June, Sanming averages a day high of 30.4 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 260 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation and about 152 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Sanming?
June is not one of Sanming’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sanming in June?
260 mm (10.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (262 mm), the driest December (42 mm).
Does it snow in Sanming in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sanming get in June?
About 152 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sanming in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
