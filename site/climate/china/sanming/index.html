Sanming at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Sanming is 206th warmest (20.6 °C year average), 1445th sunniest (about 1,765 hours of sunshine a year) and 223rd wettest (1,661 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nanping, Fujian, the nearest covered town with different numbers (71 km away), Sanming is 0.9 °C cooler in July and 0.3 °C cooler in January, with 60 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (29.9 °C), averages 19.6 °C more than its coolest, January (10.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.5 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 194 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sanming, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sanming has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 194 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.6 °C. The 10 km area around Sanming, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 19.0 °C a year, 1.6 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Sanming itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (26.249°N, 117.619°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10.0 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-577-2976), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
