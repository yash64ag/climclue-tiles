Sanming at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Sanming is 126th warmest (19.0 °C year average), 557th sunniest (about 1,765 hours of sunshine a year) and 76th wettest (1,843 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Nanping, the nearest covered town with different numbers (71 km away), Sanming is 1.6 °C cooler in July and 0.7 °C cooler in January, with 159 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.9 °C), averages 18.8 °C more than its coolest, January (9.2 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 3.9 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 194 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Sanming, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Sanming has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 194 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 18.8 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 8.4 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
