How August compares in Sanming

August is the 2nd warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd sunniest of the 12 in Sanming. Compared with July: 0.6 °C cooler, 31 mm more precipitation and 18 hours less sunshine. September is 2.6 °C cooler than August on average.

Sanming in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sanming in August?
In August, Sanming averages a day high of 31.4 °C (89 °F) and a night low of 22.6 °C (73 °F), with 180 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation and about 230 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Sanming?
August is not one of Sanming’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sanming in August?
180 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (290 mm), the driest December (47 mm).
Does it snow in Sanming in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sanming get in August?
About 230 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sanming in August?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
