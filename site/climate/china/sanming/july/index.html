How July compares in Sanming

July is the warmest month, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the sunniest month in Sanming. Compared with June: 2.5 °C warmer, 140 mm less precipitation and 96 hours more sunshine. August is 0.6 °C cooler than July on average.

Sanming in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sanming in July?
In July, Sanming averages a day high of 32.2 °C (90 °F) and a night low of 22.8 °C (73 °F), with 149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation and about 248 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Sanming?
July is not one of Sanming’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sanming in July?
149 mm (5.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (290 mm), the driest December (47 mm).
Does it snow in Sanming in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sanming get in July?
About 248 hours of bright sunshine, the sunniest month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sanming in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
