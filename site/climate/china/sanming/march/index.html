How March compares in Sanming

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Sanming. Compared with February: 3.3 °C warmer, 96 mm more precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. April is 4.8 °C warmer than March on average.

Sanming in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sanming in March?
In March, Sanming averages a day high of 18.1 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 9.3 °C (49 °F), with 220 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation and about 97 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Sanming?
March is not one of Sanming’s mild months; those are October and November, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sanming in March?
220 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is May (290 mm), the driest December (47 mm).
Does it snow in Sanming in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sanming get in March?
About 97 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sanming in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
