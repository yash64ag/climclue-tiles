How October compares in Sanming

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Sanming. Compared with September: 4.4 °C cooler, 45 mm less precipitation and 24 hours less sunshine. November is 5.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Sanming in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Sanming in October?
In October, Sanming averages a day high of 26.3 °C (79 °F) and a night low of 17.0 °C (63 °F), with 60 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 156 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Sanming?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Sanming’s mild months (October and November), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Sanming in October?
60 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is May (262 mm), the driest December (42 mm).
Does it snow in Sanming in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Sanming get in October?
About 156 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Sanming in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
