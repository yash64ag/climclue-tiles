Yebaishou at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Yebaishou is 1828th warmest (8.9 °C year average), 110th sunniest (about 2,743 hours of sunshine a year) and 1902nd wettest (397 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Lingyuan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (27 km away), Yebaishou is 0.3 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 13 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.4 °C), averages 33.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-9.5 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 135 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Yebaishou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Yebaishou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 135 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 33.9 °C. The 10 km area around Yebaishou, which the globe shows when you click there, averages 7.9 °C a year, 1.0 °C cooler than the town itself, because it takes in higher ground.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Yebaishou itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.398°N, 119.641°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-426-2996), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
