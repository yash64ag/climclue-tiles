Zhelou at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Zhelou is 276th warmest (19.3 °C year average), 1586th sunniest (about 1,516 hours of sunshine a year) and 405th wettest (1,458 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Anlong, the nearest covered town with different numbers (34 km away), Zhelou is 4.0 °C warmer in July and 3.7 °C warmer in January, with 197 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.1 °C), averages 16.0 °C more than its coolest, January (10.1 °C). Even the coolest nights average 5.8 °C (42 °F), in January. The area lies about 451 km from the sea.

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Zhelou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Zhelou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 451 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 16.0 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 5.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Zhelou itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (24.967°N, 105.817°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 10 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-590-2858), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
