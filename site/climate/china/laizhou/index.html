Why Laizhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Laizhou climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Laizhou?
July, with an average of 25.9 °C (79 °F), day highs around 29.8 °C (86 °F) and night lows around 22.5 °C (73 °F).
What is the coldest month in Laizhou?
January, with an average of -1.9 °C (28 °F), day highs around 1.9 °C (35 °F) and night lows around -5.4 °C (22 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Laizhou?
July, with 180 mm (7.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 635 mm (25.0 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Laizhou?
January, with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Laizhou?
May, with about 264 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Laizhou?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 27 cm (10.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 1 month.
When is the best time for mild weather in Laizhou?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June and September.
What type of climate does Laizhou have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.9 × 11 km, that contains Laizhou’s GeoNames location (37.181°N, 119.942°E; ClimaClue area sq-468-2999), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 188,000 (GeoNames).
