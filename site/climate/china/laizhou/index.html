Laizhou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Laizhou is 490th warmest (12.8 °C year average), 153rd sunniest (about 2,551 hours of sunshine a year) and 480th wettest (635 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Pingdu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (44 km away), Laizhou is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 9 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.9 °C), averages 27.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.9 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 21 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Laizhou, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Laizhou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 27.8 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
