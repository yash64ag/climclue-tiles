Why Duyun has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. About 524 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 19.3 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 3.0 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Duyun climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Duyun?
July, with an average of 24.8 °C (77 °F), day highs around 26.9 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 20.8 °C (69 °F).
What is the coldest month in Duyun?
January, with an average of 5.5 °C (42 °F), day highs around 8.1 °C (47 °F) and night lows around 1.6 °C (35 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Duyun?
June, with 221 mm (8.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 1,303 mm (51.3 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Duyun?
December, with 28 mm (1.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Duyun?
July, with about 197 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Duyun?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 13 cm (5.2 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Duyun?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: September and October.
What type of climate does Duyun have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 10.0 × 11 km, that contains Duyun’s GeoNames location (26.267°N, 107.517°E; ClimaClue area sq-577-2875), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 198,516 (GeoNames).
