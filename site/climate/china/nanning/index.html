Nanning at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Nanning is 66th warmest (22.3 °C year average), 577th sunniest (about 1,684 hours of sunshine a year) and 176th wettest (1,479 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Qinzhou, the nearest covered town with different numbers (99 km away), Nanning is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 1.1 °C cooler in January, with 941 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.9 °C), averages 15.5 °C more than its coolest, January (13.3 °C). Even the coolest nights average 9.6 °C (49 °F), in January. The area lies about 137 km from the sea.

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 23 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 0.4 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 115% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Nanning has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 137 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 15.5 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
