How February compares in Nanning

February is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Nanning. Compared with January: 1.6 °C warmer, 9 mm more precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. March is 3.2 °C warmer than February on average.

Nanning in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanning in February?
In February, Nanning averages a day high of 18.2 °C (65 °F) and a night low of 11.5 °C (53 °F), with 55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation and about 57 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Nanning?
February is not one of Nanning’s mild months; those are March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanning in February?
55 mm (2.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (285 mm), the driest December (26 mm).
Does it snow in Nanning in February?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for February in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanning get in February?
About 57 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanning in February?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
