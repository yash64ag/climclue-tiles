How November compares in Nanning

November is the 5th coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Nanning. Compared with October: 4.6 °C cooler, 12 mm less precipitation and 20 hours less sunshine. December is 4.3 °C cooler than November on average.

Nanning in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanning in November?
In November, Nanning averages a day high of 23.1 °C (74 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation and about 145 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Nanning?
For mild weather, yes: November is one of Nanning’s mild months (March, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanning in November?
48 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (285 mm), the driest December (26 mm).
Does it snow in Nanning in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanning get in November?
About 145 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanning in November?
Going by the averages: light layers. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
