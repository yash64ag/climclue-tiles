How June compares in Nanning

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Nanning. Compared with May: 1.9 °C warmer, 38 mm more precipitation and 7 hours more sunshine. July is 0.6 °C warmer than June on average.

Nanning in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanning in June?
In June, Nanning averages a day high of 30.6 °C (87 °F) and a night low of 24.6 °C (76 °F), with 242 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation and about 170 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Nanning?
June is not one of Nanning’s mild months; those are February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanning in June?
242 mm (9.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (274 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Nanning in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanning get in June?
About 170 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanning in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
