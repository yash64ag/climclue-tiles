Nanning weather in January

Average January weather in Nanning, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In January, Nanning averages 17.3 °C (63 °F) days and 9.6 °C (49 °F) nights, with 44 mm (1.7 in) of rain over the month and about 86 hours of sunshine.

How January compares in Nanning

January is the coolest month, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 3rd least sunny of the 12 in Nanning. Compared with December: 1.9 °C cooler, 19 mm more precipitation and 40 hours less sunshine. February is 1.6 °C warmer than January on average.

Nanning in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanning in January?
In January, Nanning averages a day high of 17.3 °C (63 °F) and a night low of 9.6 °C (49 °F), with 44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation and about 86 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Nanning?
January is not one of Nanning’s mild months; those are February, March, April, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanning in January?
44 mm (1.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (274 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Nanning in January?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for January in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanning get in January?
About 86 hours of bright sunshine, the 3rd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanning in January?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
