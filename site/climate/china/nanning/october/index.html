How October compares in Nanning

October is the 6th warmest of the 12, the 5th driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Nanning. Compared with September: 3.2 °C cooler, 64 mm less precipitation and 29 hours less sunshine. November is 4.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Nanning in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanning in October?
In October, Nanning averages a day high of 28.8 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 20.1 °C (68 °F), with 58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation and about 165 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Nanning?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Nanning’s mild months (February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanning in October?
58 mm (2.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 5th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (274 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Nanning in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanning get in October?
About 165 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanning in October?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
