How May compares in Nanning

May is the 5th warmest of the 12, the 3rd wettest of the 12 and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Nanning. Compared with April: 3.2 °C warmer, 110 mm more precipitation and 66 hours more sunshine. June is 1.9 °C warmer than May on average.

Nanning in May: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanning in May?
In May, Nanning averages a day high of 29.0 °C (84 °F) and a night low of 22.3 °C (72 °F), with 213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation and about 163 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is May a good time to visit Nanning?
May is not one of Nanning’s mild months; those are March, October, November and December, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanning in May?
213 mm (8.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making May the 3rd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (285 mm), the driest December (26 mm).
Does it snow in Nanning in May?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for May in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanning get in May?
About 163 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanning in May?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
