Nanning weather in March

Average March weather in Nanning, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In March, Nanning averages 21.6 °C (71 °F) days and 14.8 °C (59 °F) nights, with 70 mm (2.8 in) of rain over the month and about 69 hours of sunshine.

How March compares in Nanning

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 6th driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Nanning. Compared with February: 3.2 °C warmer, 18 mm more precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. April is 4.9 °C warmer than March on average.

Nanning in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Nanning in March?
In March, Nanning averages a day high of 21.6 °C (71 °F) and a night low of 14.8 °C (59 °F), with 70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation and about 69 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Nanning?
For mild weather, yes: March is one of Nanning’s mild months (February, March, April, October, November and December), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Nanning in March?
70 mm (2.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 6th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (274 mm), the driest December (25 mm).
Does it snow in Nanning in March?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for March in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Nanning get in March?
About 69 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Nanning in March?
Going by the averages: light layers and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
