Why Nehe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 792 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 43.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Nehe climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Nehe?
July, with an average of 22.3 °C (72 °F), day highs around 26.5 °C (80 °F) and night lows around 17.6 °C (64 °F).
What is the coldest month in Nehe?
January, with an average of -21.4 °C (-7 °F), day highs around -15.3 °C (4 °F) and night lows around -27.4 °C (-17 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Nehe?
July, with 148 mm (5.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 493 mm (19.4 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Nehe?
February, with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Nehe?
June, with about 276 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Nehe?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 63 cm (24.7 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in March; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 5 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Nehe?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: June.
What type of climate does Nehe have?

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 7.4 × 11 km, that contains Nehe’s GeoNames location (48.479°N, 124.870°E; ClimaClue area sq-355-3048), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 108,253 (GeoNames).
