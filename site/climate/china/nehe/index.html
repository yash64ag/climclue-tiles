Nehe at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Nehe is 679th warmest (2.5 °C year average), 95th sunniest (about 2,647 hours of sunshine a year) and 594th wettest (493 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Bei’an, the nearest covered town with different numbers (130 km away), Nehe is 0.6 °C warmer in July and 0.5 °C warmer in January, with 50 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.3 °C), averages 43.7 °C more than its coolest, January (-21.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, April, October, November and December. The area lies about 792 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Nehe, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Nehe has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 792 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 43.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
