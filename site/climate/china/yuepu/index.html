Yuepu at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Yuepu is 304th warmest (16.6 °C year average), 380th sunniest (about 1,986 hours of sunshine a year) and 229th wettest (1,358 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Yanghang, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Yuepu is about as warm in July and about as warm in January, with 43 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (28.1 °C), averages 23.6 °C more than its coolest, January (4.5 °C). No month has an average night low below 0 °C (32 °F); the coldest, January, averages 1.3 °C (single nights can be colder). The area lies about 9 km from the sea (coastal).

Station check: the nearest NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages, 5 km away, agrees with this area’s values: monthly temperatures within 1.1 °C of the station’s, yearly precipitation 116% of the station’s, and a similar seasonal rain pattern.

Why Yuepu has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Mild for its latitude; moving air from the ocean keeps winters from getting very cold and summers from getting very hot. Passing storm systems and showers bring rain in every season, with no reliable dry month. On the coast: the sea warms and cools slowly, which usually softens seasonal swings. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 23.6 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
