Tongchuan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Tongchuan is 421st warmest (14.4 °C year average), 345th sunniest (about 2,054 hours of sunshine a year) and 603rd wettest (480 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tongchuanshi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Tongchuan is 2.0 °C warmer in July and 1.5 °C warmer in January, with 58 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.1 °C), averages 26.9 °C more than its coolest, January (0.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 881 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tongchuan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tongchuan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 881 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
