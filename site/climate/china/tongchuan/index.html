Tongchuan at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Tongchuan is 440th warmest (14.1 °C year average), 345th sunniest (about 2,054 hours of sunshine a year) and 594th wettest (487 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Tongchuanshi, the nearest covered town with different numbers (23 km away), Tongchuan is 1.5 °C warmer in July and 1.2 °C warmer in January, with 34 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.8 °C), averages 26.7 °C more than its coolest, January (0.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 881 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Tongchuan, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Tongchuan has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 881 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 26.7 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, day highs and night lows, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Tongchuan itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (34.899°N, 108.951°E). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 9.1 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-491-2889), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
