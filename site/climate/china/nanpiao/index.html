Nanpiao at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Nanpiao is 1754th warmest (10.0 °C year average), 167th sunniest (about 2,693 hours of sunshine a year) and 1634th wettest (579 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Jinzhou, Liaoning (41.1°N 121.1°E), the nearest covered town with different numbers (33 km away), Nanpiao is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 1.0 °C cooler in January, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (25.1 °C), averages 33.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-8.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 42 km from the sea.

Why Nanpiao has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 42 km from the sea: close enough for ocean air to moderate the seasons on many days. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 33.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Nanpiao itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (41.098°N, 120.748°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.4 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-429-3007), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
