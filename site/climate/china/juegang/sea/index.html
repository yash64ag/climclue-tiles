Juegang sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Jiangsu · China

The sea off Juegang averages 27.5 °C (82 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 5.6 °C (42 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September.

Juegang sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Juegang?
In August, at about 27.5 °C (82 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Juegang?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from July to September. The coolest month is February, at 5.6 °C (42 °F).
How warm is the sea in Juegang in February?
About 5.6 °C (42 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Juegang?
About 16.2 °C (61 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Juegang nearby?
In August: Nanqiao (28.4 °C, 158 km), Fengxiang (28.4 °C, 164 km) and Jinshan, Shanghai (28.4 °C, 165 km).
