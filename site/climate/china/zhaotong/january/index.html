How January compares in Zhaotong

January is the coolest month, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Zhaotong. Compared with December: 1.5 °C cooler, 4 mm more precipitation and 17 hours more sunshine. February is 2.5 °C warmer than January on average.

Zhaotong in January: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhaotong in January?
In January, Zhaotong averages a day high of 8.8 °C (48 °F) and a night low of -2.2 °C (28 °F), with 9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is January a good time to visit Zhaotong?
January is not one of Zhaotong’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhaotong in January?
9 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making January the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (144 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Zhaotong in January?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 29 cm (11.5 in) of fresh snow in January (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 3.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Zhaotong get in January?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhaotong in January?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
