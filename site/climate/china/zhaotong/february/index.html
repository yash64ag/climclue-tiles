February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the 6th least sunny of the 12 in Zhaotong. Compared with January: 2.5 °C warmer, 1 mm less precipitation and 6 hours less sunshine. March is 3.9 °C warmer than February on average.

Zhaotong in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhaotong in February?
In February, Zhaotong averages a day high of 11.7 °C (53 °F) and a night low of -0.2 °C (32 °F), with 8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation and about 161 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Zhaotong?
February is not one of Zhaotong’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhaotong in February?
8 mm (0.3 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (144 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Zhaotong in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 23 cm (8.9 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), and snow lies on the ground with an average depth of 2.3 cm.
How many hours of sunshine does Zhaotong get in February?
About 161 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhaotong in February?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves, an extra layer for cooler evenings and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
