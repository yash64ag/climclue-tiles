How December compares in Zhaotong

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Zhaotong. Compared with November: 4.4 °C cooler, 8 mm less precipitation and 12 hours more sunshine. January is 1.4 °C cooler than December on average.

Zhaotong in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhaotong in December?
In December, Zhaotong averages a day high of 10.1 °C (50 °F) and a night low of -0.2 °C (32 °F), with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 150 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Zhaotong?
December is not one of Zhaotong’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhaotong in December?
5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (139 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Zhaotong in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 1.9 cm (0.7 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhaotong get in December?
About 150 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhaotong in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
