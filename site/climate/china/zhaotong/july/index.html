How July compares in Zhaotong

July is the warmest month, the wettest month and the 6th sunniest of the 12 in Zhaotong. Compared with June: 1.6 °C warmer, 16 mm more precipitation and 31 hours more sunshine. August is 0.5 °C cooler than July on average.

Zhaotong in July: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhaotong in July?
In July, Zhaotong averages a day high of 25.1 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 15.7 °C (60 °F), with 139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation and about 162 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is July a good time to visit Zhaotong?
July is not one of Zhaotong’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhaotong in July?
139 mm (5.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making July the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is July (139 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Zhaotong in July?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for July in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhaotong get in July?
About 162 hours of bright sunshine, the 6th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhaotong in July?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
