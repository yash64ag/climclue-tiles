Zhaotong in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhaotong in November?
In November, Zhaotong averages a day high of 14.0 °C (57 °F) and a night low of 4.1 °C (39 °F), with 12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Zhaotong?
November is not one of Zhaotong’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhaotong in November?
12 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (139 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Zhaotong in November?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for November in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhaotong get in November?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhaotong in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
