How November compares in Zhaotong

November is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th driest of the 12 and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Zhaotong. Compared with October: 3.8 °C cooler, 39 mm less precipitation and 16 hours more sunshine. December is 4.5 °C cooler than November on average.

Zhaotong in November: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Zhaotong in November?
In November, Zhaotong averages a day high of 12.7 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 4.2 °C (40 °F), with 13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation and about 138 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is November a good time to visit Zhaotong?
November is not one of Zhaotong’s mild months; those are May and September, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Zhaotong in November?
13 mm (0.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making November the 4th driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (144 mm), the driest December (5 mm).
Does it snow in Zhaotong in November?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 8.1 cm (3.2 in) of fresh snow in November (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Zhaotong get in November?
About 138 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Zhaotong in November?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and waterproof boots. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
