Zhaotong at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Zhaotong is 506th warmest (12.2 °C year average), 477th sunniest (about 1,898 hours of sunshine a year) and 467th wettest (655 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Liupanshui, the nearest covered town with different numbers (137 km away), Zhaotong is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.9 °C cooler in January, with 672 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (20.1 °C), averages 17.4 °C more than its coolest, January (2.6 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 785 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Zhaotong, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Zhaotong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 785 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
