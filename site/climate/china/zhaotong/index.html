Why Zhaotong has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Warm to hot summers; dry, clear winters can bring cool nights. Monsoon winds bring most of the rain in summer; winters are dry when winds blow from the land. About 785 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 17.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Zhaotong climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Zhaotong?
July, with an average of 20.1 °C (68 °F), day highs around 23.3 °C (74 °F) and night lows around 15.4 °C (60 °F).
What is the coldest month in Zhaotong?
January, with an average of 2.6 °C (37 °F), day highs around 8.8 °C (48 °F) and night lows around -2.2 °C (28 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Zhaotong?
July, with 144 mm (5.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 655 mm (25.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Zhaotong?
December, with 5 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation.
What is the sunniest month in Zhaotong?
March, with about 198 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Zhaotong?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 100 cm (39.3 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in January; snow lies on the ground (average depth 1 cm or more) in 3 months.
When is the best time for mild weather in Zhaotong?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May and September.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 9.9 × 11 km, that contains Zhaotong’s GeoNames location (27.317°N, 103.717°E; ClimaClue area sq-566-2837), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 787,845 (GeoNames).
