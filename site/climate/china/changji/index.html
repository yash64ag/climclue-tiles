Changji at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Changji is 587th warmest (8.5 °C year average), 40th sunniest (about 2,762 hours of sunshine a year) and 661st wettest (214 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Wujiaqu, the nearest covered town with different numbers (24 km away), Changji is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 1.3 °C warmer in January, with 19 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (26.1 °C), averages 40.2 °C more than its coolest, January (-14.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 2,408 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Changji, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Changji has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 2,408 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 40.2 °C.

Rain or snow is spread through much of the year.
