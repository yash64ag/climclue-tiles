How March compares in Quzhou

March is the 4th coolest of the 12, the 4th wettest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Quzhou. Compared with February: 3.8 °C warmer, 83 mm more precipitation and 13 hours more sunshine. April is 6.2 °C warmer than March on average.

Quzhou in March: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Quzhou in March?
In March, Quzhou averages a day high of 15.5 °C (60 °F) and a night low of 6.9 °C (44 °F), with 199 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation and about 109 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is March a good time to visit Quzhou?
March is not one of Quzhou’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Quzhou in March?
199 mm (7.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making March the 4th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is June (300 mm), the driest December (54 mm).
Does it snow in Quzhou in March?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.0 cm (0.8 in) of fresh snow in March (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Quzhou get in March?
About 109 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Quzhou in March?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
