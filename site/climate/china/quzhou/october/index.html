Quzhou in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Quzhou in October?
In October, Quzhou averages a day high of 24.0 °C (75 °F) and a night low of 15.5 °C (60 °F), with 64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation and about 167 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Quzhou?
For mild weather, yes: October is one of Quzhou’s mild months (October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Quzhou in October?
64 mm (2.5 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is June (300 mm), the driest December (54 mm).
Does it snow in Quzhou in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Quzhou get in October?
About 167 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Quzhou in October?
