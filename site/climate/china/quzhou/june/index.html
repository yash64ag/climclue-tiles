How June compares in Quzhou

June is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the wettest month and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Quzhou. Compared with May: 3.1 °C warmer, 89 mm more precipitation and 8 hours more sunshine. July is 3.9 °C warmer than June on average.

Quzhou in June: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Quzhou in June?
In June, Quzhou averages a day high of 29.6 °C (85 °F) and a night low of 21.9 °C (71 °F), with 300 mm (11.8 in) of precipitation and about 160 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is June a good time to visit Quzhou?
June is not one of Quzhou’s mild months; those are October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Quzhou in June?
300 mm (11.8 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making June the wettest month of the year. The wettest month is June (300 mm), the driest December (54 mm).
Does it snow in Quzhou in June?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for June in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Quzhou get in June?
About 160 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Quzhou in June?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a rain jacket or umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
