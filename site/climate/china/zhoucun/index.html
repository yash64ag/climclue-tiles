Zhoucun at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Zhoucun is 442nd warmest (13.9 °C year average), 161st sunniest (about 2,548 hours of sunshine a year) and 507th wettest (602 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Zibo, the nearest covered town with different numbers (22 km away), Zhoucun is 0.2 °C warmer in July and 0.1 °C cooler in January, with 13 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.3 °C), averages 29.0 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.8 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 120 km from the sea.

Why Zhoucun has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

A huge seasonal swing: winter air flows out of the cold continental interior, while summer is warm. A cold, dry winter high pressure keeps winters almost snow-free; summer monsoon-like flow brings most of the rain. About 120 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 29.0 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.
