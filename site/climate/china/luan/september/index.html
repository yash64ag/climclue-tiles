How September compares in Lu’an

September is the 4th warmest of the 12, the 5th wettest of the 12 and the 5th sunniest of the 12 in Lu’an. Compared with August: 4.3 °C cooler, 78 mm less precipitation and 62 hours less sunshine. October is 5.7 °C cooler than September on average.

Lu’an in September: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lu’an in September?
In September, Lu’an averages a day high of 27.1 °C (81 °F) and a night low of 18.9 °C (66 °F), with 99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 175 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is September a good time to visit Lu’an?
For mild weather, yes: September is one of Lu’an’s mild months (April, September and October), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lu’an in September?
99 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making September the 5th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (227 mm), the driest December (35 mm).
Does it snow in Lu’an in September?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for September in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Lu’an get in September?
About 175 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lu’an in September?
Going by the averages: light, breathable clothes for humid air and a compact umbrella. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
