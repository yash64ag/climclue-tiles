How December compares in Lu’an

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Lu’an. Compared with November: 5.8 °C cooler, 32 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. January is 2.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Lu’an in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lu’an in December?
In December, Lu’an averages a day high of 10.3 °C (51 °F) and a night low of 0.1 °C (32 °F), with 35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Lu’an?
December is not one of Lu’an’s mild months; those are April, September and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lu’an in December?
35 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (227 mm), the driest December (35 mm).
Does it snow in Lu’an in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.9 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Lu’an get in December?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lu’an in December?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
