Lu’an weather in December

Average December weather in Lu’an, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In December, Lu’an averages 9.6 °C (49 °F) days and 0.2 °C (32 °F) nights, with 36 mm (1.4 in) of rain and snow over the month and about 148 hours of sunshine.

How December compares in Lu’an

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the driest month and the 4th least sunny of the 12 in Lu’an. Compared with November: 5.8 °C cooler, 32 mm less precipitation and 4 hours less sunshine. January is 2.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Lu’an in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Lu’an in December?
In December, Lu’an averages a day high of 9.6 °C (49 °F) and a night low of 0.2 °C (32 °F), with 36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation and about 148 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Lu’an?
December is not one of Lu’an’s mild months; those are April and October, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Lu’an in December?
36 mm (1.4 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the driest month of the year. The wettest month is July (229 mm), the driest December (36 mm).
Does it snow in Lu’an in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.9 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Lu’an get in December?
About 148 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Lu’an in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
