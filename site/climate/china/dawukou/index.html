Why Dawukou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this area; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 978 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.7 °C. Within this area, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

Dawukou climate: questions and answers

What is the warmest month in Dawukou?
July, with an average of 23.9 °C (75 °F), day highs around 28.9 °C (84 °F) and night lows around 18.6 °C (65 °F).
What is the coldest month in Dawukou?
January, with an average of -6.8 °C (20 °F), day highs around -0.9 °C (30 °F) and night lows around -11.5 °C (11 °F).
Which month gets the most rain in Dawukou?
July, with 49 mm (1.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water), out of 198 mm (7.8 in) in a typical year.
What is the driest month in Dawukou?

What is the sunniest month in Dawukou?
June, with about 286 hours of bright sunshine (estimate for about 1961–1990).
Does it snow in Dawukou?
Yes. ERA5-Land gives about 23 cm (9.1 in) of fresh snow in a typical year (estimated as 1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow), most in March; it usually melts within days.
When is the best time for mild weather in Dawukou?
Counting months with an average temperature of 15–25 °C (59–77 °F) and under 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation as mild: May, June, July, August and September.

About these numbers

All values are modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for the 0.1° area, about 8.6 × 11 km, that contains Dawukou’s GeoNames location (39.042°N, 106.396°E; ClimaClue area sq-449-2863), averaged over the area’s land. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station. Temperature, precipitation, wind and humidity: CHELSA V2.1, 1981–2010, ~1 km cells. Dew point and snow: ERA5-Land, 1981–2010, ~9 km cells. Sunshine: CRU CL 2.0, about 1961–1990, ~18 km cells (a different period from the rest). The climate type is calculated from the area’s own monthly temperature and precipitation with the Köppen–Geiger rules of Peel et al. (2007). Population 131,880 (GeoNames).
