Dawukou at a glance

Among the 692 towns of 100,000+ people covered in China, Dawukou is 543rd warmest (10.4 °C year average), 14th sunniest (about 2,922 hours of sunshine a year) and 669th wettest (184 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Shizuishan, the nearest covered town with different numbers (7 km away), Dawukou is 0.1 °C cooler in July and 0.2 °C cooler in January, with 3 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (24.7 °C), averages 30.8 °C more than its coolest, January (-6.1 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 978 km from the sea (deep inland).

Why Dawukou has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Clear, dry air lets the ground heat strongly by day and lose heat quickly at night, so swings are large. Sinking air in the subtropical high-pressure belt, distance from moist air, or mountains blocking it keeps rain rare. About 978 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 30.8 °C. Within the 10 km area around it, monthly temperatures differ by up to 4.8 °C — typical where height changes quickly, as in hills or mountains.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers
