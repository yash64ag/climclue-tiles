Dongsheng at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Dongsheng is 1905th warmest (6.9 °C year average), 28th sunniest (about 2,920 hours of sunshine a year) and 1896th wettest (402 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Ordos, the nearest covered town with different numbers (28 km away), Dongsheng is 0.7 °C cooler in July and 0.6 °C cooler in January, with 72 mm more precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (22.0 °C), averages 32.4 °C more than its coolest, January (-10.4 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February, March, November and December. The area lies about 674 km from the sea (deep inland).

Station check: no NOAA GHCN-Daily station with 1981–2010 averages lies within 25 km of Dongsheng, so these modelled values have not been compared with a nearby station.

Why Dongsheng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 674 km from the sea: deep inland, where land heats and cools quickly, which usually makes seasons extreme. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 32.4 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Dongsheng itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (39.816°N, 109.978°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km). Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.5 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-441-2899), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
