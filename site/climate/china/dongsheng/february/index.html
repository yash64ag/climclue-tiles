How February compares in Dongsheng

February is the 3rd coolest of the 12, the 3rd driest of the 12 and the 2nd least sunny of the 12 in Dongsheng. Compared with January: 4.4 °C warmer, 2 mm more precipitation and 5 hours less sunshine. March is 6.8 °C warmer than February on average.

Dongsheng in February: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dongsheng in February?
In February, Dongsheng averages a day high of -0.5 °C (31 °F) and a night low of -11.7 °C (11 °F), with 4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation and about 207 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is February a good time to visit Dongsheng?
February is not one of Dongsheng’s mild months; those are May, June, July and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dongsheng in February?
4 mm (0.2 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making February the 3rd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (98 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Dongsheng in February?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 3.8 cm (1.5 in) of fresh snow in February (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Dongsheng get in February?
About 207 hours of bright sunshine, the 2nd least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dongsheng in February?
Going by the averages: a warm coat, a hat and gloves and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
