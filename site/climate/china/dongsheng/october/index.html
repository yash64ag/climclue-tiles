Dongsheng weather in October

Average October weather in Dongsheng, China: modelled 1981–2010 averages for the city itself (about 3 × 3 km around its centre) (climate normals, not a forecast).

In October, Dongsheng averages 12.9 °C (55 °F) days and 1.4 °C (35 °F) nights, with 17 mm (0.7 in) of rain over the month and about 236 hours of sunshine.

How October compares in Dongsheng

October is the 6th coolest of the 12, the 6th wettest of the 12 and the 5th least sunny of the 12 in Dongsheng. Compared with September: 7.1 °C cooler, 32 mm less precipitation and 2 hours less sunshine. November is 8.6 °C cooler than October on average.

Dongsheng in October: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dongsheng in October?
In October, Dongsheng averages a day high of 12.9 °C (55 °F) and a night low of 1.4 °C (35 °F), with 17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation and about 236 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is October a good time to visit Dongsheng?
October is not one of Dongsheng’s mild months; those are May, June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dongsheng in October?
17 mm (0.7 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making October the 6th wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (101 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Dongsheng in October?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for October in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dongsheng get in October?
About 236 hours of bright sunshine, the 5th least sunny of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dongsheng in October?
Going by the averages: a jacket or sweater and an extra layer for cooler evenings. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
