How December compares in Dongsheng

December is the 2nd coolest of the 12, the 2nd driest of the 12 and the least sunny month in Dongsheng. Compared with November: 6.9 °C cooler, 3 mm less precipitation and 10 hours less sunshine. January is 2.1 °C cooler than December on average.

Dongsheng in December: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dongsheng in December?
In December, Dongsheng averages a day high of -3.2 °C (26 °F) and a night low of -13.0 °C (9 °F), with 3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation and about 201 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is December a good time to visit Dongsheng?
December is not one of Dongsheng’s mild months; those are May, June and August, counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dongsheng in December?
3 mm (0.1 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making December the 2nd driest of the 12. The wettest month is July (101 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Dongsheng in December?
Yes, on average: ERA5-Land gives about 2.7 cm (1.1 in) of fresh snow in December (1 mm of water ≈ 1 cm of snow).
How many hours of sunshine does Dongsheng get in December?
About 201 hours of bright sunshine, the least sunny month of the year (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dongsheng in December?
Going by the averages: a warm coat and a hat and gloves. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
