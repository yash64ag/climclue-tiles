How August compares in Dongsheng

August is the 3rd warmest of the 12, the 2nd wettest of the 12 and the 4th sunniest of the 12 in Dongsheng. Compared with July: 2.5 °C cooler, 1 mm less precipitation and 15 hours less sunshine. September is 5.2 °C cooler than August on average.

Dongsheng in August: questions and answers

What is the weather like in Dongsheng in August?
In August, Dongsheng averages a day high of 24.9 °C (77 °F) and a night low of 14.1 °C (57 °F), with 100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation and about 259 hours of sunshine. These are 1981–2010 averages for the ~10 km area, not a forecast.
Is August a good time to visit Dongsheng?
For mild weather, yes: August is one of Dongsheng’s mild months (May, June and August), counting a month as mild with an average temperature 15–25 °C / 59–77 °F and under 100 mm / 3.9 in of precipitation. Climate only: crowds and prices are not considered.
How much does it rain in Dongsheng in August?
100 mm (3.9 in) of precipitation (rain and snow as water) on average, making August the 2nd wettest of the 12. The wettest month is July (101 mm), the driest January (2 mm).
Does it snow in Dongsheng in August?
Little or none on average: ERA5-Land gives under 0.5 cm of fresh snow for August in this area (1981–2010).
How many hours of sunshine does Dongsheng get in August?
About 259 hours of bright sunshine, the 4th sunniest of the 12 (estimate for about 1961–1990).
What should I pack for Dongsheng in August?
Going by the averages: light layers, an extra layer for cooler evenings, a compact umbrella and sunglasses. Check a forecast before you travel; these are long-term averages.
