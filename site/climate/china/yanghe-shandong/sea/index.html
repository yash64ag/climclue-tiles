Yanghe, Shandong sea temperature by month

Sea temperature · Shandong · China

The sea off Yanghe, Shandong averages 25.6 °C (78 °F) in August, its warmest month, and 4.2 °C (40 °F) in February. It is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August.

Yanghe, Shandong sea temperature: questions and answers

When is the sea warmest in Yanghe, Shandong?
In August, at about 25.6 °C (78 °F) on average (1981–2010).
Is the sea warm enough to swim in Yanghe, Shandong?
On average it is warm enough for most swimmers (24 °C / 75 °F or more) from August. The coolest month is February, at 4.2 °C (40 °F).
How warm is the sea in Yanghe, Shandong in February?
About 4.2 °C (40 °F): cold for most swimmers.
What is the average sea temperature in Yanghe, Shandong?
About 14.4 °C (58 °F) over the year, from ERA5 sea surface temperature of the nearby open sea, 1981–2010.
Where is the sea warmer than in Yanghe, Shandong nearby?
In August: Xiaying (27.0 °C, 108 km), Taoluo (26.5 °C, 108 km) and Laizhou (27.0 °C, 116 km).
