Shahecheng at a glance

Among the 2105 towns ClimaClue covers in China, Shahecheng is 1308th warmest (14.4 °C year average), 515th sunniest (about 2,480 hours of sunshine a year) and 1763rd wettest (520 mm a year), near the middle on each. Compared with Xingtai, the nearest covered town with different numbers (14 km away), Shahecheng is about as warm in July and 0.1 °C warmer in January, with 1 mm less precipitation a year. Its warmest month, July (27.6 °C), averages 28.9 °C more than its coolest, January (-1.3 °C). Average night lows are below 0 °C (32 °F) in January, February and December. The area lies about 327 km from the sea.

Why Shahecheng has this climate

Typical mechanisms for this climate type, plus facts measured for this place; not a verified local cause.

Dry air allows wide temperature swings; the seasons are often sharp. It sits on the edge of a desert: some rain-bearing systems reach it in one season, but not enough to make it humid. About 327 km from the sea: inland, so ocean moderation is weaker and seasons are usually sharper. Here the coolest and warmest months differ by 28.9 °C.

Strongly seasonal: the wettest month gets more than five times the driest.

About these numbers

Temperature, precipitation and wind: modelled monthly averages (climate normals) for Shahecheng itself, the average of the ~1 km source cells in the 3 × 3 km around its GeoNames location (36.938°N, 114.506°E). Day highs and night lows add the day–night range of the surrounding CRU cell (about 18 km), or on a few small islands the nearest weather station’s; where there is neither, they are not shown. Sunshine, dew point and snow come from coarser sources and describe the 0.1° area around it, about 8.9 × 11 km (ClimaClue area sq-470-2945), which is also what the globe shows when you click there; humidity is calculated from that dew point and the town’s own night lows and day highs. They are model estimates, not measurements at a weather station, and not forecasts.
